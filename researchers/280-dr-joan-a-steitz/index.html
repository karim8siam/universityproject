Dr. Joan A. Steitz

Dr. Steitz is a Lasker~Koshland Special Achievement Award in Medical Science laureate and pioneer of RNA biology, celebrated for discovering small nuclear ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs) and their role in pre-mRNA splicing.
