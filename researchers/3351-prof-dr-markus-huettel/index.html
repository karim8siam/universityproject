Academic focus & active research

Prof. Dr. Markus Huettel investigates pore water advection in permeable sandy marine sediments, microbial nutrient remineralization, and the long-term degradation kinetics of oil spills in coastal shelf ecosystems.

Benthic Boundary Layer Dynamics Permeable Sediment Biogeochemistry Hydrocarbon Degradation in Coastal Waters Marine Carbon Sequestration
