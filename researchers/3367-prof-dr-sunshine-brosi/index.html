Prof. Dr. Sunshine Brosi

Professor of Ethnobotany and Forest Ecology · Department of Biology / Ethnobotany Program · Frostburg State University

Academic focus & active research

Prof. Dr. Sunshine Brosi investigates Appalachian non-timber forest product harvesting sustainability, partnering with Native American tribal nations on traditional ecological land management and American chestnut blight recovery.
