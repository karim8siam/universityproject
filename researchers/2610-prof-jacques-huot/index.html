Academic focus & active research

Prof. Jacques Huot is Full Professor & Hydrogen Storage Specialist in the Institut de recherche sur l'hydrogène (IRH) at Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières (UQTR), conducting advanced research in Metal Hydride Nanocomposites, Solid-State Hydrogen Storage, Cold Rolling Synthesis.
