Professor of Biology and Freshwater Ecology · Department of Biology · Eastern Washington University

Academic focus & active research

Prof. Spruell conducts conservation genetics studies on native bull trout and redband trout, using environmental DNA (eDNA) to track aquatic species distribution throughout the Inland Northwest.
