Philosophy and aesthetics

The official department describes philosophical aesthetics, critical theory, political philosophy, materialism, psychoanalysis, decolonial theory and philosophical reflection on artistic practice. These are departmental themes, not individually advertised funded projects.

The department page also notes a winter 2026/27 professorship representation; check the current teaching and supervision arrangements before making an application.
