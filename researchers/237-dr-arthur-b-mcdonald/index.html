Dr. Arthur B. McDonald

Dr. McDonald was awarded the 2015 Nobel Prize in Physics for the discovery of neutrino oscillations, demonstrating that neutrinos have mass through seminal experiments at the Sudbury Neutrino Observatory (SNO).
