Prof Bernard Tiddeman

Professor in Computer Science and Director of the Vision, Graphics and Visualisation Group in the Department of Computer Science at Aberystwyth University. Research focuses on 3D shape modeling, facial image analysis, computer vision, machine learning, and medical imaging.

Vision, graphics and visualisation leadership

Professor Bernard Tiddeman leads research initiatives investigating 3D shape compression, retrieval, point cloud processing, and the classification of spatial relationships in biomedical imaging. He supervises PhD and MPhil candidates in computer vision and visual computing.

Verified research themes
Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Computer Graphics, Medical Image Analysis, 3D Shape Analysis
