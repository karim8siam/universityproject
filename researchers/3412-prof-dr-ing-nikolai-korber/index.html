Academic focus & active research

Prof. Dr.-Ing. Nikolai Körber directs the BioBind consortium, pioneering multi-phase hydrodynamic modeling of floating sorbent materials and high-speed recovery trawling systems in choppy seas.

Oil Spill Recovery Hydrodynamics Specialized Marine Vessel Design Autonomous Pollution Containment Wave-Sorbent Interactions
