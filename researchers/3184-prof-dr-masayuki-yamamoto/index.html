Academic focus & active research

Prof. Dr. Yamamoto is a world-renowned biomedical scientist who discovered and elucidated the Keap1-Nrf2 molecular pathway governing oxidative stress and cytoprotective gene expression, and leads the 150,000-person Tohoku Medical Megabank.
