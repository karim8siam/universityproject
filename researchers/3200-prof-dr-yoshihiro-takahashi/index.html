Prof. Dr. Yoshihiro Takahashi

Professor of Structural Engineering and Seismic Design · Department of Architecture · Tokyo City University

Faculty directory / institutional page ↗Sourced profile

Academic focus & active research

Prof. Dr. Takahashi researches non-linear seismic collapse behavior of high-rise structures, hysteretic damping dampers, and real-time sensor-based structural health assessment following major earthquakes.
