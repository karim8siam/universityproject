Prof. Dr. Michael Grätzel

Full Professor at EPFL, inventor of dye-sensitized solar cells (Grätzel cells) and pioneer of perovskite photovoltaics, recipient of the Millennium Technology Prize, Balzan Prize, and one of the most cited scientists in history.
