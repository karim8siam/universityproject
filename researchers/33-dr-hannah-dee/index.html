Dr Hannah Dee

Senior Lecturer in Computer Science in the Department of Computer Science at Aberystwyth University. Specialist in applied computer vision, shadow detection and reasoning, robot navigation, and computing education; MBE recipient for services to technology and women in IT.

Official university staff profile ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Applied computer vision and shadow analysis

Dr Hannah Dee's research addresses how computers understand illumination phenomena, shadow dynamics, and scene geometry in natural outdoor environments, with direct application to autonomous robot navigation, plant phenotyping, and visual monitoring. She also founded the BCSWomen Lovelace Colloquium, the UK's premier conference for women computing undergraduates and postgraduates.
