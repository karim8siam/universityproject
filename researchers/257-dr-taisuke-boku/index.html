Dr. Taisuke Boku

Dr. Boku directs Tsukuba's Center for Computational Sciences, having co-developed multiple generations of cutting-edge Japanese supercomputers (PACS-CS, HA-PACS, Cygnus) for astrophysics and particle physics.
