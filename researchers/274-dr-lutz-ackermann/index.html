Dr. Lutz Ackermann

Dr. Ackermann is a Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize laureate and world authority in organic synthesis, pioneering transition metal-catalyzed and electrocatalytic C-H activation for sustainable pharmaceuticals.
