Adelaide Global Citizens International Scholarship

The University of Adelaide rewards commencing international undergraduate and postgraduate coursework students with an automatic 15% to 30% tuition reduction based on prior academic merit.

Official scholarship portal ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Scholarship benefits and tiers

Eligible international students receive an ongoing reduction applied directly to their semester tuition fee invoices:

15% Scholarship Tier
Requires a prior university GPA of 5.5 on a 7.0 scale (or ATAR 85). Applied automatically upon admission.
30% Scholarship Tier
Requires a prior university GPA of 6.5 on a 7.0 scale (or ATAR 95). Applied automatically upon admission.
Eligible programs
Undergraduate and postgraduate coursework degrees across Science, Engineering, Computing, Health, and Humanities.
Duration of award
Full duration of the degree program, provided standard academic progression is maintained.

Application procedure and deadlines

No separate scholarship application is required. All international applicants submitting complete admission documentation are automatically evaluated for the scholarship.
