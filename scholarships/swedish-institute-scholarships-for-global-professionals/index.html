Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (SI)

The Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (SI) is a prestigious Swedish government scholarship funding full tuition, living expenses, and health insurance for master's degrees at accredited Swedish universities, including BTH.

Official scholarship source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Scholarship benefits and coverage

Full tuition fee waiver paid directly to BTH, SEK 12,000 monthly living allowance, SEK 10,000–15,000 travel grant, and membership in the SI Network for Future Global Leaders.
