Acadia entrance scholarships

For eligible domestic and international undergraduate applicants. This is an independent explanation of Acadia's official entrance scholarship information, including award sizes and restrictions.

Published scholarship deadline: March 1, 2027
University: Acadia UniversityCity: WolfvilleReviewed: October 13, 2026

How much funding?

Acadia says its initial scholarship offers range from CAD $500 to $2,500. Some additional scholarships and scholar-bursaries reach up to CAD $80,000 over four years. These are separate award categories with different competitive criteria; they are not guaranteed to every applicant.

Application requirements and cost

Who can apply
Eligible Canadian and international undergraduate students depending on the specific award. Special awards may be limited by need, prior schooling or residency.
Academic scores
Some scholar-bursaries require 80% in eligible admission courses or qualifying transfer scores; do not treat this as a universal scholarship cutoff.
Language
Acadia's international undergraduate page lists IELTS 6.5 overall, minimum 6.0 in each band, subject to waivers and program rules.
GRE and research papers
No general GRE or journal publication requirement for normal undergraduate entrance awards.
Application fee
Acadia's international undergraduate page lists a CAD $40 processing fee. Confirm the current amount at submission.
When to apply
Undergraduate applications generally open after September 21; eligible students complete the scholarship application in their applicant portal by March 1, 2027.
References
Some scholarships ask for supporting documents; confirm the specific award.
Direct application
Always use the university's official application system and verify your eligibility independently.
