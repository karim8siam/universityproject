Aichi Prefectural Government International Student Scholarship

A prestigious regional scholarship program funded by the Aichi Prefectural Government supporting international graduate research students studying at universities in Aichi Prefecture, including Aichi University.

Official financial aid overview ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Funding benefits and coverage

Designed to foster global talent connected to Aichi's manufacturing and economic sectors, this award provides comprehensive financial backing for graduate study:

Monthly living allowance
Approximately ¥150,000 per month living stipend provided throughout research and master's degree enrollment.
Tuition and fees
Full coverage of entrance examination fees, university admission fees, and annual tuition fees.
Travel allowance
Economy-class round-trip international airfare between the recipient's home country and Japan.
Internship and local engagement
Includes internship opportunities at companies within Aichi Prefecture to support post-graduation employment in Japan.

Nomination and selection process

Students must be nominated through their host university in Aichi Prefecture. Universities screen incoming graduate applicants and recommend top candidates to the Aichi Prefectural Government scholarship committee.
