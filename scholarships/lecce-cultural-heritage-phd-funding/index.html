Verified funding, without misleading dates

For its inaugural XL doctoral research cycle, the academy announced four ministry-funded awards across the arts and design research area: two in Design and Innovation for the Environment and Person and two in Cultural Heritage and Contemporary Arts. This was a 2024 launch allocation, not a standing annual quota. The academy subsequently issued a separate XLI cycle competition in 2025 and a final ranking of scholarship winners in February 2026.

Next-call verification steps

  1. Visit the academy's official doctoral page and look for the next numbered research cycle.
  2. Download the current call and its English version, if provided.
  3. Confirm the number of funded and unfunded places, award amount and any mandatory research period abroad.
  4. Check recognition of foreign degrees and the project proposal attachment.
  5. Submit only through the application method indicated in the active call.
