Aberystwyth University Global Citizens Scholarship

A university-wide financial award designed to reduce tuition barriers for international students pursuing undergraduate, taught master's, or doctoral research degrees at Aberystwyth University.

Official university scholarship page ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Tuition discounts by degree level and subject

Aberystwyth University awards the Global Citizens Scholarship as an automatic deduction applied directly to international tuition fees for qualifying admitted students:

Postgraduate Research (PhD / MPhil)

Humanities: £3,000 fee deduction (tuition £17,270 instead of £20,270) + free housing for one academic year.
Sciences: £3,800 fee deduction (tuition £19,070 instead of £22,870) + free housing for one academic year.

Postgraduate Taught (Master's)

Humanities: £3,000 fee deduction (tuition £19,115 instead of £22,115) + free housing.
Sciences: £3,800 fee deduction (tuition £20,020 instead of £23,820) + free housing.
MBA: £3,000 fee deduction (tuition £20,310 instead of £23,310) + free housing.

Undergraduate (Bachelor's)

Humanities: £3,000 fee deduction (£17,400 after award).
Sciences: £4,500 fee deduction (£18,755 after award).
Both include free university housing for at least one academic year.

Free accommodation and additional awards

Eligible full-time international students can combine the Global Citizens Scholarship with the International Accommodation Award, which provides a standard single room in university housing free of charge for at least one full academic year. Students may also combine this with one additional award if eligible (such as the Aber Grad Global Scholarship or International Family Award).
