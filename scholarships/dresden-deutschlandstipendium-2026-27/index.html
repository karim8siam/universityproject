HfBK Dresden Deutschlandstipendium 2026–27

The German-language university announcement lists twelve scholarships across both faculties. This is a competitive student award, not guaranteed funding for new PhD admissions.

Official institution source ↗Independent academic guide

Deadline, value and duration

Application deadline
14 October 2026, as stated on the current German-language page. The English translation shows an older 2025 date and should not override the German announcement.
Value
€300 per month, independent of income.
Funding period
1 October 2026 – 30 September 2027.
Awards
12 total across the university's two faculties; some are earmarked for Meisterschüler, restoration, theatre design or Faculty I.

Who can apply and how

Applicants must satisfy the university's published eligibility and enrollment rules. The university considers academic or artistic merit and other contextual factors including engagement and personal circumstances. An application requires a teaching-staff recommendation; ask the relevant lecturer early. The announcement directs applications to the appropriate enrolled program. It is not a scholarship that automatically follows an overseas university application.

  1. Confirm that you meet the official enrollment and program-specific conditions.
  2. Read the latest German announcement and award-specific options.
  3. Obtain the required academic recommendation and assemble the application materials.
  4. Submit through the linked official university portal by the published deadline.
  5. Confirm whether the award can be combined with other merit-based funding.

Important restrictions

The university says the award may be combined with BAföG but disallows certain overlapping merit-based support. There is no legal entitlement to an award. The published deadline falls on the review date, so availability can change within hours; this page must not be treated as evidence that applications remain open.
