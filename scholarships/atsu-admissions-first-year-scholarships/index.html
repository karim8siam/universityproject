ATSU admissions: first-year scholarship options

Competitive scholarships for newly admitted A.T. Still University students vary by college and application cycle. A listed award is not a guaranteed offer of funding.

Official university source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

How admission scholarship consideration works

ATSU Admissions lists first-year scholarships and states that eligible applicants are notified and invited to apply where an additional submission is required. Its published award table includes university school identifiers, the advertised maximum amount and number of awards. Only eligible students for the named school or program should treat a scholarship as potentially applicable.

KCOM Anne Wright Hazen Scholarship

Published up to US$10,000 per recipient, with eight awards listed in the ATSU admissions table. Selection criteria and current availability must be checked.

KCOM Alamo General Hospital Award

Published up to US$10,000 per recipient, with two awards listed. It is targeted to KCOM, not a general scholarship for all departments.

Arizona dental named scholarships

The Van Gheluwe Family Endowed Scholarship is listed for ATSU-ASDOH at up to US$1,000 with two awards. Separate school eligibility applies.

Action checklist

  1. Identify the ATSU school and program for your application.
  2. Complete admission materials by the correct program deadline; this is not a universal standalone scholarship application.
  3. Check whether automatic review, invitation, or a second application is required for the named award.
  4. Confirm citizenship, standing, demonstrated need or professional mission rules in the current call before making a financial plan.
  5. Do not count scholarship dollars as guaranteed tuition credit until awarded.

ATSU does not publish a single common closing date or uniform criteria for every listed award. Explore other ATSU internal funding ↗ · University guide ↗.
