About OpenStemField

Open academic discovery.

OpenStemField is an independent, global academic and STEM opportunity directory. Its purpose is to make university admissions, language requirements, scholarship criteria, and faculty research understandable and freely accessible worldwide.

Global directory & student community

OpenStemField features 1,662 source-reviewed universities across 10 countries worldwide (Canada, Australia, Germany, Italy, United States, United Kingdom, Japan, Sweden, Netherlands, Switzerland), documenting over 11,000 academic opportunities and 5,000 faculty profiles.
