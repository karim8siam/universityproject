About OpenField

Open academic discovery.

OpenField is an independent, early-stage academic opportunity directory. Its purpose is to make university fees, language requirements, scholarship conditions and public research information understandable without charging for official links.

What we publish

We are publishing one university at a time. Acadia University is the first deeper source-checked institutional guide. The wider 1,800-entry working spreadsheet is not a verified live scholarship database.

OpenField is not a university or a scholarship-granting institution, and mentorship is optional, not a requirement for learning about or applying to programs.
