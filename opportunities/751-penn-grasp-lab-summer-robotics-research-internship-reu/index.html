Penn GRASP Lab Summer Robotics Research Internship (REU)

Ten-week National Science Foundation REU summer research traineeship at the GRASP Lab, collaborating with world-leading roboticists on autonomous drone navigation, multi-robot coordination, and tactile robotic hands.

Academic degree / Prerequisites

Fluent English communication skills.

Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed coursework in robotics, programming (C++/Python), or control systems.

Two faculty recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

$6,000 10-week summer stipend plus on-campus housing and travel allowance
