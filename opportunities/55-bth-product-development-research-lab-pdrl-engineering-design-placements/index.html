BTH Product Development Research Lab (PDRL) Engineering Design Placements

Hands-on research and engineering design placements in PDRL at BTH, focusing on simulation-driven design, digital twin integration, model-based systems engineering, and product-service innovation.

GPA / Academic degree
Enrolled in Bachelor of Science or Master of Science in Mechanical, Aerospace, or Mechatronics Engineering.

Demonstrated English proficiency in technical engineering environments.

Not required.
Published research paper
Portfolio of CAD/CAE modeling, digital twins, structural simulation, or Python/MATLAB engineering scripts.
