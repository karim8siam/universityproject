Doctor of Medicine / Doctor of Philosophy (MD/PhD Medical Scientist Training Program)

Indiana University School of Medicine · Indianapolis, Indiana, United States

Target Discipline: biologyApplication Deadline: December 1, 2026Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

NIH-funded MSTP program training physician-scientists in clinical medicine and fundamental biomedical research, including neurogenomics, cellular immunology, and cancer therapeutics.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Full 100% tuition waiver, health insurance, and $37,000 annual living stipend for all 7-8 years
Tuition Rate
$0 (Fully funded MSTP)
Application Fee
$0 (AMCAS secondary fee waived for MSTP interviewees)

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
3.8+ undergraduate GPA with extensive laboratory research experience
English Language
IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL 100
Standardized Exam
MCAT score 514+ (90th percentile)
Application Materials
AMCAS application, research essays (MSTP significant research essay), official transcripts
Letters of Recommendation
Three to five letters of recommendation (including principal investigators)

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult Indiana University School of Medicine's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
