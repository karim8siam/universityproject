UCalgary Master of Science (MSc) in Electrical and Computer Engineering

Two-year research-based Master's degree at the Schulich School of Engineering offering specialized tracks in autonomous systems, wireless communications, and microelectronics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's degree in Electrical/Computer Engineering with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 89.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate capstone design or published engineering research preferred.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed minimum funding package of CAD $22,000/year for 2 years (thesis stream)
Application fee
CAD $145 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Offset by graduate assistantships and supervisor grants.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Fall intake deadline for international candidates)
