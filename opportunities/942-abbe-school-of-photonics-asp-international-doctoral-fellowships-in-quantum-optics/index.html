Abbe School of Photonics (ASP) International Doctoral Fellowships in Quantum Optics

Doctoral research positions at the world-renowned Abbe School of Photonics in Jena, investigating quantum imaging, ultra-intense laser-matter interactions, and photonic crystal fibers.
