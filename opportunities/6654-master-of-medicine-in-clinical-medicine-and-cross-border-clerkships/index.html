Master of Medicine in Clinical Medicine and Cross-Border Clerkships

Three-year qualifying medical master's combining clinical ward rotations at Antwerp University Hospital (UZA) and Amphia Hospital in Breda across cardiology, oncology, and pediatrics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Medicine (Geneeskunde) from an accredited university in the EEA.
English language proficiency
Dutch C1 (ITNA score B2/C1).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Medical bachelor degree diploma, transcript of clerkship competencies, and clinical health clearance.
Recommendation letters
Faculty clinical dean recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
