Bachelor of Arts in Music and Vocal Performance (Jubilee Singers Tradition)

Vocal and musical curriculum steeped in the historic legacy of the Grammy-winning Fisk Jubilee Singers (established 1871), mastering vocal literature, Negro spirituals, opera, and choral direction.
