Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Information Science for the Deaf

Four-year curriculum at the Amakubo Campus conducted with real-time automated speech-to-text captions and Japanese Sign Language (JSL), covering software engineering, cybersecurity, and computer vision.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation certificate qualifying for university entrance.
English language proficiency
Japanese Sign Language (JSL) or written Japanese comprehension.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
NTUT Special Entrance Examination for Deaf and Hard of Hearing Students.
Thesis / Research statement
High school academic transcripts, official certificate of disability (Shogaisha Techo), and personal statement.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter from high school principal or teacher.
