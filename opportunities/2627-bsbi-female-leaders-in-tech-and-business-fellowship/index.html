BSBI Female Leaders in Tech and Business Fellowship

Empowerment fellowship dedicated to advancing women in corporate leadership, venture finance, and digital tech startups across Germany and Europe.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree with minimum 3.2 GPA and documented leadership accomplishments.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for European business fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
1,000-word proposal on overcoming systemic hurdles in venture funding for female entrepreneurs.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from professional supervisors or academic professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual fellowship grant of €4,000 and executive mentorship with European female founders
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Direct fee remission applied to graduate program tuition
