Scholarship

Bourse d'Excellence Mouvement Desjardins à l'UQAR

Competitive scholarship funded by Desjardins recognizing top academic performers in management, financial analytics, and fintech at the Lévis campus.

University
UQAR Lévis campus (Université du Québec à Rimouski)
Location
Lévis, Québec, Canada
Funding
$5,000 per year plus priority consideration for Desjardins internships
Tuition
$3,200/year (Québec residents) / $9,500/year (Canadian out-of-province)
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
April 15, 2027
Requirements
Enrolled at Lévis campus in Management or Accounting with cumulative GPA >= 3.5/4.3.
