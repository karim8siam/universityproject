MEXT Japanese Government University Recommendation Scholarship at KIT

Prestige national scholarship awarded by the Japanese Ministry of Education to top international graduate candidates pursuing master's or doctoral degrees in engineering, design, or bioscience at KIT.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.3/4.0 in previous undergraduate/graduate degree from an accredited university
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80 (or Japanese JLPT N2)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed research plan (3 pages), official academic transcripts with grading scale, and letter of motivation
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation including dean or graduate department chair

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: ¥145,000 per month (Master's) or ¥148,000 per month (PhD) tax-free living allowance plus 100% full tuition waiver and roundtrip Tokyo/Osaka airfare
Application fee
Free (¥282,000 matriculation fee waived)
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800/year (100% covered by MEXT scholarship)

Application deadline
January 15, 2027
