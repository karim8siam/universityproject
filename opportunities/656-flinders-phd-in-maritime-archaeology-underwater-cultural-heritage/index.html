Flinders PhD in Maritime Archaeology & Underwater Cultural Heritage

Internationally renowned doctoral training in maritime archaeology, underwater heritage management, and digital 3D shipwreck documentation across Australia and the Indo-Pacific.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's Honours (Class 1 or 2A) or Master's in Archaeology, Anthropology, or Heritage Studies.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with 6.5 in writing and reading.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Sample thesis chapter or publication in archaeological or cultural heritage topics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee appraisals.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
RTP-equivalent doctoral stipend of AUD $33,500/year plus specialized diving and field equipment support
Application fee
AUD $100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full international research tuition offset awarded to successful scholarship candidates.
