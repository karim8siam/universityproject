Gates Cambridge Scholarships & Cambridge Trust Awards

Prestigious global scholarship awarded to approximately 80 outstanding students outside the UK annually to pursue full-time postgraduate research degrees at the University of Cambridge.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours undergraduate degree (or international equivalent A / 3.9+ GPA) with outstanding intellectual ability.
English language proficiency
Cambridge Higher requirement: IELTS 7.5 overall with minimum 7.0 in all elements.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence, leadership potential, and commitment to improving the lives of others.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters and one specific personal Gates reference on leadership.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full university and college tuition fee coverage plus annual tax-free maintenance stipend of £21,000 per year, airfare, visa costs, and discretionary funding
Application fee
£50 to £75 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for the entire 3-to-4 year doctoral duration.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
December 3, 2026 (Annual international deadline for Michaelmas 2027 entry; October 15 for US citizens)
