Doctorat de Premier Cycle en Chiropratique (D.C.)

Five-year first-professional doctoral degree in chiropractic medicine—the only university-level chiropractic program in a French-speaking public university globally.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Diplôme d'études collégiales (DEC) en sciences de la nature with R-score >= 30.5 or relevant university science bachelor's.
English language proficiency
French mastery (TFI 800 minimum).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; CasPer situational judgment test required.
Thesis / Research statement
CASPer test scores and official academic records.
Recommendation letters
Two professional references.
