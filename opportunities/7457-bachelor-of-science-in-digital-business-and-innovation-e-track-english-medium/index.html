Bachelor of Science in Digital Business and Innovation (E-Track English Medium)

Four-year English-taught degree at the Ikebukuro campus combining data analytics, fintech innovations, artificial intelligence for business, software entrepreneurship, and digital marketing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation diploma with strong analytical and mathematics background.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79+, Academic IELTS 6.0+, or Duolingo English Test 105+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT/IB) accepted and recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
High school academic transcripts, diploma certificate, personal essay (500 words), and resume.
Recommendation letters
One academic evaluation letter from a high school instructor.
