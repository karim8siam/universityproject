Master of Science in Health Professions Education

Two-year interdisciplinary degree exploring pedagogical theory, qualitative educational research methods, psychometrics, and clinical faculty development in the health sciences.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Baccalaureate or professional health sciences degree with minimum 3.25 GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all sections) or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic research proposal on an issue in clinical healthcare education.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or clinical supervisor reference letters.
