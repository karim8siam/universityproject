Seinan Gakuin Global Citizen Scholarship

Merit award honoring high-achieving international degree-seeking students who foster global understanding and participate in Kyushu community outreach programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum GPA 3.0 in university studies at Seinan.
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or TOEFL 80+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic transcripts, scholarship essay, and report on community participation.
Recommendation letters
One supervising professor recommendation.
