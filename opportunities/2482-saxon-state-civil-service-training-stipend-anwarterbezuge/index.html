Saxon State Civil Service Training Stipend (Anwärterbezüge)

State-guaranteed monthly stipend program providing complete financial independence and civil service health insurance benefits for dual administration students.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
German Abitur or equivalent secondary university entrance qualification with strong marks in German and Mathematics.
English language proficiency
Certified German language proficiency at level C1/C2; English proficiency B2 recommended.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable; candidates must pass the Saxon state administrative entrance examination.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing commitment to public service, public ethics, and democratic governance in Germany.
Recommendation letters
Two character and academic references from secondary school instructors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full state remuneration: €1,550 monthly gross living stipend across the entire 36-month degree
Application fee
Zero application and matriculation fees
Tuition / joining costs
100% tuition-free public education funded by the Free State of Saxony
