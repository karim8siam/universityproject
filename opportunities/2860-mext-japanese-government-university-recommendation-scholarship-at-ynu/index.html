MEXT Japanese Government University Recommendation Scholarship at YNU

Prestigious Monbukagakusho (MEXT) scholarship allocated directly through Yokohama National University for top international applicants enrolling in Graduate School of Engineering or Urban Innovation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 2.30 out of 3.0 on MEXT GPA grading scale
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 80 or JLPT N2
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Research plan detailing proposed laboratory work at YNU
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters including Dean's recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
¥144,000 to ¥145,000 monthly stipend, 100% tuition waiver, and round-trip economy flights
Application fee
Zero application fee for nominated scholars
Tuition / joining costs
100% waived by Japanese Government
