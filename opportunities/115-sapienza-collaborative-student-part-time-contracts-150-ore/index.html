Sapienza Collaborative Student Part-Time Contracts (150-Ore)

Official university part-time employment contracts (150 hours total) assisting in university libraries, computer laboratories, museum collections, and international student reception desks across Sapienza campuses.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled Sapienza student who has completed at least 2/5 of required academic credits with good grade average.
English language proficiency
Italian or English working knowledge according to assigned structure.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
University merit ranking (based on ECTS credits and GPA).
Recommendation letters
Infostud student record enrollment.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried university employment: €1,095 per 150-hour block (approx. €7.30 to €9.00/hr net) exempt from personal income tax
Application fee
Free application submitted via Infostud
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid student employment.
