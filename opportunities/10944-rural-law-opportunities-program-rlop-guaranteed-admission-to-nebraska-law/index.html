Rural Law Opportunities Program (RLOP - Guaranteed Admission to Nebraska Law)

Nebraska State College System · Lincoln, Nebraska, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: November 15, 2026Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Partnership with the University of Nebraska College of Law providing undergraduate full-tuition scholarships and guaranteed admission to law school for students committed to rural practice.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
100% full-tuition scholarship at NSCS colleges plus guaranteed admission to UNL College of Law
Tuition Rate
$0
Application Fee

Academic GPA
3.5+ cumulative high school GPA
English Language
Satisfied via residency
Standardized Exam
ACT score 25+ or SAT 1200+
Application Materials
RLOP application essay on rural legal needs in Nebraska, transcripts
Letters of Recommendation
Three letters of recommendation (one attorney or judge preferred)
