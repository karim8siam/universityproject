HFT Stuttgart Master of Science in Photogrammetry & Geoinformatics

English-taught master's program training geoinformatics specialists in computer vision, autonomous mapping, and geographic information science.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Above-average bachelor's degree in surveying, geoinformatics, computer science, or civil engineering
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 85
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose detailing geospatial mapping experience
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
DAAD EPOS scholarship eligibility providing €934/month living stipend and full health insurance
Application fee
€180 semester administrative fee
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition-free for EU/EEA citizens; €1,500/semester for non-EU students (Baden-Württemberg state law)
