Maastricht PhD in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (Salaried)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment at the Department of Advanced Computing Sciences (DACS) investigating neural symbolic AI, reinforcement learning, and complex networks.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics, or Data Science with high distinction.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or conference papers in deep learning, game theory, or automated reasoning.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried employee contract (CAO-NU): €2,770 to €3,539/month gross plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% annual bonus for 4 years
Application fee
€0 application fee for salaried positions
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition fees (employed doctoral position).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 31, 2027 (Rolling department call)
