Glasgow Master of Science (MSc) in Aerospace Engineering

One-year advanced degree accredited by the Royal Aeronautical Society, exploring autonomous flight controls, space propulsion systems, and aero-elastic structural design.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or international GPA 3.3/4.0) in Aeronautical, Aerospace, or Mechanical Engineering.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all skills) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prerequisites in flight dynamics, structural mechanics, and thermodynamics.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.
