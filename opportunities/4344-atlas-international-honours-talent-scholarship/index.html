ATLAS International Honours Talent Scholarship

Highly competitive scholarship for top-ranking non-EU/EEA applicants admitted to the residential Bachelor of Science Technology and Liberal Arts (ATLAS) program.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school completion (VWO, IB with 34+ points, or A-Levels with minimum ABB) including advanced Mathematics and Physics
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 100
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Motivational video and self-reflection letter explaining passion for integrating engineering with social sciences
Recommendation letters
One mathematics or physics teacher recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to €4,000 per year towards tuition fees for all three years of the program (€12,000 total)
Application fee
€0 (free selective application)
Tuition / joining costs
€4,660/year (EU/EEA intensive statutory rate) or €13,750/year (Non-EU/EEA)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027
