Wageningen University & Research WIAS Doctoral Fellowship at WBVR

Prestige four-year salaried doctoral research appointment at Wageningen Bioveterinary Research in Lelystad, developing next-generation recombinant vaccines against highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI).

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science degree with distinction in Veterinary Medicine, Virology, Molecular Biology, or Biotechnology
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in speaking and writing) or TOEFL 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, master's thesis abstract, and 3-page research motivation statement
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from virology or veterinary researchers

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: €2,770 to €3,539 per month gross salary (CAO Wageningen Research scale) plus 8% holiday allowance and end-of-year bonus
Application fee
Free
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Full Dutch social security, health, and pension coverage included.
