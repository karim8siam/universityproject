Policlinico Universitario Agostino Gemelli IRCCS Clinical Research Placements

World-class translational and clinical oncology research placements inside Policlinico Gemelli IRCCS in Rome, investigating personalized cancer therapies, molecular biomarkers, and innovative immunotherapy regimens.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduate degree in Medicine, Biotechnology, Molecular Biology, or Pharmacy with top marks.
English language proficiency
Working English fluency for international biomedical publishing.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Experience in oncology cell culture, genomic sequencing, or clinical trial methodology.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, publication list, and two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded research positions supported by Italian Ministry of Health (Ricerca Corrente) and EU Horizon Europe
Application fee
Free application directly to affiliated principal investigators
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid clinical research position.
