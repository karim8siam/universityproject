Kyoto University Yukawa Institute for Theoretical Physics (YITP) Placements

World-leading theoretical physics research placements inside YITP in Kyoto (founded by Nobel laureate Hideki Yukawa), participating in quantum field theory, superstring theory, and gravitational wave cosmology.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduate student in Theoretical Physics, High-Energy Physics, or String Theory with distinction.
English language proficiency
Technical English fluency for international collaboration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Published or pre-print research in quantum gravity, particle physics, or non-equilibrium statistical mechanics.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, publication list, and supervisor endorsement.
