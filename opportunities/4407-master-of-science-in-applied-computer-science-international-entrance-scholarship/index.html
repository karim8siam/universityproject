Master of Science in Applied Computer Science International Entrance Scholarship

Thuringian ministry-sponsored merit award supporting exceptional international students admitted to the English-language M.Sc. in Applied Computer Science.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed bachelor's degree in computer science or related discipline with grade 2.5 (German scale) or better
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation letter and certified transcripts with English translations
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation
