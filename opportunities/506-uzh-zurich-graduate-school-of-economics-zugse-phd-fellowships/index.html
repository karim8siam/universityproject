UZH Zurich Graduate School of Economics (ZUGSE) PhD Fellowships

Five-year doctoral program ranked among the top 3 in continental Europe, training researchers in microeconomic theory, macroeconomics, behavioral economics, and neuro-economics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's or superior Bachelor's in Economics, Mathematics, or Statistics with top grades.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Mandatory GRE General test score (quantitative score 165+).
Thesis / Research statement
Research paper or Master's thesis demonstrating analytical and mathematical capability.
Recommendation letters
Two to three academic letters of evaluation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Guaranteed annual stipend of CHF 44,000/year for year 1, followed by teaching/research assistantships (CHF 50,000+/year) for years 2 to 5
Application fee
CHF 100 foreign application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Nominal Swiss semester fee waived or covered.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Annual international intake deadline)
