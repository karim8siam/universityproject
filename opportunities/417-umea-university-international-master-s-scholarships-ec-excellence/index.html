Umeå University International Master's Scholarships (EC Excellence)

Umeå University's premier merit-based scholarship program for citizens of non-EU/EEA countries, awarding tuition fee remissions to top international scholars.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top-tier academic marks (typically top 10% of class, GPA 3.7+ equivalent) from recognized universities.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in each component) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence and strong scholarship motivation statement.
Recommendation letters
Applied as first choice program on Universityadmissions.se.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition waiver: Full or partial tuition fee remissions covering 100% or 50% of international tuition fees for Master's studies
Application fee
SEK 900 national application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition covered for top-ranked scholarship awardees.
