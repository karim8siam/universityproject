Vector Institute for Artificial Intelligence Research Placements

Frontier research placements inside the Vector Institute in downtown Toronto, collaborating with world-leading machine learning researchers on foundational neural architectures, AI safety, and health informatics.

Application checklist & requirements

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduate student in Computer Science, Statistics, or Applied Mathematics with top-tier grades.
English language proficiency
Fluent technical English communication.

Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Published or submitted research in deep learning, generative modeling, or reinforcement learning.
Recommendation letters
