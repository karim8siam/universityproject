Uni Bonn Center for Development Research (ZEF) Summer Research Fellowship

Three-month summer research opportunity at ZEF engaging with international interdisciplinary development projects in sub-Saharan Africa, Central Asia, and Latin America.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in graduate studies in agricultural economics, environmental sciences, or geography.
English language proficiency
English fluency required.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated interest in sustainable land use, water governance, or food security in developing regions.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.
