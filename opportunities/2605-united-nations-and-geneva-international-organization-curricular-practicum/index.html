United Nations and Geneva International Organization Curricular Practicum

Structured curricular internship embedding master's students within United Nations agencies, permanent diplomatic missions, and international human rights secretariats in Geneva.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completion of Year 1 of Master's program in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Full professional English fluency; working knowledge of French, Spanish, or Arabic desirable.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for international organization internships.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and cover letter tailored to specific UN agencies (UNOG, UNHCR, UNCTAD, WHO).
Recommendation letters
Approval from the IHEID Career Services Directorate.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
