Sapienza PhD Fellowships (Borse di Dottorato di Ricerca)

Three-year ministerial and university doctoral scholarships covering living stipends, international research mobility supplements, and full tuition waivers for top-ranking candidates in the annual public doctoral competition.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Second-cycle university Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or international equivalent 120 ECTS) with high graduation mark (105/110+).
English language proficiency
CEFR B2 level English proficiency (certified or tested during oral interview).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Original doctoral research project proposal (3–5 pages) and Master's thesis.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters submitted online by university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual gross scholarship of €16,243 per year for 3 years, plus a 50% increase for research conducted abroad and dedicated 10% research budget
Application fee
€10 Infostud concorso registration fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived for all admitted doctoral fellowship recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
June 20, 2027 (Annual national call for doctoral admissions - Ciclo XLIII)
