Western Master of Engineering Science (MESc) in Civil & Wind Engineering

Two-year research-based Master's degree working directly with the Boundary Layer Wind Tunnel Laboratory and the WindEEE Dome, studying severe storm effects on high-rise structures and bridges.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor of Engineering with minimum 78% average over the last two years.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section) or TOEFL iBT 86.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate capstone design or structural mechanics laboratory experience.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed minimum funding of CAD $22,000/year for 2 years (thesis stream)
Application fee
CAD $150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Offset through graduate research assistantships.
