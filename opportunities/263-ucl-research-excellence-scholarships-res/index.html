UCL Research Excellence Scholarships (RES)

UCL's premier doctoral fellowship awarded to 40 outstanding international and domestic PhD scholars annually, providing comprehensive tuition fee coverage and £22,278/year maintenance.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours undergraduate degree (or international equivalent A / 3.8+ GPA) or Master's with Distinction.
English language proficiency
UCL Level 2 requirement: IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all elements.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated exceptional research potential through published work, pre-prints, or undergraduate thesis.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports submitted directly via the UCL application portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full tuition fees (home or overseas rate) for 3 years plus annual tax-free living stipend of £22,278 per year and £1,000 annual research consumables budget
Application fee
Free application for doctoral research programs
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for the entire 3-year duration of the PhD.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Annual deadline for 2027 doctoral entry scholarship consideration)
