Mecklenburg-Vorpommern State Public Administration Civil Service Bursary

Guaranteed monthly civil service stipend (Anwärterbezüge) provided by the State of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern for public administration students.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
General higher education entrance qualification (Abitur or Fachhochschulreife) with good marks in German and mathematics.
English language proficiency
German language competency at C1/C2 level required for German administrative legal service.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; centralized state civil service entrance test administered.
Thesis / Research statement
Certified copies of educational credentials, CV, and clean police certificate.
Recommendation letters
Two character or school evaluations.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
