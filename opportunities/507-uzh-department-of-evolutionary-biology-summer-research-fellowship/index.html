UZH Department of Evolutionary Biology Summer Research Fellowship

Eight-week mentored research internship investigating Alpine ecosystem responses to climatic variations and biodiversity conservation across Swiss field stations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Academic degree / Prerequisites

English language proficiency
Good English conversational skills.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Interest in Alpine biodiversity, population genetics, or ecosystem dynamics.
Recommendation letters
One professor reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
