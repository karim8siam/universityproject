Brockhouse Institute for Materials Research (BIMR) Research Placements

Interdisciplinary research placements inside Canada's premier materials research facility, collaborating on electron microscopy, superconducting materials, and nuclear engineering.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class Bachelor's or Master's degree in materials science, physics, or chemistry (80%+ average).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 88+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Solid experimental foundations in electron microscopy, crystallography, or quantum materials.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters confirming laboratory research aptitude.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded research appointments: Competitive graduate stipend plus specialized microscopy and cleanroom access
Application fee
Standard departmental application fee applies
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition coverage provided through BIMR partner departments.
