Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D.) Professional Degree and Clinical Residency

Four-year ACPE-accredited Doctor of Pharmacy program providing over 1,700 hours of clinical rotations across hospital systems, ambulatory clinics, and pharmacogenomic research labs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 2.75 cumulative undergraduate GPA in prerequisite science coursework (organic chemistry, anatomy, microbiology).
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 for international students.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
PCAT optional.
Thesis / Research statement
PharmCAS application, personal statement, and on-campus or virtual faculty interview.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation (one from a science professor or pharmacist).

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
$10,000–$20,000 annual School of Pharmacy merit scholarships awarded upon admission
Application fee
$65 USD application fee
Tuition / joining costs
$42,500 USD annual professional pharmacy tuition
