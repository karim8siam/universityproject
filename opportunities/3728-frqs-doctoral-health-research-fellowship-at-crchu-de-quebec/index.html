FRQS Doctoral Health Research Fellowship at CRCHU de Québec

Competitive doctoral research fellowship supporting exceptional scholars in molecular oncology, neurobiology, and regenerative medicine.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class standing (minimum 3.7/4.3 GPA or equivalent in previous graduate studies)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or CEFR B2/C1 French proficiency for departmental enrollment
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, research proposal (3 pages), and verified undergraduate/graduate transcripts
Recommendation letters
3 letters of recommendation from academic laboratory supervisors
