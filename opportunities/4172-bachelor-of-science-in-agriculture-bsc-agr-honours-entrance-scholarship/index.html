Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (BSc Agr) Honours Entrance Scholarship

Competitive entrance award for incoming undergraduate students entering the four-year Bachelor of Science in Agriculture degree.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 85% high school average in Grade 12 English, Advanced Functions, Biology, and Chemistry
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 with no band score below 6.0
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Student Profile Form (SPF) demonstrating extracurricular passion for agri-food
Recommendation letters
One high school counselor or agriculture teacher reference

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $4,000 one-time award deducted from first-year tuition
Application fee
CAD $156 (OUAC 105 or 101 application fee)
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $7,500/year (Domestic) or CAD $34,800/year (International)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 1, 2027
