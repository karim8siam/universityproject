Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) Research Internship Pathway

Research internship and project training pathway at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) in Lot Fourteen, Adelaide, conducting world-leading research in visual question answering, robotic vision, medical machine learning, and foundation models.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / CGPA
High distinction academic record (GPA 6.0/7.0 or first-class honours equivalent in computing or mathematics).
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 sub-scores).
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Software portfolio, GitHub repository, or prior scientific paper in machine learning or computer vision.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters, CV detailing algorithmic and mathematical expertise, and research statement.
