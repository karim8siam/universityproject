McMaster Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) & Research Fellowships

Unionized teaching assistantships providing graduate scholars with university instructional experience, laboratory demonstration duties, and competitive hourly wages.

Admitted and registered graduate student in good standing.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated spoken English fluency.

Not required.

High academic marks in the course to be demonstrated or tutored.
Recommendation letters
Assigned by academic department chair.
