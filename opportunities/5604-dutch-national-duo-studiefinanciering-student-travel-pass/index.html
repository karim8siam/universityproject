Scholarship

Dutch National DUO Studiefinanciering & Student Travel Pass

Government student financing funded by DUO including basic performance grants, supplementary grants, and free nationwide public transport pass (Studenten-OV).

University
Regio College (Talland College)
Location
Zaandam & Purmerend, North Holland, Netherlands
Funding
Up to €1,100 per month in performance grants and loans plus free travel pass
Tuition
Statutory national tuition rate €1,419/year (Level 3/4)
Application Fee
Free application via DUO.nl
Deadline
August 31, 2027
Requirements
Dutch national or EU/EEA student with permanent residency or qualifying Dutch employment hours.
