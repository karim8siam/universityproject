PhD in Medical Sciences - Molecular Oncology and Immunology

Four-year medical research doctorate investigating tumor microenvironment signaling, CAR-T cell immunotherapy, viral oncology, and translational therapeutics at Teikyo University Hospital.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Six-year medical degree (MD), pharmacy degree (PharmD), or master's degree in Biological Sciences.
English language proficiency
English TOEFL 80+ or Japanese JLPT N1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Medical/academic transcripts, research thesis, CV, and detailed cancer biology research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from medical professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Graduate School of Medicine Research Assistantship (¥150,000/month) and complete tuition waiver for top doctoral researchers
Application fee
¥35,000 examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥0 with scholarship; ¥700,000/year standard
