USI Informatics Undergraduate Research & Summer Internships

Competitive summer research fellowship bringing international undergraduate scholars to Lugano to conduct independent computing and AI investigations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in 2nd or 3rd year of undergraduate degree in computer science or mathematics with top grades.
English language proficiency
Proficient in written and spoken English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, curriculum vitae, and statement of research interests.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from home university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded summer scholarship: CHF 1,500/month living bursary plus laboratory project guidance
Application fee
Free application via faculty portal
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges.
