Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Engineering: Artificial Intelligence & Robotics

Four-year doctoral research program developing autonomous robotic manipulation systems, computer vision algorithms, and neuromorphic edge computing architectures at ÉTS research centres.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or Applied Mathematics (min 3.3/4.3 GPA).
English language proficiency
French B2 or English equivalent for thesis write-up.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive doctoral dissertation proposal (5 pages) and published papers.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters with supervisor acceptance confirmation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed minimum annual funding package of CAD $26,000 for four years
