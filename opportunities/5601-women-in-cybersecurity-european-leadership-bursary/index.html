Women in Cybersecurity European Leadership Bursary

Diversity initiative offering a 40% tuition fee reduction to female engineers and security analysts entering NIIIS graduate programs.

University
Netherlands International Institute of Information Security (NIIIS)
Location
Amsterdam, North Holland, Netherlands
Funding
40% tuition fee reduction (up to €6,600 value)
Tuition
Applied directly to program tuition invoice
Application Fee
Free application
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
Female candidate accepted into an NIIIS diploma or master's degree program.
