Leibniz Supercomputing Centre (LRZ) Quantum Computing Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research position at LRZ Garching working on the Munich Quantum Valley initiative, integrating superconducting quantum processors into hybrid exascale supercomputing pipelines.
