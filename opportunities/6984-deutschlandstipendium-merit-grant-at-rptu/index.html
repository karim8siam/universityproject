Deutschlandstipendium Merit Grant at RPTU

National merit-based scholarship honoring outstanding academic performance and social engagement for bachelor's and master's students at RPTU.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding grades (top 15% of cohort).
English language proficiency
German or English proficiency according to degree program.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Enrollment certificate, transcript of records, CV, and documentation of extracurricular or community leadership.
Recommendation letters
One professor appraisal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€300 per month (€3,600 annually) co-funded 50% by the federal government and 50% by industry partners
Application fee
No application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition is free; award is paid directly to the recipient
