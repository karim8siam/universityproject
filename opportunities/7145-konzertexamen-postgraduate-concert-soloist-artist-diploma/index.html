Konzertexamen (Postgraduate Concert Soloist Artist Diploma)

Highest artistic degree awarded in German music education, reserved for extraordinary virtuosos preparing for global soloist careers with full orchestral accompaniment.

Master's diploma, concert portfolio, list of competition prizes, and CV.
