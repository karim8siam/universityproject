Uni Bern PhD in Space Exploration and Planetary Science

Three to four-year research doctorate at the Space Research & Planetary Sciences division participating directly in ESA and NASA missions (JUICE, CHEOPS, Comet Interceptor).

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Physics, Astrophysics, or Aerospace Engineering with high honors.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or experience in mass spectrometry, space instrumentation, or exoplanet atmospheric models.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried SNSF research assistant contract: CHF 50,000 to CHF 55,000/year gross for 3 to 4 years
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 750/semester.
