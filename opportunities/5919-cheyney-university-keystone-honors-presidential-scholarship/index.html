Cheyney University Keystone Honors Presidential Scholarship

Cheyney's premier merit award granted to high-achieving incoming freshmen enrolling in the Keystone Honors Academy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school cumulative GPA of 3.50+ on a 4.0 unweighted scale.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80 (or SAT score 1150+ / ACT 24+ if submitted).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word honors essay on community leadership and academic ambition.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from teachers or school guidance counselors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full scholarship: 100% tuition, mandatory fees, and room and board covered
Application fee
$50
