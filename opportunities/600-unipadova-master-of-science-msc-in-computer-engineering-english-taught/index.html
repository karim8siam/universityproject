UniPadova Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Engineering (English-taught)

Two-year English-taught Master's program at the Department of Information Engineering (DEI) covering artificial intelligence, cloud architectures, cyber-physical robotics, and internet of things.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or Electrical Engineering with minimum 70% average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in computer architecture, operating systems, algorithms, and networks.
Recommendation letters
Academic CV and motivation statement.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Padua International Excellence Scholarships covering full tuition and €8,000/year living stipend
Application fee
€30 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€1,000 to €2,700/year (fully waived for scholarship recipients).
