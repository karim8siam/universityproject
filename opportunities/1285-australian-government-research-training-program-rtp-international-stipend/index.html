Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) International Stipend

Prestigious federal research scholarship providing comprehensive funding for domestic and international PhD candidates pursuing world-leading research at UC.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class honours (H1) or research master's degree with equivalent distinction
English language proficiency
6.5 overall (no score under 6.0)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Substantial research proposal and demonstrated publication or thesis output
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports confirming research aptitude

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition waiver plus $33,500 AUD/year tax-free stipend and health cover (OSHC)
Application fee
$0 (direct application)
Tuition / joining costs
100% covered by Australian Government RTP offset
