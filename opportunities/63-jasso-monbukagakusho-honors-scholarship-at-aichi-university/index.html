JASSO Monbukagakusho Honors Scholarship at Aichi University

Japanese government (MEXT / JASSO) financial award providing ¥48,000 per month in living maintenance for high-achieving self-financed international graduate students enrolled at Aichi University.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Minimum 2.30 GPA on the JASSO 3.0 scale in previous academic year.
IELTS / Language requirements
Enrolled full-time under 'Student' (留学) residence status.
GRE / Standardized tests
Not required.
Published paper / Research thesis
Financial need declaration showing monthly allowance from home country under ¥90,000.
Recommendation letters
Nomination by Aichi University International Center following academic review.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded monthly stipend: ¥48,000 per month (¥576,000 per year) for privately financed international graduate students
Application fee
Free internal application through Aichi University International Center
Tuition / joining costs
Combinable with Aichi University's 30% international tuition fee reduction.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 20, 2027 (Annual university screening and nomination round)
