Board of Trustees Scholarship (100% Full Tuition Waiver)

Nebraska State College System · Lincoln, Nebraska, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: January 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

NSCS premier merit scholarship awarding a 100% tuition waiver for up to four years at Chadron State, Peru State, or Wayne State College.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
100% full tuition waiver renewable for four years (valued at over $23,000)
Tuition Rate
$0 (Tuition completely covered for recipients)
Application Fee
$0 (No separate scholarship fee)

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
ACT composite score of 25+ or SAT 1200+ with 3.5+ high school GPA
English Language
Satisfied via Nebraska high school diploma
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Board of Trustees scholarship application, official high school transcripts
Letters of Recommendation
Two letters of recommendation from high school teachers

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult Nebraska State College System's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
