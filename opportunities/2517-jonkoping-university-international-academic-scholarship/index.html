Jönköping University International Academic Scholarship

Merit-based tuition scholarship awarded by Jönköping University to exceptional international students admitted to master's programs in health and welfare systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Strong academic record in prior bachelor's degree (minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2/4.0 or equivalent).
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in writing and speaking).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for Swedish master's degree admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing professional health/welfare experience and motivation to study improvement science.
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Partial tuition remission: 30% reduction applied across all semesters of graduate study
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 120,000/year base tuition; reduced to SEK 84,000/year with 30% tuition scholarship

Dates and verification

Application deadline
15 January 2027 (Autumn Semester International Round)
