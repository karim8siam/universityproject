EPFL Doctoral Assistantships (Doctorat Salarié)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment inside EPFL's 22 doctoral programs, conducting frontier scientific investigations with state-of-the-art supercomputing and laboratory infrastructure.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science degree (minimum 120 ECTS) with high academic distinction in Computer Science or Engineering.
English language proficiency
Fluent technical English for international research collaboration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE recommended for non-European degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or peer-reviewed publication in top IEEE, ACM, or Nature venues.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters submitted by university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried employment contract starting from CHF 54,000/year (1st year) rising to CHF 57,000/year (4th year) with Swiss state pension (PUBLICA), accident, and social insurance
Application fee
Free application directly to EPFL doctoral schools
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Doctoral assistants are paid academic employees.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 (Summer intake deadline; December 15 for Winter intake)
