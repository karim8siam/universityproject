University of Basel Excellence Scholarships & Biozentrum Fellowships

Premier merit fellowship awarded to exceptional incoming international and Swiss graduate candidates, providing up to CHF 24,000 per year to support full-time scientific study at the University of Basel and Biozentrum.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation in the top 10% of undergraduate degree class with distinction.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for quantitative programs outside Switzerland.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic motivation, 2-page research essay, and curriculum vitae.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential academic letters of recommendation from university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual stipend of CHF 18,000 to CHF 24,000 per year plus full coverage of tuition and laboratory research expenses for up to three years
Application fee
CHF 100 foreign credential evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived for fellowship recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 28, 2027 (Strict annual deadline for Autumn Master's entry)
