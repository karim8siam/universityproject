Aberystwyth University MSc Environmental Change, Impact and Adaptation (IBERS)

Specialized postgraduate master's program at the world-renowned Institute of Biological, Environmental and Rural Sciences (IBERS), focusing on climate change mitigation, ecosystem monitoring, GIS, and sustainable land management.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
2:1 Honours degree (or equivalent) in Environmental Science, Geography, Biology, or related natural sciences.
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each sub-score.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Undergraduate research project or dissertation in environmental science, ecology, or Earth systems.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters, curriculum vitae, and personal statement.
