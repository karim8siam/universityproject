Harvard Center for Research on Computation and Society (CRCS) Placements

Interdisciplinary research center at Harvard SEAS bringing together computer scientists, public health experts, and social scientists to explore AI for social good, epidemic forecasting, and algorithmic governance.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Doctoral or advanced graduate student in Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, or Economics.
English language proficiency
Technical English communication fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research contributions in AI for public health, algorithmic fairness, or multi-agent systems.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, research statement, and two academic referee letters.
