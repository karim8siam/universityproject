Canton of Vaud Higher Education Merit Grant and Swiss Health Leadership Award

Need-and-merit based funding program supported by the Vaud Department of Health and Social Action (DSAS) to support talented students pursuing advanced degrees in applied healthcare.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored
