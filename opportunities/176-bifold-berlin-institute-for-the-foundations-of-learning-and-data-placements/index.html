BIFOLD (Berlin Institute for the Foundations of Learning and Data) Placements

Frontier research positions inside BIFOLD, Germany's national AI competence center co-hosted at Humboldt University, developing scalable deep learning systems, data management architectures, and explainable AI.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduate degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or Mathematics with distinction.
English language proficiency
Technical English communication fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated contributions in machine learning algorithms, database architectures, or deep learning theory.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, research portfolio, and two academic referee contacts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded research positions supported by the Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) and Berlin Senate
Application fee
Free application directly to affiliated BIFOLD researchers
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; funded scientific placement.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 for collaborative machine learning research allocations
