FDC International Dental Science Graduate Scholarship

Tuition waiver scholarship awarded to outstanding international dental graduates pursuing doctoral research in oral biomaterials, periodontology, and craniofacial bone healing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation from recognized dental or biomedical faculty with GPA >= 3.0/4.0
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 or JLPT N2
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Graduation dissertation or scientific paper in oral biology or pathology
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from dental school professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
50% tuition waiver (JPY 400,000/year reduction) plus JPY 48,000/month JASSO study award
Application fee
JPY 30,000
Tuition / joining costs
JPY 400,000/year (after 50% tuition scholarship exemption)
