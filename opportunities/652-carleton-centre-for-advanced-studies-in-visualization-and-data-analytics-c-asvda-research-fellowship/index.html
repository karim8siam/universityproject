Carleton Centre for Advanced Studies in Visualization and Data Analytics (C-ASVDA) Research Fellowship

Pre-doctoral and doctoral research fellowship focused on immersive visual analytics, explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), and big data modeling in partnership with national science institutions.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in computer science, statistics, or data science with first-class honors.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional.
Thesis / Research statement
Peer-reviewed research in visualization, deep learning, or high-dimensional data analytics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or research laboratory recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual research stipend of CAD $26,000 plus travel allowances for international conference dissemination
Application fee
No application fee for post-admission fellowship
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition fellowship offset provided.
