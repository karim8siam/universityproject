Sankt Ignatios Foundation International Theological Fellowship

Endowment scholarship supporting international scholars pursuing advanced study in Byzantine theology, Syriac language traditions, and diaspora cultural identity.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in humanities or theology with high academic marks (GPA 3.3+)
English language proficiency
English 6 equivalent (IELTS 6.5)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by Swedish institutions
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation essay explaining research commitment to Eastern Christian heritage
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university instructors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
50% to 100% tuition fee waiver plus SEK 4,000 per month housing grant
Application fee
SEK 900 (universityadmissions.se)
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 95,000/year (fully or partially offset by scholarship)
