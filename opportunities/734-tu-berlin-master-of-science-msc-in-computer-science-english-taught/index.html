TU Berlin Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (English-Taught)

Tuition-free two-year Master of Science taught entirely in English in Berlin, covering artificial intelligence, cognitive algorithms, distributed systems, and computer security.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
BSc in Computer Science or closely related field with minimum 120 ECTS in computing and theoretical foundations.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong coursework in algorithms, complexity theory, operating systems, and computer networks.
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
DAAD Master Studies Scholarships and Deutschlandstipendium (€300/month merit stipend); zero tuition
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €315 semester transit fee only.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 15, 2027 (Non-EU international applicant deadline for Winter semester)
