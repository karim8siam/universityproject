HUS Northern Technology Innovation Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship dedicated to developing advanced intelligent robotics, bio-inspired sensors, and energy-harvesting technologies for cold environments.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, Robotics, or Electrical Engineering completed within the last 4 years.
English language proficiency
Fluency in English or Japanese.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Japanese engineering fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Publication record in international IEEE or JSME robotics and mechanical engineering journals.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendations from international engineering professors.
