Gakushuin Graduate School of Science PhD in Theoretical Physics & Statistical Mechanics

Doctoral research conducted within the Department of Physics at the Mejiro campus, featuring world-leading investigations in mathematical physics, non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, quantum spin systems, and superconductivity.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in physics, applied physics, or mathematics with high marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and published papers in reputable journals or international conferences.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential letters of recommendation from university faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Application fee
¥35,000 application screening fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥980,000/year, highly subsidized through merit-based tuition reduction grants.
