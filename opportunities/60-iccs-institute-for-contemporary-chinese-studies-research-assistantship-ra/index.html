ICCS Institute for Contemporary Chinese Studies Research Assistantship (RA)

Paid research assistantship positions inside Aichi University's renowned Institute for Contemporary Chinese Studies (ICCS), participating in sponsored projects on Asian supply chains, comparative governance, and field surveys.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Enrolled doctoral student in the Graduate School of Modern Chinese Studies or Economics.
IELTS / Language requirements
Multilingual proficiency in Japanese, Chinese, and/or English.
GRE / Standardized tests
Not required.
Published paper / Research thesis
Documented research competence in East Asian regional studies, fieldwork methodology, or statistical databases.
Recommendation letters
Faculty advisor nomination from an ICCS affiliated research fellow.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried research appointment: ¥1,200 to ¥1,800 per hour plus conference travel support
Application fee
Free application for enrolled doctoral researchers
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid hourly research assistant appointment.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 10, 2027 for Spring semester research project allocation
