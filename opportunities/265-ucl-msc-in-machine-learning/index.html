UCL MSc in Machine Learning

World-renowned one-year taught master's degree (180 UK credits / 90 ECTS) at the Centre for Computational Statistics and Machine Learning (CSML), providing rigorous mathematical foundations in deep learning, graphical models, and reinforcement learning.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class UK Honours degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Physics with exceptional quantitative marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in each section.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong coursework background in linear algebra, multivariable calculus, probability, and Python/PyTorch.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university faculty instructors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for DeepMind Scholarships (full fees + £19,000 living allowance) and UCL awards
Application fee
£90 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£41,000 for overseas international students; £19,300 for UK home students.

Dates and verification

March 31, 2027 (Strict competitive deadline for September 2027 intake)
