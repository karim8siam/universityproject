Uni Bonn Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (English-taught)

Two-year international Master of Science degree taught 100% in English, offering research specializations in algorithms, visual computing, data science, and intelligent systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science with minimum 18 ECTS in theoretical computer science and 18 ECTS in mathematics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 87.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework rigor in algorithms and systems.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendations.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free program; DAAD and Bonn graduate foundation scholarships available
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition fees; €330/semester administration fee.
