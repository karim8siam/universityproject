Course

Medicine and Surgery (English Track - MD) Enrolment

Six-year single-cycle medical doctor degree (Laurea Magistrale a Ciclo Unico) accredited by MUR and European medical boards.

University
Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli - Facoltà di Medicina
Location
Naples & Caserta, Campania, Italy
Funding
Regione Campania ADISURC full tuition waivers and up to €7,200 annual cash living stipends
Tuition
Public university fees based on ISEE (€156 to €2,800/year max)
Application Fee
IMAT national registration test fee €130
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
High school diploma, national International Medical Admissions Test (IMAT) score, and English proficiency.
