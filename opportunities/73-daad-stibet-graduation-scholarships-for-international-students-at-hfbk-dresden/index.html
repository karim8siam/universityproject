DAAD STIBET & Graduation Scholarships for International Students at HfBK Dresden

German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) funded financial support awarded through HfBK Dresden's International Office to ensure talented international art and conservation students successfully finalize their degree diplomas without economic disruption.

Official institution source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Non-German enrolled student demonstrating artistic excellence and satisfactory progress toward degree completion.
Language proficiency
Enrolled student status at HfBK Dresden.
Standardized tests
Not required.
Portfolio / Internship
Artistic documentation of current diploma or master's project and financial declaration.
Recommendation letters
Academic reference letter from supervising HfBK Dresden studio professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded grant: €400 to €900 per month for degree-completing international students facing study costs
Application fee
Free internal application
Tuition / joining costs
Full grant support directly to recipient; study at HfBK Dresden is tuition-free.
