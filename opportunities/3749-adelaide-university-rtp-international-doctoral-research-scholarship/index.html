Adelaide University RTP International Doctoral Research Scholarship

Fully funded Australian Government scholarship supporting exceptional international researchers undertaking doctoral study in psychology or education.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class honours degree or research master's with high distinction average
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all bands)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Refined 5-page research proposal, curriculum vitae, and publications list
Recommendation letters
2 confidential academic referee reports

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
AUD $34,500/year tax-free stipend for up to 3.5 years, full tuition waiver, and Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)
Application fee
AUD $0
Tuition / joining costs
Fully funded by Australian Research Training Program (AUD $39,000/year value)
