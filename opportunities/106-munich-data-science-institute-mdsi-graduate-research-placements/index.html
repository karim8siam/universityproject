Munich Data Science Institute (MDSI) Graduate Research Placements

Cutting-edge collaborative research placements inside TUM MDSI, investigating foundational AI architectures, biomedical data science, mathematical optimization, and quantum algorithm simulations.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's or advanced Bachelor's student in Data Science, Informatics, or computational biology with outstanding marks.
English language proficiency
Technical English communication fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated expertise in deep learning frameworks (PyTorch/JAX), mathematical statistics, or high-performance computing.
Recommendation letters
CV, GitHub code portfolio, and recommendation from an academic advisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded research projects supported by the German Research Foundation (DFG) and Bavarian High-Tech Agenda
Application fee
Free application directly to affiliated MDSI labs
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid scientific assistantship or funded fellowship.
