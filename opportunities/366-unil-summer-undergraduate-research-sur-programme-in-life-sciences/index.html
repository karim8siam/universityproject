UNIL Summer Undergraduate Research (SUR) Programme in Life Sciences

Highly selective 8-week summer research fellowship bringing international undergraduate scholars to Lausanne to conduct independent laboratory investigations in world-class faculties.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in 2nd or 3rd year of undergraduate degree in biology, medicine, or biochemistry with top grades.
English language proficiency
Proficient in written and spoken English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, curriculum vitae, and statement of research interests.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from home university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded summer scholarship: Free university student housing, international travel refund, and CHF 1,500 living bursary
Application fee
Free application via SUR online portal
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Annual international summer program application deadline)
