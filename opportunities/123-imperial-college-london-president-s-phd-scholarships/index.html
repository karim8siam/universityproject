Imperial College London President's PhD Scholarships

Imperial's most prestigious doctoral fellowship awarded to 50 top-tier international and domestic PhD scholars annually, providing comprehensive tuition fee coverage, £25,150/year stipend, and research funding.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours undergraduate degree (or international equivalent A / 3.8+ GPA) or Master's with Distinction.
English language proficiency
Imperial Higher requirement: IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all elements.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated exceptional research potential through published work, pre-prints, or undergraduate thesis.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports submitted directly via the Imperial application portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full tuition fees (home or overseas rate) for 3.5 years, annual tax-free living stipend of £25,150 per year, and £2,000 annual research consumables budget
Application fee
Free application for doctoral research programs
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for the entire 3.5-year duration of the PhD.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
November 4, 2026 (Round 1 deadline for 2027 doctoral entry; Round 2 closes January 8, 2027)
