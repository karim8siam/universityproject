Alfried Krupp Senior Fellowship in Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies

Premier residential fellowship (six to twelve months) enabling world-renowned scholars to pursue major monograph or experimental synthesis projects in Greifswald without teaching obligations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Distinguished full professor or senior research director with extensive international citation record.
English language proficiency
English or German fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
10-page research proposal, curriculum vitae, list of five most important publications, and three writing samples.
Recommendation letters
Letters of evaluation from international peers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full academic salary compensation (matching university professorial salary) or €5,000/month stipend, free residential apartment in the Kolleg, and research operating budget
Application fee
No application fee
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition
