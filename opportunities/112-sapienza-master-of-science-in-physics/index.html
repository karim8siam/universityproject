Sapienza Master of Science in Physics

World-class two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) offered in English inside Sapienza's historic Department of Physics, featuring specializations in Condensed Matter, High-Energy Physics, Theoretical Physics, and Complex Systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Physics or Mathematics with solid grounding in quantum mechanics, electromagnetism, and thermodynamics.
English language proficiency
English B2 level certificate or medium of instruction verification.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and syllabus descriptions of completed mathematical and physical coursework.
Recommendation letters
Transcripts of records and academic reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Italian government MAECI grants and LazioDisco welfare allowances
Application fee
€30 pre-selection fee
Tuition / joining costs
Up to €2,924 per year maximum for international students; reduced according to economic family indicator.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 for international pre-enrollment
