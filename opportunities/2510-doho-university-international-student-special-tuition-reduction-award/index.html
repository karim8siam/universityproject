Doho University International Student Special Tuition Reduction Award

Tuition waiver scholarship awarded by Doho University to support international scholars pursuing social welfare, humanities, and arts education in Nagoya.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Prior educational GPA of 2.8/4.0 or upper-third class standing from accredited high school or university.
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 minimum (JLPT N1 strongly preferred for social welfare degree streams).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
EJU (Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students) score recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing commitment to social contribution, community welfare, and living in Japan.
Recommendation letters
Letter of academic recommendation from Japanese language school principal or university dean.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Direct tuition reduction: 30% to 50% remission of annual tuition fees across all academic years
Application fee
¥35,000 application examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥820,000/year standard tuition; reduced to ¥410,000–¥574,000/year with scholarship

Dates and verification

Application deadline
10 January 2027 (Spring Entrance International Admissions Round)
