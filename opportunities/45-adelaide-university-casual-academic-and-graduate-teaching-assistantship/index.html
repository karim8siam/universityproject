Adelaide University Casual Academic and Graduate Teaching Assistantship

Departmental academic employment pathway for enrolled postgraduate and doctoral students across STEM faculties, providing salaried tutoring, laboratory demonstrating, and marking opportunities alongside research studies.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / CGPA
Good academic standing in a postgraduate research degree (PhD/MPhil) or high-achieving Master's program.
IELTS / English language
Demonstrated native or professional fluency in English with effective oral presentation skills.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Demonstrated mastery of foundational undergraduate course curriculum in physics, computing, or mathematics.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, academic transcript, and supervisor approval for postgraduate researchers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried: AUD $54 to $158 per hour for undergraduate tutorial teaching, lab demonstration, and assessment marking
Application fee
Free (Employment application for enrolled postgraduate students)
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid employment under the University of Adelaide Enterprise Agreement.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 for Semester 1 teaching allocation; June 15, 2027 for Semester 2
