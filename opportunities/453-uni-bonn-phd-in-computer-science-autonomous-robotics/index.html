Uni Bonn PhD in Computer Science & Autonomous Robotics

Three to four-year research doctorate exploring mobile manipulation, humanoid robots, cognitive systems, 3D perception, and SLAM in Bonn's renowned Autonomous Intelligent Systems laboratories.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Robotics, or Electrical Engineering with German grade 2.0 or better.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section) or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or conference publications in robotics, computer vision, or machine learning.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded research assistant position (TV-L E13, 100%, approx. €52,000/year gross) including statutory health and social insurance
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition fees.

Application deadline
March 31, 2027 (Rolling admissions; funding allocation deadline for Fall intake)
