President's Scholarship for World Leaders

Merit scholarship awarded to entering international students demonstrating exceptional leadership qualities, academic excellence (minimum 80% entering average), and active extracurricular community engagement.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 80% entrance average or equivalent 3.5 GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (6.0 in writing) or TOEFL iBT 86.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
1,000-word personal leadership statement and verified CV.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic reference.
