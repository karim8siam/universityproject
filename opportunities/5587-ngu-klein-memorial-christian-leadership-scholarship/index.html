NGU Klein Memorial Christian Leadership Scholarship

Endowed scholarship honoring founding missionary Frederick C. Klein, awarded to students demonstrating ethical leadership and social justice service.

University
Nagoya Gakuin University
Location
Nagoya & Seto, Aichi Prefecture, Japan
Funding
¥300,000 annual cash award
Tuition
Credited against second semester tuition
Application Fee
Free application
Deadline
May 31, 2027
Requirements
Recommendation from university chaplain or high school principal; essay on ethical servant leadership.
