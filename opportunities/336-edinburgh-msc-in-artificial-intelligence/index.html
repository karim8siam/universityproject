Edinburgh MSc in Artificial Intelligence

One of the world's longest-running and most celebrated master's degrees in AI, providing rigorous training in deep learning, natural language processing, automated planning, and computer vision.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK first-class or strong 2:1 honours degree in computer science, mathematics, engineering, or cognitive science.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all subscores.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated mathematical foundations in linear algebra, calculus, and Python programming.
Recommendation letters
One confidential academic reference.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for DeepMind Scholarships and British Council STEM scholarships (£10,000 to full funding)
Application fee
£60 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£40,900 for international students for the 1-year program.
