ECU Australian Government Research Training Program (AGRTP) & Cyber Security Scholarships

Premier Commonwealth and university-funded doctoral scholarship providing complete financial support, tuition waivers, and health coverage for outstanding international research scholars.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1) or equivalent high distinction postgraduate research Master's degree.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-test) or TOEFL iBT 84.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research excellence, peer-reviewed articles, or conference proceedings.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential academic referee reports.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Living stipend of AUD $33,500/year for up to 3.5 years, 100% full tuition fee offset, and single OSHC health coverage
Application fee
AUD $0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full international tuition waiver provided through Australian Commonwealth RTP.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
August 15, 2027 (Annual international HDR scholarship round for 2028 commencement)
