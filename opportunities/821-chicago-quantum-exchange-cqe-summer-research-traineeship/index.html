Chicago Quantum Exchange (CQE) Summer Research Traineeship

Ten-week National Science Foundation REU summer research traineeship collaborating with UChicago and Argonne National Laboratory scientists on quantum communication testbeds, diamond NV centers, and quantum optics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate in physics, engineering, or computer science with minimum 3.4 GPA.
English language proficiency
Fluent English language skills.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed university coursework in modern physics, quantum mechanics, or linear algebra.
Recommendation letters
Two faculty recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
$6,500 10-week summer stipend plus on-campus housing in Hyde Park and travel allowance
Application fee
$0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
$0 tuition; all costs covered by the National Science Foundation (NSF REU).
