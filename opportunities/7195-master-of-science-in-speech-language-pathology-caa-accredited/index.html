Master of Science in Speech-Language Pathology (CAA-Accredited)

Two-year clinical master's program boasting a 100% PRAXIS examination pass rate, featuring direct patient diagnostic training in the on-campus Speech-Language-Hearing Clinic and Chicago hospitals.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Communication Sciences and Disorders or completion of prerequisite leveling courses with GPA 3.2+.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 or IELTS 7.5 for international applicants.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE general test required.
Thesis / Research statement
CSDCAS application, personal statement, official transcripts, and clinical observation records.
Recommendation letters
Three academic or clinical reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Clinical graduate assistantships providing partial tuition waivers and on-campus clinical stipends
Application fee
$60 application fee (via CSDCAS)
Tuition / joining costs
$975 per credit hour (60 credits total)
