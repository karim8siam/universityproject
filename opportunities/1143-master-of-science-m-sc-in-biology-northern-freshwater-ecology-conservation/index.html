Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Biology (Northern Freshwater Ecology & Conservation)

Thesis-based two-year graduate degree exploring fish population genomics, toxic cyanobacterial bloom mitigation, and boreal wetland bio-monitoring along the Great Lakes basin.
