THUAS Master of Science (MSc) in International Communication Management (English-Taught)

One-year intensive Master's program training strategic communication directors in crisis communication, cross-cultural stakeholder management, and reputation governance in international NGOs and corporations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in communication, business, journalism, or social sciences.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Practical communication portfolio or policy essay.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Application fee
€100 handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
