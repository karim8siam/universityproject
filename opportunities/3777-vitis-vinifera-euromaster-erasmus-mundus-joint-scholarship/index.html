Vitis-Vinifera EuroMaster Erasmus Mundus Joint Scholarship

Elite European Commission scholarship supporting outstanding international graduates undertaking the joint European Master in Viticulture and Enology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
BSc in agriculture, enology, biology, food technology, or biochemistry with top-quartile grades
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90 (English instruction)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
CV, statement of motivation, and verified record of at least 4 weeks of hands-on vineyard/winery work
Recommendation letters
2 academic letters of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to €1,400 per month living allowance, full tuition fee coverage, and €3,000 annual travel stipend
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
Fully funded under EU Erasmus Mundus funding (standard fee €9,000/year for non-EU students)
