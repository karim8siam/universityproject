Policlinico di Napoli Cardiovascular Clinical Clerkship

Three-month clinical residency rotation in cardiology, catheterization laboratories, and thoracic surgery at the Sant'Andrea delle Dame hospital complex.

University
Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli - Facoltà di Medicina
Location
Naples & Caserta, Campania, Italy
Funding
Erasmus+ clinical mobility grant eligible (€600-€750/month)
Tuition
Included in medical degree enrolment
Application Fee
None
Deadline
April 15, 2027
Requirements
Enrolled in Years 4-6 of Medicine and Surgery with completed pathology and clinical pharmacology.
