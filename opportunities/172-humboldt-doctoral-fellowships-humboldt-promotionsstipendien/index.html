Humboldt Doctoral Fellowships (Humboldt-Promotionsstipendien)

Merit-based doctoral fellowships funded by Humboldt University and foundation endowments, supporting outstanding doctoral researchers conducting innovative scientific investigations across Berlin.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Master of Science degree with above-average grade (German equivalent 'gut' or 'sehr gut', 2.0 or better).
English language proficiency
English or German proficiency matching the doctoral school working language.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis and 5-page doctoral research Exposé.
Recommendation letters
Two academic evaluation letters from university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Monthly living stipend of €1,450 to €1,750 per month plus research travel allowance for up to three years of doctoral research
Application fee
Free scholarship application through the Humboldt Graduate School
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Public university third-cycle education in Berlin.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 for Summer semester doctoral award rounds
