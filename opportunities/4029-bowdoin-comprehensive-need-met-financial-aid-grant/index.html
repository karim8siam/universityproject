Bowdoin Comprehensive Need-Met Financial Aid Grant

Historic financial aid initiative guaranteeing 100% of demonstrated financial need is met with grants rather than loans for every admitted domestic and international undergraduate student.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Demonstrated top-tier academic rigor and leadership in high school curriculum (unweighted GPA typically 3.9+)
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 105+ for non-native English speakers
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required (Undergraduate admissions)
Thesis / Research statement
Common Application or Coalition Application, Bowdoin supplemental essays, and official high school transcripts
Recommendation letters
Counselor recommendation and two academic teacher evaluations

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
100% demonstrated financial need met with zero loans (grants typically range from $40,000 to $82,000/year covering tuition, room, and board)
Application fee
$65 (need-based fee waiver easily requested)
Tuition / joining costs
$66,500/year (fully offset by need-based grant funding for qualifying families)
