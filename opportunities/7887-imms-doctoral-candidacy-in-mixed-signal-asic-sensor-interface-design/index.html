IMMS Doctoral Candidacy in Mixed-Signal ASIC & Sensor Interface Design

Fully salaried doctoral research employment (TV-L E13) developing ultra-low-power analog front-ends, high-resolution ADCs, and integrated sensor interfaces for automotive and biomedical applications.
