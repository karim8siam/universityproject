BAdW Postdoctoral Fellowship in Quantum Nanophotonics and Diamond Defects

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment developing optically active nitrogen-vacancy (NV) and silicon-vacancy colour centres in diamond for nanoscale quantum sensing and magnetic resonance.
