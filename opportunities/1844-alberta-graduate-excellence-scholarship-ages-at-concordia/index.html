Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES) at Concordia

Provincial merit award recognizing outstanding domestic and international students pursuing master's and doctoral degrees across all disciplines at CUE.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.5 GPA in preceding graduate or undergraduate coursework
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement demonstrating academic excellence and community impact in Alberta
Recommendation letters
Nomination by CUE Graduate Scholarship Committee
