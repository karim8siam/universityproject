Master of Oral Health Sciences in Geriatric Dental Care & Dysphagia

Two-year graduate curriculum specializing in interprofessional oral health management, videofluoroscopic swallowing assessment, and systemic infection prevention in elderly care.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in dental hygiene, nursing, or health sciences with GPA 2.8+
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 (Japanese) or IELTS 6.0 (English)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of interest in geriatric oral rehabilitation and swallowing dysfunction
Recommendation letters
One academic and one professional supervisor letter
