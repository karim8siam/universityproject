Master of Science (M.Sc.) in International Real Estate & Asset Finance

Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS) accredited postgraduate degree analyzing commercial real estate valuation, portfolio securitization, REIT structures, and sustainable property investments.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Real Estate, Business, or Architecture with minimum 2.3 GPA.
English language proficiency
English B2/C1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic transcript, letter of motivation, and essay on commercial real estate trends.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or employer recommendations.
