Todai PhD in Information Science and Technology

Three-year doctoral curriculum inside the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology at Hongo campus, offering world-class research in computer science, mathematical informatics, and intelligent systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Mathematical Informatics with high marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General test scores accepted.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis and 3-page doctoral research proposal matching faculty research laboratories.
Recommendation letters
Prior contact and informal acceptance letter from a graduate faculty supervisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Eligible for JST SPRING fellowships (¥2,900,000/yr) and departmental research assistantships
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800 per year (tuition exemption available based on academic merit).
