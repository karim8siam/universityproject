Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) MD-PhD Fellowship at USZ

Highly competitive national fellowship for physicians pursuing translational doctoral research in precision oncology, cardiovascular science, or immunology at USZ.

Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated clinical research experience and peer-reviewed publication
