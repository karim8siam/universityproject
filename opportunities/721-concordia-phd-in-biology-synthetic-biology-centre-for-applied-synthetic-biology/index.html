Concordia PhD in Biology & Synthetic Biology (Centre for Applied Synthetic Biology)

Pioneering doctoral training at Canada's first Centre for Applied Synthetic Biology (CASB), engineering microbial cell factories for biofuel synthesis, therapeutic proteins, and biosensors.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in biology, biochemistry, biotechnology, or genetics with minimum B+ standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each band).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior laboratory research experience in molecular cloning, CRISPR genome editing, or metabolic engineering.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from previous research directors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full 4-year funding package of CAD $23,000 to $27,000/year plus specialized laboratory consumables access
Application fee
CAD $110 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full-time research tuition supported by supervisor grants.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Annual priority round for Fall admission)
