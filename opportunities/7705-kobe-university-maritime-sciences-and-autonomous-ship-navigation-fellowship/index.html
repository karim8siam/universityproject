Kobe University Maritime Sciences and Autonomous Ship Navigation Fellowship

Graduate fellowship based at the Fukae Campus investigating maritime collision avoidance algorithms, satellite AIS telemetry, and clean hydrogen fuel-cell propulsion for cargo vessels.
