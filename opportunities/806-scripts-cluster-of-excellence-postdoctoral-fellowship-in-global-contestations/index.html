SCRIPTS Cluster of Excellence Postdoctoral Fellowship in Global Contestations

Postdoctoral appointment at the Cluster of Excellence Contestations of the Liberal Script (SCRIPTS), analyzing contemporary challenges to liberal political and economic orders from rising authoritarianism, populism, and global inequality.
