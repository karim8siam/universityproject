Concordia Master of Applied Science (MASc) in Aerospace & Sustainable Propulsion

Thesis-based Master's degree partnering with Montreal's world-leading aerospace cluster (Bombardier, Pratt & Whitney Canada, CAE), investigating sustainable aircraft propulsion, composite structures, and avionics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in mechanical, aerospace, or industrial engineering with minimum B average (3.0/4.3).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework excellence in thermodynamics, aerodynamics, or avionics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Graduate research assistantship (GRA) funding averaging CAD $18,000 to $22,000/year
Application fee
CAD $110 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Approx. CAD $8,500/term for international thesis students before departmental bursaries.
