Master of Science in Global Sustainability Science and Transdisciplinary Ecology

World-leading joint international master's degree with Arizona State University, preparing global leaders in socio-ecological systems, biodiversity policy, and climate governance.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Environmental Studies, Economics, Geography, or Social Sciences with grade 2.5 or better.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, and 1,000-word statement on global sustainability challenges.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic assessment.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Dual-degree funding with Arizona State University (ASU); DAAD study grants available
Application fee
€75 evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €390 semester fee including Lower Saxony/Hamburg regional rail pass
