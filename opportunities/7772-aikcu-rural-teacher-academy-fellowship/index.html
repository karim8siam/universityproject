AIKCU Rural Teacher Academy Fellowship

Pre-service teacher scholarship and mentoring fellowship preparing educators for secondary STEM, special education, and literacy instruction in high-need Kentucky school districts.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Admission to state-approved Teacher Education Program at AIKCU college with 3.0+ GPA
English language proficiency
State educator certification standard
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Praxis Core scores or state exemption
Thesis / Research statement
Philosophy of rural education essay (750 words) and educator resume
Recommendation letters
Two recommendations from education faculty or classroom cooperating teachers
