What this opportunity means

Eligible students registered in Acadia's MSc Computer Science may take two four-month co-op placements after completing two academic terms. This is a degree-linked route, not an independently advertised internship open to any applicant.

The program can require enrollment in an existing degree and may not be a standalone internship available to external students. Confirm current placement rules.
