Higher Diploma in Elite Field Hockey Coaching and Tactical Strategy

Two-year professional diploma training head coaches in circle penetration tactics, high-press counter-attack transitions, team culture dynamics, and game management.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary education diploma and minimum 2 years of field hockey coaching experience.
English language proficiency
Dutch B2 or English B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coaching CV, game philosophy essay (1,000 words), and match analysis video breakdown.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from club directors.
