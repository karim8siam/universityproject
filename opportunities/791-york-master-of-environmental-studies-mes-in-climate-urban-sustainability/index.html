York Master of Environmental Studies (MES) in Climate & Urban Sustainability

Canada's pioneering interdisciplinary Master of Environmental Studies at the Faculty of Environmental and Urban Change, allowing students to design customized research trajectories in urban ecology, renewable policy, and climate resilience.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's degree with minimum B+ standing (77%+).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 95.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Plan of Study statement (1,500 words) defining personalized interdisciplinary curriculum.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Graduate scholarship awards ranging from CAD $6,000 to $15,000 per academic year

Tuition / joining costs
Standard graduate tuition approx. CAD $19,500/year for international students.
