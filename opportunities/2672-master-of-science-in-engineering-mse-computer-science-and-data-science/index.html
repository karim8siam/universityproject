Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) - Computer Science and Data Science

Two-year, 90-ECTS national Swiss master's program providing advanced technical specialization in distributed cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and embedded algorithms.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Data Science with above-average GPA.
English language proficiency
English language proficiency at CEFR C1 level (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 90+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss national MSE programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and technical portfolio detailing programming competencies in Python, C++, and machine learning.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from university engineering professors.
