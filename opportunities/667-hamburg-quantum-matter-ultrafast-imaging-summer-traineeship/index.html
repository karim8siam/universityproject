Hamburg Quantum Matter & Ultrafast Imaging Summer Traineeship

Two-month summer research internship in ultrafast physics laboratories, working alongside laser scientists and quantum matter experimentalists on the Bahrenfeld science campus.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored
