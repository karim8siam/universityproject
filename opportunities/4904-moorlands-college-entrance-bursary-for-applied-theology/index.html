Moorlands College Entrance Bursary for Applied Theology

Means-tested bursary awarded to incoming undergraduate students to alleviate tuition or on-campus accommodation costs in Dorset.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Passionate Christian commitment and minimum 80 UCAS tariff points (or equivalent)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement and Christian testimony explaining vocational ministry intentions
Recommendation letters
Two references (including one from a church minister or community leader)

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to £1,500 per academic year applied against accommodation or fees
Application fee
£0 direct application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£9,250 per year (UK home students) / £12,500 per year (International)
