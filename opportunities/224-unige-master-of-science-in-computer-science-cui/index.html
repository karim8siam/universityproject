UNIGE Master of Science in Computer Science (CUI)

Comprehensive two-year Master's degree (90–120 ECTS) at the Centre Universitaire d'Informatique (CUI), covering Artificial Intelligence, Stochastic Information Processing, Cybersecurity, and Complex Systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Mathematics with solid discrete mathematics and programming foundations.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and descriptions of completed theoretical and practical computer science modules.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation and official transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for UNIGE Excellence Fellowships and campus student tutoring
Application fee
CHF 65 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 500 per semester.
