Tsukuba Master of Arts in International Public Policy (English Course)

Two-year international master's degree preparing leaders for diplomatic careers, United Nations agencies, and think tanks with special emphasis on Asia-Pacific diplomacy and human security.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Political Science, Economics, Law, or International Relations with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose and analytical essay on international security or sustainable development.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
