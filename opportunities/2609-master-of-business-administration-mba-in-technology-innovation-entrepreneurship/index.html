Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Technology, Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Accelerated, practitioner-focused MBA program equipping graduates with advanced capabilities in digital transformation, venture financing, and AI enterprise strategy in downtown Toronto.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA from a recognized university.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 per band) or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT/GRE optional; waived for applicants with 2+ years of relevant managerial experience.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of intent outlining business leadership goals, professional CV, and video introduction.
Recommendation letters
Two professional reference letters from workplace supervisors or academic advisors.
