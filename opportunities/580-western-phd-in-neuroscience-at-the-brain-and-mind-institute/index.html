Western PhD in Neuroscience at the Brain and Mind Institute

Interdisciplinary doctoral program based at the Western Brain and Mind Institute using state-of-the-art 3T and 7T MRI scanners to study cognitive development, memory, and neurodegeneration.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree or 4-year Honours Bachelor's in neuroscience, psychology, biology, or biomedical engineering with minimum 80% (A-) average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated laboratory experience in neuroimaging (fMRI), cognitive electrophysiology, or behavioral models.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed minimum funding of CAD $26,000/year for 4 years plus lab equipment grants and conference travel awards
Application fee
CAD $150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Subsidized through graduate research stipends.
