Birmingham UK Quantum Technology Hub Doctoral Training Studentships

Doctoral training at the £80 million UK Quantum Technology Hub Sensors and Timing, developing ultra-precise cold-atom gravimeters, quantum clocks, and quantum gravity sensors for subterranean surveying.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK first-class honours degree or 2:1 Master's (or international equivalent) in Physics, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 with at least 6.0 in each band.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework excellence in quantum mechanics, optics, or electromagnetism.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university lecturers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual tax-free stipend of £19,237/year for 3.5 to 4 years, 100% full tuition fee waiver, and £5,000/year research consumables allowance
Application fee
£0 application fee for research programs
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition waiver covered by EPSRC and Birmingham Quantum Technology Hub.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027 (Priority round for October 2027 commencement)
