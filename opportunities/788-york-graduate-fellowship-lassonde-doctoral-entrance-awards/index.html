York Graduate Fellowship & Lassonde Doctoral Entrance Awards

York's cornerstone merit-based doctoral funding guarantee, providing guaranteed living stipends, teaching assistantships, and tuition offsets for admitted thesis-stream graduate researchers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class academic standing (minimum 80% or 3.7/4.0 GPA) in previous university degree programs.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for international applicants with non-Canadian credentials.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence, research publications, or strong Master's thesis record.
Recommendation letters
Three academic referee appraisals submitted via the graduate portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: CAD $23,000 to $27,000/year guaranteed funding package for 4 years plus international tuition differential bursaries
Application fee
CAD $130 graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Differential tuition covered through university scholarship awards.

January 15, 2027 (Annual priority funding deadline for Fall intake)
