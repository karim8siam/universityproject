TPC Navitas High-Tech Excellence Scholarship

Tuition award granted to prospective international engineering and computing scholars admitted to Twente Pathway College with outstanding high school mathematics scores.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school graduation with high grades in Mathematics and Physics (equivalent to Dutch VWO level)
English language proficiency
IELTS 5.5 overall (minimum 5.0 in writing)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts and personal motivation letter focusing on technology innovation
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€2,500 direct tuition reduction credited against the pathway programme fees
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€15,200 for the three-term Engineering & Technology Foundation Year
