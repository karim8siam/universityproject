Course

Master of Science in Medical Biotechnology Enrolment

Two-year 120-ECTS master's program training molecular scientists in genomic editing (CRISPR), monoclonal antibody design, and oncology clinical trials.

University
Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli - Facoltà di Medicina
Location
Naples & Caserta, Campania, Italy
Funding
Vanvitelli international merit scholarships (€5,000/year)
Tuition
From €156/year based on family income
Application Fee
University application fee €30
Deadline
July 15, 2027
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Biotechnology, Biology, or Pharmacy with minimum curriculum credits in biochemistry and genetics.
