Clinical Fellowship in Advanced Resuscitation & Critical Care Emergency Medicine

One-year intensive subspecialty fellowship mastering venoarterial ECMO cannulation, traumatic shock resuscitation, advanced airway salvage, and clinical ultrasound protocols.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Medical Degree (MD) with completion of recognized Emergency Medicine or Critical Care residency.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.5 overall with minimum 7.0 in each component.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Medical school transcripts, procedure log, research proposal, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Three clinical supervisor letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $92,000 annual clinical fellowship salary with comprehensive medical benefits
Application fee
CAD $150 postgraduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $0 clinical tuition
