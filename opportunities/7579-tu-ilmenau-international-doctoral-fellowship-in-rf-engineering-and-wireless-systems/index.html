TU Ilmenau International Doctoral Fellowship in RF Engineering and Wireless Systems

Three-year doctoral research fellowship at the Institute for Information Technology investigating millimeter-wave radar, antenna-in-package solutions, and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC).
