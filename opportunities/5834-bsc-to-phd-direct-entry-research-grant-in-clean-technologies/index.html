Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum cumulative GPA of 3.7/4.0 or equivalent upper first-class honors.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional but recommended for international quantitative applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed 4-page statement of proposed doctoral investigations.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters demonstrating high research aptitude.
