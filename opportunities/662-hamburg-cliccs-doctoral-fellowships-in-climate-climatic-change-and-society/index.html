Hamburg CLICCS Doctoral Fellowships in Climate, Climatic Change, and Society

Doctoral funding at Germany's Cluster of Excellence CLICCS, investigating which climate futures are plausible, socio-ecological transitions, and global climate governance.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in earth sciences, meteorology, economics, sociology, or environmental sciences with German grade 2.0 or better.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 95.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated interest in climate feedback mechanisms, societal decarbonization, or climate risk.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried TV-L E13 contract (65-75%): approx. €32,000 to €38,000/year gross salary plus research travel funds; zero tuition
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; only standard semester contribution (€340/term including public transit).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027 (Annual international doctoral intake round)
