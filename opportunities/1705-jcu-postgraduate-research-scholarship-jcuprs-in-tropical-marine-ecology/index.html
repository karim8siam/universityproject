JCU Postgraduate Research Scholarship (JCUPRS) in Tropical Marine Ecology

Prestigious doctoral scholarship funding international and domestic PhD researchers investigating coral reef conservation, ocean acidification, and tropical marine biodiversity.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1) or Master's degree by research with high distinction
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each component
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research capability via peer-reviewed journal publications
Recommendation letters
Two comprehensive academic referee reports

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

$32,500 AUD per year tax-free living stipend plus full tuition waiver and health cover
Application fee
$0 AUD
Tuition / joining costs
$0 AUD (100% covered via RTP international fee offset)
