WUR Wageningen Food & Biobased Research Summer Traineeship

Six-week research placement in the Food & Biobased Research institute developing plant-based protein texturization, precision fermentation, and bio-based barrier packaging.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Current 3rd year undergraduate or Master's student in food technology, chemical engineering, or biotechnology.
English language proficiency
Good English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in food chemistry or biopolymer processing.
Recommendation letters
One professor reference letter.

No tuition charges.
