Master of Arts in Human Rights and Multi-level Governance (English-Taught)

Two-year international master's degree exploring international humanitarian law, transitional justice mechanisms, multi-level human rights accountability, and global civil society mobilization.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in law, political science, or international studies with minimum 80% equivalent grade.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall or Cambridge C1 Advanced.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate diploma, academic transcript, detailed CV, and 1,000-word statement of purpose.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Padua International Excellence Scholarship providing €8,000 fee waiver plus living allowance
Application fee
€30 application pre-evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
€2,600 per academic year for non-EU scholars (sliding scale for EU)
