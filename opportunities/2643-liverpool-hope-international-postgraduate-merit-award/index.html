Liverpool Hope International Postgraduate Merit Award

Merit-based financial scholarship awarded to talented international candidates admitted to full-time postgraduate taught master's programs at Liverpool Hope University.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-Class Honours (1st) or Upper Second-Class Honours (2:1) in undergraduate bachelor's degree.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing and reading).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for UK postgraduate admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing academic trajectory, community service, and career ambitions.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from prior university instructors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: up to £3,000 tuition fee discount applied directly to full-time master's tuition
Application fee
Zero direct application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£13,500/year base international tuition; reduced to £10,500/year with merit award

Dates and verification

Application deadline
31 May 2027 (Autumn Admissions International Deadline)
