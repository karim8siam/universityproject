Scott's Bluff Mississippi River Environmental Toxicology Fellowship

Applied student fellowship collecting aquatic water and sediment samples from the lower Mississippi River basin to analyze pesticide runoff, industrial polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), and endocrine disruptors.
