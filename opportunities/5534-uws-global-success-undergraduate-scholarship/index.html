UWS Global Success Undergraduate Scholarship

Tuition fee scholarship awarded to high-achieving international applicants admitted to full-time undergraduate degrees at UWS.

University
University of the West of Scotland
Location
Paisley & Lanarkshire, Scotland, UK
Funding
£2,500 annual tuition fee reduction across all 4 years
Tuition
£1,820/year (Scottish Home via SAAS) / £16,500/year (International)
Application Fee
Free automatic scholarship assessment
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
International student holding a conditional or unconditional offer from UWS.
