Birmingham Centre for Railway Research and Education (BCRRE) PhD in Railway Systems

Europe's largest university-based railway research center, investigating hydrogen-powered rolling stock, automated train operation (ATO), and resilient high-speed rail infrastructure.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class or 2:1 Honours degree in civil, mechanical, electrical engineering, or computer systems.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Interest in hydrogen-powered trains, railway cyber-physical systems, or condition monitoring.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional engineering references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Industry-sponsored 3.5-year PhD studentship of £20,500/year stipend plus full tuition waiver
Application fee
£0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Fully sponsored by railway industry research partners.
