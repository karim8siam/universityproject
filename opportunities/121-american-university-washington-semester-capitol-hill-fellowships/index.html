Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate (junior/senior) or graduate student with minimum 3.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Fluent spoken and written English communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Resume, statement of policy interests, and writing sample.
Recommendation letters
Academic advisor recommendation and home institution clearance.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Stipend/Scholarship: Up to USD $5,000 program scholarship and paid agency internship stipends ($17.50 to $22.00/hr)
Application fee
Free application for enrolled AU students; standard consortium fee for visiting students
Tuition / joining costs
Standard semester tuition applies; internship credits earned toward degree.
