Master of Science in Environmental Studies (Conservation Biology Concentration)

Two-year graduate degree combining rigorous ecological fieldwork, wildlife population biology, conservation genetics, and regional biodiversity policy in northern New England.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in biology, ecology, environmental science, or related discipline with minimum 3.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose outlining field research experience in wildlife ecology, forest conservation, or GIS.
Recommendation letters
Three academic or professional reference letters.
