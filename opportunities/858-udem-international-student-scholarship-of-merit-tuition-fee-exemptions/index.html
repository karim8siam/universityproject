UdeM International Student Scholarship of Merit & Tuition Fee Exemptions

Merit-based international tuition fee exemption awards reducing tuition fees for outstanding international graduate students enrolled at Université de Montréal.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative GPA 3.3/4.3 (B+) in previous university degree
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (no band under 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; evaluated on academic excellence
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic merit and research potential
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation required
