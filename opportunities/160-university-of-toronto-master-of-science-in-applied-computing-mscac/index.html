University of Toronto Master of Science in Applied Computing (MScAC)

World-class 16-month master's degree combining 8 months of advanced graduate coursework in AI, data science, quantum, or cybersecurity with an 8-month paid applied research internship at top tech enterprises.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Physics with minimum B+ average in final year.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 93.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, statement of purpose, and technical programming portfolio.
Recommendation letters
Three academic or industry letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Industry-funded: Guaranteed paid 8-month applied research internship paying minimum CAD $60,000 pro-rated (CAD $7,500/month)
Application fee
CAD $125 application processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $30,000 domestic / CAD $65,000 international total tuition (offset by paid 8-month industry internship).
