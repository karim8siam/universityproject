Ca' Foscari Master of Arts in Cultural Heritage and Digital Humanities

Two-year English-taught Master's program combining Venetian art history, architectural heritage preservation, photogrammetry, and digital archiving for global museums and cultural institutions.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Humanities, Art History, Archaeology, or Cultural Heritage with good academic rank.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose detailing interest in digital archival methods, 3D cultural modeling, or heritage conservation.
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation.
