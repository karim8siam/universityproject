Waterloo Co-operative Education & Undergraduate Research Assistantships (URA)

Canada's largest academic co-op and research assistantship ecosystem, connecting students with cutting-edge university laboratory projects and leading high-tech industry partners.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative average of 75% in engineering, math, or science coursework.
English language proficiency
Enrollment in good standing at University of Waterloo.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and technical transcript demonstrating relevant course foundations.
Recommendation letters
Faculty endorsement required for laboratory placements.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried placement: CAD $6,500 to $12,000 per 4-month academic term based on program level and discipline
Application fee
No application fee for registered enrolled students
Tuition / joining costs
Academic term fees apply as per standard registration status.
