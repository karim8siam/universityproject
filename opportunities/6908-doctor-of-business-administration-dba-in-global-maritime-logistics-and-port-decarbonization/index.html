Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) in Global Maritime Logistics and Port Decarbonization

Practitioner-scholar doctorate investigating green bunkering infrastructure (hydrogen, green methanol), automated container terminal operations, and EU carbon emissions trading in shipping.
