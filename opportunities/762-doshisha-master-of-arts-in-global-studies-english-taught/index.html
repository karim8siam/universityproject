Doshisha Master of Arts in Global Studies (English-Taught)

Two-year English-taught Master's program in the cultural heart of Kyoto, offering specialized research clusters in Global Society, International Business and Development, and Peace and Conflict Studies.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's degree in humanities, social sciences, or international relations with minimum 3.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic essay (2,000 words) on contemporary global politics, culture, or international business.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
