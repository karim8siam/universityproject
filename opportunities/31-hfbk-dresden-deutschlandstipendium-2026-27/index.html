HfBK Dresden Deutschlandstipendium 2026–27

Twelve competitive national scholarships awarded by Hochschule für Bildende Künste Dresden, offering €300 per month for one academic year across both arts faculties.

Official university announcement ↗Information verified 14 October 2026

Funding value and period

Scholarship stipend
€300 per month (€3,600 per year), non-taxable and income-independent.
Funding timeframe
1 October 2026 through 30 September 2027.
Closing date
14 October 2026 as published in the official German announcement.
Tuition at HfBK
Tuition-free public university; semester contribution of €354.91 applies.

Eligibility rules and submission

Open to enrolled degree students, doctoral researchers, and Meisterschüler candidates at HfBK Dresden who demonstrate academic and artistic excellence. Applications require a recommendation from an HfBK professor alongside academic and artistic documentation.
