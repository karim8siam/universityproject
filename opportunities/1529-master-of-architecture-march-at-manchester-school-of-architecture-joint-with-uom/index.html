Master of Architecture (MArch) at Manchester School of Architecture (Joint with UoM)

Ranked among the top 5 architecture schools globally, the Manchester School of Architecture (MSA) combines the faculties of Manchester Met and the University of Manchester to confer RIBA Part 2 accreditation.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
RIBA Part 1 accredited undergraduate degree in Architecture with 2:1 honours
English language proficiency
6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive architectural design portfolio of 25–30 pages demonstrating urban and technical competence
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from architecture faculty
