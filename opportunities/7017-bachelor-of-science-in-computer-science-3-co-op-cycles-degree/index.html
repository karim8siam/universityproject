Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (3 Co-Op Cycles Degree)

Top-tier 5-year bachelor's degree featuring three distinct 6-month full-time paid industry co-op employment periods at tech firms like Google, Microsoft, Lockheed Martin, and Comcast.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school GPA 3.6+ with four years of high school mathematics including calculus.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 7.0 for international applicants.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; SAT/ACT optional.
Thesis / Research statement
Common Application, high school transcript, personal essay, and resume of technical activities.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation (one counselor, one math/science teacher).

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Paid co-op earnings (average $20,000 per 6-month co-op cycle; total up to $60,000) plus Drexel Founder's merit scholarships up to $25,000/year
Application fee
$65 application fee (fee waivers available)
Tuition / joining costs
$58,965 per year

Application deadline
January 15, 2027
