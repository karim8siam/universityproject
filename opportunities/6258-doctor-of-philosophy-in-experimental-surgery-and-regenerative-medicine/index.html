Doctor of Philosophy in Experimental Surgery and Regenerative Medicine

Four-year doctoral program providing rigorous training in biomaterials engineering, 3D bioprinting of cartilage and microvasculature, and surgical robotics in UAH operating research suites.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Biomedical Engineering, Surgery, or Biology (minimum 3.5 GPA).
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (6.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for candidates with Canadian master's degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Three-page doctoral dissertation research proposal and verified publications.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters with supervisor confirmation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
