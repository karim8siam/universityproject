Cook Inlet Marine Mammal & Beluga Whale Acoustic Monitoring Project

Marine bioacoustic investigation deploying seabed hydrophone moorings to record vocalizations and foraging ecology of endangered Cook Inlet beluga whales relative to commercial ship traffic.
