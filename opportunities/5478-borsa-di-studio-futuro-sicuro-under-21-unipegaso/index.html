Scholarship

Borsa di Studio 'Futuro Sicuro' Under 21 UniPegaso

Special promotional tuition scholarship reducing annual fees for high school graduates aged between 17 and 21 enrolling in an undergraduate degree.

University
Università Telematica Pegaso e Mercatorum - Sede di Padova
Location
Padova, Veneto, Italy
Funding
Flat subsidized tuition rate of €1,700 per year (reduced from €3,000 standard)
Tuition
€1,700 per academic year
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
September 30, 2027
Requirements
Italian or EU citizens aged 17-21 with recognized high school diploma.
