Wageningen University Excellence Fellowship Programme (Full Tuition & Living Allowance)

Prestigious all-inclusive scholarship awarded to highly talented non-EEA students pursuing a full-time Master's degree at Wageningen University.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top 5% of your graduating undergraduate class with outstanding grades (GPA 3.7+/4.0).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in speaking) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence and strong letter of motivation.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition fee waiver (up to €39,000 for 2 years) plus living allowance of €11,000/year, health insurance, and visa costs
Application fee
Waived or refunded for selected scholarship recipients
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition fee waiver covering 100% of study costs.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Annual international scholarship application deadline)
