LLM in International Law and Global Justice

One-year full-time intensive master of laws examining international humanitarian law, maritime territorial disputes, international arbitration, and third-world approaches to international law (TWAIL).

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Upper second-class honours degree (2:1 or equivalent) in Law or a discipline with substantial legal components
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall with at least 6.5 in writing
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement outlining legal specialization goals, resume, and degree transcript
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters
