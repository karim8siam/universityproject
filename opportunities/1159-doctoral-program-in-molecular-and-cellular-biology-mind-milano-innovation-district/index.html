Doctoral Program in Molecular and Cellular Biology (MIND Milano Innovation District)

Three-year doctoral candidacy conducted at the MIND Innovation District alongside the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) and Human Technopole, studying RNA biology and epigenetics.
