Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT)

Three-year professional clinical doctorate emphasizing evidence-based manual therapy, human biomechanics, neuroscience rehabilitation, and 48 weeks of full-time clinical hospital internships.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with overall and science GPA 3.0+; prerequisite grades of C or better in anatomy, physiology, physics, and chemistry.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 or IELTS 7.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE test scores required.
Thesis / Research statement
PTCAS application, verified 100 observation hours under a licensed PT, and personal essay.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation including one from a practicing physical therapist.
