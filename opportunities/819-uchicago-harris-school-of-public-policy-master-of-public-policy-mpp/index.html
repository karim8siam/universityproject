UChicago Harris School of Public Policy Master of Public Policy (MPP)

Rigorous two-year Master of Public Policy rooted in microeconomics, game theory, and empirical statistics, training global leaders to apply quantitative evidence to solve pressing social and economic policy challenges.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in any discipline with strong quantitative and analytical coursework.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE/GMAT optional.
Thesis / Research statement
Motivational essay and policy analysis writing sample.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional reference letters.
