Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Four-year undergraduate program emphasizing foundational computer theory, scalable cloud architectures, machine learning, and cybersecurity with access to the Fulton Supercomputing Lab.

English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL 80
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts, ACT/SAT score (optional), and BYU ecclesiastical endorsement
Recommendation letters
One ecclesiastical endorsement and one academic evaluation
