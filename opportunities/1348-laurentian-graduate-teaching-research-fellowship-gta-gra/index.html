Laurentian Graduate Teaching & Research Fellowship (GTA/GRA)

Competitive graduate assistantship support for incoming full-time master's and PhD students across engineering, science, and environmental disciplines.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 75% (B+) academic standing in relevant undergraduate discipline
English language proficiency
6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Academic CV and 2-page statement of research interests
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters submitted directly by referees
