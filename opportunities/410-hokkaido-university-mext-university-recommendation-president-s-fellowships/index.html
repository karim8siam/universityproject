Hokkaido University MEXT University Recommendation & President's Fellowships

Japan's premier governmental and university scholarship scheme, providing exceptional international scholars with full tuition waivers and generous living stipends for doctoral research.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative GPA of 2.30 out of 3.00 on the MEXT grading scale (typically 80%+ equivalent).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 80+ (or CEFR B2 equivalent).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research capability in bachelor/master thesis and strong academic proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation addressed to the President of Hokkaido University.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition exemption plus monthly living allowance of ¥147,000/month (approx. ¥1,764,000/year) and round-trip international airfare
Application fee
¥30,000 application fee waived for nominated candidates
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for duration of scholarship award.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 20, 2027 (MEXT University Recommendation annual nomination deadline for Autumn entry)
