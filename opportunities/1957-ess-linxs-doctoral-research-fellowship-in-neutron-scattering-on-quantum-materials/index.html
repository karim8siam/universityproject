ESS & LINXS Doctoral Research Fellowship in Neutron Scattering on Quantum Materials

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment based at ESS Lund and affiliated with Lund University investigating unconventional superconductivity, topological insulators, and magnetic skyrmions.
