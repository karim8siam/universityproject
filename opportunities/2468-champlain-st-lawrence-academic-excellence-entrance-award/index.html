Champlain St. Lawrence Academic Excellence Entrance Award

Merit-based financial award honoring exceptional incoming scholars matriculating into full-time pre-university programs at Champlain College St. Lawrence in Quebec City.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 85% average in secondary diploma coursework with outstanding achievement in humanities and sciences.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in reading and writing) or equivalent language credential.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for collegiate pre-university program admission.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing collegiate academic trajectory, community engagement, and career aspirations.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic endorsement from secondary school instructors or school counselors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: CAD $2,500 entrance scholarship applied directly to tuition and educational materials
Application fee
CAD $30 collegiate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero base tuition for Quebec residents; CAD $7,200/year for non-resident and international students
