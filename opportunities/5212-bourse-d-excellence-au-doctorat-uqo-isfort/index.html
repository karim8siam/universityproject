Bourse d'Excellence au Doctorat UQO - ISFORT

Four-year funded doctoral fellowship at the Institute of Temperate Forest Sciences (ISFORT) investigating climate resilience in temperate biomes.

10-page doctoral research proposal on forest ecosystem dynamics
