Nagoya University Summer Intensive Program (NUSIP) in Automotive Engineering

Prestigious six-week summer research and study program (June–July) offering international engineering scholars direct laboratory research in autonomous vehicles, electric powertrains, and robotics in partnership with Japan's automotive industry.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate student in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or CS with 3.0+ GPA.
English language proficiency
Fluent working English communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose detailing automotive technology interests and project preferences.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation and official transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: JASSO scholarship stipend of ¥150,000, free university accommodation, site visits to Toyota, and laboratory coaching
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; fully sponsored summer program.
