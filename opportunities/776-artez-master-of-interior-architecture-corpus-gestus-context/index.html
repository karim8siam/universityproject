ArtEZ Master of Interior Architecture (Corpus / Gestus / Context)

Two-year Master's degree in Zwolle focusing on the bodily experience of space, adaptive reuse of historic buildings, and sustainable circular material systems for interior spatial design.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in interior architecture, architecture, or spatial design.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive spatial design portfolio showing drawings, physical models, and material prototypes.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter.
