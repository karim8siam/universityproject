TU Berlin PhD in Computer Science & Distributed Big Data Systems (BIFOLD)

Doctoral research conducted within the Berlin Institute for the Foundations of Learning and Data (BIFOLD), developing next-generation database architectures, distributed query optimizers, and scalable ML infrastructures.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Computer Science, Data Science, or Computer Engineering with high honors.
English language proficiency
Proficient scientific English (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis in distributed computing, database systems, or scalable stream processing.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters.
