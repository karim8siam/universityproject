New College Graduate Scholarship in Physical and Mathematical Sciences

Prestigious doctoral scholarship covering full Oxford university and college fees plus living stipend for doctoral research in physics or mathematical sciences.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class undergraduate degree (1st) or GPA 3.8+ from a world-leading university
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.5 (min 7.0 in each component)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Physics GRE recommended for international candidates
Thesis / Research statement
Research proposal (1,500 words) in theoretical physics, astrophysics, or pure mathematics
Recommendation letters
Three confidential letters from distinguished university professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition coverage plus £19,237 annual living stipend for 3-4 years
Application fee
£75 Oxford graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£0 tuition (fully covered by Clarendon/New College award)
