Swiss Federal Government Excellence Scholarship at HES-SO

Prestigious federal scholarship granted by the Swiss Confederation to foreign researchers conducting cooperative doctoral or postdoctoral investigations in engineering or life sciences at HES-SO.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree with first class honors (grade 5.5+ on Swiss grading scale)
English language proficiency
English C1 (IELTS 7.0) or French C1
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive research proposal and written letter of support from an HES-SO hosting professor
Recommendation letters
Two confidential academic letters of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CHF 1,920 monthly tax-free stipend, full tuition waiver, and Swiss mandatory health insurance
Application fee
Zero application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% waived by Swiss Federal Government
