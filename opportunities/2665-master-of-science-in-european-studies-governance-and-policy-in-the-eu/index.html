Master of Science in European Studies: Governance and Policy in the EU

One-year, 60-ECTS English-taught master's program analyzing EU institutional bargaining, common foreign policy, regulatory harmonization, and democratic legitimacy in Europe.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in European studies, political science, international relations, or public administration.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Dutch master's degree programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation letter detailing research interests in EU policymaking, institutional treaties, or European integration.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.
