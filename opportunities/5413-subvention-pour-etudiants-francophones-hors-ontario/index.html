Scholarship

Subvention pour Étudiants Francophones Hors-Ontario

Relocation and living stipend bursary for Canadian francophone students from outside Ontario moving to Toronto for undergraduate study.

University
Université de l'Ontario français
Location
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Funding
$4,000 one-off relocation grant in Year 1
Tuition
$7,200/year domestic tuition rate
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
Proof of Canadian residency outside Ontario and acceptance into an undergraduate degree at UOF.
