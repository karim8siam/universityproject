Academic Overview & Description

World-leading doctoral program based at the Wake Forest Institute for Regenerative Medicine (WFIRM), engineering human replacement organs, 3D bioprinted skin and bladders, stem cell decellularization, and microfluidic organ-on-a-chip platforms for drug testing.
