HfBK Dresden Student & Graduate Assistantship (SHK/WHK) Studio Pathway

Paid student assistantship positions (SHK/WHK) assisting studio directors with equipment safety, darkroom maintenance, digital media laboratories, and custodial archive curation across HfBK Dresden's historic Güntzstraße and Pfotenhauerstraße campuses.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate student at HfBK Dresden in good standing.
Language proficiency
Functional working proficiency in German.
Standardized tests
Not required.
Portfolio / Internship
Demonstrated technical proficiency in printmaking, metal casting, wood fabrication, or photography.
Recommendation letters
Recommendation from workshop master or supervising faculty professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried: €13.50 to €18.00 per hour for studio workshop coaching, digital fabrication labs, and exhibition preparation
Application fee
Free employment application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; official university employment contract.
