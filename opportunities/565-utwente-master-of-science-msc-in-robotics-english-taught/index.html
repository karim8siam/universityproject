UTwente Master of Science (MSc) in Robotics (English-taught)

Two-year multidisciplinary Master's degree preparing engineers in medical robotics, physical human-robot interaction, autonomous mobile systems, and robotic surgery.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or Mechatronics with strong grades.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all skills) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in control theory, kinematics, and programming (C++/Python).
Recommendation letters
Academic curriculum vitae and motivation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
University of Twente Scholarships (UTS) worth up to €22,000/year
Application fee
€100 handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
€17,000/year for non-EEA students (€2,530/year for EEA students).
