Norman Paterson School of International Affairs (NPSIA) Master of Arts in International Affairs

Canada's foremost graduate program in international affairs, preparing future diplomats, intelligence analysts, and global policy scholars in security, global political economy, and international conflict resolution.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's degree with minimum high Honours standing (B+ to A- / 78-82%).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; introductory coursework in microeconomics and macroeconomics required.
Thesis / Research statement
Writing sample (10-15 pages) demonstrating analytical and policy research rigor.
Recommendation letters
Two academic references evaluating analytical ability and research potential.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit-based departmental entrance scholarships ranging from CAD $5,000 to $16,000 per academic year
Application fee
CAD $110 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Domestic CAD $8,200/year; International approx. CAD $22,400/year.
