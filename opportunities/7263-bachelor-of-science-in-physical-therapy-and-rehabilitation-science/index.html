Bachelor of Science in Physical Therapy and Rehabilitation Science

Four-year professional degree leading to the Japanese National Physical Therapist licensure, covering musculoskeletal biomechanics, neurological rehabilitation, and clinical hospital rotations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school diploma with coursework in Biology, Chemistry, and Health Sciences.
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or higher; basic English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 60+ or IELTS 5.5+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High school graduation certificate, academic transcripts, medical health evaluation form, and statement of vocational purpose.
Recommendation letters
One academic or vocational recommendation letter.
