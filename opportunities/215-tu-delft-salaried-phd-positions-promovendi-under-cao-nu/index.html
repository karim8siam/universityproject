TU Delft Salaried PhD Positions (Promovendi under CAO-NU)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointments across TU Delft departments, conducting frontier industrial and fundamental investigations under standard Dutch collective employment contracts.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in Computer Science, Aerospace, or Electrical Engineering with high marks.
English language proficiency
Fluent technical English communication for international collaboration and scientific publishing.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis or peer-reviewed conference publications.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, research portfolio, and two academic referee contacts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Four-year salaried university employment under Collective Labour Agreement for Dutch Universities (CAO-NU) starting at €2,872/month in year 1 rising to €3,670/month in year 4 gross, plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% end-of-year bonus
Application fee
Free application directly to open vacancy
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. PhD candidates are paid university staff members with comprehensive Dutch health and pension benefits.
