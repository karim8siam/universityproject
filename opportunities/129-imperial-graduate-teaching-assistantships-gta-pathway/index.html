Imperial Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA Pathway)

Departmental academic tutoring positions for enrolled doctoral researchers, providing pedagogical training and hourly remuneration for supervising undergraduate lab classes, leading problem sessions, and grading exams.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Registered postgraduate doctoral researcher (PhD student) at Imperial College London.
English language proficiency
High level spoken English communication for classroom teaching.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Satisfactory completion of Imperial GTA training workshops (EDREC).
Recommendation letters
Approval from PhD primary research supervisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried academic employment: £22.50 to £29.75 per hour for laboratory demonstrating, problem class tutoring, and coursework marking
Application fee
Free application via department GTA administrators
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; hourly salaried university employment.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
June 30, 2027 for upcoming Autumn term teaching allocations
