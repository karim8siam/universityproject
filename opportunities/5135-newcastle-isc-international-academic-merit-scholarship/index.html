Newcastle ISC International Academic Merit Scholarship

Merit-based tuition fee reduction awarded to high-achieving international applicants accepted into Foundation or International Year One pathways.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Strong secondary school graduation marks (equivalent to BBC at A-Level or 75%+ national high school average)
English language proficiency
IELTS for UKVI 5.5 overall with no individual score below 5.0
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
500-word personal statement describing degree ambitions at Newcastle University
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of reference

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to £4,000 direct reduction applied against pathway course tuition fees
Application fee
£0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£18,500 to £22,000 per pathway program
