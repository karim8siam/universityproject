Batchelor Institute Indigenous Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship

Prestigious doctoral scholarship supporting domestic and international research candidates conducting community-led research in First Nations epistemologies, linguistics, and land care.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1) or Master's by research with distinction average
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in all bands
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated engagement with Indigenous research methodologies or language archiving
Recommendation letters
Two comprehensive academic and community referee reports

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
$33,000 AUD per year tax-free living stipend plus full tuition waiver and relocation allowance
Application fee
$0 AUD
Tuition / joining costs
$0 AUD (100% covered via RTP fee offset)
