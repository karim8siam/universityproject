Don Nicola Mazza Cultural Heritage and Intercultural Leadership Fellowship

Research fellowship honoring the legacy of Don Nicola Mazza, supporting rigorous scholarly inquiry into intercultural cooperation, educational ethics, and regional heritage preservation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Academic average of 28.5/30 or above across humanities, history, or cultural studies coursework.
English language proficiency
B2 English language certificate or higher.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Italian humanities fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
1,500-word proposal on historical documentation, archives preservation, or intercultural dialogue.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendations from professors of history or cultural heritage.
