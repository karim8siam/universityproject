PhD in Interdisciplinary Design and Nursing Sciences

Japan's only doctoral degree fusing clinical healthcare and human-centered design, investigating patient-centered medical UI/UX, therapeutic architecture, and robotic assistive devices.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Nursing, Design, Public Health, or Bioengineering.
English language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N1 or English C1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis, publication list, and 5-page interdisciplinary doctoral proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
JSPS Doctoral Fellowships (DC) providing ¥200,000/month plus research funding
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800/year (tuition waivers widely granted to doctoral fellows)
