Application checklist & requirements

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Cumulative GPA of 3.2/4.0 in health science, nursing, or community care units.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing and speaking).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for applied collegiate research projects.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of research intent detailing methodologies for evaluating community-based healthcare interventions.
Recommendation letters
Faculty recommendation from an Acknowledge Education healthcare discipline coordinator.
