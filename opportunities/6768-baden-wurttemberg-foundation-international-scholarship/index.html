Baden-Württemberg Foundation International Scholarship

State foundation scholarship promoting transatlantic and international academic exchange for top-ranked engineering students studying at Hochschule Offenburg.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top 15% academic standing in engineering or business.
English language proficiency
English B2 or German B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, study plan at Offenburg, and essay on international cooperation.
Recommendation letters
Two professor recommendations.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€4,200 merit stipend (€700/month for 6 months) for international study exchange
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition waiver for exchange fellows
