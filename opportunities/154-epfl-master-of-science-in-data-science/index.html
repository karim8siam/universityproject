EPFL Master of Science in Data Science

Comprehensive two-year Master of Science program (120 ECTS) jointly offered by Computer Science and Mathematics, covering mathematical statistics, machine learning, distributed optimization, and big data systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering with solid linear algebra and probability.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Rigorous background in calculus, statistical inference, algorithms, and Python/C++.
Recommendation letters
Three academic referee letters and complete university transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for EPFL Excellence Fellowships (CHF 20,000/yr)
Application fee
CHF 150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 730/semester (Swiss) or CHF 2,190/semester (international).
