University of Groningen Eric Bleumink Fund (EBF) Full Scholarships

Premier prestigious fellowship fund at the University of Groningen covering complete tuition fees, living expenses, international airfare, and health insurance for outstanding Master's candidates from eligible developing nations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Academic excellence in Bachelor's degree (top 5–10% of graduating class).
English language proficiency
Meet the English proficiency requirement of the chosen Master's program (typically IELTS 6.5+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by Dutch universities.
Thesis / Publications
Curriculum vitae and personal motivation letter demonstrating leadership and commitment to home country development.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from undergraduate professors and official provisional admission to RUG.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition fee coverage, international round-trip airfare, monthly living allowance, comprehensive health insurance, and books
Application fee
€100 application fee for non-EEA applicants
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived for 1- or 2-year Master's degree programs.

Application deadline
December 1, 2026 (Annual Master's admission deadline for February nomination)
