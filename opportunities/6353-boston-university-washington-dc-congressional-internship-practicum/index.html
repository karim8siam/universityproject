Boston University Washington DC Congressional Internship Practicum

Full-semester immersion placing students directly in congressional offices on Capitol Hill, committee staffs, or federal agencies for four days per week, accompanied by evening seminars in legislative drafting.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0/4.0.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 or IELTS 7.0 for international scholars.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Political resume, two policy writing samples, and personal statement of Capitol Hill goals.
Recommendation letters
Two academic faculty letters of recommendation.
