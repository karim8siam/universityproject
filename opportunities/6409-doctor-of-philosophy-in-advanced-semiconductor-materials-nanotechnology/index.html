Doctor of Philosophy in Advanced Semiconductor Materials & Nanotechnology

Three-year doctoral program developing next-generation silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN) power semiconductors for ultra-efficient electric vehicles and renewable energy grid inverters.

Academic degree / Prerequisites

Doctoral dissertation research plan (5 pages), master's thesis, and list of publications.

Two letters of recommendation from physics or engineering professors.
