St. Lawrence Estuary Ecosystem and Urban Biodiversity Field Study

Applied environmental research initiative assessing water quality parameters, microbial biodiversity, and wetland preservation along the St. Lawrence River watershed.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Cumulative collegiate GPA of 3.3/4.0 (R-score minimum 28.0) across biological and ecological coursework.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or demonstrated bilingual English-French conversational competence.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for undergraduate field research internships.
Thesis / Research statement
Research rationale statement explaining interest in aquatic bio-indicators and riparian conservation.
Recommendation letters
Letter of nomination from a Champlain St. Lawrence faculty member in Biology or Chemistry.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Undergraduate research stipend: CAD $3,000 supported by regional environmental sustainability partners
Application fee
Zero internal research application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Funded research appointment; no supplementary tuition charged for laboratory field units
