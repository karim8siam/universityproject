Scholarship

Regione Campania ADISURC Medical Student Grant

Regional government scholarship providing full tuition fee exemption, free student residence housing, and daily cafeteria vouchers.

University
Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli - Facoltà di Medicina
Location
Naples & Caserta, Campania, Italy
Funding
Full tuition waiver plus annual maintenance grant up to €7,250
Tuition
100% exempted for eligible income brackets (ISEE < €25,000)
Application Fee
Free application
Deadline
September 15, 2027
Requirements
Enrolment in UniCampania Faculty of Medicine and certified family ISEE economic documentation.
