Curtin Master of Professional Engineering (Chemical Engineering)

Engineers Australia (EA) accredited professional engineering master's degree focusing on clean hydrogen energy, mineral carbonation, sustainable battery chemical processing, and industrial plant optimization.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Engineering degree or equivalent in chemical sciences with minimum 65% CWA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in all bands.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework background in thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, transport phenomena, and reaction engineering.
Recommendation letters
Official university academic transcripts, detailed syllabus outlines, and resume.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Western Australian energy industry bursaries and Curtin merit awards
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $43,200 per year (typically 2 to 3 years full-time depending on bachelor background).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 for Semester 1 intake
