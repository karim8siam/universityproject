BTH Master in Strategic Leadership towards Sustainability (MSLS)

Globally acclaimed one-year international master's degree (60 ECTS) founded on the Framework for Strategic Sustainable Development (FSSD), training leaders in systems thinking, organizational change, and cross-sector sustainability transition.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Bachelor's degree or professional degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in any academic discipline with demonstrated passion for sustainability.
IELTS / English language
English 6 equivalent: IELTS 6.5 overall with no section below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Detailed curriculum vitae and personal motivation statement outlining prior sustainability engagement and systemic transformation goals.
Recommendation letters
Official undergraduate degree diploma, transcripts, and two letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for BTH 50% tuition scholarship and Swedish Institute full fellowships
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA applicants
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 70,000 per semester (SEK 140,000 total for 60 ECTS 1-year program). EU/EEA students study tuition-free.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Primary round via University Admissions Sweden)
