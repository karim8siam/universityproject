MEXT Japanese Government University Recommendation Scholarship

Premier Monbukagakusho (MEXT) scholarship awarded to outstanding international graduate researchers admitted to master's or doctoral programs at Meiji University.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.2/4.0 GPA in prior university degree studies.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or JLPT N1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed academic research plan in Japan, certified degree transcripts, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Dean or provost formal institutional recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition waiver, round-trip Tokyo airfare, and monthly stipend of ¥144,000 to ¥145,000
Application fee
¥0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% exempted by Japanese Government
