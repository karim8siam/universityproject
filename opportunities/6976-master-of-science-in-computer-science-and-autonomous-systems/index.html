Master of Science in Computer Science and Autonomous Systems

International master's program taught in English covering deep learning, autonomous driving, distributed databases, cyber-physical security, and verified formal methods.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Computer Engineering with top quartile standing (German grade 2.5 or better).
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE general test recommended for non-EU applicants (quantitative 155+).
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcript showing comprehensive mathematics and theoretical computer science modules, syllabus descriptions, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free public university; HiWi research assistant positions at DFKI and Fraunhofer ITWM (€600–€1,100/month)
Application fee
€60 RPTU application processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €310 semester fee including state public transit ticket
