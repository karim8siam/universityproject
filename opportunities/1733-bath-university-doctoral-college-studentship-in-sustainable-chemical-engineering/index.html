Bath University Doctoral College Studentship in Sustainable Chemical Engineering

Fully funded 3.5-year doctoral studentship at the Centre for Sustainable and Circular Technologies (CSCT) engineering bio-based polymers and catalytic decarbonization.

First-class honours degree or master's with distinction in chemical engineering or chemistry

Not required
