Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Medical Humanities and Palliative Ethics

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating clinical ethics, existential support mechanisms in end-of-life care, and healthcare equity in European clinical centers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed PhD or Dr. phil. / Dr. theol. in Bioethics, Psychology, Medical Sociology, or Philosophy within the last four years.
English language proficiency
Fluency in English (C1) and working knowledge of German (B2).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, research proposal (5 pages), two writing samples or peer-reviewed publications.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference contacts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
