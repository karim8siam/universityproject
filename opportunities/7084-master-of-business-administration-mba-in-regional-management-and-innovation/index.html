Executive graduate curriculum at the Hiroshima Campus analyzing regional manufacturing revitalization (Mazda supplier corridors), Asian supply chain strategy, and digital enterprise transformation.

Not required.

Undergraduate transcripts, corporate experience record, and business research prospectus.
