Doctoral Program in Pure and Applied Chemistry (Photocatalysis, Solar Energy Conversion & Supramolecular Materials)

Three-year doctoral candidacy in the Department of Pure and Applied Chemistry, investigating solar water splitting, photocatalytic air purification, and self-assembling metal-organic supramolecular cages.
