Lampeter Special Collections Archival Fellowship

Postgraduate research fellowship examining 30,000 rare books, incunabula, and medieval Welsh manuscripts housed at the Roderic Bowen Library.

University
University of Wales Trinity Saint David
Location
Carmarthen, Lampeter, Swansea & London, Wales, UK
Funding
£6,000 research award plus dedicated archival workstation
Tuition
Master's research tuition applies
Application Fee
Free fellowship application
Deadline
April 15, 2027
Requirements
Good honours degree in History, Literature, or Classics; reading knowledge of Latin or Middle Welsh an asset.
