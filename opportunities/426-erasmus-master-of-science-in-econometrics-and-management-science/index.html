Erasmus Master of Science in Econometrics and Management Science

World-renowned two-year master's program taught in English, specializing in Business Analytics, Econometrics, Quantitative Finance, and Operations Research.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Econometrics, Mathematics, or Statistics with strong linear algebra and probability.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE revised General Test quantitative score 160+ required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in microeconometrics, time series analysis, and operations research.
Recommendation letters
Transcripts, CV, and motivation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for NL Scholarships and Erasmus School of Economics waivers
Application fee
€100 application fee

€18,200 per academic year for non-EEA students.
