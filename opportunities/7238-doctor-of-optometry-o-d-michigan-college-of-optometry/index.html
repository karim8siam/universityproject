Doctor of Optometry (O.D. - Michigan College of Optometry)

The only doctoral optometry program in the State of Michigan, featuring patient care in the University Eye Center, ocular disease pathology, pediatric vision therapy, and laser surgical procedures.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with overall and science GPA 3.0+; prerequisite grades of C or better in biology, microbiology, calculus, organic chemistry, and optics physics.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 or IELTS 7.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
OAT (Optometry Admission Test) required (competitive score 300+).
Thesis / Research statement
OptomCAS application, verified observation hours with a practicing optometrist, and personal essay.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation including one from an optometrist.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Michigan College of Optometry Endowed Scholarships up to $12,000 annually
Application fee
$50 application fee (via OptomCAS)
Tuition / joining costs
$39,800 per academic year
