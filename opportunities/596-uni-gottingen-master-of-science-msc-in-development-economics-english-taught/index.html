Uni Göttingen Master of Science (MSc) in Development Economics (English-taught)

Germany's leading two-year Master's degree in development economics, offering rigorous training in econometric impact evaluation, microfinance, rural poverty alleviation, and climate shocks.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in economics or closely related field with at least 18 ECTS in mathematics/statistics and 60 ECTS in economics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General test recommended for international applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated commitment to empirical development analysis.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation.
