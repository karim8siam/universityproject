TU Delft Master of Science in Aerospace Engineering

World-ranked #1 two-year Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) offering world-class research tracks in Aerodynamics & Wind Energy, Flight Performance & Propulsion, Space Exploration, and Aerospace Structures.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Aerospace or Mechanical Engineering with solid mathematics, aerodynamics, and structural mechanics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE revised General Test required for applicants with non-Dutch Bachelor's degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed course syllabus descriptions mapping undergraduate credits to TU Delft prerequisites.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts, degree certificate, and two academic references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Justus & Louise van Effen scholarships and European Space Agency bursaries
Application fee
€100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€21,515 per year for non-EU students; €2,530 per year for EU/EEA students.
