Hogeschool Viaa Healthcare Talent Scholarship

Merit scholarship supporting motivated healthcare and social work students who demonstrate high empathy, academic commitment, and community volunteerism.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school graduation average >= 7.0/10 (Dutch Havo/Vwo or international equivalent)
English language proficiency
Dutch NT2-II or English IELTS 6.0 for international exchange tracks
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Proof of secondary education, motivation essay detailing healthcare values, and resume
Recommendation letters
1 letter of recommendation from school mentor or community volunteer supervisor

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition reduction of €1,500 per year plus dedicated student housing placement assistance in Zwolle
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
€2,530/year (Statutory EU/EEA fee) / €8,500/year (Non-EU rate)
