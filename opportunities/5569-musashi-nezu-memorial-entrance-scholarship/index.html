Musashi Nezu Memorial Entrance Scholarship

Prestigious merit scholarship honoring university founder Nezu Kaichirō, awarding full tuition waivers to highest-scoring incoming students.

University
Musashi University
Location
Nerima, Tokyo, Japan
Funding
Full tuition waiver (¥850,000/year value) renewable for 4 years
Tuition
¥1,190,000/year base undergraduate tuition and fees
Application Fee
Free scholarship competition with entrance exam
Deadline
January 20, 2027
Requirements
Top 3% score in Musashi general entrance examination or AO selection.
