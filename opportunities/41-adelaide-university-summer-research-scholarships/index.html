Adelaide University Summer Research Scholarships

Six-week funded summer research internship for continuing undergraduate and postgraduate coursework students to gain advanced hands-on laboratory experience within Adelaide research institutes.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / CGPA
Minimum Grade Point Average of 5.0 on a 7.0 scale across prior university study.
IELTS / English language
Enrolled student status with valid student visa extending beyond February 2027.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Written research proposal developed in consultation with Adelaide supervising academic.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement and signed agreement from project supervisor in School of Physical Sciences, CS, or Engineering.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: AUD $1,500 research stipend for 6-week laboratory project
Application fee
Free (Competitive institutional research scholarship program)
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition for research placement; stipend paid directly to student.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
Late October 2026 (Annual application round for 2026–27 summer semester)
