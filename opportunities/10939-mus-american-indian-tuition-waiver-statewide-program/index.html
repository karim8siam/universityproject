MUS American Indian Tuition Waiver (Statewide Program)

Montana University System · Helena, Montana, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: July 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Constitutional tuition waiver available to enrolled members of federally recognized American Indian tribes who are Montana residents attending any MUS campus.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
100% full undergraduate or graduate tuition waiver
Tuition Rate
$0 (Tuition completely waived)
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
Good academic standing at constituent MUS campus
English Language
Satisfied via residency
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Official tribal enrollment verification or Certificate of Degree of Indian Blood (CDIB)
Letters of Recommendation
None required

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult Montana University System's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
