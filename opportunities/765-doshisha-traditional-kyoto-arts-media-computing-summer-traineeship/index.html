Doshisha Traditional Kyoto Arts & Media Computing Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer research traineeship at the Imadegawa campus in Kyoto, collaborating on digital archiving of historic Nishijin textile weaving, Japanese Buddhist temple architecture, and interactive cultural heritage media.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in third or fourth year of undergraduate studies in Digital Media, History, or Cultural Studies with high marks.

Good English conversational skills.

Not applicable.
