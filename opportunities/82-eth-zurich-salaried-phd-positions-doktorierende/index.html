ETH Zurich Salaried PhD Positions (Doktorierende)

Three to four-year salaried doctoral research appointment at ETH Zurich, consistently ranked among the world's top 10 institutions, conducting fundamental and applied research with state-of-the-art supercomputing and laboratory infrastructure.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized Master of Science degree (minimum 120 ECTS) in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, or Mathematics with high marks.
English language proficiency
Fluent technical English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS or university-level English instruction).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis or peer-reviewed conference publications in reputable IEEE/ACM/nature journals.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement from an ETH Zurich full professor agreeing to act as formal thesis supervisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried employment contract under ETH Domain Ordinance starting from CHF 53,500/year (Rate 1) up to CHF 73,100 to CHF 84,000/year (Rate 5 in CS/Engineering)
Application fee
Free application directly to open doctoral vacancies
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. PhD candidates are paid scientific employees with comprehensive Swiss state pension (Pestalozzi/PUBLICA), accident, and health benefits.
