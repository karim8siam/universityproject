Gakushuin Oriental Cultures & Historical Archives Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer research traineeship at the Research Institute for Oriental Cultures in Tokyo, participating in the digital preservation, translation, and cataloging of rare pre-modern Japanese and East Asian manuscripts.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in third or fourth year of undergraduate studies in East Asian History, Linguistics, or Art History with high marks.
English language proficiency
Good English or Japanese conversational skills.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in Japanese history, pre-modern East Asian texts, or archival preservation.
Recommendation letters
One professor recommendation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend

¥0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges during summer traineeship.
