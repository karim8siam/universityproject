USW National Cyber Security Academy Excellence Scholarship

Tuition discount scholarship awarded to undergraduate and postgraduate students entering NCSC-certified cyber security programs at USW Newport.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum ABB at A-Level (or equivalent) in computer science, mathematics, or physics
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated interest in computer networks and information security
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
£2,500 annual tuition fee reduction throughout the degree course
Application fee
None
Tuition / joining costs
£9,000/year (Wales home), £9,250/year (UK), or £15,800/year (International)
