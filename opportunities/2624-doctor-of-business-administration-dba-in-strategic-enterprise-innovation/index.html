Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) in Strategic Enterprise Innovation

Three-year practitioner-scholar doctoral program designed for business executives to produce original applied research in digital disruption, organizational agility, and corporate sustainability.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Master of Science or MBA degree with good standing from an accredited university.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in writing.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for European professional DBA programs.
Thesis / Research statement
3,000-word doctoral research proposal examining a contemporary strategic management or digital economy topic.
Recommendation letters
Three academic and corporate executive recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Practitioner doctoral research degree with international research symposium access
Application fee
Zero application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€10,500 per academic year across the three-year doctoral cycle (€31,500 total)
