Scholarship

Wyoming Hathaway Merit Scholarship at CWC

State-funded merit scholarship providing up to $3,360 per academic year for eligible Wyoming high school graduates attending CWC.

University
Central Wyoming College
Location
Riverton & Jackson, Wyoming, USA
Funding
Up to $1,680 per semester ($3,360/year) covering near-total in-state tuition
Tuition
$3,450/year (In-State Wyoming)
Application Fee
Free FAFSA and Hathaway application
Deadline
August 15, 2027
Requirements
Wyoming high school graduate with minimum GPA 2.5 and ACT score >= 19.
