Tokyo University Hospital and Regional Healthcare Center Clinical Practicum

Intensive 16-week clinical internship rotating students through clinical chemistry, microbiology, and transfusion medicine laboratories at university hospitals in central Tokyo.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completion of all prerequisite clinical biochemistry, hematology, and microbiology practical exams.
English language proficiency
Native or JLPT N1 Japanese fluency required for hospital team coordination.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Japanese clinical internships.
Thesis / Research statement
Clinical code of conduct agreement, health screening certificate, and hepatitis B immunization records.
Recommendation letters
Approval from the Bunkyo Gakuin Clinical Training Committee.
