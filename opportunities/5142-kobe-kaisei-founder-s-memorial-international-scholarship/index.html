Kobe Kaisei Founder's Memorial International Scholarship

Tuition reduction scholarship awarded to outstanding international female students admitted to the Faculty of Humanities.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed 12 years of formal schooling with equivalent GPA >= 3.0
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or higher (or EJU Japanese score >= 220); English proficiency evaluated favorably
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
800-character Japanese statement of purpose and essay on women's global leadership
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
30% to 50% tuition reduction (valued up to ¥400,000 per academic year)
Application fee
¥15,000 entrance examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
Approx. ¥1,150,000 per academic year before scholarship

Application deadline
January 20, 2027
