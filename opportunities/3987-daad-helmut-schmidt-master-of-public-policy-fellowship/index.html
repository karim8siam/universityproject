DAAD Helmut-Schmidt Master of Public Policy Fellowship

Prestige scholarship program funded by the German Foreign Office for future leaders from developing and emerging countries to earn a Master of Public Policy at the Willy Brandt School.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First degree (Bachelor or equivalent) in social sciences, political science, law, or economics with above-average grades
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Motivational letter with policy focus, curriculum vitae (Europass format), and certified degree certificates
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from university professors or employers

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: €934/month living stipend, health insurance, travel allowance, and full MPP tuition waiver
Application fee
Free
Tuition / joining costs
€1,600/semester MPP program fee waived for DAAD scholarship recipients
