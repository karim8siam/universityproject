PEAK Bachelor of Arts in Japan in East Asia

Rigorous four-year English-medium interdisciplinary humanities and social sciences degree examining Japanese history, diplomacy, modern culture, literature, and East Asian security.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Strong high school academic profile with coursework in History, Literature, and Social Studies.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5+ or TOEFL iBT 100+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
SAT / ACT / IB / A-Level results strongly recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcript, secondary school leaving diploma, personal statement, and analytical essay.
Recommendation letters
Two recommendation letters from academic mentors.
