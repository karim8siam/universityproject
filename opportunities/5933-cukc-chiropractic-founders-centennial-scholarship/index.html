CUKC Chiropractic Founders Centennial Scholarship

Centennial scholarship honoring academic distinction and demonstrated dedication to conservative, non-invasive chiropractic patient care.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree or 90 completed semester credits with minimum 3.25 science GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80 for international applicants.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
750-word personal statement describing commitment to chiropractic healthcare.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation, including one from a practicing Doctor of Chiropractic.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
$10,000 total scholarship distributed across Doctor of Chiropractic clinical tri-mesters
Application fee
$50
Tuition / joining costs
$12,450 per trimester (CCE standard tuition)
