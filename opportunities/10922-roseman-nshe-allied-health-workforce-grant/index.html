Roseman - NSHE Allied Health Workforce Grant

Nevada System of Higher Education · Reno, Nevada, United States

Target Discipline: biologyApplication Deadline: April 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Collaborative workforce grant for students pursuing accelerated nursing, pharmacy, or diagnostic medical sonography with commitment to rural Nevada clinical service.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Tuition credit up to $4,500/year for healthcare candidates
Tuition Rate
Varies by specific NSHE nursing program
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
2.75+ prerequisite GPA in collegiate pre-health sciences
English Language
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 79
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Transcripts, clinical interest essay, Nevada rural clinical service pledge
Letters of Recommendation
Two recommendations
