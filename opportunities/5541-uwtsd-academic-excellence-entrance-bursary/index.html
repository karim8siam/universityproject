Scholarship

UWTSD Academic Excellence Entrance Bursary

Merit bursary awarded to incoming full-time undergraduate students entering UWTSD with high grades in A-Levels or equivalent qualifications.

University
University of Wales Trinity Saint David
Location
Carmarthen, Lampeter, Swansea & London, Wales, UK
Funding
£1,000 cash bursary in Year 1 of study
Tuition
£9,000/year (Wales Home) / £9,250/year (RUK) / £15,750/year (International)
Application Fee
Automatic assessment via UCAS application
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
Entry tariff of at least 128 UCAS points (ABB at A-Level or equivalent).
