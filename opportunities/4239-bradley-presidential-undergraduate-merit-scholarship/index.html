Bradley Presidential Undergraduate Merit Scholarship

Top-tier undergraduate merit scholarship awarded to incoming freshmen demonstrating exceptional academic performance and leadership.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.6/4.0 high school GPA or top 10% graduating class rank
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 79 for international students
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Test-optional for undergraduate admissions
Thesis / Research statement
High school essay showcasing intellectual curiosity and community contribution
Recommendation letters
One high school counselor or teacher letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
USD $20,000 to USD $26,000 per year for up to four years (totaling up to USD $104,000)
Application fee
USD $0 (Bradley free online application)
Tuition / joining costs
USD $38,500/year (standard undergraduate tuition)
