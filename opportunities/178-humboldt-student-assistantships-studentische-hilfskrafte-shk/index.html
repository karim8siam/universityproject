Humboldt Student Assistantships (Studentische Hilfskräfte - SHK)

Campus employment positions (up to 80 hours per month) assisting Humboldt professors in running exercise groups, tutoring undergraduate students, managing library collections, and maintaining computer clusters.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled student at Humboldt University in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Proficiency in German or English depending on department requirements.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High academic marks in the subject area of the tutoring or lab assignment.
Recommendation letters
Approval from supervising chair professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried hourly campus employment: €13.98 to €16.50 per hour under Berlin university student assistant collective agreement
Application fee
Free application through university student job portal
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid student employment.
