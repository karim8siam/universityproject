Lund Master of Science in Machine Learning, Systems and Control

Interdisciplinary master's degree combining modern machine learning, reinforcement learning, convex optimization, and autonomous control theory.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in computer science, mathematics, or engineering with strong linear algebra and programming.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (minimum 5.5 in each subscore).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated proficiency in Python, C++, and automated control theory.
Recommendation letters
Degree certificates and certified transcripts.
