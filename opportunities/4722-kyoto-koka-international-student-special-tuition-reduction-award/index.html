Kyoto Koka International Student Special Tuition Reduction Award

Tuition reduction scholarship covering 30% to 50% of annual tuition fees for eligible self-funded international students across healthcare and humanities faculties.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.0/4.0 equivalent in high school or undergraduate transcript
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or N1 (Japanese Language Proficiency Test)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose written in Japanese (800 characters) and proof of financial solvency
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter from Japanese language school or high school

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
30% to 50% tuition reduction (saving approx. ¥350,000 to ¥550,000 per academic year)
Application fee
¥30,000 application screening fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥1,380,000 standard annual tuition and laboratory fees (reduced by scholarship)
