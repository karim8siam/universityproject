King's Wellcome Trust 4-Year PhD in Cellular and Molecular Mechanisms of Health & Disease

Four-year cohort-based doctoral training program comprising three laboratory rotation projects in year one followed by a three-year primary PhD project at Guy's campus.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree with 1st Class or 2:1 Honours in biological sciences, medicine, or biochemistry.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated laboratory research experience and analytical rigor.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Enhanced tax-free stipend starting at £23,500/year plus full tuition fees and £10,000/year lab research budget for 4 years
Application fee
£0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% tuition fees covered.

Application deadline
December 8, 2026 (Annual Wellcome Trust cohort deadline)
