HfBK Dresden Doctorate and Meisterschüler Studies

Doctor of Philosophy pathways and advanced Meisterschüler studio research at Academy of Fine Arts Dresden in Saxony, Germany.

Official doctorate information ↗Information verified 14 October 2026

Doctoral disciplines and regulations

Hochschule für Bildende Künste Dresden holds the authority to confer research doctorates in specific scholarly fields: Art History and Theory, Philosophical Aesthetics, Art Technology, Conservation/Restoration, and Art Therapy. Candidates must satisfy university doctoral regulations and secure supervision from an eligible faculty professor.

Tuition and key admissions data

Tuition fees
Tuition-free public university education; ordinary semester re-registration fee is €354.91.
Degree duration
Doctorate is typically 3 to 4 years; Meisterschüler study is normally 2 years.
Admissions intake
Doctoral applications reviewed per academic semester; Fine Arts diploma intake closes 15 January from 2027.
Language
German language proficiency required for formal doctoral documentation and administrative defense.
