Master of Arts in Journalism and Digital Media Innovation

Nationally acclaimed broadcast and digital journalism program accredited by the Broadcast Journalism Training Council (BJTC), featuring industry newsrooms and live TV broadcasting studios.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree or international equivalent in any academic discipline.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all bands.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for UK journalism degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Portfolio of writing, digital audio/video media clips, or investigative article samples.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of reference (at least one academic reference).
