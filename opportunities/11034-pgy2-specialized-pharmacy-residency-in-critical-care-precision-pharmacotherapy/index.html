PGY2 Specialized Pharmacy Residency in Critical Care & Precision Pharmacotherapy

Academic Overview & Description

ASHP-accredited postgraduate residency in high-acuity surgical, medical, and cardiothoracic ICUs, emphasizing pharmacokinetics, therapeutic drug monitoring, and clinical resuscitation protocols.

Not required
