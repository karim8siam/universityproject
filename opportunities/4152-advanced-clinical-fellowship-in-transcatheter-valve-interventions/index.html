Advanced Clinical Fellowship in Transcatheter Valve Interventions

One-year clinical and translational cardiology fellowship offering intensive training in complex TAVR, MitraClip procedures, and clinical trial coordination.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
MD degree with board certification or eligibility in adult cardiology
English language proficiency
Proof of English and working French medical communication skills
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; medical licensing exams verified
Thesis / Research statement
Published clinical abstracts in cardiology or cardiovascular imaging
Recommendation letters
Three letters from cardiology program director and catheterization laboratory directors
