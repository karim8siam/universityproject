Scholarship

Borsa di Studio 'Benessere per Tutti' Santa Sofia

Need-based tuition assistance bursary awarded to adult learners entering the three-year Naturopathy or Osteopathy diploma programs.

University
Università Popolare Santa Sofia
Location
Padova & Milan, Italy
Funding
€1,000 deduction on annual tuition fees
Tuition
€2,200/year (reduced to €1,200/year with bursary)
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
September 30, 2027
Requirements
Enrolment in full-time diploma and statement of economic hardship or unemployed status.
