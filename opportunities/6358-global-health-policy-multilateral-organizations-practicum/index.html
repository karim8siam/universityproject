Global Health Policy & Multilateral Organizations Practicum

Semester-long public health policy placement embedding scholars with the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), USAID health programs, or global healthcare think tanks in Washington.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in Master of Public Health (MPH) or relevant graduate health sciences degree.

English fluency; Spanish or French beneficial.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.

Health policy writing sample, resume, and statement of international health interests.

Two academic referee letters.
