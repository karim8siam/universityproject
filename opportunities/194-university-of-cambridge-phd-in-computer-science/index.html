University of Cambridge PhD in Computer Science

Three to four-year doctoral research program inside the Cambridge Computer Laboratory at the William Gates Building, conducting frontier investigations in artificial intelligence, programming languages, security, and systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class UK Honours degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or closely related field.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall with 7.0 minimum in all components.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed 2-to-3 page research proposal matching Department of Computer Science research themes.
Recommendation letters
Two strong academic referee letters and supervisor endorsement.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Departmental EPSRC studentships and Cambridge Trust awards covering full fees plus London-equivalent stipend (£19,237 to £21,000/yr)
Application fee
£75 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition for funded students; standard overseas tuition £39,708/year.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
December 3, 2026 for main funding round consideration
