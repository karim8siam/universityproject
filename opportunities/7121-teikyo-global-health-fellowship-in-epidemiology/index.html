Teikyo Global Health Fellowship in Epidemiology

Endowed fellowship supporting outstanding medical and public health professionals from developing and emerging nations to complete graduate epidemiological training in Tokyo.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Medical degree or master's degree in health sciences with top 15% rank.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 7.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic transcripts, CV with clinical/health field experience, and global health research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two professional reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

100% tuition waiver included
