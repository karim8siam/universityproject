Cardiff EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Compound Semiconductor Manufacturing

Doctoral training conducted at the £75 million Institute for Compound Semiconductors (ICS) within the CSconnected cluster, developing high-speed optoelectronics, power electronics, and 5G/6G photonics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK first-class honours degree or 2:1 Master's (or international equivalent) in Electrical Engineering, Physics, or Materials Science.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 with at least 6.0 in each band.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework excellence in semiconductor physics, electromagnetics, or solid-state devices.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university lecturers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual tax-free stipend of £19,237/year for 4 years, 100% full tuition fee waiver, and £5,000/year research consumables allowance
Application fee
£0 application fee for research programs
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition waiver covered by EPSRC and Cardiff Institute for Compound Semiconductors.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027 (Priority round for October 2027 commencement)
