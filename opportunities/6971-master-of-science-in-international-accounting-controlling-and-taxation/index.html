Master of Science in International Accounting, Controlling and Taxation

Specialized postgraduate program at ESB Business School covering IFRS compliance, international tax architecture, predictive corporate controlling, and ESG reporting standards.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in Business, Economics, or Industrial Engineering (minimum grade 2.5).
English language proficiency
German C1 and English B2/C1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT 600+ or GMAT Focus 555+ highly recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, motivation essay, and documentation of relevant accounting internships.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation.
