Indigenous Health Continuing Medical Education Bursary

Need- and merit-based bursary supporting physicians, nurses, and allied practitioners providing primary medical care in First Nations and Inuit communities across Manitoba and Nunavut.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Licensed healthcare professional actively serving in a remote or Indigenous community.
English language proficiency
Proficient English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word personal statement describing community healthcare context.
Recommendation letters
One reference from a band council, community health director, or medical director.
