Manchester President's Doctoral Scholar (PDS) Award

The University of Manchester's premier competitive doctoral award, offering full funding and bespoke presidential leadership training to the world's most promising doctoral researchers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class UK honours degree or international equivalent (minimum 3.7+ / 4.0 GPA or 80%+).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all subscores or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High academic distinction; prior published research papers or high-distinction Master's dissertation.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters submitted via the application portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full international tuition fees covered plus annual tax-free living stipend at UKRI rate (£19,237+/year) and research consumables budget for 3.5 to 4 years
Application fee
Free application for research doctoral study
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for duration of scholarship award.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Annual international scholarship competition deadline for Autumn entry)
