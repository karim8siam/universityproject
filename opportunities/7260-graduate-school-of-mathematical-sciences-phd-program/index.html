Graduate School of Mathematical Sciences PhD Program

Renowned international doctoral program in pure and applied mathematics, encompassing algebraic geometry, differential topology, representation theory, PDE analysis, and mathematical physics.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Mathematics or Mathematical Physics with distinguished record.
English language proficiency
English proficiency demonstrated via TOEFL, IELTS, or international thesis presentation.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE Mathematics Subject Test recommended for international applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis copy, summary of research accomplishments, and doctoral research project outline.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from mathematics professors.
