Utrecht Excellence Scholarship in Medical Life Sciences at UMC Utrecht

Prestige scholarship awarded to the top 10% of international applicants admitted to Master’s degree programs in Life Sciences, Biofabrication, or Infection and Immunity.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class honours or equivalent cumulative GPA of at least 3.7/4.0 in biomedical sciences, biology, medicine, or biochemistry
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in writing (or TOEFL iBT 93)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Motivational letter highlighting clinical/laboratory research goals, CV, and verified transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from senior academic investigators

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: up to €17,500/year living stipend plus 100% full tuition fee waiver for non-EU students
Application fee
€100 (Utrecht University application fee)
Tuition / joining costs
€2,530/year (EU statutory) or €22,800/year (non-EU; fully covered by scholarship)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027
