Master of Science in Life Sciences (Biotechnology & Chemical Engineering)

Multi-cantonal master's program based in Sion and Fribourg offering specializations in Applied Biosciences, Chemical Development & Production, and Viticulture & Enology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Biotechnology, Chemistry, Life Sciences, or Agronomy with above-average marks
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90 or French B2/C1
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation letter detailing laboratory thesis topic and industrial interests
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Swiss cantonal fee subsidies; paid research assistantships (CHF 2,600/month) available
Application fee
CHF 150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 750 per semester (Swiss/EU) / CHF 1,050 per semester (Non-EU)
