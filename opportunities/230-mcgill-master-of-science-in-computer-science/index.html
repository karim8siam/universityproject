McGill Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year thesis-based Master of Science degree providing foundational and advanced training in machine learning, computational biology, cryptography, and computer systems with guaranteed graduate student funding.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Mathematics with minimum CGPA 3.2/4.0 in final two years.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, statement of purpose, and technical programming portfolio.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Guaranteed minimum funding package of CAD $21,000 per year for two years through teaching and research assistantships
Application fee
CAD $132.90 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $10,500/year for international research students (offset by McGill tuition supplements).
