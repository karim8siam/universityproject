Yale Master of Science (MS) in Computer Science

One-year intensive terminal Master's degree at the Yale School of Engineering & Applied Science designed for students seeking advanced expertise in systems, machine learning, or cybersecurity.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related quantitative field with strong GPA (typically 3.6+ / 4.0).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General test optional.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in systems programming, data structures, and algorithms.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters.
