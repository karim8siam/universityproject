McGill Graduate Excellence Fellowships & McCall MacBain Scholarships

McGill's premier graduate funding awards providing guaranteed multi-year financial support, full tuition coverage, and competitive living stipends for outstanding domestic and international doctoral scholars.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class academic standing (CGPA 3.7+ out of 4.0 or equivalent A average) across prior degree coursework.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all bands or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for quantitative programs outside Canada.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research excellence through publications, conference papers, or Master's thesis.
Recommendation letters
Two to three academic reference letters submitted electronically through the portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual living stipend of CAD $20,000 to $30,000 per year plus full international tuition fee coverage for up to four years of doctoral study
Application fee
CAD $132.90 graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for fellowship recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Departmental graduate awards nomination deadline for Fall entry)
