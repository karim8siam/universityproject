Unknown UAS Future Founder Excellence Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship awarded to promising prospective student founders admitted to the Bachelor of Science in Entrepreneurship who present high-potential scalable startup concepts.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school leaving qualification (HAVO/VWO or international equivalent high school diploma)
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall or equivalent English B2 verification
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Five-minute video pitch deck, two-page executive business concept summary, and academic transcripts
Recommendation letters
One letter of recommendation from an educator, mentor, or business associate

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to €7,500 tuition fee waiver plus €5,000 equity-free startup seed grant for the recipient's venture
Application fee
€75 (intake assessment fee)
Tuition / joining costs
€9,500/year (EU) or €13,500/year (non-EU; reduced by scholarship)
