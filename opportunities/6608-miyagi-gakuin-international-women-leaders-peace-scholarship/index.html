Miyagi Gakuin International Women Leaders Peace Scholarship

Merit scholarship awarded to international female scholars from Southeast Asia and East Asia committed to peace education, gender equity, and intercultural dialogue.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.3/4.0 GPA in high school or university studies.
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or IELTS 6.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic transcripts, essay on women's leadership in Asia, and financial disclosure.
Recommendation letters
Two recommendation letters from school principals or civic leaders.
