KI Master of Science (MSc) in Biomedicine (English-taught)

Two-year research-embedded Master's curriculum at Biomedicum (one of Europe's largest medical research laboratories), preparing students for leading international PhD programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in biomedicine, biotechnology, cellular biology, or medicine with at least 90 ECTS in biological/chemical subjects.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (no section below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Documented laboratory research experience.
Recommendation letters
Academic curriculum vitae and course transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
KI Global Master's Scholarships and Swedish Institute (SI) Global Professionals Scholarships
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 200,000/year (for non-EU/EEA students; SEK 0 for EU/EEA citizens).
