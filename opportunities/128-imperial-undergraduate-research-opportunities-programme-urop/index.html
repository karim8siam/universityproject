Imperial Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP)

Imperial's flagship summer research placement program, enabling students to work directly alongside leading researchers on live scientific research investigations for 6 to 12 weeks.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate student with minimum 2:1 / First Class predicted grade.
English language proficiency
Fluent working English communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Agreed project description co-authored with an Imperial College faculty host supervisor.
Recommendation letters
Faculty host endorsement and university academic transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Bursary of £410 to £450 per week for 6 to 12 weeks of summer research work inside Imperial laboratories
Application fee
Free application for enrolled and visiting undergraduate scholars
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid student research bursary.
