South Dakota State University - Rapid City · Rapid City, South Dakota, United States

Academic Overview & Description

CCNE-accredited practice doctorate preparing advanced practice registered nurses (APRNs) to deliver primary care across rural and Native American reservation health clinics in South Dakota.
