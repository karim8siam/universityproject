Swedish Institute (SI) Scholarship for Global Professionals at SKH

Prestigious Swedish government scholarship fully funding master's studies for talented international cultural leaders and artists seeking to drive democratic change.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3,000 hours of documented work experience in cultural management or creative arts
English language proficiency
English 6 certification (IELTS 6.5)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated alignment with cultural democracy and UN Sustainable Development Goals
Recommendation letters
Two letters of reference on official SI template

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition coverage plus SEK 12,000 per month living stipend and travel grant
Application fee
SEK 900
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 0 (100% paid by the Swedish Ministry for Foreign Affairs)
