RWTH Master of Science in Computer Science (Informatik)

Highly flexible, research-intensive master's program taught in English, offering specializations in software verification, machine learning, computer graphics, and distributed systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science equivalent to RWTH curriculum with high mathematics and theoretical CS components.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE revised General Test required for applicants with degrees outside the Bologna area.
Thesis / Research statement
Bachelor thesis or substantial software project documentation.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae and official transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free program; eligible for DAAD and student research assistantships (€1,200/mo)
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; semester fee of approx. €320 covers state-wide transit.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 1, 2027 (Winter semester intake for non-EU applicants)
