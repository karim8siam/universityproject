Bonn Hausdorff Center for Mathematics (HCM) Doctoral & Postdoctoral Fellowships

Funded by Germany's Excellence Strategy, the Hausdorff Center awards prestigious doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships working alongside Fields medalists and world leaders in pure and applied mathematics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding degree in Mathematics or Theoretical Physics (German grade 1.3 or top 5% of class).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 95.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE Mathematics Subject Test recommended for international candidates.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated mathematical rigor and research publications or exceptional thesis.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential letters of recommendation from recognized mathematics professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried TV-L E13 position (€50,000 to €58,000/year gross) plus zero tuition and dedicated international conference travel grants
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition (nominal €330/semester administration and public transit fee).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Annual Hausdorff Doctoral & Postdoc international application deadline)
