INRS Excellence Doctoral Fellowship in Industrial Bioprocesses

Doctoral fellowship supporting advanced pilot-scale bioprocessing, biomass conversion, and circular bioeconomy technologies.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Chemical Engineering, Biotechnology, or Environmental Sciences (minimum GPA 3.3/4.0).
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88 (or demonstrated French proficiency B2 for francophone curricula).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; evaluated on prior peer-reviewed publications and engineering thesis.
Thesis / Research statement
Doctoral research outline (3-5 pages) focusing on bioprocess optimization or biovalorization.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee appraisals from senior laboratory supervisors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Full funding: CAD $25,000 annual stipend plus research allowance
