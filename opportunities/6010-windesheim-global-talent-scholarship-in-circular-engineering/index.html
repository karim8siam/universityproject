Windesheim Global Talent Scholarship in Circular Engineering

Merit-based tuition scholarship awarded to top non-EU international applicants enrolling in English-taught undergraduate engineering and technology degrees at Gebouw T.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school graduation diploma equivalent to Dutch HAVO/VWO with strong grades in mathematics and physics.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no subscore below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation letter (500 words) describing passion for circular materials and technological innovation.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters from high school science teachers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€3,000 annual tuition fee reduction across all 4 undergraduate years (€12,000 total)
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
€9,200/year standard non-EU tuition (reduced to €6,200/year with scholarship)
