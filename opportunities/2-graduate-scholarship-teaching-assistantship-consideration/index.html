What this opportunity means

Applicants to qualifying research-based programs are considered for Acadia Graduate Scholarship and/or Graduate Teaching Assistantship if department requirements are met. The published February 1 first-round deadline is recurring, and does not establish every program's current opening date. Funding is not automatic.

Scholarship eligibility, award consideration and guaranteed funding are different. Determine which award requires a separate application, which is assessed automatically, and whether the applicant is a current student.
