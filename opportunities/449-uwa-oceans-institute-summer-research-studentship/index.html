UWA Oceans Institute Summer Research Studentship

Two-month summer research program at the world-class UWA Oceans Institute investigating coral reef resilience, ocean acoustics, and sustainable fisheries.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Current 3rd year undergraduate or Honours student with minimum WAM 70%.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in marine biology, oceanography, or environmental science.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tax-free stipend of AUD $4,500 for an 8-week intensive marine research project
Application fee
No application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full studentship; no tuition fees charged.
