UCalgary Biomedical Graduate Summer Research Fellowship (BGS-SRF)

Four-month competitive summer research placement at the Cumming School of Medicine providing direct mentored benchwork in immunology, cancer biology, or neurosciences.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in 3rd or 4th year undergraduate program in biological or biomedical sciences with minimum 3.2/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated academic English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed laboratory coursework in cellular biology, genetics, or physiology.
Recommendation letters
One faculty member recommendation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Living stipend of CAD $7,500 for a 16-week intensive laboratory research placement
Application fee
No fee to submit summer fellowship application
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition charges; direct research studentship.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 15, 2027 (Summer cohort application deadline)
