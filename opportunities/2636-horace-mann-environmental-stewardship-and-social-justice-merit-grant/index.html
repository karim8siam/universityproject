Horace Mann Environmental Stewardship and Social Justice Merit Grant

Institutional scholarship honoring Antioch founder Horace Mann, supporting incoming master's and doctoral scholars in environmental studies and clinical psychology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate GPA of 3.25 or higher on a 4.0 scale from an accredited institution.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80 minimum English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for Antioch graduate admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
750-word personal statement detailing dedication to ecological sustainability and community advocacy.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from academic professors or environmental NGO directors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: up to USD $6,000 annual tuition grant applied directly to graduate degree coursework
Application fee
USD $50 graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
USD $19,500/year base master's tuition; reduced with institutional stewardship grant
