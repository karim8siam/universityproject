HfBK Dresden Postgraduate Master's in Art Therapy (Aufbaustudium)

Renowned two-year postgraduate master's program (Aufbaustudium Kunsttherapie) bridging fine art practice with psychodynamic and clinical healthcare interventions, qualifying graduates for therapeutic practice in hospitals, psychiatric clinics, and rehabilitation institutes.

Official institution source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed university degree in Fine Arts, Design, Psychology, Medicine, or Social Pedagogy.
Language proficiency
German language proficiency level C1 (TestDaF or DSH-2) for clinical patient interaction.
Standardized tests
Not required by German art academies.
Portfolio / Internship
Artistic portfolio demonstrating creative competence and documentation of at least 3 months clinical/social internship experience.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, certified degree diplomas, personal motivation statement, and successful artistic entrance examination.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free public university education; eligible for Deutschlandstipendium and BAföG grants
Application fee
Free application; semester fee of approx. €354 covering student union and regional public transit
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Public university study in the Free State of Saxony is tuition-free for all nationalities.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 (Annual application deadline for Autumn semester matriculation)
