JSPS-FWU Postdoctoral Fellowship in Global Gender Studies & East Asian Public Policy

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship supported by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) examining gender labor economics, family welfare policies, and corporate governance in Japan.
