Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Management and Finance

Executive part-time MBA degree training future chief financial officers, banking directors, and risk executives in corporate restructuring, private equity, fintech disruption, and quantitative treasury management.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree (min 210 ECTS) with minimum German grade 2.5.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT or internal HfWU analytical test.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and evidence of two years professional finance experience.
Recommendation letters
One employer letter of recommendation and personal admissions interview.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
