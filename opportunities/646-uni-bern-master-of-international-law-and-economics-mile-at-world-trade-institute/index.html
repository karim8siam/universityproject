Uni Bern Master of International Law and Economics (MILE) at World Trade Institute

World's leading one-year postgraduate program at the World Trade Institute (WTI) combining international trade law, WTO dispute settlement, digital commerce, and climate border adjustments.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's or Master's in Law, Economics, or International Relations with top standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 105.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Analytical writing sample or legal essay on international trade regulation.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
