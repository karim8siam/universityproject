MIT Master of Science in Technology and Policy (TPP)

Rigorous two-year interdisciplinary Master of Science program bridging cutting-edge engineering innovation with international energy policy, cybersecurity governance, and economic regulation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in engineering or quantitative sciences with strong academic marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE scores accepted.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose demonstrating passion for technology-driven public policy challenges.
Recommendation letters
Three academic or professional recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full research assistantships (RA) providing tuition coverage and monthly stipends available for admitted students
Application fee
USD $75 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Fully subsidized for students holding research assistant appointments.
