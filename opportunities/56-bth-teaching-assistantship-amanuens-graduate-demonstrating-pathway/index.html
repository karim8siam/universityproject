BTH Teaching Assistantship (Amanuens) & Graduate Demonstrating Pathway

Departmental academic employment (Amanuens) for enrolled graduate students at BTH, assisting course directors with computer science lab supervision, engineering mechanics problem-solving sessions, and student code reviews.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Registered student in Master of Science or doctoral program at BTH in good academic standing.
IELTS / English language
Fluent spoken and written English adequate for university-level lab instruction.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Demonstrated top grades in the relevant undergraduate or postgraduate course units.
Recommendation letters
Course coordinator approval and endorsement from the relevant department head.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried: SEK 160 to SEK 220 per hour for laboratory supervision, exercise coaching, and assignment assessment
Application fee
Free employment application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; salaried employment contract under Swedish Higher Education Ordinance (HF Ch. 5).

Application deadline
August 20, 2027 for Autumn semester teaching appointments
