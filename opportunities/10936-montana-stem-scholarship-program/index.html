Montana STEM Scholarship Program

Montana University System · Helena, Montana, United States

Target Discipline: engineeringApplication Deadline: March 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

State-funded merit award for Montana high school graduates pursuing degrees in science, technology, engineering, mathematics, or allied healthcare across MUS campuses.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Tiered scholarship providing up to $6,000 total ($1,000 freshman year, up to $2,000 senior year)
Tuition Rate
Applied directly against MUS campus tuition
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
3.25+ cumulative GPA with 4 years of math and 3 years of lab science
English Language
Satisfied via Montana secondary education
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Transcripts showing STEM coursework completion
Letters of Recommendation
One STEM teacher recommendation

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult Montana University System's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
