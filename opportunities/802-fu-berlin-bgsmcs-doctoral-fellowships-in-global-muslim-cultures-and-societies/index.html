FU Berlin BGSMCS Doctoral Fellowships in Global Muslim Cultures and Societies

Doctoral funding at the Berlin Graduate School Muslim Cultures and Societies (BGSMCS), investigating historical and contemporary religious networks, political movements, and cultural transformations across the Islamic world.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Islamic Studies, Political Science, History, Sociology, or Anthropology with German grade 2.0 or better.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 95.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated interest in transnational Muslim societies, intellectual history, or political sociology.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
DAAD / Einstein Foundation salaried doctoral stipend: €1,350/month for 3 years plus travel allowances; zero tuition
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; only standard semester contribution (€312/term including public transit).
