Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Buddhist Studies and Social Philosophy

Three-year doctoral program providing rigorous philological, historical, and ethical analysis of Mahayana and Shinshu Buddhist thought and its application to modern social welfare.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Buddhist Studies, Philosophy, or Religious History with high academic honors.
English language proficiency
JLPT N1 and proficiency in reading Classical Chinese (Kanbun) and Buddhist canonical texts.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Japanese religious studies doctorates.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and 4,000-character doctoral research proposal on Shinshu Buddhist thought or social ethics.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from recognized Buddhist studies scholars.
