Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Atlantic Coastal Wetland and Estuarine Biogeochemistry

Four-year doctoral candidacy investigating blue carbon storage, coastal erosion mitigation, and eelgrass restoration within the UNESCO Bras d'Or Lake Biosphere Reserve in Cape Breton.
