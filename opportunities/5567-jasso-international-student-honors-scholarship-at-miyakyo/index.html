Scholarship

JASSO International Student Honors Scholarship at Miyakyo

Monthly stipend funded by the Japan Student Services Organization (JASSO) for privately-financed international students demonstrating scholastic excellence.

University
Miyagi University of Education
Location
Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture, Japan
Funding
¥48,000 per month for 12 months (¥576,000 total)
Tuition
Additional to standard fees
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
April 20, 2027
Requirements
Privately financed international student at Miyakyo with GPA >= 2.30/3.0.
