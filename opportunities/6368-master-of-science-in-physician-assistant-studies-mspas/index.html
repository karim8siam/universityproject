Master of Science in Physician Assistant Studies (MSPAS)

Twenty-four-month continuous master's degree accredited by ARC-PA, training compassionate primary care medical providers through didactic human anatomy labs and year-long clinical rotations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 GPA in biology, chemistry, and anatomy prerequisites.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 or IELTS 7.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional.
Thesis / Research statement
CASPA personal statement, verification of 500 hours direct patient care, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Three reference letters (one practicing physician or PA required).
