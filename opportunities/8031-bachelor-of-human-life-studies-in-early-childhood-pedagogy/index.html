Bachelor of Human Life Studies in Early Childhood Pedagogy

Four-year baccalaureate curriculum leading to Kindergarten Teacher First-Class Certification and Nursery Teacher licensure, with clinical teaching at Shirayuri kindergarten.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation with good academic performance in humanities and language
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese >= 200 for international candidates
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of vocation for child education (800 words in Japanese) and official transcripts
Recommendation letters
One letter of recommendation from high school educator
