NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) at Inholland

Competitive scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science for international students from outside the EEA.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Excellent academic record in previous secondary or undergraduate studies (minimum GPA 3.2/4.0)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in all bands
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by Dutch universities of applied sciences
Thesis / Research statement
Compelling motivation letter demonstrating professional ambition in the Netherlands
Recommendation letters
Two academic or employer recommendations

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
