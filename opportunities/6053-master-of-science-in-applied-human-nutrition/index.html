Master of Science in Applied Human Nutrition

Advanced graduate curriculum combining clinical metabolic nutrition, public health dietary policies, community food security, and functional food biochemistry.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in human nutrition, dietetics, or biological sciences with minimum 3.2 GPA in final 2 years.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 93.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word statement of research interests aligning with departmental faculty.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters.
