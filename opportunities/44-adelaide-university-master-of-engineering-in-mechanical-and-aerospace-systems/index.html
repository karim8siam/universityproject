Adelaide University Master of Engineering in Mechanical and Aerospace Systems

Engineers Australia-accredited professional master's degree in mechanical, aerospace, and mechatronic systems, offering rigorous training in fluid mechanics, aerodynamic design, propulsion, structural analysis, and autonomous systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / CGPA
Four-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) or equivalent accredited degree with minimum GPA 4.5/7.0.
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Undergraduate engineering capstone project or industry design portfolio.
Recommendation letters
Official university academic transcripts, degree certificate, and curriculum vitae.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Adelaide Global Citizens Scholarship (15%–30% discount)
Application fee
AUD $150 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $56,500 per year (indicative 2026–27 international rate; 2-year full-time professional degree)
