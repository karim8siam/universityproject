UCL Centre for Artificial Intelligence Research Placements

Frontier artificial intelligence research center in Bloomsbury, conducting foundational research in deep learning, multi-agent systems, and neural rendering in collaboration with the Alan Turing Institute.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduate degree in Computing, Data Science, or Applied Mathematics with distinction.
English language proficiency
Technical English publishing and presentation capability.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated contributions in reinforcement learning, generative models, or neural computer vision.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, research portfolio, and two academic references.
