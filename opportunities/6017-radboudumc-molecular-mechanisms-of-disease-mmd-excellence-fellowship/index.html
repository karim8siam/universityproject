Radboudumc Molecular Mechanisms of Disease (MMD) Excellence Fellowship

Prestigious research master's fellowship preparing top international students for high-impact doctoral research in oncology, immunology, and metabolic disease.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Molecular Biology, Biochemistry, Biomedical Sciences, or related field (top 10% of cohort).
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation letter, curriculum vitae, and laboratory experimental internship reports.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university science professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full scholarship: complete non-EU tuition reduction + €11,500 annual living stipend
