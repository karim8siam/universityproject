Ontario Global College Student Exchange and Mobility Fellowship

Competitive semester fellowship supporting Ontario polytechnic scholars undertaking collaborative engineering, design, or business research at partner colleges in Europe and Asia.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.5/4.0 GPA in an enrolled Ontario college diploma or degree program.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or equivalent official language proof.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
College academic transcript, host institution study plan, and essay.
Recommendation letters
Academic dean or program coordinator endorsement.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $7,500 travel allowance and academic stipend for semester abroad research
Application fee
CAD $125 consortium processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
Waived under bilateral consortium exchange agreements
