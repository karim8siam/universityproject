UniTrento CIMeC Doctoral Program in Cognitive and Brain Sciences

Interdisciplinary doctoral program at the Centre for Mind/Brain Sciences (CIMeC) using fMRI, MEG, EEG, and computational models to explore human cognition and neural mechanisms.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Cognitive Neuroscience, Psychology, Physics, or Biomedical Engineering.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Research proposal addressing neural computation, visual perception, or neuroimaging methods.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
