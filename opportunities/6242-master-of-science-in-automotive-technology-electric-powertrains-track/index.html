Master of Science in Automotive Technology (Electric Powertrains Track)

Two-year international master's degree conducted in English, teaching electric vehicle battery management, high-voltage inverters, vehicle dynamics, and autonomous driving architectures.

IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in speaking).

Not required.

Motivation letter, curriculum vitae, and bachelor's thesis summary.
