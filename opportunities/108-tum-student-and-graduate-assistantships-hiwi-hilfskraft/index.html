TUM Student and Graduate Assistantships (HiWi - Hilfskraft)

Flexible part-time academic employment contracts (up to 20 hours per week) assisting TUM professors in teaching exercise sessions, grading assignments, and maintaining laboratory software.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Matriculated student at TUM in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Proficiency in English or German depending on the teaching/lab assignment.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High academic marks in the subject area of the tutoring or software project.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement from the supervising chair professor or senior scientific staff.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried hourly university employment: €14.50 to €18.50 per hour under Bavarian university student collective regulations
Application fee
Free application via TUM job board
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; salaried student employment.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 for Summer semester appointments
