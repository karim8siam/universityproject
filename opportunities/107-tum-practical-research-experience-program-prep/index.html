TUM Practical Research Experience Program (PREP)

Prestigious 10-week summer research internship (July–August) pairing international students with TUM research chairs to conduct individual laboratory investigations across robotics, materials, and physics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate (junior/senior) or Master's student at an accredited North American or European partner university with GPA 3.0+ / 4.0.
English language proficiency
Fluent English proficiency for scientific laboratory communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Resume / CV and 1-page letter of motivation highlighting interest in specific TUM research projects.
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation from a home university faculty member.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: €1,000 living stipend allowance, free TUM summer school tuition, organized housing assistance, and social excursions
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; fully subsidized summer research program.
