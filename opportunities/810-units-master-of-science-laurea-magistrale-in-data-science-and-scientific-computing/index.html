UniTS Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) in Data Science and Scientific Computing

Prestigious joint two-year Master of Science in English organized by UniTS and SISSA, providing advanced mathematical foundations and computing skills for machine learning, high-performance computing, and computational modeling.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
BSc in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or Engineering with strong GPA (minimum 75%).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in multivariable calculus, linear algebra, algorithms, and scientific programming.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
UniTS International Talent Scholarships (€4,000/year) and ARDiS regional full accommodation and meal grants
Application fee
€30 pre-evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
Progressive income-based tuition: €156 to €2,400/year based on ISEE Parificato.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 (Non-EU international intake round 2)
