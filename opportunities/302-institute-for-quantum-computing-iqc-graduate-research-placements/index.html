Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC) Graduate Research Placements

Interdisciplinary research placements at one of the world's premier quantum centres, conducting theoretical and experimental investigations into superconducting qubits, optics, and algorithms.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High honours Bachelor's or Master's degree in physics, mathematics, or computer science (82%+ average).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Physics GRE or General GRE strongly encouraged.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong mathematical physics foundation; prior research in quantum information theory or experimental hardware.
Recommendation letters
Three academic letters confirming research potential.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded research appointment: Competitive stipend starting at CAD $28,500/year plus specialized laboratory travel grants
Application fee
Standard department application fee applies
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition support packages provided through IQC and partner departments.
