AIU Lifelong Learning and Digital Leadership Fellowship

Fellowship recognizing mid-career adult learners and tech professionals who demonstrate exceptional potential in driving organizational digital transformation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation from an accredited undergraduate program with minimum 3.3 GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 80 equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for institutional leadership awards.
Thesis / Research statement
1,000-word proposal on leveraging adaptive learning technologies to expand adult higher education.
Recommendation letters
Two professional reference letters from executive directors or academic mentors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual fellowship grant of USD $5,000 applied toward graduate degree completion
Application fee
Zero fellowship fee
Tuition / joining costs
Direct tuition reduction on graduate coursework
