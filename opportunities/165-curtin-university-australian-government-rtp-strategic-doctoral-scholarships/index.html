Curtin University Australian Government RTP & Strategic Doctoral Scholarships

Premier Australian doctoral research scholarship providing complete tuition coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $35,000 for high-achieving domestic and international PhD candidates at Curtin.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1 / 80%+ equivalent) in prior undergraduate or Master's research degree.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in all bands or TOEFL iBT 79.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research output (peer-reviewed journal papers, conference proceedings, or Honours thesis).
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports assessing research aptitude submitted through the online referee portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full tuition fee offset plus annual living stipend of AUD $35,000 per year (tax-free) for 3.5 years, relocation subsidy, and health cover
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived under RTP Fee Offset regulations.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
October 31, 2026 for Semester 1 (February 2027) intake consideration
