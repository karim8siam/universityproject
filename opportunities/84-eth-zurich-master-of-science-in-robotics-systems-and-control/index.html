ETH Zurich Master of Science in Robotics, Systems and Control

Specialized interdisciplinary master's degree program (120 ECTS) bridging mechanical engineering (D-MAVT), electrical engineering (D-ITET), and computer science (D-INFK) to develop intelligent autonomous robots, aerial vehicles, and cyber-physical systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Electrical, or Computer Engineering with top-tier academic marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for international applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
Deep coursework background in control systems, dynamic modeling, linear algebra, calculus, and C++/Python programming.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts, two letters of recommendation, and personal statement of purpose.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for ESOP merit fellowship and laboratory research assistantships
Application fee
CHF 150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 730/semester (Swiss/resident) or CHF 2,190/semester (international threefold).
