Master of Science in Medical Engineering (HealthTech & Medical Imaging)

World-class English-taught master's program situated in the heart of Germany's Medical Valley, focusing on CT/MRI imaging algorithms, surgical robotics, neural prosthetics, and biomaterials.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Medical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or Physics with minimum grade 2.5.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; subject entrance test for international candidates.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcripts, detailed module descriptions, statement of purpose, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free public university; paid research assistantships at Siemens Healthineers and Fraunhofer IIS (€900–€1,400/month)
Application fee
€0 application fee via FAU Campo portal
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €160 semester fee
