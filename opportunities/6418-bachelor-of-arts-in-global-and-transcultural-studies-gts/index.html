Bachelor of Arts in Global and Transcultural Studies (GTS)

Four-year all-English undergraduate degree training global scholars in transcultural anthropology, postcolonial literary studies, international migration, and media representations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed 12 years of elementary and secondary schooling.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80 (native English speakers exempt).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts, standardized test scores (SAT/ACT/IB optional), and personal essay.
Recommendation letters
One academic evaluation letter.
