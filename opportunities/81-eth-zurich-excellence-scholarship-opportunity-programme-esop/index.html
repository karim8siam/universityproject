ETH Zurich Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (ESOP)

ETH Zurich's premier merit fellowship awarded to exceptional incoming Master's candidates, providing CHF 24,000 per year for living expenses, complete tuition coverage, and access to the ETH Zurich Foundation network.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation in the top 10% of undergraduate Bachelor's degree class (grade A equivalent).
English language proficiency
C1 level English: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not compulsory; GRE revised General Test recommended for non-Bologna engineering degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Independent Master's thesis pre-proposal (1–2 pages) outlining a scientific research question and methodology.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from university professors, official transcripts, and CV.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: CHF 12,000 per semester (CHF 24,000 per year) living stipend plus 100% full tuition fee waiver and dedicated mentorship network
Application fee
CHF 150 application fee for international credentials (waived for holders of Swiss degrees)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived for regular duration of Master's program (3 to 4 semesters).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
November 30, 2026 at 11:59 AM CET (Strict annual deadline for Autumn Master's entry)
