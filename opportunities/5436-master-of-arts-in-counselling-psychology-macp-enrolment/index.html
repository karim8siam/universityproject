Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology (MACP) Enrolment

Intensive 30-month professional degree satisfying academic criteria for Canadian Counselling and Psychotherapy Association (CCPA) and provincial colleges (CRPO).

University
Yorkville University
Location
Toronto & Concord, Ontario / Vancouver, BC, Canada
Funding
Eligible for provincial student aid (OSAP/StudentAid BC) and monthly payment plans
Tuition
$39,500 total program tuition (domestic)
Application Fee
$75 CAD application fee
Deadline
June 1, 2027
Requirements
Bachelor's degree with minimum GPA 3.0/4.0, introductory psychology courses, statement of intent, and 2 professional references.
