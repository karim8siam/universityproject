GETI Tasmania International Student Academic Scholarship

Recognizes high-achieving international scholars entering Year 11 or 12 at Elizabeth College aiming for high Australian Tertiary Admission Ranks (ATAR).

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top 15% academic standing in prior secondary schooling (Year 9/10)
English language proficiency
IELTS 5.5 or equivalent
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
500-word personal essay on contributing to the Tasmanian school community
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from current school teachers

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
AUD $3,000 tuition fee concession
Application fee
AUD $250 GETI fee
Tuition / joining costs
Deducted from annual Tasmanian Government school tuition
