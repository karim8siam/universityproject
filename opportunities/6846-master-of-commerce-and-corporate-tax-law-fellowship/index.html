Master of Commerce and Corporate Tax Law Fellowship

Renowned graduate program preparing certified tax accountants (Zeirishi) and financial directors in Japanese corporate tax codes, auditing standards, and international tax treaties.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Commerce, Economics, or Business with minimum 2.8 GPA.
English language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N1 for Japanese track or TOEFL iBT 80 for international business track.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, graduation certificate, and 2,000-character research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.
