York Centre for Vision Research (CVR) Summer Student Fellowship

Twelve-week mentored summer research fellowship at the internationally acclaimed Centre for Vision Research, investigating biological visual processing, virtual reality perception, and computational vision models.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in third or fourth year of BSc in Neuroscience, Psychology, Computer Science, or Biology with minimum 80% average.
English language proficiency
Fluent working English proficiencies.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed laboratory coursework in cognitive neuroscience, perceptual psychology, or neural systems.
Recommendation letters
One referee letter from a faculty lab head.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $6,000 12-week summer stipend with neurophysiology and eye-tracking lab access
Application fee
No application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges during summer traineeship appointment.
