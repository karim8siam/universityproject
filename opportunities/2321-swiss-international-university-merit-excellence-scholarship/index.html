Swiss International University Merit Excellence Scholarship

Merit-based international tuition reduction granted to exceptional candidates entering postgraduate or doctoral programs in Zurich.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.3 on a 4.0 scale in prior undergraduate or graduate studies
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or equivalent English proof
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated professional track record and leadership essay
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from senior executives or academic advisors

Partial tuition fee reduction covering up to 30% of total program tuition fees
