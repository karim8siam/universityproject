Gakushuin MEXT University Recommendation Scholarships & Doctoral Tuition Waivers

Prestigious Japanese government doctoral award administered through Gakushuin University, offering complete financial independence, tuition waivers, and airfare for premier international research scholars.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum GPA of 2.30 on a 3.00 scale (equivalent to 80% or 3.4/4.0 GPA) in previous university degrees.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Superior academic record, thesis summary, and detailed proposed research outline.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from deans or academic professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
100% Fully funded: Monthly living allowance of ¥145,000/month, full tuition waiver, and round-trip economy airfare
Application fee
¥0 screening fee for MEXT applicants
Tuition / joining costs
100% covered by the Japanese Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology.
