WHU Excellence & Diversity Master's Scholarship

Merit scholarship awarded to candidates demonstrating exceptional GMAT/GRE scores, remarkable leadership potential, and diverse international backgrounds.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Excellent bachelor's degree in business, economics, or quantitative STEM field with GPA >= 3.6/4.0
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 (minimum 6.5 in all sections) or TOEFL iBT 100
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT score >= 660 (or GMAT Focus >= 615 / GRE equivalent)
Thesis / Research statement
CV, transcripts, essay answering two prompt questions, and video interview submission
Recommendation letters
2 academic or senior managerial recommendations

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to 50% tuition reduction (€16,000 to €22,000 value) awarded across the master's study duration
Application fee
€100
Tuition / joining costs
€34,800 to €42,000 total program tuition (depending on Master in Management / Master in Finance track)
