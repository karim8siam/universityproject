Intensive Workshop in Japanese Wooden Joinery and Seismic Computation

Two-week hands-on summer course in Kyoto examining traditional carpenter tool sharpening, hand-cut kigumi joints, dynamic shaking test simulations, and finite element modeling of pagoda towers.
