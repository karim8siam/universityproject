Royal Netherlands Navy Full Officer Cadetship & Study Salary

Fully funded military higher education scholarship and salaried cadet contract for qualified applicants entering the Royal Naval College to become naval officers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: 100% full tuition paid by the Ministry of Defence plus a monthly net salary (€1,450 to €2,300/month as midshipman), free meals, uniform, and naval lodging

Free
