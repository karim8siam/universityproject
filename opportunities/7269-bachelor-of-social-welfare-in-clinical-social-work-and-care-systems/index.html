Bachelor of Social Welfare in Clinical Social Work and Care Systems

Four-year specialized degree preparing students for Certified Social Worker and Care Worker qualifications, focusing on community-based welfare, disaster recovery care, and elderly assistance.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation certificate with demonstrated commitment to humanitarian and social service.
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or higher (all primary instruction conducted in Japanese).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High school academic transcripts, personal statement, and record of volunteer/social activities.
Recommendation letters
One letter of recommendation from school principal or mentor.
