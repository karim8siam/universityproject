Concordia University Doctoral Graduate Fellowships & International Tuition Awards

Prestigious university-wide doctoral entrance awards providing guaranteed four-year living stipends, teaching assistantships, and tuition offsets for high-achieving international researchers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.5/4.3 cumulative GPA (equivalent to A- standing) in Master's degree studies.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for international applicants with non-North American degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence, peer-reviewed publications, or strong Master's thesis.
Recommendation letters
Three academic referee letters submitted via the graduate portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: CAD $14,000/year base fellowship for 4 years plus departmental assistantships ($12,000/year) and International Tuition Fee Differential awards
Application fee
CAD $110 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
International differential tuition waived through Quebec and university awards.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Priority deadline for Fall admission & fellowship awards)
