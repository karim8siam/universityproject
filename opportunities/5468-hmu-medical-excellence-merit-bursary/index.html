HMU Medical Excellence Merit Bursary

Merit-based institutional bursary awarded to top-ranking medical and clinical psychology students during their clinical studies.

University
HMU Health and Medical University Erfurt
Location
Erfurt, Thuringia, Germany
Funding
€3,000 annual direct tuition offset
Tuition
Credited against semester invoices
Application Fee
Automatic assessment based on academic grade transcripts
Deadline
July 31, 2027
Requirements
Enrolled HMU student with grade 1.3 or higher in first-year examinations.
