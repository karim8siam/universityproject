Veneto ESU Regional Architecture & Design Scholarship

Regional government award ensuring equal access to higher education for international and Italian students admitted to Iuav architecture and urban design programs.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in Architecture, Design, or Urban Studies with good academic standing
English language proficiency
English B2 or Italian B2 depending on degree track
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Family economic documentation (ISEE Parificato) for financial eligibility
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Zero fee
