Master of Public Policy (MPP) at the Willy Brandt School

Two-year international master’s program taught entirely in English, featuring practical policy analysis projects, global governance concentrations, and an integrated internship.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in social science, economics, law, or humanities with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose, resume, and analytical essay on a pressing public policy issue
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional reference letters
