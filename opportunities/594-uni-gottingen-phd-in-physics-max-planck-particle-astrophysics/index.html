Uni Göttingen PhD in Physics & Max Planck Particle Astrophysics

Doctoral research program at the II. Institute of Physics collaborating directly with CERN's ATLAS experiment and the Max Planck Institute for Solar System Research.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Physics with high honors in quantum field theory, particle physics, or astrophysics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for non-EU degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or publication in ATLAS experiment data analysis or dark matter searches.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried TV-L E13 contract (€34,000 to €52,000/year gross) with full social benefits for 3 to 4 years
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition fees.
