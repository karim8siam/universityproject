Stranmillis International Commonwealth Teacher Scholarship

Merit scholarship supporting teachers from Commonwealth countries pursuing master's degrees in early years education or educational leadership in Belfast.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 2:1 honours degree or international equivalent in education
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
750-word essay on how graduate study will impact primary education in applicant's home nation
Recommendation letters
Two professional reference letters
