U of T Graduate Teaching Assistantships (CUPE 3902 Unit 1)

Departmental academic teaching appointments providing instruction leadership training and hourly remuneration for running weekly laboratory sections, grading exams, and tutoring undergraduate students.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Registered graduate student in good standing at the University of Toronto.
English language proficiency
High oral English proficiency for lecturing, recitation, and laboratory instruction.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong mastery of underlying undergraduate curriculum subject matter.
Recommendation letters
Approval from department graduate chair and course coordinator.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried academic employment: CAD $48.24 to CAD $51.10 per hour under CUPE 3902 Unit 1 collective agreement
Application fee
Free application via departmental TA job boards
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; salaried university teaching contract.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 15, 2027 for upcoming academic year teaching appointments
