Master of Science in Computer Science and Data Analytics

Two-year 120-ECTS English-taught master's program jointly organized with the Universities of Bern and Neuchâtel (Swiss Joint Master in Computer Science), covering distributed systems, machine learning, and cryptography.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science or related quantitative field with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
English language proficiency at CEFR B2/C1 level (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 90+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Swiss cantonal master's degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and academic motivation letter detailing programming competencies in Python, C++, and algorithms.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of reference from university professors.
