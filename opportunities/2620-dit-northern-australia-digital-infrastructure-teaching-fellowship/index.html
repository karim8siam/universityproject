DIT Northern Australia Digital Infrastructure Teaching Fellowship

Developmental fellowship supporting emerging telecommunications trainers in building advanced laboratory simulations and hardware testing benches in Darwin.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Advanced Diploma or Bachelor of Engineering with high distinction marks.
English language proficiency
Fluent spoken and written English capability.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for vocational teaching fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Technical design proposal for laboratory simulation modules in optical networks.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from telecommunications industry executives.
