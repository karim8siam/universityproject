LNU Salaried PhD Position in Aquatic Ecology & Baltic Sea Biogeochemistry

Four-year salaried doctoral research post based at the Kalmar campus, examining bacterioplankton dynamics, nutrient pollution, and climate change impacts on Baltic Sea coastal ecosystems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Marine Biology, Ecology, Aquatic Science, or Environmental Chemistry.
English language proficiency
Fluent working English (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis in aquatic ecology, microbial ecology, or biogeochemical cycling.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee contacts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full 4-year Swedish salaried employment: SEK 31,500 to SEK 35,000/month gross plus full healthcare and pension; zero tuition
Application fee
SEK 0 application fee for doctoral positions
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 0 tuition; all doctoral positions are fully paid employment under Swedish labor law.
