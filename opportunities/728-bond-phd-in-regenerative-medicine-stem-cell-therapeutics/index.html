Bond PhD in Regenerative Medicine & Stem Cell Therapeutics

Doctoral research based at the Clem Jones Centre for Regenerative Medicine, investigating induced pluripotent stem cells, neurodegenerative disease models, and tissue engineering for chronic ocular conditions.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Honours Class I or Master's degree in molecular biology, regenerative medicine, or cellular physiology.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in all bands).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Practical laboratory experience in stem cell culture, flow cytometry, or immunohistochemistry.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from laboratory directors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full 3-year doctoral scholarship: AUD $32,500/year stipend plus cell culture consumables funding

AUD $0 application fee
