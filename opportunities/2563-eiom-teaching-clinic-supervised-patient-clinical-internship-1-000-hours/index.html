EIOM Teaching Clinic Supervised Patient Clinical Internship (1,000 Hours)

Mandatory 1,000-hour hands-on clinical internship in the on-campus EIOM clinic in Padua, examining real patients, formulating treatment plans, and performing supervised manual treatments.
