Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Mechanical and Advanced Materials Engineering for Green Mobility

Specialized engineering master's degree connected to the Italian Motor Valley (Ferrari, Lamborghini, Ducati), focusing on lightweight composite materials, EV battery pack cooling, and electric vehicle acoustics.
