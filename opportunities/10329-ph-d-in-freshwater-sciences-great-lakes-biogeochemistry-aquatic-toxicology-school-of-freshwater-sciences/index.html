Ph.D. in Freshwater Sciences - Great Lakes Biogeochemistry & Aquatic Toxicology - School of Freshwater Sciences

University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee · Milwaukee, Wisconsin, United States

Target Discipline: environmentApplication Deadline: January 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

The nation's only standalone graduate school of freshwater sciences, utilizing the R/V Neeskay research vessel on Lake Michigan to study toxic cyanobacteria blooms, microplastic transport, quagga mussel ecosystem disruptions, and urban stormwater hydrology.
