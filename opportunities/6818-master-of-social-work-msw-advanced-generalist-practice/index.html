Master of Social Work (MSW) - Advanced Generalist Practice

CSWE-accredited MSW preparing licensed clinical social workers, community organizers, and child welfare advocates.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum 2.75 GPA (or BSW with 3.0+ for Advanced Standing).
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, 1,000-word personal statement on social justice, and resume.
Recommendation letters
Three professional or academic references.

Application fee
$50 USD fee

$460 USD per credit hour (60 credits for 2-year track; 30 credits for Advanced Standing)
