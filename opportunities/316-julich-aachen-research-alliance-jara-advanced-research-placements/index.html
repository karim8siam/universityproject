Jülich Aachen Research Alliance (JARA) Advanced Research Placements

Joint research initiative between RWTH Aachen and Forschungszentrum Jülich, enabling graduate scholars to utilize Europe's fastest supercomputers for quantum and brain simulation.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding degree in computer science, physics, or neuroscience with high quantitative marks.
English language proficiency
English language proficiency (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior experience with parallel computing, quantum simulation, or computational neuroscience.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters confirming computational aptitude.
