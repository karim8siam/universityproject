BSc (Hons) in Mental Health Nursing (NMC Registration)

Three-year qualifying mental health nursing program featuring clinical wards at South West London and St George's Mental Health NHS Trust.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
112 UCAS points from A-Levels (BBC) or BTEC Extended Diploma in Health and Social Care
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 7.0 in listening, reading, speaking and 6.5 in writing)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement showing commitment to compassionate mental health care, and DBS clearance
Recommendation letters
1 academic reference

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
NHS Learning Support Fund grant of £5,000 per year plus £1,000 regional shortage allowance
Application fee
£28.50 (UCAS fee)
Tuition / joining costs
£9,250/year (UK Home) / £17,500/year (International)
