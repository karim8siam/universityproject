Bicocca Master of Science in Materials Science

World-class two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) offered in English inside the Department of Materials Science, featuring specializations in Optoelectronics, Photovoltaics, Nanomaterials, and Energy Storage.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Physics, Chemistry, or Materials Engineering with solid grounding in quantum physics and thermodynamics.
English language proficiency
English B2 level certificate or medium of instruction verification.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and syllabus descriptions of completed mathematical and physical coursework.
Recommendation letters
Transcripts of records and academic reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Italian government MAECI grants and DSU welfare allowances
Application fee
€30 pre-selection fee
Tuition / joining costs
Up to €3,200 per year maximum for international students; reduced according to economic family indicator.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 for international pre-enrollment
