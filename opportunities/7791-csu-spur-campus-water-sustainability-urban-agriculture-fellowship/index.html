CSU Spur Campus Water Sustainability & Urban Agriculture Fellowship

Interdisciplinary graduate fellowship based at the CSU Spur Hydro and Terra buildings in Denver, conducting applied research on urban watershed management, food security, and agricultural water reuse.

IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)

Three academic letters of recommendation
