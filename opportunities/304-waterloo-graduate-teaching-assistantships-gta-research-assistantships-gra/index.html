Waterloo Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) & Research Assistantships (GRA)

Salaried academic teaching appointments under the Canadian Union of Public Employees (CUPE), providing graduate scholars with instructional experience and supplemental income.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Admission and ongoing registration in a Waterloo research graduate degree.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated spoken English proficiency: IELTS Spoken band 7.0+ or completion of ITA training.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong mastery of course curriculum to be tutored or demonstrated.
Recommendation letters
Assigned by academic graduate office.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Contractual salary: Approximately CAD $5,500 per term for a standard 10 hour/week teaching appointment
Application fee
Free application via departmental TA portal
Tuition / joining costs
Complements base departmental graduate stipend.
