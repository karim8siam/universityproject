Uppsala Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year English-taught Master's program (120 ECTS) based at the Information Technology department, covering algorithms, machine learning, embedded systems, software engineering, and an individual 30-ECTS thesis.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with major in Computer Science or Mathematics with strong grades in linear algebra, algorithms, and programming.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in each section).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and detailed summary of undergraduate mathematics and programming modules.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts, degree certificate, and passport copy.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Uppsala University Scholarships and Swedish Institute (SI) scholarships
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 72,500 per semester (SEK 290,000 total 2-year tuition for non-EU students; free for EU/EEA).
