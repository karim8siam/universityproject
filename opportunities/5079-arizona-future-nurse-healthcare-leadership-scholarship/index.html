Arizona Future Nurse Healthcare Leadership Scholarship

Merit scholarship providing substantial tuition relief to incoming BSN students demonstrating exceptional academic drive and dedication to nursing excellence.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school GPA of 3.0 or cumulative college transfer GPA of 2.75
English language proficiency
HESI A2 Composite score of 75% or TOEFL iBT 80
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
500-word personal statement detailing motivation to enter the nursing profession
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic letters of recommendation
