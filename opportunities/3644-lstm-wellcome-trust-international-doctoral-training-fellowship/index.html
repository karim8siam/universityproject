LSTM Wellcome Trust International Doctoral Training Fellowship

Prestigious four-year doctoral studentship supporting breakthrough translational research into vector biology, pathogen genomics, and vaccine efficacy in tropical endemic zones.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class Honours and Master's degree in biological sciences or medicine
English language proficiency
7.0 overall with no band below 6.5
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated commitment to low-and-middle-income country (LMIC) health research
Recommendation letters
Three academic referee letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition + £19,500/year tax-free stipend + £10,000/year research consumables for 4 years
Application fee
£0
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition covered via Wellcome Trust grant
