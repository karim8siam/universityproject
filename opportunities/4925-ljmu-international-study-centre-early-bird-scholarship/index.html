LJMU International Study Centre Early Bird Scholarship

Tuition fee reduction awarded to international pathway applicants confirming their acceptance offer before the priority intake deadline.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Good high school grades (e.g., equivalent to 5 GCSEs at grade C/4 or better)
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS for UKVI 4.5 to 5.0 (with no component below 4.0)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts, graduation certificate, and personal statement
Recommendation letters
One academic reference

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to £2,500 deduction applied directly to the pathway program tuition fee
Application fee
£0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£14,500 to £16,500 for the full three-term International Foundation Year

Dates and verification

Application deadline
June 30, 2027 (September intake)
