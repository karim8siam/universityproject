Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Agriculture, Water and Environment (Gulbali Institute Fellowship)

Three-and-a-half year doctoral scholarship within the Gulbali Institute investigating Murray-Darling hydrological flow modeling, drought-tolerant crop genetics, or soil carbon sequestration.
