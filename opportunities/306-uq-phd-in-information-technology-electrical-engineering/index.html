UQ PhD in Information Technology & Electrical Engineering

Comprehensive 3.5 to 4 year doctoral research degree exploring artificial intelligence, cyber security, data science, computer vision, and advanced electrical systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours or Master's degree in IT, CS, or electrical engineering with significant research thesis.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with 6.0 in all components.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research writing ability and prior technical publications.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded doctoral position providing AUD $35,000/year tax-free stipend, full tuition fee remission, and project consumables funding
Application fee
AUD $100 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% covered via UQ tuition fee offset scholarship.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027 (Primary international scholarship deadline for mid-year entry)
