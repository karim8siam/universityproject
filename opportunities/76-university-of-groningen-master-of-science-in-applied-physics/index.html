University of Groningen Master of Science in Applied Physics

Top-ranked two-year Master of Science program (120 ECTS) closely linked with the Zernike Institute for Advanced Materials, offering cutting-edge specializations in quantum materials, nanophysics, smart functional devices, and advanced instrumentation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Applied Physics, Physics, or Materials Science with high marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.5 in every individual sub-score.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Publications
Coursework syllabus descriptions covering quantum mechanics, electrodynamics, thermodynamics, and solid-state physics.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts, degree certificates, CV, and motivation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Eric Bleumink Fund, NL Scholarship (€5,000), and Dutch educational loans
Application fee
€100 application fee for non-EEA applicants
Tuition / joining costs
€2,694/year (EU/EEA statutory) or €24,900/year (non-EU/EEA international).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 1, 2027 (International non-EU deadline; rolling assessment)
