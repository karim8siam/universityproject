Flinders PhD in Medical Biotechnology & Nanotechnology

Doctoral research conducted within Flinders Medical Centre and the Institute for Nanoscale Science and Technology, designing targeted drug delivery systems and point-of-care clinical diagnostics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours degree in biotechnology, biochemistry, pharmacology, or biomedical engineering.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each band).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Previous research experience in mammalian cell culture, drug formulation, or nanoparticle synthesis.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports from previous thesis advisors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed 3.5-year stipend of AUD $34,000/year plus laboratory consumables allowance
Application fee
AUD $100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition covered under university research sponsorship.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 15, 2027 (Mid-year intake scholarship review)
