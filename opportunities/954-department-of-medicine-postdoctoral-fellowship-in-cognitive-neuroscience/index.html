Department of Medicine Postdoctoral Fellowship in Cognitive Neuroscience

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the birthplace of mirror neuron discoveries, investigating motor coding, action understanding, and neuro-rehabilitation using high-density EEG and TMS.
