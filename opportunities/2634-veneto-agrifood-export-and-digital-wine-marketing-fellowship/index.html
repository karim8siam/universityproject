International mobility fellowship funding study tours and commercial apprentice placements at major European wine fairs and international gourmet distribution hubs.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top 15% academic standing across wine marketing and international commercial law modules.
English language proficiency
Italian fluency and English B2 certification.

Not applicable for Italian mobility fellowships.

1,500-word proposal on digital marketing strategies for Italian PDO/PGI certified products in Northern Europe.

Two letters of recommendation from technical instructors or wine estate directors.
