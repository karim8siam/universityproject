ECU Pirate Academic Excellence Out-of-State Merit Scholarship

Competitive merit award granted to high-achieving out-of-state and international freshmen displaying exemplary scholarship and leadership potential.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school unweighted GPA 3.7+.
English language proficiency
TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
SAT 1250+ or ACT 26+ (if submitting test scores).

Admissions application and scholarship questionnaire.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to $10,000 per year ($40,000 over four years) reducing out-of-state tuition burden
Application fee
No extra scholarship fee
Tuition / joining costs
