Ritsumeikan Synchrotron Radiation Center Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at Ritsumeikan's compact electron storage ring synchrotron facility (SR Center), characterizing advanced battery materials and semiconductor thin films.
