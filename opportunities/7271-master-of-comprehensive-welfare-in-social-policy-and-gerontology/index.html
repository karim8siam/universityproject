Master of Comprehensive Welfare in Social Policy and Gerontology

Two-year master's examining comparative welfare policy, long-term care insurance systems, social gerontology, and welfare robotics implementation in aging societies.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Social Work, Sociology, Public Administration, or related discipline (min GPA 2.8/4.0).
English language proficiency
JLPT N1 or documented academic research capacity in Japanese.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate degree certificate, transcript, 3-page research proposal, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.
