University of Sydney International Research Scholarships (USydIS)

The University of Sydney's premier graduate research award offering complete tuition coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $40,109 to exceptional domestic and international PhD candidates each year.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1 / 80%+ equivalent) in prior undergraduate or Master's research degree.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by University of Sydney.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior peer-reviewed research publications or completed Honours/Master's thesis of high distinction.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports assessing research potential submitted via the online referee portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition fee offset plus living allowance stipend of AUD $40,109 per year (tax-free) for up to 3.5 years, relocation grant, and Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)
Application fee
AUD $150 application fee (scholarship consideration is automatic upon course application)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived under the USydIS fee offset.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
November 15, 2026 for Research Period 1 & 2 (March 2027 entry) consideration
