Heidelberg Master of Science in Scientific Computing

Two-year research-focused Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) based at the Interdisciplinary Center for Scientific Computing (IWR), offering advanced specializations in Optimization, Machine Learning, Parallel Computing, and High-Performance Simulation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Mathematics, Computer Science, or Physics with minimum 180 ECTS and strong numerical mathematics foundation.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcript of records showing completed coursework matching prerequisite catalog.
Recommendation letters
Official university academic transcripts, degree certificate, and CV.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for IWR merit fellowships and HiWi assistantships
Application fee
€75 uni-assist VPD processing fee for international degrees
Tuition / joining costs
€1,500 per semester for non-EU international students (€171 semester fee for EU students).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 31, 2027 for Winter semester (October 2027) intake
