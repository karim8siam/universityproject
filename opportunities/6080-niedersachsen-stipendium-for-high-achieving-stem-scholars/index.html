Niedersachsen-Stipendium for High-Achieving STEM Scholars

State of Lower Saxony merit scholarship supporting talented first-generation and international students enrolled in technical and science degrees.

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate or secondary school grade average of 2.0 or better in German grading.
English language proficiency
German B2/C1.

Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, motivational letter, and transcript.
Recommendation letters
Faculty reference letter.
