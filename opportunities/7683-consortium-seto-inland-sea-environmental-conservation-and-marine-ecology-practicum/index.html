Consortium Seto Inland Sea Environmental Conservation and Marine Ecology Practicum

Field marine science course utilizing coastal research vessels, monitoring red tide algal blooms, microplastic distribution, benthic ecosystem biodiversity, and coastal hypoxia in the Seto Inland Sea.
