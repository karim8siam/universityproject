Master in International and Development Studies (MINT)

Flagship interdisciplinary two-year master's program offering specialized tracks in conflict and peacebuilding, global environment, gender, and trade governance on the Maison de la Paix campus.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in social sciences, economics, law, or history with upper-division honors.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 per band).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not mandatory, but quantitative GRE scores strengthen application profile.
Thesis / Research statement
Scholarly writing sample (10–15 pages) demonstrating analytical rigor and critical research methodology.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential academic recommendation letters.
