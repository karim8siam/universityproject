Master of Arts in Regional Economics and Northern Communities Development

Research master's analyzing agricultural supply chains in Hokkaido, cold-region renewable energy economics, demographic aging in northern towns, and tourism revitalization.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Economics, Business, Agricultural Economics, or Social Sciences (GPA 2.8+).
English language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N2 or N1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcripts, graduation certificate, and regional economics research plan.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
