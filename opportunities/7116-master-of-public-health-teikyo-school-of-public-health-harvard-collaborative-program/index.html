Master of Public Health (Teikyo School of Public Health - Harvard Collaborative Program)

World-class bilingual English-Japanese professional MPH program conducted in direct collaboration with faculty from Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, covering epidemiology, biostatistics, environmental health, and health policy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Sciences, or related field with GPA 3.0+.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5, or Japanese JLPT N1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Official university transcripts, CV with healthcare experience, and 1,000-word statement of public health career goals.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from medical or academic supervisors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Teikyo-Harvard Dean's Global Health Fellowship covering up to 50% tuition and Harvard summer session travel
Application fee
¥35,000 application fee; ¥250,000 admission fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥1,100,000 per year (reduced with institutional grants)
