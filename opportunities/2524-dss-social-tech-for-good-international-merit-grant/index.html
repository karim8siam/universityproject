DSS Social Tech for Good International Merit Grant

Merit-based tuition scholarship awarded to talented international designers and software technologists dedicated to solving UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Bachelor's degree in design, computing, or humanities with top-quartile academic standing.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90 minimum English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for European design school admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
Design portfolio exhibiting at least three projects focused on social sustainability, UX design, or ethical tech.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from design professors or digital industry managers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full scholarship: 100% tuition coverage (€4,250 value) for the 20-week digital transformation track
Application fee
Zero application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition waiver awarded based on design excellence and social impact
