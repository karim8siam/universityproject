Twente MESA+ Institute for Nanotechnology Salaried Doctoral Positions

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment at the world-class MESA+ NanoLab (featuring 1,250 m² of cleanroom facilities) exploring quantum electronics, lab-on-a-chip sensors, and 2D materials.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Applied Physics, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, or Chemistry with high distinction.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or publication in nanofabrication, microfluidics, or quantum devices.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried employment contract (CAO-NU) of €2,770 to €3,539/month gross plus 8% holiday allowance, 8.3% year-end bonus, and €0 tuition for 4 years
Application fee
€0 application fee for salaried positions
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition fees (employed doctoral position).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 31, 2027 (Departmental vacancy round)
