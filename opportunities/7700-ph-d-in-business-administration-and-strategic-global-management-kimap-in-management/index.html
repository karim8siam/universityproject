Ph.D. in Business Administration and Strategic Global Management (KIMAP in Management)

English-taught doctoral program at Japan’s birthplace of business administration, focusing on multinational corporate strategy, behavioral finance, supply chain agility, and organizational sociology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science, MBA, or Master of Arts in Business, Economics, or related fields with top academic record (min 3.3/4.0 GPA)
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT or GRE score strongly recommended
Thesis / Research statement
Master thesis synopsis, comprehensive doctoral research proposal (up to 5 pages), and academic CV
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from business professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
