CIMBA International Executive Leadership & Merit Fellowship

Prestigious merit fellowship awarded to high-potential international professionals entering the full-time University of Iowa MBA program at CIMBA Italy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate GPA of 3.3 or higher on a 4.0 scale
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 80
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT 600+ or GRE 310+ (or 3+ years managerial experience for waiver)
Thesis / Research statement
Essay detailing cross-cultural leadership challenges in multinational environments
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic recommendation letters
