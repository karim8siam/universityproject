Doctoral Research Fellowship in Financial Technology and Cooperative Economics

Doctoral research fellowship examining digital banking transformation, ESG compliance, and the structural resilience of cooperative financial institutions during economic shocks.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Master of Science in Finance, Economics, or Quantitative Business with minimum grade 2.0 (gut).
English language proficiency
Fluent German and English academic publication capability.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for German practitioner doctoral dissertations.
Thesis / Research statement
Exposé (3,000 words) analyzing FinTech disruption, decentralized finance, or cooperative governance models.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendations from senior German economics or finance professors.
