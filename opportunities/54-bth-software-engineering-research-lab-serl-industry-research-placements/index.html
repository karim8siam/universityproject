BTH Software Engineering Research Lab (SERL) Industry Research Placements

Postgraduate research and industrial thesis opportunities inside SERL Sweden, ranked among Europe's top empirical software engineering research institutes, solving real-world challenges in cloud architectures, AI engineering, and large-scale software systems.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Enrolled master's student in Software Engineering, Computer Science or Data Science with high academic marks.
IELTS / English language
Proficiency in English suitable for industrial and scientific collaboration.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Demonstrated experience in empirical software methods, requirements engineering, DevOps, or machine learning pipelines.
Recommendation letters
Academic transcript, project repository or portfolio, and faculty recommendation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
