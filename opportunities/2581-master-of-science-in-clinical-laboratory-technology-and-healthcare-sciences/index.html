Master of Science in Clinical Laboratory Technology and Healthcare Sciences

Two-year graduate program providing advanced laboratory research training in clinical biochemistry, diagnostic microbiology, medical genetics, and clinical immunology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Medical Technology, Biology, Pharmacology, or Physical Therapy.
English language proficiency
JLPT N1 or demonstrated academic Japanese fluency for clinical research discussions.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Japanese medical technology master's degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
2,000-character Japanese research proposal addressing molecular diagnostics, hematology, or pathology.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from university science professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
MEXT-accredited graduate curriculum; eligible for JASSO Honors Scholarships and laboratory bursaries
Application fee
¥35,000 graduate examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥950,000 annual graduate tuition (plus ¥200,000 first-year enrollment fee)
