Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT)

Entry-level professional doctorate featuring cadaver dissection labs, human motion analysis technology, cardiopulmonary therapy, and community healthcare clinics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum cumulative and prerequisite science GPA 3.0.
English language proficiency
TOEFL 85 or IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE general test scores required.
Thesis / Research statement
PTCAS application, 50 verified PT observation hours, and personal statement.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation including one from a licensed physical therapist.
