UWA Master of Professional Engineering (Civil & Offshore Engineering)

Engineers Australia and EUR-ACE accredited two-to-three year professional Master's degree specializing in marine geotechnics, offshore energy systems, and coastal infrastructure.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized Bachelor of Engineering or Bachelor of Science with engineering major (minimum WAM 65%).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 82.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Engineering mathematics and structural mechanics prerequisite completion.
Recommendation letters
Two academic references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

UWA Global Excellence Scholarship provides up to AUD $48,000 fee reduction ($12,000/year)
Application fee
AUD $100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $48,200/year (before merit scholarship reductions).
