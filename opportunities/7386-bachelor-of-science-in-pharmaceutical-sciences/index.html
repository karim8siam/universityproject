Bachelor of Science in Pharmaceutical Sciences

Four-year specialized degree in the prestigious College of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences, focusing on drug discovery, pharmacokinetics, pharmaceutical biotechnology, and clinical pharmacology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school GPA of 3.2+ with advanced coursework in Chemistry, Biology, and Calculus.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international applicants.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
SAT/ACT required for freshman admission.
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts, personal essay (500 words), and resume of scientific interests.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from science instructors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

FAMU Presidential Scholarship offering full tuition, room, board, and $500 book stipend
