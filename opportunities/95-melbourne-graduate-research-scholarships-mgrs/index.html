Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships (MGRS)

The University of Melbourne's premier graduate research award offering complete tuition coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $37,000 to approximately 600 exceptional domestic and international candidates each year.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1 / 80%+ equivalent) in prior undergraduate or Master's research degree.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by University of Melbourne.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior peer-reviewed research publications or completed Honours/Master's thesis of high distinction.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports assessing research potential submitted via the online referee portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition fee offset up to AUD $135,000 plus living allowance stipend of AUD $37,000 per year (tax-free) for up to 3.5 years, relocation grant, and OSHC health coverage
Application fee
AUD $130 application fee (scholarship consideration is automatic upon course application)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived under the Melbourne Research Training Program fee offset.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
October 31, 2026 for Round 1 (February/July 2027 intake) consideration
