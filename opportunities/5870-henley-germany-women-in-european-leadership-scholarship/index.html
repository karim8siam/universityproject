Henley Germany Women in European Leadership Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship designed to increase female representation in executive boards and senior corporate management across Europe.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree or equivalent managerial track record (minimum 3 years management experience).
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all bands) or native English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; evaluated on leadership track record and executive admissions interview.
Thesis / Research statement
1,000-word essay on transformative leadership and sustainable business governance.
Recommendation letters
Two professional reference letters from senior executives or board directors.
