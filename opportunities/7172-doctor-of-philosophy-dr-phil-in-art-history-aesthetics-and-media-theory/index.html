Doctor of Philosophy (Dr. phil.) in Art History, Aesthetics and Media Theory

Supervised third-cycle academic doctorate in philosophical aesthetics, art historiography, post-digital media philosophy, and curatorial discourse.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Art History, Philosophy, Cultural Studies, or Media Studies with grade 'gut' (2.0) or higher.
English language proficiency
German C1 and English C1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Doctoral dissertation prospectus (10-15 pages), master's thesis, CV, and agreement from supervising UdK professor.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Einstein Foundation Berlin and DFG Graduiertenkolleg doctoral stipends (€1,500/month)
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €310 semester fee
