Master of Science (M.Sc.) in International Logistics and Supply Chain Management (Joint with Plymouth University)

Dual-degree master's program delivered with the University of Plymouth, providing mastery in global freight forwarding, port logistics, supply chain digitization, and warehouse simulation.
