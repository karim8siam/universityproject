USI Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence

High-level two-year English-taught Master of Science degree offered jointly with IDSIA, covering deep learning, reinforcement learning, robotics, and computer vision.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Physics with strong programming marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 (taught entirely in English).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in machine learning, algorithms, and linear algebra.
Recommendation letters
Certified transcripts and curriculum vitae.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for USI Master's Grants and Swiss cantonal living subsidies
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 4,000 per semester for international students (scholarships available).
