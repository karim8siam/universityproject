Ph.D. in Nordic Archaeology & Cultural Heritage Conservation Fellowship

Four-year cooperative doctoral fellowship operated alongside Stockholm University, utilizing isotope bioarchaeology and dendrochronology to analyze early medieval Scandinavian settlement patterns.
