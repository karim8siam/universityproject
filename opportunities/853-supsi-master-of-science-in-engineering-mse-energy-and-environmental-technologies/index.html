SUPSI Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) - Energy and Environmental Technologies

Advanced Master of Science profile at the Institute for Applied Sustainability to the Built Environment (ISAAC), training engineers in building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV), battery storage systems, and zero-emission building design.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Environmental Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or Energy Systems.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in photovoltaics, building thermal physics, and grid integration.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Swiss cantonal education subsidies and ISAAC research assistantship stipends

CHF 150 application fee
