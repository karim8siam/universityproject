Scholarship

Coleg Cymraeg Cenedlaethol Welsh-Medium Main Bursary

Incentive bursary for students undertaking at least 40 credits per academic year through the medium of Welsh at UWTSD.

University
University of Wales Trinity Saint David
Location
Carmarthen, Lampeter, Swansea & London, Wales, UK
Funding
£1,500 (£500 per year for three years)
Tuition
Additional to statutory student finance
Application Fee
Free application via Coleg Cymraeg
Deadline
May 15, 2027
Requirements
Commitment to complete specified Welsh-medium modular coursework.
