Master of Science in Health Sciences (English-taught)

Two-year interdisciplinary Master of Science taught in English, preparing students for leadership roles in international health organizations, clinical trial registries, and healthcare consultancy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Health Sciences, Nursing, Biology, Psychology, or Physiotherapy (min. 180 ECTS)
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 95 (C1 level English)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose explaining chosen major (Health Communication, Health Economics, or Health Services Research)
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter
