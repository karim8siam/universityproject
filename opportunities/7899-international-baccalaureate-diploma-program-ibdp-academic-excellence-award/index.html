International Baccalaureate Diploma Program (IBDP) Academic Excellence Award

Merit-based tuition scholarship awarded to outstanding high school scholars entering the two-year IB Diploma Program displaying intellectual curiosity and international mindedness.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Demonstrated superior academic achievement in Grade 9 and 10 (minimum 3.50 GPA equivalent)
English language proficiency
Fluent English proficiency (instruction in English)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Scholarship essay (750 words) on global citizenship and official academic records
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from current school teachers

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to 50% tuition reduction across Grades 11 and 12 (worth up to €12,000 annually)
Application fee
€0 scholarship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Direct high school tuition waiver
