Councill Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Plant Genetics and Biotechnology

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting advanced functional genomics, CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing, and stress physiology research in crop species at the Winfred Thomas Agricultural Research Station.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Ph.D. in Plant Biotechnology, Molecular Genetics, Agronomy, or related biological discipline.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated scientific communication and peer-reviewed publication record in English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for postdoctoral appointments.
Thesis / Research statement
Three-page research proposal addressing genomic crop improvement or drought tolerance mechanisms.
Recommendation letters
Three reference contacts including doctoral dissertation advisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Postdoctoral salary of USD $58,000 per annum plus comprehensive health benefits and travel grant
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
