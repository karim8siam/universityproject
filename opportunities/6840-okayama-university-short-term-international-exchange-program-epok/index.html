Okayama University Short-Term International Exchange Program (EPOK)

One or two-semester immersive academic exchange program offering English-taught courses in Japanese culture, Asian economics, environmental sustainability, and intensive Japanese.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.0 GPA on 4.0 scale at accredited partner university.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 61 or IELTS 5.5 for non-native English speakers.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Partner university institutional nomination letter, transcripts, and study plan.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
