Ca' Foscari Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) in Economics and Finance (English-Taught)

Two-year international Master of Science in English offering specialized tracks in Quantitative Finance, Risk Management, and Sustainable Economics at Ca' Foscari's Department of Economics in San Giobbe.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Economics, Business, Mathematics, or Statistics with minimum 75% average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, and calculus.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Ca' Foscari International Student Scholarships (€5,000/year) and Veneto regional ESU full meal and accommodation grants
Application fee
€30 pre-evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
Progressive income-based tuition: €156 to €2,200/year based on ISEE Parificato.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 (Non-EU international intake round 2)
