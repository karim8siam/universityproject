Institute for Chemical Reaction Design and Discovery (ICReDD) Placements

World Premier International (WPI) research placements combining artificial intelligence, quantum chemical calculations, and experimental synthesis to invent new chemical reactions.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding academic record in chemistry, computational science, or materials.
English language proficiency
High level of English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior experience in quantum chemical calculations, machine learning, or organic synthesis.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from international researchers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded research appointment: ¥200,000 to ¥250,000/month living stipend with access to computational supercomputing and automated synthesis labs
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition fully subsidized under WPI research program.
