Erasmus Salaried PhD Positions (Promovendi under CAO-NU) & Tinbergen Institute

Four-year salaried doctoral research positions in econometrics, quantitative finance, and medical informatics, providing full statutory social benefits and pension contributions.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Master of Science degree in econometrics, economics, or data science with high academic marks.
English language proficiency
Fluency in English (IELTS 7.0+ or TOEFL 100+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE required for Tinbergen Institute economics positions.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis demonstrating solid mathematical and empirical methodology.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee contacts and cover letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried employment: Gross monthly salary of €2,872 in Year 1 rising to €3,670 in Year 4, plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% end-of-year bonus
Application fee
Free application via EUR careers portal
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition fees; full employment contract under Dutch labor law.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 31, 2027 (Rolling departmental vacancy recruitment for 2027 research intakes)
