Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Biochemistry & Cellular Receptor Signaling

Doctoral research program within Duke School of Medicine, training under Nobel Laureate Robert Lefkowitz's group to investigate G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) dynamics, beta-arrestin signaling, and drug discovery.
