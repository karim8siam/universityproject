London College of Music Performance Bursary

Endowed bursary supporting gifted young classical, jazz, and pop instrumentalists admitted to LCM performance degrees.

University
University of West London
Location
Ealing & Brentford, London, England, UK
Funding
£2,000 per academic year plus subsidized masterclasses with visiting concert artists
Tuition
Credited against tuition invoices
Application Fee
None; evaluated during practical entrance audition
Deadline
May 15, 2027
Requirements
Audition score distinction (Grade 8 ABRSM/Trinity or equivalent performance standard).
