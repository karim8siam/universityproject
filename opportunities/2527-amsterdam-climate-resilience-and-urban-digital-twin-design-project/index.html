Amsterdam Climate Resilience and Urban Digital Twin Design Project

Applied design research project creating interactive 3D digital twin visualizations and citizen sensor networks to simulate urban heat mitigation and water drainage.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in DSS digital traineeship or HvA master's track in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Professional English fluency (C1 level).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Dutch studio research.
Thesis / Research statement
Concept brief proposing sensor data visualization methods for urban heat islands and flood risk in Amsterdam.
Recommendation letters
Nomination from a DSS Digital Transformation Track Lead.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
