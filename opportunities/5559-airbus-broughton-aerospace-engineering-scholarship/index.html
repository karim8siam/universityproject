Airbus Broughton Aerospace Engineering Scholarship

Industry-sponsored scholarship co-funded by Airbus supporting top female and underrepresented students in aerospace engineering.

University
Wrexham University
Location
Wrexham & St Asaph, Wales, UK
Funding
£4,000 annual bursary plus guaranteed summer engineering placement at Airbus Broughton
Tuition
Applied towards tuition and living expenses
Application Fee
Free application
Deadline
May 31, 2027
Requirements
Enrolment in BEng Aeronautical Engineering with entry tariff >= 120 points.
