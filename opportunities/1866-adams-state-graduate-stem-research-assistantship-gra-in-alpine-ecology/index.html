Adams State Graduate STEM Research Assistantship (GRA) in Alpine Ecology

Funded research assistantship at the School of STEM supporting master's scholars conducting ecological research in the San Luis Valley and Sangre de Cristo Mountain range.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate GPA of 3.2 or higher on a 4.0 scale in biological or environmental sciences
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 79
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional / recommended for assistantship competition
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose focusing on wildlife habitat ecology or high-altitude physiology
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university science faculty

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition waiver plus $13,500 USD annual living stipend
Application fee
$30 USD ($50 international)
Tuition / joining costs
$0 USD (100% covered via graduate assistantship)
