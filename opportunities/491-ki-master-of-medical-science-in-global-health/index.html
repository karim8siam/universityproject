KI Master of Medical Science in Global Health

One-year advanced degree in collaboration with WHO collaborating centers analyzing epidemiology, maternal health, climate change health impacts, and health systems in crisis.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in health sciences, medicine, or social sciences (minimum 180 ECTS).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated engagement with public health, health equity, or epidemiological investigations.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae and letter of motivation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals covering full tuition and SEK 12,000/month living stipend
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 180,000 for the full 1-year program (non-EU/EEA).
