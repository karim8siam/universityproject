Fraunhofer IOF & Abbe Center of Photonics Summer Research Internship

Eight to twelve-week summer research placement at the Fraunhofer Institute for Applied Optics and Precision Engineering (IOF) in Jena, assembling space telescope mirrors and laser radar optics.
