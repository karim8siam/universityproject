Fisk-Vanderbilt Master's-to-PhD Bridge Fellowship (Physics, Astronomy & Materials Science)

Nationally renowned bridge program enabling talented underrepresented scholars to complete a master's at Fisk with full joint research access to Vanderbilt University labs, with guaranteed transition into Vanderbilt PhD programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Physics, Astronomy, Chemistry, Materials Science, or Engineering (GPA 3.0+).
English language proficiency
English fluency (TOEFL 90+ for international scholars).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE scores not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, curriculum vitae, 2-page personal research statement, and description of scientific curiosity.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation from science professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition waiver, comprehensive medical insurance, and $34,000 annual living stipend funded by NSF and NASA
Application fee
$0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
$0 tuition for funded Bridge Fellows
