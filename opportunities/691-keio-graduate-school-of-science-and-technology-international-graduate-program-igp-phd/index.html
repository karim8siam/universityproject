Keio Graduate School of Science and Technology International Graduate Program (IGP) PhD

All-English doctoral degree conducted at the Yagami Campus in Yokohama, featuring advanced research in mechanical engineering, electronics, applied chemistry, and systems design.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in engineering, physics, mathematics, or computer science with high marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and published papers in reputable journals or international conferences.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential letters of recommendation from university faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Keio Graduate Scholarship & RA stipends providing ¥150,000/month plus full or 50% tuition reduction awards
Application fee
¥35,000 application screening fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥1,350,000/year, highly subsidized through merit-based tuition reduction grants.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 5, 2027 (Spring second round for September 2027 enrollment)
