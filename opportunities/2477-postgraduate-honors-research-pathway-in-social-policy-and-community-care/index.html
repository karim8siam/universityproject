Postgraduate Honors Research Pathway in Social Policy and Community Care

Research-intensive honors pathway preparing graduates for doctoral investigations, policy design, and executive leadership in Australian health and community sectors.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Community Services or related AQF Level 7 degree with credit average (GPA 3.0/4.0) in core units.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all bands.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Australian honours research admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
1,500-word research proposal addressing contemporary challenges in community care or elderly social welfare.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendations from senior faculty in sociology or social work.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Institutional research sponsorship and supervised thesis support for top-quartile graduates
Application fee
AUD $250 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $19,500 annual tuition for supervised postgraduate research pathway
