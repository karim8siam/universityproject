Aberystwyth Postgraduate Teaching Assistantship (PGTA) & Demonstrating Pathway

Departmental academic employment pathway for enrolled doctoral researchers across STEM faculties, providing salaried laboratory demonstrating, practical coding tutorial support, and assessment marking.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Enrolled postgraduate research student (PhD/MPhil) in good academic standing.
IELTS / English language
Fluent spoken and written English suitable for undergraduate teaching and lab safety communication.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Demonstrated subject mastery in physics, mathematics, or computer science.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement from principal research supervisor and completion of mandatory university teaching induction.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried: £17.50 to £23.80 per hour for laboratory demonstrating, coding workshops, and seminar tutoring
Application fee
Free employment application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; hourly salaried employment under Aberystwyth academic pay scale.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
September 1, 2027 for Autumn semester tutorial allocation
