Bocconi PhD Full Fellowships in Economics, Finance & Management

All admitted Bocconi PhD candidates receive fully funded fellowships covering living costs and tuition, with placement records into top international universities (LSE, Harvard, MIT, Princeton) and central banks.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's or superior Bachelor's degree with top-tier grades in quantitative social sciences, mathematics, or business.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE or GMAT score required (quantitative percentile typically 90%+ for successful candidates).
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose, analytical writing sample, and research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential academic reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition waiver plus guaranteed tax-free annual stipend of €18,500/year for 4 years and 50% allowance increase for overseas research
Application fee
€0 application fee for PhD programs
Tuition / joining costs
Full 100% tuition waiver (€0 tuition) for all admitted doctoral students.
