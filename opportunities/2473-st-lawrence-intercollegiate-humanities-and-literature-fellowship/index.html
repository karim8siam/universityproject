St. Lawrence Intercollegiate Humanities and Literature Fellowship

Academic development fellowship designed to foster critical thinking, textual analysis, and advanced rhetoric among collegiate scholars preparing for university honors programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum collegiate average of 80% across English literature, philosophy, and humanities sequences.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 or exceptional grade performance in English 101/102 college courses.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for collegiate humanities appointments.
Thesis / Research statement
1,500-word critical essay or creative manuscript demonstrating analytical rigor in modern cultural theory.
Recommendation letters
Two faculty recommendations from literature or philosophy department instructors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual research fellowship grant of CAD $3,500 and dedicated faculty mentorship
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition waiver support for supplementary humanities seminars and writing workshops
