Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) - Psychiatric Mental Health

Specialized clinical doctorate training psychiatric nurse practitioners to diagnose, prescribe psychopharmacology, and deliver psychotherapy across lifespan populations with high rural behavioral health needs.
