PEAK Bachelor of Science in Environmental Sciences

Four-year English-taught undergraduate degree combining environmental chemistry, global biodiversity, climate dynamics, energy policy, and field observation across Japan.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation diploma with exceptional achievement in Mathematics, Chemistry, and Biology.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100+ or Academic IELTS 7.5+ (native English speakers exempt).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized test scores: SAT, ACT, IB Diploma, or GCE A-Levels required.
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts, standardized test reports, personal statement (500–600 words), and essay on environmental challenges.
Recommendation letters
Two academic evaluation letters from high school instructors.
