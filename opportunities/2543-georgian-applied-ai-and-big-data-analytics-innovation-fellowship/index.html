Georgian Applied AI and Big Data Analytics Innovation Fellowship

Applied development fellowship supporting post-graduate scholars implementing data science and machine learning pipelines for regional manufacturing and healthcare partners.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed university bachelor's degree in computer science, mathematics, or engineering with minimum 78% average.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 with minimum 6.0 in reading and writing.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Ontario graduate certificate programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Data portfolio showcasing applied machine learning, predictive modeling, or database design.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or industry supervisory references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual fellowship grant of CAD $5,000 and direct industry project mentorship
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Applied directly to post-graduate certificate tuition
