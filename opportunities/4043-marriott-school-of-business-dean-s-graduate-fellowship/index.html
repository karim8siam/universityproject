Marriott School of Business Dean's Graduate Fellowship

Merit-based fellowship awarded to outstanding domestic and international applicants admitted to the Master of Accountancy or MBA programs showing exceptional leadership and analytical capability.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate cumulative GPA of 3.6/4.0 or higher in business, economics, or STEM discipline
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (or TOEFL iBT 95)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT score of 680+ or GRE equivalent
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, two essays describing career vision and ethical commitment, and official transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic letters of recommendation
