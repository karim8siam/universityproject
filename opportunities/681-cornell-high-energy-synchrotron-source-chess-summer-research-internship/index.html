Cornell High Energy Synchrotron Source (CHESS) Summer Research Internship

Ten-week National Science Foundation REU summer research traineeship at CHESS, conducting hands-on synchrotron X-ray diffraction, spectroscopy, and imaging experiments on advanced materials.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Completed university physics and calculus courses.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

$0 tuition; all costs covered by the National Science Foundation (NSF REU).
