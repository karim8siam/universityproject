DFG Doctoral Position (TV-L E13) in Trapped-Ion Quantum Computing

Three-year salaried doctoral research appointment in the Department of Quantum Optics, operating microwave-driven trapped ytterbium ion qubits and developing multi-qubit entanglement algorithms.
