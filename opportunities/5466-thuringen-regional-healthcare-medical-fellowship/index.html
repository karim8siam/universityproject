Fellowship

Thüringen Regional Healthcare Medical Fellowship

State-sponsored medical scholarship financing tuition for medical students who commit to practicing general medicine in rural Thuringia for 5 years.

University
HMU Health and Medical University Erfurt
Location
Erfurt, Thuringia, Germany
Funding
Full monthly tuition coverage (€1,500/month) plus €500 monthly living stipend
Tuition
100% funded via Thuringian State Health Ministry
Application Fee
Free application
Deadline
April 30, 2027
Requirements
Admission to HMU Human Medicine and legal commitment to serve in underserved Thuringian healthcare districts.
