Master in Management (MiM) European Multi-Campus Degree

Two-year European master's program combining studies across London, Paris, Berlin, Madrid, or Turin, featuring dual degrees, 40+ specialisations, and mandatory international internships.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 3.2/4.0 GPA or UK 2:1 equivalent.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 per band).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT or GRE score required as part of the holistic admissions dossier.
Thesis / Research statement
Essays addressing global business ethics, digital disruption, and multi-campus mobility preferences.
Recommendation letters
Two reference letters (at least one academic reference).
