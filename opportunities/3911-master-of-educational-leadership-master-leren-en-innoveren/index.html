Master of Educational Leadership (Master Leren en Innoveren)

Two-year part-time master's program empowering experienced teachers to lead school-wide innovation, research school climate, and coach pedagogical teams.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Education (PABO) or equivalent teaching qualification and minimum 2 years of teaching experience
English language proficiency
Fluent Dutch (C1 level)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Proof of employment at a primary school and school board endorsement letter
Recommendation letters
1 school director recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Eligible for Dutch Lerarenbeurs scholarship providing up to €7,000 for study costs and substitute teacher compensation
