Aichi Regional Elderly Social Care and Community Network Field Research

Community-embedded research initiative analyzing informal elderly support networks, dementia-friendly neighborhood initiatives, and integrated community care models in central Japan.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in Doho University graduate program with grade average of B or above.
English language proficiency
Japanese language capability sufficient to conduct clinical interviews with elderly residents.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for domestic research projects.
Thesis / Research statement
Research prospectus investigating local mutual assistance networks (Kyosei) in depopulating Aichi suburbs.
Recommendation letters
Approval from a supervising faculty member at Doho University.
