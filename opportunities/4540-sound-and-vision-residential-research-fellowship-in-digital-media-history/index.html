Sound and Vision Residential Research Fellowship in Digital Media History

Six-month funded fellowship at the Hilversum Media Park analyzing longitudinal political debates, public broadcasting evolution, or digital culture.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Ph.D. or advanced doctoral candidate in media studies, digital humanities, history, or computer science
English language proficiency
English fluency (Dutch reading ability advantageous)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Four-page research proposal detailing computational dataset requirements and analytical methodology
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from professors of media studies or history
