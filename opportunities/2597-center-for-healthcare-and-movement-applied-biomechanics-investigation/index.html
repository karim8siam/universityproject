Center for Healthcare and Movement Applied Biomechanics Investigation

Applied biomechanics research project utilizing 3D motion analysis, surface electromyography, and wearable inertial sensors to assess knee ligament reconstruction rehabilitation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in ESP Year 2 or 3 with satisfactory completion of all clinical examinations.
English language proficiency
Fluent academic English capability (IELTS 6.5 minimum).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for applied Dutch healthcare research.
Thesis / Research statement
Research brief detailing methodologies for 3D motion capture and force plate analysis in ACL rehabilitation.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement from an ESP Biomechanics research supervisor.
