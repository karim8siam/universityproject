Governor Guinn Millennium Scholarship (Statewide Program)

Nevada System of Higher Education · Reno, Nevada, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: June 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Flagship Nevada merit scholarship funding up to $10,000 toward tuition and fees for qualifying high school graduates attending any NSHE collegiate institution.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
$80 per university credit / $60 per state college credit up to $10,000 total lifetime award
Tuition Rate
Covers tuition directly across NSHE institutions
Application Fee
$0 (No separate scholarship application fee)

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
3.25+ weighted cumulative high school GPA or SAT 1070+ / ACT 21+
English Language
Satisfied via Nevada high school graduation
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Nevada high school transcripts showing 4 years of English and 4 years of math
Letters of Recommendation
Automatic certification via Nevada Department of Education

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult Nevada System of Higher Education's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
