UCL Engineering Summer Research Internships

Competitive summer research placement program, enabling undergraduate students to work directly alongside leading researchers on live scientific investigations in photonics, biomedical implants, and nanotech.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate student with minimum 2:1 / First Class predicted grade.
English language proficiency
Fluent working English communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Agreed project description co-authored with a UCL faculty host supervisor.
Recommendation letters
Faculty host endorsement and university academic transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Bursary of £420 to £450 per week for 8 to 10 weeks of summer research work inside UCL laboratories
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid student research bursary.
