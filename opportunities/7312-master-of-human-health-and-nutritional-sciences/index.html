Master of Human Health and Nutritional Sciences

Two-year graduate research program investigating functional nutrient metabolism, sports conditioning nutrition, dietary therapy for lifestyle diseases, and food safety biochemistry.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Nutritional Science, Biology, Food Science, or Sports Health (min GPA 2.8/4.0).
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or documented English research capability.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcript, graduation certificate, 2-page research proposal, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.
