TUM Master of Science in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence

Interdisciplinary master's degree (120 ECTS) uniting mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and computer science to develop intelligent cognitive robots, autonomous navigation, and machine perception.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or Informatics with strong mathematics foundations.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88; German language certificate (A1 level) within one year.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Subject tests or GRE General required for specific international regions.
Thesis / Research statement
Rigorous coursework background in linear algebra, multivariable calculus, control engineering, and programming.
Recommendation letters
Complete academic transcripts, module catalog descriptions, and statement of purpose.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Bavarian research assistantships and industrial master thesis contracts
Application fee
€75 uni-assist VPD processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
€6,000 per semester for non-EU international students; €85 semester fee for EU students.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 31, 2027 for Winter intake
