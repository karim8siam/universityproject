Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) Clinical Fellowship

Three-year CAPTE-accredited doctorate integrating high-volume clinical rotations across major regional trauma centers and outpatient sports clinics.

English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 100
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE required (recommended 150 Verbal, 150 Quantitative, 4.0 Analytical Writing)
Thesis / Research statement
50 hours of verified physical therapy clinical observation
Recommendation letters
Three letters including one from a licensed physical therapist
