UNSW Master of Engineering in Photovoltaics and Solar Energy

World-leading solar energy master's program from the institution that pioneered modern silicon solar cells, focusing on photovoltaic manufacturing, grid integration, and clean energy storage.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor of Engineering in relevant discipline with 65% average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (6.0 in all subscores).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in electrical engineering, semiconductor physics, or materials.
Recommendation letters
Academic reference letters and transcripts.
