UNBC Northern Health Leaders Graduate Fellowship

Doctoral and master's fellowship supporting innovative community-based epidemiology and rural health delivery systems across northern British Columbia.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.5/4.33 undergraduate or master's GPA in health sciences or related disciplines.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 (no band under 6.5) or TOEFL iBT 94.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
1,500-word proposed thesis outline on northern rural health equity.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $18,500 annual research stipend plus full northern travel allowance
Application fee
CAD $125
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $5,850 per academic year (subsidized domestic/international research rate)
