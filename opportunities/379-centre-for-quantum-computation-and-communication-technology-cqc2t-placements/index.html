Centre for Quantum Computation and Communication Technology (CQC2T) Placements

World-renowned research placements at the forefront of atomic-scale silicon quantum computing, conducting single-atom spin manipulation and quantum architecture development.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours or equivalent Master's with high marks in physics, electrical engineering, or quantum information.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated experimental cleanroom skills or theoretical quantum physics papers.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee endorsements.
