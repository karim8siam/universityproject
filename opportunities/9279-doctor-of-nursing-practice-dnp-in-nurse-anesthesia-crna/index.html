Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) in Nurse Anesthesia (CRNA)

COA-accredited 36-month doctoral curriculum preparing Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetists (CRNAs) with clinical rotations across Mayo Clinic Health System, Abbott Northwestern, and Hennepin Healthcare.
