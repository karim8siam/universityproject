TUM Salaried Doctoral Positions (Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in - TV-L E13)

Three to four-year salaried university employment contracts at TUM chairs, combining cutting-edge scientific research with teaching assistance and mandatory membership in the structured TUM Graduate School.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) with high marks in Computer Science, Engineering, or Mathematics.
English language proficiency
Fluent technical English proficiency for scientific publishing and laboratory collaboration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis or peer-reviewed conference publications in IEEE, ACM, or Springer venues.
Recommendation letters
Written supervision confirmation and employment endorsement from a TUM chair professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried employment contract under TV-L E13 tariff (100% full-time €54,000 to €62,000 gross per year) with comprehensive German social insurance, health coverage, and state pension
Application fee
Free application directly to open chair vacancies
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Doctoral candidates are paid research staff members; mandatory semester administrative fee of approximately €85.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 31, 2027 (Continuous rolling recruitment across TUM research chairs)
