Adelaide University Global Citizens International Scholarship

Major tuition fee reduction scholarship for commencing international undergraduate and postgraduate coursework students at Adelaide University, providing an ongoing 15% or 30% reduction on annual tuition fees based on prior academic merit.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / CGPA
15% tier: Minimum university GPA 5.5/7.0 (or ATAR 85). 30% tier: Minimum university GPA 6.5/7.0 (or ATAR 95).
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or equivalent program-specific cutoff.
GRE
Not required for Australian coursework admissions.
Published research paper
Not required for undergraduate or postgraduate coursework scholarships.
Recommendation letters
Official university academic transcripts and award certificates evaluated automatically during admission.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Partial funding: 15% to 30% reduction on annual international tuition fees
Application fee
No separate scholarship fee; AUD $150 international degree admission application fee applies
Tuition / joining costs
Annual tuition varies by program (approx. AUD $44,000–$57,000/year); scholarship deducts 15% or 30% per year

Dates and verification

Application deadline
December 1, 2026 for Semester 1 (February intake); May 1, 2027 for Semester 2 (July intake)
