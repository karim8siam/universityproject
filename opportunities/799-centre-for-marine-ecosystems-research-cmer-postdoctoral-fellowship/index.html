Centre for Marine Ecosystems Research (CMER) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year research appointment at the Centre for Marine Ecosystems Research, examining blue carbon sequestration in Australian seagrass meadows, coastal mangrove restoration, and marine heatwave recovery.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
PhD in Marine Ecology, Biological Oceanography, or Coastal Environmental Science.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Published works in blue carbon ecosystems, seagrass dynamics, or coastal biogeochemical cycling.
Recommendation letters
Two research references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual research fellowship of AUD $85,000 to $98,000 plus 17% superannuation and boat research time
Application fee
AUD $0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Not applicable; salaried postdoctoral appointment.
