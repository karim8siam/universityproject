Kyushu University Graduate School of Information Science and Electrical Engineering (ISEE) English PhD

Three-year research doctoral program taught entirely in English covering cybersecurity, computer vision, natural language processing, semiconductor devices, and high-performance computing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Applied Mathematics with solid academic results.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis summary and prospective research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
JSPS DC Doctoral Research Fellowships (¥200,000/month) and Kyudai Research Assistantships
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800/year (tuition exemption available for up to 100% upon application).
