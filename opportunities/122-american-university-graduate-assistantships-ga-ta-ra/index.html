American University Graduate Assistantships (GA / TA / RA)

Departmental teaching and research assistantships providing tuition credits and competitive hourly stipends for conducting recitation sessions, grading problem sets, and managing scientific lab instruments.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Full-time matriculated AU graduate student with minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA.
English language proficiency
High oral English proficiency for tutorial instruction.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Outstanding grades in foundational quantitative or policy courses.
Recommendation letters
Departmental recommendation and faculty supervisor approval.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried campus academic employment: USD $18.00 to $24.00 per hour or fixed semester stipend ($10,000/term) plus tuition credit remissions
Application fee
Free application through departmental graduate directors
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition remission credits provided alongside hourly wages.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 for Fall departmental assistantship appointments
