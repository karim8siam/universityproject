TU Delft Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science (EEMCS), offering tracks in Data Science & Technology, Software Technology, and Artificial Intelligence.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or closely related field with high grades in discrete mathematics and algorithms.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General test required for non-Dutch undergraduate degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework background in data structures, algorithms, logic, and computer systems.
Recommendation letters
Complete university academic transcripts and syllabus descriptions.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for TU Delft Excellence scholarships and student assistantships

€100 application fee
