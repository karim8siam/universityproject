Umeå University Salaried Doctoral Positions (Doktorandanställning) in Molecular Biology

Four-year fully salaried doctoral research employment contracts inside the Umeå Centre for Microbial Research (UCMR) and Department of Molecular Biology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS) in molecular biology, biochemistry, or genetics with high academic standing.
English language proficiency
Fluency in English (IELTS 6.5+ or equivalent).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis demonstrating capacity for independent laboratory research.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation and personal statement.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried employment: SEK 31,500 to SEK 35,000/month gross salary with Swedish social security, pension, and paid annual leave
Application fee
Free application via Umeå recruitment portal
Tuition / joining costs
100% tuition-free for all admitted doctoral researchers.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 15, 2027 (Spring doctoral recruitment round across science and medicine faculties)
