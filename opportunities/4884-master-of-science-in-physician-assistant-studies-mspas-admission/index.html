Master of Science in Physician Assistant Studies (MSPAS) Admission

Highly competitive 27-month clinical program preparing medical providers for national certification (PANCE) and clinical practice.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.25 overall GPA and 3.25 science prerequisite GPA; minimum 1,000 hours of direct patient healthcare experience
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 7.0
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional
Thesis / Research statement
CASPA personal narrative, verified clinical hours log, and situational judgment test
Recommendation letters
Three references (including at least one practicing PA or physician)

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for federal HRSA rural healthcare loan repayment grants ($50,000)
Application fee
$60 supplemental fee (plus CASPA application fee)
Tuition / joining costs
$88,500 total program tuition across seven trimesters

Application deadline
