Acknowledge Education Community Leadership and Social Justice Fellowship

Prestigious fellowship supporting future social work leaders dedicated to driving evidence-based reforms in disability support, child welfare, and housing security.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Distinction average (75%+) across all completed higher education coursework.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or equivalent native English education.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for community leadership appointments.
Thesis / Research statement
1,000-word proposal outline for a community empowerment initiative benefiting disadvantaged urban populations.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of endorsement from recognized community service executives or department heads.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fellowship grant of AUD $5,000 and executive executive mentorship with non-profit directors
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Direct fee remission applied against final year undergraduate tuition
