GSMS Fully Funded Doctoral Research Fellowship in Healthy Ageing

Four-year fully salaried doctoral research appointment (Promovendus) at the Graduate School of Medical Sciences investigating cellular senescence and immune longevity.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in biomedical sciences, medicine, molecular biology, or biochemistry with grade 8.0/10 or higher
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall with no band score below 6.5, or TOEFL iBT 100
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis manuscript and 2,000-word doctoral research proposal
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation from academic laboratory supervisors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAO Universitair Medische Centra (UMC) salary: €2,901 (year 1) to €3,677 (year 4) gross monthly plus 8.3% end-of-year allowance
