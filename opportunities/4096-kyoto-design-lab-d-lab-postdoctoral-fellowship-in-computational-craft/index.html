KYOTO Design Lab (D-Lab) Postdoctoral Fellowship in Computational Craft

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the KYOTO Design Lab exploring the fusion of robotic clay 3D printing, CNC urushi lacquerware finishing, and generative algorithmic design.
