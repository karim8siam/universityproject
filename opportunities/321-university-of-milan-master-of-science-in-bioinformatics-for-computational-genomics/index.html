University of Milan Master of Science in Bioinformatics for Computational Genomics

Cutting-edge joint master's program taught in English with Politecnico di Milano, combining machine learning, next-generation sequencing analytics, and computational genomics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in computer science, mathematics, biotechnology, or biology with solid quantitative marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or CEFR B2 certificate.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated interest in computational biology and programming.
Recommendation letters
Transcripts, CV, and motivation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for Excellence Scholarships and DSU regional grants (€7,000/yr + tuition waiver)
Application fee
€30 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Base tuition €156 to €2,800/year depending on family ISEE bracket.
