UC Berkeley Master of Science in Computer Science (Plan I Thesis)

Two-year research-focused Master of Science program culminating in an original scientific thesis supervised by world-renowned EECS faculty.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in computer science or quantitative sciences with strong academic marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework excellence in algorithms, operating systems, and theory.
Recommendation letters
Three academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Departmental research assistantships providing tuition coverage and monthly stipends available for admitted students
Application fee
USD $140 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Fully subsidized for students holding research assistant appointments.
