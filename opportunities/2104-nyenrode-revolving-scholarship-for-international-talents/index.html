Nyenrode Revolving Scholarship for International Talents

Prestigious alumni-funded revolving scholarship enabling ambitious international students to attend Nyenrode, with recipients committing to contribute back to the fund upon career establishment.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree with minimum 3.3 GPA or top 15% class rank
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL 92
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Nyenrode Admissions Test (LTP) or GMAT 620+ / GRE equivalent
Thesis / Research statement
Essay on personal commitment to 'pay it forward' by supporting future students
Recommendation letters
Two academic or executive referee letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to 50% tuition reduction (€12,000 to €22,000) funded by university alumni foundation
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
€28,000 total MSc tuition (reduced to €14,000 with scholarship)
