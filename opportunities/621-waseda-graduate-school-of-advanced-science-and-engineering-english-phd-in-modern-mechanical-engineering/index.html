Waseda Graduate School of Advanced Science and Engineering English PhD in Modern Mechanical Engineering

Three-year research doctoral program taught entirely in English exploring humanoid robotics (Humanoid Robotics Institute), surgical robotics, smart actuators, and bio-mechatronics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Robotics, Mechanical Engineering, or Mechatronics with solid academic results.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis summary and prospective research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
JSPS DC Doctoral Research Fellowships (¥200,000/month) and Waseda Teaching/Research Assistantships
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥1,350,000/year (50% merit tuition waiver available upon application).
