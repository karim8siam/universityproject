Kyoto University Amgen Scholars Summer Research Program

Prestigious eight-week summer research program (June–August) offering undergraduate scholars from around the world direct laboratory research apprenticeships under Kyoto faculty in molecular biology, chemistry, and bioengineering.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate student at an accredited university worldwide who has completed at least one year of study with 3.2+ GPA.
English language proficiency
Fluent working English communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose detailing biotechnology/chemical research interests and target laboratory preferences.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation and official transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Living stipend of ¥250,000, free university accommodation, round-trip economy airfare to Kyoto, and scientific symposium travel
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; fully sponsored 8-week summer internship.
