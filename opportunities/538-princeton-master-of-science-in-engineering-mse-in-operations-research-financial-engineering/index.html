Princeton Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) in Operations Research & Financial Engineering

Two-year research-based Master of Science in Engineering program combining stochastic modeling, convex optimization, machine learning, and quantitative finance.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in engineering, mathematics, physics, or quantitative economics with minimum 3.6/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Mandatory GRE General test score.
Thesis / Research statement
Rigorous preparation in multivariable calculus, linear algebra, probability, and computer programming.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters.
