DAAD EPOS Energy & Environmental Management (EEM) Full Scholarship

Full international DAAD scholarship covering all living expenses, monthly allowance (€934), airfare, and German language training for energy professionals.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in engineering or economics with above-average grades
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
At least two years of professional work experience in energy or environment; motivation essay
Recommendation letters
One academic and one current employer recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€934/month living stipend, travel allowance, health insurance, and study support
Application fee
None
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition-free program (only standard semester contribution €290 applies)
