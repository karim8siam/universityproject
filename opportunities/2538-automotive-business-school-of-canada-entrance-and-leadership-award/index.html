Automotive Business School of Canada Entrance and Leadership Award

Merit scholarship funded by Canadian automotive industry partners supporting incoming students enrolled in the Bachelor of Business (Automotive Management) program.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 80% cumulative average in final year of secondary or prior post-secondary studies.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no individual score below 6.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Canadian applied college programs.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word essay explaining passion for automotive innovation, electric mobility, and retail leadership.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic or professional recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: CAD $3,000 entrance scholarship applied directly to semester tuition fees
Application fee
CAD $125 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $8,800 per semester international tuition; reduced by scholarship award
