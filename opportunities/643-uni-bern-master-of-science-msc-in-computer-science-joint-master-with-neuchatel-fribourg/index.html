Uni Bern Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (Joint Master with Neuchâtel & Fribourg)

Two-year English-taught Master of Science jointly offered by the Universities of Bern, Neuchâtel, and Fribourg, specializing in Machine Learning, Distributed Systems, and Information Security.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or closely related field with minimum 180 ECTS.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in software engineering, distributed systems, and computer vision.
Recommendation letters
Academic transcript and curriculum vitae.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships and teaching assistantship positions (CHF 32/hour)
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 750/semester.
