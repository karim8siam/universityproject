MAST Bari Creative Sound & Music Production Scholarship

Merit-based music scholarship for exceptional instrumentalists, electronic producers, and sound engineers enrolling in full-time academic diplomas.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation certificate with strong musical or artistic background.
English language proficiency
Italian B2 or English B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; live performance audition or 3-track portfolio review.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, artistic portfolio (audio/video links), and personal statement.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from music teachers or producers.
