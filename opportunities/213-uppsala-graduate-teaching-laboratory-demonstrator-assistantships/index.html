Uppsala Graduate Teaching & Laboratory Demonstrator Assistantships

Campus employment positions (övningsassistenter / labbassistenter) enabling graduate students to tutor weekly recitation sessions, mentor programming workshops, and assist in grading laboratory exams.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled Master's or doctoral student at Uppsala in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Proficiency in English or Swedish depending on the target course instruction language.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High academic grade in the assigned course module.
Recommendation letters
Approval from course examiner and director of studies.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried hourly university employment: SEK 165 to SEK 205 per hour for supervising exercise classes, coding labs, and grading assignments
Application fee
Free application via Uppsala course examiners
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid student employment.
