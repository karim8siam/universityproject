Consortium Inter-University Credit Transfer Program in Disaster Reduction and Human Renovation

Interdisciplinary collaborative curriculum co-taught by Kobe University and Hyogo prefectural institutes, analyzing seismic structural engineering, community disaster risk reduction (DRR), and disaster psychology.
