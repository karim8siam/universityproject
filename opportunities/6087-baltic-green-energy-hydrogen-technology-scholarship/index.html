Baltic Green Energy & Hydrogen Technology Scholarship

Merit scholarship supporting exceptional international students pursuing renewable energy engineering degrees at the Baltic campus.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in engineering, physics, or energy systems with minimum 2.3 German GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or German B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation letter on offshore wind hydrogen production.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendations.
