Leiden University Excellence Scholarships (LExS)

Prestigious university scholarship awarded to outstanding non-EU/EEA international students admitted to Master's degree programs at Leiden University, providing full tuition coverage and living allowance grants.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation with cumulative grade point average (CGPA) in top 10% of class or equivalent to first class honors.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all sections) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence, curriculum vitae, and statement of intent.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters submitted directly through the online admission portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full tuition fee waiver (€19,600 to €21,500 per year) plus €10,000 per year contribution to living expenses for outstanding non-EEA Master's applicants
Application fee
€100 application fee (refunded upon matriculation)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived for the entire two-year 120 ECTS duration.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Strict annual deadline for international LExS scholarship consideration)
