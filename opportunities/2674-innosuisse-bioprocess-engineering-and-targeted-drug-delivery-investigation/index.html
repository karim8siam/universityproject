Innosuisse Bioprocess Engineering and Targeted Drug Delivery Investigation

Applied pharmaceutical engineering research project developing continuous biomanufacturing processes and targeted lipid nanoparticle formulations for therapeutic mRNA delivery.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in Master of Science in Life Sciences or Biomedical Engineering with grade average of 5.0/6.0.
English language proficiency
English B2/C1 fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Swiss institutional laboratory projects.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of research intent detailing experience in bioreactor operation, cell culture, or lipid nanoparticles.
Recommendation letters
Nomination from an FHNW School of Life Sciences research group leader in Muttenz.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Research stipend of CHF 4,500 supported by the FHNW School of Life Sciences and Swiss Innosuisse grants
Application fee

Tuition / joining costs
Fully sponsored cantonal research appointment
