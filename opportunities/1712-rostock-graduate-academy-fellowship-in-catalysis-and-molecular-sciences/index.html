Rostock Graduate Academy Fellowship in Catalysis and Molecular Sciences

Doctoral scholarship sponsored by the University of Rostock Graduate Academy in conjunction with the Leibniz Institute for Catalysis (LIKAT) supporting sustainable chemical synthesis.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in chemistry or materials science with grade 1.5 or better
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis abstract and research project outline in organometallic catalysis
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters from university professors
