Stanford Master of Science in Computer Science

Globally celebrated terminal Master of Science degree (45 units) offering specialized tracks in Artificial Intelligence, Information Management & Analytics, Systems, Human-Computer Interaction, and Theory.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or closely related quantitative field with minimum 3.6 GPA.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE optional.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, 2-page statement of purpose, and technical programming portfolio.
Recommendation letters
Three academic or professional recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Course Assistantships (CAs) covering 10-unit tuition plus USD $12,500/quarter stipend
Application fee
USD $125 application processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
USD $62,500 per academic year (45 total units over five academic quarters).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
December 5, 2026 for Fall 2027 cohort intake
