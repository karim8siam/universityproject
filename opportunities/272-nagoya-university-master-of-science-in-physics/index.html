Nagoya University Master of Science in Physics

Two-year English-medium research Master's degree inside the Department of Physics (home to Sakata model and Nobel-winning CP violation discoveries), investigating particle physics, cosmology, and condensed matter.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor of Science in Physics or Mathematics with solid quantum mechanics and thermodynamics background.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Physics subject test recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework background in theoretical and experimental particle physics.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic recommendation and official transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for MEXT University Recommendation scholarships and Nagoya tuition exemptions
Application fee
¥30,000 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800 per year plus one-time admission fee of ¥282,000.
