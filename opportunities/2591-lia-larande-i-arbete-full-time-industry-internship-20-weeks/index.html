LIA (Lärande i Arbete) Full-Time Industry Internship (20 Weeks)

Five-month full-time workplace immersion (Lärande i Arbete - LIA) embedding students in commercial agile sprint teams at tech firms in Malmö, Lund, and Copenhagen.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Successful completion of all Year 1 mobile programming, database, and UI/UX design units.
English language proficiency
Professional English or Swedish communication skills for team collaboration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swedish workplace learning.
Thesis / Research statement
CV, GitHub profile link, and signed LIA agreement with approved host technology company.
Recommendation letters
Approval from the Malmö Yrkeshögskola LIA Coordinator.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Mandatory 20-week industry immersion with Nordic software development agencies and product firms
Application fee
Zero internship fee
Tuition / joining costs
Included within tuition-free state program
