SUPSI Building-Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer research traineeship at the ISAAC solar testing center in Mendrisio, performing outdoor solar module characterization, electrical degradation analysis, and architectural facade integration testing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate or early Master's student in Architecture, Civil Engineering, or Energy Systems with strong grades.
English language proficiency
Good English or Italian working proficiencies.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed coursework in building physics, solar technology, or architectural design.
Recommendation letters
One referee letter from a faculty professor.
