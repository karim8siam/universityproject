McGill Master of Science in Neuroscience (IPN)

Premier interdisciplinary graduate degree program inside the Integrated Program in Neuroscience (IPN) based at The Neuro (Montreal Neurological Institute-Hospital), investigating neurodevelopment, cellular neurobiology, and neural computing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Neuroscience, Biology, Physiology, or Computer Science with minimum 3.3 CGPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework background in molecular biology, neuroanatomy, or neuro-imaging.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters and commitment from a confirmed IPN thesis supervisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Guaranteed minimum stipend of CAD $24,000 per year plus tuition support provided by primary supervisor
Application fee
CAD $132.90 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $10,500/year (subsidized by IPN international fee waiver awards).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 for Fall 2027 intake
