Jönköping Master of Science (MSc) in Materials and Manufacturing (English-Taught)

Comprehensive two-year Master's degree focusing on casting technologies, component design, failure analysis, and circular metallic materials in partnership with Scandinavia's foundry industry.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science, or Chemical Engineering with at least 90 ECTS in engineering.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (minimum 5.5 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in metallurgy, solid mechanics, and manufacturing technologies.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for Swedish Institute (SI) Global Professionals Scholarships (full tuition + SEK 12,000/month living stipend)
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 150,000/year for non-EU/EEA students (EU/EEA citizens pay SEK 0).
