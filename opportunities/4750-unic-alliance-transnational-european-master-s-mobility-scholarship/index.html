UNIC Alliance Transnational European Master's Mobility Scholarship

Erasmus+ funded mobility scholarship supporting students completing joint master's coursework across at least two UNIC alliance member universities.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in urban studies, sociology, economics, geography, or architecture with 3.3 GPA or higher
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all components) or TOEFL iBT 95
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
1,200-word statement on post-industrial urban challenges and CV
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university faculty

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€1,400 per month Erasmus+ living grant plus €1,500 travel and installation subsidy
Application fee
None
Tuition / joining costs
€2,530/year statutory EU tuition or €15,600/year non-EU tuition (reduced by alliance grants)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 15, 2027
