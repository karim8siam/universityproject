Internship

NHS Scotland Adult Nursing Clinical Practicum

50% clinical practice rotation in NHS Greater Glasgow & Clyde or NHS Lanarkshire hospitals covering acute medicine and surgery.

University
University of the West of Scotland
Location
Paisley & Lanarkshire, Scotland, UK
Funding
Scottish Paramedic and Nursing Bursary (PNB) of £10,000 per year tax-free
Tuition
Full tuition paid by Scottish Government for eligible residents
Application Fee
None
Deadline
January 27, 2027
Requirements
Enrolment in UWS BSc Adult Nursing and criminal record PVG check clearance.
