King's Master of Arts (MA) in War Studies

World-leading one-year postgraduate program within King's renowned Department of War Studies exploring international security, diplomacy, strategic doctrine, and global conflict resolution.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or international GPA 3.3/4.0) in history, international relations, politics, or economics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all skills) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong analytical writing sample (approx. 2,000 words).
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Departmental bursaries and Sir Michael Howard Scholarships (£10,000 fee contribution)
Application fee
£75 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£29,472/year for overseas students.
