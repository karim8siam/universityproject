Cardiff School of Journalism Master of Arts (MA) in International Journalism

World-renowned international journalism program located in Cardiff's flagship Central Square building adjacent to BBC Cymru Wales, training correspondents in investigative reporting, digital multimedia, and broadcast documentary.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree in any subject with demonstrable interest in global media.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with at least 6.5 in all sub-scores.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Portfolio of published journalistic writing or analytical broadcast reporting.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional journalistic references.
