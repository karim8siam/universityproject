Canada-ASEAN Scholarships and Educational Exchanges for Development (SEED)

Federal scholarship program for Southeast Asian scholars to undertake short-term study or collaborative research in Canada aligned with the UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in an eligible Southeast Asian higher education institution with minimum 80% equivalent grade average.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Passport, transcript, letter of intent, and institutional exchange agreement.
Recommendation letters
Home university dean nomination.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $10,200 for undergraduate exchange or CAD $15,900 for graduate research terms
Application fee
CAD $0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Covered under bilateral inter-university agreements
