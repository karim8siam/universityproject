POLiS Post-Lithium Energy Storage International Master's Scholarship

Prestige scholarship awarded by the German Research Foundation (DFG) Cluster of Excellence POLiS to top international students admitted to the Master in Energy Science and Technology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Chemistry, Physics, Materials Science, or Chemical Engineering with excellent marks
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed CV, two-page statement of motivation regarding battery research, and transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from former academic professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full scholarship: €934 per month (€11,208/year) living allowance plus lab consumables stipend
Application fee
€75
Tuition / joining costs
Non-EU tuition €1,500/semester waived or reimbursed through POLiS funding
