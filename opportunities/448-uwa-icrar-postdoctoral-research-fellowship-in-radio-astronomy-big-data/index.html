UWA ICRAR Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Radio Astronomy & Big Data

Three-year postdoctoral research appointment at the International Centre for Radio Astronomy Research (ICRAR) working directly with Square Kilometre Array (SKA) pre-cursor data.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
PhD in Astrophysics, Astronomy, Physics, or High Performance Computing.
English language proficiency
Fluent professional English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Extensive publications in radio interferometry, galaxy evolution, or Square Kilometre Array (SKA) data processing pipelines.
Recommendation letters
Three professional reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried academic Level A/B appointment: AUD $92,000 to $108,000/year plus 17% superannuation employer contribution
Application fee
Free application via UWA job portal
Tuition / joining costs
Salaried position; no tuition fees.
