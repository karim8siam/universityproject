Immunology Frontier Research Center (IFReC) World Premier Placements

World Premier International (WPI) research placements at one of the world's most cited immunology research centres, investigating innate immunity, immune regulation, and cancer immunotherapy.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding academic record in biology, immunology, or medicine.
English language proficiency
High level of English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior experience in flow cytometry, single-cell RNA-sequencing, or cellular imaging.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from international researchers.
