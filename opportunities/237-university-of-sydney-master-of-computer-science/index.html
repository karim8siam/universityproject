University of Sydney Master of Computer Science

Two-year research-oriented Master's degree comprising advanced coursework and a major year-long research thesis project under individual faculty mentorship, serving as a direct pathway to doctoral research.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with major in Computer Science or Software Engineering with minimum 65%+ credit average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each sub-band or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Rigorous foundation in computer systems, algorithms, data structures, and mathematics.
Recommendation letters
Official university academic transcripts, syllabus outlines, and statement of intent.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Faculty of Engineering International Merit Scholarships (up to 20% tuition fee reduction)
Application fee
AUD $150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $53,000 per year (total 96 credit points over two full academic years).
