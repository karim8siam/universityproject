Federico II Doctoral Fellowships (Borse di Dottorato) in Aerospace & Mechanical Engineering

Premier 3-year doctoral research program in aerospace engineering, aerodynamics, propulsion systems, and advanced composite materials.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Second-cycle Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with high marks in aerospace, mechanical, or electrical engineering.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency (CEFR B2 or higher).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis summary and scientific research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters submitted directly via the application system.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded 3-year fellowship: €16,243 net/year (tax-free) plus 50% mobility supplement for international research stays and €1,624 annual research budget
Application fee
€50 competitive examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% tuition-free for all doctoral fellowship recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
June 15, 2027 (Annual doctoral admission call deadline for 40th cycle)
