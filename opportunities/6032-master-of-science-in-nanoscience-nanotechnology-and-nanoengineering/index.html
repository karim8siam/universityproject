Two-year European Master of Science providing comprehensive cleanroom training, quantum electron transport modeling, and nanodevice fabrication.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Physics, Chemistry, Electrical Engineering, or Materials Science (high distinction).
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 94.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE recommended for international quantitative applicants.

Academic transcripts, motivation statement, and resume.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.
