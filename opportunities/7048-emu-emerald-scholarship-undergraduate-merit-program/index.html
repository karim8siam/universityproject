EMU Emerald Scholarship (Undergraduate Merit Program)

Flagship university scholarship automatically awarded to qualified domestic and international freshmen based on high school academic excellence.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school cumulative GPA 3.25 to 4.0 scale.
English language proficiency
TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Admissions application and official secondary school transcripts.
Recommendation letters
One counselor recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
$3,000 to $8,000 per year ($12,000 to $32,000 over four years) renewable based on academic standing
Application fee
No extra fee
Tuition / joining costs
Directly reduces EMU undergraduate tuition
