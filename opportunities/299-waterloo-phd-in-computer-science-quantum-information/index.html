Waterloo PhD in Computer Science & Quantum Information

One of the world's leading computer science doctoral programs, offering cutting-edge research in quantum computing, artificial intelligence, systems, algorithms, and cryptography.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or closely related discipline with at least an A- (80%) average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall (7.0 in writing and speaking) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE required for international applicants who completed degrees outside Canada.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior peer-reviewed research papers or Master's thesis in computer science.
Recommendation letters
Three confidential academic references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed minimum funding of CAD $28,500/year for 4 years through research assistantships, teaching assistantships, and scholarships
Application fee
CAD $125 graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition offset provided through IDSA and departmental base funding.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Fall admission cycle deadline; earlier application advised)
