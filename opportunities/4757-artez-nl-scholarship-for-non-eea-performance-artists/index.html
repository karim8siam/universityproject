ArtEZ NL Scholarship for Non-EEA Performance Artists

Prestigious Dutch Ministry of Education scholarship awarded to exceptionally talented international master's students from outside the EEA.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized Bachelor of Arts in dance, theatre, visual arts, or performance
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in all bands) or equivalent
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Artistic portfolio (video links to original choreographic or performance works) and 1,000-word artistic research proposal
Recommendation letters
Two artistic or academic referee recommendations

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€5,000 one-time financial contribution towards living expenses in the first study year
Application fee
€50 audition assessment fee
Tuition / joining costs
€2,530/year (EU/EEA) or €11,500/year (Non-EEA)
