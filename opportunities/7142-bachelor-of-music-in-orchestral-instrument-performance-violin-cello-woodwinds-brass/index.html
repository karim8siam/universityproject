Bachelor of Music in Orchestral Instrument Performance (Violin, Cello, Woodwinds, Brass)

Four-year intensive conservatory degree offering solo masterclasses, orchestral excerpt training with the North German Philharmonic Rostock, chamber music, and score analysis.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
General university entrance qualification (Abitur or recognized international school-leaving certificate).
English language proficiency
German language certificate at level B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; in-person instrumental audition (three pieces of contrasting epochs) and music theory exam mandatory.
Thesis / Research statement
Audition repertoire list, CV, secondary certificates, and certified translation.
Recommendation letters
One letter of recommendation from an instrumental instructor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free public conservatory; Deutschlandstipendium €300/month available for top auditionees
Application fee
€50 entrance audition fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €185 semester administrative fee including local transit
