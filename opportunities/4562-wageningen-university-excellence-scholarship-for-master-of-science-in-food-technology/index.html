Wageningen University Excellence Scholarship for Master of Science in Food Technology

Full-tuition scholarship for outstanding non-EEA students admitted to the Master of Science in Food Technology or Molecular Life Sciences.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class bachelor's degree with GPA of at least 80% or equivalent to Dutch grade 8.0+
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Official university transcripts, curriculum vitae, and two-page letter of motivation
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters on official university letterhead

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition fee waiver (approx. €20,000/year) plus €11,000 annual living expenses contribution
Application fee
Free application for scholarship upon university program admission
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition covered
