UWA PhD in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence

Three to four-year research doctorate exploring deep learning, computer vision, autonomous robotics, privacy-preserving machine learning, and natural language processing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours or Master's by Research in Computer Science/Software Engineering.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 82.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Peer-reviewed publications in machine learning, computer vision, or algorithms preferred.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
RTP-equivalent scholarship: AUD $35,000/year tax-free stipend for 3.5 years plus international tuition sponsorship
Application fee
AUD $100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Fully sponsored through RTP international fee offsetting.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 31, 2027 (Round 1 international postgraduate research scholarship deadline)
