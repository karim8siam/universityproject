VU Fellowship Programme (VUFP) & Holland Scholarship

Prestigious merit scholarship awarded to outstanding international students from outside the EU/EEA pursuing full-time master's degree programs at VU Amsterdam.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding academic record with GPA equivalent to at least 8.0 on Dutch 10-point scale (top 10% of cohort).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Convincing motivation letter highlighting academic excellence and future societal contribution.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded tuition waiver: 100% full international tuition fee coverage plus optional €5,000 one-time living allowance contribution
Application fee
€100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition covered for scholarship awardees.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Annual scholarship application deadline for non-EEA international master's applicants)
