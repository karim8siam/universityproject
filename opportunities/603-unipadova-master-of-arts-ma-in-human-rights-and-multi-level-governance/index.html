UniPadova Master of Arts (MA) in Human Rights and Multi-Level Governance

Renowned two-year English-taught Master's program at the 'Antonio Papisca' Human Rights Centre preparing experts for the United Nations, European Union institutions, and international human rights NGOs.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in political science, law, international relations, or sociology with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose addressing international humanitarian law or refugee protection.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
