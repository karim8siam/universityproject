Umeå Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence

Rigorous master's degree focusing on automated reasoning, machine learning foundations, intelligent agents, and responsible ethical AI architectures.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in computer science or mathematics with solid programming background.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (minimum 5.5 in each subscore).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated proficiency in algorithms, data structures, and mathematical statistics.
Recommendation letters
Degree certificates and certified transcripts.
