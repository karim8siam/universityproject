Tonsley Innovation District Robotics & Autonomous Systems Research Fellowship

Applied robotics research fellowship situated in South Australia's Tonsley high-tech precinct, developing robotic manipulation, marine inspection drones, and defense manufacturing automation.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's by Research or Honours Class 1 in Robotics, Mechatronics, or Autonomous Systems.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Published works in field robotics, SLAM algorithms, or underwater unmanned vehicles.
Recommendation letters
Two research references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
AUD $35,000/year tax-exempt research fellowship plus project funding through the Australian Research Council

Full tuition sponsorship provided.
