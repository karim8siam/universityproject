Heidelberg Academic Student Assistantships (HiWi)

Campus employment positions (up to 80 hours per month) assisting Heidelberg professors in running exercise groups, tutoring undergraduate students, managing library collections, and maintaining scientific computers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled student at Heidelberg University in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Proficiency in German or English depending on department requirements.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High academic marks in the subject area of the tutoring or lab assignment.
Recommendation letters
Approval from supervising chair professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried hourly campus employment: €13.98 to €17.50 per hour under Baden-Württemberg university student assistant regulations
Application fee
Free application through university student job portal
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid student employment.
