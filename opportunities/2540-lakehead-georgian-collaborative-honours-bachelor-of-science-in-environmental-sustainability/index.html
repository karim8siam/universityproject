Lakehead-Georgian Collaborative Honours Bachelor of Science in Environmental Sustainability

Collaborative program awarding both a Lakehead University Honours BSc degree and a Georgian College Environmental Technology diploma in four years.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school completion with Grade 12 English, Advanced Functions, and Biology or Chemistry with minimum 70% average.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 per skill).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Canadian collaborative undergraduate degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing interest in watershed management, conservation, and environmental policy.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.
