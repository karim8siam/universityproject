Tinbergen Institute Advanced Economic & Econometric Placements

Joint graduate institute of Erasmus University, University of Amsterdam, and VU Amsterdam, providing elite research training in econometrics, finance, and macroeconomics.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in economics or econometrics with top quantitative marks.
English language proficiency
Fluent English skills.

Theoretical or empirical working papers in economics or finance.

Two academic letters confirming technical rigor.
