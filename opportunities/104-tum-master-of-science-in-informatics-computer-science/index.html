TUM Master of Science in Informatics (Computer Science)

Flagship two-year English-taught Master's degree (120 ECTS) at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT) in Garching, offering specialized tracks in Algorithms, AI, Distributed Systems, and Security.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Informatics or closely related field with curriculum equivalence in theoretical computer science, algorithms, and systems.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GATE / GRE scores required for applicants holding undergraduate degrees from India, China, or Vietnam.
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed curriculum analysis mapping undergraduate credits to TUM bachelor modules, and motivation essay.
Recommendation letters
Official university academic transcripts and curriculum vitae.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for TUM scholarships, HiWi assistantships, and DAAD funding
Application fee
€75 uni-assist preliminary review documentation (VPD) fee for non-German credentials
Tuition / joining costs
€6,000 per semester for non-EU international students (EU/EEA students pay €85 semester fee).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 31, 2027 for Winter semester (October 2027) intake
