RWCMD International Postgraduate Excellence Scholarship

Major merit scholarship awarded based on exceptional performance audition or design portfolio presentation at UCAS Conservatoires entry.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in music, drama, or equivalent professional conservatory training
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each subskill)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Audition performance or scenographic portfolio submission via UCAS Conservatoires
Recommendation letters
Two artistic and academic reference letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
£5,000 to £10,000 tuition fee reduction awarded directly against first-year international fees
Application fee
£28.50 UCAS Conservatoires fee plus £65 audition assessment fee
Tuition / joining costs
£10,200/year (UK/Home) or £24,500/year (International)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027
