Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Clinical Psychology and Cognitive Behavioral Science

Three-year doctoral program providing clinical training and empirical research methodologies in clinical psychological assessment, counseling therapies, and neurodevelopmental support.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Clinical Psychology, Cognitive Science, or Child Development with honors.
English language proficiency
JLPT N1 certification and scientific English reading capability.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Japanese psychology doctorates.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and 4,000-character doctoral research proposal on cognitive behavioral interventions or developmental disorders.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from clinical psychology faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Supervised doctoral research with clinical psychology center assistantships and research grants

¥35,000 doctoral application fee
