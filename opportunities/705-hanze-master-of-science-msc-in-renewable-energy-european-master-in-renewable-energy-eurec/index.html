Hanze Master of Science (MSc) in Renewable Energy - European Master in Renewable Energy (EUREC)

Prestigious European EUREC Master's program delivered across partner European institutions, providing hands-on training in solar photovoltaic systems, wind turbine engineering, and grid integration at EnTranCe.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in engineering, physics, or mathematics with solid foundation in thermodynamics and circuits.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated technical coursework in fluid mechanics, electrical engineering, or energy systems.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Erasmus+ mobility grants (€450/month) and Hanze energy transition study bursaries
Application fee
€100 handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
€14,800/year for non-EU students; €2,601/year for EU students.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 31, 2027 (International applicant deadline for Autumn intake)
