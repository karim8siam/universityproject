Nagoya University Teaching Assistantships (TA) & Research Assistantships (RA)

University employment opportunities for enrolled Master's and PhD students providing financial assistance for assisting in seminar instruction, computer lab supervision, and experimental semiconductor fabrication.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled graduate student at Nagoya University in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
English or Japanese language proficiency matching the laboratory or lecture needs.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Excellence in coursework relevant to assigned laboratory tasks.
Recommendation letters
Approval from laboratory supervising professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried campus employment: ¥1,300 to ¥1,900 per hour assisting in laboratory investigations and course instruction
Application fee
Free application through department administration
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; hourly salaried university employment.
