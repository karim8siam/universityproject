Doctor of Philosophy in Structural Biology and Supramolecular Medicine

Advanced doctoral research co-supervised with RIKEN scientists, utilizing 950 MHz NMR and cryo-electron microscopy to resolve atomic structures of membrane receptors and oncogenic protein complexes.
