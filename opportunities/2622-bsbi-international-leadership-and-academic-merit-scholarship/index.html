BSBI International Leadership and Academic Merit Scholarship

Tuition reduction scholarship awarded to high-achieving international scholars enrolling in full-time business and management degrees at the BSBI Berlin campus.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Cumulative GPA of 3.2/4.0 or upper second-class honours in prior degree.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in reading and writing).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for BSBI programs.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word essay explaining how study in Berlin will foster entrepreneurial innovation and social leadership.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic or professional recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: up to 50% tuition reduction applied across the entire master's or bachelor's program
Application fee
Zero admissions application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€13,500 base master's tuition; reduced to €6,750 with 50% excellence scholarship

Dates and verification

Application deadline
28 February 2027 (May Intake Admissions Round)
