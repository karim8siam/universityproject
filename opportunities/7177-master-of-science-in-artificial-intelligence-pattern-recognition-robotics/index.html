Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence (Pattern Recognition & Robotics)

English-taught graduate program exploring deep learning theory, neuro-symbolic AI, computer vision, autonomous robotics, and natural language processing at the legendary FAU Pattern Recognition Lab.

IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90.

Transcripts with mathematics and programming courses, CV, and motivation statement.

Two academic recommendations.
