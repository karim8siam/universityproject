FHNW Institute for 4D Technologies Applied Artificial Intelligence Fellowship

Applied technology fellowship supporting talented computer scientists developing computer vision algorithms, real-time 3D spatial reconstruction, and digital twin models at Campus Brugg-Windisch.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or Computer Vision with minimum grade 5.2/6.0.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss technological fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Scholarly publication portfolio and 2,500-word proposal addressing 3D reconstruction, point cloud AI, or spatial computing.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from computer vision or machine learning professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual fellowship grant of CHF 8,000 and dedicated research workstation at Campus Brugg-Windisch
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full fellowship appointment; zero tuition fees

Application deadline
15 February 2027 (Spring Fellowship Selection)
