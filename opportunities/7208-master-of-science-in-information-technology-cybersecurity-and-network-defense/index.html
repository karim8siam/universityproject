Master of Science in Information Technology - Cybersecurity and Network Defense

Designated National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense program exploring threat hunting, cryptographic protocols, cloud security management, and incident response governance.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, or closely related field with GPA 3.0 or higher.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcripts, professional resume, and 500-word statement of technology goals.
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic letters of recommendation.
