Carolina Public Interest Law Fellowship (Summer Grants)

University of North Carolina School of Law · Chapel Hill, North Carolina, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: March 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Endowed summer grant supporting first- and second-year law students working in unpaid government agencies, public defender offices, and non-profit legal aid organizations.
