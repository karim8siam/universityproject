Cégep Garneau International Academic Excellence & Merit Award

Highly competitive Quebec provincial award granting complete international tuition exemption to outstanding overseas students enrolling in technical DEC programs.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 80% or 3.3/4.0 GPA in prior secondary or collegiate studies
English language proficiency
French B2/C1 proficiency (TCF or DELF)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing career vision in Quebec's technology or health sector
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from secondary school teachers
