UCalgary PhD in Computer Science (Thesis)

Four-year doctoral program focusing on machine learning, human-computer interaction, visual analytics, cybersecurity, high-performance computing, and quantum software.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized MSc in Computer Science or related quantitative field with minimum 3.3/4.0 GPA (typically 3.7+ competitive).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with at least 6.5 in all bands or TOEFL iBT 97.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional but strongly recommended for international degree holders.
Thesis / Research statement
Evidence of independent research capability through thesis work or conference papers.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed minimum annual financial package of CAD $26,000/year for 4 years via teaching assistantships, research fellowships, and faculty grants
Application fee
CAD $145 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Covered through graduate research and teaching stipends.

January 15, 2027 (Fall admission cycle deadline for international candidates)
