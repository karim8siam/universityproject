University of Toronto Excellence Awards (UTEA) in Science

Competitive 14-week summer research award enabling undergraduate students to conduct full-time experimental or theoretical research in physical, engineering, and life sciences alongside leading U of T professors.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate student in good academic standing (minimum B+ / 77% average).
English language proficiency
Working English fluency for laboratory operations.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
1-page research project proposal co-developed with a U of T faculty supervisor.
Recommendation letters
Faculty research supervisor endorsement and university transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: CAD $7,500 award for 14 weeks of full-time summer research inside U of T laboratories
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; full student research award.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 1, 2027 (Annual deadline for Summer 2027 term research)
