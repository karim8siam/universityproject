BTH Scholarship Programme for Prospective Students

Official merit-based tuition fee reduction covering 50% of tuition fees for non-EU/EEA students admitted to campus-based Master's degree programs at Blekinge Institute of Technology.

Official source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Key specifications & funding coverage

Funding status
Partial funding: 50% tuition fee reduction
Tuition and fees
SEK 70,000 per semester (SEK 140,000/year); reduced to SEK 35,000 per semester with BTH scholarship
Application fee
SEK 70,000 per semester (non-EU standard tuition; 50% covered by scholarship)
Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Swedish national autumn admission round 1 deadline)
Academic GPA requirement
Demonstrated academic excellence in previous undergraduate Bachelor of Science degree
English language requirement
Swedish English 6 equivalent (IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 5.5 in each section)
Required references
Letters of academic recommendation, motivation essay, and passport copy

Application procedure and guidance

Prospective candidates should review full academic requirements directly on the official university portal before submitting documents.
