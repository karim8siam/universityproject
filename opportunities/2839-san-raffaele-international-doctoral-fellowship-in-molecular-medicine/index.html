San Raffaele International Doctoral Fellowship in Molecular Medicine

Prestigious doctoral fellowship hosted at the San Raffaele Scientific Institute (SRSI) investigating gene editing, cancer immunotherapy, cellular therapy, and clinical neurobiology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Molecular Biology, Biotechnology, or Medicine with grade 108/110 or higher
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 88
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis summary and 2-page statement of scientific interest
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from research supervisors
