PhD in Comparative Literature and Anglo-American Cultural Studies

Three-year research doctorate in English and American literary history, modern Japanese comparative literature, translation theory, and Christian humanities.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Arts in Literature, Linguistics, or Cultural Studies.
English language proficiency
English C1 (TOEFL 95+ or IELTS 7.5+) and Japanese fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis copy, CV, publication summary, and doctoral dissertation proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from senior literature professors.
