Westminster International Law School LLM Fee Scholarship

Competitive merit scholarship awarded to outstanding international law scholars enrolling in full-time Master of Laws (LLM) degrees at Regent Street.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class or strong upper second-class honours degree (2:1) in law or international relations.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in writing and reading.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word personal statement demonstrating academic excellence and human rights commitment.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from law professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
