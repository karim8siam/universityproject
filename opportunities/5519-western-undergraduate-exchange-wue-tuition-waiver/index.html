Scholarship

Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) Tuition Waiver

Regional tuition savings program offering residents of 15 western states tuition capped at 150% of the in-state rate.

University
Central Wyoming College
Location
Riverton & Jackson, Wyoming, USA
Funding
Saves approximately $4,500/year over standard out-of-state tuition
Tuition
WUE rate ~$5,175/year
Application Fee
No separate fee; automatic assessment
Deadline
August 1, 2027
Requirements
Permanent legal resident of a participating WICHE Western state (e.g., CO, MT, UT, ID, WA, OR, CA).
