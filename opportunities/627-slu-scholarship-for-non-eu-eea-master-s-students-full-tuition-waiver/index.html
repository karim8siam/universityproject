SLU Scholarship for Non-EU/EEA Master's Students (Full Tuition Waiver)

SLU's premier merit-based scholarship for fee-paying international students admitted to English-taught Master's programs in Environmental Science, Forestry, and Plant Biology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding academic record in previous undergraduate degree (typically top 5% of class).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in each section) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Extensive CV highlighting environmental science, plant biology, or forestry coursework.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters and personal statement.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition fee waiver (worth up to SEK 320,000 for the two-year program) for top international students
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee via UniversityAdmissions.se
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition waiver covering entire Master's duration.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Annual University Admissions round application deadline)
