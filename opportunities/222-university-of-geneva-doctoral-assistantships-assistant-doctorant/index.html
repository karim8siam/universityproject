University of Geneva Doctoral Assistantships (Assistant-Doctorant)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment inside UNIGE departments, conducting frontier scientific investigations with state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure in Geneva and CERN.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science degree (minimum 120 ECTS) with high academic distinction in Physics, Mathematics, or CS.
English language proficiency
Fluent technical English for international research collaboration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or peer-reviewed publication in high-impact international journals.
Recommendation letters
Supervisory commitment letter from a UNIGE full professor and two academic references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried employment contract starting from CHF 48,000/year rising to CHF 53,000/year with Swiss pension, social security, and accident insurance
Application fee
Free application directly to open doctoral positions
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Doctoral assistants are paid academic employees.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 for Autumn intake (rolling department vacancies)
