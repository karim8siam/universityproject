Scholarship

Bourse d'Excellence à l'Admission UOF

Merit-based admission scholarship awarded to outstanding high school and international applicants entering full-time undergraduate study at UOF.

University
Université de l'Ontario français
Location
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Funding
$5,000 renewable annually based on maintaining GPA >= 3.5/4.0
Tuition
$7,200/year (Domestic Ontario) / $21,500/year (International)
Application Fee
No additional scholarship application fee
Deadline
March 31, 2027
Requirements
Admission average >= 85%, verified French proficiency (DELF B2), and demonstrated community leadership.
