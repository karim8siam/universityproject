Basel Master of Science in Quantum Science and Technology

Cutting-edge two-year Master's degree inside the Department of Physics, covering Spin-Qubit Quantum Computing, Quantum Sensing, Nanoscale Transport, and Spintronics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Physics, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering with solid quantum mechanics and linear algebra.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for non-Swiss degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and descriptions of completed theoretical and practical physics modules.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation and official transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Swiss Nanoscience Institute grants and campus tutoring
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 720 per semester.
