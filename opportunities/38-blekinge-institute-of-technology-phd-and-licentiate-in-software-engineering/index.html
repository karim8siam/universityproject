Blekinge Institute of Technology PhD and Licentiate in Software Engineering

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment (or two-year licentiate) in the Department of Software Engineering at BTH, ranked among Europe's premier research centers for empirical software engineering and industrial cooperation.

Official source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Key specifications & funding coverage

Funding status
Fully funded: salaried university doctoral employment (doktorandanstallning) under Swedish collective agreements
Tuition and fees
Zero tuition. Appointed PhD researchers hold salaried employment contracts with comprehensive Swedish state social benefits and pension contributions.
Application fee
Free (Tuition-free third-cycle doctoral education across Swedish public universities)
Application deadline
April 15, 2027 (Departmental recruitment intake; verify specific active job vacancy call)
Academic GPA requirement
Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in Software Engineering, Computer Science or related quantitative field
English language requirement
Swedish English 6 level proficiency (IELTS 6.5+ or university-level English instruction verification)
Required references
Two academic or industry research reference letters, research statement, and CV

Application procedure and guidance

Prospective candidates should review full academic requirements directly on the official university portal before submitting documents.
