Scholarship

Nagoya City Resident Special Tuition Reduction

Substantial entrance fee reduction for students whose parents have resided in Nagoya City for at least one consecutive year.

University
Nagoya City University (Takiko Campus)
Location
Nagoya, Aichi Prefecture, Japan
Funding
¥100,000 reduction on admission fees (¥232,000 vs ¥332,000 for non-residents)
Tuition
¥535,800 per academic year
Application Fee
Automatic assessment via certificate of residence (Jūminhyō)
Deadline
February 15, 2027
Requirements
Admission to NCU and verified Nagoya City family residency.
