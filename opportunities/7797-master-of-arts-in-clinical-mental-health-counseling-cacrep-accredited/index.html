Master of Arts in Clinical Mental Health Counseling (CACREP-Accredited)

Comprehensive 60-credit clinical graduate program preparing students for state licensure as Professional Counselors (LPC), with intensive training in trauma therapy, marriage counseling, and 700 hours of clinical practicum.
