ESCP Sustainable Business and Energy Transition Research Initiative

Empirical management research project analyzing corporate decarbonization strategies, renewable investment financing, and European Green Deal regulatory compliance.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in MSc in Energy Management or Master in Management sustainability track with top-quartile grades.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall or equivalent English fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for internal research appointments.
Thesis / Research statement
Research concept note analyzing European carbon market dynamics or corporate ESG disclosure compliance.
Recommendation letters
Nomination from an ESCP London faculty chair in Energy or Finance.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Research stipend of £5,000 funded by the ESCP Energy Management Centre
Application fee
Zero internal research fee
Tuition / joining costs
Funded research appointment; no tuition charged for research project units
