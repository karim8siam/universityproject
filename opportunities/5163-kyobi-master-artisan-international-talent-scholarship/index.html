KYOBI Master Artisan International Talent Scholarship

Tuition discount scholarship awarded to international students demonstrating outstanding artistic portfolios in architecture, sculpture, or fine crafts.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed 12 years of formal schooling with strong artistic background
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or higher (or EJU Japanese >= 220)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Creative visual portfolio of 10-15 works (drawings, architectural models, or craft pieces)
Recommendation letters
One artistic or academic recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
¥300,000 to ¥600,000 annual tuition reduction
Application fee
¥20,000 entrance examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
Approx. ¥1,580,000 per academic year (including studio materials)
