VU Amsterdam Master of Science in Computer Science (Joint Degree with UvA)

Elite joint master's program with the University of Amsterdam, offering specializations in Computer Systems & Security, Big Data Engineering, and Foundations of Computing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science with courses in computer architecture, operating systems, and networks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Substantial software development project or Bachelor's thesis.
Recommendation letters
Academic reference letters and transcripts.
