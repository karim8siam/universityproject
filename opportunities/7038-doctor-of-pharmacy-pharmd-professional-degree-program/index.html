Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) Professional Degree Program

Four-year professional clinical pharmacy doctorate at the Bill Gatton College of Pharmacy, featuring interprofessional healthcare teams and rural community pharmacotherapy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 65 semester hours of collegiate pre-pharmacy coursework with science GPA 2.5 or higher.
English language proficiency
English fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
PCAT not required; admissions interview required.
Thesis / Research statement
PharmCAS application, personal statement, and official transcripts.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation including one from a pharmacist or science professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Gatton College of Pharmacy Dean's Scholarships up to $10,000 annually
Application fee
$50 application fee (via PharmCAS)
