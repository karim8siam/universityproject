LMU Munich Excellence & Deutschlandstipendium Scholarships

Prestigious national merit scholarship co-funded by the German Federal Government and industrial partners, supporting high-achieving science and humanities scholars.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top academic marks (top 10% of cohort, German grade equivalent 1.5 or better).
English language proficiency
English or German proficiency conforming to program enrollment.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated social engagement, leadership, and scientific promise.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae and academic transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit grant: €3,600/year (€300/month) tax-free living stipend combined with corporate mentoring and exclusive laboratory project access
Application fee
Free application via LMU scholarship portal
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition is completely free at LMU Munich.
