Scholarship

NGU International Student Special Tuition Reduction (30%)

Special tuition fee discount program granting all qualified privately financed international undergraduate students a 30% reduction on annual tuition.

University
Nagoya Gakuin University
Location
Nagoya & Seto, Aichi Prefecture, Japan
Funding
Approximately ¥240,000 to ¥320,000 annual direct tuition deduction
Tuition
Reduced tuition applied (from ¥1,150,000 standard to ~¥850,000/year)
Application Fee
Free application upon enrolment
Deadline
April 15, 2027
Requirements
International student with College Student visa status maintaining passing grade averages.
