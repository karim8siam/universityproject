Nagoya University Doctoral Mentoring Fellowship & MEXT Grants

Nagoya University's premier international doctoral fellowship, providing full tuition and admission fee exemptions plus living allowances for outstanding privately financed and government-sponsored international PhD students.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Demonstrated top-tier academic record in prior undergraduate and Master's degree studies.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for quantitative programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis or peer-reviewed publications demonstrating independent research capability.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from university academic mentors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Monthly living stipend of ¥180,000 to ¥200,000 per month plus 100% full tuition and matriculation fee exemption for up to three years of doctoral study
Application fee
¥10,000 screening fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived under the scholarship system.
