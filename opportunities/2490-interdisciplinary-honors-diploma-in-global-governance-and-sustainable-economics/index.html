Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrollment in a Laurea Magistrale degree at UniPD or UniVR with undergraduate graduation mark of at least 100/110.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or equivalent certified English capability.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Italian collegiate honors programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and academic study plan outlining supplementary honors courses (minimum 70 hours/year).
Recommendation letters
Academic reference from undergraduate thesis supervisor.
