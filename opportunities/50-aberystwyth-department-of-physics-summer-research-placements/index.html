Aberystwyth Department of Physics Summer Research Placements

Six to eight week summer research internships in the Department of Physics for talented students to conduct hands-on laboratory or computational research in solar physics, materials science, or planetary exploration.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate student in Physics, Mathematics or Computer Science with strong academic marks.
IELTS / English language
Enrolled student status at accredited university.
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Brief personal research statement and academic transcript.
Recommendation letters
Academic reference from a university physics lecturer and confirmed host supervisor in Aberystwyth Physics.
