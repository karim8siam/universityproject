LMU Master of Science in Data Science (Elite Graduate Program)

Interdisciplinary elite master's degree taught in English, combining advanced machine learning, computational statistics, high-performance computing, and data ethics.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Statistics with top academic standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 95.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for non-European degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework mastery in linear algebra, probability, and algorithms.
Recommendation letters
Academic reference letters and personal statement.
