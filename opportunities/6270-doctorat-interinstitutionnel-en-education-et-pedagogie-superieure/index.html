Doctorat Interinstitutionnel en Éducation et Pédagogie Supérieure

Joint network doctoral degree pooling faculty expertise across UQAM, UQTR, UQAC, and UQAR to investigate pedagogical innovation, educational technology integration, and inclusive learning systems.

Not required.

Three academic reference letters.
