Master of Science in Applied Nanotechnology (High-Tech Materials & Lab-on-a-Chip)

The only Applied Nanotechnology master's in the Netherlands, collaborating with MESA+ NanoLab on microfluidic lab-on-a-chip diagnostic devices, functional nanomaterials, and biosensors.
