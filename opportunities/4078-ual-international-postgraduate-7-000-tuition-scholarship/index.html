UAL International Postgraduate £7,000 Tuition Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship awarded to exceptional prospective international students accepted onto full-time taught master's courses across UAL’s six colleges.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class or upper second-class honours degree (2:1 or equivalent) in art, design, or relevant discipline
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or 7.0 overall (depending on course) with minimum 6.0 in each section
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Creative digital portfolio, personal statement explaining creative methodology, and transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from creative or academic practitioners

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Partial funding: £7,000 tuition fee discount awarded to qualifying international master’s students
Application fee
Free
Tuition / joining costs
£15,400/year (UK domestic) or £29,330/year (International; reduced by scholarship)
