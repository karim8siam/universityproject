TUA International Theological Research Scholarship

Merit scholarship supporting exceptional international students enrolling in the English-language Master of Theology program.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized academic Bachelor of Theology or Bachelor of Arts in Religious Studies/Philosophy with high marks
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, transcript showing biblical Greek and Hebrew credits, and 750-word statement of research interest
Recommendation letters
2 letters of academic recommendation from theology professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to €5,000 per year tuition grant plus subsidized housing at the Apeldoorn campus international house
Application fee
€50
Tuition / joining costs
€2,530/year (Statutory EU fee) / €7,800/year (Non-EU rate before scholarship)
