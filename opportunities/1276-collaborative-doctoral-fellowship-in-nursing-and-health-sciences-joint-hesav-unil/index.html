Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Nursing and Health Sciences (Joint HESAV / UNIL)

Four-year joint doctoral candidacy conducted between HESAV and the Faculty of Biology and Medicine at UNIL, exploring palliative care interventions, patient safety culture, and healthcare ethics.
