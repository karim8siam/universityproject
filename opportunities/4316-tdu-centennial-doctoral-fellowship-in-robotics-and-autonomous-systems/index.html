TDU Centennial Doctoral Fellowship in Robotics and Autonomous Systems

Three-year fully funded doctoral research fellowship at the Tokyo Senju Campus investigating collaborative robotics and sensorimotor AI.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Engineering or Science with grade point average equivalent to 3.3/4.0
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 (JLPT N2 recommended)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis copy and 2,000-word doctoral research plan in robotics or control
Recommendation letters
Two letters from academic dissertation mentors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition waiver (¥900,000/year value) plus annual living stipend of ¥1,200,000 and research equipment grant
Application fee
¥30,000
Tuition / joining costs
¥900,000/year (fully waived under fellowship)

Application deadline
January 15, 2027
