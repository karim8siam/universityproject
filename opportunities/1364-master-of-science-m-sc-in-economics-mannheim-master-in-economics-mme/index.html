Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Economics (Mannheim Master in Economics - MME)

Germany's highest-ranked economics master's program taught in English, offering rigorous foundations in empirical econometrics, game theory, and macroeconomic policy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Economics or related field with at least 60 ECTS in economics/quantitative methods
English language proficiency
C1 level English (IELTS 7.0+ or TOEFL 95+)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE Revised General Test recommended for international applicants
Thesis / Research statement
Letter of motivation and comprehensive transcript
Recommendation letters
Academic reference letters
