Scholarship

Subvention pour la Vitalité Linguistique du Nord

Financial bursary intended for students engaged in French-language community broadcasting, journalism, or theatre arts in Northern Ontario.

University
Université de Sudbury / University of Sudbury
Location
Sudbury, Ontario, Canada
Funding
$3,500 annual grant
Tuition
$6,800/year standard tuition
Application Fee
None
Deadline
June 15, 2027
Requirements
Active contribution to French-language community cultural or media projects.
