ETH Student Summer Research Fellowship (ETH SSRF) in Computer Science

Highly selective, fully funded 2-month summer research fellowship (July 1 to August 31) inside the Department of Computer Science (D-INFK) at ETH Zurich, offering international students direct research collaboration with faculty mentors, housing, travel reimbursement, and social activities.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate (completed at least 2 years of study) or master's student in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering outside ETH Zurich.
English language proficiency
Fluent working knowledge of English (tested during interview; certificates optional).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Research statement of purpose outlining technical interests and laboratory preferences.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts, CV, and one or two academic reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: CHF 4,000 living stipend for 2 months (July–August), organized accommodation, full round-trip travel reimbursement, and visa cost coverage
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; fully paid international fellowship.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
December 15, 2026 (Annual international application deadline for following summer)
