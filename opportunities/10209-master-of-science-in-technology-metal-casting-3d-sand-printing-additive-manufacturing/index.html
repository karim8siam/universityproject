Master of Science in Technology - Metal Casting & 3D Sand Printing Additive Manufacturing

University of Northern Iowa · Cedar Falls, Iowa, United States

Target Discipline: engineeringApplication Deadline: March 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Operating one of the largest industrial 3D sand mold printing facilities in North America, this program teaches rapid metal casting prototypes, foundry metallurgy, binder jet additive manufacturing, and quality inspection.
