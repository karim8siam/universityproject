Tribunale di Trani Legal Practicum & Judicial Traineeship

Six-month judicial clerkship at the historic Tribunal of Trani observing criminal hearings, drafting judicial decrees, and case research.

University
Unicusano Trani (Università Niccolò Cusano - Polo di Trani)
Location
Trani, Barletta-Andria-Trani, Italy
Funding
€500 monthly ministerial reimbursement
Tuition
Curricular credits included in law degree
Application Fee
None
Deadline
April 30, 2027
Requirements
Law student at Unicusano with at least 180 ECTS completed and passing grades in Civil and Criminal Procedure.
