UBC Master of Data Science (MDS)

Intensive 10-month professional Master's program designed to teach data wrangling, machine learning, statistical modeling, data visualization, and cloud-scale data engineering through hands-on industry capstones.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's degree with minimum B+ average (76%+) and prerequisite coursework in programming, calculus, and linear algebra.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all components or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Resume / CV detailing analytical, technical, and mathematical problem-solving experience.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional reference letters evaluating quantitative and team collaboration skills.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for merit-based entrance scholarships (CAD $5,000 to $15,000) and educational student loans
Application fee
CAD $168.25 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $53,000 total program tuition for international students; CAD $35,500 for domestic Canadian students.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027 (Final international deadline for Autumn cohort intake)
