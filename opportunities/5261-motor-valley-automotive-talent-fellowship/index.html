Motor Valley Automotive Talent Fellowship

Competitive merit scholarship co-funded by Motor Valley industrial partners covering full tuition and living expenses for top-ranked incoming international students.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class bachelor's degree in mechanical, automotive, aerospace, or electronic engineering with minimum 80% mark
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section) or TOEFL iBT 85
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE quantitative score > 158 strongly recommended
Thesis / Research statement
1,000-word statement of purpose on motorsport or electric vehicle engineering career goals
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters from engineering professors
