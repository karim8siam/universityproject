Master of Arts in Peacebuilding and Collaborative Development

Two-year graduate degree delivering theoretical grounding and applied field competencies in mediation, structural justice, community peacebuilding, and human security.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 GPA in relevant social science field
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each section)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of intent outlining conflict analysis or development focus
Recommendation letters
One academic and one professional community reference
