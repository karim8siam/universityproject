Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at Science Tokyo

Prestigious national scholarship awarded to top international master's and doctoral students through the International Graduate Program (IGP).

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum GPA of 2.3 out of 3.0 on the official MEXT scale (approx 3.2+/4.0 GPA)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85+
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for quantitative fields
Thesis / Research statement
Formal MEXT field of study and research program outline
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from academic deans or professors
