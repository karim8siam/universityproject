ATSRI Clinician Researcher Development Program (CRDP)

Structured research pathway through the A.T. Still Research Institute (ATSRI) connecting graduate and clinical students with senior investigators for hands-on clinical trials, neuromusculoskeletal studies, and biomechanics research.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

GPA / Academic degree
Enrolled student in Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences or Doctor of Health Sciences in good academic standing.
Language / Citizenship
Demonstrated academic and scientific communication skills.
Standardized tests
Not required.
Experience / Research statement
Structured clinical or translational research interest statement outlining problem hypothesis and methodology.
Recommendation letters
Sponsorship endorsement from an ATSRI primary research investigator.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded research mentorship, laboratory instrumentation access, and conference travel grants up to $2,500
Application fee
Free application for matriculated students
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; funded research development pathway.
