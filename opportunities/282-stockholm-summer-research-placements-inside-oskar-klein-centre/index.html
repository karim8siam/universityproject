Stockholm Summer Research Placements inside Oskar Klein Centre

Two to six-month structured research traineeships inside the Oskar Klein Centre for Cosmoparticle Physics, collaborating on dark matter searches, IceCube neutrino data, and gravitational wave theory.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled university student at an accredited European or international partner university.
English language proficiency
English language proficiency at CEFR B2 level (IELTS 6.5 equivalent).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Signed Erasmus+ Learning Agreement for Traineeships or project proposal.
Recommendation letters
Home university exchange coordinator approval and transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Erasmus+ mobility stipend (€450 to €650 per month) plus university research hosting facilities
Application fee
Free enrollment under exchange agreements
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition.
