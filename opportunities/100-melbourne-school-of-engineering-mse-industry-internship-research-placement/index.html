Melbourne School of Engineering (MSE) Industry Internship & Research Placement

Structured 10-to-15-week vocational engineering internship placement embedding graduate engineers with industry leaders (CSIRO, Telstra, Arup, Boeing) and advanced university research laboratories.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled student in Master of Engineering or Master of IT with minimum 65% course average.
English language proficiency
Professional English communication skills for workplace and lab collaboration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Resume / CV, technical portfolio, and statement of project interest.
Recommendation letters
Academic endorsement from MSE graduate internship coordinator.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried/Stipend: AUD $800 to AUD $1,200 per week for 10-to-15-week industry-embedded research practicums (ENGR90033)
Application fee
Free enrollment for approved graduate students
Tuition / joining costs
Included in standard enrolled degree tuition; vocational stipends paid by industry partners.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 for Semester 2 industry project allocations
