Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Great Lakes Aquatic Ecology & Boreal Watershed Science

Four-year doctoral candidacy hosted in collaboration with regional federal research centres, analyzing invasive fish species management, benthic macroinvertebrates, and microplastics in Lake Superior.
