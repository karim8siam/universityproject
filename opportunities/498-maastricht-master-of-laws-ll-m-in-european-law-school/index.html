Maastricht Master of Laws (LL.M.) in European Law School

One-year advanced legal master's examining constitutional integration, EU competition policy, fundamental rights, and transnational commercial dispute resolution.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) or equivalent legal degree with substantial private or public law foundation.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (writing 6.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 (writing 24).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Motivation letter demonstrating interest in EU institutional law or comparative legal systems.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.
