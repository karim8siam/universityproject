RMIT Master of Artificial Intelligence

Two-year industry-aligned Master's degree teaching deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, autonomous agents, and ethical AI deployment.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or closely related STEM discipline with minimum GPA 2.0/4.0 (3.0+ competitive).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in programming (Python/Java), data structures, and linear algebra.
Recommendation letters
Academic transcript and statement of intent.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
RMIT Academic Excellence Scholarships worth up to AUD $10,000 fee contribution
Application fee
AUD $100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $44,160/year for international students.
