Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Applied Physics and Optics Research Assistantship

Funded doctoral fellowship conducting cutting-edge experimental research in optical sensors, non-linear optics, crystal growth, and space radiation physics partnered with NASA Marshall Space Flight Center.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Physics, Applied Physics, or Electrical Engineering with graduate GPA of 3.2/4.0.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General Test scores required (GRE Physics subject test strongly recommended).
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed research statement outlining prior laboratory work in laser optics, materials physics, or astrophysics.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters from physics faculty verifying experimental acumen.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: 100% tuition remission plus USD $24,000 annual graduate research stipend
Application fee
USD $45 graduate admissions fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charged to funded graduate research assistants
