Master of Engineering in Circular Economy and Resource Management

Advanced engineering program addressing circular product design, battery recycling metallurgy, industrial wastewater treatment, and life cycle assessment (LCA).

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Engineering or Science in Environmental, Chemical, or Mechanical Engineering (minimum grade 2.5).
English language proficiency
German C1 or English B2 depending on chosen modules.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts with 210 ECTS, CV, and motivation letter.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
