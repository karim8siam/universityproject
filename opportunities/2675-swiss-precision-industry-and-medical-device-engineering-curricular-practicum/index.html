Swiss Precision Industry and Medical Device Engineering Curricular Practicum

Six-month engineering work practicum embedding students within leading Swiss biomedical, robotics, and industrial instrumentation enterprises in Basel and Aargau.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completion of Year 1 of Bachelor or Master of Science in Engineering in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Conversational German (B1/B2) and fluent English required for Swiss enterprise operations.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss industrial internships.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, technical portfolio, and cover letter addressed to Swiss engineering partner firms.
Recommendation letters
Approval from the FHNW Industry Internship Coordinator.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Paid industry practicum: CHF 2,400 to CHF 3,200 monthly compensation with Swiss MedTech and robotics firms
Application fee
Included in standard semester matriculation
Tuition / joining costs
Standard semester tuition applies

Application deadline
01 April 2027 (Autumn Industry Placement Intake)
