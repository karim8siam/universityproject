FAU Emerging Talents Initiative (ETI) Postdoctoral Research Grant

Prestigious institutional fellowship designed to support exceptional early-career postdocs at FAU in establishing independent scientific profiles and securing national research awards.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed doctorate within the previous three years with outstanding distinction.
English language proficiency
English C1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Independent research project outline (5 pages), CV, publication list, and departmental hosting confirmation.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of support from full professors.
