Todai Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year English-medium research Master's degree inside the Department of Computer Science, focusing on theoretical foundations, programming languages, computer systems, and machine learning.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or closely related quantitative discipline.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General test recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong coursework background in algorithms, discrete mathematics, computer architecture, and systems.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic recommendation and official transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for MEXT University Recommendation scholarships and Todai tuition exemptions
Application fee
¥30,000 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800 per year plus one-time admission fee of ¥282,000.
