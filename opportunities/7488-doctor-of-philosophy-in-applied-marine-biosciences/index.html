Doctor of Philosophy in Applied Marine Biosciences

Three-year doctoral dissertation research program producing global leaders in fish germ-cell manipulation, marine bioactive discovery, deep-sea ecology, and ocean biogeochemical cycles.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Marine Sciences, Biology, or Fisheries Science with honors.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 85+ or IELTS 6.5+ (English dissertation track available).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis copy, publication list, 4-page doctoral research project proposal, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendations from graduate research supervisors.
