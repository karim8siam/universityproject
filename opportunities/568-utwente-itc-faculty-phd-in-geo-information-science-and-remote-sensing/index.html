UTwente ITC Faculty PhD in Geo-Information Science and Remote Sensing

Four-year doctoral research program at the renowned Faculty ITC investigating satellite radar interferometry, natural disaster early warning systems, and climate change land-use dynamics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Geo-Information Science, Remote Sensing, Geography, or Earth Observation with superior standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated skills in satellite imagery processing (SAR, hyperspectral), GIS, and machine learning.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried CAO-NU employment contract (€2,770 - €3,539/month gross) plus field research equipment funding for 4 years
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition fees.
