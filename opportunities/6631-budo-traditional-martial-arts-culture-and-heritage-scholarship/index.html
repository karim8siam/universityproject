Budo (Traditional Martial Arts) Culture and Heritage Scholarship

Merit award dedicated to undergraduate scholars practicing Judo, Kendo, Kyudo, or Naginata, fostering the international philosophical transmission of Japanese Budo.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Hold at least Shodan (1st dan black belt) in a recognized Japanese martial art.
English language proficiency
Japanese B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Dan ranking certificate, competition record, and essay on Budo philosophy.
Recommendation letters
Dojo master or federated sensei recommendation.
