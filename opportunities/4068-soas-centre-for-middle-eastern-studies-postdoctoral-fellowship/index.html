SOAS Centre for Middle Eastern Studies Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year research fellowship conducting archival and field-based research on political transitions, water politics, or sectarian dynamics in the Middle East and North Africa.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
PhD in Middle Eastern Studies, Politics, History, or Anthropology awarded within past 3 years
English language proficiency
Fluency in English and working knowledge of Arabic, Persian, or Turkish
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, summary of doctoral dissertation, two writing samples, and 4-page research proposal
Recommendation letters
Three academic letters of appraisal
