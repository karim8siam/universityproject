UChicago Kenneth C. Griffin Department of Economics Fully Funded Doctoral Fellowships

World's most celebrated doctoral program in economics, birthplace of 34 Nobel Prizes in Economic Sciences, providing five years of guaranteed living stipends, comprehensive health insurance, and full tuition waivers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.8/4.0 GPA in undergraduate economics, mathematics, or quantitative sciences.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 105.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General Test required (quantitative score typically in 95th+ percentile).
Thesis / Research statement
Exceptional mathematical and analytical aptitude, sample research paper, or honors thesis.
Recommendation letters
Three detailed letters of recommendation from economics and mathematics faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
100% Fully Funded: Annual living stipend of $46,000/year for 5 years, complete tuition waiver ($62,000/year), and full U-SHIP health insurance
Application fee
$90 application fee (waiver available)
Tuition / joining costs
100% covered by university doctoral fellowship awards.
