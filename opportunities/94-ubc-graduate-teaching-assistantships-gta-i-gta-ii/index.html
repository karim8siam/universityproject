UBC Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA I / GTA II)

Official academic teaching positions providing substantial financial support, instruction leadership training, and classroom teaching experience across undergraduate lecture courses, tutorials, and laboratory sections.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Registered UBC graduate student in good standing in a Master's or PhD degree program.
English language proficiency
High level spoken English communication for tutorial instruction and laboratory demonstration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior completion with distinction in relevant course modules.
Recommendation letters
Approval from department graduate advisor and course instructor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried academic employment: CAD $35.13 per hour (GTA II) to CAD $40.50 per hour (GTA I) under CUPE 2278 union collective agreement
Application fee
Free application submitted via departmental TA portals
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; salaried university employment with comprehensive benefits.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 for upcoming Winter session (Terms 1 & 2) appointments
