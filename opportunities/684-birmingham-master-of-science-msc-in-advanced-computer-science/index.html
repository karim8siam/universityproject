Birmingham Master of Science (MSc) in Advanced Computer Science

Advanced one-year MSc accredited by the British Computer Society (BCS), offering specialized research modules in deep learning, natural language processing, cryptography, and complex systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or equivalent GPA 3.3/4.0) in Computer Science or closely related discipline with substantial programming.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-tests) or TOEFL iBT 88.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong academic record in algorithms, data structures, and object-oriented programming.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Birmingham Global Masters Scholarships (£2,000 to £10,000 tuition fee reductions for international scholars)
Application fee
£50 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£29,880 full international tuition fee (before scholarship deductions).
