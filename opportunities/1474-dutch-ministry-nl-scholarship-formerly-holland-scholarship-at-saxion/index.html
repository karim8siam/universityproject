Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) at Saxion

Competitive scholarship financed by the Dutch Ministry of Education and Saxion for exceptional non-EEA students enrolling in English-taught master's degrees.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Excellent bachelor's GPA (equivalent to Dutch 7.5+ or top 20% of class)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall with 6.0 in all bands
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Compelling motivation letter explaining high-tech engineering goals in Twente
Recommendation letters
One academic and one professional recommendation
