Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering Direct English Track

English-medium four-semester graduate degree in Jülich preparing engineers for international medical technology and biomedical diagnostics industries.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in biomedical engineering, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or physics (minimum 210 ECTS)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Verified undergraduate degree certificate and detailed syllabus transcript
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
DAAD scholarships, Deutschlandstipendium awards, and research assistant positions available
Application fee
Free application via uni-assist for international applicants (€75)
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition fees; approx. €320/semester administrative fee
