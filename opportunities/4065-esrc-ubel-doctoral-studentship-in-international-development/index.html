ESRC UBEL Doctoral Studentship in International Development

Three-year fully funded PhD studentship through the ESRC UCL-Birkbeck-SOAS Doctoral Training Partnership (UBEL), focusing on political economy, labor relations, or state-building in Africa or Asia.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master’s degree with Merit or Distinction in Development Studies, Economics, Sociology, or Political Science
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in writing)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive research proposal (3,000 words), bibliography, and writing sample
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from graduate thesis supervisors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: UKRI stipend of £21,237 per year tax-free (London rate) plus 100% full tuition fees and fieldwork travel grant
Application fee
Free
Tuition / joining costs
Fully covered by ESRC Research Council grant
