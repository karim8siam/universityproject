Peter Behrens School of Arts Graduate Fellowship in Spatial Scenography

Two-year graduate design fellowship providing studio workspace and project funding for innovative exhibition and temporary architectural installations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's in architecture, interior architecture, or design with grade 2.3 or better
English language proficiency
English B2 and German B2/C1
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive creative portfolio demonstrating spatial concept design
Recommendation letters
Two letters from design professors or creative directors
