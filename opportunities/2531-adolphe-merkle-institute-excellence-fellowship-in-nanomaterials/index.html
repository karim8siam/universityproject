Adolphe Merkle Institute Excellence Fellowship in Nanomaterials

Prestigious fellowship awarded by the world-renowned Adolphe Merkle Institute at the University of Fribourg supporting exceptional researchers in fundamental and applied nanoscience.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Chemistry, Materials Science, Physics, or Bio-engineering with top 10% academic standing.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 in English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE Subject Test in Chemistry or Physics recommended for international applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing interest in bio-inspired nanomaterials, polymer chemistry, or nanomedicine.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from senior chemistry or physics faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit fellowship: CHF 20,000 per academic year supporting master's and doctoral research in materials science
Application fee
CHF 150 international admissions fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 985 per semester international tuition; fully covered by fellowship living grant

Application deadline
