AlmaLaurea National Graduate Report Doctoral Scholarship

Three-year cooperative doctoral scholarship co-sponsored with the University of Bologna Department of Statistical Sciences studying longitudinal educational trajectories.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Laurea Magistrale (Master of Science) in statistics, economics, or data science with grade 105/110 or higher
English language proficiency
English B2 minimum
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and 3,000-word proposed doctoral research outline on higher education outcomes
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters evaluating quantitative empirical research capability

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€1,650 net monthly stipend (€19,800/year) plus 10% research budget increment and 50% increase during foreign research stays
Application fee
€30 University of Bologna doctoral application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full doctoral tuition exemption under Italian public PhD regulations
