Master of Arts in Sound Studies and Sonic Arts

World-leading English-taught postgraduate degree bridging sound design, spatial audio composition, acoustic ecology, sonic installations, and auditory culture.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Music, Architecture, Sound Design, Media Art, or Engineering.
English language proficiency
English C1 (TOEFL 90 or IELTS 6.5).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Portfolio of 3-5 sonic works or audio installations, project proposal, CV, and transcripts.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from acoustic or arts professionals.
