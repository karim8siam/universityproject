Curtin Western Australian Energy & Mining Industry Research Internships

Structured 12-week vocational research internship embedding graduate engineers with Western Australian resource, energy, and engineering pioneers (BHP, Rio Tinto, Woodside, Chevron).

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled Master of Professional Engineering student with minimum 65% CWA.
English language proficiency
Professional English communication skills for workplace and site safety.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Resume / CV, technical engineering portfolio, and project preference statement.
Recommendation letters
Academic endorsement from Curtin engineering internship coordinator.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried internship: AUD $850 to AUD $1,200 per week for 12-week professional industry research practicums
Application fee
Free enrollment for approved Curtin graduate students
Tuition / joining costs
Included in enrolled degree curriculum; industry stipend paid directly.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 for Semester 2 industry practicum placements
