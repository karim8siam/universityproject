University of Missouri · Columbia, Missouri, United States

Target Discipline: biologyApplication Deadline: September 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Comprehensive clinical veterinary doctorate featuring 40 hours/week in the Veterinary Health Center during clinical rotations, focusing on equine medicine, livestock reproduction, and oncology.
