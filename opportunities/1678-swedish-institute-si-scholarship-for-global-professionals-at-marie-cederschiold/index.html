Swedish Institute (SI) Scholarship for Global Professionals at Marie Cederschiöld

Prestigious Swedish Government scholarship fully funding international professionals pursuing master's degrees in social welfare, civil society leadership, or health systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Demonstrated academic excellence and minimum 3,000 hours of documented work experience
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 5.5 in each section)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
SI leadership CV and proof of professional engagement
Recommendation letters
Two letters of reference on SI official templates

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
100% full tuition coverage, SEK 12,000/month living allowance, travel grant, and insurance

SEK 900

100% covered under Swedish Government scheme
