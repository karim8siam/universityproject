Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at Hiroshima

Prestigious national scholarship awarded by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) through Hiroshima University recommendation for graduate studies.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum GPA 2.30/3.00 on MEXT calculation formula in preceding degree
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85+ (CEFR B2 or above)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Clear scientific research proposal aligned with host professor's laboratory
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation including one from Dean of previous institution
