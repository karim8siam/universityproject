Aberystwyth University PhD and MPhil in Solar System Physics and Space Weather

Postgraduate research degree in the Department of Physics investigating solar atmospheric dynamics, coronal mass ejections, solar-terrestrial interactions, space weather forecasting, and planetary surface physics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Upper second-class (2:1) or first-class Honours degree in Physics, Astrophysics, Mathematics, or Earth Sciences.
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each component).
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Undergraduate or master's physics thesis, research project report, or conference paper.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters, research statement, and endorsement from prospective physics faculty advisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded studentships available via STFC research council, AberDoc, and departmental fellowships
Application fee
Free direct postgraduate application
Tuition / joining costs
£5,006/year (UK rate) or £22,410/year (International); covered for awarded research studentships.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 9, 2027 for funded scholarship round; rolling applications for self-funded candidates
