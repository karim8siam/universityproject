Bachelor of Music in Orchestral Conducting and Classical Composition

Four-year intensive conservatory curriculum providing podium time with professional orchestras, score analysis, aural training, instrumentation, and rehearsal technique.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school leaving certificate (Abitur or equivalent foreign diploma).
English language proficiency
German language certificate at level B2 (Goethe-Zertifikat or TestDaF).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, list of previously studied and conducted repertoire, and 3 original compositions (for composition track).
Recommendation letters
One artistic recommendation from an established conductor or pedagogue.
