Clemson University R1 Doctoral Research Assistantship in Automotive Engineering

Prestige graduate assistantship awarded to exceptional doctoral candidates conducting research in connected vehicle infrastructure, battery management algorithms, or active suspension dynamics at CU-ICAR.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical, Electrical, or Automotive Engineering with minimum 3.5/4.0 GPA
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for competitive consideration
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose outlining automotive research interests, curriculum vitae, and official transcripts
Recommendation letters
Three letters of academic recommendation from engineering professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
100% fully funded: $32,000 to $36,000/year living stipend plus 100% full tuition waiver and health insurance
Application fee
$90
Tuition / joining costs
$23,000/year (fully offset by institutional graduate assistantship fee waiver)
