Stanford PhD in Computer Science

Five-year doctoral research program inside the Gates Computer Science Building in Silicon Valley, conducting frontier investigations in artificial intelligence, computer vision, robotics, systems architecture, and theory.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering with top marks.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 minimum.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research accomplishments in machine learning, systems, theory, robotics, or security.
Recommendation letters
Three confidential academic letters of recommendation evaluating research creativity.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Five-year guaranteed funding package including full tuition and USD $54,000 annual living stipend through research assistantships and teaching fellowships
Application fee
USD $125 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Full tuition and fees paid by departmental graduate fellowship.
