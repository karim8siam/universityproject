Griffith Vice Chancellor's International Postgraduate Excellence Scholarship

Prestigious award covering 50% of total postgraduate coursework tuition for exceptionally high-achieving international scholars entering Griffith University.

Not required

Demonstrated academic excellence and personal statement
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation
