Japanese Government (MEXT) National Teacher Training Scholarship at Hokkyodai

Prestigious Monbukagakusho (MEXT) national scholarship providing full financial support to international educators conducting graduate-level educational research in Japan.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.2 cumulative GPA out of 4.0 in bachelor's degree
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or IELTS 6.5
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive pedagogical research plan for teacher education in Japan
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from school principal or university dean

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full 100% tuition waiver plus ¥143,000 JPY monthly living stipend and return flights
Application fee
¥0 (waived for MEXT candidates)
Tuition / joining costs
¥0 (100% government tuition waiver)
