European Policy and International NGO Professional Practicum

Four-month professional internship placement placing students with international human rights organizations, European policy think tanks, and civic institutions in Rome and Brussels.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in master's degree in political science, law, or economics with satisfactory collegiate credit completion.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 or CEFR C1 in English; working knowledge of French or Spanish desirable.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for European NGO internships.
Thesis / Research statement
Cover letter and Europass CV detailing international volunteer experience and policy analysis skills.
Recommendation letters
Approval from the Collegio Mazza Career Guidance Committee.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Funded placement: €800 monthly living allowance and collegiate accommodation continuity
