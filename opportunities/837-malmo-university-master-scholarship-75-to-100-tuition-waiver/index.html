Malmö University Master Scholarship (75% to 100% Tuition Waiver)

Merit-based scholarship awarded to high-achieving non-EU/EEA students applying to English-taught master's degrees in biomedical sciences, computer science, and design at Malmö University.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-choice application to Malmö University with top academic grades in undergraduate degree (minimum 80% or 3.5/4.0 GPA).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence and strong motivation letter.
Recommendation letters
Academic reference letters submitted with scholarship dossier.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full or 75% tuition fee waiver for the full 2-year duration of the Master's programme (valued at up to SEK 260,000)
Application fee
SEK 900 central application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Reduces annual international tuition to SEK 0 or under SEK 35,000/year.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Annual international scholarship round for Autumn intake)
