Master of Science in Speech-Language Pathology (SLP)

CAA-accredited professional master's degree training speech-language clinicians in neurogenic swallowing disorders (dysphagia), pediatric speech sound disorders, and voice pathology.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Communication Sciences or leveling prerequisite coursework with GPA 3.0+.
English language proficiency
TOEFL 85 or IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE required.
Thesis / Research statement
CSDCAS application, personal statement, and verified transcripts.
Recommendation letters
Three academic or clinical recommendations.
