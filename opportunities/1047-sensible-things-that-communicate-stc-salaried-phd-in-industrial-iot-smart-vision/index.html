Sensible Things that Communicate (STC) Salaried PhD in Industrial IoT & Smart Vision

Four-year salaried doctoral research post within the STC research center, developing ultra-low-power intelligent camera nodes and wireless mesh sensor networks for industrial forest and mining operations.
