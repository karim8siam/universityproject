Master of Science in Aerospace Engineering and Satellite Systems (Utsunomiya Campus)

Advanced engineering program designing CubeSats, composite rocket aerodynamics, satellite orbital mechanics, and autonomous drone navigation algorithms in partnership with JAXA.
