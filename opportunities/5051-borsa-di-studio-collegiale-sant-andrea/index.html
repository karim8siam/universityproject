Borsa di Studio Collegiale Sant'Andrea

Merit- and income-based collegiate scholarship providing full or partial subsidization of residential room, board, and academic tutorial fees in Bergamo Alta.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school diploma with minimum grade of 80/100, or university average of at least 27/30
English language proficiency
Italian B2 proficiency or English B2 for international candidates
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, motivational letter, and ISEE financial indicator documentation
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€3,500 to €5,000 residential fee waiver across the academic year
Application fee
€30 application registration fee
Tuition / joining costs
Subsidized collegiate rate (approx. €2,500 to €4,800/yr for room, board, and tutoring)
