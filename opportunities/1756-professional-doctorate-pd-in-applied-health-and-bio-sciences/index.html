Professional Doctorate (PD) in Applied Health and Bio-Sciences

Four-year Dutch national Professional Doctorate (PD) program bridging university research and life science industry practice at the Leiden Centre for Applied Bioscience.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in applied life sciences, bioinformatics, or pharmaceutical technology
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or Cambridge C1
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and 2,500-word proposal on translational healthcare biotechnology
Recommendation letters
Two academic and industry supervisor letters
