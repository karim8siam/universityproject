FU Berlin Dahlem Neurobiology Summer Research Traineeship

Two-month summer research internship in Dahlem neurobiology laboratories, working with Drosophila models, confocal microscopy, and electrophysiological patch-clamp recordings to investigate synaptic plasticity and memory mechanisms.
