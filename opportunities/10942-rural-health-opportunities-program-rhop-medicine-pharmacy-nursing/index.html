Rural Health Opportunities Program (RHOP - Medicine, Pharmacy & Nursing)

Nebraska State College System · Lincoln, Nebraska, United States

Target Discipline: biologyApplication Deadline: November 15, 2026Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Cooperative program between NSCS colleges and the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC), offering full-tuition waivers and guaranteed early admission to UNMC professional schools.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Full 100% undergraduate tuition waiver plus guaranteed seat in UNMC medical/pharmacy/nursing school
Tuition Rate
$0 (Full tuition waiver at NSCS colleges)
Application Fee
$30

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
3.5+ cumulative high school GPA with strong biology and chemistry grades
English Language
Satisfied via residency
Standardized Exam
ACT composite 24+ (math and science 24+)
Application Materials
RHOP application, rural healthcare motivation essay, official transcripts
Letters of Recommendation
Three letters of recommendation (one healthcare practitioner)

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult Nebraska State College System's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
