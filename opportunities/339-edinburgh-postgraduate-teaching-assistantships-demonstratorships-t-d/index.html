Edinburgh Postgraduate Teaching Assistantships & Demonstratorships (T&D)

Contractual academic employment for registered doctoral researchers, assisting in tutorial leadership, coding laboratory exercises, and student assessment.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled PhD student in the School of Informatics or School of Engineering.
English language proficiency
Spoken English fluency certified through university assessment.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Subject mastery of the assigned course curriculum.
Recommendation letters
Course organizer approval.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Contractual salary: £18.50 to £24.00 per hour for tutoring, laboratory demonstration, and marking
Application fee
Free internal application via Informatics T&D portal
Tuition / joining costs
Provides supplemental income during doctoral research.
