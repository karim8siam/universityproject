Post-Graduate Research Fellowship in Clinical Biomechanics and Fascial Neurobiology

Clinical research fellowship conducting empirical investigations into fascial innervation, autonomic nervous system regulation, and musculoskeletal biomechanics in chronic pain patients.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Diploma in Osteopathy (D.O.) or Master's degree in Rehabilitation Sciences with minimum 105/110.
English language proficiency
Scientific English fluency (B2/C1 level) for international journal publication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Italian clinical research fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
2,000-word research proposal evaluating fascial mechanotransduction or posture-gait kinematics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from university biomechanics or anatomy professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Institutional research sponsorship: €3,500 annual grant plus full access to Padua gait laboratory facilities
Application fee
Zero internal application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition waiver support for specialized research seminar credits
