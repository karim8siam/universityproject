IUCPQ Foundation Doctoral Excellence Fellowship in Cardiovascular Science

Fully funded four-year doctoral research fellowship at the IUCPQ Research Centre investigating molecular pathways of vascular stiffening and cardiac calcification.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
3.3/4.0 or French grade mention bien in biochemistry, pharmacology, or medicine
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TFI (Test de français international) 750 for clinical components
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; research master thesis evaluated
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or published co-authored article in cardiopulmonary science
Recommendation letters
Three academic letters of recommendation evaluating research capacity

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition coverage plus CAD $32,000 annual living stipend and conference travel allowance
Application fee
CAD $95 (Université Laval graduate application fee)
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $4,400/year (Quebec resident) or CAD $19,800/year (International, differential fee waivers available)
