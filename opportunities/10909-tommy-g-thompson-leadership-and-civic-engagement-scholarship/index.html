Academic Overview & Description

Statewide endowed scholarship honoring undergraduate students demonstrating outstanding civic leadership, public service, and academic integrity.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Full $2,500 annual scholarship plus participation in the Thompson Center leadership symposium
Tuition Rate
$5,461/year (Net cost reduced to $2,961)
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
3.3+ cumulative GPA at any UW branch campus
English Language
Satisfied via enrollment
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Essay on bipartisan civic leadership, resume, academic transcripts
Letters of Recommendation
Two letters of recommendation (one civic leader, one faculty)
