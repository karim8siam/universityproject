TU/e Master of Science in Embedded Systems

Specialized two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) partnering closely with ASML, Philips, and NXP to engineer real-time cyber-physical systems, FPGA hardware, and autonomous embedded controllers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or Embedded Systems with high marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework background in digital circuit design, microcontrollers, real-time systems, and C/C++.
Recommendation letters
Complete university academic transcripts and syllabus descriptions.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Brainport industrial scholarships and ALSP fee waivers

€100 application fee
