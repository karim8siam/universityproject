Brussels European Institutions and Policy Consultancy Curricular Internship

Four-to-six-month professional internship placing graduate students within European Commission directorates-general, European Parliament offices, and international NGOs in Brussels.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Successful completion of all Semester 1 core European Studies coursework.
English language proficiency
Full professional English fluency; knowledge of French advantageous for Brussels institutions.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for European institutional internships.
Thesis / Research statement
Europass CV and targeted cover letter tailored to European Commission directorates or public affairs consultancies.
Recommendation letters
Approval from the FASoS Internship Coordinator.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Erasmus+ trainee mobility grant: €450 to €600 per month living support in Brussels
Application fee
Included in standard annual program tuition
Tuition / joining costs
Elective curricular master's degree component
