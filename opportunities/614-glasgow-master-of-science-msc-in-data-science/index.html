Glasgow Master of Science (MSc) in Data Science

One-year intensive Master of Science program teaching machine learning, deep learning, big data architectures, information retrieval, and human-in-the-loop data science.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or equivalent GPA 3.3/4.0) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related quantitative field.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Solid programming proficiency in Python/Java and linear algebra.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter and statement of purpose.
