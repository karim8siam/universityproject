YukonU Northern Research Institute Excellence Bursary

Competitive scholarship for full-time scholars enrolled in northern science, climate adaptation, or First Nations governance degrees.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 75% entrance average or 3.0 college GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 79.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word essay explaining commitment to northern community advancement.
Recommendation letters
One academic or community reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $5,000 annual educational bursary plus Arctic field travel grant
Application fee
CAD $50
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $4,400/year (domestic standard), CAD $14,200/year (international)
