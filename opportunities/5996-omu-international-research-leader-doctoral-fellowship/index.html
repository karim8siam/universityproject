OMU International Research Leader Doctoral Fellowship

JST-sponsored doctoral fellowship for outstanding researchers conducting advanced studies in artificial photosynthesis, clean energy conversion, or nanomaterials.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Chemistry, Physics, Chemical Engineering, or Materials Science (minimum 3.3/4.0 GPA).
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 85 or IELTS 6.5 (or JLPT N1 for Japanese curricula).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Four-page doctoral research project plan and master's thesis abstract.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from graduate research advisors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full scholarship: ¥200,000 monthly research stipend + complete tuition fee waiver
Application fee
¥282,000 entrance fee (exempted)
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800/year (100% exempted for fellowship scholars)
