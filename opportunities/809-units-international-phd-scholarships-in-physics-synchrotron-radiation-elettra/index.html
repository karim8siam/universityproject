UniTS International PhD Scholarships in Physics & Synchrotron Radiation (Elettra)

Doctoral research program joint with Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste and the FERMI Free Electron Laser, focusing on time-resolved X-ray spectroscopy, quantum materials, and topological insulators.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Second-cycle Master's degree in Physics, Materials Science, or Applied Mathematics with high marks (105/110 or above).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or CEFR B2 level certificate.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High academic merit, thesis abstract, and proposed research plan for condensed matter or synchrotron physics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters submitted online.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: €16,243/year net for 3 years, 50% stipend increase for stays abroad, and beamtime at Elettra Synchrotron; zero tuition
Application fee
€30 public competition application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% full tuition waiver for all admitted doctoral fellowship holders.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
June 15, 2027 (Annual Italian National Doctoral Call - 43rd Cycle)
