Cooperative Doctoral Research Position (Ph.D.) in Precision Mechatronics and Optics

Three-to-four-year salaried cooperative doctoral research appointment jointly supervised with ETH Zurich, conducting experimental research on micro-optics, piezo-actuators, and precision laser machining.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering, Micro-Engineering, Applied Physics, or Mechatronics with high honors.
English language proficiency
Fluent scientific English communication (IELTS 7.0 or equivalent).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss cooperative doctoral appointments.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and scientific portfolio highlighting experimental precision engineering or optical metrology.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters from doctoral supervisors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full Swiss research salary: CHF 54,000 to CHF 62,000 per annum in partnership with ETH Zurich
Application fee
Zero tuition charges for doctoral researchers
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition-free doctoral employment under Swiss SNSF and cantonal collective agreements

Dates and verification

Application deadline
15 March 2027 (Spring Doctoral Vacancy Call)
