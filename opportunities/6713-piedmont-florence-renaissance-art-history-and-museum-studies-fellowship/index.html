Piedmont Florence Renaissance Art History and Museum Studies Fellowship

Intensive residential semester fellowship examining Medici patronage, fresco restoration techniques, and Renaissance humanism directly in Florentine archives and collections.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA on 4.0 scale.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 for non-native English speakers.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Art history analytical writing sample (1,500 words) and statement of research intent.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from college faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
$5,000 USD fellowship providing direct museum access, archival privileges, and travel stipend
Application fee
$75 USD application processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
$14,500 USD comprehensive semester study abroad tuition
