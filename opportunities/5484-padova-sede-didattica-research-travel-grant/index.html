Padova Sede Didattica Research Travel Grant

Travel grant reimbursing transportation and accommodation expenses for students traveling to Padova from rural Veneto for thesis examinations.

University
Università Telematica Pegaso e Mercatorum - Sede di Padova
Location
Padova, Veneto, Italy
Funding
€500 per examination session
Tuition
Standard tuition applies
Application Fee
None
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
Enrolled student at Sede di Padova resident more than 100km from exam location.
