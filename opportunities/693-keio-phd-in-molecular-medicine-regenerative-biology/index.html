Keio PhD in Molecular Medicine & Regenerative Biology

Four-year medical doctorate located at the Shinanomachi Campus in central Tokyo, conducting pioneering research in induced pluripotent stem (iPS) cells, neural regeneration, and precision oncology.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree or 6-year medical degree (MBBS/MD) with high academic distinction.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior laboratory research experience in stem cell biology, neuroscience, or genetics.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation.
