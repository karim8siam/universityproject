ATSU Hometown Scholars & Community Health Endowed Scholarships

Targeted scholarship initiative connecting incoming and continuing health professions students with institutional endowments and partner Community Health Centers (CHCs) to support aspiring healthcare leaders dedicated to serving medically underserved populations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

GPA / Academic degree
Minimum 3.0 cumulative undergraduate GPA in biomedical or health sciences.
Language / Citizenship
U.S. citizen, permanent resident, or eligible international applicant depending on program.
Standardized tests
Not required for internal scholarship consideration.
Experience / Research statement
Demonstrated record of healthcare leadership, volunteerism, or primary care service in underserved rural/urban communities.
Recommendation letters
Official ATSU admissions acceptance, FAFSA / institutional financial aid form, and endorsement from a Community Health Center (CHC) leader.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit and need-based institutional scholarships ranging from $5,000 to full tuition support for future community healthcare leaders
Application fee
Free internal application through ATSU ScholarshipUniverse portal
Tuition / joining costs
Subsidizes tuition across ATSU-KCOM, SOMA, ASHS, and CGHS degree programs.
