Master of Fine Arts (MFA) in Creative Writing

Highly competitive and fully funded studio MFA program in fiction, poetry, and creative nonfiction, rooted in the legacy of alumna Flannery O’Connor and home to the renowned journal Arts & Letters.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor degree in any discipline with minimum 3.0 GPA
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 90 for international applicants
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Writing portfolio (up to 25 pages of fiction/nonfiction or 10-15 poems) and statement of artistic purpose
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation evaluating literary talent and teaching potential

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
