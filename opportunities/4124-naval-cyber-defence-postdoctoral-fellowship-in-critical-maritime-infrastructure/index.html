Naval Cyber Defence Postdoctoral Fellowship in Critical Maritime Infrastructure

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the Faculty of Military Sciences examining cyber vulnerability in shipboard industrial control systems (ICS), AIS spoofing, and GPS-denied maritime navigation.
