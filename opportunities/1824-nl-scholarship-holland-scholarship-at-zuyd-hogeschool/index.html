NL Scholarship (Holland Scholarship) at Zuyd Hogeschool

Prestigious national scholarship assisting talented non-EU/EEA students enrolling in full-time bachelor's and master's degrees at Zuyd University of Applied Sciences.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with high academic standing (GPA 3.2+ or 75%+)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Motivational essay explaining interest in Brightlands sustainability innovation
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
EUR 5,000 one-time financial grant awarded by the Dutch Ministry of Education
Application fee
EUR 0
Tuition / joining costs
EUR 9,500/year (offset by EUR 5,000 grant)
