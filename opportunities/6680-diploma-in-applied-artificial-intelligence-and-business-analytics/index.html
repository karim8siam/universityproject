Diploma in Applied Artificial Intelligence and Business Analytics

Two-year intensive applied diploma covering machine learning pipelines, predictive analytics, enterprise Python systems, and business intelligence dashboards.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school diploma with grade 12 Mathematics (minimum 70%).
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 80.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts, personal statement of career objectives in AI.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from academic or employer supervisors.
