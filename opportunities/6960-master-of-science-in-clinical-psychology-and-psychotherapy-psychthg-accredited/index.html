Master of Science in Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy (PsychThG-Accredited)

Polyvalent master's program qualifying graduates for the German state licensing examination (Approbation) as psychotherapists, emphasizing cognitive-behavioral and systemic therapy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Psychology meeting the German licensing prerequisites (minimum GPA 2.3 or international equivalent).
English language proficiency
German language proficiency at level C1 (TestDaF or DSH-2); English B2 recommended.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Certified academic transcripts, motivation statement, and clinical internship documentation.
Recommendation letters
One academic and one clinical letter of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
