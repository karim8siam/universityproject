FHNW Swiss Cantonal Engineering and Life Sciences Merit Scholarship

Cantonal merit scholarship supporting exceptional Swiss and international students pursuing master's degrees in engineering, mechatronics, and biotechnology at FHNW campuses.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Engineering, Computer Science, or Life Sciences with minimum grade 5.0/6.0 on the Swiss scale.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 in English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for Swiss university of applied sciences admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing interest in Industry 4.0 automation, medical technology, or sustainable engineering.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from engineering or science faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: up to CHF 5,000 annual study grant applied toward tuition and specialized laboratory costs
Application fee
CHF 100 admissions evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 1,000 per semester international tuition; subsidized by cantonal study grant
