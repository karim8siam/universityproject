Maîtrise en ingénierie (M.Sc.A. en génie électrique et robotique)

Two-year research master's degree focusing on microgrid power electronics, industrial robotics, sensor networks, and autonomous industrial control systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Electrical or Computer Engineering (GPA >= 3.2/4.3).
English language proficiency
French proficiency (TFI 750 or B2 level).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and academic transcripts.
Recommendation letters
Two reference forms.
