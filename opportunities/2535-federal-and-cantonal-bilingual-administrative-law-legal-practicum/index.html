Federal and Cantonal Bilingual Administrative Law Legal Practicum

Six-month bilingual legal practicum embedding law graduates in cantonal administrative tribunals and federal offices in Fribourg and Bern, drafting legal opinions and appellate briefs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completion of Bachelor of Law (BLaw) at UniFR or recognized Swiss law faculty with credit average.
English language proficiency
Bilingual proficiency in French and German (minimum B2/C1 in both official languages).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss legal internships.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, transcript of legal exams, and cover letter addressed to the Cantonal Department of Justice.
Recommendation letters
Approval from the UniFR Faculty of Law Dean of Studies.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Paid legal practicum: CHF 2,200 monthly compensation with cantonal courts and public administration bodies
Application fee
Included in standard semester matriculation
Tuition / joining costs
Standard semester tuition applies
