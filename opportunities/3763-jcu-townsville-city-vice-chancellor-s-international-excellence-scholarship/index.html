JCU Townsville City Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship

High-value international merit scholarship awarded to outstanding students enrolling in Business, Law, or Governance programs at the Townsville City campus.

Academic degree / Prerequisites

English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each subband) or 7.0 for Law

Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Official academic transcripts, statement of motivation, and verified passport copy
Recommendation letters
2 letters of recommendation
