Doctoral Research Fellowship in Responsible AI and Human-Centered Digital Systems

Four-year salaried doctoral research fellowship evaluating ethical AI architectures, algorithmic accountability, and participatory civic tech platforms in metropolitan Amsterdam.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Computer Science, Human-Computer Interaction, or Cognitive Science with distinction.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 or native English fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Dutch university-partnered doctoral research.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and 2,500-word research proposal addressing algorithmic transparency or public sector AI governance.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters from HCI or computer science faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded Dutch NWO research appointment: €2,770 to €3,539 monthly salary with comprehensive benefits
Application fee
Zero application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% tuition-free doctoral position in partnership with Dutch research universities
