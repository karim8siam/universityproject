Australian Government RTP & Griffith University Postgraduate Research Scholarship (GUPRS)

Highly competitive flagship doctoral scholarship funding domestic and international scholars conducting impactful research across all disciplines.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class honours (H1) or research master's degree with equivalent distinction
English language proficiency
6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed 2,000-word research proposal aligned with Griffith research institute
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports confirming research aptitude

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
100% tuition fee waiver, $33,500 AUD/year tax-free stipend, and overseas health cover (OSHC)
Application fee
$0
Tuition / joining costs
100% covered by Australian Government RTP offset
