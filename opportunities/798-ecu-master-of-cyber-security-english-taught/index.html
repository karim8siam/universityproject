ECU Master of Cyber Security (English-Taught)

Two-year professional Master's degree accredited by the Australian Computer Society (ACS), delivering advanced training in cloud security, ethical hacking, digital forensics, and security governance at ECU's Joondalup campus.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized Bachelor's degree in computing, engineering, or related discipline with minimum 65% average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in speaking and writing.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated programming competence in Python, C++, or networking fundamentals.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.
