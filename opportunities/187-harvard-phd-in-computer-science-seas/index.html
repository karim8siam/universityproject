Harvard PhD in Computer Science (SEAS)

Five-year doctoral research program inside the John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS) in Allston, conducting frontier investigations in artificial intelligence, cryptography, systems, and quantum computer science.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering with top marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research accomplishments in theoretical computer science, AI, systems, or security.
Recommendation letters
Three confidential academic letters of recommendation evaluating research creativity.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Five-year guaranteed funding package including full tuition and USD $45,696 annual stipend via research and teaching fellowships
Application fee
USD $105 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition for funded doctoral candidates.
