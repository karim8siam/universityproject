Scholarship

Borsa di Studio 'BAT Territorio e Futuro' Unicusano

Tuition discount scholarship sponsored by local commerce associations for high school graduates residing in the BAT province.

University
Unicusano Trani (Università Niccolò Cusano - Polo di Trani)
Location
Trani, Barletta-Andria-Trani, Italy
Funding
€1,200 annual tuition reduction across the three-year bachelor's degree
Tuition
€2,200/year (subsidized from €3,400 standard)
Application Fee
Free application
Deadline
September 15, 2027
Requirements
Residency in Barletta, Andria, Trani or surrounding municipalities with high school diploma >= 80/100.
