Master of Engineering in Next-Generation Electric Vehicles and Solar Car Systems

World-leading engineering program where graduate students design, build, and test high-efficiency carbon-fiber solar race cars and commercial electric vehicle powertrains.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or Materials Science with 2.8+ GPA.
English language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N2 or English B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, CAD design portfolio, and research proposal on EV aerodynamics or motor efficiency.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.
