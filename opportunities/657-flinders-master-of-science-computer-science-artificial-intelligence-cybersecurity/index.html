Flinders Master of Science (Computer Science) - Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity

Advanced two-year coursework and research Master's degree delivered at the Tonsley Innovation District, covering machine learning, autonomous systems, and advanced network cybersecurity.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized Bachelor's degree in computing, engineering, or related discipline with minimum 65% average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.0 overall with minimum 6.0 in speaking and writing.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated programming competence in Python, C++, or Java.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.
