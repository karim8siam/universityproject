HWR Berlin Deutschlandstipendium National Merit Scholarship

Prestigious national scholarship awarded to exceptional domestic and international students enrolled in bachelor's and master's degree courses at HWR Berlin.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
German undergraduate grade of 1.7 or better (equivalent to top 10% class rank)
English language proficiency
English C1 or German C1
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Letter of motivation detailing social engagement and academic achievements
Recommendation letters
One letter of recommendation from a university professor

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
300 EUR monthly stipend (3,600 EUR per year) co-funded by the Federal Government and corporate sponsors
Application fee
0 EUR
Tuition / joining costs
0 EUR public university tuition
