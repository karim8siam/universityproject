Navitas ICM Academic Merit Scholarship

Tuition scholarship awarded each academic term to the two highest-achieving international students completing UTP Stage II with exceptional GPAs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled full-time in UTP Stage II with a minimum semester GPA of 3.8/4.0
English language proficiency
Direct-entry English satisfied upon admission
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated peer tutoring and active involvement in ICM student leadership
Recommendation letters
Faculty lecturer endorsement
