Toyota Technological Institute Graduate Research Fellowship

Prestige industry-endowed scholarship awarded to outstanding domestic and foreign engineering students admitted to the Master’s or PhD program at TTI Nagoya.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.5/4.0 GPA in previous undergraduate or graduate engineering/physics degree
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, detailed research plan (3 pages), and verified transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from university engineering professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: ¥150,000/month living stipend plus full tuition waiver (subsidized by Toyota endowment)
Application fee
Free (matriculation fee waived for scholarship recipients)
Tuition / joining costs
¥600,000/year (fully covered by institutional scholarship waiver)

Application deadline
January 15, 2027
