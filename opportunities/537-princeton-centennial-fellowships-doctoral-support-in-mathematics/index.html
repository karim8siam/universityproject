Princeton's legendary doctoral mathematics program, closely affiliated with the Institute for Advanced Study, producing Fields Medalists and leaders across all branches of modern mathematics.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Outstanding degree in Mathematics with exceptional mastery of advanced algebra, analysis, and topology.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 8.0 or TOEFL iBT 105.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE Mathematics Subject Test strongly recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Evidence of original mathematical thought and deep proof-writing ability.
Recommendation letters
Three confidential academic evaluations from mathematicians.
