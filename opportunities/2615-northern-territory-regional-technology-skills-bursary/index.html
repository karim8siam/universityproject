Northern Territory Regional Technology Skills Bursary

Tuition assistance bursary supporting international students undertaking high-demand telecommunications and network engineering qualifications in the Northern Territory.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completion of secondary education with strong mathematics and physics grades (minimum 65% average).
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 with no band below 5.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Australian technical vocational programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement explaining motivation to study telecommunications in Darwin and build a tech career in Australia.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter from secondary school instructor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Regional skills bursary: AUD $2,000 tuition fee reduction across the two-year advanced diploma
Application fee
AUD $250 enrollment application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $7,800/year standard diploma tuition; reduced to AUD $6,800/year with bursary

Dates and verification

Application deadline
20 January 2027 (Term 1 Regional Intake)
