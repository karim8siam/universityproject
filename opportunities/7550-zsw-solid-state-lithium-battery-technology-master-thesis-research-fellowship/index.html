ZSW Solid-State Lithium Battery Technology Master Thesis Research Fellowship

Six-month paid master thesis position at the ZSW Laboratory for Battery Technology in Ulm, developing sulfide-based solid electrolytes and scalable roll-to-roll electrode coating.
