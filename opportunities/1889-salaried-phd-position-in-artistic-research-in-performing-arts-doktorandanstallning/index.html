Salaried PhD Position in Artistic Research in Performing Arts (Doktorandanställning)

Four-year salaried doctoral research employment at SKH conducting practice-based research into choreographic composition, bodily dramaturgy, and somatic performance epistemology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Fine Arts degree (120 ECTS) in performing arts, dance, or circus
English language proficiency
English 6 proficiency (IELTS 6.5+)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive artistic research proposal and documented history of professional exhibitions/performances
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from senior artistic researchers

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried: SEK 33,000 to SEK 37,500 per month (approx. SEK 420,000/year) plus Swedish social benefits
Application fee
SEK 0 (tuition-free doctoral education in Sweden)
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 0 (salaried employee status with Swedish state benefits)
