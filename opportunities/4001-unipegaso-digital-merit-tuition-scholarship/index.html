UniPegaso Digital Merit Tuition Scholarship

Merit-based financial aid reduction awarded to outstanding applicants entering bachelor’s or master’s degree programs demonstrating high previous graduation marks.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school diploma score of at least 85/100 (or bachelor’s graduation score of 100/110 for master’s entry)
English language proficiency
B2 Italian or English proficiency certification depending on selected curriculum
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required

High school / bachelor degree certificates and certified transcripts
Recommendation letters
Not required
