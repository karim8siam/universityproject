American University Dean's Fellowships & Graduate Merit Awards

AU's top merit fellowship awarded to high-achieving incoming graduate students, providing full tuition coverage and competitive annual living stipends to support full-time research in Washington, D.C.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.5 cumulative GPA in previous undergraduate or graduate coursework.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General Test optional/recommended depending on graduate department.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence through writing sample or undergraduate thesis.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters submitted electronically via the online portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: 100% full tuition waiver plus an annual living stipend of USD $20,000 to $28,000 for doctoral and exceptional master's scholars
Application fee
USD $55 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full graduate tuition completely waived for fellowship recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Priority scholarship consideration deadline for Fall admission)
