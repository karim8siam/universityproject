Borsa di Ricerca 'Costa dei Trabocchi' in Monitoraggio Costiero e Rischi Idrogeologici

Twelve-month postgraduate research fellowship utilizing satellite SAR interferometry and drone photogrammetry to monitor coastal cliff erosion and landslide hazards along the Adriatic coast.
