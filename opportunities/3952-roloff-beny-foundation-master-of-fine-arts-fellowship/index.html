Roloff Beny Foundation Master of Fine Arts Fellowship

Prestige scholarship awarded to exceptional incoming MFA students focusing on photographic arts, printmaking, and lens-based media installations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree in fine arts, visual arts, or related humanities discipline with minimum 3.3 GPA
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5, or TOEFL iBT 94
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive creative portfolio of 20 high-resolution images/video links and artist statement
Recommendation letters
Three professional or academic letters of recommendation
