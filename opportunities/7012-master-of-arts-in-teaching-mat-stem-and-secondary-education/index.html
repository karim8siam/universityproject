Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) - STEM and Secondary Education

One-year accelerated master's granting New Jersey state teacher certification in mathematics, biology, chemistry, physics, social studies, or English.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with at least 30 credit hours in target teaching discipline (GPA 3.0+).
English language proficiency
English fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Praxis Core academic skills test or approved SAT/ACT waiver.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, statement of teaching philosophy, and resume.
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic letters of recommendation.
