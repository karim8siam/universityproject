SUPSI Institute of Mechanical Engineering and Materials Technology (MEMTi) Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research conducted within the MEMTi institute in Manno, developing patient-specific orthopedic implants, wear-resistant coatings, and computational finite element models of human joints.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, or Materials Science with superior standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis in biomechanical simulation, orthopedic implant design, or composite materials.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried Swiss doctoral contract: CHF 54,000/year gross under SNSF scales joint with ETH Zurich or EPFL
Application fee
CHF 150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges under university research employment.
