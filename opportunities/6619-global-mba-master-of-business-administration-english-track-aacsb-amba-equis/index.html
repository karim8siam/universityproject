Global MBA - Master of Business Administration (English Track, AACSB/AMBA/EQUIS)

Triple-accredited intensive executive MBA using 100% Harvard case study discussions, focusing on strategic innovation, Asian supply chain diplomacy, digital finance, and corporate sustainability.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA and at least 3 years of professional work experience.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT 600+ or GRE equivalent recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcripts, professional CV, two admissions essays, and interview.
Recommendation letters
Two professional reference letters from senior executives.
