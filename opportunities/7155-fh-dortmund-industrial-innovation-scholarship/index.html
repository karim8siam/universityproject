FH Dortmund Industrial Innovation Scholarship

Merit award honoring high-achieving undergraduate and graduate students in engineering, computer science, and design with distinguished academic records and social commitment.

German or English proficiency per program.

Not required.

Transcripts, scholarship motivation letter, and activities CV.
Recommendation letters
One faculty recommendation.
