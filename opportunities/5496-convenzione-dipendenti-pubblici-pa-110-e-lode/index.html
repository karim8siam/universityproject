Scholarship

Convenzione Dipendenti Pubblici 'PA 110 e Lode'

National Italian ministerial agreement offering public administration employees specialized degree tracks with 50% tuition reduction.

University
Unicusano Trani (Università Niccolò Cusano - Polo di Trani)
Location
Trani, Barletta-Andria-Trani, Italy
Funding
50% fixed tuition deduction across all bachelor's and master's degrees
Tuition
Subsidized ministerial tariff (€1,500/year)
Application Fee
None
Deadline
October 15, 2027
Requirements
Proof of active employment in Italian public administration or regional civic bodies.
