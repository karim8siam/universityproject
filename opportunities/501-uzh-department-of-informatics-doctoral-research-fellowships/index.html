UZH Department of Informatics Doctoral Research Fellowships

Fully salaried four-year doctoral research fellowship at UZH Department of Informatics conducting research in artificial intelligence, software engineering, visual analytics, or blockchain systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or Mathematics with top academic standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional but recommended for international degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated programming competence and Master's thesis in computer science.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded salaried appointment: CHF 52,000 to CHF 58,000/year gross under SNSF doctoral scales plus comprehensive Swiss health coverage and travel allowance
Application fee
CHF 100/500 application fee (credited upon enrollment)
Tuition / joining costs
Nominal CHF 720/semester tuition (covered by department fellowship).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Annual international graduate school application round)
