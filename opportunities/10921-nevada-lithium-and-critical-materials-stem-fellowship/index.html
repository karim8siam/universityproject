Nevada Lithium and Critical Materials STEM Fellowship

Nevada System of Higher Education · Reno, Nevada, United States

Target Discipline: chemistryApplication Deadline: March 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Inter-institutional fellowship across UNR, UNLV, and DRI investigating closed-loop lithium extraction, battery cathode recycling, and geothermal mineral extraction in Nevada.
