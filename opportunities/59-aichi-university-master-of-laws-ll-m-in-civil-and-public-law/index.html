Aichi University Master of Laws (LL.M.) in Civil and Public Law

Two-year Master of Laws degree program in the Graduate School of Law providing specialized advanced study in commercial law, civil liability, international economic law, and comparative constitutional systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Bachelor's degree in Law (LL.B.) or recognized 4-year undergraduate degree with legal or political coursework.
IELTS / Language requirements
JLPT N1 or N2 Japanese proficiency suitable for legal coursework and statutory interpretation.
GRE / Standardized tests
Not required.
Published paper / Research thesis
Research Plan (Kenkyu Keikakusho Form 3) specifying intended legal research theme and methodology.
Recommendation letters
Graduation certificate, university transcript, and statement of purpose.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for 30% international student tuition reduction, Aichi University Scholarship, and JASSO Honors awards
Application fee
¥30,000 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Approx. ¥770,000 per year (reduced to approx. ¥539,000 with automatic 30% international fee reduction).
