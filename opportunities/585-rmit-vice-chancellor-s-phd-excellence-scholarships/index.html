RMIT Vice-Chancellor's PhD Excellence Scholarships

RMIT's most prestigious doctoral research award, providing top living stipends, professional development allowances, and tuition waivers for high-achieving domestic and international researchers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1) or equivalent international GPA (minimum 3.8/4.0 or WAM 80%+).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research excellence (first-author publications, conference papers, or Honours/Master's thesis).
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Living stipend of AUD $37,000/year for 3.5 years plus AUD $5,000 annual research grant and 100% tuition fee waiver
Application fee
Waived for scholarship applicants
Tuition / joining costs
Full 100% international tuition waiver (worth AUD $46,000/year).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 31, 2027 (Annual international postgraduate research scholarship closing date)
