Premier English-taught M.Sc. degree covering advanced digital communications, embedded machine learning, secure IoT architectures, and network protocols.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
BSc in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or Information Technology with top 20% ranking
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE recommended (Quantitative 160+)
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed module syllabus comparing bachelor courses to TUHH prerequisites
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters
