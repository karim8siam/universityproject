Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Serious Games and Game Development (Sweden Game Arena Flagship)

Two-year master's curriculum connected to the Sweden Game Arena startup incubator, designing interactive simulation games, gamified rehabilitation systems, and educational virtual environments.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with major in informatics, computer science, game design, or media with solid grades
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 5.5 each section) or TOEFL 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated portfolio in game design, programming (C#/C++), or interactive multimedia systems
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Swedish Institute (SI) Scholarship (100% tuition + SEK 12,000/month stipend) + game incubator incubation access
Application fee
SEK 900
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 135,000/year (fully offset by SI scholarship)
