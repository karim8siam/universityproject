Doctor of Clinical Psychology (DClinPsy) NHS Funded Traineeship

Three-year full-time professional doctorate providing salaried NHS Band 6 trainee psychologist contracts while studying at Stratford.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
At least 2:1 honours degree in psychology conferring Graduate Basis for Chartered Membership (GBC) with BPS
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 7.0 in writing and speaking)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Clearing house application demonstrating 12+ months full-time clinical psychology assistant experience
Recommendation letters
One academic and one clinical psychologist reference

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full NHS Band 6 salary (approx. £35,392 per annum plus London weighting) and complete university tuition fee coverage
Application fee
Clearing House for Postgraduate Courses in Clinical Psychology fee applies
Tuition / joining costs
Fully funded by NHS Health Education England
