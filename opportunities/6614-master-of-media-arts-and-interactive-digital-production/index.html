Master of Media Arts and Interactive Digital Production

Two-year master's program investigating interactive spatial projection mapping, algorithmic sound synthesis, generative AI filmmaking, and experimental virtual reality exhibitions.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in media, fine arts, computer science, or architecture.
English language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N1 or English B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Digital media portfolio on USB/web, 1,500-word master's project thesis proposal, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.
