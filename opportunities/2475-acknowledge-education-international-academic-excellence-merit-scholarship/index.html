Acknowledge Education International Academic Excellence Merit Scholarship

Tuition reduction scholarship awarded to high-achieving international scholars enrolling in accredited bachelor's programs at Acknowledge Education campuses.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Equivalent of an Australian ATAR 75 or higher in prior upper-secondary education or equivalent post-secondary qualification.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no sub-score below 6.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Australian higher education undergraduate admission.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word statement explaining commitment to human services, community welfare, and academic excellence.
Recommendation letters
Two certified letters of reference from secondary school instructors or academic advisors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Partial tuition remission: 20% reduction across all three years of undergraduate degree study
Application fee
AUD $250 international admissions assessment fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $18,000/year base tuition; reduced to AUD $14,400/year with academic merit scholarship

30 January 2027 (Trimester 1 Intake)
