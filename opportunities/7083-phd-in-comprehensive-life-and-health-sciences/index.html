PhD in Comprehensive Life and Health Sciences

Three-year doctoral program investigating molecular pathophysiology, community gerontological care, physical therapy biomechanics, and radiological medicine.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Health Science, Medicine, Nursing, or Biological Sciences.
English language proficiency
English TOEFL 80+ or Japanese JLPT N1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis copy, CV, peer-reviewed publications, and detailed doctoral research proposal.
Recommendation letters
Two academic evaluation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
