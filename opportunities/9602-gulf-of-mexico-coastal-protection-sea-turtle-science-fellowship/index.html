Gulf of Mexico Coastal Protection & Sea Turtle Science Fellowship

Competitive research fellowship operating at the Sea Life Facility, researching Kemp's ridley sea turtle nesting kinematics, satellite telemetry tracking, and plastic ingestion pathology.
