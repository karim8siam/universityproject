ÉTS Master of Applied Science (M.Sc.A.) in Aerospace Engineering

Applied thesis master's degree focusing on avionics, composite materials aerodynamics, propulsion thermodynamics, and unmanned aerial systems design in state-of-the-art ÉTS flight simulation labs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Aerospace, or Electrical Engineering (min 3.0/4.3 GPA).
English language proficiency
French language proficiency (B2); technical English reading skills.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcript, CV, and three-page research statement.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from academic or industry supervisors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
