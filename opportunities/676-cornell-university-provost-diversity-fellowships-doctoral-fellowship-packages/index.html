Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.7/4.0 GPA (or equivalent first-class academic standing) in prior undergraduate and graduate studies.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 105.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional or field-dependent.
Thesis / Research statement
Exemplary academic trajectory, prior research papers, or distinguished undergraduate thesis.
Recommendation letters
Three detailed academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

100% Fully Funded: Annual living stipend of $45,000 to $48,000/year, full tuition coverage ($29,500/year), and Cornell SHIP health insurance for 5 years
Application fee
$105 application fee (waiver available)
Tuition / joining costs
100% covered by university doctoral fellowship awards.
