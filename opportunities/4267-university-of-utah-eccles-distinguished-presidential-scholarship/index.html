University of Utah Eccles Distinguished Presidential Scholarship

Utah's premier undergraduate scholarship covering full tuition, housing, and an experiential learning stipend for high-achieving leaders.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.9 unweighted high school GPA with challenging STEM curriculum
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 80 for international applicants
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Test-optional
Thesis / Research statement
Eccles leadership essay and documented public service involvement
Recommendation letters
Two letters from secondary school teachers

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition and mandatory fees plus on-campus housing, meal plan, and USD $3,000 research stipend for four years
Application fee
USD $55
