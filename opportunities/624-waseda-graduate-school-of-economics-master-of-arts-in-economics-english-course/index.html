Waseda Graduate School of Economics Master of Arts in Economics (English Course)

Two-year English-taught Master's program preparing students for doctoral research or international finance careers, with specializations in Microeconomic Theory, Macroeconomics, and Applied Econometrics.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Economics, Mathematics, or Social Sciences with strong quantitative marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General quantitative score strongly recommended.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose and analytical essay on economic theory or empirical econometrics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.
