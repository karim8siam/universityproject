Bourse d'Excellence du Réseau UQ pour Doctorants Internationaux

Prestigious province-wide merit scholarship awarded to outstanding international PhD candidates conducting collaborative research across two or more constituent campuses of the Université du Québec network.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree with minimum cumulative grade point average of 3.7/4.3.
English language proficiency
French proficiency B2/C1 (TFI or TEF); English proficiency valued.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Four-page thesis research proposal and verified academic curriculum vitae.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of academic recommendation from tenured professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $25,000 annual doctoral award plus differential tuition exemption
Application fee
CAD $110
Tuition / joining costs
Exempted via provincial differential waiver
