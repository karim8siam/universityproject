London Primary Teacher Training Bursary

Department for Education tax-free bursary awarded to eligible candidates training to teach in high-priority subjects in London schools.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK bachelor's degree (2:2 or higher) or recognized international equivalent
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all components)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing commitment to London primary school teaching
Recommendation letters
Two references (academic and professional)

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to £28,000 tax-free training bursary depending on degree class and subject
Application fee
£0 application fee (apply via DfE Apply)
Tuition / joining costs
£9,250 tuition fee loan eligible through Student Finance England

Application deadline
June 30, 2027
