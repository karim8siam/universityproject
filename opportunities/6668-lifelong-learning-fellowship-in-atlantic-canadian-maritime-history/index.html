Lifelong Learning Fellowship in Atlantic Canadian Maritime History

Research and lecture fellowship supporting independent scholars in documenting naval heritage, Mi'kmaq seafaring traditions, and Atlantic Canadian socioeconomic trade routes.

Curriculum vitae, research proposal (1,500 words), and sample published or archival work.

Two academic or institutional letters of reference.
