Aichi University International Student Tuition Fee Reduction

A university-wide financial assistance system providing an automatic 30% reduction on annual tuition fees for privately financed international students holding College Student visa status in Japan.

Official university financial aid source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Tuition reduction amount and coverage

Aichi University offers a formal tuition reduction system to ease financial burdens for self-supported international students. Eligible undergraduate and graduate students receive a 30% deduction from their annual tuition fees upon submitting the required documentation during admission procedures.

Reduction percentage
30% reduction applied directly to annual tuition fees.
Target study levels
Degree programs in Graduate Schools, Undergraduate Faculties, Law School, and Junior College.
Allowance limit condition
Applicants receiving less than ¥90,000 on average as a monthly allowance from their families (excluding tuition/entrance fees) are eligible for this reduction.
Status of residence requirement
Must hold or obtain the status of residence of "College Student" (留学) under Japanese immigration regulations.
Estimated graduate tuition after reduction
Standard graduate tuition of approximately ¥750,000/year is reduced to approximately ¥525,000/year.

Additional internal scholarship opportunities

Recipients of the tuition reduction can also apply for Aichi University internal merit awards:

Study Grant Program (Gakusyu Shoreikin)

¥200,000 per year merit award allocated to approximately 20 privately financed international students who achieve high GPA marks and credit completion.

General Scholarship (Ippankyufu)

¥120,000 per year awarded to selected students demonstrating academic excellence and verified financial hardship.

Supporters' Association Scholarship

¥100,000 per year awarded to 13 privately financed international students of exceptional caliber.

Application procedure and deadlines

Students must request the tuition fee reduction at the time of enrollment procedure submission. For spring intake students, visa confirmation procedures must be completed by April 1; otherwise, eligibility takes effect in the autumn term.
