Juris Doctor (J.D.) in Corporate Law and International Human Rights

Ranked among the nation’s top law schools, Fordham Law at Lincoln Center offers immersion in corporate restructuring, dispute resolution, public interest advocacy, and international commercial arbitration.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor degree from an accredited institution (median GPA 3.75)

IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL 100 for foreign applicants
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
LSAT or GRE accepted (median LSAT 167)
Thesis / Research statement
LSAC CAS registration, personal statement (2 pages), and optional diversity essay
Recommendation letters
Two to three letters of recommendation
