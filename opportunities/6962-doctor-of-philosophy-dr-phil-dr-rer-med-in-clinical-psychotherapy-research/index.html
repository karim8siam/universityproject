Doctor of Philosophy (Dr. phil. / Dr. rer. med.) in Clinical Psychotherapy Research

Supervised doctoral dissertation in empirical psychotherapy efficacy, digital interventions, trauma treatment, or palliative neuropsychological care.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Psychology, Medicine, or Health Sciences with minimum grade 'gut' (2.0 or higher).
English language proficiency
English C1 or German C1 depending on dissertation language.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Dissertation research proposal (10-15 pages), CV, master's thesis copy, and faculty mentor agreement.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of evaluation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Doctoral research fellowship with €1,450/month stipend and clinical trial workspace
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition for matriculated doctoral researchers; €180 semester admin fee
