Doctor of Occupational Therapy (OTD)

Three-year entry-level doctoral degree accredited by ACOTE, preparing practitioners in neuro-rehabilitation, pediatric developmental therapies, sensory processing, and assistive technology design.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA and 3.0 prerequisite science GPA.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement through OTCAS, verification of 40 observation hours, and resume.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation (one licensed occupational therapist required).
