Bristol Master of Science (MSc) in Climate Change Science and Policy

One-year interdisciplinary Master's degree at the Cabot Institute for the Environment bridging geophysical climate modeling, international environmental law, and net-zero policy design.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or international GPA 3.3/4.0) in earth sciences, environmental science, geography, or economics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all skills) or TOEFL iBT 95.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement demonstrating engagement with climate adaptation or carbon mitigation.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.
