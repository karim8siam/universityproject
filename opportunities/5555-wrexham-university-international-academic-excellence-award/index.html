Scholarship

Wrexham University International Academic Excellence Award

Automatic tuition reduction scholarship awarded to high-achieving international applicants admitted to full-time undergraduate and postgraduate courses.

University
Wrexham University
Location
Wrexham & St Asaph, Wales, UK
Funding
Up to £3,000 per year tuition fee deduction
Tuition
£9,000/year (Wales Home) / £9,250/year (RUK) / £15,500/year (International)
Application Fee
No separate application required
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
International student offer-holder with strong academic transcripts.
