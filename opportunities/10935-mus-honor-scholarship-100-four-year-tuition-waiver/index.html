MUS Honor Scholarship (100% Four-Year Tuition Waiver)

Montana University System · Helena, Montana, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: March 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Premier state merit scholarship awarded to the top 200 Montana high school graduates each year, conferring a 100% waiver of undergraduate tuition at any MUS institution.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
100% full tuition waiver renewable for up to four consecutive academic years
Tuition Rate
$0 (Tuition completely waived for honor recipients)
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
Rigorous high school college-prep curriculum with 3.8+ GPA or top 10% class rank
English Language
Satisfied via Montana high school graduation
Standardized Exam
ACT or SAT composite score ranking
Application Materials
MUS Honor Scholarship application and certified high school transcripts
Letters of Recommendation
High school principal or counselor verification

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult Montana University System's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
