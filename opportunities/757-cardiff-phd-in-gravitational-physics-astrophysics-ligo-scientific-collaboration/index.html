Cardiff PhD in Gravitational Physics & Astrophysics (LIGO Scientific Collaboration)

Doctoral research conducted within the Gravity Exploration Institute, developing numerical relativity waveforms, gravitational wave detection algorithms, and astrophysical interpretation for the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA network.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class or 2:1 Master's degree in Physics, Astronomy, or Applied Mathematics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated knowledge in general relativity, gravitational wave data analysis, or numerical relativity.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
STFC-funded 3.5-year PhD studentship: £19,237 annual stipend plus full tuition waiver and supercomputing access
Application fee
£0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Fully sponsored by STFC and Cardiff Gravity Exploration Institute.
