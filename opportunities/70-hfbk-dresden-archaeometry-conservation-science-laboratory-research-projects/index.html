HfBK Dresden Archaeometry & Conservation Science Laboratory Research Projects

Interdisciplinary research projects inside HfBK Dresden's Archaeometry Laboratory, applying advanced spectroscopic, chromatographic, and chemical imaging techniques to investigate historical binders, pigment degradation, and monument preservation.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science or Diplom in Chemistry, Conservation Science, Physics, or Materials Science.
Language proficiency
Fluency in German or English adequate for scientific laboratory reporting.
Standardized tests
Not required.
Portfolio / Internship
Laboratory experience in spectroscopic analysis (FTIR, Raman, XRF), chromatography, or materials degradation modeling.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, research publication list or master's thesis, and academic reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded research positions and project stipends (€1,500 to €2,400/month) supported by DFG and Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF)
Application fee
Free application directly to lab
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; salaried scientific researcher position.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 31, 2027 for collaborative heritage science projects
