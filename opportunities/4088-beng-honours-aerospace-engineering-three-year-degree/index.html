BEng (Honours) Aerospace Engineering (Three-Year Degree)

Three-year Royal Aeronautical Society (RAeS) accredited degree in Bristol’s aerospace corridor, mastering aerodynamic wind tunnel testing, jet propulsion, and composite structure design.
