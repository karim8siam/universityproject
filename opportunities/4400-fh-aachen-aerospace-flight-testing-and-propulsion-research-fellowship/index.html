FH Aachen Aerospace Flight Testing and Propulsion Research Fellowship

Two-year research fellowship at the Institute of Aerospace Technology evaluating electric aircraft propulsion and low-drag wing configurations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Bachelor or Master of Science in aerospace, mechanical, or electrical engineering with GPA 2.0 or better
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis summary and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) sample projects
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from aerospace or mechanics professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€2,100 monthly stipend (TV-L E13 75%) plus flight test instrumentation and conference budget
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition; statutory semester contribution of approx. €320 (including public transit ticket)
