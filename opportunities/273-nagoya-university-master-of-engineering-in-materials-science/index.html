Nagoya University Master of Engineering in Materials Science

Two-year Master of Engineering program inside Nagoya University's Graduate School of Engineering, offering world-renowned training in GaN wide-bandgap semiconductors, power electronics, and advanced alloys.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Engineering in Materials Science, Applied Physics, or Chemistry with solid semiconductor background.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework background in solid-state physics, crystallography, and electronic devices.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts, degree certificate, and personal statement.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
