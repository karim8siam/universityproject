CSU Flagship Doctoral Assistantship in Atmospheric Science & Climate Modeling

Fully funded Ph.D. research assistantship within the world-renowned Department of Atmospheric Science at Fort Collins, investigating aerosol-cloud interactions, satellite meteorology, and extreme storm dynamics.
