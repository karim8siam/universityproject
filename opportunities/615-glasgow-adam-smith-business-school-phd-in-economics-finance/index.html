Glasgow Adam Smith Business School PhD in Economics & Finance

Four-year doctoral program at the historic Adam Smith Business School investigating macroeconomic dynamics, financial econometrics, and behavioral asset pricing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree with Merit or Distinction in Economics, Finance, or Econometrics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all skills) or TOEFL iBT 95.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General quantitative score strongly recommended for international applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
Substantial research proposal (approx. 2,000 words) in macroeconomics, empirical finance, or trade.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual stipend of £21,237/year for 4 years plus full tuition fees and £1,500 annual conference allowance
Application fee
£0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% tuition fees covered.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 15, 2027 (Annual Adam Smith funding deadline)
