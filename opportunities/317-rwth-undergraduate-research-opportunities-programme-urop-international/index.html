RWTH Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP International)

Ten-week structured summer research internship program in RWTH Aachen laboratories, providing international undergraduate scholars with direct research project mentorship.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in good standing at a recognized university with cumulative GPA equivalent to 3.2/4.0+.
English language proficiency
Fluent English language skills.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of research motivation and CV.
Recommendation letters
Letter of recommendation from home university professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded summer internship: €2,000 living bursary plus comprehensive German language and cultural immersion program
Application fee
Free application via UROP portal
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition charges.
