NITech MEXT Special Graduate Engineering Fellowship

Prestige national scholarship awarded to exceptional prospective international graduate students entering master’s or doctoral degree programs in materials science, robotics, or electrical engineering at NITech.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.4/4.0 GPA in prior undergraduate or master’s engineering degree
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Research proposal aligned with NITech laboratory research, detailed CV, and official transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation from faculty deans or advisors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: ¥145,000/month stipend plus 100% full tuition exemption and roundtrip international flights to Nagoya
Application fee
Free (¥282,000 matriculation fee waived)
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800/year (100% covered by MEXT scholarship)
