Ontario Colleges Premier's Award for Applied Excellence Scholarship

Annual provincial academic scholarship recognizing outstanding prospective students entering accredited two-year or four-year diploma and applied degree programs across Ontario.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 80% cumulative average on senior secondary coursework.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 88 for international applicants.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcript, statement of academic goals, and documentation of community impact.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $5,000 merit award distributed towards full-time tuition across participating Ontario colleges
Application fee
CAD $125 application processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $4,200 (domestic) / CAD $16,800 (international) per academic year

Application deadline
April 30, 2027
