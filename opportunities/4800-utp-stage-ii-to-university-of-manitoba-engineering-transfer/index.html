UTP Stage II to University of Manitoba Engineering Transfer

Guaranteed pathway into Year 2 of Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, or Biosystems Engineering upon achieving the competitive AGPA threshold.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completion of 30 UTP Stage II credits with minimum 2.50 AGPA (higher for competitive engineering streams)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Completed Grade 12 Math, Chemistry, and Physics transcripts
Recommendation letters
Not required
