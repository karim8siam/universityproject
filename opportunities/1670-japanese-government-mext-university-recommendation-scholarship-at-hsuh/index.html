Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at HSUH

Prestigious national scholarship awarded to exceptional international scholars pursuing doctoral degrees in dentistry, pharmacy, or pharmaceutical biology at HSUH.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 2.3 out of 3.0 on Japanese MEXT evaluation scale
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL iBT 75+
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Formal biomedical research plan matched with an HSUH graduate laboratory
Recommendation letters
Dean's letter of recommendation from home university
