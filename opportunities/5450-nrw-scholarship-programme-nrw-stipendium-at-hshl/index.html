Scholarship

NRW Scholarship Programme (NRW-Stipendium) at HSHL

State and corporate funded scholarship rewarding high-achieving STEM students studying at HSHL.

University
Hamm-Lippstadt University of Applied Sciences (HSHL)
Location
Hamm & Lippstadt, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany
Funding
€300 monthly stipend (€3,600 per year) and corporate mentorship
Tuition
Tuition-free (€0 tuition; €310 semester fee including NRW transit pass)
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
July 31, 2027
Requirements
Enrolled HSHL student with above-average grades in engineering or natural sciences.
