Koyama Astronomical Observatory Graduate Research Fellowship

Two-year graduate fellowship conducting observational astrophysics and developing near-infrared spectrographs on the 1.3-meter Araki Telescope.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in physics, astronomy, or mathematics with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80 (or JLPT N2)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate thesis summary and four-page research plan in observational astronomy
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from astronomy or physics professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
