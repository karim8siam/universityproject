Adelaide University Master of Computer Science in AI and Machine Learning

Advanced two-year postgraduate degree delivering specialized training in artificial intelligence, deep learning, computer vision, and distributed systems, developed in collaboration with the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML).

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / CGPA
Bachelor degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or related field with minimum GPA 4.5/7.0.
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing 21).
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Demonstrated programming competence in Python, C++, or Java through completed coursework or degree project.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts, degree completion certificate, and detailed syllabus of computer science units.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Adelaide Global Citizens Scholarship (15%–30% fee reduction)
Application fee
AUD $150 international student application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $54,900 per year (indicative 2026–27 rate; 2-year full-time 48-unit coursework & project program)
