Master of Science in Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy (Approbation Track)

Four-semester polyvalent master's program conforming to the new German Psychotherapists Act, with clinical rotations at Trier University Outpatient Clinics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Academic degree / Prerequisites

English language proficiency
German language proficiency at level C2 or C1 with DSH-3
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required

Proof of completed clinical diagnostics and psychopathology modules
