Bocconi Master of Science (MSc) in Economic and Social Sciences

Rigorous two-year Master of Science preparing students for doctoral research or international institutions (IMF, World Bank, OECD) with deep mathematical modeling training.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with high GPA and substantial background in microeconomics, macroeconomics, and mathematics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 89.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT or GRE test score strongly recommended for international applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic dossier and curriculum vitae.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of evaluation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Bocconi Graduate Merit Awards provide 100% full-tuition waivers (€16,000/year value)
Application fee
€100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€16,000/year (offset by full or partial merit scholarships).
