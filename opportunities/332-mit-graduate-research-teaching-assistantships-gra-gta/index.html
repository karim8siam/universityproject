MIT Graduate Research & Teaching Assistantships (GRA / GTA)

Academic research and teaching appointments providing graduate scholars with full tuition remissions, medical insurance, and competitive salaries while instructing MIT undergraduate courses.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in good standing in an MIT Master's or doctoral program.
English language proficiency
Spoken English assessment for teaching assistants.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Subject mastery in relevant lecture or laboratory course.
Recommendation letters
Faculty course head endorsement.
