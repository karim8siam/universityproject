Cambridge MPhil in Advanced Computer Science (ACS)

Intensive 9-month taught and research master's degree preparing graduates for doctoral research, comprising advanced specialized modules and an individual 4-month research project under faculty mentorship.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class UK Honours degree in Computer Science or Mathematics with exceptional quantitative mastery.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall with minimum 7.0 in each section.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Extensive coursework background in compilers, algorithms, formal logic, and machine learning.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university faculty instructors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Cambridge Trust merit bursaries and international college awards
Application fee
£75 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
