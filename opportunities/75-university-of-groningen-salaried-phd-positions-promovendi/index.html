University of Groningen Salaried PhD Positions (Promovendi)

Four-year salaried doctoral research positions (Promovendi) at the University of Groningen governed by the Dutch Collective Labour Agreement (CAO-NU), conducting funded research within world-class institutes including Zernike, Stratingh, and Bernoulli.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Recognized Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, or related engineering discipline.
English language proficiency
Fluent spoken and written English (IELTS 6.5+ or master's study conducted in English).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Publications
Master's research thesis or peer-reviewed scientific publications.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, research statement, and two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Salaried employment contract under Dutch CAO-NU starting at €3,204/month (year 1) up to €4,051/month (year 4) plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% end-of-year bonus
Application fee
Free application directly to advertised vacancy
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Appointed doctoral candidates are university employees with full Dutch pension, healthcare, and social security benefits.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 (Rolling departmental intakes across science & engineering institutes)
