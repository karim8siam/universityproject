Cardiff Master of Science (MSc) in Advanced Computer Science with AI

British Computer Society (BCS) accredited one-year Master of Science offering specialized modules in deep learning, automated reasoning, computer vision, and distributed high-performance cloud computing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or equivalent GPA 3.3/4.0) in Computer Science with substantial programming.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-tests) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Strong academic record in algorithms, data structures, and software engineering.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Cardiff International Excellence Scholarships (£3,000 to £6,000 tuition fee reductions)
Application fee
£0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£27,450 full international tuition fee (before scholarship deductions).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
June 30, 2027 (International intake deadline for September 2027)
