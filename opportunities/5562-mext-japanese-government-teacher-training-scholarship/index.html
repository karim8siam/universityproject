Scholarship

MEXT Japanese Government Teacher Training Scholarship

Prestigious full scholarship funded by MEXT for international practicing teachers to conduct educational research at Miyakyo.

University
Miyagi University of Education
Location
Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture, Japan
Funding
Full tuition waiver, round-trip international airfare, plus monthly living allowance of ¥143,000
Tuition
100% exempted
Application Fee
Free application through Japanese Embassies
Deadline
February 15, 2027
Requirements
Under 35 years old, university graduate with at least 5 years active teaching experience in home country.
