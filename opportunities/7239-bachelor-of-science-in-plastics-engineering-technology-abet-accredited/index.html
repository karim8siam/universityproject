Bachelor of Science in Plastics Engineering Technology (ABET-Accredited)

One of only two ABET-accredited plastics engineering programs in the nation, providing hands-on training with million-dollar industrial injection molders, extrusion lines, polymer testing rheology, and CAD mold design.
