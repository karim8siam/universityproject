Master of Marine Biology and Ecology

Two-year premier postgraduate program offering intensive field coursework at JCU's Orpheus Island Research Station and the Great Barrier Reef World Heritage Area.

IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each band)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)

Academic transcripts and statement of marine field research experience
