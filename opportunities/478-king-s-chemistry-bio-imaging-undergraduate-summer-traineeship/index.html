King's Chemistry & Bio-imaging Undergraduate Summer Traineeship

Eight-week hands-on summer research studentship in central London labs investigating molecular imaging probes, radiopharmaceuticals, and chemical biology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Current 2nd or 3rd year undergraduate student in Chemistry, Pharmacy, or Biochemistry with 2:1 standing.
English language proficiency
Competent English communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Enthusiasm for experimental synthetic chemistry or medical radiochemistry.
Recommendation letters
One academic tutor reference.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Living bursary of £2,400 for an 8-week structured laboratory research internship
Application fee
No application fee
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition charges.
