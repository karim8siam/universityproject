University of Groningen Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence

Selective two-year Master of Science program (120 ECTS) at the Bernoulli Institute, exploring autonomous systems, deep learning, cognitive robotics, multi-agent systems, and human-machine interaction.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, or quantitative STEM discipline with strong academic marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each sub-score (or TOEFL iBT 90).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Publications
Documented coursework in machine learning, algorithms, linear algebra, calculus, and programming (Python/C++).
Recommendation letters
Academic transcripts, motivation letter, curriculum vitae, and online academic background questionnaire.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Eric Bleumink Fund, NL Scholarship, and Dutch state student finance
Application fee
€100 application fee for non-EEA applicants
Tuition / joining costs
€2,694/year (EU/EEA) or €24,900/year (non-EU/EEA international).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 1, 2027 (Non-EU applicant deadline via Studielink and OAS)
