Doshisha Graduate School of Science and Engineering English PhD in Intelligent Information Engineering

All-English doctoral degree conducted at the Kyotanabe Campus, featuring advanced research in autonomous robotics, intelligent signal processing, neural network computation, and human-computer symbiosis.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in engineering, computer science, or applied mathematics with high marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and published papers in reputable journals or international conferences.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential letters of recommendation from university faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Doshisha Graduate Scholarship & RA stipends providing ¥140,000/month plus up to 100% tuition reduction awards
Application fee
¥35,000 application screening fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥1,150,000/year, highly subsidized through merit-based tuition reduction grants.
