Assistant Professor (Tenure-Track) in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Tenure-track professorship teaching undergraduate and graduate computer science courses, establishing an independent AI research group, and supervising doctoral candidates.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Ph.D. in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or Computational Mathematics with international postdoctoral experience.
English language proficiency
Fluent teaching and research capability in English; willingness to learn French or German within two years.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss university professorship hiring.
Thesis / Research statement
Outstanding publication track record in top-tier AI venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR) and grant acquisition record.
Recommendation letters
Three reference letters from distinguished international computer science professors.
