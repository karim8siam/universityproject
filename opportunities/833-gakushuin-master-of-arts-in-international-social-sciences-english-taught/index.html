Gakushuin Master of Arts in International Social Sciences (English-Taught)

Two-year English-taught Master's program in Tokyo combining international economics, cross-border corporate management, international law, and political science for careers in multinational organizations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor's degree in economics, business, law, or political science with minimum 3.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic essay (2,000 words) on international economic policy, trade governance, or global business strategy.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Gakushuin University Merit Scholarships providing 30% to 100% tuition reduction awards

¥20,000 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
