Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) - Comparative Veterinary Medicine & Zoological Health

University of Tennessee · Knoxville, Tennessee, United States

Target Discipline: biologyApplication Deadline: September 15, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Comprehensive clinical veterinary doctorate featuring over 12 months of clinical rotations in the John and Ann Tickle Small Animal Hospital and Large Animal Hospital, avian and zoological medicine, and oncology.
