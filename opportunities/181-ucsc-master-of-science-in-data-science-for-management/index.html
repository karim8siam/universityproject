UCSC Master of Science in Data Science for Management

Two-year English-taught Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in Milan, uniting statistical machine learning, predictive analytics, deep data architectures, and strategic corporate decision-making.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Economics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or Engineering with minimum 75% equivalent GPA.
English language proficiency
English proficiency at CEFR B2 level (IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL iBT 80+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework background in calculus, statistics, programming, and microeconomics.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, transcript with course descriptions, and motivation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for UCSC International Scholarships (up to 30% tuition fee reduction)
Application fee
€100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€8,900 per year for non-EU students (reduced with merit scholarship); €3,500 to €8,900 based on income.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 (Priority intake round for non-EU applicants)
