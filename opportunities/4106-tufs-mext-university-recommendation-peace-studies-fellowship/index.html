TUFS MEXT University Recommendation Peace Studies Fellowship

Prestige Japanese Government scholarship awarded to top international students entering the Master’s Program in Peace and Conflict Studies (PCS) at TUFS.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class standing or minimum 3.3/4.0 GPA in previous undergraduate social science or humanities degree
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Three-page research proposal on peacebuilding or conflict resolution, CV, and university transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from senior academic professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full funding: ¥145,000 per month living stipend plus 100% full tuition waiver and roundtrip Tokyo airfare
Application fee
Free (¥282,000 matriculation fee waived)
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800/year (100% covered by MEXT scholarship)

Application deadline
January 15, 2027
