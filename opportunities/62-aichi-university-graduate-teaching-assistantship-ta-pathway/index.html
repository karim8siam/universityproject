Aichi University Graduate Teaching Assistantship (TA) Pathway

Departmental teaching assistantship opportunities for graduate students across Law, Economics, Letters, and Chinese Studies, assisting faculty with undergraduate seminars, language practicals, and examination logistics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Enrolled Master's or Doctoral student in good academic standing at Aichi University.
IELTS / Language requirements
Fluent communication skills in Japanese (and English or Chinese for language tutorials).
GRE / Standardized tests
Not required.
Published paper / Research thesis
Strong coursework record in the assigned academic field.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement from teaching course professor and graduate school dean.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried appointment: ¥1,200 to ¥1,600 per hour for undergraduate tutorial instruction, seminar assistance, and ICT lab support
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid student employment.

Application deadline
