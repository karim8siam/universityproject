UMass President's Public Service Fellowship

University of Massachusetts Office of the President · Boston, Massachusetts, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: March 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

System-wide fellowship providing stipends for undergraduate and graduate students working in full-time summer public service internships across Massachusetts municipalities.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Stipend of $6,000 for a 10-week summer public service placement
Tuition Rate
$0
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
3.0+ cumulative GPA at any UMass campus, sophomore standing or above
English Language
Satisfied via university enrollment
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Fellowship proposal, community organization endorsement letter, resume
Letters of Recommendation
Two letters of recommendation (one faculty, one community leader)

Institutional Guidance

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult University of Massachusetts Office of the President's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
