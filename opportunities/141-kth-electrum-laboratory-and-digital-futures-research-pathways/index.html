KTH Electrum Laboratory and Digital Futures Research Pathways

High-impact interdisciplinary research environment uniting KTH, Stockholm University, and RISE to investigate smart societies, cyber-physical control systems, and microelectronics at the Electrum cleanroom.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduate degree in Electrical Engineering, Applied Physics, or Computer Science with distinction.
English language proficiency
Technical English fluency for international collaboration.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Prior experience in semiconductor cleanroom fabrication, networked control, or IoT systems.
Recommendation letters
Academic curriculum vitae, publication list, and two referee contacts.
