Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) & UniSA President's Scholarships

Prestigious Australian Government and institutional scholarships for domestic and international students pursuing higher degrees by research (PhD and Master by Research) at UniSA.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours (H1) or Master's by Research with high distinction record
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Peer-reviewed journal publication or conference proceeding strongly valued
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee reports assessing research competence

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
AUD $34,210/year living stipend (tax-free) + 100% tuition fee offset + health cover (OSHC)
Application fee
AUD $0
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition fee waiver for 3.5 years (PhD) or 2 years (Research Master's)
