Kino Bay Center Marine & Coastal Research Fellowship

Marine field research fellowship stationed at Prescott College's marine laboratory in Sonora, Mexico, studying marine mammal acoustics, island seabird nesting, and sustainable artisanal fisheries in the Gulf of California.
