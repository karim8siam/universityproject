Maastricht European Borderlands and Cross-Border Governance Project

Empirical social science research initiative assessing cross-border labor mobility, emergency services integration, and environmental regulations between the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in FASoS master's or doctoral program with top-quartile grades in political science or law.
English language proficiency
Fluent academic English; working knowledge of Dutch, German, or French an asset.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Dutch university research.
Thesis / Research statement
Research concept note analyzing cross-border public health coordination in the Meuse-Rhine Euroregion.
Recommendation letters
Nomination from a FASoS professor in European politics.
