tUL Binational Transboundary Academic Excellence Scholarship

Cross-border scholarship granted by the Dutch and Flemish ministries supporting international students pursuing binational master's programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, or Biology (minimum 70% or 3.2/4.0 GPA).
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (with no sub-score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, academic transcripts, and 500-word statement on transboundary scientific collaboration.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€4,000 annual scholarship covering tuition differential and cross-border mobility allowance
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
€1,116 (standard EEA fee) / €4,500 - €8,900 (non-EEA fee)
