Berkeley Graduate Fellowships & Chancellor's Fellowships

UC Berkeley's most prestigious competitive graduate award, awarded across all engineering and science departments to support the world's most promising incoming doctoral researchers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation with highest academic distinction (typically top 5% of class, 3.85+ GPA).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0+ or TOEFL iBT 90+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Optional depending on department.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated scientific originality, publications, or exceptional technical leadership.
Recommendation letters
Three outstanding recommendation letters from research mentors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full tuition coverage plus annual living stipend of USD $42,000/year and comprehensive university health insurance for up to four years
Application fee
Standard graduate application fee ($140 international)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for duration of award.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Awarded automatically upon departmental graduate admission nomination)
