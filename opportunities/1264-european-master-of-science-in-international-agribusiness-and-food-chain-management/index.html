European Master of Science in International Agribusiness and Food Chain Management

Two-year master's curriculum conducted in Dronten and partner European agricultural universities, examining global grain commodities, dairy supply chain resilience, and carbon-neutral farm management.

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in agriculture, economics, agribusiness, or food science with solid academic record
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in all components) or TOEFL 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in agricultural economics, farm accounting, or supply chain logistics
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters
