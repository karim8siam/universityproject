Residency in Clinical Medical Physics & Radiation Therapy

University of Colorado Hospital · Aurora, Colorado, United States

Target Discipline: physicsApplication Deadline: December 15, 2026Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

CAMPEP-accredited clinical residency providing rigorous hospital-based clinical training in stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS), proton therapy dosimetry, and linear accelerator quality assurance.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Salaried hospital resident appointment at $74,000/year with comprehensive health benefits, retirement matching, and AAPM board examination fee support
Tuition Rate
$0 (Fully Salaried Residency)
Application Fee
$50

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
3.5+ graduate GPA in CAMPEP-accredited medical physics M.S. or Ph.D.
English Language
TOEFL 90 or IELTS 7.0 for international applicants
Standardized Exam
General GRE optional; ABR Part 1 completion preferred
Application Materials
Graduate transcripts, curriculum vitae, 750-word statement of clinical career goals
Letters of Recommendation
Three letters of recommendation from medical physicists or radiation oncologists

For official application submission procedures, portal logins, and faculty matching, consult University of Colorado Hospital's detailed profile or visit the department application portal.
