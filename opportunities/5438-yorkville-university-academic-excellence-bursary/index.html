Yorkville University Academic Excellence Bursary

Merit award dedicated to incoming undergraduate business and design students demonstrating exceptional pre-university academic distinction.

University
Yorkville University
Location
Toronto & Concord, Ontario / Vancouver, BC, Canada
Funding
$6,000 total scholarship ($1,500 credited across first four quarters)
Tuition
Offset against standard tuition fees
Application Fee
Automatic assessment with admissions application
Deadline
May 30, 2027
Requirements
Admission to an undergraduate program with entering GPA >= 3.5 on a 4.0 scale.
