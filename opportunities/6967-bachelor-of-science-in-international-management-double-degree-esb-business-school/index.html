Bachelor of Science in International Management Double Degree (ESB Business School)

Prestigious four-year international double degree with mandatory two-year study and internship residency at top partner business schools worldwide (France, UK, USA, Mexico, China).

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Excellent secondary school graduation marks (German Abitur equivalent 1.8 or better).
English language proficiency
English proficiency IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL iBT 95, plus B2 proficiency in target country language (French/Spanish/German).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; university oral admissions interview and assessment test mandatory.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, verified secondary school diploma, and motivation letter.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Erasmus+ mobility grants and Baden-Württemberg State Stipend up to €1,200/month during international link years
Application fee
€75 uni-assist application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€1,500/semester for non-EU students; €0 for EU/EEA students; €172 semester fee
