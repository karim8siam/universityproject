Academic Overview & Description

State-funded grant assisting eligible North Carolina residents attending any of the 36 NCICU member independent colleges and universities.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Annual state award up to $7,640 renewable for all four undergraduate years
Tuition Rate
Credited directly against member college tuition
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
Good academic standing at an NCICU member institution, North Carolina resident
English Language
Satisfied via residency
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Completed FAFSA application by state priority deadline
Letters of Recommendation
Automatic consideration
