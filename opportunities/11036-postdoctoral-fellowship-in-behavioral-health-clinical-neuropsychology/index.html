Postdoctoral Fellowship in Behavioral Health & Clinical Neuropsychology

University of Colorado Hospital · Aurora, Colorado, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: December 30, 2026Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

Two-year postdoctoral residency affiliated with the Department of Psychiatry focusing on neurocognitive assessment of traumatic brain injuries, neurodegenerative dementias, and neurosurgical evaluations.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Institutional annual stipend of $62,000/year with dental, health, vision benefits, and Colorado licensure support
Tuition Rate
$0 (Fully Funded)
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
Ph.D. or Psy.D. in clinical or counseling psychology from an APA-accredited program
English Language
Not required for US degrees
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Curriculum vitae, APPCN application form, 2 redacted clinical neuropsychological reports
Letters of Recommendation
Three letters of recommendation from clinical supervisors
