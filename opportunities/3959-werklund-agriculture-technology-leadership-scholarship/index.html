Werklund Agriculture Technology Leadership Scholarship

Prestige scholarship awarded to students entering precision agriculture or digital agriculture degree programs demonstrating leadership and technological innovation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 80% entering academic average or cumulative post-secondary GPA of 3.3/4.0
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no section below 6.0
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
750-word essay detailing vision for smart farm technology implementation and resume
Recommendation letters
Two letters from agronomy instructors or commercial farm operators

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $10,000 per year towards tuition and on-farm applied research costs
Application fee
CAD $100
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $7,200/year (domestic) or CAD $18,500/year (international)
