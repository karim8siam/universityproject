Master of Science in Forensic Science (FEPAC-Accredited)

Rigorous laboratory master's degree accredited by the Forensic Science Education Programs Accreditation Commission (FEPAC), covering forensic DNA typing, arson gas chromatography, ballistic mass spectrometry, and court testimony.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Biology, Forensic Science, or related physical science with GPA 3.0+.
English language proficiency
TOEFL 79 or IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE general test required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, criminal background check, CV, and statement of forensic career objectives.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters from science professors.
