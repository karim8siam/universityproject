Nurse Faculty Loan Program (NFLP) Endowed Fellowship

Indiana University School of Nursing · Indianapolis, Indiana, United States

Target Discipline: biologyApplication Deadline: March 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

HRSA-funded federal fellowship offering up to 85% student loan cancellation for master's and doctoral students who serve as nurse educators upon graduation.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Up to $35,500/year in educational loan forgiveness and tuition support
Tuition Rate
Covers tuition and fees directly
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
Enrolled in MSN, DNP, or PhD nursing education track with 3.2+ GPA
English Language
Satisfied via program enrollment
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
NFLP application and nurse faculty commitment agreement
Letters of Recommendation
Faculty academic recommendation
