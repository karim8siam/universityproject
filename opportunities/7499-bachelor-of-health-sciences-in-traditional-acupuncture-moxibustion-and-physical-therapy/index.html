Bachelor of Health Sciences in Traditional Acupuncture, Moxibustion and Physical Therapy

Four-year professional degree at the Kasuga Campus for visually impaired students, qualifying graduates for the National Acupuncturist, Moxibustionist, and Anma-Massage-Shiatsu Practitioner licenses.
