UBC PhD in Computer Science

Four to five-year doctoral research program in UBC's world-renowned Department of Computer Science, offering cutting-edge labs in artificial intelligence, computer graphics, systems, robotics, and human-computer interaction.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science or related quantitative field with minimum A- (80%) average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (6.5 min band) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE score recommended for international applicants.
Thesis / Research statement
High-quality Master's thesis or peer-reviewed publications in top computer science venues (NeurIPS, ICML, SIGGRAPH, OSDI).
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters commenting directly on research aptitude and technical creativity.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Guaranteed minimum funding package of CAD $28,000 per year through research assistantships (RA), GTA, and departmental awards
Application fee
CAD $168.25 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition fully covered by International Tuition Award and department research bursary.
