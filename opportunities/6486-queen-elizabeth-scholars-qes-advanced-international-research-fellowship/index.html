Queen Elizabeth Scholars (QES) Advanced International Research Fellowship

Prestigious national fellowship funding Canadian doctoral candidates and postdoctoral fellows conducting collaborative research across Commonwealth nations in global health, climate resilience, and sustainable development.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.7/4.0 GPA in an accredited Canadian doctoral or postdoctoral program.
English language proficiency
Fluent English or French proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Institutional nomination letter, 1,500-word international research project proposal, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Dean of Graduate Studies endorsement and two academic references.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $15,000 to CAD $30,000 stipend covering travel, accommodation, and research expenses abroad
Application fee
CAD $0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full fellowship subvention
