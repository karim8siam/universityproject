Bourse d'Excellence Collège O'Sullivan de Québec

Merit scholarship awarded to top applicants enrolling in technical computer science or digital animation collegiate programs.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Quebec High School Diploma (DES) or equivalent with minimum 80% average in mathematics and science
English language proficiency
French proficiency: DELF B2, TFI 750, or equivalent
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
500-word letter of motivation in French articulating career ambition in Quebec's technology sector
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of reference
