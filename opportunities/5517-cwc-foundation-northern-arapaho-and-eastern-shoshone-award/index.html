CWC Foundation Northern Arapaho and Eastern Shoshone Award

Dedicated institutional scholarship supporting enrolled tribal citizens of the Wind River Reservation pursuing undergraduate degrees at CWC.

University
Central Wyoming College
Location
Riverton & Jackson, Wyoming, USA
Funding
$2,000 per academic year
Tuition
Applied directly to tuition account
Application Fee
Free application
Deadline
July 15, 2027
Requirements
Enrolled member of Eastern Shoshone or Northern Arapaho Tribe, maintaining GPA >= 2.0.
