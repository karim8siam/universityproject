THUAS World Citizen Talent Scholarship & NL Scholarship (€5,000 Award)

Prestigious Dutch government and university-funded scholarship providing financial assistance to talented international students from outside the European Economic Area admitted to THUAS master's programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Admitted to a full-time Master's program at THUAS with top academic rank (minimum 75% or 3.3/4.0 GPA).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with no sub-score below 6.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated commitment to global citizenship, sustainability, or digital innovation.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or employer recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€5,000 financial contribution toward tuition fees and living expenses for non-EU/EEA master's students
Application fee
€100 application handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
Reduces annual international tuition to under €5,000/year.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 1, 2027 (Annual international scholarship round for September intake)
