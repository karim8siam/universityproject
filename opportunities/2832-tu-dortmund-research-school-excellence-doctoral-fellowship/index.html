TU Dortmund Research School Excellence Doctoral Fellowship

Prestigious doctoral fellowship supporting talented international researchers in machine learning, biochemical engineering, or mathematical statistics at the TU Dortmund Graduate Center.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
German GPA equivalent of 1.5 or better in Master of Science coursework
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE recommended (Quantitative 160+)
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis manuscript and one published conference or journal paper
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Full TV-L E13 salary (€52,000 - €57,000 gross per year with pension and health coverage)
