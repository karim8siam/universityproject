Cologne Graduate School in Management, Economics and Social Sciences (CGS) Fellowship

Prestigious doctoral scholarship providing three years of full financial backing in empirical economics, behavioral sociology, behavioral decision-making, and econometric policy evaluation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top 10% academic standing in master's degree in economics, sociology, or quantitative social science
English language proficiency
C1 English proficiency (IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 100)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE or GMAT strongly recommended
Thesis / Research statement
Sample research paper or master's thesis demonstrating quantitative empirical methods
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation
