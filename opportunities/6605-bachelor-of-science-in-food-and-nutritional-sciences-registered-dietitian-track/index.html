Bachelor of Science in Food and Nutritional Sciences (Registered Dietitian Track)

Four-year comprehensive curriculum training registered national dietitians (Kanri Eiyoshi) in clinical medical nutrition, biochemical metabolism, community health promotion, and food safety.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation diploma with strong biology and chemistry grades.
English language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score 220+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High school transcripts, graduation certificate, and personal motivation essay.
Recommendation letters
One high school principal or science teacher recommendation.
