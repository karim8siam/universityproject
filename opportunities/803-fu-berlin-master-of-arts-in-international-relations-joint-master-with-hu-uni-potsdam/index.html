FU Berlin Master of Arts in International Relations (Joint Master with HU & Uni Potsdam)

Premier joint English and German Master of Arts offered by the Otto Suhr Institute at FU Berlin together with Humboldt University and the University of Potsdam, analyzing global governance, conflict analysis, and European foreign policy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Political Science, International Relations, Law, or Economics with strong grades (minimum 75%).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with at least 6.5 in all bands or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in political theory, international law, and quantitative/qualitative empirical methods.
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
DAAD Master Studies Scholarships and Deutschlandstipendium (€300/month merit stipend); zero tuition
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €312 semester transit fee only.

Application deadline
May 15, 2027 (Non-EU international intake deadline)
