LJMU Vice-Chancellor's PhD Studentship in Observational Astrophysics

Full doctoral studentship at the world-renowned Astrophysics Research Institute (ARI) utilizing data from the Liverpool Telescope on La Palma to investigate supernovae, stellar evolution, and galactic transients.
