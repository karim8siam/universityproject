Stratingh & Zernike Molecular Science Research Placements

Frontier research projects inside the Stratingh Institute for Chemistry and Zernike Institute for Advanced Materials, collaborating directly with groups pioneering light-driven molecular motors, photopharmacology, and smart functional materials.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled graduate student in Chemistry, Physics, or Materials Science with strong laboratory background.
English language proficiency
Fluency in English suitable for international laboratory environments.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Publications
Demonstrated synthesis or characterization skills in organic chemistry, spectroscopy, or nanoscale physics.
Recommendation letters
Academic transcript, CV, and endorsement from research group leader.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded master thesis research grants and laboratory stipends supported by European Research Council (ERC) and NWO
Application fee
Free application directly to institute
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; funded laboratory placement.
