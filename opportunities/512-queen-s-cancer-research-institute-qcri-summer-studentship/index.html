Queen's Cancer Research Institute (QCRI) Summer Studentship

Four-month summer research opportunity at the Queen's Cancer Research Institute conducting laboratory investigations in cancer genomics, tumor immunology, and clinical trial analytics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate student currently enrolled in 2nd or 3rd year biology, life sciences, or biochemistry with minimum 3.3/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in molecular genetics or immunology.
Recommendation letters
One professor reference letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Summer research living stipend of CAD $6,500 for a 16-week mentored laboratory placement
Application fee
No application fee
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition charges; direct studentship.
