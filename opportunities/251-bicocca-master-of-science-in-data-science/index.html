Bicocca Master of Science in Data Science

Two-year English-taught Master of Science (120 ECTS) in the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication (DISCo), focusing on big data infrastructure, statistical learning, business intelligence, and deep neural networks.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or Engineering with minimum 75% equivalent GPA.
English language proficiency
English proficiency at CEFR B2 level (IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL iBT 80+).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework background in calculus, linear algebra, probability, and programming.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, detailed undergraduate transcript with course descriptions, and passport copy.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Lombardy regional DSU scholarships (full tuition waiver + up to €6,000/yr living grant)
Application fee
€30 pre-selection evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
€1,000 to €3,200 per year based on country of origin / ISEE (waived with DSU scholarship).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 (Pre-selection round for non-EU international applicants)
