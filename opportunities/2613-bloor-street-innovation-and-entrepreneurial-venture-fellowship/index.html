Bloor Street Innovation and Entrepreneurial Venture Fellowship

Venture fellowship providing seed capital and executive mentorship to entrepreneurial students scaling technology-enabled business ventures in Ontario.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Demonstrated academic excellence (GPA 3.5+) and proven startup enterprise initiative.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or equivalent native English education.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for entrepreneurial fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Ten-page commercial business plan for an innovative digital enterprise or sustainable business venture.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of endorsement from enterprise founders or business professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual fellowship grant of CAD $5,000 plus incubator desk space in downtown Toronto
Application fee
Zero fellowship fee
Tuition / joining costs
Direct tuition reduction on graduate or upper-division coursework
