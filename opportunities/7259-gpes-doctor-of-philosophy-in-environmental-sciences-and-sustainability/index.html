GPES Doctor of Philosophy in Environmental Sciences and Sustainability

Three-year doctoral dissertation program conducting advanced scientific and policy research in environmental sustainability, renewable systems, and socio-ecological resilience.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Environmental Science, Geoscience, Engineering, or relevant discipline.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0+ or TOEFL iBT 90+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis abstract, peer-reviewed publications (if any), 5-page doctoral research proposal, and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two confidential letters of recommendation from graduate advisors.
