McGill PhD in Computer Science

Four to five-year doctoral research program in the School of Computer Science at McConnell Engineering Building, offering frontier research in artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning, robotics, and bioinformatics in collaboration with Mila.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or closely related field with minimum CGPA 3.4/4.0.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (6.5 min band) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE recommended for international degree holders.
Thesis / Research statement
High-quality research thesis or published papers in top computer science venues (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, IROS).
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters evaluating scientific originality and research potential.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Guaranteed minimum funding package of CAD $25,000 per year plus full tuition waiver through Research Assistantships and Teaching Assistantships
Application fee
CAD $132.90 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Full tuition and fees paid by departmental graduate fellowship.
