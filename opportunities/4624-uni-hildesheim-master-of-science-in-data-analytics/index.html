Uni Hildesheim Master of Science in Data Analytics

Rigorous international English-taught master's covering advanced machine learning algorithms, deep neural architectures, and distributed streaming data systems.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in computer science, mathematics, or physics with above-average GPA
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each component) or TOEFL iBT 80
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and 750-word statement on machine learning project experiences
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters
