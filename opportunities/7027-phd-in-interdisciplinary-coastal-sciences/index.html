PhD in Interdisciplinary Coastal Sciences

Innovative doctoral program based across Greenville and the Outer Banks Coastal Studies Institute, uniting coastal geomorphology, marine ecology, storm hazard modeling, and coastal resource governance.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
B.S. or M.S. in Marine Science, Geosciences, Coastal Engineering, or Biology with GPA 3.2+.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 85 or IELTS 7.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE general test required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, 3-page research proposal, and master's thesis abstract.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation from research scientists.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
