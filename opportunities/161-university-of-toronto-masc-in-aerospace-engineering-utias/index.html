University of Toronto MASc in Aerospace Engineering (UTIAS)

Two-year research-intensive Master of Applied Science degree at the University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies (UTIAS), investigating autonomous flight, microsatellites, green aviation, and space robotics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Four-year Bachelor of Applied Science or Engineering with minimum mid-B (75%+) average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 93.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated competence in aerodynamics, flight dynamics, structures, or orbital mechanics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters and transcript of records.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Minimum guaranteed funding package of CAD $24,500 per year plus tuition allowance for two full years
Application fee
CAD $125 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Covered by department research assistantship and MASc funding package.

Application deadline
February 15, 2027 for September 2027 intake
