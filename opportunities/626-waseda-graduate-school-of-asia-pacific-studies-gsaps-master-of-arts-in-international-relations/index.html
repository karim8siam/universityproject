Waseda Graduate School of Asia-Pacific Studies (GSAPS) Master of Arts in International Relations

World-renowned two-year bilingual/English Master's program analyzing East Asian geopolitical diplomacy, transnational business networks, and international development cooperation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in international relations, political science, history, or economics with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Research proposal addressing Asia-Pacific regional integration, security, or development.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
