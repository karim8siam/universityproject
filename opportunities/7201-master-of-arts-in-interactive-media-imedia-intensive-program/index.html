Master of Arts in Interactive Media (iMedia Intensive Program)

Fast-track 10-month master's degree preparing UX/UI designers, front-end web developers, digital content strategists, and interactive visual storytellers with a fully funded global client field project.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum cumulative GPA 3.0.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 7.0 for international applicants.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Transcripts, resume, portfolio or creative samples, and 500-word statement of purpose.
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
School of Communications graduate fellowships covering up to 40% of tuition and funded fly-in international media projects
Application fee
$60 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
$39,500 for the full 10-month intensive program
