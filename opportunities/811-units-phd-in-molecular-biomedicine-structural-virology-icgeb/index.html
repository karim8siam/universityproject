UniTS PhD in Molecular Biomedicine & Structural Virology (ICGEB)

Three-year research doctorate conducted in partnership with the International Centre for Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology (ICGEB) in Trieste, deciphering molecular structures of emerging pathogens and cancer signaling cascades.
