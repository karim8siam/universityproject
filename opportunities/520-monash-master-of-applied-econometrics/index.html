Monash Master of Applied Econometrics

Two-year advanced program at Monash Business School covering high-dimensional financial econometrics, causal inference, time-series forecasting, and machine learning in macroeconomic policy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with a quantitative major (economics, mathematics, statistics) and minimum WAM 65%.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in multivariable calculus and introductory econometrics.
Recommendation letters
Academic transcript and resume.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Monash Business School Dean's International Postgraduate Scholarships (up to AUD $12,000)
Application fee
AUD $100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
AUD $53,000/year (before merit scholarship reductions).
