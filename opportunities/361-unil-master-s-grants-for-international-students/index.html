UNIL Master's Grants for International Students

Prestigious Swiss excellence grants awarded to ten outstanding international graduates each year to pursue Master's studies at the University of Lausanne.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation with distinguished academic results (equivalent to top 10% of class) from an accredited foreign university.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated academic excellence and compelling motivation letter.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from academic professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Living grant of CHF 1,600/month (CHF 19,200/year) for the complete normal duration of the Master's degree plus complete waiver of semester tuition fees
Application fee
CHF 200 administrative fee (refunded if grant is awarded)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived for grant recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
November 1, 2026 (Annual international grant competition deadline for Autumn 2027 entry)
