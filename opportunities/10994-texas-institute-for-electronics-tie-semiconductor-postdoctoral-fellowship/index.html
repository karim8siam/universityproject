Texas Institute for Electronics (TIE) Semiconductor Postdoctoral Fellowship

University of Texas System · Austin, Texas, United States

Target Discipline: engineeringApplication Deadline: February 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

State-supported semiconductor defense fellowship utilizing cleanroom fabrication facilities at UT Austin to research 3D heterogeneous packaging and microchip security.

English Language
Satisfied by terminal doctorate

Not required

Curriculum vitae, 3 high-impact microelectronics publications, 3-page research proposal

Three letters of recommendation from international semiconductor researchers
