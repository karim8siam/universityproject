Fellowship

PhD in Translational Medicine and Clinical Oncology Fellowship

Three-year Italian ministerial doctoral fellowship (Borsa di Dottorato MUR) conducting research in targeted cancer immunotherapy and precision biomarkers.

University
Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli - Facoltà di Medicina
Location
Naples & Caserta, Campania, Italy
Funding
Ministerial doctoral stipend €16,243 gross per year plus 10% research budget
Tuition
Tuition-free doctoral studies
Application Fee
Free competition entry
Deadline
June 15, 2027
Requirements
Second-cycle master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) in Medicine, Biology, or Biotechnology, and competitive oral examination.
