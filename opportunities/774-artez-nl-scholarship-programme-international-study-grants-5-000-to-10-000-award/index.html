ArtEZ NL Scholarship Programme & International Study Grants (€5,000 to €10,000 Award)

Prestigious Dutch government and university-funded scholarship providing financial assistance to talented international students from outside the EEA admitted to ArtEZ graduate programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Admitted to a full-time Master's program at ArtEZ with an outstanding creative portfolio and academic background.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with no sub-score below 6.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated critical commitment to artistic innovation, sustainability, or social engagement.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional artistic reference letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€5,000 to €10,000 financial contribution toward tuition fees and living expenses for non-EU/EEA master's students
Application fee
€100 application handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
Subsidizes annual international tuition down to under €4,000/year.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 1, 2027 (Annual international scholarship round for September intake)
