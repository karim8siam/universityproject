Albert Hirschman Postdoctoral Fellowship in Democratic Governance

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Albert Hirschman Centre on Democracy, supporting cutting-edge scholarship on contemporary challenges to constitutional democracy and civic freedoms.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Ph.D. in Political Science, International Relations, Sociology, or History completed within the last 4 years.
English language proficiency
Fluent academic English communication and presentation capability.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss postdoctoral fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Scholarly publication portfolio and 3,500-word proposal on democratic resilience, populism, or digital civic participation.
Recommendation letters
Three academic recommendation letters from international political science professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Postdoctoral salary of CHF 74,000 per annum plus research travel allowance and private office
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full postdoctoral appointment; no tuition assessed
