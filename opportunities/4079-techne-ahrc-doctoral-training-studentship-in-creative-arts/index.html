techne AHRC Doctoral Training Studentship in Creative Arts

Three-year fully funded doctoral research appointment across UAL faculties, supporting practice-based or historical PhD research in visual arts, speculative design, or fashion theory.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master’s degree with Merit or Distinction in fine art, design, visual culture, or creative technologies
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 overall with at least 6.5 in writing
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive PhD research proposal (1,500 words), digital portfolio, and writing sample
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of appraisal

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
