Aberystwyth University PhD and MPhil in Computer Science

Three-year Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) and one-year Master of Philosophy (MPhil) research degrees in the Department of Computer Science, with supervision across Intelligent Robotics, Vision & Graphics, Advanced Reasoning, and Bioinformatics.

Research clusters and active project themes

The Department of Computer Science at Aberystwyth University was one of the first computing departments in the UK. Research candidates work alongside academic staff in four specialized research groups:

Intelligent Robotics

Autonomous mobile robotics, robot navigation in unstructured environments, bio-inspired sensor networks, and multi-robot swarm coordination.

Vision, Graphics & Visualisation

3D point cloud processing, shape analysis and retrieval, facial modeling, medical image analysis, and augmented reality applications.

Advanced Reasoning

Fuzzy-rough hybrid reasoning, randomised search heuristics, dynamic feature selection for complex data, and theoretical computer science.

Bioinformatics & Computational Biology

Biomedical ontologies, translational bioinformatics, cancer genomics, plant phenomics, and systems biology modeling.

Entry requirements and application process

Degree requirement
An upper second class honours degree (UK 2:1) or international equivalent in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Mathematics, or a related discipline.
English proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with at least 6.0 in each sub-test, or equivalent approved test.
Research proposal
Required. Applicants must submit a structured proposal (approx. 1,500 words) defining background, research questions, methodology, and aligned departmental supervisors.
Application fee
£0. Direct application through the online Aberystwyth Postgraduate Admissions Portal is free.
Tuition fees
Full-time international research tuition is £22,870/year (Sciences rate for 2027/28); UK Home rate aligns with Research Council fee (£4,786).
Funding options
Applicants can be considered for AberDoc PhD Scholarships, Departmental Doctoral Overseas Scholarships, and UKRI doctoral training partnerships.
