UNIGE Master of Science in Astrophysics

World-leading two-year Master of Science program (120 ECTS) hosted at the Geneva Observatory in Sauverny, offering specialized training in Exoplanetary Science, Stellar Physics, Observational Cosmology, and Space Instrumentation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Physics or Mathematics with rigorous background in classical and quantum mechanics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Physics subject test recommended for non-European credentials.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, detailed descriptions of undergraduate coursework, and motivation letter.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters and complete university transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Master of Excellence Fellowships (CHF 15,000/yr) and Geneva Observatory assistantships
Application fee
CHF 65 application processing fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 500 per semester for Swiss and international students.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 28, 2027 for Autumn intake
