Melbourne Centre for Data Science (MCDS) Research Pathway

Interdisciplinary research collaboration hub linking the School of Mathematics and Statistics with the School of Computing and Information Systems, investigating statistical machine learning and high-dimensional data.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's or doctoral qualification in Statistics, Mathematics, or Computer Science with strong quantitative records.
English language proficiency
Technical English fluency for peer-reviewed academic publication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Published research in statistical theory, machine learning foundations, or data-driven sciences.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters and comprehensive publication list.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend

Application fee
Free application directly to Centre leadership
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; funded research appointment.
