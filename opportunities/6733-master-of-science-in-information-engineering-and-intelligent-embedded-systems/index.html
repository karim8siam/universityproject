Master of Science in Information Engineering and Intelligent Embedded Systems

English-taught international master's degree preparing engineers in advanced microcontroller design, autonomous systems, cyber-physical architectures, and edge computing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or Mechatronics with German grade 2.5 or better.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcripts, detailed syllabus of completed engineering mathematics, and letter of motivation.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free public university; €300/month Deutschlandstipendium merit scholarship available
Application fee
€75 uni-assist application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€0 tuition; €295 semester contribution including regional transit pass
