Flensburg Wind Engineering Graduate Research Fellowship

Two-year research fellowship at the Wind Energy Technology Institute (WETI) modeling offshore turbulence and floating wind turbine dynamics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Bachelor of Science in mechanical, electrical, or aerospace engineering with grade 2.0 or better
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research proposal focusing on aeroelastic or structural wind turbine modeling
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from university engineering professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€2,200 monthly research stipend (TV-L E13 75%) plus wind tunnel and offshore sensor testing access
Application fee
Free application

Zero tuition fees; statutory semester ticket approx. €280
