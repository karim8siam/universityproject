THUAS Master of Science (MSc) in Cyber Security Academy (English-Taught)

Two-year executive Master's program delivered in partnership with the Cyber Security Academy The Hague, covering critical infrastructure protection, digital forensics, threat modeling, and cyber governance.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or Electrical Engineering with solid programming foundations.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated coursework in cryptography, network protocols, or operating systems.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Dutch Cyber Security Academy industrial bursaries and tuition reduction grants
Application fee
€100 handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
€13,500/year for non-EU students; €2,601/year for EU students.
