King's College London Doctoral Studentships in Informatics & AI

King's flagship funded doctoral studentship scheme supporting groundbreaking research in machine learning, cybersecurity, autonomous systems, and medical imaging.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class UK Honours degree or Master's with Distinction in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated programming competence and research proposal aligned with Informatics faculty.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters submitted via King's Apply.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: UKRI London rate tax-free living stipend of £21,237/year for 3.5 years plus 100% full international tuition fee waiver and £1,500 annual research grant
Application fee
£0 for doctoral research studentship application
Tuition / joining costs
Full international tuition fees covered (£31,500/year value).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 22, 2027 (Annual Faculty PGR studentship competition deadline)
