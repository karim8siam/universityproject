Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (SISGP) at EHS

Prestigious Swedish government scholarship funding full tuition, healthcare insurance, and monthly living costs for future global leaders.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Demonstrated academic excellence in bachelor degree and at least 3,000 hours of documented work experience
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 5.5 in each section
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by Swedish universities
Thesis / Research statement
CV in official Europass format and leadership proof
Recommendation letters
Two letters of reference (at least one from an employer)

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
100% full tuition coverage plus SEK 12,000 monthly living stipend and travel grant
Application fee
SEK 900
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 120,000/year (fully covered by scholarship)
