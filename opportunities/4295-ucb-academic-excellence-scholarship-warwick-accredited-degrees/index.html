UCB Academic Excellence Scholarship (Warwick-Accredited Degrees)

Merit scholarship awarded to top-achieving domestic and international students enrolling in undergraduate or postgraduate degrees accredited by the University of Warwick.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
ABB at A-Level (or equivalent international secondary school marks with minimum 80%)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 with no subtest below 5.5 (or IELTS 6.5 for clinical programs)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
500-word personal statement detailing professional aspirations and industry focus
Recommendation letters
One academic reference letter

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
£2,500 tuition fee discount for the first year of full-time study
Application fee
£27.50 (UCAS) or £0 (direct postgraduate application)
Tuition / joining costs
£9,250/year (UK Home) or £14,000/year (International)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 25, 2027
