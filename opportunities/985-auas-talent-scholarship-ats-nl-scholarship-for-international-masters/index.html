AUAS Talent Scholarship (ATS) & NL Scholarship for International Masters

Merit-based scholarships awarded to talented non-EEA students enrolled in English-taught programs at Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High academic merit with average grade of at least 7.5 on Dutch 10-point scale or international equivalent
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Compelling motivation statement demonstrating commitment to applied urban innovation
Recommendation letters
One academic letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€5,000 one-off stipend (NL Scholarship) or up to €6,000 fee waiver (ATS)
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
Applied directly to reduce non-EU tuition balance
