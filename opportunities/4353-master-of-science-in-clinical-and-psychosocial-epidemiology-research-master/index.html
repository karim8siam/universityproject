Master of Science in Clinical and Psychosocial Epidemiology (Research Master)

Two-year elite research master utilizing multi-omics and longitudinal phenotypic data from 167,000 participants in the Lifelines biobank.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in medicine, biomedical sciences, psychology, or epidemiology with GPA > 7.5/10
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Letter of motivation and writing sample demonstrating quantitative research aptitude
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation
