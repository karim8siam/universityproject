DTG Padua Doctoral Research Scholarship in Precision Mechatronics (Vicenza Campus)

Three-year fully funded PhD studentship at the Department of Management and Engineering (DTG) in Vicenza investigating advanced sensor systems for industrial machinery.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Laurea Magistrale or Master of Science in Engineering with minimum 105/110
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or equivalent English certification
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Research proposal (up to 8 pages) on mechatronics control or smart manufacturing
Recommendation letters
Two letters from academic dissertation supervisors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€16,243 annual gross stipend plus 50% increase for overseas research and €1,600 annual research budget
Application fee
€30 (University of Padua doctoral competition fee)
Tuition / joining costs
€0 (full tuition waiver for scholarship winners)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027
