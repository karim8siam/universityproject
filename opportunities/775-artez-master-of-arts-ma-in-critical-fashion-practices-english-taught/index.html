ArtEZ Master of Arts (MA) in Critical Fashion Practices (English-Taught)

Two-year research-driven Master of Arts in Arnhem interrogating fashion as a cultural, political, and ecological system, exploring post-capitalist garment production and regenerative textile materials.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Fashion Design, Textile Design, Cultural Studies, or Visual Arts with high artistic merit.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Creative portfolio showing speculative garments, bio-textiles, or fashion essays.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from design educators.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
ArtEZ Excellence Grants of up to €4,000 plus materials and exhibition production stipends
Application fee
€100 handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
€11,200/year for non-EU students; €2,601/year for EU students.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 (Non-EU applicant deadline for September intake)
