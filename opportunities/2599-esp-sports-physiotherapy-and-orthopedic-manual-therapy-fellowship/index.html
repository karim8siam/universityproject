ESP Sports Physiotherapy and Orthopedic Manual Therapy Fellowship

Postgraduate fellowship training physiotherapists in advanced manual mobilization, exercise physiology, and neuromuscular re-education for high-performance athletes.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation from accredited BSc in Physiotherapy with minimum grade average 7.5/10 on the Dutch scale.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Dutch clinical fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Clinical case study analyzing return-to-sport protocols in elite professional athletes.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from sports medicine physicians or clinical directors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual fellowship grant of €4,500 and clinical mentorship with Olympic sports physiotherapists
Application fee
Zero fellowship fee
Tuition / joining costs
Direct fee remission applied to advanced specialization modules
