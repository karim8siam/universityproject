Higher Diploma in Community Urban Regeneration and Social Work

Three-year applied degree preparing social workers to lead neighborhood cohesion programs, youth diversion initiatives, and family support systems in diverse urban settings.

Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Certificate of good conduct (VOG), motivation letter, and interview.
Recommendation letters
One reference from a community organization or teacher.
