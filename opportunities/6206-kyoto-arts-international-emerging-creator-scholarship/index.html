Kyoto Arts International Emerging Creator Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship awarded to the most outstanding international applicants submitting exceptional creative portfolios in fine art, design, or manga.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Artistic portfolio consisting of 10 to 15 original artworks.
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or English B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Artist statement (800 words) detailing creative philosophy.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from art instructors or curators.
