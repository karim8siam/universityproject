PhD in Molecular Medicine & Macromolecular Complexes (Cluster of Excellence)

Interdisciplinary doctoral position in the Macromolecular Complexes Cluster, investigating membrane protein machines, cellular quality control, and ribosome assembly using state-of-the-art cryo-electron microscopy.
