TU/e Brainport High-Tech Academic & Industrial Internships

Industrial and academic graduation research internships embedding graduate engineers within the semiconductor and precision engineering facilities of ASML, Philips, NXP, and TNO.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled Master's student at TU/e with completion of first-year core modules.
English language proficiency
Working English communication.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Resume / CV and technical project portfolio.
Recommendation letters
Approval from TU/e master thesis supervisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried internship: €800 to €1,200 per month for 3-to-6-month graduation research internships directly embedded with ASML, Philips, NXP, or TNO
Application fee
Free enrollment for admitted students
Tuition / joining costs
Included in enrolled degree curriculum; monthly compensation paid by company/lab.
