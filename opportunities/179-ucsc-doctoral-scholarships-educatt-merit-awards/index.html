UCSC Doctoral Scholarships & Educatt Merit Awards

Three-year doctoral scholarships covering living stipends, international research travel supplements, and complete tuition waivers for top-ranking candidates in the annual doctoral competition.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Second-cycle university Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or international equivalent 120 ECTS) with high academic marks.
English language proficiency
CEFR B2 level English proficiency (certified or assessed during interview).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Original doctoral research proposal (3–5 pages) and Master's thesis.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters submitted online by university professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual scholarship of €16,243 gross per year for 3 years, plus a 50% increase for research conducted abroad and subsidized housing support through Educatt
Application fee
Free application for doctoral competition
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely waived for all admitted doctoral scholarship recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
June 15, 2027 (Annual national call for doctoral admissions - Ciclo XLIII)
