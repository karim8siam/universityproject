SSE Master of Science (MSc) in Finance (FT Ranked #1 in Nordics)

Two-year Master of Science program ranked #1 in Northern Europe, preparing students for investment banking, quantitative asset management, and corporate restructuring.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum 90 ECTS in business administration/economics and 30 ECTS in finance/mathematics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GMAT (min 600) or GRE General test score required.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement and detailed CV.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or professional recommendations.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
SSE Corporate Partner Scholarships covering 100% full tuition fees (SEK 360,000 value)
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU applicants
Tuition / joining costs
SEK 180,000/year for non-EU/EEA students (SEK 0 for EU/EEA citizens).
