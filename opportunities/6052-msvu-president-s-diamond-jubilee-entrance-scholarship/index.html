MSVU President's Diamond Jubilee Entrance Scholarship

Premier renewable merit scholarship awarded to entering undergraduate students demonstrating extraordinary academic distinction and community leadership.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation average of 95% or higher on university-preparatory coursework.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Leadership narrative essay and resume of extracurricular achievements.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of nomination from school principals or teachers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $36,000 total four-year scholarship (CAD $9,000 per year guaranteed renewal)
Application fee
CAD $75
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $9,200/year (domestic), CAD $21,500/year (international)
