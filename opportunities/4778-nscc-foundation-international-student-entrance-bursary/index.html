NSCC Foundation International Student Entrance Bursary

Merit- and need-based tuition fee reduction for newly admitted international polytechnic students pursuing technical diplomas at NSCC Ivany.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation certificate with minimum 70% average in mathematics and science
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall with no individual band below 5.5 (or TOEFL iBT 80)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
500-word personal statement describing community service and career goals in Nova Scotia
Recommendation letters
One academic or community letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $3,500 direct tuition reduction applied in the first academic year
Application fee
CAD $75 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $12,870 per academic year for international diploma programs
