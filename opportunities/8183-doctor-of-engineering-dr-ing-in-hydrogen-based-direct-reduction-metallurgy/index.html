Doctor of Engineering (Dr.-Ing.) in Hydrogen-Based Direct Reduction Metallurgy

Funded doctoral research based in the Ledebur-Bau pilot plant, investigating kinetic reaction mechanisms of hydrogen reduction in iron ore pellets to eliminate CO2 emissions.
