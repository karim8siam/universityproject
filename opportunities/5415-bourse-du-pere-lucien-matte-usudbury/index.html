Scholarship

Bourse du Père Lucien Matte USudbury

Endowed scholarship recognizing high academic merit and dedicated volunteer service to Franco-Ontarian or Northern communities.

University
Université de Sudbury / University of Sudbury
Location
Sudbury, Ontario, Canada
Funding
$4,000 per academic year
Tuition
$6,800/year (Domestic Ontario)
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
April 30, 2027
Requirements
Admission to an undergraduate program at USudbury, minimum GPA 3.3/4.0, and community letter of endorsement.
