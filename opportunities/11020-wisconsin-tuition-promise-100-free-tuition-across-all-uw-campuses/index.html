Wisconsin Tuition Promise (100% Free Tuition Across All UW Campuses)

The Board of Regents of the University of Wisconsin System · Madison, Wisconsin, United States

Target Discipline: social-sciencesApplication Deadline: February 1, 2027Official admissions notice ↗

Academic Overview & Description

System-wide guarantee enacted by the Board of Regents covering 100% of undergraduate tuition and fees for up to eight semesters for Wisconsin residents from households earning under $62,000.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
100% full tuition and mandatory campus fees covered after federal and state aid
Tuition Rate
$0 (Full tuition coverage for eligible students)
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
Wisconsin resident graduating from high school, household income <= $62,000
English Language
Satisfied via Wisconsin secondary completion
Standardized Exam
Not required
Application Materials
Completed FAFSA application by priority deadline
Letters of Recommendation
Automatic consideration
