Leeds Trinity Postgraduate Research and Teaching Fellowship

Prestigious university fellowship funding doctoral scholars while providing mentored teaching experience across undergraduate humanities, psychology, or business seminars.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-Class undergraduate degree and Master's degree with Distinction in a relevant academic area.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for UK doctoral fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Comprehensive doctoral research proposal and sample of written academic publications.
Recommendation letters
Three academic references verifying research competence and teaching potential.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Annual stipend of £16,500 plus full doctoral tuition fee waiver for three years
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% full tuition waiver covered by university fellowship
