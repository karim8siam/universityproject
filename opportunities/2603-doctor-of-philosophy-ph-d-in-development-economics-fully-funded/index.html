Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Development Economics (Fully Funded)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment conducting rigorous theoretical and empirical investigations in international trade, development finance, and global macroeconomics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Economics, Econometrics, or Quantitative Public Policy with top-decile honors.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 105.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE General Test mandatory (Quantitative score typically 164+ / 90th percentile).
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and 3,000-word doctoral research proposal in empirical microeconomics or macro-finance.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters from international economics professors.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full Swiss SNSF salary: CHF 49,500 to CHF 55,000 per annum with comprehensive social benefits
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition fees for funded doctoral assistants under Swiss cantonal agreements
