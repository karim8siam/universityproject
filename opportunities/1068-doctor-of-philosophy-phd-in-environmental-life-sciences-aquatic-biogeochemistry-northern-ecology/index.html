Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Environmental & Life Sciences (Aquatic Biogeochemistry & Northern Ecology)

Four-year doctoral candidacy in the Environmental & Life Sciences Graduate Program, investigating watershed nutrient dynamics, carbon cycling in boreal catchments, or Arctic freshwater biodiversity.
