Amsterdam Merit Scholarship (AMS) in Law, Economics and Regulation

Highly selective scholarship awarded to outstanding non-EU/EEA graduate scholars enrolling in Law and Economics master's programs at the University of Amsterdam.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top 10% of graduating undergraduate class (equivalent to Dutch grade 8.0/10 or 3.7/4.0 GPA)
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 (minimum 6.5 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL 100
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Strong quantitative percentile (above 85th percentile recommended)
Thesis / Research statement
Academic writing sample demonstrating rigorous legal or economic analysis
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from university professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€25,000 full tuition waiver plus living allowance stipend
Application fee
€100
Tuition / joining costs
€18,200/year (fully offset by scholarship)
