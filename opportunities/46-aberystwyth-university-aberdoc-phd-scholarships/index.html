Aberystwyth University AberDoc PhD Scholarships

Premier doctoral research scholarship at Aberystwyth University providing full tuition fees, a living stipend of £20,780 per annum for three years, and up to £1,000 yearly research travel support for outstanding UK and international PhD candidates.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
First-class Honours degree or high 2:1 (or international equivalent bachelor's/master's degree).
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or equivalent secure English test.
GRE
Not required by UK universities.
Published research paper
Comprehensive doctoral research proposal (minimum 1,000 to 1,500 words) aligned with departmental themes.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters, curriculum vitae, official degree transcripts, and AberDoc competition form.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full UK tuition waiver, President's international difference award, and maintenance allowance of £20,780/year + £1,000 travel fund
Application fee
Free application through Aberystwyth online postgraduate admission portal
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition covered for up to 3 years; additional £1,000 per annum travel and conference allowance provided.
