HfBK Dresden Degree in Conservation and Restoration of Cultural Heritage

Prestigious five-year integrated diploma and master's degree in art conservation, training conservators in specialized workshops for easel painting, polychrome sculpture, architectural murals, and archaeometric chemical analysis.

Official institution source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
General higher education entrance qualification (Abitur or certified international equivalent secondary school certificate).
Language proficiency
German language proficiency minimum B2/C1.
Standardized tests
Not required.
Portfolio / Internship
Documentation of a mandatory one-year pre-study conservation internship (Vorpraktikum) in a museum or conservation workshop.
Recommendation letters
Portfolio of drawing and restoration studies, CV, and passing the practical multi-day entrance examination in chemistry, drawing, and manual dexterity.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Tuition-free public German education; specialized studio equipment and analytical laboratory access provided
Application fee
Free application; €354.91 semester administrative contribution
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition for domestic and international students alike.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Annual portfolio and Vorpraktikum documentation deadline)
