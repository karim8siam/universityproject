UNIL Salaried Doctoral Assistantships (Assistant·e diplômé·e)

Four to five-year salaried doctoral research positions in life sciences, oncology, and geosciences, conducting original research alongside world-class Swiss laboratory investigators.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
University Master's degree in biological sciences, medicine, or biochemistry with high honors.
English language proficiency
Fluent English language skills.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis demonstrating experimental rigor.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee contacts and thesis advisor recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried Swiss university contract: CHF 52,000 to CHF 58,000/year gross salary with Swiss healthcare, pension, and paid leave
Application fee
Free application via UNIL recruitment portal
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges under salaried university appointment.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
March 31, 2027 (Departmental spring recruitment for doctoral laboratory assistants)
