KPUM International Doctoral Fellowship in Translational Oncology

Four-year doctoral medical science program exploring chimeric antigen receptor (CAR)-T cell immunotherapy and cancer neoantigen vaccine development.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation from a 6-year medical, dental, or veterinary school, or Master of Science with high GPA.
English language proficiency
English TOEFL 85 or IELTS 6.5.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Doctoral research plan and letter of acceptance from a KPUM department chair.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
¥180,000 monthly research stipend plus full doctoral tuition waiver and hospital bench fee subsidy
Application fee
¥30,000
Tuition / joining costs
¥0 for fellowship recipients (regular tuition ¥535,800/year)
