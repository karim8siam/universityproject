PhD in Sports Science & Physiological Biomechanics (Swedish Winter Sports Centre)

Doctoral research based at the Swedish Winter Sports Research Centre (NVC) in Östersund, monitoring elite athlete endurance physiology, high-altitude performance, and wearable biomechanical sensors.
