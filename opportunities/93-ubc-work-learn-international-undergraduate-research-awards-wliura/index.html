UBC Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Awards (WLIURA)

Competitive 16-week summer research internships providing international and domestic students with direct laboratory apprenticeships under UBC faculty mentors across biological sciences, environment, and engineering.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate or visiting research student in good academic standing (minimum 75% average).
English language proficiency
Fluent working English communication skills.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Short research proposal or motivation statement matching faculty lab focus.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement from sponsoring UBC faculty member and updated academic transcript.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: CAD $18.50 to CAD $24.00 per hour subsidized campus research appointments (up to CAD $6,500 per term)
Application fee
Free application through UBC CareersOnline portal
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; hourly salaried research placement.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 28, 2027 (Annual deadline for Summer research terms)
