Yale Master of Arts (MA) in International and Development Economics (IDE)

One-year intensive graduate program in the Department of Economics emphasizing quantitative economic analysis, econometrics, and international policy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with coursework in multivariable calculus, linear algebra, and intermediate economics.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Mandatory GRE General test score (quantitative 165+).
Thesis / Research statement
Statement of purpose addressing global economic development challenges.
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation.
