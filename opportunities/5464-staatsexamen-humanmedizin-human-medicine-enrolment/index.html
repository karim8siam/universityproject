Staatsexamen Humanmedizin (Human Medicine) Enrolment

Accredited six-year German medical degree program offering admission independent of high school NC grades based on candidate aptitude.

University
HMU Health and Medical University Erfurt
Location
Erfurt, Thuringia, Germany
Funding
CHANCEN eG income-contingent repayment plan and KfW student loan eligible
Tuition
€1,500/month during preclinical and clinical phases
Application Fee
Admissions assessment fee €250
Deadline
May 15, 2027
Requirements
Abitur or equivalent university entrance qualification, multi-stage entrance exam, and personal interview.
