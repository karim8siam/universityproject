Advanced Masterclass in Organoid Technology and CRISPR Gene Editing in Stem Cells

Two-week hands-on laboratory course at the Hubrecht Institute and UMC Utrecht mastering 3D Matrigel organoid culture, electroporation CRISPR-Cas9 knockouts, and confocal live-cell imaging.
