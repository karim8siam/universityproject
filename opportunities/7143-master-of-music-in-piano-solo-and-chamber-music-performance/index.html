Master of Music in Piano Solo and Chamber Music Performance

Two-year postgraduate soloist curriculum preparing concert pianists for international competitions, recital tours, piano duo repertoire, and collaborative concert performance.

Audition program list, video recording prescreening link, CV, and bachelor's diploma.

Two letters of recommendation from concert artists.
