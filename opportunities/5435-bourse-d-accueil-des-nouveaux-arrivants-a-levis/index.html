Scholarship

Bourse d'Accueil des Nouveaux Arrivants à Lévis

Integration bursary for international francophone students relocating to Lévis, co-sponsored by Ville de Lévis and UQAR.

University
UQAR Lévis campus (Université du Québec à Rimouski)
Location
Lévis, Québec, Canada
Funding
$3,000 in first-year tuition credits
Tuition
$18,500/year (International)
Application Fee
No application fee
Deadline
June 30, 2027
Requirements
International student holding a valid Québec CAQ and Canadian study permit.
