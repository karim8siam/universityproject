Diplom / Meisterschüler in Fine Arts (Painting, Sculpture, Installation, New Media)

Flagship five-year fine arts curriculum taught in dedicated professor-led master studios, culminating in the prestigious Meisterschüler title and annual university exhibition.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
University entrance qualification (Abitur or foreign equivalent; waived for exceptional artistic talent).
English language proficiency
German language proficiency level B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; submission of creative portfolio (20-30 original artworks) and in-person workshop exam.
Thesis / Research statement
Artistic portfolio, CV, and statement of creative intention.
Recommendation letters
One artistic recommendation letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
