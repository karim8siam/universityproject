MIT Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering

Pioneering thesis-based graduate degree providing foundational mastery and laboratory research in biomechanics, robotics, micro-nano manufacturing, and clean thermal fluid systems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in mechanical engineering or related physical sciences (GPA 3.8+ / 4.0).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE optional.
Thesis / Research statement
Engineering design portfolio or undergraduate thesis research.
Recommendation letters
Three academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded via Research Assistantships (RA) with full tuition coverage and USD $45,000/year living stipend
Application fee
USD $75 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition coverage through sponsored research assistantships.
