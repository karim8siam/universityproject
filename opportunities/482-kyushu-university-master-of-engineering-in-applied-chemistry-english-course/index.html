Kyushu University Master of Engineering in Applied Chemistry (English Course)

Two-year Master of Engineering program providing intensive training in molecular catalysis, polymer chemistry, battery materials, and organic electronics at Ito Campus.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, or Materials Science with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL iBT 79+.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate laboratory research thesis or capstone project in synthetic or physical chemistry.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend

¥30,000 examination fee
