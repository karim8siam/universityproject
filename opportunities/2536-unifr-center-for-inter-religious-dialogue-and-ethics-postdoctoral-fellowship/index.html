UniFR Center for Inter-Religious Dialogue and Ethics Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting interdisciplinary research into intercultural ethics, inter-religious cooperation, and democratic secularism at the Faculty of Theology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Ph.D. in Theology, Philosophy, Religious Studies, or Sociology completed within the last four years.
English language proficiency
Fluency in French or German and English (working knowledge of classical languages an asset).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Swiss humanities fellowships.
Thesis / Research statement
Scholarly publication record and 3,000-word research proposal addressing contemporary religious pluralism in Europe.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters from international humanities scholars.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Postdoctoral salary of CHF 72,000 per annum plus research travel fund and private office
Application fee
Zero fellowship application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full postdoctoral appointment; zero tuition fees
