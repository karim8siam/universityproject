Hanze Master of Science (MSc) in Data Science for Life Sciences (English-Taught)

Two-year applied Master's program bridging big data analytics, machine learning, and biological genomics, preparing graduates for biomedical data science roles in pharmaceutical and biotechnology sectors.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in bioinformatics, computer science, biomedical engineering, or life sciences.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Practical programming skills in Python or R and foundational genetics or biochemistry coursework.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Hanze Excellence Scholarships of up to €5,000 plus industry-partnered applied research stipends
Application fee
€100 handling fee
Tuition / joining costs
€9,200/year for non-EU students; €2,601/year for EU students.
