Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) Placements

Interdisciplinary artificial intelligence institute at Stanford bringing together computer scientists, neuroscientists, ethicists, and medical researchers to advance human-centered foundation models and AI safety.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduate student in Computer Science, Cognitive Science, or Engineering with top-tier standing.
English language proficiency
Technical English communication fluency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research contributions in foundation models, AI ethics, cognitive science, or neuro-symbolic AI.
Recommendation letters
Curriculum vitae, research statement, and two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
