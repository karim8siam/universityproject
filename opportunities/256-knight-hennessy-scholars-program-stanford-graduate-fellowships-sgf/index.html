Knight-Hennessy Scholars Program & Stanford Graduate Fellowships (SGF)

Stanford's premier multidisciplinary graduate fellowship, awarding up to 100 fully funded graduate scholarships annually to students across all seven Stanford schools to address global challenges.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Graduation with exceptional academic distinction (typically top 5% of class, 3.8+ GPA).
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE optional depending on graduate department.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated visionary leadership, independent thought, and civic commitment.
Recommendation letters
Three recommendation letters submitted through the online application system.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Full tuition coverage plus annual living stipend of USD $54,000/year, academic enrichment grants, and round-trip travel allowance for up to three years of graduate study
Application fee
Free scholarship application (separate from Stanford graduate degree application fee)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for duration of scholarship award.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Stanford Graduate Fellowship departmental nomination deadline; Knight-Hennessy Scholars annual awards)
