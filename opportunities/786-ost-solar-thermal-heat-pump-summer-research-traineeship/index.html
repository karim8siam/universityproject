OST Solar Thermal & Heat Pump Summer Research Traineeship

Eight-week summer research traineeship at the SPF Institute for Solar Technology in Rapperswil, performing acoustic and thermal testing on high-efficiency heat pumps and solar collector pilot test benches.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate or early Master's student in Mechanical Engineering, Energy Technology, or Physics with strong grades.
English language proficiency
Good English or German working proficiencies.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed coursework in heat transfer, thermodynamics, or fluid mechanics.
Recommendation letters
One referee letter from an engineering professor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CHF 1,800/month 8-week summer stipend plus Lake Zurich laboratory access
Application fee
CHF 0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges.
