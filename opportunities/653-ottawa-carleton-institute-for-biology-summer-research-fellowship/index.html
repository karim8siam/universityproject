Ottawa-Carleton Institute for Biology Summer Research Fellowship

Intensive 16-week mentored summer research traineeship investigating stress physiology, neurobiology, and ecosystem conservation in state-of-the-art laboratory facilities.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in third or fourth year of BSc in Biology, Biochemistry, or Environmental Science with minimum 80% average.
English language proficiency
Fluent working English proficiencies.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed laboratory coursework in cellular biology, molecular genetics, or ecology.
Recommendation letters
One academic referee letter from a faculty lab director.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $7,500 16-week summer research stipend with experimental laboratory access
Application fee
No fee for internal traineeship
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition charges during summer traineeship appointment.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 28, 2027 (Summer 2027 laboratory traineeship intake)
