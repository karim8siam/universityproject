Deutschlandstipendium Academic Excellence Grant at FAU

Federal merit scholarship honoring high academic performers who demonstrate social responsibility, overcoming structural barriers, or international leadership.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top 10-15% academic standing in prior academic record.
English language proficiency
German or English proficiency per degree program.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Enrollment verification, transcripts, CV, and documentation of extracurricular leadership.
Recommendation letters
One professor evaluation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
€300 per month (€3,600 annually) co-funded 50% by the federal government and 50% by Siemens, Schaeffler, or regional foundations
Application fee
No extra fee
Tuition / joining costs
Paid directly to student; tuition is free
