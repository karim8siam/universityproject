Dutch Government Teacher Tuition Subsidy (Tegemoetkoming Leraren) at IPABO

Government-funded grant specifically designated for aspiring and working teachers in the Netherlands to cover 100% of PABO tuition fees.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Admission to accredited PABO teacher education program at IPABO and Dutch/EEA citizenship
English language proficiency
Dutch language proficiency CEFR B2/C1 (Staatsexamen NT2 Programma II)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Proof of enrollment, DUO application form, and secondary diploma
Recommendation letters
Not required

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition fee grant of €2,530 per year paid directly by the Dutch Ministry of Education (DUO)
Application fee
€0
Tuition / joining costs
€2,530/year (Statutory Dutch/EEA tuition fee; covered by subsidy)
