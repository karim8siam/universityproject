Aberystwyth University MSc Data Science (Conversion Route)

Intensive 1-year conversion master's program designed for graduates of any academic discipline to transition into data science, artificial intelligence, big data analytics, and computational modeling.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
2:2 Bachelor's Honours degree (or international equivalent) in any discipline; STEM or non-STEM backgrounds accepted.
IELTS / English language
Academic IELTS 6.0 overall with minimum 5.5 in each component (reading, writing, speaking, listening).
GRE
Not required.
Published research paper
Curriculum vitae and motivation letter explaining interest in data analytics, machine learning, and programming.
Recommendation letters
One academic or professional referee letter and official undergraduate transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for International Excellence Scholarships (£2,000–£8,000 fee reduction)
Application fee
Free application directly via the university portal
Tuition / joining costs
£22,410 per year (International; eligible for Aberystwyth International Accommodation Award)

Dates and verification

Application deadline
July 31, 2027 for September 2027 intake; early application advised for international visa processing
