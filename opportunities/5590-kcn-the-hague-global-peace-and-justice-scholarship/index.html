Scholarship

KCN The Hague Global Peace and Justice Scholarship

Merit and dedication scholarship awarded to incoming international students from developing nations pursuing leadership in humanitarian peacebuilding.

University
King's College of the Netherlands
Location
The Hague, South Holland, Netherlands
Funding
€4,000 annual tuition fee reduction
Tuition
€7,500/year (EU/EEA) / €9,500/year (Non-EU)
Application Fee
No additional scholarship fee
Deadline
May 31, 2027
Requirements
Admission to BA in International Leadership, strong academic background, and essay on peacemaking in conflict zones.
