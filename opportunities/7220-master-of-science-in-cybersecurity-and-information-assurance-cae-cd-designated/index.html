Master of Science in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance (CAE-CD Designated)

Designated National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense degree based 10 minutes from Manhattan, focusing on network penetration testing, digital forensic analysis, operating system security, and cryptosystem architecture.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or IT with minimum GPA 3.0.
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.0.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE waived for GPA 3.0+.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate transcripts, technical resume, and 500-word statement of cybersecurity objectives.
Recommendation letters
Two professional or academic recommendations.
