FU Berlin PhD in Molecular Biology & Plant Biochemistry (Dahlem Centre of Plant Sciences)

Doctoral research conducted at the Dahlem Centre of Plant Sciences (DCPS), investigating hormonal signaling pathways, abiotic stress acclimation, and epigenetic gene regulation in plants under shifting climatic regimes.
