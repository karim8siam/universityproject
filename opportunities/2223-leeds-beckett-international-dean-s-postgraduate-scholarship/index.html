Leeds Beckett International Dean's Postgraduate Scholarship

Merit-based international tuition scholarship automatically awarded to high-achieving overseas graduates enrolling in full-time taught master's degree courses.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Equivalent of UK 2:1 honours degree (minimum 60% or 3.0/4.0 GPA)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing career vision in sport, computing, or engineering
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee contacts

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
£3,000 tuition fee discount automatically deducted from master's course fee
Application fee
£0
Tuition / joining costs
£16,000/year (reduced to £13,000 with scholarship)
