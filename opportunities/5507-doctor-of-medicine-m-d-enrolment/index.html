Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) Enrolment

Four-year medical degree curriculum delivering early clinical contact and rural hospital rotations throughout central and northern Michigan.

University
Central Michigan University
Location
Mount Pleasant, Michigan, USA
Funding
CMU Medicine Dean's Endowed Scholarships up to $30,000/year
Tuition
$44,200/year (In-State) / $63,500/year (Out-of-State)
Application Fee
AMCAS application fee + CMU secondary fee $100
Deadline
November 1, 2027
Requirements
Bachelor's degree with pre-med science prerequisites, competitive MCAT score, and AMCAS submission.
