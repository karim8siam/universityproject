Corso di Laurea Magistrale in Scienze e Tecnologie Alimentari (Food Science & Tech)

Two-year advanced scientific degree focusing on Mediterranean food processing, olive oil and wine biotechnology, food safety microbial genetics, and functional ingredient formulation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in food science, biology, chemistry, or agricultural sciences with minimum 180 ECTS.
English language proficiency
Italian B2 and English B1.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate degree certificate, transcript of records, curriculum vitae, and passport.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
ADISU Puglia regional scholarships providing up to €6,500 plus free canteen meals and housing
Application fee
€140 regional student tax
Tuition / joining costs
€1,850 maximum annual tuition (sliding scale based on ISEE indicator)
