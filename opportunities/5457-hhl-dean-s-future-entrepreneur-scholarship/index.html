Scholarship

HHL Dean's Future Entrepreneur Scholarship

Prestigious merit scholarship awarded to incoming MBA and M.Sc. candidates with an exceptional entrepreneurial track record or venture proposal.

University
HHL Leipzig Graduate School of Management
Location
Leipzig, Saxony, Germany
Funding
Up to €15,000 partial tuition reduction
Tuition
€28,500 total (M.Sc.) / €39,500 total (MBA)
Application Fee
No additional scholarship fee
Deadline
April 30, 2027
Requirements
Admission to HHL Master in Management or MBA, GMAT >= 650 or equivalent GRE, and pitch deck submission.
