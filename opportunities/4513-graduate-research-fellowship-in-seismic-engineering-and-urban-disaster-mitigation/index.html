Graduate Research Fellowship in Seismic Engineering and Urban Disaster Mitigation

Two-year master's research fellowship at the Disaster Prevention Research Institute studying earthquake ground motion simulation and structural dampers.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in civil engineering, architecture, or geophysics with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA
English language proficiency
JLPT N2 or IELTS 6.0 for international applicants
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Undergraduate thesis summary and four-page research plan on earthquake resistance
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from civil engineering professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
¥100,000 monthly research stipend plus full access to shake table testing facilities in Machida
Application fee
¥35,000 graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥900,000 per academic year (tuition waiver grants available)
