Kolleg Visiting Scholar Grant in Peatland Ecology and Wetland Carbon

Three to six month residential grant supporting leading wetlands scientists collaborating with the Greifswald Mire Centre on peatland rewetting, paludiculture, and greenhouse gas flux metrics.
