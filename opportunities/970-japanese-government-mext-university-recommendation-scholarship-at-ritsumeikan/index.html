Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at Ritsumeikan

Prestigious national scholarship awarded by the Japanese government (MEXT) upon university recommendation for outstanding international students pursuing Master's or PhD degrees in science and engineering.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum GPA 2.30/3.00 on MEXT calculation formula
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85+ (CEFR B2+)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Substantial research plan approved by host professor at Ritsumeikan
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation including one from previous Dean

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
¥145,000/month living stipend + 100% full tuition waiver + round-trip flights to Japan
Application fee
¥0
Tuition / joining costs
Fully waived for the standard duration of the degree
