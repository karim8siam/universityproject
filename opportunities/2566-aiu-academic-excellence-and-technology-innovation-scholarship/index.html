AIU Academic Excellence and Technology Innovation Scholarship

Institutional merit grant supporting incoming undergraduate and graduate scholars pursuing degrees in cybersecurity, software analysis, and information technology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Prior educational GPA of 3.2 or higher on a 4.0 scale from an accredited high school or university.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 71 minimum.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for AIU career-focused admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
500-word essay explaining how technological innovation impacts career progression and community service.
Recommendation letters
Two letters of professional or academic recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: up to USD $4,000 tuition grant awarded across undergraduate or graduate degree study
Application fee
USD $50 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
USD $14,500/year base undergraduate tuition; reduced with institutional grant
