Royal Academy Zurich International Leadership & Merit Scholarship

Merit-based international tuition reduction granted to exceptional candidates entering executive master's or doctoral research programs in Zurich.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2 on a 4.0 scale in prior undergraduate or graduate studies
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or equivalent English proof
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated professional achievements and leadership vision essay
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from senior corporate executives or professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Partial tuition fee discount covering up to 25% of total program tuition fees
Application fee
CHF 150
Tuition / joining costs
