Waterloo President's Graduate Scholarship & International Doctoral Student Award (IDSA)

Waterloo's flagship doctoral awards package providing guaranteed financial assistance and international fee remissions for outstanding students pursuing doctoral research across all faculties.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class academic standing (minimum 80% / A- average or equivalent) across prior degree coursework.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall with minimum 7.0 in writing and speaking or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for quantitative programs outside North America.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research potential through publications, conferences, or Bachelor/Master thesis.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters submitted electronically through the Waterloo portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual living stipend of CAD $20,000 to $28,500/year plus full international tuition fee differential offset for up to four years of doctoral study
Application fee
CAD $125 graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition differential covered for award recipients.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 1, 2027 (Departmental graduate awards nomination deadline for Fall entry)
