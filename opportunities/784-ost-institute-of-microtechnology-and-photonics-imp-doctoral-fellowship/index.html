OST Institute of Microtechnology and Photonics (IMP) Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research conducted within the Institute of Microtechnology and Photonics (IMP), developing ultra-short pulsed laser micromachining, nanometer-precision optical surface metrology, and fiber optic sensors.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Optical Engineering, Photonics, Microtechnology, or Physics with superior standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis in laser micro-machining, interferometry, or optical coating technologies.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried Swiss doctoral contract: CHF 54,000/year gross under SNSF scales joint with ETH Zurich or EPFL
Application fee
CHF 150 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges under university research employment.
