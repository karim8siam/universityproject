Internet of Things and People (IoTaP) Research Centre Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the IoTaP Research Centre, developing user-centered IoT architectures, cooperative smart city sensors, and privacy-preserving ubiquitous data systems.
