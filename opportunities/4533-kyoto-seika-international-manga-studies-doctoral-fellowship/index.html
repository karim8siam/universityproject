Kyoto Seika International Manga Studies Doctoral Fellowship

Three-year doctoral research fellowship at the International Manga Research Center investigating global graphic narrative theory and archival curation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in visual arts, literature, cultural studies, or art history with minimum 3.2 GPA
English language proficiency
JLPT N1 or IELTS 6.5 (dissertations may be submitted in Japanese or English)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and comprehensive doctoral research plan on manga aesthetics or cross-cultural reception
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters from professors of art, literature, or media studies

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition waiver plus ¥120,000 monthly living stipend and access to the Kyoto International Manga Museum archives
Application fee
¥35,000 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition covered
