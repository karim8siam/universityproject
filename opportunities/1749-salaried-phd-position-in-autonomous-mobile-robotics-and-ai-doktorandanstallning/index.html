Salaried PhD Position in Autonomous Mobile Robotics and AI (Doktorandanställning)

Four-year salaried doctoral research employment at the Center for Applied Autonomous Sensor Systems (AASS) developing semantic mapping and multi-robot coordination for industrial logistics.
