KTH Salaried Doctoral Student Employment (Doktorandanställning)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointments across KTH research divisions, combining cutting-edge scientific investigation with teaching duties under standard Swedish employment contracts and comprehensive health coverage.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related quantitative field.
English language proficiency
Swedish English 6 proficiency (IELTS 6.5+ or university-level English instruction verification).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's research thesis or peer-reviewed publication in software, control, or machine learning.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters, research statement, and complete transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Four-year salaried university employment under Swedish state collective agreements starting from SEK 32,500/month rising to SEK 38,000/month gross, with full Swedish social benefits and pension
Application fee
Free application directly to advertised vacancy
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Appointed PhD candidates are salaried Swedish university employees.

Application deadline
April 15, 2027 (Departmental vacancy intake; verify individual active job call)
