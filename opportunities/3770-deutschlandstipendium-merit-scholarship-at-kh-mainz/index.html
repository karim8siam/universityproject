Deutschlandstipendium Merit Scholarship at KH Mainz

National scholarship recognizing students who combine outstanding academic performance with active civic, church, or community volunteering.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled at KH Mainz with minimum grade 2.0 or better in German grading scale (or equivalent Abitur average)
English language proficiency
German language proficiency TestDaF 4 / DSH-2 / CEFR C1

Not required

Resume, transcript of records, and documentation of social engagement or volunteer work
Recommendation letters
1 letter of recommendation from an academic instructor or community organisation
