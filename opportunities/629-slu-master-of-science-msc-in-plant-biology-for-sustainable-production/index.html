SLU Master of Science (MSc) in Plant Biology for Sustainable Production

Two-year research-embedded Master's curriculum at the Uppsala BioCenter, preparing students for leading international PhD programs in plant molecular biology and crop breeding.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Biology, Agronomy, or Biotechnology with at least 90 ECTS in biological subjects.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 (no section below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Documented laboratory research experience in genetics or plant physiology.
Recommendation letters
Academic curriculum vitae and transcript.
