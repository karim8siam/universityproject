Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Computational Sciences and High-Performance Analytics

Interdisciplinary master's program training computational scientists in parallel numerical algorithms, petascale simulation, machine learning on supercomputers, and scientific visualization.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
B.Sc. in Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, or Computational Physics with minimum 75% equivalent mark
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated proficiency in C/C++, MPI/OpenMP parallel computing, and numerical linear algebra
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters
