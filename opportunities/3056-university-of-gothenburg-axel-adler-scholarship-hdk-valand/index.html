University of Gothenburg Axel Adler Scholarship (HDK-Valand)

Merit-based full tuition scholarship awarded to top-ranking non-EU/EEA applicants admitted to master's programs at HDK-Valand.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Top artistic portfolio evaluation and strong undergraduate academic performance
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 5.5 in each section) or TOEFL 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Outstanding artistic portfolio submitted through the University of Gothenburg portal
Recommendation letters
Two academic or artistic referee letters
