TU/e Master of Science in Computer Science and Engineering

Two-year Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, offering specializations in Algorithms, Software Science, Big Data, and Information Security.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science with solid grounding in discrete mathematics, algorithms, and software design.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae and course descriptions mapping completed modules to TU/e bachelor prerequisites.
Recommendation letters
Official university transcripts and degree certificates.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for ALSP scholarships, Holland Scholarships, and student tutoring positions
Application fee
€100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
€18,000 per year for non-EU students; €2,530 per year for EU/EEA students.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
May 1, 2027 (February 1 for ALSP scholarship applicants)
