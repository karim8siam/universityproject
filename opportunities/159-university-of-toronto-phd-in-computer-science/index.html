University of Toronto PhD in Computer Science

Five-year doctoral research program in U of T's globally celebrated Department of Computer Science (DCS), offering frontier research in machine learning, neural networks, computer systems, graphics, and quantum computing.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science or Mathematics with minimum A- (80%) average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (6.5 min band) or TOEFL iBT 93.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE scores recommended for international degree holders.
Thesis / Research statement
High-quality research thesis or published papers in top computer science venues (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, SIGGRAPH, SOSP).
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference letters evaluating scientific originality and research potential.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Guaranteed minimum funding package of CAD $29,000 per year plus full tuition waiver through Research Assistantships and Teaching Assistantships
Application fee
CAD $125 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition. Full tuition and fees paid by departmental graduate fellowship.
