CBS Best-Performer Scholarship for International Students

Merit-based tuition reduction covering up to 50% of tuition fees for outstanding international candidates admitted to English-taught degree programs.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Excellent previous academic grade point average (German grade equivalent 2.0 or better)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 83
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required; GMAT optional but advantageous
Thesis / Research statement
1,000-word essay on a global corporate sustainability or digital disruption challenge
Recommendation letters
One academic and one professional letter of recommendation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Up to 50% tuition reduction (saving approximately €6,000–€7,500 per year)
Application fee
€0 application fee for direct university evaluation
Tuition / joining costs
€14,880 to €16,680 per academic year for international students
