Basel Teaching Assistantships (Hilfsassistierende)

Departmental academic employment (Hilfsassistent) for enrolled undergraduate and graduate students at Basel, providing hourly compensation for conducting weekly tutorial exercise sessions and supervising lab sections.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled Basel student in good standing who has passed the course module with high grades.
English language proficiency
Proficiency in German or English depending on the course language.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
High academic marks in foundational mathematics, algorithms, or physics.
Recommendation letters
Approval from the course professor or head teaching assistant.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried campus employment: CHF 25.00 to CHF 29.00 per hour for coaching exercise sessions, running coding labs, and grading assignments
Application fee
Free application via Basel departmental portals
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; paid hourly university employment.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
August 15, 2027 for Autumn semester teaching allocations
