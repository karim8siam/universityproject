BA (Honours) Fashion Design (Central Saint Martins)

Three-year world-leading undergraduate degree in menswear, womenswear, knitwear, or print design, culminating in the globally watched Central Saint Martins graduate runway show.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Foundation Diploma in Art and Design plus A-levels or equivalent international qualifications
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 with minimum 5.5 in each skill
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Rigorous digital portfolio showcasing design development, mood boards, sketchbooks, and garments
Recommendation letters
One academic reference via UCAS
