Kyushu University Master of Arts in International Relations & East Asian Studies (LL.M./M.A.)

One to two-year English-taught Master's program analyzing transnational commercial law, diplomatic security in the Asia-Pacific, and global environmental governance.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Law, Political Science, Economics, or Humanities with minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Analytical writing sample or research proposal on East Asian economic integration or international law.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
