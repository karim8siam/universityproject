UAlberta Career Discovery Graduate Internship Fellowship

Competitive fellowship matching master's and doctoral scholars with governmental ministries, cleantech incubators, and corporate research hubs throughout Edmonton and Calgary.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.3/4.0 cumulative GPA in graduate coursework.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.0 for international graduate scholars.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic transcript, professional resume, and 500-word statement of intent.
Recommendation letters
Thesis supervisor written approval.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
CAD $12,000 stipend covering an eight-month industrial or public policy placement
Application fee
CAD $0 for active UAlberta students
Tuition / joining costs
Standard continuing graduate enrollment fees apply
