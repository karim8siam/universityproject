Basel Master of Science in Molecular Biology (Biozentrum)

World-class two-year Master of Science program (90–120 ECTS) inside the state-of-the-art Biozentrum, offering specialized training in Structural Biology, Cell Motility, Infection Biology, and Computational Biology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor of Science in Molecular Biology, Biochemistry, or Genetics with top academic standing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Curriculum vitae, detailed descriptions of undergraduate coursework, and motivation letter.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters and complete university transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Self-funded; eligible for Biozentrum fellowships and laboratory assistantships
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 720 per semester for Swiss and international students.

Application deadline
