Higher Vocational Diploma in Remedial Pedagogy and Special Educational Needs

Four-year accredited HBO diploma training educational specialists to diagnose learning disabilities (dyslexia, dyscalculia), design individualized learning plans, and support neurodivergent youth.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
HAVO, VWO, or MBO-4 diploma.
English language proficiency
Dutch language proficiency level B2 (NT2-II) or English B2.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Secondary diplomas, statement of pedagogical motivation, and certificate of good conduct (VOG).
Recommendation letters
One academic or professional reference.
