SUPSI IDSIA Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning Doctoral Fellowships

Doctoral research conducted within the world-renowned Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (IDSIA), investigating recurrent neural architectures, bio-inspired robotics, and autonomous drone navigation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, or Applied Mathematics with top marks (minimum 5.0/6.0 Swiss scale or 3.5/4.0 GPA).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated programming and research excellence in deep reinforcement learning, neural networks, or autonomous robotics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried Swiss doctoral contract: CHF 52,000 to CHF 62,000/year gross under SNSF scales joint with USI; minimal tuition
Application fee
CHF 150 international application fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 1,600/semester international tuition fee.
