Archbishop of Rupert's Land Theological Scholarship

Endowed scholarship supporting master's candidates preparing for ordained ministry and community pastoral leadership in Western Canada.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2/4.0 or B+ average.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Theological vocation statement (1,000 words) and pastoral background summary.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters and one diocesan bishop or parish rector endorsement.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full tuition waiver plus CAD $6,000 living bursary per academic year
Application fee
CAD $75
Tuition / joining costs
CAD $7,800/year (covered by diocesan endowment)
