JHU Applied Physics Laboratory (APL) Graduate Research Internship

Competitive 10-12 week paid research internship at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory in Laurel, MD, developing deep space mission instrumentation, cyber operations, and quantum sensing.
