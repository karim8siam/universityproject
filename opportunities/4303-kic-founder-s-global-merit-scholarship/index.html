KIC Founder's Global Merit Scholarship

Tuition reduction scholarship awarded by KIC to privately funded international graduate students admitted to the Global Innovator Master's Course.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum 3.0/4.0 undergraduate GPA
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 75
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement outlining social challenge to be addressed using ICT
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from former university professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
30% to 50% tuition reduction worth up to ¥750,000 per academic year

¥10,000 (international document screening fee)
