Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT)

Post-baccalaureate clinical doctorate accredited by CAPTE, focusing on neuromusculoskeletal rehabilitation, cardiopulmonary exercise physiology, clinical anatomy dissection, and 36 weeks of full-time clinical internships.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Undergraduate degree with minimum 3.0 cumulative and prerequisite science GPA

IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE required (verbal, quantitative, and analytical writing)
Thesis / Research statement
PTCAS personal essay and 50+ hours of verified physical therapy clinical observation
Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation (including one licensed physical therapist)
