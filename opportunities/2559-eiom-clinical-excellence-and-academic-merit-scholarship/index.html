EIOM Clinical Excellence and Academic Merit Scholarship

Merit-based financial bursary supporting high-achieving healthcare students matriculating into full-time osteopathic medicine training at EIOM Padua.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school graduation diploma with minimum score of 85/100, or healthcare degree with minimum 102/110.
English language proficiency
Italian B2/C1 proficiency; scientific English reading proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Standardized testing not required for Italian clinical osteopathy programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement detailing commitment to evidence-based healthcare and osteopathic manual therapy.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or clinical letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: €1,500 annual tuition reduction awarded to top-ranking entrance candidates
Application fee
€100 admissions evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
€6,200/year base tuition; reduced to €4,700/year with clinical merit scholarship
