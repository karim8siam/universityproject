Vice-Chancellor's Excellence Scholarship for Postgraduate Studies

Competitive merit scholarship providing 50% tuition reductions to high-calibre international scholars entering postgraduate taught master's degree programs at Newcastle University.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class UK bachelor's degree equivalent (GPA 3.7+ or 75%+)
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement demonstrating academic excellence, leadership, and community impact
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
50% tuition fee reduction for the duration of the master's degree
Application fee
GBP 0
