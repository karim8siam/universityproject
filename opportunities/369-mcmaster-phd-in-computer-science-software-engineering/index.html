McMaster PhD in Computer Science & Software Engineering

Four-year research-intensive doctoral program exploring artificial intelligence, algorithm design, formal methods, safety-critical software, and bioinformatics.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or Mathematics with at least a B+ (78%) average.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (6.5 in each component) or TOEFL iBT 92.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
General GRE optional but encouraged.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or peer-reviewed conference publications in computing.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Guaranteed minimum funding package of CAD $26,000/year for 4 years through research assistantships, teaching assistantships, and scholarships
Application fee
CAD $110 graduate application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Subsidized through departmental base funding allocations.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 31, 2027 (Fall admission cycle deadline for international applicants)
