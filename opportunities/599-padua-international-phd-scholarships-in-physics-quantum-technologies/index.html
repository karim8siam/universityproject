Padua International PhD Scholarships in Physics & Quantum Technologies

Prestigious doctoral scholarships in the Department of Physics and Astronomy 'Galileo Galilei' exploring quantum communication networks, satellite quantum cryptography, and dark matter detection.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master of Science in Physics, Astronomy, or Applied Mathematics with high academic honors.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis in quantum optics, condensed matter, or particle physics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Annual tax-free stipend of €16,290/year for 3 years plus 50% allowance increase for overseas research, €1,600/year travel grant, and 100% tuition waiver
Application fee
€30 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Full 100% tuition fee waiver for scholarship recipients.
