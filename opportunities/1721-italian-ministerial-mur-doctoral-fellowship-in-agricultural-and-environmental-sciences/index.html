Italian Ministerial (MUR) Doctoral Fellowship in Agricultural and Environmental Sciences

Three-year PhD scholarship funded by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) investigating drought-resilient crops, olive grove microbiome ecology, and soil organic carbon.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Laurea Magistrale or second-cycle master's degree in agronomy, biology, or biotechnology
English language proficiency
English proficiency at B2 level or higher
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed 3,500-word doctoral research project on sustainable Mediterranean agroecosystems
Recommendation letters
CV, list of scientific publications, and two referee letters
