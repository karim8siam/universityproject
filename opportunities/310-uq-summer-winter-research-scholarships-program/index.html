UQ Summer & Winter Research Scholarships Program

Intensive 6 to 10-week laboratory immersion program allowing undergraduate and master's scholars to work alongside leading UQ professors on frontier scientific projects.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate student with cumulative GPA 5.5/7.0 or higher.
English language proficiency
Equivalent to standard UQ English language requirement.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Academic transcript and project preference selection.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement by hosting UQ faculty researcher.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Funded bursary: AUD $1,500 to $3,000 research stipend for 6 to 10 weeks of supervised laboratory research
Application fee
Free application for eligible university scholars
Tuition / joining costs
No tuition charges for internship research program.
