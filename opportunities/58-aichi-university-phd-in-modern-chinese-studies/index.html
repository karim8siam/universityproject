Aichi University PhD in Modern Chinese Studies

Doctoral research program (Hakushi-koki-katei) within Japan's pioneering Graduate School of Modern Chinese Studies, affiliated with the Institute for Contemporary Chinese Studies (ICCS), offering deep multidisciplinary investigation into Chinese politics, economy, and society.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect the published university and program information available at the last review. Where a requirement is not specified, that is not an exemption.

GPA / Academic degree
Completed Master's degree in Asian Studies, International Relations, Economics, or related social sciences.
IELTS / Language requirements
JLPT N1 or proven academic Japanese proficiency; reading comprehension in modern Chinese or English depending on thesis topic.
GRE / Standardized tests
Not required.
Published paper / Research thesis
Master's degree thesis (or summary of completed academic papers) and comprehensive doctoral research plan (Form 3).
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters, graduation certificate, and official academic transcripts.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Eligible for Aichi University Doctoral Research Fellowships, ICCS Research Assistantships (RA), and Monbukagakusho (MEXT) Honors Scholarships
Application fee
¥30,000 application examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
Standard tuition approx. ¥720,000 per year (reduced by 30% to approx. ¥504,000 for eligible international students).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 20, 2027 (Phase 2 winter selection for April 2027 enrollment)
