Carlo Bo Merit Scholarship for Multilingual Interpreting Talents

Merit-based tuition scholarship awarded to the top-scoring applicants on the national entrance examination for the three-year Degree in Linguistic Mediation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
High school diploma with final mark of at least 85/100 (or international equivalent)
English language proficiency
Fluent Italian (C1) and B2/C1 in first chosen foreign language (English, French, Spanish, or German)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Passing grade on the Carlo Bo language aptitude and general knowledge admission test
Recommendation letters
One high school language teacher recommendation letter

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
50% tuition reduction worth up to €3,400 per academic year
Application fee
€100 (SSML Carlo Bo admission exam fee)
Tuition / joining costs
€6,800/year (standard tuition for degree program)
