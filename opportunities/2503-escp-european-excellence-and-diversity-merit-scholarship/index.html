ESCP European Excellence and Diversity Merit Scholarship

Merit-based financial award supporting high-potential international candidates matriculating into the flagship Master in Management program at ESCP London.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-Class Honours (1st) or Upper Second-Class Honours (2:1) in undergraduate degree.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 with at least 6.5 in all components (or TOEFL iBT 100).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Competitive GMAT score (680+ / GMAT Focus 625+) or GRE equivalent (quantitative 160+).
Thesis / Research statement
Personal statement highlighting international leadership trajectory, cultural adaptability, and career objectives.
Recommendation letters
Two academic or managerial letters of recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Merit award: up to 50% tuition reduction (£11,750 per academic year) awarded upon admission
Application fee
£180 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£23,500/year base tuition; reduced to £11,750/year with 50% excellence scholarship

Dates and verification

Application deadline
10 March 2027 (Round 2 Admissions and Scholarship Call)
