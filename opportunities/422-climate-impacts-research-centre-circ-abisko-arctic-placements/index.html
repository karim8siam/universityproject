Climate Impacts Research Centre (CIRC) Abisko Arctic Placements

Field research internships at the Abisko Scientific Research Station in northern Sweden, studying permafrost thaw, tundra carbon exchange, and aquatic ecosystems.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored
