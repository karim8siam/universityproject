Curtin University PhD in Computing and Information Systems

Four-year advanced doctoral research program conducting world-leading research in high-performance computing, artificial intelligence, blockchain, cybersecurity, and data-intensive radio astronomy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours or Master of Science by Research in Computing.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Completed Master's thesis or publication record in data science, AI, or cybersecurity.
Recommendation letters
Written confirmation of supervision support from an academic staff member in the School of EECMS.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Eligible for Curtin Strategic Scholarships (AUD $35,000/yr tax-free) and industry-funded CRC projects
Application fee
Free application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition for RTP scholarship holders; standard international tuition AUD $41,400/year.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
April 30, 2027 for Mid-Year (Semester 2) admissions
