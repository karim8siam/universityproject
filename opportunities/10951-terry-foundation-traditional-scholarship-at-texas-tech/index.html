Texas Tech University System · Lubbock, Texas, United States

Academic Overview & Description

Premier full-ride scholarship covering entire cost of attendance (tuition, fees, books, and on-campus room and board) for outstanding Texas high school leaders.

Funding & Financial Support

Funding Award
Comprehensive award valued at up to $25,000/year for eight semesters of undergraduate study
Tuition Rate
$0 (Fully covered)
Application Fee
$0

Admission & Candidate Requirements

Academic GPA
Top 20% class rank with strong community leadership and financial need
English Language
Satisfied via Texas high school graduation
Standardized Exam
SAT or ACT scores submitted
Application Materials
Terry Foundation application, leadership essay, FAFSA
Letters of Recommendation
Two letters of recommendation from high school principals or teachers
