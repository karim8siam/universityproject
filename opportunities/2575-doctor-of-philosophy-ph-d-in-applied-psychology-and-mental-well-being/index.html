Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Applied Psychology and Mental Well-being

Doctoral research program supported by the Centre for Applied Psychological Research, investigating clinical interventions, workplace well-being, and social psychology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree with Merit or Distinction in Psychology, Neuroscience, or Cognitive Science.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 with at least 6.0 in each sub-test.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for UK doctoral research admissions.
Thesis / Research statement
2,500-word research proposal addressing adolescent mental health, cognitive resilience, or clinical behavioral interventions.
Recommendation letters
Two academic reference letters from senior psychology researchers.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Supervised doctoral research with university fee waivers and departmental teaching assistantships
Application fee
Zero application fee
Tuition / joining costs
£14,800 annual tuition for international full-time doctoral research students
