McMaster Engineering Co-op & Undergraduate Student Research Awards (USRA)

Paid research and industrial internships connecting student engineers with leading research laboratories and high-technology ventures across southern Ontario's innovation corridor.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Minimum cumulative average of 75% in engineering or science coursework.
English language proficiency
Enrollment in good academic standing.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Resume and technical transcript showing relevant project achievements.
Recommendation letters
Endorsement by departmental co-op office.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried placements: CAD $6,000 to $11,000 per 4-month academic term based on program level
Application fee
Free application for enrolled McMaster students
Tuition / joining costs
Standard co-op enrollment fee applies.
