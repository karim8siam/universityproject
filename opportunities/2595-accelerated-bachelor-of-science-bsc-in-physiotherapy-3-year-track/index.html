Accelerated Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Physiotherapy (3-Year Track)

Intensive, three-year full-time English-taught Bachelor of Science program delivering 240 ECTS credits, manual therapy masterclasses, and 40 weeks of clinical hospital internships worldwide.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Secondary school diploma with high grades in Biology, Physics/Chemistry, and Mathematics.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.0 with at least 5.5 per component (or TOEFL iBT 80).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Dutch undergraduate bachelor's degrees.
Thesis / Research statement
Successful completion of the mandatory ESP online intake assessment and anatomy assignment.
Recommendation letters
One academic reference and motivation video submission.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
NVAO-accredited 240 ECTS degree; statutory tuition for EU citizens and global clinical placements
Application fee
Zero Studielink registration fee
Tuition / joining costs
€2,530/year for EU/EEA citizens; €9,600/year for non-EU international students
