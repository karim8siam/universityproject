Australian Institute for Bioengineering and Nanotechnology (AIBN) Placements

Interdisciplinary research placements in nanomedicine, biomaterials, stem cell regenerative therapy, and precision therapeutics at one of Australia's premier research institutes.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First Class Honours or equivalent Master's with high marks in bioengineering, materials, or molecular biology.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated experimental laboratory skills and research publications.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee endorsements.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded research appointments: AUD $35,000/yr tax-free stipend plus dedicated laboratory consumable funding
Application fee
Standard UQ application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Tuition offset included for all scholarship awardees.
