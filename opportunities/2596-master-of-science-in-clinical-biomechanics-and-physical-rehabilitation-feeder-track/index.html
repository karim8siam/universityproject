Master of Science in Clinical Biomechanics and Physical Rehabilitation (Feeder Track)

Research-oriented clinical master's track training clinical specialists to evaluate neuromuscular disorders, sports injury recovery kinetics, and post-surgical rehabilitation.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Bachelor of Science in Physiotherapy with minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2/4.0.
English language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Dutch rehabilitation master's programs.
Thesis / Research statement
Bachelor's thesis and 1,500-word research proposal evaluating musculoskeletal gait kinematics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic references from university physiotherapy faculty.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?
