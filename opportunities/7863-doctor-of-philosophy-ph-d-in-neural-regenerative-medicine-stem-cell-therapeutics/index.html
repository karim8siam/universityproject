Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Neural Regenerative Medicine & Stem Cell Therapeutics

World-leading doctoral program investigating intravenous autologous mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) transplantation protocols for spinal cord trauma, stroke recovery, and neuroprotection.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
MD, MBBS, or Master of Science in biological sciences with minimum 3.30 GPA
English language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 (Japanese not mandatory for international lab researchers)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed doctoral research prospectus (3-5 pages) in neural regeneration and CV
Recommendation letters
Two letters of recommendation from medical or life sciences professors

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded doctoral research assistantships with monthly stipends up to ¥120,000 and complete tuition coverage for top scholars
Application fee
¥30,000 entrance examination fee
Tuition / joining costs
¥535,800 annual prefectural tuition
