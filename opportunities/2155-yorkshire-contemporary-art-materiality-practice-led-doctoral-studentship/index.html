Yorkshire Contemporary Art & Materiality Practice-Led Doctoral Studentship

Three-year practice-led doctoral research studentship investigating sustainable bioplastics, reclaimed pigments, and ecological ethics in contemporary sculpture.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class or 2:1 honours degree (or master's with distinction) in fine art or curation
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Detailed practice-based doctoral proposal (1,500 words) on sustainable sculptural materiality
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters from studio supervisors
