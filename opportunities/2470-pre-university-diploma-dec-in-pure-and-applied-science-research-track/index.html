Rigorous collegiate science curriculum offering university-equivalent foundation coursework in calculus, analytical mechanics, electromagnetism, and organic chemistry.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed secondary education including advanced secondary Mathematics (TS or SN 5), Chemistry, and Physics.
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 or equivalent TOEFL iBT score of 80 or higher.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for collegiate-level STEM preparation.
Thesis / Research statement
Laboratory science portfolio and statement of interest in physics and engineering disciplines.
Recommendation letters
Two academic recommendation letters verifying quantitative competence and laboratory acumen.
