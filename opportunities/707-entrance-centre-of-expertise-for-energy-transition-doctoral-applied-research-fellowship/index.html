EnTranCe Centre of Expertise for Energy Transition Doctoral / Applied Research Fellowship

Collaborative applied doctoral research at the EnTranCe testing facility on the Zernike Campus, developing regional green hydrogen distribution, biomethane production, and smart hybrid electricity grids.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Sustainable Energy, or Chemical Engineering with high marks.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis or publication in hydrogen production, microgrid optimization, or renewable heating.
Recommendation letters
Two academic referee letters.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Salaried research position (Dutch CAO-HBO): €3,200 to €4,100/month gross plus 8% holiday allowance and pension
Application fee
€0 application fee
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition charges; salaried appointment joint with University of Groningen.
