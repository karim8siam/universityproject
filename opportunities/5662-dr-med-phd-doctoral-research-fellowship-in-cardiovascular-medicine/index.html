Dr. med. / PhD Doctoral Research Fellowship in Cardiovascular Medicine

Three-year structured doctoral fellowship conducted at the Heart Center Brandenburg (Herzzentrum Brandenburg in Bernau) investigating minimally invasive cardiac valve interventions.
