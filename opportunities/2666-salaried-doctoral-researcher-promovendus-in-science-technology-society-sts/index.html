Salaried Doctoral Researcher (Promovendus) in Science, Technology & Society (STS)

Four-year salaried doctoral research employment investigating how emerging digital technologies, artificial intelligence, and biomedical innovations shape European society and public policy.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Completed Master of Arts or Master of Science degree with minimum grade 7.5/10 in STS, sociology, or history.
English language proficiency
Fluent academic English communication (IELTS 7.0 or equivalent).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not applicable for Dutch doctoral employment.
Thesis / Research statement
Master's thesis and 2,500-word doctoral research proposal examining the societal impact of technological innovations.
Recommendation letters
Three academic reference contacts including master's thesis supervisor.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Full Dutch university employment: €2,770 to €3,539 gross monthly salary with holiday allowance and pension
Application fee
Zero application fee
Tuition / joining costs
100% tuition-free doctoral position under Dutch CAO NU collective agreement
