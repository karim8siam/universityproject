UniTrento Master of Science (MSc) in Environmental Meteorology

Joint international Master's degree between the University of Trento and the University of Innsbruck (Austria) specializing in atmospheric boundary layer physics and Alpine meteorology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in environmental engineering, physics, mathematics, or earth sciences.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Solid mathematical preparation in differential equations and thermodynamics.
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters.
