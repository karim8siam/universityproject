Western Robarts Research Institute Summer Student Research Fellowship

Four-month competitive summer research placement at Robarts Research Institute conducting translational research in cardiovascular health, medical imaging, and neurobiology.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in 2nd or 3rd year undergraduate biology, medical biophysics, or biochemistry with minimum 75% average.
English language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Coursework in cellular biology, physiology, or immunology.
Recommendation letters
One professor reference letter.

No tuition charges; direct studentship.
