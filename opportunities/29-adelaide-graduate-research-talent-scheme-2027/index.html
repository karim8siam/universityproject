Adelaide Graduate Research Talent Scheme 2027

Major competitive postgraduate research scholarship at Adelaide University providing a full tuition fee offset and living allowance stipend for PhD and MPhil candidates.

Official university source ↗Information verified 14 October 2026

Funding value, duration and scope

The scholarship offers full tuition coverage and a living stipend. While the 2026 published full-time stipend was AUD 36,500 per year, prospective students should confirm the updated 2027 indexation rate directly with the university. Coverage typically lasts up to 3.5 to 4 years for PhD candidates and up to 2 years for Master of Philosophy researchers.

Closing deadline
2 November 2026 for Round 1 consideration.
Stipend amount
AUD 36,500 per year (indexed annually) plus 100% tuition fee waiver.
Eligible degree levels
Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) and Master of Philosophy (MPhil).

Key eligibility restrictions

Round 1 specifically admits domestic applicants and international applicants who completed or are completing an Australian or New Zealand qualification. Overseas-only qualification holders are directed to subsequent international scholarship intake rounds.

Application procedure

  1. Obtain written endorsement from an eligible principal supervisor at Adelaide University.
  2. Prepare your research proposal, academic transcripts, structured CV, and English evidence.
  3. Submit your combined degree admission and scholarship application through the official portal before 2 November 2026.
