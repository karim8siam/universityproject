BU Washington Journalism & Investigative Reporting Semester

Comprehensive media internship placing journalism scholars in major Washington bureaus (NPR, CNN, Roll Call, The Washington Post) reporting on congressional hearings, White House press conferences, and the Supreme Court.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Cumulative GPA of 3.2 in Journalism, Media Science, or Communications.
English language proficiency
IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Journalism portfolio of published articles/multimedia clips (5 samples) and CV.
Recommendation letters
Two recommendations from journalism professors or editors.
