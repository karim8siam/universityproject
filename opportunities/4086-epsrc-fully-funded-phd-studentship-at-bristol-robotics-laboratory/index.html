EPSRC Fully Funded PhD Studentship at Bristol Robotics Laboratory

Three-and-a-half-year fully funded PhD appointment based at the Bristol Robotics Laboratory, investigating biomimetic soft tactile sensors and reinforcement learning for dexterous robotic manipulation.

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class Honours degree (or MSc with Distinction) in Robotics, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or Computer Science
English language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all subtests)
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required
Thesis / Research statement
Five-page doctoral research project proposal, curriculum vitae, and academic transcripts
Recommendation letters
Two academic letters of evaluation

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: UKRI tax-free living stipend of £19,237 per year for 3.5 years plus 100% full tuition fees and £5,000 lab equipment budget
