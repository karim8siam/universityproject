UZH Master of Science (MSc) in Informatics (English-taught)

Two-year Master of Science offering specialized tracks in Artificial Intelligence, Software Systems, Data Science, and People and Computing, taught 100% in English in Zurich.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or closely related field with minimum 180 ECTS.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Research statement
Rigorous coursework in algorithms, theoretical computer science, and object-oriented programming.
Recommendation letters
Academic transcript and curriculum vitae.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
UZH Foundation merit grants and research assistantship positions (CHF 28 to 35/hour)
Application fee
CHF 500 foreign credential evaluation fee
Tuition / joining costs
CHF 1,220/semester (including non-resident surcharge; approx. USD $2,700/year).

Dates and verification

Application deadline
February 28, 2027 (Fall semester international application deadline)
