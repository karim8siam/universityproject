Hanze Health Hub & Healthy Ageing Summer Practice Traineeship

Ten-week hands-on clinical and technology internship developing wearable sensor technologies and physical exercise interventions to support independent living among geriatric populations.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled in Bachelor's or Master's in Physiotherapy, Health Sciences, Nursing, or Biomedical Engineering.
English language proficiency
Good English conversational abilities.

Not applicable.

Completed coursework in human physiology, biomechanics, or medical rehabilitation.
