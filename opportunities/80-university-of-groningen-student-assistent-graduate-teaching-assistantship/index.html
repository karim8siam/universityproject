University of Groningen Student-Assistent & Graduate Teaching Assistantship

Departmental academic employment (Student-Assistent) for enrolled students across the Faculty of Science and Engineering, providing hourly paid laboratory demonstration, exercise teaching, and assessment marking.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
Enrolled graduate or senior undergraduate student at RUG in good academic standing.
English language proficiency
Fluent spoken English adequate for undergraduate teaching.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required.
Thesis / Publications
Grade 7.5 or higher in the course unit applied to tutor.
Recommendation letters
Course coordinator approval and departmental recommendation.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully salaried: €15.50 to €18.75 per hour under Dutch CAO-NU for lab supervision, exercise instruction, and tutorial mentoring
Application fee
Free employment application
Tuition / joining costs
Zero tuition; official university employment contract.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
August 15, 2027 for Autumn semester tutorial and laboratory appointments
