UBC Four Year Doctoral Fellowships (4YF)

UBC's prestigious Four Year Doctoral Fellowship (4YF) provides guaranteed financial support of at least CAD $18,200 per year plus tuition for outstanding domestic and international PhD students across all disciplines.

Official university details ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Application checklist & requirements

These requirements reflect published university guidelines available at the last review. Review official department guidelines before submission.

Academic degree / Prerequisites
First-class academic standing (A- / 80% or equivalent 3.7+ CGPA) across prior degree coursework.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with minimum 6.5 in all bands or TOEFL iBT 100.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Recommended for quantitative programs outside Canada/US; subject tests accepted.
Thesis / Research statement
Demonstrated research potential through published conference papers or Master's thesis.
Recommendation letters
Three confidential academic reference letters submitted electronically through the eServices portal.

How much will it cost? How much funding is available?

Financial support / stipend
Fully funded: Minimum CAD $18,200 annual stipend plus full tuition coverage for up to four years of doctoral study
Application fee
CAD $168.25 international application fee (CAD $114.00 domestic)
Tuition / joining costs
Full tuition completely covered for four years under 4YF fellowship regulations.

Dates and verification

Application deadline
January 15, 2027 (Departmental graduate awards nomination deadline for September intake)
