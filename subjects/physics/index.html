Scholarship · Published deadline

2027 undergraduate entrance scholarships

International and Canadian students applying to eligible Acadia bachelor's programs may be considered for entrance awards. Initial offers are not a promise of additional competitive scholarships. Separate scholarship forms are due March 1, 2027; scholarships are not guaranteed.

Funding
Initial awards CAD $500–$2,500; selected awards up to CAD $80,000 over four years (competitive)
Application cost
CAD $40 undergraduate processing fee listed for international applicants (reconfirm on portal).
Deadline
2027-03-01

Honours Summer Research Awards (HSRA)

Supports up to ten weeks of summer research for students enrolled in Acadia honours programs who have completed two or three years of full-time study. Not a general international external research internship.

Funding
Up to CAD $5,683 in total employer funding; take-home salary is lower

Not stated
