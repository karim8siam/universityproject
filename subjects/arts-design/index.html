Master's · 2027 dated round

MFA in Craft Media — Fall 2027

AUArts MFA in Craft Media is a 20-month, 60-credit practice-led master's in ceramics, fibre, glass and jewellery/metals. Fall 2027 applications opened October 1, 2026; January 15, 2027 is priority for entrance scholarship consideration and June 1, 2027 is the final general deadline, subject to available places.

Funding: Merit-based scholarships and possible teaching assistantships; not guaranteed.
Fee: CAD 150 international or CAD 110 domestic application fee, non-refundable.
Deadline: 2027-06-01

Scholarship · 2027 dated round

2027 MFA Craft Media Entrance Awards

AUArts automatically considers MFA applicants for entrance scholarship awards up to CAD 5,000 when their complete admission application is submitted by January 15, 2027. Award decisions are based on merit and are not guaranteed.

Funding: Competitive entrance award of up to CAD 5,000; not automatic funding.
Fee: No separate award application; MFA application CAD 150 international or CAD 110 domestic.
Deadline: 2027-01-15

Scholarship · Funding program information

MFA Graduate Teaching Assistantship

Alberta University of the Arts lists a Graduate Teaching Assistantship of CAD 5,000 that may be offered up to three times during the MFA program. The listing describes possible funding rather than a confirmed available assistantship position.

Funding: CAD 5,000 if awarded; a student may receive up to three over the two-year MFA, subject to selection.
Fee: No separate GTA application fee established; associated MFA admission fee applies.
Deadline: No separate open application deadline independently confirmed.
