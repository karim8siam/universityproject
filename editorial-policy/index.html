Verification policy

Sources before claims.

Official university or funding-agency sources come first. We record the source URL and review date, summarize requirements in original wording, and mark unknowns rather than inventing them.

Our publishing rules

Dated opportunities
A listing must have a confirmed deadline before we call it a current dated opportunity. Dated cards automatically leave active listings once the deadline passes.
Degree programs
An active academic program is not proof of a presently open vacancy or available funding. Dates are confirmed with the department.
Researcher profiles
We use officially published professional information. This never implies the professor can accept students or has a grant.
Funding
Scholarship consideration is not an award. Monetary figures are published with their currency, conditions and relevant year.
Corrections
Email shahriyarkarimsiam@gmail.com with a source URL. Conflicts should be marked for re-verification.
Commercial independence
We do not charge for accessing official university pages; future ads or mentorship cannot change factual eligibility rankings.
