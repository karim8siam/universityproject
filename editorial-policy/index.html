Verification policy

Sources before claims.

Official university or funding-agency sources come first. We record the source URL and review date, summarize requirements in original wording, and mark unknowns rather than inventing them.

Our publishing rules

Dated opportunities
A listing must have a confirmed deadline before we call it a current dated opportunity. Dated cards automatically leave active listings once the deadline passes.
Degree programs
An active academic program is not proof of a presently open vacancy or available funding. Dates are confirmed with official department portals.
Researcher profiles
We use officially published professional information. This never implies the professor can accept students or has a currently vacant lab grant.
Funding
Scholarship consideration is not a guaranteed award. Monetary figures are published with their currency, conditions and relevant intake cycle.
Corrections & Ideas
Email shahriyarkarimsiam@gmail.com or post in our Discord student community with a source URL. Conflicting information is audited immediately.
Commercial independence
We do not charge for accessing official university pages; free consultancy and community discussions cannot alter factual admission standards.
