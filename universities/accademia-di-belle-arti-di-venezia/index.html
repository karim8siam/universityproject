Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia: Fine Arts, Venice Biennale & ESU Scholarships

Founded in 1750 with Giovanni Battista Tiepolo as its first president, the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia is one of Italy's most prestigious state institutions for higher artistic education (AFAM). Situated along the Venetian canals, it is celebrated for painting, sculpture, graphic arts, new art technologies, and close synergy with the Venice Biennale.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Veneto Regional ESU Full Study Grant & Venetian Accommodation Award

Needs-and-merit based regional scholarship open to domestic and international fine arts students ensuring equitable access to historic art education in Venice.

AFAM Doctoral Fellowship in Contemporary Visual Arts & Venetian Heritage

Doctoral research program based in Venice exploring artistic practice-as-research, ephemeral sculpture in fragile ecosystems, and Venetian archival restoration.

Master of Fine Arts (Diploma di II Livello) in Painting & Contemporary Pictorial Languages

Two-year master's curriculum training master painters in color theory, monumental composition, experimental supports, and critical discourse in contemporary art.

New Technologies for Art (NTA) Digital Interactive Media Assistantship

Applied artistic research post developing interactive multimedia installations, virtual reality museum experiences, and generative video art.

La Biennale di Venezia International Art & Architecture Exhibition Practicum

Preeminent exhibition internship rotating through the Giardini and Arsenale pavilions during the Venice Art/Architecture Biennale, mastering curatorial installation and pavilion operations.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Conservation & Restoration of Venetian Lagoon Artworks

Two-year postdoctoral appointment examining nanolime consolidants, biocides, and microclimatic preservation of Renaissance paintings and historic stone in high-humidity lagoon environments.

Master of Fine Arts (Diploma di II Livello) in Printmaking & Artists' Books

Studio master's degree exploring traditional intaglio and relief printing alongside digital hybrid printmaking, photopolymer gravure, and limited-edition artist book design.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Three-year undergraduate diploma (Diploma Accademico di Primo Livello) or equivalent bachelor's degree in fine arts, design, or architecture evaluated via Universitaly.
Doctoral admission eligibility
AFAM Research Doctorate (Dottorato di Ricerca AFAM) in artistic and cultural heritage; requires an MFA/MA, extensive artistic portfolio, and an original project thesis.
Language proficiency
Italian language proficiency at CEFR B2 level required for most academic courses; portfolio reviews and studio tutorials also accommodate English.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive artistic portfolio of 15–20 documented original works accompanied by an artist statement and proposed thesis direction.
Application fee
€30 to €50 administrative examination fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, artistic portfolio, Declaration of Value (DoV) or CIMEA comparability certificate, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is based on family financial assessment (ISEE parificato), ranging between €156 and €2,400 per year. Regional ESU scholarships (Azienda Regionale per il Diritto allo Studio Universitario di Venezia) provide qualifying international students with full tuition fee waivers, student housing in Venice, and up to €7,200 annual cash grants.
