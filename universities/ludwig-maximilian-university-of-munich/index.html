Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU Munich)

Established in 1472, Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU Munich) is one of Europe's most prestigious and historic research universities and a continuous German University of Excellence. Boasting 43 Nobel laureates (including Max Planck, Wilhelm Röntgen, Werner Heisenberg, and Ferenc Krausz), LMU leads the world in quantum physics, molecular medicine (the Gene Center Munich), astrophysics, and neuroscience. Cutting-edge molecular bioscience placements at the Gene Center Munich, conducting structural biology and cryo-electron microscopy investigations of cellular machineries.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science or Bachelor of Arts degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in relevant subject with grade equivalent to 'gut' (minimum 2.5 or better on German 1.0-5.0 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (grade 2.0 or better) in relevant discipline; formal agreement of doctoral supervision by an LMU university professor (*Doktorvater/mutter*).
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency for English-taught degrees: Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80-90 (German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive scientific exposé (5-10 pages) describing doctoral project framework, literature review, and methodology.
Application fee
No tuition fee; nominal semester fee (Semesterbeitrag) of approx. €150/semester including student services and transit ticket options.
Required documentation
Certified university transcripts, graduation certificates, curriculum vitae, module handbook, and academic letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is completely free of charge for all domestic and international students. Doctoral researchers are employed on salaried public-sector research employment contracts (Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in under TV-L E13), earning gross annual salaries of €50,000 to €56,000 per year with full statutory healthcare, unemployment insurance, and pension contributions.

Verified faculty profiles and research leaders
