University of the Nations

UofN · Kailua-Kona, Hawaii, United States

The University of the Nations is an international training university headquartered in Kailua-Kona, Hawaii, operating across more than 600 locations in 160 nations. Operating through seven colleges—including Health Care, Agriculture, Education, and Science & Technology—UofN pairs modular, experiential training with cross-cultural field immersions, disaster response, and indigenous community development.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Kailua-Kona · Global Intercultural Education, Humanitarian Relief & Tropical Agriculture. The University of the Nations is an international training university headquartered in Kailua-Kona, Hawaii, operating across more than 600 locations in 160 nations. Operating through seven colleges—including Health Care, Agriculture, Education, and Science & Technology—UofN pairs modular, experiential training with cross-cultural field immersions, disaster response, and indigenous community development.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Successful completion of the foundational Discipleship Training School (DTS) prerequisite curriculum followed by secondary school completion diploma or equivalent. Demonstrated English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 65+ or IELTS 6.0+) or bilingual track placement for non-native English speakers. Rolling admissions aligned with quarterly modular academic terms (January, April, July, September).
