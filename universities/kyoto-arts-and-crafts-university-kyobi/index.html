Kyoto Arts and Crafts University (KYOBI)

Institution Overview & Profile

Kyoto Arts and Crafts University (KYOBI - Kyoto Bijutsu Kogeidaigaku), located in the cultural heart of Kyoto with campuses in Higashiyama and Nantan Sonobe, is Japan's only university dedicated exclusively to the fusion of traditional Japanese architectural preservation, fine decorative crafts (urushi lacquerware, ceramics, wood carving), and contemporary architectural and digital product design.

Key Academic Strengths

  • Japan's premier university granting national First-Class Registered Architect (Ikkyu Kenchikushi) exam eligibility with traditional wooden construction mastery
  • World-class master craftsmen teaching authentic centuries-old techniques in Japanese lacquerware (urushi), metalwork, and ceramic arts
  • Direct restoration practicums on historic temples, shrines, and National Treasure structures across Kyoto and Nara
  • Higashiyama campus located within walking distance of Sanjusangendo, Kyoto National Museum, and historic artisan quarters

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Bachelor of Arts (Gakushi)

Bachelor of Arts in Traditional Architecture & Heritage Preservation

Four-year curriculum integrating structural timber engineering, Japanese joinery (kigumi), seismic reinforcement of historic temples, and CAD architectural drafting.

Bachelor of Arts (Gakushi)

Bachelor of Arts in Fine Crafts & Product Design

Four-year studio degree specializing in ceramics, lacquer art (urushi), traditional woodwork, and contemporary industrial product prototyping.

Master of Fine Arts (Shushi)

Master of Fine Arts in Architecture & Heritage Conservation

Two-year postgraduate research program focusing on advanced structural diagnostics of historic timber buildings and cultural heritage disaster mitigation.
