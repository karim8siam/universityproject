University of Humanistic Studies: Ethics, Care & Dutch PhD Research Guide

The University of Humanistic Studies (UvH - Universiteit voor Humanistiek), established in 1989 in the historic heart of Utrecht, is an independent, state-accredited research university in the Netherlands. Internationally renowned for its interdisciplinary scholarship combining philosophy, ethics, psychology, and sociology, UvH focuses on humanization, care ethics, social sustainability, and meaningful aging.

Salaried Dutch Doctoral Position (PhD) in Care Ethics and Social Justice

Four-year salaried doctoral appointment within the Department of Care Ethics, investigating vulnerability, institutional eldercare ethics, and democratic care practices.

Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship at University of Humanistic Studies

Prestigious national scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education and UvH, awarded to outstanding international students pursuing master's studies in Utrecht.

Master of Arts in Care Ethics and Policy

Unique graduate program examining ethical dilemmas in healthcare delivery, public welfare policy, institutional moral counseling, and relational autonomy.

Centre for Humanistic Social Studies Research Assistantship

Research assistantship contributing to empirical studies on social resilience, intergenerational solidarity, and existential meaning in contemporary Dutch society.

Dutch National Healthcare and Hospice Ethics Practicum

Twenty-week professional internship embedded in hospital ethics committees, mental health clinics, and eldercare facilities across Utrecht and Amsterdam.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Philosophy of Humanism & Existential Meaning

Two-year postdoctoral position exploring contemporary existentialism, secular meaning-making practices, and moral citizenship in pluralistic democracies.

Master of Arts in Humanistic Studies: Meaningful Education & Civil Society

Three-year interdisciplinary master's degree preparing humanist counselors, educational policy consultants, and civic leaders in transformative dialogical methodologies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from a Dutch university or international research university equivalent (180 ECTS) in humanities, social sciences, philosophy, or healthcare.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Master of Arts or Master of Science) with distinction (minimum Dutch grade of 7.5 or international GPA 3.5+); competitive selection for advertised doctoral vacancies.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all subtests), TOEFL iBT 90, or Cambridge C1 Advanced; Dutch language proficiency (NT2-II) required for Dutch-taught tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applicants must submit an original 2,500-word research proposal detailing theoretical frameworks, empirical methodology, and ethical relevance.
Application fee
€0 (Studielink application without additional institutional registration fee).
Required documentation
Bachelor's and master's degree certificates, certified transcripts of records, curriculum vitae, motivation letter, and academic writing sample.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is legally fixed at €2,530 per year for EU/EEA students. For non-EU/EEA international students, master's tuition is approximately €9,600 per academic year. Employed doctoral candidates (promovendi) receive full Dutch university collective labor agreement (CAO) salaries (€38,000–€48,000/year gross; zero tuition fees).
