Institutional Overview

Tio Business School (Hogeschool Tio), established in 1969 with flagship campuses in Utrecht, Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Eindhoven, and Groningen, is the premier private university of applied sciences in the Netherlands. Consistently ranked by the Keuzegids Hbo as the #1 small university of applied sciences for over ten consecutive years, Tio is renowned for accelerated three-year bachelor's degrees in hotel management, international business, commerce entrepreneurship, and digital marketing.
