Institutional Overview

Unicusano Trani is the premier accredited university center of Università Niccolò Cusano for the Province of Barletta-Andria-Trani (BAT) in Apulia. Located on Via Alberolongo in Trani, this center provides blended and distance degree programs, in-person academic counseling, and exam sessions across economics, law, political science, psychological sciences, and industrial engineering.
