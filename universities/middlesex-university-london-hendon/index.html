Middlesex University London: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located on an award-winning flagship campus in Hendon, North London, Middlesex University educates over 19,000 students in London and maintains international branch campuses in Dubai and Mauritius. Renowned for its state-of-the-art Ritterman Building and Grove creative arts center, Middlesex excels in applied computer science, assistive social robotics, cyber defense, forensic psychology, and nursing clinical excellence.
