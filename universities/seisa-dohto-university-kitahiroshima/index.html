Seisa Dohto University

Seisa Dohto University is a private university in Kitahiroshima, Hokkaido (adjacent to Sapporo), celebrated for its Department of Design and Architecture, community social welfare education, and athletic sports sciences.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in close proximity to the Hokkaido Ballpark F Village in Kitahiroshima, Seisa Dohto provides hands-on degree tracks in cold-climate timber architecture, graphic design, community social welfare, and athletic coaching. The university is recognized for its ethos of inclusive education and community revitalization across Hokkaido.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials and personal interviews. Foreign applicants require JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 200, an artistic portfolio for design candidates, and an admissions interview.
