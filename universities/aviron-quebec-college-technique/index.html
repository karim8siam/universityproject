Aviron Québec Technical College: Industrial Mechanics, Welding & Applied Tech Guide

Aviron Québec Collège Technique is a premier specialized vocational and technical institute in Québec City, celebrated for intensive professional diplomas (DEP/AEC) in industrial drafting, automobile mechanics, precision welding, electricity, and advanced construction technologies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of secondary school diploma (DES) or recognized international equivalent secondary schooling (minimum 11-12 years) for technical diplomas.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering doctoral degrees; provides Ministry-accredited Diplômes d'études professionnelles (DEP) and Attestations d'études collégiales (AEC).
Language proficiency
Proficiency in French or English depending on instructional language stream (B2 level recommended for French vocational courses).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of technical interest outlining career goals in trades, mechanics, or electrical engineering.
Application fee
CAD $250 international student application and document evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified high school or post-secondary transcripts, birth certificate/passport copy, English/French proficiency proof, and Québec Acceptance Certificate (CAQ) application.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international technical vocational diplomas averages CAD $16,500 to $24,000 for full multi-semester programs. Québec vocational training bursaries, international student mobility grants, and paid industry apprenticeships are available.
