Anderson University: Cybersecurity, Engineering, Nursing & Data Analytics Guide

Established in 1917 in Anderson, Indiana, Anderson University (AU) is an accredited comprehensive university celebrated for academic rigor across cybersecurity, engineering, data analytics, nursing, and business administration. AU's Center for Security Studies and Cyber Defense is an NSA-recognized National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD), conducting critical cybersecurity research and training.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited college or university with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0 (conditional admission available for 2.75-2.99 GPA).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Business Administration (DBA); requiring an accredited master's degree (MBA or MS), minimum 3.25 graduate GPA, and extensive professional managerial experience.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80, IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0), or Duolingo English Test 105+ for non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for DBA and graduate research programs: 1,500-word statement of research interest highlighting quantitative or qualitative inquiry objectives.
Application fee
$0 online application fee for undergraduate and graduate applications.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts from all post-secondary institutions, professional resume/CV, two letters of recommendation, and personal statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition at Anderson University is approximately $34,400 USD per academic year. Graduate tuition ranges between $580 and $750 USD per credit hour depending on the discipline. More than 98% of full-time students receive financial aid, including generous Presidential scholarships up to $22,000 USD/year and graduate assistantships.
