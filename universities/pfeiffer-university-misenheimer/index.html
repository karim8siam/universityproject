Pfeiffer University

Pfeiffer University · Misenheimer, North Carolina, United States

Pfeiffer University is a private regional university affiliated with the United Methodist Church, with its main campus in Misenheimer, North Carolina, and its modern Center for Health Sciences in downtown Albemarle. Celebrated for physician assistant studies, occupational therapy, nursing, criminal justice, and health administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Pfeiffer features the Center for Health Sciences in Albemarle (housing cutting-edge anatomical simulation suites, cadaver labs, and mock surgical operating theaters), the historic Misenheimer undergraduate campus, and the Charlotte professional center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions review high school GPA (minimum 2.5 recommended) with test-optional application options. Master of Science in Physician Assistant Studies and Master of Science in Occupational Therapy require competitive CASPA/OTCAS centralized applications, prerequisite health sciences GPAs (3.0+), and clinical healthcare hours.
