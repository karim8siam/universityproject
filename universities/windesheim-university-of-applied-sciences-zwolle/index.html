Windesheim gebouw T

Windesheim University of Applied Sciences (Gebouw T - Technology & Engineering Centre) in Zwolle is one of the Netherlands' top-ranked universities of applied sciences, internationally celebrated for sustainable plastics engineering, civil engineering, and green hydrogen technology.

Academic overview & excellence

Gebouw T is the technological heart of the Zwolle campus, housing specialized industrial laboratories in circular polymers, automated composite manufacturing, industrial robotics, and hydrogen energy systems in direct partnership with leading European manufacturing consortia.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a Dutch HAVO/VWO diploma or accredited international equivalent (e.g., German Abitur, International Baccalaureate). English-taught bachelor's programs require IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 80. EU/EEA students pay statutory tuition (€2,530/year); non-EU students pay institutional fees.

Four-year bachelor's curriculum located in Gebouw T exploring mechanical design, thermal dynamics, polymer recycling, and robotic composite manufacturing.
