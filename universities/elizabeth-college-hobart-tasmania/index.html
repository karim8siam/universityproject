Elizabeth College: Senior Secondary STEM, Marine Studies & Arts Academy Guide

Elizabeth College in Hobart, Tasmania, is a premier state senior secondary college renowned for advanced pre-tertiary education, university transition pathways with the University of Tasmania, the Elizabeth College Performing Arts and Media Academy, marine biology science, and digital computing.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Elizabeth College provides Year 11 and Year 12 pre-tertiary education (Tasmanian Certificate of Education and ATAR) preparing graduates for direct university entrance worldwide.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Senior secondary college; graduates transition to Australian and international universities.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 5.5 overall (or completion of GETI Intensive English Language Course in Hobart).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of study intent detailing anticipated pre-tertiary subjects (STEM, arts, humanities) and target university degrees.
Application fee
AUD $250 GETI international student application fee.
Required documentation
Certified transcripts for the past two academic years, school recommendation, passport copy, and evidence of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international students enrolled through Government Education Training International (GETI) Tasmania is approximately AUD $14,500 to $16,800 per year. GETI academic merit bursaries, Tasmanian Government study awards, and homestay subsidies are accessible.
