Stedelijk Dalton College Alkmaar is an acclaimed pre-university educational institution in North Holland, recognized for applying Helen Parkhurst's Dalton Plan principles of freedom, cooperation, and independent inquiry to advanced STEM and social science curricula.

Academic overview & excellence

Centrally located in the historic cheese-trading city of Alkmaar, Stedelijk Dalton College prepares students for university study through personalized inquiry-driven education. The college houses modern biological science greenhouses, robotics testing suites, digital design studios, and collaborative study atriums. Operating in partnership with the University of Amsterdam and Utrecht University, scholars engage in independent scientific investigations in marine coastal biology, sustainable agriculture, and Dutch legal constitutionalism.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to the senior VWO (pre-university) and Dalton academic honors streams requires an official Dutch primary education advice of VWO or international secondary school equivalent credentials demonstrating self-regulatory study skills, high mathematics scores, and analytical capability. For international applicants, English proficiency (CEFR B2) and Dutch language aptitude are evaluated during an interview.
