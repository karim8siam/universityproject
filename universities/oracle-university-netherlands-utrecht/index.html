Academic overview & excellence

Located in the Dutch tech corridor in Utrecht, Oracle University Netherlands delivers professional education, role-based cloud architect learning paths, and globally benchmarked certifications. The center maintains modern cloud sandbox laboratories, hands-on enterprise migration simulation suites, and specialized programs for Dutch multinational corporations, financial institutions, and government ministries migrating mission-critical workloads to cloud infrastructure.

Research admissions & criteria

Courses and master certification tracks are open to software engineers, database administrators, systems architects, and IT university graduates. Formal admission requires basic proficiency in relational databases, Linux operating systems, or object-oriented programming (Java/Python).
