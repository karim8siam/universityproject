Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1916, Tokyo Medical University operates a flagship 900-bed acute care teaching hospital adjacent to its central campus in Nishi-Shinjuku. TMU is globally recognized for pioneering photodynamic therapy (PDT) and robotic thoracic surgery for lung cancer, saliva-based metabolomic cancer diagnostics, immune checkpoint therapeutic trials, and comprehensive hospital nursing education.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to the Faculty of Medicine (MD degree) requires exceptional performance in university entrance examinations in chemistry, biology, physics, mathematics, and interviews. Admission to the Graduate School of Medicine (PhD in Medical Sciences) requires an MD, DDS, DVM, or recognized biomedical master's degree, submission of an approved clinical research protocol, and departmental supervisor endorsement.
