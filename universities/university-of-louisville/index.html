University of Louisville

UofL · Louisville, Kentucky, United States

The University of Louisville (UofL), founded in 1798, is a premier public metropolitan Carnegie R1 research university in Louisville, Kentucky. UofL made medical history by performing the world's first successful self-contained artificial heart implant and hand transplant. Anchored by the Health Sciences Center, UofL Health, and the J.B. Speed School of Engineering, UofL conducts over $150 million in annual sponsored research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UofL encompasses 12 colleges and schools, notably the School of Medicine, the J.B. Speed School of Engineering, the Louis D. Brandeis School of Law, and the School of Public Health and Information Sciences. It is home to the Christina Lee Brown Envirome Institute and the Additive Manufacturing Competency Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions operate via the Common Application (holistic review; test-optional; recommended GPA 3.0+). Graduate and professional biomedical programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, competitive GPA, letters of recommendation, and department-specific exams or statements.
