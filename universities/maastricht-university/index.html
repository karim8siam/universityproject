Maastricht University (Universiteit Maastricht)

Established in 1976 in the historic Roman-founded city of Maastricht, Maastricht University (Universiteit Maastricht - UM) is widely recognized as the most international university in the Netherlands, with over 55% of its student body originating from outside the country. Renowned globally for pioneering Problem-Based Learning (PBL) in Europe, UM excels in European and International Law, Cognitive Neuroscience, Data Science and AI, and Sustainable Health. Doctoral researchers are employed as salaried academic staff members under Dutch university agreements. Explore verified academic courses, scholarship packages, and faculty leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited university in an aligned discipline with high academic standing (Dutch grading equivalent 7.0/10 or international GPA 3.2+/4.0).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (or equivalent 120 ECTS postgraduate degree) in an aligned discipline with demonstrable thesis research capability.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in writing); higher for law and psychology (IELTS 7.0/TOEFL 100).
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral candidates, an extensive 3 to 5-page research plan detailing theoretical context, research questions, experimental design, and prospective UM faculty mentors.
Application fee
€100 handling fee for non-EU applicants with non-Dutch diplomas via Studielink and MyUM portals (reimbursed upon registration).
Required documentation
Certified university transcripts, degree certificates, detailed curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and two academic recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In the Netherlands, PhD candidates are not students but university employees ('promovendus') hired on salaried contracts governed by the Collective Labour Agreement for Dutch Universities (CAO-NU). Doctoral salaries start at €2,770/month gross in year 1 and rise to €3,539/month gross in year 4, plus an 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% end-of-year bonus (SEK/EUR zero tuition charged). For Master's programs, the UM Holland-High Potential Scholarship covers full tuition fees plus an annual living allowance of €15,300.
