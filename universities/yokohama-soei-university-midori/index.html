Yokohama Sōei University

Yokohama Sōei University is an accredited private healthcare and education institution in Midori-ku, Yokohama, celebrated for its rigorous Nursing and Child Education faculties with exceptional national licensure pass rates.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating state-of-the-art clinical simulation suites and early childhood demonstration classrooms, Yokohama Sōei prepares certified nurses, public health nurses, and licensed preschool/kindergarten educators. The university maintains extensive clinical practicum affiliations with top Kanagawa hospitals.

Research admissions & international criteria

Applicants must possess a recognized high school diploma. International candidates require JLPT N1 or N2 for healthcare curricula, accompanied by an entrance essay and rigorous structured personal interview.
