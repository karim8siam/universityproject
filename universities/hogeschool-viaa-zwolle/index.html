Hogeschool Viaa: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located in Zwolle within the province of Overijssel, Hogeschool Viaa has been ranked multiple times as the #1 medium-sized university of applied sciences in the Netherlands by Keuzegids Hbo. Rooted in values of Christian social responsibility, Viaa delivers accredited four-year bachelor's degrees in Nursing, Social Work, Primary Teacher Education (PABO), and Theology, fostering close-knit academic mentorship, clinical simulation wards, and leading applied research in youth care and community wellbeing.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Havo, Vwo, or Mbo level 4 diploma; matching interview and language readiness
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Dutch (primary instructional language) with specialized international nursing exchange semesters in English
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
€0
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
