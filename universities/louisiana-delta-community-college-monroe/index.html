Louisiana Delta Community College

Louisiana Delta CC · Monroe, Louisiana, United States

Louisiana Delta Community College is a comprehensive public community college headquartered in Monroe, Louisiana. LDCC operates eight campuses across northeast Louisiana, providing industry-aligned training in process technology, industrial maintenance, nursing, and business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

LDCC is a vital workforce training hub for the Gulf Coast industrial corridor. Programs include Process Technology (PTEC) certified by the North American Process Technology Alliance (NAPTA), paramedicine, and university transfer associate degrees.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Open-door admissions policy requires high school diploma or equivalent (HiSET/GED). Selective healthcare and technical programs require criminal background checks, drug screenings, and placement testing thresholds.
