SSML San Marco - Scuola Superiore per Mediatori Linguistici di Vicenza

Master simultaneous conference interpreting, specialized legal-business translation, and diplomatic communications at SSML San Marco in Vicenza.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Authorized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR D.M. 04/08/2003), SSML San Marco in Vicenza is an elite higher institute for linguistic mediation, training simultaneous interpreters, technical translators, and international communication specialists across European and Asian languages.

Institution type
MUR-Authorized Higher Institute for Linguistic Mediators (Classe L-12)
Campus location
Corso Santi Felice e Fortunato Campus, Central Vicenza
Accreditation
Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) / D.M. 04/08/2003
Admissions channel
Language admission test in September and October (rolling until filled)
Instruction language
Italian, English, German, Spanish, French, Russian, and Chinese
Financial aid & support
Regione Veneto ESU Scholarships, SSML Academic Merit Vouchers

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Degree in Mediazione Linguistica (Classe L-12) legally equivalent to Italian university bachelor's
  • Simultaneous conference interpreting booths and SDL Trados computer-assisted translation suites
  • Strong corporate ties across Veneto's high-export manufacturing and luxury fashion districts
  • Specialized tracks in International Business Diplomacy, Digital Communication, and Legal Translation

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Corso di Laurea Triennale in Mediazione Linguistica (Classe L-12) (Undergraduate Degree) — Three-year professional bachelor's preparing interpreters and translators in two mandatory foreign languages plus specialized sector terminology.
  • Master in Traduzione Specialistica e Localizzazione Digitale (Master's Degree) — Professional master's covering software localization, medical-pharmaceutical translation, and neural translation post-editing.
  • Corso di Perfezionamento in Interpretariato di Conferenza (Advanced Specialization) — High-intensity postgraduate diploma focusing on simultaneous, consecutive, and chuchotage interpreting in EU institutional contexts.
