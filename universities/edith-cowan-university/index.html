Edith Cowan University

Named after the first woman elected to an Australian Parliament, Edith Cowan University (ECU) in Perth, Western Australia, is one of the world's top young universities (ranked in the world's top 100 young universities). Celebrated internationally as the headquarters of the national Cyber Security Cooperative Research Centre (Cyber Security CRC), the ECU Security Research Institute (SRI), the Exercise Medicine Research Institute (EMRI - world leaders in oncology exercise medicine), and the Western Australian Academy of Performing Arts (WAAPA), ECU provides cutting-edge research facilities and generous postgraduate scholarships.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ECU Australian Government Research Training Program (AGRTP) & Cyber Security Scholarships

Premier Commonwealth and university-funded doctoral scholarship providing complete financial support, tuition waivers, and health coverage for outstanding international research scholars.

ECU PhD in Cybersecurity & Cyber Threat Intelligence (Security Research Institute)

Doctoral research conducted within the ECU Security Research Institute (SRI) and Cyber Security CRC, designing AI-driven malware detection tools, zero-day exploit mitigations, and automated threat hunting pipelines.

ECU PhD in Exercise Medicine & Oncology (Exercise Medicine Research Institute)

World-leading doctoral training at the Exercise Medicine Research Institute (EMRI), investigating exercise as medicine to suppress cancer tumor growth, alleviate chemotherapy toxicity, and enhance patient survivorship.

ECU Master of Cyber Security (English-Taught)

Two-year professional Master's degree accredited by the Australian Computer Society (ACS), delivering advanced training in cloud security, ethical hacking, digital forensics, and security governance at ECU's Joondalup campus.

Centre for Marine Ecosystems Research (CMER) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year research appointment at the Centre for Marine Ecosystems Research, examining blue carbon sequestration in Australian seagrass meadows, coastal mangrove restoration, and marine heatwave recovery.

ECU Security Operations Centre (SOC) Summer Cybersecurity Traineeship

Eight-week intensive summer research placement working inside ECU's operational Security Operations Centre, analyzing live security event telemetry, incident response logs, and automated defense rules.

ECU Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Exercise Oncology & Clinical Trials

Full-time research role at the Exercise Medicine Research Institute, coordinating clinical randomized controlled trials evaluating high-intensity interval training for prostate and breast cancer patients receiving systemic therapy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years) from an accredited university with a minimum credit average (65% or 5.0/7.0 GPA scale) in a relevant discipline; computing degrees require programming fundamentals.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours, or recognized Master's degree with a substantial thesis component (minimum 25% research) demonstrating independent research competence.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no individual band below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 84 (minimum 17 in all sections); Nursing, Health, and Education require IELTS 7.0 (minimum 7.0 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
A formal research proposal of 1,500 to 2,000 words outlining research objectives, context, methodology, significance, and identified ECU supervisory team.
Application fee
AUD $0 application fee for direct online postgraduate research applications.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree award certificates, comprehensive CV, research proposal, and 2 academic referee reports.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Higher Degree by Research (HDR) candidates awarded Australian Government Research Training Program (AGRTP) or ECU Higher Degree by Research Scholarships receive 100% full international tuition fee offsets for up to 3.5 years, along with a tax-free annual living stipend of AUD $33,500 to $35,000 per year and standard overseas student health cover (OSHC).
