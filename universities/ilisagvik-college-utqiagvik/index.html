Iḷisaġvik College

Iḷisaġvik · Utqiaġvik, Alaska, United States

Iḷisaġvik College is Alaska’s only federally recognized Tribal College, located in Utqiaġvik (formerly Barrow) on the Arctic Ocean, celebrated for indigenous leadership, Arctic environmental science, and vocational trades.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Rooted in the Iñupiat values of harmony with nature, linguistic preservation, and subsistence living, Iḷisaġvik provides accredited associate and bachelor's degrees, heavy equipment maintenance, Arctic tundra ecology, and indigenous language revitalization.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Operates an open-door admission policy welcoming all students, with specialized native language assessment and vocational apprenticeship placement.
