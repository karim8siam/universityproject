University of Applied Sciences Leiden: Bio-Sciences, Digital Forensics & Health Guide

Located inside the prestigious Leiden Bio Science Park—Europe's top life sciences cluster—University of Applied Sciences Leiden (Hogeschool Leiden) is an innovative Dutch higher education institution. Established in 1986, the university is renowned for genomic analysis, digital forensics, medical diagnostic laboratory sciences, and community healthcare innovation.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

NL Scholarship (Holland Scholarship) at Hogeschool Leiden

Government-supported study award for non-EU/EEA international students entering full-time undergraduate or master's degrees in biotechnology and health sciences at Hogeschool Leiden.

Master of Science in Applied Sciences (Biotechnology and Diagnostics)

Applied master's degree developed in partnership with multinational biotech pharmaceutical companies in Leiden focusing on antibody engineering, assay development, and diagnostics.

Professional Doctorate (PD) in Applied Health and Bio-Sciences

Four-year Dutch national Professional Doctorate (PD) program bridging university research and life science industry practice at the Leiden Centre for Applied Bioscience.

Leiden Bio Science Park Metabolomics & Genome Sequencing Research Project

Applied research investigation at the Leiden Centre for Applied Bioscience (LCAB) deploying nanopore sequencing and mass spectrometry to track antimicrobial resistance genes.

Dutch National Police Digital Forensics & Cybercrime Investigation Internship

Twenty-week practical internship with cybersecurity forensic laboratories recovering encrypted digital evidence, reverse-engineering malware, and analyzing network telemetry.

LCAB Postdoctoral Lector Fellowship in Innovative Vaccine Technologies

Two-year research appointment at Hogeschool Leiden directing student research teams and collaborating with Janssen and Leiden University Medical Center on mRNA vaccine delivery.

Hogeschool Leiden Talent Scholarship in Data Science and Bio-Informatics

Merit-based tuition reduction awarded by the university foundation to gifted international students pursuing interdisciplinary computational biology and data science studies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in biotechnology, biology, computer science, or healthcare from a recognized institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Professional Doctorate (PD) in Health and Applied Science; entry requires Master's credential and collaborative industry research appointment.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught master's and research programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for postgraduate and Professional Doctorate entry: 1,500-word applied research proposal detailing collaboration with Leiden Bio Science Park partners.
Application fee
EUR 0 for Dutch national Studielink registration (some specialized programs charge EUR 100 handling fee for foreign diploma evaluations).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, detailed diploma supplement with ECTS credits, CV, motivation letter, and verified English proficiency certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

For EU/EEA citizens, statutory tuition is set by the Dutch government at approximately EUR 2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA international students, institutional tuition ranges from EUR 8,900 to EUR 11,500 per year. International students can apply for the prestigious NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) providing EUR 5,000 in one-time study assistance.
