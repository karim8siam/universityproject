Lamar University

Lamar Univ · Beaumont, Texas, United States

Lamar University is a Carnegie R2 doctoral public research university in Beaumont, Texas, and a member of the Texas State University System. Located in the heart of the Gulf Coast energy corridor, Lamar is a leader in chemical engineering, port management, and deaf studies.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lamar comprises the College of Engineering, College of Education and Human Development, College of Business, and Center for Advances in Port Management. Research focuses on industrial flaring reduction, wastewater treatment, petrochemical logistics, and speech and hearing sciences.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school class rank and GPA under test-optional guidelines. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.75 to 3.0 GPA, departmental essays, and discipline prerequisites.
