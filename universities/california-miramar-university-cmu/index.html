Institution Overview & Profile

California Miramar University (CMU), located in San Diego, California, is an accredited private university specializing in practical, industry-aligned bachelor, master, and doctoral degree programs in artificial intelligence, computer science, business administration, cyber security, and sports management.

Institution TypePrivate Polytechnic University
Main CampusSan Diego Main Campus (Camino Del Rio North), California
AccreditationDistance Education Accrediting Commission (DEAC) / CHEA Recognized
Admissions RouteRolling bi-monthly term admissions with online application
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 6.0/6.5 / TOEFL iBT 71+ / Duolingo 100+)
Financial Aid & GrantsCMU International Merit Scholarships, Military Tuition Discounts & Corporate Grants

Key Academic Strengths

  • STEM-designated Master of Science programs eligible for up to 3 years of post-study OPT work authorization in the USA
  • Curricular Practical Training (CPT) integrated throughout graduate programs for immediate workforce experience
  • Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) designed for working executives and senior technology leaders
  • Dynamic San Diego location with active connections to southern California biotech, defense, and software firms
