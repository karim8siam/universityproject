Tio Business School: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded over 50 years ago and operating flagship campuses in Amsterdam and Utrecht, Tio Business School consistently achieves #1 rankings in the Dutch Keuzegids Hbo for over a decade. Tio provides small-scale, accelerated degree programs (enabling students to earn an accredited bachelor's in just three years) taught in English and Dutch, backed by mandatory international internships, corporate networking, and top-tier hospitality management credentials.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Secondary school diploma (Havo, Vwo, IB, or international 12-year diploma); admissions intake interview
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
English & Dutch (Full bachelor's and master's degrees offered completely in English)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
€95 (Intake assessment fee)
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
