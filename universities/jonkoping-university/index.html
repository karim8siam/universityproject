Jönköping University

Located on the southern shores of Lake Vättern, Jönköping University (Högskolan i Jönköping - JU) is one of Sweden's leading non-governmental foundation universities, internationally renowned for Jönköping International Business School (JIBS - the first business school in Sweden to hold double EQUIS and AACSB accreditations) and the School of Engineering. Renowned as Europe's top center for family business and entrepreneurship research, and a national hub for cast iron materials and industrial product realization, JU offers salaried PhD positions and career-oriented international Master's programs.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Jönköping University Scholarship for International Master's Students (30% Tuition Waiver)

Merit-based scholarship awarded to high-achieving non-EU/EEA students applying to English-taught master's degrees in business, engineering, and digital technologies at Jönköping University.

JIBS Salaried PhD Position in Entrepreneurship & Family Business

Four-year salaried doctoral research post based at the Centre for Family Entrepreneurship and Ownership (CeFEO), Europe's premier institute for family business research, governance, and intergenerational succession.

Jönköping Master of Science (MSc) in Materials and Manufacturing (English-Taught)

Comprehensive two-year Master's degree focusing on casting technologies, component design, failure analysis, and circular metallic materials in partnership with Scandinavia's foundry industry.

Jönköping PhD in Industrial Product Realisation & Smart Manufacturing

Doctoral research conducted within the research environment SPARK, advancing digital engineering workflows, cyber-physical production lines, and closed-loop manufacturing automation.

Jönköping Master of Science (MSc) in AI Engineering (English-Taught)

Two-year Master of Science addressing deep learning, computer vision, natural language understanding, and edge AI deployment in autonomous vehicles and medical robotics.

Jönköping Foundry Technology & Component Casting Summer Traineeship

Eight-week hands-on industrial casting internship at the School of Engineering's pilot foundry, testing sand mold permeability, thermal analysis, and microstructure evolution in cast iron and aluminum alloys.

JIBS Assistant Professor in International Entrepreneurship & Innovation Policy

Tenure-track Assistant Professorship delivering teaching in international business master's programs and conducting empirical research at CeFEO on corporate entrepreneurship and digital venture creation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) from an internationally recognized higher education institution with a major in business, engineering, computing, or social sciences (minimum 65-70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS credits total, with at least 60 ECTS at advanced level) or equivalent international qualification; selection is based on academic merit, research quality, and faculty fit.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3 to 5 page doctoral research plan addressing research problem, theoretical context, proposed methodology, and significance.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying through the central Swedish universityadmissions.se portal; €0 fee for EU citizens.
Required documentation
Certificates and diplomas of completed degrees, official transcripts, documentation of English proficiency, copy of passport, and Master's thesis.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

At Jönköping University and throughout Sweden, PhD candidacies are classified as salaried state/foundation employment (doktorandanställning). Admitted PhD candidates pay zero tuition fees and receive a collective-bargaining salaried monthly wage of SEK 31,000 to SEK 34,500 per month (approx. SEK 372,000 to SEK 414,000/year gross) with full Swedish national healthcare, parental leave, and pension benefits. International Master's students can compete for Jönköping University Scholarships covering 30% of tuition fees.
