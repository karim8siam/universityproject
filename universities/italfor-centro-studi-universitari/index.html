ITALFOR University (Istituto Tecnico e Accademico di Formazione e Ricerca) in Bari is a leading Mediterranean higher education and continuous academic training center specializing in biomedical sciences, telecommunications law, digital economics, and criminology.

Academic overview & excellence

Based in the metropolitan port capital of Bari, ITALFOR provides structured university tutoring, laboratory certifications, and degree programs in convention with accredited public and telematic universities. The institute features modern forensic simulation rooms, digital legal databases, corporate finance seminar suites, and preparatory modules for professional national concorsi. ITALFOR scholars participate in Mediterranean maritime trade studies, cybersecurity jurisprudence, and public healthcare administrative research.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to degree tracks and specialization diplomas administered at ITALFOR requires possession of an accredited Italian secondary school diploma (Maturità) or international equivalent evaluated via CIMEA. Post-graduate programs require a recognized Laurea Magistrale or equivalent 5-year degree. Applicants submit formal enrollment forms, academic records, copy of identification, and proof of Italian language competency (B2 minimum for non-native applicants).
