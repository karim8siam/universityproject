University of South Carolina Upstate Greenville Campus

USC Upstate Greenville · Greenville, South Carolina, United States

The University of South Carolina Upstate Greenville Campus provides high-demand bachelor's degree completion and graduate programs at the University Center of Greenville (UCG). Partnering directly with Prisma Health, BMW Manufacturing, and regional tech firms, USC Upstate Greenville delivers top-ranked nursing, engineering technology management, and business analytics education in South Carolina's economic corridor.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Greenville · Advanced Healthcare, Engineering Technology & Business Innovation. The University of South Carolina Upstate Greenville Campus provides high-demand bachelor's degree completion and graduate programs at the University Center of Greenville (UCG). Partnering directly with Prisma Health, BMW Manufacturing, and regional tech firms, USC Upstate Greenville delivers top-ranked nursing, engineering technology management, and business analytics education in South Carolina's economic corridor.

Research admissions & international qualifications

60 transferable semester credits or completed Associate of Arts/Associate of Science degree with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 (higher GPA required for nursing and engineering programs). TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Fall priority deadline July 1, 2027; Spring priority deadline December 1, 2026.
