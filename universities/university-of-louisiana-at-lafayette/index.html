University of Louisiana at Lafayette

UL Lafayette · Lafayette, Louisiana, United States

The University of Louisiana at Lafayette is a public Carnegie R1 research university in Lafayette, Louisiana, celebrated as the second-largest university in the state. UL Lafayette is a leader in computer science, coastal ecology, Francophone studies, and informatics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UL Lafayette established the nation's first Master of Science in Computer Science in 1962 and operates the Center for Advanced Computer Studies (CACS), the Ecology Center, and the National Incident Management Systems and Advanced Resilience Institute (NIMSAT).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.5) and standardized test scores (ACT/SAT). Graduate computer science and engineering programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, transcripts, and faculty review.
