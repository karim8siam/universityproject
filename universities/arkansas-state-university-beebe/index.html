ASU Beebe: Veterinary Technology, Agriculture & Computer Systems Guide

Arkansas State University-Beebe is an accredited public university campus operating locations in Beebe, Searcy, Heber Springs, and Little Rock Air Force Base, celebrated for its AVMA-accredited veterinary technology, agricultural equipment mechanics, computer systems, and nursing programs.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
ASU Beebe specializes in associate degrees (AA, AS, AAS) and professional technical certificates with seamless 2+2 bachelor's articulation to Arkansas State University (Jonesboro).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate institution offering associate degrees; upper-level and graduate degrees offered through university center partnerships.
Language proficiency

Research statement / Proposal
Online university application with declaration of degree major.
Application fee
Free online admission application.
Required documentation
Official high school or prior college transcripts, ACT/SAT or Accuplacer placement scores, and immunization records (MMR).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In-state tuition is approximately $107 per credit hour; out-of-state tuition is approximately $182 per credit hour. Arkansas Academic Challenge scholarships, ASU-Beebe Foundation merit awards, and military tuition assistance for Little Rock AFB are accessible.
