Canadian Mennonite University: Peace Studies, Environmental Science & Music Guide

Chartered by the Province of Manitoba in 1998, Canadian Mennonite University (CMU) is an accredited university in Winnipeg renowned for peacebuilding, ecological sustainability, music therapy, and interdisciplinary humanities. Blending rigorous analytical study with community-rooted experiential education, CMU offers undergraduate science degrees and graduate research in peace and conflict transformation.

Merit-based university award supporting exceptional master's scholars pursuing advanced studies in conflict resolution, restorative justice, and community peacebuilding.

Master of Arts in Peacebuilding and Collaborative Development

Two-year graduate degree delivering theoretical grounding and applied field competencies in mediation, structural justice, community peacebuilding, and human security.

Joint Doctoral Research Fellowship in Restorative Justice and Peace Studies

Doctoral research fellowship conducted through CMU's Centre for Faith and Peacemaking examining systemic reconciliation models and restorative justice frameworks.

CMU Prairie Agro-Ecology and Watershed Research Project

Field-based scientific investigation monitoring wetland biodiversity, regenerative agricultural soil matrices, and riverine ecosystem resilience across Manitoba.

Metis and First Nations Community Mediation Practicum

Supervised 300-hour applied practicum pairing students with Indigenous restorative justice programs, urban legal aid initiatives, and community reconciliation councils.

CMU Centre for Resilience Research Fellowship in Ecological Economics

Research appointment at the CMU Centre for Resilience studying regenerative community enterprise models, urban food systems, and carbon mitigation policies.

Manitoba Academic Excellence Entrance Award in Environmental Studies

Entrance scholarship recognizing outstanding students enrolling in environmental science, interdisciplinary ecology, and resource conservation programs at CMU.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized four-year undergraduate baccalaureate degree with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0 (or equivalent B grade average) in pertinent discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative research opportunities; direct doctoral admissions via joint Manitoba university consortium protocols.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each test component), TOEFL iBT 80 (with minimum 19 in each sub-score), or Duolingo English Test 115+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate research: 1,500-word statement of academic interest and scholarly objectives addressing conflict transformation, theology, or ecological ethics.
Application fee
$80 CAD for domestic Canadian applicants; $150 CAD for international applicants.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts from all post-secondary institutions, two academic references, curriculum vitae, and personal statement of research purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Canadian Mennonite University is approximately $750 CAD per 3-credit course for domestic students and $1,320 CAD per 3-credit course for international candidates. Comprehensive entrance scholarships, peacebuilder fellowships up to $6,000 CAD, and on-campus academic assistantships offer robust financial support.
