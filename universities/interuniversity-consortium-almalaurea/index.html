Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1994 at the University of Bologna with the support of the Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR), AlmaLaurea is an accredited public inter-university consortium uniting 80 Italian universities. It is internationally recognized as Europe's premier observatory for graduate profiling, statistical education research, and academic labor market mobility.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • National graduate profiling, social origin tracking, and academic equity analysis
  • Labor market outcome modeling and longitudinal wage econometrics
  • Higher education quality assurance, dropout mitigation, and curriculum evaluation
  • Big data analytics on university-to-workplace transitions across the European Union

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • AlmaLaurea Postdoctoral Fellowship in Higher Education Econometrics (Postdoctoral Fellowship) — Advanced empirical research analyzing longitudinal microdata on over 3.5 million Italian and European university graduates.
  • Graduate Internship in Statistical Data Mining & Survey Analytics (Graduate Practicum) — Intensive six-month training in survey weighting, non-response imputation, and econometric modeling within the Italian National Statistical System (SISTAN).
  • Executive Masterclass in University Quality Assurance & Labor Placement Metrics (Professional Certificate) — Specialized training for university chancellors, evaluators, and ANVUR accreditation officers on performance indicators.
