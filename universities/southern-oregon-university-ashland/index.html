Southern Oregon University

Southern Oregon · Ashland, Oregon, United States

Southern Oregon University (SOU) is an acclaimed public liberal arts university located in Ashland, Oregon, in the foothills of the Siskiyou and Cascade mountain ranges. Renowned for its close artistic partnership with the Tony Award-winning Oregon Shakespeare Festival, SOU is also celebrated for environmental studies (surrounded by the Cascade-Siskiyou National Monument), outdoor adventure leadership, and sustainability initiatives, including being the first Bee Campus USA and committing to 100% renewable energy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SOU comprises the School of Arts and Communication, the School of Business, Communication, and the Environment, the School of Education, and the School of Social Sciences. It features the Schneider Museum of Art, the Digital Media Center (in partnership with Rogue Valley PBS), modern forensic chemistry labs, and the SOU Sustainability Farm.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.50, average 3.4) and college-preparatory coursework on a test-optional basis. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, personal statement, official transcripts, and program-specific letters of reference.
