Ostfalia University of Applied Sciences

Ostfalia University of Applied Sciences (Ostfalia Hochschule) across Wolfenbüttel, Wolfsburg, Salzgitter, and Suderburg is Lower Saxony's major regional applied engineering, automotive technologies, and water management powerhouse.

Academic overview & research priorities

With four dynamic campuses situated throughout the industrial triangle of Lower Saxony, Ostfalia operates 12 faculties: Automotive Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil & Environmental Engineering, Supply Engineering, Law, Social Work, Business, and Transport-Sports-Tourism-Media. The university enjoys world-class industrial partnerships with Volkswagen AG in Wolfsburg, Salzgitter AG steel, and major regional water and environmental authorities.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or international equivalent evaluated via uni-assist. Master's degree tracks require a relevant bachelor's degree with a minimum grade equivalent of 2.5. German language proficiency (DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4) is required for German-taught degrees; English tracks require IELTS 6.5.
