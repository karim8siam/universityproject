Kyoto Prefectural University

Kyoto Prefectural University, located adjacent to the UNESCO World Heritage Shimogamo Shrine in Kyoto, is a historic public institution renowned for its Graduate School of Life and Environmental Sciences, traditional Japanese garden architecture, and classical Japanese literature.

Academic overview & excellence

KPU offers intensive bachelor's and graduate programs in bioenvironmental sciences, agricultural genetics, architectural heritage conservation, and public welfare. It maintains experimental agricultural farms and botanical research stations in suburban Kyoto.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions requires the National Center Test (Common Test for University Admissions) or the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students (EJU) with JLPT N1/N2. Graduate research programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, research plan, and departmental professor acceptance.

Scholarship

Kyoto Prefectural Government Honor Scholarship for International Researchers

Prefectural merit scholarship for international graduate scholars conducting advanced research in environmental sustainability or Japanese cultural heritage.

Funding: ¥100,000 monthly living stipend plus full exemption from prefectural tuition fees Tuition: ¥535,800/year (fully exempted for scholarship recipients) Deadline: January 31, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Life and Environmental Sciences

Two-year research master's degree exploring Kyoto traditional vegetable (Kyo-yasai) genetics, soil microbial communities, and watershed biodiversity.

Funding: JASSO Monbukagakusho honors scholarship eligibility (¥48,000/month) Tuition: ¥535,800 per academic year Deadline: January 15, 2027
PhD

Doctor of Philosophy in Environmental & Agricultural Sciences

Three-year doctoral curriculum specializing in plant pathology, molecular breeding of woody perennials, and biocontrol insect ecology.

Funding: JSPS DC1/DC2 Research Fellowship eligibility (¥200,000/month plus research budget) Tuition: ¥535,800 per year Deadline: February 15, 2027
Fellowship

Kyoto Historic Timber Architecture & Garden Preservation Fellowship

Postgraduate fellowship analyzing seismic retrofitting of wooden machiya townhouses and historic temple garden water conservation techniques.

Funding: ¥1,800,000 annual research fellowship funded via Kyoto Heritage Trust Tuition: ¥0 Deadline: April 1, 2027
Internship

Kyoto Botanical Gardens & KPU Herbarium Field Practicum

Three-month summer botanical practicum curating living collections, endangered alpine plant cryopreservation, and greenhouse climate controls.

Funding: ¥80,000 monthly living allowance at Kyoto Botanical Gardens Tuition: ¥0 Deadline: April 30, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Arts in Japanese Literature & Cultural Heritage

Four-year undergraduate degree exploring Heian court literature, classical waka poetry, Kyoto historical archives, and museum curatorial studies.

Funding: Tuition waiver options for international students (30–50% reduction) Tuition: ¥535,800 per year Deadline: February 5, 2027
Course

Six-week professional certificate exploring lactic acid bacteria in traditional Tsukemono pickles, koji fermentation microbiology, and gastro-tourism economics.

Funding: Kyoto Prefectural Food Industry Association subsidy available Tuition: ¥120,000 total course fee Deadline: August 15, 2027
