Newcastle University: Ageing & Health, Energy & Computing Guide

Originating as the School of Medicine and Surgery in 1834, Newcastle University is a world-renowned Russell Group research university in northeast England. Celebrated globally for the National Innovation Centre for Ageing (NICA), the National Innovation Centre for Data (NICD), marine engineering, and urban water resilience, Newcastle delivers world-class graduate programs and funded PhD studentships.

Newcastle University Overseas Research Scholarship (NUORS) in Healthy Ageing

Prestigious doctoral scholarship at the Newcastle University Biosciences Institute investigating cellular senescence, mitochondrial dysfunction, and cognitive health in human ageing.

MSc in Cloud Computing and Scalable Distributed Systems

One-year advanced master's program taught at the Urban Sciences Building covering cloud microservices, Kubernetes orchestration, serverless computing, and edge data architectures.

EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training (CDT) in Geospatial Systems (Newcastle-Nottingham)

Four-year integrated doctoral training program bridging satellite Earth observation, lidar mapping, spatial artificial intelligence, and digital twins for resilient infrastructure.

National Innovation Centre for Data (NICD) Enterprise AI Research Project

Collaborative research project at the National Innovation Centre for Data deploying large language models and predictive analytics to solve real-world industrial and public sector challenges.

Port of Tyne & Offshore Renewable Energy Catapult Engineering Internship

Six-to-twelve month placement at the Blyth offshore testing facility evaluating blade aerodynamics, turbine foundation fatigue, and subsea cable dynamics for North Sea wind farms.

Newcastle Academic Track (NUAcT) Postdoctoral Fellowship in Global Biogeochemistry

Five-year prestigious independent research fellowship with guaranteed progression to a permanent academic faculty position, investigating ocean carbon sinks and trace nutrient fluxes.

Vice-Chancellor's Excellence Scholarship for Postgraduate Studies

Competitive merit scholarship providing 50% tuition reductions to high-calibre international scholars entering postgraduate taught master's degree programs at Newcastle University.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A 2:1 honours degree (or international equivalent with GPA 3.2+/4.0 or 65%+) in a relevant subject from a recognized university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-class or strong master's degree (Merit or Distinction) with demonstrated research dissertation; submission of aligned doctoral research proposal.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (with no sub-score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 90 (with minimum 20 in all components); 7.0 for medical and law faculties.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research proposal (1,500 to 2,000 words) defining problem significance, literature review, proposed methodology, and supervisor alignment.
Application fee
GBP 0 for standard postgraduate applications; GBP 50 for selected competitive master's programs.
Required documentation
Full academic transcripts, degree award certificates, two academic reference letters, personal statement of purpose, curriculum vitae, and English certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

UK home postgraduate research tuition follows standard UKRI fee rates (approximately GBP 4,786/year). International tuition for laboratory and engineering master's programs ranges from GBP 26,400 to GBP 31,500 per year. Fully funded UKRI (EPSRC, BBSRC, NERC) and Newcastle University Overseas Research Scholarships (NUORS) cover 100% of international tuition and provide a tax-free living stipend of GBP 19,237 per year.
