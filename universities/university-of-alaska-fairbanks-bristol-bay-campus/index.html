University of Alaska Fairbanks Bristol Bay Campus

UAF Bristol Bay · Dillingham, Alaska, United States

The University of Alaska Fairbanks Bristol Bay Campus serves southwest Alaska from its hub in Dillingham, providing vital regional education in fisheries management, sustainable environmental science, Yup'ik indigenous studies, and allied healthcare. UAF Bristol Bay bridges indigenous ecological knowledge with Western scientific methodologies across the world's most productive wild salmon ecosystem.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or regional equivalent. Open admissions for certificate and associate programs; specific prerequisites for allied health and scientific tracks. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates whose prior secondary instruction was not conducted in English. Fall semester priority March 15, 2027; Spring semester priority November 1, 2026.
