LINXS Lund: Synchrotron X-ray, Neutron Scattering & Structural Biology Guide

The LINXS Institute of Advanced Neutron and X-ray Science is a premier advanced research institute hosted at Lund University in Skåne, Sweden. Positioned at the epicentre of European mega-science alongside MAX IV—the world's most brilliant synchrotron X-ray radiation source—and the European Spallation Source (ESS)—the world's most powerful neutron source—LINXS serves as a global collaborative nexus. It unites world-leading physicists, chemists, structural biologists, and materials scientists across thematic research themes in soft matter, life sciences, energy materials, and cultural heritage.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Master of Science programs in Physics (Synchrotron Radiation Research), Chemistry, or Biophysics administered through Lund University Faculty of Science; requires Bachelor's with 90 ECTS in physics/chemistry.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year salaried doctoral studentship in neutron or synchrotron physics/materials science; requires Master of Science in physics, physical chemistry, or structural biology with first-class thesis.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 20 in writing); English 6 proficiency required for all graduate researchers.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral and postdoctoral appointments: 3-5 page research project plan outlining synchrotron/neutron experimental methodology, beamline feasibility, and theoretical framework.
Application fee
900 SEK for master's programs via UniversityAdmissions.se; 0 SEK for doctoral employment applications.
Required documentation
Bachelor's and Master's diplomas, complete transcripts, master's thesis, publications list, CV, two academic recommendation letters, and cover letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 0 SEK for EU/EEA citizens. International master's tuition is approximately 145,000 to 175,000 SEK per year. Doctoral researchers are employed as full-time university staff with a starting salary of approximately 31,500 to 35,000 SEK per month (0 SEK tuition and full Swedish pension and healthcare benefits).
