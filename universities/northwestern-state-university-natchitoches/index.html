Northwestern State University

Northwestern State · Natchitoches, Louisiana, United States

Northwestern State University of Louisiana is a distinguished public university located in historic Natchitoches, Louisiana. Renowned for its pioneering nursing school, the Louisiana Scholars' College, military education, and creative arts.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NSU is home to the Louisiana Scholars' College (the state's designated honors college in the liberal arts and sciences), the College of Nursing and Allied Health, and the Center for Possum Cove archaeological surveys. It was one of the earliest US universities to launch an electronic campus.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school diploma with a core GPA of 2.35+ or ACT score of 20+. Graduate admissions require a baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale.
