Kentucky State University

Kentucky State · Frankfort, Kentucky, United States

Kentucky State University is a public historically Black land-grant university (HBCU) in Frankfort, Kentucky. Founded in 1886, KSU is internationally renowned for its pioneering Aquaculture Research Center, sustainable organic agriculture, and public administration programs.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

KSU houses the College of Agriculture, Community, and the Sciences, the School of Business and Technology, and the Atwood Research Facility. It is globally recognized for pawpaw fruit germplasm conservation, freshwater aquaculture nutrition, and environmental toxicology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5 recommended) and course preparation with test-optional paths. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.75 GPA, statement of goals, and discipline prerequisites.
