The Texas State University System

TSUS · Austin, Texas, United States

The Texas State University System (TSUS) is the oldest multi-campus higher education system in Texas, coordinating eight member institutions stretching from the Texas Hill Country to the Gulf Coast. Encompassing Texas State University in San Marcos, Sam Houston State University in Huntsville, Lamar University in Beaumont, Sul Ross State University in Alpine, and three technical state colleges, TSUS drives transformative education in water resources, maritime port logistics, and criminal justice.
