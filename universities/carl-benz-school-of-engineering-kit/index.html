Carl Benz School of Engineering (KIT): B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering Guide

The Carl Benz School of Engineering is the international mechanical engineering college of the prestigious Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), a German University of Excellence and member of the TU9 alliance. Named after Karl Benz, inventor of the automobile and KIT alumnus, the Carl Benz School delivers elite English-taught Bachelor of Science programs in Mechanical Engineering with specialized concentrations in Automotive Engineering, Energy Engineering, and Global Production Management. Students benefit from individual mentoring, modern labs, and close industrial ties with Porsche, Daimler, and Bosch.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduates transition directly into KIT's internationally acclaimed Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, or Energy Technologies; requires accredited Bachelor of Science in engineering with strong mathematical foundations.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral research conducted within KIT departments and research centres; requires recognized master's degree in engineering or physics with first-class honors.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 88 overall or IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section) for all English-language programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Motivation letter detailing technical passion, project experience, and rationale for studying mechanical engineering at KIT; SAT/ACT recommended for international high school leavers.
Application fee
€150 application processing fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school graduation certificate (Abitur, IB, or equivalent), transcripts of records, English proficiency score, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and SAT score (optional but recommended).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the elite Carl Benz School college program is approximately €7,000 per semester (including specialized tutoring, German language courses, and industrial excursions), plus the standard state tuition of €1,500 per semester for non-EU students and €180 student union fee. Merit-based scholarships and industrial sponsorships from German automotive leaders provide partial tuition waivers.
