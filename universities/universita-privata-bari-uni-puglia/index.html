Academic overview & excellence

Conveniently located near the central station in the metropolitan city of Bari, Uni Puglia Polo di Alta Formazione acts as an authorized university center partnering with accredited Italian universities (Pegaso, San Raffaele, Mercatorum). It delivers comprehensive degree pathways in law, primary education sciences, sports sciences, and financial economics, offering in-person tutoring, digital laboratory suites, and proctored university degree examinations.

Research admissions & criteria

Admissions follow standard Italian university criteria. For Laurea Triennale courses, applicants must hold an Italian high school diploma (Maturità) or a certified foreign equivalent (12 years of study). Laurea Magistrale and professional master's programs require a relevant undergraduate degree. Non-EU candidates require official translation, legalization, and Dichiarazione di Valore or CIMEA Statement of Comparability.
