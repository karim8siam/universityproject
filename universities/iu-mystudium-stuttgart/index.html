Academic overview & research priorities

The myStudium model merges small-group campus tutorials in central Stuttgart (2–3 days per week) with flexible on-demand digital learning. Students benefit from local study lounges, career coaching, peer study cohorts, and direct access to Stuttgart’s industrial and automotive powerhouse economy.

Research admissions & international criteria

Higher education entrance qualification (Abitur, Fachabitur, or completed vocational master certification). Flexible enrollment with monthly starts, test-optional admission, and personal study advisor consultation.
