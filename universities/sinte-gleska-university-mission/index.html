Sinte Gleska University

Sinte Gleska · Mission, South Dakota, United States

Sinte Gleska University (SGU) is a tribally chartered institution of higher education established by the Rosebud Sioux Tribe (Sicangu Lakota Oyate) in Mission, South Dakota. As a pioneering Tribal College and University (TCU), it was the first to offer accredited four-year baccalaureate and master's degrees founded on Lakota culture, philosophy, and tribal sovereignty.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SGU provides culturally grounded higher education spanning education, arts and sciences, tribal management, environmental science, and human services. Its curriculum integrates traditional Lakota epistemologies (Wolakota) with accredited academic standards, featuring the Lakota Studies Department and the Sicangu Heritage Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Sinte Gleska University maintains an open admissions policy welcoming enrolled tribal citizens and non-tribal students. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, official transcripts, letters of intent demonstrating dedication to tribal communities, and letters of recommendation.
