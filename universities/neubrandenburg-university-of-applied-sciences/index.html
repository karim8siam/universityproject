Institutional Overview

Neubrandenburg University of Applied Sciences (Hochschule Neubrandenburg), founded in 1991 in the Mecklenburg Lake District, is a forward-looking public institution recognized for agricultural sciences, food technology, geoinformatics, landscape architecture, nursing, and social work. The university is an innovation driver for northeast Germany's bioeconomy and rural development.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Center of excellence in food technology, pilot brewery, and functional food processing plants
  • Department of Geodesy and Geoinformatics with high-precision drone surveying and GIS laboratories
  • Comprehensive agricultural management programs supporting northern European agronomy
  • Applied nursing science and health care management research serving rural medical systems

Featured Academic Degrees & Curricula

Bachelor's Degree

Bachelor of Science in Food Technology (Lebensmitteltechnologie)

Seven-semester engineering degree focusing on food processing, product development, quality management, and biochemical food safety.

Master's Degree

Master of Engineering in Geodesy and Geoinformatics

Three-semester advanced program in spatial data infrastructure, satellite geodesy, photogrammetry, and 3D urban laser modeling.

Bachelor's Degree

Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (Agrarwirtschaft)

Comprehensive agricultural science program covering precision farming, animal husbandry, agricultural economics, and soil ecology.
