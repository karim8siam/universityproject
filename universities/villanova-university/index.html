Villanova University

Villanova · Villanova, Pennsylvania, United States

Villanova University is a premier private Augustinian Catholic doctoral university founded in 1842 on Philadelphia's historic Main Line in Villanova, Pennsylvania. Renowned for academic excellence at the Villanova School of Business (VSB), the College of Engineering, the Fitzpatrick College of Nursing, and the Charles Widger School of Law, Villanova combines rigorous scholarship with a strong ethical commitment to community service.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Villanova comprises six colleges: the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, Villanova School of Business (VSB), College of Engineering, M. Louise Fitzpatrick College of Nursing, Charles Widger School of Law, and the College of Professional Studies. The university operates the Drosdick Innovation Center and the Center for Resilient Water Systems.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly competitive through the Common Application. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a high GPA (typically 3.3+), statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 90+ or IELTS 7.0+).
