Fukuoka Jogakuin University: Cross-Cultural Communication, Psychology & Media Guide

Established in 1885 in Fukuoka, Fukuoka Jogakuin University is a distinguished private institution celebrated for empowering women and international scholars in foreign languages, clinical psychology, cultural studies, and digital media. With deep historic roots as Kyushu's first modern educational academy for women, Fukujo offers dynamic undergraduate and graduate degree courses emphasizing intercultural dialogue and global community leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of a 16-year school education curriculum resulting in an accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent recognized by the Japanese Ministry MEXT) with a minimum GPA of 2.8 on a 4.0 scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Graduate School of Human Sciences doctoral courses; requiring a Master's degree (Shushi) in psychology or humanities, research thesis, and oral examination.
Language proficiency
Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) N2 or N1 for Japanese-taught tracks; TOEFL iBT 78 or IELTS 6.0 for international cultural and English studies programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate applicants: 2,000-character (Japanese) or 1,200-word (English) research proposal (Kenkyu Keikakusho) articulating clear methodology and bibliographical review.
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY standard Japanese university graduate admission screening fee.
Required documentation
Official undergraduate graduation certificate and academic transcript, JLPT/TOEFL scorecard, research proposal, two letters of academic recommendation, and statement of financial support.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Fukuoka Jogakuin University is approximately ¥820,000 to ¥1,050,000 JPY per year (plus a one-time admission fee of ¥200,000 JPY). International students are eligible for a 30% to 50% tuition reduction scholarship based on academic review, in addition to JASSO monthly honors scholarships.
