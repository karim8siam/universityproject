University of Colorado Board of Regents

CU Board of Regents · Denver, Colorado, United States

The University of Colorado Board of Regents is the constitutional governing body of the University of Colorado System, overseeing four premier campuses: the flagship University of Colorado Boulder, the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus, the University of Colorado Denver, and the University of Colorado Colorado Springs (UCCS). Managing an annual budget of $5.9 billion and generating $1.5 billion in sponsored research, the Regents lead the nation in aerospace, quantum physics, and biomedical therapeutics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions standards established by the Board of Regents for all CU campuses. Comprehensive review of secondary school GPA, curriculum rigor, and personal statement. TOEFL iBT 79+ (100+ CU Boulder engineering) or IELTS 6.5+ across CU institutions. CU Boulder Early Action November 15, 2026; Regular decision January 15, 2027; comprehensive campuses rolling through July 1, 2027.
