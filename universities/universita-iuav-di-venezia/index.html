Università Iuav di Venezia: Architecture, Urban Resilience & Design Guide

Università Iuav di Venezia, established in 1926 in the historic heart of Venice, is Italy's premier public university dedicated exclusively to architecture, urban planning, design, visual arts, and conservation of historical coastal built heritage.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Veneto ESU Regional Architecture & Design Scholarship

Regional government award ensuring equal access to higher education for international and Italian students admitted to Iuav architecture and urban design programs.

Master of Science in Architecture (English Track)

Two-year European-accredited Master of Science taught entirely in English, training architects to address urban resilience, historic building retrofitting, and climate adaptation.

Ph.D. in Architecture, City and Design Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research program exploring Venetian lagoon urbanism, climate change adaptation of historic cities, circular construction materials, and design theory.

Venice Lagoon Climate Adaptation & MOSE Barrier Urban Impact Study

Interdisciplinary research study analyzing rising sea levels, salt marsh degradation, and architectural heritage preservation techniques across the historic lagoon of Venice.

Venice Biennale of Architecture & International Exhibition Practicum

Six-month architectural curatorial and exhibition design internship working alongside international pavilion curators and architectural installations at La Biennale di Venezia.

Iuav Postdoctoral Fellowship in Heritage Conservation & Material Diagnostics

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the LabSCo (Laboratory for the Testing of Materials and Structures) investigating non-destructive testing of historic masonry in marine environments.

Associate Professor in Sustainable Urbanism & Climate Resilient Cities

Permanent associate professorship in urban planning and design, leading advanced design studios, supervising doctoral research, and securing competitive European research funding.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (Laurea) in Architecture, Urban Planning, Design, or Arts (180 ECTS) with design portfolio review.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) in Architecture or related spatial discipline with high honors and research proposal.
Language proficiency
Italian-taught tracks: Italian B2 (CELI/CILS). English-taught tracks: English B2 (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 78).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive digital architecture/design portfolio (PDF) and 1,500-word statement of design philosophy or research intent.
Application fee
€30 application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, design portfolio, curriculum vitae, and passport scan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is determined progressively based on family income (ISEE indicator), ranging from €156 for eligible lower-income brackets up to approximately €2,750 per year. Regional Veneto scholarships (ESU) provide full tuition waivers, student housing, and living allowances.
