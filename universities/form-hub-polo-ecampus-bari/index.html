FORM-HUB Polo di Studio eCampus Bari is an accredited university teaching centre in Bari affiliated with Università Telematica eCampus, providing recognized academic degree programs, tutorial assistance, and digital learning infrastructure across Puglia.

Academic overview & excellence

FORM-HUB offers accredited bachelor's and master's degree paths in economics, law, psychology, and engineering. The centre provides in-person exam sessions, personalized academic tutoring, career placement services, and state-accredited professional certifications.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires an accredited Italian high school diploma (Maturità) or international equivalent with Declaration of Value. Flexible year-round matriculation is available without preliminary quota restrictions. Tutoring programs guide students through digital coursework and in-person examinations.
