Texas College

Texas College · Tyler, Texas, United States

Texas College is a historic private, historically Black college (HBCU) founded in 1894 by Christian Methodist Episcopal (CME) ministers in Tyler, Texas. Grounded in Christian principles and dedicated to empowering underserved scholars, Texas College provides personalized liberal arts education, academic remediation, and civic leadership development.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Texas College is organized into four academic divisions: Business and Computer Science, Education, General Studies and Humanities, and Natural and Computational Sciences. It offers 12 baccalaureate degree programs and an associate degree, with special strength in criminal justice, social work, business administration, and biology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are accessible and mission-driven. Applicants are evaluated based on high school diploma or GED equivalent (minimum 2.0 GPA), official high school or college transfer transcripts, and completion of the Texas College application for admissions.
