California Polytechnic State University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1901 and set amidst 9,000 acres of picturesque rolling hills and vineyards near the Pacific Ocean, Cal Poly San Luis Obispo is the premier polytechnic institution of the California State University system. Educating over 22,000 students, Cal Poly is acclaimed for graduating immediate industry leaders in engineering, aerospace, computer science, and viticulture. The College of Engineering is one of the largest and highest-ranked undergraduate engineering colleges in the nation, maintaining close partnerships with Apple, Google, Boeing, NASA JPL, and Tesla.
