Institutional Overview

University Centre Leeds, located in the cultural and digital technology hub of Leeds, West Yorkshire, is a higher education institution offering university-level degrees, foundation degrees, and top-up qualifications validated by the Open University and Leeds Beckett University. The institution is renowned for digital media, game art, cyber security, healthcare leadership, and performing arts.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Industry-standard digital media studios, VR motion capture labs, and high-end gaming suites
