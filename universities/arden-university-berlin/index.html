Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 2:2 or equivalent) in a related field; significant managerial experience considered for MBA/Executive tracks.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Focuses on career-oriented undergraduate and postgraduate degrees; does not offer doctoral PhD programs directly.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-test) or equivalent English proficiency certificate.
Research statement / Proposal
Personal statement detailing professional aspirations in the Berlin startup ecosystem or European commercial sectors.
Application fee
Free direct online application through Arden University Berlin portal.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete academic transcripts, passport copy, resume/CV, personal statement, and one academic/employer reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual undergraduate tuition for international students averages €9,500 to €11,500. Master's tuition ranges from €12,500 to €15,000 for full programs. Berlin Innovation Scholarships (up to 30% reduction), early bird discounts, and corporate sponsorships are available.
