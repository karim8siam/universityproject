Liverpool John Moores University: Astrophysics, Maritime Engineering & Sports Science

Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU) is a vibrant, research-active British public university tracing its history to 1823, renowned globally for the Astrophysics Research Institute (operators of the Liverpool Telescope), world-ranked Sports and Exercise Sciences, and maritime transport logistics.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

LJMU Vice-Chancellor's PhD Studentship in Observational Astrophysics

Full doctoral studentship at the world-renowned Astrophysics Research Institute (ARI) utilizing data from the Liverpool Telescope on La Palma to investigate supernovae, stellar evolution, and galactic transients.

MSc in Sport and Clinical Biomechanics

World-class postgraduate program hosted in the School of Sport and Exercise Sciences, analyzing human locomotion, neuromuscular function, injury prevention, and elite athletic performance.

Ph.D. in Maritime Cyber Security & Autonomous Ship Operations

Doctoral research position based at the Liverpool Logistics, Offshore and Marine Research Institute (LOOM) evaluating cyber vulnerabilities in GPS, AIS, and remote ship control networks.

Forensic Anthropology & Bioarchaeological Skeletal Research Project

Interdisciplinary bioarchaeological investigation analyzing historic human skeletal collections, trace element isotopes, and facial reconstruction techniques using 3D micro-CT scanners.

Premier League & Olympic High Performance Sports Science Practicum

Supervised high-performance sports science internship working alongside fitness directors, nutritionists, and match analysts in Premier League football clubs and UK Athletics.

Royal Society-Newton Postdoctoral Fellowship in Time-Domain Astronomy

Three-year postdoctoral research fellowship analyzing real-time astronomical alerts from the Vera C. Rubin Observatory and robotic telescope arrays.

Lecturer / Senior Lecturer in Maritime Law & Global Logistics

Academic faculty position within the Liverpool Business School and Maritime Centre, teaching international maritime law, charter party disputes, and green shipping corridors.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:1 Honours degree or international equivalent in a relevant subject area.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First class or upper second class (2:1) UK Honours degree or Master's degree with Merit/Distinction in related disciplines.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with a minimum of 6.0 in each sub-test (reading, listening, speaking, writing).
Research statement / Proposal
1,500–2,000 word research proposal detailing methodology, literature review, and alignment with LJMU research institutes.
Application fee
Zero application fee for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, personal statement, two references (at least one academic), and passport scan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time domestic postgraduate tuition is £9,500 to £11,000 per year. International student tuition is £16,500 to £18,500 per year. Fully funded UKRI and university PhD studentships cover 100% of fees plus an annual tax-free living stipend (£19,237).
