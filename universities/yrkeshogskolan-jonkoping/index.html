Yrkeshögskolan Jönköping: Technical Degrees, Automation & IT Guide

Operated by the Municipality of Jönköping under approval from the Swedish National Agency for Higher Vocational Education (MYH), Yrkeshögskolan Jönköping is a premier technical higher vocational institute in southern Sweden's manufacturing and logistics corridor. Offering specialized programs in Industrial Automation, PLC Systems, Logistics Management, and Web/App Development, YH Jönköping bridges academic theory with intensive workplace learning (LIA).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Swedish State CSN Student Aid and Living Support Grant (Studiemedel)

Swedish state CSN student funding providing monthly non-repayable grants and subsidized living loans for students enrolled in accredited technical programs at Yrkeshögskolan Jönköping.

Higher Vocational Diploma (YH-Examen) in Industrial Automation and Robotics Engineering

Two-year professional diploma training automation engineers in PLC programming (Siemens TIA Portal), industrial robotics, SCADA systems, and Industry 4.0 sensor integration.

Advanced Diploma in Full-Stack Web and Cloud Software Development

Two-year qualification focusing on modern frontend frameworks (React, Vue), backend API architecture (Node.js, Python), relational databases, and cloud deployment pipelines.

Jönköping Logistics Corridor Smart Supply Chain and Autonomous AGV Project

Applied development project testing automated guided vehicles, RFID pallet tracking, and warehouse management software integration in the Jönköping logistics corridor.

LIA (Lärande i Arbete) Industrial Engineering Work Placement (22 Weeks)

Five-month full-time industrial workplace immersion (Lärande i Arbete) embedding students in manufacturing automation lines, commissioning PLC panels, and programming robotics.

Småland Manufacturing Innovation and Sustainable Industry Fellowship

Industry-sponsored fellowship promoting green automation and energy efficiency in the Småland manufacturing cluster, supporting technicians building sustainable industrial controls.

Lead Instructor in PLC Automation, Siemens TIA Portal & Industrial Robotics

Full-time technical instructor position lecturing on industrial automation systems, designing hardware lab workshops, and supervising LIA student placements in Jönköping.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of Swedish upper secondary education (Gymnasieexamen or recognized international secondary school diploma) with prerequisite courses in Mathematics 2 and English 6.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Higher vocational degrees (Yrkeshögskoleexamen) qualify graduates for specialized technical positions and articulate into university bachelor's completion programs.
Language proficiency
Swedish language proficiency (Svenska 1 or Svenska som andraspråk 1) and English language competence equivalent to Swedish English 6 (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent).
Research statement / Proposal
Online application submitted via the YH Jönköping admissions portal with certified grade transcripts and CV.
Application fee
Zero application fee; all Swedish higher vocational admissions evaluations are completely free.
Required documentation
Certified high school diploma, official transcripts, proof of prerequisite mathematics/science courses, and copy of ID.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In accordance with Swedish higher vocational legislation, all approved programs at Yrkeshögskolan Jönköping are 100% tuition-free for citizens of Sweden, the EU/EEA, and Switzerland. Admitted students qualify for Swedish government CSN student finance grants and loans (approximately SEK 12,056 per month) throughout their two-year study period.
