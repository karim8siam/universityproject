Johannes Gutenberg University of Mainz: Nuclear Physics, mRNA & Materials Science

Johannes Gutenberg University of Mainz (JGU Mainz), founded in 1477, is one of Germany's largest and most research-intensive universities, member of the German U15, internationally famed for the PRISMA+ Cluster of Excellence (MAMI electron accelerator), polymer research (MPI-P), and biotechnology (home to BioNTech).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

PRISMA+ Excellence Cluster Doctoral Fellowship in Precision Particle Physics

Prestigious doctoral fellowship at the PRISMA+ Cluster of Excellence investigating dark matter, neutrino physics, and subatomic precision measurements at the MESA and MAMI electron accelerators.

Master of Science in Biomedicine & Translational Oncology

Elite English-taught Master of Science combining molecular immunology, mRNA vaccine biotechnology, and experimental oncology in close collaboration with the University Medical Center Mainz.

Salaried Doctoral Researcher (TV-L E13) in Supramolecular Polymer Chemistry

Three-year funded doctoral position at the Department of Chemistry exploring stimulus-responsive hydrogels, covalent adaptable networks, and bio-degradable nanocarriers.

Quantum Spintronics & Topological Magnetic Insulator Research Project

Fundamental physics project investigating skyrmions, antiferromagnetic spin currents, and ultrafast optical spin manipulation for next-generation non-volatile neuromorphic computing.

BioNTech & University Medical Center Translational mRNA Clinical Internship

Six-month clinical and laboratory internship in Mainz analyzing lipid nanoparticle (LNP) delivery mechanisms, in vitro mRNA transcription, and immune assays for personalized cancer therapeutics.

Gutenberg Research College (GRC) Advanced Postdoctoral Fellowship

Prestigious two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Gutenberg Research College for exceptional emerging scholars in book history, printing history, digital humanities, or philosophy.

W3 Professorship in Theoretical High-Energy Particle Physics

Permanent full professorship at the Institute of Physics within PRISMA+, leading theoretical research in quantum field theory, electroweak precision observables, and dark sector physics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor of Science or Bachelor of Arts (180 ECTS) in relevant subject with above-average grades.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (M.Sc., M.A., Diplom) with grade 'gut' (2.0 or better) in physics, chemistry, biology, or humanities.
Language proficiency
English programs: TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 6.5. German programs: DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research outline (2,000 words) with written supervisory agreement from a JGU Mainz professor.
Application fee
€75 evaluation fee via uni-assist for international applicants with non-German degrees.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's certificate, transcript of records with grading scale, CV, motivation letter, and language certificates.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees for domestic and international students. The semester fee (Semesterbeitrag) is approximately €350 per semester, which includes a comprehensive semester ticket for regional public transit across Rhineland-Palatinate and the Frankfurt/Rhine-Main metropolitan area.
