University of Arkansas at Monticello

UAM · Monticello, Arkansas, United States

The University of Arkansas at Monticello (UAM), established in 1909, is a regional public university in southeastern Arkansas. UAM is renowned as the home of the state's only School of Forestry and Natural Resources and the Arkansas Forest Resources Center, making it a pivotal research hub for sustainable timberland ecology, wildlife management, and agricultural sciences in the Mississippi River Delta region.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UAM offers certificates, associate, baccalaureate, and master's degrees through the College of Forestry, Agriculture and Natural Resources, School of Nursing, School of Mathematical and Natural Sciences, School of Education, and School of Business. Its 1,600-acre campus features extensive experimental forests and wildlife research tracts.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with a minimum 2.0 GPA or equivalent ACT/SAT scores. Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.7–3.0 GPA, submit transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a statement of professional intent.
