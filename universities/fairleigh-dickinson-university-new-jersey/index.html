Fairleigh Dickinson University

Fairleigh Dickinson University (FDU) is New Jersey's largest private comprehensive university, operating the Metropolitan Campus (Teaneck/Hackensack) and the Florham Campus (Madison), renowned for cybersecurity, pharmacy, clinical psychopharmacology, and AACSB business.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1942, FDU is a pioneering global university with campuses in New Jersey, Vancouver (Canada), and Wroxton (England). The university operates four major colleges: Silberman College of Business, Gildart Haase School of Computer Sciences and Engineering, School of Pharmacy and Health Sciences, and Becton College of Arts and Sciences. FDU holds Special Consultative Status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC).

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applications submit through the Common Application or FDU online application with transcripts, counselor recommendation, and personal essay. FDU is test-optional for SAT/ACT. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0, academic transcripts, resume, and letters of recommendation. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.0+.
