PennWest California

PennWest California · California, Pennsylvania, United States

PennWest California (formerly California University of Pennsylvania) is a prominent campus of Pennsylvania Western University located along the Monongahela River in California, Pennsylvania. Acclaimed for mechatronics engineering technology, exercise science, digital forensics, commercial drone technology, and nursing.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

PennWest California operates the Eberly Science and Technology Center, the Convocation Center, and specialized robotics and mechatronics automation laboratories. It has pioneered online graduate master's degrees in exercise science and wellness for over two decades.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5 recommended) with test-optional application pathways. Graduate programs require an accredited baccalaureate degree with a minimum 2.80 cumulative GPA and program-specific prerequisites.
