Texas A&M University at Galveston

TAMUG · Galveston, Texas, United States

Texas A&M University at Galveston (TAMUG) is the premier ocean-oriented campus of the Texas A&M University System, located on Pelican Island in Galveston, Texas. Home to the Texas A&M Maritime Academy—one of only six state maritime academies in the nation—TAMUG leads the Gulf Coast in marine biology, maritime transportation, coastal engineering, and ocean sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TAMUG provides focused academic programs across Marine Sciences, Ocean Engineering, Maritime Business Administration, and Marine Transportation. Operating oceanographic research vessels and radar training simulators, its researchers partner closely with the NOAA Gulf of Mexico Disaster Response Center and the Texas Coastal Spine barrier project.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions follow Texas A&M University requirements with maritime physical standards for US Coast Guard licensing tracks. Graduate applicants require an accredited baccalaureate in marine science, engineering, or related fields (minimum 3.0 GPA), letters of recommendation, CV, and a statement of ocean research objectives.
