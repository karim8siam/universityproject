Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1854, Hamline features the College of Liberal Arts and the School of Education and Leadership. Celebrated for pioneering forensic sciences, paralegal education (first ABA-approved paralegal program in Minnesota), public administration, and nationally acclaimed MFA in Creative Writing programs.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.40), course rigor, and personal statement; SAT/ACT is test-optional. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor’s degree with 3.00+ GPA, resume, writing sample, and TOEFL iBT 80+ / IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates.
