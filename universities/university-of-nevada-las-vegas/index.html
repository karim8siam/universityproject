University of Nevada, Las Vegas

UNLV · Las Vegas, Nevada, United States

The University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) is a top-tier Carnegie R1 public research university located in the vibrant urban metropolis of Las Vegas. Internationally recognized as the global gold standard in hospitality management through the William F. Harrah College of Hospitality, UNLV is also celebrated for solar energy engineering, radiochemistry, gaming regulation, and high-pressure physics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNLV features 15 academic colleges including the Harrah College of Hospitality, Howard R. Hughes College of Engineering, Kirk Kerkorian School of Medicine, William S. Boyd School of Law, and the College of Sciences. UNLV operates the Harry Reid Research and Technology Park and the International Gaming Institute (IGI).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic for undergraduate applicants. Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
