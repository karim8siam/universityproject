Academic overview & excellence

Founded over half a century ago, the University of Alberta Press publishes peer-reviewed scholarly monographs, literary non-fiction, and poetry that shape intellectual discourse across North America. Operating under the rigorous editorial review of the UAlberta Scholarly Publishing Committee, the press emphasizes open-access digital publishing, translation of Indigenous languages, and typographical craftsmanship, earning numerous Governor General's Awards and national book design accolades.

Research admissions & criteria

Postgraduate editorial fellowships, graduate research assistantships, and open-access publishing grants are offered to qualified scholars in the humanities, social sciences, and library and information studies (MLIS). Applicants must hold an undergraduate or master's degree in English, comparative literature, history, or Indigenous studies. Manuscript submissions undergo rigorous double-blind peer review before editorial board authorization.
