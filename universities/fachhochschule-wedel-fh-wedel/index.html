Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located on the outskirts of Hamburg in Wedel, Fachhochschule Wedel is a renowned private state-recognized university of applied sciences specializing in computer science, software engineering, business informatics, and industrial engineering with tight ties to Northern Germany's tech ecosystem.

Institution type
Private State-Recognized University of Applied Sciences
Campus location
Wedel (Hamburg Metropolitan Region), Schleswig-Holstein
Accreditation

Admissions channel
FH Wedel Student Secretariat & Admissions
Instruction language
German and English (bilingual master's tracks available)
Financial aid & support
Deutschlandstipendium, FH Wedel Förderverein bursaries, and corporate tech sponsorships

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Applied computer science, cloud computing, and distributed systems
  • Game development, interactive 3D media, and computer graphics
  • Business informatics, IT consulting, and enterprise resource planning
  • Industrial engineering and intelligent automation systems

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Applied Computer Science (M.Sc.) (Graduate Degree) — Four-semester advanced program focusing on software architecture, artificial intelligence, and cloud-native systems.
  • Bachelor of Science in Games Engineering (Undergraduate Degree) — Technical engineering degree concentrating on game engine architecture, real-time rendering, physics simulation, and C++ programming.
  • Master of Science in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (Graduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary master's degree exploring deep learning, big data analytics, and operational AI in manufacturing.
