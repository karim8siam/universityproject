Southern Connecticut State University

Southern Connecticut · New Haven, Connecticut, United States

Southern Connecticut State University (SCSU) is a public comprehensive university located on an urban-suburban 168-acre campus in historic New Haven, Connecticut. Known for its strong commitment to social justice, environmental sustainability, and STEM innovation, SCSU is acclaimed for its Werth Center for Coastal and Marine Studies, high-tech science building with optics and nanotechnology suites, accredited school of business, and leading school of nursing.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SCSU comprises the College of Health and Human Services, the College of Arts and Sciences, the School of Business, and the College of Education. The university operates the state-of-the-art Academic Science and Laboratory Building, the Center for Nanotechnology, and coastal research vessels studying Long Island Sound marine environments.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.3), curriculum rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ minimum GPA, official transcripts, department-specific writing essays, and letters of recommendation.
