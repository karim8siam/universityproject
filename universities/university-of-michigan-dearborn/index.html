University of Michigan-Dearborn

UM-Dearborn · Dearborn, Michigan, United States

The University of Michigan-Dearborn is a premier comprehensive public university founded in 1959 on 200 acres of land donated by the Ford Motor Company. Celebrated for industry-embedded engineering education, electric vehicle mobility research, cybersecurity, and automotive manufacturing excellence, UM-Dearborn delivers high-impact degrees backed by the prestigious University of Michigan tradition.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UM-Dearborn houses four colleges: the College of Engineering and Computer Science (CECS), College of Arts, Sciences, and Letters (CASL), College of Business, and College of Education, Health, and Human Services. The university is home to the Institute for Advanced Vehicle Systems (IAVS) and the Cybersecurity Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and rigor. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
