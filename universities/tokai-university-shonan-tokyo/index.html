Academic overview & research priorities

Founded by Dr. Shigeyoshi Matsumae in 1942, Tokai University operates flagship campuses in Shonan (Hiratsuka, Kanagawa), Tokyo (Yoyogi and Takanawa), and Shizuoka (School of Marine Science and Technology). Tokai is internationally famed for its Tokai Challenger solar vehicle victories at the World Solar Challenge in Australia, its university-owned research vessel Bosei Maru, and cutting-edge engineering laboratories in robotics, semiconductors, and aeronautics.

Research admissions & international criteria

International applicants to undergraduate programs must hold a secondary school diploma, JLPT N2 or N1 (or IELTS 6.0+ for select English-medium courses), academic transcripts, and personal statement. Graduate programs in Science and Technology require a recognized bachelor's or master's degree, a detailed research plan, and supervisor agreement. Tokai University General Scholarship and MEXT allocations provide extensive financial support.
