Institutional Overview

Central Methodist University (CMU), founded in 1854 in historic Fayette, Missouri, is an established private four-year university. Renowned for its distinctive undergraduate major in marine biology (supported by a dedicated inland oceanarium research laboratory), music education, computer science, and nursing, CMU provides personalized mentorship and generous institutional scholarships.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Comprehensive nursing program with 100% NCLEX-RN first-time pass rates
  • Active undergraduate research symposium and regional environmental field stations
