Academic overview & excellence

Located at the heart of the North Campus in Edmonton, the University of Alberta Career Centre empowers undergraduate and graduate scholars through specialized work-integrated learning, career navigation research, and employer networking. It hosts the prestigious Science Internship Program (SIP), the Arts Work Experience (AWE), and engineering co-operative education, bridging academic discovery with the burgeoning energy, aerospace, and biomedical sectors of Alberta.

Research admissions & criteria

Students enrolled in degree programs across the University of Alberta are eligible to register for Career Centre fellowships, venture incubators, and co-op work terms. International students require an active Canadian study permit with valid off-campus work authorization or a Co-op Work Permit. GPA criteria vary by faculty internship stream, typically demanding a cumulative GPA of 2.7 to 3.3.

UAlberta Career Discovery Graduate Internship Fellowship

Competitive fellowship matching master's and doctoral scholars with governmental ministries, cleantech incubators, and corporate research hubs throughout Edmonton and Calgary.

Funding: CAD $12,000 stipend covering an eight-month industrial or public policy placement Tuition: Standard continuing graduate enrollment fees apply Deadline: March 15, 2027

Arts Work Experience (AWE) Humanities and Communications Internship

Structured 4 to 12-month undergraduate professional placement in public relations, policy analysis, museum curation, and international trade agencies.

Funding: Paid full-time work terms with average earnings of CAD $3,200 to CAD $3,800 monthly Tuition: CAD $850 co-op course registration fee per term Deadline: February 15, 2027
Internship

Science Internship Program (SIP) Computational & Biopharma Practicum

8 to 16-month practical industry immersion for science students exploring machine learning at Amii, genomics research at DynaLIFEDx, or environmental remediation.

Funding: Salaried positions averaging CAD $4,200 monthly across AI and clinical biomanufacturing firms Tuition: CAD $900 SIP course fee per work term Deadline: January 30, 2027
Scholarship

Postdoctoral Career Pathways and Innovation Commercialization Award

Seed grant empowering postdoctoral fellows in nanotechnology, biomedical engineering, and artificial intelligence to commercialize lab discoveries.

Funding: CAD $8,000 research translation grant and seed funding for commercial ventures Tuition: Full tuition waiver Deadline: May 30, 2027
Master's

Master of Arts in Work-Integrated Learning and Workplace Pedagogies

Two-year academic thesis program investigating workforce dynamics, cooperative education outcomes, equity in workplace onboarding, and remote labor markets.

Funding: Departmental graduate teaching assistantships up to CAD $18,500 annually Tuition: CAD $6,100 (domestic) / CAD $15,800 (international) per academic year Deadline: April 1, 2027
PhD

Doctor of Philosophy in Human Resources Management and Labor Economics

Four-year doctoral program providing rigorous quantitative training in occupational mobility, labor market econometrics, and human capital formation.

Funding: Guaranteed minimum funding package of CAD $26,000 per year for four years Tuition: CAD $6,400 (domestic) / CAD $16,400 (international) per year Deadline: January 15, 2027
Course

Continuing education course in active listening, vocational psychometric profiling, career narrative framing, and inclusive workplace transition methodologies.

Funding: University staff and alumni discount covering 30% of registration fees Tuition: CAD $1,450 total certificate tuition Deadline: September 1, 2027
