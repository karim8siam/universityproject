Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in Tsuchiura near Lake Kasumigaura and Tsukuba Science City, Tsukuba International University operates the Faculty of Medical and Health Sciences. The university delivers intensive practical education across four clinical hospital disciplines—Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Radiological Technology, and Medical Technology—providing advanced allied health professionals to Ibaraki and Greater Tokyo medical centers.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation, Japanese language competency (JLPT N2 or higher), submission of academic records, and personal interviews. Clinical health tracks require medical health evaluation and demonstrated science foundations in biology, chemistry, and physics.
