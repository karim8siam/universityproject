Institutional Overview

Université du Québec à Rimouski (UQAR) Lévis campus serves as the premier public university institution for the Chaudière-Appalaches and Greater Québec City metropolitan area. Located on Boulevard Alphonse-Desjardins, the Lévis campus hosts thriving faculties in management sciences, administrative computing, engineering, education, and nursing.

  • Strategic partnership with Desjardins Financial Group and Chaudière-Appalaches industrial clusters
