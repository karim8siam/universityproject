Academic overview & excellence

Headquartered on Constitution Square in Ottawa, Universities Canada provides a unified voice for higher education, research, and innovation across Canada. It administers over 120 scholarship and fellowship programs on behalf of the Government of Canada, private foundations, and international corporations. Through its public policy advocacy, it influences federal investments in research councils (CIHR, NSERC, SSHRC), international student mobility initiatives, and university-industry commercialization partnerships.

Research admissions & criteria

Scholarships and fellowships administered by Universities Canada require enrollment at an accredited Canadian member university. Competitive programs such as the Queen Elizabeth Scholars (QES) and the Canada-ASEAN Scholarships require institutional nomination by the university's international exchange or graduate studies office. Standard eligibility includes high academic standing (minimum 80% or 3.5 GPA) and proven community leadership.
