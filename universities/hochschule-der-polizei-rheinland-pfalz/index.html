Hochschule der Polizei Rheinland-Pfalz

HdP RLP · Büchenbeuren, Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany

Hochschule der Polizei Rheinland-Pfalz (HdP RLP) at Campus Hahn delivers accredited bachelor’s and master’s dual programs in modern policing, forensic investigation, criminological science, and cybercrime defense.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The institution combines academic legal education, operational police psychology, tactical leadership, and cutting-edge forensic technologies. Students receive civil service candidate status with monthly trainee remuneration and guaranteed career integration into state security leadership.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require the German Allgemeine Hochschulreife, Fachhochschulreife, or accredited international equivalent, alongside police fitness vetting, German language fluency (C1/C2), and EU citizenship.
