University of Illinois Springfield

UIS · Springfield, Illinois, United States

The University of Illinois Springfield (UIS), established in 1969 as Sangamon State University and integrated into the world-class University of Illinois System in 1995, is an accredited public regional university situated in the Illinois state capital. UIS is celebrated for its deep integration with state legislative governance, public policy leadership, top-tier online degree programs, and an NSA-designated Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UIS delivers programs through the College of Public Affairs and Administration, the College of Health, Science & Technology, the College of Business and Management, and the College of Liberal Arts and Social Sciences. Its campus is home to the Center for State Policy and Leadership, the Illinois Legislative Staff Internship Program (ILSIP), and the Emiquon Field Station for floodplain ecological research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission evaluates high school transcripts via Common App or UIS portal (test-optional; recommended GPA 2.75+). Graduate and master's degree applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.5–3.0 GPA, submit transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a statement of academic objectives.
