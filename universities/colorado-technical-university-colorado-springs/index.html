Academic overview & excellence

Founded in 1965 to train military veterans in technical careers near the NORAD aerospace defense command in Colorado Springs, CTU holds institutional accreditation from the Higher Learning Commission (HLC). Its computer engineering and electrical engineering bachelor's degrees are accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET, and its business programs are accredited by ACBSP. It is recognized as a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD) by the NSA and DHS.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions require proof of high school graduation or GED equivalence. Degree programs in engineering require high school mathematics through pre-calculus. Graduate admission requires an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.0 GPA (3.0 for doctoral programs). International applicants must provide certified course-by-course transcript evaluations (WES/ECE) and English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5).
