Kobe Yamate University

Kobe Yamate University (integrated into Kansai University of International Studies - Kobe Yamate Campus) is a historic academic campus in central Kobe, celebrated for disaster resilience management, international tourism, psychological sciences, and urban community safety.

Academic overview & research priorities

Overlooking Kobe harbor from the slopes of Mount Rokko, the Kobe Yamate campus hosts the Faculty of Contemporary Society and Faculty of International Communication. The institution pioneered Safety Management and Disaster Prevention studies following the Great Hanshin-Awaji Earthquake of 1995, building international partnerships in disaster risk reduction and community revitalization.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions accept Japanese and international scholars through the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students (EJU) or JLPT N2, accompanied by high school graduation transcripts, an essay on disaster management or international tourism, and an interview.
