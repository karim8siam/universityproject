Academic overview & research priorities

Established by the University System of Georgia as the nation’s first four-year public college founded in the 21st century, GGC emphasizes student-faculty research collaboration across information technology, biochemistry, criminal justice, and digital cinema production.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants require high school graduation with college preparatory curriculum (GPA >= 2.00). Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT) may be submitted. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 74+ or IELTS 6.0+.
