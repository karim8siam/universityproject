Landesrektorenkonferenz Baden-Württemberg e.V.

Landesrektorenkonferenz Baden-Württemberg e.V. is the central association of the nine research universities of Baden-Württemberg, coordinating state-wide science policy and doctoral research.

Academic overview & research priorities

Based in Stuttgart, the LRK unites the rectorates of the nine state universities (Freiburg, Heidelberg, Hohenheim, KIT Karlsruhe, Konstanz, Mannheim, Stuttgart, Tübingen, and Ulm). It acts as the premier governance forum steering federal research excellence initiatives, high-performance computing infrastructure, and doctoral qualification frameworks.

Research admissions & international criteria

Coordinates inter-university graduate colleges, policy fellowships, and doctoral exchange networks across the state of Baden-Württemberg. Candidates must be admitted doctoral scholars or university administrators within the nine LRK member universities.
