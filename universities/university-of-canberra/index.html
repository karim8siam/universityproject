University of Canberra: Higher Degree Research, Degrees & International Awards

The University of Canberra is a dynamic public institution in the nation's capital known for applied research, deep public policy connections, cybersecurity, applied ecology, sports science, and high graduate employability.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) International Stipend

Prestigious federal research scholarship providing comprehensive funding for domestic and international PhD candidates pursuing world-leading research at UC.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Applied Ecology & Environmental Water Governance

Cutting-edge doctoral research hosted at the Institute for Applied Ecology analyzing Murray-Darling freshwater systems, ecological genomics, and invasive species management.

Master of Science (MSc) in Cyber Security & Forensic Computing

Specialized postgraduate program covering cloud security, penetration testing, threat intelligence, and digital evidence analysis in collaboration with national agencies.

Centre for Deliberative Democracy & Global Governance Research Fellowship

Research fellowship investigating participatory democratic processes, global citizen assemblies, and algorithmic governance at the internationally recognized centre.

Canberra Health Services & UC Sports Medicine Clinical Practicum

Clinical immersion training in musculoskeletal rehabilitation, sports biomechanics, and athlete load monitoring in partnership with the Australian Institute of Sport.

Vice-Chancellor's Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Three-year early-career research fellowship designed to foster research leaders in sustainable systems, public health innovation, and intelligent data systems.

Master of Public Policy (MPP) in Governance & Digital Transformation

Practitioner-oriented degree leveraging Canberra's parliamentary and policy institutions to master regulatory design, digital government, and policy evaluation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Australian bachelor's degree or overseas equivalent with a minimum credit average (65%+) or equivalent GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with first-class or upper second-class honours (H1 or H2A), or a research master's with demonstrated thesis track record.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 across all bands) or PTE Academic 58+; TOEFL iBT 79+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for HDR (MPhil/PhD): 1,500–2,500 word research proposal aligned with a confirmed UC supervisory team.
Application fee
Free directly through the UC International Application Portal.
Required documentation
Certified transcripts and degree certificates, referee reports, CV, and verified English proficiency test results.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition ranges from $32,000 to $41,500 AUD per year depending on the discipline. Research Training Program (RTP) scholarships provide full tuition fee offsets and annual living stipends of $33,500 AUD for high-achieving HDR candidates.
