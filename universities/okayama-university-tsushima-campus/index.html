Academic overview & excellence

Centered at its expansive green Tsushima Campus in Okayama City, Okayama University traces its medical origins back to 1870. The university encompasses ten faculties and eight graduate schools, notably the Graduate School of Natural Science and Technology, the Institute for Planetary Materials (IPM in Misasa), and the Graduate School of Environmental and Life Science. Okayama University was designated by the Japanese Ministry of Education (MEXT) as a Top Global University.

Research admissions & criteria

International applicants for undergraduate degree tracks apply through the Special Selection for Privately Financed International Students with EJU (Examination for Japanese University Admission) scores. Master's and doctoral degree programs offer English-medium tracks (Discovery Program for Global Learners and Natural Sciences) requiring a relevant bachelor's degree, GRE/research proposal, and TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0.
