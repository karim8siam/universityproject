University of Maine System

UMS · Bangor, Maine, United States

The University of Maine System (UMS) is the statewide public higher education partnership uniting six accredited universities across Maine: the flagship University of Maine in Orono, the University of Southern Maine in Portland, and regional universities in Augusta, Farmington, Fort Kent, and Presque Isle. UMS is renowned globally for the Advanced Structures and Composites Center (ASCC), offshore floating wind turbines, and forestry bio-economy innovations.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions processed through constituent UMS universities. High school diploma or GED equivalent; test-optional holistic review of academic coursework and personal essay. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates across UMS campuses. Fall priority admission February 1, 2027; Regular rolling admission closes July 1, 2027.
