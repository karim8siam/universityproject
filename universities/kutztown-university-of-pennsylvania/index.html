Kutztown University of Pennsylvania

Kutztown · Kutztown, Pennsylvania, United States

Kutztown University of Pennsylvania (KU) is a comprehensive public university established in 1866, nestled in the rolling countryside of Kutztown, Pennsylvania, between Reading and Allentown. A member of the Pennsylvania State System of Higher Education (PASSHE), Kutztown is internationally acclaimed for visual and commercial arts, education, business administration, and computer science and information technology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

KU comprises four colleges: the College of Visual and Performing Arts, College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, College of Business, and College of Education. The university operates the Sharadin Arts Building and the Pennsylvania German Cultural Heritage Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and transcripts with test-optional application options. Graduate master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.8–3.0 GPA, personal statement, two letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+). Graduate assistantships provide full tuition waivers and stipends.
