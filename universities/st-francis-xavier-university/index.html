St. Francis Xavier University: Research Degrees, Coady Institute & Scholarships Guide

St. Francis Xavier University (StFX) in Antigonish, Nova Scotia, is one of Canada's most prestigious primarily undergraduate and graduate universities, celebrated for the Coady Institute, world-class environmental sciences, health research, and strong alumni network.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

StFX Graduate Research Fellowship & Dean's Merit Award

Merit-based entrance award awarded to high-achieving applicants entering thesis-based Master of Science or Master of Arts programs at StFX.

Master of Science in Earth & Environmental Sciences

Research-intensive Master of Science program focusing on environmental biogeochemistry, coastal paleoclimatology, hydrogeology, and geothermal energy in Atlantic Canada.

Ph.D. in Educational Studies Cooperative Doctoral Fellowship

Collaborative inter-university doctoral program in Nova Scotia exploring educational leadership, critical pedagogy, community development, and indigenous curriculum reconciliation.

Atlantic Coastal Ecosystems & Carbon Sequestration Investigation

Field-based environmental investigation tracking blue carbon stocks, salt marsh restoration, and greenhouse gas flux dynamics along the Northumberland Strait.

Coady Institute Community Leadership & Social Innovation Practicum

Hands-on experiential leadership placement embedded with non-profit community organizations, cooperative movements, and indigenous economic development bodies.

Brian Mulroney Institute of Government Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship researching public policy, Atlantic economic development, bilateral Canada-US trade relations, or indigenous self-governance.

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in Aquatic Ecology & Biodiversity

Full-time faculty appointment in the Department of Biology teaching undergraduate and graduate aquatic ecology, supervising thesis students, and leading an externally funded research program.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year undergraduate honors degree (or equivalent) in a related discipline with minimum 75% average (B+) across senior courses.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in relevant field with minimum 80% average (A-) and documented research or publication record.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page research proposal detailing proposed thesis topic, prospective supervisor, and methodology.
Application fee
CAD $100 non-refundable graduate application fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, statement of research intent, curriculum vitae, and three academic reference letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic graduate tuition ranges from CAD $7,800 to $9,500 annually. International student tuition is approximately CAD $19,200 to $22,800 annually. Research assistantships, Dean's fellowships, and Coady international bursaries offer robust funding.
