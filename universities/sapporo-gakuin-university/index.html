Sapporo Gakuin University

Sapporo Gakuin University (SGU) is a comprehensive private liberal arts, business, and psychology university in Hokkaido, operating modern campuses in Ebetsu and Shin-Sapporo with strong community-engaged research in northern regional economics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1946 in post-war Hokkaido, SGU has expanded across its verdant Ebetsu campus and its cutting-edge Shin-Sapporo urban campus. The university is organized into five faculties: Business Administration, Economics, Humanities, Law, and Psychology. SGU is celebrated for regional revitalization research, clinical counseling psychology, Indigenous Ainu culture studies, and international exchange partnerships across East Asia and North America.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions for international applicants involve the EJU examination or SGU international entrance exam, including an essay in Japanese and personal interview. Graduate programs require a completed 16-year bachelor's degree, comprehensive research proposal (1,500-2,000 words in Japanese), transcripts, and oral examination. Applicants generally require JLPT N2 or N1 for Japanese-taught tracks.
