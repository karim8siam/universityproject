Texas Tech University System

TTU System · Lubbock, Texas, United States

The Texas Tech University System is a major public university system headquartered in Lubbock, Texas, comprising five distinct universities: Texas Tech University, Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center, Angelo State University, TTUHSC El Paso, and Midwestern State University. Delivering over $250 million in annual research, the TTU System leads internationally in wind energy engineering, arid agriculture, severe storm meteorology, and border health sciences.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions processed through constituent TTU System universities. High school diploma or accredited equivalent with holistic review (class rank, GPA, curriculum rigor). TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates across TTU System institutions. Fall priority admission February 1, 2027; Spring priority admission November 1, 2026.
