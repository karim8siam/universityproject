Institution Overview & Profile

The Nova Scotia College of Early Childhood Education (NSCECE), founded in 1970 in Halifax, is a specialized non-profit college dedicated to pedagogical training in early human development, child care policy, inclusive classroom education, and family support systems.

Institution TypePrivate Non-Profit Specialized College
Main CampusQuinpool Road Campus, Halifax, Nova Scotia
AccreditationNova Scotia Department of Education and Early Childhood Development
Admissions RouteFall and winter cohort admissions with vulnerable sector clearance
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in writing and speaking)
Financial Aid & GrantsNova Scotia Early Childhood Educator Student Bursary Program & NSCECE Grants

Key Academic Strengths

  • Operates two licensed on-site lab childcare centres offering immediate observational and teaching practicums
  • Curriculum strictly aligned with the Nova Scotia Early Learning Curriculum Framework
  • High graduate licensing and employment rates across provincial daycare and pre-primary initiatives
  • Focus on neurodiverse inclusion, trauma-informed pedagogy, and Indigenous cultural awareness in early learning

Scholarship

Nova Scotia ECE Workforce Enhancement Bursary

Provincially funded tuition reimbursement bursary available to full-time domestic and international students committing to working in licensed Nova Scotia child care centres.

Funding: Up to CAD $4,000 per academic year in non-repayable tuition support Deadline: May 30, 2027
Course

Diploma in Early Childhood Education Enrolment

Two-year academic and experiential program covering child psychology, play-based curriculum design, emergent literacy, and family dynamics.

Funding: Eligible for Nova Scotia Student Loans and Canada Student Grants Deadline: June 30, 2027
Internship

NSCECE Lab School Student Teaching Practicum

Four successive 4-week field practicums conducted in licensed lab schools with direct mentorship from master early childhood educators.

Funding: Fully integrated into diploma program tuition; provincial practicum completion stipends available Deadline: April 15, 2027
Grant

Margie MacDonald Memorial Child Development Grant

Need-based memorial grant assisting students demonstrating outstanding empathy and innovative pedagogy in early childhood intervention.

Funding: CAD $1,500 grant applied directly toward second-year tuition fees Deadline: July 15, 2027
Course

Inclusive Early Learning Post-Diploma Specialization

One-year post-diploma specialization providing focused expertise in neurodivergence, sensory processing integration, and individual program planning (IPP).

Funding: Provincial workforce upskilling bursaries available for employed educators Deadline: August 15, 2027
Scholarship

Emerging Indigenous & Mi'kmaq Educator Award

Scholarship supporting Indigenous students enrolled in early childhood education, fostering culturally responsive curriculum development across Atlantic First Nations communities.

Funding: CAD $3,500 annual award plus book allowance Deadline: June 1, 2027

Research fellowship examining the socio-economic impacts of universal early childhood education access in rural Nova Scotia communities.
