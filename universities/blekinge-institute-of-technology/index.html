Blekinge Institute of Technology (BTH)

Established in 1989 in Karlskrona, Blekinge Institute of Technology (Blekinge Tekniska Högskola) is a top-tier Swedish public institute of technology renowned globally for Software Engineering, Mechanical Engineering & Simulation-Driven Design, Strategic Sustainability, and close industrial co-production with leading Scandinavian corporations. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

BTH Scholarship Programme for Prospective Students

Official merit-based tuition fee reduction covering 50% of tuition fees for non-EU/EEA students admitted to campus-based Master's degree programs at Blekinge Institute of Technology.

Swedish Institute (SI) Scholarship for Global Professionals

Prestigious fully funded Swedish government scholarship covering 100% of tuition fees at BTH, a monthly living allowance of SEK 12,000, travel grants, and SI Network membership.

MSc in Mechanical Engineering - Structural Mechanics

Two-year master's program (120 ECTS) focusing on finite element analysis, simulation-driven design, nonlinear mechanics, structural dynamics, and digital product validation.

Master in Strategic Leadership towards Sustainability (MSLS)

Globally recognized one-year master's degree (60 ECTS) founded on the Framework for Strategic Sustainable Development (FSSD), training leaders in systems thinking and circular transformation.

PhD & Licentiate in Software Engineering

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment (or two-year licentiate) in the Department of Software Engineering at BTH, ranked among Europe's premier research centers.

SERL Sweden Industry Research & Thesis Placements

Postgraduate research and industrial thesis opportunities in SERL Sweden solving real-world challenges with industry partners including Ericsson, Volvo, Spotify, and Telenor.

PDRL Engineering Design Placements

Hands-on research and engineering design placements in PDRL at BTH focusing on digital twins, simulation-driven design, and model-based systems engineering.

Teaching Assistantship (Amanuens) & Demonstrating

Departmental academic employment (SEK 160–220/hr) for enrolled graduate students assisting with computer science laboratory coaching and engineering mechanics exercises.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or related technical field (minimum 180 ECTS credits), including completed thesis work of at least 15 ECTS.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master's degree satisfying Swedish third-cycle general syllabus criteria with relevant thesis research background.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper secondary school English 6 (IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 overall with writing minimum 20).
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by Swedish universities.
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying through University Admissions Sweden.
Required documentation
Degree certificate, complete official transcripts with grading rubric, English proficiency test score, CV, and copy of valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Swedish higher education regulations, EU/EEA and Swiss citizens study tuition-free. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is normally SEK 70,000 to SEK 75,000 per semester (SEK 140,000 to SEK 150,000 per year). Qualifying international students can apply for the BTH Scholarship Programme (covering 50% of tuition fees) or the Swedish Institute (SI) Scholarship (covering 100% of tuition plus SEK 12,000/month living allowance).
