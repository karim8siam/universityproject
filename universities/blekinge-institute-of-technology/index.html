Blekinge Institute of Technology (Blekinge Tekniska Högskola)

Established in 1989 in Karlskrona, Blekinge Institute of Technology (BTH) is a top-tier Swedish public institute of technology renowned globally for software engineering, strategic sustainability, and industrial co-production. Review verified campus-based master's curricula, BTH scholarship fee reductions, tuition-free doctoral positions, and faculty profiles before submitting applications via University Admissions Sweden.

Programs, degrees & academic offerings

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

BTH Scholarship Programme for Prospective Students

Official merit-based tuition fee reduction covering 50% of tuition fees for non-EU/EEA students admitted to campus-based Master's degree programs at Blekinge Institute of Technology.

Blekinge Institute of Technology PhD and Licentiate in Software Engineering

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment (or two-year licentiate) in the Department of Software Engineering at BTH, ranked among Europe's premier research centers for empirical software engineering and industrial cooperation.

BTH Scholarship Programme

The Blekinge Institute of Technology (BTH) Scholarship Programme offers high-achieving non-EU/EEA international students a 50% tuition fee reduction on eligible master's programs.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science or equivalent technical field (minimum 180 ECTS credits), including completed thesis work of at least 15 ECTS.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or related quantitative discipline satisfying Swedish third-cycle general syllabus criteria.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper secondary school English 6 (IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 overall with writing minimum 20).
Research proposal / Intent
For master's: statement of purpose and academic transcripts via University Admissions Sweden. For PhD: comprehensive research statement and portfolio aligned with advertised department vacancies.
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying through University Admissions Sweden.
Required documentation
Degree certificate, complete official transcripts with grading rubric, English proficiency test score, CV, and copy of valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Swedish higher education regulations, EU/EEA and Swiss citizens study tuition-free. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is normally SEK 70,000 per semester (SEK 140,000 per year / SEK 280,000 for a two-year 120-credit Master's program). Qualifying international students can apply for the BTH Scholarship Programme, which covers 50% of tuition fees.
