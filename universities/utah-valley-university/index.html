Utah Valley University

UVU · Orem, Utah, United States

Utah Valley University (UVU) is Utah's largest public university, founded in 1941 on a 225-acre campus in Orem in the heart of Utah's Silicon Slopes tech hub. Nationally celebrated for its dual-mission model combining community college accessibility with rigorous baccalaureate and master's degree programs, UVU is renowned for collegiate aviation, digital media, cybersecurity, mechatronics, and business leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UVU encompasses eight colleges and schools: the College of Engineering and Technology, Woodbury School of Business, College of Science, College of Health and Public Service, School of the Arts, School of Education, College of Humanities and Social Sciences, and the School of Aviation Sciences. UVU operates its own airport training hub at Provo Municipal Airport.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are open-access and test-optional, evaluating high school credentials. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
