Ambrose University: Biology, Leadership & Interdisciplinary Research Guide

Ambrose University is a distinguished private liberal arts and science university in Calgary, Alberta. Recognized for intimate seminar instruction, rigorous undergraduate research apprenticeships, and graduate degrees in educational leadership, behavioural science, and interdisciplinary humanities, Ambrose pairs foundational scholarly inquiry with direct community impact.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year recognized bachelor's degree (or international equivalent) with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0 in the final 60 credits of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not currently offering independent doctoral degrees; collaborative research transfer and master's credentials with partner Canadian institutions.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 88 (minimum 20 in each subscore), IELTS Academic 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each band), or Duolingo English Test 115+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research-focused graduate admissions: 1,500-word statement of intent and scholarly objectives aligned with Ambrose graduate faculty.
Application fee
$100 CAD non-refundable application fee for Canadian applicants ($140 CAD for international applicants).
Required documentation
Official post-secondary academic transcripts, two confidential academic reference letters, curriculum vitae, and personal statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Ambrose University is approximately $690 CAD per 3-credit course for domestic students and $1,150 CAD per 3-credit course for international students. Multiple merit scholarships, entrance bursaries up to $5,000 CAD, and research assistantships through the Ambrose Research Fund provide substantial financial support.
