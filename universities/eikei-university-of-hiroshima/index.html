Eikei University of Hiroshima: Social System Design, SDGs & Project-Based Master's

Eikei University of Hiroshima (Eikei Daigaku), established in 2021 by the Hiroshima Prefectural Government, is a progressive, forward-thinking public university. Designed around 21st-century competencies, Eikei focuses on Social System Design, digital transformation (DX), design thinking, sustainability, and interdisciplinary problem-solving.

Hiroshima Prefectural International Leadership Scholarship at Eikei

Prestigious prefectural scholarship supporting high-potential international graduate scholars dedicated to creating innovative social systems and addressing the SDGs.

Master of Science in Social System Design & Digital Innovation (DX)

Interdisciplinary project-based master's curriculum combining system dynamics, design thinking, business modeling, data analytics, and ethical technology deployment.

Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Regional Innovation & Smart Cities with Hiroshima Uni

Three-year joint doctoral research appointment investigating smart urban mobility, autonomous public transit in aging rural communities, and data-driven civic governance.

Center for Social System Innovation Laboratory Research Assistantship

Applied research post modeling disaster evacuation behaviors, micro-mobility logistics, and community resilience algorithms in western Japan.

Chugoku Regional Circular Economy & Renewable Energy Practicum

Three-month industrial practicum with regional manufacturing leaders in Hiroshima analyzing industrial waste valorization, green hydrogen supply chains, and ESG auditing.

JSPS-Eikei Postdoctoral Fellowship in Global Peace Engineering & Conflict Resolution

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship supported by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) investigating technology-enabled peace mediation and humanitarian digital infrastructure.

Master of Arts in Global Leadership & Cross-Cultural Project Management

Executive-style master's degree training international project managers, NGO directors, and corporate sustainability heads in cross-cultural leadership and strategic facilitation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education with a bachelor's degree in any discipline from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral research conducted via joint affiliated graduate programs in social system engineering and regional innovation with Hiroshima University.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79+, IELTS Academic 6.0+, or Duolingo English Test 105+; Japanese language proficiency is not mandatory for English-track programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2–3 page project proposal outlining a real-world societal challenge, proposed systemic intervention, and alignment with the SDGs.
Application fee
¥17,000 to ¥30,000 JPY examination fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's certificate of graduation, academic transcript, letters of recommendation, CV, and statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is approximately ¥535,800 JPY per year (admission fee of ¥282,000 JPY for residents of Hiroshima Prefecture, ¥394,000 JPY for non-residents). International students receive substantial tuition fee reduction scholarships (30% to 100%), and Japanese Government MEXT scholarships provide full funding.
