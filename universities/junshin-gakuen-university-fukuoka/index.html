Junshin Gakuen University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded upon Catholic educational foundations of wisdom and compassion, Junshin Gakuen University delivers advanced four-year baccalaureate training across four vital healthcare disciplines: Radiological Technology, Medical Laboratory Science, Health and Nursing, and Clinical Engineering. Equipped with university-grade CT/MRI simulation suites, flow cytometry labs, and hemodialysis units, Junshin Gakuen trains high-calibre licensed clinical professionals for major university hospitals throughout Japan.
