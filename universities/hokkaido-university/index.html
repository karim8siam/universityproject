Hokkaido University (Sapporo, Japan)

Established in 1876 in Sapporo, Hokkaido, Hokkaido University (Hokudai) is one of Japan's designated Imperial Universities and top research powerhouses. Founded under the motto 'Boys, be ambitious!' by Dr. William S. Clark, Hokudai is celebrated for world-changing chemistry (Nobel Laureate Akira Suzuki for Suzuki-Miyaura cross-coupling; home to the Institute for Chemical Reaction Design and Discovery - ICReDD), cryosphere and Arctic environmental science, veterinary medicine, and materials science. Hokkaido University MEXT University Recommendation & President's Fellowships

Japan's premier governmental and university scholarship scheme, providing exceptional international scholars with full tuition waivers and generous living stipends for doctoral research.

Hokkaido University PhD in Chemical Sciences and Engineering (AGP Course)

Advanced 3-year doctoral research program covering computer-driven chemical discovery, catalytic reaction design, macromolecular chemistry, and energy materials.

Hokkaido University Master of Environmental Science (English Program)

World-class 2-year English-medium master's program exploring cryosphere dynamics, polar ecology, climate change modeling, and environmental remediation.

Hokkaido University Master of Engineering in Materials Science and Engineering

Cutting-edge international master's degree focusing on advanced semiconductor materials, structural ceramics, eco-materials, and nanostructured metallurgy.

Institute for Chemical Reaction Design and Discovery (ICReDD) Placements

World Premier International (WPI) research placements combining artificial intelligence, quantum chemical calculations, and experimental synthesis to invent new chemical reactions.

Hokkaido Summer Institute (HSI) Laboratory Placements

Intensive summer laboratory placement program bringing world-leading professors and international students to Sapporo for hands-on frontier scientific experiments.

Hokkaido University Teaching & Research Assistantships (TA/RA)

On-campus academic assistantships supporting graduate researchers while they mentor undergraduate laboratory experiments, grade exercises, and support academic research projects.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
16 years of formal education / recognized Bachelor's degree equivalent; passing of the Hokkaido University graduate entrance examination and interview.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or professional degree equivalent; formal preliminary acceptance by a Hokkaido University prospective laboratory professor (*Kyoju*) and research plan approval.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency for English-taught graduate programs: TOEFL iBT 80+ or Academic IELTS 6.5+ (Japanese programs require JLPT N1 or N2).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 3-5 page research plan (*Kenkyu Keikaku-sho*) detailing theoretical framework, experiment design, and lab alignment.
Application fee
¥30,000 entrance examination application fee; admission fee of ¥282,000 and annual tuition of ¥535,800 (fully waived for MEXT and top international scholarship scholars).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, graduation certificates, 2 letters of recommendation, health certificate, and research plan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is fully waived for scholars receiving Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarships and Hokkaido University President's Fellowships. Recipients receive 100% full tuition exemption (saving ¥535,800/year plus the ¥282,000 matriculation fee) and monthly tax-free living allowances of ¥147,000 to ¥180,000/month.
