Leeds Arts University: Creative Practice, Digital Fashion & Fine Art Guide

Established in 1846 as the Leeds Government School of Art and Design, Leeds Arts University is the only specialist arts university in the North of England. Famous for cultivating generation-defining artists and designers (including Barbara Hepworth, Henry Moore, and Damien Hirst), the university provides world-class studio facilities, printmaking workshops, digital fashion suites, and postgraduate creative practice research.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited UK 2:1 honours degree (or international equivalent with minimum 60% or 3.0/4.0 GPA) in an art, design, architecture, or creative media discipline, plus a comprehensive creative portfolio.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative doctoral research programs facilitated in partnership with the University of Leeds and the Yorkshire Doctoral Training Partnership.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.0 overall (with at least 5.5 in each individual component), Cambridge C1 Advanced, or Pearson PTE 56.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for MA applicants: 1,000-word creative proposal outlining practice-led research themes, conceptual framework, and proposed studio methodology.
Application fee
£0 application fee for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Certified undergraduate diploma and academic transcripts, digital visual portfolio (15-20 images or video showreel), curriculum vitae, and two academic/creative reference letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Leeds Arts University is £9,250 per year for UK domestic students and £17,500 to £18,500 per year for international postgraduate students. Leeds Arts University offers International Vice-Chancellor's Postgraduate Scholarships providing up to £3,000 in tuition reductions.
