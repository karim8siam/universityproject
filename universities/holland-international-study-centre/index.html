Institution Overview & Profile

Holland International Study Centre (Holland ISC), located in Amsterdam, is an accredited higher education pathway institution operated in partnership with leading Dutch research universities (including Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, Erasmus University Rotterdam, and Tilburg University) providing intensive International Foundation Year programs in business, engineering, science, and social sciences with guaranteed progression routes.

Institution TypeInternational University Preparation Centre (Study Group Partner)
Main CampusAmsterdam Campus (Johan Cruijff Boulevard), North Holland, Netherlands
AccreditationDutch Ministry of Education Recognition / Partnered with Dutch Research Universities
Admissions RouteDirect online application with secondary school qualification review
Language of InstructionEnglish (100% English instruction; IELTS 5.0 to 5.5 overall)
Financial Aid & GrantsHolland ISC Academic Merit Scholarships & Dutch University Progression Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Direct academic progression routes to world-ranked Dutch research universities (WO) and universities of applied sciences (HBO)
  • Intensive academic English, European university study skills, and subject-specific foundation tracks
  • Prime Amsterdam location with state-of-the-art modern digital classrooms and student support networks
  • Over 90% progression rate into undergraduate bachelor degree programs across the Netherlands
