Showa University Yokohama Campus

Showa University (Yokohama Campus) is a premier medical sciences university campus and teaching hospital in Aoba-ku, Yokohama, celebrated for multidisciplinary healthcare education across medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, and nursing.

Academic overview & research priorities

Showa University is internationally recognized for its pioneering integrated first-year residential curriculum and interprofessional medical education (IPE), training doctors, dentists, pharmacists, and nurses collaboratively. The Yokohama Northern Hospital (Showa University Fujigaoka and Northern Hospitals) acts as a tertiary emergency and advanced cancer surgical center.

Research admissions & international criteria

Highly competitive admissions via national entrance examinations for School of Medicine, School of Dentistry, School of Pharmacy, and School of Nursing and Rehabilitation Sciences. Graduate applicants require professional healthcare licensure, academic transcripts, and departmental research defense.
