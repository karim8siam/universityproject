Boise State University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1932 on the south bank of the Boise River, Boise State University enrolls over 26,000 students across more than 200 academic degrees. Classified as an R2 Doctoral University with High Research Activity, Boise State is a key research partner of Micron Technology, the Idaho National Laboratory (INL), and the semiconductor industry—housing the Micron School of Materials Science & Engineering and leading advanced research in quantum materials, biomass fuels, and raptor biology.
