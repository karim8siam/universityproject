Academic overview & excellence

Located in the Upper Rhine economic corridor near Strasbourg and Freiburg, Hochschule Offenburg encompasses four faculties: Electrical Engineering, Medical Technology & Computer Science; Mechanical & Process Engineering; Media; and Business Administration. The university maintains the Peter Osypka Institute for Medical Engineering, the Institute for Energy Systems Technology (INES), and the Center for Artificial Intelligence.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a recognized higher education entrance certificate. International Graduate School applications require an above-average bachelor's degree in engineering or related sciences. English-taught programs require TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0.
