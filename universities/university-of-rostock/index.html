University of Rostock: Marine Technology, Catalysis & Life Sciences Guide

Founded in 1419, the University of Rostock (Universität Rostock) is one of the oldest universities in the world and the oldest in the Baltic Sea region. Known internationally for maritime engineering, homogeneous catalysis, interdisciplinary life sciences, and demography, Rostock offers high-calibre tuition-free higher education backed by leading Leibniz and Max Planck partner institutes.

Rostock Graduate Academy Fellowship in Catalysis and Molecular Sciences

Doctoral scholarship sponsored by the University of Rostock Graduate Academy in conjunction with the Leibniz Institute for Catalysis (LIKAT) supporting sustainable chemical synthesis.

Master of Science in Computational Science and Engineering

Two-year English-taught master's curriculum combining high-performance computing, numerical simulation, and mathematical modeling for industrial and engineering systems.

Salaried Doctoral Researcher (TV-L E13) in Ocean Engineering & Marine Cybernetics

Three-year salaried research position at the Chair of Ocean Engineering investigating autonomous underwater navigation, flow acoustics, and Baltic deep-water sensor systems.

Baltic Coastal Ecology and Sediment Dynamics Field Investigation

Joint collaborative research initiative with the Leibniz Institute for Baltic Sea Research Warnemünde (IOW) evaluating seasonal oxygen depletion and biogeochemical cycles.

Fraunhofer IGD Ocean Technology & Computer Vision Internship

Six-month applied research placement at the Fraunhofer Institute for Computer Graphics Research (IGD) Rostock focusing on underwater image restoration and sonar target recognition.

Rostock Postdoctoral Fellowship in Homogeneous Catalysis for Carbon Neutrality

Two-year postdoctoral appointment at LIKAT Rostock engineering Earth-abundant metal catalysts for carbon dioxide hydrogenation and sustainable polymer synthesis.

Deutschlandstipendium National Merit Scholarship at Uni Rostock

Co-funded federal and private scholarship awarded to talented domestic and international students enrolled at the University of Rostock demonstrating academic merit and societal engagement.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science or Bachelor of Engineering degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in a relevant subject with a German grade equivalent of 2.5 or better.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed master's degree (300 ECTS total academic qualification) with proven scientific research capability and acceptance by a university doctoral faculty board.
Language proficiency
German language proficiency at DSH-2 / TestDaF 4 for German-taught degrees; IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught master's degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral candidates: comprehensive research proposal (10-15 pages) approved by a Rostock university professor acting as doctoral supervisor (Doktorvater).
Application fee
EUR 75 via uni-assist for international credential evaluations (direct university application is free).
Required documentation
Certified degree certificate, complete academic transcript with grading scale, CV, language certificates, and statement of research motivation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Public university education in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern is tuition-free for both domestic and international students. Enrolled students pay a semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately EUR 310 per semester, which includes a comprehensive regional transit ticket (Semesterticket) and student services access.
