Stockholm School of Economics (Handelshögskolan i Stockholm)

Founded in 1909 by Swedish business leaders, the Stockholm School of Economics (Handelshögskolan i Stockholm - SSE) is one of Europe's most prestigious standalone business and economics universities. Consistently ranked by the Financial Times as the #1 business school in the Nordic countries and top 20 in Europe, SSE produces business leaders, central bankers, and world-renowned academic researchers. Operating in the heart of Stockholm, SSE doctoral students receive fully funded salaried employment positions under Swedish collective agreements with zero tuition fees. SSE Full PhD Fellowships in Economics & Finance (Fully Salaried)

SSE's premier fully funded doctoral fellowship with placement records into top international institutions (IMF, World Bank, Federal Reserve, LSE, Harvard) and European central banks.

SSE Master of Science (MSc) in Finance (FT Ranked #1 in Nordics)

Two-year Master of Science program ranked #1 in Northern Europe, preparing students for investment banking, quantitative asset management, and corporate restructuring.

SSE Master of Science (MSc) in Economics

Two-year program with specializations in International Economics and Applied Economic Analysis, taught entirely in English in Stockholm.

SSE SITE Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Transition Economics

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Stockholm Institute of Transition Economics (SITE) analyzing economic development, institutional reforms, and energy transition policy in Eastern Europe.

SSE Master of Science (MSc) in Business & Management (Data Analytics Track)

Two-year management curriculum fusing strategy, marketing, machine learning for business analytics, and ethical digital transformation.

SSE Business Lab Summer Venture Incubator Traineeship

Eight-week startup incubation residency at SSE Business Lab (which incubated Klarna and Budbee), offering office space, legal advisory, and direct angel investor access.

SSE Mistra Center for Sustainable Markets (MISUM) Senior Researcher

Three-year research position at MISUM conducting empirical research on sustainable capital markets, biodiversity risk in asset pricing, and green corporate governance.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) with high academic standing. Competitive candidates typically present GMAT scores of 650+ (Focus Edition 595+) or GRE equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (or equivalent 4-year degree) in economics, finance, business administration, mathematics, or statistics with top academic honors.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 100 (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 3-page research proposal detailing research inquiries, methodology, relevant literature, and potential supervisor alignment.
Application fee
SEK 0 application fee for PhD programs; SEK 900 for non-EU Master's applicants via the SSE direct application portal.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, GMAT/GRE test score report, 2 confidential letters of recommendation, and personal motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In Sweden, doctoral education is completely free of tuition fees for all students regardless of nationality. Furthermore, SSE doctoral candidates are hired on fully funded salaried employment contracts (doktorandanställning) receiving approximately SEK 33,000 to SEK 37,000 per month with full Swedish social security, health, and parental benefits for four years. For Master's programs, SSE and corporate partners offer full tuition scholarship waivers covering the SEK 360,000 two-year tuition.
