University of Pennsylvania

Founded in 1740 by Benjamin Franklin, the University of Pennsylvania (Penn) is an Ivy League research powerhouse and one of the nine colonial colleges founded before the American Declaration of Independence. Home to the legendary Wharton School, Penn Medicine (where Nobel laureates Katalin Karikó and Drew Weissman pioneered mRNA vaccine technology), Penn Engineering, and the School of Arts and Sciences, Penn commands over $1.4 billion in annual sponsored research, guaranteeing full multi-year doctoral funding fellowships for all admitted PhD candidates.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Penn Biomedical Graduate Studies (BGS) Fully Funded Doctoral Fellowships

World-leading doctoral training across 7 biomedical graduate groups at Penn Medicine (home of Nobel Prize-winning mRNA technology), providing five years of guaranteed living stipends, healthcare, and tuition waivers.

Penn Engineering PhD in Computer and Information Science (CIS)

Top-ranked doctoral program in computer science, offering cutting-edge research in machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics (GRASP Lab), database systems, and cryptography.

Penn Engineering Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) in Robotics (GRASP Lab)

Prestigious interdisciplinary Master of Science delivered by the world-famous General Robotics, Automation, Sensing & Perception (GRASP) Laboratory, investigating autonomous flying micro-drones, legged locomotion, and surgical robotics.

Penn PhD in Physics and Astronomy - Quantum Matter & Nanotechnology

Doctoral research conducted within the Laboratory for Research on the Structure of Matter (LRSM) and the Singh Center for Nanotechnology, investigating 2D materials, topological quantum matter, and quantum optics.

Penn Perry World House Postdoctoral Fellowship in International Affairs

Two-year postdoctoral appointment at Perry World House, Penn's global policy hub, connecting academic research with international policymakers on emerging technologies, climate security, and international governance.

Penn GRASP Lab Summer Robotics Research Internship (REU)

Ten-week National Science Foundation REU summer research traineeship at the GRASP Lab, collaborating with world-leading roboticists on autonomous drone navigation, multi-robot coordination, and tactile robotic hands.

Penn Medicine Postdoctoral Researcher in mRNA Therapeutics & Lipid Nanoparticles

Postdoctoral research role at the Penn Institute for RNA Innovation, designing next-generation modified mRNA sequences, organ-specific lipid nanoparticles (LNPs), and therapeutic cancer vaccines.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree with a superior academic record (minimum 3.5/4.0 GPA or equivalent first-class standing); prerequisite technical coursework in relevant engineering or scientific disciplines.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's or Master's degree with distinguished academic honors; demonstrated capacity for high-impact independent research through publications or honors theses.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall (minimum 7.0 in speaking) or TOEFL iBT 100 (minimum 24 in all sections); higher for Computer Science and Biomedical Graduate Studies.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-page Statement of Purpose detailing intellectual background, research problems of interest, faculty mentors with whom the applicant wishes to work, and future scholarly aspirations.
Application fee
$90 to $100 non-refundable application fee submitted via the Penn Graduate Admissions system (application fee waivers available for eligible scholars).
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 3 academic letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, and GRE scores (where specified by individual departments).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

The University of Pennsylvania provides full 5-year guaranteed funding packages to all admitted PhD students in good academic standing. The standard doctoral package covers 100% of full tuition ($46,000/year graduate rate), comprehensive Penn Student Insurance Plan (PSIP, valued at over $4,200/year), full general university fees, and an annual living stipend of $43,000 to $46,500 for 10 to 12 months (combining Penn fellowships, research assistantships, and teaching fellowships).
