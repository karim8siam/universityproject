Academic overview & excellence

Located in the scenic residential and cultural Maruyama district of Sapporo, this campus functions as Hokusho University's urban satellite and graduate education center, working alongside the main Ebetsu campus. Facilities include athletic human performance biomechanics suites, psychological clinical counseling clinics, early childhood development ateliers, and northern health wellness research laboratories. Scholars at Hokusho conduct high-impact research on subarctic winter athletic performance, geriatric rehabilitation, and educational psychology.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate and graduate admission at Hokusho University is conducted through the Admissions Center in accordance with MEXT standards. International applicants submit Japanese language proficiency certification (JLPT N2 or N1 for degree programs), certified academic transcripts, personal statement of intent, and participate in an interview. Graduate applicants in Lifelong Learning or Sports Science must submit a detailed research plan (Kenkyu Keikakusho) and undergraduate thesis abstract.
