Central Alabama Community College

Central Alabama Community College (CACC) is an accredited public comprehensive community college headquartered in Alexander City, Alabama, with additional campus sites in Childersburg, Talladega, and Prattville. Accredited by SACSCOC, CACC provides accessible associate degrees, university transfer pathways, and premier technical career training programs.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Alexander City, Alabama, USA

Institutional Overview

CACC offers Associate in Arts (A.A.), Associate in Science (A.S.), and Associate in Applied Science (A.A.S.) degrees in high-demand disciplines such as Nursing (RN), Mechatronics, Industrial Electronics, Machine Tool Technology, and Welding. CACC seamlessly articulates credits with major four-year Alabama public universities including Auburn University and the University of Alabama through the STARS statewide agreement.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

CACC operates an open-door admissions policy for secondary school graduates and GED recipients. International candidates must submit credential evaluations and proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 61 or IELTS 5.5). In-state tuition is approximately $165 per credit hour. Federal Pell Grants, Alabama work-study, and competitive college foundation scholarships are available to enrolled students.
