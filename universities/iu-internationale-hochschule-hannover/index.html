Academic overview & research priorities

Located in Lower Saxony’s capital, IU Hannover combines rigorous academic curriculum with direct corporate employment. Students alternate weekly between classroom lectures in Hannover and corporate training at major logistics hubs, medical clinics, IT firms, and municipal social agencies. Corporate partners finance 100% of study tuition and provide monthly trainee compensation.

Research admissions & international criteria

German Abitur, Fachhochschulreife, or completed vocational training plus a successful corporate partner interview. No tuition is paid by students, as partner corporations assume 100% of tuition costs.
