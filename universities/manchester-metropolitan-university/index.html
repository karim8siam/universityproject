Manchester Metropolitan University: Manchester School of Art, STEM & Doctoral Studies

Manchester Metropolitan University (Manchester Met / MMU) is a major public university located on a dynamic urban campus in central Manchester. Globally celebrated for the historic Manchester School of Art (dating back to 1838), the Manchester Fuel Cell Innovation Centre, advanced sports biomechanics, and sustainable fashion.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Manchester Met Vice-Chancellor's International Postgraduate Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship supporting international students enrolling in full-time taught master's degrees at Manchester Metropolitan University.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Sustainable Fashion & Circular Textile Materials

Doctoral research conducted within the prestigious Manchester Fashion Institute evaluating closed-loop textile recycling, microbial cellulose fabrics, and sustainable supply chain governance.

Master of Science (MSc) in Advanced Materials & Electrochemical Energy Storage

Advanced master's curriculum exploring additive manufacturing of functional ceramics, 2D nanomaterials (graphene), solid-state electrolytes, and hydrogen energy systems.

Manchester Fuel Cell Innovation Centre (MFCIC) Hydrogen Research Assistantship

Applied research post at MFCIC developing low-platinum electrocatalysts for PEM water electrolysis and industrial hydrogen fuel cell stacks.

Manchester Institute of Sport Elite Athlete Biomechanics Practicum

Hands-on practicum working inside the Manchester Institute of Sport analyzing high-speed motion capture kinematics and force plate metrics alongside Olympic and Premier League athletes.

Faculty of Science & Engineering Postdoctoral Fellowship in Graphene Nanocomposites

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship researching printed graphene sensor arrays, electrochemically active supercapacitors, and smart composite coatings.

Master of Architecture (MArch) at Manchester School of Architecture (Joint with UoM)

Ranked among the top 5 architecture schools globally, the Manchester School of Architecture (MSA) combines the faculties of Manchester Met and the University of Manchester to confer RIBA Part 2 accreditation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK honours degree (minimum 2:2, with 2:1 preferred for competitive courses) or international equivalent in an appropriate field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with merit or distinction (or international equivalent), accompanied by a formal supervisory endorsement from an MMU faculty mentor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 to 6.0 in all bands depending on program); Architecture and creative writing require 7.0 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
For research degrees: 2,000-word proposal detailing research questions, theoretical context, proposed methodology, and resource requirements.
Application fee
£0 (free direct online application via the Manchester Met portal).
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, two academic reference letters, personal statement, and portfolio (for Art and Design courses).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition typically ranges from £17,500 to £20,500 per year. Manchester Met Vice-Chancellor Scholarships provide competitive fee discounts of £2,000 to £3,000 for high-achieving international postgraduates, and UKRI-aligned doctoral studentships cover 100% of tuition plus an annual living stipend of £19,237.
