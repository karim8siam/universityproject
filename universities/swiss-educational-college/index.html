Swiss Educational College: Luxury Hospitality, Alpine Tourism & Business Guide

Located in Visp in the heart of the Swiss Alps (Canton of Valais, near Zermatt and the Matterhorn), the Swiss Educational College (SEC) is an accredited Swiss higher education institute specializing in international hospitality management, luxury brand strategy, sustainable alpine tourism, and global event management. SEC combines practical Swiss hotel precision with cutting-edge digital service analytics.

Merit-based institutional scholarship awarding substantial tuition fee relief to high-achieving international candidates enrolling in postgraduate hospitality programs in Switzerland.

One-year intensive master's degree training executive hotel managers in luxury customer experience design, revenue management algorithms, hotel asset valuation, and wine business.

Three-year practitioner doctorate developing empirical frameworks for four-season alpine tourism diversification, snowmaking economics, and zero-carbon hotel operations.

Applied environmental and hospitality research project quantifying greenhouse gas emissions across mountain hotel supply chains and evaluating local farm-to-table procurement.

Swiss 5-Star Luxury Resort Operations & Concierge Practicum

Six-month paid professional internship placed within premier Swiss luxury resorts in Zermatt, St. Moritz, and Geneva managing front office operations and guest relations.

SEC Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Luxury Customer Experience and AI Analytics

Two-year research fellowship conducting empirical investigations on hyper-personalized luxury service delivery, predictive guest sentiment analytics, and AI concierge bots.

Valais Regional Women in Hospitality Leadership Grant

Targeted scholarship promoting gender parity in international luxury hotel general management, resort directorship, and hospitality entrepreneurship.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (or international equivalent 180 ECTS) in business, tourism, hospitality, or social sciences with a minimum GPA of 2.7 on a 4.0 scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative DBA research programs in Alpine Tourism Economics and Luxury Branding hosted in partnership with accredited international university faculties.
Language proficiency
English proficiency at CEFR B2/C1 level (IELTS Academic 6.0 overall, TOEFL iBT 80, or Duolingo English Test 105) or certified degree taught in English.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for master's and executive candidates: 1,000-word statement outlining proposed investigation into luxury customer retention or circular tourism practices.
Application fee
150 CHF non-refundable application and candidate file evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified copies of undergraduate degree and transcripts with English translations, passport copy, comprehensive CV, two letters of recommendation, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the Swiss Educational College averages 14,500 CHF to 18,500 CHF per academic year for master's programs. SEC provides Swiss Alpine Excellence Scholarships offering up to 30% tuition fee reductions, alongside paid 5-star Swiss hotel internship placements.
