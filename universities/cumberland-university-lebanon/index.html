Academic overview & excellence

Chartered in 1842, Cumberland University is one of the oldest institutions of higher education in Tennessee. Located just east of Nashville, Cumberland provides comprehensive undergraduate and graduate programs across three schools: the Jeanette C. Rudy School of Nursing & Health Professions, the Labry School of Science, Technology & Business, and the School of Humanities, Education & the Arts. Notable alumni include U.S. Supreme Court justices, Nobel Peace Prize recipient Cordell Hull, and dozens of governors and members of Congress.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate freshman admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 3.0 recommended) and optional ACT/SAT scores. Nursing admission requires completion of prerequisite sciences with a minimum 3.0 GPA and satisfactory score on the TEAS exam. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 70 or IELTS 6.0.
