Angelo State University: Cybersecurity, Applied Physics, Nursing & Agriculture Guide

Established in 1928 and a proud member of the prestigious Texas Tech University System, Angelo State University (ASU) is a comprehensive public doctoral university situated in San Angelo, West Texas. ASU is celebrated for its multi-million dollar Carr Academic Scholarship endowment, state-of-the-art cybersecurity and physics labs, nationally ranked nursing programs, and 6,000-acre agricultural research center.

Angelo State University Carr Distinguished Academic Scholarship

Endowed scholarship granting renewable annual funding and an automatic Texas in-state tuition fee waiver to qualifying domestic and international scholars.

Master of Science in Computer Science and Information Security

Two-year graduate program emphasizing cloud network defense, secure cryptographic protocol implementation, artificial intelligence, and database architectures.

Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) Clinical Research Fellowship

Three-year CAPTE-accredited clinical doctoral program producing physical therapy practitioners and researchers conducting clinical neuromuscular rehabilitation studies.

ASU Center for Security Studies Border Intelligence and Cyber Threat Grant

Funded research project assessing advanced persistent threats (APT), geospatial intelligence telemetry, and infrastructure cybersecurity along international transportation corridors.

ASU Management, Agriculture & Tech Corporate Cooperative Internship

Four-month cooperative immersion with Texas agricultural technology firms, energy companies, and healthcare providers managing precision agronomy and IT operations.

ASU Postdoctoral Fellowship in Precision Agriculture & Rangeland Ecology

Two-year postdoctoral position conducting applied research at ASU's Management, Instruction and Research (MIR) Center testing drought-tolerant forage and remote livestock tracking.

Angelo State University Blue & Gold International Leadership Grant

University leadership grant assisting global students who demonstrate academic promise, civic responsibility, and active contributions to campus internationalization.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited institution with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 2.75 out of 4.0 (or 3.0 in the last 60 undergraduate hours).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) and collaborative doctoral programs; requiring an accredited bachelor's degree, prerequisite sciences (minimum 3.00 GPA), and competitive GRE scores.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0), or Duolingo English Test 105+ for international non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate thesis tracks: 1,000-word statement of research intent describing scientific questions in cybersecurity, physics, or animal agriculture.
Application fee
$50 USD non-refundable application fee for international and domestic applicants ($40 for military veterans).
Required documentation
Official university transcripts from all post-secondary institutions, proof of English language proficiency, financial statement of support, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition at Angelo State University is approximately $9,200 USD per year for Texas residents and $22,400 USD per year for non-residents. Significantly, non-resident and international students receiving at least $1,000 USD in competitive academic scholarships qualify for a waiver granting Texas in-state tuition rates, reducing tuition by over $13,000 USD annually.
