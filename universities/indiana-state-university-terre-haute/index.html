Indiana State University

ISU · Terre Haute, Indiana, United States

Indiana State University (ISU) is a public doctoral research university in Terre Haute, Indiana, renowned for professional aviation flight technology, packaging engineering, nursing, criminology, and athletic training.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

ISU operates Indiana’s premier public collegiate flight academy at Terre Haute Regional Airport, hosts pioneering research in bat ecology and conservation biology, and integrates community-based experiential learning into all academic degree pathways.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation with cumulative GPA >= 2.5 under test-optional guidelines. Graduate programs require a recognized bachelor's degree, minimum 2.7–3.0 GPA, and department-specific credentials.
