Academic overview & excellence

Located in the historic footwear and chemical processing heart of Rhineland-Palatinate, Campus Pirmasens hosts the Department of Applied Logistics and Polymer Sciences (Fachbereich Angewandte Logistik- und Polymerwissenschaften). The campus features specialized cleanrooms, pharmaceutical compounding laboratories, polymer processing extruders, and microfluidic testing benches. Academic programs bridge technical chemistry, pharmaceutical product formulation, logistics engineering, and medical device material science, maintaining strong cooperative links with global chemical conglomerates (BASF) and pharmaceutical manufacturers.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to bachelor's degrees (B.Sc. in Applied Chemistry, Pharmaceutical Engineering, Plastics Technology) requires German university entrance qualification (Fachhochschulreife or Abitur) or equivalent international secondary school certification. Master's admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Process Engineering, or Materials Science with a minimum German grade of 2.5, proof of German language proficiency (TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2 for German-taught degrees), and formal application through uni-assist.
