Vechtdal College Ommen - Pre-University Science & Ecological Center

Vechtdal College in Ommen is a leading pre-university gymnasium and atheneum (VWO) institution in Overijssel, celebrated for its river ecology research, renewable energy projects along the Vecht river, and university bridge programs with the University of Twente.

Academic overview & excellence

Located in the verdant Overijsselse Vecht river basin, Vechtdal College Ommen combines rigorous pre-university academics with regional ecological and technological fieldwork. The campus features state-of-the-art analytical chemistry laboratories, environmental monitoring stations, a technology maker workshop, and a digital learning commons. In partnership with the University of Twente and regional water authority Waterschap Drents Overijsselse Delta, senior scholars conduct environmental telemetry, hydrological flood modeling, and sustainable biomass investigations.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to the senior VWO (Gymnasium and Atheneum) academic tracks at Vechtdal College Ommen requires an official Dutch primary education VWO recommendation or international equivalent secondary school credentials indicating superior performance in Mathematics, Physics, and Natural Sciences. For international or bilingual stream applicants, English proficiency (CEFR B2) and Dutch language aptitude are assessed via entrance portfolio and interview.
