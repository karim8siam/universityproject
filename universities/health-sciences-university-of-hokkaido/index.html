Health Sciences University of Hokkaido: Medicine, Pharmacy & MEXT Guide

Health Sciences University of Hokkaido (HSUH - Higashi Nippon Gakuen) is a leading private medical and healthcare university in northern Japan, with campuses in Tobetsu and Sapporo. Operating accredited faculties and graduate schools in Dentistry, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Nursing, Rehabilitation, and Psychological Sciences, HSUH trains premier biomedical clinicians and healthcare researchers.

Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at HSUH

Prestigious national scholarship awarded to exceptional international scholars pursuing doctoral degrees in dentistry, pharmacy, or pharmaceutical biology at HSUH.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Pharmaceutical Sciences & Molecular Therapeutics

Four-year doctoral program investigating medicinal plant natural products from Hokkaido flora, organic synthesis, drug delivery nanotechnology, and cellular pharmacology.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Dental Science & Oral Biomaterials

Doctoral research exploring biocompatible ceramic implants, periodontal tissue regeneration, oral microbiology, and 3D bioprinting for craniofacial reconstruction.

Hokkaido Medicinal Plant Bioactive Natural Products Research Assistantship

Laboratory research assistantship isolating and characterizing novel bioactive secondary metabolites from northern Japanese indigenous flora for anticancer drug discovery.

Sapporo University Hospital Advanced Clinical Pharmacy Practicum

Twenty-week clinical hospital rotation at HSUH University Hospital in Sapporo, participating in ward rounds, therapeutic drug monitoring, and clinical chemotherapy protocols.

JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowship at HSUH in Craniofacial Molecular Genetics

Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) fellowship supporting an international postdoctoral scholar investigating signal transduction pathways in embryonic craniofacial morphogenesis.

Master of Science in Rehabilitation Science & Physical Therapy Robotics

Two-year master's program analyzing neuromuscular motor control, robotic gait retraining exoskeletons, and functional recovery after stroke.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of a 4-year undergraduate degree in pharmacy, nursing, biological science, or healthcare from an accredited institution (16 years of prior education).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completion of a 6-year professional medical/dental/pharmacy degree or master's degree (18 years total education) with successful doctoral thesis proposal defense.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2/N1 required for clinical programs; CEFR B2 (IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL 75+) accepted for laboratory-based doctoral research groups.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral entry: 2,500-word research proposal formatted to HSUH Graduate School guidelines aligned with a confirmed supervising professor.
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY application screening fee.
Required documentation
Graduation diploma, academic transcripts, research proposal, health certificate, letters of recommendation, and Japanese or English language certification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is approximately ¥1,000,000 JPY to ¥1,400,000 JPY per year depending on the graduate school. International students are eligible for HSUH international tuition reductions (up to 50% waiver), and qualified candidates can secure Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarships.
