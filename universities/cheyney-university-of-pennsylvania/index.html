Cheyney University of Pennsylvania

Cheyney University of Pennsylvania holds the historic distinction of being the first institution of higher education established for African Americans in the United States (founded 1837), operating as a leading public HBCU within the Pennsylvania State System of Higher Education (PASSHE).

Academic overview & excellence

Cheyney excels in applied biosciences, aquaculture, chemistry, cybersecurity, business administration, and communications. Its flagship Bioscience Science & Technology Hub hosts public-private partnerships conducting cutting-edge commercial biotechnology and urban agricultural research.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate applicants submit high school transcripts with a recommended minimum GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale. Cheyney offers test-optional admissions for SAT/ACT. International candidates submit TOEFL (minimum 70) or IELTS (minimum 6.0) scores.
