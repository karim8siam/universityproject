University of Pittsburgh

The University of Pittsburgh (Pitt) is a world-class public research university and AAU member, consistently ranked among the top recipients of NIH biomedical research funding globally and celebrated for the Jonas Salk polio vaccine, organ transplantation, and neuro-technologies.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1787 on the American frontier as the Pittsburgh Academy, the University of Pittsburgh has evolved into a global powerhouse in medicine, computer science, bioengineering, and philosophy of science. Enrolling more than 34,000 students on its urban campus anchored by the iconic 42-story Cathedral of Learning, Pitt is closely partnered with the USD $24 billion UPMC healthcare system. Pitt scientists developed the world’s first licensed polio vaccine, pioneered modern multivisceral organ transplantation under Thomas Starzl, and are currently creating brain-computer interfaces allowing paralyzed individuals to control robotic limbs with direct sensory feedback.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Bioengineering (Swanson School of Engineering) (Doctorate) — World-renowned interdisciplinary doctoral program bridging neural engineering, cardiovascular fluid dynamics, and biomaterial tissue scaffolding.
  • Doctor of Medicine (MD Program, Pitt School of Medicine) (Doctorate) — Consistently ranked among the top 15 medical schools in the US, featuring the pioneering Three Rivers medical curriculum and clinical rotations at UPMC.
  • Master of Science in Information Science (MSIS, Big Data & Cybersecurity) (Master's) — Cutting-edge degree developing software architectures for high-throughput enterprise systems, data mining, and defensive cybersecurity.
  • Bachelor of Science in Neuroscience (Bachelor's) — Elite undergraduate program offering intensive laboratory apprenticeships within the University of Pittsburgh Brain Institute.
