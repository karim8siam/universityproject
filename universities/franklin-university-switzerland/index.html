Franklin University Switzerland: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Franklin University Switzerland (FUS), located on the hills of Lugano overlooking Lake Lugano in the Italian-speaking canton of Ticino, is a unique institution uniquely accredited in both Switzerland and the United States. Renowned for its mandatory Academic Travel program, FUS excels in international management, global climate diplomacy, digital marketing, and sustainable business transformation.

Master of Science (MSc) in International Management and Responsible Leadership

Dual Swiss-US accredited master's program providing immersive coursework in corporate sustainability, cross-cultural negotiations, ESG reporting, and embedded international consulting field visits.

Master of Arts (MA) in Climate Studies, Global Diplomacy and Sustainable Food Systems

One-year accelerated master's degree exploring global climate treaties, alpine ecological transitions, food security governance, and corporate supply chain decarbonization.

Franklin University Switzerland Global Leadership Presidential Scholarship

Franklin's most prestigious graduate financial award supporting outstanding international scholars dedicated to ethical leadership, multilateral dialogue, and social entrepreneurship.

Taylor Institute for Global Studies Research Assistantship in Digital Diplomacy

Six-month research residency within the Taylor Institute examining the role of social media in international crises, AI in consular communications, and diplomatic negotiation protocols.

Lugano Swiss FinTech & Digital Asset Innovation Practicum

Four-month practical internship in Canton Ticino's Plan ₿ blockchain initiative in Lugano, analyzing digital asset regulatory compliance, cryptocurrency payments, and enterprise tokenization.

Swiss-Italian Alpine Cultural Heritage and Transnational Studies Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral appointment leading field documentation of UNESCO World Heritage sites in Ticino and Northern Italy, analyzing vernacular stone masonry and borderland cultural memory.

Master of Science in Digital Marketing and Global Customer Experience

One-year accelerated master's curriculum combining omnichannel digital campaigns, consumer neuromarketing, predictive data analytics, and corporate brand management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized bachelor's degree from an accredited university with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Franklin focuses on master's and undergraduate curricula, offering collaborative doctoral pathways with European partner institutions.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in each band) or IELTS Academic 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-page Statement of Purpose explaining academic objectives, global perspectives, and anticipated contributions to FUS's international cohort.
Application fee
CHF 90 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, certified bachelor's diploma, resume/CV, 2 confidential letters of recommendation, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for graduate master's programs at Franklin University Switzerland is approximately CHF 38,000 for the full academic program. Over 70% of admitted graduate students receive Franklin University Switzerland Merit Scholarships, Dean's Awards, or Graduate Assistantships providing tuition reductions ranging from CHF 8,000 to CHF 15,000 alongside paid campus research appointments.
