Institution Overview & Profile

North West Regional College (NWRC), with main campuses across Derry~Londonderry, Limavady, and Strabane in Northern Ireland, is a major tertiary education institution delivering university-level Higher National Diplomas (HND), Foundation Degrees validated by Ulster University and Queen's University Belfast, and cutting-edge industrial innovation through the Business Support Centre (BSC).

Institution TypePublic Further and Higher Education College (Northern Ireland Executive)
Main CampusStrand Road Campus, Derry~Londonderry (plus Limavady and Strabane)
AccreditationDepartment for the Economy (DfE) / Validated by Ulster University & QUB / QAA
Admissions RouteNWRC Direct Higher Education Portal (admissions open January to August)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS Academic 6.0 overall with minimum 5.5 in each band)
Financial Aid & GrantsStudent Finance NI (Tuition Fee & Maintenance Grants) & NWRC Hardship Support

Key Academic Strengths

  • Degree-awarding partnerships with Ulster University and Queen's University Belfast
  • Award-winning Business Support Centre (BSC) providing technical R&D support to regional industry
  • Cutting-edge Food Technology Centre and Applied Industry 4.0 Prototyping Suites
  • Low tuition fees compared to Great Britain universities (£2,800/year for full-time foundation degrees)

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Foundation Degree (Level 5)

Foundation Degree in Science (FdSc) in Software Development

Two-year degree validated by Ulster University covering full-stack cloud computing, relational databases, Java, and cyber security.

Foundation Degree (Level 5)

Foundation Degree in Engineering (Renewable Energy and Electrical Systems)

Ulster University-validated engineering program focusing on wind turbine integration, solar PV microgrids, and PLC automation.

Higher National Diploma (Level 5)

Higher National Diploma (HND) in Applied Hospitality & Tourism Management

Two-year professional management curriculum with culinary leadership and international event operations.

Verified Academic Opportunities (7)

Scholarship

NWRC Higher Education Excellence Entrance Scholarship

Tuition discount awarded to high-achieving school leavers enrolling in Ulster University-validated STEM foundation degrees at NWRC.

Funding: £1,000 cash bursary paid across the first year of full-time higher education study Deadline: August 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Foundation Degree in Software Development (Ulster Validated) Enrolment

Two-year higher education program with guaranteed direct transfer into Year 3 of the B.Sc. (Hons) Computing at Ulster University Magee.

Funding: Full Student Finance NI tuition fee loan eligibility (£2,800/year) Deadline: August 31, 2027
Bachelor's

FdEng in Electrical & Renewable Energy Engineering Admission

Comprehensive engineering curriculum training technicians for Northern Ireland's booming onshore wind and green hydrogen grid installations.

Funding: Eligible for Department for the Economy (DfE) technical apprentice subsidies Deadline: August 31, 2027
Internship

NWRC Business Support Centre Industrial R&D Internship

Six-month paid technical internship assisting regional SMEs through Invest NI Innovation Vouchers in 3D prototyping and automation.

Funding: £11.44/hour wage (National Living Wage) plus industry innovation mentoring Deadline: May 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Foundation Degree in Hospitality & Tourism Management Admission

Applied hospitality curriculum validated by Ulster University, featuring an integrated 400-hour industry work placement in the Causeway Coast.

Funding: Tourism NI industry placement awards (£500–£1,000) Deadline: August 31, 2027
Bachelor's

Northern Ireland Higher Level Apprenticeship (HLA) in Software Engineering

Earn-while-you-learn programme where students work 4 days a week with IT employers (e.g., Allstate, Fujitsu) and attend NWRC 1 day per week.

Funding: 100% funded tuition (no student loan needed) plus starting salary of £17,000–£21,000/year Deadline: June 30, 2027
Grant

NWRC Student Hardship and Childcare Support Grant

Discretionary grant aiding higher education students facing childcare costs, travel burdens, or sudden financial hardship in the North West.

Funding: Up to £2,000 non-repayable grant per academic year Deadline: October 31, 2027
