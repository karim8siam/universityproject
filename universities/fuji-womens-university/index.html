Fuji Women's University: Food Science, Human Ecology & Social Welfare Guide

Founded in 1925 by Catholic sisters in Sapporo, Fuji Women's University (Fuji Joshi Daigaku) is Hokkaido's historic private university dedicated to women's higher education. Renowned for food sciences, nutritional biochemistry, clinical welfare, and English literature, FWU pairs rigorous laboratory research in Hokkaido agricultural bioresources with community-oriented human life sciences.

Tuition reduction scholarship awarded to outstanding international female scientists pursuing graduate degrees in functional foods, nutritional biochemistry, and food safety.

Two-year postgraduate research curriculum providing advanced clinical and laboratory training in bioactive Hokkaido food components, clinical dietetics, and metabolic health.

FWU Doctoral Research Fellowship in Nutritional Biochemistry and Human Aging

Three-year doctoral position investigating cellular mechanisms of dietary antioxidants, lipid metabolism regulation, and preventative nutrition in aging populations.

Applied scientific research project analyzing anthocyanin profiles and probiotic antimicrobial peptides derived from Hokkaido haskap berries and fermented dairy whey.

Sixteen-week immersive clinical internship in major Sapporo medical centers designing therapeutic enteral nutrition regimens and post-operative dietary plans.

Fuji Women's University Research Fellowship in Child Development and Social Policy

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment examining municipal child-rearing support infrastructure, work-life balance policies, and family resilience in depopulating regions of Japan.

MEXT Japanese Government Honors Scholarship for FWU International Students

Government scholarship awarded via institutional nomination to privately financed international students demonstrating high scholastic achievement at Fuji Women's University.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year university bachelor's degree (or international 16-year education equivalent) with verified credits in nutrition, biochemistry, or social sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Shushi) in human life sciences, nutrition, or related field; submission of research thesis and passing oral defense examination.
Language proficiency
Japanese language proficiency at JLPT N1 or N2 for Japanese-language courses; CEFR B2 / IELTS 6.0+ for designated research fellowship tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2,000-character Japanese (or 1,500-word English) graduate research plan outlining experimental design and alignment with FWU laboratory directors.
Application fee
JPY 35,000 university graduate entrance examination fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcript, graduation certificate, JLPT or English proficiency test report, letter of recommendation, and research proposal.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Fuji Women's University is approximately JPY 780,000 to JPY 950,000 per year, plus an initial matriculation fee of JPY 200,000 in the first year. International students receive a 30% to 50% tuition reduction scholarship through the FWU International Student Support Fund, and qualifying doctoral scholars can access Japanese MEXT and JASSO monthly stipends.
