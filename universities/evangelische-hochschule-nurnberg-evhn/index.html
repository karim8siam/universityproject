Lutheran University of Applied Sciences Nuremberg (EVHN)

The Evangelische Hochschule Nürnberg (EVHN) is a state-recognized, church-sponsored university of applied sciences based in Nuremberg, Bavaria. Rooted in Christian social ethics and human dignity, EVHN offers rigorous academic degree programs in social work, nursing science, health management, religious pedagogy, and social economy.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Nuremberg, Bavaria, Germany

Institutional Overview

EVHN's educational model synthesizes empirical social scientific methodology, practical welfare field internships, and ethical reflection. The university maintains deep operational partnerships with Diakonie Deutschland, Bavarian healthcare providers, municipal social welfare offices, and European universities through Erasmus+ collaborations.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Prospective students apply for winter semester commencement (deadline July 15) or summer semester (January 15). Prerequisites include the German Fachhochschulreife or international high school equivalent, along with German language proficiency at the TestDaF 4 / DSH-2 level. Practical pre-study internships in social or healthcare organizations are typically required.
