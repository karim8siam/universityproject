Academy of Leadership Sciences Switzerland: Executive Leadership, Public Law & Diplomacy Guide

Established in 2014 in Zurich, the Academy of Leadership Sciences Switzerland (ALSS) is an accredited international Swiss higher education institute specializing in advanced executive leadership education, organizational governance, international law, and conflict diplomacy. ALSS offers flexible, blended, and campus-based graduate degrees tailored for senior executives, diplomats, healthcare directors, and civil society leaders globally.

Three-year practitioner doctorate developing empirical frameworks for transformational leadership, corporate governance agility, and organizational resilience during systemic disruptions.

Applied legal and diplomatic research project evaluating the efficacy of Swiss neutral mediation frameworks in contemporary multilateral humanitarian negotiations.

Four-month practical internship working alongside international humanitarian organizations and non-profit foundations in Geneva and Zurich evaluating strategic program governance.

Two-year research fellowship conducting empirical investigations into ethical decision-making heuristics among C-suite executives and public sector ministers.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (or recognized equivalent 180 ECTS) in social sciences, business, law, or healthcare administration with a minimum GPA of 2.8 on a 4.0 scale, plus at least two years of professional leadership experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) or Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Leadership; requiring an accredited master's degree, minimum 3.0 GPA, significant managerial experience, and defensible research proposal.
Language proficiency
English proficiency at CEFR B2/C1 level (IELTS Academic 6.5 overall, TOEFL iBT 88+, or Duolingo English Test 110) or certified degree taught in English.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for master's and doctoral applicants: 1,500-word research prospectus detailing proposed empirical inquiry in leadership styles, public governance, or international diplomacy.
Application fee
150 CHF non-refundable application and evaluation dossier fee.
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree certificates and academic transcripts, comprehensive curriculum vitae, two letters of professional/academic recommendation, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the Academy of Leadership Sciences Switzerland ranges from 12,500 CHF to 16,800 CHF for master's programs and approximately 18,500 CHF to 24,000 CHF for three-year doctoral degrees. ALSS provides Swiss Global Leadership Bursaries offering up to 25% tuition fee reductions and structured semester installment options.
