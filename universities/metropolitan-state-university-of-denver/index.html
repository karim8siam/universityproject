Metropolitan State University of Denver

MSU Denver · Denver, Colorado, United States

Metropolitan State University of Denver is a public university located on the Auraria Campus in downtown Denver, Colorado. Renowned for its world-class Aviation and Aerospace Science program, cybersecurity, brewery operations, and healthcare simulation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MSU Denver operates the Aerospace and Advanced Manufacturing Sciences building, the Gina and Frank Day Health Institute, and state-of-the-art flight simulation laboratories equipped with Frasca Level 6 flight training devices and satellite ground stations.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic performance or transfer college coursework under test-optional review. Flight training majors must secure an FAA Medical Certificate prior to flight lab enrollment.
