Ritsumeikan University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Ritsumeikan University, founded in 1869 in the imperial capital of Kyoto, is one of Japan's most prestigious private research institutions and a core member of the elite 'Kansai-Four' (Kankan-Douritsu). World-renowned for its pioneering robotics and biomechatronics laboratories at the Biwako-Kusatsu Campus (BKC), digital humanities archiving in Kyoto, and its bilingual international relations curriculum.

Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at Ritsumeikan

Prestigious national scholarship awarded by the Japanese government (MEXT) upon university recommendation for outstanding international students pursuing Master's or PhD degrees in science and engineering.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Advanced Robotics & Bio-Mechatronics

Doctoral research program based at the Biwako-Kusatsu Campus (BKC), creating bio-inspired soft robotic grippers, wearable rehabilitation exoskeletons, and autonomous underwater exploration drones.

Master of Science in Information Science and Engineering (English Track)

Two-year English-taught Master of Science equipping students with competencies in computer vision, deep reinforcement learning, natural language processing, and cloud software engineering.

Doctoral Program in Digital Humanities & 3D Cultural Heritage Archiving

Doctoral research conducted at the Art Research Center (ARC) in Kyoto, digitizing Japan's ancient temples, ukiyo-e woodblock prints, and traditional performing arts using high-precision 3D lidar and AI.

Master of Arts in International Relations (Global Governance & East Asian Security)

Premier English-taught graduate program in Kyoto analyzing geopolitical dynamics in East Asia, international peacebuilding, sustainable development goals, and global governance.

Ritsumeikan Synchrotron Radiation Center Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at Ritsumeikan's compact electron storage ring synchrotron facility (SR Center), characterizing advanced battery materials and semiconductor thin films.

BKC Robotics & Human-Machine Collaboration Summer Laboratory Internship

Eight-week intensive summer laboratory research internship at the Biwako-Kusatsu Campus, building tele-operated haptic manipulators and assistive walking exoskeletons.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education with a bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline; English proficiency certificate and academic transcript evaluation.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completion of an accredited Master's degree or professional graduate degree; pre-consent from a prospective Ritsumeikan graduate faculty advisor.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: TOEFL iBT 80+, IELTS Academic 6.0+, or TOEIC 780+ for English-medium graduate programs; JLPT N1 for Japanese tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research plan (1,500–2,000 words) defining background, research questions, experimental design, and targeted laboratories.
Application fee
¥10,000 application fee for international English admissions (¥0 for MEXT scholars).
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diploma, academic transcripts, research proposal, two academic letters of recommendation, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international graduate engineering students at Ritsumeikan University averages ¥1,150,000 to ¥1,450,000 per year. Ritsumeikan offers generous internal tuition reductions (20%, 50%, or 100% fee remission for over 70% of admitted international students) in addition to nominating top applicants for the full Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship providing ¥145,000 per month.
