Bavarian Virtual University (vhb): Digital Higher Ed, AI & Bavarian Universities Hub

Virtuelle Hochschule Bayern (vhb) is a joint institutional network established by all 34 state and state-recognized universities and universities of applied sciences across Bavaria, offering over 600 accredited online university course modules, digital twin laboratories, and open education credentials.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Enrolled student at any Bavarian university or university of applied sciences (TUM, LMU, FAU Erlangen, Würzburg, etc.) or accredited international academic transcript.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral students utilize vhb advanced methodology and research ethics modules; doctoral credentials awarded by host university.
Language proficiency
German (B2/C1) or English (B2/C1) depending on course language track.
Research statement / Proposal
Online enrollment with verification of university matriculation (Matrikelnummer).
Application fee
Free online registration for matriculated university students.
Required documentation
Proof of university enrollment (Immatrikulationsbescheinigung), identity document, and course selection declaration.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Courses are completely tuition-free (0 EUR) for students matriculated at any of the 34 member universities across Bavaria. For external international individual learners, modular enrollment fees range from €40 to €150 per course. Bavarian State Ministry for Science and Arts digital education subsidies apply.
