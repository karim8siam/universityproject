University of Parma: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Parma (Università degli Studi di Parma / UniPR), founded in the 10th century, is one of the world's oldest academic institutions, located in the heart of Italy's famous Food Valley and Motor Valley. Globally celebrated for mirror neuron discoveries in cognitive neuroscience, computer vision for autonomous driving (VisLab), and food safety engineering, UniPR operates major science and medical research centers.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Information Technology & Autonomous Driving

Doctoral research conducted within the Department of Engineering and Architecture, developing deep neural perception, LiDAR SLAM, and cooperative vehicle control in collaboration with VisLab and the Motor Valley.

PhD in Food Science & Human Nutrition (Food Valley Innovation)

Doctoral program at the international hub of European food safety, investigating food authenticity, polyphenols and human cardiometabolic health, and sustainable microbial fermentations.

ER.GO Regional Board for the Right to Higher Education Study Grant

Comprehensive welfare and academic study grant provided by the Emilia-Romagna regional authority (ER.GO) for international and domestic students enrolled at the University of Parma.

Master of Science in Communication Engineering & Wireless IoT Networks

Two-year Master of Science taught in English, focusing on 5G/6G physical layer design, optical fiber networks, satellite communications, and sensor networks for industrial IoT.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Chemical Sciences & Supramolecular Catalysis

Doctoral research program within the Department of Chemistry, Life Sciences and Environmental Sustainability, synthesizing macrocyclic receptors, molecular machines, and green organic catalysts.

Department of Medicine Postdoctoral Fellowship in Cognitive Neuroscience

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the birthplace of mirror neuron discoveries, investigating motor coding, action understanding, and neuro-rehabilitation using high-density EEG and TMS.

VisLab Autonomous Driving Engineering Graduate Traineeship

Six-month research internship at VisLab (University of Parma's historic autonomous driving lab), testing real-time neural network obstacle detection and camera-radar sensor fusion.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized undergraduate bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in a related discipline from an accredited institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or international Master of Science); admission evaluated via curriculum vitae, project proposal, and an oral examination/interview.
Language proficiency
B2 level certificate in English (IELTS 6.0+, TOEFL iBT 78+) for English-taught degrees; Italian B2 certification for Italian-medium programs.
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-5 page research project proposal demonstrating critical knowledge of the state of the art, proposed methodologies, and alignment with Parma research groups.
Application fee
€30 to €50 administrative application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma, academic transcripts, Europass CV, research project, declaration of value or CIMEA statement of comparability, and two recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Parma is progressive, calculated based on the student's family economic indicator (ISEE Parificato). For students with ISEE up to €25,500, tuition is completely waived (€0). Above this, maximum tuition is capped at approximately €2,300 per year. International students are fully eligible for ER.GO regional scholarships granting up to €7,500 per year in cash stipends plus free university meals and accommodation. All doctoral positions are funded at €16,243 to €20,000 net per year.
