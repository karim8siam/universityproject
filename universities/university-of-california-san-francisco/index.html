University of California, San Francisco

UCSF · San Francisco, California, United States

The University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) is the world's premier academic health sciences center and the only campus in the University of California system dedicated exclusively to graduate and professional education in health and biomedical sciences. Located across historic Parnassus Heights and Mission Bay in San Francisco, UCSF is the leading public recipient of National Institutes of Health (NIH) research funding in the nation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UCSF comprises the School of Medicine, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, School of Nursing, and Graduate Division. Its researchers have won six Nobel Prizes and made historic breakthroughs, including discovering oncogenes, developing recombinant DNA techniques, creating hepatitis B vaccines, and inventing artificial gene therapies.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are among the most selective in the world. Medical programs require AMCAS applications, CASPer exams, MCAT scores, and substantial clinical exposure. Biomedical doctoral programs are fully funded through the Graduate Division and require extensive bench research experience, academic transcripts, and letters of recommendation.
