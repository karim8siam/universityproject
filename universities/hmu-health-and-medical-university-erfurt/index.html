HMU Health and Medical University Erfurt

Explore state examination human medicine degrees, clinical psychology master's, neurocognitive health labs, and clinical fellowships at HMU Erfurt.

Institutional Overview

HMU Health and Medical University Erfurt is an elite, state-recognized private university in Thuringia dedicated exclusively to human medicine, psychology, psychotherapy, and health sciences. Located at the historic Dalbergsweg in Erfurt, HMU combines rigorous theoretical medical science with early clinical integration across premier academic teaching hospital networks in central Germany.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • NC-free state-certified medical education leading to the German Medical Licensure (Approbation als Arzt)
  • Small-cohort instruction with 1:8 faculty-to-student ratio and advanced medical simulation center
  • Direct clinical clerkships and bedside training at certified academic teaching hospitals throughout central Germany
  • Specialized Master in Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy fulfilling the new German Psychotherapy Act (PsychThG)

Featured Academic Degrees & Curricula

Master's Degree

Staatsexamen Humanmedizin (Human Medicine)

Six-year, 12-semester state examination curriculum leading to physician licensure (Approbation) following M1, M2, and M3 national boards.

Master's Degree

M.Sc. in Klinische Psychologie und Psychotherapie

Four-semester professional master's fully accredited under the new PsychThG for direct entry into the psychotherapeutic licensing exam.

Bachelor's Degree

B.Sc. in Medizinpädagogik (Medical Pedagogy)

Higher education pedagogical training for future clinical instructors and health sector vocational educators.
