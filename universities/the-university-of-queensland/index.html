The University of Queensland (Brisbane, Australia)

Established in 1909 in Brisbane, The University of Queensland (UQ) is a founding member of Australia's prestigious Group of Eight (Go8) and ranked consistently among the world's top 40 to 50 universities. UQ is internationally acclaimed for discoveries that transformed global healthcare (including the Gardasil cervical cancer vaccine and the Nanopatch), sustainable energy, and quantum photonics. UQ Graduate School Scholarships (UQGSS) & RTP Research Scholarships

The University of Queensland's premier competitive doctoral scholarship scheme, awarding fully funded tuition offsets and generous living stipends to outstanding international researchers.

UQ PhD in Information Technology & Electrical Engineering

Comprehensive 3.5 to 4 year doctoral research degree exploring artificial intelligence, cyber security, data science, computer vision, and advanced electrical systems.

UQ Master of Computer Science (Cyber Security & Data Analytics)

Advanced two-year coursework and industry capstone degree providing rigorous training in distributed systems, cryptographic security, and scalable cloud engineering.

UQ Master of Biotechnology (Biomedical & Synthetic Biology)

World-class biotechnology research and industry training hosted at UQ's cutting-edge bioscience institutes, focusing on drug delivery, vaccines, and molecular biology.

Australian Institute for Bioengineering and Nanotechnology (AIBN) Placements

Interdisciplinary research placements in nanomedicine, biomaterials, stem cell regenerative therapy, and precision therapeutics at one of Australia's premier research institutes.

UQ Summer & Winter Research Scholarships Program

Intensive 6 to 10-week laboratory immersion program allowing undergraduate and master's scholars to work alongside leading UQ professors on frontier scientific projects.

UQ Casual Academic Teaching Fellowships & Lab Demonstratorships

Paid academic teaching contracts for postgraduate researchers, leading tutorial discussions, laboratory demonstration sessions, and coursework assessment in engineering and IT.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree with a minimum Grade Point Average (GPA) of 5.0 on UQ's 7-point scale (approximately 70-75%+) in a relevant discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours or Master's degree with a substantial research thesis (minimum 25% research component) and high academic standing.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each subband) or TOEFL iBT 87 overall (minimum 21 in writing, 19 in other bands).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-3 page research project outline detailing background, research questions, proposed methodology, and endorsed UQ advisor alignment.
Application fee
AUD $100 non-refundable international application fee submitted via UQ's online portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, graduation certificates, 2 academic referee reports, detailed CV, and project proposal.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international research higher degree candidates is fully covered under the UQ Graduate School Scholarships (UQGSS) and Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP). Recipients receive 100% full tuition fee offset scholarships, overseas student health cover (OSHC), and tax-free living allowances.
