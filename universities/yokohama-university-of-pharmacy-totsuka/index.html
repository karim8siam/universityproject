Yokohama University of Pharmacy

Yokohama University of Pharmacy (Hamayaku) is a leading pharmaceutical university in Totsuka, Yokohama, celebrated for pioneering Japan’s first Department of Traditional Chinese and Kampo Medicine alongside advanced clinical pharmacology.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating an expansive botanical research garden with over 1,000 medicinal plant species, Hamayaku provides 6-year clinical pharmacist degree tracks and 4-year pharmaceutical science programs. Research centers specialize in Kampo phytochemistry, targeted oncology drug delivery, and analytical toxicology.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions for the 6-year pharmacy and 4-year pharmaceutical sciences curricula require high school completion with strong scientific aptitude. International applicants require certified Japanese proficiency (JLPT N1 or N2) and science exams.
