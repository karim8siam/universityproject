Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1917 in Setagaya, Tokyo, Kokushikan University is a major private university renowned for martial arts and Olympic athletic training, emergency medical services, disaster prevention engineering, and international Asian studies.

Institution type
Private University
Campus location
Setagaya Campus (Main), Machida Campus & Tama Campus, Tokyo
Accreditation
Japan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / MEXT
Admissions channel
Kokushikan University Admissions Center (Nyushi Center)
Instruction language
Japanese (with specialized English courses and Asian language programs)
Financial aid & support
Kokushikan Academic Merit Scholarships, MEXT international scholarships, and JASSO honors bursaries

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Disaster prevention, emergency medical technology, and paramedic training (first in Japan)
  • Physical education, martial arts (Judo, Kendo), and Olympic athletic development
  • Asia-Japan geopolitical relations and historical cultural studies
  • Civil engineering, seismic resilience, and urban infrastructure design

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Engineering in Disaster Mitigation & Urban Safety (Graduate Degree) — Two-year research master's degree focusing on seismic structural retrofitting, earthquake engineering, and urban fire safety.
  • Bachelor of Science in Emergency Medical System (Paramedic Science) (Undergraduate Degree) — First 4-year degree in Japan qualifying graduates for the national paramedic examination (Kyukyu Kyumeishi).
  • Master of Arts in Global Asian Studies (Graduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary graduate curriculum exploring East Asian diplomatic history, trade law, and cultural diplomacy.
