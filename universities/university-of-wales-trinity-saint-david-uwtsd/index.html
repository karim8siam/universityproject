Institutional Overview

University of Wales Trinity Saint David (UWTSD / Prifysgol Cymru Y Drindod Dewi Sant) is a historic collegiate university chartered in 1828 by King George IV, making it the oldest university institution in Wales. Operating historic campuses in Lampeter and Carmarthen, major waterfront innovation campuses in Swansea, and a teaching center in London, UWTSD excels in humanities, automotive engineering, creative arts, and bilingual Welsh education.
