Academic overview & research priorities

Operating three core departments—Tourism and Travel Management, Business Administration, and Computer Science—Hochschule Worms is one of Germany's leading institutions for aviation and airline management. Located in the Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region, the university boasts flight simulator facilities, direct airline industry partnerships (Lufthansa, Fraport), and extensive dual-degree international exchange agreements.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or recognized foreign school diploma submitted through uni-assist. Master's admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in business, tourism, computer science, or aviation management (minimum German grade 2.5). English-taught programs require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 87; German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4.
