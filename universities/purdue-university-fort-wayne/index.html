Purdue University Fort Wayne

PFW · Fort Wayne, Indiana, United States

Purdue University Fort Wayne is the premier public comprehensive university in northeastern Indiana, granting prestigious Purdue University degrees. Acclaimed for ABET-accredited engineering, computer science, music technology, nursing, and business analytics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

PFW occupies a 688-acre campus along the St. Joseph River, featuring the Engineering, Technology, and Computer Science Building, the Rhinehart Music Center, and the Wireless Communication and Applied Research Center. Students participate in industrial co-ops and applied engineering with regional defense and aerospace manufacturing leaders.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.4), college-preparatory mathematics, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA in an aligned technical or professional discipline.
