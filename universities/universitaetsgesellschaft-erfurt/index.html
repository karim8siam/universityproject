Universitätsgesellschaft Erfurt e.V.

Universitätsgesellschaft Erfurt e.V. is the historic academic foundation and university society that spearheaded the re-founding of the University of Erfurt, providing prestigious research prizes, scholarships, and academic publication grants.

Academic overview & research priorities

Collaborating closely with the University of Erfurt, the society supports multidisciplinary scholarship in the humanities, social sciences, teacher education, and public policy, funding doctoral symposia and public science dialogues across Thuringia.

Research admissions & international criteria

Awards and fellowships are open to enrolled students, doctoral candidates, and early-career researchers at the University of Erfurt or affiliated research institutes upon faculty nomination or competitive application.
