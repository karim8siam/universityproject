Pace University

Pace University · New York, New York, United States

Pace University is a leading private metropolitan university with its primary campus in Lower Manhattan beside City Hall and the Brooklyn Bridge, and a picturesque suburban campus in Pleasantville, Westchester County. Renowned for the Lubin School of Business, Elisabeth Haub School of Law, Seidenberg School of Computer Science and Information Systems, and Dyson College of Arts and Sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Pace features One Pace Plaza in downtown Manhattan, the Helene Fuld School of Nursing simulation labs, and the environmental law powerhouse Elisabeth Haub School of Law in White Plains (consistently ranked #1 in environmental law by U.S. News). Pace boasts one of the largest corporate internship programs in the New York metropolitan region.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and selective. Undergraduate applicants submit high school GPA (average 3.5), academic transcripts, optional SAT/ACT scores, and personal essays. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA and discipline-specific prerequisites.
