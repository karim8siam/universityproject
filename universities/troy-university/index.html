Troy University

Troy University · Troy, Alabama, United States

Troy University is a comprehensive public institution headquartered in Troy, Alabama, with campuses across the state and international learning partnerships worldwide. Acclaimed for the Center for Materials and Manufacturing Sciences (CMMS), the Sorrell College of Business, and the School of Nursing, Troy combines applied technological research with strong global student engagement.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Troy is organized into five colleges: Arts and Sciences, the Sorrell College of Business, Communication and Fine Arts, Education, and Health and Human Services. The Center for Materials and Manufacturing Sciences anchors research in polymer recycling, advanced composites, and medical polymer synthesis.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.0 with 20 ACT, or 2.50+ test-optional). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.50+ cumulative GPA (3.0+ on last 60 hours), official transcripts, letters of recommendation, and program-specific entrance exams.
