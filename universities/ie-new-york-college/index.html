IE New York College

IENYC · New York, New York, United States

IE New York College (IENYC), located in the heart of SoHo in Manhattan, is an accredited, degree-granting institution born from the legacy of the Glasgow Caledonian New York College (GCNYC) and backed by the global innovation of IE University. IENYC offers cutting-edge Master of Science degrees dedicated to business for social impact, sustainable fashion, risk management, and ethical corporate governance.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with demonstrable commitment to sustainability, social impact, or ethical leadership. TOEFL iBT 90+ or IELTS 7.0+ for candidates without a prior degree from an English-medium university.
