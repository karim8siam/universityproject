University of Tennessee

UT Knoxville · Knoxville, Tennessee, United States

The University of Tennessee, Knoxville (UT Knoxville / UTK) is the state of Tennessee's public flagship land-grant research university and an elite Carnegie R1 institution, founded in 1794. Partnering with the U.S. Department of Energy's Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL)—the nation's premier science and energy national lab—UT Knoxville is internationally celebrated for advanced nuclear engineering, supercomputing, materials science, supply chain management, and forensic anthropology at the legendary 'Body Farm'.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UT Knoxville comprises 11 colleges including the Tickle College of Engineering, Haslam College of Business, College of Arts and Sciences, College of Veterinary Medicine, and the College of Law. Flagship research centers include the UT-Oak Ridge Innovation Institute (UT-ORII), the Forensic Anthropology Center, and the Reliability and Maintainability Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic rigor, core GPA, and leadership. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
