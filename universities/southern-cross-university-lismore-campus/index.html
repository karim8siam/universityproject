Southern Cross University (Lismore): Plant Science, Forestry & Health Guide

Southern Cross University's Lismore campus is the foundational main campus of SCU, set across 75 hectares of subtropical rainforest grounds in the Northern Rivers region of New South Wales. Renowned for world-leading research in plant science, regenerative agriculture, forestry, geochemistry, and complementary medicine, SCU Lismore hosts state-of-the-art facilities including the Southern Cross Plant Science research centre, the National Marine Science Centre, and comprehensive health simulation laboratories.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (AQF Level 7) or equivalent in a relevant discipline with a credit average (65%+); relevant professional experience considered for select management pathways.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-class or upper second-class Honours degree (AQF Level 8) or Research Master's degree; submission of research proposal and written endorsement from an SCU supervisor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no individual band below 6.0) for standard postgraduate entry; nursing and allied health degrees require IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 7.0 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral applicants: 2-3 page research outline detailing study significance, theoretical framework, research design, and potential field or lab requirements.
Application fee
$0 AUD for direct online applications.
Required documentation
Certified transcripts, degree certificates, CV, English language test results, research proposal (HDR candidates), and contact details of two academic referees.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic students benefit from Commonwealth Supported Places (CSP) with tuition ranging from $4,500 to $11,500 AUD per year. International student tuition ranges from $28,800 to $35,200 AUD per year. SCU awards Vice Chancellor's Academic Excellence Scholarships offering up to $8,000 AUD per year tuition fee relief to high-achieving international applicants.
