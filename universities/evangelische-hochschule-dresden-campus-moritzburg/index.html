Academic overview & research priorities

Located near the baroque hunting lodge of Moritzburg in Saxony, the university trains certified social pedagogues, community educators, and diaconal leaders with an emphasis on ethics, child welfare, inclusion pedagogy, and intergenerational social work.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require German higher education entrance qualification (Abitur or Fachhochschulreife) and proof of voluntary social year (FSJ) or social internship experience. Selection via motivational personal interview. German language proficiency at DSH-2 or C1 level.
