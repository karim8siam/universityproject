Tokyo University of Agriculture: Fermentation Science, Agronomy, Forestry & Food Safety Guide

Tokyo University of Agriculture (Tokyo NODAI), founded in 1891 by Viscount Enomoto Takeaki, is Japan's first and largest private agricultural university, internationally renowned for its Department of Fermentation Science, tropical agriculture, brewing technology, and environmental forestry.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from a recognized four-year university with a Bachelor's degree in agriculture, life sciences, fermentation, or chemistry.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in agricultural sciences, applied biological chemistry, or bio-business with an approved research abstract.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 or N1 for Japanese-medium labs, or English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.0+) for international research programs.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2,000-character Japanese or 1,000-word English research plan outlining proposed laboratory experiments and prospective professor contact.
Application fee
¥35,000 application examination fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, research proposal, statement of purpose, and two academic recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual graduate tuition averages ¥850,000 to ¥1,100,000 depending on laboratory equipment needs. The Tokyo NODAI International Student Special Scholarship offers a 30% to 50% tuition deduction, supplemented by MEXT and JASSO monthly living allowances.
