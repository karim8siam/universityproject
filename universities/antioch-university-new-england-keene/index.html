Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0; prerequisite courses in life sciences or social sciences depending on department.
Doctoral admission eligibility
PsyD and Environmental PhD require relevant master's or exceptional undergraduate honors, clinical/field background, and an in-person or virtual interview.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 minimum for non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose reflecting social justice values, commitment to environmental stewardship, or clinical therapeutic philosophy.
Application fee
$50 online application fee (fee waivers available upon request).
Required documentation
Official post-secondary transcripts, current resume/CV, three letters of recommendation, and personal reflective essay.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition averages $950 to $1,150 per credit hour depending on the program. Doctoral PsyD tuition is approximately $36,000 annually. Antioch Opportunity Grants, Center for Climate Preparedness fellowships, and federal graduate loans are accessible.
