MINT-Kolleg Baden-Württemberg | Stuttgart

The MINT-Kolleg Baden-Württemberg is a joint academic initiative of the University of Stuttgart and Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), providing foundational excellence in mathematics, physics, and computer science.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating specialized institutes at the University of Stuttgart and KIT, the MINT-Kolleg provides intensive preparatory courses, introductory semester modules, and diagnostic learning analytics designed to bridge the transition from school to rigorous university-level engineering and STEM degree programs.

Research admissions & international criteria

Prospective or enrolled STEM students at University of Stuttgart or KIT. Offers voluntary preparatory semester courses (Propädeutikum), parallel semester tutoring, and online diagnostic assessments. Open to EU and international scholars.
