Landesstudienkolleg Sachsen-Anhalt

Landesstudienkolleg Sachsen-Anhalt is the state preparatory college affiliated with the Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg, preparing international scholars for German university degrees.

Academic overview & research priorities

Located in historic Halle (Saale), the Landesstudienkolleg provides intensive two-semester specialized preparatory courses (T-Kurs for engineering/math, M-Kurs for medicine/biology, W-Kurs for economics/social sciences, and G/S-Kurs for humanities) culminating in the official Feststellungsprüfung (FSP).

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require a secondary school leaving certificate from abroad that qualifies for university study in the home country, plus certified German language proficiency at CEFR B1 or B2 level. Admission is granted via an on-site entrance examination (Aufnahmetest).
