Academic overview & excellence

Strategically located in Europe's largest maritime port city of Rotterdam, the University of Excellence Netherlands delivers executive doctoral programs, international masterclasses, and applied research in global supply chain security, maritime decarbonization, and corporate governance. The institution convenes multilateral dialogues between international port authorities, multinational energy corporations, and emerging market leaders.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to executive master's and doctoral degrees requires an accredited bachelor's or master's degree from a recognized university with a minimum 3.0 GPA, alongside documented professional leadership or public policy experience. Instruction is conducted in English.
