Collegium Maius - University of Erfurt Historic Faculty

The historic Collegium Maius is the ceremonial heart and intellectual cradle of the University of Erfurt, celebrated as the medieval academic home where Martin Luther studied and today hosting prestigious humanities symposia, theological research, and ecclesiastical history colloquia.

Academic overview & research priorities

Constructed in the 14th century and reconstructed following World War II, Collegium Maius serves as the official seat of the Evangelical Church of Central Germany and an academic event hub for the University of Erfurt’s faculties of Catholic Theology, Protestant Studies, and Max Weber Centre for Advanced Cultural and Social Studies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Graduate scholars, doctoral researchers, and visiting fellows affiliate through the University of Erfurt Faculty of Humanities or the Max Weber Centre. Admissions require a high-standing Bachelor or Master of Arts in history, theology, philosophy, or religious studies.
