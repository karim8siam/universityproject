South Westphalia University of Applied Sciences

South Westphalia University of Applied Sciences (Fachhochschule Südwestfalen) is a major engineering, agronomy, and informatics university across five campuses in the powerhouse industrial region of North Rhine-Westphalia.

Academic overview & research priorities

Serving the dense manufacturing and Mittelstand industrial corridor of South Westphalia, FH Südwestfalen maintains five dynamic campuses: Iserlohn (Computer Science, Manufacturing), Soest (Sustainable Agriculture, Electrical Engineering), Meschede (Industrial Engineering), Hagen (Electrical Power), and Lüdenscheid (Plastic Technology). The university is renowned for hands-on dual-study engineering programs, agricultural biotechnology, and corporate automotive innovation labs.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate degree admission requires a German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or foreign equivalent recognized by uni-assist. Master's applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, agricultural science, computer science, or business (minimum German grade 2.5). English-taught master's require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85; German programs require TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2.
