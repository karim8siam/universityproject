College of Western Idaho

The College of Western Idaho (CWI) is the largest comprehensive community college in the state of Idaho, delivering accredited associate degrees, university transfer pathways, and polytechnic workforce education across the Boise metropolitan area.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating vibrant campuses across Nampa and Boise, College of Western Idaho empowers students with affordable, high-quality higher education. Accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities (NWCCU), CWI excels in university transfer programs articulating directly to Boise State University, University of Idaho, and Idaho State University. Key applied disciplines include cybersecurity, software development, advanced mechatronics, registered nursing, and biotechnology.

Research admissions & criteria

CWI operates an open-door admissions policy for high school graduates and GED recipients. Degree-seeking students must submit high school transcripts or official GED scores. International applicants must provide proof of English proficiency (TOEIL/IELTS 6.0), complete an international financial declaration, and obtain an F-1 student visa. Course prerequisites apply for selective-entry health science programs.
