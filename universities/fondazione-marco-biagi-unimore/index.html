Marco Biagi Foundation (University of Modena and Reggio Emilia): Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 2002 to honour the legacy of Professor Marco Biagi, the Foundation operates within the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia (UNIMORE) as an autonomous university foundation. Bringing together legal scholars, industrial sociologists, labor economists, and occupational physicians, the Foundation hosts the prestigious International Conference in Commemoration of Marco Biagi, publishes the Comparative Labor Law & Policy Journal, and runs an international inter-disciplinary doctoral research school.
