Southeastern Louisiana University

Southeastern Louisiana · Hammond, Louisiana, United States

Southeastern Louisiana University is a dynamic public university situated in Hammond, Louisiana, at the crossroads of Interstates 12 and 55 between Baton Rouge and New Orleans. As the third-largest university in Louisiana, Southeastern is renowned for its ABET-accredited engineering technology programs, top-tier school of nursing, innovative supply chain management curriculum, and the Turtle Cove Environmental Research Station located in the Lake Pontchartrain estuary.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Southeastern comprises five academic colleges: Science and Technology, Nursing and Health Sciences, Business, Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, and Education. Highlights include the Kinesiology and Health Studies Building, modern robotics and CNC manufacturing labs, and Turtle Cove's 2,000-acre marshland research laboratory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.35) and ACT/SAT scores. Graduate admissions require an accredited baccalaureate degree with a minimum 2.75 GPA (or 3.0 on the last 60 credit hours), GRE/GMAT scores (program-dependent), statement of purpose, and academic letters of recommendation.
