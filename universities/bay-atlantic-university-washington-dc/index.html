Bay Atlantic University: Cyber Security, Big Data, MBA & International Affairs

Bay Atlantic University (BAU) is a private, non-profit university in downtown Washington, D.C., situated near the White House, celebrated for accredited undergraduate and master's degrees in cybersecurity, data analytics, MBA, and international affairs within the global BAU Global Network.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.7 GPA in related academic area; resume and interview required for graduate admissions.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctorates; offers career-focused bachelor's and master's degree programs.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80, IELTS 6.5, or Duolingo 105 for non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose (500-750 words) detailing international educational goals in Washington D.C.
Application fee
$45 international student application fee.
Required documentation
Official university transcripts, degree diploma, certified English translations, passport copy, bank financial statement, and two recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition is approximately $13,500 per year ($560 per credit). Graduate tuition is approximately $1,100 per credit ($19,800 for full 36-credit master's programs). Global Scholarship awards (up to 50% tuition reduction) and Washington fellowship grants are available.
