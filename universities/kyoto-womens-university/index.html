Institutional Overview

Kyoto Women's University (Kyoto Joshi Daigaku), founded in 1899 and set against the scenic eastern hills of Higashiyama in Kyoto, is one of Japan's most prestigious private women's universities. Renowned for its Faculty of Contemporary Society, Faculty of Home Economics (Food and Nutrition), Faculty of Human Development and Education, and the historic Shin Buddhist educational traditions, the university maintains top national employment and national exam pass rates.
