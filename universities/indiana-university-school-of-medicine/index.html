Indiana University School of Medicine

IU Medicine · Indianapolis, Indiana, United States

Indiana University School of Medicine is the largest allopathic medical school in the United States, anchored at the academic medical center in Indianapolis with nine regional campuses across Indiana. Conducting over $500 million in annual biomedical research, IU School of Medicine is a global leader in Alzheimer's disease genomics, pediatric oncology at Riley Hospital for Children, and organ transplantation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Completed bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with pre-medical prerequisites (biology, general/organic chemistry, biochemistry, physics). Competitive MCAT score and AMCAS application required. Demonstrated English proficiency required for international scholars (TOEFL iBT 100+ or IELTS 7.5+ for graduate biomedical sciences). AMCAS primary application deadline November 15, 2026; Secondary application deadline December 15, 2026; PhD graduate biomedical priority December 1, 2026.
