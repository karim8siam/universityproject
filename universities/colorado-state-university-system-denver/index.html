Colorado State University System

The Colorado State University System encompasses CSU Fort Collins (flagship R1 research university), CSU Pueblo (regional Hispanic-Serving Institution), and CSU Global (accredited online university), conducting over $450 million in annual research in veterinary medicine, atmospheric science, clean energy, and water sustainability.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating world-class facilities including the Cooperative Institute for Research in the Atmosphere (CIRA), the James L. Voss Veterinary Teaching Hospital, and the state-of-the-art CSU Spur Campus at the National Western Center in Denver, the CSU System leads North American discovery in food systems, hydrology, public health, and engineering.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions standards vary by campus: CSU Fort Collins requires competitive high school or undergraduate GPA (typically 3.4+) and holistic review; CSU Pueblo provides accessible regional admissions; and CSU Global offers rolling monthly starts for online graduate and undergraduate scholars.
