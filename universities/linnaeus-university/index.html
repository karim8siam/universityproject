Linnaeus University (Linnéuniversitetet)

Named in honour of the world-famous botanist Carl Linnaeus who was born in the region, Linnaeus University (Linnéuniversitetet - LNU) was created in 2010 through the merger of Växjö University and Kalmar University. Situated in Småland with vibrant campuses in Växjö and Kalmar, LNU is a national leader in ecological sciences, forestry and wood technology, data science and software engineering, and aquatic ecology. Linnaeus University offers salaried doctoral candidacies under Swedish state employment regulations.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) from an internationally recognized higher education institution with a major in the relevant field (minimum 65-70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS credits total, with at least 60 ECTS at advanced level) or equivalent international qualification; selection is based on academic merit, research quality, and faculty fit.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3 to 5 page doctoral research plan addressing research problem, theoretical context, proposed methodology, and significance.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying through the central Swedish universityadmissions.se portal; €0 fee for EU citizens.
Required documentation
Certificates and diplomas of completed degrees, official transcripts, documentation of English proficiency, copy of passport, and Master's thesis.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

At Linnaeus University and throughout Sweden, PhD candidacies are classified as state employment (doktorandanställning). Admitted PhD candidates pay zero tuition fees and receive a collective-bargaining salaried monthly wage of SEK 31,500 to SEK 35,000 per month (approx. SEK 380,000 to SEK 420,000/year gross) with full Swedish national healthcare, parental leave, and pension benefits. International Master's students can compete for Linnaeus University Scholarships covering 75% of tuition fees.
