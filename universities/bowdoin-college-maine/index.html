Bowdoin College: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Chartered in 1794 in Brunswick, Maine, Bowdoin College educates 1,900 undergraduates on an iconic 215-acre coastal campus. Consistently ranked among the top ten liberal arts colleges in the United States, Bowdoin pioneered need-blind admissions with zero student loans for all admitted undergraduates. The college operates the Schiller Coastal Studies Center on Orr’s Island, the Bowdoin Scientific Station on Kent Island (Bay of Fundy), and the historic Peary-MacMillan Arctic Museum, anchoring world-class research in coastal ecology, Arctic anthropology, polar oceanography, and international relations.
