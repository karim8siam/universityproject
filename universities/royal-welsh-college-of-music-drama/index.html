Institutional Overview

The Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama (Coleg Brenhinol Cerdd a Drama Cymru), established in 1949 and set within the magnificent grounds of Bute Park in Cardiff, is the National Conservatoire of Wales. RWCMD is ranked among the United Kingdom's leading conservatoires for acting, stage management, scenic design, orchestral performance, opera, and composition, with state-of-the-art theatres and concert halls.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Richard Burton Theatre Company producing professional public seasons in Cardiff and London
  • World-class concert hall (Dora Stoutzker Hall) and specialized opera school
  • Award-winning design for performance department excelling in theatre scenography and puppetry
  • Over 90% professional industry placement across West End, BBC, Welsh National Opera, and Royal Shakespeare Company
