University of Saskatchewan: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Saskatchewan (USask) is a premier Canadian U15 research-intensive university, distinguished by unique national science facilities including the Canadian Light Source (CLS) synchrotron, the Sylvia Fedoruk Canadian Centre for Nuclear Innovation, and VIDO (Vaccine and Infectious Disease Organization), one of the world's foremost containment-level infectious disease research institutes.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

VIDO-InterVac Doctoral Fellowships in Emerging Infectious Diseases and Vaccinology

Doctoral research studentship based at Canada's premier high-containment infectious disease center (VIDO), engineering next-generation mucosal vaccines against zoonotic pathogens.

PhD in Computer Science & Scientific High-Performance Computing

Doctoral program within the Department of Computer Science, developing numerical simulation algorithms, parallel high-performance solvers, and biomedical machine learning models.

Master of Science in Water Security & Hydrological Modeling

Interdisciplinary Master's degree hosted by the Global Institute for Water Security, modeling glacier melt, snowpack runoff, and agricultural water availability under changing northern climates.

Doctor of Philosophy in Physics & Synchrotron Radiation Science

Doctoral research conducted on-site at the Canadian Light Source (CLS), utilizing high-energy X-ray absorption spectroscopy and resonant scattering to inspect functional quantum materials.

PhD in Plant Sciences & High-Throughput Crop Phenomics

Doctoral program at the College of Agriculture and Bioresources, employing root imaging, drone hyperspectral phenotyping, and genomic selection to breed climate-resilient canola and wheat.

Feduruk Centre Postdoctoral Fellowship in Nuclear Imaging & Radiochemistry

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Saskatchewan Centre for Cyclotron Sciences, developing novel radiolabeled molecular imaging probes for oncological diagnostic PET scans.

USask Engineering & Canadian Light Source Summer Research Internship

Four-month summer internship designing precision vacuum components, cryogenic instrumentation, and automated beamline positioning software for the Canadian Light Source.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A four-year undergraduate degree with a minimum cumulative weighted average of 70% (B equivalent) in the last two years of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or equivalent in a relevant field with a minimum 75% weighted average; secured commitment from a faculty member to serve as research supervisor.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 19 in all sections).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-5 page research statement detailing objectives, methodology, preliminary literature review, and facility requirements (such as CLS or VIDO).
Application fee
CAD $120 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, and statement of research interests.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition at USask is highly competitive: standard Master's and PhD programs average CAD $9,000 to $14,000 per year. Fully funded doctoral students receive guaranteed minimum funding packages of CAD $24,000 per year through University Graduate Scholarships (UGS), Graduate Teaching Fellowships, and faculty research grants.
