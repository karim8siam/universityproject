ADG Business School: Cooperative Banking, FinTech, Management & Leadership Guide

Founded in 2011 and based at Schloss Montabaur in Rhineland-Palatinate, the ADG Business School an der Steinbeis-Hochschule is a specialized university institute dedicated to cooperative financial institutions, digital banking, and sustainable enterprise management. Operating under the renowned Steinbeis transfer-oriented academic model, ADG seamlessly combines dual study frameworks with scientific rigor for the cooperative banking sector across Germany.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed bachelor's degree or equivalent (180 or 210 ECTS) in economics, business administration, industrial engineering, or related discipline with a minimum German grade of 2.5 (gut).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral qualifications administered through the Steinbeis University Ph.D. program; requiring an outstanding master's degree, comprehensive research proposal, and cooperative partner funding.
Language proficiency
German language proficiency at level C1 (DSH-2, TestDaF 4x4) for German-taught modules; English proficiency B2/C1 (TOEFL iBT 88+ or IELTS 6.5) for bilingual programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for master's transfer project: 1,000-word corporate project description outlining real-world operational challenges at an employer or partner organization.
Application fee
€0 application fee for initial online evaluation and admission review.
Required documentation
Undergraduate diploma and certified transcript of records, employment contract or project-partner confirmation, updated CV, and letter of motivation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at ADG Business School averages €490 to €690 per month (€11,760 to €16,560 for the entire master's program), which in the vast majority of cases is funded in full or co-funded by cooperative partner banks (Volksbanken, Raiffeisenbanken) and corporate employers. Steinbeis transfer scholarships and study grants are also available.
