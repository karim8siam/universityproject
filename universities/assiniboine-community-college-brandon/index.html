Assiniboine Community College: Sustainable Agriculture, Public Safety & Applied Tech Guide

Assiniboine Community College is an accredited public post-secondary institution in Brandon, Manitoba, celebrated for the Prairie Innovation Centre for Sustainable Agriculture, advanced public safety training, agribusiness technology, and applied trades education.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of Canadian Grade 12 or equivalent secondary education, or a recognized undergraduate degree for postgraduate certificate streams.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral programs; focus on applied polytechnic diplomas and post-graduate certificates.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 79 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of educational intent describing technical career objectives in agriculture or technology.
Application fee
CAD $200 non-refundable international application processing fee via the Assiniboine application portal.
Required documentation
Certified high school or post-secondary transcripts, notarized graduation certificates, English proficiency proof, and valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic annual tuition averages CAD $4,800 to $6,500. International student tuition is approximately CAD $14,200 to $17,800 annually. Entrance bursaries, Manitoba Scholarship and Bursary Initiative (MSBI) awards, and work-integrated learning stipends are available.
