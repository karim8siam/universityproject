Saint Joseph's College of Maine

Saint Joseph's Maine · Standish, Maine, United States

Saint Joseph's College of Maine is a private Catholic liberal arts college established by the Sisters of Mercy in 1912, located on an idyllic 474-acre lakefront campus along Sebago Lake in Standish, Maine. Acclaimed for nursing, marine ecology, sustainable agriculture, and healthcare administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Saint Joseph's operates the Mercy Hall Nursing Simulation Center, the Pearson's Town Farm (an educational certified organic campus farm), and the Lake Science Center with direct research access to Sebago Lake (Maine's deepest lake). It was one of the earliest pioneers of distance education in healthcare administration.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5 recommended) with test-optional reviews. Nursing candidates require secondary review with biology and chemistry benchmarks. Online graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's with a 2.75 GPA.
