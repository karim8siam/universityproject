The Beta Theta Pi Leadership Institute and Foundation at the University of Kentucky sponsors merit-based undergraduate leadership scholarships, ethical governance fellowships, and academic excellence grants for collegiate scholars in engineering, business, and public policy.

Academic overview & research priorities

Affiliated with the Epsilon Omicron Chapter at the University of Kentucky and the National Beta Theta Pi Foundation, the institute fosters principled leadership, intellectual growth, and civic responsibility. Fellows participate in executive leadership symposiums and faculty-guided undergraduate research initiatives.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open to enrolled undergraduate students and incoming freshmen at the University of Kentucky demonstrating superior academic standing, leadership character, and dedication to campus governance. Application requires leadership portfolio and academic transcript.
