Nordakademie University of Applied Sciences

Nordakademie University of Applied Sciences (NORDAKADEMIE) in Elmshorn and Hamburg is Germany's premier business-funded dual university, nationally celebrated for cooperative bachelor degrees, applied industrial engineering, and executive master's programs.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1992 by major Northern German employers (including Airbus, Beiersdorf, and Philips), NORDAKADEMIE operates two state-of-the-art campuses: an expansive green campus in Elmshorn for dual undergraduate studies and a waterfront executive graduate campus in Hamburg's Dockland. The university boasts one of Germany's highest employment rates (over 99% upon graduation), integrating rigorous university semesters with full-time paid practical corporate apprenticeships.

Research admissions & international criteria

Dual bachelor's admission requires the German Abitur/Fachhochschulreife and successful completion of NORDAKADEMIE's multi-stage online aptitude assessment, followed by an employment contract with one of 300+ partner corporations. Master's applicants require a relevant bachelor's degree with a minimum German grade of 2.5.
