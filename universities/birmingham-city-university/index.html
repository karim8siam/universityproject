Birmingham City University: Royal Birmingham Conservatoire, STEAM & Masters

Birmingham City University (BCU) is a major public university in the UK's second city. Celebrating its globally acclaimed Royal Birmingham Conservatoire, prestigious Birmingham School of Art, STEAMhouse collaborative innovation center, and world-class health simulation facilities, BCU excels in creative arts, engineering, and digital technologies.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

BCU Vice-Chancellor's International Postgraduate Scholarship

Merit-based international tuition scholarship open to all full-time postgraduate taught master's students enrolling across BCU faculties.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Music Composition & Electroacoustic Technology

Doctoral research conducted within the state-of-the-art Royal Birmingham Conservatoire exploring algorithmic composition, multichannel spatial audio, and interactive digital performance.

Master of Science (MSc) in Cyber Security & Cloud Architecture

Cisco and AWS-aligned master's curriculum exploring ethical hacking, cloud security architecture, digital forensics, and industrial control network resilience.

STEAMhouse Center for Collaborative Innovation Industrial Research Assistantship

Applied research post at STEAMhouse bridging arts and STEM to co-create sustainable industrial products, circular polymers, and smart wearable electronics.

University Hospitals Birmingham NHS Foundation Trust Health Simulation Practicum

Hands-on clinical internship utilizing BCU's £41M simulation suites and Queen Elizabeth Hospital clinical wards to master acute resuscitation and diagnostic imaging.

Faculty of Computing, Engineering & the Built Environment Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship researching digital twin infrastructure, structural fiber-optic sensing, and carbon accounting in major UK civil engineering works.

Master of Arts (MA) in User Experience (UX) Design

Creative graduate degree training UX researchers and product designers in human-centered design, usability testing, accessibility heuristics, and design systems.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK honours degree (minimum 2:2, with 2:1 preferred for competitive programs) or international equivalent in an appropriate academic field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with merit or distinction (or international equivalent), accompanied by a formal supervisory endorsement from a BCU faculty mentor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in all bands) for most postgraduate courses; 6.5 overall for computing, research degrees, and health courses.
Research statement / Proposal
For MPhil/PhD: 1,500–2,500 word research proposal outlining research aims, methodological framework, resource requirements, and academic significance.
Application fee
£0 (free direct online application via the BCU portal).
Required documentation
Certified degree certificate, official transcript of records, two academic reference letters, personal statement, and portfolio/audition (for creative disciplines).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition ranges from approximately £16,000 to £18,500 per year. BCU International Scholarships provide automatic merit fee reductions of up to £2,000, and UKRI-aligned doctoral studentships cover 100% of tuition plus an annual living stipend of £19,237.
