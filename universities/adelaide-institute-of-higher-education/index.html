Adelaide Institute of Higher Education: Business Analytics & Health Admin Guide

Located in the heart of Adelaide's cultural precinct, Adelaide Institute of Higher Education (AIHE) is an accredited Australian higher education provider registered with the Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency (TEQSA). AIHE delivers practitioner-oriented undergraduate and postgraduate degrees in business analytics, information systems, enterprise leadership, and health services management.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

AIHE International Academic Excellence Scholarship

Merit-based tuition waiver awarded to high-achieving international scholars enrolling in Master of Business Administration and Master of Business Analytics at AIHE.

Master of Business Analytics (Big Data and Predictive Modeling)

Two-year TEQSA-accredited master's program providing comprehensive mastery in Python for data analysis, SQL databases, predictive machine learning, and data visualization with Tableau.

AIHE Collaborative Doctoral Research Candidature in Health Informatics

Doctoral research candidature conducted in collaboration with South Australian health networks investigating hospital patient flow optimization, clinical data governance, and predictive telemetry.

South Australian Digital Health & Predictive Patient Flow Research Project

Applied empirical research project utilizing time-series machine learning models to predict patient admission surges and optimize nurse staffing schedules in metropolitan Adelaide hospitals.

Adelaide Innovation Precinct & Lot Fourteen Data Analytics Internship

Twelve-week professional work-integrated learning internship placed with tech firms and startups at Lot Fourteen innovation precinct analyzing commercial datasets and customer churn models.

AIHE Research Fellowship in Algorithmic Governance and Enterprise Ethics

Two-year academic research appointment investigating ethical compliance frameworks, regulatory oversight, and consumer trust in automated AI decision-making systems in Australian retail banking.

South Australian Regional Student Support and Community Bursary

Tuition support grant assisting domestic and international students who demonstrate strong academic dedication and community leadership in South Australia.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (AQF Level 7) in any discipline with a minimum pass/credit average (60%+) or equivalent international tertiary credential.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Postgraduate applied research pathways; collaborative doctoral transfers organized with South Australian research universities.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands), PTE Academic 58+, or TOEFL iBT 80 (with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for postgraduate research projects: 1,500-word applied research proposal detailing predictive analytics methodology or organizational management challenge.
Application fee
$0 AUD for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree awards, curriculum vitae, statement of purpose, passport copy, and evidence of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate coursework degrees at AIHE is approximately $14,000 AUD per semester ($28,000 AUD per academic year / $56,000 AUD total degree tuition). Generous International Academic Excellence Scholarships provide up to 25% tuition fee reductions, alongside flexible installment payment options.
