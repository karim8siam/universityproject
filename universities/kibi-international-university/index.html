Institution Overview & Profile

Kibi International University (KIUI), founded in 1990 in the scenic castle city of Takahashi with campuses in Okayama and Minamiawaji, is a distinguished private Japanese university renowned for its specialized Department of Cultural Property Conservation (cultural heritage restoration), wine and agricultural biotechnology, digital animation arts, and international sports sciences.

Institution TypePrivate International University (Juntoku Educational Corporation)
Main CampusIga Campus (Takahashi), Okayama Campus & Minamiawaji Regional Agriculture Campus
AccreditationJapan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / MEXT Accredited
Admissions RouteInternational Student Entrance Examination & EJU (Examination for Japanese University Admission)
Language of InstructionJapanese (JLPT N2/N1 for standard degree tracks); selected English seminar modules
Financial Aid & GrantsMEXT Honors Scholarship (Gakushushoreihi), KIUI 30%–50% Tuition Fee Reduction & Takahashi Municipal Housing Subsidies

Key Academic Strengths

  • Department of Cultural Property Restoration: one of Japan's foremost centres for restoring national historical treasures, oil paintings, and wooden artifacts
  • Pioneering Faculty of Regional Creation and Agriculture in Minamiawaji researching precision viticulture and wine fermentation
  • Department of Animation and Media Arts training illustrators and 3D animators for Tokyo and Kansai production studios
  • Generous tuition reductions and comprehensive support for foreign international students

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Graduate Degree

Graduate School of Cultural Property Restoration (Master's / Ph.D.)

Advanced scientific and artisan training in the conservation, pigment analysis, and physical restoration of Japanese historic scrolls, ceramics, and Western oil paintings.

Undergraduate Degree

Faculty of Agriculture and Food Science

Four-year agricultural curriculum in Minamiawaji examining plant biotechnology, climate-resilient horticulture, and food fermentation science.

Undergraduate Degree

Department of Animation and Manga Arts

Comprehensive creative program covering 2D hand-drawn animation, digital storyboarding, 3D CGI modeling, and character conceptualization.
