National American University

National American · Rapid City, South Dakota, United States

National American University is a private, regionally accredited multi-campus and online university founded in Rapid City, South Dakota. Widely recognized for its specialized programs in intelligence management, national security, strategic leadership, and nursing.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

National American University houses the Henley-Putnam School of Strategic Security, the College of Business, Technology, and Health Management, and the School of Nursing. Specialized curricula focus on counterterrorism, operational intelligence, and strategic protection.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation or GED. The Henley-Putnam School of Strategic Security programs evaluate professional resumes, background verification, and prior military, law enforcement, or corporate security experience.
