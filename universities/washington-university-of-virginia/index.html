Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or accredited secondary school completion certificate for undergraduate programs; accredited bachelor's degree with 2.5+ GPA for master's programs; master's degree with 3.0+ GPA for doctoral programs. TOEFL iBT 70+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants from non-English speaking institutions. Fall semester priority July 15, 2027; Spring semester priority December 1, 2026.
