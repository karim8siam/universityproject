Southern University and A&M College

Southern University Baton Rouge · Baton Rouge, Louisiana, United States

Southern University and A&M College (SUBR) is the historic flagship campus of the Southern University System—the only historically Black university system in the United States. Situated atop Scott's Bluff overlooking the Mississippi River in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, SUBR is an 1890 land-grant university globally recognized for its College of Sciences and Engineering, pioneering doctoral programs in science and math education, renowned 'Human Jukebox' marching band, and distinguished heritage of producing African American engineers, scientists, and agricultural leaders.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SUBR encompasses the College of Sciences and Engineering, the College of Agricultural, Family and Consumer Sciences, the College of Business, the College of Humanities and Interdisciplinary Studies, and the School of Nursing. Key facilities include the Pinchback Engineering Building, the Environmental Toxicology Research Laboratories, and the Southern University Agricultural Research and Extension Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core curriculum GPA (minimum 2.50) and ACT/SAT scores. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.70 GPA (3.0 for doctoral programs), official GRE scores, department-specific research statement of purpose, and three letters of academic recommendation.
