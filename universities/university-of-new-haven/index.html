University of New Haven

UNewHaven · West Haven, Connecticut, United States

The University of New Haven is a private doctoral research university located along the Long Island Sound coastline in West Haven, Connecticut. World-renowned for forensic science and criminal justice through the Henry C. Lee Institute of Forensic Science, UNewHaven is equally celebrated for cybersecurity, national security studies, marine biology, and engineering excellence.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNewHaven houses five colleges: the Henry C. Lee College of Criminal Justice and Forensic Sciences, Tagliatela College of Engineering, College of Arts and Sciences, Pompea College of Business, and the School of Health Sciences. The campus hosts the Henry C. Lee Institute, an international center for forensic training and crime scene reconstruction.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are test-optional and holistic. Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 75+ or IELTS 6.0+).
