Arizona Christian University: Liberal Arts, Cultural Research & Behavioral Science Guide

Arizona Christian University (ACU) is an accredited private university in Glendale, Arizona, celebrated for its Center for Christian Cultural Research, competitive Firestorm athletics, behavioral health programs, and teacher education.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from a CHEA-recognized accredited institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75 for master's programs.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral degrees; provides undergraduate and master's credentials.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0 for international applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose detailing academic goals and alignment with university institutional mission.
Application fee
$50 application fee (waived during preview days).
Required documentation
Official secondary and college transcripts, personal spiritual/academic statement, and two academic or character recommendations.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate annual tuition is approximately $32,000. Graduate tuition averages $550 to $700 per credit hour. Over 90% of students receive financial aid, including Presidential Scholarships, Arizona Christian grants, and athletic fellowships.
