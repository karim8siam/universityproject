Weber State University

Weber State · Ogden, Utah, United States

Weber State University is a comprehensive public polytechnic and regional teaching institution founded in 1889, situated along the scenic foothills of the Wasatch Mountains in Ogden, Utah. Renowned for engineering technology, automotive service technology, nursing and healthcare leadership, and professional sales, Weber State is an essential driver of the Northern Utah aerospace and defense innovation cluster adjacent to Hill Air Force Base.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Weber State comprises seven academic colleges: the College of Engineering, Applied Science & Technology (EAST), Dumke College of Health Professions, Goddard School of Business & Economics, College of Science, Telitha E. Lindquist College of Arts & Humanities, Moyes College of Education, and the College of Social & Behavioral Sciences. The university operates the Miller Advanced Research and Solutions (MARS) Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, transcripts, and core curriculum with open and test-optional admissions. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, professional statement, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
