SAE University College: Creative Media, Games Programming & Audio Engineering Guide

Established in 1976 as the world's first audio engineering institute and now accredited as a full Australian University College by TEQSA, SAE University College operates state-of-the-art creative media campuses across Adelaide, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Perth. SAE delivers globally recognized undergraduate and graduate degrees in game development, virtual reality, sound engineering, animation, and digital film production.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

SAE Creative Media International High Achiever Scholarship

Prestigious merit scholarship awarded to promising creative media creators enrolling in degree courses across animation, game design, audio, and visual effects.

Master of Creative Industries in Games Design and Virtual Worlds

Two-year postgraduate degree advancing skills in real-time ray tracing, procedural world generation, VR spatial audio, and commercial indie studio production.

Australian Interactive Media & Immersive Audio Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research fellowship exploring 3D spatial acoustics, psychoacoustics, and real-time head-related transfer functions (HRTF) in immersive gaming environments.

SAE Emerging Media Lab Generative AI & Digital Production Project

Applied research initiative testing generative diffusion models for real-time 3D texture synthesis and automated rigging in interactive film environments.

Australian Games Studio & VFX Production Industry Internship

Three-month embedded studio internship with Australian game developers and visual effects studios collaborating on commercial console releases and streaming media.

SAE Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Acoustic Engineering and DSP

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting frontier research on multichannel beamforming, room impulse response modeling, and embedded audio DSP algorithms.

SAE Women in Games and Audio Technology Bursary

Targeted educational grant promoting female participation, leadership, and technical innovation in game programming, sound engineering, and interactive technology.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Australian Bachelor's degree (or international equivalent) in creative media, computer science, design, or communications with a minimum credit average (65% or 2.7/4.0 GPA), plus a creative portfolio.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative doctoral pathways hosted in partnership with Australian university research centers in interactive media and acoustic engineering.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score), TOEFL iBT 79, or Pearson PTE 58.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for master's project: 1,000-word creative industries project proposal articulating production scope, software tools, and commercial distribution strategy.
Application fee
$0 AUD direct online application fee.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts and degree certificates, digital creative portfolio (showreel, GitHub code, or audio mix stems), curriculum vitae, and statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at SAE University College ranges from $22,000 to $29,500 AUD per academic year. SAE offers Creative Media Excellence Scholarships providing up to 25% tuition fee remissions for high-achieving domestic and international students, as well as production grant sponsorships.
