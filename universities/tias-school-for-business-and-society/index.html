TIAS School for Business and Society: Sustainable Finance, Executive MBA & Leadership

TIAS School for Business and Society, the premier joint business school of Tilburg University and Eindhoven University of Technology, is ranked among Europe's top business schools, holding triple-crown accreditation (AACSB, AMBA, EQUIS) and renowned for responsible leadership and sustainable finance.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

TIAS Sustainable Leadership & Social Impact International Scholarship

Prestigious merit scholarship awarded to exceptional candidates who demonstrate strong leadership potential and a proven commitment to ESG principles and societal impact.

Full-Time Master of Science in Business Administration (FT-MScBA)

One-year NVAO-accredited Master of Science based in Utrecht offering specializations in Business Analytics, Finance, Investment Management, and Change Management.

Executive PhD / Doctorate in Business Administration (DBA) in Sustainable Finance

Four-year executive doctoral program conducted in collaboration with Tilburg University, examining climate risk pricing, ESG reporting standards, and green bond performance.

European Circular Economy & Corporate Governance Research Study

Applied empirical research study analyzing EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) compliance and supply chain emissions across multinational enterprises.

Amsterdam Financial Center ESG & Impact Investing Practicum

Six-month structured business internship based in Amsterdam/Utrecht with institutional pension funds, private equity firms, and sustainable investment banks.

TIAS Postdoctoral Fellowship in Behavioral Economics & Responsible Leadership

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating executive behavioral biases in strategic decision-making, algorithmic governance, and corporate integrity systems.

Associate Professor / Professor of Sustainable Business Models

Senior faculty post to teach executive MBA and Master's programs, conduct cutting-edge research on circular business models, and lead executive corporate advisory projects.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited university with strong academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Executive PhD / Doctorate in Business Administration (DBA): Master's degree plus at least 5 years of senior management experience.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-test) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, detailed letter of motivation, and essay addressing sustainable business impact.
Application fee
€100 application assessment fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcripts, CV, two reference letters, and motivation essay.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for full-time Master of Science in Business Administration is approximately €23,500 for the full program. TIAS offers generous merit-based scholarships between €5,000 and €10,000 for top-tier international and EU candidates.
