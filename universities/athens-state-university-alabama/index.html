Athens State University: Upper-Division STEM, Cybersecurity & Business Guide

Athens State University is Alabama's only specialized upper-division public university, situated near the Huntsville aerospace and defense corridor, renowned for affordable bachelor's completion, cybersecurity, management of technology, and educator certification.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree with a 3.0 cumulative GPA for graduate programs (MS in Cyber Security, MS in Strategic Leadership).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Upper-division institution offering bachelor's completion and master's degrees; does not offer doctorates directly.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.0 for international degree seekers.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of career focus and transfer degree alignment.
Application fee
$35 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Official transcripts from all previously attended colleges (minimum 36-60 transferable credit hours for undergraduate entry) and government photo ID.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In-state tuition is approximately $265 per credit hour; out-of-state tuition is approximately $530 per credit hour (with regional reciprocal discounts). Alabama transfer scholarships, DoD STEM grants, and federal financial aid are widely awarded.
