Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Carson City · Premier Public College of the Sierra Nevada. Western Nevada College serves western Nevada across campuses in Carson City, Fallon, and Douglas, providing comprehensive collegiate pathways in applied technologies, nursing, sustainable engineering sciences, and university transfer programs. Affiliated with the Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE), WNC pairs state-of-the-art laboratory facilities with accessible workforce partnerships.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or accredited secondary credential. Open access admissions with specialized criteria for professional allied health, nursing, and technical associate programs. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants whose secondary instruction was not in English. Rolling admissions for general programs; Priority fall admission February 1, 2027; Spring semester priority November 1, 2026.
