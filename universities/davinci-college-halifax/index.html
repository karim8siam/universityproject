Institution Overview & Profile

daVinci College, located in downtown Halifax, Nova Scotia, is an established applied arts and media institution providing specialized career education in 2D/3D animation, game design, audio production, graphic design, and web development.

Institution TypePrivate Career Polytechnic College
Main CampusDowntown Halifax Campus, Nova Scotia
AccreditationNova Scotia Department of Advanced Education / Private Career Colleges Division
Admissions RouteRolling admissions with portfolio and entrance interview
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5, or equivalent)
Financial Aid & GrantsNova Scotia Student Assistance & daVinci Talent Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Industry-standard 2D/3D animation, CGI character modelling, and digital VFX
  • Sound design, mixing, and audio production studios for film, television, and gaming
  • Interactive media programming, user experience design, and digital web development
  • Practicum placements with Atlantic Canadian game studios, creative agencies, and production houses
