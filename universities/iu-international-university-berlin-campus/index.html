IU International University of Applied Sciences - Berlin Campus

IU International University of Applied Sciences (International Campus Berlin) is Germany’s largest state-accredited university, offering modern English-taught degree programs in computer science, artificial intelligence, cyber security, data analytics, and digital business.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in Berlin’s startup hub, IU Berlin blends on-campus interactive seminars with proprietary AI-powered digital learning platforms. Programs are accredited by FIBAA and the German Science Council (Wissenschaftsrat), offering practical technical curricula aligned with global industry demands and direct placement pipelines into Berlin tech enterprises.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor admissions require high school graduation certificate and English proficiency (IELTS 6.0 / Duolingo 105). Master admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree with 3.0+ GPA equivalent and prerequisite quantitative or computing credits.
