The University of Kansas

University of Kansas · Lawrence, Kansas, United States

The University of Kansas (KU) is the flagship Carnegie R1 public research university of Kansas and a member of the prestigious Association of American Universities (AAU), situated on Mount Oread in Lawrence. Generating over $380M in annual research expenditures, KU is globally celebrated for the top-tier School of Pharmacy, the KU Medical Center, the Biodiversity Institute, and the Higuchi Biosciences Center.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

KU comprises 14 academic schools spanning Lawrence and the KU Medical Center in Kansas City, Wichita, and Salina. Renowned research entities include the Institute for Bioengineering Research, the Center for Environmentally Beneficial Catalysis (CEBC), the Information and Telecommunication Technology Center (ITTC), and the Life Span Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission is evaluated based on high school GPA and curriculum rigor (assured admission for 3.25+ GPA). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, program-specific standardized test scores (GRE/GMAT where required), personal research statement, CV, and three letters of recommendation.
