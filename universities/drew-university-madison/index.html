Drew University

Drew University ('University in the Forest') is an esteemed private liberal arts and graduate university in Madison, NJ, famous for the RISE Institute (retired industrial scientists mentoring undergraduate researchers), Caspersen School of Graduate Studies, and Drew Theological School.

Academic overview & research priorities

Located 25 miles west of Manhattan on a forested 186-acre arboretum campus, Drew offers rigorous undergraduate liberal arts education and specialized postgraduate programs. Its celebrated Research Institute for Scientists Emeriti (RISE) pairs students with former Bell Labs, Merck, and Bristol-Myers Squibb research directors in active pharmaceutical and environmental labs. Drew also runs semester programs directly on Wall Street, the United Nations, and London.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants submit the Common Application or Coalition Application with official transcripts, counselor recommendation, and writing essay. Drew is test-optional. Graduate applications to the Caspersen School require an accredited bachelor's degree (minimum GPA 3.0), statement of goals, and academic writing samples. International applicants must provide TOEFL iBT 80+, IELTS 6.5+, or Duolingo 110+.
