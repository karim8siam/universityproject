Peru State College

Peru State · Peru, Nebraska, United States

Peru State College is the historic first higher education institution in Nebraska, established in 1867 on the scenic oak-covered bluffs above the Missouri River in Peru, Nebraska. Known as the 'Campus of a Thousand Oaks,' it is renowned for teacher education, criminal justice, wildlife ecology, and visual arts.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Peru State features the historic T.J. Majors Hall, the Hoyt Science Hall, and the modern Center for Applied Science and Technology. Surrounded by the Missouri River riparian corridor, it offers extraordinary living laboratories for environmental fieldwork and wildlife management.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Peru State College maintains an accessible admissions policy welcoming high school graduates and transfer students. Freshmen applicants submit official high school transcripts (minimum 2.0 GPA) with test-optional reviews. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75 GPA.
