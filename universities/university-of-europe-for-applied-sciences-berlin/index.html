Academic overview & research priorities

Located in central Berlin near Potsdamer Platz, UE Berlin operates three core faculties: Business & Tech, Art & Design, and Sports & Media. The university is a premier launchpad for Berlin's startup and creative ecosystems, delivering English-taught degrees equipped with motion capture studios, virtual reality labs, and direct career pipelines into European tech enterprises.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a recognized secondary school qualification (Abitur / High School Diploma). Art and design programs require a portfolio review and creative interview. Tech and business master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline (minimum 180 or 210 ECTS). English proficiency of IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL iBT 79+ is required.
