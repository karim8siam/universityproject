University of Cologne: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Cologne (Universität zu Köln), founded in 1388, is one of the oldest and largest universities in Europe, recognized as a German University of Excellence. Renowned worldwide for plant sciences (CEPLAS Cluster of Excellence), aging research (CECAD), quantum matter (ML4Q), and economics, Uni Köln hosts over 50,000 students in one of Germany's vibrant cultural hubs.

CEPLAS Cluster of Excellence Doctoral Fellowship in Plant Phenomics & Synthetic Agriculture

Three-year doctoral position within the Cluster of Excellence on Plant Sciences (CEPLAS), dissecting C4 photosynthesis evolution, microbiome interactions, and drought resilience in agricultural crops.

International Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Physics (Quantum Matter & Astrophysics)

Rigorous two-year physics program integrated with the ML4Q (Matter and Light for Quantum Computing) Excellence Cluster, studying topological quantum devices, submillimeter astronomy, and condensed matter physics.

Cologne Graduate School in Management, Economics and Social Sciences (CGS) Fellowship

Prestigious doctoral scholarship providing three years of full financial backing in empirical economics, behavioral sociology, behavioral decision-making, and econometric policy evaluation.

CECAD Cologne Cluster of Excellence Cellular Stress Responses in Aging Research Assistantship

Research position investigating mitochondrial proteostasis, lipid metabolism changes during cellular senescence, and autophagy regulation in human disease models.

Cologne Center for eHumanities (CCeH) Digital Cultural Heritage Research Internship

Five-month research practicum creating digital critical editions, semantic graph databases, and 3D architectural reconstructions of medieval Rhineland cultural heritage.

Kekulé Postdoctoral Fellowship in Homogeneous Catalysis and Coordination Chemistry

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship designing Earth-abundant iron and cobalt catalysts for cross-coupling reactions, carbon dioxide chemical fixation, and green polymer synthesis.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Computational Sciences and High-Performance Analytics

Interdisciplinary master's program training computational scientists in parallel numerical algorithms, petascale simulation, machine learning on supercomputers, and scientific visualization.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline with strong academic marks (at least 2.5 or better on the German grading scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science, Master of Arts, or Diplom with above-average grades ('gut' or 'sehr gut'); approved doctoral thesis proposal and supervisor acceptance.
Language proficiency
For English programs: TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS Academic 6.5. For German programs: DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 in all examination sections.
Research statement / Proposal
A 4-6 page doctoral research project outline detailing background literature, hypotheses, methodological approach, and proposed timeline.
Application fee
€75 application fee for international credentials evaluated through uni-assist.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, academic transcripts, Europass CV, letter of motivation, and 2 academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Cologne is completely free (€0 tuition charges) for both domestic and international students. Enrolled students pay only a semester social contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €320, which includes a comprehensive regional transit ticket covering all public trains and buses across North Rhine-Westphalia. Doctoral candidates in Excellence Clusters are employed under German collective agreements (TV-L E13) with gross annual salaries between €52,000 and €56,000.
