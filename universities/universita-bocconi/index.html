Università Bocconi (Milan)

Founded in 1902 in Milan, Università Bocconi is one of Europe's top private research universities, consistently ranked in the global top 5 for Business, Economics, and Finance. Recognized globally for pioneering analytical approaches to economics, quantitative finance, decision sciences, and international law, Bocconi offers fully funded doctoral programs, generous merit awards, and an unparalleled European corporate and academic placement network. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Bocconi PhD Full Fellowships in Economics, Finance & Management

All admitted Bocconi PhD candidates receive fully funded fellowships covering living costs and tuition, with placement records into top international universities (LSE, Harvard, MIT, Princeton) and central banks.

Bocconi PhD in Statistics and Computer Science

Four-year interdisciplinary PhD focusing on machine learning foundations, Bayesian statistics, high-dimensional probability, optimization, and algorithmic game theory.

Bocconi Master of Science (MSc) in Economic and Social Sciences

Rigorous two-year Master of Science preparing students for doctoral research or international institutions (IMF, World Bank, OECD) with deep mathematical modeling training.

Bocconi Master of Science (MSc) in Data Science and Business Analytics

Two-year curriculum combining statistical learning, deep learning, scalable computing, and business optimization, taught entirely in English in Milan.

Bocconi IGIER Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Applied Economics

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Innocenzo Gasparini Institute for Economic Research (IGIER) collaborating with leading European macroeconomists and political economists.

Bocconi B4i Startup & Innovation Summer Venture Traineeship

Three-month summer incubation traineeship at Bocconi for Innovation (B4i) providing pre-seed capital access, legal advisory, and customer development training.

Bocconi-Politecnico di Milano Joint MSc in Transformative Sustainability

Pioneering joint degree between Bocconi University and Politecnico di Milano integrating ESG finance, environmental engineering, life-cycle assessment, and sustainable supply chains.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Undergraduate degree (Bachelor's or equivalent) with high academic standing. Competitive candidates typically present GMAT scores of 650+ (Focus Edition 595+) or GRE equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (or equivalent 4-year degree) in economics, finance, mathematics, statistics, computer science, or social sciences with top academic honors.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 89 (higher recommended for doctoral programs); Cambridge C1 Advanced/C2 Proficiency.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 3-page research statement highlighting research questions, theoretical literature, quantitative methodology, and potential Bocconi faculty alignment.
Application fee
€100 application fee for Master of Science programs; €0 application fee for Bocconi PhD programs.
Required documentation
Complete official transcripts, CV, GMAT/GRE test score report, 2 confidential letters of recommendation, and personal motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

For doctoral students, Bocconi awards 100% full-tuition waivers along with guaranteed tax-free living stipends of €18,000 to €21,000 per year for four years to all admitted PhD candidates, plus a 50% stipend increase during approved study-abroad research periods. For Master's programs, Bocconi Graduate Merit Awards provide 100% or 50% tuition reductions covering standard tuition fees (€16,000/year).
