Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Aurora · Anschutz Medical Campus #1 Ranked Academic Medical Center in Colorado. University of Colorado Hospital (UCH), the academic flagship of UCHealth situated on the Anschutz Medical Campus, is recognized nationally for groundbreaking clinical trials, organ transplantation, advanced neurosciences, and comprehensive cancer care. Partnered with the University of Colorado School of Medicine, UCH delivers world-class clinical fellowships and residency programs pioneering cellular therapies, precision oncology, and translational medicine.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications accepted via ERAS (Electronic Residency Application Service) and National Resident Matching Program (NRMP) or specialized fellowship matching programs through CU Anschutz. ECFMG certification required for international medical graduates, including English clinical proficiency evaluation. Fellowship and residency applications open July 1, 2026; Match submission deadline November 15, 2026; Rolling clinical appointments through February 1, 2027.
