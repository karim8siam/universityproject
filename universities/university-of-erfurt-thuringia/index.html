University of Erfurt: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Originally founded in 1379 and attended by Martin Luther, the University of Erfurt was re-established in 1994 as a specialized modern reform university dedicated to humanities, cultural studies, social sciences, and teacher education. Serving approximately 6,000 students on an attractive green campus, Uni Erfurt is home to the prestigious Max Weber Centre for Advanced Cultural and Social Studies, the Willy Brandt School of Public Policy, and the Gotha Research Centre housing world-renowned Enlightenment archives. Erfurt promotes interdisciplinary modular degrees and pioneering research into religious diversity, global governance, and communication psychology.
