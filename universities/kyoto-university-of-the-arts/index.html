Kyoto University of the Arts

Kyoto University of the Arts (formerly Kyoto造形芸術大学) is one of Japan's most comprehensive and celebrated private universities of fine arts and design, nestled on the scenic hillside of Mount Hiei, nurturing world-acclaimed animators, manga artists, character designers, and contemporary painters.

Academic overview & excellence

KUA offers immersion in classical Japanese traditional craft disciplines alongside frontier digital arts, spatial design, and film production. The university hosts the Tohoku Earthquake Arts Restoration Center and the Ultra Factory fabrication laboratory directed by world-famous sculptor Kenji Yanobe.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires creative portfolio submissions, artistic practical examinations (drawing or design), and personal interviews. Undergraduate international applicants submit EJU or JLPT N2 certificates. Master's (MFA) and doctoral candidates submit comprehensive digital portfolios and detailed artistic research statements.

One-year post-graduate artist residency at the Ultra Factory developing monumental kinetic metal sculptures, inflatable structures, and robotic interactive art.

Funding: ¥1,500,000 project materials budget plus full master-machinist workshop access Tuition: ¥0 Deadline: March 31, 2027

Three-month professional studio placement in 2D digital animation, keyframe layout, 3D character rigging, and anime background concept painting.

Funding: ¥100,000 monthly living stipend placed at Kyoto digital animation studios Tuition: Included in enrollment Deadline: April 15, 2027

Three-year practice-based doctoral degree combining a major body of original creative artwork with a written dissertation in art theory and contemporary aesthetics.
