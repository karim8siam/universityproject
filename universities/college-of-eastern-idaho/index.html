College of Eastern Idaho

The College of Eastern Idaho (CEI) in Idaho Falls is a comprehensive accredited public institution, internationally recognized for its close academic and technical research partnerships with the Idaho National Laboratory (INL) in clean nuclear energy, cybersecurity, and radiation safety.

Academic overview & excellence

Serving nine counties in southeastern Idaho, CEI offers university-transfer associate degrees (AA/AS) and specialized technical certificates. The campus houses state-of-the-art radiation simulation suites, cybersecurity defense laboratories, advanced machining shops, and nursing clinical simulation centers. Through its proximity and formal collaborative programs with the Idaho National Laboratory (INL)—the US Department of Energy's lead nuclear energy laboratory—CEI prepares highly skilled technicians, health professionals, and engineers for the modern nuclear and high-technology workforce.

Research admissions & criteria

CEI operates an open-door admissions policy for high school graduates and GED holders seeking undergraduate degrees. Admission to selective-entry programs (such as Registered Nursing, Radiation Protection, and Cybersecurity) requires completion of prerequisite college coursework with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.7 to 3.0/4.0, standardized testing (TEAS for nursing), official high school or college transcripts, and verification of English language proficiency (TOEFL iBT 61 or IELTS 5.5 for international students).

Bachelor's

Associate of Science in Radiation Protection & Health Physics

Two-year applied associate degree developed with INL, instructing students in radiation detection instrumentation, ALARA safety protocols, radioactive waste management, and nuclear environmental monitoring.

Funding: DOE Idaho National Laboratory technical scholarships covering up to $4,000 annually Tuition: $3,100 per year for in-district; $6,200 for out-of-state Deadline: July 1, 2027
Fellowship

Idaho National Laboratory (INL) Technical Apprenticeship Fellowship

Highly competitive DOE co-op fellowship placing CEI nuclear, mechanical, and electrical technology students in hands-on operations at the Advanced Test Reactor and Materials and Fuels Complex.

Funding: Full tuition coverage, health benefits, and $22.50 per hour paid laboratory training Tuition: $0 Deadline: April 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Associate of Applied Science in Cybersecurity and Network Architecture

Two-year comprehensive degree teaching network defense, ethical hacking, industrial control system (ICS/SCADA) security, digital forensics, and Cisco networking protocols.

Funding: National Science Foundation CyberCorps scholarship stipends available Tuition: $3,100 in-district; $6,200 out-of-state Deadline: August 1, 2027
Scholarship

College of Eastern Idaho Foundation Presidential Scholarship

Top merit scholarship awarded to entering freshmen demonstrating academic excellence, community service leadership, and commitment to completing a two-year degree or transfer path at CEI.

Funding: $2,500 annual award covering full tuition and required textbooks Tuition: $0 Deadline: March 1, 2027
Internship

Eastern Idaho Regional Medical Center Nursing Practicum

Four-hundred hours of supervised clinical rotations in intensive care, emergency trauma, pediatrics, and surgical units at Eastern Idaho Regional Medical Center (EIRMC).

Funding: Hospital uniform stipend and loan forgiveness commitments upon graduation Tuition: Included in nursing curriculum credit hours Deadline: May 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Associate of Science in Environmental Geology and Hydrology

Two-year university-transfer science degree exploring groundwater hydrogeology, volcanology of the Snake River Plain, geographic information systems, and environmental soil chemistry.

Funding: Snake River Aquifer environmental research grants up to $1,500 Tuition: $3,100 in-district; $6,200 out-of-state Deadline: August 1, 2027
Course

Certificate in Certified Nuclear Materials Handling & Criticality Safety

Sixteen-week workforce certificate preparing technicians in radioactive isotope glovebox manipulation, fissile material packaging, criticality prevention calculations, and OSHA hazmat.

Funding: Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) grant eligible Tuition: $1,850 Deadline: September 1, 2027
