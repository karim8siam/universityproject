ISM International School of Management

The International School of Management (ISM Munich Campus) is a leading state-accredited private business school in Bavaria offering high-impact undergraduate and postgraduate programs in international management, business intelligence, finance, and luxury marketing.

Academic overview & research priorities

ISM combines rigorous business education with mandatory international exchange semesters, hands-on consulting projects, and intensive language training. Located in Munich, ISM maintains strong industry linkages with DAX-40 corporations, venture capital firms, and technology leaders, offering AACSB-member accredited education.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor programs require the German Abitur, Fachhochschulreife, or international secondary school equivalent, along with an admissions test (case study and interview). Master programs require an accredited bachelor’s degree in business or economics with 2.50+ GPA (German scale) and English proficiency (TOEFL 85 / IELTS 6.5).
