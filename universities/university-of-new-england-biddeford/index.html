University of New England - Biddeford

UNE Biddeford · Biddeford, Maine, United States

The University of New England's coastal Biddeford Campus sits at the mouth of the Saco River overlooking the Atlantic Ocean in southern Maine. UNE is Maine's premier provider of health professionals and home to the Arthur P. Girard Marine Science Center, world-class osteopathic medical research, neuroscience laboratories, and environmental sustainability institutes.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma with rigorous college preparatory science and math courses. Common Application with test-blind admission policy for undergraduate programs. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates without English-medium secondary instruction. Early Action deadline November 15, 2026; Regular Decision deadline February 15, 2027.
