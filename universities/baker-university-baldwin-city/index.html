Baker University: Kansas Liberal Arts, School of Education & Nursing Guide

Founded in 1858, Baker University is the oldest chartered institution of higher education in Kansas, celebrated for its historic Baldwin City campus, acclaimed School of Education (doctoral EdD), School of Nursing (Stormont Vail Health partner), and pre-health sciences.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA for graduate school; teaching license required for advanced educational leadership master's.
Doctoral admission eligibility
EdD in Educational Leadership requires a Master's degree, professional educational administrative experience, and an academic writing sample.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 for international degree applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose detailing personal character, academic achievements, and educational goals.
Application fee
$20 online application fee (often waived for campus visitors).
Required documentation
Official secondary school or college transcripts, personal essay, resume/CV, and two letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate annual tuition is approximately $33,500. Graduate tuition averages $625 to $850 per credit hour. Over 95% of full-time undergraduate students receive institutional aid, including Presidential Scholarships (up to $18,000/year) and Baker Trustee awards.
