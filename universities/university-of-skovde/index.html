University of Skövde: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Skövde (Högskolan i Skövde), located in Skövde in southwestern Sweden, is an internationally celebrated specialist university recognized globally as the Nordic hub for video game development and Sweden Game Arena (incubating multi-million selling games like Valheim, Goat Simulator, and V Rising). Skövde also excels in systems biology, biomedical bioinformatics, cognitive neuroscience, and virtual engineering.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Serious Games and Game Development (Sweden Game Arena Flagship)

Two-year master's curriculum connected to the Sweden Game Arena startup incubator, designing interactive simulation games, gamified rehabilitation systems, and educational virtual environments.

PhD in Systems Biology and Biomedical Bioinformatics (Systems Biology Research Centre)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment at the Systems Biology Research Centre (SBRC), modeling complex cellular signaling pathways, oncological biomarker identification, and multi-omics integration.

University of Skövde Master's Scholarship for International Students

Merit-based partial tuition waiver awarded directly by the University of Skövde to top international applicants enrolled in on-campus master's degree programs.

Virtual Engineering & Digital Twin Manufacturing Research Assistantship

Applied research post at the Virtual Engineering lab developing real-time digital twins, ergonomic virtual reality worker evaluations, and discrete event simulation models for automotive engine assembly lines.

Sweden Game Arena Video Game Prototyping and Startup Studio Internship

Five-month immersion in Sweden Game Arena incubator at Science Park Skövde, testing gameplay mechanics, participating in publisher pitching sessions, and preparing games for commercial Steam release.

Cognitive Neuroscience & Consciousness Studies Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment exploring altered states of consciousness, sleep mentation EEG recordings, and the behavioral effects of mindfulness interventions.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Data Science and Industrial Decision Support

Comprehensive one-year or two-year graduate curriculum in predictive machine learning, artificial neural networks, big data processing frameworks, and visual analytics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A completed bachelor's degree (equivalent to a Swedish Kandidatexamen) in computer science, informatics, bioinformatics, or game development.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Second-cycle degree (Master's level) with at least 240 ECTS credits, an approved thesis in informatics or systems biology, and strong research alignment.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B from Swedish upper secondary school, or IELTS Academic 6.5 (no section below 5.5), or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research outline explaining the research questions, game technology/computational methodology, relevant datasets, and academic objectives.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee administered via University Admissions Sweden (antagning.se).
Required documentation
Officially certified bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, master's thesis or portfolio, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students at the University of Skövde ranges from SEK 130,000 to SEK 145,000 per academic year (EU/EEA citizens study tuition-free). International applicants are eligible for Swedish Institute (SI) Global Professionals Scholarships (covering 100% tuition plus SEK 12,000/month living stipend) or University of Skövde Master's Scholarships providing 50% tuition reductions. Doctoral candidates are fully employed on salaried Swedish academic contracts (SEK 32,500 to 36,000 per month).
