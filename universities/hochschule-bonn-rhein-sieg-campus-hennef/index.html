Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg - Campus Hennef

The Hennef campus of Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (H-BRS) is a specialized academic facility in North Rhine-Westphalia, dedicated to social security management, applied rehabilitation science, and digital administrative governance.

Academic overview & excellence

Situated on the scenic Sieg river east of Bonn, Campus Hennef houses H-BRS's prestigious Department of Social Security Studies (Fachbereich Sozialversicherung). Operating in direct partnership with German statutory social accident insurance (DGUV), health insurance bodies, and national pension funds, Campus Hennef delivers specialized undergraduate and postgraduate degrees in social law, health economics, disability management, and public administration. Students enjoy state-of-the-art seminar rooms, specialized administrative case-study libraries, and exceptional employment placement rates.

Research admissions & criteria

Admissions to bachelor's degrees (B.A. in Social Security Management) and master's programs at H-BRS Campus Hennef require general higher education entrance qualification (Abitur or Fachhochschulreife) or recognized international equivalent. For dual study programs, a preliminary training contract with a participating statutory social insurance institution or public agency is required. International applicants must submit verified German proficiency (TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2) and academic transcripts evaluated via uni-assist.
