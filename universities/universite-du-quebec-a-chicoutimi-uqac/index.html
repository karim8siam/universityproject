Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

The Université du Québec à Chicoutimi (UQAC) is a dynamic research university in the Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean region of Québec, globally renowned for its international atmospheric icing research centre (CENGIVRE), aluminum metallurgical engineering, and digital art production studios.

Institution type
Public Research University (Université du Québec Network)
Campus location
Chicoutimi Campus & NAD Centre Montréal
Accreditation
Ministère de l'Enseignement supérieur du Québec
Admissions channel
Fall term (March 1) and Winter term (November 1)
Instruction language
French (primary instructional language; research thesis in English permitted)
Financial aid & support
FRQNT Doctoral Scholarships, UQAC Merit Awards, Mitacs Accelerate

Institutional Research Strengths

  • World-leading International Research Centre on Atmospheric Icing (CENGIVRE)
  • Centre for Aluminum Research (CURAL) with industrial Rio Tinto partnerships
  • School of Digital Arts, Animation and Design (NAD-UQAC) based in Montréal
  • Extensive boreal ecological observatory and sustainable forestry stations

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Doctorat en ingénierie (Ph.D. in Engineering) (Doctoral Degree) — Advanced doctoral research in materials engineering, power line icing mitigation, and smart mechanical systems.
  • Maîtrise en gestion de projet (Master in Project Management) (Graduate Degree) — Internationally accredited PMI degree preparing senior directors for mega-infrastructure and engineering ventures.
  • Baccalauréat en développement de jeux vidéo (Video Game Development) (Undergraduate Degree) — Specialized computer science and digital animation degree co-delivered at NAD-UQAC Montréal.
