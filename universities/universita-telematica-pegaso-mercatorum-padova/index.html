Institutional Overview

Università Telematica Pegaso e Universitas Mercatorum - Sede di Padova is a major academic and examination center serving students and working professionals across Veneto and northeastern Italy. Located on Viale Codalunga near Padova's central transport corridors, the center provides accredited digital bachelor's and master's degrees in digital business, law, education, psychology, and engineering.
