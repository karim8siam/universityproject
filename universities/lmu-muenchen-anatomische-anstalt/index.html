LMU München - Anatomische Anstalt und Biomedizinisches Zentrum

The Anatomische Anstalt at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (LMU) is one of Europe's most storied institutes of human anatomy and molecular structural biology, driving breakthroughs in developmental neuroscience and cellular architecture.

Academic overview & excellence

Located in the Pettenkoferstraße medical district of Munich, the Anatomische Anstalt operates as a cornerstone of the LMU Faculty of Medicine and the Biomedical Center (BMC) Munich. It houses cutting-edge research groups in neuroanatomy, developmental biology, light-sheet fluorescence microscopy, and cellular organelle dynamics. Medical students and biomedical graduate scholars engage in macroscopic dissection, cryo-electron microscopy, single-cell spatial transcriptomics, and clinical translational neurodegeneration studies within a world-renowned Bavarian scientific ecosystem.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to doctoral programs (Dr. med., Dr. rer. nat.) and Master's degrees at the LMU Anatomische Anstalt is administered via the Munich Medical Research School (MMRS) or the Graduate School of Systemic Neurosciences (GSN-LMU). Applicants must possess an outstanding Master's degree in Biology, Biochemistry, or Medicine (German grade 2.0 or better), verified English language mastery (C1 or IELTS 7.0+), a research proposal aligned with institute research chairs, and academic references.
