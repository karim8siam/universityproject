HAS University of Applied Sciences: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

HAS University of Applied Sciences (HAS green academy), with modern campuses in 's-Hertogenbosch (Den Bosch) and Venlo, is the Netherlands' top-ranked specialist university for agriculture, food innovation, agribusiness, and environmental green technologies. Situated in the world's most productive agricultural export cluster, HAS is internationally revered for practical agritech, vertical farming, and circular food processing.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Applied Food Science and Circular Food Innovations

Two-year master's curriculum conducted in state-of-the-art food processing pilot plants, creating plant-based protein alternatives, upcycled food waste ingredients, and clean-label formulations.

Precision Horticulture and High-Tech Greenhouse Automation Practicum

Five-month industrial internship deploying autonomous harvesting robotics, spectral LED lighting recipes, and computer-vision disease diagnosis inside Dutch commercial greenhouses.

Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (HAS green academy International Talent Award)

Merit award co-funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science and HAS green academy, recognizing exceptional non-EEA students with visionary leadership in agriculture.

Center of Expertise in Circular Agriculture and Soil Health Research Fellowship

Applied research post investigating regenerative farming practices, biochar soil amendments, and microbial biodiversity enhancements on commercial dairy and arable farms in North Brabant.

Master of Science in Agri-Food Data Science and Supply Chain Logistics

Two-year postgraduate degree delivering specialized knowledge in agricultural supply chain modeling, cold-chain temperature sensor analytics, food traceability algorithms, and demand forecasting.

Applied Industrial Research Fellowship in Plant-Based Protein Extraction & Texturization

Two-year applied research appointment optimizing pea, faba bean, and lupin protein isolates, utilizing twin-screw extruders to mimic fibrous meat textures for next-generation alternative proteins.

Joint Doctoral Trajectory in Urban Agriculture & Closed-Loop Circular Nutrient Recovery (with WUR)

Four-year joint doctoral program between HAS green academy and Wageningen University researching struvite phosphorus precipitation, vertical aeroponic systems, and zero-discharge urban hydroponics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A relevant Bachelor of Science degree or recognized equivalent in food technology, agronomy, biotechnology, environmental science, or agribusiness.
Doctoral admission eligibility
HAS conducts collaborative external doctoral research in partnership with research universities including Wageningen University & Research (WUR) and TU Eindhoven.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in each sub-score).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-3 page Letter of Motivation and applied research concept describing an innovation project in food systems, circular agriculture, or green biotech.
Application fee
€0 application fee via Studielink.nl.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcript of records, curriculum vitae, copy of passport, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students at HAS green academy is approximately €9,200 to €11,000 per academic year (EU/EEA students pay the Dutch statutory fee of approximately €2,530 per year). International students are eligible for the Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (€5,000 one-time award) and HAS Top Talent Grants. Dutch industry internships pay student stipends averaging €800 to €1,300 per month.
