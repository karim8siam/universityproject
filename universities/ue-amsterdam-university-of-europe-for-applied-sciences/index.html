Institution Overview & Profile

UE Amsterdam (University of Europe for Applied Sciences Amsterdam Campus), situated in a historic canal-side building on Passeerdersgracht in the Jordaan district of Amsterdam, is a state-recognized, FIBAA-accredited European university campus delivering English-taught bachelor and master degree programs in digital product management, creative technology, UI/UX design, visual communication, and data science.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Private University of Applied Sciences
Main CampusAmsterdam Canal Campus (Passeerdersgracht), North Holland, Netherlands
AccreditationFIBAA Accredited / German Science and Humanities Council / Dutch Higher Education Registered
Admissions RouteOnline application, creative portfolio review, and video interview
Language of InstructionEnglish (100% English medium of instruction; IELTS 5.5 to 6.5)
Financial Aid & GrantsUE Early Bird Scholarships, Creative Digital Talent Grants & Cultural Diversity Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Historic central Amsterdam campus combining classical canal architecture with cutting-edge digital media studios
  • Interdisciplinary curricula merging digital design, business model innovation, and software engineering
  • Close partnerships with Amsterdam's world-leading creative agencies, game studios, and tech multinationals
  • Dual European mobility allowing students to spend study semesters at UE campuses in Berlin, Hamburg, or Potsdam
