University of Arkansas - Fort Smith

UAFS · Fort Smith, Arkansas, United States

The University of Arkansas - Fort Smith (UAFS) is a regional public university in western Arkansas along the Arkansas-Oklahoma border. Founded in 1928, UAFS is praised for its hands-on career education, strong partnerships with regional manufacturing and healthcare industries, and robust programs across engineering technology, dental hygiene, business, and health sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UAFS delivers certificate, associate, baccalaureate, and master's credentials through the College of Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics (STEM), the College of Health, Education & Human Sciences, and the College of Business and Industry. The university is home to the Boreham Library and the state-of-the-art Baldor Technology Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate applicants apply online with official high school or prior college transcripts (test-optional; recommended minimum 2.25 cumulative GPA). Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and statements of career intent.
