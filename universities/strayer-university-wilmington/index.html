Strayer University

Strayer University · Wilmington, Delaware, United States

Strayer University is an accredited private university founded in 1892 in Baltimore and Washington, D.C., operating campus locations across the eastern United States—including its prominent Wilmington, Delaware campus—alongside an extensive online learning platform. Strayer specializes in career-aligned bachelor's and master's degrees in business administration, information technology, cybersecurity, healthcare administration, and criminal justice designed for working adult learners.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Strayer University is accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE) and its business programs are accredited by ACBSP. It offers innovative learning options such as the Jack Welch Management Institute (JWMI) Executive MBA and the Strayer 'Graduation Fund' which provides tuition-free classes as milestones are achieved.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Strayer University operates an open and flexible admissions policy for high school graduates and adult learners. Graduate applicants must possess an accredited baccalaureate degree with a minimum 2.50 GPA, submit official transcripts, and complete an entrance consultation without requiring standardized GMAT or GRE exams.
