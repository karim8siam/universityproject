Rostock University of Music and Theatre

The Rostock University of Music and Theatre (Hochschule für Musik und Theater Rostock / HMT Rostock) is one of Germany's most prestigious arts conservatories, situated in a converted medieval Franciscan monastery overlooking the Warnow river in the Baltic port city of Rostock.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1994, HMT Rostock delivers master-level conservatory training across two main divisions: the Institute for Music Performance (Orchestral Instruments, Piano, Voice, Conducting, Composition) and the Institute for Dramatic Arts (Acting, Drama Direction, Pop & World Music). HMT Rostock boasts state-of-the-art concert halls, exceptional acoustics, an extensive chamber music network across Scandinavia and the Baltic, and high placement in European symphony orchestras.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission is determined primarily through rigorous in-person entrance examinations and artistic auditions (instrumental performance, music theory, aural training, or dramatic monologue auditions). Applicants must submit verified secondary school qualifications, proof of German language proficiency (typically B2 for performance; C1 for music education), and a creative repertoire list.
