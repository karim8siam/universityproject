Hanse- und Universitätsstadt Rostock - Science Office

The Science and University Liaison Office of the Hanseatic and University City of Rostock coordinates collaborative research, science communication, maritime economic transfer, and doctoral municipal fellowships between the City of Rostock and its higher education institutions.

Academic overview & research priorities

Bridging municipal governance with the University of Rostock, Hochschule Wismar, and maritime research institutes (Leibniz Institute for Baltic Sea Research, Fraunhofer IGD, Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research), the office funds urban climate resilience studies, smart port logistics, and demographic aging research.

Research admissions & international criteria

Fellowships and grants are open to enrolled doctoral candidates and postdoctoral researchers whose academic projects intersect with municipal urban planning, coastal climate adaptation, or regional economic transformation.
