Alfa-college - Campus Hardenberg

The Alfa-college Campus Hardenberg at Parkweg is an accredited regional education center (ROC) in Overijssel delivering high-level tertiary vocational diplomas (MBO Level 4), technical engineering certifications, and university bridging pathways.

Academic overview & excellence

Located in the Vechtdal industrial and technological corridor of northeastern Overijssel, Alfa-college Campus Hardenberg is a modern vocational and technical college hub. The campus features high-tech mechatronics laboratories, health simulation nursing clinics, sustainable construction workshops, and direct university-of-applied-sciences (HBO) articulation pathways to Windesheim, NHL Stenden, and Hanze University of Applied Sciences.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to MBO Level 4 vocational diploma and HBO-propedeuse transition programs requires a Dutch VMBO theoretical or mixed diploma (VMBO-tl/gl), HAVO completion, or equivalent international secondary school certification. International candidates apply through the centralized intake portal with certified Dutch translation of diplomas and demonstration of Dutch language proficiency at CEFR B1/B2 level.

Four-year registered nursing diploma preparing students for direct BIG registration, acute clinical hospital care, medical pharmacotherapy, and seamless articulation into HBO-V.

Internship

Structured dual work-study apprenticeship in sustainable timber frame construction, heat pump HVAC installation, and net-zero energy building retrofit across the Vechtdal region.

Funding: Paid BBL vocational work term with monthly collective labor wages of €1,200 to €1,600 Tuition: Subsidized by employer training fund Deadline: May 15, 2027

Annual vocational scholarship awarded by regional industrial partners to top-performing students entering technical mechatronics, renewable energy, and digital ICT programs.

Funding: €1,500 grant covering professional tool kits, laptop, and technical work safety attire Tuition: Applied directly to educational expenses Deadline: June 30, 2027
Fellowship

HBO Bridging Propedeuse Fellowship in Applied Computer Science

Accelerated academic bridging pathway allowing top Level 4 Software Developer students to take HBO-level modules in database design, algorithms, and agile software development.

Funding: €2,000 university transition scholarship co-sponsored by Windesheim University of Applied Sciences Tuition: €1,419 statutory tuition Deadline: April 30, 2027

Sixteen-week practical placement maintaining robotic milking systems, automated barn ventilation telemetry, and agricultural nitrogen sensor arrays in Overijssel.
