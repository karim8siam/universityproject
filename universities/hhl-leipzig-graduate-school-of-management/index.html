Institutional Overview

HHL Leipzig Graduate School of Management (Handelshochschule Leipzig), founded in 1898, is Germany's oldest business school and consistently ranked among the world's premier management institutions. AACSB and ACQUIN accredited, HHL has produced over 500 company founders and four German unicorns, delivering top-tier Master in Management, MBA, and doctoral programs focused on ethical leadership and technological entrepreneurship.
