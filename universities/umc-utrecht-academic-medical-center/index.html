UMC Utrecht: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Operating in close partnership with Utrecht University at the Utrecht Science Park, UMC Utrecht employs over 12,000 healthcare professionals, researchers, and educators. UMC Utrecht is world-famous as the home of groundbreaking organoid stem cell technology (pioneered by Hans Clevers) and the world’s first high-field MRI-guided linear accelerator (Unity MR-Linac). The center integrates six strategic multidisciplinary research programs: Brain, Infection & Immunity, Circulatory Health, Regenerative Medicine & Stem Cells, Personalized Cancer Care, and Child Health.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Application via Utrecht University Graduate School of Life Sciences
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Dutch (clinical patient care) & English (biomedical graduate research)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
€100 (Utrecht University application fee)
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
