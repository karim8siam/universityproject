College of Southern Nevada

The College of Southern Nevada (CSN) is the largest and most ethnically diverse public higher education institution in Nevada, operating three major campuses across Las Vegas, Henderson, and North Las Vegas, renowned for hospitality management, healthcare simulation, advanced manufacturing, and software engineering.

Academic overview & excellence

A key pillar of the Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE), CSN delivers over 180 associate degrees, university-transfer programs, and applied baccalaureates. The college houses world-class facilities, including the Engelstad School of Health Sciences clinical simulation complex, the Ralph & Betty Engelstad School of Hospitality kitchens and demonstration restaurants, and the Westside Education Center. CSN partners directly with global gaming resorts, the Las Vegas Medical District, and aerospace tech clusters to deliver high-impact workforce training.

Research admissions & criteria

CSN maintains an inclusive open-access admissions policy for high school graduates and adult learners. Selective-entry procedures govern limited-capacity programs (including Registered Nursing, Dental Hygiene, Surgical Technology, and Physical Therapist Assistant), requiring minimum prerequisite GPAs of 2.75 to 3.25/4.0 and aptitude exam scores (Kaplan or TEAS). International applicants must provide evaluated high school/college credentials, proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 61 or IELTS 6.0), and proof of financial sponsorship for F-1 visa issuance.
