University of Neuchâtel (Université de Neuchâtel)

Founded in 1838 as the Academy of Neuchâtel and established as a full university in 1909, the University of Neuchâtel (Université de Neuchâtel - UniNE) is a prestigious French-speaking public research university nestled along Lake Neuchâtel in western Switzerland. A founding member of the BeNeFri academic network with the Universities of Bern and Fribourg, UniNE is internationally celebrated for chemical ecology and insect-plant communication, hydrogeology and geothermal energy at the Centre for Hydrogeology and Geothermics (CHYN), Swiss and international migration research (nccr – on the move), and sports law at the International Centre for Sports Studies (CIES). Admitted doctoral researchers receive formal Swiss cantonal research contracts and salaried stipends.

UniNE Excellence Doctoral Grants & Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) Fellowships

Competitive Swiss doctoral research funding providing formal cantonal employment contracts, high standard of living, and full laboratory integration for outstanding international scholars.

UniNE PhD in Chemical Ecology & Plant-Insect Interactions

World-class doctoral research at UniNE's flagship Laboratory of Fundamental and Applied Research in Chemical Ecology, unraveling how plants emit volatile signals to recruit predatory insects for natural crop pest control.

UniNE Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (Joint BeNeFri Master with Fribourg & Bern)

Joint English-taught Master's degree organized by the Universities of Neuchâtel, Fribourg, and Bern, allowing students to combine advanced modules in distributed systems, cybersecurity, and data analytics across all three Swiss campuses.

UniNE PhD in Hydrogeology and Geothermics at the Centre for Hydrogeology (CHYN)

Doctoral research conducted within the world-renowned Centre for Hydrogeology and Geothermics (CHYN), investigating subsurface karst aquifer flow, geothermal heat extraction, and Alpine groundwater security under climate change.

Swiss National Centre of Competence in Research (nccr - on the move) Research Fellow

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the national research hub nccr - on the move, analyzing global human mobility, labor migration governance, and social inclusion policies in European and Swiss contexts.

UniNE Centre for Hydrogeology Alpine Groundwater Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer field traineeship in the Swiss Jura mountains and Valais Alps, performing groundwater tracer tests, spring discharge measurements, and isotopic water analysis.

UniNE Postdoctoral Researcher in Applied Microeconomics & Resource Policy

Postdoctoral appointment at the Faculty of Economics and Business (FSE), modeling energy transition policy incentives, consumer behavior, and carbon pricing mechanisms in European markets.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) from an accredited university in a relevant academic discipline (minimum grade B or 70% equivalent); joint BeNeFri programs require foundational computing or mathematics coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 90 to 120 ECTS credits) with superior academic standing; written endorsement and formal acceptance from a UniNE professor who agrees to act as thesis supervisor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught Master's and PhD programs; French B2/C1 for French-medium degree curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
A 4 to 6 page doctoral research project detailing state of the art, research methodology, equipment requirements, and prospective milestone timetable.
Application fee
CHF 100 non-refundable application fee for Swiss degree holders; CHF 150 for applicants with foreign university qualifications.
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree diplomas, official grade transcripts, curriculum vitae, research statement, and written agreement from a UniNE thesis director.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at the University of Neuchâtel are among the most accessible in Europe: standard semester tuition is only CHF 515 per semester for Swiss residents and CHF 790 per semester for international students. Doctoral candidates are employed as Swiss cantonal research assistants (assistants-doctorants), receiving a formal collective salary of CHF 47,000 to CHF 53,000 per year gross under Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) salary scales.
