Southern Illinois University Carbondale

SIU Carbondale · Carbondale, Illinois, United States

Southern Illinois University Carbondale (SIU Carbondale) is a flagship public doctoral research university situated among the rolling hills of the Shawnee National Forest in Carbondale, Illinois. Classified among R2: Doctoral Universities with High Research Activity, SIU is celebrated nationwide for its nationally ranked aviation and automotive technology programs, premier School of Law, innovative Fermentation Science Institute, and pioneering forestry and wildlife ecology research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SIU encompasses the College of Agricultural, Life, and Physical Sciences, the College of Engineering, Computing, Technology, and Mathematics, the College of Health and Human Sciences, the College of Liberal Arts, and the School of Law. Key research centers include the Fermentation Science Institute, the Transportation Education Center (TEC) at Southern Illinois Airport, and the Advanced Coal and Energy Research Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.75 for guaranteed admission) and college-prep coursework. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.70 GPA (3.0 for doctoral programs), official GRE scores (departmental requirement), academic statement of purpose, and three letters of reference.
