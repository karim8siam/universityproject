Australian Catholic University, Ballarat Campus: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Dating back to 1895 as the Aquinas Training College, ACU's Ballarat Campus features landscaped heritage gardens, historic brick architecture, and advanced simulation hospitals. Serving as a crucial regional training centre for Western Victoria, the campus provides immersive degrees in nursing, paramedicine, physiotherapy, and education with deep integration into Grampians Health and regional Victorian schools.
