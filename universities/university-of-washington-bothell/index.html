University of Washington Bothell

UW Bothell · Bothell, Washington, United States

The University of Washington Bothell (UW Bothell) is a thriving public research university founded in 1990, co-located on a 128-acre campus in the Puget Sound tech corridor of Bothell, Washington. Famous for its restored 58-acre North Creek Wetland—one of the largest floodplain wetland restorations in the Pacific Northwest—UW Bothell is celebrated for computer science and software engineering, interactive media, nursing, and biomedical innovation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UW Bothell spans five schools: the School of Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics (STEM), School of Interdisciplinary Arts & Sciences (IAS), School of Business, School of Nursing & Health Studies, and the School of Educational Studies. The university is deeply integrated with the Seattle tech ecosystem (Amazon, Microsoft, Nintendo).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic through the Coalition or Common Application. Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, provide a statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and meet English proficiency criteria (TOEFL 92+ or IELTS 7.0+).
