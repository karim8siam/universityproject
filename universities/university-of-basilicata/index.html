University of Basilicata: Geosciences, Archaeology & Agricultural Sciences Guide

Established in 1982, the University of Basilicata (Università degli Studi della Basilicata - UNIBAS) is a prominent public research university in southern Italy, operating campuses in Potenza and the UNESCO World Heritage city of Matera. UNIBAS is celebrated for seismic risk engineering, Mediterranean agricultural sciences, geomorphology, and digital heritage technologies.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ARDSU Basilicata Regional University Study Grant

Need- and merit-based regional government scholarship granting complete tuition exemption, cost-of-living allowances, and campus residence to domestic and international students.

Master of Science in Georesources and Seismic Hazard Mitigation

Two-year postgraduate engineering degree specializing in active seismic monitoring, slope stability modeling, earthquake engineering, and geohazard mitigation in the Apennines.

Italian Ministerial (MUR) Doctoral Fellowship in Agricultural and Environmental Sciences

Three-year PhD scholarship funded by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) investigating drought-resilient crops, olive grove microbiome ecology, and soil organic carbon.

Matera UNESCO Digital Heritage & Archaeometry Research Project

Interdisciplinary research collaboration utilizing lidar scanning, drone photogrammetry, and spectroscopic analysis to document rock-cut architectural settlements in the Sassi of Matera.

Basilicata Regional Geological Survey & Hydrological Monitoring Internship

Four-month practical training placement with the regional civil protection and environmental agency tracking aquifer levels, landslide sensors, and river basin discharge.

UNIBAS Postdoctoral Research Fellowship (Assegno di Ricerca) in Structural Dynamics

One-year renewable research fellowship (Assegno di Ricerca) developing base-isolation mechanisms and fibre-reinforced polymer retrofitting for historic masonry buildings.

University of Basilicata Merit Fee Exemption and Global Excellence Award

Institutional merit award granting comprehensive tuition relief and book grants to top-performing international and domestic students enrolling in STEM degrees at UNIBAS.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited three-year undergraduate degree (Laurea triennale or international bachelor's equivalent) in an aligned discipline with verified curricular prerequisites.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or 5-year single cycle degree) with verified research thesis; selection via national public competition (concorso pubblico).
Language proficiency
B2 level certification in Italian (CELI, CILS) for Italian-taught curricula; B2 English (IELTS 6.0+ or Cambridge FCE) for English-taught master's or PhD programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral concorso: detailed 3,000-word research proposal outlining research methodology, objectives, and alignment with UNIBAS doctoral curricula.
Application fee
EUR 30 to EUR 50 administrative concorso competition fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master degree certificate with Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) or CIMEA Statement of Comparability, official transcripts, CV, and identity document.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at UNIBAS is progressive and calculated based on the student's family economic indicator (ISEE parificato for international candidates). Annual tuition ranges from EUR 0 for eligible students (ISEE under EUR 22,000) to a maximum of EUR 1,800. Regional ARDSU Basilicata scholarships provide free accommodation, cafeteria vouchers, and annual grants up to EUR 7,000.
