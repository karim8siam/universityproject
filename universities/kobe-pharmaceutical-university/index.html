Kobe Pharmaceutical University (Kobeyakka)

Kobe Pharmaceutical University (Kobeyakka) is one of Japan’s oldest and most prestigious private pharmaceutical universities, founded in 1932 in Kobe and celebrated for medicinal chemistry, pharmacogenomics, natural drug discovery, and clinical hospital pharmacy.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1932 as the Kobe Women's College of Pharmacy and reorganized in 1994 as a coeducational pharmaceutical university, Kobeyakka has educated generations of Japan’s leading clinical pharmacists, pharmacological researchers, and drug discovery scientists. Situated on the scenic heights of Mount Rokko in Higashinada-ku, Kobe, the university features advanced laboratories for nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR), mass spectrometry, a medicinal botanical garden with over 1,200 medicinal plant species, and intimate research alliances with Kobe University Hospital and the RIKEN Center for Biosystems Dynamics Research.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Pharmaceutical Sciences (4-Year Doctoral Program) (Doctorate) — Advanced doctoral degree focusing on clinical pharmacy, drug interactions, molecular oncology targets, and pharmacological mechanisms.
  • PhD in Pharmaceutical Sciences (3-Year Drug Discovery Course) (Doctorate) — Research-intensive doctorate dedicated to synthetic chemical biology, structural biochemistry, and bio-organic drug design.
  • Faculty of Pharmacy 6-Year Clinical Degree (Yakugakubu) (Bachelor's) — Government-standardized six-year curriculum qualifying graduates for the National Examination for Pharmacists, with six months of intensive hospital and community pharmacy internships.
  • Master of Science in Pharmaceutical Sciences (2-Year Master's) (Master's) — Two-year graduate program in drug metabolism, analytical biochemistry, and pharmacological toxicology.
