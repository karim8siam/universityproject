Lund University (Lund, Sweden)

Founded in 1666 in Lund, Scania, Lund University is one of northern Europe's oldest, largest, and most prestigious universities, consistently ranked among the world's top 80 to 100 institutions. Lund is an international science powerhouse, housing the world-leading MAX IV synchrotron radiation facility and the European Spallation Source (ESS). With discoveries including medical ultrasound, Bluetooth technology, and attosecond physics (Prof. Anne L'Huillier, Nobel Prize in Physics 2023), Lund leads the world in nanotechnology, medicine, and engineering. Lund University Global Scholarship Programme (LUGS)

Lund University's premier merit-based scholarship program for citizens of non-EU/EEA countries, awarding tuition fee remissions to top international scholars.

Lund University Salaried Doctoral Positions (Doktorandanställning)

Four-year fully salaried doctoral research employment contracts across physics, materials science, and computing faculties, conducting frontier research with comprehensive social benefits.

Lund Master of Science in Wireless Communication

World-renowned two-year master's program taught in close partnership with Ericsson and Sony, exploring 5G/6G wireless networks, MIMO signal processing, and antenna systems.

Lund Master of Science in Machine Learning, Systems and Control

Interdisciplinary master's degree combining modern machine learning, reinforcement learning, convex optimization, and autonomous control theory.

MAX IV Laboratory & European Spallation Source (ESS) Collaborative Placements

Research placements at the world's brightest X-ray synchrotron facility (MAX IV) and next-generation neutron spallation source (ESS), investigating novel quantum materials and biomolecules.

Lund LTH Engineering Summer Research & Ideon Science Park Placements

Structured research and industrial internships connecting student engineers with leading research chairs at LTH and innovative high-tech ventures at Ideon Science Park.

Lund University Teaching Assistantships & Laboratory Mentoring

Pedagogical teaching appointments enabling PhD researchers to teach undergraduate laboratory classes and supervise project assignments, extending fully paid doctoral employment.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) equivalent to a Swedish Kandidatexamen with a major relevant to the applied program.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS with at least 60 ECTS at advanced level) with an independent thesis and proven research competence.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: English 6 / English B (Academic IELTS 6.5 overall, no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 3-5 page research project proposal and work plan submitted for advertised doctoral employment positions.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA students submitting master's applications via Universityadmissions.se. Doctoral studies are completely tuition-free for all nationalities.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, degree certificates, detailed syllabus descriptions, CV, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral education is 100% free of tuition fees for all nationalities. Doctoral researchers are employed as university staff (Doktorandanställning) under Swedish collective union agreements, receiving starting gross monthly salaries of SEK 31,500 to SEK 35,000/month with comprehensive Swedish healthcare and pension benefits.
