Duke University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Duke University, situated in Durham, North Carolina within the Research Triangle, is one of the world's premier private research universities. World-renowned for the Pratt School of Engineering, Duke University School of Medicine, Nicholas School of the Environment, and Duke Quantum Center, the university is celebrated for transformative discoveries in G protein-coupled receptors, metamaterials, and interpretable machine learning.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Metamaterials & Quantum)

Doctoral research program within the Pratt School of Engineering, investigating electromagnetic metamaterials, invisibility cloaks, reconfigurable microwave surfaces, and trapped-ion quantum computing.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Computer Science (Interpretable AI & Theory)

PhD program led by world-renowned computer scientists, pioneering interpretable and transparent machine learning algorithms, discrete optimization, computational geometry, and quantum algorithms.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Biochemistry & Cellular Receptor Signaling

Doctoral research program within Duke School of Medicine, training under Nobel Laureate Robert Lefkowitz's group to investigate G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) dynamics, beta-arrestin signaling, and drug discovery.

Master of Environmental Management (MEM) - Nicholas School of the Environment

Two-year professional master's degree exploring energy systems, coastal ocean policy, carbon offsets, and environmental economics at Duke's world-renowned Nicholas School.

Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering (Biomaterials & Neural Engineering)

Two-year Master of Science program focusing on neural prosthetics, tissue engineering, ultrasound imaging transducers, and cardiovascular fluid dynamics.

Duke Cancer Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship in Immuno-Oncology

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the Duke Cancer Institute, investigating neoantigen vaccine discovery, T-cell exhaustion reversal, and oncolytic virotherapy.

Duke Quantum Center (DQC) Summer Graduate Research Internship

Twelve-week research placement at the Duke Quantum Center, testing quantum gate fidelity, ion-trap microwave control, and fault-tolerant quantum error-correcting codes.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year US bachelor's degree or international equivalent with strong academic credentials (typically GPA 3.5+ on a 4.0 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant scientific or engineering discipline; exceptional academic performance, prior research output, and alignment with Duke graduate faculty.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: TOEFL iBT minimum 90 or IELTS Academic overall band score 7.0 or higher.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of Purpose (1-2 pages) describing research interests, past technical achievements, and prospective Duke faculty mentors.
Application fee
$95 non-refundable application fee (fee waivers available for eligible applicants upon request).
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, statement of purpose, CV, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, and English language test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

All admitted full-time doctoral (PhD) students in good standing at Duke University receive full financial support, consisting of a 100% tuition waiver (worth over $64,000/year), full individual health insurance coverage and health fees, and a guaranteed living stipend of $43,000 to $48,000 per year for five continuous years through research and teaching fellowships.
