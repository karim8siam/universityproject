Laramie County Community College

LCCC Wyoming · Cheyenne, Wyoming, United States

Laramie County Community College is a comprehensive public community college in Cheyenne, Wyoming. Renowned for its equine sciences, wind energy technology, nursing, cybersecurity, and applied bachelor's degrees, LCCC serves southeastern Wyoming and the Front Range.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

LCCC offers over 80 certificate, associate, and applied bachelor's (BAS) degree options. It features the modern Health Sciences complex, the state-of-the-art Ludden Speech Clinic, and an expansive indoor equine arena with specialized veterinary tech facilities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Open-door admission policy requires high school diploma or GED equivalent. Bachelor of Applied Science and clinical healthcare programs require specialized program applications, background checks, and prerequisite grade thresholds.
