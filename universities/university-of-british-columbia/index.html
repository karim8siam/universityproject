The University of British Columbia (UBC)

Established in 1908 in Vancouver, The University of British Columbia (UBC) is consistently ranked among the world's top 40 public research universities. UBC attracts over $770 million in annual research funding across quantum matter, computer science, biomedical technology, and environmental engineering. Explore verified graduate degrees, four-year doctoral funding packages, salaried teaching assistantships, and world-renowned faculty profiles.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UBC Four Year Doctoral Fellowships (4YF)

UBC's prestigious Four Year Doctoral Fellowship (4YF) provides guaranteed financial support of at least CAD $18,200 per year plus tuition for outstanding domestic and international PhD students across all disciplines.

UBC PhD in Computer Science

Four to five-year doctoral research program in UBC's world-renowned Department of Computer Science, offering cutting-edge labs in artificial intelligence, computer graphics, systems, robotics, and human-computer interaction.

UBC Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year thesis-based Master of Science degree providing foundational and advanced training in algorithms, machine learning, systems architecture, and software engineering with guaranteed graduate student funding.

UBC Master of Data Science (MDS)

Intensive 10-month professional Master's program designed to teach data wrangling, machine learning, statistical modeling, data visualization, and cloud-scale data engineering through hands-on industry capstones.

UBC Advanced Materials and Process Engineering Laboratory (AMPEL) Placements

Interdisciplinary research placements inside UBC AMPEL, bringing together engineers, physicists, and chemists to develop state-of-the-art semiconductor devices, quantum biomaterials, and clean-energy storage materials.

UBC Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Awards (WLIURA)

Competitive 16-week summer research internships providing international and domestic students with direct laboratory apprenticeships under UBC faculty mentors across biological sciences, environment, and engineering.

UBC Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA I / GTA II)

Official academic teaching positions providing substantial financial support, instruction leadership training, and classroom teaching experience across undergraduate lecture courses, tutorials, and laboratory sections.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree with minimum B+ average (typically 76% at UBC or 3.3/4.0 GPA) in relevant academic disciplines.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree with clear evidence of research ability, academic publications, or exceptional undergraduate honours degree (first-class average A- / 80%+).
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5, or TOEFL iBT 100 overall with minimum 22 in each subscore.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page research statement detailing research objectives, methodology, and identified UBC faculty supervisors.
Application fee
CAD $168.25 application fee for international applicants; CAD $114.00 for Canadian citizens and permanent residents.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 3 academic letters of reference, curriculum vitae, and statement of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for research-based Master's and PhD programs is heavily subsidized. International research Master's students pay approximately CAD $9,500 per year, and PhD students receive the UBC Four Year Doctoral Fellowship (4YF) or International Tuition Award, effectively offsetting international tuition fees down to domestic rates (CAD $5,500/yr).
