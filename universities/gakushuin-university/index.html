Gakushuin University (学習院大学)

Tracing its roots back to 1847 as an educational institution for the Kyoto imperial court and re-established in Tokyo in 1877 as the Peers' School, Gakushuin University is one of Japan's most historic and prestigious private universities. Situated in Mejiro in central Tokyo, Gakushuin is celebrated for its highly selective Faculty of Science (world-class research in theoretical physics, statistical mechanics, and organic synthetic chemistry), the Faculty of International Social Sciences, and the Research Institute for Oriental Cultures.

Doctoral research conducted within the Department of Physics at the Mejiro campus, featuring world-leading investigations in mathematical physics, non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, quantum spin systems, and superconductivity.

Four-year chemistry doctorate investigating novel chiral organocatalysts, transition-metal catalyzed C-H bond activation, and total synthesis of bioactive natural products.

Two-year English-taught Master's program in Tokyo combining international economics, cross-border corporate management, international law, and political science for careers in multinational organizations.

Gakushuin Quantum Spin Liquids & Frustrated Magnetism Postdoctoral Fellowship

Postdoctoral research appointment investigating quantum spin liquid states, exotic topological order, and fractional excitations in geometrically frustrated magnetic insulators.

Eight-week summer research traineeship at the Research Institute for Oriental Cultures in Tokyo, participating in the digital preservation, translation, and cataloging of rare pre-modern Japanese and East Asian manuscripts.

Gakushuin Project Assistant Professor in Functional Macromolecular Chemistry

Full-time faculty role conducting independent research and laboratory teaching in synthetic macromolecular chemistry, stimuli-responsive polymers, and organic luminescent materials.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of a 16-year school education curriculum culminating in a recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA or equivalent grade B+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree or professional degree; submission of Master's thesis and passing marks in oral defense and doctoral screening.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 85 for English-medium research programs; Japanese JLPT N1 for Japanese-medium graduate divisions.
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research proposal detailing proposed doctoral research topic, theoretical framework, research methodology, and anticipated academic contributions.
Application fee
¥35,000 non-refundable screening fee submitted via the Gakushuin online admissions portal.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, complete official academic transcripts, research proposal, 2 letters of recommendation, and English proficiency test report.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for graduate programs at Gakushuin are approximately ¥850,000 to ¥1,100,000 per year. Admitted international students benefit from Gakushuin University Special International Student Scholarships covering 30% to 100% of tuition, and MEXT University Recommendation Scholarship recipients receive 100% full tuition waivers, round-trip international airfare, and a monthly tax-free living stipend of ¥145,000 per month.
