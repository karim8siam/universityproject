HAN University of Applied Sciences: Automotive, Engineering & NL Scholarships

HAN University of Applied Sciences (Hogeschool van Arnhem en Nijmegen) is one of the largest and most respected applied science universities in the Netherlands, renowned for its international engineering expertise, Automotive Centre of Expertise, renewable energy systems, and life sciences.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) at HAN

Competitive scholarship financed by the Dutch Ministry of Education and HAN for exceptional non-EEA students enrolling in English-taught master's programs.

Master of Science in Automotive Systems & Clean Mobility (Joint Program)

Internationally renowned master's program focusing on electric powertrains, autonomous vehicle perception, battery management systems, and vehicle dynamics.

Collaborative Applied Doctoral Candidacy in Smart Grids with Radboud University

Four-year salaried doctoral research project conducted between HAN Centre of Expertise Sustainable Energy and Radboud University investigating hydrogen storage and local microgrid balancing.

Automotive Centre of Expertise (ACE) Autonomous Driving Research Assistantship

Applied development position working with Dutch automotive consortia on cooperative automated shuttles and camera-LiDAR fusion algorithms.

DAF Trucks & VDL Industrial Graduation Internship

Six-month master's graduation internship based at major Dutch vehicle manufacturers researching heavy electric truck thermal management or hydrogen combustion.

Centre of Expertise Sustainable Energy Postdoctoral Applied Fellowship

Two-year applied research fellowship leading collaborative industrial projects on industrial electrification, seasonal heat storage, and regional energy hubs.

Master of Science in Engineering Systems & Sustainable Energy Track

Advanced applied master's examining PV integration, wind turbine dynamics, power electronics converters, and grid compliance standards.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Science in Engineering, Automotive, Computing, or related discipline from a recognized institution with sound academic performance.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct doctoral degrees in the Netherlands are conferred via university partnerships (e.g., Radboud University or TU Eindhoven); professional professorships recruit doctoral researchers for joint projects.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each subscore) or TOEFL iBT 90+.
Research statement / Proposal
For master's entry: detailed motivation letter, CV, and technical background summary; for research contracts: technical project proposal.
Application fee
€0 (applications submitted via Studielink and HAN OSIRIS portal).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, passport copy, CV, proof of English, and motivation statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition ranges from €9,500 to €11,800 per year. Dutch Ministry NL Scholarships provide €5,000 awards to talented non-EEA applicants.
