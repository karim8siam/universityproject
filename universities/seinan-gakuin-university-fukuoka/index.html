Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1916 by American Baptist missionary C.K. Dozier in Nishijin, Sawara-ku, Fukuoka, Seinan Gakuin is known as 'Seinan' throughout western Japan. The university comprises seven faculties: Humanities, Intercultural Studies, Commerce, Economics, Law, Human Sciences, and International Studies. Seinan maintains one of Japan's oldest and most respected international study abroad exchange networks, connecting Kyushu to over 100 partner universities globally.

Research admissions & international criteria

International applicants apply through the Special Entrance Examination for International Students, submitting EJU examination scores, an essay in Japanese, and attending an oral interview. Graduate school candidates require a recognized 16-year bachelor's degree, a well-developed research plan in Japanese (or English for International Studies), academic transcripts, and interview. JLPT N1 or N2 is required for Japanese-language programs.
