Bridgewater State University

Bridgewater State University (BSU) is the largest comprehensive state university in Massachusetts outside the UMass system, celebrated as the historic birthplace of American teacher education and home to premier aviation science and photonics programs.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1840 by education reformer Horace Mann as Bridgewater Normal School, Bridgewater State University is one of the oldest continuously operating public institutions of higher education in the United States. Today, BSU educates over 9,600 undergraduate and graduate students across its 278-acre campus in southeastern Massachusetts. The university features the state-of-the-art Bartlett College of Science and Mathematics, the world-class BSU Aviation Training Center at New Bedford Regional Airport, and the Cyber Range for defensive cybersecurity operations.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Aviation Science (Flight Training Track) (Bachelor's) — AABI-accredited commercial pilot degree featuring flight training on BSU's modern fleet of Cirrus SR20 and Piper Archer aircraft at New Bedford.
  • Bachelor of Science in Photonics and Optical Engineering (Bachelor's) — Specialized STEM degree offering cleanroom micro-fabrication, laser physics, fiber optics, and semiconductor fabrication.
  • Master of Education in Educational Leadership (MEd) (Master's) — Distinguished graduate degree preparing school principals, curriculum directors, and educational policymakers.
  • Master of Science in Computer Science: Cybersecurity Concentration (Master's) — Hands-on graduate computing program utilizing the BSU Cyber Range for simulated network war-games, malware analysis, and cloud defense.
