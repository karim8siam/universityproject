Southern Cross University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Southern Cross University (SCU), situated across the Northern Rivers of New South Wales (Lismore, Coffs Harbour) and the Gold Coast in Queensland, is a progressive Australian public university. Ranked in the top tier of Australian research institutions for oceanography, plant genetics, and geochemistry, SCU houses the National Marine Science Centre (NMSC) and pioneered the innovative six-week SCU Term Model.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Coastal Geochemistry and Ocean Acidification

Three-and-a-half year doctoral scholarship at the National Marine Science Centre (NMSC) measuring nitrous oxide and methane fluxes from subtropical estuaries and coral reef bleaching resilience.

Master of Science in Forest Science and Agroforestry Ecosystems

Two-year postgraduate degree delivered through the SCU Term Model, studying subtropical forest silviculture, bushfire ecology, timber plantation genetics, and carbon farming.

Southern Cross University Vice Chancellor's Academic Excellence Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship designed to reward high-achieving international applicants enrolling in undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs at SCU Gold Coast, Lismore, or Coffs Harbour.

Southern Cross Plant Science Plant Genomics & Medicinal Crop Chemistry Fellowship

Applied research post at Southern Cross Plant Science sequencing tea tree, hemp, and macadamia genomes, identifying biosynthetic gene clusters for novel pharmaceutical and agricultural compounds.

Great Barrier Reef Coral Larval Restoration & Aquaculture Practicum

Four-month practical marine science internship deploying coral larval reseeding robots and larval nurseries to rehabilitate degraded reef patches along the Great Barrier Reef.

GNIBI College of Indigenous Australian Peoples Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral position at Gnibi College examining Bundjalung cultural revitalisation, Indigenous land care practices, and cultural curriculum frameworks in regional Australian universities.

Master of Information Technology (Applied Artificial Intelligence & Remote Sensing Analytics)

Two-year master's program taught in focused 6-week terms, mastering machine learning algorithms, deep learning for satellite Earth observation, and big data management for environmental monitoring.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
An Australian bachelor's degree or recognized overseas equivalent with a minimum weighted average mark (WAM) of 60% or relevant professional standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1), or Master by Research with distinction; confirmed SCU faculty research supervisor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no individual band score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing 21, speaking 18).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2,000-word research proposal specifying hypotheses, theoretical framework, research design, project budget, and ethics clearances.
Application fee
AUD $0 online direct application fee for international candidates.
Required documentation
Certified degree transcripts, award certificates, CV, 2 academic referee reports, and supervisor pre-agreement documentation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition at Southern Cross University ranges between AUD $27,000 and $33,000 per year. Fully funded doctoral candidates receive Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) or SCU Postgraduate Research Scholarships providing a tax-free annual stipend of AUD $33,500, full tuition offset waivers, and overseas student health cover (OSHC).
