SEU Carolina Regional Campus

SEU Carolina · Fort Mill, South Carolina, United States

SEU Carolina is an accredited regional extension site of Southeastern University (SEU), located in Fort Mill, South Carolina, serving the greater Charlotte metropolitan area. Known for ministry leadership, business administration, humanitarian relief, and media communications.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SEU Carolina integrates university degree coursework with hands-on ministry and corporate practicums in the vibrant Charlotte regional corridor. It features modern lecture halls, audio-visual broadcast studios, and student collaborative leadership incubators.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.0 recommended) with test-optional application options. Applicants submit official transcripts, a Christian character essay, and one personal or pastoral recommendation.
