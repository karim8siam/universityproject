Saxion Deventer: Applied Computer Science, Urban Design & Engineering Guide

Saxion University of Applied Sciences is one of the largest universities of applied sciences in the Netherlands, educating over 27,000 students. Its picturesque Deventer campus—located within walking distance of the historic Hanseatic city centre along the River IJssel—specializes in high-demand international programs in Information Technology, Software Engineering, Urban and Regional Planning, Environmental Technology, and International Business. Saxion's living technology philosophy embeds students directly into real regional engineering and municipal innovation challenges.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree in engineering, urban technology, computer science, or environmental management; academic pre-assessment evaluating technical prerequisites.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; applied university focusing on professional Bachelor's and Master's degrees with applied research professorships (lectoraten).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.0 or 5.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 80 for bachelor's programs; IELTS 6.5 overall for master's programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of motivation explaining choice of program, technical enthusiasm, and alignment with living technology themes.
Application fee
€0 application fee for direct international applications via Studielink.
Required documentation
Secondary school graduation diploma, complete official transcripts, proof of English proficiency, motivation letter, curriculum vitae, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per academic year. Non-EEA international tuition ranges from €8,500 to €11,500 per year. Saxion awards numerous prestigious scholarships including the Holland Scholarship / NL Scholarship (€5,000) and Saxion Living Technology Scholarships.
