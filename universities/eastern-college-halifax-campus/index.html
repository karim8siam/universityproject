Institution Overview & Profile

Eastern College Halifax is a premier post-secondary career college in Atlantic Canada offering modular, hands-on diploma programs in healthcare technology, digital network support, cyber security, legal administration, and supply chain management.

Institution TypePrivate Career College
Main CampusMumford Road Campus, Halifax, Nova Scotia
AccreditationNova Scotia Department of Advanced Education / National Association of Career Colleges (NACC)
Admissions RouteRolling monthly start dates with entrance assessment
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 6.0 overall or Eastern College English Placement Test)
Financial Aid & GrantsCanada-Nova Scotia Integrated Student Loans & Eastern College Merit Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Modular half-day schedule enabling concurrent work and study commitments
  • Accredited healthcare diplomas with clinical field placements across the Nova Scotia Health Authority
  • Industry-certified technology curricula mapped to CompTIA, Cisco, and Microsoft credentials
  • Dedicated career services offering lifelong employment search support across Atlantic Canada
