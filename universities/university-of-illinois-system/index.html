University of Illinois System

UI System · Urbana-Champaign, Illinois, United States

The University of Illinois System is a world-class public university system comprising three premier universities: the flagship University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC), and the University of Illinois Springfield (UIS). Conducting more than $1 billion in annual sponsored research, the UI System leads the globe in supercomputing at NCSA, quantum computing, autonomous robotics, medicine, and public policy.

Conducting more than $1 billion in annual sponsored research, the UI System leads the globe in supercomputing at NCSA, quantum computing, autonomous robotics, medicine, and public policy.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through constituent campuses (UIUC, UIC, UIS). Holistic review of secondary school course rigor, unweighted GPA, standardized exams (test-optional), and personal essays. TOEFL iBT 80+ (103+ UIUC engineering) or IELTS 6.5+ (7.5+ UIUC). Early action priority deadline November 1, 2026; Regular decision deadline January 5, 2027.
