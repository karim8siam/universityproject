Aberystwyth University

Established in 1872 on the west coast of Wales, Aberystwyth University is a historic UK research institution recognized for pioneer work in Computer Science, Intelligent Robotics, Solar-Terrestrial Physics, Environmental Sciences (IBERS), and Materials Physics. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Aberystwyth University offers doctoral (PhD, 3 years full-time) and taught/research master's (MSc, MRes, MPhil) degrees across Computer Science, Physics, Earth & Life Sciences, and Humanities.

AberDoc PhD Scholarships

Prestigious competitive doctoral awards covering full UK tuition, an annual living stipend of £20,780/yr, and a £1,000/yr research training support grant across all doctoral research departments.

MSc Data Science (Conversion Route)

One-year full-time master's degree open to graduates from any quantitative or STEM background covering machine learning, data engineering, statistical modeling, and applied AI.

PhD & MPhil in Solar System Physics & Space Weather

Funded doctoral research in the Solar System Physics Group collaborating with NASA, ESA, and UK Space Agency on solar coronal dynamics, solar wind, and planetary ionospheres.

MSc Environmental Change, Impact & Adaptation

Specialist master's program taught within the Institute of Biological, Environmental and Rural Sciences (IBERS) focusing on climate change mitigation, ecological modeling, and carbon accounting.

Department of Physics Summer Research Placements

6-to-8 week funded undergraduate and postgraduate research internships working directly with academic researchers on semiconductor physics, diamond films, or solar observation processing.

Postgraduate Teaching Assistantship (PGTA) & Demonstrating

Hourly paid teaching, laboratory demonstrating, and marking opportunities (£17.00–£23.50/hr) for registered postgraduate researchers across STEM departments.

PhD & MPhil in Computer Science

3-year PhD and 1-year MPhil research degrees with active clusters in autonomous robotics, fuzzy reasoning, 3D vision, and bioinformatics.

Global Citizens & Housing Award

Automatic international tuition discounts of £3,000–£3,800/year for Master's and PhD candidates, combinable with complimentary university single-room housing for at least one year.

Admissions and eligibility requirements

Doctoral (PhD) entry threshold
An upper second class honours degree (UK 2:1) or international equivalent, or a recognised Master's degree in a relevant discipline. Applicants must present a coherent research proposal (1,500–2,000 words).
Taught Master's (MSc)
Minimum UK 2:2 honours degree (or international equivalent with minimum 55% / 2.8 GPA) in a relevant subject area or quantitative background.
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (with minimum 6.0 in reading, writing, listening, and speaking) for STEM research and taught degrees; 7.0 for select humanities.
Application fee
£0. Direct applications through the official Aberystwyth Postgraduate Admissions Portal are free of charge.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by Aberystwyth University for research or taught postgraduate programs.
Supervisor pre-contact
Strongly recommended for PhD/MPhil candidates prior to formal proposal submission.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship schemes

For the 2026/2027 academic session, Aberystwyth University sets full-time international tuition at £20,270 for Humanities disciplines and £22,870 for Science/Laboratory disciplines. With the automatic Global Citizens Scholarship, international tuition is discounted to £17,270 (Humanities) and £19,070 (Sciences).

Qualifying full-time international students receive the International Accommodation Award, providing complimentary university single-room housing for at least one full academic year (worth £6,000+). For doctoral researchers, the university awards competitive AberDoc PhD Scholarships providing full UK fee waivers and £20,780/yr maintenance.
