Aberystwyth University

Established in 1872 on the west coast of Wales, Aberystwyth University is a historic UK research institution recognized for pioneer work in Computer Science, Intelligent Robotics, Vision Graphics, International Politics, and Biological Sciences. Review doctoral programs, verified funding schemes, and faculty research profiles before beginning an application.

Research degrees and postgraduate study

Aberystwyth University offers doctoral (PhD, 3 years full-time) and research master's (MPhil, 1 year full-time; MRes) degrees across three main academic faculties: the Faculty of Business and Physical Sciences, the Faculty of Earth and Life Sciences, and the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences. The Department of Computer Science hosts specialist research clusters in Intelligent Robotics, Advanced Reasoning, Vision Graphics & Visualisation, and Bioinformatics & Computational Biology.

PhD & MPhil in Computer Science

3-year PhD and 1-year MPhil research degrees with active projects in autonomous robotics, fuzzy reasoning, 3D shape analysis, and computational biology. Applications open year-round with supervisor matching.

Global Citizens & Housing Award

Automatic international tuition discounts of £3,000–£3,800/year for Master's and PhD candidates, combinable with the International Accommodation Award granting free university housing for at least one year.

AberDoc PhD Scholarships

Prestigious multi-year doctoral scholarships covering UK-rate tuition fees, an annual living stipend (historically £20,780+), and a £1,000/year research travel grant. Competition closes annually in January.

Admissions and eligibility requirements

Doctoral (PhD) entry threshold
An upper second class honours degree (UK 2:1) or international equivalent, or a recognised Master's degree in a relevant discipline. Applicants must present a coherent research proposal (1,500–2,000 words).
English language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (with minimum 6.0 in reading, writing, listening, and speaking) for computer science and STEM research; 7.0 overall for certain humanities disciplines.
Application fee
£0. Direct applications through the official Aberystwyth Postgraduate Admissions Portal are free of charge.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by Aberystwyth University for research degrees.
Supervisor pre-contact
Strongly encouraged. Prospective research students should identify a compatible supervisor in the relevant department prior to submitting a formal doctoral proposal.
References and documentation
Two academic reference letters, certified degree certificates and transcripts, CV, and a passport copy for international applicant CAS issuance.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship schemes

For the 2026/2027 and 2027/2028 academic sessions, Aberystwyth University sets full-time international research tuition at £20,270 for Humanities disciplines and £22,870 for Laboratory/Science disciplines. With the automatic Global Citizens Scholarship, international research tuition is reduced to £17,270 (Humanities) and £19,070 (Sciences).

In addition, qualifying international students receive the International Accommodation Award, providing complimentary university single-room housing for at least one full academic year (equivalent to a £6,000+ living benefit). For doctoral candidates, the competitive AberDoc PhD Scholarship and Departmental Doctoral Overseas Scholarships provide UK-rate fee coverage and living maintenance.

Research groups and faculty profiles

The Department of Computer Science conducts research across four core groups: Intelligent Robotics, Vision Graphics & Visualisation, Bioinformatics & Computational Biology, and Advanced Reasoning. Below are three verified faculty members:

Prof Bernard Tiddeman

Director of the Vision, Graphics and Visualisation Group; specialist in 3D shape analysis, facial analysis, computer vision, and machine learning.

Prof Qiang Shen

FREng, FLSW; Chair of UK REF 2029 Computer Science Subpanel; internationally recognized pioneer in fuzzy systems, approximate reasoning, and computational intelligence.

Dr Hannah Dee

Senior Lecturer; expert in applied computer vision, shadow reasoning, robot navigation, and computing education diversity; founder of BCSWomen Lovelace Colloquium.

Guidance for international research applicants

  1. Browse the department's research clusters to find an area matching your background.
  2. Draft a structured research proposal outlining problem statement, literature context, methodology, and timeline.
  3. Contact the academic staff member informally with your CV and draft proposal before submitting your formal application.
  4. Apply via the Aberystwyth Postgraduate Admissions Portal; application is free.
  5. If seeking AberDoc or UKRI studentship funding, ensure complete submission before the annual January funding deadlines.
