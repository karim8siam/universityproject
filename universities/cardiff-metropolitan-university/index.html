Cardiff Metropolitan University: Art & Design, Sports Science & Postgraduate Research

Cardiff Metropolitan University (Prifysgol Fetropolitan Caerdydd) is an established Welsh university celebrated for world-class sports science and high performance training, the internationally acclaimed Cardiff School of Art & Design (CSAD), digital technologies, food science, and applied health research.

Cardiff Met Vice-Chancellor's International Postgraduate Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship supporting international students enrolling in full-time taught master's degrees at Cardiff Met.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Sports Biomechanics & Human Performance

Doctoral research conducted inside the National Indoor Athletics Centre and biomechanics laboratories analyzing elite athlete mechanics and injury epidemiology.

Master of Arts (MA) in Design & Material Futures at Cardiff School of Art & Design

Internationally recognized design program investigating circular economy materials, bio-design, digital fabrication, and sustainable product ecosystems.

Food Industry Centre (FIC) Agri-Food Safety & Technology Research Assistantship

Applied research post supporting the Welsh food and drink sector on micro-filtration, novel protein formulation, and microbial spoilage prevention.

Sport Wales High Performance Athlete Support Practicum

Hands-on internship working alongside Sport Wales coaches and elite Olympic athletes delivering strength and conditioning regimens and GPS telemetry.

Cardiff School of Technologies Postdoctoral Fellowship in Cyber Security & Smart Cities

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship researching edge sensor encryption, zero-trust industrial network security, and AI-driven automated threat response.

Master of Science (MSc) in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

Postgraduate master's degree exploring statistical data mining, machine learning algorithms, database warehousing, and predictive modeling for industry.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK honours degree (minimum 2:2, with 2:1 preferred for competitive programs) or international equivalent in an appropriate field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in a relevant discipline (or a first/upper-second class bachelor's degree with significant research experience).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in all bands) for most postgraduate programs; 6.5 overall for research degrees and sports sciences.
Research statement / Proposal
For MPhil/PhD: 1,500–2,500 word research proposal outlining background, research questions, methodology, and ethical implications.
Application fee
£0 (free direct online application via Cardiff Met portal).
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, two academic reference letters, personal statement, and portfolio (for Art & Design courses).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition typically ranges from £15,000 to £17,500 per year. Cardiff Met Vice Chancellor Scholarships and Commonwealth / Global Wales Scholarships provide fee discounts between £1,500 and £5,000 for talented international postgraduates.
