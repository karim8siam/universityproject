University of London

The University of London is an iconic federal research university established by royal charter in 1836. Comprising 17 world-renowned member institutions (including UCL, King's College London, LSE, and Queen Mary) alongside its central University of London Worldwide distance education system, it is one of the oldest, largest, and most prestigious federal collegiate universities globally.

Official institutional website ↗Location: London, England, United Kingdom

Institutional Overview

The University of London Worldwide delivers internationally accredited degrees across law (LL.B, LL.M), computer science, economics, finance, humanities, and public health in collaboration with academic direction from member colleges. Its historic central headquarters at Senate House in Bloomsbury houses one of Europe's greatest humanistic and social science research libraries.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions requirements vary by qualification. Undergraduate applicants typically require GCE A-Levels (e.g., AAB-BBB) or international equivalents (IB 32-34 points). Postgraduate programs require a UK second-class upper honours degree (2:1) or international equivalent, with IELTS Academic 6.5-7.0. Global scholarships and Commonwealth distance learning awards are offered.
