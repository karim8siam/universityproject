Blue Ridge Community College

Gain hands-on skills in mechatronics engineering, healthcare sciences, and craft brewing at Blue Ridge Community College in North Carolina.

Official institution website ↗Independent Academic Atlas

Institution Overview & Profile

Blue Ridge Community College, situated in the Blue Ridge Mountains of western North Carolina with campuses in Henderson and Transylvania counties, is a premier two-year public institution renowned for its cutting-edge mechatronics, advanced manufacturing, brewing and distillation sciences, and comprehensive university transfer degrees.

Institution TypePublic Comprehensive Community College (North Carolina Community College System)
Main CampusHenderson County Campus (Flat Rock) & Transylvania County Campus (Brevard)
AccreditationSouthern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC)
Admissions RouteOpen-door admissions policy via CFNC (College Foundation of North Carolina)
Language of InstructionEnglish (TOEFL iBT 61, IELTS 5.5, or placement testing for international applicants)
Financial Aid & GrantsBlue Ridge Educational Foundation Scholarships & North Carolina Longleaf Commitment Grants

Key Academic Strengths

  • Southeastern Advanced Molding and Manufacturing Technology Centre (SAMMTC)
  • Apprenticeship Blue Ridge: tuition-free company-paid youth and adult technical apprenticeships
  • Craft Beverage Fermentation and Distillation program serving the regional brewing economy
  • Seamless Comprehensive Articulation Agreement (CAA) transferring credits to all 16 UNC system universities

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Associate Degree (AAS)

Associate in Applied Science in Mechatronics Engineering Technology

Two-year technical degree covering industrial robotics, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), electrical motors, and automated manufacturing systems.

Associate Degree (AS)

Associate in Science (University Transfer)

Sixty-credit transfer pathway directly articulating into engineering, computer science, and biological science bachelor's degrees across the UNC system.

Associate Degree (AAS)

Brewing, Distillation and Fermentation (AAS)

Hands-on instruction in yeast microbiology, brewhouse engineering, sensory evaluation, and quality assurance chemistry.

Verified Academic Opportunities (7)

Scholarship

Blue Ridge Educational Foundation Academic Scholarship

Endowed scholarship awarding full and partial tuition assistance to curriculum students pursuing engineering, technology, or health sciences.

Funding: Up to $2,500 per academic year covering tuition, required books, and laboratory fees Deadline: April 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Apprenticeship Blue Ridge Salaried Technical Contract

Four-year registered apprenticeship pairing students with regional manufacturing corporations with full tuition coverage and paid wages.

Funding: 100% covered college tuition plus starting hourly wage of $15.00–$18.00/hour Deadline: March 31, 2027
Bachelor's

Associate in Science to UNC Engineering Articulation Pathway

Two-year degree program providing guaranteed junior-year transfer into engineering faculties at NC State University or UNC Charlotte.

Funding: Eligible for federal Pell Grants and NC Community College scholarships Deadline: July 15, 2027
Internship

Craft Brewing & Distillation Laboratory Practicum

Paid semester internship embedded within regional commercial craft breweries (e.g., Sierra Nevada, Oskar Blues) in western North Carolina.

Funding: $16.00/hour seasonal wage plus commercial sensory evaluation certification Deadline: April 30, 2027
Grant

North Carolina Longleaf Commitment Tuition Grant

State legislative grant covering 100% of tuition and mandatory fees for eligible graduating high school seniors attending Blue Ridge CC.

Funding: Full tuition and fee coverage for up to two consecutive academic years Deadline: August 1, 2027
Bachelor's

Associate Degree Nursing (ADN) Healthcare Admission

Five-semester registered nursing program preparing students for the NCLEX-RN licensing exam with clinical rotations in Pardee and AdventHealth.

Funding: Eligible for North Carolina Forgivable Education Loans for Service (FELS) Deadline: January 31, 2027 (Competitive admission)
Bachelor's

Automotive Systems Technology Master Technician Certificate

ASE-certified technical training in electric vehicle (EV) drivetrains, computer diagnostics, electronic braking, and suspension systems.

Funding: Snap-on Tool Grant discount program providing up to 50% tool rebates Deadline: July 15, 2027
