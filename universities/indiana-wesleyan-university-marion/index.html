Indiana Wesleyan University

IWU Marion · Marion, Indiana, United States

Indiana Wesleyan University (IWU) is a premier private Christian comprehensive university in Marion, Indiana, celebrated for nursing, clinical counseling, business leadership, occupational therapy, and pre-med natural sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

IWU is home to the renowned School of Nursing—one of the largest in the Midwest—and the Ott Hall of Sciences and Nursing. The university emphasizes holistic liberal arts learning, integration of faith and scholarly discipline, and comprehensive clinical education.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.6), curriculum strength, and personal statements under test-optional guidelines. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree and program-specific clinical clearances.
