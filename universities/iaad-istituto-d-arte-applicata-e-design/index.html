Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree or equivalent (minimum 180 ECTS) in Industrial Design, Architecture, Engineering, or Visual Communication.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral degrees; post-master creative residencies and studio apprenticeships available.
Language proficiency
English B2 (IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL iBT 78+) for English-taught master's tracks, or Italian B2 for Italian-taught diplomas.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive design portfolio demonstrating 10-15 conceptual sketches, 3D digital renderings, clay/foam models, or digital design studies.
Application fee
€150 non-refundable application and portfolio assessment fee.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificate, portfolio of works, motivation letter, and two academic/professional recommendations.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition averages €8,500 to €15,500 per academic year depending on undergraduate or master's program. Early-bird merit scholarships, partner design studio awards, and regional DSU Piemonte housing bursaries are available.
