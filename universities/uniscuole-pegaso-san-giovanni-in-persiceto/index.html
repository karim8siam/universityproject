Academic overview & excellence

Located in the heart of Emilia-Romagna's 'Motor Valley' and agricultural basin, Uniscuole Persiceto serves as an authorized regional academic hub for Università Telematica Pegaso and Universitas Mercatorum. The center provides proctored university examination halls, state-of-the-art e-learning multimedia suites, individual faculty thesis mentorship, and specialized laboratories in educational psychology and regional corporate law.

Research admissions & criteria

Enrollment in undergraduate and master's degree courses is regulated by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR). Candidates for Laurea Triennale must hold an Italian Diploma di Maturità or an equivalent international secondary qualification (12 years of formal schooling). Laurea Magistrale programs require a relevant bachelor's degree. International students require a CIMEA comparability certificate or Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) alongside Italian B2 proficiency.
