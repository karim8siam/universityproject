Concordia University

Located in the vibrant multicultural metropolis of Montreal, Concordia University is one of Canada's leading next-generation comprehensive research universities. Home to the internationally celebrated Gina Cody School of Engineering and Computer Science (the first engineering faculty in Canada named after a woman), the John Molson School of Business, and the Concordia Institute for Information Systems Engineering (CIISE), Concordia excels in artificial intelligence, synthetic biology, cybersecurity, aerospace engineering, and interactive digital arts.

Concordia University Doctoral Graduate Fellowships & International Tuition Awards

Prestigious university-wide doctoral entrance awards providing guaranteed four-year living stipends, teaching assistantships, and tuition offsets for high-achieving international researchers.

Gina Cody School PhD in Computer Science & Applied Cybersecurity

Doctoral research conducted within the Concordia Institute for Information Systems Engineering (CIISE), focusing on artificial intelligence, cloud security, cryptography, and automated software engineering.

Concordia Master of Applied Science (MASc) in Aerospace & Sustainable Propulsion

Thesis-based Master's degree partnering with Montreal's world-leading aerospace cluster (Bombardier, Pratt & Whitney Canada, CAE), investigating sustainable aircraft propulsion, composite structures, and avionics.

Concordia PhD in Biology & Synthetic Biology (Centre for Applied Synthetic Biology)

Pioneering doctoral training at Canada's first Centre for Applied Synthetic Biology (CASB), engineering microbial cell factories for biofuel synthesis, therapeutic proteins, and biosensors.

Milieux Institute for Arts, Culture and Technology Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Milieux Institute, investigating the intersections of digital games, critical media culture, electronic textiles, and ethical AI in contemporary society.

Concordia Applied AI Summer Laboratory Traineeship (Innovation Lab)

Twelve-week mentored summer research internship developing real-world deep learning models, natural language processing pipelines, and computer vision systems in collaboration with industry partners.

Concordia Postdoctoral Fellow in Industrial Cybersecurity & Cyber-Physical Systems

Full-time research role at the Concordia Institute for Information Systems Engineering (CIISE), designing zero-trust security frameworks, vulnerability detection tools, and resilient control protocols for critical infrastructure.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with high academic standing equivalent to a Canadian B average (minimum 3.0/4.3 GPA or 73-76%); STEM programs typically require 3.2+ GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (or equivalent) in a relevant discipline with high academic standing (minimum B+ average, 3.3/4.3 GPA) and evidence of scholarly research output.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-test) or TOEFL iBT 85 (minimum 20 in writing); Gina Cody School requires IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 90 for direct doctoral entry.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2 to 3 page Statement of Research Purpose outlining proposed research questions, methodology, and identified faculty supervisor.
Application fee
CAD $110 non-refundable application fee submitted via Concordia's MyConcordia portal.
Required documentation
Complete official transcripts, curriculum vitae, 3 letters of reference, statement of purpose, and proof of language proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time doctoral students in research degree programs benefit from substantial funding support. Concordia guarantees multi-year Concordia Doctoral Graduate Fellowships of CAD $14,000 per year for up to 4 years, supplemented by departmental teaching and research assistantships, bringing typical annual support to CAD $24,000 to $28,000/year plus International Tuition Differential waivers.
