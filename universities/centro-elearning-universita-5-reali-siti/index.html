Centro E-Learning Università dei 5 Reali Siti: Digital Pedagogy, Law & Agritech Guide

Located in Stornarella in the historic Tavoliere delle Puglie region (Foggia, Apulia), the Centro E-Learning Università dei 5 Reali Siti—headquartered at Palazzo degli Studi Padre Pio—serves as an accredited university study center and e-learning polo. Partnering with major accredited Italian online and state universities, it delivers university degree courses, master's certifications, and applied regional research in agricultural law, digital education, and territorial economic development.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Borsa di Studio E-Learning per lo Sviluppo Territoriale Pugliese

Merit bursary dedicated to students in Southern Italy pursuing digital university degrees in economics, educational sciences, or digital public administration.

Master Universitario di Primo Livello in Diritto ed Economia dell'Agroalimentare

First-level university master's degree focusing on EU Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) regulations, organic certification law, and agri-business management in the Mediterranean basin.

Dottorato di Ricerca in Tecnologie Didattiche e Digital Humanities

Doctoral research fellowship exploring intelligent tutoring algorithms, accessible digital pedagogy, and virtual classroom efficacy in peripheral European communities.

Progetto di Ricerca Applicata sull'Agricoltura di Precisione nel Tavoliere

Applied environmental investigation assessing satellite multispectral monitoring and sensor-driven micro-irrigation systems across farms in the Foggia Tavoliere.

Tirocinio Formativo in Progettazione E-Learning e Gestione Piattaforme LMS

Six-month practical internship developing interactive multimedia learning modules, learning management system (LMS) architectures, and student tracking analytics.

Assegno di Ricerca Postdottorale in Diritto Amministrativo Digitale

One-year renewable research fellowship analyzing the legal framework of algorithmic administrative acts and automated decisions in local government procedures.

Borsa di Merito per Studenti Lavoratori 'Palazzo degli Studi Padre Pio'

Incentive bursary assisting working professionals, civil servants, and teachers completing accredited undergraduate and postgraduate distance degree courses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Italian Laurea Triennale (three-year bachelor's degree) or recognized international equivalent (180 ECTS) in humanities, jurisprudence, economics, or agricultural sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral pathways hosted in partnership with accredited Italian universities; requiring a Laurea Magistrale (minimum final mark 100/110) and research proposal.
Language proficiency
Italian language proficiency at minimum level B2/C1 for Italian-taught degree courses; B2 English for international digital modules.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for post-graduate master diplomas: 1,000-word dissertation proposal focusing on digital public administration, agro-food economics, or educational technologies.
Application fee
€0 application fee for preliminary orientation and academic transcript evaluation.
Required documentation
Certified copy of university degree certificate with transcript of exams, valid ID/passport, tax code (Codice Fiscale), and detailed curriculum vitae.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

University fees through the Centro E-Learning 5 Reali Siti range between €1,800 and €3,200 per academic year, significantly reduced via regional Apulia regional grants (ADISU Puglia) and study conventions. Special installment plans and merit bursaries are available for students from Southern Italy and international candidates.
