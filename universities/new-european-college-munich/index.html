Academic overview & research priorities

Located in Munich’s Giesing district and partnered with Wittenborg University of Applied Sciences, NEC combines boutique seminar pedagogy with Munich’s Fortune 500 corporate network (BMW, Siemens, Allianz), emphasizing entrepreneurship, luxury management, and marketing.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation diploma (Abitur, IB, High School Diploma with 4 APs, or equivalent). English proficiency required: IELTS 6.0+ (bachelor) or IELTS 6.5+ (master/MBA). Rolling admissions with intakes in October, February, April, and June.
