Georgian College: Applied Degrees, Automotive Engineering & Scholarships Guide

Established in 1967, Georgian College of Applied Arts and Technology is one of Ontario's premier public colleges, with its main campus in Barrie and regional campuses across Central Ontario. Renowned as the home of the Automotive Business School of Canada (ABSC), alongside pioneer programs in marine technology, advanced manufacturing, nursing, and computer studies, Georgian offers over 130 accredited degrees, diplomas, and graduate certificates featuring extensive co-op work terms.

Collaborative program awarding both a Lakehead University Honours BSc degree and a Georgian College Environmental Technology diploma in four years.

Applied research initiative testing solar photovoltaic generation, battery energy storage systems, and smart microgrid controls at the Barrie campus.

Four-month paid work-integrated learning semester placing students with major automotive manufacturers, dealership groups, and mobility tech firms across North America.

Georgian Applied AI and Big Data Analytics Innovation Fellowship

Applied development fellowship supporting post-graduate scholars implementing data science and machine learning pipelines for regional manufacturing and healthcare partners.

Professor of Mechanical and Advanced Manufacturing Engineering Technology

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Ontario College Graduate Certificates require completion of a recognized post-secondary diploma or university bachelor's degree with minimum 65% (C+) academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Degree and diploma programs articulate into collaborative university bachelor's and master's credentials through Lakehead-Georgian partnership agreements.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 88 for degree/graduate certificate level programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted through ontariocolleges.ca or the Georgian International Application Portal including academic transcripts.
Application fee
CAD $125 non-refundable international admissions processing fee (CAD $110 domestic via OCAS).
Required documentation
Secondary school diploma transcripts, post-secondary graduation certificates, proof of English language proficiency, and passport identification page.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international students generally ranges from CAD $8,200 to $10,500 per semester (approximately CAD $16,400 to $21,000 per academic year) depending on the program of study and specialized laboratory fees. Domestic Ontario students pay approximately CAD $1,400 to $2,400 per semester. Georgian awards over CAD $3 million annually in donor-funded entrance scholarships and academic bursaries.
