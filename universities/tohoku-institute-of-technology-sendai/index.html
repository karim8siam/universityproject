Academic overview & research priorities

Operating two prominent campuses in Sendai—Yagiyama (Faculty of Engineering and Graduate School) and Nagamachi (Faculty of Architecture and Faculty of Life and Environmental Sciences)—Tohtech has served as a driving technological powerhouse in northern Japan since 1964. Its specialized laboratories lead breakthrough research in earthquake disaster mitigation, renewable energy microgrids, high-frequency telecommunications, and industrial user interface design.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate entrance requires certified high school credentials with rigorous math and physics coursework, JLPT N2 or equivalent (for Japanese-taught programs), and an entrance exam. Master's and doctoral degree admissions require a recognized engineering bachelor's or master's degree, a specialized research proposal, transcript submission, and faculty supervisor matching prior to formal application.
