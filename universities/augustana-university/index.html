Augustana University: Genetic Counseling, Physical Therapy & Nursing Guide

Founded in 1860 and located in South Dakota's largest city, Sioux Falls, Augustana University is an accredited comprehensive private university celebrated for academic excellence across the health sciences, genomics, physical therapy, computer science, and business. Partnering with Sanford Health (one of the largest health systems in the United States), Augustana houses premier clinical research and genetic counseling facilities.

Augustana University Distinguished Trustee Honors Scholarship

Augustana's highest merit award recognizing incoming domestic and international scholars who exhibit exceptional academic excellence, moral leadership, and community service.

Master of Science in Genetic Counseling (MSGC)

ACGC-accredited master's program delivered in partnership with Sanford Imagenetics, providing clinical genomic counseling training in oncology, cardiology, and prenatal genetics.

Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) Clinical Research Fellowship

Two-year accelerated CAPTE-candidate hybrid doctoral program blending rigorous online didactic learning with intensive hands-on clinical lab immersions in Sioux Falls.

Sanford Imagenetics Precision Medicine Genomics Research Project

Applied genomic medicine project evaluating clinical implementation barriers for preemptive pharmacogenomic panel testing across primary healthcare clinics in the upper Midwest.

Sioux Falls Biomedical & Technology Industry Internship Practicum

Four-month practical immersion working alongside clinical data architects and software developers at Sanford Health, Avera Health, and regional biotech contractors.

Augustana Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Bioethics and Genomic Counseling

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating ethical issues in direct-to-consumer genetic testing, incidental genomic findings, and equitable genetic service access.

Global Viking International Student Leadership Scholarship

Institutional scholarship granted to international undergraduate and graduate candidates who demonstrate strong academic dedication and intercultural leadership on campus.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited institution with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0 in relevant science or quantitative discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT); requiring an accredited bachelor's degree, completion of prerequisite sciences (minimum 3.0 GPA), and clinical observation hours.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score), or Duolingo English Test 105+ for non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate applicants: 1,000-word personal statement articulating scientific interests, clinical exposure, and career goals in genomic medicine or rehabilitation.
Application fee
$0 online application fee for undergraduate and graduate applications ($25 for specific CAS portal applications).
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts from all post-secondary institutions, comprehensive CV, two letters of recommendation, and personal statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition at Augustana University is approximately $38,500 USD per academic year. Graduate tuition ranges between $650 and $950 USD per credit hour depending on the program. 100% of full-time undergraduate students receive institutional scholarships up to $26,000 USD/year, significantly offsetting costs.
