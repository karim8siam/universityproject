Stockholm University of the Arts: Performing Arts, Film & Artistic Research Guide

Established in 2014 through the merger of the School of Dance and Circus, the University College of Opera, and the Stockholm Academy of Dramatic Arts, Stockholm University of the Arts (Stockholms konstnärliga högskola - SKH) is Sweden's national public higher education institution for the performing arts, film, media, and circus. SKH is accredited to award doctoral degrees in artistic research and conducts world-leading artistic inquiry.

SKH International Master's Artistic Excellence Scholarship

Full tuition waiver awarded to exceptionally gifted non-EU/EEA international artists admitted to Master of Fine Arts programs in choreography, film, or performing arts at SKH.

Master of Fine Arts (MFA) in Film and Media (Documentary Practices)

Two-year European master's curriculum emphasizing innovative narrative techniques, cinematic dramaturgy, ethical documentary storytelling, and experimental visual methodologies.

Salaried PhD Position in Artistic Research in Performing Arts (Doktorandanställning)

Four-year salaried doctoral research employment at SKH conducting practice-based research into choreographic composition, bodily dramaturgy, and somatic performance epistemology.

Stockholm Sound Arts & Spatial Acoustic Immersion Project

Artistic investigation deploying ambisonic spatial audio recording and sound synthesis to explore auditory perception and immersive performance environments.

Royal Dramatic Theatre (Dramaten) Dramaturgy & Direction Practicum

Fourteen-week professional stage residency assisting senior theatre directors with dramaturgical analysis, actor rehearsal coaching, and lighting design at Dramaten.

SKH Senior Research Fellowship in Circus Arts & Aerial Kinematics

Two-year research appointment leading the Department of Circus investigating risk aesthetics, aerial apparatus innovation, and somatic movement knowledge in contemporary circus.

Swedish Institute (SI) Scholarship for Global Professionals at SKH

Prestigious Swedish government scholarship fully funding master's studies for talented international cultural leaders and artists seeking to drive democratic change.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Fine Arts degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in performing arts, dance, film direction, opera, or relevant creative arts discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master of Fine Arts degree (minimum 120 ECTS) in relevant artistic field; submission of comprehensive artistic portfolio and doctoral project plan.
Language proficiency
English 6 proficiency equivalent: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
For master's and doctoral programs: artistic portfolio, work samples, and a 2,000-word artistic research proposal articulating research questions and methodological approaches.
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee for non-EU/EEA applicants via University Admissions Sweden.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcript, documentation of artistic achievements, CV, and verified English proficiency certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Swedish law, education is tuition-free for citizens of Sweden, EU/EEA countries, and Switzerland. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition for fine arts master's programs is approximately SEK 240,000 to SEK 280,000 per academic year. Admitted doctoral researchers hold salaried employment contracts (doktorandanställning) earning approximately SEK 32,000 to SEK 36,000 per month.
