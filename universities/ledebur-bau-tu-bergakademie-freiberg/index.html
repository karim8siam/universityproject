Academic overview & research priorities

Named in honor of pioneering metallurgist Adolf Ledebur (discoverer of ledeburite), the Ledebur-Bau at TU Bergakademie Freiberg (founded 1765, the world's oldest mining university) houses world-class research foundries, vacuum induction melting furnaces, pilot rolling mills, and electron microscopy laboratories dedicated to advanced metallurgy, sustainable steelmaking, and metal circularity.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions through TU Bergakademie Freiberg for bachelor’s, master’s, and Diplom-Ingenieur programs in Materials Science, Metallurgy, and Foundry Technology. International applicants require recognized bachelor degree in metallurgy or mechanical engineering and German B2/C1 or English C1.
