Maine College of Art & Design

MECA&D · Portland, Maine, United States

Maine College of Art & Design is a private art and design college in the vibrant downtown arts district of Portland, Maine. Founded in 1882, MECA&D is celebrated for metalsmithing, woodworking, ceramics, illustration, and socially engaged contemporary art.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MECA&D offers BFA, MFA, and Master of Arts in Teaching degrees anchored in 24/7 studio culture. It houses the Institute of Contemporary Art (ICA at MECA&D), the Joanne Waxman Library, and state-of-the-art digital fabrication and printmaking studios.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are portfolio-driven, evaluating 10 to 15 creative works demonstrating technical curiosity, personal voice, and craftsmanship alongside high school transcripts and artist statements.
