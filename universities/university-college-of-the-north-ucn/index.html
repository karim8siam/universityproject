Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

University College of the North (UCN) is a community-embedded post-secondary institution dedicated to serving northern Manitoba through culturally grounded university degrees, diploma programs, and research tailored to Indigenous self-determination and northern resource stewardship.

Institution type
Public University College
Campus location
The Pas Campus & Thompson Campus, Manitoba
Accreditation
Government of Manitoba / Universities Canada Affiliate
Admissions channel
Fall (September) and Winter (January) intake sessions
Instruction language
English (with Cree and Dene language learning centres)
Financial aid & support
Manitoba Student Aid, UCN Foundation Bursaries, Indspire Awards

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Culturally responsive Bachelor of Nursing in collaboration with UM
  • Indigenous teacher education (Kenanow Learning Model)
  • Boreal ecosystem research and northern wildlife conservation
  • Extensive network of 12 regional community access centres across Manitoba

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Nursing (Joint UCN/UM Program) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year comprehensive nursing degree preparing registered nurses for remote, rural, and northern community healthcare practice.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Aboriginal and Northern Studies (Undergraduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary degree covering Indigenous legal orders, northern policy, land claims, and circumpolar history.
  • Bachelor of Education (Kenanow Bachelor of Education) (Undergraduate Degree) — Holistic teacher preparation grounded in Indigenous worldviews, northern school curricula, and bilingual pedagogy.
