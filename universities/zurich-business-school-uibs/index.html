Zurich Business School (UIBS): Global Management & Swiss Business Guide

Established in 2002 with main campuses in Zurich and Geneva, United International Business Schools (UIBS Zurich) is an accredited private Swiss business school. Recognized for flexible American-European dual-degree frameworks, small interactive seminar cohorts, and cross-cultural executive training, UIBS prepares international managers for careers in European finance, supply chain management, and international diplomacy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits or international 3-4 year equivalent) in any discipline with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) entry requires an accredited Master's degree (MSc or MBA) and minimum three years of professional managerial experience.
Language proficiency
English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score), TOEFL iBT 85, or Duolingo English Test 110+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate research and DBA programs: 1,500-word applied research proposal outlining research methodology and real-world corporate problem resolution.
Application fee
CHF 150 non-refundable admissions evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete official transcripts, copy of passport, two professional or academic letters of recommendation, and personal statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate master's programs at UIBS Zurich is approximately CHF 15,300 per academic year (approximately CHF 22,950 total degree tuition for a 90-ECTS Master in International Management). Institutional merit scholarships providing up to 30% tuition fee reductions and flexible quarterly payment plans provide strong financial options.
