New York University

NYU · New York, New York, United States

New York University is one of the world's leading private global research universities, anchored in Greenwich Village, Manhattan, with degree-granting campuses in Abu Dhabi and Shanghai. Globally renowned for Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Stern School of Business, Tisch School of the Arts, and NYU Grossman School of Medicine.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NYU spans historic Washington Square Park, Brooklyn's MetroTech Center (Tandon School of Engineering), and the NYU Langone Health medical complex in Midtown East. NYU produces groundbreaking discoveries across artificial intelligence, quantitative finance, quantum optics, and cinematic arts.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective with an overall acceptance rate of approximately 8%. Applicants submit either SAT/ACT or international credentials, alongside academic transcripts, personal essays, and recommendation letters. Graduate programs require rigorous discipline-specific credentials.
