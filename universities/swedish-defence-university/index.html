Swedish Defence University: War Studies, Crisis Management & Doctoral Fellowships

The Swedish Defence University (Försvarshögskolan / SEDU) in Stockholm is Sweden's premier public institution for higher education and research in war studies, international security, political science, crisis management, and defence systems, playing a central role in Nordic and NATO security strategy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in Political Science, War Studies, International Relations, or related social science field via Universityadmissions.se.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS total with 60 ECTS at advanced level) in War Studies, Political Science, or International Security with a completed scientific thesis.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B (IELTS 6.5 with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral employment calls: 5-page research plan detailing research questions, theoretical approach, methodology, and security relevance.
Application fee
900 SEK application fee via Universityadmissions.se (EU/EEA students exempt).
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diplomas, complete transcripts of records, thesis sample, CV, and letters of reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition is approximately 110,000 to 125,000 SEK per year. PhD candidates are employed as full-time university researchers with starting salaries of ~32,000 SEK/month, paying zero tuition fees.
