Università Telematica Internazionale UNINETTUNO (Polo Puglia)

Università Telematica Internazionale UNINETTUNO is a prestigious, state-accredited digital research university recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR), operating its strategic regional and Mediterranean research polo in Bari, Puglia.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established by Ministerial Decree in 2005 from the historic NETTUNO consortium of 43 Italian and European state universities, UNINETTUNO is Italy’s top-ranked online research university according to national evaluation agency ANVUR. The university serves more than 16,000 students from over 160 countries through a proprietary psycho-pedagogic e-learning platform. The Polo Tecnologico Puglia in Bari coordinates research collaborations in digital cultural heritage, software engineering, Mediterranean environmental law, and tele-health technologies.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Corso di Laurea Magistrale in Ingegneria Informatica (Master Computer Engineering) (Master's) — Two-year accredited master's offering tracks in Big Data, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity with remote virtualized labs and on-campus exams.
  • Corso di Laurea Triennale in Ingegneria Gestionale (BSc Management Engineering) (Bachelor's) — Three-year degree integrating production logistics, operations research, information systems, and corporate finance.
  • Master Universitario in Beni Culturali Digitali (Digital Heritage) (Master's) — Interdisciplinary graduate master's in 3D photogrammetry, virtual museum curation, and archival digitisation.
  • Dottorato di Ricerca in Tecnologie per la Società Digitale (PhD Digital Society) (Doctorate) — Three-year doctoral research program investigating algorithmic fairness, tele-medicine systems, and e-learning cognitive architectures.
