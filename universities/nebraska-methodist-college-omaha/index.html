Nebraska Methodist College

Nebraska Methodist · Omaha, Nebraska, United States

Nebraska Methodist College is a private healthcare-specialized college in Omaha, Nebraska, affiliated with the Methodist Health System. A regional leader in nursing, allied health sciences, healthcare management, and clinical sonography.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Nebraska Methodist operates the Division of Nursing and the Division of Allied Health and Arts & Sciences. Facilities include a 10,000-square-foot clinical simulation center, virtual anatomy labs, and direct clinical rotations across Methodist Health System hospitals.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts (minimum 2.5 unweighted GPA) and prerequisite science coursework. Graduate healthcare programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, professional healthcare resume, and references.
