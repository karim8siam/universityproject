Champlain College St. Lawrence: Admissions, Pathways, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Champlain Regional College St. Lawrence campus, established in 1958 in historic Quebec City, is the sole public English-language CEGEP in the Capitale-Nationale region. Offering accredited pre-university Science, Social Science, and Arts diplomas (DEC) alongside career programs in Tourism and Business Management, St. Lawrence prepares graduates for seamless matriculation into leading Canadian and international universities.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from secondary school (Quebec DES or international high school diploma equivalent) with strong academic standing in prerequisite mathematics, chemistry, and physics for science streams.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Academic college programs serve as third-level university feeder pathways; graduates matriculate directly with advanced credits into baccalaureate and graduate-preparatory degrees.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency (IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80) and compliance with Quebec Law 14 language requirements for eligible English-stream instruction.
Research statement / Proposal
Applicants submit comprehensive transcripts through SRAM (Service régional d'admission du Montréal métropolitain) or direct collegiate admissions portal.
Application fee
CAD $30 non-refundable SRAM collegiate application fee.
Required documentation
Certified high school academic records, birth certificate, proof of Canadian residency or international study authorization, and language certificates.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Quebec public education funding, Canadian citizens and permanent residents residing in Quebec pay no base tuition fees for full-time regular DEC study (nominal student association and campus service fees approximate CAD $180 per semester). International students pay standard Quebec collegiate tuition ranging from CAD $7,000 to $10,500 per semester depending on the technical or pre-university program stream.
