Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located in Edmonton, Alberta, The King's University is an accredited Christian university known for excellence in undergraduate liberal arts, chemical education, environmental science, and community-engaged research.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • King's Centre for Visualization in Science (KCVS) global climate and chemical education
  • Environmental science, boreal forest conservation, and ecological ethics
  • Computing science with interdisciplinary data analytics and digital ethics
  • Bachelor of Education with integrated provincial teaching certification

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Chemistry (Four-Year) (Undergraduate Degree) — Rigorous laboratory degree accredited by the Canadian Society for Chemistry (CSC), featuring active undergraduate research.
  • Bachelor of Science in Environmental Studies (Undergraduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary program integrating ecological monitoring, GIS mapping, environmental policy, and environmental ethics.
  • Bachelor of Education (After-Degree) (Professional Degree) — Two-year professional certification program for elementary and secondary teaching in Alberta.
