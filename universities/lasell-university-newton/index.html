Lasell University

Lasell Univ · Newton, Massachusetts, United States

Lasell University is a private university in Newton, Massachusetts, located just miles from downtown Boston. Lasell is nationally acclaimed for its 'Connected Learning' model, immersing students in professional projects across fashion design, athletic training, hospitality, and communication.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lasell offers 40+ undergraduate and graduate degrees emphasizing real-world client problem solving. Signature schools include the School of Fashion, School of Health Sciences, and School of Communication and the Arts, with extensive internships throughout the Boston metropolitan area.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional, reviewing high school course rigor, GPA (average 3.2), and writing samples. Graduate programs in athletic training, criminal justice, and management require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 GPA.
