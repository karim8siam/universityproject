University of North Texas at Dallas

UNT Dallas · Dallas, Texas, United States

The University of North Texas at Dallas (UNT Dallas) is the only four-year public university located within the city limits of Dallas, Texas. Founded in 2000, UNT Dallas is dedicated to socioeconomic mobility, community health, criminal justice, urban leadership, and public law through the UNT Dallas College of Law located in downtown Dallas.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNT Dallas houses four schools: the School of Behavioral Health and Human Services, School of Business, School of Education, and the UNT Dallas College of Law. The university is a designated Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) and minority-majority urban campus focused on workforce-aligned education.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5) and transcripts with test-optional policies. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.0+).
