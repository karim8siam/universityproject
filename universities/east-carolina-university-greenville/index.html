Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1907 to transform eastern North Carolina, ECU is home to the state's leading primary-care medical school (Brody School of Medicine), the Coastal Studies Institute in Wanchese on the Outer Banks, and the College of Engineering and Technology. ECU is nationally distinguished for its maritime archaeology research vessel fleet, rural health leadership, and advanced biomedical technology.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applications require official high school transcripts, counselor recommendation, and writing essay. Test-optional admission is available for students meeting state GPA thresholds. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree (minimum GPA 3.0), program-specific prerequisites, GRE/MAT (where required), and letters of reference. International students require TOEFL iBT 78 or IELTS 6.5.
