CQUniversity Emerald: Precision Agriculture, Mining Engineering & Rural Health

CQUniversity Emerald Campus is the regional agricultural and mining education hub in the Central Highlands of Queensland, celebrated for its 2,100-hectare research station, precision cropping technology, heavy machinery diesel fitting, and rural healthcare delivery.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Australian Bachelor's degree or recognized equivalent in agriculture, business, engineering, or environmental sciences for master's programs.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's with First-Class Honours, or Master by Research, plus structured research proposal aligned with agricultural precision tech.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or PTE Academic 58.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral and research applicants must submit a 1,500-word proposal on rural agronomy, mining logistics, or water management.
Application fee
Free online direct application through CQUniversity international portal.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree awards, English language test certificate, resume/CV, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual undergraduate tuition for international students averages AUD $27,000 to $34,000. Australian Destination Australia scholarships of up to AUD $15,000 per year, CQUni International Student scholarships (25% fee reduction), and agricultural industry awards are available.
