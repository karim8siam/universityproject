Trinity College

Trinity College · Hartford, Connecticut, United States

Trinity College is a historic, highly selective private liberal arts college situated on a 100-acre English-style collegiate gothic campus in Hartford, Connecticut. Founded in 1823 as the second-oldest college in Connecticut, Trinity is celebrated as one of the few liberal arts institutions offering an ABET-accredited engineering program, world-class neuroscience, urban studies, and international affairs.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Trinity delivers over 40 majors and interdisciplinary minors through dedicated departments in the Arts, Humanities, Natural Sciences, and Social Sciences. Its Roy Nutt Mathematics, Engineering & Computer Science Center, Clement Chemistry Building, and neuroscience laboratories provide direct undergraduate access to advanced laser optics and neuro-electrophysiology rigs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective and evaluated holistically (Common Application; test-optional). Evaluation emphasizes rigorous secondary school curriculum (honors/AP/IB), academic GPA, personal essays, extracurricular leadership, and counselor/teacher recommendations.
