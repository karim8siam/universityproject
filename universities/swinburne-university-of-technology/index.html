Swinburne University of Technology: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Swinburne University of Technology is an internationally recognized public research institution based in Hawthorn, Melbourne. Renowned globally for deep space astronomy, gravitational wave discovery (OzGrav), advanced manufacturing, and cyber security, Swinburne combines high-impact scientific research with direct commercial and aerospace translation.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

OzGrav Doctoral Research Studentships in Gravitational Wave Astrophysics

Doctoral studentship at the ARC Centre of Excellence for Gravitational Wave Discovery (OzGrav) at Swinburne, analyzing LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA detector data and supercomputing simulations of neutron star mergers.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Cloud Security & Decentralized Trust Systems

PhD position at the Cyber Security Lab within Swinburne's School of Science, Computing and Engineering Technologies, exploring zero-trust cloud architectures and blockchain consensus protocols.

PhD in Ultrafast Laser Nanofabrication and Photonic Materials

Doctoral research at the Centre for Micro-Photonics and Translational Atomaterials, utilizing ultrafast femtosecond lasers for 3D nano-printing and optical metamaterial manufacturing.

Master of Artificial Intelligence (Machine Learning & Autonomous Systems)

Two-year Master of AI program providing advanced competencies in deep neural networks, computer vision, natural language understanding, and industry-partnered applied research capstones.

Master of Design (Industrial Design & Advanced Digital Manufacturing)

Two-year design master's degree exploring additive manufacturing, circular material systems, biodesign, and user-centered smart product prototyping at the Swinburne Design Factory.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Space Technology & Satellite Communications

Two-year postdoctoral research post at the Space Technology and Industry Institute, designing optical ground stations and RF communication payloads for low Earth orbit nanosatellites.

Swinburne Data Science & Supercomputing Research Practicum

Three to six-month technical research internship optimizing scientific pipelines on Swinburne's national GPU supercomputer, OzSTAR, for gravitational wave and biomedical dataset analytics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized bachelor's degree in a related technical discipline with an Australian credit average (65%+) or equivalent international grade.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1) or research master's degree with demonstrated publication output; supervisor confirmation prior to submission.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79 (reading 18, writing 21).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 4-5 page research proposal outlining problem statement, research questions, methodologies, and proposed equipment.
Application fee
AUD $0 for direct online research degree applications.
Required documentation
Certified degree transcripts, graduation awards, research proposal, CV, two academic reference letters, and supervisor endorsement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International higher degree by research (HDR) students are supported through Australian Government RTP and Swinburne University Postgraduate Research Awards (SUPRA), which cover 100% of international tuition fees and provide a tax-free living allowance of AUD $34,841 per year for up to 3.5 years. Standard international coursework tuition is AUD $38,000–$44,000 per year.
