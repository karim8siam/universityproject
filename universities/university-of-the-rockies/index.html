Rockies · Denver, Colorado, United States

Operating from Denver, CO, Rockies provides scholar-practitioner doctorates and master's degrees tailored for licensed mental health practitioners, corporate consultants, and human service leaders.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Denver · Graduate Institution in Applied Psychology, Executive Coaching & Human Services. Founded in Colorado in 1998, the University of the Rockies is an accredited graduate institution dedicated to advanced study in psychology, organizational leadership, behavioral health, and executive coaching. Operating from Denver, CO, Rockies provides scholar-practitioner doctorates and master's degrees tailored for licensed mental health practitioners, corporate consultants, and human service leaders.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Conferred master's degree with 3.0+ GPA for Doctor of Psychology (Psy.D.) and Ph.D. programs; conferred bachelor's degree with 2.5+ GPA for master's programs. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates without prior English-instruction degrees. Rolling admissions with quarterly terms: Fall October 1, 2026; Winter January 15, 2027; Spring April 1, 2027; Summer July 1, 2027.
