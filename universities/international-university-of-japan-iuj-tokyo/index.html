Academic overview & excellence

Founded with the backing of Japan's major economic federations (Keidanren), the International University of Japan operates its Tokyo Office in Minato-ku to connect its residential Minamiuonuma campus with multinational corporations, Japanese ministries (METI, MOFA), and global development banks. IUJ houses two AACSB-accredited graduate schools: the Graduate School of International Relations (GSIR) and the Graduate School of International Management (GSIM). Over 85% of scholars hail from more than 70 countries, forming a top-tier international policy and business network.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to IUJ Master's and PhD programs is conducted entirely in English. Applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree (minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA), submit standardized test scores (GMAT or GRE for MBA/MSc Economics; institutional waivers available for qualifying profiles), verified English language scores (TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+), a statement of purpose outlining international development or corporate leadership goals, and two professional/academic letters of recommendation.
