New York University Berlin

New York University Berlin is NYU’s European academic and research center in the historic KulturBrauerei in Prenzlauer Berg, offering advanced study in European politics, art, and urban sociology.

Academic overview & research priorities

An essential hub of NYU’s global network university, NYU Berlin immerses undergraduate and graduate scholars in German language, European Union geopolitics, contemporary gallery arts, film studies, and environmental sustainability, with direct access to Berlin's historic archives and cultural institutions.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions through New York University's central admissions portal or global study abroad exchange agreements. Requires matriculation at an accredited university with competitive GPA (3.00+), academic essays, and faculty recommendations.
