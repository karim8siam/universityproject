University of Wyoming

UWyo · Laramie, Wyoming, United States

The University of Wyoming (UWyo / UW) is Wyoming's sole public land-grant flagship research university, situated at 7,220 feet between the Laramie and Medicine Bow mountain ranges in Laramie, Wyoming. Renowned for energy engineering at the School of Energy Resources, atmospheric flight research aboard the King Air atmospheric aircraft, wildlife migration ecology, and supercomputing at the NCAR-Wyoming Supercomputing Center, UWyo anchors discovery across the Rocky Mountain West.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UWyo encompasses seven colleges: Agriculture, Life Sciences and Natural Resources; Engineering and Physical Sciences; Arts and Sciences; Business; Education; Health Sciences; and the College of Law. Flagship research centers include the School of Energy Resources (SER), the Wyoming Migration Initiative, and the High Bay Research Facility.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 3.0) and curriculum. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
