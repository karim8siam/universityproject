Brandenburg University of Technology: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Brandenburg University of Technology Cottbus-Senftenberg (BTU) is the second largest university and the only technical university in the State of Brandenburg, Germany. Situated at the heart of the Lusatia energy transition region, BTU leads Europe in hybrid-electric flight drives (CHESCO), power plant decarbonization, lightweight construction, and environmental resource management.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

PhD in Hybrid Electric Aircraft Propulsion and Turbomachinery

Three-year research position in the CHESCO hybrid-electric test center designing aerodynamic compressors, high-power-density motor integration, and thermal management for zero-emission passenger aviation.

International Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Artificial Intelligence and Cognitive Systems

English-taught graduate program exploring neuro-symbolic reasoning, machine learning pipelines, autonomous robotic control, semantic web architectures, and explainable computational models.

Lausitz Energy Transition Doctoral Scholarship (Lusatia Decarbonization Initiative)

Doctoral research funding supporting international researchers investigating regional energy transition models, hydrogen supply logistics, grid battery storage, and structural transformation away from lignite coal.

Center for Hybrid Electric Systems Cottbus (CHESCO) Power Electronics Research Assistantship

Research role focusing on wide-bandgap silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN) power converters operating under high-altitude aerospace pressures and high-voltage conditions.

Brandenburg Climate Neutral Infrastructure and Mine-Land Reclamation Research Internship

Six-month applied field research internship monitoring soil microbial remediation, groundwater acidification neutralization, and wetland restoration in former open-cast mining landscapes.

Fraunhofer IWU / BTU Cottbus Joint Postdoctoral Fellowship in Green Hydrogen & Electrolyzer Materials

Two-year postdoctoral position synthesizing non-precious catalyst coatings and proton-exchange membranes for megawatt-scale industrial water electrolyzers producing green hydrogen fuels.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Environmental and Resource Management (ERM)

Pioneering international master's degree uniting environmental engineering, ecological economics, environmental law, and international climate treaties to train sustainability leaders.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized bachelor's degree in engineering, natural sciences, or computer science with high academic standing and relevant subject-matter coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science or Diplom degree with above-average grades, written research proposal, and formal acceptance by a BTU university professor.
Language proficiency
For English programs: TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS Academic 6.5. For German programs: TestDaF level 4 in all sections or DSH-2.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 4-6 page doctoral research synopsis detailing project goals, state-of-the-art literature review, work packages, and experimental timeline.
Application fee
€75 application processing fee via uni-assist for international credential evaluation.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, academic transcripts, curriculum vitae in Europass format, statement of motivation, and 2 letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg is free of tuition charges (€0 tuition) for both domestic and international students. Students pay only a nominal semester administration fee of approximately €320, which includes a regional public transportation transit ticket covering all trains and buses across Brandenburg and Berlin. Doctoral researchers are employed on standard German collective wage agreements (TV-L E13) with gross annual salaries between €50,000 and €56,000.
