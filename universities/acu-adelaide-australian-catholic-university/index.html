Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent Australian Level 7 qualification) in education, humanities, theology, or related field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master by Research or Bachelor with First-Class Honours, submitted research proposal, and aligned academic supervision at ACU.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) for humanities and education; 7.0 for clinical areas.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research proposal (1,500-2,000 words) defining ethical framework, methodology, and primary bibliography.
Application fee
AUD $110 international application fee (waived for direct online submissions during designated application weeks).
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree parchment, English proficiency results, resume/CV, and two professional or academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Postgraduate annual tuition for international students averages AUD $25,000 to $32,000. ACU International Student scholarships (50% fee reduction), Peter Faber business fellowships, and Catholic education diocesan sponsorships are available.
