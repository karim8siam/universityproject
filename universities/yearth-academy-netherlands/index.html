YEARTH Academy Netherlands

YEARTH Academy is one of the highest-rated executive academies in the Netherlands, located in the central business district of Utrecht with executive centers in Amsterdam. Accredited by CEDEO and the NRTO (Nederlandse Raad voor Training en Opleiding), YEARTH delivers post-academic executive masterclasses, leadership master programs, and organizational psychology diplomas.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Utrecht & Amsterdam, Netherlands

Institutional Overview

The educational philosophy of YEARTH Academy integrates neuroscience, behavioral economics, and pragmatic executive leadership frameworks. Programs emphasize intensive interactive cohorts, individual executive coaching, peer-to-peer strategic case dissections, and high-impact personal development labs designed for corporate executives, public managers, and entrepreneurs.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions to YEARTH programs evaluate candidates based on managerial experience (minimum 3 to 5 years leadership or specialized professional tenure), undergraduate or master's degrees (HBO/WO), and admissions intake interviews. Professional employer development sponsorships and Dutch STAP/NL leert door tax incentives frequently fund enrolled leaders.
