Goethe University Frankfurt: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Goethe University Frankfurt (Goethe-Universität Frankfurt am Main) is one of Germany's most prestigious and research-intensive universities, founded in 1914 as a unique citizen-endowed institution. Renowned for the Frankfurt School of critical theory and its close proximity to the European Central Bank, it is an international leader in structural biology, theoretical physics, finance, and high-performance scientific computing.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) with minimum German grade 2.5 ('gut') or international equivalent in matching field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (120 ECTS) with above-average grades; formal supervision agreement (Betreuungszusage) from a Goethe University professor.
Language proficiency
German proficiency (DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4) for German-taught degrees; English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 90+ or IELTS 6.5-7.0) for international English-taught Master's and structured PhD programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research expository (5-8 pages) with bibliography, work plan, and scientific methodology.
Application fee
€75 for initial uni-assist verification for non-EU degrees; €0 for direct doctoral registration.
Required documentation
Certified transcripts, degree diplomas, CV, research proposal, supervisor confirmation letter, and language certificates.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Goethe University Frankfurt is zero for all domestic and international students in standard doctoral and Master's programs. Students pay only a statutory semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €390 per semester, which includes a comprehensive public transit ticket covering the entire state of Hesse. Doctoral candidates generally receive salaried research contracts (TV-L E13, €48,000+/year) or DAAD scholarships.
