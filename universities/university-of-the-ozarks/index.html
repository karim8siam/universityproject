University of the Ozarks

Ozarks · Clarksville, Arkansas, United States

The University of the Ozarks is a historic private Presbyterian university founded in 1834, nestled at the foothills of the scenic Ozark Mountains in Clarksville, Arkansas. Famed for pioneering the nation's first neurodiversity college support program (Jones Learning Center in 1971), Ozarks is equally renowned for exceptional undergraduate research in environmental science, marine biology, business, and art in an internationally diverse community.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The university provides undergraduate liberal arts and professional degrees across divisions of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Social Sciences and Business, and Humanities and Fine Arts. The campus sits minutes from the Ozark National Forest, the Arkansas River, and the Big Piney Creek outdoor recreation corridor.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate applicants, evaluating high school GPA (minimum 2.5) and essay. International students submit TOEFL (minimum 71) or IELTS (minimum 6.0).
