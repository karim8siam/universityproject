Malmö Yrkeshögskola: Advanced Mobile, AI & Applied Tech Programs Guide

Located in the thriving tech hub of Malmö in southern Sweden's Öresund region, Malmö Yrkeshögskola (MY) is an authorized Swedish Higher Vocational Education institution (Yrkeshögskola) inspected and certified by the Swedish National Agency for Higher Vocational Education (Myndigheten för yrkeshögskolan - MYH). Grounded in direct co-creation with Nordic tech giants, MY delivers cutting-edge programs in Mobile App Engineering (iOS/Android), Applied Artificial Intelligence, and Cloud Automation.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Swedish State CSN Study Finance and Living Grant (Studiemedel)

Swedish government CSN student funding providing monthly non-repayable grants and subsidized living loans for students enrolled in accredited tech programs at Malmö Yrkeshögskola.

Higher Vocational Diploma (YH-Examen) in Mobile Application Development (iOS & Android)

Two-year professional diploma program training full-stack mobile software engineers in Swift, Kotlin, SwiftUI, Jetpack Compose, and cross-platform Flutter architectures.

Advanced Diploma in Applied Artificial Intelligence and Cloud Automation

Intensive two-year qualification focusing on machine learning operations (MLOps), large language model deployment, computer vision, and cloud microservices on AWS and Azure.

Öresund Tech Innovation and Mobile Edge Computing Project

Applied development project testing on-device neural network models, CoreML optimizations, and real-time sensor processing on mobile hardware in the Malmö tech cluster.

LIA (Lärande i Arbete) Full-Time Industry Internship (20 Weeks)

Five-month full-time workplace immersion (Lärande i Arbete - LIA) embedding students in commercial agile sprint teams at tech firms in Malmö, Lund, and Copenhagen.

Malmö Tech Cluster Diversity in Code and Mobile Innovation Fellowship

Industry-sponsored fellowship promoting diversity in the Nordic tech ecosystem, supporting underrepresented software developers building high-impact mobile solutions.

Lead Instructor in iOS Architecture, Swift & Mobile Cloud Services

Full-time senior instructor role lecturing advanced iOS engineering, mentoring student sprint teams, and designing curriculum aligned with Apple ecosystem developments.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from upper secondary school (Gymnasieexamen or recognized international secondary school diploma) with prerequisite courses in Mathematics 2 and English 6 or equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Higher vocational degrees (Yrkeshögskoleexamen) articulate into advanced software engineering positions, corporate technical leadership, and European university bachelor's completion tracks.
Language proficiency
Swedish language proficiency (Svenska 1/Svenska som andraspråk 1) and English language competence equivalent to Swedish English 6 (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent).
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via the MY web portal including certified transcripts, CV, and participation in an online logic and programming aptitude test.
Application fee
Zero application fee; Swedish higher vocational education admissions are free of charge.
Required documentation
Certified secondary diploma, complete grade transcripts, proof of prerequisite mathematics and English courses, and identification copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In accordance with Swedish higher education and vocational legislation, all approved programs at Malmö Yrkeshögskola are completely tuition-free for citizens of Sweden, the EU/EEA, and Switzerland. Eligible students qualify for Swedish government CSN student finance grants and loans (up to SEK 12,000 per month) throughout their studies.
