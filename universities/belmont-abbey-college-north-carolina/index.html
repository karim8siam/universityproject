Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1876 by Benedictine monks near Charlotte, North Carolina, Belmont Abbey College is a private Catholic liberal arts college recognized by the Cardinal Newman Society for academic fidelity. It provides nationally recognized undergraduate programs in business management, motorsport management, biology, and classical liberal arts rooted in the 1,500-year Benedictine tradition.

  • Motorsport management and sports entertainment marketing (partnered with Charlotte Motor Speedway)
  • Benedictine classical liberal arts, philosophical ethics, and Western civilization core
  • Pre-medical sciences, human biology, and nursing clinical pathways
  • Business administration, accounting, and government policy

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Arts in Motorsport Management (Undergraduate Degree) — First-in-the-nation bachelor's degree dedicated to the multi-billion-dollar business, event operations, and sponsorship of motorsports.
  • Bachelor of Science in Biology (Pre-Health / Pre-Med Track) (Undergraduate Degree) — Intensive laboratory degree preparing students for medical school, veterinary medicine, and biomedical research.
  • Master of Science in Leadership (Graduate Degree) — Values-based online and hybrid graduate leadership degree focusing on ethical organizational governance and strategic decision-making.
