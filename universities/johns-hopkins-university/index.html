Johns Hopkins University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Johns Hopkins University (JHU), founded in 1876 as America's first research university, is a global powerhouse consistently ranking #1 in United States higher education research and development spending. World-renowned for its School of Medicine, Bloomberg School of Public Health, Applied Physics Laboratory (APL), and Whiting School of Engineering, JHU drives foundational breakthroughs across artificial intelligence, cancer immunology, and biomedical engineering.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Biomedical Engineering (Ranked #1 in USA)

Consistently ranked the #1 Biomedical Engineering doctoral program in the world, integrating advanced engineering principles with clinical discovery across JHU's medical campuses.

PhD in Computer Science & Center for Language and Speech Processing (CLSP)

Doctoral research program within JHU's renowned Center for Language and Speech Processing, leading global innovation in large language models, machine translation, and speech recognition.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Molecular Biology and Genetics

Elite biomedical graduate program situated at the Johns Hopkins School of Medicine, training future scientific leaders in epigenetics, gene regulation, structural biology, and CRISPR technologies.

Master of Public Health (MPH) - Bloomberg School of Public Health

The world's premier Master of Public Health program at the #1 ranked Bloomberg School of Public Health, offering comprehensive training in epidemiology, biostatistics, and global health policy.

Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) in Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Multidisciplinary graduate degree hosted by the Laboratory for Computational Sensing and Robotics (LCSR), exploring medical surgical robots, bio-inspired robotics, and autonomous navigation.

Bloomberg-Kimmel Institute for Cancer Immunotherapy Postdoctoral Fellowship

Prestigious postdoctoral training program at the Bloomberg-Kimmel Institute, pioneering immune checkpoint blockade therapies, CAR-T cell engineering, and personalized cancer vaccines.

JHU Applied Physics Laboratory (APL) Graduate Research Internship

Competitive 10-12 week paid research internship at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory in Laurel, MD, developing deep space mission instrumentation, cyber operations, and quantum sensing.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year accredited US bachelor's degree or international equivalent with strong academic credentials (typically GPA 3.5+ on 4.0 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant scientific or technical field; exceptional academic record, demonstrated prior research experience, and strong faculty alignment.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT minimum 100 or IELTS Academic overall band score 7.0 or higher.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of Purpose (1-2 pages) articulating specific research interests, scientific background, and target JHU faculty labs.
Application fee
$75 to $115 non-refundable application fee (fee waivers available for eligible applicants upon request).
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, statement of purpose, curriculum vitae, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, and English language test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

All admitted full-time doctoral (PhD) students at Johns Hopkins University receive full financial support, consisting of a 100% tuition waiver (worth over $63,000/year), full individual health insurance and dental coverage, and a guaranteed minimum living stipend of $43,000 to $48,000 per year for five years through teaching and research assistantships. Terminal Master's tuition is approximately $62,000/year, with competitive departmental fellowships available.
