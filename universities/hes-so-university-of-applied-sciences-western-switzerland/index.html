HES-SO: Engineering, Life Sciences & Design in Western Switzerland

The University of Applied Sciences and Arts Western Switzerland (HES-SO - Haute école spécialisée de Suisse occidentale) is Switzerland's largest university of applied sciences network with over 21,000 students across six French-speaking cantons (Fribourg, Geneva, Jura, Neuchâtel, Valais, Vaud), celebrated for innovation in microtechnology, bioengineering, and health sciences.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Swiss Federal Government Excellence Scholarship at HES-SO

Prestigious federal scholarship granted by the Swiss Confederation to foreign researchers conducting cooperative doctoral or postdoctoral investigations in engineering or life sciences at HES-SO.

Master of Science in Life Sciences (Biotechnology & Chemical Engineering)

Multi-cantonal master's program based in Sion and Fribourg offering specializations in Applied Biosciences, Chemical Development & Production, and Viticulture & Enology.

SNSF Cooperative Doctoral Position in Microengineering & Medical Implant Sensors

Four-year cooperative doctoral fellowship conducted jointly with EPFL and HE-Arc (Neuchâtel), developing bio-resorbable pressure sensors and microfluidic diagnostic implants.

Swiss Innosuisse Alpine Hydropower & High-Altitude Glacial Lake PV Project

Applied energy engineering project based at HES-SO Valais-Wallis designing floating photovoltaic systems on alpine reservoir lakes to maximize winter solar yield in sub-zero climates.

Health Valley Geneva & Vaud MedTech Device Curricular Practicum

Six-month professional engineering placement embedded within medical technology manufacturers and bio-diagnostic firms in the Swiss Health Valley along Lake Geneva.

HES-SO Foundation Precision Micro-Manufacturing Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Haute école Arc Ingénierie in Saint-Imier focusing on ultra-short laser micromachining, surface functionalization, and micro-optics.

Professor of Artificial Intelligence & Embedded Edge Computing

Permanent professorship at the School of Engineering and Architecture of Fribourg (HEIA-FR), teaching embedded AI, tinyML, and leading funded Innosuisse industry research.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree from a recognized university or university of applied sciences in a corresponding discipline with minimum grade 5.0 (Swiss scale) or ECTS B.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (M.Sc., M.A.) with distinction; doctoral degrees are conferred cooperatively with Swiss cantonal universities (UNIGE, UNIL, EPFL).
Language proficiency
French B2/C1 for French-medium curricula; English B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90) for international Master of Science tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of academic purpose, portfolio of scientific or engineering work, and detailed CV in Swiss format.
Application fee
CHF 150 administrative application dossier fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcripts with ECTS credit details, CV, identity document, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for Swiss and EU/EEA students is CHF 500 to CHF 750 per semester (CHF 1,000 to 1,500 / ~$1,150–1,720 USD per year). Tuition for non-EU international students is CHF 1,050 to 1,500 per semester. Doctoral and scientific collaborators hold salaried Swiss federal contracts starting at CHF 60,000.
