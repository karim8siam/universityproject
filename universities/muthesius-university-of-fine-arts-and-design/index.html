Institutional Overview

Muthesius University of Fine Arts and Design (Muthesius Kunsthochschule) in Kiel is the sole higher institution of fine arts and design in the northern state of Schleswig-Holstein. Renowned for its experimental studio pedagogy, Muthesius offers degrees in fine art, industrial design, communication design, spatial strategies, and art education.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Interdisciplinary workshops for ceramics, glass, woodworking, printmaking, and digital media
  • Pioneering Master of Arts in Spatial Strategies (Raumstrategien) exploring public interventions
  • Industrial Design department emphasizing sustainable marine technologies and eco-materials
  • Exhibition opportunities in the university gallery (Spichernhöfe) and international art fairs

Featured Academic Degrees & Curricula

Bachelor's Degree

Bachelor of Arts in Fine Art (Freie Kunst)

Eight-semester studio degree focusing on painting, sculpture, conceptual art, and experimental media installations.

Master's Degree

Master of Arts in Spatial Strategies (Raumstrategien)

Advanced interdisciplinary postgraduate program interrogating architectural space, urban curation, and site-specific installations.

Bachelor's Degree

Bachelor of Arts in Industrial Design

Studio-based design program combining ergonomics, circular material design, transport systems, and digital 3D prototyping.
