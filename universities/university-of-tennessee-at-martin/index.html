University of Tennessee at Martin

UT Martin · Martin, Tennessee, United States

The University of Tennessee at Martin (UT Martin / UTM) is a premier public comprehensive regional university located in Northwest Tennessee, established in 1900. Renowned for its dedicated 640-acre teaching and demonstration farm, veterinary health technology, agricultural business, mechanical engineering, and teacher education, UT Martin delivers high-impact experiential degrees within the UT system.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UT Martin encompasses five colleges: the College of Agriculture and Applied Sciences, College of Engineering and Natural Sciences, College of Business and Global Affairs, College of Education, Health, and Behavioral Sciences, and the College of Humanities and Fine Arts. The campus features the Ned Ray McWherter Agricultural Complex and the Latimer-Smith STEM Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students, evaluating high school core GPA (minimum 2.7). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
