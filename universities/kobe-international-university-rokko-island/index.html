Kobe International University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1968 and relocated in 1995 to a modern waterfront campus on Rokko Island in Kobe, Kobe International University (KIU) is an Anglican educational institution dedicated to global economic development and compassionate human rehabilitation. KIU features two distinguished faculties: the Faculty of Economics (with departments in International Economics and Tourism/Business Management) and the Faculty of Rehabilitation (training licensed physical therapists).

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
EJU (Examination for Japanese University Admission) for international students; secondary diploma
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Japanese & English (Bilingual international business courses available)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
¥20,000 (Entrance exam fee)
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees and student contributions vary across degree levels and residency status. Verified opportunity records list typical costs between ¥850,000/year (Economics) / ¥1,450,000/year (Physical Therapy) before tuition reduction and departmental funding packages.
