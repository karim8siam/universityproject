Nevada Christian Institute

Nevada Christian · Las Vegas, Nevada, United States

Nevada Christian Institute is a private biblical and ministerial higher education institution in Las Vegas, Nevada. Dedicated to preparing pastoral leaders, Christian counselors, and non-profit directors for urban community outreach.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Nevada Christian Institute offers accredited associate, bachelor's, and certificate programs in biblical studies, Christian leadership, pastoral counseling, and church administration. Curriculum emphasizes practical urban ministry in the greater Las Vegas metropolitan area.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require a high school diploma or GED, personal statement of Christian faith and ministerial calling, pastoral endorsement letter, and character references.
