Academic overview & excellence

Operating from its tranquil learning campus in Heerde (Gelderland), Children at Risk Training delivers specialized postsecondary and professional training in childhood trauma counseling, post-crisis rehabilitation, child protection policy, and family restoration. In cooperation with international universities and NGOs (such as University of the Nations and UNICEF partners), the institute conducts clinical field research across disaster and conflict zones worldwide.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a completed secondary school education or bachelor's degree in psychology, education, nursing, or social work. Applicants must demonstrate emotional maturity, submit background clearance certificates, and complete an interview with the admissions committee. English language proficiency is required for international field courses.
