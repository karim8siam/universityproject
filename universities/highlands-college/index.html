Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Birmingham · Higher Education in Ministry, Leadership & Social Sciences. Highlands College is an accredited higher education institution in Birmingham, Alabama, dedicated to developing leaders through rigorous academic degrees, professional leadership training, and hands-on practicums. Offering degree credentials in ministry leadership, community advocacy, and organizational management, Highlands pairs experiential learning with values-based academic study.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or recognized international secondary school completion certificate with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0. Admissions interview and personal statement required. TOEFL iBT 70+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants whose secondary instruction was conducted in a language other than English. Priority fall admission deadline March 1, 2027; Regular rolling admission closes July 15, 2027.
