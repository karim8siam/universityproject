Institution Overview & Profile

The Deutsche Hochschule für Prävention und Gesundheitsmanagement (DHfPG) Studienzentrum Stuttgart is a state-recognized, system-accredited private university of applied sciences specializing in dual bachelor and master degree programs that integrate academic theory with concurrent enterprise employment in healthcare, fitness economics, clinical prevention, and corporate health management.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Private Dual University of Applied Sciences (Duale Hochschule)
Main CampusStudienzentrum Stuttgart (Fritz-Walter-Weg, NeckarPark)
AccreditationAHPGS / German Science and Humanities Council (Wissenschaftsrat)
Admissions RouteDual contract with approved healthcare/sports partner enterprise
Language of InstructionGerman (TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2 / CEFR C1) with English module offerings
Financial Aid & GrantsFull tuition reimbursement by employer plus monthly enterprise training salary

Key Academic Strengths

  • Pioneering dual study model combining distant modular e-learning with compact on-site seminar blocks in Stuttgart
  • 100% employer tuition coverage standard across dual corporate partnership contracts
  • Interdisciplinary curriculum linking preventative medicine, exercise physiology, and digital health analytics
  • Network of over 4,300 corporate healthcare, clinic, rehabilitation, and sports partner enterprises
