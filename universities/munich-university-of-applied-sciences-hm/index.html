Institutional Overview

Munich University of Applied Sciences (Hochschule München / HM), established in 1971, is the second largest university of applied sciences in Germany with over 18,000 students. Located in the innovation capital of Munich, HM is renowned for mechanical and automotive engineering, computer science, industrial design, architecture, and applied social sciences.
