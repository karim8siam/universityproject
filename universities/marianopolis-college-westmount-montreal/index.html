Marianopolis College: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1908 and located on an 11-acre historic campus in Westmount, Marianopolis College is Quebec's premier private non-profit English college (CEGEP). Consistently graduating students with the province's highest R-Scores, Marianopolis prepares students for admission into prestigious medical schools, law faculties, engineering faculties, and global universities across Canada, the United States, and the UK.
