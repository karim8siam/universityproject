University of California, Merced

UC Merced · Merced, California, United States

The University of California, Merced (UC Merced) is the 10th and newest campus in the world-renowned University of California system, founded in 2005 in California's San Joaquin Valley. Recognized as the fastest-growing public research university in the United States and the only American research campus where all buildings are LEED-certified, UC Merced is acclaimed for solar energy technology, Sierra Nevada water sustainability, and bioengineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UC Merced comprises the School of Engineering, School of Natural Sciences, and School of Social Sciences, Humanities and Arts. It operates unique research enterprises including the Sierra Nevada Research Institute (SNRI), the Health Sciences Research Institute (HSRI), and the University of California Solar (UC Solar) research consortium.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are evaluated through the University of California application (holistic review; test-free). Graduate and doctoral applicants require a baccalaureate degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, transcripts, statements of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
