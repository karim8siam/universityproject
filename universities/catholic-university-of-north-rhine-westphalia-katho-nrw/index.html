Catholic University of North Rhine-Westphalia (KatHO NRW)

The Catholic University of North Rhine-Westphalia (Katholische Hochschule Nordrhein-Westfalen - KatHO NRW) is Germany’s largest state-accredited church-sponsored university of applied sciences, renowned for social work, healthcare pedagogy, practical theology, and childhood education.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1971 by merging several historic academies of social pedagogy, KatHO NRW educates over 5,300 students across four dynamic campuses: Cologne, Münster, Paderborn, and Aachen. While sponsored by the Roman Catholic dioceses of North Rhine-Westphalia, KatHO NRW is fully state-recognized and welcomes students of all backgrounds and faiths. The university operates prestigious research institutes such as the German Institute for Addiction and Prevention Research (DISuP) and the Institute for Applied Care Research (IAP), shaping social legislation, community mental health policies, and healthcare ethics across the Federal Republic.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Arts in Klinische Sozialarbeit (Clinical Social Work) (Master's) — Accredited multi-campus graduate program focusing on psychosocial diagnostics, psychotherapy intervention models, and social counseling in psychiatric settings.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Soziale Arbeit (Social Work) (Bachelor's) — Flagship seven-semester professional degree conferring the state license as a Social Worker/Social Pedagogue (Staatlich anerkannte/r Sozialarbeiter/in).
  • Master of Science in Pflegewissenschaft (Nursing Science) (Master's) — Advanced academic degree in clinical evidence-based nursing, healthcare organizational design, and palliative care pedagogy.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Kindheitspädagogik (Childhood Pedagogy) (Bachelor's) — Professional curriculum qualifying educators in developmental psychology, family advisory systems, and inclusive early-childhood institutions.
