Kettering University

Kettering Univ · Flint, Michigan, United States

Kettering University is a private STEM and business university in Flint, Michigan, globally renowned for its cooperative education model, automotive engineering, electric vehicles, and robotics innovation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Kettering combines intense academic study terms with paid, professional cooperative work experiences at over 400 corporate partners. It operates the Kettering University Mobility Research Center (autonomous vehicle test track) and Crash Safety Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school performance, mathematics rigor (calculus recommended), and technical interests under test-optional policies. Graduate engineering admissions require an ABET-accredited bachelor's degree and minimum 3.0 GPA.
