Academic overview & excellence

Established as the common digital gateway for Ontario's public college ecosystem, the Ontario Colleges Higher Education Consortium coordinates academic program data, credentials validation, and transfer pathways across more than 5,000 postsecondary programs. Headquartered in Guelph, the consortium partners with colleges such as Humber, Seneca, George Brown, Conestoga, and Sheridan to facilitate seamless articulation between applied diplomas, undergraduate bachelor degrees, and postgraduate certificates.

Research admissions & criteria

Prospective Canadian and international students apply directly through the centralized ontariocolleges.ca admissions portal. Academic eligibility requires an Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD) with required senior courses or international secondary equivalent. Postgraduate certificates require a completed postsecondary diploma or bachelor's degree. English language requirements typically specify IELTS 6.0 (undergraduate) or 6.5 (graduate).
