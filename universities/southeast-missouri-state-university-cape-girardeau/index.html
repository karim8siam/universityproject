Southeast Missouri State University

SEMO · Cape Girardeau, Missouri, United States

Southeast Missouri State University (SEMO) is a comprehensive public university overlooking the Mississippi River in Cape Girardeau, Missouri. Celebrating over 150 years of academic excellence, SEMO is recognized nationwide for its pioneering cybersecurity program (designated an NSA Center of Academic Excellence), unmanned aircraft systems (drones), accredited business school, and premier performing arts conservatories at the River Campus.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SEMO encompasses the College of Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics, the Harrison College of Business and Computing, the College of Humanities and Social Sciences, the College of Education, Health and Human Studies, and the Holland College of Arts and Media. It features cutting-edge cybersecurity cyber ranges, advanced manufacturing labs, and the 252-acre David M. Barton Agriculture Research Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), college prep coursework, and optional ACT/SAT scores. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75 minimum GPA (3.0 for computer science and cybersecurity), statement of purpose, and academic letters of reference.
