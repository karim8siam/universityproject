Université de Montréal: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Université de Montréal (UdeM) is one of the world's premier French-language research universities and an international focal point for artificial intelligence, machine learning, biomedical engineering, and health sciences. Home to Mila (Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute), UdeM fosters transformative graduate programs and multi-million-dollar laboratory networks.

UdeM International Student Scholarship of Merit & Tuition Fee Exemptions

Merit-based international tuition fee exemption awards reducing tuition fees for outstanding international graduate students enrolled at Université de Montréal.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Computer Science & Deep Learning (Mila Collaboration)

Doctoral research program conducted in collaboration with Mila (Quebec AI Institute), focusing on deep learning foundations, neural scaling, reinforcement learning, and AI alignment.

Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence

Rigorous thesis-based and course-based Master of Science in Computer Science covering algorithm design, machine learning architectures, automated reasoning, and data engineering.

IVADO & CRM Doctoral Research Fellowships in Data Science & Operational Mathematics

Excellence fellowships sponsored by IVADO and Centre de Recherches Mathématiques (CRM) for PhD researchers investigating high-dimensional optimization, operations research, and stochastic systems.

Master of Science (MSc) in Biomedical Engineering & Neural Prosthetics

Interdisciplinary graduate degree exploring neurotechnology, biomaterials, medical signal processing, and tissue engineering at the interface of medicine and engineering.

IRIC Postdoctoral & Graduate Research Fellowships in Cancer Systems Biology

High-impact research fellowship at the Institute for Research in Immunology and Cancer (IRIC) dedicated to unraveling drug resistance, chromatin dynamics, and targeted cancer therapies.

Mila International AI Graduate Research Internship & Visiting Scholar Cohort

Research internship pairing international graduate researchers with leading Mila professors to work on generative models, reinforcement learning, and sustainable AI infrastructure.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year undergraduate bachelor's degree with minimum GPA 3.0/4.3 (B average) or international equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science or Master of Arts in relevant domain with minimum GPA 3.3/4.3; approved research proposal and faculty advisor confirmation.
Language proficiency
French proficiency (B2/C1 TFI, DALF or TCF) for French-taught programs; English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 90+ or IELTS 6.5-7.0) for English-designated research programs and Mila AI cohorts.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 3-5 page research proposal outlining research methodology, literature review, and alignment with department laboratories.
Application fee
CAD $120 non-refundable application fee paid online.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV, 2-3 academic recommendation letters, research intent letter, and language test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition at UdeM averages CAD $18,000–$24,000 per year for Master's programs, while doctoral students typically benefit from UdeM International Student Exemption Scholarships reducing tuition to Quebec domestic rates (approx. CAD $3,800/year) alongside departmental research assistantships and FRQNT awards ($25,000+/year).
