Academic overview & excellence

Founded upon a heritage of engineering and technical colleges dating back to the 19th century, HTWK Leipzig offers comprehensive programs across Civil Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering & Information Technology, Media, and Business Administration. The university houses state-of-the-art testing centers for concrete and timber construction, the Leipzig Research Center for Digital Technologies, and the Life Science Engineering laboratory.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a recognized higher education entrance certificate (Abitur or equivalent). International applicants apply through uni-assist. Master's admissions require an above-average bachelor's degree in a corresponding subject. German proficiency of DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4 is required for German-taught tracks; English tracks require IELTS 6.5.
