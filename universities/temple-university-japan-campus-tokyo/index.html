Temple University, Japan Campus

Temple University, Japan Campus (TUJ) is the oldest and largest full-degree-granting American university in Japan, designated as an official Foreign University by MEXT, awarding Philadelphia Temple University degrees in Tokyo.

Academic overview & research priorities

Established in 1982 in Tokyo (now located in a purpose-built campus in Sangen-jaya, Setagaya-ku, adjacent to Showa Women's University), TUJ enables students to earn accredited American undergraduate, master's (LL.M., Management, TESOL), and doctoral degrees entirely in English in Tokyo. TUJ is celebrated for global business studies, computer science, international law, and cultural studies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5-3.0), personal statement, and counselor recommendation. Standardized tests (SAT/ACT) are optional. Non-native English speakers require TOEFL iBT 79+, IELTS 6.0+, or Duolingo 110+. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree (GPA 3.0+), statement of purpose, CV, and recommendation letters. TUJ sponsors Japanese student visas for full-time international students.
