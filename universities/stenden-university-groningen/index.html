NHL Stenden: Circular Plastics, Design-Based Education & Hospitality Guide

NHL Stenden University of Applied Sciences (operating campuses in Groningen and Leeuwarden) is a pioneering Dutch institution globally celebrated for its Design-Based Education (DBE) model, the internationally acclaimed Circular Plastics research center, and Europe's largest hotel management school.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 or 240 ECTS) in chemistry, materials science, engineering, or hospitality/business management.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent PhD degrees; focus on accredited professional Master of Science and Master of Arts programs.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 to 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 to 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 80 to 90.
Research statement / Proposal
A 500-1,000 word motivation letter detailing alignment with the Design-Based Education (DBE) collaborative learning philosophy.
Application fee
Zero application fee via Studielink.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, certified transcripts, CV, motivation letter, and passport identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 annually. Non-EU/EEA international students pay approximately €9,500 to €11,800 per academic year. The NL Scholarship and NHL Stenden Excellence Scholarship award €5,000 to top international master's candidates.
