Brigham Young University–Hawaii (BYU–Hawaii)

Explore ocean and marine sciences, Pacific Island cultural leadership, and international business at BYU-Hawaii on the North Shore of Oahu.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Brigham Young University–Hawaii is a private baccalaureate university on the North Shore of Oahu. Fostering an exceptionally diverse student body representing over 70 countries from Asia and the Pacific, BYU-Hawaii integrates spiritual development, rigorous undergraduate academics, and practical career development in close synergy with the Polynesian Cultural Center.

Institution type
Private Liberal Arts University (Sponsored by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints)
Campus location

Accreditation

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Most internationally diverse undergraduate university campus in the United States
  • I-WORK program providing full financial self-reliance and loan-free education
  • Jonathan Napela Center for Pacific Island Studies and Polynesian cultural revitalization
  • Marine biology field stations directly on Oahu's coral reef coastal waters

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Marine Biology (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year coastal oceanography and coral reef ecology degree with extensive scuba, boat, and reef monitoring laboratory fieldwork.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Pacific Islands Studies (Undergraduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary major covering Oceanic indigenous governance, oral traditions, maritime navigation, and environmental resilience.
  • Bachelor of Science in Hospitality and Tourism Management (Undergraduate Degree) — AACSB-aligned degree program integrating real-world operations, financial analytics, and eco-tourism stewardship.
