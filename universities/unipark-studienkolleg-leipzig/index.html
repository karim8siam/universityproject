Unipark Studienkolleg Leipzig

Unipark Leipzig · Leipzig, Saxony, Germany

Unipark Studienkolleg Leipzig is a specialized international academic preparatory institute in Saxony, preparing non-EU students for the state university entrance assessment (Feststellungsprüfung - FSP) in M, T, and W disciplines.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Located in central Leipzig, Unipark combines small-cohort STEM and economics foundation courses with intensive Telc/TestDaF German language certification, university admission counseling, and laboratory science instruction.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires high school graduation from the applicant's home country, certified school grade transcripts, and German language proficiency at level B1 or B2.
