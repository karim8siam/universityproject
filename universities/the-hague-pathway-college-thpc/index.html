The Hague Pathway College (THPC)

Secure guaranteed progression into top-tier Dutch bachelor's degrees in international law, business, and engineering at THPC in The Hague.

Institution Overview & Profile

The Hague Pathway College (THPC), situated directly on the campus of The Hague University of Applied Sciences (THUAS) in the international city of peace and justice, provides accredited International Foundation Year programmes in Management, Social Sciences, Technology, and Design in partnership with Navitas, offering guaranteed direct progression into English-taught Bachelor's degrees at THUAS.

Institution TypeInternational Higher Education Pathway College (in partnership with THUAS & Navitas)
Main CampusThe Hague University of Applied Sciences Main Campus, Johanna Westerdijkplein, The Hague
AccreditationDutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science Recognized Pathways / Navitas Global
Admissions RouteDirect online application via Navitas International Portal (intakes in Sept & Jan)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS Academic for UKVI/International 5.0 to 5.5 depending on pathway)
Financial Aid & GrantsTHPC Navitas Academic Merit Scholarships & THUAS Holland Scholarship Eligibility

Key Academic Strengths

  • Direct integration into the state-of-the-art campus of The Hague University of Applied Sciences (THUAS)
  • Guaranteed progression into world-renowned degrees: International Law, European Studies, Industrial Design
  • Located in The Hague, the international legal capital hosting the International Court of Justice and OPCW
  • Comprehensive visa, housing, and Dutch residence permit (IND) assistance for international students
