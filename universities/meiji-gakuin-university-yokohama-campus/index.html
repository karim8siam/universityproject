Academic overview & excellence

Set on expansive green hills in Totsuka, Yokohama, this campus is home to MGU's Faculty of International Studies, Faculty of Sociology, Faculty of Economics, and the prestigious International Peace Research Institute (PRIME). Operating under the historic Christian founding motto 'Do for Others,' Meiji Gakuin is an academic pioneer in global peacebuilding, human rights advocacy, international development economics, and multicultural coexistence (tabunka kyōsei). Campus facilities include a high-tech international conference hall, multilingual language learning commons, and specialized peace archives.

Research admissions & criteria

Admissions to undergraduate degrees at Meiji Gakuin University Yokohama Campus are conducted via General Entrance Exams, Recommendation Exams, and specialized International Student EJU screenings. Graduate School admissions (Peace Studies, International Studies, Sociology, Economics) require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA, submission of a rigorous graduation thesis abstract and 2,000-character research proposal (Kenkyu Keikakusho), language proficiency (JLPT N1 for Japanese tracks or TOEFL iBT 85+/IELTS 6.5+ for English programs), and an academic interview.
