Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1939 by the Sisters of Saint Dominic in suburban Caldwell, New Jersey, Caldwell University is a private Catholic university celebrated for its internationally recognized Ph.D. and master's programs in Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA), art therapy, nursing, and computer science.

Institution type

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Applied Behavior Analysis (Ph.D. and M.A. accredited by ABAI)
  • Art therapy and mental health counseling (pioneering CACREP/AATA programs)
  • Nursing and public health clinical training in metropolitan New York networks
  • Business administration, cybersecurity, and criminal justice

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Ph.D. in Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) (Doctoral Degree) — Internationally distinguished doctoral program preparing researchers and clinical supervisors for autism and developmental interventions.
  • Master of Arts in Clinical Mental Health Counseling with Art Therapy (Graduate Degree) — Accredited dual counseling and art therapy program leading to licensure as a professional counselor (LPC) and registered art therapist (ATR).
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year CCNE-accredited program with high NCLEX pass rates and clinical rotations across Northern New Jersey medical centres.
