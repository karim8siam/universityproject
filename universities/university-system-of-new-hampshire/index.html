University System of New Hampshire

USNH · Concord, New Hampshire, United States

The University System of New Hampshire (USNH) is the largest provider of postsecondary education in the Granite State, comprised of three accredited public institutions: the flagship research institution University of New Hampshire (UNH) in Durham, Plymouth State University, and Keene State College. Conducting over $160 million in annual research, USNH is ranked among the top NASA-funded public universities globally in space plasma science, marine aquaculture, and cybersecurity.
