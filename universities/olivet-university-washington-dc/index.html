Olivet University

Olivet University · Washington, DC, United States

Olivet University is an international private Christian university operating educational sites across the United States, including its Washington DC campus, San Francisco campus, and Anza main campus. Renowned for theological studies, digital journalism, computer technology, and sacred music.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Olivet hosts the Olivet Theological Seminary, the Ralph D. Winter Library, the Olivet Institute of Technology, and the Jubilee College of Music. It prepares multinational Christian leaders, mission workers, and technological innovators across degree programs from bachelor's to doctorate.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require high school graduation for undergraduate degrees, or an accredited bachelor's/master's for graduate degrees. Applicants submit transcripts, Christian ministry statements, and demonstration of English language proficiency (TOEFL 70+ / IELTS 6.0+).
