Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center at Amarillo

TTUHSC Amarillo · Amarillo, Texas, United States

Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center (TTUHSC) at Amarillo is the regional academic healthcare and medical research hub of the Texas Panhandle. Housing the prestigious Jerry H. Hodge School of Pharmacy, the School of Medicine Amarillo Campus, and the Harrington Cancer and Health Foundation, the campus delivers world-class clinical care, biomedical discovery, and pharmaceutical innovations.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TTUHSC Amarillo operates specialized divisions in Pharmaceutical Sciences, Pediatric Medicine, Surgery, and Internal Medicine. It features the Center of Excellence for Translational Neuroscience and Therapeutics, the Clinical Research Institute, and state-of-the-art sterile compounding and mass-spectrometry drug discovery laboratories.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Professional programs require specialized centralized applications (PharmCAS for Pharmacy, TMDSAS for Medicine). Prerequisites include rigorous organic chemistry, biochemistry, biology, and physics coursework. Applicants must complete interviews, CASPer situational judgment assessments, and submit professional evaluation letters.
