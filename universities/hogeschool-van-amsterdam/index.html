Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (AUAS / Hogeschool van Amsterdam) is one of the Netherlands' largest universities of applied sciences, situated in the heart of Amsterdam's innovation districts. Renowned for its Centre for Applied AI, Digital Society School, and Smart Cities initiative, AUAS pairs hands-on professional higher education with real-world urban technology partnerships.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Master of Science in Applied Artificial Intelligence (Responsible Urban AI)

One-year professional Master of Science preparing AI engineers to deploy ethical, human-centric machine learning systems in healthcare, smart mobility, and municipal public services.

AUAS Talent Scholarship (ATS) & NL Scholarship for International Masters

Merit-based scholarships awarded to talented non-EEA students enrolled in English-taught programs at Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences.

Master of Science in Digital Design (International Program)

Intensive one-year full-time master's program taught in English, educating design leaders in speculative design, ethics of emerging technology, and complex system interaction.

Centre for Applied AI Practice-Based Research Fellowship in Responsible IT

Two-year applied research post at the Centre for Applied AI, designing audit toolkits and transparent algorithmic decision pipelines for public municipal administration in Amsterdam.

Master in Urban Technology & Smart City Engineering

One-year applied master's program analyzing municipal energy transitions, urban sensor networks, electric vehicle infrastructure, and climate adaptation in dense metropolitan zones.

Digital Society School (DSS) Design Transformation Fellowship

Twenty-week hands-on digital transformation residency at the Digital Society School, working in multidisciplinary teams alongside United Nations agencies on civic digital tools.

AUAS Smart Mobility Lab Graduate Engineering Internship

Six-month research internship analyzing micro-mobility patterns, smart cycling routes, and automated shuttle integration across the Greater Amsterdam transport network.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A relevant bachelor's degree in computer science, digital design, business information systems, or urban planning.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree; practice-based doctoral candidacies conducted in collaboration with research universities (e.g. University of Amsterdam or VU Amsterdam).
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, letter of motivation (approx. 500 words), and a design/code portfolio demonstrating problem-solving in digital innovation.
Application fee
€0 application fee via Studielink.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, certified transcripts, portfolio/project links, motivation letter, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is set at the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition ranges from €9,800 to €13,500 per year for bachelor's and master's degrees. Deserving international students can apply for the AUAS Talent Scholarship (ATS) offering up to 50% tuition reduction or the national NL Scholarship granting €5,000 in first-year study support.
