City St George's, University of London: Masters, PhD Studentships & Bayes Business School

City St George's, University of London represents the historic merger of City, University of London and St George's, University of London, creating a health, medical science, technology, business (Bayes Business School), and journalism powerhouse in the heart of London.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Upper second-class honours degree (UK 2:1) or international equivalent in an appropriate academic discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with merit or distinction (or international equivalent), accompanied by a formal supervisory endorsement.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-test); Bayes Business School and Journalism require 7.0 (minimum 6.5 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
For research degrees: 2,000–3,000 word proposal detailing aims, literature context, methodology, and ethical considerations.
Application fee
£0 for most postgraduate courses; £50 for Bayes MSc applications.
Required documentation
Degree certificates, official transcripts, two academic references, CV, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Postgraduate tuition typically ranges from £21,500 to £33,000 per year for international students (Bayes MSc programs may range up to £34,500). Full PhD studentships funded by UKRI and institutional endowments cover tuition fees plus an annual London-weighted stipend of £21,237.
