College of Southern Idaho

The College of Southern Idaho (CSI) in Twin Falls is a premier public community college in the Magic Valley, acclaimed for agricultural science, aquaculture, water resource technology, veterinary nursing, and advanced mechatronics.

Academic overview & excellence

Spanning a beautifully landscaped 350-acre campus in the Snake River Canyon region, the College of Southern Idaho is one of the fastest-growing comprehensive public institutions in the Intermountain West. CSI operates specialized academic facilities including the Herrett Center for Arts and Science (featuring the Faulkner Planetarium and modern observatory), a campus aquaculture fish hatchery, an equine and livestock pavilion, and advanced manufacturing automation labs. The college serves as the agricultural research hub for Idaho's dairy, trout, and potato industries.

Research admissions & criteria

CSI offers an open admissions policy for secondary school graduates seeking associate degrees or technical certificates. Selective admissions apply to competitive programs such as Registered Nursing, Veterinary Technology, and Commercial Pilot Aviation. International applicants must submit official secondary school transcripts (evaluated by NACES members), proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 61 or IELTS 5.5), and financial guarantee documentation for the I-20 student visa.
