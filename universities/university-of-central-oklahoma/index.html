University of Central Oklahoma

UCO · Edmond, Oklahoma, United States

The University of Central Oklahoma (UCO), established in 1890 in Edmond, Oklahoma, is the state's oldest public institution of higher education. Operating as a major metropolitan university in the Oklahoma City area, UCO is internationally acclaimed for housing the W. Roger Webb Forensic Science Institute—the largest forensic science education program in North America—along with renowned programs in contemporary music (ACM@UCO), nursing, and engineering physics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UCO delivers programs through the College of Mathematics and Science, the Forensic Science Institute, the College of Fine Arts and Design, the College of Business, and the College of Education and Professional Studies. Its campus features the Biomolecular Research Center and state-of-the-art forensic ballistics, DNA profiling, and digital evidence laboratories.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission operates on rolling deadlines through UCO's direct portal (test-optional; recommended GPA 2.7+). Graduate applicants must possess an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, submit transcripts, letters of recommendation, and department-specific materials.
