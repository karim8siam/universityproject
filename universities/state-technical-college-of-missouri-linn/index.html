State Technical College of Missouri

State Tech Missouri · Linn, Missouri, United States

State Technical College of Missouri ('State Tech'), located on a modern 360-acre campus in Linn, Missouri, is the state's only public technical college with a statewide mission. Acclaimed as one of the best community and technical colleges in the nation by WalletHub and Forbes, State Tech maintains an extraordinary 99% graduate career placement rate, offering elite training in nuclear technology, aviation maintenance, precision machining, and civil engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

State Tech operates specialized multi-million-dollar training facilities including the Utility Technician Training Center, the Vehicle and Power Center, the Nilges Technology Center, and an on-campus airport for aviation maintenance. The curriculum is developed in direct lockstep with Fortune 500 industrial partners.

Research admissions & international qualifications

State Tech practices open technical admissions for applicants with a high school diploma or GED equivalent. High-demand programs (such as Nuclear Technology, Aviation Maintenance, and Physical Therapist Assistant) require specific ACT/Accuplacer math scores, technical aptitude testing, and interview evaluations.
