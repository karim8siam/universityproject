Institution Overview & Profile

Liverpool John Moores University International Study Centre (LJMU ISC), located at Rodney House on Mount Pleasant in the vibrant knowledge quarter of Liverpool, provides premier International Foundation Years and International Year One pathway programs in partnership with Study Group, opening guaranteed progression routes into hundreds of undergraduate and postgraduate degrees at LJMU.

Institution TypeUniversity International Pathway Centre (LJMU in partnership with Study Group)
Main CampusRodney House Campus, Mount Pleasant, Liverpool Knowledge Quarter
AccreditationOffice for Students (OfS) Registered / Quality Assurance Agency (QAA) Monitored
Admissions RouteDirect online application via Study Group international portal
Language of InstructionEnglish (Academic IELTS for UKVI 4.5 to 5.5 depending on pathway route)
Financial Aid & GrantsLJMU ISC International Progression Scholarships (£1,000–£3,000)

Key Academic Strengths

  • Direct campus integration with full access to LJMU Aldham Robarts and Avril Robarts libraries
  • Guaranteed degree progression to Liverpool John Moores University upon reaching academic pass thresholds
  • World-leading sports science faculty (LJMU is consistently ranked top 10 globally for sports science)
  • Dedicated English language tuition and small interactive seminar groups to bridge international learning styles

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Pre-Degree Foundation

International Foundation Year in Engineering, Computing and Life Sciences

Three-term intensive pathway preparing students for entry into Year 1 of BEng Mechanical Engineering, BSc Computer Science, or MPharm Pharmacy.

First-Year Degree Equivalent

International Year One in Business and Management

Credit-bearing first-year equivalent providing direct progression into Year 2 of BSc Business Management or Accounting and Finance.

Postgraduate Preparation

Pre-Master's in Engineering and Science

One or two-term postgraduate bridging programme offering research methodology, academic English, and subject preparation for MSc programmes.
