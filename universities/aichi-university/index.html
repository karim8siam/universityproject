Aichi University (愛知大学)

Founded in 1946 with historic roots in the East Asia Common Culture Academy (Toa Dobun Shoin), Aichi University is a distinguished private university in Japan with urban campuses in Nagoya and Toyohashi. Renowned for its Graduate School of Modern Chinese Studies, Law, Economics, and the Institute for Contemporary Chinese Studies (ICCS), review verified master's and doctoral degrees, international tuition fee reductions, paid assistantships, and faculty profiles.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Aichi University operates seven academic faculties and six graduate schools offering master's and doctoral curricula:

30% International Tuition Reduction

Official university financial support system granting an automatic 30% tuition fee reduction to privately financed international students holding College Student visa status.

JASSO Monbukagakusho Honors Scholarship

Japanese government financial award providing ¥48,000 per month (¥576,000/year) in living maintenance for high-achieving international graduate students.

PhD in Modern Chinese Studies

Three-year doctoral program within Japan's pioneering Graduate School of Modern Chinese Studies, affiliated with ICCS, investigating Chinese politics, economy, and society.

Master of Laws (LL.M.) in Civil & Public Law

Two-year master's program in the Graduate School of Law providing specialized advanced study in commercial law, civil liability, international economic law, and constitutional systems.

Master's in Economics & International Communication

Two-year Master's degree programs with research seminars in international finance, open macroeconomics, global trade, cultural anthropology, and comparative languages.

ICCS Research Assistantship (RA)

Paid research assistantship positions (¥1,200–¥1,800/hr) inside the Institute for Contemporary Chinese Studies for doctoral candidates participating in sponsored Asian studies projects.

Regional Industry & International Fieldwork Internship

Credit-bearing internship placements in Greater Nagoya with automotive suppliers, international trading companies, and regional export associations.

Graduate Teaching Assistantship (TA) Pathway

Salaried academic tutoring positions (¥1,200–¥1,600/hr) for enrolled graduate students assisting faculty with undergraduate seminars, language labs, and examination logistics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education culminating in an accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent recognised by Japan's MEXT).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognised Master's degree or professional degree in a related academic discipline.
Language proficiency
Instruction in most graduate divisions is conducted primarily in Japanese; JLPT N1 or N2 is required for application. English proficiency is evaluated for International Communication and Economics programs.
Research proposal (Kenkyu Keikakusho)
Mandatory. Applicants must submit a structured research plan outlining research objective, literature context, methodology, and target faculty advisors.
Application fee
Entrance examination fee is typically ¥30,000 for standard international graduate applications.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required for Japanese graduate admissions.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Aichi University is normally between ¥700,000 and ¥900,000 per year depending on the graduate school. Qualifying self-financed international students who complete the required residency verification receive an official 30% reduction on annual tuition fees, lowering costs to approximately ¥490,000 to ¥630,000 per year.

Aichi University additionally awards merit-based Study Grants (Gakusyu Shoreikin) of ¥200,000/year to high-achieving international students, Supporters' Association Scholarships of ¥100,000/year, and facilitates university nominations for MEXT and JASSO honors scholarships (¥48,000/month).

Verified faculty profiles and research leaders

Research leads and institute directors at Aichi University:

Prof. Chunli Li

Professor of Economics and Director of the Institute for Contemporary Chinese Studies (ICCS); specialist in Chinese economic development and automotive industrial organization.

Prof. Hiromoto Kaji

Professor of International Relations in the Graduate School of Modern Chinese Studies; expert in Chinese foreign policy and East Asian security architecture.

Prof. Yuki Hirose

President of Aichi University and Professor of Law; specialist in commercial law, insurance law, and civil liability with extensive institutional academic leadership.

Prof. Yutaka Kurihara

Professor of Economics; international finance specialist researching exchange rate dynamics, European economic integration, and financial market behavior.

Prof. Katsushi Tabata

Professor of Economics; researcher in open economy macroeconomics, international financial transmission mechanisms, and monetary policy shocks.
