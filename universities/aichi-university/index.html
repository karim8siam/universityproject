Aichi University (愛知大学)

Founded in 1946 with historic roots in the East Asia Common Culture Academy (Toa Dobun Shoin), Aichi University is a distinguished private university in Japan with urban campuses in Nagoya and Toyohashi. Review verified graduate programs, international tuition fee reductions, research databases, and faculty profiles before preparing application materials.

Graduate schools and research degrees

Aichi University operates seven academic faculties and six graduate schools offering two-year Master's and three-year Doctoral degree curricula: the Graduate School of Law, Graduate School of Economics, Graduate School of Business Administration, Graduate School of Modern Chinese Studies, Graduate School of International Communication, and Graduate School of Letters.

Economics & International Communication

Two-year Master's degree programs with specialized research seminars in international finance, open macroeconomics, global business, cultural anthropology, and comparative languages.

30% International Tuition Reduction

Official university financial support system granting an automatic 30% tuition fee reduction to privately financed international students holding College Student visa status.

Aichi Prefectural Scholarship

Competitive regional scholarship for international graduate research students in Aichi Prefecture, offering full tuition coverage and monthly living allowances of approximately ¥150,000.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education culminating in an accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent recognised by Japan's MEXT).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognised Master's degree or professional degree in a related academic discipline.
Language proficiency
Instruction in most graduate divisions is conducted primarily in Japanese; JLPT N1 or N2 is required for application. English proficiency is evaluated for International Communication and Economics programs.
Research proposal (Kenkyu Keikakusho)
Mandatory. Applicants must submit a structured research plan outlining research objective, literature context, methodology, and target faculty advisors.
Application fee
Entrance examination fee is typically ¥35,000 for standard international graduate applications.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, official academic transcripts, research proposal, statement of purpose, academic recommendation, and residence documentation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Aichi University is normally between ¥700,000 and ¥900,000 per year depending on the graduate school. Qualifying self-financed international students who complete the required residency verification receive an official 30% reduction on annual tuition fees, lowering costs to approximately ¥490,000 to ¥630,000 per year.

Aichi University additionally awards merit-based Study Grants (Gakusyu Shoreikin) of ¥200,000/year to high-achieving international students, Supporters' Association Scholarships of ¥100,000/year, and facilitates university nominations for MEXT and JASSO honors scholarships.

Verified faculty profiles and research areas

Research across Aichi University encompasses economics, legal studies, modern Chinese affairs, and cross-cultural communication. Below are three verified faculty members:

Prof Yuki Hirose

President of Aichi University and Professor of Law; specialist in commercial law, insurance law, and civil liability with extensive institutional academic leadership.

Prof Yutaka Kurihara

Professor of Economics; international finance specialist researching exchange rate dynamics, European economic integration, and financial market behavior.

Prof Katsushi Tabata

Professor of Economics; researcher in open economy macroeconomics, international financial transmission mechanisms, and monetary policy shocks.
