Florida Southern College

Florida Southern College (FSC) is a top-ranked comprehensive private college in Lakeland, Florida, globally famed for its Frank Lloyd Wright architecture, nationally recognized marine biology research, and AACSB-accredited business school.

Academic overview & research priorities

FSC emphasizes engaged learning across the Barney Barnett School of Business and Free Enterprise, the School of Arts and Sciences, and the Ann Blanton Edwards School of Nursing. Its lakeside campus features active research in coral reef restoration, citrus greening disease genomics, and quantitative accounting.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applications are evaluated holistically via the Common Application. Graduate applicants must submit official collegiate transcripts with a minimum 3.0 GPA, professional resumes, and program-specific letters of recommendation.
