Catholic University of Sacred Heart (Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore)

Founded in 1921 in Milan, Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore (UCSC) is the largest private university in Europe, enrolling over 40,000 students across campuses in Milan, Rome, Brescia, Piacenza, and Cremona. Home to the world-renowned Policlinico Universitario Agostino Gemelli in Rome (consistently ranked Italy's #1 hospital), UCSC leads research in clinical oncology, biotechnology, data science for management, and agricultural sustainability. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UCSC Doctoral Scholarships & Educatt Merit Awards

Three-year doctoral scholarships covering living stipends, international research travel supplements, and complete tuition waivers for top-ranking candidates in the annual doctoral competition.

UCSC PhD in Science (Dottorato in Scienze)

Three-year interdisciplinary doctoral curriculum based at the Brescia and Milan campuses, investigating experimental physics, nanostructured surfaces, biomolecular chemistry, and mathematical modeling.

UCSC Master of Science in Data Science for Management

Two-year English-taught Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in Milan, uniting statistical machine learning, predictive analytics, deep data architectures, and strategic corporate decision-making.

UCSC Master of Science in Healthcare Management

Two-year English-medium Master's program (120 ECTS) based at the School of Medicine and Policlinico Gemelli campus in Rome, preparing global managers for hospitals, pharmaceutical firms, and healthcare policy agencies.

Policlinico Universitario Agostino Gemelli IRCCS Clinical Research Placements

World-class translational and clinical oncology research placements inside Policlinico Gemelli IRCCS in Rome, investigating personalized cancer therapies, molecular biomarkers, and innovative immunotherapy regimens.

UCSC International Internship & Traineeship Programme

Structured two to six-month internship placements embedding international scholars within Milan's corporate, media, and design sectors alongside university research divisions.

UCSC Student Collaborations (Collaborazioni Studentesche)

Official university part-time employment contracts (up to 150 hours total) assisting in university libraries, computer laboratories, campus orientation desks, and international student services.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years, 180 ECTS) in relevant subject area with minimum GPA equivalent to Italian 24/30 or 75%+.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale, 120 ECTS) with high graduation mark (typically 100/110+) and successful public evaluation of research project proposal and oral examination.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to CEFR B2/C1: Academic IELTS 6.0/6.5 minimum or TOEFL iBT 80+ for English-medium graduate curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
For PhD: detailed research project proposal of 3 to 5 pages outlining research aims, state of the art, methodology, and expected scientific contributions.
Application fee
€100 application fee for international master's degree applicants.
Required documentation
University degree certificate, official transcripts with grading table, CV, motivation letter, and identity document.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for international students at UCSC are determined by an income-based bracket system (typically €3,500 to €8,900 per year for non-EU students). Doctoral candidates holding university and ministerial scholarships receive complete 100% tuition waivers plus an annual living stipend of €16,243 gross.
