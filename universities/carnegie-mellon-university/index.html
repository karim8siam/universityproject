Carnegie Mellon University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, is a world-renowned private global research university globally celebrated as the birthplace of artificial intelligence and software engineering. Home to the legendary School of Computer Science (SCS), the Robotics Institute (the world's largest robotics research group), and the Software Engineering Institute (SEI), CMU is universally ranked #1 in computer science.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Robotics (Robotics Institute Full Fellowship)

Elite doctoral program at the world-famous Robotics Institute, investigating autonomous mobile navigation, robotic manipulation, vision-language action models, and human-robot teaming.

Master of Science in Machine Learning (MSML - School of Computer Science)

World-leading two-year master's curriculum delivered by the Machine Learning Department, covering deep neural architectures, probabilistic graphical models, reinforcement learning, and statistical learning theory.

Carnegie Institute of Technology Dean's Doctoral Excellence Fellowship

Top presidential award for incoming doctoral students in Electrical & Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Materials Science, providing unencumbered multi-year research funding.

Software Engineering Institute (SEI) Cyber Threat & AI Safety Research Assistantship

Research role at the SEI—a federally funded research and development center (FFRDC)—evaluating adversarial attacks on neural networks, software supply chain security, and automated malware analysis.

Human-Computer Interaction Institute (HCII) Summer Interaction Design Practicum

Ten-week immersive summer residency within the HCII designing spatial computing interfaces, voice conversational agents, generative AI design co-pilots, and assistive technologies for neurodiverse users.

Center for Nonlinear Analysis (CNA) Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Applied Mathematics

Two-year postdoctoral position in the Department of Mathematical Sciences conducting research on calculus of variations, mathematical materials science, fluid dynamics, and machine learning mathematics.

Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Advanced Mobile & IoT Systems)

Top-ranked two-year graduate engineering curriculum covering heterogeneous computing architectures, low-power embedded edge AI, neuromorphic circuits, and high-frequency wireless communications.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with an outstanding GPA (typically 3.7+ on a 4.0 scale) in computer science, engineering, or mathematics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate or master's degree with exceptional academic records, substantial prior research experience, competitive GRE scores (where applicable), and compelling letters.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT minimum 100 (sub-scores of 25+ in each section) or IELTS Academic minimum 7.5 (no sub-band below 7.0).
Research statement / Proposal
A concise 2-page Statement of Purpose detailing research questions, technical accomplishments, software/algorithmic contributions, and named CMU faculty.
Application fee
USD $100 non-refundable application fee per program.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, CV/resume, personal statement, and official English test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for professional master's programs at Carnegie Mellon is approximately USD $54,000 per year. However, all admitted doctoral candidates across the School of Computer Science and College of Engineering receive full doctoral fellowships providing 100% tuition coverage, university health insurance, and guaranteed annual living stipends ranging from USD $40,000 to $46,000 per year.
