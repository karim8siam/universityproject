Temple University

Temple University · Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, United States

Temple University is a world-class public Carnegie R1 research university in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Established by Russell Conwell, Temple is internationally celebrated for the Fox School of Business, the Lewis Katz School of Medicine, the Beasley School of Law, and the Tyler School of Art and Architecture, driving over $300M in annual sponsored research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Comprising 17 schools and colleges across five campuses in Pennsylvania and international branches in Rome and Tokyo, Temple offers hundreds of degree programs. Its research centers lead global advances in genomics, retrovirology (including groundbreaking CRISPR HIV-elimination research), urban health equity, and advanced materials engineering.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are competitive and vary by program. Undergraduate applicants are assessed through Common Application or Temple application (test-optional). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree (minimum 3.0 GPA), department-specific standardized test scores (GRE/GMAT where required), letters of recommendation, and a statement of scholarly goals.
