Texas A&M University-Corpus Christi

TAMU Corpus Christi · Corpus Christi, Texas, United States

Texas A&M University-Corpus Christi (TAMU-CC), known as 'The Island University,' is a Carnegie R2 doctoral research institution situated on Ward Island in Oso Bay, Corpus Christi, Texas. TAMU-CC is globally acclaimed for the Harte Research Institute for Gulf of Mexico Studies, the Lone Star UAS Center of Excellence & Innovation, and top-tier geospatial engineering programs.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TAMU-CC features five colleges: Science and Engineering, Liberal Arts, Business, Education and Human Development, and Nursing and Health Sciences. It leads federal research in unmanned aircraft systems, coastal wetlands ecology, Gulf of Mexico fisheries policy, satellite geodesy, and marine genomics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate applicants are admitted through holistic review of high school rank, GPA, and test scores (test-optional). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75+ GPA (3.0+ for doctoral programs), program-specific prerequisites, personal statement, and academic reference letters.
