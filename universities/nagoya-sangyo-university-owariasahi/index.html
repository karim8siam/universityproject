Nagoyasangyo University

Nagoya Sangyo University (NSU) is a forward-looking private university in Owariasahi, Aichi Prefecture, renowned across Japan as a pioneer in Carbon Neutrality Studies, Environmental Information Systems, and Business Informatics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Home to the Institute for Environmental Information Systems, NSU leads Japanese academic research in corporate greenhouse gas accounting, life cycle assessment (LCA), and environmental IoT sensing. The university collaborates closely with the Greater Nagoya industrial and automotive manufacturing cluster to develop circular economy business models.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation and formal entrance examinations. For international students, requirements include EJU Japanese score >= 200 or JLPT N2, transcript evaluation, an essay on environmental management or business informatics, and an admissions interview.
