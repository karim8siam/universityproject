Academy of Public Administration Meissen: Digital Governance, Judicial Administration & Law Guide

Established in 1992 on the historic banks of the Elbe river in Meissen, the Hochschule für öffentliche Verwaltung und Rechtspflege (FH) Meißen - Fortbildungszentrum (HSF Meißen) is Saxony's premier state university of applied sciences for civil service leadership. Educating career civil servants for state ministries, municipal governments, courts, and tax administrations, HSF Meißen combines legal scholarship with digital e-government and judicial administration.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Free State of Saxony Civil Service Candidate Salary and Study Sponsorship

State civil service sponsorship guaranteeing full monthly remuneration, healthcare benefits, and immediate career placement as a tenured civil servant upon graduation.

Diplom-Verwaltungswirt (FH) in Digital Public Administration & E-Governance

Three-year integrated dual degree preparing digital administrators in administrative law, enterprise software architectures, cyber defense in public infrastructure, and change management.

Saxony Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Public Law and Administrative Science

Three-year doctoral research fellowship conducted jointly with Leipzig University researching administrative procedure modernization and constitutional constraints on algorithmic discretion.

Centre for Judicial Administration (Rechtspflege) Applied Research Project

Applied legal investigation analyzing procedural efficiency, electronic court files (e-Akte), and consumer insolvency law across Saxon district and appellate courts.

Saxon State Ministry and Municipal District Authority Practical Rotation

Six-month practical training phase rotated across Saxon state ministries (Interior, Justice, Finance) and district administrative offices managing public law casework.

HSF Meißen Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Public Governance and Tax Law

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment analyzing municipal fiscal equalization schemes, cross-border tax fraud detection, and electronic tax filing compliance.

Meissen Pro Civitate Public Sector Excellence and Innovation Award

Honorary award conferred upon outstanding graduating civil service candidates whose final thesis demonstrates exceptional practical innovation in public administration.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed bachelor's degree or German Diplom (FH) in administrative science, law, public economics, or related social science with a minimum German grade of 2.5 (gut).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct doctoral research collaborations coordinated in partnership with the Faculty of Law at Leipzig University and the Faculty of Business and Economics at TU Dresden.
Language proficiency
German language proficiency at level C1/C2 (DSH-2, TestDaF 4x4, or Goethe C1/C2) required due to state legal and civil service examination statutes.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for advanced administrative research fellowships: 1,000-word proposal on public sector digital modernization, administrative procedural law, or tax compliance.
Application fee
€0 application fee for civil service selection procedures.
Required documentation
Certified copy of higher education entrance qualification (Abitur) or university degree, civil service candidate appointment letter, updated CV, and police clearance certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 0 EUR. As appointed civil service candidates (Beamtenanwärter), students receive a monthly training salary (Anwärterbezüge) of approximately 1,350 EUR to 1,550 EUR per month throughout all three years of study, funded by the Free State of Saxony.
