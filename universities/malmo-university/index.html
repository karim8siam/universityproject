Malmö University (Malmö universitet)

Established in 1998 and granted full research university status in 2018, Malmö University (Malmö universitet - Mau) is Sweden's ninth-largest university, situated at the heart of the dynamic transnational Öresund region connecting Sweden with Copenhagen. Internationally celebrated for pioneer research at the Biofilms Research Center for Biointerfaces (BRCB), the Faculty of Odontology, the Malmö Institute for Studies of Migration, Diversity and Welfare (MIM), and visionary interaction design, Malmö University delivers salaried doctoral positions under Swedish collective labor agreements.

Malmö University Master Scholarship (75% to 100% Tuition Waiver)

Merit-based scholarship awarded to high-achieving non-EU/EEA students applying to English-taught master's degrees in biomedical sciences, computer science, and design at Malmö University.

Malmö Salaried PhD Position in Biofilms and Biointerfaces (BRCB)

Four-year salaried doctoral research post based at the Biofilms Research Center for Biointerfaces (BRCB), investigating bacterial adhesion, transdermal drug delivery, and anti-biofilm coatings for medical implants.

Malmö Master of Science (MSc) in Interaction Design (English-Taught)

World-renowned two-year Master's degree focusing on tangible interaction, Internet of Things (IoT) experiences, participatory design methods, and ethical digital infrastructures.

Malmö PhD in International Migration and Ethnic Relations (MIM)

Doctoral research conducted within the Malmö Institute for Studies of Migration, Diversity and Welfare (MIM), examining migration governance, labor market integration, border regimes, and urban citizenship.

Internet of Things and People (IoTaP) Research Centre Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the IoTaP Research Centre, developing user-centered IoT architectures, cooperative smart city sensors, and privacy-preserving ubiquitous data systems.

Malmö Biomedical Surface Science Summer Laboratory Traineeship

Eight-week laboratory traineeship at the BRCB, utilizing quartz crystal microbalance with dissipation (QCM-D) and atomic force microscopy to observe biomolecular interactions on implant surfaces.

Malmö Senior Researcher in Transdermal Drug Delivery & Skin Barrier Science

Senior research scientist post investigating the molecular biophysics of the stratum corneum lipid matrix, lipid nanoparticle skin penetration, and non-invasive transdermal drug delivery systems.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) from an internationally recognized higher education institution with a major in health sciences, technology, design, or social sciences (minimum 65-70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS credits total, with at least 60 ECTS at advanced level) or equivalent international qualification; selection is based on academic merit, research quality, and faculty fit.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3 to 5 page doctoral research plan addressing research problem, theoretical context, proposed methodology, and significance.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying through the central Swedish universityadmissions.se portal; €0 fee for EU citizens.
Required documentation
Certificates and diplomas of completed degrees, official transcripts, documentation of English proficiency, copy of passport, and Master's thesis.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

At Malmö University and throughout Sweden, PhD candidacies are classified as salaried state employment (doktorandanställning). Admitted PhD candidates pay zero tuition fees and receive a collective-bargaining salaried monthly wage of SEK 31,500 to SEK 35,000 per month (approx. SEK 380,000 to SEK 420,000/year gross) with full Swedish national healthcare, parental leave, and pension benefits. International Master's students can compete for Malmö University Master Scholarships covering 75% to 100% of tuition fees.
