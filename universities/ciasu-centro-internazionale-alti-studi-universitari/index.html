Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located in the historic Masseria Giardinelli in Fasano, CIASU (Centro Internazionale Alti Studi Universitari) is a premier inter-university consortium and post-graduate research institute dedicated to Euro-Mediterranean cooperation, advanced administrative training, agrifood biotechnology, and digital public policy.

Institution type
Inter-University Advanced Studies Consortium
Campus location
Masseria Giardinelli Campus, Fasano (Brindisi), Puglia
Accreditation
Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR) Affiliate Consortium
Admissions channel
Annual postgraduate intake (November and March)
Instruction language
Italian & English (multilingual Euro-Mediterranean seminars)
Financial aid & support
Regione Puglia 'Pass Laureati' vouchers, CIASU Consortium Fellowships

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Strategic Euro-Mediterranean research hub at historic Masseria Giardinelli
  • Inter-university consortial programs in partnership with UniBa, UniSalento, and Poliba
  • Specialized research in Xylella fastidiosa epidemic resilience and olive agro-ecosystems
  • Executive public policy and European structural funds administration masterclasses

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master Universitario di II Livello in Euro-Mediterranean Governance & Diplomacy (Postgraduate Master) — Executive post-master's program analyzing maritime boundary law, EU cohesion policy, and cross-border commercial diplomacy.
  • Corso di Alta Formazione in Agrifood Biosystems & Precision Agriculture (Advanced Specialization) — Specialized postgraduate certificate covering drone multispectral sensing, soil carbon management, and Mediterranean crop protection.
  • Master di I Livello in Management dei Beni Culturali e Turismo Sostenibile (Master's Degree) — Professional degree preparing directors for UNESCO historical sites, masseria eco-resorts, and cultural heritage foundations.
