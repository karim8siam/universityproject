University of Antwerp - Netherlands Academic Cross-Border Training Center

The Netherlands Academic Cross-Border Training Center of the University of Antwerp connects Belgian and Dutch clinical hospitals, delivering binational accredited medical clerkships, translational pharmaceutical science, and Benelux public health research.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating through bilateral academic clinical agreements between the University of Antwerp (Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences) and major teaching hospitals in the southern Netherlands (Amphia Ziekenhuis Breda and Elisabeth-TweeSteden Ziekenhuis Tilburg), this cross-border center enables Dutch and international medical students to complete accredited clinical rotations and translational health research across the Netherlands-Flanders border corridor.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to bachelor's degree programs in Medicine requires passing the competitive Flanders Medical Admissions Exam (Toelatingsexamen Arts). Dutch and international applicants with an accredited secondary school diploma (VWO or European equivalent) are eligible to register. Clinical master's electives and doctoral fellowships in translational oncology and infectious disease epidemiology require an accredited medical degree (MD) or MSc in biomedical sciences and Dutch C1 proficiency.

Master of Medicine in Clinical Medicine and Cross-Border Clerkships

Three-year qualifying medical master's combining clinical ward rotations at Antwerp University Hospital (UZA) and Amphia Hospital in Breda across cardiology, oncology, and pediatrics.

Funding: Benelux Cross-Border Mobility Grant providing €1,200 travel allowance per hospital rotation Tuition: €1,116 per academic year for EEA students / €6,000 for non-EEA Deadline: June 1, 2027
PhD

Doctor of Philosophy in Translational Infectious Disease Epidemiology (Benelux Hub)

Four-year doctoral research fellowship at the Vaccine & Infectious Disease Institute (VAXINFECTIO) investigating cross-border pathogen genomic surveillance and vaccine efficacy.

Funding: Full FWO/NWO cross-border doctoral salary of €2,450 net monthly for four years Tuition: €0 tuition fee Deadline: April 15, 2027
Internship

Benelux Cross-Border Hospital Clinical Rotation Elective

Eight-week clinical immersion for senior medical clerks in acute trauma surgery and interventional cardiology rotating between Antwerp and Breda.

Funding: Erasmus+ clinical mobility grant of €450 monthly plus hospital accommodations Tuition: €0 placement fee Deadline: May 15, 2027
Fellowship

Queen Elisabeth Medical Research Foundation Fellowship in Neuro-Oncology

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Center for Oncological Research (CORE) investigating glioblastoma blood-brain barrier permeability and nanoparticle drug targeting.

Funding: €32,000 annual postdoctoral research fellowship plus laboratory consumable funds Tuition: Waived Deadline: March 31, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Pharmaceutical Sciences - Drug Development & Clinical Trials

Two-year comprehensive master's in high-throughput drug screening, clinical trial biostatistics, pharmacovigilance, and European Medicines Agency (EMA) regulatory affairs.

Funding: Flemish Master Mind Scholarship providing €10,000 grant plus tuition waiver Tuition: €1,116 (EEA) / €5,800 (non-EEA) per academic year Deadline: June 15, 2027
Scholarship

Breda-Antwerp Cross-Border Public Health Merit Scholarship

Merit award supporting medical and health science scholars investigating cross-border patient mobility, emergency ambulance dispatch protocols, and Benelux health disparities.

Funding: €4,000 annual scholarship funded by cross-border health insurance funds (CZ and CM) Tuition: Applied towards annual tuition and clinical equipment Deadline: July 1, 2027
Course

Certificate in Good Clinical Practice (ICH-GCP) & Clinical Trial Auditing

Four-week intensive online certification covering ICH-GCP E6(R2) compliance, investigator brochure preparation, serious adverse event (SAE) reporting, and clinical trial ethics.

Funding: TransCelerate BioPharma internationally recognized certification Tuition: €850 total course tuition Deadline: October 1, 2027
