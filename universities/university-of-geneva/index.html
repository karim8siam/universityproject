University of Geneva (Université de Genève - UNIGE)

Founded in 1559 by John Calvin as the Geneva Academy, the University of Geneva (Université de Genève - UNIGE) is Switzerland's third largest university and one of Europe's top 20 research universities. UNIGE has produced or hosted 10 Nobel laureates and 3 Fields Medalists. Partnered intimately with CERN, the World Health Organization (WHO), and the Geneva Observatory (where Michel Mayor and Didier Queloz discovered the first exoplanet), UNIGE excels in astrophysics, quantum information, particle physics, and international law. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UNIGE Master of Excellence Fellowships (Faculty of Science)

UNIGE Faculty of Science's premier merit fellowship awarded to exceptional incoming international and Swiss Master's candidates, providing up to CHF 15,000 per year to support full-time scientific study in Geneva.

University of Geneva Doctoral Assistantships (Assistant-Doctorant)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment inside UNIGE departments, conducting frontier scientific investigations with state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure in Geneva and CERN.

UNIGE Master of Science in Astrophysics

World-leading two-year Master of Science program (120 ECTS) hosted at the Geneva Observatory in Sauverny, offering specialized training in Exoplanetary Science, Stellar Physics, Observational Cosmology, and Space Instrumentation.

UNIGE Master of Science in Computer Science (CUI)

Comprehensive two-year Master's degree (90–120 ECTS) at the Centre Universitaire d'Informatique (CUI), covering Artificial Intelligence, Stochastic Information Processing, Cybersecurity, and Complex Systems.

CERN & Geneva Observatory Collaborative Particle Physics Placements

Frontier collaborative research placements inside the Department of Nuclear and Corpuscular Physics (DPNC) at UNIGE, conducting experiments at the Large Hadron Collider (ATLAS, FASER) and AMS-02 on the ISS.

UNIGE Summer Research Internships in Life Sciences & Physics

Structured summer research program inside UNIGE's Faculty of Science laboratories, offering students direct research mentorship in molecular biology, chemical biology, and experimental physics.

UNIGE Teaching Assistantships (Sous-assistants d'enseignement)

Departmental academic employment (sous-assistant) for enrolled undergraduate and graduate students at UNIGE, providing hourly compensation for conducting weekly tutorial exercise sessions and supervising lab sections.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in relevant subject area from an internationally recognized university with above-average grades.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science degree (minimum 120 ECTS) with high academic marks, proven research thesis capability, and agreement from a UNIGE full professor to serve as doctoral supervisor.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to CEFR C1: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100 for English-taught graduate curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of purpose detailing academic milestones, research interests, and preferred UNIGE laboratories.
Application fee
CHF 65 application processing fee for holders of non-Swiss university degrees (waived for Swiss degree holders).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, certified copy of degree diploma, curriculum vitae in English or French, motivation essay, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Geneva is heavily subsidized by the Republic and Canton of Geneva. Tuition is standard at only CHF 500 per semester (CHF 1,000 per year) for both Swiss and international students. Selected international master's candidates can receive the Master of Excellence Fellowship (CHF 10,000 to CHF 15,000 per year). Doctoral assistants are paid salaried employees receiving CHF 48,000 to CHF 53,000 per year.
