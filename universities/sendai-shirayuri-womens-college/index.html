Sendai Shirayuri Women's College

Sendai Shirayuri Women's College is a historic private Catholic women's college in Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture, renowned for human development studies, clinical psychology, nutritional science, and preschool teacher education.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded by the Sisters of Saint Paul of Chartres, Sendai Shirayuri delivers compassionate higher education rooted in Christian humanism. The college features modern early childhood education centers, nutritional testing laboratories, and psychological counseling clinics that prepare women for leadership roles in healthcare, education, and human welfare throughout Northern Japan.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation and academic evaluation. For foreign students: JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 200, personal statement, high school transcripts, and an admissions interview.
