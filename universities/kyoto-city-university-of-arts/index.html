Kyoto City University of Arts: Traditional Japanese Arts, Fine Arts, Music & Design Guide

Kyoto City University of Arts (Kyoto Geidai / KCUA) is Japan's oldest public municipal arts university, founded in 1880 in the cultural heart of Kyoto, celebrated worldwide for traditional Japanese painting (Nihonga), lacquerware (Urushi), ceramics, contemporary sculpture, and musical composition.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited four-year university with a Bachelor's degree in fine arts, design, crafts, or music.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or terminal MFA/MMus in arts or music with an outstanding portfolio and theoretical research plan.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 or N1 required for studio critique and seminar interaction; English proficiency supported in theoretical research.
Research statement / Proposal
A comprehensive portfolio of 10-15 original creative works accompanied by a 2,000-character Japanese research statement (Kenkyu Keikakusho).
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee payable via Japanese banking or designated portal.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, official transcript, portfolio/score recording, research plan, and two letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual tuition is ¥535,800 with an admission fee of ¥282,000 (discounted to ¥141,000 for Kyoto citizens). International students can apply for Monbukagakusho (MEXT) scholarships, Kyoto City International Foundation awards, and 50% tuition reduction waivers based on artistic merit.
