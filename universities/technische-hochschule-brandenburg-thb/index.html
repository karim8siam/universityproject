Technische Hochschule Brandenburg (THB)

Technische Hochschule Brandenburg (THB) is a prominent public university of applied sciences in the Berlin-Brandenburg metropolitan region, known for computer science, robotics, cybersecurity, medical informatics, and renewable energy technologies.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1992 on a revitalized military barracks campus along the Havel River, Technische Hochschule Brandenburg hosts over 2,600 students across three cutting-edge departments: Computer Science and Media, Engineering, and Business and Management. THB combines hands-on engineering education with intensive industrial partnerships, hosting regional innovation hubs, 3D printing laboratories, autonomous vehicle testing grounds, and the Interdisciplinary Center for Security and Privacy. Its programs maintain low student-to-professor ratios and robust practical semester integrations.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Security Management (Cybersecurity) (Master's) — Specialized master's program training information security officers in risk assessment, network hardening, digital forensics, and compliance.
  • Master of Science in Medizininformatik (Medical Informatics) (Master's) — Interdisciplinary graduate curriculum bridging software engineering, medical imaging algorithms, electronic health records, and clinical AI.
  • Bachelor of Science in Angewandte Informatik (Applied Computer Science) (Bachelor's) — Comprehensive six-semester undergraduate computing degree covering modern software engineering, web architectures, and algorithms.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Maschinenbau (Mechanical Engineering) (Bachelor's) — Practical engineering program featuring extensive laboratory sessions in CAD/CAM, finite element analysis, thermodynamics, and robotics.
