Lakeland College: Agricultural Sciences, Energy & Environmental Technology Guide

Established in 1913, Lakeland College is an internationally recognized comprehensive college situated across two campuses in Vermilion and Lloydminster along the Alberta-Saskatchewan border. Renowned for its innovative Student-Managed Farm (SMF) enterprise—Canada's leading commercial-scale student learning farm—Lakeland delivers hands-on education in agriculture sciences, agribusiness, heavy crude energy technology, fire and emergency services, and environmental conservation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Collaborative post-credential and university transfer pathways require accredited post-secondary diploma or undergraduate degree in relevant discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; comprehensive college offering diplomas, certificates, and applied bachelor's degrees.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 6.0 in all skill bands) or TOEFL iBT 79 for international student entry across academic diploma streams.
Research statement / Proposal
Not required; personal portfolio or experience documentation required for specific agriculture and emergency services streams.
Application fee
$90 CAD non-refundable application fee for international and domestic applicants.
Required documentation
Official secondary school transcripts, post-secondary records if applicable, English language proficiency documentation, and identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic students averages $4,800 to $7,200 CAD per academic year. International tuition ranges from $14,000 to $18,500 CAD per year depending on specialized laboratory requirements. Lakeland awards over $1.5 million CAD annually in student awards, bursaries, and merit scholarships.
