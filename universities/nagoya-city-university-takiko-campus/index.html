Institutional Overview

Nagoya City University (Nagoya Shiritsu Daigaku / Meishidai) is a prestigious public university corporation founded in 1950. Its picturesque Takiko Campus (Yamanoue Campus) in Mizuho-ku houses the Graduate School and Faculty of Economics, School of Humanities and Social Sciences, and Data Science Institute, delivering elite public higher education deeply connected to the Greater Chubu manufacturing and financial powerhouses.
