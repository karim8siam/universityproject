Media Solution Center Baden-Württemberg e.V.

Media Solution Center Baden-Württemberg e.V. is an elite research cluster bridging the High-Performance Computing Center Stuttgart (HLRS), Stuttgart Media University (HdM), and the creative arts.

Academic overview & research priorities

Co-founded by the High-Performance Computing Center Stuttgart (HLRS) and Stuttgart Media University, the MSC Baden-Württemberg pioneers the confluence of exascale supercomputing, artificial intelligence, volumetric streaming, and high-fidelity scientific visualization.

Research admissions & international criteria

Coordinates postgraduate research fellowships, doctoral projects, and technological masterclasses. Applicants must be enrolled or prospective researchers in computing, digital media, or computational engineering at Baden-Württemberg university partners.
