Institutional Overview

Université de l'Ontario français (UOF) is a forward-thinking, French-language public university established in the heart of downtown Toronto. Founded to meet modern societal and economic demands, UOF organizes its curriculum into four transdisciplinary hubs: digital cultures, human plurality, urban environments, and economy and social innovation.
