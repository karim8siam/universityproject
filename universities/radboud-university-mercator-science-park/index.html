Radboud University - Mercator Technology & Science Park

The Mercator Technology & Science Park (Mercator 1) at Radboud University in Nijmegen is a European innovation nexus integrating the High Field Magnet Laboratory (HFML), FELIX laser laboratories, neurotechnology startups, and advanced semiconductor chemistry.

Academic overview & excellence

Mercator Park connects Radboud's Faculty of Science with cutting-edge commercial incubators and spinouts. Scholars conduct frontier research in continuous high-field magnetic physics (up to 38 Tesla), molecular microbiology, and cognitive neuromorphic computing.

Research admissions & criteria

Bachelor's admissions requires a Dutch VWO diploma or international equivalent with strong mathematics and physics. Master of Science (English-taught) requires an accredited BSc with a minimum GPA of 7.0/10 (Dutch scale) and English proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90). Fully funded salaried PhD researcher positions are appointed under Dutch CAU collective agreements.

PhD

HFML-FELIX High Magnetic Field & Free Electron Laser PhD Fellowship

Four-year doctoral appointment utilizing 38-Tesla continuous magnetic fields combined with infrared free-electron lasers to explore quantum Hall effects in 2D materials.

Funding: Fully salaried 4-year Dutch employment contract: €2,770/month (Year 1) rising to €3,539/month (Year 4) plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus Tuition: €0 tuition (salaried staff researcher) Deadline: February 15, 2027

Master of Science in Physics and Astronomy (Neurophysics Track)

Two-year international master's degree exploring mathematical biophysics, neural coding, brain-computer interfaces, and spiking neural networks.

Funding: Radboud Scholarship Programme covering full tuition fees and €10,800 living allowance Tuition: €2,530/year (EU/EEA), €17,000/year (non-EU/EEA) Deadline: April 1, 2027
Fellowship

Mercator Innovation Center Deep Tech Spinout Fellowship

One-year entrepreneurial fellowship supporting post-doctoral scientists launching commercial ventures in quantum algorithms, AI medical diagnostics, or clean tech.

Funding: €35,000 proof-of-concept seed grant funded through Mercator Launch incubator Tuition: €0 Deadline: March 31, 2027
Internship

NXP Semiconductors & Radboud Mercator RF Microelectronics Practicum

Six-month research internship testing automotive radar transceivers, GaN-on-Si radio frequency amplifiers, and secure embedded crypto-hardware.

Funding: €800 monthly internship allowance placed at NXP Nijmegen high-tech campus Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 1, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Science in Molecular Life Sciences (English-Taught)

Three-year research bachelor's degree at the intersection of chemistry, molecular biology, and medicine, analyzing biochemical disease pathways and rational drug design.

Funding: Dutch government student finance (DUO) loan and grant eligible for EU students Tuition: €2,530/year (EU/EEA), €15,500/year (non-EU) Deadline: May 1, 2027
Scholarship

Donders Institute Cognitive Neuroscience Postdoctoral Award

Postdoctoral research project exploring 7-Tesla functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) of human memory consolidation and synaptic plasticity.

Funding: €45,000 annual research grant co-funded by the Netherlands Organisation for Scientific Research (NWO) Tuition: €0 Deadline: June 15, 2027

Four-week hands-on technical masterclass in ISO 5 cleanroom protocols, ultra-high vacuum (UHV) turbopump maintenance, and liquid helium-4 / helium-3 dilution refrigeration.

Funding: Dutch High Tech Systems and Materials (HTSM) subsidy eligible Tuition: €1,200 course fee Deadline: September 15, 2027
