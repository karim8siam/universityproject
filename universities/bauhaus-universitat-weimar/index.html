Bauhaus-Universität Weimar: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar is one of Europe's most distinctive and internationally acclaimed universities, situated on the historic campus where Walter Gropius founded the legendary Bauhaus in 1919. A member of the European University coalition Film and Media Arts, Bauhaus Weimar unites architecture, civil and environmental engineering, media informatics, and fine art.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in civil engineering, computer science, media informatics, or architecture with minimum German grade 2.5 ('gut') or international equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree with above-average grades; written confirmation of supervision (Betreuungszusage) from a Bauhaus-Universität Weimar professor.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency at B2/C1 level (TOEFL iBT 85+, IELTS 6.5+) for English-taught degrees; German DSH-2 for German-taught tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A 4-6 page doctoral research expository including state of the art, scientific methodology, timetable, and literature citations.
Application fee
€0 for direct online application through the university portal (or €75 via uni-assist for certain degrees).
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, official transcripts, CV, statement of motivation, portfolio (for design/architecture programs), and language test results.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar is zero (€0) for all domestic and international students in all consecutive Bachelor's, Master's, and doctoral programs. Students pay only a statutory semester fee (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €250 per semester, which includes a regional railway and transit ticket covering the entire state of Thuringia. Admitted doctoral researchers typically receive salaried TV-L E13 contracts (€42,000–€52,000/year gross) or DAAD scholarships.
