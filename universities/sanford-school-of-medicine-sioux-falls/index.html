Sanford School of Medicine

Sanford School of Medicine · Sioux Falls, South Dakota, United States

The Sanford School of Medicine of the University of South Dakota is South Dakota's only medical school, headquartered in Sioux Falls and Vermillion, South Dakota. Nationally revered for its pioneering Frontier and Rural Medicine (FARM) program, rural primary care physician training, pediatrics, and infectious disease research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Sanford School of Medicine operates major clinical campuses in Sioux Falls (Sanford Health and Avera Health systems), Rapid City, and Yankton, alongside basic biomedical research laboratories at the Lee Medical Building in Vermillion. It is a recipient of the prestigious Spencer Foreman Award for Outstanding Community Service from the AAMC.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Medical school admission is through AMCAS. Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with prerequisite biology, general chemistry, organic chemistry, physics, and mathematics. Priority is granted to South Dakota residents and individuals with demonstrated ties to the Northern Great Plains region.
