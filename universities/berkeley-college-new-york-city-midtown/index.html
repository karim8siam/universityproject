Institution Overview & Profile

Berkeley College New York City Midtown Campus, located on 43rd Street in Manhattan between Fifth and Madison Avenues, is an accredited private career-focused institution established in 1931, offering bachelor and associate degrees in fashion merchandising, interior design, financial services, criminal justice, and international business.

Institution TypePrivate Career-Focused Business & Design College
Main CampusMidtown Manhattan Campus (East 43rd Street), New York City
AccreditationMiddle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE) / IACBE

Key Academic Strengths

  • Prime Midtown Manhattan location adjacent to Grand Central Terminal, fashion houses, and corporate headquarters
  • World-class Fashion Merchandising and Management curriculum with direct participation in New York Fashion Week
  • Mandatory internships integrated into every degree program through an extensive network of NYC employers
  • Larry L. Luing School of Business accredited by the International Accreditation Council for Business Education (IACBE)
