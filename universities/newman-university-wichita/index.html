Newman University

Newman Univ · Wichita, Kansas, United States

Newman University is a private Catholic liberal arts university in Wichita, Kansas, sponsored by the Adorers of the Blood of Christ. Widely recognized for allied health sciences, nursing, pre-med education, and social work throughout the Great Plains.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Newman University houses the School of Healthcare Professions, School of Arts and Sciences, and the School of Business and Technology. Facilities include the Bishop Gerber Science Center, cardiac catheterization simulation suites, and sonography labs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.25) under test-optional review. Healthcare and graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.75 to 3.0 GPA and clinical references.
