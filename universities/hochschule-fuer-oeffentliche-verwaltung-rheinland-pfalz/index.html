Hochschule für öffentliche Verwaltung Rheinland-Pfalz (HöV RLP)

HöV RLP · Mayen, Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany

Hochschule für öffentliche Verwaltung Rheinland-Pfalz (HöV RLP) in Mayen is the central university for civil service training, preparing executives for state ministries, county administrations, and municipal councils.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

HöV delivers accredited bachelor’s and master’s dual programs combining administrative legal jurisprudence, public economics, HR psychology, and digital municipal management. Students receive full monthly civil service trainee compensation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires the German university entrance qualification (Abitur/Fachhochschulreife) and successful employment/training candidacy with a participating state or municipal authority in Rhineland-Palatinate.
