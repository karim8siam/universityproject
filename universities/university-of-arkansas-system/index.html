University of Arkansas System

UA System Arkansas · Little Rock, Arkansas, United States

The University of Arkansas System is the flagship public university system of the Natural State, spanning twenty campuses and divisions—including the flagship University of Arkansas in Fayetteville, the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) in Little Rock, and the statewide Division of Agriculture. Leading the nation in poultry science, agricultural economics, supply chain analytics, and nanomedicine, the UA System powers Arkansas's knowledge economy.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through constituent system universities (Fayetteville, Little Rock, Fort Smith, Monticello, Pine Bluff, UAMS). Holistic review of GPA and secondary coursework. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates across UA System campuses. Priority fall admission February 1, 2027; Regular rolling admission closes July 15, 2027.
