Academic overview & research priorities

Chartered in 1829 as the first Baptist college west of the Allegheny Mountains, Georgetown College excels in undergraduate research mentorship in chemistry, biology, mathematics, and pre-law. Its distinctive Oxford Honors Program offers fully funded junior-year tutorial study at Regent’s Park College, University of Oxford.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants submit high school transcripts and personal essays. Georgetown College is test-optional for admissions and scholarships. Graduate education programs require a Bachelor degree with 2.75+ GPA and state educator prerequisites.
