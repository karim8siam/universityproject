Oxford Business College: Pearson BTEC HND, Business & Management Guide

Established in 1985 in the historic university city of Oxford, Oxford Business College (OBC) is an accredited independent higher education college offering Pearson BTEC Higher National Diplomas (HND) in Business, university top-up degrees, and executive management qualifications in partnership with UK universities.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Pearson HND entry requires GCE A-Levels or Level 3 equivalent; university top-up degrees require completed HND or foundation degree (minimum 240 credits).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate and postgraduate diploma college; does not confer doctoral degrees directly.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each skill) or equivalent recognized English certificate.
Research statement / Proposal
Personal statement (500 words) describing career ambitions and reasons for studying in Oxford.
Application fee
Free online application directly via the OBC website.
Required documentation
Passport copy, secondary school certificates, complete transcripts, personal statement, and one academic or employment reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for UK home students is approximately £6,000 to £9,250 per year (covered by Student Finance England). International student tuition averages £9,500 to £12,500 annually. Oxford Business College Academic Excellence scholarships and flexible installment bursaries are accessible.
