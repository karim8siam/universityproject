Texas Christian University

TCU · Fort Worth, Texas, United States

Texas Christian University (TCU) is an acclaimed private Carnegie R2 research university situated in Fort Worth, Texas. Renowned for its world-class Neeley School of Business, the Burnett School of Medicine at TCU, the College of Science & Engineering, and celebrated programs in fine arts and health sciences, TCU combines world-class scholarship with a vibrant campus culture.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TCU is organized into ten constituent colleges and schools. Its state-of-the-art facilities include the Burnett Medical School complex, the Center for Supply Chain Innovation, the Andrews Institute of Mathematics & Science Education, and advanced chemistry NMR imaging suites, driving breakthrough inquiry across medicine, business analytics, and materials science.

Research admissions & international qualifications

TCU admissions are highly competitive. Undergraduate candidates are evaluated through holistic review of high school curriculum, grades, standardized test scores (test-optional), leadership, and essays. Graduate and medical applicants require an accredited baccalaureate degree (3.0+ minimum GPA), competitive MCAT/GMAT/GRE scores, and letters of recommendation.
