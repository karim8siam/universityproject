Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Europa-Universität Flensburg (EUF) is Germany's northernmost university, situated on the Danish border. Renowned for its focus on European integration, cross-border minority linguistics, teacher education, and sustainable energy and environmental management (EEM).

Institution type
Public University (Land Schleswig-Holstein)
Campus location
Campus Flensburg (Sandberg hill overlooking Flensburg Fjord)
Accreditation
Ministry of General and Vocational Education Schleswig-Holstein / ZEvA
Admissions channel
Winter semester (July 15) and Summer semester (January 15)
Instruction language
German, English, and Danish (interdisciplinary multilingual campus)
Financial aid & support
DAAD EPOS, Deutschlandstipendium, Schleswig-Holstein Doctoral Fellowships

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Center for Sustainable Energy Systems (ZNES) and international EEM degrees
  • Interdisciplinary Centre for European Studies (ICES)
  • Cross-border German-Danish teacher training and minority rights scholarship
  • Pioneering research in transformation economics and ecological post-growth theory

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Engineering in Energy and Environmental Management (EEM) (Graduate Degree) — English-taught international master's training energy planners from developing and emerging economies in renewable mini-grids and climate policy.
  • Master of Arts in European Studies (MAES) (Graduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary English-taught degree examining EU law, European integration history, foreign policy, and migration governance.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Bildungswissenschaften (Educational Sciences) (Undergraduate Degree) — Flagship German teacher education program offering versatile subject combinations for secondary and vocational school teaching.
