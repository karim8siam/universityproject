Universities Scotland

Universities Scotland is the national representative body and higher education policy institute for Scotland's 19 world-class universities and higher education institutions, headquartered on Queen Street in Edinburgh.

Academic overview & excellence

Universities Scotland shapes national policy across higher education teaching, cutting-edge research funding, widening access initiatives, and international student visas in coordination with the Scottish Government, the Scottish Funding Council (SFC), and UK Research and Innovation (UKRI).

Research admissions & criteria

Policy fellowships, doctoral policy internships, and higher education governance awards are open to postgraduate scholars and academic staff across all 19 Scottish universities (including Edinburgh, Glasgow, St Andrews, and Aberdeen).
