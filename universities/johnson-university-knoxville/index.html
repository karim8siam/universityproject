Johnson University

Johnson Univ · Knoxville, Tennessee, United States

Johnson University is a private Christian university headquartered on a 175-acre campus on the French Broad River in Knoxville, Tennessee, celebrated for intercultural studies, teacher education, clinical mental health counseling, and pastoral ministry.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Affiliated with the Christian churches and churches of Christ, Johnson integrates theological education with professional bachelor’s and master’s degrees. Programs feature overseas cross-cultural internships, CACREP-accredited counseling, and teacher licensure.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), academic transcripts, personal essays reflecting Christian character, and references under test-optional guidelines.
