Umeå University (Umeå, Sweden)

Established in 1965 in Umeå, northern Sweden, Umeå University is one of Scandinavia's premier research institutions and a globally recognized centre of scientific discovery. Umeå made history as the home of the seminal CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing discovery by Prof. Emmanuelle Charpentier at the Umeå Centre for Microbial Research (UCMR, Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2020). Umeå leads Europe in infection biology, artificial intelligence, autonomous robotics in extreme environments, and Arctic climate research. Umeå University International Master's Scholarships (EC Excellence)

Umeå University's premier merit-based scholarship program for citizens of non-EU/EEA countries, awarding tuition fee remissions to top international scholars.

Umeå University Salaried Doctoral Positions (Doktorandanställning) in Molecular Biology

Four-year fully salaried doctoral research employment contracts inside the Umeå Centre for Microbial Research (UCMR) and Department of Molecular Biology.

Umeå Master of Science in Robotics and Control

Cutting-edge two-year master's program exploring autonomous mobile robots, field robotics for forestry/mining, human-robot interaction, and computer vision.

Umeå Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence

Rigorous master's degree focusing on automated reasoning, machine learning foundations, intelligent agents, and responsible ethical AI architectures.

Umeå Centre for Microbial Research (UCMR) Frontier Research Placements

Research placements at the prestigious centre where CRISPR-Cas9 was co-discovered, investigating molecular mechanisms of infection biology and antimicrobial resistance.

Climate Impacts Research Centre (CIRC) Abisko Arctic Placements

Field research internships at the Abisko Scientific Research Station in northern Sweden, studying permafrost thaw, tundra carbon exchange, and aquatic ecosystems.

Umeå University Teaching Assistantships & Laboratory Mentoring

Pedagogical teaching appointments enabling PhD researchers to teach undergraduate laboratory classes and supervise project assignments, extending fully paid doctoral employment.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) equivalent to a Swedish Kandidatexamen with a major relevant to the applied program.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS with at least 60 ECTS at advanced level) with an independent thesis and proven research competence.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: English 6 / English B (Academic IELTS 6.5 overall, no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 3-5 page research project proposal and work plan submitted for advertised doctoral employment positions.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA students submitting master's applications via Universityadmissions.se. Doctoral studies are completely tuition-free for all nationalities.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, degree certificates, detailed syllabus descriptions, CV, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral education is 100% free of tuition fees for all nationalities. Doctoral researchers are employed as university staff (Doktorandanställning) under Swedish collective union agreements, receiving starting gross monthly salaries of SEK 31,500 to SEK 35,000/month with comprehensive Swedish healthcare and pension benefits.
