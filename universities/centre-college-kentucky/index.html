Institutional Overview

Centre College, founded in 1819 in historic Danville, Kentucky, is one of America's most distinguished premier liberal arts colleges. Renowned for its groundbreaking 'Centre Commitment'—guaranteeing graduation in four years, study abroad, and an internship or research experience—Centre ranks nationally for undergraduate study abroad participation, Rhodes Scholars, and pre-law and pre-medical placement.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • The 'Centre Commitment' guaranteeing graduation in 4 years, a study abroad experience, and a mentored internship

  • Distinguished host of two U.S. Vice Presidential Debates (2000 and 2012) showcasing political leadership
  • Phenomenal 98% placement rate into graduate schools or employment within one year of graduation
