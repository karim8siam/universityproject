University of Nottingham Malaysia

The University of Nottingham Malaysia is the historic first overseas branch campus of a British university, established in 2000 under the Royal Charter of the Russell Group University of Nottingham, awarding internationally accredited UK degrees.

Academic overview & excellence

Situated on a lush 125-acre garden campus in Semenyih, the university delivers British curriculum degree programs across engineering, pharmacy, biosciences, economics, and international relations, with full semester exchange and dual-degree mobility to the UK campus in Nottingham.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate entry requires A-Levels, IB Diploma (typically 30-34 points), or recognized foundation qualifications. English language proficiency (IELTS 6.0–6.5 or GCSE English grade C/4). Master's and PhD programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum UK 2:1 honors equivalent.

Scholarship

University of Nottingham Malaysia High Achiever Scholarship

Prestigious merit scholarship honoring top-performing international and Malaysian students entering foundation and undergraduate degree courses.

Funding: 25% to 50% tuition fee reduction automatically applied across all undergraduate academic years Tuition: MYR 48,000 to 58,000 per year (subsidized UK international fee equivalent to ~£8,500/year) Deadline: January 15, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Environmental Monitoring & Management

One-year UK-accredited Master of Science teaching GIS remote sensing, tropical peatland conservation, environmental impact assessment, and pollution remediation.

Funding: ASEAN postgraduate research bursaries eligible Tuition: MYR 51,000 total program tuition (~£9,000) Deadline: February 28, 2027
PhD

Doctor of Philosophy in Chemical & Environmental Engineering

Doctoral research exploring palm oil mill effluent (POME) anaerobic biogas conversion, carbon capture, and ionic liquid membrane separation.

Funding: Dean's Doctoral Research Scholarship (DDRS): full tuition waiver plus MYR 2,500/month living stipend for 3 years Tuition: MYR 0 for scholarship recipients Deadline: March 31, 2027
Fellowship

Crops for the Future (CFF) Tropical Agriculture Research Fellowship

Postgraduate research fellowship investigating underutilized indigenous crops (bambara groundnut, moringa) for climate-resilient global food systems.

Funding: MYR 48,000 annual research fellowship funded via agricultural biotechnology endowments Tuition: MYR 0 Deadline: April 30, 2027
Internship

British Council & Nottingham Malaysia ASEAN Mobility Internship

Eight-week inter-campus research internship placing students in policy think-tanks and research laboratories in both Malaysia and the UK.

Funding: £1,200 travel grant plus accommodation at the University of Nottingham UK Jubilee Campus Tuition: MYR 0 Deadline: May 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Mechanical Engineering

Four-year British accredited engineering degree with optional 2+2 or 1+3 campus transfer to the University of Nottingham UK.

Funding: Dual IMechE and Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM) accredited degree Tuition: MYR 54,000 per year (~£9,500/year) Deadline: June 30, 2027
Course

Certificate in Big Data Analytics & Artificial Intelligence

Ten-week professional executive certificate in Apache Spark, Python predictive machine learning, deep neural networks, and cloud database architectures.

Funding: HRD Corp claimable for enterprise participants Tuition: MYR 4,800 course fee (~£850) Deadline: August 31, 2027
