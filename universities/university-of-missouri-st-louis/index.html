University of Missouri-St. Louis

UMSL · St. Louis, Missouri, United States

The University of Missouri-St. Louis (UMSL) is the premier public doctoral research university in the St. Louis metropolitan area. Established in 1963, UMSL is nationally recognized for exceptional programs in criminology and criminal justice, optometry, tropical ecology in partnership with the Missouri Botanical Garden, and cybersecurity education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMSL comprises nine colleges and schools: the College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business Administration, College of Education, College of Nursing, College of Optometry, Pierre Laclede Honors College, School of Social Work, and the UMSL/WUSTL Joint Undergraduate Engineering Program. UMSL is a Carnegie R2 research institution.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school credentials. Graduate admissions require a bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
