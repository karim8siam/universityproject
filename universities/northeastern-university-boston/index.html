Northeastern University

Northeastern · Boston, Massachusetts, United States

Northeastern University is a world-renowned top-tier private Carnegie R1 global research university headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts. Globally acclaimed as the pioneer and world leader in cooperative education (co-op), artificial intelligence, and experiential robotics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Northeastern features the Khoury College of Computer Sciences, College of Engineering, and Bouvé College of Health Sciences, alongside an interconnected global network across London, Toronto, Vancouver, Seattle, and Silicon Valley. Research expenditures exceed $260M annually across biotechnology, cybersecurity, and nanomedicine.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective (acceptance rate ~6%), evaluating secondary school curriculum rigor, standardized testing (test-optional), extracurricular impact, and leadership essays. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 3.2 to 3.5 GPA and research portfolio.
