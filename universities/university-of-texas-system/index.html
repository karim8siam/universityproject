University of Texas System

UT System · Austin, Texas, United States

The University of Texas System is one of the largest and most prestigious public university systems in the United States, comprised of nine academic universities and five health institutions—including UT Austin, UT Southwestern Medical Center, and MD Anderson Cancer Center. Conducting more than $4.3 billion in annual research, the UT System leads the world in cancer immunology, semiconductor microelectronics, clean energy, and artificial intelligence.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through constituent UT institutions (UT Austin, UT Dallas, UT Arlington, UT San Antonio, UT El Paso, UT Rio Grande Valley, UT Tyler, UT Permian Basin). Holistic review via ApplyTexas or Common App. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates across UT System universities. UT Austin priority deadline November 1, 2026; Regular deadline December 1, 2026; other UT institutions rolling through March 1, 2027.
