Liverpool Hope University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Research Guide

Dating its academic foundations to 1844, Liverpool Hope University is an acclaimed public university in Liverpool, England, operating across two stunning campuses: Hope Park in Childwall and the Creative Campus in Everton. As Europe's only ecumenical university foundation, Liverpool Hope is renowned for teaching excellence, high student satisfaction, and distinguished research in Education, Humanities, Robotics, Psychology, and Environmental Sciences.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Undergraduate Bachelor's degree with a minimum UK 2:2 Honours (or international equivalent GPA 2.8/4.0) from a recognized university in a relevant academic subject.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Merit or Distinction) in a relevant discipline, alongside a comprehensive doctoral research proposal (2,000–3,000 words) and two academic references.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in reading and writing (or TOEFL iBT 88 equivalent; Pearson PTE 58).
Research statement / Proposal
Undergraduate applications submitted via UCAS; postgraduate taught and research applications submitted directly online via the Liverpool Hope Application Portal.
Application fee
Zero application fee for direct postgraduate applications; standard UCAS fee applies for undergraduate entry.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, academic transcripts of all study years, English language test certificate, personal statement, CV, and two references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition for UK domestic students is standardly £9,250 per year. For international students, full-time undergraduate and postgraduate taught master's tuition generally ranges from £13,000 to £14,500 per academic year. Liverpool Hope provides competitive International Postgraduate Scholarships offering £1,500 to £3,000 fee waivers to eligible candidates.
