Kanto Gakuin University: Architectural Design, Surface Engineering & Business Guide

Established in 1884 upon Christian humanist foundations, Kanto Gakuin University is an established comprehensive private university based in Yokohama, Kanagawa Prefecture. Operating across its historic Kanazawa-Hakkei campus, Kanazawa-Bunko, and the ultra-modern Yokohama Kannai campus, Kanto Gakuin educates over 11,000 students. The university is celebrated internationally for its groundbreaking Materials and Surface Engineering Research Institute—a global leader in metal surface modification—as well as acclaimed programs in architecture, structural earthquake engineering, and international economics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited 16-year Bachelor's degree in engineering, architecture, economics, or relevant field; approved preliminary study proposal and faculty advisor consent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree; submission of Master's thesis, comprehensive doctoral research project outline, and defense during the graduate admissions examination.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 or N1 for Japanese-language graduate seminars; TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS Academic 6.0+ for designated English-language scientific research tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate degree entry: 1,500-word research proposal outlining scientific hypothesis, experimental methodology, and faculty laboratory alignment.
Application fee
¥35,000 JPY application examination fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's certificate of graduation, complete academic transcripts, research proposal, two letters of recommendation, certificate of Japanese or English language ability, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for graduate science and engineering programs averages ¥950,000 to ¥1,250,000 JPY per academic year (humanities and economics degrees average ¥780,000 JPY/year). Kanto Gakuin provides an automatic 30% tuition fee reduction for eligible privately funded international students, alongside merit-based university scholarships.
