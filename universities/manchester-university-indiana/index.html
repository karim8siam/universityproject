Manchester University

Manchester Univ · North Manchester, Indiana, United States

Manchester University is a private university in North Manchester and Fort Wayne, Indiana, affiliated with the Church of the Brethren. Famous for establishing the world's first Peace Studies program in 1948 and pioneering the Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D.) and Pharmacogenomics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Manchester features the College of Pharmacy, Natural & Health Sciences, and the Gladdys Muir Peace Studies Institute. It is globally recognized for its master's and dual-degree programs in Pharmacogenomics (PGx) and clinical athletic training.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.7 recommended) under test-optional review. Doctor of Pharmacy applicants submit PharmCAS applications with chemistry and biology prerequisites, letters, and committee interviews.
