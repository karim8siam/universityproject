University of Amsterdam - Oudemanhuispoort Historical Campus

The Oudemanhuispoort is the iconic 17th-century historical center of the University of Amsterdam (UvA) in the heart of Amsterdam's canal ring, internationally celebrated as the historical birthplace of UvA's Law Faculty and Europe's premier institute for international law and legal philosophy.

Academic overview & excellence

The Oudemanhuispoort campus, famed for its centuries-old open-air book market, houses the Amsterdam Law School and humanities research institutes. Scholars examine public international law, European constitutionalism, international criminal courts, and digital platform jurisprudence.

Research admissions & criteria

Bachelor's admissions requires an accredited secondary school diploma equivalent to the Dutch VWO. Master of Laws (LL.M.) programs conducted in English require an accredited Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) with strong grades and English proficiency (IELTS 7.0 with at least 6.5 in each component, or TOEFL 100).

One-year world-renowned LL.M. degree examining state sovereignty, the International Court of Justice (ICJ) in The Hague, international humanitarian law, and the law of the sea.

Salaried doctoral research position investigating International Criminal Court (ICC) evidentiary rules, war crime prosecution precedents, and reparations for victim communities.

Funding: Four-year Dutch salaried doctoral appointment: €2,770–€3,539/month with pension and holiday allowance Tuition: €0 tuition (salaried university employee) Deadline: February 15, 2027
Fellowship

Amsterdam Center for International Law (ACIL) Postdoctoral Award

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship analyzing international treaties, state responsibility in climate litigation, and investor-state dispute settlement (ISDS) reforms.

Funding: €38,000 annual research fellowship with visiting fellow privileges at the Peace Palace Library in The Hague Tuition: €0 Deadline: March 15, 2027

International Court of Justice & The Hague Peace Palace Legal Clerkship

Nine-month competitive legal internship assisting judges and registry officials with treaty interpretation, case law bench memos, and oral proceeding summaries.

Funding: €1,200 monthly travel and accommodation stipend between Amsterdam and The Hague Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 1, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Laws in European and International Law

Three-year qualifying law bachelor's examining European Union constitutional law, comparative civil and common law, contract law, and human rights treaties.

Funding: Holland Scholarship (€5,000 one-off) eligible for non-EEA students Tuition: €2,530/year (EU), €11,200/year (non-EU) Deadline: May 1, 2027

Award dedicated to exceptional graduate students researching EU digital platform regulations (DSA/DMA), algorithmic surveillance, and automated decision-making law.
