University of Public Administration

The Thuringian University of Applied Sciences for Public Administration (Thüringer Fachhochschule für öffentliche Verwaltung - VFHS) in Gotha is the state academy training senior executive civil servants, police inspectors, and municipal administrators for the Free State of Thuringia.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated on a historic campus in Gotha, VFHS operates two specialized faculties: the Faculty of Public Administration and the Faculty of Police and Security Law. As an internal state university (Verwaltungsfachhochschule), all enrolled students are appointed as civil service candidates (Beamtenanwärter) and receive an official monthly training salary while alternating between academic theory and practical ministerial internships.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires participation in the centralized Thuringian state civil service selection examination (Auswahlverfahren des Thüringer Landesverwaltungsamtes), possession of the German Fachhochschulreife or Abitur, medical fitness for civil service appointment, and German or EU citizenship. No tuition fees are charged, and candidates receive monthly civil service stipends.
