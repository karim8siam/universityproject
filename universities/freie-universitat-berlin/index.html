Freie Universität Berlin

Founded in 1948 during the early Cold War with international American and Allied support, Freie Universität Berlin (FU Berlin) is one of Germany's premier Universities of Excellence under the German Excellence Strategy and an anchor of the Berlin University Alliance. Nestled in the leafy, historic science quarter of Dahlem (where over a dozen Nobel laureates worked), FU Berlin is internationally renowned for political science (Otto Suhr Institute), international relations, area studies, humanities, organic chemistry, and molecular biology.

Two-month summer research internship in Dahlem neurobiology laboratories, working with Drosophila models, confocal microscopy, and electrophysiological patch-clamp recordings to investigate synaptic plasticity and memory mechanisms.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in a relevant subject area with an above-average final grade (German grade 2.5 or better, equivalent to 75%+ or 3.0/4.0 GPA).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 300 ECTS aggregate) with a thesis graded 'good' (gut) or 'very good' (sehr gut); written confirmation of supervision (Betreuungszusage) from an FU Berlin professor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all components) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught programs; German C1 (DSH-2 / TestDaF 4x4) for German-medium degree tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A 5-page doctoral research expose detailing theoretical foundation, research questions, methodological approach, and preliminary timetable.
Application fee
€0 application fee; no tuition fees charged at public German research universities.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma certificates, academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, research expose, and professor supervision confirmation agreement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees are charged at Freie Universität Berlin for domestic and international students alike. Students pay only a nominal semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €312 per semester, which includes full regional public transit access (Deutschlandticket) throughout Berlin and Germany. Doctoral researchers are supported via TV-L E13 salaried positions (€48,000 to €54,000/year gross).
