Academic overview & excellence

Operating from historic seminar facilities in the Oltrarno district of Florence, the Florence Centre of Piedmont University offers accredited undergraduate and graduate coursework accredited through SACSCOC. Students engage in direct archival research at the Uffizi, Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale, and Bargello, supported by Italian language faculty and international scholars.

Research admissions & criteria

Open to undergraduate and graduate students enrolled at Piedmont University and accredited partner universities across North America and Europe. Applicants must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 and submit an academic writing sample alongside faculty recommendations.
