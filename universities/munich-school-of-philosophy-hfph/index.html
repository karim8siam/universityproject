Munich School of Philosophy (Hochschule für Philosophie München - HFPH)

The Munich School of Philosophy (Hochschule für Philosophie München - HFPH) is a state-recognized Jesuit institution of higher education located in central Munich, Bavaria. Celebrating a century of philosophical inquiry since 1925, HFPH is renowned across Europe for deep scholarship in metaphysics, environmental ethics, epistemology, and the ethics of digital technologies.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Munich, Bavaria, Germany

Institutional Overview

HFPH offers accredited Bachelor of Arts, Master of Arts, and doctoral (Dr. phil.) degrees in philosophy, alongside innovative executive master's programs in ethics and leadership. The faculty maintains interdisciplinary research institutes, notably the Center for Global Questions (Zentrum für Globale Fragen) and the Center for Social and Environmental Ethics, collaborating closely with LMU Munich and TU Munich.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at HFPH welcome scholars from diverse cultural, philosophical, and religious traditions. Degree applications for winter semester require the German Abitur or international secondary school equivalent, certified academic transcripts, and a reflective essay on philosophical questions. Doctoral candidates require a master's in philosophy (min 2.0 grade) and a faculty supervisor endorsement.
