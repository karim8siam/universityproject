North Carolina A&T State University

NC A&T · Greensboro, North Carolina, United States

North Carolina Agricultural and Technical State University is the largest Historically Black University (HBCU) in the United States, located in Greensboro, North Carolina. A premier Carnegie R2 doctoral research university leading the nation in graduating Black engineers and agricultural scientists.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NC A&T comprises the College of Engineering, College of Agriculture and Environmental Sciences, and College of Science and Technology. Major research centers include the NSF Engineering Research Center for Revolutionizing Metallic Biomaterials and the Center for Advanced Transportation Systems.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (average 3.7 unweighted) with strong STEM coursework under holistic review. Graduate degree admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, personal goals statement, and letters of recommendation.
