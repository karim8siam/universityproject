Sapporo City University

Sapporo City University (SCU) is a public municipal university in Sapporo, Hokkaido, uniquely celebrating the synergy between Design and Nursing across its Soen and Art Forest (Geijutsu no Mori) campuses.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 2006 by the City of Sapporo, SCU links human-centered design and healthcare sciences. The Soen Campus in central Sapporo houses the School of Nursing, adjacent to major municipal medical centers, while the Geijutsu no Mori Campus houses the School of Design. Together, they pioneer interdisciplinary research in healthcare design, universal accessibility, medical robotics ergonomics, and northern community health.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate entrance requires the National Center Test / Common Test for University Admissions or the EJU examination for foreign students, followed by campus interview and practical aptitude tests. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in Nursing, Design, Engineering, or Health Sciences, research plan in Japanese (minimum JLPT N1/N2), and oral defense.
