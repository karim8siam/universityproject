John Cage Center - eCampus Bitonto - Accademia Musicale

The John Cage Center in Bitonto is a prestigious higher music academy and accredited eCampus study centre in the metropolitan city of Bari, offering contemporary musical composition, sound engineering, electronic music, and arts administration programs.

Academic overview & excellence

Inspired by avant-garde musical pioneer John Cage, the centre combines classical conservatoire conservatory-level instrument instruction with advanced sound design laboratories, recording studios, and university degree programs in musicology, entertainment law, and media production.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to musical performance and composition programs requires an audition and portfolio presentation of original works. Academic degree pathways require an accredited high school diploma. International students must demonstrate proficiency in Italian or English.
