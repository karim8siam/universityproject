Georgia College & State University

Georgia College & State University (GCSU) is Georgia’s designated Public Liberal Arts University, renowned for undergraduate research mentorship, the Flannery O’Connor literary heritage, and AACSB-accredited business analytics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Set in Georgia’s historic antebellum capital, GCSU combines small-seminar liberal arts instruction with cutting-edge laboratories in the College of Arts and Sciences and the J. Whitney Bunting College of Business and Technology. It houses the Flannery O’Connor Collection and active aquatic ecology stations along Lake Sinclair.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic GPA, curriculum rigor (AP/IB/Dual Enrollment), and optional standardized test scores. Graduate programs require an accredited Bachelor degree (typically 3.0+ GPA), letters of recommendation, and program-specific writing samples.
