University of New Mexico - Taos

UNM Taos · Taos, New Mexico, United States

The University of New Mexico - Taos is an accredited branch campus of UNM nestled in the high-desert mesa beneath the Sangre de Cristo Mountains at 280 La Entrada Road in Ranchos de Taos, NM. Designated as a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI), UNM-Taos delivers exceptional academic degrees in nursing, solar energy and natural resources, traditional Southwestern studio arts, and seamless UNM transfer degrees.
