Eastern Connecticut State University

Eastern Connecticut State University is Connecticut’s designated public liberal arts university in Willimantic, recognized for faculty-mentored undergraduate research, environmental earth science, digital art and media, and pre-health biology.

Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the New England Commission of Higher Education (NECHE), Eastern blends liberal arts inquiry with advanced scientific laboratory investigations in its state-of-the-art Science Building and Fine Arts Instructional Center. Undergraduates participate in faculty-mentored research in Long Island Sound coastal ecology, paleoclimate geology, and early childhood education.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with a minimum 2.50 GPA; Eastern is test-optional. Holistic evaluation considers extracurricular engagement, academic course rigor, personal essay, and recommendations.
