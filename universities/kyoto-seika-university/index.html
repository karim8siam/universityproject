Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1968 in northern Kyoto, Kyoto Seika University is globally renowned as the world's first university to establish an academic Faculty of Manga and a graduate doctoral program in Manga Studies. It operates the famous Kyoto International Manga Museum in partnership with the City of Kyoto and provides premier degrees in contemporary visual art, animation, product design, and architectural humanities.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Faculty of Manga: comic art, character design, and academic Manga Studies (Ph.D.)
  • Animation production, CGI character rigging, and stop-motion filmmaking
  • Traditional and contemporary Kyoto crafts (ceramics, textiles, printmaking)
  • Architecture, interior design, and environmental visual communication

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Doctor of Philosophy in Manga Studies (Ph.D.) (Doctoral Degree) — First-in-the-world doctoral degree examining manga semiotics, visual storytelling grammar, and global popular culture history.
  • Master of Fine Arts in Animation & Digital Media (Graduate Degree) — Two-year studio master's degree developing original animated short films, character animation, and experimental narrative media.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Character Design & Comic Art (Undergraduate Degree) — Studio-intensive four-year degree training professional manga creators, illustrators, and visual concept artists.
