Bayerische Akademie für Außenwirtschaft (BAA München)

The Bayerische Akademie für Außenwirtschaft (BAA München) is Bavaria's premier professional higher education academy dedicated to international trade management, global supply chain compliance, cross-border customs law, and European export economics.

Academic overview & excellence

Founded in Munich with backing from the Bavarian Ministry of Economic Affairs and leading industrial chambers (IHK), the Bayerische Akademie für Außenwirtschaft delivers accredited diplomas and executive master's certificates in global trade governance. The academy specializes in international contract negotiation, tariff structuring, sanctions compliance, and Eurasian logistics networks, collaborating closely with multinational export conglomerates across southern Germany.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to diploma and continuing studies programs requires a recognized secondary education certificate (Abitur or Fachhochschulreife) and vocational training, or a bachelor's degree in business administration, economics, or jurisprudence. Non-German credentials require comparability verification via the Bavarian Zeugnisanerkennungsstelle. Language requirements demand German B2/C1 and English B2 for international trade streams.

Bachelor's

Diplom-Betriebswirt (BAA) in International Trade and Export Management

Two-year professional diploma covering European customs regulations, international trade financing (letters of credit, Incoterms 2020), export risk management, and foreign market entry strategies.

Funding: Bavarian Master-Craftsman Grant (Aufstiegs-BAföG) covering up to 75% of tuition costs Tuition: €4,800 per academic year Deadline: August 31, 2027
Master's

Executive Master in Global Supply Chain Governance and Customs Law

Fourteen-month executive postgraduate diploma examining dual-use export controls, European Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (LkSG) compliance, and automated customs clearance architectures.

Funding: IHK München Export Scholarship providing €2,500 tuition subsidies Tuition: €6,200 total program tuition Deadline: September 15, 2027
Scholarship

Bavarian Foreign Trade Merit Scholarship for International Scholars

Merit-based scholarship supporting outstanding international scholars from Central and Eastern Europe pursuing foreign trade specializations in Bavaria.

Funding: Full €4,800 tuition waiver and €600 monthly living stipend for one academic year Tuition: Fully waived Deadline: July 1, 2027
Fellowship

Transatlantic Trade Policy & Sanctions Compliance Research Fellowship

Nine-month research fellowship investigating EU-US trade agreements, transatlantic digital tariffs, and international sanctions compliance for German Mittelstand exporters.

Funding: €18,000 annual research grant and visiting scholar desk at the Munich Trade Center Tuition: Waived Deadline: May 30, 2027
Internship

Bavarian Industrial Export Management Internship

Six-month practicum at Bavarian automotive, machinery, or aerospace exporters in Munich, managing export licensing, certificate of origin filings, and foreign client logistics.

Funding: Paid corporate placement with monthly salary of €1,400 to €1,800 Tuition: €0 placement fee Deadline: June 30, 2027
PhD

Doctoral Research Assistantship in European Customs Law and Geoeconomics

Three-year collaborative doctorate in partnership with Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München examining multilateral WTO dispute settlement and carbon border adjustment mechanisms (CBAM).

Funding: TV-L E13 65% doctoral salary approximately €32,000 annually Tuition: €0 tuition (Bavarian state higher education framework) Deadline: April 15, 2027
Course

Intensive 3-day executive masterclass in International Commercial Terms (Incoterms 2020), carrier liability limitations, and CISG international sales contracts.

Funding: Company group discounts and Bildungsurlaub tax deduction eligible Tuition: €980 complete seminar tuition Deadline: October 15, 2027
