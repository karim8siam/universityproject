Louisiana State University of Alexandria

LSU Alexandria · Alexandria, Louisiana, United States

Louisiana State University of Alexandria is a public undergraduate university in Alexandria, Louisiana, and a premier regional institution of the LSU System. LSUA is widely celebrated for nursing, allied health, business administration, and teacher education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

LSUA provides student-centered bachelor's degree education with small classes and applied clinical instruction. The School of Nursing is renowned for industry-leading NCLEX pass rates, while the College of Business prepares leaders in agricultural business, management, and accounting.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school core GPA of 2.0 or higher, completion of Louisiana TOPS high school curriculum, and ACT/SAT scores or test-optional evaluation pathways.
