Institutional Overview

Stichting Geert Grote Universiteit (GGU Deventer) is a distinguished academic foundation and higher institute in the historic Hanseatic city of Deventer. Named after Geert Grote, the 14th-century founder of the Modern Devotion (Devotio Moderna) which shaped Erasmus and northern European humanist thought, GGU delivers university-level seminars, masterclasses, and research fellowships in philosophical anthropology, spiritual heritage, and societal ethics.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Epicenter of Devotio Moderna research, connecting 14th-century intellectual history with modern societal renewal
  • Prime location in Deventer's medieval Latin school quarter, where Erasmus of Rotterdam was educated
  • Intimate academic colloquia combining philosophy of mind, contemplative ethics, and social economics
  • Direct preservation and translation of historic Hanseatic manuscripts and spiritual treatises
