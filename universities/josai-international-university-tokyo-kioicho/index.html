Academic overview & excellence

Situated in the historic diplomatic and governmental heart of Tokyo adjacent to Nagatacho and Akasaka, the Kioicho Campus provides state-of-the-art multimedia editing studios, sound recording laboratories, digital film screening halls, and global business seminar rooms. The campus hosts JIU's Faculty of Media Studies and the Graduate School of Business Design. Students benefit from direct proximity to Japanese television networks, international film production companies, advertising conglomerates in Roppongi and Ginza, and multinational embassies.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to undergraduate degrees and graduate programs at JIU Tokyo Kioicho Campus is open to domestic and international applicants. Undergraduates submit high school transcripts, personal statements, and language credentials (JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-medium programs, or TOEFL iBT 70+/IELTS 5.5+ for English Global College tracks). Graduate applicants in Media Studies or Business Design submit a portfolio of creative/analytical work, a 2,000-word research plan (Kenkyu Keikakusho), and letters of recommendation.
