York University - Nebraska

York University · York, Nebraska, United States

York University (formerly York College) is an independent Christian liberal arts university founded in 1890, situated on a 50-acre campus in York, Nebraska. Renowned for values-centered education, biology and pre-health preparation, criminal justice, business administration, and organizational leadership, York provides personalized faculty mentorship and practical field internships in a tight-knit Midwestern academic community.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

York University offers bachelor's degrees and select master's programs through divisions of Natural Sciences & Mathematics, Business, Education, and Humanities. The university operates the Holthus Science Center and the Bartholomew Performing Arts Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, transcripts, and personal character statements with test-optional pathways. York University offers merit scholarships covering up to $12,000/year for incoming undergraduate scholars based on academic performance.
