Cape Breton University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Cape Breton University (CBU), situated on Cape Breton Island in Sydney, Nova Scotia, is an energetic public Canadian university globally recognized for clean energy research at the Verschuren Centre for Sustainability in Energy and the Environment, its unique MBA in Community Economic Development, Indigenous post-secondary education via Unama'ki College, and marine coastal ecosystem science.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Verschuren Centre Clean Technology & Industrial Carbon Capture Research Fellowship

Advanced applied research placement at the Verschuren Centre scaling up hollow-fiber membrane contactors for industrial post-combustion CO2 capture and geothermal mine-water heat recovery.

Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Community Economic Development

Canada's premier MBA focused on regional resilience, Indigenous business partnerships, cooperative enterprise models, public policy, and local economic development.

Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Atlantic Coastal Wetland and Estuarine Biogeochemistry

Four-year doctoral candidacy investigating blue carbon storage, coastal erosion mitigation, and eelgrass restoration within the UNESCO Bras d'Or Lake Biosphere Reserve in Cape Breton.

CBU International Student Academic Merit and Community Leadership Award

Endowed scholarship program awarded to incoming international graduate students exhibiting outstanding academic merit, community engagement, and global citizenship.

Cape Breton Former Coal Mining Legacy & Geothermal District Energy Practicum

Five-month applied field research internship monitoring geothermal energy extraction from flooded historical coal mine shafts in Glace Bay, providing low-carbon heating for municipal infrastructure.

Unama'ki College Postdoctoral Fellowship in Mi'kmaw Two-Eyed Seeing (Etuaptmumk)

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at Unama'ki College advancing research that harmonizes Indigenous ecological knowledge with Western scientific methodologies in natural resource co-management.

Master of Science in Applied Computing and Industrial Data Intelligence

Two-year graduate degree delivering specialized competencies in edge artificial intelligence, predictive industrial maintenance, IoT sensor networks, and big data pipeline optimization.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A four-year bachelor's degree from a recognized university with a minimum cumulative average of 75% (B equivalent) in undergraduate coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science or Master of Arts with minimum 80% average; accepted thesis proposal and confirmation of faculty research supervision.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 80 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research proposal detailing clean tech, community development, or environmental objectives, methodology, and facility needs.
Application fee
CAD $120 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree awards, CV, 2 confidential academic references, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition at Cape Breton University ranges from CAD $16,500 to $22,000 per year. Fully funded research master's and doctoral candidates receive research assistantships, Nova Scotia Graduate Scholarships (CAD $15,000 to $20,000), and Verschuren Centre industrial stipends averaging CAD $22,000 to $26,000 per year.
