Northeast Community College

Northeast CC · Norfolk, Nebraska, United States

Northeast Community College is a comprehensive public community college based in Norfolk, Nebraska. Known across the Midwest for precision agriculture, wind energy technology, nursing, and veterinary technology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Northeast CC operates the Chuck M. Pohlman Agriculture Complex, the Applied Technology Building, and extended campuses in O'Neill, South Sioux City, and West Point. It serves a 20-county agricultural region with high-tech farm test plots and wind turbine simulators.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Northeast CC operates an open-access admissions policy for high school graduates and GED completers. Health sciences programs (Registered Nursing, Paramedic, Physical Therapist Assistant) require competitive secondary admission and prerequisite science benchmarks.
