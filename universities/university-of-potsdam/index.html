University of Potsdam: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Located adjacent to Berlin amidst historic Prussian palace grounds and the prestigious Albert Einstein Science Park, the University of Potsdam (Universität Potsdam) is Brandenburg's premier research university. Deeply integrated with world-leading non-university research institutes (GFZ, PIK, AWI, Max Planck), Potsdam offers zero public tuition fees and world-class scientific training.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in a relevant discipline (minimum 180 ECTS credits) with a German equivalent grade of 2.5 or better.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science or equivalent graduate degree satisfying Brandenburg higher education doctoral regulations, plus written supervisory confirmation from a Potsdam faculty member.
Language proficiency
For English-taught programs: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all subtests) or TOEFL iBT 80+. For German-taught courses: DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, detailed bachelor module description/syllabus, and academic statement of research interests.
Application fee
€0 direct application via university portal or €75 via uni-assist for international credential evaluation.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, official transcripts with grading rubric, English language certification, CV, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

The University of Potsdam charges zero tuition fees for all regular undergraduate, master's, and doctoral programs for domestic, EU, and non-EU international students alike. Students pay only a mandatory semester administrative fee (Semestergebühr) of approximately €316, which includes a comprehensive Berlin-Brandenburg public transit ticket (Semesterticket).
