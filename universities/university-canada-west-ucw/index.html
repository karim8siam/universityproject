Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

University Canada West (UCW) is an innovative business- and technology-oriented institution located in the heart of downtown Vancouver, delivering ACBSP-accredited business administration and digital media degrees tailored for global career mobility.

Institution type
Private Contemporary Business University
Campus location
Vancouver House & West Pender Campuses, Downtown Vancouver
Accreditation
Degree Quality Assessment Board (DQAB) of BC / ACBSP / NCMA
Admissions channel
Rolling Admissions (Fall, Winter, Spring, Summer terms)
Instruction language
English (IELTS 6.5 minimum overall)
Financial aid & support
Founder's Scholarship, International Student Leaders Award, MBA Grants

Institutional Research Strengths

  • ACBSP-accredited Master of Business Administration (MBA)
  • Digital transformation and enterprise business analytics
  • Prime downtown Vancouver campus hubs at iconic Vancouver House
  • Extensive industry networking across Canadian tech and commerce hubs

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) (Graduate Degree) — Intensive business leadership degree with electives in business analytics, digital marketing, leadership, and fintech.
  • Bachelor of Commerce (BCom) (Undergraduate Degree) — Comprehensive commerce education focusing on managerial accounting, supply chain logistics, and entrepreneurship.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Business Communication (BABC) (Undergraduate Degree) — Integrated media and communications degree emphasizing digital media storytelling, PR, and corporate brand management.
