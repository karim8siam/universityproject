Institution Overview & Profile

Arizona College of Nursing (Mesa Campus) is an accredited specialized healthcare institution in the Phoenix metropolitan area offering an accelerated, high-touch 3-year Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) degree, high-fidelity clinical simulation laboratories, and direct clinical hospital partnerships across Arizona health systems.

Institution TypePrivate Specialized Healthcare College
Main CampusMesa Campus (Dobson Road), Phoenix Metro Area, Arizona
AccreditationCommission on Collegiate Nursing Education (CCNE) / ABHES / Arizona State Board of Nursing
Admissions RouteRolling cohort admissions (3 starts per year) with HESI A2 entrance exam
Language of InstructionEnglish (TOEFL iBT 80+ or HESI A2 English Composite 75%+)
Financial Aid & GrantsFederal Title IV Aid, Arizona College Opportunity Grants & Healthcare Tuition Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Accelerated 3-year Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) with zero prerequisite course waiting lists
  • State-of-the-art simulation centre featuring high-fidelity human patient simulators and modern hospital ward mockups
  • Clinical rotations integrated across premier Phoenix valley medical systems and regional hospital networks
  • Rigorous NCLEX-RN board preparation integrated throughout all core nursing clinical semesters
