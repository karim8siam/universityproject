Academic overview & research priorities

Located in the high-tech Baden-Württemberg engineering corridor south of Stuttgart, Reutlingen University operates five specialized schools: ESB Business School, School of Engineering, School of Informatics, School of Applied Chemistry, and the School of Textiles & Design. The university maintains an exceptional industry placement rate, dual-degree international networks, and research centers in smart manufacturing, biomaterials, and cloud architectures.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate programs require the German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or recognized foreign university entrance diploma submitted via Studienkolleg or uni-assist. Master's admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, business, informatics, or natural sciences (minimum German grade 2.5). English-taught degrees mandate IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88; German programs require TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2.
