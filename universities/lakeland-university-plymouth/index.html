Lakeland University

Lakeland Univ · Plymouth, Wisconsin, United States

Lakeland University is a private liberal arts university in Plymouth, Wisconsin, with an international campus in Tokyo, Japan. Lakeland is nationally renowned for its Cooperative Education program, enabling students to gain full-time corporate work experience and graduate with minimal debt.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lakeland features the Kellett School of Undergraduate and Graduate Studies, offering flexible delivery models across Wisconsin and internationally. Signature programs include accounting, forensic science, hospitality management, and computer science co-op positions.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic records (average GPA 3.2) under test-optional policies. Graduate MBA and MS in Leadership applicants submit official transcripts from an accredited bachelor's degree, professional resume, and letters of recommendation.
