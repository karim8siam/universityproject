Karlshochschule International University

Karlshochschule International University is a state-recognized, non-profit private foundation university in Karlsruhe, celebrated for critical management education, intercultural studies, and social transformation.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating entirely in English and accredited by FIBAA with multiple premium seals, Karlshochschule challenges conventional business orthodoxies through constructive management theory, cultural semiotics, international relations, and ethical civic leadership.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate secondary school diploma (Abitur, IB, or equivalent) and an interactive admissions interview day. English proficiency required at CEFR B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 80+). No numerus clausus (NC) cutoff.
