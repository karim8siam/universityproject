Research Park at South Dakota State University

Research Park at SDSU · Brookings, South Dakota, United States

The Research Park at South Dakota State University is a premier university research innovation district situated on 125 acres in Brookings, South Dakota, adjacent to SDSU's flagship campus. Renowned for agricultural biosciences, precision farming technology, animal disease diagnostics, and biofuel engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The Research Park operates the Brookings Innovation Center, the POET Biorefining Laboratory, and the South Dakota Center for Farm Management. It connects SDSU's Jerome J. Lohr College of Engineering and College of Agriculture, Food and Environmental Sciences with multinational agribusiness and biotech corporations.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Academic opportunities at the Research Park operate in conjunction with South Dakota State University Graduate School. Applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA in an aligned STEM, agricultural, or computational discipline.
