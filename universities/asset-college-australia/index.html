Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of Australian Year 12 or equivalent secondary education, or demonstrated relevant industry experience for mature applicants.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on nationally recognized vocational qualifications.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 5.5 to 6.0 overall or equivalent vocational language literacy and numeracy (LLN) test.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of career intent focusing on security risk management or corporate compliance.
Application fee
AUD $150 course enrollment administration fee.
Required documentation
Certified identity documents, verified educational transcripts, LLN assessment test results, and criminal record check for security licensing.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition averages AUD $2,800 to $6,500 depending on diploma or advanced diploma program. Flexible modular fee structures, Queensland Certificate 3 Guarantee funding for eligible domestic residents, and corporate employer sponsorships are available.
