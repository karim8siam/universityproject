International Business University: Business Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Located in the heart of Canada's financial capital at 80 Bloor Street West in downtown Toronto, International Business University (IBU) is an independent, degree-granting business university accredited under the Ontario Postsecondary Education Quality Assessment Board (PEQAB). Designed for the digital economy, IBU offers accelerated, career-focused Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) degrees and specialized MBA programs featuring industry integration and personalized executive mentorship.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of an undergraduate baccalaureate degree from an accredited institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0/4.0 (B grade) in business, economics, engineering, or related fields.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate and graduate degree programs provide academic foundations articulating into doctoral research and executive D.B.A. programs.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 per skill) or TOEFL iBT 88 minimum.
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via the IBU portal including transcripts, personal statement, professional resume, and video interview.
Application fee
CAD $100 non-refundable domestic application fee; CAD $150 for international applicants.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV, English language test report, two letters of recommendation, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international undergraduate students is approximately CAD $7,500 per term across an accelerated 8-term degree model (approx. CAD $20,000 to $22,500 per academic year). Domestic students pay approximately CAD $3,200 per term. IBU offers substantial Global Excellence Entrance Scholarships and Founder's Bursaries providing up to CAD $10,000 across degree studies.
