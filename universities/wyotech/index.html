WyoTech

WyoTech · Laramie, Wyoming, United States

WyoTech is a nationally accredited technical institute based in Laramie, Wyoming, renowned across the United States for world-class, intensive hands-on training in automotive technology, diesel mechanics, advanced collision repair, and precision welding. Accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC), WyoTech delivers 9-month accelerated diploma and Associate of Occupational Studies (AOS) degree programs that feed top mechanics into global industrial giants.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Laramie · Premier National Technical Institute for Automotive, Diesel & Welding Technologies. WyoTech is a nationally accredited technical institute based in Laramie, Wyoming, renowned across the United States for world-class, intensive hands-on training in automotive technology, diesel mechanics, advanced collision repair, and precision welding. Accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC), WyoTech delivers 9-month accelerated diploma and Associate of Occupational Studies (AOS) degree programs that feed top mechanics into global industrial giants.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or approved homeschool credential. Valid driver's license and admissions interview required. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 5.5+ for international candidates. Year-round quarterly cohort starts in January, April, July, and October.
