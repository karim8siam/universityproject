Institutional Overview

Niederrhein University of Applied Sciences (Hochschule Niederrhein), situated in Krefeld and Mönchengladbach in western Germany, is one of the largest and most prestigious universities of applied sciences in the country with over 14,000 students. The university is internationally famous for housing Europe's largest faculty of textile and clothing technology, alongside leading engineering, chemistry, computer science, and business faculties.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Europe's largest academic institution for textile and clothing technology (FTB Research Institute)
  • Premier chemistry faculty with advanced polymer, coating, and surface engineering research
  • Innovative dual study (Duales Studium) combining vocational training with bachelor's degrees
  • Close partnerships with North Rhine-Westphalian chemical and manufacturing giants (Bayer, Lanxess, Evonik)
