Kyoto University of Education

Experience Japan's premier national teacher training university, STEM didactic laboratories, and educational psychology at Kyoto University of Education.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1876, Kyoto University of Education (Kyoto Kyoiku Daigaku) is a distinguished national university in Fushimi, Kyoto, dedicated solely to teacher education, pedagogical theory, STEM educational sciences, and educational leadership. It operates affiliated primary, secondary, and special needs schools across Kyoto.

Institution type
National University Corporation (Kokuritsu Daigaku Hojin)
Campus location
Fukakusa Campus, Fushimi-ku, Kyoto (near JR Fujinomori Station)
Accreditation
National Institution for Academic Degrees and Quality Enhancement of Higher Education (NIAD-QE)
Admissions channel
National Center Test & Secondary Entrance Exams in February/March; MEXT Teacher Training in Autumn
Instruction language
Japanese (English used in international comparative education research)
Financial aid & support
MEXT Japanese Government Scholarships, National Tuition Exemption (100% or 50% waiver)

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Distinguished national teacher training university with over 145 years of educational heritage
  • Comprehensive network of on-site affiliated elementary, junior high, and special needs laboratory schools
  • Pioneering Center for Environmental Education and Science Didactics
  • MEXT designated host university for Teacher Training Program international research fellows

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Education (Faculty of Education) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year comprehensive degree with specializations in science didactics, mathematics education, language arts, social studies, and fine arts pedagogy.
  • Master of Education (Graduate School of Teacher Education) (Professional Master's) — Professional degree (Kyoshoku Daigakuin) preparing master teachers, school principals, and instructional design supervisors.
  • Joint PhD in Educational Sciences (United Graduate School of Education) (Doctoral Degree) — Doctoral research program delivered in consortium examining cognitive learning mechanisms, comparative pedagogy, and educational policy.
