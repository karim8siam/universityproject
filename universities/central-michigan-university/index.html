Institutional Overview

Central Michigan University (CMU) is a premier public doctoral research university founded in 1892 in Mount Pleasant, Michigan. Serving over 15,000 students across seven academic colleges, CMU is nationally distinguished for its College of Medicine, Great Lakes environmental research station on Beaver Island, neurosciences institute, and College of Business Administration.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Carnegie R2 Doctoral University with High Research Activity and world-class Great Lakes Institute
  • CMU College of Medicine dedicated to training primary care physicians for rural and underserved communities
  • Biological station on Beaver Island in northern Lake Michigan providing living limnology laboratory conditions
  • Pioneering College of Science and Engineering with robust automotive, polymer, and neuroscience labs
