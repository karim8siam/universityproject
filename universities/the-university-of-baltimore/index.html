The University of Baltimore

University of Baltimore · Baltimore, Maryland, United States

The University of Baltimore (UBalt) is an innovative public university of the University System of Maryland, situated in the historic midtown cultural district of Baltimore. Known for its renowned School of Law, the Merrick School of Business, and the College of Public Affairs, UBalt specializes in professional, pre-law, forensic psychology, and public administration education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UBalt is organized into four academic units: the School of Law, the College of Public Affairs, the Merrick School of Business, and the Yale Gordon College of Arts and Sciences. Its cutting-edge John and Frances Angelos Law Center features one of the most environmentally sustainable law buildings in the world (LEED Platinum).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school or transfer collegiate records on a rolling basis. Graduate and law school admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, standardized test scores (LSAT for Law; GRE optional for most graduate programs), a statement of goals, and professional references.
