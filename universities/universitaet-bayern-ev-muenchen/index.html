Universität Bayern e.V. - Bayerische Universitätenkonferenz

Universität Bayern · Munich, Bavaria, Germany

Universität Bayern e.V. is the collective association and leadership conference of all eleven public research universities in the Free State of Bavaria, coordinating state-wide research initiatives, high-performance computing, and doctoral mobility.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Connecting world-renowned institutions including LMU Munich, TU Munich, FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, and the universities of Würzburg, Bayreuth, Augsburg, Regensburg, Passau, and Bamberg, the alliance drives Bavaria's €5.5 billion High-Tech Agenda in AI, quantum technology, and green aerospace.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Coordinates collaborative inter-university doctoral academies and postdoctoral funding programs open to outstanding master’s and PhD graduates enrolled at Bavarian research universities.
