Carpigiani Gelato University: Food Chemistry, Artisan Gelato & Dairy Science Guide

Carpigiani Gelato University is the premier global institution dedicated to training professionals in artisanal gelato production, food science chemistry, thermal engineering, sensory analysis, and international gelateria management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Secondary school diploma or recognized undergraduate degree in food science, hospitality, culinary arts, or chemistry for advanced master diplomas.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral credentials; operates applied research laboratories in partnership with regional universities across Emilia-Romagna.
Language proficiency
Proficiency in English or Italian (courses offered in multiple languages including English, Italian, Spanish, and French).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of career ambition detailing culinary business plans or dairy technology focus.
Application fee
€150 registration and workshop lab materials fee.
Required documentation
Curriculum vitae, academic credentials, identity document/passport, and proof of food safety hygiene awareness.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for professional diploma modules typically ranges from €1,200 to €4,500 depending on specialization level (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, and Master). Culinary entrepreneurship stipends and industry equipment grants are available for international scholars.
