Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located on the Flensburg Fjord on the German-Danish border, Hochschule Flensburg is Germany's northernmost university of applied sciences, with a rich maritime legacy dating back to 1886 and renowned modern research in maritime technologies, wind energy, biotechnology, and business informatics.

Institution type
Public University of Applied Sciences (Fachhochschule)
Campus location
Flensburger Campus, Flensburg, Schleswig-Holstein
Accreditation

Admissions channel

German and English (multiple bilingual and English engineering degrees)
Financial aid & support
Zero tuition fees, Deutschlandstipendium, and Interreg cross-border research funding

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Wind engineering, offshore energy grids, and renewable power systems
  • Marine engineering, ship propulsion, and maritime environmental protection
  • Biotechnology, bioprocess engineering, and marine natural products
  • Cross-border Danish-German business and logistics management

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Wind Engineering (Graduate Degree) — Internationally renowned English-taught engineering program focusing on aerodynamics, grid integration, and structural turbine design.
  • Bachelor of Science in Marine Engineering (Schiffstechnik) (Undergraduate Degree) — Accredited engineering program preparing officers and technical superintendents for commercial maritime operations and green vessel design.
  • Bachelor of Science in Biotechnology & Process Engineering (Undergraduate Degree) — Laboratory-intensive program investigating microbial fermentation, downstream processing, and enzyme engineering.
