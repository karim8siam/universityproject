Steinbeis School of Management and Innovation (SMI)

Steinbeis SMI · Berlin, Germany

Steinbeis School of Management and Innovation (Steinbeis SMI) in Berlin offers state-accredited executive bachelor’s, master’s, and MBA degrees focused on creative leadership, digital marketing, AI innovation, and corporate sustainability.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Operating as a premier institute of Steinbeis University, SMI links academic rigor with hands-on enterprise projects. Its alumni lead brand innovation, digital media platforms, and venture-backed startups across Berlin and international tech centers.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires a recognized bachelor's degree (or university entrance qualification for B.A.), professional employment or startup project, and passing the SMI admissions interview.
