Northeastern University Toronto

Northeastern University Toronto is the Canadian graduate research campus of top-tier global research institution Northeastern University, situated in the First Canadian Place financial core and delivering industry-aligned master’s degrees and cooperative research.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Northeastern University Toronto expands Northeastern’s world-renowned cooperative education (co-op) ecosystem into Canada’s premier commercial and technological capital. Located in downtown Toronto, the campus offers accredited master's degrees recognized by the Ontario Ministry of Colleges and Universities. Degree programs span Computer Science (Align MSCS), Project Management, Regulatory Affairs for Drugs and Biologics, and Information Systems. Trainees engage in experiential research alongside leading multinational technology firms, hospital consortia, and venture funds in the Toronto-Waterloo Innovation Corridor.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Computer Science (Align MSCS) (Master's) — Transformative master's curriculum allowing graduates from non-computer science disciplines to transition into software engineering and machine learning through intensive bridge modules and paid co-op.
  • Master of Science in Regulatory Affairs for Drugs, Biologics, and Medical Devices (Master's) — Industry-aligned graduate credential exploring global health authority regulations (Health Canada, FDA, EMA), clinical trial protocols, and post-market safety.
  • Master of Science in Project Management (Master's) — PMI-accredited master's degree developing skills in agile project delivery, enterprise risk analysis, and technology governance.
  • Graduate Certificate in Cloud Software Engineering (Certificate) — Targeted four-course certificate focusing on distributed microservices, containerization, and enterprise cloud architectures.
