Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited 4-year undergraduate university (16 years of formal education) or evaluated equivalent for master's degree admission.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or recognized professional graduate degree, submitted master's thesis, and structured doctoral research proposal in Japanese or English.
Language proficiency
JLPT N1 or N2 for Japanese-taught tracks; TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.0 for international global programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Research plan (Kenkyu Keikaku-sho) of 2,000 to 3,000 Japanese characters detailing theoretical framework and literature.
Application fee
¥30,000 international screening examination fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, graduation certificate, JLPT or English proficiency score report, research plan, and letter of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual undergraduate tuition is approximately ¥1,050,000 to ¥1,280,000. Graduate school tuition is approximately ¥750,000 to ¥920,000 per year. MEXT Japanese Government scholarships, JASSO honors scholarships, and Aichi University international tuition reduction grants (up to 50% waiver) are accessible.
