MacEwan University: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Located in downtown Edmonton, Alberta, MacEwan University is a prominent public undergraduate and polytechnic university known for intensive student-faculty research partnerships, digital innovation, and strong business and community health programs. MacEwan President's International Student Merit Scholarship

Merit-based competitive entrance scholarship granted to top international students entering full-time undergraduate degree programs at MacEwan University.

Post-Baccalaureate Certificate in Information Technology Systems

Intensive applied post-baccalaureate program emphasizing enterprise cloud infrastructure, relational database administration, and security auditing.

MacEwan Urban Wildlife & Biodiversity Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research initiative examining North Saskatchewan river valley biodiversity, urban mammalian movement ecology, and ecological corridor maintenance.

MacEwan Centre for Arts and Culture Design Innovation Research Project

Interdisciplinary research initiative investigating human-centered visual communication, indigenous design ontologies, and inclusive digital accessibility.

Edmonton Downtown Community Health & Social Action Practicum

Supervised clinical and community practicum embedded within municipal inner-city outreach services and public youth advocacy networks.

MacEwan Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Cellular & Molecular Biology

Full-time postdoctoral research fellowship investigating stress-induced epigenetic signaling and plant-microbe interactions in western Canadian cultivars.

MacEwan University Assistant Lecturer in Strategic Public Communication

Full-time tenure-track teaching position teaching corporate communication strategies, crisis response management, and digital content creation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from a recognized post-secondary institution with a minimum GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0 in the final two years of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative third-cycle graduate research pathways require recognized master's credentials and departmental research alignment.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 86 with minimum 21 in each section, or IELTS Academic 6.5 with no band below 5.5 (or Duolingo English Test 115).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of academic intent (500–750 words) outlining career trajectory, community engagement, and disciplinary focus.
Application fee
CAD $110 non-refundable application fee for international applicants via ApplyAlberta.
Required documentation
Official post-secondary transcripts, secondary school certificates, proof of English language proficiency, and two references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International undergraduate tuition averages approximately CAD $18,500 to $22,800 per academic year (30 credits) depending on the faculty (Arts, Science, Nursing, or Business). Institutional awards include the President's Scholarship ($5,000) and international student merit entrance bursaries.
