Sapienza University of Rome (Sapienza Università di Roma)

Founded in 1303 by Pope Boniface VIII, Sapienza University of Rome is the oldest and largest university in Rome, enrolling over 115,000 students. Consistently ranked #1 in the world for Classics and Ancient History, Sapienza is also a powerhouse in Physics, Aerospace, Artificial Intelligence, and Automation. Review verified English-taught Master's degrees, fully funded doctoral scholarships, laboratory internships, and faculty profiles including Nobel laureate Giorgio Parisi.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Sapienza PhD Fellowships (Borse di Dottorato di Ricerca)

Three-year ministerial and university doctoral scholarships covering living stipends, international research mobility supplements, and full tuition waivers for top-ranking candidates in the annual public doctoral competition.

Sapienza PhD in Computer Science (Dottorato in Informatica)

Three-year doctoral curriculum in the Department of Computer Science (DI) at Sapienza, conducting cutting-edge research in algorithm design, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, NLP, and distributed systems.

Sapienza Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics

Two-year English-taught Master of Science (120 ECTS) in the Department of Computer, Control and Management Engineering (DIAG), focusing on computer vision, autonomous navigation, human-robot interaction, and deep learning.

Sapienza Master of Science in Physics

World-class two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) offered in English inside Sapienza's historic Department of Physics, featuring specializations in Condensed Matter, High-Energy Physics, Theoretical Physics, and Complex Systems.

Sapienza DIAG Robotics and Perception Lab Research Projects

Cutting-edge laboratory research positions inside DIAG at Sapienza, developing collaborative industrial manipulators, surgical robotics, vision-based drone control, and safe human-robot interaction systems.

Sapienza Erasmus+ Traineeship & International Laboratory Internships

Two to six-month structured laboratory traineeships enabling international students to participate in experimental biological, chemical, and physical sciences research at Sapienza's university laboratories.

Sapienza Collaborative Student Part-Time Contracts (150-Ore)

Official university part-time employment contracts (150 hours total) assisting in university libraries, computer laboratories, museum collections, and international student reception desks across Sapienza campuses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science or Laurea degree (minimum 3 years, 180 ECTS) in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, or Mathematics satisfying curricular requirements.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Second-cycle Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale, 120 ECTS) with high academic distinction and competitive public examination (concorso) score based on qualifications and oral defense.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to CEFR B2/C1: Academic IELTS 6.0/6.5 minimum or TOEFL iBT 80+ for English-medium graduate curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
For PhD: detailed research project proposal (progetto di ricerca) of 3 to 5 pages outlining objectives, theoretical framework, state of the art, and experimental methods.
Application fee
€10 to €30 pre-selection application fee submitted via the university Infostud portal.
Required documentation
University diploma, complete official transcripts, Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) or CIMEA comparability certificate, CV, and identity document.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Italian public university regulations, annual tuition fees are income-based (calcolata in base all'ISEE parificato). For international students from non-EU countries, tuition is capped between €1,000 and €2,924 per year. Admitted PhD students holding ministerial scholarships study 100% tuition-free and receive an annual living allowance of €16,243.
