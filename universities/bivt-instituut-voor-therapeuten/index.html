BivT (Instituut voor Toegepaste Psychologie & Therapie)

BivT (Bijscholing Instituut voor Therapeuten) is a leading accredited Dutch higher educational institute for applied psychology, clinical hypnotherapy, trauma therapy, and psychosocial counselling, headquartered in Beverwijk near Amsterdam, with nationwide training centers in Utrecht, Eindhoven, and Zwolle.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Beverwijk & Utrecht, Netherlands

Institutional Overview

Accredited by the CRKBO (Centraal Register Kort Beroepsonderwijs), CPION, and recognized by premier Dutch professional healthcare associations (including NVPA, VBAG, and RBCZ), BivT delivers post-HBO certified diploma pathways in Psychosocial Basic Knowledge (PsBK), EMDR therapy, cognitive behavioral techniques, and systemic family constellations.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions require a prior higher vocational (HBO) or university (WO) degree in healthcare, social work, nursing, or human sciences. Modules operate in modular seminar blocks, blending rigorous neurobiological theory, evidence-based psychotherapeutic protocols, and live supervised clinical peer sessions. Course fees qualify for Dutch healthcare professional development subsidies.
