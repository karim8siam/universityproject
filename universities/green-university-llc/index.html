Green University · Boca Raton, Florida, United States

Green University is a specialized private institution based in Boca Raton, Florida, dedicated to higher education in sustainability, ecological design, renewable energy management, and corporate ESG governance. Delivering undergraduate and master's degree pathways, Green University trains environmental advocates and eco-entrepreneurs to lead corporate sustainability transitions.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Boca Raton · Sustainable Architecture, Renewable Energy & Green Business. Green University is a specialized private institution based in Boca Raton, Florida, dedicated to higher education in sustainability, ecological design, renewable energy management, and corporate ESG governance. Delivering undergraduate and master's degree pathways, Green University trains environmental advocates and eco-entrepreneurs to lead corporate sustainability transitions.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma or accredited equivalent for undergraduate entry; recognized bachelor's degree with 2.5+ GPA for master's programs. TOEFL iBT 70+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Rolling admissions across quarterly terms in September, January, April, and July.
