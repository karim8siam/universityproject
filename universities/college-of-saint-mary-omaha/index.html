College of Saint Mary

The College of Saint Mary (CSM) in Omaha, Nebraska, is an accredited Catholic university founded by the Sisters of Mercy, celebrated for developing ethical leaders in the health sciences, nursing, occupational therapy, physician assistant studies, and legal education.

Academic overview & excellence

Situated on an attractive 25-acre campus in central Omaha, College of Saint Mary is dedicated to empowering women through undergraduate degrees while offering co-educational graduate programs in healthcare professions. CSM features a state-of-the-art Gross Anatomy cadaver laboratory, clinical pediatric and adult rehabilitation clinics, high-fidelity nursing simulation wards, and an on-campus Mothers Living & Learning residential program. Academic partnerships connect students directly with the Omaha healthcare ecosystem, including Nebraska Medicine, Children's Hospital & Medical Center, and CHI Health.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admission to CSM evaluates high school cumulative GPA (minimum 2.5/4.0 recommended), standardized test scores (test-optional available), and high school transcripts. Admission to competitive professional graduate programs (such as the Doctor of Occupational Therapy, Master of Science in Physician Assistant Studies, or Master of Science in Nursing) requires an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA, completed science prerequisites, direct patient care experience hours, and letters of recommendation.
