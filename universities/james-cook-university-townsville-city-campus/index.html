James Cook University, Townsville City Campus: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located in the heart of Townsville’s central business and legal district, JCU Townsville City Campus bridges university academics with the Queensland Supreme and District Courts, local government, and northern Australian trade agencies. The high-tech, multi-story campus features moot courtrooms, collaborative enterprise spaces, and financial trading simulations, reinforcing JCU’s global leadership in tropical innovation and regional enterprise.
