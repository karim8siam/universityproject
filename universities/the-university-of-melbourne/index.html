The University of Melbourne

Founded in 1853, The University of Melbourne is Australia's #1 ranked university and a foundational member of the prestigious Group of Eight (Go8). Located in Parkville, Melbourne, the university conducts over AUD $1.3 billion in annual research expenditures across computer science, biomedicine, engineering, and quantum physics. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships (MGRS)

The University of Melbourne's premier graduate research award offering complete tuition coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $37,000 to approximately 600 exceptional domestic and international candidates each year.

University of Melbourne Doctoral Degree (PhD) in Engineering & IT

Four-year advanced doctoral research degree conducting world-leading investigations across electrical engineering, autonomous systems, cybersecurity, biomedical technology, and distributed cloud computing.

University of Melbourne Master of Computer Science

Two-year research-oriented Master's degree comprising advanced coursework and a major year-long 50-point research thesis project under individual faculty mentorship, serving as a direct pathway to doctoral research.

University of Melbourne Master of Engineering (Biomedical with Business)

EUR-ACE and Engineers Australia accredited professional engineering degree integrating tissue engineering, biomechanics, neuroengineering, and commercial medical device entrepreneurship.

Melbourne Centre for Data Science (MCDS) Research Pathway

Interdisciplinary research collaboration hub linking the School of Mathematics and Statistics with the School of Computing and Information Systems, investigating statistical machine learning and high-dimensional data.

Melbourne School of Engineering (MSE) Industry Internship & Research Placement

Structured 10-to-15-week vocational engineering internship placement embedding graduate engineers with industry leaders (CSIRO, Telstra, Arup, Boeing) and advanced university research laboratories.

University of Melbourne Graduate Research Teaching Demonstratorships

Sessional academic employment enabling graduate students to tutor weekly exercise classes, supervise engineering computer labs, conduct workshops, and grade assessments across undergraduate degree programs.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Australian Bachelor's degree (Honours) or equivalent international qualification with minimum weighted average mark (WAM) of 75%+ or 3.3/4.0 GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completion of a 4-year undergraduate degree with First Class Honours (H1 - 80%+) or a Master's degree with a substantial research thesis component (minimum 25% of total coursework).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section) or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing 21, speaking 18, reading 13, listening 13); higher thresholds (IELTS 7.0) for professional fields.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2 to 3-page research proposal specifying research questions, background literature, methodology, and potential faculty supervisors.
Application fee
AUD $130 application fee for international applicants (waived for specific scholarship applicants and partner institutions).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, grading scale guide, curriculum vitae, two academic referee reports, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) and Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships, domestic and selected international PhD candidates study completely tuition-free with full fee offsets (saving up to AUD $135,000) plus an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $37,000.
