Università degli Studi di Foggia

The Università degli Studi di Foggia (UniFg) is an accredited Italian state research university renowned for agricultural biotechnology, Mediterranean food science, clinical medicine at the Policlinico Riuniti, and educational pedagogy.

Academic overview & excellence

Established as an autonomous state university in 1999, UniFg has grown rapidly into a center of scientific excellence in southern Italy. It comprises six primary academic departments: Agriculture, Food, Natural Resources and Engineering (DAFNE); Clinical and Experimental Medicine; Medical and Surgical Sciences; Law; Economics; and Humanities. The university leads cutting-edge research in precision smart farming, functional foods, cardiovascular surgery, and digital learning methodologies.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to Laurea Triennale programs requires an Italian Diploma di Maturità or an equivalent international 12-year secondary school credential. Medical degree (Laurea Magistrale a Ciclo Unico in Medicina e Chirurgia) admissions are strictly governed by the national MUR test. Master's (Laurea Magistrale) courses require a recognized bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline. Non-EU applicants must apply via Universitaly and demonstrate Italian B2 or English B2 depending on program language.
