The University of Foggia (Università degli Studi di Foggia) is a prominent public university in southern Italy, internationally recognized as a center of excellence for precision agriculture, Mediterranean agrifood biotechnology, nutritional sciences, and criminal law.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Laurea triennale (three-year Bachelor's degree) or recognized foreign equivalent with at least 180 ECTS in agricultural sciences, economics, or biology.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Laurea Magistrale (Master's degree) with minimum final grade of 100/110 and successful performance in the public national competitive entrance exam (concorso).
Language proficiency
Italian B2 for Italian-taught degrees; English B2 (IELTS 6.0+ or CEFR B2 certificate) for English-taught graduate tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applicants submit a 5-page research project proposal in Italian or English detailing thesis framework and expected milestones.
Application fee
€30 to €50 public competition registration fee.
Required documentation
Legalized academic diploma, official transcript of exams, Europass CV, research project proposal, and identity document.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is income-tiered under Italy's ISEE indicator, typically ranging from €0 (no-tax area for ISEE under €22,000) up to approximately €1,900 per year. Fully funded doctoral scholarships funded by MUR and PNRR provide €16,243 per year tax-free.
