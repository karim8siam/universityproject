Hochschule Stralsund

Hochschule Stralsund (HOST) is an innovative seaside campus on the Baltic coast, renowned as Germany's pioneer in hydrogen renewable energy technologies, motorsport engineering (Baltic Racing Team), medical informatics, and international tourism management.

Academic overview & excellence

HOST combines compact campus living overlooking Rügen Island with state-of-the-art laboratory facilities. It hosts the Institute for Renewable Energy Systems (IRES) and is a leader in regional Baltic hydrogen economy demonstration projects.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a recognized secondary school diploma (Abitur/Fachabitur) or equivalent university entrance qualification. International applicants apply through Uni-Assist with German proficiency (TestDaF 3/4 or DSH-1/2). Master's in Renewable Energy Systems is offered in English (IELTS 6.5).

Scholarship

Baltic Green Energy & Hydrogen Technology Scholarship

Merit scholarship supporting exceptional international students pursuing renewable energy engineering degrees at the Baltic campus.

Funding: €3,600 annual educational scholarship co-sponsored by regional offshore wind consortia Tuition: €0 tuition fees (semester fee €150 covering student services) Deadline: February 28, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Renewable Energy Systems (English-Taught)

Three-semester international master's degree conducted in English, teaching photovoltaic system design, wind turbine aerodynamics, fuel cells, and hydrogen electrolysis.

Funding: Institute for Renewable Energy Systems (IRES) research student contracts: €1,000/month Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: January 31, 2027
PhD

Doctoral Research Fellowship in Baltic Offshore Electrolysis

Cooperative doctoral research project investigating direct seawater electrolysis and membrane degradation under fluctuating offshore wind power inputs.

Funding: Salaried TV-L E13 research position (€49,500 per annum) for 3 years Tuition: €0 Deadline: March 15, 2027
Fellowship

Baltic Racing Formula Student Electric Powertrain Fellowship

Hands-on engineering fellowship designing carbon-fiber monocoques, regenerative braking inverters, and high-voltage battery enclosures for international competition.

Funding: €4,000 engineering stipend and leadership role in Formula Student electric racecar development Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 1, 2027
Internship

MV Werften Maritime Offshore Logistics Practicum

Twenty-week mandatory engineering internship analyzing heavy lift transport vessels, offshore wind foundation installation, and port logistics.

Funding: €950 monthly company stipend placed with Baltic maritime engineering firms Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 30, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Science in Medical Informatics and Biomedical Engineering

Seven-semester undergraduate curriculum covering hospital information systems, DICOM medical imaging processing, AI diagnostic algorithms, and telemedicine security.

Funding: Deutschlandstipendium (€300/month) eligible upon enrollment Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: July 15, 2027
Course

Certificate in Offshore Wind Energy Operations & Safety Management

Six-week intensive certificate covering Global Wind Organisation (GWO) basic safety training, offshore turbine SCADA monitoring, and blade maintenance.

Funding: GWO certified industrial training subsidy eligible Tuition: €1,600 total course fee Deadline: August 30, 2027
