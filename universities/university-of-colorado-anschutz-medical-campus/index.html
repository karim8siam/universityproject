University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus

CU Anschutz · Aurora, Colorado, United States

The University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus (CU Anschutz) in Aurora, Colorado, is the largest academic health sciences center in the Rocky Mountain region and a world-renowned biomedical research institution. Originating in 1883 and relocated to a cutting-edge 256-acre medical campus, CU Anschutz encompasses the School of Medicine, Skaggs School of Pharmacy, Colorado School of Public Health, and two nationally ranked hospitals: UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital and Children's Hospital Colorado.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

CU Anschutz generates over $700 million in annual sponsored research funding. It is internationally celebrated for revolutionary cancer clinical trials at the CU Cancer Center, breakthrough neurobiology at the Linda Crnic Institute for Down Syndrome, immunology, and diabetes therapeutics at the Barbara Davis Center for Diabetes.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Medical admissions are processed via AMCAS with CASPer exams, MCAT scores, and rigorous clinical prerequisites. Biomedical doctoral programs are fully funded through the Graduate School and require extensive bench research experience, academic transcripts, and letters of recommendation.
