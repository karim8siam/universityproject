University Park Campus

University Park Campus is the flagship 330-acre landscaped campus of the University of Nottingham, a world top-100 Russell Group institution renowned globally for MRI body imaging invention (Sir Peter Mansfield Nobel Prize), sustainable chemistry, and green engineering.

Academic overview & excellence

Centered around Highfields Lake and the Trent Building, University Park houses the School of Chemistry, School of Physics and Astronomy, Faculty of Engineering, and the Biodiscovery Institute, driving pioneering research in magnetic resonance, composite aerospace manufacturing, and plant genomics.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions requires standard A-Level grades typically AAA-AAB or IB 36-34 points. Postgraduate research programs (PhD/MRes) require an upper second-class honours degree (2:1) or master's degree in a relevant STEM discipline with an approved project proposal. English: IELTS 6.5 to 7.0.
