Wisconsin Lutheran College

WLC · Milwaukee, Wisconsin, United States

Wisconsin Lutheran College (WLC) is an independent Christian liberal arts college founded in 1973, situated on a picturesque suburban campus in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, directly adjacent to the Milwaukee Regional Medical Center. Acclaimed for undergraduate biology, pre-medical health sciences, nursing, neuroscience, and servant leadership, WLC provides intensive undergraduate research collaborations and state-of-the-art laboratory training in the Generac Hall science complex.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WLC offers more than 40 undergraduate majors and graduate professional programs across the School of Arts and Sciences, School of Business, and School of Professional Studies. WLC maintains research partnerships with the Medical College of Wisconsin and the Versiti Blood Research Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, core coursework, and personal essays with test-optional reviews. Merit scholarships covering $10,000 to $20,000/year are automatically awarded to eligible undergraduate applicants based on academic credentials.
