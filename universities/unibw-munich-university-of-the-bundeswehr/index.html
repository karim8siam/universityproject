Universität der Bundeswehr München

The Bundeswehr University Munich (Universität der Bundeswehr München - UniBw Munich) in Neubiberg is an elite federal research institution, home to the dtec.bw center for digital innovation, leading European research in aerospace, cybersecurity, satellite navigation, and autonomous defense systems.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1973 on a dedicated 140-hectare research campus in Neubiberg just south of Munich, UniBw operates under a trimester system enabling intensive study cycles. While primarily educating officer candidates of the German Armed Forces, UniBw conducts extensive open civilian research funded through the Research Center for Digitalization and Technology of the Bundeswehr (dtec.bw) and the Research Institute CODE (Cyber Defence).

Research admissions & international criteria

Military students enter through the Bundeswehr Officer Candidate Selection Center (OPZ Cologne). Civilian master's and doctoral programs in Cyber Security, Aerospace, and Computer Science are open to qualifying German and international applicants holding a recognized bachelor's or master's degree in a STEM field, subject to background clearance and faculty chair acceptance.
