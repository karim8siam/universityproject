University of South Florida

USF · Tampa, Florida, United States

The University of South Florida (USF) is an elite member of the Association of American Universities (AAU) and one of the nation's fastest-rising public Carnegie R1 research institutions, situated on a sprawling campus in Tampa, Florida. Driving over $600 million in annual research, USF is internationally celebrated for medical breakthroughs at USF Health, patent generation, marine science at St. Petersburg, cybersecurity, and public health.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

USF encompasses 14 colleges and schools including the Morsani College of Medicine, College of Engineering, College of Marine Science, College of Public Health, Muma College of Business, and the Taneja College of Pharmacy. Key institutes include the Global Virus Network Center of Excellence, Florida Center for Cybersecurity (Cyber Florida), and the Clean Energy Research Center (CERC).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA and SAT/ACT scores. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
