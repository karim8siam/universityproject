Polo di Ricerca e Studi Universitari eCampus

ERSAF Polo di Ricerca e Studi Universitari in Cerignola is an established higher education and research hub in the province of Foggia, operating in partnership with Università Telematica eCampus to deliver university degrees in law, education, literature, and healthcare administration.

Academic overview & excellence

The Cerignola centre delivers undergraduate and postgraduate curricula tailored to regional development needs in Southern Italy. Its research initiatives focus on educational innovation, special needs pedagogy, digital learning methodologies, and agricultural legal frameworks.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires an accredited Italian high school diploma or recognized foreign equivalent. Prospective students receive individualized study counseling, access to online digital libraries, and in-person tutor sessions for examination preparation.
