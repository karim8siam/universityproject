University of Education Zurich: Educational Research, Collaborative Doctorates & Master's

The University of Education Zurich (Pädagogische Hochschule Zürich / PHZH) is the largest teacher education university in Switzerland, located next to Zurich main station. In close collaboration with the University of Zurich (UZH) and ETH Zurich, PHZH conducts cutting-edge research in teaching and learning, educational technology, subject didactics, and school development.

Joint PHZH & University of Zurich (UZH) Doctoral Fellowship in Education Sciences

Four-year salaried doctoral research fellowship delivered in partnership with the University of Zurich (UZH) investigating learning cognition, classroom discourse, and educational equity.

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship (ESKAS) at PHZH

Prestigious federal scholarship awarded by the Swiss Confederation to foreign researchers pursuing doctoral or postdoctoral research in Zurich.

Master of Science in Subject Didactics & Teaching Methodology (Joint Degree)

Postgraduate master's degree exploring mathematical didactics, scientific inquiry learning, curriculum design, and evidence-based instruction.

Center for Education & Digital Transformation (ZfED) Research Assistantship

Applied research post investigating computational thinking pedagogy, AI-based formative assessment tools, and digital media ethics in Swiss schools.

Zurich Cantonal Schools Clinical Teaching & Didactics Practicum

Supervised teaching internship in Zurich secondary schools mastering adaptive lesson design, individual feedback protocols, and inclusive classroom practices.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in School Development & Educational Governance

Two-year postdoctoral appointment examining school leadership, data-driven school improvement, quality assurance mechanisms, and educational federalism.

Master of Advanced Studies (MAS) in Adult Education & Organizational Coaching

Executive continuing education degree preparing corporate trainers and adult educators in systemic coaching, workplace learning psychology, and organizational change.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree in education, social sciences, psychology, or a teaching-relevant discipline from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 120 ECTS or 300 ECTS total) with above-average grades; doctoral degree conferred jointly with the University of Zurich (UZH) Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences.
Language proficiency
German C2 for teacher credentialing; German C1 or English C1 (TOEFL 95+, IELTS 7.0+) for specialized research master's and joint doctoral cohorts.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research exposé (5–8 pages) outlining research questions, theoretical pedagogical framework, and empirical methodology.
Application fee
100 to 150 CHF administrative application fee.
Required documentation
University degrees, certified transcripts, CV, language certificates, and statement of research intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international students at PHZH is regulated by the Canton of Zurich at approximately 720 to 1,220 CHF per semester. Fully funded doctoral assistant positions (Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in) provide salaried contracts of 65,000 to 78,000 CHF per year.
