Vita-Salute San Raffaele University: Medicine, Gene Therapy & Philosophy Guide

Vita-Salute San Raffaele University (Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele - UniSR) in Milan is an elite Italian private university integrated within the San Raffaele Research Hospital, globally celebrated for world-first ex-vivo gene therapy approvals (Strimvelis, Libmeldy), neurosciences, and bioethics.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

San Raffaele International Doctoral Fellowship in Molecular Medicine

Prestigious doctoral fellowship hosted at the San Raffaele Scientific Institute (SRSI) investigating gene editing, cancer immunotherapy, cellular therapy, and clinical neurobiology.

International Master of Science in Cognitive Neuroscience (MSc)

Two-year English-taught Master of Science combining cognitive neuropsychology, fMRI and MEG neuroimaging, brain-computer interfaces, and neural computation at UniSR.

Ph.D. in Cellular & Molecular Gene Therapy for Rare Monogenic Diseases

Doctoral research position at the world-leading San Raffaele Telethon Institute for Gene Therapy (SR-Tiget) engineering lentiviral vectors and CRISPR base editors for hematopoietic stem cells.

San Raffaele Experimental Neuro-Immunology & Multiple Sclerosis Project

Translational research study at the Institute of Experimental Neurology (INSPE) tracking neuroinflammation, microglial activation, and remyelination in neurodegenerative disease models.

San Raffaele Research Hospital Clinical Immuno-Oncology Practicum

Six-month clinical residency embedded within the Phase I Clinical Trial Center of San Raffaele Hospital, monitoring CAR-T cell immunotherapy infusions and cytokine release syndrome.

SR-Tiget Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Epigenetic Editing & Stem Cells

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the San Raffaele Telethon Institute exploring targeted DNA methylation and zinc-finger epigenetic editors for long-term gene silencing in vivo.

Full Professor in Molecular Oncology & Cancer Epigenetics

Permanent professorial appointment in the Faculty of Medicine and Surgery, directing an independent cancer biology laboratory and leading clinical translation at the San Raffaele Cancer Center.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science in Biology, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, or Biomedical Engineering (180 ECTS) with high academic GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (M.Sc., Laurea Magistrale) in Life Sciences or Medicine with honors and demonstrated laboratory research experience.
Language proficiency
English programs: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 each band) or TOEFL iBT 88.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research proposal (1,500 words) specifying alignment with San Raffaele Scientific Institute (SRSI) research units.
Application fee
€100 application assessment fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diploma, transcript of exams, curriculum vitae, statement of research interests, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the International MD Program is tiered based on family income, ranging from €8,000 to €20,140 per year. Master of Science programs range from €4,000 to €11,000 per year. Fully funded PhD fellowships provide €21,000 to €24,000 net per year plus research allowance.
