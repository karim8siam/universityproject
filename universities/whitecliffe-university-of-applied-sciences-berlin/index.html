Academic overview & research priorities

Rooted in design thinking and digital media production, Whitecliffe Berlin combines interactive studio teaching with contemporary digital industry practices. Students develop high-impact portfolios in UI/UX architecture, digital animation, virtual production, and creative tech entrepreneurship.

Research admissions & international criteria

Applicants must submit an artistic or digital design portfolio alongside secondary school qualifications. All programs are delivered in English, requiring IELTS 6.0+ or equivalent, accompanied by a creative interview.
