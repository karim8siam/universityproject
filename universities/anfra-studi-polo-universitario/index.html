Anfra Studi - Polo Universitario

Anfra Studi is an accredited university educational hub (Polo Universitario) located in Taranto, Puglia. Partnering with certified Italian telematics and state university networks, Anfra Studi delivers legally recognized Laurea Triennale, Laurea Magistrale, and master universitari degrees across law, economics, education, and digital engineering.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Taranto & Puglia, Italy

Institutional Overview

Operating modern educational campuses with advanced digital learning environments and in-person tutorial support, Anfra Studi serves southern Italian students and working professionals. Programs conform strictly to Italian MIUR guidelines (Ministero dell'Università e della Ricerca), featuring integrated tutoring, exam preparation sessions, and regional corporate internships.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at Anfra Studi are open year-round with rolling matriculation dates. Candidates require an Italian Diploma di Maturità (upper secondary school diploma) or foreign equivalent recognized by Dichiarazione di Valore. Regional DSU Puglia tuition reductions, flexible payment installments, and corporate partnership scholarships are available.
