Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1849 just outside Ann Arbor in Ypsilanti, EMU is Michigan's second-oldest public university. The university has undergone an engineering renaissance through the GameAbove College of Engineering and Technology, operating cutting-edge labs in autonomous drone testing, cyber defense, organic polymers and coatings, and flight aviation simulation at Willow Run Airport.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.75 for regular admission; 3.5+ for honors). EMU is test-optional for ACT/SAT. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 2.75-3.0, academic transcripts, resume, and letters of recommendation. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
