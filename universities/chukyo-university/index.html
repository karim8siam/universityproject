Chukyo University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Chukyo University (Chukyo Daigaku), located in Nagoya and Toyota City in Aichi Prefecture (Japan's industrial and automotive heartland), is one of Japan's most prominent private universities. Widely celebrated as Japan's premier sports science and athletic training institution—producing numerous Olympic figure skating and swimming gold medalists—Chukyo also excels in cognitive computer science, mechanical robotics, and international management.

Doctoral Program in Health and Sport Sciences (Biomechanics & Athletic Performance Analytics)

Three-year doctoral candidacy within Japan's foremost sports science complex in Toyota City, utilizing 3D infrared motion capture, high-speed EMG, and environmental altitude chambers to optimize human movement.

Master of Science in Cognitive Computer Science and Human-Robot Interaction

Two-year master's research program located in Toyota City, investigating computer vision, wearable cognitive sensors, social robotics, and AI algorithms for automotive autonomous driving.

Chukyo University Founder Umemura Academic Excellence International Scholarship

Endowed merit scholarship established by the Umemura Educational Institution honoring exceptional international scholars entering master's or doctoral programs at Chukyo.

Automotive Mechatronics & Autonomous Driving AI Research Assistantship

Applied research post in Toyota City testing deep reinforcement learning controllers on full-scale electric vehicle testbeds, collaborating with leading Japanese automotive parts suppliers.

Elite Athlete High-Performance Training Center Sports Science Practicum

Four-month practical training internship assisting national Olympic athletes, measuring lactate thresholds, conducting ForceDecks dual force plate diagnostics, and analyzing recovery biomarkers.

JSPS / Chukyo Postdoctoral Fellowship in Global Sports Management and Olympic Economics

Two-year postdoctoral appointment analyzing the economic legacy of international athletic competitions, digital fan engagement algorithms, and public health sport infrastructure investments in East Asia.

Master of Arts in Global Communication and Digital Media Studies

Two-year master's curriculum combining media sociology, cross-cultural psychology, natural language processing sentiment analysis, and international public relations.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education with a bachelor's degree in a related discipline from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
A master's degree or recognized professional degree; approved research proposal and pre-acceptance from a Chukyo graduate faculty advisor.
Language proficiency
Proficiency in Japanese (JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-medium divisions) or English (TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0 for international research tracks).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research plan in English or Japanese stating research goals, methodology, academic relevance, and facility requirements.
Application fee
¥30,000 entrance examination fee.
Required documentation
Graduation diploma, certified transcripts, research plan, CV, 2 recommendation letters, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international graduate programs at Chukyo University averages ¥850,000 to ¥1,150,000 per year (approximately USD $5,800 to $7,800). International students are eligible for Chukyo University International Student Tuition Reductions (providing 30% to 50% tuition waivers), JASSO Honors Scholarships (¥48,000 per month), and Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarships providing full tuition exemptions and ¥145,000 per month in living stipends.
