Steinbeis Center of Management and Technology (SCMT)

SCMT · Filderstadt, Baden-Württemberg, Germany

Steinbeis Center of Management and Technology (SCMT) operates as a leading post-graduate business institute within the Steinbeis network, offering project-integrated Master of Business Administration and M.Sc. programs.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SCMT pioneeered the Steinbeis Project Competence Concept, where students are employed as Project Managers in multinational enterprises while completing advanced coursework in digital transformation, industrial management, and global marketing.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires a recognized bachelor’s degree in business, engineering, or IT, passing the SCMT Assessment Center, and successful matching with an international enterprise project partner.
