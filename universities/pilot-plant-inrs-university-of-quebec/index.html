Pilot Plant-INRS, University of Quebec

The Pilot Plant at Institut National de la Recherche Scientifique (INRS), part of the Université du Québec network, is an advanced technological platform dedicated to biotechnology, environmental decontamination, green chemistry, and bioprocess scale-up.

Academic overview & excellence

Dedicated exclusively to graduate research and doctoral training, INRS operates state-of-the-art pilot facilities for industrial biofermentation, waste valorization, environmental nanotechnology, and renewable water treatment systems in partnership with federal research councils.

Research admissions & criteria

Prospective master's and doctoral scholars must hold an accredited bachelor's or master's degree in chemical engineering, environmental science, biotechnology, or biochemistry with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2/4.0. Strong bilingual fluency (French/English) is supported.
