Università LUM Giuseppe Degennaro: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1995 and legally recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR), Università LUM Giuseppe Degennaro is situated on a sprawling modern campus in Casamassima, near Bari. LUM integrates elite academic education with the bustling Southern Italian enterprise ecosystem through its landmark LUM School of Management, dynamic clinical hospital affiliations for its English-taught Medicine degree, and leading corporate partnerships in Mediterranean fintech and agri-business governance.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
National/institutional admissions test (IMAT for Medicine); secondary school diploma / bachelor's degree
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Italian & English (Single-cycle Medicine and select Management MScs taught 100% in English)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
€140
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
