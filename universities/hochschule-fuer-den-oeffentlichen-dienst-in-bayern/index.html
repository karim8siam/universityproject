Hochschule für den öffentlichen Dienst in Bayern (HföD Bayern)

HföD Bayern · Munich, Bavaria, Germany

Hochschule für den öffentlichen Dienst in Bayern (HföD Bayern) is Bavaria’s premier state civil service university, providing dual university training for executive civil servants across general administration, taxation, policing, and judiciary.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

With decentralized campuses across Bavaria (Munich, Hof, Herrsching, Sulzbach-Rosenberg, Starnberg), HföD students hold civil service candidate status (Beamtenanwärter) and receive monthly salaries from the State of Bavaria while completing accredited bachelor’s degrees.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires the Bavarian state civil service selection examination (Bayerischer Landespersonalausschuss - LPA-Test), university entrance qualification, German/EU citizenship, and medical fitness.
