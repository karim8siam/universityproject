Florida National University

Florida National University (FNU) is a regionally accredited Hispanic-Serving Institution in the Miami metropolitan area offering career-focused undergraduate and graduate degree programs in allied health, nursing, criminal justice, and business informatics.

Academic overview & research priorities

FNU provides rigorous clinical training and professional degree pathways across its Hialeah and South Campus locations. Accredited by SACSCOC and CCNE, FNU excels in distance education, nursing simulation laboratories, and practical health management education.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation credentials or GED with placement diagnostics. Graduate programs require an accredited baccalaureate degree with a minimum 2.50 GPA, official transcripts, and programmatic prerequisites.
