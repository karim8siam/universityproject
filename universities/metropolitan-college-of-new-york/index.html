Metropolitan College of New York

MCNY · New York, New York, United States

Metropolitan College of New York is a private non-profit college in Lower Manhattan, New York. Founded in 1964 by educational reformer Audrey Cohen, MCNY is celebrated for its 'Purpose-Centered Education' model across emergency management, public administration, and business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MCNY's curriculum integrates academic coursework with semester-long 'Constructive Action' field projects addressing real-world institutional challenges. Key units include the School for Public Affairs and Administration, School for Business, and Audrey Cohen School for Human Services.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions evaluate high school transcripts or equivalency (GED/HiSET) and personal statements. Graduate Master of Public Administration (MPA) and MBA applicants submit an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.7 GPA and professional resume.
