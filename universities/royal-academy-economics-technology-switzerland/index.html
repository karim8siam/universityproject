Royal Academy of Economics and Technology in Switzerland: Degrees & Research Guide

Based in Zurich with facilities in Lucerne, The Royal Academy of Economics and Technology in Switzerland (OUS Royal Academy) is a specialized private Swiss business and technology academy. Accredited by international quality bodies (ASIC Premier Institution, EFMD, ECBE), the Academy provides practical executive master's and doctoral qualifications in international management, digital finance, hospitality, and leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS credits or equivalent) with documented professional work experience in business, management, or technical fields.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 120 ECTS credits) in business administration, economics, or engineering management, plus a defended doctoral research proposal.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency (IELTS Academic 6.5, TOEFL iBT 85, or certified prior education conducted entirely in English).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 5–8 page research proposal detailing research problem, conceptual framework, empirical methodology, and practical managerial contributions.
Application fee
CHF 150 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree certificates and transcripts, CV/resume, personal statement, two reference letters, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for executive master's degree programs is approximately CHF 12,500 to CHF 16,500 per academic year. For doctoral programs (Doctor of Business Administration / PhD), tuition is CHF 8,900 to CHF 11,500 per year over a 3-year research cycle. Merit-based academic tuition fee reductions up to 25% are awarded to qualifying international scholars.
