Institutional Overview

Università degli Studi Link (Link Campus University), founded in Rome and maintaining a dedicated Southern Italian regional campus in the UNESCO World Heritage city of Matera, is an accredited private university known for international relations, business management, digital communications, cybersecurity, and creative cultural heritage management.

Type
State-Recognized Private University (Università Non Statale Legalmente Riconosciuta)
Campus
Matera Regional Campus, Basilicata, Italy
Accreditation
Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) / ANVUR
Admissions
Evaluation of high school / bachelor diploma and motivational entrance interview
Language
Italian and English (bilingual tracks; minimum B2 proficiency)
Aid
Link Merit Scholarships, Basilicata Regional Grants (ARDISU) & Corporate Bursaries

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Strategic focus on digital transformation, enterprise cybersecurity, and strategic intelligence
  • Cultural heritage preservation, multimedia production, and creative industries in Matera
  • International faculty with senior diplomatic, intelligence, and business executive experience
  • Blended interactive learning combining digital seminars with immersive workshops
