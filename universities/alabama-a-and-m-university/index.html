Alabama A&M University: Optics, Plant Genomics & STEM Graduate Guide

Founded in 1875 in the Huntsville aerospace corridor, Alabama Agricultural and Mechanical University (AAMU) is a premier historic land-grant university. Renowned for pioneering contributions in laser optics, materials physics, urban planning, and crop genetics, AAMU collaborates extensively with NASA Marshall Space Flight Center and the Redstone Arsenal research ecosystem.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

AAMU Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA) in Laser Optics and Materials

Funded research assistantship at the AAMU Center for Irradiation of Materials supporting doctoral and master's researchers developing optical crystals and radiation-hardened electronics.

Master of Science in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence

Rigorous 33-credit hour graduate program offering research tracks in autonomous systems, machine learning, cybersecurity, and cloud data architecture.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Applied Physics and Materials Science

Flagship doctoral curriculum emphasizing experimental non-linear optics, ion-beam synthesis, nanostructured thermoelectrics, and aerospace sensor coatings.

USDA-NIFA Agricultural Plant Breeding & Forestry Research Project

Federal grant-funded investigation exploring genomic selection for drought resistance in soybean cultivars and sustainable timber management in the Tennessee River valley.

NASA Marshall Space Flight Center & Redstone Arsenal Engineering Internship

Ten-week hands-on engineering internship placed at NASA Marshall Space Flight Center developing propulsion test telemetry and thermal protection materials.

AAMU Postdoctoral Fellowship in Nanomaterials for Energy Harvesting

Two-year postdoctoral appointment synthesizing functional two-dimensional chalcogenides and investigating carrier mobility for waste heat energy harvesting.

AAMU Presidential Academic Merit Scholarship for Graduate Studies

Institutional merit scholarship awarded to incoming graduate students who have achieved top scholastic honours in mathematics, computer science, or engineering.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited baccalaureate degree from a recognized institution with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 2.75 to 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science in physics, materials science, or related engineering discipline with a minimum graduate GPA of 3.3 out of 4.0.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS Academic 6.5, or Duolingo English Test 105+ for international non-native English applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral statement of research objectives (minimum 1,000 words) specifying alignment with AAMU materials science, optics, or plant genetics laboratories.
Application fee
$45 USD domestic application fee; $70 USD for international applicants.
Required documentation
Official post-secondary transcripts, three confidential recommendation letters, statement of purpose, and resume/CV.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In-state graduate tuition is approximately $422 USD per credit hour ($8,440 USD/year for full-time study); out-of-state and international graduate tuition is approximately $844 USD per credit hour ($16,880 USD/year). Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA) and Teaching Assistantships (GTA) provide full tuition waivers and annual stipends ranging from $15,000 to $24,000 USD.
