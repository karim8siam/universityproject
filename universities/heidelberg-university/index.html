Heidelberg University (Ruprecht-Karls-Universität Heidelberg)

Founded in 1386, Heidelberg University is Germany's oldest university and a continuous University of Excellence. Associated with 56 Nobel laureates (including Max Born, Robert Bunsen, and 2014 Chemistry Nobelist Stefan Hell), Heidelberg is a global powerhouse in fundamental Physics, Medicine, Molecular Biology, and Scientific Computing. Across the Neuenheimer Feld science campus, Heidelberg leads pioneering research in synthetic quantum matter, visual machine learning, and cancer biophysics. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Heidelberg Graduate Academy Fellowships (LGF Scholarships)

Merit-based doctoral fellowships funded under the Baden-Württemberg State Graduate Funding Act (LGFG), supporting outstanding doctoral researchers conducting innovative scientific investigations across Heidelberg.

Heidelberg Salaried Doctoral Positions (Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in - TV-L E13)

Three to four-year salaried university employment contracts at Heidelberg University research chairs, combining cutting-edge scientific investigation with teaching assistance and structured doctoral training.

Heidelberg Master of Science in Scientific Computing

Two-year research-focused Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) based at the Interdisciplinary Center for Scientific Computing (IWR), offering advanced specializations in Optimization, Machine Learning, Parallel Computing, and High-Performance Simulation.

Heidelberg Master of Science in Physics

English-taught two-year Master's program (120 ECTS) in Neuenheimer Feld, partnering with the Max Planck Institutes for Nuclear Physics and Astronomy to train researchers in Quantum Dynamics, Particle Physics, and Biophysics.

German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ) Collaborative Placements

Frontier research positions inside DKFZ (Germany's premier biomedical cancer research center) in Heidelberg, developing innovative oncology immunotherapies, radiation biophysics, and genomic diagnostics.

Heidelberg Summer Research Internships in Molecular Life Sciences

Structured laboratory research internships inside the Center for Molecular Biology of Heidelberg University (ZMBH), embedding students in cutting-edge protein folding, chaperone mechanics, and cell signaling projects.

Heidelberg Academic Student Assistantships (HiWi)

Campus employment positions (up to 80 hours per month) assisting Heidelberg professors in running exercise groups, tutoring undergraduate students, managing library collections, and maintaining scientific computers.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or related discipline (minimum 180 ECTS credits) matching specific subject-area curriculum criteria.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) with high academic distinction, written confirmation of supervision (Betreuungszusage) from a Heidelberg professor, and entry into the Graduate Academy.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught graduate curricula; German proficiency required for German-medium degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research statement (Exposé) of 3 to 5 pages outlining the planned doctoral research problem, methodology, and work plan.
Application fee
Zero application fee via university portal; €75 uni-assist preliminary review documentation (VPD) fee for international degree holders.
Required documentation
University diploma, complete official transcripts with grading scale, CV in tabular format, motivation letter, and uni-assist VPD certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Baden-Württemberg state higher education law, tuition for EU/EEA students is zero (only an administrative semester contribution of approximately €171 is charged). For non-EU international students, tuition is €1,500 per semester. Doctoral candidates (Wissenschaftliche Mitarbeiter) are salaried university employees who pay zero tuition and receive full health and pension benefits.
