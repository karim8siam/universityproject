Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1837 in Greencastle, DePauw blends intensive liberal arts inquiry with distinctive pre-professional centers of distinction. Its College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, School of Business and Leadership, and School of Creative Arts operate world-renowned signature cohorts including Management Fellows, Media Fellows, Science Research Fellows, and Environmental Fellows. DePauw consistently ranks among the top universities in the US for Fulbright scholars and executive leadership placement.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission evaluates high school academic transcripts, secondary school report, counselor recommendation, and writing essays. DePauw is test-optional for ACT/SAT. International students must demonstrate English proficiency via TOEFL (minimum 85), IELTS (minimum 6.5), or Duolingo (minimum 115). All admitted international scholars are automatically evaluated for merit scholarships up to full tuition.
