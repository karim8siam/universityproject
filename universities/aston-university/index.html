Aston University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Aston University, located on a modern city-centre campus in Birmingham, is one of the UK's leading technological universities. Renowned for its world-class Aston Institute of Photonic Technologies (AIPT)—one of the largest optical communications research groups in Europe—and the Energy and Bioproducts Research Institute (EBRI), Aston excels in high-bandwidth fiber communications, bioenergy, and applied artificial intelligence.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Aston Institute of Photonic Technologies (AIPT) PhD in Ultra-High Capacity Optical Fiber Networks

Doctoral studentship at the world-renowned Aston Institute of Photonic Technologies (AIPT), developing petabit-per-second optical transmission systems, spatial division multiplexing, and optical AI hardware.

Energy & Bioproducts Research Institute (EBRI) PhD in Thermal Biomass Gasification

Doctoral research conducted within EBRI's demonstration power plant, optimizing intermediate pyrolysis and thermal catalytic gasification of agricultural residues into clean aviation bio-fuels.

Master of Science (MSc) in Artificial Intelligence with Business Strategy

One-year Master of Science connecting deep neural architectures, machine learning deployments, and cloud analytics with enterprise strategic decision-making and digital transformation.

PhD in Statistical Physics of Complex Networks and Machine Learning

Doctoral research within the Non-linearity and Complexity Research Group (NCRG), applying mean-field theory and statistical physics to analyze loss landscapes and generalizability in deep neural networks.

Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering (Sensors & Medical Devices)

Specialized postgraduate program exploring biosensors, micro-electromechanical systems (MEMS), clinical instrumentation, and medical regulatory compliance in Birmingham's healthcare corridor.

AIPT Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Optical Frequency Combs & Microresonators

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at AIPT, developing chip-scale Kerr micro-combs for ultra-precise optical metrology and parallel coherent telecommunications.

EBRI Sustainable Bioenergy & Circular Chemical Systems Summer Internship

Eight-week technical internship at the Energy and Bioproducts Research Institute, testing biochar soil amendments and characterizing synthetic fuels produced from municipal solid wastes.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A UK 2:1 or recognized international equivalent undergraduate degree in a relevant STEM or business discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
A recognized master's degree with Merit or Distinction, or a First-Class Honours bachelor's degree; comprehensive research proposal and confirmed Aston academic supervisor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all components) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in all sections).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 1,000 to 1,500 word research proposal outlining context, research methodology, proposed equipment, and key academic references.
Application fee
£0 for direct postgraduate research applications via Aston's online research portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree awards, CV, research proposal, two academic reference letters, statement of purpose, and English language test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time UKRI and EPSRC-funded doctoral students at Aston University receive a 100% full tuition fee waiver and a tax-free living stipend of at least £19,237 per year for 3.5 years. International tuition for self-funded students ranges from £21,000 to £24,500 per year for Master's and PhD degrees, with Aston Vice-Chancellor's International Scholarships providing up to £10,000 in fee discounts.
