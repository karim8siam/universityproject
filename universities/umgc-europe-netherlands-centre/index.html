UMGC Europe - Netherlands Academic Centre

University of Maryland Global Campus (UMGC) Europe operates an accredited transatlantic higher education center supporting NATO Joint Force Command Brunssum and allied international personnel in Limburg, Netherlands, delivering US regionally accredited degrees in cybersecurity, management, and intelligence studies.

Academic overview & excellence

UMGC Europe offers flexible hybrid and digital degree pathways accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE). Courses are taught in English by distinguished American and European faculty with state-of-the-art virtual cyber laboratories.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions requires graduation from an accredited high school or GED with open enrollment principles. Master's degrees require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.5–3.0 GPA. Civilians, defense personnel, and international students residing in the Low Countries are eligible.
