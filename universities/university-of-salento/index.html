University of Salento: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Located in the historic Baroque city of Lecce on the southern Adriatic coast, the University of Salento (Università del Salento - UniSalento) is a top public Italian research university. Internationally recognized for advanced nanomaterials, marine biology, physics, and Mediterranean archaeological conservation, UniSalento pairs world-class laboratories with low living costs.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ADiSU Puglia Regional Government Full Study Grant & Housing Bursary

Comprehensive public regional need-and-merit scholarship guaranteeing tuition-free study, free student refectory meals, and accommodation in Lecce.

Master of Science in Materials and Nanotechnology Engineering

Interdisciplinary English-language master's curriculum exploring nanostructured semiconductors, 2D graphene materials, biomedical sensors, and energy storage devices.

Doctoral Fellowship in Physics and Nanosciences at CNR Nanotec & UniSalento

Three-year fully funded doctoral research program conducted in partnership with the CNR Institute of Nanotechnology (Nanotec) in Lecce.

Marine Biological Station of Porto Cesareo Coastal Ecology Research Project

Field research placement situated at the Marine Protected Area of Porto Cesareo investigating Mediterranean coralligenous habitats and alien species migration.

Salento Baroque Architecture & Archaeological Heritage Diagnostics Practicum

Practical archaeological diagnostics practicum utilizing 3D laser scanning and X-ray fluorescence analysis on Salento Baroque monuments and Roman mosaics.

UniSalento Postdoctoral Assegno di Ricerca in Polaritonics & Quantum Fluidics

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment exploring room-temperature exciton-polariton condensates, non-equilibrium quantum fluids of light, and optical switches.

Ricercatore a Tempo Determinato (RTD-A) in Robotics and Industrial Automation

Three-year assistant professorship delivering undergraduate engineering lectures and conducting research on collaborative industrial robots and digital twins.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (Laurea Triennale or equivalent minimum 180 ECTS credits) in a relevant engineering, scientific, or humanities discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Second-cycle Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with honors standing, selected through national public competitive examination (concorso pubblico).
Language proficiency
CEFR B2 level English certification (IELTS Academic 6.0, Cambridge B2 First, or TOEFL iBT 78) for international English-medium curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, transcript of academic records, statement of motivation, and research prospectus (for PhD applicants).
Application fee
€25 pre-enrollment application fee via the Esse3 student portal.
Required documentation
Certified university diploma, official transcript of records, CIMEA Statement of Comparability or Declaration of Value, and valid passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at UniSalento is determined by student family economic standing (ISEE parificato). Students with an ISEE below €24,000 pay zero tuition fees (exempt under the Italian 'No Tax Area' policy, paying only the mandatory €140 regional tax). International students can apply for the ADiSU Puglia regional scholarship, which provides full tuition coverage, free meals, and cash stipends up to €6,600 per year.
