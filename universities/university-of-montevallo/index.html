University of Montevallo

Montevallo · Montevallo, Alabama, United States

The University of Montevallo is Alabama's public liberal arts university, situated on a historic Olmsted Brothers-designed campus in the geographic center of Alabama. Founded in 1896, Montevallo is celebrated for its tight-knit intellectual community, nationally recognized programs in mass communication and visual arts, environmental education, and the famous College Night theatrical tradition.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Montevallo offers comprehensive bachelor's and master's degrees through four colleges: the College of Arts and Sciences, Michael E. Stephens College of Business, College of Education and Human Development, and the College of Fine Arts. The university also operates the James Wylie Shepherd Observatory and the University of Montevallo Environmental Education Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduates, evaluating high school transcripts and GPA (minimum 2.5). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 71+ or IELTS 6.0+).
