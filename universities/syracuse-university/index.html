Syracuse University

Syracuse University · Syracuse, New York, United States

Syracuse University is an internationally renowned private R1 research university located in Syracuse, New York. Acclaimed worldwide for the S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications (the nation's foremost media school) and the Maxwell School of Citizenship and Public Affairs (consistently ranked the #1 public affairs school in America), Syracuse is also globally prominent in gravitational wave astrophysics (LIGO contributor), veterans education, architecture, and computer engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Syracuse comprises 13 schools and colleges, including Newhouse, Maxwell, the College of Engineering and Computer Science, the School of Architecture, the College of Law, and the Falk College of Sport and Human Dynamics. The university operates modern cleanrooms, the Syracuse Biomaterials Institute, and supercomputing clusters for astrophysics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic rigor (average GPA 3.7), personal essays, and extracurricular leadership on a test-optional basis. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ minimum GPA (3.5+ for competitive doctoral fellowships), GRE scores (program-specific), detailed research statement of purpose, and three academic letters of recommendation.
