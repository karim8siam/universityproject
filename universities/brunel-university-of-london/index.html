Brunel University of London: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Brunel University London is a research-intensive campus university in Uxbridge, West London, named after Britain's greatest engineer, Isambard Kingdom Brunel. Internationally acclaimed for materials science—notably the Brunel Centre for Advanced Solidification Technology (BCAST)—Brunel is also a global leader in structural integrity, civil environmental engineering, industrial design, and public health toxicology.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Brunel Centre for Advanced Solidification Technology (BCAST) PhD in Sustainable Lightweight Alloys

Four-year PhD studentship at BCAST researching closed-loop aluminium recycling, high-pressure die casting microstructures, and ultra-high-strength magnesium alloys for automotive decarbonization.

Master of Science (MSc) in Artificial Intelligence and Autonomous Systems

British Computer Society (BCS) accredited master's program providing deep expertise in deep learning, robotic vision, swarm intelligence, natural language models, and AI safety ethics.

Commonwealth Shared Scholarships and Brunel Vice-Chancellor's Postgraduate Awards

Fully funded postgraduate scholarship co-sponsored by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) for talented students from developing Commonwealth nations.

Institute of Environment, Health and Societies Research Assistantship in Endocrine Disruptors & Ecotoxicology

Two-year research post evaluating pharmaceutical residues and PFAS contamination in Thames river catchments, quantifying hormonal disruption in aquatic wildlife.

Brunel Design School User Experience (UX) and Human-Centred Prototyping Practicum

Four-month studio placement working alongside London product design agencies, developing accessible physical and digital touchpoints, user journey architectures, and wearable device prototypes.

Isambard Kingdom Brunel Early Career Fellowship in Structural Integrity and Aerospace Materials

Three-year research fellowship utilizing non-destructive testing, acoustic emission sensors, and finite element micro-crack propagation models on next-generation composite fuselage components.

Master of Science (MSc) in Aerospace Engineering with Advanced Simulation and Flight Dynamics

Accredited master's program utilizing Brunel's Merlin flight simulator, subsonic and supersonic wind tunnels, exploring computational aerodynamics, space flight mechanics, and unmanned aerial vehicle autopilots.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A UK 2:1 or recognized international equivalent bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline (or 2:2 with relevant professional industry experience).
Doctoral admission eligibility
A UK Master's degree with Merit or Distinction, or a first-class honours bachelor's degree; approved doctoral proposal and supervisor commitment.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-scores) or Pearson PTE Academic 59 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
A 1,500-2,500 word research proposal detailing context, specific research objectives, methodological design, equipment requirements, and timetable.
Application fee
£0 application fee for direct online postgraduate research and master's submissions.
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, CV, 2 academic references, personal statement, and evidence of English language qualifications.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition at Brunel University London generally ranges from £21,000 to £26,000 per year. Fully funded doctoral students receive UKRI-aligned stipends of £21,237 per year (incorporating London weighting) plus complete international tuition fee coverage and dedicated laboratory research consumables grants.
