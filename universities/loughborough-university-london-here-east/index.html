Loughborough University London: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 2015 as part of the London 2012 Olympic legacy at the Here East digital and technology quarter, Loughborough University London is the postgraduate campus of the UK's premier university for sport-related subjects (consistently ranked #1 in the world by QS). The campus brings together more than 1,000 master's and doctoral researchers collaborating alongside global sports organizations, creative tech accelerators, and international NGOs.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Minimum UK 2:1 honours degree or international equivalent; personal statement and CV
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.

English
Research statement / Proposal
