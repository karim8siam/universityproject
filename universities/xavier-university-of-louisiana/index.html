Xavier University of Louisiana

XULA · New Orleans, Louisiana, United States

Xavier University of Louisiana (XULA) is a private Catholic historically Black university (HBCU) founded in 1925 by Saint Katharine Drexel in New Orleans, Louisiana. World-renowned as the premier national producer of African American students who graduate from medical school, XULA is acclaimed for the College of Pharmacy, biomedical sciences, chemistry, public health, and cancer health disparities research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

XULA comprises the College of Arts and Sciences and the College of Pharmacy, alongside the Center for Minority Health and Health Disparities Research and Education. The university operates state-of-the-art research laboratories in the Norman C. Francis Science Complex and the Pharmacy Pavilion.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, math/science coursework, and personal statements with test-optional reviews. Graduate and professional admissions (Pharm.D., master's) require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, official transcripts, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+). Merit scholarships are awarded upon admission.
