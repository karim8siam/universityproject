Texas Tech University

Texas Tech · Lubbock, Texas, United States

Texas Tech University is the flagship Carnegie R1 public research university of the Texas Tech University System, located in Lubbock on the South Plains of West Texas. Generating over $230M in annual research expenditures, Texas Tech is globally renowned for the National Wind Institute, the Davis College of Agricultural Sciences & Natural Resources, and the Whitacre College of Engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Comprising 13 colleges and schools, Texas Tech delivers over 150 master's and 60 doctoral degrees. Flagship research entities include the National Wind Institute (operating the VorTECH tornado simulator and atmospheric radar arrays), the Fiber and Biopolymer Research Institute, and the Institute of Genomics for Health and Agriculture.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school class rank and standardized test scores (assured admission for top 10% of Texas classes). Graduate and doctoral applicants require a baccalaureate degree from an accredited university (3.0+ GPA recommended), competitive GRE/GMAT scores (program-specific), statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
