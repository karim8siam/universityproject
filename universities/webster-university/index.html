Webster University

Webster · St. Louis, Missouri, United States

Webster University is a private, nonprofit global university founded in 1915 in Webster Groves, Missouri, a charming suburban community within the St. Louis metropolitan region. Renowned for international education, cybersecurity, business administration at the Walker School of Business & Technology, media communications, and clinical counseling, Webster operates international campuses in Vienna, Geneva, Leiden, Tashkent, and Athens.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Webster comprises five schools and colleges: the George Herbert Walker School of Business & Technology, College of Humanities and Social Sciences, School of Communications, Leigh Gerdine College of Fine Arts, and the School of Education. The university operates the state-of-the-art East Academic Building and the Sverdrup Media Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, essays, and extracurricular involvement with test-optional reviews. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, personal statement, CV, and English proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+). Merit-based international graduate scholarships are awarded upon admission.
