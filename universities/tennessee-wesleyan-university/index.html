Tennessee Wesleyan University

Tennessee Wesleyan · Athens, Tennessee, United States

Tennessee Wesleyan University (TWU) is a private comprehensive institution affiliated with the United Methodist Church, based in Athens, Tennessee. Known for its renowned Fort Sanders Nursing Department in Knoxville, TWU is acclaimed for small class sizes, rigorous pre-professional training, and ethical leadership development.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TWU comprises the Goodfriend School of Business, the Fort Sanders Department of Nursing, and the Department of Natural Sciences. Its curriculum emphasizes clinical nursing excellence, allied health sciences, management, and interdisciplinary liberal arts.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school grade point averages (minimum 2.25) and personal background. Nursing admissions require specialized clinical science prerequisite completions and acceptable TEAS exam results. Graduate programs in nursing and business require an accredited baccalaureate degree with 3.0+ GPA.
