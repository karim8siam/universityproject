Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of an Australian Associate Degree, Bachelor's degree, or recognized overseas equivalent with satisfactory academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; articulation pathways to Australian university degree completion available.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 5.5 to 6.0 overall (minimum 5.0 in any band) or equivalent PTE Academic score 42 to 50.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of study purpose highlighting career goals and commitment to Australian vocational qualification standards.
Application fee
AUD $250 non-refundable international student enrollment processing fee.
Required documentation
Certified academic certificates, passport identification, proof of English language proficiency, and GTE (Genuine Student) documentation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition averages AUD $8,500 to $13,500 per academic year depending on certificate or advanced diploma program. Flexible quarterly installment plans and academic progress merit bursaries are available for international students.
