Institution Overview & Profile

Kobe College (Kobe Jogakuin Daigaku), founded in 1875 by American missionary educators, is one of Japan's oldest and most prestigious private women's liberal arts colleges. Situated on the scenic hilltop of Okada-yama in Nishinomiya, its campus is a designated National Important Cultural Property designed by renowned architect William Merrell Vories. Kobe College is celebrated for its departments of English literature, music performance, psychological science, and human sciences.

Key Academic Strengths

  • Okada-yama Campus: 12 historic buildings designated as National Important Cultural Properties of Japan
  • Exceptional School of Music with conservatory-level training in piano, vocal arts, and orchestral instruments
  • Pioneering tradition of women's higher education, critical thinking, and international global citizenship
  • Small seminar classes fostering close mentorship and superior employment rates in global corporations and teaching
