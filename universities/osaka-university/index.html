Osaka University (Osaka, Japan)

Established in 1931 as one of Japan's designated Imperial Universities, Osaka University (Handai) is consistently ranked among Japan's top 3 research universities and the world's top 80 institutions. Handai is internationally celebrated for pioneer breakthroughs in immunology (home to the World Premier International Immunology Frontier Research Center - IFReC), humanoid robotics (Prof. Hiroshi Ishiguro's laboratory), quantum materials, and mechanical engineering. Osaka University MEXT University Recommendation & OU Fellowships

Japan's premier governmental and university scholarship scheme, providing exceptional international scholars with full tuition waivers and generous living stipends for doctoral research.

Osaka University PhD in Information Science and Technology

Advanced 3-year doctoral research program covering cyber-physical systems, multi-agent systems, deep learning architectures, network security, and quantum information.

Osaka University Master of Engineering in Precision Engineering & Robotics

Internationally renowned master's program taught in English, providing direct laboratory training in humanoid robotics, nanomachining, laser engineering, and micro-electromechanical systems.

Osaka University Master of Science in Physics (Special Integrated Science Course - SISC)

Rigorous 2-year English-medium master's program exploring experimental and theoretical physics, condensed matter, subatomic particle physics at RCNP, and laser fusion.

Immunology Frontier Research Center (IFReC) World Premier Placements

World Premier International (WPI) research placements at one of the world's most cited immunology research centres, investigating innate immunity, immune regulation, and cancer immunotherapy.

Osaka University FrontierLab Mini Short-Term Research Exchange

Three to twelve-month laboratory placement program allowing international science and engineering students to conduct independent experimental research under Handai professors.

Osaka University Teaching & Research Assistantships (TA/RA)

On-campus academic assistantships supporting graduate researchers while they mentor undergraduate laboratory experiments, grade exercises, and support academic research projects.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
16 years of formal education / recognized Bachelor's degree equivalent; passing of the Osaka University graduate entrance examination and interview.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or professional degree equivalent; formal preliminary acceptance by an Osaka University prospective laboratory professor (Kyoju) and research plan approval.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency for English-taught graduate programs: TOEFL iBT 80+ or Academic IELTS 6.5+ (Japanese programs require JLPT N1 or N2).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 3-5 page research plan (Kenkyu Keikaku-sho) detailing theoretical framework, experiment design, and lab alignment.
Application fee
¥30,000 entrance examination application fee; admission fee of ¥282,000 and annual tuition of ¥535,800 (fully waived for MEXT and top international scholarship scholars).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, graduation certificates, 2 letters of recommendation, health certificate, and research plan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is fully waived for scholars receiving Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarships and Osaka University Doctoral Fellowships. Recipients receive 100% full tuition exemption (saving ¥535,800/year plus the ¥282,000 matriculation fee) and monthly tax-free living allowances of ¥147,000 to ¥180,000/month.
