Institute of Energy Process Engineering and Chemical Engineering

The Institute of Energy Process Engineering and Chemical Engineering (IEC) at TU Bergakademie Freiberg is Germany’s premier academic center for high-temperature carbon conversion, synthetic fuels, chemical hydrogen carriers, and circular carbon technologies.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating large-scale pilot gasification, pyrolysis, and catalytic synthesis testing plants, IEC pioneers industrial decarbonization pathways for chemical manufacturing, metallurgy, and heavy energy sectors. Research clusters encompass circular carbon economies (chemical recycling of plastics), synthesis gas processing, underground hydrogen storage, and Fischer-Tropsch e-fuel production.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open to master’s and doctoral researchers in chemical engineering, process engineering, metallurgy, and applied chemistry. Applicants submit recognized university credentials, academic transcripts, and a detailed research proposal.
