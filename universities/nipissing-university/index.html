Nipissing University: Graduate Research, Faculty & Scholarship Portal

Nipissing University is a premier publicly funded liberal arts and science university in North Bay, Ontario, celebrated for top student satisfaction, environmental biology research, Indigenous education, and high-impact scholarship.

Merit-based entrance award automatically evaluated for high-ranking applicants entering Master of Arts, Master of Science, or Master of Education research programs at Nipissing.

Interdisciplinary doctoral program exploring social, environmental, and institutional sustainability within indigenous pedagogy, ecological systems, and educational transformation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year undergraduate honors degree (B.A., B.Sc., or B.Ed.) with minimum 75% average (B+) across upper-level coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in Educational Sustainability or related disciplines with minimum 80% average (A-) and documented research capacity.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 each band) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-4 page research proposal outlining thesis problem, methodology, faculty alignment, and bibliography.
Application fee
CAD $130 non-refundable graduate application processing fee via the Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC).
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, statement of research intent, curriculum vitae, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic graduate tuition averages CAD $7,400 to $9,200 annually. International student tuition is approximately CAD $19,500 to $23,000 annually. Research assistantships, Dean's entrance scholarships, and Tri-Council funding provide substantial support.
