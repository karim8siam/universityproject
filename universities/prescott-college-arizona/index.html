Prescott College

Prescott College · Prescott, Arizona, United States

Prescott College is an innovative private liberal arts college nestled in the pine-covered Bradshaw Mountains of Prescott, Arizona. Nationally celebrated for experiential wilderness education, environmental studies, regenerative agriculture, and social justice.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Prescott College is renowned for field-based learning, operating the Kino Bay Center for Cultural and Ecological Studies in Sonora, Mexico, the Center for Nature and Place-Based Early Childhood Education, and the Frantz Arts Center. Students participate in extended backcountry wilderness orientations across the American Southwest.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Prescott College evaluates applicants holistically based on academic essays, personal readiness for self-directed experiential learning, and transcripts (minimum 2.5 GPA). Standardized tests (SAT/ACT/GRE) are completely optional.
