OST University of Applied Sciences of Eastern Switzerland (OST - Ostschweizer Fachhochschule)

Formed through the strategic alliance of the renowned HSR Rapperswil, FHS St. Gallen, and NTB Buchs, OST Eastern Switzerland University of Applied Sciences (Ostschweizer Fachhochschule) is the leading public university of applied sciences across the six cantons of Eastern Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Situated along Lake Zurich and the Rhine Valley, OST is an international powerhouse in precision optics and photonics (Institute of Microtechnology and Photonics - IMP), renewable energy and solar technologies (SPF Institute for Solar Technology), computer science, and landscape architecture.

OST Swiss Innosuisse Research Assistantships & Master of Science Fellowships

Competitive Swiss research assistantships providing formal cantonal employment contracts, high standard of living, and direct participation in high-tech industrial innovation projects funded by Innosuisse.

OST Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) - Photonics & Precision Optics

Nationally accredited Master of Science in Engineering profile delivered at the Buchs campus in the Swiss Photonics Valley, specializing in laser material processing, optical metrology, and micro-optical sensors.

OST Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) - Energy and Environment (SPF Solar Tech)

Advanced Master of Science profile at the SPF Institute for Solar Technology in Rapperswil, training engineers in photovoltaic thermal collectors (PVT), heat pump systems, and building energy decarbonization.

OST Institute of Microtechnology and Photonics (IMP) Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research conducted within the Institute of Microtechnology and Photonics (IMP), developing ultra-short pulsed laser micromachining, nanometer-precision optical surface metrology, and fiber optic sensors.

OST Institute for Software (IFS) Applied AI Research Fellow

Applied research post at the Institute for Software in Rapperswil, collaborating with Swiss industrial leaders on automated software verification, cloud-native architectures, and robust computer vision models.

OST Solar Thermal & Heat Pump Summer Research Traineeship

Eight-week summer research traineeship at the SPF Institute for Solar Technology in Rapperswil, performing acoustic and thermal testing on high-efficiency heat pumps and solar collector pilot test benches.

OST Senior Research Engineer in Laser Micro-Machining & Advanced Photonics

Senior research engineering role at the IMP Buchs campus, leading industry-collaborative Innosuisse projects on femtosecond laser micromachining of brittle materials, medical implants, and precision semiconductor dies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in engineering, computer science, or natural sciences with above-average academic standing (minimum grade B or 5.0/6.0 on the Swiss grading scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 90 to 120 ECTS credits) with high honors; doctoral candidates are employed under Swiss research contracts and registered in cooperation with Swiss or international research universities (such as ETH Zurich).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) profiles; German C1 for German-medium degree tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A motivation letter (1,000 words) and portfolio demonstrating engineering design projects, programming proficiency, or experimental laboratory experience.
Application fee
CHF 100 non-refundable application fee for Swiss degree holders; CHF 150 for applicants with foreign university qualifications.
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree diplomas, official grade transcripts, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at OST are among the most accessible in Switzerland: standard semester tuition is CHF 800 to CHF 1,000 per semester for Swiss residents and CHF 1,500 per semester for international students. Doctoral research assistants (wissenschaftliche Assistenten) are employed on formal Swiss cantonal research contracts, receiving an annual salary of CHF 52,000 to CHF 64,000 per year gross under Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) and Innosuisse funding scales.
