Illinois College

Illinois College · Jacksonville, Illinois, United States

Illinois College is a historic private liberal arts college in Jacksonville, Illinois, founded in 1829, renowned for environmental biology, pre-medical sciences, business administration, and the signature BreakAway experiential learning program.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

As Illinois’s first college to graduate a class, IC emphasizes undergraduate research, guaranteed graduation in four years through the IC Promise, and hands-on laboratory and field inquiry. Flagship facilities include the Starhill Forest Arboretum and the Parker Science Building.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional, reviewing secondary school GPA, curriculum rigor, personal essays, and extracurricular involvement. International students submit TOEFL or IELTS scores.
