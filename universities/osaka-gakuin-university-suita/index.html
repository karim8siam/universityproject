Academic overview & excellence

Tracing its origins to the Kansai Accounting School founded in 1940, Osaka Gakuin University is situated on an expansive campus in Suita, Northern Osaka. OGU comprises seven undergraduate faculties: Commerce, Business Administration, Economics, Law, Foreign Languages, International Studies, and Informatics, alongside four graduate schools. The university maintains prominent international partnerships with over 50 universities across North America, Europe, and Asia.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation records and standardized EJU scores for international applicants. Graduate school admissions in Commerce, Economics, and Corporate Law require a bachelor's degree in a related discipline and an oral examination on a submitted research proposal. English-medium programs require TOEFL iBT 75 or IELTS 6.0.
