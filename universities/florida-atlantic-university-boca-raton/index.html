Academic overview & research priorities

Established in 1961, FAU is designated as an R1: Doctoral University – Very High Research Activity institution. Across its Boca Raton main campus and specialized coastal research sites including Harbor Branch Oceanographic Institute in Fort Pierce, FAU leads pioneering investigations in ocean energy generation, autonomous underwater vehicles, Alzheimer's neurobiology, and coastal wetland resilience.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with competitive GPA (average 3.7–4.3 weighted), SAT or ACT examination scores, and academic transcripts. Master's and PhD programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in a related discipline (minimum GPA 3.0/4.0), letters of recommendation, and a statement of research interests. Graduate assistantships provide tuition remission and living stipends.
