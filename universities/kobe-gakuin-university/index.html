Institution Overview & Profile

Kobe Gakuin University, established in 1966 and one of the largest comprehensive private universities in the Kansai region with over 11,000 students, operates two flagship campuses: the Port Island Campus overlooking Kobe Harbor and the verdant Arise Campus in Nishi-ku. Renowned for its Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, pioneering Faculty of Contemporary Social Studies (specializing in disaster mitigation and crisis management), and Faculty of Law.

Key Academic Strengths

  • Top-tier Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences with integrated 6-year clinical pharmacist degree
  • Pioneering Disaster Mitigation and Crisis Management programs born from the 1995 Great Hanshin Earthquake
  • Ultra-modern Port Island waterfront campus adjacent to Kobe Biomedical Innovation Cluster (KBIC)
  • Vibrant cooperative internships with the City of Kobe, Hyogo Prefectural Government, and multinational ports

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Professional Doctoral Degree

Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D. - 6-Year Clinical Program)

Six-year clinical pharmacy curriculum preparing licensed pharmacists for hospital intensive care units, pharmaceutical R&D, and clinical pharmacology.

Undergraduate Degree

Bachelor of Contemporary Social Studies (Disaster Management)

First-of-its-kind program in Japan exploring community disaster resilience, crisis communication, evacuation logistics, and humanitarian aid.

Graduate Degree

Graduate School of Law (Master's / Ph.D.)

Advanced legal research focusing on maritime trade law, international commercial arbitration, intellectual property, and environmental compliance.
