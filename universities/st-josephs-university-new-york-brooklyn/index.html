St. Joseph's University, New York - Brooklyn

St. Joseph's NY Brooklyn · Brooklyn, New York, United States

St. Joseph's University, New York is an independent university located in the historic Clinton Hill neighborhood of Brooklyn, with a sister campus in Long Island. Founded by the Sisters of St. Joseph, the university is acclaimed for its leading nursing school, the renowned Dillon Child Study Center (one of the first laboratory preschools in NYC), cutting-edge forensic psychology, and accessible liberal arts education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

St. Joseph's comprises the School of Arts and Sciences, the School of Professional and Graduate Studies, and the Department of Nursing. Its Brooklyn campus features the historic Tuohy Hall, state-of-the-art computer science and digital media laboratories, and clinical healthcare simulation suites.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic records (average 3.3 GPA), personal essays, and extracurricular involvement on a test-optional basis. Graduate programs require an accredited baccalaureate degree with a 3.0+ minimum GPA, official transcripts, letters of recommendation, and program-specific personal statements.
