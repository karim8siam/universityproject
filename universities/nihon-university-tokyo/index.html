Nihon University

Nihon University (日本大学 - Nichidai), headquartered in Tokyo, is Japan's largest private university, established in 1889 as Nihon Law School. Spanning 16 colleges and 87 departments, it possesses one of the largest alumni networks in Japanese corporate and political leadership.

Academic overview & excellence

Nihon University boasts renowned faculties in Science and Technology, Medicine, Dentistry, Law, and Art. Its College of Science and Technology in Surugadai operates world-class aerospace wind tunnels, quantum science labs, and earthquake engineering shake tables.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires graduation from an accredited high school (or equivalent 12 years of education). International applicants submit EJU (Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students) or JLPT scores (typically N1/N2), coupled with university entrance examinations and interviews.
