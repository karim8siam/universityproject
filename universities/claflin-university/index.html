Claflin University

Claflin University is a prestigious, nationally ranked private historically Black university (HBCU) affiliated with the United Methodist Church, celebrated for excellence in STEM research, molecular biology, cancer research, and business entrepreneurship.

Academic overview & excellence

Consistently ranked among the top ten HBCUs nationally by U.S. News & World Report, Claflin features state-of-the-art research laboratories funded by NIH, NSF, and NASA in nanobiotechnology, bioinformatics, environmental chemistry, and digital forensics.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate applicants submit high school credentials with an average entering GPA of 3.3. Graduate applicants for master's programs in biotechnology, business, or education require an accredited bachelor's degree and undergraduate GPA of 3.0+. International students submit TOEFL (79+) or IELTS (6.0+).

Accelerated 30-credit ACBSP-accredited MBA program offered online and hybrid, focusing on strategic management, entrepreneurship, and global finance.
