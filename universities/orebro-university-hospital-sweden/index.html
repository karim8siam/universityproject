USÖ Örebro University Hospital: Clinical Medicine, Oncology & Surgery Guide

Universitetssjukhuset Örebro (USÖ) is one of Sweden's top seven university hospitals, integrated with Örebro University's Faculty of Medicine and Health, acclaimed for world-class clinical research in colorectal surgery, inflammatory bowel disease, clinical oncology, robotic surgery, and cardiovascular medicine.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Medical degree (MD), Bachelor/Master of Science in Biomedicine, Nursing, or Pharmacy from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Clinical PhD candidates require a Medical Degree (läkarexamen) or relevant Master of Science, clinical residency, and project approval by the regional ethics board.
Language proficiency
Swedish (C1 level / TISUS for patient-contact clinical roles); fluent English for biomedical laboratory research.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed clinical research protocol (Forskningsplan) of 5 to 8 pages defining patient cohorts, biostatistical power, and methodology.
Application fee
Free application for Swedish university medical positions.
Required documentation
Medical license (läkarlegitimation), degree certificates, official academic transcripts, research proposal, and supervisor letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Clinical doctoral positions are fully funded salaried appointments by Region Örebro Län and Örebro University (approx. SEK 34,000 to SEK 42,000 per month). Medical education is tuition-free for Swedish/EU citizens. ALF government medical research grants and Swedish Cancer Society (Cancerfonden) fellowships are widely awarded.
