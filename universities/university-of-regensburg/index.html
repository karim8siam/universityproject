University of Regensburg: Spintronics, Nanoscience, Biochemistry & Oncology Guide

The University of Regensburg (Universität Regensburg) is a leading Bavarian research institution renowned for its Collaborative Research Centers (SFB) in quantum spintronics, nanoscale physics, organic synthesis, and molecular medicine.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree in physics, chemistry, biology, or closely related field with minimum German grade 2.5.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science degree with excellent standing (grade 2.0 or better) and formal written supervisory consent from a Regensburg professor.
Language proficiency
English B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 88+) for English-taught graduate degrees; German DSH-2 / TestDaF 4 for German-taught subjects.
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research proposal detailing planned experimental or theoretical investigations.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the university admissions portal.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificate, official transcript of records, curriculum vitae, letter of intent, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free (€0) for all students regardless of nationality. A mandatory semester administration and regional transport fee (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €170 per semester is required. Excellent funding is provided via DFG Collaborative Research Centers and salaried TV-L positions.
