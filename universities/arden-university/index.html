Arden University: Cloud Computing, Data Analytics & Digital Healthcare Guide

Established in 1990 in Coventry and awarded university title in 2015, Arden University is an innovative UK degree-awarding public university with study centres across London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, and Berlin. Celebrated for high-impact blended and digital higher education, Arden specializes in data analytics, cloud computing, healthcare management, cyber security, and enterprise leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized UK honours degree (minimum 2:2 or international equivalent with GPA 2.7+/4.0); relevant professional work experience considered for mature applicants.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Professional Doctorate in Business Administration (DBA); entry requires accredited Master's degree and minimum three years of managerial or technical leadership.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (with no sub-test below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 88 (minimum 20 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for DBA and research candidates: 2,000-word applied research proposal detailing organizational problem statement, methodology, and theoretical framework.
Application fee
GBP 0 for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Full academic transcripts, degree award certificates, two professional or academic reference letters, statement of purpose, and curriculum vitae.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate master's degrees at Arden University is approximately GBP 9,500 to GBP 11,500 for UK home students and GBP 14,000 to GBP 15,500 for international students. Multiple international regional scholarships providing up to GBP 2,500 in fee discounts, flexible monthly installment plans, and corporate partner bursaries make study highly accessible.
