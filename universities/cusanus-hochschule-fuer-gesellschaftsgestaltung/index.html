Institution Overview & Profile

Cusanus Hochschule für Gesellschaftsgestaltung (formerly in Bernkastel-Kues, now based in Koblenz) is a recognized private German university celebrated across Europe for its pioneering work in pluralist economics, philosophy of social transformation, ecological transition, and democratic institutional design.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Specialized University (Hochschule für Gesellschaftsgestaltung)
Main CampusFlorinsmarkt Campus, Koblenz, Rhineland-Palatinate
AccreditationGerman Science Council (Wissenschaftsrat) / Ministry of Science Rhineland-Palatinate
Admissions RouteDirect institutional dossier submission & exploratory interview
Language of InstructionGerman (TestDaF 4 / Goethe C1) with bilingual English seminar components
Financial Aid & GrantsDeutschlandstipendium, BAföG eligibility & Cusanus Solidarity Fund (Umgekehrter Generationenvertrag)

Key Academic Strengths

  • Pioneering European centre for pluralist, heterodox, and ecological economics
  • Interdisciplinary philosophy integrating social responsibility, degrowth, and democracy
  • Low student-to-faculty ratios fostering intensive participatory seminar dialogues
  • Income-share agreement model (Generationenvertrag) enabling debt-free studies

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Graduate Degree

Master of Arts in Ökonomie und Gesellschaftsgestaltung (Economics & Social Transformation)

Graduate Degree

Master of Arts in Philosophie und Gesellschaftsgestaltung

Verified Academic Opportunities (7)

Scholarship

Cusanus Solidarity Generation Contract (Umgekehrter Generationenvertrag)

Ethical income-share financing agreement enabling students to study tuition-free and repay a percentage of income only after reaching a stable salary threshold.

Funding: 100% tuition coverage during the study period; income-dependent deferred repayment Deadline: July 15, 2027 (Winter semester)
Master's

Master of Arts Ökonomie und Gesellschaftsgestaltung Enrolment

Two-year postgraduate degree analyzing heterodox economic theory, ecological macroeconomics, money creation, and circular socio-ecological systems.

Funding: Eligible for federal BAföG assistance and Heinrich Böll / Rosa Luxemburg foundation grants Deadline: July 15, 2027
Fellowship

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Pluralist Economics and Social Ecology

Two-year salaried research fellowship investigating sustainable financial systems, commons governance, and regenerative regional economics.

Funding: Full TV-L E13 postdoctoral salary (approx. €52,000–€58,000 gross per annum) Deadline: March 31, 2027
Scholarship

Deutschlandstipendium at Cusanus Hochschule

Federal national scholarship matched by private donors rewarding academic excellence and proven engagement in ecological or civic causes.

Funding: €300 per month (€3,600 per year) paid directly to the student for one year (renewable) Deadline: August 15, 2027
Master's

Master of Arts in Philosophie und Gesellschaftsgestaltung Admission

Four-semester advanced program examining critical theory, democratic theory, ethics of artificial intelligence, and transformative epistemologies.

Funding: Eligible for internal solidarity fee reduction Deadline: July 15, 2027
Internship

Transformative Field Practicum in Regional Community Innovation

Mandatory four-month practical semester embedded within ecological farming cooperatives, regional transition towns, or public energy utilities.

Funding: Field grant subsidy of €600/month provided through university partner consortium Deadline: February 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Arts in Philosophie, Politik und Ökonomik Admission

Undergraduate degree providing deep cross-disciplinary foundations in political theory, macroeconomic structures, and systemic sustainability.

Funding: Covered by solidarity financing agreements Deadline: August 31, 2027

Notable Faculty & Researchers (3)

Prof. Dr. Silja Graupe

Vice President & Professor of Economics and Philosophy · Institute for Economy and Social Design

Prof. Dr. Silja Graupe is co-founder of Cusanus Hochschule and an international authority on pluralist economics, critiquing orthodox financial models and championing cognitive freedom in economic pedagogy.

Pluralist Economics Epistemology of Economics Imagination and Transformation

Prof. Dr. Reinhard Loske

Professor of Sustainability and Societal Transformation · Institute for Ecological Transformation

Prof. Dr. Reinhard Loske is a former senator and lead author of major climate reports, researching planetary boundary governance, circular economies, and socio-ecological policy.

Ecological Macroeconomics Climate Policy Degrowth and Post-Growth

Dr. Stephan Panther

Professor of Institutional and International Economics · Department of Economics

Dr. Stephan Panther investigates the historical evolution of economic institutions, public trust, and socio-economic divergence within the European Union.

Institutional Economics Comparative Capitalism Political Economy of Europe
