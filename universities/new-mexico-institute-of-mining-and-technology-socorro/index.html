New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology

New Mexico Tech · Socorro, New Mexico, United States

New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology (New Mexico Tech) is a world-renowned public STEM research university in Socorro, New Mexico. Known internationally for energetic materials testing, hydrology, astrophysics at the VLA, and petroleum recovery.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

New Mexico Tech operates the Energetic Materials Research and Testing Center (EMRTC), Langmuir Laboratory for Atmospheric Research, and the Magdalena Ridge Observatory. It is an epicenter for explosive engineering and geophysical research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5; 3.0+ recommended for engineering) with strong precalculus and science scores. Graduate programs require an accredited STEM bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA and faculty sponsorship.
