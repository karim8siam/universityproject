Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1916 in Calgary, SAIT is one of Canada's leading polytechnic institutes, renowned for industry-driven applied research in green building technologies, automated manufacturing, energy systems, and digital transformations.

Institution type
Public Polytechnic & Applied Technology Institute
Campus location

Financial aid & support
Over CAD $5 million in annual scholarships, bursaries, and applied research grants

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Applied Research and Innovation Services (ARIS) green building technologies
  • Petroleum, renewable energy transitions, and hydrogen infrastructure
  • Automated manufacturing, robotics, and industrial IoT
  • Cybersecurity, software development, and digital transformation

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Construction Project Management (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year degree integrating building information modeling (BIM), sustainability metrics, and major infrastructure management.
  • Bachelor of Applied Technology in Petroleum Engineering (Applied Degree) — Specialized post-diploma program focusing on carbon capture, reservoir characterization, and geothermal energy development.
  • Postgraduate Certificate in Cyber Security for Industrial Control Systems (Graduate Certificate) — Advanced training in critical infrastructure defense, SCADA security, and operational technology risk management.
