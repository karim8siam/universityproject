Institutional Overview

Miyagi University of Education (Miyagi Kyōiku Daigaku / Miyakyo) is a prestigious national university founded in 1965 on Mount Aoba in Sendai, Japan. Serving as the primary center for pedagogical research and teacher training in the Tohoku region, Miyakyo is celebrated for its comprehensive faculties in school education, special needs education, science pedagogy, disaster resilience education, and regional community revitalization.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Tohoku region's flagship national university dedicated to teacher education and pedagogical innovation
  • Pioneering Disaster Prevention and Safety Education Center established following the 2011 Great East Japan Earthquake
  • Comprehensive network of affiliated laboratory elementary, junior high, and special needs schools on campus
  • Over 85% success rate on prefectural public school teacher employment examinations (Kyōin Saiyō Shiken)
