Seton Hall University

Seton Hall · South Orange, New Jersey, United States

Seton Hall University is a prominent private Catholic research university chartered in 1856, located on a picturesque 58-acre campus in South Orange, New Jersey, with its health sciences campus in Nutley and law school in downtown Newark. Globally recognized for Seton Hall University School of Law, the School of Diplomacy and International Relations, Stillman School of Business, and health sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Seton Hall operates the School of Diplomacy and International Relations (affiliated with the United Nations Association of the USA), the Interprofessional Health Sciences (IHS) Campus in Nutley/Clifton (shared with Hackensack Meridian School of Medicine), and Seton Hall Law in Newark. Its alumni hold leadership positions across the UN, federal judiciary, and Fortune 500 corporations.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.65), coursework profile, and optional test scores (test-optional). School of Law requires competitive LSAT scores and CAS subscription. Graduate diplomacy, business, and health sciences programs require accredited bachelor's degrees with minimum 3.0 GPAs.
