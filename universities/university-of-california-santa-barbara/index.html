University of California, Santa Barbara

UC Santa Barbara · Santa Barbara, California, United States

The University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB), situated on a stunning Pacific coastal promontory in Santa Barbara, California, is a premier Carnegie R1 research university celebrated for producing six Nobel Laureates among its active faculty. Internationally renowned for its world-leading Materials department, the Kavli Institute for Theoretical Physics (KITP), marine sciences, and quantum computing, UCSB is a powerhouse of scientific discovery.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UCSB comprises the College of Letters and Science, the College of Engineering, and the unique College of Creative Studies (CCS). With over $250 million in annual sponsored research, it houses the Materials Research Laboratory (an NSF MRSEC), the Marine Science Institute, and the California Nanosystems Institute (CNSI).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective via the University of California application (test-free; average GPA 4.15–4.3 weighted). Graduate and doctoral applicants require a completed bachelor's degree with strong academic preparation (minimum 3.3+ GPA), letters of recommendation, and clear research interests.
