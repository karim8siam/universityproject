Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Indianapolis · NLN Center of Excellence in Nursing Education & Clinical Science. Indiana University School of Nursing is one of the premier nursing institutions in the nation, designated an NLN Center of Excellence across multiple dimensions. Anchored at the academic health center in Indianapolis, IU School of Nursing offers top-ranked BSN, MSN, DNP, and PhD programs, driving breakthrough clinical research in cancer survivorship, chronic illness self-management, and health equity informatics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma with competitive GPA and prerequisite sciences for undergraduate BSN; accredited BSN with 3.0+ GPA and active RN license for MSN and DNP programs; accredited master's in nursing for PhD programs. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international nursing applicants. BSN Fall admission priority January 15, 2027; MSN/DNP priority February 1, 2027; PhD in Nursing priority December 1, 2026.
