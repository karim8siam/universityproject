Liverpool Institute for Performing Arts (LIPA): Sound Tech, Theatre & Music Guide

Co-founded by Sir Paul McCartney in Liverpool, the Liverpool Institute for Performing Arts (LIPA) is an internationally celebrated specialist higher education conservatory for music, sound technology, acting, theatre design, and creative management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 2:1) in music, sound engineering, performing arts, or substantial professional portfolio equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Specialist conservatory focusing on bachelor's and master's professional conservatory training; doctoral students affiliate with Liverpool partner universities.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score).
Research statement / Proposal
Audition performance video or technical production portfolio (sound mixing, design drawings, or production management documentation).
Application fee
Audition fee typically £30-£45 (waived for low-income candidates); UCAS application fee applies for undergraduate degree entry.
Required documentation
Audition recordings or digital portfolio, academic certificates, personal statement of artistic intent, and two references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

UK undergraduate tuition is £9,250 per year. International undergraduate tuition is approximately £19,800 to £21,500 annually. Paul McCartney scholarships, LIPA International bursaries, and creative industry placement stipends are awarded annually.
