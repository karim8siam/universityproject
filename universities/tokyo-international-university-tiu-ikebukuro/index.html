Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1965, Tokyo International University opened its landmark 22-story urban campus in Ikebukuro, Toshima Ward, in 2023, expanding alongside its traditional research campus in Kawagoe. TIU is renowned for its English Track (E-Track) Program, educating students from over 75 nations with fully accredited degree tracks in English, while offering joint academic pathways with Willamette University in Oregon.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to the English Track (E-Track) programs is conducted entirely in English without Japanese language prerequisite. Applicants require secondary school graduation credentials, English language test scores (TOEFL iBT 79+, IELTS 6.0+, or Duolingo 105+), transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a statement of purpose. TIU Tuition Reduction Scholarships provide 30%, 50%, 80%, or 100% tuition exemptions.
