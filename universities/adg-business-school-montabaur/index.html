Steinbeis University Business School · Schloss Montabaur Campus

ADG Business School: Cooperative Banking, Digital Finance & Executive Management Guide

ADG Business School an der Steinbeis-Hochschule, situated at historic Schloss Montabaur, is Germany's premier academic business school for cooperative banking, wealth management, sustainable finance, and executive leadership, partnering directly with the German Cooperative Financial Network (Genossenschaftliche FinanzGruppe Volksbanken Raiffeisenbanken).

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 or 210 ECTS) in economics, business administration, finance, or congruent field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on accredited state-recognized Bachelor of Arts and Master of Science degrees.
Language proficiency
German B2/C1 for corporate banking seminars, with good working knowledge of professional English.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae detailing banking or corporate finance experience, accompanied by a letter of motivation.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the online ADG admissions portal.
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree certificate, academic transcript of records, employment verification letter, and personal identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition averages €14,000 to €19,500 for full degree programs. Cooperative banking employers typically sponsor 50% to 100% of employee tuition fees. Early-bird merit reductions and Steinbeis education loan funds are available.
