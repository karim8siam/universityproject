Northern Illinois University

Northern Illinois · DeKalb, Illinois, United States

Northern Illinois University is a public Carnegie R2 doctoral research university located in DeKalb, Illinois, 65 miles west of Chicago. World-renowned for particle accelerator physics at Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory and Argonne National Laboratory.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NIU comprises the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, College of Engineering and Engineering Technology (CEET), and the College of Business. Key centers include the Center for Secondary Analysis of Atomic and Nuclear Data and the Northern Illinois Center for Accelerator and Detector Development (NICADD).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 3.0 for guaranteed admission; test-free holistic review). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, personal goals statement, and letters of recommendation.
