European School of Physiotherapy: Fast-Track BSc, Clinical Internships & Faculty Guide

Operating within the Faculty of Health at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (Hogeschool van Amsterdam - HvA), the European School of Physiotherapy (ESP) is an internationally celebrated higher education institution established in 1990. As the premier English-taught accelerated physiotherapy program in Europe, ESP condenses a standard four-year Dutch Bachelor of Science into three intensive academic years (240 ECTS credits), welcoming students from over 40 countries and featuring 40 weeks of global clinical internships.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) for International Health Scholars

Prestigious national scholarship financed by the Dutch Ministry of Education supporting exceptional non-EEA students entering the European School of Physiotherapy.

Accelerated Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Physiotherapy (3-Year Track)

Intensive, three-year full-time English-taught Bachelor of Science program delivering 240 ECTS credits, manual therapy masterclasses, and 40 weeks of clinical hospital internships worldwide.

Master of Science in Clinical Biomechanics and Physical Rehabilitation (Feeder Track)

Research-oriented clinical master's track training clinical specialists to evaluate neuromuscular disorders, sports injury recovery kinetics, and post-surgical rehabilitation.

Center for Healthcare and Movement Applied Biomechanics Investigation

Applied biomechanics research project utilizing 3D motion analysis, surface electromyography, and wearable inertial sensors to assess knee ligament reconstruction rehabilitation.

International Clinical Physiotherapy Internship Rotations (40 Weeks Worldwide)

Extensive 40-week clinical internship curriculum dividing students into two 20-week rotations across accredited sports clinics, neurological rehabilitation centers, and teaching hospitals globally.

ESP Sports Physiotherapy and Orthopedic Manual Therapy Fellowship

Postgraduate fellowship training physiotherapists in advanced manual mobilization, exercise physiology, and neuromuscular re-education for high-performance athletes.

Senior Lecturer in Clinical Biomechanics and Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation

Permanent faculty position lecturing musculoskeletal pathology, supervising clinical simulation labs, and assessing student clinical internships at the Nicolaes Tulphuis campus in Amsterdam.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Secondary school diploma equivalent to Dutch VWO or high-standard HAVO (e.g., International Baccalaureate, German Abitur, UK A-Levels) with prerequisite coursework in Biology and Mathematics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Accelerated BSc graduates articulate directly into European Master of Science in Physiotherapy and doctoral clinical biomechanics research programs.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 80 (computerized entrance interview also required).
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via Studielink.nl followed by the ESP selection procedure, including an online cognitive intake test, human anatomy self-study assignment, and video motivation interview.
Application fee
Zero application fee through Studielink for Dutch higher education.
Required documentation
Secondary school diploma transcripts, certified English translations, copy of passport, curriculum vitae, and completed ESP intake questionnaire.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA, Swiss, and Surinamese citizens is the statutory Dutch tuition rate of approximately €2,530 per academic year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is approximately €9,600 per academic year across the intensive three-year cycle. Admitted students are eligible for the Holland Scholarship and HvA talent grants.
