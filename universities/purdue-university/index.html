Purdue University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Purdue University is a premier American public land-grant research university in West Lafayette, Indiana, known worldwide as the 'Cradle of Astronauts' (alma mater of Neil Armstrong and Gene Cernan). Consistently ranked among the world's top engineering and computing institutions, Purdue excels in hypersonics, microelectronics fabrication, cybersecurity, autonomous aviation, and digital agriculture.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Aeronautics and Astronautics (Hypersonics, Propulsion & Space Systems)

Premier doctoral program conducting Mach 6+ quiet wind tunnel testing, scramjet combustion diagnostics, satellite astrodynamics, and autonomous spacecraft navigation.

Master of Science (M.S.) in Computer Science (Systems, AI & Distributed Computing)

Two-year graduate degree delivering rigorous training in compiler optimization, distributed cloud systems, generative artificial intelligence, and operating systems kernel development.

Ross and Andrews Doctoral Fellowships for Engineering & Physical Sciences

Purdue's highest presidential doctoral awards granted to incoming PhD students with outstanding academic records and exceptional potential for transformative engineering research.

Birck Nanotechnology Center Graduate Microelectronics and Quantum Hardware Research Fellowship

Research position in the 25,000-square-foot Scifres Nanofabrication Cleanroom, fabricating 2D semiconductor transistors, topological quantum bits, and heterogeneous packaging architectures.

Purdue Applied Digital Agriculture & Drone Sensor Analytics Summer Research Internship

Ten-week hands-on summer research program analyzing hyperspectral satellite imagery, UAV multispectral plant sensors, and edge AI algorithms deployed at the Purdue Agronomy Center for Research and Education.

Purdue Quantum Science and Engineering Institute (PQSEI) Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral appointment synthesizing defect-engineered diamond nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers, chiral molecular semiconductors, and optical quantum sensing instruments.

Master of Science in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (Supply Chain Optimization)

Top-ranked graduate curriculum in linear and stochastic integer programming, resilient supply chain optimization, healthcare logistics simulations, and queuing network models.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.2 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent) in a relevant STEM discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate or master's degree with a minimum 3.5/4.0 GPA; established research experience, competitive GRE scores (where required), and alignment with graduate faculty.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT minimum 80 (writing 22, speaking 18, reading 19, listening 14) or IELTS Academic minimum 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-page Statement of Purpose detailing technical background, career goals, specific research questions, and named Purdue faculty mentors.
Application fee
USD $75 non-refundable application fee for international applicants.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, 3 letters of recommendation, CV/resume, personal history statement, and official English test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for out-of-state and international graduate students at Purdue is approximately USD $29,100 per year (held flat under Purdue's historic decade-plus tuition freeze). However, over 95% of admitted doctoral engineering and science students receive full graduate assistantships (RA/TA) providing guaranteed annual stipends of USD $32,000 to $38,000, 100% tuition remission, and comprehensive medical health insurance coverage.
