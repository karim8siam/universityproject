University of Missouri-Kansas City

UMKC · Kansas City, Missouri, United States

The University of Missouri-Kansas City is a public research university in Kansas City, Missouri, and part of the University of Missouri System. A powerhouse for health sciences, engineering, law, computing, and performing arts.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMKC operates the Health Sciences District (Schools of Medicine, Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Nursing), the School of Science and Engineering, and the Henry W. Bloch School of Management, producing high-impact urban research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, class rank, and standardized test scores (test-flexible). Graduate programs require a bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of goals, and recommendations.
