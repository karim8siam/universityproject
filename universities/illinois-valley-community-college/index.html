Illinois Valley Community College

IVCC · Oglesby, Illinois, United States

Illinois Valley Community College (IVCC) is a public comprehensive community college serving the Illinois River Valley, renowned for agricultural technology, manufacturing automation, nursing, and seamless university transfer pathways.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Operating on a scenic 425-acre campus in Oglesby, IVCC delivers affordable university parallel degrees (Associate in Arts and Associate in Science) alongside career-focused AAS degrees in advanced manufacturing, cyber systems, and certified nursing.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Operates under open-door admissions for all high school graduates and adult learners. Competitive application processes apply to selective healthcare programs including the Registered Nursing AAS.
