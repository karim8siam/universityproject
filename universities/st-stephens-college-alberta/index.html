St. Stephen's College at the University of Alberta

St. Stephen's College is an historic graduate theological and clinical therapy college situated directly on the University of Alberta campus in Edmonton. Established in 1908, the college is globally renowned for pioneer graduate programs in art therapy, psychospiritual counselling, and inclusive multifaith theology.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Edmonton, Alberta, Canada

Institutional Overview

Affiliated with the University of Alberta and accredited by the Association of Theological Schools (ATS), St. Stephen's offers Master of Psychotherapy and Spirituality (MPS), Master of Theological Studies (MTS), Doctor of Ministry (DMin), and Graduate Certificates in Spiritually-Informed Creative Arts. Courses combine deep psychological theory, clinical supervision, and social advocacy.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions to St. Stephen's College welcome scholars and clinicians seeking holistic, multifaith, and clinical counselling certifications. Requirements include a recognized four-year bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 GPA, background coursework in psychology or religious studies, academic writing samples, and reflective personal statements. Bursary awards and research travel grants are awarded annually.
