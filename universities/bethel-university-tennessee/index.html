Institution Overview & Profile

Bethel University, chartered in 1842 in McKenzie, Tennessee, is a private comprehensive university affiliated with the Cumberland Presbyterian Church. Renowned across the Southeast for its Physician Assistant (PA) master's program, online criminal justice and business degrees, and national-championship collegiate bass fishing team, Bethel combines professional preparation with Christian service.

Institution TypePrivate Comprehensive University (Cumberland Presbyterian Affiliated)
Main CampusMcKenzie Campus (100 acres in West Tennessee) & Online Division
