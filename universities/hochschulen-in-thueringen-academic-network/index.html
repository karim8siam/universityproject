Hochschulen in Thüringen - Academic Network

Campus Thüringen is the state-wide higher education alliance uniting all ten public universities and universities of applied sciences in the Free State of Thuringia, coordinating joint degree mobilities, digital learning platforms, and STEM research clusters.

Academic overview & research priorities

Member institutions include Friedrich Schiller University Jena, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, TU Ilmenau, University of Erfurt, and Nordhausen/Jena/Erfurt/Schmalkalden universities of applied sciences. Campus Thüringen spearheads joint research in photonics, optics, material engineering, humanities, and AI.

Research admissions & international criteria

Prospective students apply through individual member university admissions portals or centralized uni-assist services. Cross-institutional credit transfer and shared doctoral colloquia require enrollment at an accredited Thuringian public university.
