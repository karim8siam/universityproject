The College of Idaho

The College of Idaho is the state's oldest private liberal arts college, renowned for its distinctive PEAK Curriculum (Professional, Ethical, Art, Knowledge), producing seven Rhodes Scholars and preparing top graduates for medical, law, and doctoral admissions.

Academic overview & excellence

Through the innovative PEAK Curriculum, every College of Idaho student graduates with a major and three minors spanning the humanities, natural sciences, social sciences, and professional fields. The campus features the Orma J. Smith Museum of Natural History and the Whittenberger Planetarium.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluates high school transcripts, academic rigor, and personal character. Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT) are optional. International students submit proof of English proficiency (TOEFL 79, IELTS 6.5, or Duolingo 105). Over 98% of students receive generous institutional merit scholarships.
