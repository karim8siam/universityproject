Robertson College (Winnipeg Campus)

Robertson College Winnipeg is a landmark Canadian career training institution founded in 1911. Located on Main Street in downtown Winnipeg, Manitoba, Robertson provides accelerated diploma credentials across health care, technology, business administration, and community services, partnering with prominent provincial employers for direct workforce placement.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada

Institutional Overview

Programs at Robertson College emphasize modular career curricula, interactive hybrid instruction, and integrated mandatory practicums. Accredited by Manitoba Advanced Education and Training, Robertson prepares students for high-demand certifications including Pharmacy Technician, Cyber Security Analyst, Accounting Specialist, and Dental Assistant.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Robertson College offers flexible continuous monthly start dates throughout the calendar year. Admissions criteria include Manitoba Grade 12 completion or mature student assessment, criminal record checks for healthcare tracks, and English language proficiency (IELTS 6.0). Financial options include Manitoba Student Aid, Canada-Manitoba Job Grants, and institutional entrance scholarships.
