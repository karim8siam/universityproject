Prefectural University of Hiroshima

Prefectural University of Hiroshima (PUH) is a renowned public university in western Japan, acclaimed for bioresource and marine sciences on the Seto Inland Sea, regional business leadership, and advanced health sciences.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating three specialized campuses across Hiroshima Prefecture—Hiroshima Campus (Comprehensive Humanities & Business), Shobara Campus (Biological and Environmental Sciences), and Mihara Campus (Health and Welfare)—PUH is an engine of regional development. The university houses research institutes for Seto Inland Sea estuarine biosciences, peace studies, and gerontological healthcare technology.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate international admission requires the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students (EJU) in Japanese and designated subject tests. Graduate school admissions require a recognized bachelor's degree (minimum 16 years of education), research proposal in Japanese or English, official transcripts, and faculty interview. International applicants must show Japanese JLPT N2/N1 or English TOEFL iBT 75+.
