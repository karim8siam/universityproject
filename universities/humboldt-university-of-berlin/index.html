Humboldt University of Berlin (Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin)

Founded in 1810 by Wilhelm von Humboldt, Humboldt University of Berlin is the mother of modern universities, originating the Humboldtian ideal of integrating teaching and research. Alma mater of 57 Nobel laureates including Albert Einstein, Max Planck, and Erwin Schrödinger, Humboldt is a certified University of Excellence within the Berlin University Alliance. Located across Mitte and the Adlershof science campus, Humboldt excels in Computer Science, Quantum Optics, Complex Systems, and Materials Science. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Humboldt Doctoral Fellowships (Humboldt-Promotionsstipendien)

Merit-based doctoral fellowships funded by Humboldt University and foundation endowments, supporting outstanding doctoral researchers conducting innovative scientific investigations across Berlin.

Humboldt University Salaried Doctoral Research (Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in - TV-L HU)

Three to four-year salaried university employment contracts at Humboldt University research chairs, combining cutting-edge scientific investigation with teaching assistance and structured doctoral training.

Humboldt Master of Science in Computer Science (Informatik)

Two-year research-focused Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) based at the Science Campus Adlershof, offering advanced specializations in Algorithms & Complexity, Data Science, Adaptive Systems, and Distributed Computing.

Humboldt Master of Science in Optical Sciences / Physics

English-taught two-year interdisciplinary Master's program (120 ECTS) in Adlershof, partnering with the Max Born Institute and Helmholtz-Zentrum Berlin to train experts in Quantum Optics, Ultrafast Lasers, and Nanophotonics.

BIFOLD (Berlin Institute for the Foundations of Learning and Data) Placements

Frontier research positions inside BIFOLD, Germany's national AI competence center co-hosted at Humboldt University, developing scalable deep learning systems, data management architectures, and explainable AI.

Humboldt Adlershof Technology Park Student Research Fellowships

Structured laboratory research internships inside Germany's premier science and technology park in Berlin-Adlershof, embedding students in cutting-edge photonics, renewable materials, and analytics enterprises.

Humboldt Student Assistantships (Studentische Hilfskräfte - SHK)

Campus employment positions (up to 80 hours per month) assisting Humboldt professors in running exercise groups, tutoring undergraduate students, managing library collections, and maintaining computer clusters.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science, Physics, Mathematics, or closely related discipline (minimum 180 ECTS credits) satisfying subject-specific curriculum prerequisite tables.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) with high academic distinction, written supervisory commitment (Betreuungszusage) from a Humboldt full professor, and admission to the doctoral committee.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught graduate curricula; German proficiency (CEFR C1) required for German-taught programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research statement (Exposé) of 3 to 5 pages outlining the planned doctoral research problem, methodology, and work plan.
Application fee
Zero application fee via university portal; €75 uni-assist preliminary review documentation (VPD) fee for international degree holders.
Required documentation
University diploma, complete official transcripts with grading scale, CV in tabular format, motivation letter, and uni-assist VPD certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under German public higher education regulations, tuition at Humboldt University of Berlin is zero for both domestic and international students. All students pay only a mandatory semester fee of approximately €315, which includes the Berlin public transportation semester ticket. Salaried doctoral researchers (Wissenschaftliche Mitarbeiter) are paid university employees under the TV-L HU collective agreement.
