Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of Australian Year 12 or recognized international high school diploma / secondary school qualification.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on nationally recognized vocational qualifications.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 5.5 to 6.0 overall (minimum 5.0 in each band) or equivalent PTE score.
Research statement / Proposal
Genuine Student (GS) statement identifying interest in regional Tasmanian community services or IT careers.
Application fee
AUD $250 non-refundable enrollment registration fee.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, passport bio-page, English language evidence, and national police check for community care placements.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition averages AUD $8,000 to $12,500 annually. Flexible quarterly payment plans and regional Tasmanian study incentive fee discounts are offered to international students.
