Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The University of California Washington Center (UCDC) is the residential instructional and research hub of the UC System in the heart of the national capital. Bringing together top undergraduate scholars, graduate researchers, and distinguished faculty from all ten UC campuses, UCDC conducts elite seminars in federal policy analysis, constitutional law, international diplomacy, and science governance.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Enrollment open to matriculated University of California undergraduate juniors/seniors and graduate researchers through campus-specific UCDC competitive selection processes. Satisfied via UC campus home matriculation (TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international UC students). Fall term deadline April 1, 2027; Winter/Spring term deadline October 15, 2026.
