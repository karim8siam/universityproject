University of Padua (Università degli Studi di Padova)

Founded in 1222, the University of Padua (Università degli Studi di Padova - UniPD) is the world's fifth-oldest surviving university and second-oldest in Italy. Renowned historically as the academic home where Galileo Galilei taught physics and astronomy for 18 years, and where Elena Cornaro Piscopia became the first woman in history to receive a PhD in 1678, Padua is today one of Italy's premier research giants, excelling in quantum communication, robotics, astrophysics, human rights, and biomedical sciences. Six-week research placement at the world's oldest university botanical garden (UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1545), investigating rare plant seed banking and Alpine flora responses to climate change.

Two-year postdoctoral position at the Venetian Institute of Molecular Medicine (VIMM) investigating mitochondrial calcium uniporter structure and function in cardiovascular pathology.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (Laurea or foreign equivalent, minimum 180 ECTS) in a relevant subject area with an above-average academic transcript (typically minimum 70%+ or 3.0/4.0 GPA).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or equivalent 120 ECTS post-bachelor degree) with excellent academic marks, strong thesis research background, and detailed research proposal.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 85+ for English-taught graduate programs; CEFR B2 certification.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 4-page research proposal detailing state of the art, research objectives, methodology, and prospective UniPadova department alignment.
Application fee
€30 application fee for Master's and PhD programs via the UniPadova online portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts with certified translations, curriculum vitae, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and research proposal.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral students receiving University of Padua PhD scholarships pay ZERO tuition fees (€0 tuition) and receive a guaranteed tax-free living stipend of €16,290 per year (increased by 50% during research visits abroad) plus an annual research budget of €1,629 to €2,443. For Master's students, tuition is income-based (up to €2,700/year for non-EU students), and Padua International Excellence Scholarships provide full tuition waivers plus a €8,000/year living allowance.
