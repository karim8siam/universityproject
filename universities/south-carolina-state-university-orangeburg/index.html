South Carolina State University

SC State · Orangeburg, South Carolina, United States

South Carolina State University (SC State) is a distinguished public historically Black 1890 land-grant university located in Orangeburg, South Carolina. Renowned for its excellence in STEM, SC State houses the only undergraduate Nuclear Engineering program in South Carolina and has produced generations of African American military generals, engineers, educators, and civic leaders.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SC State comprises the College of Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, and Transportation (STEM-T), the College of Education, Humanities, and Social Sciences, and the School of Business. It features the James E. Clyburn University Transportation Center, advanced radiochemistry laboratories, and an agricultural research extension station.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, completion of college preparatory curriculum units, and standardized test scores (SAT/ACT test-optional). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75 GPA, department-specific writing samples, and academic references.
