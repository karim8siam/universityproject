Eindhoven University of Technology (Technische Universiteit Eindhoven - TU/e)

Founded in 1956, Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e) is a top-tier European research university located in the heart of Brainport Eindhoven—Europe's foremost high-tech innovation ecosystem. TU/e collaborates intimately with industrial giants including ASML, Philips, and NXP. TU/e leads the world in Embedded Systems, Process Mining, Nanotechnology, and Precision Robotics. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

TU/e ALSP (Amandus H. Lundqvist Scholarship Program)

Prestigious university scholarship awarded to outstanding non-EU/EEA international students admitted to Master's degree programs at TU/e, providing full tuition waiver and annual living allowance grants.

TU/e Salaried PhD Positions (Promovendi under CAO-NU)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointments across TU/e departments, conducting frontier industrial and fundamental investigations under standard Dutch collective employment contracts.

TU/e Master of Science in Computer Science and Engineering

Two-year Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, offering specializations in Algorithms, Software Science, Big Data, and Information Security.

TU/e Master of Science in Embedded Systems

Specialized two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) partnering closely with ASML, Philips, and NXP to engineer real-time cyber-physical systems, FPGA hardware, and autonomous embedded controllers.

TU/e Eindhoven Artificial Intelligence Systems Institute (EAISI) Projects

Cutting-edge artificial intelligence institute connecting engineering and computer science, developing engineering AI, edge intelligence chips, autonomous driving software, and trustworthy healthcare algorithms.

TU/e Brainport High-Tech Academic & Industrial Internships

Industrial and academic graduation research internships embedding graduate engineers within the semiconductor and precision engineering facilities of ASML, Philips, NXP, and TNO.

TU/e Graduate Student Assistantships (Student-Assistenten)

Part-time campus employment for enrolled students coaching exercise sessions, running computer laboratory sessions, and assisting faculty in grading assignments.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Engineering, Computer Science or related quantitative field (minimum 180 ECTS credits) matching specific foundational course requirements.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) with excellent academic marks, strong thesis research evidence, and successful competitive interview for an advertised doctoral vacancy.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all subscores) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 21 in each component).
Research statement / Proposal
For Master's: statement of purpose and academic transcripts via Studielink. For PhD: comprehensive research statement aligned with advertised vacancy calls.
Application fee
€100 application fee for international applicants holding non-Dutch degrees (refunded upon successful matriculation).
Required documentation
Complete official transcripts, degree certificate, detailed descriptions of core undergraduate mathematics/programming modules, curriculum vitae, and passport scan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Dutch national higher education regulations, EU/EEA students pay the statutory tuition fee of €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is approximately €18,000 per year for Master's programs. Outstanding admitted international students can receive the ALSP (Amandus H. Lundqvist Scholarship Program), covering 100% of tuition fees plus a €5,000 living allowance grant. Doctoral candidates (Promovendi) are paid university employees under the Dutch CAO-NU collective agreement.
