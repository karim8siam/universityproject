International School of Stuttgart (Degerloch Campus)

The International School of Stuttgart (ISS Degerloch Campus) is an accredited non-profit IB World School delivering the International Baccalaureate Primary Years, Middle Years, and Diploma Programs (IBDP) in an inclusive English-language international learning community.

Academic overview & research priorities

Located in the high-tech economic hub of Baden-Württemberg, ISS provides rigorous preparation in experimental sciences, higher mathematics, visual arts, and international humanities. Graduates earn the International Baccalaureate Diploma (recognized by the Baden-Württemberg Kultusministerium as equivalent to the German Abitur) and matriculate to world-leading universities.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require previous school transcripts, teacher recommendations, student writing samples, and an English language diagnostic assessment. IBDP entrance requires completion of Grade 10 with strong marks in mathematics and English.
