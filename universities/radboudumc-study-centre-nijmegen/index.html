Studiecentrum Radboudumc

The Study Centre at Radboud University Medical Center (Studiecentrum Radboudumc) in Nijmegen is the educational and medical training hub of Radboud University, celebrated globally for clinical research in oncology, immunology, infectious diseases, and regenerative medicine.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating at the forefront of European medical education, the Radboudumc Study Centre features advanced surgical simulation suites, anatomical dissection theatres, and the Radboud Institute for Molecular Life Sciences (RIMLS) and Radboud Institute for Health Sciences (RIHS).

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to bachelor's medical programs follows the centralized Dutch numerus fixus selection. Master's (Biomedical Sciences / Molecular Mechanisms of Disease) and PhD programs require an accredited bachelor's in life sciences, exceptional laboratory records, and English proficiency (IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 100).
