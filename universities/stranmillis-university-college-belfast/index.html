Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1922 on a picturesque 46-acre woodland campus in south Belfast, Stranmillis University College is a distinguished university college of Queen's University Belfast. It is recognized as a leader in initial teacher education, early childhood studies, pastoral care, and educational research across the UK and Ireland.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Degrees awarded and validated by prestigious Russell Group member Queen's University Belfast
  • Over a century of excellence in primary and post-primary teacher education
  • Centre for Research in Educational Underachievement (CREU)
  • Exceptional student satisfaction and graduate employment rates across Northern Ireland

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Education (BEd Primary / Post-Primary) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year professional initial teacher education degree accredited by GTCNI qualifying teachers across the UK and internationally.
  • Master of Teaching (MTeach) (Postgraduate Degree) — Advanced professional master's for practicing teachers focusing on pedagogical inquiry, inclusive education, and curriculum leadership.
  • BA (Hons) Early Childhood Studies (Undergraduate Degree) — Comprehensive three-year degree exploring child development psychology, play-based learning, safeguarding, and family support.
