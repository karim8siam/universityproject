Academic overview & research priorities

Situated on a modern campus in the Metternich district of Koblenz near the Rhine and Moselle confluence, the University of Koblenz became an independent autonomous university in 2023. It operates four major faculties: Education, Humanities, Mathematics/Natural Sciences, and Computer Science. Koblenz is celebrated as the birthplace of Computervisualistik in Germany and is an international hub for web science and environmental ecotoxicology.

Research admissions & international criteria

International applicants to English-language master's programs (such as Web and Data Science or Applied Computer Science) require an accredited bachelor's degree in computer science or related mathematics field (minimum GPA 2.5 on German scale) and English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 88+ or IELTS 6.5+). Doctoral research applicants must hold a recognized master's degree and submit a verified dissertation plan endorsed by a university professor.
