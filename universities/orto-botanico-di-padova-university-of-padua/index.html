Orto Botanico di Padova (University of Padua Botanical Garden)

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1545 by the Venetian Republic for the University of Padua, the Orto Botanico di Padova is the world's oldest botanical garden still situated in its original location. Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site, it houses over 7,000 botanical species and the advanced Biodiversity Garden bioclimatic greenhouses.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Continuous botanical scientific operation since 1545 (Goethe's Palm, historic Hortus Simplicium)
  • High-tech Biodiversity Garden featuring 5 advanced bioclimatic pavilions simulating global biomes
  • World-class historical herbarium (Padova Herbarium) containing over 500,000 dried plant specimens
  • Direct integration with UniPD Department of Biology (Ecology, Evolution and Genetics)

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Dottorato di Ricerca in Bioscienze (Curriculum: Botanica ed Ecologia Vegetale) (Doctoral Degree) — Three-year research doctorate exploring plant phylogenomics, endangered Alpine flora conservation, and ethnopharmacology.
  • Master of Science in Evolutionary Biology (Plant Evolutionary Genetics Track) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year English-taught degree exploring plant adaptive genomics, evolutionary developmental biology, and herbarium genomics.
  • Corso di Perfezionamento in Gestione e Conservazione del Verde Storico e Orti Botanici (Postgraduate Diploma) — Specialized professional qualification in historic garden curation, UNESCO site management, and living collections curation.
