Kanagawa University: Engineering, Global Business & MEXT Scholarships Guide

Founded in 1928, Kanagawa University (Jindai) is a major comprehensive private university situated on its verdant main campus in Rokkakubashi, Yokohama, alongside the innovative Minato Mirai campus in central Yokohama's port district. Educating over 18,000 students across 10 faculties and 8 graduate schools, Kanagawa University is celebrated for its Institute of Nonwritten Cultural Materials, advanced engineering laboratories, international business curricula, and generous Japanese government (MEXT) scholarship pathways.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of a 16-year school education curriculum with an accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent recognized by the Japanese Ministry of Education, MEXT); preliminary research plan and supervisor endorsement required.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree or professional degree; submission of master's thesis, comprehensive doctoral research proposal, and passing of the graduate school entrance examination/interview.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 or N1 for Japanese-medium graduate degree programs; TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS Academic 6.0+ for English-track graduate courses and research degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate applicants: 2,000-character (Japanese) or 1,000-word (English) research proposal detailing research objectives, methodology, and literature review.
Application fee
¥35,000 JPY application screening fee (¥10,000 JPY for non-degree research student status).
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, official academic transcripts, research proposal, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, JLPT or English proficiency test report, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for graduate programs averages ¥750,000 to ¥1,150,000 JPY per academic year (with an admission matriculation fee of ¥200,000 JPY in the first year). Kanagawa University offers an outstanding Tuition Reduction Scholarship granting a 30% to 50% tuition waiver to privately financed international students who maintain satisfactory academic standing.
