Henley Business School Germany

Henley Business School Germany, operating from Munich, is the German campus of the world-renowned triple-accredited (AMBA, EQUIS, AACSB) business school of the University of Reading, delivering executive leadership education across DACH and Central Europe.

Academic overview & excellence

Henley Germany specializes in the Flexible Executive MBA, executive coaching credentials, and corporate sustainability strategy. With over 75 years of heritage, Henley blends British academic rigor with Bavarian commercial dynamism in automotive, technology, and biotechnology sectors.

Research admissions & criteria

Executive MBA candidates typically possess at least three years of managerial experience and an accredited bachelor's degree (or equivalent professional qualification). Applicants complete an interview and submit an analytical leadership portfolio in English.
