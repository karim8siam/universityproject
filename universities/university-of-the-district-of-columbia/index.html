University of the District of Columbia

UDC · Washington, District of Columbia, United States

The University of the District of Columbia (UDC) is the only public university in the nation's capital and the only urban land-grant historically Black university (HBCU) in the United States, established in 1851. Located in Washington, D.C., UDC is celebrated for urban sustainability and agriculture through CAUSES, the David A. Clarke School of Law (UDC Law) with its top-ranked clinical legal education, and biomedical engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UDC encompasses six colleges and schools: the College of Agriculture, Urban Sustainability and Environmental Sciences (CAUSES), School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS), UDC David A. Clarke School of Law, College of Arts and Sciences, and the School of Business and Public Administration. UDC operates the Muirkirk Research Farm and the Center for Urban Resilience.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, transcripts, and GPA. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.0+).
