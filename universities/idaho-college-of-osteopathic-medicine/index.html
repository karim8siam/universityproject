Idaho College of Osteopathic Medicine

ICOM · Meridian, Idaho, United States

Idaho College of Osteopathic Medicine (ICOM) is Idaho’s first medical school, delivering accredited Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (DO) degrees dedicated to training rural primary care physicians across Idaho, Montana, Wyoming, and the Pacific Northwest.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Located on the Idaho State University Meridian Health Science Center campus, ICOM features a 94,000-square-foot facility with simulation operating suites, osteopathic manipulative medicine (OMM) labs, and clinical affiliations throughout the Mountain West.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires a bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution, completion of premed science coursework, competitive MCAT scores (typically 500+), AACOMAS application, and demonstrated commitment to osteopathic medicine and rural healthcare.
