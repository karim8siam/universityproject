Scuola CHANGE: Systemic Family Psychotherapy, Relational Clinical Care Guide

Scuola CHANGE in Bari is an accredited Italian postgraduate institute of psychotherapy recognized by the Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR), renowned for its four-year specialization in systemic-relational psychotherapy, family mediation, adolescent crisis intervention, and clinical psychopathology.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Master's degree in Psychology (LM-51) or Medicine and Surgery (LM-41), with registration or pending registration in the official Italian professional register (Albo degli Psicologi or Medici).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year postgraduate specialization conferring the legally recognized title of Psychotherapist (Psicoterapeuta) under Italian law (art. 3 L. 56/89).
Language proficiency
Italian language fluency (C1 level required for clinical client dialogue and supervision).
Research statement / Proposal
Individual motivational and clinical aptitude interview evaluating relational maturity and theoretical orientation.
Application fee
€100 admissions screening and personal evaluation interview fee.
Required documentation
University graduation diploma with transcripts, certificate of professional register enrollment, curriculum vitae, and photo ID.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual tuition for the four-year accredited specialization program is approximately €3,600 to €4,200. Regional Apulian postgraduate training vouchers, regional healthcare scholarships, and subsidized clinical supervision placements are available.
