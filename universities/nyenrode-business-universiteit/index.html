Nyenrode Business Universiteit: Full-time MBA, Management & Accountancy Guide

Established in 1946 by captains of Dutch industry (including KLM, Shell, and Unilever) at the historic 13th-century Nyenrode Castle estate in Breukelen, Nyenrode Business Universiteit is the only private research university in the Netherlands. Internationally renowned for its core values—Leadership, Entrepreneurship, and Stewardship—Nyenrode delivers prestigious AMBA- and EQUIS-accredited Master of Science in Management, Full-time MBA, and executive PhD programs.

Nyenrode Revolving Scholarship for International Talents

Prestigious alumni-funded revolving scholarship enabling ambitious international students to attend Nyenrode, with recipients committing to contribute back to the fund upon career establishment.

Full-time Master of Science in Management (MSc in Management)

Sixteen-month top-ranked master's program offering specialized tracks in Digital Business & Innovation, Financial Management, and Global Business Strategy across Breukelen and Amsterdam.

Nyenrode Doctoral Fellowship in Corporate Governance and Stewardship

Four-year executive and full-time doctoral research fellowship at Nyenrode Corporate Governance Institute examining supervisory board efficacy, stewardship culture, and ESG accountability.

Nyenrode European Institute for Sustainable Leadership Research Grant

Applied management research project investigating how European business enterprises balance long-term environmental sustainability with short-term capital market demands.

Amsterdam Financial District Corporate Strategy & Consulting Internship

Five-month corporate strategy internship with Fortune 500 multinationals and consulting firms on Amsterdam's Zuidas (KPMG, ING, Heineken) executing strategic transformation projects.

Nyenrode Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Auditing and Accounting Ethics

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at Nyenrode Faculty of Accountancy investigating audit quality metrics, fraud detection algorithms, and ethical professional skepticism.

Nyenrode Women in Leadership and Stewardship Scholarship

Targeted scholarship supporting female emerging leaders and executives enrolling in full-time management and MBA degree programs at Nyenrode's Breukelen estate.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (B.Sc. or B.A.) from a recognized university in business, economics, engineering, or social sciences with sound quantitative foundations (minimum GPA 3.0/4.0 or Dutch equivalent 6.8/10).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Nyenrode PhD Program; requiring an outstanding Master's degree (M.Sc. or MBA with distinction), approved research proposal, and supervisor matching.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 92 (minimum 22 in all sub-scores), IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands), or Cambridge C1 Advanced.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for MBA and master's applicants: 1,000-word personal leadership statement and case evaluation highlighting ethical stewardship.
Application fee
€0 application fee for direct university admissions portal submissions.
Required documentation
Certified university diploma and transcripts of records, CV, motivation letter, Nyenrode Admissions Test (LTP) or GMAT/GRE score, and two recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Nyenrode Business Universiteit is approximately €23,500 to €28,000 for the Master of Science in Management and €42,000 to €45,000 for the Full-time MBA. Nyenrode offers Revolving Scholarships, Corporate Stewardship Grants providing up to 50% tuition fee reductions, and Dutch student finance loans.
