Washington University in St. Louis

WashU · St. Louis, Missouri, United States

Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) is a world-renowned private Carnegie R1 research university founded in 1853. Internationally acclaimed for the Washington University School of Medicine, McKelvey School of Engineering, Olin Business School, and Arts & Sciences, WashU drives pioneering breakthroughs in genomic sequencing, neuroscience, biomedical engineering, and plant biology with over $950 million in annual research expenditures.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WashU comprises seven academic divisions: the School of Medicine, McKelvey School of Engineering, College of Arts & Sciences, Olin Business School, School of Law, Sam Fox School of Design & Visual Arts, and the Brown School of Social Work. The university operates the McDonnell Genome Institute, the Siteman Cancer Center, and the Danforth Campus.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective and test-optional. Graduate doctoral admissions offer full funding packages including multi-year living stipends ($36,000–$42,000/year), 100% tuition coverage, and health insurance. Applicants submit transcripts, letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, and English proficiency (TOEFL 90+ or IELTS 7.0+).
