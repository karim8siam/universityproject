Bournemouth University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Bournemouth University (BU), located on England's south coast across Talbot and Lansdowne campuses, is internationally celebrated for the National Centre for Computer Animation (NCCA)—one of the world's foremost schools for visual effects and 3D animation, whose graduates have won multiple Academy Awards for films like Inception, Interstellar, and Blade Runner 2049. BU also excels in cyber security, forensic archaeology, and tourism management.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

National Centre for Computer Animation (NCCA) PhD in Real-Time Generative 3D Visual Effects

Four-year doctoral candidacy within the world-famous NCCA researching neural volume rendering, real-time cloth and fluid dynamics, and generative diffusion models for cinematic visual effects.

Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Animation and Visual Effects (NCCA Flagship)

One-year world-leading master's degree training visual effects technical directors (TDs), combining programmable shaders, physical simulation, compositing, and production pipeline programming.

Master of Science (MSc) in Cyber Security and Human Factors

NCSC-certified master's degree exploring socio-technical security vulnerabilities, behavioral threat intelligence, penetration testing, and human factors in incident response.

Bournemouth University Executive Dean's International Scholarship

Highly competitive scholarship awarded directly by the Executive Deans of Faculties to outstanding international students demonstrating academic distinction and creative excellence.

Department of Archaeology and Anthropology Paleolithic Ecology & Human Evolution Fellowship

Two-year research appointment investigating Pleistocene human footprints, prehistoric coastal foraging adaptations, and environmental transitions along the UNESCO Jurassic Coast.

London Visual Effects & Film Studio Motion Capture Residency

Four-month studio practicum operating Vicon optical motion capture systems, cleaning kinematic data, and integrating live-action digital doubles into feature film post-production pipelines.

Department of Tourism and Hospitality Postdoctoral Fellowship in Low-Carbon Coastal Tourism

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at BU's world-top-10 tourism research centre, modeling carbon footprints of seaside holiday destinations and designing regenerative tourism policy frameworks.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A UK 2:1 or recognized international equivalent honours degree in a relevant discipline (or 2:2 with extensive professional industry portfolio).
Doctoral admission eligibility
UK Master's degree with Merit or Distinction, or first-class honours bachelor's degree; approved research proposal and confirmed academic supervisor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 to 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 to 6.0 in all sub-bands) or equivalent Pearson PTE Academic.
Research statement / Proposal
A 1,500-word research proposal articulating objectives, methodology, state-of-the-art context, and technical computing or studio requirements.
Application fee
£0 application fee for direct online postgraduate research and master's submissions.
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, CV, portfolio of creative/technical work (where applicable), 2 academic references, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition at Bournemouth University ranges between £16,500 and £21,500 per year. Fully funded doctoral candidates receive UKRI-aligned matched studentships providing 100% international tuition fee coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend of £19,237 plus specialized graphics workstation grants.
