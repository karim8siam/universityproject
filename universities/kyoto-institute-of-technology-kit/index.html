Kyoto Institute of Technology: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Tracing its roots to the Kyoto Crafts High School (founded 1902) and Kyoto Sericultural Training School (founded 1899), Kyoto Institute of Technology is situated on the picturesque Matsugasaki Campus in Sakyo Ward, Kyoto. KIT is unique among Japanese national universities for its philosophy of “Monotsukuri” (craftsmanship-driven engineering), fusing traditional Japanese textile and architectural aesthetics with cutting-edge materials science, macromolecular chemistry, robotics, and genome editing. KIT is home to the KYOTO Design Lab (D-lab) and the Center for Fiber and Textile Science, regularly collaborating with international institutions and Kyoto traditional craft industries.
