University of Maryland, Baltimore

UMB · Baltimore, Maryland, United States

The University of Maryland, Baltimore (UMB) is Maryland's premier public health, law, and human services university, established in 1807 in downtown Baltimore. Home to the nation's fifth oldest medical school, UMB is an elite biomedical powerhouse driving over $650 million in annual research, clinical breakthroughs, and public health policy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMB comprises six professional schools—Medicine, Law, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Nursing, and Social Work—alongside an interdisciplinary Graduate School. The campus is adjacent to the University of Maryland Medical Center and operates the Institute of Human Virology (IHV), the Center for Vaccine Development and Global Health (CVD), and the Institute for Genome Sciences (IGS).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective and administered through individual professional schools. Doctoral biomedical applicants require strong undergraduate STEM preparation, minimum 3.2 GPA, laboratory research credentials, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 90+ or IELTS 7.0+).
