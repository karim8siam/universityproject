Academic overview & research priorities

Established in 2001 to drive regional high-tech economic revitalization, HU offers accelerated bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral degrees in analytics, computer science, interactive media, biotechnology, and advanced manufacturing with mandatory industry experiential learning.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.20) and STEM coursework; test scores optional. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree in a quantitative or technical discipline with 3.00+ GPA, statement of purpose, resume, and TOEFL iBT 80+ / IELTS 6.5+.
