Academic overview & excellence

Founded in 1881 as the Meiji Law School by visionary jurists, Meiji University is one of Tokyo's prestigious 'MARCH' private research universities. The Izumi Campus features the state-of-the-art Izumi Library (housing over 400,000 volumes and dynamic media learning commons), the Izumi Media Building, and advanced foreign language institutes. It hosts foundational curricula in Law, Commerce, Political Science and Economics, Literature, Business Administration, Information and Communication, and Global Japanese Studies.

Research admissions & criteria

Domestic undergraduate admissions occur via the Common Test for University Admissions and faculty-specific general entrance examinations. International applicants apply through the Special Admissions for Privately Financed International Students, requiring the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students (EJU in Japanese, Japan and the World, Mathematics) alongside JLPT N1/N2 and TOEFL/IELTS English scores.
