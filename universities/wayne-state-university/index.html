Wayne State University

Wayne State · Detroit, Michigan, United States

Wayne State University is a major comprehensive urban Carnegie R1 public research university located in the cultural Midtown center of Detroit, Michigan. Founded in 1868, Wayne State is celebrated for the Wayne State University School of Medicine, College of Engineering, Law School, and Mike Ilitch School of Business, serving as a national powerhouse for health disparities research, automotive engineering, and urban community revitalization.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Wayne State enrolls students across 13 schools and colleges, featuring the nation's premier perinatal research branch, the smart mobility and battery engineering centers, and the Karmanos Cancer Institute. Wayne State forms Michigan's University Research Corridor alongside UM and MSU.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions review high school GPA, core preparation, and personal statements with test-optional policies. Graduate doctoral applicants receive Graduate Teaching or Research Assistantships ($24,000–$30,000/year plus tuition remission). Application requires an accredited bachelor's degree, transcripts, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
