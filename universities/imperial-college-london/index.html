Imperial College London

Founded in 1907 in South Kensington, Imperial College London is one of the world's most prestigious universities, consistently ranked among the top 10 globally. Solely focused on science, engineering, medicine, and business, Imperial generates over £1.2 billion in annual income and boasts 14 Nobel laureates. Explore verified graduate degrees, prestigious President's PhD Scholarships, research placements, and world-leading faculty profiles.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Imperial College London President's PhD Scholarships

Imperial's most prestigious doctoral fellowship awarded to 50 top-tier international and domestic PhD scholars annually, providing comprehensive tuition fee coverage, £25,150/year stipend, and research funding.

Imperial College PhD in Computing

Three to four-year doctoral research program inside Imperial's Department of Computing in South Kensington, investigating Artificial Intelligence, Programming Languages, Autonomous Systems, and Cryptography.

Imperial College MSc in Advanced Computing

Intensive one-year full-time taught master's degree (90 ECTS) providing advanced specialized modules in deep learning, automated reasoning, parallel computing, computer vision, and an individual 3-month research project.

Imperial College MSc in Biomedical Engineering

World-leading one-year postgraduate master's program offering specialized streams in Biomechanics, Biomaterials, Neurotechnology, and Medical Physics based at Imperial's state-of-the-art White City and South Kensington campuses.

Imperial I-X Artificial Intelligence Research Initiative Placements

Interdisciplinary artificial intelligence research initiative based at Translation & Innovation Hub (I-HUB) in White City, conducting high-impact research at the intersection of AI, data science, and real-world engineering.

Imperial Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP)

Imperial's flagship summer research placement program, enabling students to work directly alongside leading researchers on live scientific research investigations for 6 to 12 weeks.

Imperial Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA Pathway)

Departmental academic tutoring positions for enrolled doctoral researchers, providing pedagogical training and hourly remuneration for supervising undergraduate lab classes, leading problem sessions, and grading exams.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK First Class or Upper Second Class (2:1) Honours Bachelor's degree or international equivalent (typically 80%+ or 3.5/4.0 GPA) in computing, engineering, mathematics, or physics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with Distinction or First Class Honours Bachelor's degree with demonstrable scientific research ability, publications, or thesis excellence.
Language proficiency
Imperial Higher College English requirement: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all elements) or TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 22 in all sections).
Research statement / Proposal
Concise 2 to 3-page research proposal detailing research hypothesis, novelty, methodology, and identified Imperial academic research group.
Application fee
£80 application fee for postgraduate taught master's courses; free application for postgraduate doctoral research (PhD).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 2 academic reference letters, curriculum vitae, personal statement of purpose, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate taught master's programs ranges from £40,000 to £45,000 per year for overseas international students (£18,500 to £22,000 for UK home students). For doctoral researchers, the Imperial President's PhD Scholarships completely cover all tuition fees (home or overseas) and provide an annual tax-free stipend of £25,150 plus £2,000/year consumables funding.
