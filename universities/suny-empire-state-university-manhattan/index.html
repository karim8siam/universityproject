Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1971 by SUNY Chancellor Ernest L. Boyer, SUNY Empire State University operates modern learning centers in Manhattan (Hudson Street), Brooklyn, and Saratoga Springs alongside its massive digital campus. The university is nationally recognized for individualized degree planning, credit for prior learning (Pla), and accredited programs across business, allied health, nursing, science, and human services.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation (or GED) with an online application and transcripts. ACT/SAT scores are not required. Graduate school admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0, essay on educational goals, resume, and letters of recommendation. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+.
