Yokohama National University: Marine Technology, Semiconductor & Chemistry Guide

Yokohama National University (YNU - 横浜国立大学) is a prestigious Japanese national university located in the port city of Yokohama, renowned for naval architecture and ocean engineering, advanced power semiconductor devices, risk management, and chemical science.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

MEXT Japanese Government University Recommendation Scholarship at YNU

Prestigious Monbukagakusho (MEXT) scholarship allocated directly through Yokohama National University for top international applicants enrolling in Graduate School of Engineering or Urban Innovation.

Master of Engineering in Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering

Flagship master's program utilizing YNU's deep wave basin and ship towing tanks to explore autonomous surface vessels, marine floating wind foundations, and computational hydrodynamics.

Ph.D. in Wide Bandgap Power Semiconductors & Diamond Electronics Fellowship

Doctoral research program developing gallium nitride (GaN), silicon carbide (SiC), and diamond semiconductor transistors for ultra-high-efficiency electric grid inverters and EV power modules.

Tokyo Bay Coastal Ecology & Urban Flood Defense Modeling Project

Interdisciplinary research study combining high-resolution storm surge simulation, seawall structural resilience, and estuarine wetland preservation across Tokyo Bay.

Yokohama Industrial Port & Automotive Battery Testing Practicum

Three to six-month technical internship working with automotive OEMs (Nissan, Mitsubishi) and maritime battery manufacturers in the Keihin industrial zone.

JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowship for International Researchers at YNU

Prestigious two-year international postdoctoral fellowship conducted in partnership with YNU faculty in electrochemistry, solid-state batteries, or organic synthesis.

Associate Professor in Autonomous Surface Vessels & Marine Robotics

Permanent faculty appointment in the Faculty of Engineering, teaching marine vehicle dynamics, path planning algorithms, and supervising towing tank experimental research.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of education and an accredited Bachelor's degree in engineering, science, or social sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or professional equivalent with demonstrated thesis or journal publication output.
Language proficiency
English programs: TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0. Japanese programs: JLPT N1 or N2.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research proposal (Kenkyu-Keikaku-sho) in English or Japanese (2,000 words) endorsed by prospective faculty host.
Application fee
¥30,000 examination application fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, research plan, two letters of recommendation, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual graduate tuition is standard Japanese national university tuition: ¥535,800 (~$3,550 USD). Admission fee is ¥282,000 (one-time). Japanese Government MEXT scholarships, JASSO honors scholarships, and full/half tuition exemptions are available for qualified international students.
