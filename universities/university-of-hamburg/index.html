HAMBURG · GERMAN UNIVERSITY OF EXCELLENCE & CLIMATE COMPUTING HUB

University of Hamburg: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Hamburg (Universität Hamburg, UHH), founded in 1919, is one of Germany's flagship Universities of Excellence and the premier comprehensive research university in northern Germany. Globally celebrated as the home of the Max Planck Institute for Meteorology and the CLICCS (Climate, Climatic Change, and Society) Cluster of Excellence, UHH is also a world leader in photon science at DESY and manuscript cultures.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

CLICCS Cluster of Excellence Doctoral Fellowship in Earth System Modeling & Climate Scenarios

Three-year doctoral position within the School of Integrated Climate and Earth System Sciences (SICSS), running coupled ICON climate models on the Levante supercomputer at the German Climate Computing Center (DKRZ).

International Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Physics (Photon Science and Quantum Technologies at DESY)

English-taught graduate curriculum closely integrated with the Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron (DESY) and European XFEL, exploring attosecond laser physics, synchrotron radiation, and quantum condensed matter.

Hamburg Merit Scholarship for Outstanding International Postgraduate Students

Merit-based living allowance scholarship financed by the Free and Hanseatic City of Hamburg, awarded to outstanding international students enrolled in regular degree programs at Universität Hamburg.

Centre for Structural Systems Biology (CSSB) Cryo-EM Infection Research Assistantship

Research role at CSSB on the Bahrenfeld science campus resolving atomic-resolution structures of pathogen-host complexes using high-end Titan Krios transmission electron microscopes.

Centre for the Study of Manuscript Cultures (CSMC) Digital Epigraphy & Paleography Practicum

Five-month research practicum utilizing multispectral imaging and mobile computed tomography to non-destructively recover illegible ancient manuscripts from Middle Eastern and East Asian collections.

Hamburg Centre for Ultrafast Imaging (CUI) Postdoctoral Fellowship in Molecular Quantum Dynamics

Two-year postdoctoral position within the CUI Cluster of Excellence observing chemical bond-breaking in real time using femtosecond X-ray pulses generated by the European XFEL.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Intelligent Adaptive Systems and Autonomous Robotics

English-taught master's program focusing on biologically inspired artificial intelligence, multi-modal sensor fusion, humanoid robotic manipulation, and reinforcement learning.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline with strong academic marks (at least grade 2.5 on the German grading scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or Diplom with above-average grades ('gut' or 'sehr gut'); approved doctoral dissertation proposal and faculty supervisor commitment.
Language proficiency
For English programs: TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS Academic 6.5. For German programs: DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 in all examination sections.
Research statement / Proposal
A 4-6 page doctoral research project outline detailing background literature, hypotheses, methodological approach, and proposed timeline.
Application fee
€75 application fee for international credentials evaluated through uni-assist.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, academic transcripts, Europass CV, letter of motivation, and 2 academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Hamburg is completely free of charge (€0 tuition) for all students regardless of nationality. Students pay only a modest semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €340, which includes a Deutschlandticket giving unlimited regional and local public transportation across all of Germany. Doctoral researchers are employed under German collective agreements (TV-L E13) with gross annual salaries between €52,000 and €58,000.
