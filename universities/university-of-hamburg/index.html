University of Hamburg (Universität Hamburg)

Founded in 1919, the University of Hamburg (Universität Hamburg - UHH) is one of Germany's premier Universities of Excellence under the German Excellence Strategy. UHH boasts four internationally acclaimed Clusters of Excellence: CLICCS (Climate, Climatic Change, and Society), CUI: Advanced Imaging of Matter, the Cluster of Excellence Quantum Universe, and Understanding Written Artefacts (UWA). Partnering closely with the DESY synchrotron radiation research centre, Max Planck Institutes, and the European XFEL, UHH provides tuition-free graduate education and premier research infrastructure.

Hamburg CLICCS Doctoral Fellowships in Climate, Climatic Change, and Society

Cutting-edge doctoral research at the CUI: Advanced Imaging of Matter Cluster of Excellence, utilizing DESY's PETRA III, FLASH, and European XFEL to image atomic motion in real time.

Structural biology doctoral training conducted at the Centre for Structural Systems Biology (CSSB) on the DESY campus, deciphering molecular mechanisms of human viral and bacterial pathogens.

Interdisciplinary research within Germany's premier Cluster of Excellence Understanding Written Artefacts, applying scientific imaging, multispectral scanning, and material analysis to historical manuscripts.

Postdoctoral appointment at the Center for Earth System Research and Sustainability (CEN), developing coupled physical-biogeochemical models to assess the impact of climate change on North Sea and North Atlantic ecosystems.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in a relevant subject area with an above-average final grade (German grade 2.5 or better, equivalent to 75%+ or 3.0/4.0 GPA).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 300 ECTS aggregate) with a thesis graded 'good' (gut) or 'very good' (sehr gut); written confirmation of supervision (Betreuungszusage) from a UHH professor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all components) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught programs; German C1 (DSH-2 / TestDaF 4x4) for German-medium degree tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A 5-page doctoral research expose detailing theoretical foundation, research questions, methodological approach, and preliminary timetable.
Application fee
€0 application fee; no tuition fees charged at public German research universities.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma certificates, academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, research expose, and professor supervision confirmation agreement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees are charged at the University of Hamburg for domestic and international students alike. Students pay only a nominal semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €340 per semester, which includes full regional public transit access (Deutschlandticket) throughout Hamburg and Germany. Doctoral researchers are supported via TV-L E13 salaried positions (€48,000 to €54,000/year gross).
