Open University of the Netherlands: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Established in 1984 by the Government of the Netherlands in Heerlen, the Open University of the Netherlands (Open Universiteit - OU) is the state-funded national university for open and distance higher education. Renowned globally for educational technology, computer science, environmental sciences, and psychology, OU combines open admission flexibility with high research rigor.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science or Arts degree (180 ECTS credits) in a related discipline evaluated by the university board of examiners.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science or Master of Arts degree satisfying Dutch third-cycle university regulations, approved by the Doctoral Committee (Promotiecommissie).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each subtest (or TOEFL iBT 90). For Dutch-medium programs: NT2-II certification.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose, resume detailing academic or industry background, and research outline (for doctoral positions).
Application fee
€0 (Studielink centralized Dutch national application portal).
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree certificates and transcripts, proof of English/Dutch language proficiency, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is governed by Dutch statutory higher education law. For Dutch and EU/EEA citizens without a prior Dutch master's degree, statutory tuition is €2,601 per academic year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition ranges from €6,800 to €9,500 per year depending on module pace. Doctoral researchers (Promovendi) are employed as academic staff with zero tuition and full Dutch university salaries (€3,000–€3,800/month).
