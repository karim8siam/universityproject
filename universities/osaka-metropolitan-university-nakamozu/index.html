Osaka Metropolitan University - Nakamozu Campus

The Nakamozu Campus of Osaka Metropolitan University (OMU / 公立大学法人大阪公立大学), formerly the main campus of Osaka Prefecture University, is a world-class public STEM and agricultural science research hub situated in Sakai, Osaka.

Academic overview & excellence

Formed by the historic 2022 merger of Osaka City University and Osaka Prefecture University, OMU is Japan's largest public metropolitan university. Nakamozu Campus hosts the Graduate School of Engineering, Graduate School of Agriculture, and the Research Center for Artificial Photosynthesis (ReCAP).

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires completion of 12 years of formal education for undergraduate faculties (via EJU and university examinations). Master's and doctoral degrees require an accredited degree, research plan, and Japanese or English language proficiency (TOEFL iBT 80+ or JLPT N2/N1).
