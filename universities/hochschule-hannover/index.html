Hochschule Hannover

Hochschule Hannover is the second largest university in Lower Saxony's capital, celebrated for its cutting-edge engineering faculties, world-renowned Photojournalism and Documentary Photography department, bioprocess engineering, and applied computer science.

Academic overview & excellence

HsH offers over 60 accredited bachelor's and master's degree courses characterized by deep integration with Lower Saxony's industrial champions (Continental, Volkswagen, Sennheiser). Campuses feature advanced mechatronics test rigs, broadcast studios, and microbiology cleanrooms.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requirements include a recognized higher education entrance qualification (Abitur or Fachhochschulreife). International applicants submit Uni-Assist VPD documents and proof of German proficiency (TestDaF 4 or DSH-2). Selected engineering and design master's pathways accept English certification (IELTS 6.5).

State of Lower Saxony merit scholarship supporting talented first-generation and international students enrolled in technical and science degrees.

Master's

Master of Science in Sensor and Automation Technology

Three-semester advanced engineering degree focusing on industrial IoT, optical measurement sensors, PLC automation, and autonomous robotics.

Funding: Industrial research assistant positions: €1,200–€1,600/month Tuition: €0 tuition fees Deadline: January 15, 2027
PhD

Dr.-Ing. Cooperative Doctoral Research Fellowship in Automotive Mechatronics

Three-year cooperative doctoral fellowship conducted jointly with Leibniz University Hannover, developing battery management systems and autonomous vehicle telematics.

Funding: Salaried TV-L E13 scientific researcher contract (€48,000–€52,000/annum) Tuition: €0 Deadline: March 1, 2027
Fellowship

Prestige residency hosted within Germany's top-ranked photojournalism department, supporting long-term investigative documentary photography projects.

Funding: €5,000 production stipend and solo exhibition at Lumix Festival of Young Photojournalism Tuition: €0 Deadline: March 31, 2027
Internship

Six-month industrial research practicum embedded in Continental headquarters in Hanover, developing smart tire tread pressure and acoustic sensors.

Funding: €1,250 monthly corporate internship salary plus subsidized corporate meals Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Seven-semester accredited engineering degree training specialists in industrial fermentation, bioreactor engineering, pharmaceutical purification, and biocatalysis.

Funding: BAföG state educational support and Deutschlandstipendium eligible Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: July 15, 2027
