City of Oxford College: Engineering, Digital Tech & Higher Apprenticeships Guide

City of Oxford College is the flagship further and higher education campus of Activate Learning, situated in the historic city center of Oxford along the River Thames (Oxpens Road campus). Working in close educational partnership with Oxford Brookes University and regional high-tech employers in the Oxfordshire science and technology cluster, City of Oxford College delivers T-Levels, Higher National Certificates/Diplomas (HNC/HND), Foundation Degrees, and Higher Apprenticeships in software engineering, digital media, business management, and mechanical engineering.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Collaborative post-diploma top-up degrees to B.Sc. (Hons) and postgraduate study pathways with Oxford Brookes University require accredited HND (Level 5) with merit profile.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; further and higher education college delivering Level 4 and Level 5 HNC/HND credentials, foundation degrees, and university top-up degrees.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in all bands) for higher education diploma programs; 5.5 overall for Level 3 further education courses.
Research statement / Proposal
Not required; personal statement detailing technical interest, career aspirations, and relevant work experience required for higher education applications.
Application fee
£0 for direct international college applications.
Required documentation
Secondary school certificates (GCSEs or international equivalent), academic transcripts, proof of English language proficiency, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic higher education students ranges from £6,165 to £7,500 per academic year. International tuition for higher education diploma programs ranges from £9,500 to £11,500 per year. Student finance maintenance loans and Activate Learning bursaries provide financial support to eligible applicants.
