Shanghai University Tokyo Campus

Shanghai University Tokyo Campus is the official Japanese overseas campus of Shanghai University (Project 211 / Double First-Class), offering accredited dual-degree programs in international economics, Chinese-Japanese bilateral trade, and cross-cultural communication.

Academic overview & research priorities

Approved by the Chinese Ministry of Education and operating in Shinjuku, Tokyo, the campus allows students to complete full undergraduate curricula in Tokyo and Shanghai. Programs integrate Mandarin Chinese and Japanese business negotiation, cross-border digital commerce, and East Asian economic integration.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, English or Japanese proficiency, and Mandarin Chinese language foundation. Students without prior Chinese study complete an intensive bilingual preparatory semester.
