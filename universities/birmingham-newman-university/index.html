Birmingham Newman University: Psychology, Education & Social Justice Guide

Established in 1968 in Bartley Green, Birmingham, Birmingham Newman University is a public university recognized for teaching excellence, community engagement, and social justice. Renowned across the United Kingdom for teacher education, applied developmental psychology, forensic criminology, and youth work, Newman delivers intimate seminar learning and practitioner-led research.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Birmingham Newman Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship

Merit-based tuition discount awarded to outstanding international students enrolling in full-time master's degree programs at Birmingham Newman University.

Master of Arts in Education (Inclusion and Special Educational Needs)

Advanced graduate curriculum designed for practicing teachers and educational leaders developing evidence-based interventions for neurodivergent pupils and inclusive classrooms.

Newman Doctoral Research Studentship in Youth Mental Health and Resilience

Three-year fully funded PhD studentship at the Centre for Science, Knowledge and Belief in Society examining protective factors and community interventions for adolescent anxiety in urban UK.

West Midlands Restorative Justice & Youth Crime Prevention Project

Collaborative empirical study with West Midlands youth offending teams evaluating the long-term recidivism impacts of peer mediation and restorative cautioning interventions.

Birmingham City Council Community Mental Health Practicum

Sixteen-week supervised placement within NHS community mental health trusts and local authority centres providing psychoeducational support for underserved urban communities.

Newman Postdoctoral Fellowship in Critical Pedagogy and Anti-Racist Education

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment evaluating institutional interventions to dismantle systemic attainment gaps and decolonize initial teacher education curricula.

Newman Alumni & Community Partner Master's Tuition Award

Fee reduction award supporting postgraduate students dedicated to public sector service, voluntary community development, and educational leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A 2:2 or 2:1 honours degree (or international equivalent with GPA 2.8 to 3.2+ out of 4.0) in a relevant social science, psychology, or education discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-class or strong master's degree (Merit or Distinction) with proven research dissertation; doctoral proposal aligned with university research centers.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (with minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 88 (minimum 20 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research statement (2,000 words) defining problem scope, state of the art, methodology, and proposed supervisory team in education, psychology, or theology.
Application fee
GBP 0 for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Full academic transcripts, degree award certificates, two academic reference letters, personal statement of purpose, curriculum vitae, and English test certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

UK home postgraduate tuition is approximately GBP 8,500 to GBP 9,500 per year. International tuition for full-time master's programs is approximately GBP 14,500 to GBP 16,000 per year. Newman Vice-Chancellor's Excellence Scholarships, postgraduate bursaries, and alumni discounts provide up to GBP 3,000 in direct tuition fee reductions.
