North Carolina Independent Colleges and Universities (NCICU) represents North Carolina's 36 private, non-profit colleges and universities—including Duke University, Wake Forest University, Davidson College, Elon University, Campbell University, and ten historic HBCUs. Generating over $1.5 billion in annual research and conferring nearly one-third of all bachelor's degrees in North Carolina, NCICU institutions lead the nation in biomedical research, law, liberal arts, and fintech.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through member independent universities (Duke, Wake Forest, Davidson, Elon, High Point, etc.). Holistic review of secondary transcripts, essays, and extracurricular leadership. TOEFL iBT 80+ (100+ Duke/Wake Forest) or IELTS 6.5+ (7.5+ Duke) for international candidates. Early decision/action November 1, 2026; Regular decision January 5, 2027.
