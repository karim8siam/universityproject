Loyola University Maryland

Loyola Maryland · Baltimore, Maryland, United States

Loyola University Maryland is a private Jesuit Catholic university in Baltimore, Maryland. Established in 1852, Loyola is renowned for the Sellinger School of Business, speech-language pathology, clinical psychology, and Jesuit liberal arts education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Loyola comprises the Sellinger School of Business and Management, Loyola College of Arts and Sciences, and the School of Education. It operates the Loyola Clinical Centers (interdisciplinary community healthcare clinic) and extensive undergraduate research colloquia.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are test-optional, evaluating secondary school curriculum rigor, GPA (average 3.65), and personal character essays. Graduate programs in psychology and speech pathology require an accredited bachelor's degree, prerequisite coursework, and professional letters.
