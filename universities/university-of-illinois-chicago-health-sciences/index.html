University of Illinois Chicago Health Sciences

UIC Health Sciences · Chicago, Illinois, United States

University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) Health Sciences encompasses seven accredited health colleges—Medicine, Pharmacy, Dentistry, Nursing, Applied Health Sciences, Public Health, and Social Work—forming Illinois's principal educator of physicians, dentists, and pharmacists. Situated within the Illinois Medical District, UIC Health Sciences conducts over $400 million in sponsored research, spearheading discoveries in health equity, immunopharmacology, neurobiology, and precision medicine.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Chicago · Illinois Medical District Largest Medical Center & Health Sciences Enterprise. University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) Health Sciences encompasses seven accredited health colleges—Medicine, Pharmacy, Dentistry, Nursing, Applied Health Sciences, Public Health, and Social Work—forming Illinois's principal educator of physicians, dentists, and pharmacists. Situated within the Illinois Medical District, UIC Health Sciences conducts over $400 million in sponsored research, spearheading discoveries in health equity, immunopharmacology, neurobiology, and precision medicine.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through respective health science admissions portals (AMCAS for Medicine, PharmCAS for Pharmacy, DENTPIN/ADEA AADSAS for Dentistry, SOPHAS for Public Health, NursingCAS for Nursing). TOEFL iBT 80+ (subscores: 19 reading, 17 listening, 20 speaking, 21 writing) or IELTS 6.5+ for international health sciences graduate applicants. AMCAS deadline November 1, 2026; PharmCAS deadline March 1, 2027; PhD biomedical programs deadline December 1, 2026.
