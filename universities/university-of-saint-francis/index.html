University of Saint Francis

USF Fort Wayne · Fort Wayne, Indiana, United States

The University of Saint Francis (USF) is a private Franciscan Catholic university founded in 1890, situated on a scenic 70-acre lakeside campus in Fort Wayne, Indiana. Celebrated for elite healthcare education—graduating the region's top nurses and physician assistants—as well as animated arts and digital media at USF Downtown, Saint Francis combines Catholic intellectual values with cutting-edge professional clinical preparation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

USF comprises four primary colleges and divisions: the College of Health Sciences, College of Arts, Sciences, and Business, Division of Creative Arts, and the Keith Busse School of Business and Entrepreneurial Leadership. USF operates advanced clinical simulation centers and downtown studio arts facilities in Fort Wayne.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
