Frostburg State University

Frostburg State University is a premier regional public university situated in the Appalachian highlands of Western Maryland, celebrated for its Wildlife & Fisheries biology, sustainable energy engineering, and recreation management.

Academic overview & research priorities

Part of the University System of Maryland, Frostburg State boasts specialized research centers including the Appalachian Laboratory (UMCES partner), the Gira Center for Communications and Information Technology, and state-of-the-art materials testing and watershed ecology laboratories.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, curriculum rigor, and extracurricular achievement (test-optional). Graduate applicants must possess an accredited baccalaureate degree with minimum 3.0 GPA, program-specific prerequisites, and professional letters of reference.
