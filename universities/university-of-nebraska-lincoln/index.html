University of Nebraska–Lincoln

UNL · Lincoln, Nebraska, United States

The University of Nebraska–Lincoln (UNL) is the flagship land-grant research university of Nebraska and an elite Carnegie R1 institution founded in 1869. Internationally recognized for agricultural robotics, drought mitigation science, high-power laser physics, plant phenomics, and materials nanoscience, UNL drives over $350 million in annual research, anchoring innovation across the Great Plains.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNL spans nine colleges including the College of Agricultural Sciences and Natural Resources (CASNR), College of Engineering, College of Arts and Sciences, and the College of Law. Key research centers include the National Drought Mitigation Center (NDMC), the Extreme Light Laboratory, Nebraska Innovation Campus, and the Center for Plant Science Innovation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school performance and core competencies. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
