Aeres Hogeschool Dronten: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Aeres Hogeschool Dronten, located on reclaimed fertile polder land in Flevoland in the central Netherlands, is a premier Dutch university of applied sciences specializing in commercial agriculture, agribusiness management, precision smart farming, and equine sports management. Operating commercial student farms and research polders, Aeres is internationally recognized for practical agrarian enterprise education.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

European Master of Science in International Agribusiness and Food Chain Management

Two-year master's curriculum conducted in Dronten and partner European agricultural universities, examining global grain commodities, dairy supply chain resilience, and carbon-neutral farm management.

Flevoland Polder Autonomous Agricultural Machinery & Precision Weeding Practicum

Five-month industrial internship deploying autonomous laser weeding rovers, variable-rate fertilizer applicators, and soil conductivity sensors across extensive arable polder farms.

Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (Aeres Dronten International Leadership Award)

Competitive scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education in cooperation with Aeres, awarded to exceptional non-EEA students entering degree programs at the Dronten campus.

Professorship in Soil Health and Regenerative Arable Farming Research Fellowship

Applied research post investigating minimum tillage techniques, diverse multi-species cover crop rotations, and biological nematode suppression on marine clay polder soils.

Master of Science in Equine Sports Management and Performance Physiology

Specialized postgraduate program exploring equine biomechanics, high-performance training regimens, veterinary sports medicine, and international equestrian event organization.

Applied Industrial Research Fellowship in Farm-Scale Anaerobic Digestion & Nitrogen Stripping

Two-year applied researcher appointment optimizing on-farm mono-manure anaerobic digestion units, biomethane grid injection, and circular ammonium sulfate fertilizer recovery.

External Collaborative Doctoral Candidacy in Climate-Neutral Polder Drainage and Water Management (with WUR)

Four-year joint doctoral trajectory between Aeres Dronten and Wageningen University modeling controlled drainage-sub-irrigation systems to prevent soil subsidence and greenhouse gas emissions in low-lying polders.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A relevant Bachelor of Science degree in agriculture, animal science, agribusiness, food technology, or environmental management.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Aeres conducts applied professional doctoral trajectories in cooperation with research universities including Wageningen University & Research (WUR).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in each sub-score).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-3 page Letter of Motivation and agricultural enterprise study plan outlining corporate internship objectives, farm business ideas, and career trajectory.
Application fee
€0 application fee via Studielink.nl.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcript of records, curriculum vitae, copy of passport, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students at Aeres Hogeschool Dronten is approximately €8,900 to €10,500 per academic year (EU/EEA students pay the statutory Dutch tuition of approximately €2,530 per year). International students are eligible for the Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (€5,000 one-time award) and Aeres International Talent Bursaries. Paid Dutch farm and corporate agribusiness internships pay student allowances averaging €700 to €1,200 per month.
