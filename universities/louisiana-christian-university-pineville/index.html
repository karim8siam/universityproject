Louisiana Christian University

Louisiana Christian · Pineville, Louisiana, United States

Louisiana Christian University is a private Christian liberal arts university in Pineville, Louisiana, affiliated with the Louisiana Baptist Convention. Founded in 1906, LCU offers respected programs in nursing, pre-medical biology, business, education, and Christian ministries.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

LCU features the School of Nursing and Allied Health, School of Missions and Ministries, and the C.S. Lewis Honors Program. Academic curricula integrate Christian worldview perspectives with rigorous clinical and professional internships throughout central Louisiana.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require high school transcripts (minimum 2.0 unweighted GPA for general admission; 2.5+ for merit awards) and personal essays under test-optional guidelines. Nursing candidates must meet clinical prerequisite requirements and achieve passing TEAS scores.
