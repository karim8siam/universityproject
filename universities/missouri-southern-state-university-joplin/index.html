Missouri Southern State University

MSSU · Joplin, Missouri, United States

Missouri Southern State University is a comprehensive public university in Joplin, Missouri, designated with a statewide mission in international education. Known for environmental health, criminal justice, dental hygiene, and business administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MSSU features the Plaster College of Business, College of Health Sciences, and College of Arts and Sciences. The university maintains specialized training facilities including modern simulation hospital wards, environmental sampling labs, and the Beimdiek Recreation Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.25) under test-optional criteria. Graduate programs in environmental health and management require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.75 to 3.0 GPA and professional recommendations.
