Utah State University

Utah State · Logan, Utah, United States

Utah State University (USU) is Utah's public flagship land-grant and space-grant Carnegie R1 research university, established in 1888 in scenic Cache Valley in Logan, Utah. Home to the world-renowned Space Dynamics Laboratory (SDL)—a DoD University Affiliated Research Center with flight hardware on hundreds of NASA satellite missions—USU is equally celebrated for water research at the Utah Water Research Laboratory, agriculture, and education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

USU comprises eight colleges including the College of Engineering, S.J. & Jessie E. Quinney College of Natural Resources, College of Agriculture and Applied Sciences, Emma Eccles Jones College of Education and Human Services, and the Jon M. Huntsman School of Business. USU operates the Space Dynamics Laboratory (SDL) and the Utah Water Research Laboratory (UWRL).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA and coursework. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
