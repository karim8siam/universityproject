César Ritz Colleges Switzerland: Hospitality, Tourism & Executive Master's

Named after the legendary hotelier César Ritz ('the king of hoteliers and the hotelier to kings'), César Ritz Colleges Switzerland is an elite Swiss hospitality business school with campuses in Brig and Le Bouveret on Lake Geneva. Consistently ranked top 5 in the world by QS for Hospitality & Leisure Management, it combines Swiss hospitality perfection with sustainable business innovation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized bachelor's degree in any discipline from an accredited university with solid academic grades (minimum 2.5/4.0 GPA or equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Executive doctoral research delivered in partnership with accredited international university partners (e.g., University of Derby) in sustainable luxury and tourism governance.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 79+; Cambridge First Certificate (FCE).
Research statement / Proposal
For master's admission: comprehensive resume/CV, statement of motivation (500 words) detailing leadership ambitions in global hospitality, and interview.
Application fee
110 CHF non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree diploma, certified transcripts, CV, copy of passport, two letters of recommendation, and financial guarantee.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for master's programs at César Ritz Colleges averages approximately 42,000 to 48,000 CHF for the full accredited degree, which includes luxury campus accommodation and full board. Swiss Education Group Merit Scholarships provide tuition awards of up to 20%–30% for high-achieving international candidates.
