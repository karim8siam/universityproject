Christelijke Hogeschool Ede: Journalism, Social Work & NL Scholarships

Christelijke Hogeschool Ede (CHE / Christian University of Applied Sciences Ede) is consistently ranked among the top mid-sized universities of applied sciences in the Netherlands. Renowned for its award-winning School of Journalism and Communication, applied social studies, ethics, healthcare, and educational leadership in the Foodvalley innovation region.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) at CHE

Competitive scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education and CHE for exceptional non-EEA students enrolling in English-taught master's degrees.

Master of Science in Social Work & Community Mental Health Leadership

Two-year applied master's program exploring systemic family intervention, psychiatric rehabilitation, trauma-informed care, and community resilience in urban and rural environments.

Collaborative Applied Doctoral Candidacy in Media Ethics with VU Amsterdam

Four-year salaried doctoral research project conducted between CHE School of Journalism and Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam investigating automated news algorithms and digital journalistic integrity.

Centre of Expertise Contemporary Faith & Society Research Assistantship

Applied research post investigating social cohesion, interfaith dialogue, and civil society voluntary participation in Dutch multicultural neighborhoods.

Dutch National Broadcasting & Digital Newsroom Practicum

Six-month investigative journalism internship embedded in major Dutch national media outlets and broadcasting organizations mastering digital fact-checking and investigative data reporting.

Applied Postdoctoral Fellowship in Healthcare Professionalism & Ethics

Two-year applied research fellowship leading collaborative healthcare projects on moral deliberation, clinical ethics consultancies, and nurse resilience in long-term care.

Master of Science in Educational Leadership & School Transformation

Executive-oriented master's degree training school principals and curriculum coordinators in instructional leadership, educational law, data-informed school improvement, and teacher mentoring.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Arts or Bachelor of Science in Journalism, Social Work, Nursing, Education, or related discipline from a recognized institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct doctoral degrees in the Netherlands are conferred via university partnerships (e.g., Radboud University, VU Amsterdam, or Utrecht University) via CHE research chairs.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each subscore) or TOEFL iBT 90+.
Research statement / Proposal
For master's entry: detailed motivation letter, CV, and professional portfolio; for research contracts: technical project proposal.
Application fee
€0 (applications submitted via Studielink and CHE portal).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, passport copy, CV, proof of English, and motivation statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition is approximately €8,900 to €10,500 per year. Dutch Ministry NL Scholarships provide €5,000 cash grants to high-achieving non-EEA applicants.
