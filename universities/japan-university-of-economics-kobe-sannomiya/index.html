Japan University of Economics - Kobe Sannomiya Campus

Japan University of Economics (Nihon Keizai Daigaku) Kobe Sannomiya Campus is an urban business institution situated in central Kobe, delivering hands-on degrees in international business, entertainment management, tourism, and global economics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Part of the Tsuzuki Education Group, JUE Kobe specializes in practical business education tailored to Asia-Pacific international commerce. Located in the bustling Sannomiya commercial corridor, the campus features innovative concentrations in fashion business, pop culture economics, digital hospitality marketing, and international trade.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, JLPT N2 or EJU scores for international applicants, a statement of commercial motivation, and an interview assessing entrepreneurial drive.
