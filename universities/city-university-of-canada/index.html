City University of Canada: Counselling Psychology & Educational Leadership Guide

Operating prominent campuses in Vancouver, Victoria, Calgary, and Edmonton, City University of Canada (CityU in Canada) is a distinguished, accredited non-profit institution. Renowned across Western Canada for graduate training in Master of Counselling and Master of Education in Leadership, CityU prepares registered clinical counsellors, psychologists, and school administrators through rigorous practitioner-scholar models.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

CityU Canada Mental Health Leadership & Indigenous Clinical Bursary

Need- and merit-based institutional bursary supporting graduate scholars committed to delivering mental health services in rural, Indigenous, and vulnerable urban communities.

Master of Counselling (Counselling Psychology)

Flagship graduate practitioner degree recognized by the BC Association of Clinical Counsellors (BCACC) and College of Alberta Psychologists (CAP) for professional clinical registration.

Master of Education in Leadership and Educational Administration

Two-year practitioner-focused graduate program for educators seeking principalship, curriculum supervision, and executive leadership roles in Canadian school systems.

Western Canadian Trauma-Informed Clinical Intervention Research Project

Applied qualitative and empirical research initiative evaluating somatic and cognitive-behavioural interventions for post-traumatic stress and workplace psychological injury.

CityU Community Clinical Practicum and Internship Residency

Intensive 500-hour supervised clinical placement in community clinics, youth centres, and hospital outpatient units delivering direct individual and family therapy.

CityU Canada Faculty Research Fellowship in Restorative School Leadership

Core faculty teaching and research appointment directing graduate education cohorts, supervising capstone action-research portfolios, and leading anti-racist pedagogy initiatives.

CityU Canada Newcomer & Immigrant Family Mental Health Award

Tuition award celebrating students who bring multilingual capacity and cross-cultural competency to mental health service delivery for immigrant and refugee communities in Canada.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year baccalaureate degree from a recognized institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75 to 3.0 out of 4.0; completion of 12 credits of foundational psychology coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not currently offering independent doctoral degrees; collaborative research transfer and graduate leadership pathways.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing and speaking) or TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 22 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate counselling admissions: 1,000-word statement of intent detailing clinical self-awareness, personal philosophy, and commitment to ethical practice.
Application fee
$50 CAD for domestic Canadian applicants; $150 CAD for international applicants.
Required documentation
Official post-secondary transcripts, comprehensive CV, two professional/academic letters of reference, and admissions interview.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the Master of Counselling degree is approximately $745 CAD per quarter credit (approximately $46,000 CAD total program tuition). Domestic and international candidates can access CityU merit entrance bursaries, provincial student loans, and paid clinical clinic practicum placements across British Columbia and Alberta.
