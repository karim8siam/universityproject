McGill University (Montreal, Canada)

Founded in 1821 in Montreal, McGill University is consistently ranked among the world's top 35 universities and stands as one of Canada's most celebrated research institutions. With 12 Nobel laureates and 148 Rhodes Scholars (more than any other Canadian university), McGill pioneers discoveries in medicine, artificial intelligence (co-founding Mila with Yoshua Bengio and Joelle Pineau), neuroscience at The Neuro, and quantum materials. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

McGill Graduate Excellence Fellowships & McCall MacBain Scholarships

McGill's premier graduate funding awards providing guaranteed multi-year financial support, full tuition coverage, and competitive living stipends for outstanding domestic and international doctoral scholars.

McGill PhD in Computer Science

Four to five-year doctoral research program in the School of Computer Science at McConnell Engineering Building, offering frontier research in artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning, robotics, and bioinformatics in collaboration with Mila.

McGill Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year thesis-based Master of Science degree providing foundational and advanced training in machine learning, computational biology, cryptography, and computer systems with guaranteed graduate student funding.

McGill Master of Science in Neuroscience (IPN)

Premier interdisciplinary graduate degree program inside the Integrated Program in Neuroscience (IPN) based at The Neuro (Montreal Neurological Institute-Hospital), investigating neurodevelopment, cellular neurobiology, and neural computing.

Mila - Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute Collaborative Placements

Frontier research placements inside Mila in Montreal (the world's largest academic deep learning community), collaborating with leading researchers on neural network theory, health AI, and trustworthy machine learning.

McGill Science Undergraduate Research Awards (SURA)

Competitive 15-week summer research award enabling undergraduate students to conduct full-time experimental or theoretical research in physical, chemical, and computational sciences alongside leading McGill professors.

McGill Graduate Teaching Assistantships (AGSEM)

Departmental academic teaching appointments providing instruction leadership training and hourly remuneration for running weekly laboratory sections, grading exams, and tutoring undergraduate students.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Appropriate Bachelor's degree with standing equivalent to at least a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 3.2 out of 4.0 (typically 75%+) in relevant discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in relevant discipline with high academic standing (CGPA 3.4/4.0+), demonstrable thesis research competence, and strong academic recommendation letters.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no individual band below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 86 overall (minimum 20 in each component); higher for CS/Neuroscience (IELTS 7.0).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of purpose describing research milestones, theoretical framework, and target McGill faculty supervisor alignment.
Application fee
CAD $132.90 non-refundable application fee submitted through the McGill online portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 2 confidential letters of recommendation, curriculum vitae, and personal statement of research interests.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for research-stream Master's and PhD programs is heavily subsidized. Under the McGill Graduate Excellence Fellowships and departmental base funding policies, doctoral students in good standing receive guaranteed minimum funding packages of CAD $22,000 to $28,000 per year plus tuition fee remissions that offset international tuition rates.
