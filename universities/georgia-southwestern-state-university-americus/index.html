Academic overview & research priorities

Steeped in history—having educated President Jimmy Carter and Rosalynn Carter—GSW provides individualized collegiate learning through the Rosalynn Carter Institute for Caregivers, School of Nursing, and College of Business and Computing.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with 2.50+ core GPA; test-optional admissions path available. Graduate admissions require accredited bachelor’s degree with 3.00+ GPA and official transcripts. International students require TOEFL iBT 69+ or IELTS 6.0+.
