Hyogo-Kobe University Consortium (HKUC)

The Hyogo-Kobe University Consortium (HKUC / 大学コンソーシアムひょうご神戸) unites 45 universities, local governments, and industrial enterprises across Hyogo Prefecture, driving inter-university credit exchanges, regional disaster resilience research, and global scholar retention.

Academic overview & research priorities

Consortium members include Kobe University, University of Hyogo, Kwansei Gakuin University, and Konan University. HKUC delivers unified credit-transfer courses in Hanshin earthquake disaster risk reduction, marine biosciences in the Seto Inland Sea, international port governance, and cross-institutional career incubation.

Research admissions & international criteria

Joint programs and consortium fellowships are open to enrolled undergraduate and graduate students at any HKUC member institution. International scholar initiatives accept students across partner universities via consortium nomination.
