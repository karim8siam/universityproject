Breda University of Applied Sciences: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Breda University of Applied Sciences (BUas), formerly NHTV Breda, is a globally oriented Dutch higher education institution situated on a state-of-the-art green campus in Breda. Renowned internationally for its Games, Media, AI, Built Environment, and Logistics departments, BUas partners closely with entertainment powerhouses like Sony, Ubisoft, and Epic Games.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Master in Game Technology (Professional Master of Science)

One-year intensive Master of Science program focusing on advanced game engine programming, procedural content generation, GPU rendering pipelines, and real-time multiplayer networking.

BUas NL Scholarship & Talent Grant for International Students

Prestige scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science and BUas for non-EEA students applying for English-taught master's or bachelor's programs.

Master of Science in Applied Artificial Intelligence & Digital Logistics

Applied Master's program equipping professionals to deploy predictive machine learning models, digital twin simulations, and autonomous vehicle tracking in global logistics chains.

BUas Innovation Hub Practice-Based Research Fellowship in XR and Virtual Production

Applied research post at BUas's XR Stage, investigating real-time in-camera visual effects (ICVFX), volumetric capture, and photorealistic virtual environments for cinema and simulation.

Master of Science in Media Innovation & Interactive Experience

One-year interdisciplinary Master of Science training strategic media innovators in generative media workflows, user experience research, and platform business models.

Master of Science in Sustainable Tourism Destination Management

International Master of Science endorsed by UN Tourism, including field research in Southeast Asia or Australia investigating eco-tourism management and carbon offsetting.

BUas Cradle Research Internship in Generative AI for Game Design

Five-month research internship at the Cradle laboratory, developing machine learning agents, automated level layout synthesis, and realistic NPC dialog systems in Unreal Engine.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A relevant bachelor's degree in computer science, software engineering, digital media, game development, or mathematics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree; professional research appointments and practice-based PhDs conducted in consortium with research university partners.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Portfolio of prior digital projects, games, or software code (e.g. GitHub link), and a motivation letter outlining career objectives.
Application fee
€0 direct application fee (Studielink processing).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcripts, CV, portfolio, motivation letter, copy of passport, and proof of English language proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is set at the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition ranges from €10,900 to €13,800 per year for bachelor's and master's degrees. Non-EU students with exceptional academic merit can apply for the NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) providing a €5,000 reduction in first-year tuition.
