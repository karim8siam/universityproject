WHU - Otto Beisheim School of Management: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1984 under the patronage of the Koblenz Chamber of Commerce and renowned philanthropist Otto Beisheim, WHU operates modern campuses in Vallendar (near Koblenz) and Düsseldorf. Consistently ranked among the top European business schools by the Financial Times, WHU is celebrated as Germany’s foremost entrepreneurial powerhouse—having educated the founders of Europe’s leading technology unicorns (such as Zalando, Delivery Hero, and HelloFresh).
