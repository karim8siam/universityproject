University of California, Berkeley (UC Berkeley)

Established in 1868 overlooking the San Francisco Bay, the University of California, Berkeley is globally recognized as the world's premier public research university. With 110 Nobel laureates, 25 Turing Award winners, and 14 Fields Medalists, Berkeley has shaped modern human civilization through the discovery of 16 chemical elements (including Berkelium and Californium), Berkeley Unix (BSD), the RISC computer architecture, CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing (Jennifer Doudna), and foundational artificial intelligence (BAIR). UC Berkeley's most prestigious competitive graduate award, awarded across all engineering and science departments to support the world's most promising incoming doctoral researchers.

The world's highest-ranked public EECS doctoral program, offering unmatched research in foundational artificial intelligence, robotics, computer systems, and microelectronics.

Two-year research-focused Master of Science program culminating in an original scientific thesis supervised by world-renowned EECS faculty.

UC Berkeley Master of Science in Civil and Environmental Engineering

World-leading engineering master's program focusing on structural mechanics, earthquake engineering, sustainable water resources, and climate adaptation.

Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research (BAIR) Lab Placements

One of the world's premier AI research hubs, bringing together over 50 faculty and 300 graduate students investigating deep reinforcement learning, computer vision, and foundation models.

UC Berkeley Undergraduate Research Apprentice Program (URAP)

Structured undergraduate research program pairing students directly with leading faculty on original scientific and engineering investigations.

UC Berkeley Graduate Student Instructor (GSI) & Teaching Fellowships

Academic teaching appointments providing graduate scholars with full tuition remissions, medical insurance, and competitive salaries while instructing Berkeley undergraduate courses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year U.S. Bachelor's degree or recognized international equivalent with exceptional academic marks (typically 3.7+ / 4.0 GPA) in relevant quantitative disciplines.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Outstanding undergraduate or Master's degree in relevant discipline with demonstrable scientific research ability, significant publications, and compelling letters of recommendation.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 22 in each subscore); requirements vary by graduate department.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page Statement of Purpose and personal history statement detailing research accomplishments, intellectual interests, and faculty alignment at Berkeley.
Application fee
USD $140 international graduate application fee submitted via the UC Berkeley Graduate Division portal (waivers available).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 3 academic letters of recommendation, resume / CV, and academic writing sample or research papers.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for doctoral researchers is 100% fully covered for the duration of academic study. All admitted PhD students in good standing receive guaranteed multi-year financial support packages comprising full tuition and fee remissions, comprehensive health insurance, and monthly living stipends (USD $40,000 to $48,000 per year) via Graduate Student Researcher (GSR) appointments, Graduate Student Instructor (GSI) appointments, or fellowships.
