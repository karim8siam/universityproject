Motorvehicle University of Emilia-Romagna: Supercar Engineering & Automotive Innovation

The Motorvehicle University of Emilia-Romagna (MUNER) is an elite Italian educational hub created by the region of Emilia-Romagna in collaboration with legendary automotive icons—Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati, Ducati, Dallara, Pagani, AlphaTauri—and top regional universities (Bologna, Ferrara, Modena and Reggio Emilia, Parma).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ER.GO Regional Automotive Excellence Scholarship & Living Stipend

Regional government scholarship supporting talented international and Italian engineering students enrolled in MUNER high-performance automotive degree curricula.

Master of Science in High Performance Car Design (M.Sc.)

Specialized two-year international Master of Science taught entirely in English focusing on vehicle dynamics, chassis design, lightweight carbon composite structures, and aerodynamics.

Ph.D. in Automotive for Intelligent Mobility & Powertrain Electrification

Doctoral research program supported by Lamborghini and Dallara investigating electric powertrain thermal management, battery BMS, and torque-vectoring architectures.

Autonomous Racing & High-Speed ADAS Sensor Fusion Project

Experimental research project deploying LiDAR, computer vision, and neural motion planning controllers onto scale and full-size prototype racecars.

Ferrari / Ducati / Lamborghini Track & Laboratory Curricular Internship

Six-month intensive thesis internship integrated into the engineering departments and racing divisions of partner Motor Valley automakers.

Motor Valley Sustainable Motorsport Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment examining hydrogen internal combustion, synthetic e-fuels, and circular carbon fibers for endurance racing.

Associate Professor in Autonomous Vehicle Dynamics & Mechatronics

Tenured associate professorship based in Modena/Bologna teaching high-performance vehicle chassis control, active suspension design, and electronic stability.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science (180 ECTS) in Mechanical, Electronic, Aerospace, Automotive, or Computer Engineering with strong GPA and technical portfolio.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral positions administered through partner universities (Unibo, Unimore, UniFe, UniParma) in automotive powertrain and autonomous driving systems.
Language proficiency
English proficiency at minimum B2 level (IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 78, or Cambridge First Certificate).
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, detailed syllabus analysis of previous math and mechanical coursework, and letter of motivation detailing automotive passion.
Application fee
€50 administrative application fee via the partner university application portal.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of exams, syllabus of all completed engineering courses, passport, and statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees are calculated according to the Italian ISEE economic evaluation index, ranging from €156 (full fee exemption for lower incomes) up to approximately €2,800 annually. International students benefit from ER.GO regional scholarships covering accommodation and living stipends.
