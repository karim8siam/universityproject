North Central University

North Central · Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States

North Central University is a private Christian university in downtown Minneapolis, Minnesota, affiliated with the Assemblies of God. Renowned for music and worship arts, pastoral leadership, business administration, and urban ministry.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

North Central operates the College of Fine Arts, College of Church Leadership, and the College of Arts and Sciences. Situated in the heart of downtown Minneapolis, the campus provides extensive urban non-profit and music recording studio partnerships.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require high school graduation or GED (minimum 2.5 GPA), statement of personal Christian faith and vocational aspirations, and pastoral or character recommendations.
