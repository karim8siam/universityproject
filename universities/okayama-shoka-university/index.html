Okayama Shoka University

Okayama Shoka University (OSU) is a distinguished private commercial university in Okayama, Western Japan, offering specialized degree programs in commercial law, accounting, regional economics, and international trade.

Academic overview & research priorities

OSU comprises the Faculty of Law, Faculty of Economics, and Faculty of Commerce. Recognized across the Chugoku and Seto Inland Sea regions, the university has an established track record in preparing graduates for the certified public tax accountant (Zeirishi) examinations, civil service careers, and East Asian cross-border commercial trade.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation and entrance examinations. For international students, requirements include EJU Japanese score >= 200 or JLPT N2, transcript evaluation, an essay on commercial economics, and an oral interview.
