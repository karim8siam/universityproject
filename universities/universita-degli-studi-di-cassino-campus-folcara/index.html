Università degli Studi di Cassino e del Lazio Meridionale (Campus Folcara): Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1979 and centered upon the modern suburban Campus Folcara in Cassino, UNICAS is a recognized center of research excellence in Southern and Central Italy. Its world-renowned robotics research teams (LAI - Laboratory of Industrial Automation and Robotics), sustainable mechanical laboratories, and Department of Economics and Law collaborate closely with major industrial leaders including Stellantis, automotive clusters, and regional legal tribunals.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
TOLC admission test / high school diploma; bachelor's degree for Laurea Magistrale entry
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Italian & English (numerous engineering and economics degrees taught fully in English)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
€16 (State regional tax only)
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
