Osaka Metropolitan University: Urban Science, Quantum Physics & Medicine Guide

Osaka Metropolitan University (OMU - 大阪公立大学), formed in 2022 by integrating Osaka City University and Osaka Prefecture University, is Japan's largest public metropolitan university, world-renowned for artificial photosynthesis, quantum physics, veterinary medicine, and urban sustainability.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

OMU President's Fellowship for International Doctoral Researchers

Highly competitive doctoral fellowship offered by OMU to top-ranking international research scholars admitted into Natural Science, Engineering, or Life Science divisions.

Master of Science in Artificial Photosynthesis & Green Chemistry

Cutting-edge Master of Science program situated at the Research Center for Artificial Photosynthesis (ReCAP), developing catalysts to produce hydrogen and solar fuels from water and CO2.

Ph.D. in Quantum Information & Solid-State Spintronics Fellowship

Doctoral research program investigating topological quantum materials, silicon-based spin qubits, and diamond NV centers for ultra-sensitive quantum sensing.

Osaka Bay Coastal Resilience & Urban Heat Island Mitigation Project

Interdisciplinary municipal science initiative utilizing high-resolution meteorological sensor networks and GIS microclimate modeling to reduce heat stress across Osaka.

Kansai Bio-Pharma & Regenerative Medicine Laboratory Practicum

Six-month clinical research internship based at OMU Hospital and Kansai pharmaceutical laboratories, screening monoclonal antibodies and testing stem cell therapies.

ReCAP Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Solar Fuels & Photocatalysis

Two-year postdoctoral position at the Research Center for Artificial Photosynthesis exploring metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) and photo-electrochemical water splitting cells.

Associate Professor in Veterinary Infectious Diseases & Zoonosis Control

Permanent faculty appointment in the Graduate School of Veterinary Science (Rinku Campus), leading surveillance research on avian influenza, tick-borne pathogens, and antimicrobial stewardship.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of school education and an accredited Bachelor's degree in a corresponding scientific or humanitarian discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or professional graduate degree with demonstrated experimental, clinical, or theoretical research output.
Language proficiency
English programs: TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0. Japanese-taught programs: JLPT N1 or N2.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive research proposal (Kenkyu-Keikaku-sho) in English or Japanese (2,000 words) endorsed by prospective faculty host.
Application fee
¥30,000 examination application fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, research plan, two letters of recommendation, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual graduate tuition is ¥535,800 (~$3,550 USD) for all students. Admission fees are ¥282,000 (reduced to ¥141,000 for Osaka residents). Full and half tuition exemption programs exist for international students, with prestigious MEXT and JSPS doctoral research fellowships available.
