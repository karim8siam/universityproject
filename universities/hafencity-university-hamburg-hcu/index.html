HafenCity University Hamburg (HCU)

Explore world-class architecture master's, urban planning degrees, coastal climate adaptation labs, and DAAD fellowships at HCU Hamburg.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Hamburg, Germany

Institutional Overview

HafenCity Universität Hamburg (HCU) is Europe's only public university dedicated exclusively to architecture, civil engineering, geodetic science, and metropolitan development. Situated in Hamburg's futuristic HafenCity district, HCU integrates engineering, design, and social sciences into a comprehensive curriculum focused on sustainable urbanization and smart cities.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Unique European focus unifying architecture, civil engineering, geomatics, and urban planning under one roof
  • Home to the CityScienceLab in cooperation with MIT Media Lab exploring data-driven metropolitan governance
  • Prime location in Europe's largest inner-city regeneration zone providing living laboratory conditions
  • Cutting-edge research in hydraulic engineering, coastal resilience, and timber-hybrid architecture
