HMKW Hochschule für Medien, Kommunikation und Wirtschaft

Media University of Applied Sciences (formerly HMKW Hochschule für Medien, Kommunikation und Wirtschaft) is a state-recognized German university specializing in media, graphic design, journalism, digital communication, and business psychology.

Academic overview & excellence

With campuses in Berlin, Cologne, and Frankfurt, Media University offers accredited bachelor's and master's degree programs combining theoretical communication science with extensive studio production, PR campaign labs, and digital game design.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a recognized general higher education entrance qualification (Abitur/IB) and successful completion of the university's entrance interview and portfolio review (for design and journalism tracks). English-taught tracks require IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 80.
