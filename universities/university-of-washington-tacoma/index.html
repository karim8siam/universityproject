University of Washington Tacoma

UW Tacoma · Tacoma, Washington, United States

The University of Washington Tacoma (UW Tacoma) is an urban-serving public university located in historic revitalized warehouse structures in downtown Tacoma, Washington. Renowned for computer science and cybersecurity at the School of Engineering & Technology, urban environmental science at the Center for Urban Waters, healthcare leadership, and social work, UW Tacoma drives South Sound economic transformation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UW Tacoma spans five schools: the School of Engineering & Technology (SET), School of Interdisciplinary Arts & Sciences, Milgard School of Business, School of Nursing & Healthcare Leadership, and the School of Social Work & Criminal Justice. The campus is home to the Center for Urban Waters on the Thea Foss Waterway.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are test-optional and evaluate academic transcripts, GPA, and personal essays. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 92+ or IELTS 7.0+).
