Institutional Overview

Carroll University, chartered in 1846 in Waukesha, Wisconsin (Milwaukee metropolitan area), is Wisconsin's pioneer four-year institution of higher learning. Renowned nationally for its premier healthcare programs—including physical therapy (DPT), occupational therapy, athletic training, and nursing—Carroll blends rigorous professional clinical preparation with an innovative Cross-Cultural Experience curriculum.
