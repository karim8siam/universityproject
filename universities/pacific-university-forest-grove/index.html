Pacific University

Pacific University · Forest Grove, Oregon, United States

Pacific University is a top-ranked private comprehensive university nestled in Forest Grove and Hillsboro, Oregon. Globally recognized for healthcare professions, the College of Optometry, clinical psychology, education, and environmental sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Pacific University features the College of Optometry (one of only 23 in North America), the Hillsboro Health Professions Campus, and the historic Marsh Hall in Forest Grove. Its clinical health clinics serve thousands of low-income patients throughout Washington County and the Pacific Northwest.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are test-optional, reviewing high school academic performance (average 3.6 GPA), essays, and extracurricular involvement. Professional programs (Optometry, Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy) require specialized centralized applications (OptomCAS, PTCAS), clinical shadowing, and prerequisite science coursework.
