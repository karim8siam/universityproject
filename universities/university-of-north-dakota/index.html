University of North Dakota

UND · Grand Forks, North Dakota, United States

The University of North Dakota (UND) is North Dakota's oldest public research university and flagship institution, founded in 1883 in Grand Forks. World-renowned for operating the premier John D. Odegard School of Aerospace Sciences—the largest and most comprehensive collegiate aviation and space studies program in the world—UND is also celebrated for energy and petroleum engineering at the Energy & Environmental Research Center (EERC), unmanned aircraft systems (UAS), and medicine.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UND comprises seven colleges: the John D. Odegard School of Aerospace Sciences, College of Engineering and Mines, School of Medicine & Health Sciences, College of Arts & Sciences, College of Nursing & Professional Disciplines, Nistler College of Business & Public Administration, and School of Law. UND leads the Northern Plains UAS Test Site.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.75) and core course prerequisites. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
