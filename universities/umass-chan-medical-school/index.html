UMass Chan Medical School

UMass Chan Medical School · Worcester, Massachusetts, United States

UMass Chan Medical School, founded in 1962 in Worcester, Massachusetts, is the Commonwealth's only public medical school and a world-renowned biomedical research institution. Anchored by the T.H. Chan School of Medicine, Morningside Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences, and Tan Chingfen Graduate School of Nursing, UMass Chan is celebrated globally as the birthplace of RNA interference—honored with the 2006 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine awarded to faculty member Dr. Craig C. Mello.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMass Chan commands over $300 million in annual sponsored research funding. It houses world-class research institutes including the RNA Therapeutics Institute (RTI), the Horae Gene Therapy Center, the Neurobiology Department, and the Program in Molecular Medicine. The school is closely affiliated with the clinical operations of UMass Memorial Health.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Medical school admissions are processed via AMCAS with secondary applications and CASPer exams. Biomedical PhD applications are submitted directly through the Morningside Graduate School with transcripts, personal statements, CVs detailing extensive laboratory research, and three letters of recommendation.
