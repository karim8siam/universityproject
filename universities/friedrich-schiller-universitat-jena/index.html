Friedrich-Schiller-Universität Jena: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Friedrich-Schiller-Universität Jena (University of Jena), founded in 1558, is a classic German research university world-renowned as the cradle of modern optical science, laser physics, and precision engineering alongside Carl Zeiss and Ernst Abbe. A member of the Coimbra Group, Uni Jena is celebrated for the Abbe School of Photonics and its Cluster of Excellence 'Balance of the Microverse'.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Abbe School of Photonics (ASP) International Doctoral Fellowships in Quantum Optics

Doctoral research positions at the world-renowned Abbe School of Photonics in Jena, investigating quantum imaging, ultra-intense laser-matter interactions, and photonic crystal fibers.

Cluster of Excellence 'Balance of the Microverse' Doctoral Fellowships

Doctoral positions at the Microverse Cluster of Excellence, studying the chemical dynamics and molecular signals that govern complex host-microbiome interactions and ecosystem balance.

Master of Science in Photonics (Abbe School of Photonics)

Elite two-year Master of Science taught entirely in English, training optical engineers in optical design, laser material processing, fiber optics, and semiconductor lithography.

PhD in Natural Product Chemical Biology & Drug Discovery

Doctoral research conducted jointly between the University of Jena and Leibniz-HKI, synthesizing and isolating new antimicrobial secondary metabolites from fungal and bacterial sources.

Master of Science in Computational and Data Science

Interdisciplinary English-language Master's degree combining high-performance computing, numerical optimization, Bayesian statistics, and machine learning for scientific data analysis.

Max Planck Institute for Biogeochemistry Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral appointment in collaboration with the University of Jena and MPI-BGC, modeling terrestrial biosphere-atmosphere carbon and water fluxes on a global scale.

Fraunhofer IOF & Abbe Center of Photonics Summer Research Internship

Eight to twelve-week summer research placement at the Fraunhofer Institute for Applied Optics and Precision Engineering (IOF) in Jena, assembling space telescope mirrors and laser radar optics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in optics, physics, chemistry, biology, or computer science with a minimum German grade of 2.5 ('gut') or international equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with above-average grades; formal supervision agreement from a University of Jena professor or admission into an international graduate research school (e.g. ASP or JSMC).
Language proficiency
English proficiency at B2/C1 level (TOEFL iBT 90+ or IELTS 6.5+) for English-taught master's and doctoral programs; German DSH-2 for German-taught degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
A 4-6 page doctoral research expository including state of the art, proposed methodology, work schedule, and literature citations.
Application fee
€0 for direct application to the University of Jena online portal.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, supervision agreement, and proof of language competence.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Friedrich-Schiller-Universität Jena is zero for all domestic and international students in all consecutive Master's and doctoral degrees. Students pay only a modest semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €290 per semester, which includes a regional railway ticket covering the entire state of Thuringia. Admitted doctoral researchers typically receive salaried TV-L E13 contracts (€42,000–€52,000/year gross) or DAAD scholarships.
