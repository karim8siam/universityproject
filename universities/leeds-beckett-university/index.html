Leeds Beckett University: Sport Science, Data Analytics & Civil Engineering Guide

Tracing its history back to the Leeds Mechanics Institute founded in 1824, Leeds Beckett University is a major British public university educating over 28,000 students across two vibrant campuses (City Campus and Headingley Campus). Globally renowned for the Carnegie School of Sport, Leeds Beckett is an international leader in athletic performance science, data analytics, civil engineering, and international business.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Leeds Beckett International Dean's Postgraduate Scholarship

Merit-based international tuition scholarship automatically awarded to high-achieving overseas graduates enrolling in full-time taught master's degree courses.

MSc Sport and Exercise Science (Carnegie School of Sport)

One-year world-renowned master's program providing advanced mastery in environmental exercise physiology, athletic biomechanics, sports nutrition, and elite performance tracking.

Carnegie School of Sport Doctoral Studentship in Elite Athlete Tracking

Three-year doctoral research studentship investigating GPS tracking telemetry, fatigue kinetics, and injury prevention in professional athletes in the £45m Carnegie building.

Leeds Beckett Centre for Applied Civil Engineering Infrastructure Project

Applied civil engineering investigation testing low-carbon geopolymer cements and smart fiber-optic strain monitoring in highway bridges and retaining walls.

Yorkshire Elite Sports Clubs & Premier League Analytics Internship

Ten-month paid industrial placement working as performance analyst alongside Premier League football clubs, Leeds Rhinos rugby league, and national governing bodies.

Carnegie Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Sport Physiology and Recovery

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment examining environmental heat acclimation strategies, hypoxia training adaptations, and nutritional recovery kinetics.

Carnegie Women in Sport Leadership and Science Scholarship

Targeted educational grant promoting female participation, leadership, and scientific innovation across elite athletic performance and international sports management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited UK 2:2 honours degree (or international equivalent with minimum 55-60% or 2.8/4.0 GPA) in sports science, engineering, computing, business, or related field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with merit or distinction (or first-class honours bachelor's degree) with an approved doctoral research proposal aligned with university research institutes.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.0 overall (with at least 5.5 in each individual skill component), Pearson PTE 54, or Cambridge C1 Advanced.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for postgraduate research (MPhil/PhD): 1,500-word research proposal articulating scientific methodology, theoretical framework, and prospective supervisor alignment.
Application fee
£0 application fee for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates and official transcripts, copy of valid passport, two academic reference letters, CV, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Leeds Beckett University is £9,250 per year for UK domestic students and approximately £16,000 to £17,500 per year for international postgraduate students. Leeds Beckett provides International Dean's Scholarships offering up to £3,000 in tuition reductions, alongside fully funded Carnegie doctoral bursaries.
