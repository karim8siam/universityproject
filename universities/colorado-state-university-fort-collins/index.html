Colorado State University

Colorado State University (CSU) is Colorado's premier public land-grant research powerhouse (Carnegie R1), world-renowned for its top-ranked College of Veterinary Medicine and Biomedical Sciences, Walter Scott Jr. College of Engineering, and Department of Atmospheric Science.

Academic overview & excellence

CSU generates over $450 million in annual research expenditures. Its sprawling Fort Collins campuses feature the Energy Institute at the Powerhouse Energy Campus, the Atmospheric Chemistry Lab at the foothills, and the Flint Animal Cancer Center.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions requires high school graduation with competitive GPA (average 3.6-3.8). Graduate admissions requires an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, transcripts, and department-specific GRE requirements. International students submit TOEFL 80 or IELTS 6.5.
