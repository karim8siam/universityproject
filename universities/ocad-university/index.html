OCAD University: Art, Design, Digital Media & Inclusive Innovation Guide

OCAD University (Ontario College of Art and Design) is Canada's oldest and largest public university dedicated to art, design, and digital media education, renowned for its Sharp Centre for Design and groundbreaking inclusive design laboratories.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA), Bachelor of Design (BDes), or related honours degree with minimum 75% average (B+) in final two years.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not currently offering independent doctoral degrees; collaborative research residency and master-level terminal MFA/MDes pathways available.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 22 in writing, 20 in others).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive portfolio of 15-20 creative works, digital projects, or design prototypes accompanied by a 1,000-word statement of intent.
Application fee
CAD $110 non-refundable graduate application fee payable via the Graduate Application Portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, digital portfolio, statement of intent, CV, and three letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic graduate tuition averages CAD $8,200 to $10,500 annually. International student graduate tuition is approximately CAD $24,500 to $28,000 annually. Graduate teaching assistantships (GTA) and OCAD U entrance scholarships offset tuition costs.
