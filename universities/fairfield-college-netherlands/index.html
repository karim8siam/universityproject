Fairfield College Netherlands

Fairfield College Netherlands is an international business and vocational higher education institute situated in the central Dutch university city of Utrecht, with an executive campus branch in The Hague. Offering accredited diploma credentials, bachelor's top-ups, and executive certificates in international commerce, supply chain management, and digital marketing, Fairfield College prepares global students for the dynamic European trade economy.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Utrecht & The Hague, Netherlands

Institutional Overview

The educational model is delivered entirely in English, featuring interactive case-study seminars, corporate consultancy projects, and enterprise simulations. Fairfield College aligns its curricula with European Qualifications Framework (EQF) standards, providing accelerated pathways into international commerce, NGO logistics, and sustainable business management.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions operate on multiple intakes (September, February, and May). Candidates submit high school diplomas or prior post-secondary transcripts, evidence of English competence (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 78), and a statement of motivation. Fairfield offers international merit bursaries covering up to €2,500 of annual tuition fees.
