ECP UNIPEGASO Vicenza (Centro Studi Universitari)

ECP UNIPEGASO Vicenza (Centro Studi Universitari di Vicenza - CSU) is an officially certified higher education hub serving students and professionals in the Veneto region. Partnered with Università Telematica Pegaso and Universitas Mercatorum, CSU Vicenza delivers ministerial-accredited degrees across economics, digital management, law, engineering, and educational sciences.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Vicenza, Veneto, Italy

Institutional Overview

CSU Vicenza provides an integrated blended model combining interactive digital university portals with on-site didactic assistance, computer laboratories, and local examination sessions in Vicenza. The curriculum connects academic theory directly with the dynamic industrial fabric of the northeast of Italy, offering dedicated modules in mechatronics, logistics, and small enterprise finance.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Matriculation is open year-round with rolling university registrations. Applicants present an Italian upper secondary diploma (Diploma di Maturità) or international certificate of equivalence. Subsidized tuition rates, conventions with public administration bodies, and corporate agreements with Veneto industrial associations guarantee competitive pricing.
