Hawaii Pacific University

HPU · Honolulu, Hawaii, United States

Hawaii Pacific University (HPU) is a dynamic private university headquartered at Aloha Tower in Honolulu, renowned for marine biology, oceanography, international business, nursing, and Pacific Rim diplomacy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

HPU leverages its unique Pacific archipelago location to offer hands-on marine science at Oceanic Institute in Waimanalo, sustainable island resource management, Asia-Pacific diplomacy, and transcultural healthcare education.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), academic course rigor, and personal statement. Graduate programs require a recognized bachelor’s degree, statement of purpose, transcripts, and program-specific prerequisites.
