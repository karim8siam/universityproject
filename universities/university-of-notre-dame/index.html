University of Notre Dame

Notre Dame · Notre Dame, Indiana, United States

The University of Notre Dame is one of the world's premier private Catholic research universities, founded in 1842 in Notre Dame, Indiana, adjacent to South Bend. A distinguished member of the Association of American Universities (AAU), Notre Dame is internationally celebrated for transformative scholarship in aerospace aerodynamics, peace studies, nanomaterials, chemical and biomolecular engineering, and architecture.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Notre Dame features eight colleges and schools: the College of Arts and Letters, College of Science, College of Engineering, Mendoza College of Business, Notre Dame Law School, Keough School of Global Affairs, School of Architecture, and the Graduate School. Flagship research centers include the Kroc Institute for International Peace Studies and the Notre Dame Turbomachinery Laboratory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are among the most competitive in the world (holistic review via Common App). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a high GPA (typically 3.5+), statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 100+ or IELTS 7.5+).
