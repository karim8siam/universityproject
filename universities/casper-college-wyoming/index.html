Institutional Overview

Casper College, established in 1945 atop Casper Mountain foothills in central Wyoming, is one of the largest and most comprehensive community colleges in the Rocky Mountain region. Offering over 140 academic degrees and certificates, Casper College is renowned for mineral and petroleum engineering technology, agriculture, nursing, theatre arts, and seamless university transfer agreements.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Center for Energy and Petroleum Technology equipped with industrial drill rigs and simulation labs
  • Partnership with University of Wyoming (UW-Casper) for direct completion of 4-year bachelor's degrees on campus
  • Nationally accredited visual arts and world-class Gertrude Krampert Theatre complex
  • Extensive Western agricultural research facilities and collegiate rodeo team
