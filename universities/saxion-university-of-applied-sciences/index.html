Saxion University of Applied Sciences: Applied Nanotechnology, Robotics & NL Scholarships

Saxion University of Applied Sciences (Hogeschool Saxion) is one of the Netherlands' premier higher education institutions for applied engineering and technology. Located in the high-tech Twente innovation corridor adjacent to the University of Twente, Saxion is celebrated for Applied Nanotechnology, circular textiles, mechatronics, and IT.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) at Saxion

Competitive scholarship financed by the Dutch Ministry of Education and Saxion for exceptional non-EEA students enrolling in English-taught master's degrees.

Master of Science in Applied Nanotechnology (High-Tech Materials & Lab-on-a-Chip)

The only Applied Nanotechnology master's in the Netherlands, collaborating with MESA+ NanoLab on microfluidic lab-on-a-chip diagnostic devices, functional nanomaterials, and biosensors.

Collaborative Applied Doctoral Candidacy in Microfluidics with University of Twente

Four-year salaried doctoral research project conducted between Saxion Applied Nanotechnology Research Group and University of Twente MESA+ Institute developing organ-on-chip platforms.

Mechatronics & Mobile Robotics Research Assistantship

Applied development role working with Dutch industrial automation consortia on unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) for precision agriculture and inspection drones.

ASML & Demcon High-Tech Systems Industrial Graduation Internship

Six-month master's graduation internship based at high-tech multinationals in Twente/Eindhoven researching sub-nanometer positioning systems or laser optics.

Sustainable & Functional Textiles Applied Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year applied research fellowship leading collaborative industrial projects on chemical cellulose recycling (SaXcell process), smart textiles, and zero-waste yarn spinning.

Master of Science in Robotics Systems Engineering

Advanced applied master's examining collaborative robots (cobots), computer vision for quality control, industrial IoT communication, and autonomous mobile manipulators.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Science in Engineering, Physics, Chemistry, Computing, or related discipline from a recognized university with strong academic performance.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct doctoral degrees in the Netherlands are conferred via research university partnerships (e.g., University of Twente); Saxion Research Groups hire doctoral researchers for joint projects.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each subscore) or TOEFL iBT 90+.
Research statement / Proposal
For master's entry: detailed motivation letter, CV, and technical portfolio; for research contracts: technical project proposal.
Application fee
€0 (applications submitted via Studielink and Saxion portal).
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree diploma, transcript of records, passport copy, CV, proof of English, and motivation statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition ranges from €9,500 to €11,500 per year. Dutch Ministry NL Scholarships provide €5,000 awards to high-achieving non-EEA applicants.
