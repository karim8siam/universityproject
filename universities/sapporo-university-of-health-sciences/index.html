Sapporo University of Health Sciences

Sapporo University of Health Sciences is a specialized medical sciences university in Sapporo dedicated to educating compassionate nursing professionals, occupational therapists, and physical therapists for Northern Japan’s regional healthcare system.

Academic overview & research priorities

Comprising the Faculty of Health Sciences with Departments of Nursing, Physical Therapy, and Occupational Therapy, the university maintains one of the highest national certification passing rates in Hokkaido. It operates advanced clinical simulation suites and partners with leading hospitals across Sapporo for clinical rotations.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation and formal examinations testing human biology, chemistry, and mathematics. International applicants require JLPT N1 or N2, academic transcripts, an admissions interview, and a health screening.
