New Mexico Highlands University

NMHU · Las Vegas, New Mexico, United States

New Mexico Highlands University (NMHU) is a public Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) nestled in the Sangre de Cristo Mountains, renowned for environmental forestry, social work, southwest cultural history, and chemistry.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NMHU combines small class sizes with intensive hands-on field research in mountain hydrology, wildfire ecology, restorative social work, and biochemical synthesis. The university hosts national forestry research consortia and accredited clinical social work programs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts (GPA >= 2.5 preferred) under test-optional guidelines. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree, personal statement, and official transcripts.
