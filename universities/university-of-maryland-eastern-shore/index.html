University of Maryland Eastern Shore

UMES · Princess Anne, Maryland, United States

The University of Maryland Eastern Shore (UMES) is a distinguished historically Black 1890 land-grant research institution located in Princess Anne on Maryland's Eastern Shore. Renowned for marine and estuarine sciences, agricultural sustainability, pharmaceutical sciences, and aviation science, UMES provides exceptional hands-on doctoral and professional education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMES offers specialized programs across five schools: Agricultural and Natural Sciences, Pharmacy and Health Professions, Education, Social Sciences and The Arts, and Business and Technology. The university houses the NOAA Living Marine Resources Cooperative Science Center (LMRCSC) and leading agricultural extension research facilities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts, GPA, and college preparatory coursework. Graduate programs require a bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three recommendation letters, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
