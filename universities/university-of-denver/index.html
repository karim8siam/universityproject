University of Denver

DU · Denver, Colorado, United States

The University of Denver (DU) is a premier private Carnegie R1 research university founded in 1864, situated on a 125-acre campus south of downtown Denver with panoramic views of the Rocky Mountains. Acclaimed for the Josef Korbel School of International Studies, Sturm College of Law, Daniels College of Business, Ritchie School of Engineering & Computer Science, and the Graduate School of Professional Psychology, DU is a global powerhouse for international relations, renewable energy engineering, and social science research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

DU encompasses ten colleges and graduate schools: the Josef Korbel School of International Studies, Sturm College of Law, Daniels College of Business, Ritchie School of Engineering and Computer Science, College of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, Graduate School of Social Work, and Morgridge College of Education. The university operates the Mount Evans Observatory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, course rigor, and personal essays with test-optional reviews. Graduate doctoral programs offer multi-year fellowships and Graduate Research Assistantships ($28,000–$36,000/year plus full tuition remission and health insurance). Application requires transcripts, statement of purpose, three recommendation letters, and English proficiency (TOEFL 88+ or IELTS 6.5+).
