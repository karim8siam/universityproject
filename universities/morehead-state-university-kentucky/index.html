Morehead State University

Morehead State · Morehead, Kentucky, United States

Morehead State University is a comprehensive public university in Morehead, Kentucky, situated in the foothills of the Daniel Boone National Forest. Renowned worldwide for its space science and small satellite engineering program.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Morehead State features the Space Science Center, the Ernst and Sara Lane Volgenau College of Education, and the Elmer R. Smith College of Business and Technology. Its 21-meter space tracking antenna is part of NASA's Deep Space Network.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.0 for standard admission; 3.0+ for Space Science honors). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.75 to 3.0 GPA and departmental recommendations.
