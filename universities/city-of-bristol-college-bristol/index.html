City of Bristol College: Aerospace Engineering, Creative Arts & Digital Tech Guide

City of Bristol College is one of the largest further and higher education colleges in the South West of England, with major centers including the Advanced Technology Centre (ATC) at Parkway and College Green, celebrated for aerospace engineering apprenticeships, computing, creative arts, and healthcare.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Foundation degrees and HND programs require 64 to 80 UCAS tariff points from A-Levels, BTEC Level 3 Extended Diplomas, or recognized international secondary awards.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Further and higher education college; university level degrees delivered in partnership with the University of Plymouth and University of the West of England (UWE Bristol).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each sub-test) for international applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Personal statement detailing career aspirations in aerospace, engineering, or creative industries.
Application fee
Free direct application for FE; UCAS application fee (£28.50) for full-time higher education foundation degrees.
Required documentation
Complete academic transcripts, secondary school certificates, passport copy, personal statement, and one academic reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for UK higher education foundation degrees is £7,500 to £9,250 per year (eligible for Student Finance England). International student tuition averages £9,800 to £13,200 annually. Advanced Technology scholarships, West of England apprenticeships, and travel bursaries are available.
