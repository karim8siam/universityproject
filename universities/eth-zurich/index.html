ETH Zurich (Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule Zürich)

Founded in 1855, ETH Zurich is one of the world's premier science and technology universities, consistently ranked among the top 10 globally. Counting 22 Nobel laureates including Albert Einstein, ETH Zurich excels in Computer Science, Robotics, Systems Engineering, Ultrafast Lasers, and Quantum Physics. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (ESOP)

Prestigious merit fellowship covering living expenses of CHF 12,000 per semester (CHF 24,000/year) plus full tuition waiver for outstanding incoming Master's candidates.

Salaried PhD Positions (Doktorierende)

Fully funded 3-to-4-year university employee contract starting at CHF 53,500/year up to CHF 84,000/year with comprehensive Swiss social security and state pension.

MSc in Computer Science (D-INFK)

Two-year master's program (120 ECTS) in Machine Intelligence, Systems & Software, Theoretical Computer Science, Information Security, and Visual Computing.

MSc in Robotics, Systems and Control

Interdisciplinary master's degree (120 ECTS) bridging mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering to build autonomous machines and robotic systems.

Robotic Systems Lab (RSL) Research Placements

Funded research positions and master thesis projects inside RSL developing dynamic quadruped robots (ANYmal), robotic locomotion, and machine learning control.

ETH Student Summer Research Fellowship (SSRF)

Fully funded two-month summer research program (July–August) in D-INFK for external students providing CHF 4,000 living stipend, housing, and round-trip travel.

Teaching Assistantships (Hilfsassistenten) Pathway

Salaried academic tutoring positions (CHF 28.00–CHF 34.50/hr) for enrolled students coaching exercise sessions, running coding labs, and grading assignments.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, or Mathematics from an internationally accredited university with top academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science degree (minimum 120 ECTS) with proven thesis research capability and written agreement from an ETH Zurich professor to serve as thesis supervisor.
Language proficiency
C1 English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (or TOEFL iBT 100) required for master's programs taught in English.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
GRE revised General Test recommended for graduates of universities outside the Bologna framework.
Application fee
CHF 150 non-refundable application fee for applicants holding international undergraduate credentials.
Application portal (eApply)
All international Master's and ESOP applications must be submitted via ETH's central eApply system by the November 30 / December 15 deadlines.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

ETH Zurich maintains exceptionally low tuition rates subsidized by the Swiss Federal Government. Tuition is CHF 730 per semester for Swiss residents, and CHF 2,190 per semester for international students who move to Switzerland for study. High-achieving master's applicants can win the ESOP Scholarship (CHF 24,000/year plus full tuition waiver), while doctoral researchers receive full salaries starting above CHF 53,500/year.

Verified faculty profiles and research leaders

Distinguished researchers and laboratory heads at ETH Zurich:

Prof. Dr. Timothy Roscoe

Full Professor of Computer Science; Systems Group; pioneer of multikernel operating systems (Barrelfish) and hardware-software co-design.

Prof. Dr. Marco Hutter

Professor of Robotic Systems; Head of Robotic Systems Lab (RSL); leader in dynamic legged locomotion, reinforcement learning, and ANYmal robotics.

Prof. Dr. Ursula Keller

Professor of Physics; inventor of the SESAM ultrafast mode-locked laser; world pioneer in attosecond science and European Inventor Award winner.
