Queen Mary University of London: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Queen Mary University of London (QMUL) is a prestigious member of the UK's Russell Group of research-intensive universities and one of the largest colleges of the federal University of London. Located across historic campuses in East London (Mile End, Whitechapel, and Charterhouse Square), QMUL is world-renowned for its Centre for Digital Music, School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, and the Barts and The London School of Medicine and Dentistry.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

EPSRC & QMUL Principal's PhD Studentships in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

Fully funded EPSRC and Principal's doctoral studentships within the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) covering computer vision, conversational AI, and trustworthy machine learning.

PhD in Digital Music & Audio Signal Processing (Centre for Digital Music)

Doctoral research within the world-famous Centre for Digital Music (C4DM), investigating generative music modeling, audio spatialization, semantic audio analysis, and acoustic intelligence.

Master of Science (MSc) in Artificial Intelligence with Industrial Experience

Comprehensive postgraduate degree covering deep learning, natural language processing, robotic manipulation, and featuring an optional 8-month paid industrial placement in London's tech cluster.

PhD in Antennas, Metamaterials & Terahertz Communications

Doctoral studentship at the Antennas & Electromagnetics Research Group, designing reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, 6G wireless communication arrays, and terahertz metamaterials.

Barts Cancer Institute Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Cancer Genomics

Postdoctoral research role at the Barts Cancer Institute in Whitechapel, investigating tumour microenvironments, clonal evolution, and computational biomarkers for personalized oncology.

Master of Laws (LLM) in Technology, Media and Telecommunications Law

Specialized postgraduate law program from the globally acclaimed Centre for Commercial Law Studies, examining algorithmic regulation, international cyber law, and intellectual property in digital tech.

QMUL Mile End Summer Undergraduate & Masters Robotics Research Internship

Eight-week summer research placement within the Centre for Advanced Robotics at Queen Mary (ARQ), building soft robotic actuators, surgical haptics, and autonomous drones.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A 2:1 or above at undergraduate level in a related subject from a recognized UK or international university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
A recognized master's degree with Merit or Distinction, or a First-Class Honours bachelor's degree; comprehensive research proposal and confirmed QMUL academic supervisor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing and reading) or TOEFL iBT 92 (minimum 21 in writing and 18 in other bands).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 1,500 to 2,000 word research proposal outlining context, research questions, methodologies, and alignment with QMUL research groups.
Application fee
£0 for postgraduate research (PhD) applications directly through the QMUL online portal.
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, CV, research proposal, two academic reference letters, statement of purpose, and English language test certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time UKRI-funded doctoral students at QMUL pay £0 tuition and receive an enhanced London living stipend of at least £21,237 per year tax-free for 3.5 to 4 years. For international students on self-funded or institutional scholarship tracks, annual tuition is £26,500–£31,500 for postgraduate research and taught MSc degrees, with international scholarships offering £3,000 to full fee remission.
