University of Oklahoma Tulsa

OU Tulsa · Tulsa, Oklahoma, United States

The University of Oklahoma Tulsa, based at the modern 60-acre J. Donald Schusterman Center in midtown Tulsa, OK, is a multidisciplinary graduate and professional campus uniting the OU Health Sciences Center and the Norman campus. OU-Tulsa houses the OU-TU School of Community Medicine, the Hudson College of Public Health, and the Gallogly College of Engineering.
