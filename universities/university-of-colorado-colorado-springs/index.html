University of Colorado Colorado Springs

UCCS · Colorado Springs, Colorado, United States

The University of Colorado Colorado Springs (UCCS), established in 1965 at the foot of Pikes Peak, is a vibrant public regional research institution within the University of Colorado system. Renowned for its close ties to the aerospace and defense sector, UCCS is celebrated for the National Cybersecurity Center, space systems engineering, the William J. Hybl Sports Medicine and Performance Center, and nursing education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UCCS comprises the College of Engineering and Applied Science, the Helen and Arthur E. Johnson Beth-El College of Nursing and Health Sciences, the College of Letters, Arts & Sciences, the College of Business, and the School of Public Affairs. It operates unique research enterprises including the Space and Cybersecurity Center and the Altitude Performance Laboratory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts through UCCS's application or the Common App (test-optional; recommended GPA 3.0+). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited bachelor's or master's degree, minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and a statement of academic purpose.
