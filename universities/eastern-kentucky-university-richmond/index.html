Eastern Kentucky University

Eastern Kentucky University (EKU) is a regional comprehensive university in Richmond, Kentucky, internationally recognized for its College of Justice, Safety, and Military Science, aviation flight training, forensic science, and nursing.

Academic overview & research priorities

EKU operates world-class fire safety and combustion testing facilities, an FAA-certified collegiate flight training fleet, accredited forensic chemistry laboratories, and rural nursing clinics. It is known nationwide as the premier institution for emergency services and Homeland Security education, preparing professionals who safeguard national infrastructure.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require a high school diploma with minimum 2.50 GPA; EKU is test-optional. Forensic science, professional aviation flight, and nursing have secondary competitive admission requirements.
