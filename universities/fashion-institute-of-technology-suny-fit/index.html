Fashion Institute of Technology (SUNY FIT)

FIT SUNY · New York, New York, United States

The Fashion Institute of Technology (FIT) is an internationally celebrated public college of art, design, business, and technology within the State University of New York (SUNY), located in Manhattan's Chelsea neighborhood. FIT is globally acclaimed for fashion design, textile surface engineering, cosmetics and fragrance marketing, and sustainable materials science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

FIT comprises the School of Art and Design, the Jay and Patty Baker School of Business and Technology, the School of Liberal Arts and Sciences, and the School of Graduate Studies. Its cutting-edge facilities include the Textile Testing Laboratory, the Natural Dye Garden, the DTech Innovation Lab, and the Museum at FIT (holding over 50,000 historic garments).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic records, personal essays, and specialized creative art/design portfolios. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, competitive professional experience, letters of recommendation, and industry portfolio reviews.
