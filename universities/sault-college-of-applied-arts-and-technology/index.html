Institution Overview & Profile

Sault College of Applied Arts and Technology, located in northern Ontario at the nexus of Lake Superior and Lake Huron, is globally acclaimed for its professional flight aviation program, forestry ecosystem management, robotics engineering technology, and Indigenous education.

Key Academic Strengths

  • Commercial flight aviation and airline transport pilot training
  • Forestry technician and natural environment conservation technology
  • Robotics and advanced automation engineering technology
  • Anishinaabe education, Indigenous student services, and cultural immersion

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Advanced Diploma

Aviation Technology - Flight

Three-year integrated flight training program qualifying graduates for the Canadian Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL) with multi-engine instrument rating.

Advanced Diploma

Natural Environment Technologist - Conservation and Management

Comprehensive field-based training in GIS spatial mapping, wildlife population management, fisheries assessment, and forest biology.

Advanced Diploma

Robotics and Automation Engineering Technology

Rigorous curriculum encompassing industrial robotics programming, PLC logic, vision systems, and automated cell integration.

Scholarship

Sault College International Academic Excellence Scholarship

Tuition discount awarded to high-achieving international applicants enrolling in diploma and degree programs at Sault College.

Funding: CAD $2,500 credited directly toward first-year tuition fees Deadline: May 1, 2027
Master's

Aviation Technology - Flight Admission and Flight Fee Grant

Enrolment in the prestigious Sault College Aviation program with subsidized multi-engine flight simulator training hours.

Funding: Ontario flight fee subsidy covering aircraft fuel and maintenance surcharge allotments Deadline: February 1, 2027 (Highly competitive equal consideration)
Fellowship

Northern Ontario Forestry & Invasive Species Research Practicum

Field-based summer research fellowship monitoring emerald ash borer and boreal forest regeneration in Algoma district woodlands.

Funding: CAD $7,500 research stipend and field logistics lodging Deadline: March 15, 2027
Grant

Robotics and Automation Capstone Project Innovation Award

Project funding for final-year engineering students fabricating automated manufacturing solutions for regional industrial enterprises.

Funding: CAD $4,000 prototype funding plus access to the Sault College Advanced Automation Lab Deadline: April 15, 2027
Internship

Natural Environment Technologist Conservation Internship

Cooperative placement with the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources or St. Marys River Fisheries Assessment Unit.

Funding: CAD $19.50/hour full-time seasonal wage for 16 weeks Deadline: March 1, 2027
Scholarship

Indigenous Student Post-Secondary Transition Scholarship

Bursary for First Nations, Métis, and Inuit learners entering full-time diploma programs, supported by the Indigenous Studies Centre.

Funding: CAD $2,000 per term (up to CAD $4,000 per year) renewable based on academic progress Deadline: August 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Civil Engineering Technology Infrastructure Practicum

Three-year diploma pathway preparing technicians for municipal water treatment, bridge inspection, and geotechnical surveying in Northern Ontario.

Funding: Eligible for Ontario Learn and Stay grant for high-priority northern occupations Deadline: June 30, 2027
