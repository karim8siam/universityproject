The University of West Alabama

University of West Alabama · Livingston, Alabama, United States

The University of West Alabama (UWA) is a historic public regional university located in Livingston, Alabama, in the heart of the fertile Black Belt region. Renowned for its Julia Tutwiler College of Education, the College of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, and the Black Belt Museum, UWA is acclaimed for environmental conservation biology, rural educator preparation, and community economic development.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UWA delivers undergraduate and graduate education through five colleges: Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Liberal Arts, Business and Technology, the Julia Tutwiler College of Education, and the Division of Online Programs. Its 600-acre campus houses the Cahaba River biodiversity research stations, the Black Belt Museum paleontology collections, and the Center for the Study of the Black Belt.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.0) and standardized test scores (test-optional available). Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75+ GPA (3.0+ on last 60 hours), statement of purpose, and two professional or academic letters of recommendation.
