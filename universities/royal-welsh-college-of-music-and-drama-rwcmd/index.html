Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Based in the historic grounds of Bute Park in Cardiff, the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama (Coleg Brenhinol Cerdd a Drama Cymru) is the National Conservatoire of Wales. It is internationally renowned for producing world-class performing artists, opera singers, orchestral musicians, theatrical designers, stage managers, and actors.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Orchestral performance and chamber music (partnership with Welsh National Opera and BBC NOW)
  • Vocal studies, opera training, and the David Seligman Opera School
  • Design for performance, set and costume design, and puppetry
  • Stage management, technical theatre, and lighting design

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Music in Performance (MMus) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year intense conservatory training in solo instruments, orchestral leadership, or vocal studies with professional placement schemes.
  • Master of Arts in Design for Performance (Graduate Degree) — Internationally acclaimed scenography program training designers for national theatre, film studios, and opera productions.
  • Bachelor of Arts (Hons) in Acting (Undergraduate Degree) — World-renowned three-year conservatory degree with London and Cardiff industry showcase performances.
