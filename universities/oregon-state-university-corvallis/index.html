Oregon State University

Oregon State · Corvallis, Oregon, United States

Oregon State University is Oregon's flagship public land-, sea-, space-, and sun-grant research university, headquartered in Corvallis, Oregon. Internationally renowned for oceanography, forestry, agricultural sciences, computer science, robotics, and civil engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OSU is one of only three universities in the US holding all four land, sea, space, and sun grant designations. It operates the Hatfield Marine Science Center in Newport, the College of Forestry's Peavy Hall (a mass-timber architectural marvel), the O.H. Hinsdale Wave Research Laboratory, and the Collaborative Innovation Complex.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission is holistic, reviewing high school core GPA (average 3.6), coursework rigor, and test-optional credentials. Graduate programs (MS, Ph.D.) require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, departmental applications, and relevant academic background.
