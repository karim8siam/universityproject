Fukuoka Women's University: Environmental Science, Food Nutrition & Global Leadership

Fukuoka Women's University (FWU / Fukuoka Joshi Daigaku), founded in 1923, is a historic public prefectural university in Fukuoka, Japan. It is celebrated for its International College of Arts and Sciences, graduate programs in Environmental Science, Human Environmental Sciences, Food and Nutritional Sciences, and global leadership.

Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at FWU

Prestigious national scholarship awarded to exceptional international women scholars pursuing master's or doctoral degrees in environmental science, nutrition, or global studies.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Environmental Biotechnology & Marine Conservation

Doctoral research analyzing microplastic degradation in Hakata Bay, mangrove carbon sequestration, and marine microbial enzymes in East Asian coastal systems.

Master of Science in Food and Nutritional Sciences & Molecular Metabolism

Advanced master's degree investigating dietary polyphenols, gut microbiota interactions, molecular mechanisms of metabolic syndrome, and clinical nutritional therapy.

Atmospheric Chemistry & Asian Dust (Kosa) Transport Laboratory Assistantship

Funded research post evaluating transboundary air pollution across the Tsushima Strait, PM2.5 chemical composition, and secondary organic aerosol formation.

Fukuoka Regional Marine Ecology & Water Quality Practicum

Three-month field practicum with Fukuoka City Environmental Bureau conducting coastal water monitoring, red tide surveillance, and tidal flat habitat assessments.

JSPS-FWU Postdoctoral Fellowship in Global Gender Studies & East Asian Public Policy

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship supported by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) examining gender labor economics, family welfare policies, and corporate governance in Japan.

Master of Arts in International Exchange & Transcultural Communication

Interdisciplinary master's curriculum training international cultural mediators, bilingual educators, and NGO project directors across the Asia-Pacific region.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education with a bachelor's degree in environmental science, nutrition, biology, humanities, or social sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or equivalent in a relevant field; applicants must submit an academic thesis and research plan endorsed by an FWU faculty advisor.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 70+, IELTS 5.5+, or Japanese JLPT N2 depending on the research laboratory's primary working language.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2–3 page research proposal outlining problem statement, methodology, and connection to FWU environmental or nutritional laboratories.
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY examination screening fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's certificate of graduation, academic transcript, letters of recommendation, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is approximately ¥535,800 JPY per year (admission fee of ¥282,000 JPY for Fukuoka residents, ¥423,000 JPY for non-residents). International students regularly receive 50% to 100% tuition exemptions based on academic merit, and MEXT scholars receive full tuition exemption plus a monthly living stipend.
