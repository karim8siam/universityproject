Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Northern State features the Harvey Jewett IV Regional Science Education Center, the Johnson Fine Arts Center, and the School of Business with its Center of Excellence in International Business. Students benefit from close faculty mentorship and cutting-edge laboratory spaces.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with an unweighted GPA of 2.6 or ACT score of 18+. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75 GPA and English proficiency demonstration.
