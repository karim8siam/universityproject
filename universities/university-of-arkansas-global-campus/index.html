Research admissions & international qualifications

Freshmen: 3.0+ high school GPA with test-optional policy. Transfer students: 2.0+ GPA with 24+ transferable collegiate credits. Graduate: Conferred bachelor's with 3.0+ GPA. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates without prior English-medium secondary or collegiate schooling.
