California College San Diego

California College San Diego (CCSD) is an established private vocational and career college offering focused degree programs in respiratory therapy, diagnostic healthcare, software development, and medical administration.

Academic overview & research priorities

CCSD delivers career-oriented clinical healthcare training and technological disciplines with intensive laboratory simulations. Clinical partnerships span Southern California regional healthcare systems, equipping graduates for immediate state and national board credentialing.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require a high school diploma, GED, or recognized equivalent. Healthcare programs require prerequisite coursework in anatomy and physiology, background checks, drug screening, and an admissions interview.
