Trinity Washington University

Trinity Washington · Washington, District of Columbia, United States

Trinity Washington University is a historic private institution founded by the Sisters of Notre Dame de Namur in Washington, D.C. Designated as both a Predominantly Black Institution (PBI) and a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI), Trinity is renowned for its historic undergraduate College of Arts and Sciences (dedicated to women's education), the School of Nursing and Health Professions, and social justice leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Trinity operates five academic units: the historic College of Arts and Sciences, the School of Nursing and Health Professions, the School of Education, the School of Business and Graduate Studies, and the School of Professional Studies. Its modern Payden Academic Center features advanced nursing simulation suites and digital science laboratories.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are accessible, holistic, and mission-centered. Undergraduate applicants are evaluated based on high school academic transcripts (minimum 2.50 GPA; test-optional). Nursing and health professions programs require clinical prerequisite science grades and interviews.
