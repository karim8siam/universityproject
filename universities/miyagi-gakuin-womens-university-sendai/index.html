Academic overview & excellence

Surrounded by the forested hills of northern Sendai ('City of Trees'), Miyagi Gakuin Women's University has championed women's academic leadership for over 135 years. Accredited by the Japan University Accreditation Association (JUAA), the university comprises the Faculty of Liberal Arts and the Faculty of Education, housing the Department of Food and Nutritional Sciences (registered dietitian qualifying institute), Department of Music (with the celebrated acoustic Music Hall), and the Institute for Christian Culture.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admission is open to female applicants holding a high school graduation certificate. International candidates apply through the Special Entrance Examination for International Students, submitting EJU examination results (Japanese as a Foreign Language), JLPT N2 or higher, an essay, and an in-person or online interview. Music majors undergo practical instrumental/vocal performance auditions.

Four-year performance degree in piano, vocal arts, orchestral strings, or pipe organ, featuring regular masterclasses, chamber music recitals, and music therapy certification.

Funding: MGWU Musical Talent Scholarship awarding up to ¥500,000 towards private studio lessons Tuition: ¥1,580,000 per academic year Deadline: February 10, 2027

Master of Arts in Human Life Sciences & Community Nutrition

Two-year graduate thesis investigating functional food phytochemicals, elderly dysphagia meal design, and post-disaster food supply resilience in the Tohoku region.

Funding: Tohoku Regional Nutrition Research Grant providing ¥400,000 annual research allowance Tuition: ¥790,000 per academic year Deadline: January 15, 2027

Merit scholarship awarded to international female scholars from Southeast Asia and East Asia committed to peace education, gender equity, and intercultural dialogue.

Funding: Full tuition waiver and free campus residence hall accommodation in Sendai Tuition: 100% exempted Deadline: June 30, 2027

Four-week clinical hospital residency evaluating inpatient enteral nutrition regimens, diabetic glycemic management, and renal diet counseling alongside hospital physicians.

Twelve-month laboratory research fellowship isolating novel lactic acid bacteria and koji molds from traditional Miyagi miso and sake breweries to develop probiotic health supplements.
