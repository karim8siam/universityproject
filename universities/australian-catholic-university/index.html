Australian Catholic University: Sports Science, Education, Nursing & RTP Scholarships

Australian Catholic University (ACU) is a national public research university with major campuses in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra, and Ballarat, plus a campus in Rome. Ranked #1 in Australia and top 20 globally for nursing and education, ACU is internationally renowned for human performance at the Mary MacKillop Institute for Health Research (MMIHR).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Australian Government RTP & ACU Research Stipend Scholarship

Prestigious doctoral scholarship providing comprehensive funding for domestic and international PhD candidates pursuing research in exercise metabolism, education, or theology.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Exercise Physiology & Skeletal Muscle Metabolism

Doctoral research conducted within the world-renowned Mary MacKillop Institute for Health Research investigating mitochondrial biogenesis, carbohydrate ingestion kinetics, and sarcopenia prevention.

Master of High Performance Sport & Athletic Load Monitoring

Internationally renowned graduate program covering GPS tracking telemetry, fatigue biomarkers, velocity-based training, and injury risk mitigation alongside AFL and NRL teams.

Institute for Positive Psychology and Education (IPPE) Research Assistantship

Applied quantitative research post analyzing self-concept development, academic motivation in disadvantaged youth, and teacher well-being across international cohorts.

St Vincent's Health & Mater Health Clinical Nursing Practicum

Clinical immersion training in tertiary intensive care, emergency departments, and cardiac surgery units at St Vincent's Hospital in Sydney and Melbourne.

Institute for Religion and Critical Inquiry (IRCI) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting advanced philosophical and historical research on ethics, classical philosophy, and religious pluralism at IRCI in Melbourne and Rome.

Master of Teaching (Secondary) with STEM Teaching Specialization

Two-year teacher education degree qualifying graduates to teach secondary school mathematics, sciences, and digital technologies across Australian education systems.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Australian bachelor's degree or overseas equivalent with a minimum credit average (65%+) or equivalent GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-class honours degree (H1) or research master's degree with a demonstrated track record of scholarly research output.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or PTE Academic 58+; Nursing and Education degrees require 7.0 (minimum 7.0 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for Higher Degree Research (PhD/MPhil): 2,000-word proposal aligned with ACU research institutes and confirmed supervisors.
Application fee
$0 (free direct application via the ACU International Online Portal).
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, academic transcripts, two confidential referee reports, CV, and verified English proficiency report.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition typically ranges from $31,500 to $39,000 AUD per year. Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) and ACU Stipend Scholarships cover 100% of tuition fees and provide a generous tax-free living stipend of $33,500 AUD/year for up to 3.5 years.
