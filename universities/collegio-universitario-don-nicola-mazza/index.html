Collegio Mazza: Merit Fellowships, University Residency & Academic Guide

Collegio Universitario Don Nicola Mazza, founded in 1954 and legally recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) as a prestigious College of Merit (Collegio Universitario di Merito), operates premier academic residential colleges in Padua, Verona, and Rome. In close partnership with the University of Padua and University of Verona, Collegio Mazza offers interdisciplinary honors training, research mentorship, international exchange, and substantial merit-based residency scholarships.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Admission is open to students enrolled in Bachelor's, Master's (Laurea Magistrale), and single-cycle degrees at partner universities who maintain an exceptional academic grade average (minimum 27/30).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral researchers (Dottorato di Ricerca) enrolled at the University of Padua or Verona are eligible for subsidized postgraduate residential research fellowships.
Language proficiency
Italian language fluency (B2/C1 for academic courses) and English proficiency (minimum B2 certified by Cambridge, IELTS, or TOEFL).
Research statement / Proposal
Applicants undergo competitive evaluation including high school/university transcripts, a motivation essay, and an interdisciplinary oral interview with faculty.
Application fee
Zero application fee; collegiate admission contest is completely free of charge.
Required documentation
University matriculation certificate, transcript of exams with grade point average, certified ISEE financial indicator (for income-based fee reductions), and academic curriculum vitae.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Collegiate fees are scaled progressively based on verified family economic standing (ISEE Universitario). Annual accommodation, full dining, and honors educational programming range from €2,000 for lower-income tiers up to €8,500 for upper income tiers. Merit winners and INPS scholarship recipients frequently study with 100% full board tuition waivers.
