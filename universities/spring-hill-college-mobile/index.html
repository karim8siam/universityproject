Spring Hill College

Spring Hill · Mobile, Alabama, United States

Spring Hill College is an accredited private Jesuit Catholic liberal arts college in Mobile, Alabama. Founded in 1830, it is the oldest Catholic college in the Southeast, the third-oldest Jesuit college in the United States, and the oldest institution of higher learning in Alabama. Nestled beneath centuries-old live oaks along the historic Avenue of the Oaks, Spring Hill is acclaimed for pre-health medicine, marine biology (in partnership with the Dauphin Island Sea Lab), nursing, and values-centered Jesuit business education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Spring Hill emphasizes the Jesuit principle of cura personalis (care for the whole person). It comprises the Division of Nursing, the Division of Business, the Division of Sciences, and the Division of Humanities. Marine biology students conduct fieldwork directly at the world-renowned Dauphin Island Sea Lab on the Gulf Coast.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), academic curriculum rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores with a holistic review of character and extracurricular involvement. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75+ GPA, official transcripts, and personal statement.
