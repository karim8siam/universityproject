Kansas State University

K-State · Manhattan, Kansas, United States

Kansas State University (K-State) is the historic flagship public land-grant Carnegie R1 doctoral research university of Kansas, founded in 1863 in Manhattan, Kansas ('The Little Apple'). Internationally renowned for agricultural sciences and veterinary medicine, grain and bakery science, biosecurity at the Biosecurity Research Institute (BRI), engineering, and architecture, K-State is situated directly adjacent to the federal National Bio and Agro-Defense Facility (NBAF), making it the global epicenter of agricultural biosafety and animal health research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

K-State comprises nine academic colleges: the College of Agriculture, College of Veterinary Medicine, Carl R. Ice College of Engineering, College of Architecture, Planning & Design (APDesign), College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business Administration, College of Health and Human Sciences, College of Education, and K-State Olathe. The university operates the Biosecurity Research Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and core preparation with test-optional reviews. Graduate doctoral programs offer competitive Graduate Teaching and Research Assistantships ($22,000–$30,000/year plus full tuition remission). Master's and doctoral applicants submit transcripts, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
