Arnhem Business School: International Business, Logistics & Green Supply Chain Guide

Established in 1983 as the international business faculty of HAN University of Applied Sciences, Arnhem Business School (ABS) is an internationally accredited Dutch business school in Gelderland. Renowned across Europe for international business management, sustainable logistics, circular supply chains, and automotive engineering systems, ABS pairs Dutch commercial innovation with cross-border European market integration.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

NL Scholarship (Holland Scholarship) at Arnhem Business School

Government-supported study award for talented non-EU/EEA international students entering full-time master's degrees in business, logistics, and engineering systems at ABS.

Master of Science in Engineering Systems (Sustainable Energy & Mobility)

Two-year English-taught master's degree specializing in electric vehicle powertrains, hydrogen fuel-cell integration, and smart charging infrastructure.

Dutch National Professional Doctorate (PD) in Clean Energy Mobility Systems

Four-year salaried Professional Doctorate (PD) program at the HAN Automotive Research Center optimizing high-capacity battery packs and hydrogen combustion for zero-emission freight transport.

Rhine-Waal European Logistics Corridor & Multimodal Transport Research

Applied scientific research project investigating carbon abatement and AI-driven synchromodal freight scheduling along the Rotterdam-Arnhem-Ruhr inland shipping corridor.

Port of Rotterdam & DAF Trucks International Supply Chain Internship

Twenty-week practical internship with major Dutch logistics operators analyzing container terminal turnaround times, warehouse automation, and predictive spare parts replenishment.

HAN Postdoctoral Lector Fellowship in Hydrogen Fuel Cell Powertrains

Two-year research appointment at the HAN Automotive Institute directing experimental test-cell research and collaborating with European truck manufacturers on hydrogen propulsion.

HAN Excellence Study Award in International Business Management

Merit award sponsored by HAN University of Applied Sciences providing direct tuition deductions to high-achieving international applicants entering business and logistics programs.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in business administration, logistics, engineering, or economics from a recognized institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Professional Doctorate (PD) in Energy and Mobility Transition conducted in collaboration with Dutch technical university consortia.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught master's degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Applied postgraduate research concept (1,500 words) defining logistics decarbonization or circular business model innovation in the Arnhem-Nijmegen economic region.
Application fee
EUR 0 for Dutch centralized Studielink registration.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, detailed transcript of records with ECTS breakdown, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and verified English proficiency certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

For EU/EEA citizens, statutory annual tuition is set by the Dutch government at approximately EUR 2,530. For non-EU/EEA international students, institutional tuition ranges from EUR 8,965 to EUR 11,500 per year. Admitted international students can apply for the prestigious NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) providing EUR 5,000 in one-time study assistance.
