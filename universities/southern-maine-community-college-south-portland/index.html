Southern Maine Community College

SMCC Maine · South Portland, Maine, United States

Southern Maine Community College (SMCC) is the largest and oldest community college in Maine, occupying a magnificent coastal campus on the shores of Casco Bay at historic Spring Point Ledge in South Portland, with a branch campus in Brunswick at the former naval air station. Acclaimed for marine science, precision composites, nursing, cybersecurity, and culinary arts, SMCC serves as a vital economic lifeline for northern New England.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SMCC features unmatched marine facilities including oceanfront docks, marine research vessels, modern welding and CNC machine tooling suites, a composites manufacturing center in Brunswick, and high-fidelity nursing simulation labs. The college offers over 40 degree and certificate programs with seamless transfer agreements to top four-year universities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

SMCC maintains an open-door admissions policy for individuals with a high school diploma or equivalent. Competitive allied health programs (Nursing, Radiography, Respiratory Therapy) require specialized secondary school math/science prerequisites, background checks, and departmental placement examinations.
