UGA Gwinnett delivers top-tier professional master's degrees, doctorates in higher education management, MBA concentrations, and executive certifications from the Terry College of Business, Mary Frances Early College of Education, and School of Social Work.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Conferred bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited institution with minimum 3.0 undergraduate GPA. Application submitted through the UGA Graduate School. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates. Fall priority admission May 1, 2027; Spring priority deadline October 15, 2026; Summer deadline April 1, 2027.
