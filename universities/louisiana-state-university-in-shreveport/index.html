Louisiana State University in Shreveport

LSU Shreveport · Shreveport, Louisiana, United States

Louisiana State University in Shreveport is a public university in Shreveport, Louisiana, and an integral member of the LSU System. LSUS is nationally recognized for its top-ranked AACSB-accredited online MBA, computer science, biological sciences, and healthcare administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

LSUS houses the College of Business, College of Arts and Sciences, and the state-of-the-art Cyber Collaboratory (an immersive AI, robotics, and cyber intelligence lab). It collaborates closely with LSU Health Shreveport on collaborative biomedical research and clinical epidemiology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.0) and standardized tests (ACT/SAT). Graduate business, computer science, and healthcare administration programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA and professional resume.
