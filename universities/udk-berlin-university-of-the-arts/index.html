Berlin University of the Arts

The Berlin University of the Arts (Universität der Künste Berlin / UdK Berlin) is Europe's largest, most historic, and most comprehensive university of the arts, tracing its lineage to 1696 across four distinguished colleges: Fine Arts, Architecture/Media/Design, Music, and Performing Arts.

Academic overview & research priorities

Centered in Berlin-Charlottenburg with dozens of studios across Berlin, UdK Berlin is one of the world's most influential creative incubators. The university comprises the College of Fine Arts (Bildende Kunst), College of Architecture, Media and Design (Design, Visual Communication, Sound Studies), College of Music, and College of Performing Arts. It also houses the Berlin Center for Advanced Studies in Arts and Sciences (BAS).

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission is world-renowned for its artistic selectivity. Candidates submit an extensive creative portfolio (Mappe), followed by multi-day practical examinations, studio workshops, and artistic interviews. Academic master's and doctoral degrees in Art Theory, Architecture, or Sound Studies require a congruent university degree, research prospectus, and German language proficiency (typically C1 for academic tracks; B2 for pure fine arts).
