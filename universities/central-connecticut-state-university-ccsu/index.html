Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1849 as Connecticut's oldest publicly funded academy for higher learning, Central Connecticut State University (CCSU) is the largest comprehensive university in the Connecticut State Colleges and Universities (CSCU) system, celebrated for its ABET-accredited engineering, biomolecular sciences, and criminal justice programs.

Institution type
Public Comprehensive University (CSCU System)
Campus location
165-Acre Suburban Campus in New Britain, Connecticut (near Hartford)
Accreditation
New England Commission of Higher Education (NECHE) / ABET / AACSB
Admissions channel
Fall (priority May 1) and Spring (priority November 1)
Instruction language
English (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL iBT 79)
Financial aid & support
Presidential Merit Scholarships, Connecticut Free College Grants, Graduate Assistantships

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Center for Biomolecular Research featuring state-of-the-art flow cytometry and confocal microscopy
  • ABET-accredited mechanical, civil, and electrical engineering linked to Pratt & Whitney aerospace corridor
  • Nationally respected Department of Criminology & Criminal Justice
  • Pioneering Institute for Technology & Business Development (ITBD)

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Biomolecular Sciences (Graduate Degree) — Two-year research-intensive master's exploring cell signaling, CRISPR gene editing, and molecular neurobiology.
  • Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering (BSME) (Undergraduate Degree) — ABET-accredited engineering program specializing in computational fluid dynamics, aerospace propulsion, and advanced materials.
  • Master of Science in Cybersecurity Operations (Graduate Degree) — Specialized NSA-designated curriculum addressing defensive cyber infrastructure, threat hunting, and industrial control security.
