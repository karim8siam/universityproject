Nevada System of Higher Education

NSHE · Reno, Nevada, United States

The Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE) governs all public higher education institutions in Nevada, encompassing two research universities (UNLV and UNR), the Desert Research Institute (DRI), the Nevada State University, and four comprehensive community colleges. Driving over $350 million in annual research, NSHE spearheads statewide initiatives in lithium battery materials, arid water hydrology, and clean energy workforce training.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Reno · Constitutional Higher Education Governance, Desert Research & STEM Pipelines. The Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE) governs all public higher education institutions in Nevada, encompassing two research universities (UNLV and UNR), the Desert Research Institute (DRI), the Nevada State University, and four comprehensive community colleges. Driving over $350 million in annual research, NSHE spearheads statewide initiatives in lithium battery materials, arid water hydrology, and clean energy workforce training.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions processed through constituent NSHE universities and colleges. High school diploma or GED with minimum GPA standards (2.5+ for community colleges; 3.0+ for research universities). TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates across NSHE institutions. Fall priority admission February 1, 2027; Spring priority admission November 1, 2026.
