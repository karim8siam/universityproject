University of Central Missouri

UCM · Warrensburg, Missouri, United States

The University of Central Missouri (UCM), founded in 1871 in Warrensburg, Missouri, is a comprehensive public university acclaimed for collegiate aviation, cybersecurity, education, and occupational safety. Owning and operating its own airport—the Max B. Swisher Skyhaven Airport—UCM trains airline pilots, flight operations specialists, and aerospace technicians.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UCM comprises the College of Health, Science and Technology, the Harmon College of Business and Professional Studies, and the College of Education. Its specialized centers include the Missouri Innovation Campus in Lee's Summit, the Skyhaven Flight Training Center, and the National Center for Aviation Safety.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission operates on rolling deadlines (test-optional; minimum GPA 2.5 recommended). Graduate applicants must possess an accredited baccalaureate degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, submit transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a statement of professional intent.
