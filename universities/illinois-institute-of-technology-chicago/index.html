Illinois Institute of Technology

Illinois Tech · Chicago, Illinois, United States

Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech) is a private, Carnegie R1 research university in Chicago, internationally acclaimed for engineering, computer science, architecture, applied mathematics, and tech entrepreneurship.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Located on an iconic Mies van der Rohe-designed campus in Chicago, Illinois Tech is known for the Kaplan Institute for Innovation and Tech Entrepreneurship, the signature Interprofessional Projects Program (IPRO), and cutting-edge research in microgrid power engineering, cybersecurity, and biomedical materials.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions evaluate academic rigor in mathematics and science, high school GPA, essays, and extracurricular accomplishments under test-optional guidelines. Graduate applications require relevant undergraduate degrees, statements of purpose, and official transcripts.
