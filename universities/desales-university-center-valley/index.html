Academic overview & research priorities

Situated on a scenic 550-acre campus in Center Valley, DeSales combines Christian humanism with cutting-edge professional clinical and STEM facilities. The university houses the Gambet Center for Business and Healthcare Education, featuring high-fidelity human patient simulation laboratories, standardized patient suites, and digital digital forensics security command centers.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate programs require high school transcripts, counselor recommendation, and personal essay. SAT/ACT scores are test-optional for most majors. Graduate healthcare programs (PA, DPT, MSN) require a completed bachelor's degree, prerequisite science GPAs (minimum 3.0-3.2), clinical observation hours, and CASPA/PTCAS submissions. International applicants must submit TOEFL (minimum 80) or IELTS (minimum 6.5).
