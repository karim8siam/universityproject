University of Maryland Global Campus

UMGC · Adelphi, Maryland, United States

The University of Maryland Global Campus (UMGC) is a premier public open-access university established in 1947, headquartered in Adelphi, Maryland, within the Washington, D.C. metropolitan region. As the online and adult learning institution of the University System of Maryland, UMGC educates over 90,000 students worldwide, specializing in cybersecurity and information technology—as an NSA/DHS designated Center of Academic Excellence—data analytics, biotechnology, business administration, and national security.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMGC comprises the School of Cybersecurity & Information Technology, School_of_Business, and the School of Arts & Sciences, delivering career-aligned degrees across 140+ global locations including military installations in Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. The university is a world leader in virtual cyber competitions and digital laboratory simulations.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school completion or previous college transfer credits with open, test-optional admissions. Graduate master's and doctoral admissions require an accredited bachelor's or master's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, professional statement, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+). Competitive military and merit scholarships are available.
