University of New Orleans

UNO · New Orleans, Louisiana, United States

The University of New Orleans is a public research university located on the south shore of Lake Pontchartrain in New Orleans, Louisiana. UNO is celebrated for naval architecture and marine engineering, hotel and tourism administration, and jazz studies.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNO operates the renowned School of Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering (one of only a handful in North America), the Pontchartrain Institute for Environmental Sciences, and the Lester E. Kabacoff School of Hotel, Restaurant and Tourism Administration.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5) and standardized test scores or test-optional requirements. Graduate engineering and science programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA and discipline-specific prerequisites.
