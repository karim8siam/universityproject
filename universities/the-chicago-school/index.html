The Chicago School

The Chicago School · Chicago, Illinois, United States

The Chicago School (formerly The Chicago School of Professional Psychology) is a leading private nonprofit university dedicated exclusively to psychology, behavioral health, and nursing. Operating campuses in Chicago, Los Angeles, Anaheim, San Diego, Washington D.C., Dallas, and online, it is widely acclaimed for its APA-accredited Clinical Psychology Psy.D. programs and the pioneering Illinois College of Osteopathic Medicine.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The Chicago School delivers over 30 degree programs organized through the Department of Clinical Psychology, the Department of Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA), the Department of Forensic Psychology, and the College of Nursing and Advanced Health Professions. Its practitioner-scholar model emphasizes integrated multicultural clinical competence and community health engagement.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are competitive and evaluated through departmental faculty committees. Applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's or master's degree (minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA), submit official transcripts, letters of recommendation, a comprehensive personal essay detailing clinical/career objectives, and complete formal admissions interviews.
