Educating over 300,000 students annually, MinnState delivers comprehensive education spanning technical trades, liberal arts, advanced engineering, and healthcare, powering Minnesota's economic engine.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Open admissions for two-year state colleges (high school diploma or GED); comprehensive holistic review for four-year state universities (minimum 2.75+ cumulative GPA or upper 50% high school class rank). TOEFL iBT 61+ for colleges, 71+ for universities (or IELTS 6.0+). Rolling admissions with priority fall deadline July 1, 2027; spring priority deadline December 1, 2026.
