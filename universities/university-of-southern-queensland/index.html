University of Southern Queensland: Space Astrophysics, Agriculture & RTP Stipends

The University of Southern Queensland (UniSQ) is a premier regional Australian research institution recognized for world-class astrophysics at Mt Kent Observatory (NASA partner), precision agriculture, composite materials, space technology, and flexible online and on-campus postgraduate education.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UniSQ International Higher Degree by Research Stipend Scholarship

Prestigious doctoral scholarship awarded competitively to exceptional international scholars pursuing PhD research in astrophysics, aerospace, or agriculture.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Space Astrophysics & Exoplanetary Science

Doctoral research conducted with Mt Kent Observatory analyzing exoplanet discoveries, stellar magnetic activity, and robotic telescope control in partnership with NASA.

Master of Science in Precision Agriculture & Autonomous Farm Systems

Two-year master's program based at the Centre for Agricultural Engineering exploring machine vision weed detection, smart irrigation sensors, and drone crop monitoring.

Centre for Future Materials (CFM) Polymer Composites Research Assistantship

Applied research post investigating sustainable bio-resins, fire-resistant structural composites, and automated fiber placement for aerospace and railway infrastructure.

Hypersonic Wind Tunnel & Spaceflight Engineering Practicum

Work-integrated practicum at the Institute for Advanced Engineering and Space Sciences conducting hypersonic wind-tunnel diagnostics and launch payload integration.

Centre for Health Research Postdoctoral Fellowship in Rural Telehealth

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship designing remote digital clinical interventions and evaluating mental healthcare access across regional Australian populations.

Master of Engineering Science in Renewable Energy & Power Systems

Engineers Australia-accredited advanced master's covering utility-scale solar generation, wind energy conversion, HVDC transmission, and grid stabilization.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed bachelor's degree (AQF Level 7) from an Australian university or recognized overseas equivalent with a minimum GPA of 4.5/7.0 (Credit average).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1) or Second Class Honours (Division A), or a research master's with a substantial thesis component.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or equivalent PTE Academic 58+ or TOEFL iBT 79+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for HDR candidates: 2–3 page research proposal outlining objectives, methodology, and identified UniSQ research supervisor.
Application fee
Free directly via the UniSQ International Application Portal.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree parchment, two academic referee reports, CV, and research statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition ranges from $28,000 to $38,500 AUD per year depending on the discipline. Research Training Program (RTP) and UniSQ Research Stipends provide full tuition fee offsets and annual living allowances of $33,500 AUD for top doctoral researchers.
