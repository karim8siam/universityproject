Nagoya Institute of Technology: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1905 as Nagoya Higher Technical School, Nagoya Institute of Technology is situated on the Gokiso Campus in Showa Ward, Nagoya. Educating 6,000 engineering students in the heart of the Chukyo Industrial Region (home to Toyota, Denso, and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries), NITech is globally renowned for ceramics research at the Ceramics Research Laboratory (CRL in Tajimi). NITech leads breakthrough scientific research in piezoelectric ceramics, functional nitrides, voice speech synthesis, and automotive materials.
