National Louis University - Florida

NLU Florida · Tampa, Florida, United States

Founded in 1886, National Louis University is a private, non-profit institution famous for founding the National Kindergarten College and the National Parent Teacher Association (PTA). NLU's Florida regional presence, situated in Tampa, features prestigious doctoral and master's degree pathways through the National College of Education (NCE) and the College of Psychology and Behavioral Sciences.
