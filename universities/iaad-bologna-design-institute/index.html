IAAD Bologna: Transportation Design, Digital Media & Interior Architecture Guide

IAAD (Istituto d'Arte Applicata e Design) is one of Europe's premier private higher education institutes for design, operating major campuses in Turin and Bologna. Situated in the heart of Italy's famous 'Motor Valley' near Ducati, Ferrari, Lamborghini, and Maserati, IAAD Bologna delivers Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) accredited Bachelor of Arts and Master's degrees in Transportation Design, Digital Communication Design, Interior Design, and Textile Design, working in direct co-design studios with Italy's most celebrated industrial brands.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree in design, architecture, engineering, or fine arts; comprehensive creative portfolio and admissions interview evaluating project methodology.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; elite design academy offering 3-year AFAM first-level academic diplomas (equivalent to Bachelor) and 1st/2nd level Master degrees.
Language proficiency
B2 English proficiency for English-taught master's programs; B2 Italian for Italian-taught bachelor's tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Creative portfolio presentation: 10-15 projects demonstrating sketching ability, CAD rendering, conceptual ideation, and problem-solving methodology.
Application fee
€150 admission evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school graduation certificate, portfolio (PDF or web link), motivation letter, curriculum vitae, copy of passport, and transcripts.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for European and international students ranges from €9,800 to €15,500 per academic year depending on the specific department and degree level. IAAD awards merit scholarships covering up to 50% of tuition in collaboration with corporate sponsors including Italdesign, Pininfarina, and Ducati.
