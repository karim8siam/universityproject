ArtEZ University of the Arts

ArtEZ University of the Arts is one of the premier higher education institutions for the arts, design, architecture, and performing arts in the Netherlands, situated across historic campuses in Arnhem, Enschede, and Zwolle. Internationally celebrated for the Arnhem Fashion Design program (alma mater of Viktor & Rolf and Iris van Herpen), the master's programs in Critical Fashion Practices, Interior Architecture, and Music Therapy, ArtEZ pioneers research into sustainable artistic production, circular fashion, and critical spatial practices.

ArtEZ NL Scholarship Programme & International Study Grants (€5,000 to €10,000 Award)

Prestigious Dutch government and university-funded scholarship providing financial assistance to talented international students from outside the EEA admitted to ArtEZ graduate programs.

ArtEZ Master of Arts (MA) in Critical Fashion Practices (English-Taught)

Two-year research-driven Master of Arts in Arnhem interrogating fashion as a cultural, political, and ecological system, exploring post-capitalist garment production and regenerative textile materials.

ArtEZ Master of Interior Architecture (Corpus / Gestus / Context)

Two-year Master's degree in Zwolle focusing on the bodily experience of space, adaptive reuse of historic buildings, and sustainable circular material systems for interior spatial design.

ArtEZ Professorship in Fashion & Technology Doctoral / Practice-Based Research Fellowship

Practice-based doctoral research investigating the integration of wearable technology, smart bio-materials, and circular digital workflows in contemporary fashion design.

ArtEZ Centre of Expertise for Creative Transitions Applied Research Associate

Applied research post investigating how creative artistic interventions can catalyze community resilience, ecological awareness, and civic dialogue in Dutch and European cities.

ArtEZ Bio-Design & Material Futures Summer Laboratory Traineeship

Eight-week hands-on biomaterial traineeship experimenting with mycelium cultivation, bacterial cellulose dyeing, and algae-based biopolymers for circular product design.

ArtEZ Senior Researcher / Lecturer in Sustainable Design Practices

Senior academic role teaching in international master's programs and directing research projects on regenerative design ethics, circular economy frameworks, and social design methodologies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits, equivalent to Dutch HBO or WO Bachelor) in fine arts, design, fashion, architecture, or related cultural disciplines; competitive artistic portfolio assessment.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in arts, design, or cultural humanities; doctoral studies are conducted through collaborative third-cycle research professorships joint with Dutch and European research universities.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 21 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
A critical artistic research statement (1,500 words) and a curated portfolio showcasing creative projects, conceptual rigor, and material exploration.
Application fee
€0 application fee for EU students; €100 non-refundable handling fee for non-EU/EEA applicants (deducted from tuition upon registration).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcript of records, digital portfolio, research proposal, curriculum vitae, and English proficiency certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students in Master's degree programs at ArtEZ is approximately €8,500 to €11,500 per year (statutory tuition of €2,601/year applies to EU/EEA citizens). Outstanding international applicants from outside the European Economic Area are eligible for the Dutch Government's NL Scholarship (€5,000 to €10,000 merit award) as well as ArtEZ institutional study bursaries.
