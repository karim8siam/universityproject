Academic overview & excellence

Founded in 1891 as the State College for Colored Students under the second Morrill Land-Grant Act, Delaware State University has evolved into a premier research institution. Comprising the College of Agriculture, Science & Technology; the College of Humanities, Education & Social Sciences; the College of Business; and the Wesley College of Health & Behavioral Sciences, DSU houses the renowned Center for Research and Education in Optical Sciences and Applications (CREOSA) and operates one of the nation's top collegiate aviation flight academies.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate freshman admission requires a minimum high school GPA of 2.0 (test-optional). Graduate degree applications require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.8 GPA. International applicants submit TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
