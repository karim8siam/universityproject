Institution Overview & Profile

The University of Groningen (Zernike Campus / Campus Groningen), named after Nobel laureate Frits Zernike in the northern Netherlands, is one of Europe's premier science, technology, and energy research complexes, housing the Faculty of Science and Engineering, the Stratingh Institute for Chemistry (home to 2016 Nobel Laureate Ben Feringa), the Zernike Institute for Advanced Materials (ZIAM), and the Energy Academy Europe.

Key Academic Strengths

  • Home to the research laboratories of Nobel Laureate in Chemistry Prof. Dr. Ben Feringa (molecular motors and nanomachines)
  • Zernike Institute for Advanced Materials (ZIAM) consistently ranked among the world's top 10 materials research institutes
  • Energy Academy Europe—the Dutch national hub for clean hydrogen, smart electrical grids, and energy transition research
  • Over 4,000 international researchers and students in advanced robotics, cognitive AI, quantum physics, and biomedical engineering

Notable Faculty & Researchers (3)

Prof. Dr. Ben L. Feringa

Nobel Laureate & Jacobus van 't Hoff Distinguished Professor · Stratingh Institute for Chemistry, Zernike Campus

Prof. Dr. Ben L. Feringa was awarded the 2016 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the design and synthesis of molecular machines. His laboratory at Zernike Campus leads global breakthroughs in light-driven rotary molecular motors, responsive smart materials, and photopharmacological cancer therapies.

Synthetic Molecular Motors Nanomachines Photopharmacology

Prof. Dr. Maria Antonietta Loi

Chair of Photophysics & Optoelectronics · Zernike Institute for Advanced Materials (ZIAM)

Prof. Dr. Maria Antonietta Loi directs the Photophysics and Optoelectronics group at ZIAM, conducting pioneering investigations into hybrid halide perovskite solar cells, single-walled carbon nanotube physics, and advanced semiconductor carrier dynamics.

Perovskite Photovoltaics Carbon Nanotube Optoelectronics Quantum Transport

Prof. Dr. Lambert Schomaker

Professor of Artificial Intelligence · Bernoulli Institute for Mathematics, Computer Science and AI

Prof. Dr. Lambert Schomaker is an international pioneer in document artificial intelligence, pattern recognition, and neural network algorithms, leading groundbreaking machine learning analyses of ancient historical manuscripts including the Dead Sea Scrolls.

Handwriting Recognition AI Machine Learning in Paleography Biomimetic Autonomous Robotics
