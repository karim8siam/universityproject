Cardiff University (Prifysgol Caerdydd)

Founded in 1883 by Royal Charter, Cardiff University (Prifysgol Caerdydd) is the only Welsh member of the prestigious Russell Group of research-intensive UK universities. Ranked among the top 150 universities globally, Cardiff is celebrated for world-leading research in brain imaging at the Cardiff University Brain Research Imaging Centre (CUBRIC), compound semiconductor engineering at the Institute for Compound Semiconductors (ICS), gravitational physics (co-discovering gravitational waves at LIGO), and the world-renowned School of Journalism, Media and Culture.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Cardiff EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Compound Semiconductor Manufacturing

Doctoral training conducted at the £75 million Institute for Compound Semiconductors (ICS) within the CSconnected cluster, developing high-speed optoelectronics, power electronics, and 5G/6G photonics.

Cardiff PhD in Cognitive Neuroscience & Neuroimaging at CUBRIC

Doctoral research conducted at the Cardiff University Brain Research Imaging Centre (CUBRIC), utilizing one of Europe's most powerful MRI scanners (Siemens 7T and Connectom 3T) to map human brain microstructure and connectivity.

Cardiff Master of Science (MSc) in Advanced Computer Science with AI

British Computer Society (BCS) accredited one-year Master of Science offering specialized modules in deep learning, automated reasoning, computer vision, and distributed high-performance cloud computing.

Cardiff School of Journalism Master of Arts (MA) in International Journalism

World-renowned international journalism program located in Cardiff's flagship Central Square building adjacent to BBC Cymru Wales, training correspondents in investigative reporting, digital multimedia, and broadcast documentary.

Cardiff PhD in Gravitational Physics & Astrophysics (LIGO Scientific Collaboration)

Doctoral research conducted within the Gravity Exploration Institute, developing numerical relativity waveforms, gravitational wave detection algorithms, and astrophysical interpretation for the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA network.

Cardiff Catalysis Institute (CCI) Summer Research Fellowship

Eight-week mentored summer research project investigating gold catalysis, CO2 utilization, and sustainable bio-derived chemical transformations at the Cardiff Catalysis Institute.

Cardiff Research Associate in Compound Semiconductor Photonics

Full-time research role at the Institute for Compound Semiconductors, fabricating photonic integrated devices, quantum well lasers, and optical transceivers for next-generation telecommunication networks.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK Upper Second Class Honours degree (2:1) or international equivalent (minimum 65-70% or 3.2/4.0 GPA) in an appropriate field; quantitative computing and engineering require solid mathematics preparation.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree with Merit or Distinction (or UK 2:1 Honours degree with demonstrable research experience) and a rigorous academic research proposal.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90; Medicine, Journalism, and Law require IELTS 7.0 (minimum 6.5 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
A formal research proposal of 1,500 to 2,500 words outlining research questions, literature context, methodology, equipment needs, and identified Cardiff academic supervisor.
Application fee
£0 application fee for postgraduate research (PhD) applications; £25 for selected taught master's degrees.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, degree award certificates, 2 academic reference letters, detailed research proposal, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time doctoral researchers awarded UKRI (EPSRC, MRC, BBSRC, ESRC, STFC) studentships or Cardiff University Doctoral Academy Scholarships receive 100% full tuition fee coverage (at home/international rates) and an annual tax-free living stipend of at least £19,237 per year (aligned with UKRI national rates) plus an annual research training support grant (RTSG) for consumables and conference travel.
