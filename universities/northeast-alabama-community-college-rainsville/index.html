Northeast Alabama Community College

NACC · Rainsville, Alabama, United States

Northeast Alabama Community College is a premier public community college in Rainsville, Alabama, on Sand Mountain. Regularly ranked among the top 10 community colleges in the United States by the Aspen Institute for student success, retention, and transfer outcomes.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NACC houses the Workforce Development Center, the Cecil B. Word Mathematics and Science Building, and the Tom Bevill Lyceum. Known for industrial systems technology, mechatronics, registered nursing, and university transfer curricula.

Research admissions & international qualifications

NACC operates an open-door admission policy for individuals holding a high school diploma or GED certificate. Specialized programs in nursing and emergency medical services require secondary selective admissions.
