Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1985 in Orlando, Florida Christian University has educated thousands of international leaders, ministers, mental health professionals, and corporate executives across the Americas. FCU operates pioneering bilingual programs integrating psychological theory with pastoral care ethics, clinical neuro-coaching, and global business leadership.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate programs require secondary school completion credentials, transcripts, and personal Christian testimony. Master's and doctoral degrees in Clinical Psychology, Pastoral Counseling, and Leadership require a recognized bachelor's or master's degree, professional curriculum vitae, and a comprehensive research proposal evaluating psychological interventions or pastoral systems.
