Transylvania University

Transylvania University · Lexington, Kentucky, United States

Transylvania University is a historic private liberal arts university located in downtown Lexington, Kentucky. Established in 1780 during the American Revolution, Transylvania is the oldest university in Kentucky and the 16th oldest in the nation, renowned for exceptional medical and law school placement rates, the Brown Science Center, and rigorous undergraduate research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Transylvania offers over 45 majors and pre-professional programs across the Divisions of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Humanities, and Social Sciences. Its distinctive academic calendar features a dedicated May term for immersive field research and travel courses, paired with high-performance instrumentation in chemistry and biology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are selective and holistic, evaluating high school academic rigor, GPA, personal essays, and extracurricular commitment (test-optional available). All admitted students are automatically evaluated for merit scholarships up to full tuition.
