Hasselt University

Transboundary University Limburg (transnationale Universiteit Limburg - tUL), founded through a unique intergovernmental treaty between the Kingdom of the Netherlands and Flanders, is an innovative binational university partnership between Hasselt University and Maastricht University.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating across Dutch and Flemish Limburg campuses, tUL specializes in cross-border European programs in biomedical sciences, biostatistics, data science, and environmental technology. Its research institutes include the Centre for Environmental Sciences (CMK) and Biomedical Research Institute (BIOMED).

Research admissions & criteria

Admissions accepts accredited Dutch VWO, Flemish ASO, or international secondary credentials. Graduate Master of Science degrees in Statistics, Data Science, and Biomedical Sciences require an accredited STEM bachelor's degree (GPA 3.0+), CV, and English proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90).
