Oral Roberts University

Oral Roberts · Tulsa, Oklahoma, United States

Oral Roberts University is a prominent private Christian charismatic university located on a landmark 300-acre campus in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Known worldwide for whole-person education, the Global Learning Center, nursing, engineering, and digital media.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

ORU features the Global Learning Center with virtual and augmented reality classrooms, the J.D. McKean Library, the Stanley White Center for Nursing and Health Sciences, and the Mabee Center. Students from over 140 nations study across business, engineering, ministry, and health sciences.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.6 recommended) with test-optional application options. International students must present TOEFL (68+), IELTS (6.0+), or equivalent. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's with a 3.0 GPA.
