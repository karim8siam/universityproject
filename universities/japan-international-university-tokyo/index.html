JAPAN INTERNATIONAL UNIVERSITY

Japan International University (JIU Tokyo) is a higher education and professional training institution in Tokyo specializing in bilingual business administration, international commerce, Japanese language pedagogy, and digital creative media for global scholars.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in metropolitan Tokyo, JIU Tokyo focuses on equipping international students and corporate personnel with practical Japanese corporate communication, cross-border commercial strategy, East Asian tourism operations, and multimedia design. Curricula incorporate intensive JLPT preparation alongside hands-on corporate internships.

Research admissions & international criteria

Applicants must have completed 12 years of formal education (high school diploma equivalent). Language prerequisites include JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 200 for Japanese-taught tracks, or TOEFL 70 / IELTS 5.5 for English-medium programs.
