Institution Overview & Profile

Skyup Academy, situated in Montebelluna (Treviso) in northern Italy's Veneto region, is an elite higher academy of digital arts specializing in 3D computer animation, visual effects (VFX), Unreal Engine game development, concept art, and digital sculpting for the international entertainment and video game industries.

Institution TypeSpecialized Academy of Digital Arts & Computer Graphics
Main CampusMontebelluna Creative Campus, Treviso, Veneto
AccreditationAutodesk Authorized Training Center / Unreal Engine Authorized Academic Partner / Chaos Group Center
Admissions RouteArtistic portfolio review and admissions interview (rolling enrollment)
Language of InstructionItalian (with bilingual English software instruction and international masterclasses)
Financial Aid & GrantsSkyup Studio Talent Scholarships & Deferred Zero-Interest Tuition Financing

Key Academic Strengths

  • Official Unreal Engine Authorized Academic Partner and Autodesk Training Center
  • Direct instructor mentorship from senior artists working on major Hollywood blockbusters and AAA games
  • State-of-the-art studio workstations equipped with high-end GPUs, render farms, and motion capture facilities
  • Over 92% alumni placement rate in international VFX houses and gaming studios within one year
