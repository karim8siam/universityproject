University of Bridgeport

UB · Bridgeport, Connecticut, United States

The University of Bridgeport (UB), founded in 1927, is a private, non-sectarian university situated on a picturesque 60-acre campus along Long Island Sound in Bridgeport, Connecticut. UB is globally celebrated as the home of the Fones School of Dental Hygiene—the world's first dental hygiene school—and offers internationally recognized degrees in engineering, biomedical sciences, chiropractic medicine, and business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UB is organized into the College of Science and Society, the College of Engineering, Business, and Education, and the College of Health Sciences. Its modern engineering and science facilities include the Bauer Hall Innovation Center, robotic vision laboratories, and the on-campus Fones Dental Hygiene Clinic serving the Greater Bridgeport community.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate applicants apply via Common App or UB's direct portal (test-optional; minimum recommended GPA 2.75). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited bachelor's or master's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and a statement of academic objectives.
