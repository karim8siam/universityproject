Oklahoma City University

OCU · Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, United States

Oklahoma City University is a prestigious private university situated in the heart of Oklahoma City. Renowned globally for the Wanda L. Bass School of Music, the School of Theatre, Meinders School of Business, Kramer School of Nursing, and OCU School of Law.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OCU features the Wanda L. Bass School of Music (an All-Steinway School), the Meinders School of Business with its full financial trading floor, the Kramer School of Nursing, and downtown Oklahoma City Law Center. Its alumni have earned numerous Tony, Grammy, and Emmy awards.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are competitive and holistic. Undergraduate applicants present high school GPA (average 3.6), honors and leadership achievements, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Auditions or portfolios are mandatory for performing arts disciplines.
