Southeastern Illinois College

SIC · Harrisburg, Illinois, United States

Southeastern Illinois College (SIC) is an accredited public community college located in Harrisburg, Illinois, nestled near the scenic Shawnee National Forest. Renowned across the nation for its perennially top-ranked Speech and Debate (Forensics) team, SIC is an educational anchor for southern Illinois, offering robust programs in biofuels, coal mining technology, nursing, welding, and university transfer degrees.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SIC's 148-acre campus features the George T. Dennis Visual and Performing Arts Center, the Ella Elizabeth Hise Museum of Regional Art, the modern Diesel and Welding Training Labs, and environmental research stations adjacent to the Shawnee National Forest. The college partners with major universities for dual-enrollment and seamless baccalaureate completion.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Southeastern Illinois College maintains an open admissions policy for secondary school graduates and high school equivalency (GED) holders. Specialized programs in nursing and mining technology require separate application reviews, entrance assessments, and clinical background verification.
