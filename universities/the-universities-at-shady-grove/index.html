The Universities at Shady Grove

USG · Rockville, Maryland, United States

The Universities at Shady Grove (USG) is an innovative partnership of nine public universities from the University System of Maryland (USM) operating on one high-tech campus in Rockville, MD. Located adjacent to the I-270 Technology Corridor and the National Institutes of Health (NIH), USG offers 80+ top undergraduate and graduate degrees from institutions like UMCP, UMBC, and UMB in biomedical sciences, cybersecurity, data science, and public health.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Upper-division transfer admissions: applicants complete their first 60 credits (Associate degree) at Montgomery College or other accredited colleges, then matriculate seamlessly into their partner USM degree program at USG. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ depending on the specific USM degree-granting partner institution. Priority fall transfer deadline March 1, 2027; Priority spring transfer deadline October 15, 2026.
