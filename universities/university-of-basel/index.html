University of Basel (Universität Basel)

Founded in 1460, the University of Basel (Universität Basel) is the oldest university in Switzerland. Associated with 10 Nobel laureates and 2 Fields Medalists (including Erasmus of Rotterdam, Daniel Bernoulli, and 2016 Chemistry Nobelist Jacques Dubochet), Basel is the academic anchor of Europe's premier life sciences pharmaceutical hub (partnered with Novartis and Roche). Through the world-famous Biozentrum and the Swiss Nanoscience Institute (SNI), Basel leads globally in molecular structural biology, spin-qubit quantum science, and distributed information systems. University of Basel Excellence Scholarships & Biozentrum Fellowships

Premier merit fellowship awarded to exceptional incoming international and Swiss graduate candidates, providing up to CHF 24,000 per year to support full-time scientific study at the University of Basel and Biozentrum.

University of Basel Doctoral Assistantships (Doktorierende)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment inside Basel departments and the Swiss Nanoscience Institute (SNI), conducting frontier investigations with world-class cleanrooms and bio-laboratories.

Basel Master of Science in Molecular Biology (Biozentrum)

World-class two-year Master of Science program (90–120 ECTS) inside the state-of-the-art Biozentrum, offering specialized training in Structural Biology, Cell Motility, Infection Biology, and Computational Biology.

Basel Master of Science in Quantum Science and Technology

Cutting-edge two-year Master's degree inside the Department of Physics, covering Spin-Qubit Quantum Computing, Quantum Sensing, Nanoscale Transport, and Spintronics.

Swiss Nanoscience Institute (SNI) Collaborative Placements

Frontier collaborative research placements inside the Swiss Nanoscience Institute (SNI) at Basel, conducting fundamental and applied investigations in quantum physics, nanomedicine, and molecular electronics.

Biozentrum Summer Research Fellowships in Life Sciences

Highly selective 8-week summer research program inside the Biozentrum, offering international students direct research collaboration with faculty mentors in molecular biology, neurobiology, and structural biochemistry.

Basel Teaching Assistantships (Hilfsassistierende)

Departmental academic employment (Hilfsassistent) for enrolled undergraduate and graduate students at Basel, providing hourly compensation for conducting weekly tutorial exercise sessions and supervising lab sections.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in relevant subject area from an internationally recognized university with above-average grades.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science degree (minimum 120 ECTS) with high academic distinction, proven research thesis capability, and agreement from a Basel professor to serve as thesis advisor.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to CEFR C1: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100 for English-taught graduate curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of purpose detailing academic milestones, research interests, and preferred Basel laboratories.
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee for holders of foreign degrees (CHF 50 for holders of Swiss degrees).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, certified copy of degree diploma, curriculum vitae in English, motivation essay, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Basel is heavily subsidized by the cantons of Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft. Tuition is standard across all faculties at CHF 720 per semester (CHF 1,440 per year) for Swiss and international students alike. Selected international graduate scholars receive Biozentrum fellowships (CHF 24,000 per year) or Swiss Nanoscience Institute awards. Doctoral candidates are salaried university employees receiving CHF 47,000 to CHF 52,000 per year.
