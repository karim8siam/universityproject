Dalarna University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Dalarna University (Högskolan Dalarna), with campuses in Falun and Borlänge, is an innovative Swedish public university world-famous for its European Solar Engineering School (ESES) founded in 1999. Dalarna is also recognized across Scandinavia for pioneering research in microdata analysis, spatial statistics, intelligent transportation, and sustainable heritage tourism.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

European Solar Engineering School (ESES) Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Solar Energy Engineering

Two-year world-renowned master's program founded in cooperation with the International Solar Energy Society (ISES), covering photovoltaic cell physics, solar thermal engineering, and district heating integration.

PhD in Microdata Analysis (Data Science, Machine Learning & Business Intelligence)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment developing novel machine learning architectures, geospatial data mining, and predictive decision-support systems for transport and logistics.

Dalarna University International Student Tuition Waiver Scholarship

Merit-based partial tuition waiver awarded directly by Dalarna University to top international applicants enrolled in on-campus master's degree programs.

Solar Energy Research Center (SERC) Photovoltaic and Thermal Systems Research Assistantship

Applied research residency at SERC testing building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV), bifacial solar module snow yield degradation, and hybrid photovoltaic-thermal (PVT) solar collectors.

Sustainable Destination Development and Nordic Tourism Field Research Practicum

Four-month summer research practicum analyzing visitor carrying capacity, heritage conservation, and low-carbon tourism mobility models around Dalarna's historic cultural landscapes.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Applied AI for Industrial Process Optimization and Energy Forecasting

Two-year postdoctoral position deploying recurrent neural networks and transformer models to predict industrial district heating loads and optimize metallurgical furnace schedules.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Business Intelligence and Big Data Analytics

Comprehensive one-year or two-year graduate curriculum in predictive modeling, data warehousing, neural network classifiers, and strategic decision-making architectures.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A completed bachelor's degree (equivalent to a Swedish Kandidatexamen) in a relevant engineering, science, or business analytics field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Second-cycle degree (Master's level) with at least 240 ECTS credits (including 60 ECTS at advanced level) and a demonstrated research thesis.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B from Swedish upper secondary school, or IELTS Academic 6.5 (no section below 5.5), or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A concise 3-4 page research proposal outlining research methodology, relevant quantitative datasets, literature framework, and scientific objectives.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee administered via University Admissions Sweden (antagning.se).
Required documentation
Officially certified bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, syllabus copies where required, copy of passport, and CV/motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA students at Dalarna University ranges from SEK 135,000 to SEK 150,000 per academic year (EU/EEA citizens study completely tuition-free). International applicants are eligible for Swedish Institute (SI) Global Professionals Scholarships (covering 100% tuition plus SEK 12,000/month living stipend) or Dalarna University Tuition Fee Scholarships (providing 50% tuition fee reductions). Doctoral candidates are fully employed on salaried Swedish academic contracts (SEK 32,500 to 36,000 per month).
