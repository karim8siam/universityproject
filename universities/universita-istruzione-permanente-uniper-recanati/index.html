Università d'Istruzione Permanente Uniper Recanati

The Università d'Istruzione Permanente (Uniper) in Giacomo Leopardi's historic hometown of Recanati is an esteemed Italian cultural institution delivering advanced academic courses, literary philology seminars, and regional art history research across the Marche.

Academic overview & excellence

Founded under the auspices of the Municipality of Recanati, Uniper delivers lifelong higher education, academic humanities research, and intergenerational cultural preservation. Located in the Centro Culturale Città di Recanati, the institute conducts philological analysis of 19th-century Italian poetry, Mediterranean archaeology, Renaissance painting in the Marche (Lorenzo Lotto, Raphael), and environmental preservation of the central Apennines.

Research admissions & criteria

Uniper welcomes adult scholars, university students, and international cultural researchers. Formal admission to university certificate modules requires secondary education completion (Diploma di Maturità) or equivalent foreign qualification. Literary and historical seminar fellowships are awarded based on curriculum vitae, academic writing samples, and research alignment with Leopardian or Marche regional studies.

Bachelor's

Diploma Accademico in Filologia Leopardiana e Letteratura Italiana dell'Ottocento

Two-year academic diploma exploring the poetic syntax of the 'Canti', philosophical nihilism in the 'Operette morali', and historical linguistics of early 19th-century Italian literature.

Funding: Regione Marche cultural heritage student subsidies covering up to 60% of registration fees Tuition: €850 per academic year Deadline: October 15, 2027
Fellowship

Borsa di Ricerca 'Giacomo Leopardi' per Giovani Studiosi di Lettere e Filosofia

Six-month research fellowship for doctoral candidates examining unedited correspondence, manuscripts, and philosophical fragments from the Leopardi family archive.

Funding: €8,000 research stipend and archive residency at the Centro Nazionale di Studi Leopardiani Tuition: Waived Deadline: May 30, 2027
Bachelor's

Corso Biennale in Storia dell'Arte Rinascimentale e Conservazione dei Beni Culturali

Two-year curriculum examining Lorenzo Lotto's masterpieces in Recanati, Renaissance fresco conservation, iconological cataloging, and museum curation in central Italy.

Funding: Municipal scholarship covering full course costs for local residents under 30 Tuition: €950 per academic year Deadline: September 30, 2027
Internship

Tirocinio di Catalogazione Archivistica e Digitalizzazione Museale

Four-month practical training internship in high-resolution archival digitization, Dublin Core metadata tagging, and optical character recognition (OCR) of historical civic documents.

Funding: Monthly expense allowance of €500 provided by the Museo Civico Villa Colloredo Mels Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 30, 2027
Scholarship

Premio per la Ricerca Storico-Territoriale nelle Marche

Annual scholarly competition awarding unpublished historical monographs on communal governance, monastic scriptoria, or agrarian revolts in the Macerata territory.

Funding: €2,000 publication grant and monograph print subvention Tuition: Covered by prize Deadline: July 15, 2027
Master's

Master Breve in Archeologia Medievale e Paesaggi Fortificati dell'Adriatico

One-year specialized course investigating feudal castles, maritime coastal watchtowers, stratigraphy analysis, and GIS landscape archaeology across the Marche seaboard.

Funding: Fieldwork bursary of €1,200 for topographic archaeological surveys Tuition: €1,200 total program tuition Deadline: November 15, 2027
Course

Corso Monografico di Paleografia Latina e Decifrazione Diplomatica

Ten-week hands-on paleography workshop transcribing Carolingian minuscule, Gothic cursives, and Renaissance notarial instruments from central Italian archives.

Funding: Subsidized through Marche regional cultural education funds Tuition: €450 total course tuition Deadline: September 15, 2027
