James Cook University: Marine Science, Tropical Ecology & Coral Reef Guide

Established in 1970, James Cook University (JCU) is Australia's leading tropical research institution with main campuses in Townsville and Cairns. Ranked number one globally for marine biology and coral reef science, JCU delivers world-class graduate education in marine ecology, tropical disease biology, environmental science, and sustainable engineering.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

JCU Postgraduate Research Scholarship (JCUPRS) in Tropical Marine Ecology

Prestigious doctoral scholarship funding international and domestic PhD researchers investigating coral reef conservation, ocean acidification, and tropical marine biodiversity.

Master of Marine Biology and Ecology

Two-year premier postgraduate program offering intensive field coursework at JCU's Orpheus Island Research Station and the Great Barrier Reef World Heritage Area.

JCU Doctoral Research Fellowship in Coral Reef Adaptation & Genomics

Three-and-a-half year PhD candidate position at the ARC Centre of Excellence for Coral Reef Studies deciphering molecular adaptation of scleractinian corals to rising sea temperatures.

Great Barrier Reef Coastal Hydrodynamics & Mangrove Carbon Research

Targeted coastal research investigation deploying acoustic doppler current profilers and carbon flux sensors to quantify blue carbon sequestration in tropical mangrove estuaries.

AIMS-JCU Marine Science Industry & Field Internship

Twelve-week research placement at the Australian Institute of Marine Science (AIMS) National Sea Simulator (SeaSim) working on controlled coral breeding and larval restoration.

JCU Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Tropical Infectious Diseases

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Australian Institute of Tropical Health and Medicine (AITHM) developing peptide vaccines against parasitic helminths and arboviruses.

JCU Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship

Competitive merit scholarship granting ongoing 25% tuition fee waivers to international students enrolling in engineering, computer science, and renewable energy degrees at JCU.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed recognized Bachelor's degree (AQF Level 7) in science, engineering, or related discipline with a minimum credit average (65%+) or equivalent GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor degree with First Class Honours (Class I) or research Master's degree with demonstrated substantial research output and published thesis.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each subtest), TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 21 in writing), or Pearson PTE Academic 58+.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2,000-word doctoral research proposal developed in consultation with an approved JCU tropical science or engineering principal supervisor.
Application fee
$0 AUD for online direct postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts and degree awards, research curriculum vitae, two academic supervisor referee reports, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic research candidates receive Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) fee offsets. International student tuition is approximately $38,500 AUD to $44,000 AUD per year for laboratory sciences. Competitive JCU Postgraduate Research Scholarships (JCUPRS) provide $32,000 AUD/year tax-free stipends and full tuition fee waivers.
