University of Northern British Columbia (UNBC)

The University of Northern British Columbia is Canada's premier green university, internationally recognized for its frontier research in environmental science, indigenous community health, natural resources management, and cold-climate sustainable engineering.

Academic overview & excellence

UNBC offers intensive interdisciplinary undergraduate and graduate curricula designed in direct partnership with northern and First Nations communities. Its signature Prince George campus serves as an active living laboratory for bioenergy, forestry ecology, and northern health sciences.

Research admissions & criteria

Postgraduate candidates require a recognized bachelor's degree with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 (B) or higher on a 4.33 scale, alongside proof of English proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90). Graduate research applicants must establish departmental faculty supervisor support prior to formal admission.
