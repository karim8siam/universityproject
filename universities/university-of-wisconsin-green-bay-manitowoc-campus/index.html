University of Wisconsin-Green Bay Manitowoc Campus

UW-Green Bay Manitowoc · Manitowoc, Wisconsin, United States

The University of Wisconsin-Green Bay Manitowoc Campus is a picturesque shoreline campus situated on the western bank of Lake Michigan. Offering foundational associate degrees, bachelor's degree-completion programs, and freshwater environmental research opportunities, UW-Green Bay Manitowoc provides personalized faculty mentorship and maritime-linked academic pathways within the prestigious University of Wisconsin System.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or equivalent secondary completion credential. Holistic admissions reviewing secondary GPA, curriculum rigor, and extracurricular involvement. TOEFL iBT 71+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international students whose secondary education was not delivered in English. Rolling admissions; priority fall admission deadline June 1, 2027; spring priority deadline December 1, 2026.
