Tohoku Seikatsu Bunka University

Tohoku Seikatsu Bunka University in Sendai is a distinguished specialized private institution dedicated to clinical dietetics, nutritional sciences, visual arts, and living space design, established by the Mishima Gakuen educational foundation.

Academic overview & research priorities

Perched in the Nijinooka district of Izumi Ward, Sendai, TSBU operates two primary faculties: the Faculty of Home Economics (featuring the Department of Food and Nutrition and the Department of Living Culture Design) and the Faculty of Fine Arts (Department of Fine Arts). TSBU is acclaimed for its high pass rates on the Japanese National Registered Dietitian examination and its active contributions to Tohoku's regional culinary industries, contemporary art exhibitions, and community craft design.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation, submission of academic transcripts, a statement of purpose, and Japanese language ability (JLPT N2 or equivalent). Applicants to the Faculty of Fine Arts must submit a comprehensive portfolio of studio works (paintings, digital media, or craft objects) and undertake an on-site practical drawing evaluation.
