Sapporo Medical University

Sapporo Medical University (SapMed) is a prestigious public prefectural medical university in Sapporo, Hokkaido, globally recognized for its pioneering clinical trials in neural regeneration using autologous mesenchymal stem cells, cancer immunotherapy, and advanced orthopedic rehabilitation.

Academic overview & research priorities

SapMed consists of the School of Medicine, School of Health Sciences, and Advanced Research Centers. Its researchers achieved world-first regenerative therapies for human spinal cord injury and ischemic stroke using intravenous autologous mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs). The university conducts extensive clinical translational research in peptide cancer vaccines and cardiovascular genetics.

Research admissions & international criteria

Medical school admission is exceptionally competitive via the National Common Test and specialized university examinations. The Graduate School of Medicine accepts domestic and international Ph.D. candidates with medical degrees (MD/MBBS) or master degrees in life sciences, evaluated via research proposal and oral examination.
