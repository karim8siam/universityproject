National Louis University

National Louis · Tampa, Florida, United States

National Louis University is a private university founded in 1886, with an active regional campus in Tampa, Florida. Widely celebrated for teacher education, clinical mental health counseling, business leadership, and culinary hospitality.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

National Louis operates the National College of Education, Kendall College of Culinary Arts and Hospitality Management, and the College of Professional Studies and Advancement. Programs emphasize urban educational access, blended learning, and clinical social work.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA or transfer credit under a supportive holistic framework. Graduate education and counseling programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.75 GPA, statement of professional goals, and two recommendations.
