Texas A&M University Central Texas

TAMU Central Texas · Killeen, Texas, United States

Texas A&M University Central Texas (A&M-Central Texas) is an upper-level military-friendly public university located in Killeen, Texas. Established to provide accessible junior-, senior-, and graduate-level education, the university serves a diverse student body across Central Texas and the adjacent Fort Cavazos military installation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Operating through the College of Business Administration, the College of Education and Human Development, and the College of Arts and Sciences, A&M-Central Texas offers specialized programs in Computer Information Systems, Cybersecurity, Aviation Science, and Clinical Mental Health Counseling. It houses the Center for Applied Research and Technology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

As an upper-level institution, undergraduate admission requires completion of at least 30 transferable semester credit hours with a minimum 2.0 cumulative GPA. Graduate admissions require an earned bachelor's degree with a 2.50+ overall GPA or 3.0+ on the last 60 hours, alongside departmental review.
