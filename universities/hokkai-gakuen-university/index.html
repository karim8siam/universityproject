Hokkai-Gakuen University: Civil Engineering, Cold-Region Economics & Law Guide

Established in 1885 in Sapporo, Hokkai-Gakuen University is the oldest and largest comprehensive private university on Japan's northern island of Hokkaido. Operating two modern campuses (Toyohira and Yamahana), HGU is internationally celebrated for cold-climate civil engineering, environmental geotechnical science, northern regional economics, and legal administration.

Hokkai-Gakuen International Student Special Tuition Exemption Award

Merit-based institutional award granting tuition fee reductions to qualified overseas candidates pursuing graduate studies in engineering, economics, and humanities.

Master of Engineering in Cold-Region Infrastructure and Geotechnics

Two-year master's program providing specialized engineering training in seismic-resistant structural design, snow disaster prevention, and durable cold-weather concrete.

Doctor of Philosophy in Cold-Climate Environmental Engineering

Three-year doctoral program investigating thermodynamic phase-change mechanics in frozen soils, ice accretion on transmission towers, and cold-region renewable energy.

Hokkaido Cold-Region Development & Regional Economics Research Grant

Funded research project assessing regional economic rejuvenation, geothermal district heating systems, and northern logistics corridors across Hokkaido.

Sapporo Urban Transportation and Snow Infrastructure Practicum

Three-month internship collaborating with Sapporo City transportation engineering bureaus and construction contractors monitoring winter road heating and snow removal telemetry.

Hokkai-Gakuen Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Environmental Geotechnics

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting experimental testing on soil liquefaction under freeze-thaw cycles and microbial-induced calcite precipitation for slope stabilization.

Hokkai Educational Foundation Northern Pioneer Scholarship

Foundation scholarship established to support international scholars who demonstrate exemplary academic performance, community involvement, and dedication to northern studies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (16 years of formal education) in engineering, economics, law, or humanities with a minimum GPA of 2.8 on a 4.0 scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Graduate School of Engineering and Graduate School of Law doctoral courses; requiring a Master's degree (Shushi), thesis submission, and academic defense.
Language proficiency
Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) N2 or N1 for Japanese-taught tracks; TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0 for international engineering research programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate applicants: 2,000-character Japanese or 1,200-word English research proposal (Kenkyu Keikakusho) outlining clear methodology.
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY standard Japanese graduate entrance examination fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate and academic transcripts, JLPT/TOEFL scorecards, research plan, two letters of recommendation, and personal statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Hokkai-Gakuen University is approximately ¥850,000 to ¥1,150,000 JPY per year (plus a one-time admission fee of ¥200,000 JPY). International students are eligible for a 30% to 50% tuition reduction scholarship based on academic review, in addition to JASSO monthly honors scholarships.
