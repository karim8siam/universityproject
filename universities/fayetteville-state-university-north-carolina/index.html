Fayetteville State University

Fayetteville State University (FSU) is a historic public historically Black university (HBCU) in Fayetteville, NC, constituent campus of the University of North Carolina System, acclaimed for cybersecurity, defense analytics, business, forensic science, and nursing.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1867 by seven African American civic leaders, FSU is North Carolina's second-oldest public institution. Situated adjacent to Fort Liberty (one of the world's largest military installations), FSU delivers cutting-edge programs through the Broadwell College of Business and Economics, the College of Health, Science, and Technology, and the College of Humanities and Social Sciences. FSU is designated an NSA Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts and GPA (minimum 2.5; NC Promise tuition eligible). FSU is test-optional for ACT/SAT. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 2.75-3.0, academic transcripts, statement of objectives, and letters of recommendation. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
