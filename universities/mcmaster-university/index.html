McMaster University (Hamilton, Ontario)

Founded in 1887 in Hamilton, Ontario, McMaster University is consistently ranked among Canada's top 4 and the world's top 80 research universities. Renowned as the birthplace of evidence-based medicine and problem-based learning (PBL), McMaster is home to the McMaster Nuclear Reactor (supplying a significant portion of the world's medical isotopes), the Brockhouse Institute for Materials Research, and leading artificial intelligence initiatives. Verified degree programs and opportunities

McMaster International Excellence Graduate Awards & Provost's Scholarships

McMaster's premier international doctoral funding scheme, awarding guaranteed living stipends and tuition waivers to high-achieving international researchers.

McMaster PhD in Computer Science & Software Engineering

Four-year research-intensive doctoral program exploring artificial intelligence, algorithm design, formal methods, safety-critical software, and bioinformatics.

McMaster Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (Thesis)

Two-year thesis-based Master of Science degree providing foundational graduate research training and original software or algorithmic investigations under faculty supervision.

McMaster Master of Applied Science (MASc) in Biomedical Engineering

World-class interdisciplinary graduate training at the School of Biomedical Engineering, bridging microfluidics, biomaterials, medical imaging, and smart health devices.

Brockhouse Institute for Materials Research (BIMR) Research Placements

Interdisciplinary research placements inside Canada's premier materials research facility, collaborating on electron microscopy, superconducting materials, and nuclear engineering.

McMaster Engineering Co-op & Undergraduate Student Research Awards (USRA)

Paid research and industrial internships connecting student engineers with leading research laboratories and high-technology ventures across southern Ontario's innovation corridor.

McMaster Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) & Research Fellowships

Unionized teaching assistantships providing graduate scholars with university instructional experience, laboratory demonstration duties, and competitive hourly wages.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
4-year Honours Bachelor's degree from an accredited university with minimum B+ average (equivalent to 77-79% or 3.3/4.0 GPA) in relevant academic discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in relevant discipline with minimum B+ average (typically 80%+ / A- in major coursework) and demonstrable research thesis competency.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 88 overall (minimum 20 in each section); higher for CS/ECE (IELTS 7.0).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of research intent describing research questions, academic background, and prospective McMaster faculty supervisor alignment.
Application fee
CAD $110 non-refundable graduate application fee submitted via the McMaster online portal.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, 2 confidential academic reference letters, curriculum vitae, and statement of research interests.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for research-stream graduate degrees is supported by departmental funding packages and scholarships. For doctoral students in good standing, McMaster provides guaranteed minimum base funding of CAD $25,000 to $29,000 per year (comprising teaching assistantships, research stipends, and departmental scholarships) plus international tuition fee differentials.
