Academic overview & excellence

Located in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains just west of Denver, Colorado Christian University delivers nationally ranked degree programs through its College of Undergraduate Studies and College of Adult and Graduate Studies. Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), CCU houses the prestigious Centennial Institute, a national public policy think tank that convenes the Western Conservative Summit. Key academic areas include nursing (CCNE-accredited), industrial psychology, and cybersecurity.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate applicants submit high school transcripts, SAT/ACT scores (optional), a personal spiritual testimony, and an academic essay. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.5 to 3.0 GPA depending on the program. International applicants apply with official credential evaluations, proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5), and financial affidavit for F-1 visa issuance.
