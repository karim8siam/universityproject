Research admissions & international qualifications

Conferred master's degree from an accredited institution with 3.0+ GPA for doctoral entry; conferred bachelor's degree for graduate master's programs. Executive hybrid residency options available. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates. Bi-monthly executive graduate terms: Fall I August 15, 2027; Fall II October 15, 2026; Spring I January 5, 2027.
