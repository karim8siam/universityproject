MAST University - Bari

MAST (Music, Art, Sound & Technology) Music Academy in Bari is an accredited Italian higher education institute dedicated to contemporary music performance, audio production, electronic music composition, and music business management.

Academic overview & excellence

MAST Bari delivers European-accredited Bachelor's (Diploma Accademico di I Livello) and Master's pathways in contemporary pop/rock performance, music production, sound engineering, and film scoring. The campus features commercial recording facilities, Dolby Atmos mixing theatres, and live rehearsal stages.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires an accredited high school diploma (Maturità) and passing an artistic audition or portfolio review. Master's candidates submit recordings of original works and attend an interview. English and Italian language competency is supported.
