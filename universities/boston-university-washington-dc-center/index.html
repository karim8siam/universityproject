Academic overview & excellence

Located in the Dupont Circle historic district of Washington, D.C., Boston University's Washington Center combines rigorous academic coursework with high-level professional internships across Capitol Hill, federal regulatory agencies, global embassies, and international NGOs. The center houses modern seminar suites, residential student suites, a broadcast media studio, and policy briefing rooms. Scholars pursue specialized curricula in American Politics, Public Policy, International Relations, Law & Media, and Global Health Governance.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to the BU Washington DC Program requires formal application through Boston University Study Abroad. Candidates must be enrolled undergraduate or graduate students in good academic standing with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0/4.0. Applicants submit an academic statement of purpose detailing policy or journalistic objectives, a professional resume tailored for federal or non-profit placement, official university transcripts, and two academic references. International students must possess valid F-1 visa status and English fluency.

Internship

Boston University Washington DC Congressional Internship Practicum

Full-semester immersion placing students directly in congressional offices on Capitol Hill, committee staffs, or federal agencies for four days per week, accompanied by evening seminars in legislative drafting.

Funding: Full academic credit (16 credits) plus housing subsidy awards up to $3,000 Tuition: $32,850 per semester (standard BU tuition) Deadline: March 15, 2027
Master's

Master of Arts in Global Policy and International Affairs (DC Semester)

Two-year graduate program through the Frederick S. Pardee School of Global Studies, integrating a semester residency in Washington, D.C. focused on multilateral diplomacy, international security, and trade negotiations.

Funding: Pardee School Graduate Merit Scholarships covering up to 50% of tuition Tuition: $65,700 full academic year Deadline: January 15, 2027
Fellowship

Albert & Jessie Danielsen Fellowship in Public Policy & Ethics

Post-baccalaureate residential fellowship supporting research on bioethics in federal healthcare policymaking, religious pluralism in public debate, and institutional integrity in democratic governance.

Funding: $24,000 annual research stipend plus Dupont Circle housing allowance Tuition: $0 Deadline: April 1, 2027
Internship

BU Washington Journalism & Investigative Reporting Semester

Comprehensive media internship placing journalism scholars in major Washington bureaus (NPR, CNN, Roll Call, The Washington Post) reporting on congressional hearings, White House press conferences, and the Supreme Court.

Funding: $2,500 travel and reporting grant for student investigative assignments Tuition: $32,850 semester tuition Deadline: March 1, 2027
Scholarship

Washington Academic Center Dean's Leadership Scholarship

Merit-based award recognizing students with exemplary civic leadership, high academic standing, and demonstrated dedication to public service careers in the federal or international sector.

Funding: $6,000 tuition grant awarded to outstanding participants in the DC program Tuition: $0 Deadline: May 1, 2027
Internship

Global Health Policy & Multilateral Organizations Practicum

Semester-long public health policy placement embedding scholars with the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), USAID health programs, or global healthcare think tanks in Washington.

Funding: $1,500 monthly living stipend for 16-week placement Tuition: Covered through semester tuition registration Deadline: April 15, 2027
Course

Executive Certificate in Federal Regulatory Affairs & Lobbying Compliance

Eight-week executive seminar instructing government relations professionals in the Administrative Procedure Act, Congressional lobbying disclosure requirements, and federal agency rulemaking.

Funding: Corporate and institutional professional development eligible Tuition: $3,600 Deadline: August 1, 2027
