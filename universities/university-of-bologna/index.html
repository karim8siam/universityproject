University of Bologna (Alma Mater Studiorum - Università di Bologna)

Founded in 1088, the Alma Mater Studiorum - Università di Bologna is universally recognized as the oldest continuously operating university in the Western world. Today, Unibo is Italy's leading multidisciplinary research university, hosting the European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC Leonardo supercomputer) at the Bologna Technopole, internationally prestigious faculties of engineering, sciences, agriculture, and humanities, and expansive doctoral programs funded through Italian PNRR and EU Horizon Europe initiatives.

Unibo International PhD Scholarships in Data Science and Computation

Doctoral research program joint with the EuroHPC Leonardo supercomputing center and CINECA, focusing on scalable deep learning, quantum computing algorithms, and exascale simulation.

Unibo Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) in Artificial Intelligence (English-Taught)

Premier two-year international Master of Science in AI taught entirely in English, covering symbolic AI, neural networks, computer vision, natural language processing, and autonomous robotics.

Unibo PhD in Automotive Engineering for Intelligent Mobility (MUNER Consortium)

Doctoral research conducted within the MUNER consortium linking Bologna with Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati, and Ducati, developing electrified propulsion and connected autonomous vehicles.

Unibo PhD in Agricultural, Environmental and Food Science and Technology (STAAA)

Three-year research doctorate investigating sustainable agricultural practices, agroecological management, climate adaptation in Mediterranean cropping systems, and food safety.

Alma AI (Bologna Institute for Artificial Intelligence) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Postdoctoral research fellowship at Alma AI, investigating neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence, ethical machine learning constraints, and real-time decision optimization.

Bologna European Molecular Biology & Medical Genetics Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer research placement at the Department of Medical and Surgical Sciences (DIMES) in Bologna, investigating cancer genetics, non-coding RNA, and targeted therapies.

Unibo Assistant Professor (RTD-A) in Sustainable Chemical Engineering & Catalysis

Three-year fixed-term Assistant Professorship (Ricercatore a Tempo Determinato Tipologia A) developing electrocatalytic CO2 reduction systems and green industrial chemical synthesis.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years, equivalent to Italian Laurea) with strong academic standing (minimum 70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA); subject-specific curricular prerequisites in mathematics, computing, or humanities.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized second-cycle Master's degree (Italian Laurea Magistrale or equivalent) with high honors; passing score in the national competitive public doctoral entrance exam (valutazione titoli e colloquio).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 85; B2 English proficiency certificate accepted for several English-taught Laurea Magistrale programs.
Research statement / Proposal
A formal doctoral research project (progetto di ricerca) of 3,000 to 5,000 words detailing background literature, objectives, methodology, work packages, and bibliography.
Application fee
€42 to €52 administrative application fee submitted via the Unibo online Studenti Online portal.
Required documentation
Graduation diploma with official translation, academic transcript of records, detailed curriculum vitae, research project proposal, and 2 letters of reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at the University of Bologna are progressively calculated based on family economic indicators (ISEE Parificato). For international students from non-OECD nations, tuition is typically capped at €156 to €1,000 per year. Admitted doctoral candidates receive fully funded state scholarships of €1,400 net per month (€16,243 to €19,000/year gross) plus a 50% stipend increase during approved research stays abroad.
