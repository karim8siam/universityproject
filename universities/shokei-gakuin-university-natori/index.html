Shokei Gakuin University

Shokei Gakuin University is a private Christian university in Natori, suburban Sendai, offering interdisciplinary programs in comprehensive human sciences, clinical psychology, nutrition, and environmental science.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded by American Baptist missionaries, Shokei Gakuin delivers holistic education focused on human welfare, regional environmental conservation, and local community revitalization. The university features an on-campus clinical counseling center and food technology laboratories that serve the Sendai metropolitan basin.

Research admissions & international criteria

High school graduation credentials and entrance examinations. For international students: JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 200, personal statement, official transcripts, and an interview.
