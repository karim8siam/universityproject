Institutional Overview

Drenthe College, with its specialized technical campus on De Haar in Assen (Overijssel/Drenthe border adjacent to the world-famous TT Circuit Assen), is a leading regional institution of vocational and applied higher technical education. The Assen campus is internationally acclaimed for automotive engineering, smart mobility technologies, sustainable hydrogen powertrains, precision engineering, and health technologies.
