Internationales Begegnungszentrum (IBZ Ilmenau)

The Internationales Begegnungszentrum (IBZ Ilmenau) is a scholarly residence and cultural exchange institute on the campus of TU Ilmenau, accommodating international visiting professors, postdoctoral fellows, and Humboldt awardees conducting advanced technical research.

Academic overview & research priorities

IBZ Ilmenau fosters international scholarly integration, interdisciplinary colloquiums, and intercultural networking for visiting scientists in nano-optics, renewable energy systems, media technology, and mechanical engineering. In collaboration with the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation and the TU Ilmenau International Office, IBZ provides residential research fellowships, guest apartments, and family support.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open to international visiting researchers, guest professors, and postdocs with approved academic appointments or research fellowships at TU Ilmenau or regional research institutes (IMMS, Fraunhofer IDMT). Application requires an institutional host agreement and CV.
