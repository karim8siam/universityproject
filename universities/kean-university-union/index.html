Kean University

Kean Univ · Union, New Jersey, United States

Kean University is a comprehensive public research university in Union, New Jersey, designated as the state's urban research university and home to Wenzhou-Kean University in China. Kean is widely celebrated for STEM disciplines, architecture, and biotechnology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Kean operates the Dorothy and George Hennings College of Science, Mathematics and Technology, Michael Graves College of Architecture and Design, and Nathan Weiss Graduate College. Research focuses on urban sustainability, computational genomics, and neuro-oncology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school courses, GPA, and personal essays under test-optional policies. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and discipline-specific prerequisites.
