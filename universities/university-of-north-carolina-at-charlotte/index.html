University of North Carolina at Charlotte

UNC Charlotte · Charlotte, North Carolina, United States

The University of North Carolina at Charlotte (UNC Charlotte / Charlotte) is North Carolina's premier urban research institution and an elite Carnegie R1 public university. Located in America's second-largest financial center, Charlotte is globally recognized for motorsports engineering at the North Carolina Motorsports and Automotive Research Center, data science, cybersecurity, precision optical engineering, and urban logistics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Charlotte spans nine colleges including the William States Lee College of Engineering, College of Computing and Informatics (CCI), Belk College of Business, College of Health and Human Services, and the School of Data Science. Key institutes include the Energy Production and Infrastructure Center (EPIC) and the Center for Precision Metrology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are competitive through the Common Application. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 83+ or IELTS 6.5+).
