Umwelt-Campus Birkenfeld (Hochschule Trier)

Umwelt-Campus Birkenfeld (Environmental Campus Birkenfeld) of Trier University of Applied Sciences is Europe's premier zero-emission university campus, globally ranked #1 in Germany for sustainability (UI GreenMetric) across renewable energy, circular economy, and green business.

Academic overview & research priorities

Located in the Hunsrück-Hochwald National Park region, Umwelt-Campus Birkenfeld operates entirely on renewable energy, carbon-neutral biomass heat, and rainwater recycling. Operating two departments—Environmental Planning/Technology and Environmental Business/Law—the campus hosts prominent institutes including the Institute for Applied Material Flow Management (IfaS) and the Environmental Law Institute (IUR).

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor's admissions require the German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or recognized foreign school-leaving certificate evaluated via uni-assist. Master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, environmental science, law, or business (minimum German grade 2.5). English-taught tracks require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80; German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
