Kyoto Tachibana University

Study artificial intelligence, historical cultural heritage, and nursing sciences in ancient Kyoto at Kyoto Tachibana University.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Nestled on the green foothills of Mount Yamashina in eastern Kyoto, Kyoto Tachibana University is an agile, rapidly growing comprehensive university. Known for its world-class brass band club ('Orange Devils'), top-ranked Faculty of Nursing, Faculty of AI and Global Engineering, and vibrant Japanese historical heritage studies.

Institution type
Private Comprehensive Coeducational University
Campus location
Yamashina Green Hillside Campus, Kyoto (near JR Yamashina Station)
Accreditation
Japan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / MEXT
Admissions channel
General entrance examination in January; international student admission in November
Instruction language
Japanese & English (multidisciplinary global curriculum)
Financial aid & support
Tachibana Incentive Scholarships, JASSO Honors Grants, MEXT Recommendation

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Brand-new Faculty of AI & Informatics focusing on applied data science and generative algorithms
  • Premier clinical nursing and physical therapy programs affiliated with top Kansai medical centers
  • Celebrated Kyoto Historical Heritage Studies investigating Heian archaeological excavations
  • World-famous Kyoto Tachibana Wind Orchestra ('Orange Devils') renowned internationally

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Information Science (Faculty of AI & Informatics) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year computing degree specializing in machine learning, cloud architecture, game design, and digital media informatics.
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year nursing degree preparing candidates for the Japanese National Nursing Examination with clinical rotations at Kyoto University Hospital.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Historical Heritage Studies (Undergraduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary archaeology and cultural preservation degree conducting active excavations and digital archiving of Kyoto temples and shrines.
