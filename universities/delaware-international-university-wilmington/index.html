Delaware International University

Delaware International University (DIU) is an institution of international higher education in Wilmington, Delaware, providing specialized graduate degree programs in cross-border corporate governance, fintech informatics, and global business administration.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in the corporate capital of the United States, DIU focuses on international enterprise compliance, algorithmic finance, cyber law, and cross-border commercial trade. Courses are delivered via executive hybrid and online learning frameworks tailored for working multinational professionals.

Research admissions & international criteria

Graduate admission requires an earned bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution with a minimum 2.50 GPA, curriculum vitae, personal statement of purpose, and English proficiency (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 75).
