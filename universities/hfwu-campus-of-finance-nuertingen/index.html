Academic overview & excellence

Located in the prosperous economic hub of Baden-Württemberg near Stuttgart, the Campus of Finance at HfWU is a premier academic institution in German-speaking Europe for banking, investment finance, and institutional asset management. The institute delivers executive MBA and Master of Science degrees, publishes high-impact empirical research on corporate debt and ESG investing, and maintains close research partnerships with the Stuttgart Stock Exchange, Landesbank Baden-Württemberg (LBBW), and major asset management companies. Students benefit from dedicated Bloomberg terminals, quantitative portfolio simulations, and direct executive networking.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to graduate finance programs (M.Sc. and MBA) at the Campus of Finance requires an accredited bachelor's degree in Economics, Business Administration, Industrial Engineering, or Mathematics (German grade 2.5 or better), verified proficiency in English (TOEFL iBT 88+ or IELTS 6.5+) and German (for bilingual tracks), minimum one to two years of professional financial sector experience for executive degrees, and successful completion of a personal admissions interview.
