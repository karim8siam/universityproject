SOMT University of Physiotherapy: Manual Therapy, Musculoskeletal Ultrasound & Sports Medicine

SOMT University of Physiotherapy (Stichting Opleidingen Musculoskeletale Therapie) in Amersfoort is the Netherlands' leading specialized academic institute for musculoskeletal physiotherapy, manual therapy, clinical ultrasound, and sports rehabilitation.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Dutch DUO Lifelong Learning Healthcare Credit (Levenlanglerenkrediet)

Dutch national Ministry of Education funding mechanism allowing practicing physiotherapists and health clinicians up to age 55 to finance accredited Master of Science tuition at SOMT.

Master of Science in Manual Therapy (MSc)

Three-year part-time clinical Master of Science providing advanced competencies in spinal biomechanics, neuro-musculoskeletal diagnostics, and manipulative joint interventions.

Ph.D. in Musculoskeletal Health & Chronic Spinal Pain Mechanisms

Four-year joint doctoral research appointment with Maastricht University investigating central sensitization, quantitative sensory testing, and motor control adaptations in chronic neck and back pain.

Point-of-Care Musculoskeletal Ultrasound (MSK-US) Diagnostic Study

Clinical imaging trial evaluating high-frequency diagnostic ultrasound reliability in detecting rotator cuff tendinopathies and tendon elastography changes following rehabilitation.

High Performance Sports Rehabilitation & Return-to-Play Clinical Practicum

Six-month clinical residency embedded inside Olympic training centers (Papendal) and professional Dutch Eredivisie football club medical departments.

SOMT-Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam Musculoskeletal Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research fellowship investigating tendon mechanics, loading protocols, and individualized rehabilitation algorithms using wearable inertial sensors.

Associate Professor / Senior Lecturer in Pelvic & Spinal Rehabilitation

Senior faculty post to lead Master's level curriculum delivery, mentor postgraduate thesis students, and manage applied clinical trials in pelvic health and lumbar stability.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science in Physiotherapy (Fysiotherapie) with Dutch BIG registration or international equivalent license to practice.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science in Physiotherapy, Health Sciences, or Human Movement Sciences with documented clinical research aptitude.
Language proficiency
Dutch B2/C1 for clinical patient interaction; English B2 (IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90) for international Master tracks and publications.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, clinical practice verification, and letter of motivation outlining specialized physiotherapy objectives.
Application fee
€100 registration and admissions evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor of Physiotherapy diploma, BIG-register proof or foreign professional license, transcript of records, and identity document.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for accredited Master of Science programs is approximately €6,500 to €8,900 per academic year for both Dutch and EU/EEA students. Financial assistance is accessible via the Dutch DUO student financing scheme and accredited healthcare employer subsidies.
