Kansas City Kansas Community College

KCKCC · Kansas City, Kansas, United States

Kansas City Kansas Community College (KCKCC) is an urban public community college in Wyandotte County, renowned for registered nursing, mortuary science, biomanufacturing, culinary arts, and transfer liberal arts degrees.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Operating its main campus in Kansas City, Kansas, along with the Thomas R. Burke Technical Education Center, KCKCC serves as a vital community gateway for upward mobility, offering ABFSE-accredited mortuary science and top-ranked allied health degrees.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Operates under open-door admissions for all high school graduates and GED recipients. Selective competitive admission applies to registered nursing, physical therapist assistant, and mortuary science.
