Medizinische Hochschule Brandenburg Theodor Fontane (MHB)

Medizinische Hochschule Brandenburg Theodor Fontane (MHB) is a prestigious state-recognized university dedicated to medicine, dentistry, and psychology, located in Brandenburg an der Havel and Neuruppin. Founded to train compassionate medical professionals for eastern Germany, MHB pioneers reformed medical curricula (Brandenburger Modellstudiengang Medizin - BMM) based on problem-based learning and early clinical immersion.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Brandenburg an der Havel & Neuruppin, Germany

Institutional Overview

MHB operates across four major university hospitals (Universitätsklinikum Brandenburg an der Havel, Ruppiner Kliniken Neuruppin, Immanuel Klinikum Bernau, and Ernst von Bergmann Klinikum Potsdam). Its medical doctor (Staatsexamen Humanmedizin), dentistry (Zahnmedizin), and psychotherapy master's degrees emphasize small-group tutorials, clinical OSCE examinations, and rural healthcare research.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at MHB do not follow standard German NC (Numerus Clausus) university quotas; instead, selection is based on personal motivation, social competence, vocational healthcare experience, and multi-station selection interviews (Auswahltage). Medical students can access loan forgiveness programs through regional hospital partner sponsorships in exchange for rural medical service commitments.
