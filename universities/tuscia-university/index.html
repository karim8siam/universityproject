Tuscia University: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Established in 1979 in the historic medieval city of Viterbo, north of Rome, Tuscia University (Università degli Studi della Tuscia - UNITUS) is a distinguished Italian state university. Celebrated across Europe for agricultural innovation, forestry sciences, conservation of cultural heritage, and biotechnology, UNITUS pairs low tuition fees with deep research institutes.

LazioDiSCo Regional Government Full Study Grant & Housing Bursary

Comprehensive public regional need-and-merit scholarship guaranteeing tuition-free study, meal vouchers, and lodging for eligible international students.

Master of Science in Forestry and Environmental Sciences

Renowned master's program focused on sustainable forest management, alpine and Mediterranean ecosystem conservation, carbon sequestration, and precision forestry.

Doctoral Fellowship in Ecology and Sustainable Management of Environmental Resources

Three-year fully funded doctoral research program examining Mediterranean biodiversity dynamics, climate change adaptation, and circular bioeconomy systems.

DAFNE Agri-Food Quality and Precision Agriculture Laboratory Project

Research placement utilizing modern mass spectrometry and drone hyperspectral imaging to evaluate soil nutrient cycles and olive grove pest resistance.

Tuscia Cultural Heritage Diagnostics & Restoration Practicum

Practical archival and artifact conservation practicum conducted across historic Tuscia archaeological sites and museum conservation laboratories.

UNITUS Postdoctoral Assegno di Ricerca in Forest Ecophysiology and Carbon Sinks

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating eddy covariance carbon flux monitoring and tree drought resilience across Italian Mediterranean forests.

Ricercatore a Tempo Determinato (RTD-A) in Plant Protection and Pathology

Three-year non-tenure track assistant professorship delivering undergraduate lectures and advancing genomic research on emerging olive and grapevine pathogens.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (Laurea Triennale) in a related discipline evaluated by the academic didactic committee (minimum 180 ECTS credits).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Second-cycle Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with honors standing, evaluated through national competitive public examination (concorso pubblico).
Language proficiency
B2 level English proficiency according to the CEFR (IELTS Academic 6.0 or Cambridge B2 First) for English-taught master's curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, transcript of records, statement of motivation, and research outline (for PhD applicants).
Application fee
€10 to €30 pre-enrollment evaluation fee depending on course regulations.
Required documentation
Official university transcripts, degree diploma, declaration of value (Dichiarazione di Valore) or CIMEA comparability certificate, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at Tuscia University are determined by the student's family economic indicator (ISEE parificato). Fees range from €156 (mandatory regional tax for students with ISEE under €24,000) up to a maximum cap of approximately €2,300 per academic year. Qualifying international students can apply for the LazioDiSCo regional scholarship, which covers 100% of tuition plus a living grant up to €7,200 per year.
