Kyushu University (Ito Campus, Fukuoka)

Founded in 1911 as one of Japan's legendary National Seven Imperial Universities, Kyushu University (Kyudai) is a top designated national university headquartered in Fukuoka. With Japan's largest university campus—the sprawling Ito Campus—Kyushu University is an international scientific powerhouse leading the nation's clean energy transition as home to the International Institute for Carbon-Neutral Energy Research (I²CNER), the Next-Generation Fuel Cell Research Center, and cutting-edge supercomputing centers. One to two-year English-taught Master's program analyzing transnational commercial law, diplomatic security in the Asia-Pacific, and global environmental governance.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
16 years of formal education culminating in a recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent academic certification) with superior academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (or professional degree) in a relevant discipline with demonstrable thesis research and academic publications.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 80+ for international English-taught graduate programs; JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-medium courses.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2 to 3-page research plan in English or Japanese outlining research purpose, proposed methodologies, and prospective supervisor endorsement.
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee for general graduate application; completely waived for Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship recipients.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, graduation certificates, thesis abstract, research proposal, 2 recommendation letters, and prospective supervisor acceptance letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for national universities in Japan is standardized by the Ministry of Education (MEXT). Standard tuition is ¥535,800 per year (approx. USD $3,600/year) plus a one-time admission fee of ¥282,000. MEXT Japanese Government Scholarship recipients receive 100% full tuition and admission fee exemptions, round-trip international airfare, and a tax-free monthly living stipend of ¥144,000 (Master's) to ¥147,000 (PhD) per month.
