Fukuoka Institute of Technology: Graduate Engineering, MEXT & Robotics Research

Fukuoka Institute of Technology (FIT / Fukuoka Kogyo Daigaku) is a prominent engineering and applied sciences university in Fukuoka, Kyushu, celebrated for robotics, semiconductor device engineering, electronic materials, and high industry employment across Japan's 'Silicon Island'.

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at FIT

Prestigious national scholarship awarded to exceptional international engineering students pursuing master's or doctoral degrees at FIT.

Doctor of Engineering in Electronic Engineering & Semiconductor Devices

Doctoral research program investigating wide-bandgap semiconductors, gallium nitride (GaN) power devices, and nano-electronic fabrication in Kyushu's semiconductor hub.

Master of Engineering in Intelligent Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Advanced engineering master's covering humanoid robotics, computer vision, sensor fusion, and robotic manipulation for manufacturing automation.

FIT Information & Communication Systems Laboratory Research Assistantship

Applied research vacancy evaluating 6G wireless communication architectures, edge computing security, and vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANET).

Kyushu Silicon Island Semiconductor Fab Industrial Internship

Three-month industrial internship with regional semiconductor manufacturers and cleanrooms mastering wafer lithography, thin-film deposition, and packaging.

JSPS-FIT Postdoctoral Fellowship in Advanced Functional Materials

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship supported by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) researching polymer nanocomposites and thermoelectric materials.

Master of Information Science in Artificial Intelligence & Big Data Analytics

Graduate program focusing on deep neural networks, natural language processing, predictive maintenance algorithms, and cyber security.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education with a bachelor's degree in engineering, computer science, or physical sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in engineering or relevant scientific discipline with verified thesis or peer-reviewed publication.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 72+, IELTS 5.5+, or Japanese JLPT N2 (depending on designated supervisor's working language).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research proposal (2–3 pages) in English or Japanese matching a FIT laboratory research theme.
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY entrance examination screening fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, letters of recommendation from undergraduate dean/advisor, and research plan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual graduate tuition at FIT is approximately ¥850,000 to ¥1,050,000 JPY. International students receive a 30% to 50% tuition reduction scholarship, and Japanese Government MEXT scholarship holders receive full tuition exemption plus a monthly stipend of ¥144,000–¥145,000 JPY.
