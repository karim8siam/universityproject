Institution Overview & Profile

Dresden International University (DIU), founded in 2003 as the affiliated continuing-education and postgraduate university of Technische Universität Dresden (TU Dresden), is a state-recognized private institution offering high-calibre international Master's degrees in clinical medicine, specialized healthcare, intellectual property law, and automotive engineering.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Postgraduate University (Associated with TU Dresden)
Main CampusFreiberger Straße Campus, Dresden, Saxony (World Trade Center Dresden)
AccreditationGerman Science Council (Wissenschaftsrat) / ZEvA / FIBAA / ACQUIN
Admissions RouteDirect DIU online application with verified degree credentials (rolling)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL iBT 85) for international tracks; German C1 for clinical tracks
Financial Aid & GrantsDIU Merit Tuition Reductions & Corporate Educational Grants

Key Academic Strengths

  • Direct synergy and faculty exchange with TU Dresden (Excellence University)
  • World-class clinical master's programs in Osteology, Chiropractic, and Clinical Research
  • Specialized Master of Laws (LL.M.) in Intellectual Property Rights and International Business
  • High proportion of international postgraduate scholars from over 35 nations

Scholarship

DIU International Postgraduate Excellence Scholarship

Merit-based partial tuition scholarship awarded to international medical doctors and engineers admitted to Master of Science programs at DIU.

Funding: €3,000 direct tuition reduction applied across the standard study duration Deadline: June 30, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Clinical Research Enrolment

Two-year postgraduate curriculum qualifying medical professionals for leadership in international multicenter clinical trials and ICH-GCP audits.

Funding: Flexible installment plan and employer educational tax deduction in Germany Deadline: July 31, 2027
Master's

M.Sc. in Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Aerospace Practicum

Specialized postgraduate engineering training conducted in collaboration with Fraunhofer IKTS Dresden and the German Society for NDT (DGZfP).

Funding: Industrial research assistantship possibilities at Fraunhofer IKTS (€1,200/month) Deadline: June 15, 2027
Master's

Master of Laws (LL.M.) in IP Law and Technology Transfer

Intensive European patent and technology law program taught by patent judges, European Patent Office (EPO) examiners, and corporate counsel.

Funding: Early-registration fee reduction of €1,000 Deadline: July 15, 2027
Fellowship

DIU Clinical Trial Management Innovation Fellowship

Six-month research fellowship hosted at the University Hospital Carl Gustav Carus Dresden analyzing patient recruitment telemetry in oncology trials.

Funding: €2,200 monthly research stipend plus full access to the medical campus bioinformatics suites Deadline: April 15, 2027
Grant

Erasmus+ Staff & Student Mobility Grant at DIU

European mobility funding for postgraduate students conducting thesis research at partner universities in Austria, Switzerland, and the UK.

Funding: €450 to €600 per month subsistence allowance Deadline: February 28, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Prevention and Therapy Management Enrolment

Interdisciplinary master's combining preventative healthcare, sports science, clinical nutrition, and chronic disease rehabilitation.
