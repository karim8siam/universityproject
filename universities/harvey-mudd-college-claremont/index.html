Academic overview & strategic disciplines

As a member of the Claremont Colleges consortium, Harvey Mudd combines rigorous mathematics and science education with a substantial humanities and social sciences curriculum. Its hallmark Clinic Program engages teams of juniors and seniors in solving real-world R&D challenges sponsored by corporate and national laboratory clients.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective, evaluating secondary academic transcripts, mathematics preparation through calculus, physics and chemistry coursework, essays, and teacher recommendations. Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT) are evaluated under test-optional policies.
