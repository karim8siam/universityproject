we4you - TU Ilmenau International Center

we4you is the dedicated international student and scholar support center at Technische Universität Ilmenau, facilitating integration, degree navigation, international fellowships, and research exchanges in engineering and computer science.

Academic overview & research priorities

Embedded within TU Ilmenau—internationally acclaimed for mechanical engineering, microelectronics, audio engineering, and communications technology—we4you coordinates welcoming services, cultural mentorship, DAAD integration scholarships, and corporate research transition programs.

Research admissions & international criteria

International students apply through uni-assist for TU Ilmenau’s bachelor, master, and doctoral degree programs. Requirements include certified secondary/undergraduate transcripts and language proficiency (English IELTS 6.5+ or German DSH-2 depending on the program).
