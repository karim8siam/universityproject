Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located in Orange County, California State University, Fullerton (CSUF) enrolls over 41,000 students and is a designated Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) renowned for top-ranked programs in business administration (the largest business school in California), computer science, public health, teacher education, and gravitational-wave astrophysics.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Gravitational-Wave Physics and Astronomy Center (GWPAC / LIGO Collaboration)
  • College of Business and Economics (AACSB-accredited, largest in California)
  • Computer Science, cyber-physical systems, and human-computer interaction
  • Public health, kinesiology, and biotechnology clinical laboratories

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Software Engineering (Accelerated Online) (Graduate Degree) — Top-ranked professional master's degree focusing on cloud architecture, agile methodologies, and enterprise software security.
  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (Undergraduate Degree) — ABET-accredited degree providing foundational computer science, algorithm theory, artificial intelligence, and operating systems.
  • Master of Science in Physics (Gravitational Wave Astrophysics Track) (Graduate Degree) — World-class research master's degree directly participating in LIGO gravitational-wave detection and quantum optics.
