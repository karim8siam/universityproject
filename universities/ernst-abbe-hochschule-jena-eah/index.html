Ernst-Abbe-Hochschule Jena (EAH Jena)

Ernst-Abbe-Hochschule Jena (EAH Jena) is Thuringia’s largest and oldest university of applied sciences, situated in the historic global capital of optical engineering and precision mechanics alongside Carl Zeiss and Schott.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1991 and named after physicist and industrial reformer Ernst Abbe, EAH Jena hosts over 4,400 students across engineering, business administration, and social sciences. Deeply embedded within the legendary "Optics Valley" of Jena, the university maintains intimate joint laboratories and dual-study programs with world giants like ZEISS, Jenoptik, and Schott AG. EAH Jena is internationally celebrated for optometry, laser technology, biomedical engineering, environmental technology, and precision mechatronics.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Laser- und Optotechnologien (Laser and Optical Technologies) (Master's) — Internationally recognized program in laser material processing, optical simulation, fiber optics, and interferometry.
  • Master of Engineering in Medizintechnik (Medical Engineering) (Master's) — Advanced engineering degree focusing on biomedical electronics, orthopedic implants, biocompatible polymers, and endoscopy.
  • Bachelor of Science in Augenoptik / Optometrie (Optometry) (Bachelor's) — Germany's flagship optometric science degree qualifying clinicians in vision correction, refractive diagnostics, and contact lens physics.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Feinwerktechnik / Mechatronik (Precision Mechatronics) (Bachelor's) — Rigorous engineering training in micro-positioning, optomechanical assemblies, and robotic automation.
