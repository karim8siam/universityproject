Academic overview & research priorities

Located in Jacksonville near the St. Johns River and the Atlantic seaboard, Florida Coastal University emphasizes practice-oriented learning in coastal zone planning, admiralty law concepts, port logistics, and environmental regulatory compliance. Students engage in practical legal clinics, environmental field research, and corporate supply chain simulations.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate programs require secondary school graduation with competitive grades, submission of transcripts, and an essay on coastal or legal advocacy. Master's degree applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree in business, political science, or environmental science, submitting official transcripts and professional references.
