A.T. Still University (ATSU)

Founded in 1892 in Kirksville, Missouri by Dr. Andrew Taylor Still as the world's founding institution of osteopathic medicine, A.T. Still University is a premier health professions institution operating campuses in Missouri and Arizona. Review verified graduate health doctorates, biomedical master's programs, ATSRI research fellowships, teaching assistantships, and clinical faculty.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

ATSU offers graduate health doctorates, biomedical research degrees, and competitive clinical fellowships:

Hometown Scholars & Community Health Endowments

Institutional scholarships ($5,000 to full tuition support) for aspiring health professions students endorsed by partner Community Health Centers (CHCs) dedicated to underserved populations.

Doctor of Health Sciences (DHSc Online)

Applied online professional doctorate (70 credit hours) focusing on healthcare leadership, evidence evaluation, and an applied research project across multiple clinical concentrations.

MS in Biomedical Sciences (KCOM)

Rigorous two-year biomedical research master's degree (32.5 credits) including laboratory thesis research in molecular biology, neurobiology, or musculoskeletal physiology.

ATSRI Clinician Researcher Development Program (CRDP)

Funded research mentorship pathway and travel awards up to $2,500 through the A.T. Still Research Institute for students engaging in clinical and translational trials.

ATSRI Summer Student Research Immersion Experience

Eight-week funded summer laboratory research internship ($15–$20/hr, 30 hrs/week) under ATSRI faculty mentors, analyzing patient data and preparing scientific abstracts.

Pre-Doctoral Anatomy & Clinical Skills Teaching Fellowship

12-month salaried academic teaching fellowship providing full tuition waiver and $18,000–$24,000 living stipend for advanced cadaveric dissection coaching and clinical skills tutoring.

Admissions and eligibility requirements

DHSc admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's (min. 3.0/4.0 GPA) or Master's degree (min. 2.5/4.0 GPA) with healthcare background, resume, personal essay, and virtual interview.
MS in Biomedical Sciences
Accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.65 cumulative and science GPA; coursework in biology, organic chemistry, physics, and calculus. Note: KCOM MS currently restricts admission to U.S. citizens and permanent residents.
Standardized testing
GRE, MCAT, or DAT required within past three years for KCOM MS; not universally required for online DHSc.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency required for foreign academic credential holders via official course-by-course evaluation.
Application fee
Application fees apply through central application services (PostbacCAS) or ATSU direct portals.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is established by individual schools across ATSU. For biomedical sciences and health sciences doctoral studies, annual tuition typically ranges between $15,000 and $35,000. Admitted students can access internal scholarships through ScholarshipUniverse, including the Hometown Scholars Program and competitive Pre-Doctoral Anatomy Fellowships that include 100% tuition coverage and annual living stipends.
