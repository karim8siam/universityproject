A.T. Still University (ATSU)

A health professions university with programs and research activity in Missouri and Arizona. Compare professional doctoral education, biomedical master's research, institutional scholarships and university research centers before choosing an application route.

Official university source ↗Research checked, not sponsored

Degree and research pathways are different

ATSU offers healthcare professional degrees, research activities and graduate health-science study through multiple schools. The online Doctor of Health Sciences (DHSc) is a practice-focused professional doctorate: 70 credit hours and an applied research project, not a traditional dissertation PhD. Its admissions route differs from the Kirksville College of Osteopathic Medicine MS in Biomedical Sciences, a research-aligned master's including lab/science preparation and a thesis component.

Doctor of Health Sciences (online)

70 credits; professional leadership, evidence evaluation and applied research project. No dissertation required. Program describes four concentration options and multiple starts, but specific 2027 closing dates were not verified.

MS in Biomedical Sciences (KCOM)

32.5 minimum credits including nine thesis credits and research advisor involvement. Only U.S. citizens or permanent residents are currently eligible, a decisive restriction for overseas students.

Clinical professional study

Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine and other professional healthcare degrees have their own required prerequisites, professional accreditation and financial terms. Do not assume this MS or DHSc admits a candidate into medical school.

Admissions and eligibility

DHSc direct-entry bachelor's route
Accredited bachelor's or higher, minimum 3.0/4 cumulative GPA, official transcripts, resume, essay and virtual interview. Foreign credentials require equivalency evaluation.
DHSc standard master's route
Accredited master's or higher, minimum 2.5/4 cumulative GPA, plus current admission requirements and documentation.
MS biomedical coursework
Minimum 2.65 overall and science GPA, specified biology, physics and chemistry with laboratories, English and mathematics prerequisites.
MS standardized tests
GRE, MCAT or DAT within three years of application, plus two recommendation letters under current KCOM requirements.
MS citizenship restriction
Official admissions explicitly exclude applicants who are neither U.S. citizens nor permanent residents. International applicants should not apply expecting an exception without a policy change.
DHSc international eligibility
Foreign degree evaluation is described, but online enrollment, residence rules and any visa implications require direct confirmation; online study should not be assumed to establish U.S. student-visa eligibility.

Fees, financial aid and scholarship status

ATSU publishes school- and program-specific tuition schedules. The price of a healthcare doctorate cannot safely be represented by one university-wide number. DHSc students may also have a technology fee; total expenses depend on the current year and program schedule. Always consult the official ATSU tuition page before an application.

The university lists competitive admission awards, some automatically considered through a completed application and others by invitation. Examples include the KCOM Anne Wright Hazen Scholarship (listed at up to US$10,000 for certain first-year recipients) and named smaller endowments. These are limited, school-specific awards, not universal tuition waivers.

The Enrollment Services pages also describe internal/endowed ATSU awards, often US$500–$5,000 annually, and the ScholarshipUniverse system for currently admitted/enrolled students. Amounts, eligibility, and next cycle dates should be checked within the current student portal and award terms.

Research centers and faculty

The A.T. Still Research Institute coordinates collaborative work in osteopathic manual medicine, oral health and community health. Its Center for Research in Osteopathic Manipulative Medicine investigates objective ways of measuring palpation and related clinical skills, with practice-based research and educational projects. The Center for Oral Health Research addresses clinical, behavioral and access-to-care problems. Active centers are not synonymous with a paid student research vacancy.

For prospective international students

  1. Identify the exact ATSU school, not only the umbrella university.
  2. Check whether the program admits non-U.S. citizens or non-permanent residents (KCOM MS: it currently does not).
  3. Distinguish online professional research projects from campus-based PhD supervision and from medical licensure pathways.
  4. Compare documented total tuition/fees and scholarships tied to your actual program.
  5. Verify the current application cycle through ATSU admissions before preparing exam scores or credential evaluations.

The KCOM MS lists a May 15 application deadline in its standing guide, without a verified current-year closing date. It should not be treated as a confirmed open 2027 deadline. No institution-wide funded PhD opening was verified.
