University of Maryland

UMD · College Park, Maryland, United States

The University of Maryland, College Park is the flagship public research university of the state of Maryland and an elite member of the Association of American Universities (AAU). Located minutes from Washington, D.C., UMD is internationally celebrated for pioneering research in quantum computing, cybersecurity, atmospheric and oceanic sciences, aerospace engineering, and public policy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMD comprises 12 colleges and schools, notably the A. James Clark School of Engineering, the College of Computer, Mathematical, and Natural Sciences (CMNS), and the School of Public Policy. The university partners extensively with major federal research laboratories including NASA Goddard, NIST, NOAA, and NIH, and operates premier research institutes such as the Joint Quantum Institute (JQI) and the Maryland Cybersecurity Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions use holistic evaluation via the Common App. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, official transcripts, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency scores (TOEFL iBT 96+ or IELTS 7.0+).
