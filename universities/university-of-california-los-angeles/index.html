University of California, Los Angeles

UCLA · Los Angeles, California, United States

The University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), established in 1919 in Westwood, Los Angeles, is ranked as the #1 public university in the United States and is a global titan in education, research, and cultural innovation. Receiving over $1.7 billion annually in sponsored research awards, UCLA faculty have won 16 Nobel Prizes, made foundational breakthroughs including hosting the first ARPANET transmission (the birth of the Internet), and pioneered modern surgical oncology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UCLA comprises the College of Letters and Science and 12 prestigious professional schools, including the David Geffen School of Medicine, the Samueli School of Engineering, the School of the Arts and Architecture, the School of Theater, Film and Television, and the Anderson School of Management. It operates the UCLA Health System, consistently ranked among the top hospitals in the nation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admission to UCLA is extremely competitive. Undergraduate applications are processed via the University of California application (over 140,000 applicants; test-free). Graduate and doctoral applicants require superior academic records (minimum 3.5+ GPA typically), letters of recommendation, and demonstrated research achievement.
