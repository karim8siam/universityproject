Academic overview & excellence

Creighton is one of only 28 Jesuit colleges and universities in the United States, offering comprehensive doctoral professional programs in medicine, pharmacy, physical therapy, law, and dentistry. It operates two health sciences campuses in Omaha and Phoenix, Arizona.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate applicants submit the Common Application with high school transcripts (average GPA 3.8, test-optional). Professional programs (M.D., Pharm.D., J.D., D.D.S.) maintain rigorous centralized application processes (AMCAS, PharmCAS, LSAC, AADSAS). International applicants submit proof of English proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 80).
