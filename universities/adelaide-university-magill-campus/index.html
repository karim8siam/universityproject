Magill, Adelaide, South Australia, Australia · Premier Education, Psychology & Creative Arts Campus

Adelaide University Magill Campus: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

As a landmark campus of South Australia's newly unified Adelaide University, Magill Campus combines parkland serenity with state-of-the-art specialist facilities including television and radio broadcast suites, allied health clinics, sensory analysis suites, and interactive education laboratories. Magill serves as a vibrant nucleus for research in developmental psychology, cognitive sciences, contemporary arts, and community wellbeing.
