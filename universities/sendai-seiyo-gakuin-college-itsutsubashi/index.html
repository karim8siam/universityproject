Sendai Seiyo Gakuin College, Itsutsubashi Campus

Sendai Seiyo Gakuin College (Itsutsubashi Campus) is a premier junior and professional healthcare college in downtown Sendai, providing certified programs in dental hygiene, clinical nutrition, nursing, and international business communication.

Academic overview & research priorities

Located near Sendai Station, the modern Itsutsubashi campus hosts clinical dental hygiene training suites, medical nutrition laboratories, and professional business language studios. Graduates consistently achieve outstanding pass rates on Japanese national medical and dental hygiene licensure examinations, serving the healthcare needs of the Tohoku region.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation and entrance evaluation in biological sciences and Japanese language. Foreign applicants require JLPT N2, transcript validation, and a health clearance.
