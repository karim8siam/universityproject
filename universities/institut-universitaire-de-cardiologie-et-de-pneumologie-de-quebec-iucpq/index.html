Institut Universitaire de Cardiologie et de Pneumologie de Québec (IUCPQ)

The Institut Universitaire de Cardiologie et de Pneumologie de Québec (IUCPQ), affiliated with Université Laval, is an internationally acclaimed specialized university research hospital dedicated to cardiology, respirology, and obesity-metabolism science.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Designated as a world-leading suprarregional specialized health center, IUCPQ is affiliated with Université Laval and serves as a premier research and clinical training hub in North America. The IUCPQ Research Centre hosts more than 70 principal investigators, 850 clinicians, and hundreds of graduate and post-doctoral trainees. Its breakthroughs in transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR), bariatric metabolic surgery, sleep apnea biomechanics, and cardiopulmonary genomics have established international standards of care.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Cardiopulmonary Hemodynamics & Interventional Structural Heart Care
  • Translational Obesity, Bariatric Surgery & Type 2 Diabetes Biology
  • Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) & Asthma Genomics
  • Cardiometabolic Biomarkers & Advanced Thoracic Imaging

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Doctorat en médecine moléculaire et cardiovasculaire (PhD Molecular Medicine) (Doctorate) — Advanced doctoral research training in cardiac lipidology, vascular calcification, and post-infarction tissue remodeling.
  • Maîtrise en santé respiratoire et sciences cliniques (MSc Respiratory Health) (Master's) — Graduate clinical and experimental science program focusing on ventilatory physics, lung microbiome, and pulmonary hypertension.
  • Postdoctoral Fellowship in Bariatric and Metabolic Surgery (Postdoctoral) — High-volume clinical and translational laboratory fellowship studying gut incretin mechanisms and metabolic remission.
  • Fellowship in Advanced Structural Heart Interventions & TAVR (Clinical Fellowship) — Specialized post-residency fellowship in catheter-based cardiac interventions and echocardiographic guidance.
