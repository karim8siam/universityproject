University of North Alabama

UNA · Florence, Alabama, United States

The University of North Alabama (UNA) is Alabama's oldest public university, founded in 1830 on a historic 130-acre campus in Florence, Alabama. Located along the Tennessee River in the storied Shoals music region, UNA is internationally celebrated for entertainment industry music management, nursing and health professions, computer science, and business education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNA operates five colleges: the Anderson College of Nursing and Health Professions, College of Arts, Sciences, and Engineering (CASE), Sanders College of Business and Technology, College of Education and Human Sciences, and the School of the Arts. UNA is famous for its live lion mascot habitat and the Muscle Shoals Sound music recording legacy.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students. Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.0+).
