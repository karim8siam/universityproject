Ball State University: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Founded in 1918 in Muncie, Indiana, Ball State University is a prominent public research institution known across North America for its pioneering 'Immersive Learning' approach. Celebrated for its College of Architecture and Planning, telecommunications and journalism, educational psychology, and sustainable environmental design, Ball State provides comprehensive graduate funding.

Ball State College of Architecture and Planning Graduate Fellowship

Merit fellowship awarded to exceptional incoming graduate students entering accredited professional architecture and urban planning master's programs.

Master of Science in Information and Communication Sciences

Top-ranked interdisciplinary professional master's curriculum combining enterprise cloud architecture, cybersecurity management, and business leadership.

Ball State Educational Psychology & Cognitive Sciences Doctoral Assistantship

Four-year doctoral assistantship conducting funded cognitive research on learning technologies, neural correlates of executive function, and psychometric measurement.

Ball State Center for Emerging Media Design & Development Project

Applied research lab placement investigating user experience (UX) design, transmedia storytelling, and interactive public communication installations.

Ball State Geothermal Energy Operations & Campus Sustainability Practicum

Practical engineering internship operating the largest ground-source closed-loop geothermal district energy system installed on any North American university campus.

Ball State Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Aquatic Ecology and Fisheries

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating Laurentian Great Lakes fish population dynamics, toxic cyanobacterial algal blooms, and riverine ecosystem restoration.

Assistant Professor of Architecture and Urban Design

Tenure-track faculty position teaching core architectural design studios and lecture courses in sustainable building systems and contemporary urbanism.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree from a recognized college or university with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale (or 3.0 in the major).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in a relevant discipline with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2 on a 4.0 scale and departmental faculty endorsement.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS Academic 6.5, or Duolingo English Test 110.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive Statement of Purpose (500–1,000 words) discussing academic objectives, professional background, and targeted research focus.
Application fee
USD $60 non-refundable application fee for international graduate applicants.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, certified English translations, degree certificates, English language scores, and three letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International out-of-state graduate tuition is approximately USD $27,400 per academic year. Graduate Assistantships (GA) awarded by academic departments provide a full tuition waiver (covering up to 100% of out-of-state tuition) plus an annual academic stipend ranging from USD $12,500 to USD $18,000.
