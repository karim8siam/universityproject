Linköping University: Computer Science, Materials & Salaried PhD Guide

Linköping University (LiU - Linköpings universitet) is one of Sweden's top comprehensive research universities, celebrated for pioneering computer science in Scandinavia and world-leading research at the Laboratory of Organic Electronics (LOE). LiU is home to Sweden's National Supercomputer Centre (NSC) and the Wallenberg Autonomous Systems Program (WASP).

Salaried Swedish Doctoral Position (PhD) in Organic Bioelectronics

Four-year salaried doctoral appointment within the Laboratory of Organic Electronics (LOE), researching electronic plants, organic electrochemical transistors, and bio-interfacing neural probes.

Linköping University International Master's Scholarship (50%–100% Tuition Waiver)

Merit-based university award for non-EU/EEA fee-paying students admitted to master's programs at LiU who demonstrate exceptional academic achievement.

Master of Science in Computer Science: Autonomous Systems & Artificial Intelligence

World-class 2-year master's program integrated with the WASP initiative, offering advanced coursework in reinforcement learning, automated planning, and computer vision.

National Supercomputer Centre (NSC) Berzelius AI Cluster Research Assistantship

Research assistantship working on Sweden's most powerful AI supercomputer, Berzelius, training massive multimodal foundation models and molecular dynamics simulations.

Mjärdevi Science Park Industrial AI & Embedded Software Practicum

Six-month professional placement embedded within Linköping Science Park (Mjärdevi), collaborating with Ericsson, Saab, or innovative AI startups.

WASP Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Safe Autonomous Robotics

Two-year postdoctoral appointment funded by the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP), investigating formal verification and safety certification for autonomous drones.

Master of Science in Materials Science and Nanotechnology

Advanced graduate program utilizing thin film electron microscopy, synchrotron X-ray diffraction, and cleanroom synthesis for two-dimensional materials and semiconductors.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (equivalent to a Swedish Kandidatexamen of 180 ECTS) in relevant subject from an internationally accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS, including 60 ECTS at master's level and an independent thesis) with competitive selection based on research excellence.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B (IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no section below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applications require a detailed 3,000-word research proposal aligned with the specific advertised research vacancy.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee paid via the national UniversityAdmissions.se portal (EU/EEA students exempt).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records with course syllabus descriptions, proof of English, passport copy, and curriculum vitae.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 100% free for citizens of the EU, EEA, and Switzerland. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition ranges from SEK 125,000 to SEK 165,000 per academic year. All doctoral positions in Sweden are salaried employee contracts (starting at approximately SEK 33,500 per month with full healthcare and social benefits; zero tuition).
