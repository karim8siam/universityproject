Alabama State University: Biomedical Sciences, Forensic Science & Physical Therapy Guide

Founded in 1867 by nine freed African American men, Alabama State University (ASU) is a historic public HBCU located in Montgomery, Alabama. Renowned for civil rights heritage, biomedical sciences, physical therapy, and forensic science, ASU conducts nationally competitive research funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF) and the National Institutes of Health (NIH) through its Center for Nanobiotechnology Research (CNBR).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

NSF-CREST Nanobiotechnology Research Assistantship (GRA) at ASU

Prestigious graduate assistantship at the Center for Nanobiotechnology Research (CNBR) investigating functionalized nanoparticles for targeted drug delivery and rapid pathogen detection.

Master of Science in Forensic Science and Analytical Toxicology

Two-year specialized graduate curriculum delivering comprehensive forensic laboratory training in gas chromatography-mass spectrometry (GC-MS), forensic DNA profiling, and ballistics.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Microbiology & Infectious Disease Biotechnology

Flagship doctoral curriculum exploring antimicrobial resistance mechanisms, microbial physiology, nanoparticle-mediated vaccine adjuvants, and respiratory viral pathogenesis.

ASU Environmental Microbiology & Riverine Pathogen Transport Project

Field-based scientific investigation tracking waterborne microbial pathogen loads, antibiotic-resistant coliform strains, and heavy metal concentrations in the Alabama River basin.

Alabama Department of Forensic Sciences (ADFS) Practicum Internship

Twelve-week operational internship at the Montgomery Regional Laboratory assisting state forensic scientists with controlled substance identification and forensic serology.

ASU Postdoctoral Fellowship in Nanomaterial Antimicrobial Coatings

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment synthesizing antimicrobial silver and zinc oxide nanocomposites for surgical instruments and medical device surfaces.

ASU Graduate Presidential Academic Scholarship in STEM

Merit scholarship awarded by the ASU Graduate School to talented students enrolling in mathematics, computational biology, and applied sciences master's degrees.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from an accredited institution with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale (3.0 for science programs).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science in biological sciences or related discipline with a minimum graduate GPA of 3.25; or exceptional bachelor's degree with GPA 3.5+.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS Academic 6.5, or Duolingo English Test 105+ for international applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research statement (1,000 words) describing career objectives and research interests in nanobiotechnology, microbiology, or infectious diseases.
Application fee
$50 USD domestic application fee; $75 USD for international applicants.
Required documentation
Official post-secondary transcripts, three confidential letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, and resume/CV.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In-state graduate tuition is approximately $412 USD per credit hour ($8,240 USD/year for full-time study); out-of-state and international graduate tuition is approximately $824 USD per credit hour ($16,480 USD/year). Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA) sponsored by NSF and NIH provide full tuition remissions and annual stipends of $20,000 to $26,000 USD.
