Academic overview & excellence

Headquartered in Aurora, Colorado as an autonomous campus of the renowned Colorado State University System, CSU Global holds regional institutional accreditation from the Higher Learning Commission (HLC). Serving adult learners, active-duty military, and international working scholars, CSU Global excels in career-relevant bachelor's and master's degrees in computer science, cybersecurity, project management (PMI-GAC accredited), organizational leadership, and finance.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate degree admission requires a high school diploma or equivalent, with a streamlined pathway for transfer students holding 12 or more accredited college credits (minimum 2.0 GPA). Master's candidates must submit official transcripts documenting a bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.8 GPA. International scholars must provide credential evaluations and demonstrate English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 70, IELTS 6.0, or Duolingo 95).
