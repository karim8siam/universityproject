Montana State University Billings

MSU Billings · Billings, Montana, United States

Montana State University Billings is a comprehensive urban university in Billings, Montana, nestled beneath the Rimrocks. Renowned for allied health sciences, clinical rehabilitation counseling, business administration, and applied science technologies.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MSU Billings comprises the College of Liberal Arts and Social Sciences, College of Business, College of Health Professions and Science, and City College. It serves as Montana's primary urban health science and vocational training center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation or transfer college coursework with test-optional policies. Graduate programs in counseling, health administration, and education require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA and references.
