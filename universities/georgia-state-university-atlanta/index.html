Academic overview & research priorities

GSU is a powerhouse of applied urban research, conducting cutting-edge scholarship in neurosciences, virology, public policy, risk management, and digital media across its downtown Atlanta campus and peripheral colleges.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, course rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree with 3.00+ GPA, statement of purpose, academic writing sample, and TOEFL iBT 80+ / IELTS 6.5+.
