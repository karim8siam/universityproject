Yale University (New Haven, Connecticut)

Founded in 1701 in New Haven, Connecticut, Yale University is one of the world's most renowned research universities and the third-oldest institution of higher education in the United States. A founding member of the Ivy League, Yale is associated with 65 Nobel laureates, five U.S. Presidents, and numerous foreign heads of state. Renowned globally for breakthroughs in quantum computing, biomedical science, comparative literature, and economics, Yale provides guaranteed full funding packages to 100% of admitted regular doctoral students for up to five full years. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Yale PhD in Applied Physics & Quantum Information (100% Fully Funded)

Five-year doctoral program at the Yale Quantum Institute (YQI), internationally famous as the birthplace of circuit quantum electrodynamics (cQED) and superconducting qubits.

Yale PhD in Computer Science (100% Fully Funded)

Five-year doctoral program covering artificial intelligence, distributed systems, quantum computing, computer vision, robotics, theoretical computer science, and security.

Yale Combined Program in the Biological and Biomedical Sciences (BBS) Fellowships

Interdepartmental doctoral gateway providing training across 8 tracks, including Molecular Cell Biology, Genetics, Immunobiology, Neuroscience, and Computational Biology.

Yale Master of Science (MS) in Computer Science

One-year intensive terminal Master's degree at the Yale School of Engineering & Applied Science designed for students seeking advanced expertise in systems, machine learning, or cybersecurity.

Yale Master of Arts (MA) in International and Development Economics (IDE)

One-year intensive graduate program in the Department of Economics emphasizing quantitative economic analysis, econometrics, and international policy.

Yale School of the Environment (YSE) Summer Research Internship

Ten-week mentored summer research fellowship at the Yale School of the Environment conducting field investigations at the Yale-Myers Forest in forest ecology, carbon dynamics, and hydrology.

Yale Postdoctoral Associate in Solar Energy & Photochemistry

Two-year postdoctoral position at the Yale Energy Sciences Institute developing molecular and heterogeneous catalysts for solar-driven CO2 reduction and water oxidation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Conferred Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with an outstanding academic record (typically 3.6+ GPA for STEM and quantitative master's programs).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Conferred Bachelor's or Master's degree in an aligned discipline with exceptional scholastic achievement, proven research potential, and strong faculty alignment.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 to 8.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100+ (sub-scores 25+ recommended); required for applicants whose primary language of instruction was not English.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 3-page Statement of Academic Purpose detailing specific research inquiries, methodology, relevant laboratory experience, and faculty members of interest.
Application fee
USD $105 application fee submitted through the Yale Graduate School of Arts and Sciences (GSAS) online portal; fee waivers available upon application.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, and writing sample where required.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

All admitted PhD students in the Yale Graduate School of Arts and Sciences receive 100% full financial support for up to five years. This guaranteed package includes a 100% full tuition waiver (worth over $51,000/year), full comprehensive Yale Health hospitalization and prescription insurance coverage, and an annual living stipend of USD $45,500 to $50,500 per year.
