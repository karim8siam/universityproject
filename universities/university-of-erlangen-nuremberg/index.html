Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor of Science in engineering, physics, computing, or related discipline with grade 2.5 (gut) or better on the German grading scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (300 ECTS) with excellent standing (grade 2.0 or better) and formal written acceptance from an FAU doctoral advisor.
Language proficiency
English B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 90+) for international English-taught degrees, or German DSH-2 / TestDaF 4 for German-taught tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page statement of research interests outlining proposed thesis problem, experimental framework, and publication intentions.
Application fee
Zero application fee through the university's Campo online application portal.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree diploma, curriculum vitae, motivation letter, and two academic reference letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is completely free (€0) for domestic and international students. A mandatory student services and transit contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €145 per semester applies. Substantial research contracts (TV-L E13) and DAAD grants provide strong funding.
