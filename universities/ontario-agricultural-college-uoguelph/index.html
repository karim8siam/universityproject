Ontario Agricultural College (OAC) - University of Guelph

The Ontario Agricultural College (OAC) of the University of Guelph is Canada’s historic, premier academic institution for agriculture, food innovation, environmental sciences, and rural development, consistently ranked among the top agricultural faculties worldwide.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1874—predating the University of Guelph by almost a century—the Ontario Agricultural College is an internationally acclaimed center for agri-food research, animal biosciences, soil health, and sustainable plant agriculture. OAC encompasses six academic departments and operates major research stations across Ontario in partnership with the Ontario Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (OMAFRA). Its researchers spearhead global breakthroughs in DNA barcoding, livestock genomics, precision drone agronomy, and climate-resilient crop breeding.

  • Sustainable Plant Agriculture & Crop Genomic Resilience
  • Food Science, Dairy Microbiology & Nutritional Bio-Fortification
  • Soil Health, Carbon Sequestration & Agro-Ecosystem Ecology
  • Animal Welfare, Precision Livestock Farming & Quantitative Genetics

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Plant Agriculture (Crop Breeding and Genomics) (Doctorate) — World-class doctoral research exploring marker-assisted selection, abiotic stress physiology, and gene editing in staple field and horticultural crops.
  • MSc in Food Science (Quality Assurance and Safety) (Master's) — Comprehensive master's degree focusing on thermal processing, food pathogen detection, biopolymers, and functional food development.
  • Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (Honours) (Bachelor's) — Flagship four-year honours degree offering majors in Animal Science, Crop, Horticulture and Turfgrass Science, and Honours Agricultural Business.
  • Master of Environmental Sciences (MEnvSc in Land and Water Conservation) (Master's) — Course-based or major research project degree preparing environmental scientists for watershed protection, soil remediation, and carbon accounting.
