Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OBU features the Jane E. and Nick K. Hurley College of Science and Mathematics, the Paul Dickinson College of Business, and the historic Raley Chapel. Students participate in interdisciplinary research, medical mission trips, and undergraduate honors symposia.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission evaluates high school transcripts, GPA (recommended 3.0+), and standardized test scores (test-optional available). Graduate programs require a baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited institution with minimum 2.75 GPA.
