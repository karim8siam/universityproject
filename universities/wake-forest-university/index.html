Wake Forest University

Wake Forest · Winston-Salem, North Carolina, United States

Wake Forest University is a top-30 premier private collegiate university founded in 1834, situated on a picturesque 340-acre campus in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. Governed by the motto Pro Humanitate ('For Humanity'), Wake Forest is internationally celebrated for pioneering organ 3D bioprinting at the Wake Forest Institute for Regenerative Medicine (WFIRM), law, biomedical sciences, and undergraduate teaching excellence.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Wake Forest comprises seven colleges and schools: Wake Forest College, the School of Medicine, School of Business, School of Law, Graduate School of Arts and Sciences, and the School of Divinity. The campus houses the world-famous Wake Forest Institute for Regenerative Medicine (WFIRM) in the Innovation Quarter in downtown Winston-Salem.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective (test-optional holistic review via Common App). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a high GPA (typically 3.4+), statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 95+ or IELTS 7.0+).
