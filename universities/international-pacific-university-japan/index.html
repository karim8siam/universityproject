International Pacific University: Global Sports, Education & Management Guide

International Pacific University (IPU) in Okayama is an innovative global university established by the Soshi Educational Group (with partner sister campuses in New Zealand), celebrated for athletic science, Olympic judo and sports training, teacher education, and international business management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited 4-year undergraduate university (16 years of education) for master's programs in education and physical education.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral degrees; specializes in undergraduate and master's education with international sister campuses.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 for Japanese-taught courses; TOEFL iBT 75 or IELTS 5.5 for International Leadership degree cohorts.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of athletic achievement or educational purpose (800-1,000 words).
Application fee
¥30,000 entrance examination fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school graduation diploma, complete transcripts, language test scores, athletic portfolio (if applying for sports track), and recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual undergraduate tuition is approximately ¥1,180,000 to ¥1,380,000. IPU Global Sports scholarships, international student tuition reductions (up to 50%), and Soshi Gakuen academic bursaries are awarded annually.
