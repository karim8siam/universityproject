Charleston Southern University

Charleston Southern University (CSU) is an independent comprehensive Christian liberal arts university located in North Charleston, South Carolina. Accredited by SACSCOC and affiliated with the South Carolina Baptist Convention, CSU provides over 60 undergraduate and graduate programs spanning business, nursing, engineering, computer science, and aeronautics.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Charleston, South Carolina, USA

Institutional Overview

CSU emphasizes integration of faith, intellectual inquiry, and career readiness. It hosts the College of Science and Mathematics, the Hans A. Nielsen College of Business, and South Carolina's only collegiate aeronautics program offering BS in Aeronautics degrees with FAA pilot certification. Division I athletics (NCAA Big South Conference) complement a vibrant collegiate campus life.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at CSU evaluate high school academic records, SAT/ACT scores (optional for standard admission), personal essays, and character references. International applicants submit TOEFL (min 71) or IELTS (min 6.0). Over 95% of full-time undergraduate students receive institutional aid, including President's Scholarships and athletic awards.
