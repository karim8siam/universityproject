Academy of Fine Arts Dresden (HfBK Dresden)

Founded in 1764, Hochschule für Bildende Künste Dresden is one of Europe's oldest and most prestigious visual arts institutions. Beyond classical fine arts, HfBK Dresden is renowned internationally for its pioneering degree in Conservation and Restoration of Cultural Heritage, postgraduate Art Therapy, doctoral research in art history/theory and archaeometry, and specialized heritage science laboratories. Verified degree programs and opportunities

HfBK Dresden offers tuition-free integrated diplomas, postgraduate master's, doctoral degrees (Dr. phil.), and Meisterschüler studies:

Deutschlandstipendium (National Scholarship)

Competitive merit-based scholarship providing €300 per month (€3,600/year) across both faculties supporting enrolled undergraduate, postgraduate, and Meisterschüler students.

DAAD STIBET & Graduation Scholarships

German Academic Exchange Service grant funding (€400–€900/month) administered by the International Office for degree-completing international students.

Postgraduate Master's in Art Therapy (Aufbaustudium)

Two-year specialized master's program bridging fine art practice with psychodynamic and clinical healthcare interventions for therapeutic practice in psychiatric and medical clinics.

Conservation & Restoration of Cultural Heritage

Five-year integrated diploma and master's degree training conservators in specialized workshops for easel painting, polychrome sculpture, architectural murals, and archaeometry.

Doctorate (Dr. phil.) & Meisterschüler Studies

Doctoral research degrees in art history, philosophy/aesthetics, art technology, and conservation, alongside elite 2-year post-diploma Meisterschüler studio apprenticeships.

Archaeometry & Conservation Science Research Projects

Interdisciplinary laboratory research positions applying FTIR, XRF, Raman spectroscopy, and chromatography to investigate historical pigments and cultural heritage degradation.

Museum Conservation & Restoration Practical Semester

Embedded practical training placements partnering with Staatliche Kunstsammlungen Dresden (SKD), Green Vault, Gemäldegalerie Alte Meister, and State Heritage Offices.

Student & Graduate Assistantship (SHK/WHK) Studio Pathway

Salaried student workshop positions (€13.50–€18.00/hr) assisting studio directors with printmaking labs, metal casting, digital fabrication, and archive curation.

Admissions and eligibility requirements

Art Therapy master's entry
Completed university degree in Fine Arts, Design, Psychology, Medicine, or Social Work; portfolio of artistic work; proof of at least 3 months clinical/social internship; artistic entrance exam.
Conservation & Restoration entry
Abitur or certified international equivalent secondary school certificate; mandatory one-year pre-study conservation internship (Vorpraktikum) in a museum or workshop; multi-day practical entrance exam in chemistry, drawing, and dexterity.
Doctoral (Dr. phil.) eligibility
Recognized master's degree or university diploma in Art History, Philosophy, Chemistry, or Conservation; formal written acceptance by an HfBK Dresden doctoral supervisor.
Language proficiency
German language proficiency (TestDaF or DSH-2; level C1 for Art Therapy; B2/C1 for Conservation and Fine Arts).
Application fee
€0. Application to HfBK Dresden degree programs is free of charge.
Standardized testing (GRE/GMAT)
Not required by German art academies and universities.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Higher education at public institutions in the Free State of Saxony is tuition-free (€0 tuition) for both European and non-European international students. Students only pay an administrative semester contribution of approximately €354 per semester, which includes a comprehensive regional public transit semester pass.

Enrolled students can apply for the national Deutschlandstipendium (€300/month), international student DAAD STIBET graduation grants, and academic assistantship employment across studios and laboratories.
