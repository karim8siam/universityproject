Institution Overview & Profile

The Scuola di Psicoterapia Funzionale (Società Italiana di Psicoterapia Funzionale - SIF) sede di Padova is a state-recognized postgraduate clinical specialization institute accredited by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR), delivering specialized 4-year specialization diplomas in body-mind functional psychotherapy, psychodiagnostics, and psychosomatic clinical interventions.

Institution TypeMUR-Accredited Postgraduate Specialization Institute (Scuola di Specializzazione)
Main CampusVia Vicenza Campus, Padova, Veneto
AccreditationItalian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) / National Order of Psychologists (CNOP)
Admissions RoutePsychology or Medicine university degree plus state exam registration
Language of InstructionItalian (C1 CEFR)
Financial Aid & GrantsMUR Specialization Tax Deductions & SIF Clinical Trainee Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Official MUR recognition conferring the legal qualification of Psychotherapist in Italy and the European Union
  • Pioneering Functional Psychotherapy methodology integrating emotional, cognitive, postural, and physiological functioning
  • Direct clinical placement network within Veneto regional local health authorities (ULSS) and hospital psychiatric units
  • Supervised clinical practice with video-recorded psychotherapy sessions and individual clinical supervision

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Postgraduate Clinical Specialization (MUR)

Diploma di Specializzazione quadriennale in Psicoterapia Funzionale

Four-year accredited clinical curriculum comprising 2,000 hours of theoretical instruction, experiential personal analysis, and hospital clinical internships.

Master di Alta Formazione

Master Clinico in Psicodiagnostica Funzionale e Disturbi Psicosomatici

One-year advanced training in psychophysiological stress measurement, projective testing, and psychosomatic clinical assessment.

Corso di Perfezionamento

Corso di Perfezionamento in Interventi sullo Stress e Benessere Organizzativo

Applied executive certificate focused on biofeedback, workplace burnout prevention, and organizational health protocols.

Borsa di Merito Clinico SIF Padova

Tuition fee waiver awarded to newly enrolled doctors in psychology or medicine demonstrating exceptional clinical research promise.

Course

Four-year statutory postgraduate training course qualifying graduates as licensed psychotherapists under Italian law (Legge 56/89).

Veneto Regional Hospital Clinical Psychology Practicum

500-hour annual mandatory clinical placement conducted in public hospital mental health centres (CSM) and neuro-psychiatric clinics across the Veneto region.

Course

Advanced 12-month program in psychophysiological telemetry, heart rate variability (HRV) analysis, and projective diagnostic testing.

Funding: Installment payment options and regional voucher eligibility Deadline: November 15, 2027
Grant

Annual scientific grant dedicated to founder Luciano Rispoli supporting empirical investigation into neurobiological markers of chronic stress.
