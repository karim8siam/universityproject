University of Sioux Falls

USF · Sioux Falls, South Dakota, United States

The University of Sioux Falls (USF) is a private Christian liberal arts university founded in 1883 in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. Celebrated for high-impact nursing education, exercise science, business management, and education, USF leverages its location in South Dakota's economic and healthcare capital—home to Sanford Health and Avera Health—to provide extensive clinical internships and corporate co-ops.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

USF delivers undergraduate and graduate degrees through four schools: the College of Arts and Sciences, Fredrikson School of Education, Vucurevich School of Business, and the School of Nursing. The campus features state-of-the-art nursing simulation laboratories and the McDonald Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students, evaluating high school GPA (minimum 2.6). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
