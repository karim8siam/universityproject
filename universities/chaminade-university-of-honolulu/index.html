Institutional Overview

Chaminade University of Honolulu, founded in 1955, is a distinguished private Marianist university situated on the slopes of Kalaepohaku in Honolulu, Hawaii. Celebrated for its world-class forensic sciences division, marine and environmental sciences, nursing school, and Native Hawaiian and Pacific Islander health equity initiatives, Chaminade integrates rigorous academic scholarship with community service and cultural respect.
