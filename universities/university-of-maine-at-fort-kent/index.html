University of Maine at Fort Kent

UMFK · Fort Kent, Maine, United States

The University of Maine at Fort Kent (UMFK), founded in 1878 on the Canadian border in the scenic St. John River Valley, is a regional public university celebrated for rural healthcare, conservation law enforcement, forestry, and bilingual French-Acadian cultural heritage. UMFK provides small class sizes, high-touch faculty mentorship, and experiential outdoor learning.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMFK offers associate and bachelor's degrees through the Division of Nursing, the Division of Arts and Sciences, and the Division of Professional Programs. Its campus features modern nursing clinical simulation laboratories, forestry research plots in the North Maine Woods, and the Acadian Archives / Archives acadiennes.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission operates on rolling deadlines via the UMFK direct portal or Common Application (test-optional; recommended minimum GPA 2.5). Nursing candidates must complete prerequisite high school biology and chemistry with a minimum 2.75 GPA.
