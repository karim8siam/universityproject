Australian Federation College: Business, Information Systems & Vocational Pathways

Australian Federation College (AFC) is a dynamic Australian higher education and vocational institute based in Greater Sydney, accredited by ASQA and offering nationally recognized qualifications in enterprise management, business analytics, digital marketing, and software design.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

AFC Future Business & Technology Leadership Scholarship

Merit-based financial bursary granted to prospective international students showing superior academic records and commitment to entrepreneurship or IT management.

Advanced Diploma of Business & Strategic Project Management

Two-year nationally recognized Australian qualification covering corporate strategic planning, organizational risk management, financial forecasting, and agile project delivery.

Applied Research Project in Digital Marketing Analytics & Consumer Behavior

Postgraduate research fellowship exploring algorithmic customer segmentation, predictive lifetime value models, and privacy-preserving data analytics in Australian e-commerce.

Sydney Small Business Digital Transformation & Cloud Migration Initiative

Applied enterprise research evaluating cloud SaaS adoption barriers, cyber risk posture, and automated payment gateways among Western Sydney family businesses.

Parramatta Commercial District Business Traineeship & Marketing Practicum

Twelve-week practical business work placement embedded within professional services firms, accounting agencies, and digital marketing agencies across Western Sydney.

AFC Enterprise Leadership & Innovation Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating corporate sustainability governance, ethical procurement standards, and social entrepreneurship across Australasia.

Senior Lecturer in Strategic Management & Enterprise Risk

Full-time faculty appointment responsible for designing and delivering vocational and higher education courses in corporate governance, change management, and business ethics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of recognized Bachelor's degree or advanced diploma with relevant professional work experience in commercial or technological fields.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Articulation pathways into postgraduate research with affiliated Australian university partners.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic overall band 6.0 (with no individual band score below 5.5) or PTE Academic 50.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose outlining career aspirations and alignment with Australian industry standards.
Application fee
AUD $200 international enrollment application fee.
Required documentation
Certified high school / college transcripts, passport copy, English proficiency results, and curriculum vitae.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for accredited diplomas and advanced diplomas ranges from AUD $12,500 to $16,000 annually. International students can access flexible installment payment schedules and merit-based bursaries of up to AUD $3,000.
