Institutional Overview

Université Sainte-Anne, established in 1890 on the shores of Saint Mary's Bay in Church Point (Pointe-de-l'Église), Nova Scotia, is the only French-language post-secondary institution in Nova Scotia. The university is renowned for its immersive French programs, coastal marine biology research, Acadian cultural preservation, and teacher education.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • World-renowned French immersion program with historic language contract pledge
  • Centre de recherche marine (CRM) studying lobster health, coastal ecosystems, and aquaculture
  • Centre acadien preserving international archives of Acadian diaspora history and folklore
  • Interdisciplinary environmental science, business management, and education faculties
