Hof University of Applied Sciences

Hof University of Applied Sciences is one of Bavaria's most international and green universities, celebrated for its Institute of Information Systems (iisys), Institute for Biopolymers (ibp), and specialized English-taught master's degrees in artificial intelligence and circular materials.

Academic overview & excellence

Hof University operates campuses in Hof, Münchberg, and Kronach. Münchberg is home to Bavaria's historic textile and materials engineering institute, while Hof hosts high-performance GPU compute clusters for enterprise AI and deep learning research.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate programs require higher education entrance qualifications and German competency. Master's programs offer comprehensive English-taught tracks requiring an accredited bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline (GPA >= 2.5 on German scale) and English proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90).

State of Bavaria funded scholarship rewarding international students with outstanding academic records enrolled in technical master's degrees.

Master of Science in Applied Artificial Intelligence (English-Taught)

Three-semester international master's degree focusing on deep neural networks, computer vision, natural language processing, and industrial edge computing.

Funding: Institute of Information Systems (iisys) student research assistantships: €1,100/month Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: January 15, 2027
PhD

Doctoral Fellowship in Biopolymers & Circular Plastics Degradation

Three-year research fellowship at the Institute for Biopolymers (ibp) synthesizing biodegradable PHA polyesters and studying marine compostability kinetics.

Funding: Salaried TV-L E13 research fellowship (€48,000/annum) co-supervised with University of Bayreuth Tuition: €0 Deadline: March 1, 2027
Fellowship

Fraunhofer Center for High Temperature Materials (HTL) Research Residency

Postgraduate fellowship at the Fraunhofer application center in Münchberg developing carbon-fiber-reinforced silicon carbide composites for aerospace thermal protection.

Funding: €1,800 monthly living stipend and access to pilot-scale ceramic composite sintering furnaces Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 1, 2027
Internship

Twenty-week practical engineering term testing brushless DC motors, automotive door actuators, and lightweight composite seat structures.

Funding: €1,200 monthly corporate internship stipend placed at Brose Coburg/Bamberg Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 1, 2027
Bachelor's

Seven-semester Bachelor of Engineering at the historic Münchberg textile campus, teaching smart electronic textiles, 3D weaving, medical compression fibers, and composite reinforcement.

Funding: Bavarian textile industry association scholarships (€2,000/year) Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: July 15, 2027
