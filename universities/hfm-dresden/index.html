Hochschule für Musik Carl Maria von Weber Dresden

The Hochschule für Musik Carl Maria von Weber Dresden is one of Europe's most storied state music universities, deeply tied to the Saxon State Orchestra (Staatskapelle Dresden) and the Dresden Philharmonic, educating world-class soloists, orchestral musicians, opera singers, and sound directors.

Academic overview & excellence

HfM Dresden provides elite bachelor's, master's, and concert exam (Meisterklasse) programs across orchestral instruments, historical performance practice, jazz/rock/pop, composition, and sound engineering (Tonmeister). Students benefit from world-caliber rehearsal concert halls and acoustic recording suites.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission is strictly based on performance examinations and artistic auditions. Bachelor's applicants require general higher education entrance qualification (Abitur or equivalent) and German language proficiency (TestDaF level 3 or B2), while Master's applicants must hold an accredited music bachelor's and pass an advanced audition.

Verified opportunities & funding programs (7)

Scholarship

HfM Dresden International Masterclass Artist Scholarship

Merit scholarship awarded to top auditioning international instrumentalists and vocalists entering graduate studies at HfM Dresden.

Funding: €4,800 annual stipend plus Meisterklasse rehearsal studio privileges Tuition: €0 state tuition (semester contribution €310 covering regional transit) Deadline: March 1, 2027
Master's

Master of Music in Orchestral Instrument Performance

Two-year elite performance master's with direct audition training alongside musicians of the Sächsische Staatskapelle Dresden and Dresden Philharmonic.

Funding: Erasmus+ and DAAD graduate support eligible Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: March 15, 2027
PhD

Meisterklassenexamen (Concert Exam / Highest Soloist Diploma)

Two-year postgraduate concert soloist program—the highest artistic qualification awarded by German music universities, culminating in two public concerto performances.

Funding: Saxony state postgraduate arts scholarship (€900/month) Tuition: €0 tuition Deadline: April 1, 2027
Fellowship

Dresden Sound Engineering & Tonmeister Fellowship

Specialized acoustic and recording fellowship for graduate Tonmeister sound directors producing live concert broadcasts and orchestral recordings.

Funding: €7,200 annual fellowship stipend and direct studio engineering access Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 15, 2027
Internship

Staatskapelle Dresden Orchestral Academy Internship

Ten-month professional orchestral academy placement performing in rehearsals, symphony concerts, and opera productions at the Semperoper Dresden.

Funding: €1,100 monthly academy stipend plus participation in Semperoper productions Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 1, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Music in Composition and Music Theory

Four-year comprehensive undergraduate degree in contemporary classical composition, instrumentation, counterpoint, and electronic music synthesis.

Funding: Deutschlandstipendium (€300/month) eligible upon enrollment Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: March 1, 2027
Course

Certificate in Historical Performance & Baroque Violin Practice

One-semester intensive masterclass in 17th- and 18th-century Saxon court music, gut string ornamentation, and basso continuo realization.

Funding: Heinrich Schütz Heritage Fund bursary available Tuition: €1,200 total certificate fee Deadline: May 30, 2027
