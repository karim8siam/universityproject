Polo Universitario eCampus Ceglie Messapica

Polo Universitario eCampus Ceglie Messapica is an accredited university educational centre situated in the Valle d'Itria in Brindisi, Puglia. Representing Università Telematica eCampus, the centre provides accredited degree programs across economics, engineering, letters, law, psychology, and motor sciences, combined with on-site academic advising and student tutoring.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Ceglie Messapica, Brindisi, Italy

Institutional Overview

Students at eCampus Ceglie Messapica access 24/7 digital lectures delivered by renowned Italian professors alongside in-person exam sessions and individual faculty mentoring. The curriculum adheres strictly to Italian ministerial requirements (D.M. 509/99 and D.M. 270/04), enabling students to earn recognized degrees while maintaining professional and personal commitments.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions are open throughout the year with no rigid intake windows. Candidates require an Italian secondary school diploma (Maturità) or recognized foreign equivalent. Tuition fees range between €2,000 and €3,900 annually, with regional financial aid, agreements with public sector unions, and merit-based tuition discounts available.
