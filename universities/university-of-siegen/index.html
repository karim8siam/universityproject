Academic overview & research priorities

Operating across multiple vibrant campuses in Siegen—including the Adolf-Reichwein-Straße campus and the historic Unteres Schloss city center—the University of Siegen conducts world-leading interdisciplinary research across five faculties. Siegen is famous for hosting European Research Council (ERC) laureates in theoretical quantum optics and physical chemistry, and is an international hub for small and medium-sized enterprise (Mittelstand) digital transformation.

Research admissions & international criteria

International master's programs in Nanoscience, Mechatronics, and Computer Science are conducted in English, requiring an accredited bachelor's degree in a relevant technical field (German equivalent 2.5 or better) and English proficiency (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 88+). Doctoral applicants apply directly to faculty research chairs with a comprehensive dissertation proposal.
