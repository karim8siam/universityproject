Technasium Duiven - Candea College STEM Research Academy

Technasium Duiven at Candea College in the Arnhem-Nijmegen region is an accredited Dutch Technasium STEM academy, renowned for project-based engineering, design technology, and university research partnerships with Radboud University and Wageningen University.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating under the official Dutch Technasium network certification, Technasium Duiven delivers the specialized curriculum 'Onderzoeken & Ontwerpen' (O&O - Research & Design). Students work in high-technology design studios equipped with 3D printers, CNC milling machines, laser cutters, and biological incubators. Real-world technological challenges are commissioned by regional multinational engineering firms, environmental water boards, and university laboratories. Key research domains include circular packaging design, smart microgrid automation, and agricultural biotechnology.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to the Technasium stream at Candea College requires a primary school VWO or HAVO recommendation with proven scientific curiosity, spatial reasoning, and teamwork aptitude. International students entering the academy must present verified transcripts demonstrating high performance in Mathematics and Natural Sciences, accompanied by an admissions interview and English or Dutch language capability.
