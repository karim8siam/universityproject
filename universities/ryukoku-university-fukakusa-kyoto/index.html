Ryukoku University

Ryukoku University is one of Japan's most historic private universities, established in Kyoto in 1639 as a Buddhist seminary, now renowned for economics, transnational law, international intercultural studies, and science and technology.

Academic overview & research priorities

Centered at the Fukakusa campus in Fushimi-ku, Kyoto (with campuses at Omiya and Seta in Shiga), Ryukoku University integrates 385 years of Buddhist humanist heritage with comprehensive modern scientific and social inquiry. The Fukakusa campus houses the Faculties of Economics, Business Administration, Law, Policy Science, and International Studies, alongside celebrated institutes for peace research and world Buddhist cultures.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate international applicants take the Special Entrance Examination for International Students including EJU scores, written essay, and Japanese oral interview. Graduate school admissions require an accredited 16-year bachelor's degree, comprehensive research proposal (2,000 words in Japanese or English), official university transcripts, and faculty interview. International applicants must show JLPT N1/N2 or TOEFL iBT 80+.
