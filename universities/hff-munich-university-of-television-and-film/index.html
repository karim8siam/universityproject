University of Television and Film Munich

The University of Television and Film Munich (Hochschule für Fernsehen und Film München - HFF München) is Germany's preeminent film school, having produced Academy Award winners, celebrated directors, and leaders of the global cinematic industry.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1966 and situated in a state-of-the-art cinematic complex at Bernd-Eichinger-Platz in Munich's Kunstareal, HFF Munich offers prestigious diploma curricula across Fiction Feature Directing, Documentary Directing, Cinematography, Screenwriting, and Creative Production. Notable alumni include Academy Award winners Florian Henckel von Donnersmarck (The Lives of Others), Caroline Link (Nowhere in Africa), Wim Wenders, and Roland Emmerich.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission is extraordinarily competitive, with acceptance rates often below 3%. Applicants undergo a multi-stage admission procedure comprising creative portfolio submission, artistic film treatments, photographic essays, and intense multi-day practical filmmaking assessments. Under Bavarian higher education regulations, degree studies are tuition-free.
