Institutional Overview

Università degli Studi Giustino Fortunato, legally recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research, operates a major university hub in Padova (Veneto). Renowned for its prestigious Faculty of Law, innovative Bachelor in Aeronautical Sciences and Air Transportation, psychology, and education degrees, UniFortunato combines flexible digital learning with on-site seminars and scientific conferences.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Unique Bachelor of Science in Aviation and Aerospace Transportation Management
  • Accredited five-year single-cycle Master of Laws (Laurea Magistrale a Ciclo Unico in Giurisprudenza)
  • High-level research center in behavioural sciences, clinical psychology, and neuroethics
  • Modern blended educational model with personalized e-tutors and live workshops in Padova

Featured Academic Degrees & Curricula

Bachelor's Degree

Laurea Triennale in Scienze e Tecnologie dei Trasporti Aeronautici

Specialized three-year degree training flight operations managers, airport directors, air navigation controllers, and aerospace logistics specialists.

Master's Degree

Laurea Magistrale a Ciclo Unico in Giurisprudenza

Five-year qualifying law degree covering European civil law, criminal procedure, administrative justice, and international corporate jurisprudence.

Master's Degree

Laurea Magistrale in Psicologia Comportamentale e Forense

Two-year postgraduate degree preparing licensed psychologists in forensic diagnostics, investigative psychology, and clinical interventions.
