Fontys Venlo: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Fontys Venlo University of Applied Sciences is the international business, engineering, and logistics campus of Fontys Hogescholen, strategically situated in Venlo near the Dutch-German border. Venlo is Europe's premier agro-logistics hotspot; Fontys Venlo is renowned for applied supply chain management, autonomous greenhouse robotics, green technology, and applied software engineering.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in International Logistics and Supply Chain Management (Joint with Plymouth University)

Dual-degree master's program delivered with the University of Plymouth, providing mastery in global freight forwarding, port logistics, supply chain digitization, and warehouse simulation.

GreenTech Campus Applied Software Engineering & Agri-Food Automation Internship

Five-month full-time industrial internship programming embedded vision systems, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), and cloud monitoring for indoor smart farming in the Venlo Greenport cluster.

Fontys Venlo International Talent Tuition Award (NL Scholarship Program)

Scholarship financed by the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science in cooperation with Fontys, awarded to exceptional non-EEA students entering degree programs at the Venlo campus.

Center of Expertise in Agro-Logistics and Circular Supply Chain Systems Research Practicum

Six-month research practicum modeling food waste reduction, multimodal freight switching, and returnable packaging networks at Fresh Park Venlo, one of the largest fresh produce distribution centers in Europe.

Master of Science in Applied Artificial Intelligence and Enterprise Digital Transformation

Applied master's program focusing on edge AI models for industrial robotics, automated optical quality inspection, enterprise generative assistants, and cyber-resilient software architectures.

Applied Industrial Research Fellowship in Autonomous Cold-Chain Logistics and Robotics

Two-year applied researcher contract building automated temperature-controlled storage systems, robotic pallet decanting, and IoT sensor networks for pharmaceutical and fresh food distribution.

External Collaborative Doctoral Candidacy in Agri-Food Circular Packaging and Supply Chain Resilience

Four-year joint doctoral trajectory conducted between Fontys Venlo and a Dutch research university partner, investigating bio-composite shipping containers, lifecycle analysis, and reusable logistics packaging.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A relevant Bachelor of Science degree or recognized equivalent in logistics, engineering, computer science, or business administration.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Universities of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands conduct collaborative external PhD programs in partnership with research universities (e.g., TU Eindhoven, Maastricht University).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in each sub-score).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-3 page Letter of Motivation and professional study plan outlining applied project ideas, corporate internship objectives, and career trajectory.
Application fee
€0 application fee via Studielink.nl.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, curriculum vitae, copy of valid passport, and proof of English language proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students at Fontys Venlo is approximately €9,300 to €11,500 per academic year (EU/EEA students pay the statutory Dutch tuition of approximately €2,530 per year). International students are eligible for the Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (€5,000 one-time award) and Fontys Talent Scholarships, as well as paid Dutch industry internships paying €800 to €1,400 per month.
