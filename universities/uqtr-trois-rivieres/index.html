Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières (UQTR)

The Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières is a premier francophone research institution recognized globally for its Hydrogen Research Institute (IRH), pulp and paper bio-refinery engineering, and clinical chiropractic and forensic sciences.

Academic overview & excellence

UQTR conducts ground-breaking applied research in renewable energy storage, green hydrogen technologies, and neurobiology. With modern engineering cleanrooms and clinical training hospitals, UQTR attracts international graduate scholars across STEM and health fields.

Research admissions & criteria

Graduate admissions require a recognized undergraduate degree (Baccalauréat) with a minimum cumulative average of 3.2/4.3 (or European equivalent). As the primary language of instruction is French, candidates must provide proof of French proficiency (TFI 750 or DELF/DALF B2/C1), while doctoral theses in engineering can be written in English.
