Yokohama College of Commerce - Tsurumi Campus

Yokohama College of Commerce (Shodai) is a specialized business university located in Tsurumi, Yokohama, renowned for its practical curriculum in maritime logistics, international trade, tourism management, and business informatics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Strategically situated near the Port of Yokohama—Japan’s premier international trading hub—Shodai educates future commercial leaders through its Faculty of Commerce. Specialized tracks focus on international trade law, port logistics supply chains, retail marketing, and hospitality tourism.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate Japanese language proficiency (JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese 200+ for international applicants), high school academic transcripts, and an in-person admissions interview.
