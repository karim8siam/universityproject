Montana Christian College

Montana Christian · Kalispell, Montana, United States

Montana Christian College is an accredited Christian college in Kalispell, Montana, situated in the Flathead Valley near Glacier National Park. Specializing in biblical studies, pastoral leadership, Christian counseling, and wilderness education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Montana Christian College offers accredited associate and bachelor's degrees accredited by ABHE. Highlights include biblical hermeneutics, experiential outdoor ministry training, and faith-based community leadership practica.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require a completed application, official high school transcripts or GED (minimum 2.0 GPA), personal statement of faith and life goals, and a pastoral or character endorsement.
