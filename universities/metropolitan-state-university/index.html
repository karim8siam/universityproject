Metropolitan State University

Metro State · St. Paul, Minnesota, United States

Metropolitan State University (Metro State) is an urban comprehensive public university founded in 1971 in St. Paul and Minneapolis, Minnesota. A member of the Minnesota State Colleges and Universities system, Metro State is acclaimed for cybersecurity and computer science—hosting Minnesota's premier Cyber Range—nursing, dental hygiene, community psychology, and business administration, serving a diverse adult learner population in the Twin Cities.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Metro State operates through five colleges: the College of Sciences, College of Nursing and Health Sciences, College of Management, College of Liberal Arts, and the School of Urban Education. The university operates the Minnesota Cyber Range and the Science Education Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school diplomas or prior college transfer credits with accessible, test-optional admissions. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+). Graduate scholarships and assistantships are available.
