Northern Michigan University

Northern Michigan · Marquette, Michigan, United States

Northern Michigan University is a comprehensive public university situated on the shores of Lake Superior in Marquette, Michigan. Renowned for environmental science, outdoor recreation leadership, frozen forensic anthropology, medicinal plant chemistry, and wildlife biology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NMU houses the College of Arts and Sciences and College of Technology and Occupational Sciences. World-class facilities include the FROST (Forensic Research Outdoor Station) facility, the native cold-climate forest test plots, and the Superior Dome.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.25; test-blind holistic review). Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, personal goals statement, and letters of recommendation.
