Belmont University: Music Business, Healthcare & Pharmacy Guide

Belmont University is a nationally ranked, premier comprehensive private Christian university situated on a historic 93-acre campus in Nashville, Tennessee. Renowned globally as the world's leading institution for music industry education through the Mike Curb College of Entertainment and Music Business, Belmont also boasts top-tier programs in healthcare, pharmacy, nursing, law, and business. With cutting-edge recording studios on Music Row, the Thomas F. Frist Jr. College of Medicine, and high graduate employment, Belmont is an intellectual and cultural catalyst in the Southeast.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited U.S. college or recognized international institution with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0; prerequisite coursework required for health science and business programs.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) requires completed pharmacy prerequisites and minimum 2.5 science GPA; Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) and Doctor of Occupational Therapy (OTD) require competitive GRE and clinical observation hours.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 overall or IELTS Academic 6.5 overall for international applicants whose native language is not English.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate applications: 500-word personal essay detailing professional aspirations, ethical leadership alignment, and specific reasons for selecting Belmont.
Application fee
$50 USD application fee ($100 USD for law and medicine).
Required documentation
Official post-secondary transcripts, certified translations, two letters of recommendation, resume, personal statement, and official English test score report.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition is approximately $41,600 USD per academic year. Graduate tuition ranges from $1,150 to $1,550 USD per credit hour depending on the specific professional school. Belmont awards over $100 million USD in institutional scholarships and grants annually, including Curb Entertainment Fellowships and merit scholarships.
