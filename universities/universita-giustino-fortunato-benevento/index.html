Università Giustino Fortunato: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 2006 by Ministerial Decree and named after the renowned southern Italian historian and statesman Giustino Fortunato, the university is headquartered in Benevento with key learning and examination centres in Milan, Rome, Catania, and Padua. UniFortunato is widely celebrated as Italy’s leading academic institution for Aviation and Air Transport Management, operating accredited curricula in aerodrome law, airline economics, and flight safety human factors in partnership with the Italian Air Force and civil aviation authorities.
