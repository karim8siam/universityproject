Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The University of Connecticut - Waterbury campus, located in the heart of downtown Waterbury, connects the resources of an R1 flagship research university with intimate classroom cohorts. UConn Waterbury delivers full four-year degrees in allied health, psychology, digital media and design, and business data analytics, alongside gateway engineering research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma with rigorous preparatory curriculum. UConn Common Application or Coalition Application with standardized test-optional review. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international applicants. Priority fall admission January 15, 2027; Regular fall deadline March 1, 2027; Spring semester deadline October 15, 2026.
