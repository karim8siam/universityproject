Facoltà Teologica Pugliese: Mediterranean Hermeneutics, Patristics & Philosophy Guide

Facoltà Teologica Pugliese is a pontifical academic institution established by the Holy See in Apulia, with major campuses in Bari and Molfetta, celebrated for ecumenical theology, Patristic scholarship, and Mediterranean dialogue.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Ecclesiastical Baccalaureate in Theology or equivalent academic degree in philosophy or humanities for Licentiate admission.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Canonical Licentiate in Sacred Theology (S.T.L.) with a grade of Magna cum Laude (or equivalent minimum 8.5/10).
Language proficiency
Italian language fluency (B2/C1) and working knowledge of classical languages (Latin, Biblical Greek, or Hebrew) for advanced theological research.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research prospectus detailing bibliographic sources and hermeneutical methodology.
Application fee
€80 canonical administrative registration fee.
Required documentation
Ecclesiastical or civil academic transcripts, baptismal or civil identification certificate, and letter of presentation from ecclesiastical authority or academic supervisor.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Academic tuition is highly accessible, averaging €1,200 to €2,400 annually for canonical degree programs. Diocesan bursaries, Holy See international student grants, and regional study scholarships are provided.
