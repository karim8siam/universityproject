University of Nevada, Reno

UNR · Reno, Nevada, United States

The University of Nevada, Reno (UNR) is the historic land-grant Carnegie R1 public flagship research university of Nevada, established in 1874. Situated in the Sierra Nevada foothills, UNR is globally renowned for earthquake and structural engineering—operating the world's largest multi-shake table earthquake simulation laboratory—as well as mining engineering, lithium battery materials, neurosciences, and atmospheric science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNR comprises 11 colleges and schools including the College of Engineering, Mackay School of Earth Sciences and Engineering, University of Nevada Reno School of Medicine, College of Agriculture, Biotechnology & Natural Resources, and the College of Science. Key hubs include the Earthquake Engineering Laboratory and the Nevada Seismological Laboratory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 3.0 core) and college prep curriculum. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
