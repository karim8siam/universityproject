Sophia University Yotsuya Campus

Sophia University (上智大学 - Jōchi Daigaku), located in the heart of Tokyo at Yotsuya Campus across from the Imperial Akasaka Estate, is Japan's premier international Catholic Jesuit research university, celebrated globally for multilingual education, global studies, and diplomacy.

Academic overview & excellence

Sophia was Japan's first university to introduce all-English undergraduate and graduate degree programs in Global Studies and Green Science. Its nine undergraduate faculties and ten graduate schools emphasize ethics, sustainability, international development, and global governance.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate English-taught programs require SAT/ACT or IB credentials and English proficiency (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 83+). Japanese-taught tracks utilize EJU or standard university entrance examinations. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, research proposal, and letters of recommendation.
