Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

NCOI Hogeschool (NCOI University of Applied Sciences) is the largest provider of accredited higher professional education for working adults and corporate executives in the Netherlands. NVAO-accredited, NCOI offers practice-oriented Bachelor's, Master's, and Executive MBA degrees structured for full-time professionals across the Netherlands.

Institution type
Private Accredited University of Applied Sciences (Aangewezen Hogeschool)
Campus location
Headquarters in Hilversum with over 30 executive teaching locations across the Netherlands
Accreditation
NVAO (Accreditation Organisation of the Netherlands and Flanders) / Ministry of OCW
Admissions channel
Monthly flexible starting moments throughout the calendar year
Instruction language
Dutch (selected executive masterclasses in English)
Financial aid & support
Dutch Government Lifelong Learning Credit (Levenlanglerenkrediet), Employer Tax Subsidies

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Largest accredited educator for working professionals in the Netherlands (over 40,000 active students)
  • Fully accredited by NVAO with state-recognized HBO Bachelor and Master titles
  • Executive MBA tracks with specializations in Big Data, Digital Strategy, and Healthcare Management
  • Practical modular examination based on real-world employer business cases rather than rote testing

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Executive Master of Business Administration (MBA) (Executive Master's) — Two-year NVAO-accredited management program combining corporate financial strategy, leadership psychology, and digital business transformation.
  • Master of Health Administration (MHA) (Professional Master's) — Specialized healthcare governance degree preparing medical directors and hospital managers for integrated care and cost efficiency.
  • HBO Bachelor Bedrijfskunde (Business Administration) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year flexible work-study bachelor's covering organizational design, supply chain optimization, and commercial strategy.
