St. Catherine University

St. Catherine · St. Paul, Minnesota, United States

St. Catherine University ('St. Kate's') is a prestigious private Catholic liberal arts university operating campuses in St. Paul and Minneapolis, Minnesota. Founded in 1905 by the Sisters of St. Joseph of Carondelet, it is home to one of the largest private women's colleges in the nation alongside comprehensive co-educational graduate programs. Acclaimed nationwide for the Henriette Schmoll School of Health, St. Kate's is a pioneering powerhouse in occupational therapy, physical therapy, nursing, and public health.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

St. Kate's comprises the College for Women and the Graduate College, housing the Henriette Schmoll School of Health, the School of Humanities, Arts, and Sciences, and the School of Business. It features cutting-edge pediatric and adult occupational therapy simulation clinics, high-fidelity nursing hospital suites, and the National Center for STEM Elementary Education.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate women's college admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), coursework rigor, and leadership on a test-optional basis. Graduate health programs (OTD, DPT, PA, MPH) are co-educational, requiring centralized applications (OTCAS, PTCAS, CASPA), prerequisite science courses with a 3.0+ GPA, clinical observation hours, and professional recommendations.
