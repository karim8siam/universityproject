Staatliche Studienakademie Riesa - Berufsakademie Sachsen

BA Riesa · Riesa, Saxony, Germany

Staatliche Studienakademie Riesa, transitioning into the Duale Hochschule Sachsen (DHS), offers university-level dual bachelor degree programs integrating academic theory with corporate industrial employment.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Specialized in mechanical engineering, energy engineering, biotechnology, and event management, students alternate quarterly between academic seminars in Riesa and paid professional employment with partner industrial enterprises.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires Allgemeine Hochschulreife, Fachhochschulreife, or vocational master craftsman certification, combined with a valid dual training contract with an approved partner company.
