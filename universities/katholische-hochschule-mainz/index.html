Katholische Hochschule Mainz: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1971 and situated in the university city of Mainz, Katholische Hochschule Mainz (KH Mainz) accommodates over 1,500 students across its Faculties of Social Work and Social Sciences, Health and Nursing, and Practical Theology. Recognized for small learning cohorts, high professor-to-student ratios, and extensive clinical networks, KH Mainz addresses critical societal challenges including demographic ageing, child protection, palliative care, and ethical healthcare leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Doctoral admission eligibility
