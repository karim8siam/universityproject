A.MI. University - Libera Università Internazionale delle Discipline Bionaturali

A.MI. University is a pioneering Italian educational institute established in 1984 in Milan, dedicated to certified professional training, bio-energetic research, and international qualifications in bionatural disciplines and integrative wellness.

Academic overview & excellence

Recognized as a leading Italian institution in bionatural disciplines in accordance with Lombardy Regional Law 2/2005, A.MI. University provides comprehensive clinical training in prano-practice, reflexology, traditional naturopathy, and holistic biofield assessments. Headquartered in Milan, the university conducts empirical bio-physical investigations into human bio-electromagnetism and cellular vibrational therapies.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to higher diplomas and research certificates requires a high school diploma (Diploma di Maturità) or equivalent foreign secondary qualification. Master-level and clinical specialization tracks require prior university study in health sciences, biology, sports sciences, or psychology. Fluency in Italian is required for clinical practicums.
