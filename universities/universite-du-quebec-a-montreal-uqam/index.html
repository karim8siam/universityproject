University of Quebec in Montreal: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1969 as the urban cornerstone of the Université du Québec network, UQAM educates more than 35,000 students across its Central and Science Campuses in Montreal’s Quartier Latin and Quartier des Spectacles. UQAM is celebrated for interdisciplinary research excellence through its School of Management (ESG UQAM), the Faculty of Science, and the Institute of Environmental Sciences. With vibrant ties to Montreal’s tech cluster, UQAM conducts internationally funded research in machine learning, cognitive psychology, hydro-climatology, and feminist sociology.
