Academic overview & research priorities

Tracing its heritage back to 1888 when it was founded by the Jodo Pure Land Buddhist denomination in Nagoya, Tokaigakuen University operates campuses in Nagoya (Tenpaku Ward) and Miyoshi City. Its Faculty of Human Health is recognized throughout central Japan for training elite registered dietitians and sports wellness specialists, while the Faculty of Management leads research in automotive supply chain business, digital marketing, and regional entrepreneurship.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to undergraduate degree programs requires a certified high school completion certificate, Japanese language competence (JLPT N2 or higher), submission of academic records, and an interview. Graduate admissions to the Graduate School of Management or Health require a recognized bachelor's degree, comprehensive research proposal, and interview with prospective faculty supervisors.
