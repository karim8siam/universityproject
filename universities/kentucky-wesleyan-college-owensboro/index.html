Kentucky Wesleyan College

Kentucky Wesleyan · Owensboro, Kentucky, United States

Kentucky Wesleyan College is a private Methodist-affiliated liberal arts college in Owensboro, Kentucky. Established in 1858, KWC is celebrated for pre-veterinary and pre-medical zoology, criminal justice, business administration, and civic leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

KWC offers distinctive undergraduate programs combining liberal arts foundations with pre-professional internships. Signature disciplines include the Department of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, the Ralph Center for the Arts, and undergraduate honors research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic, evaluating high school transcripts (average GPA 3.4), course rigor, and personal character under test-optional review. Pre-professional health tracks require strong secondary chemistry and mathematics foundations.
