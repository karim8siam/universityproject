University of Massachusetts Office of the President

UMass System · Boston, Massachusetts, United States

The University of Massachusetts Office of the President directs Massachusetts's flagship public university system, encompassing five world-class campuses: UMass Amherst, UMass Boston, UMass Dartmouth, UMass Lowell, and the UMass Chan Medical School in Worcester. Generating over $800 million in annual research, the UMass System is a global powerhouse in artificial intelligence, RNA interference therapeutics, and clean energy.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through individual UMass campuses (Amherst, Boston, Dartmouth, Lowell, Chan Medical). Competitive review of secondary school GPA, curriculum rigor, and extracurricular achievement. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ (100+ for graduate programs) for international candidates. Early action priority deadline November 5, 2026; Regular decision deadline January 15, 2027.
