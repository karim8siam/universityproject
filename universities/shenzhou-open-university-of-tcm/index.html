Institution Overview & Profile

Shenzhou Open University of TCM, established in 1991 in Amsterdam, is Europe's premier educational institution for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). Delivering comprehensive, English-taught and Dutch-taught diplomas in Acupuncture, Chinese Herbal Medicine, Tuina massage, and Western Basic Medicine, Shenzhou is fully accredited by Dutch professional healthcare associations (Zhong, NVA, NWP) and affiliated with premier TCM universities in Beijing and Guangzhou.

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Professional Higher Diploma

Diploma in Acupuncture (Three-Year Professional Program)

Three-year curriculum covering meridian theory, point location, pulse and tongue diagnosis, moxibustion, cupping, and clinical internships.

Postgraduate Diploma

Diploma in Chinese Herbal Medicine

Two-year specialized course exploring over 300 herbal substances, classical formulas, herbal safety, and pharmacology.

Accredited Foundation Diploma

Western Basic Medicine (WBM - Westerse Medische Basiskennis)

CPION/Plato-accredited foundation in anatomy, physiology, and pathology required for health insurance recognition in the Netherlands.
