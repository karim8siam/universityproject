University of Nebraska-Lincoln Kimmel Education and Research Center

UNL Kimmel Center · Nebraska City, Nebraska, United States

The University of Nebraska-Lincoln Kimmel Education and Research Center, located in historic Nebraska City, is a specialized field research facility operated by UNL Extension and the Institute of Agriculture and Natural Resources (IANR). Encompassing active research orchards, experimental vineyards, and demonstration facilities, the center advances sustainable fruit production, agroforestry, conservation biology, and rural economic development across the Great Plains.
