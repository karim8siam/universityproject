Leipzig University: Global Studies, Leibniz Physics & Doctoral Excellence

Founded in 1409, Leipzig University (Universität Leipzig) is one of the world's oldest continuously operating universities. Renowned for alumni like Goethe, Leibniz, and Angela Merkel, Uni Leipzig is a powerhouse in quantum physics, global and European studies, bioinformatics, and cognitive neuroscience.

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

German Research Foundation (DFG) Doctoral Fellowship in Quantum Matter (TV-L E13)

Fully salaried doctoral research investigating correlated electron phenomena, 2D van der Waals materials, and quantum phase transitions at the Felix Bloch Institute.

Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree (EMJMD) in Global Studies

Prestigious joint international master's program coordinated by Leipzig University with London School of Economics, Vienna, Roskilde, and Wroclaw.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Bioinformatics & Computational Genomics

English-taught master's exploring non-coding RNA algorithms, phylogenetic reconstruction, deep learning for proteomics, and structural biology.

Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology Collaborative Research Assistantship

Cooperative research vacancy alongside the Nobel Prize-winning MPI team analyzing hominin paleogenomics, Neanderthal admixture, and prehistoric human migrations.

Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ) Catchment Ecology Practicum

Applied research practicum at the UFZ Leipzig monitoring aquatic micropollutants, biodiversity decline in agricultural catchments, and wetland restoration.

Centre for the Study of Culture and Societal Change Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating post-socialist transformation, regional socio-economic polarization, and memory politics in Central and Eastern Europe.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Physics with Specialization in Soft Matter & Biophysics

Flagship International Physics Studies Program (IPSP) taught entirely in English exploring polymer physics, biological membranes, single-molecule mechanics, and statistical mechanics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in a corresponding discipline with an above-average final grade (equivalent to German grade 2.5 or better).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's degree or Diplom with academic distinction ('magna cum laude' or better), accompanied by formal supervisor acceptance (Betreuungszusage).
Language proficiency
English-taught programs require CEFR B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 90+); German-taught programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral candidates: detailed 5–10 page research exposé outlining theoretical foundation, empirical methodology, and projected dissertation schedule.
Application fee
€0 for direct admission portal / €75 for uni-assist preliminary review where required.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificate, diploma supplement, transcript of records, CV, letter of motivation, and language certificates.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 100% free for all domestic and international students at Leipzig University. Students only pay a semester social contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €265 per semester, which includes a complete regional public transit pass for Central Germany (MDV network).
