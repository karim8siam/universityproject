Minnesota State University Moorhead

MSU Moorhead · Moorhead, Minnesota, United States

Minnesota State University Moorhead is a comprehensive public university in Moorhead, Minnesota, across the Red River from Fargo, North Dakota. Renowned for its 3D animation, biotechnology, nursing, and education programs within the Fargo-Moorhead tri-college consortium.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MSUM features the School of Art (acclaimed 3D animation and film production), the Paseka School of Business, and the Department of Bioscience. It participates in the tri-college university agreement allowing students to cross-register at North Dakota State University.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (automatic admission with 3.0+ GPA or top 50% class rank) under test-optional review. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA and letters of recommendation.
