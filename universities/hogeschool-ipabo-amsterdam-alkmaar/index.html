Hogeschool IPABO (Amsterdam & Alkmaar): Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Dedicated exclusively to initial and continuing teacher education (PABO - Pedagogische Academie voor het Basisonderwijs), Hogeschool IPABO educates future educators across campuses in Amsterdam and Alkmaar. Consistently receiving top ratings in the Dutch National Student Survey (NSE) and Keuzegids Hbo, IPABO is renowned for its Centre for Educational Leadership, inclusive urban multilingual classroom strategies, and pioneering research into neurodiversity and STEM education in primary schools.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Havo, Vwo, or Mbo level 4 diploma; national PABO entrance knowledge tests in Geography, History, and Science
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Dutch (instructional language) with specialized international English modules
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
€0
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
