Academic overview & research priorities

Located in West Phoenix, GCU operates modern colleges in Science, Engineering, and Technology, Nursing and Health Care Professions, and Business. The university features advanced biomedical anatomy labs, an engineering cyber-warfare range, and extensive athletic facilities.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with unweighted GPA >= 3.00 (or 2.50+ with qualifying test score). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree with 2.80+ GPA. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 68+ or IELTS 6.0+.
