North Platte Community College

NPCC · North Platte, Nebraska, United States

North Platte Community College is a public two-year college in North Platte, Nebraska, and a primary campus of Mid-Plains Community College (MPCC). Celebrated for electrical technology, diesel power, welding, associate degree nursing, and agriculture.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NPCC operates the North Campus and South Campus in North Platte, housing modern technical centers for diesel mechanics, electrical utility linework, and simulation healthcare labs. Serves an 18-county rural footprint in west-central Nebraska.

Research admissions & international qualifications

NPCC maintains an open-door admissions policy for all high school graduates or GED completers. Health science and technical programs have program-specific safety and academic prerequisite guidelines.
