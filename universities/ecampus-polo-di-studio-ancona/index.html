Polo di Studio eCampus Ancona

Polo di Studio eCampus Ancona is an accredited regional higher education center in the Marche region, delivering flexible digital and in-person tutoring across economics, engineering, law, psychology, and educational sciences.

Academic overview & excellence

eCampus Ancona provides MIUR-accredited bachelor's and master's degree courses with 24/7 digital learning platforms combined with on-site exam sessions, dissertation defenses, and regional professional development seminars.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a recognized high school diploma (Maturità) for first-cycle degrees or a relevant bachelor's degree for postgraduate degrees. Year-round modular rolling admissions are supported with individualized prior learning credit assessments (CFU validation).
