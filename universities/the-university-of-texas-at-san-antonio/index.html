The University of Texas at San Antonio

UTSA · San Antonio, Texas, United States

The University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA) is an elite Carnegie R1 public research university and designated Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) located in San Antonio, Texas. Globally celebrated as a national powerhouse in cybersecurity, cloud computing, and data science, UTSA houses the National Security Collaboration Center (NSCC) and the School of Data Science, driving over $150M in annual research expenditures.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UTSA delivers accredited academic programs across seven colleges, including the Klesse College of Engineering and Integrated Design, the College of Sciences, and the Carlos Alvarez College of Business. Key research facilities include the Cybersecurity Manufacturing Innovation Institute (CyManII), the Brain Health Consortium, and the Center for Advanced Measurements in Extreme Environments (CAMEE).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school curriculum, rank, and test scores (guaranteed admission for top 25% of Texas high schools; test-optional). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, program-specific GRE/GMAT scores (waivers available), professional statement, and letters of recommendation.
