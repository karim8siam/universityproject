University of Wisconsin-Platteville

UW-Platteville · Platteville, Wisconsin, United States

The University of Wisconsin-Platteville (UW-Platteville) is a premier public polytechnic and comprehensive university founded in 1866 in the scenic Driftless Area of Southwest Wisconsin. Renowned throughout the Midwest for top-ranked engineering programs at the College of EMS—Wisconsin's second-largest engineering producer—as well as industrial technology, agricultural sciences on the 430-acre Pioneer Farm, and forensic investigation, UW-Platteville champions hands-on technical education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UW-Platteville spans three colleges: the College of Engineering, Mathematics and Science (EMS), College of Business, Industry, Life Science and Agriculture (BILSA), and the College of Liberal Arts and Education. The campus features the state-of-the-art Sesquicentennial Hall engineering complex and the Pioneer Farm dairy research center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core curriculum, GPA, and test scores (optional). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
