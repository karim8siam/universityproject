Neumont College of Computer Science

Neumont College · Salt Lake City, Utah, United States

Neumont College of Computer Science is a private STEM institution in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah, dedicated entirely to computer science and software development. Known for accelerated three-year bachelor's degrees and industry-sponsored Enterprise Projects.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Neumont College features bachelor's degree tracks in Computer Science, Software and Mobile Applications Development, Web Design and Development, Information Systems and Cybersecurity, and Game Development. Students complete practical corporate client sprints.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.5), computer science interest, problem-solving aptitude, and admissions interview. Standardized testing (ACT/SAT) is optional.
