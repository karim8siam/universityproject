Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1965 in Miami-Dade County, FIU is one of the ten largest public universities in the United States. FIU houses world-class research centers including the Wall of Wind (a 12-fan hurricane simulator capable of 157+ mph category 5 winds), the Medina Aquarius Program (the world's only underwater ocean research laboratory), the Knight Foundation School of Computing, and the Kimberly Green Latin American and Caribbean Center.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants require an accredited high school diploma with competitive GPA (average 4.0–4.6 weighted), SAT or ACT scores, and transcripts. Master's and doctoral degrees require an accredited bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline (minimum GPA 3.0/4.0), GRE scores (where specified), research statement, and letters of recommendation. Presidential and Dean's Fellowships provide comprehensive doctoral funding.
