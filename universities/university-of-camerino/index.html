University of Camerino: Pharmacy, Physics & ERDIS Scholarship Guide

The University of Camerino (Università degli Studi di Camerino - UNICAM), founded in 1336 by papal edict, is one of the world's oldest universities. Ranked consistently as Italy's #1 university for small-scale institutions by CENSIS, UNICAM boasts an ultra-modern International School of Advanced Studies, cutting-edge nanoscience labs, and a vibrant multicultural campus.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Marche Regional ERDIS Full Study Grant & University Housing Award

Complete regional welfare scholarship providing international students in Camerino with comprehensive tuition coverage, dormitory lodging, and living allowances.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences

Three-year doctoral program within UNICAM's International School of Advanced Studies, investigating green catalytic processes, targeted drug delivery, and pharmaceutical synthesis.

Master of Science in Chemistry and Advanced Chemical Methodologies

Two-year English-taught master's degree accredited with the Euromaster Chemistry Quality Label, specializing in analytical spectroscopy and supramolecular chemistry.

Quantum Physics & Nanoscience Laboratory Research Assistantship

Research assistantship working on quantum entanglement, macroscopic quantum resonators, and cryogenic optomechanics in collaboration with European quantum hubs.

Marche Pharmaceutical Industrial Internship & Quality Control Practicum

Six-month clinical and analytical quality control placement embedded in Marche's pharmaceutical cluster, performing HPLC, mass spectrometry, and validation.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Computational Structural Biology & Drug Design

Two-year postdoctoral appointment applying machine learning and molecular mechanics simulations to identify novel allosteric inhibitors for multi-drug resistant pathogens.

Master of Science in Computer Science: Software Engineering & Cybersecurity

English-taught master's degree focusing on formal methods, secure distributed systems, cloud microservices, and mobile application engineering.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Three-year Bachelor's degree (Laurea) or recognized international undergraduate degree evaluated via Universitaly pre-enrolment.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) with competitive examination including academic CV, 3,000-word research proposal, and oral interview (via videoconference).
Language proficiency
B2 English certificate (IELTS Academic 6.0+, TOEFL iBT 78+, or Cambridge B2 First); Italian language proficiency not required for English-taught master's or PhDs.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applicants must submit an original research project adhering to the UNICAM International School of Advanced Studies (SAS) format.
Application fee
€10 to €40 pre-admission evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, CIMEA Statement of Comparability or Declaration of Value (DoV), passport copy, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees are strictly income-based (ISEE parificato), ranging from €156 regional tax to a maximum of approximately €2,000 per year. Regional ERDIS Marche scholarships guarantee 100% tuition waivers, free university accommodation, daily campus cafeteria meals, and cash stipends of up to €7,200 per year.
