Institution Overview & Profile

Brigham Young University–Idaho (BYU-Idaho), established in 1888 in Rexburg, Idaho, is a major private undergraduate university owned and operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS). Serving over 40,000 campus and online students, BYU-Idaho is renowned for its high-quality, student-centered teaching model, exceptionally affordable subsidized tuition, and premier programs in computer science, software engineering, business, and agriculture.

Institution TypePrivate Undergraduate University (LDS Church Educational System)

Key Academic Strengths

  • One of the most affordable accredited universities in the United States ($2,400–$4,800/semester)
  • Dedicated undergraduate focus with 100% of classes taught directly by faculty without graduate teaching assistants
  • Robust software engineering and computing program with graduates recruited heavily by Silicon Valley and Utah's Silicon Slopes
  • Extensive experiential learning through student-run enterprises, agricultural research farms, and tech incubators
