Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1971 in Oestrich-Winkel in the Rheingau region, EBS Business School is one of Germany's oldest and most prestigious private business schools, holding AACSB and EQUIS accreditations and renowned for corporate finance, strategic management, real estate, and legal studies.

Institution type
Private State-Recognized University (Non-profit SRH Higher Education network)
Campus location
Oestrich-Winkel (Rheingau) & Wiesbaden (EBS Law School), Hesse
Accreditation
AACSB, EQUIS, German Science and Humanities Council (Wissenschaftsrat)
Admissions channel
EBS Admissions and Selection Committee
Instruction language
English (100% of business degree curriculum) and German (law)
Financial aid & support
EBS Alumni Foundation, institutional merit scholarships, and BAföG funding

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Corporate finance, private equity, and banking regulation
  • Strategic management, entrepreneurship, and family enterprise research
  • Real estate economics and sustainable urban asset management (REMI)
  • Automotive and mobility management research

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Finance (M.Sc.) (Graduate Degree) — Top-ranked international finance degree preparing graduates for investment banking, asset management, and corporate valuation.
  • Doctor of Philosophy in Business Studies (Ph.D. / Dr. rer. pol.) (Doctoral Degree) — Intensive 3-year research doctorate with publication requirements in leading peer-reviewed FT50 journals.
  • Bachelor of Science in International Business (Undergraduate Degree) — Triple-accredited undergraduate program with integrated mandatory study abroad semester and corporate internships.
