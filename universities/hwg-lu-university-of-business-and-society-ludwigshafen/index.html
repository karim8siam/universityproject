Ludwigshafen University of Business and Society

Hochschule für Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft Ludwigshafen (HWG LU - Ludwigshafen University of Business and Society) is a prominent public university in the Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region, renowned for international management, health and social care, and its prestigious East Asia Institute.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated directly on the Rhine opposite Mannheim in one of Europe's top chemical and industrial corridors (home to BASF headquarters), HWG LU is recognized for its four specialized departments: Management/Controlling/Healthcare, Marketing/HR, Social/Healthcare Services, and the renowned East Asia Institute (Ostasiatische Institut - OAI), which offers intensive Japanese and Chinese business studies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or recognized foreign school-leaving certificate via uni-assist. Master's degrees require an accredited bachelor's degree in business administration, social work, or healthcare management (minimum German grade 2.5). German proficiency DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 is required for German tracks; IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 85 for English tracks.
