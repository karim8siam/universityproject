Missouri University of Science and Technology

Missouri S&T · Rolla, Missouri, United States

Missouri University of Science and Technology is an elite public STEM research university in Rolla, Missouri, and a premier member of the University of Missouri System. Celebrated globally for mining and explosives engineering, metallurgical research, advanced ceramics, and aerospace engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Missouri S&T features the Kummer Institute for Student Success, Research and Economic Development, an on-campus experimental underground mine, and the Materials Research Center. It is home to the world's only Master of Science in Explosives Engineering.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Selective admissions evaluate secondary school core math/science GPA, standardized test scores (test-optional), and engineering aptitude. Graduate engineering programs require an ABET-accredited degree with minimum 3.0 GPA and faculty sponsorship.
