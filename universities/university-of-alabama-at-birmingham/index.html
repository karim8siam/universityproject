University of Alabama at Birmingham

UAB · Birmingham, Alabama, United States

The University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB), established in 1969, is a premier comprehensive public R1 research university and academic medical powerhouse. Located in downtown Birmingham, UAB is the largest single employer in Alabama. Anchored by the world-renowned Marnix E. Heersink School of Medicine and UAB Hospital—the 8th largest hospital in the nation—UAB commands over $770 million in annual research and grant expenditures.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UAB comprises 12 academic divisions, notably the Heersink School of Medicine, School of Health Professions, School of Nursing, School of Engineering, and College of Arts and Sciences. It is globally recognized for groundbreaking cancer clinical trials at the O'Neal Comprehensive Cancer Center and the nation's #1 ranked Master of Science in Health Administration program.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions operate via the Common Application (holistic review; test-optional; recommended GPA 3.0+). Graduate and professional biomedical programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, strong GPA (minimum 3.0–3.5), GRE or MCAT scores where applicable, letters of recommendation, and research experience.
