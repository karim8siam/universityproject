Ukrainian Free University Munich

The Ukrainian Free University (UFU) in Munich is a historic private graduate university founded in Vienna in 1921 and state-recognized by the Bavarian Ministry of Sciences, dedicated to Ukrainian studies, international law, and Central-Eastern European politics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating from its historic Munich headquarters in Nymphenburg, UFU is the only Ukrainian-language university outside Ukraine. Accredited by the Free State of Bavaria to grant state-recognized master's and doctoral degrees, UFU operates three faculties: Ukrainian Studies (Literature, Linguistics, History), Philosophy, and Law & Social Sciences. The university acts as a premier intellectual bridge between Ukraine and the European Union.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to master's degree programs requires a completed four-year bachelor's degree from a recognized university. Doctoral programs require a master's or specialist degree with high honors (minimum grade 2.0 or Ukrainian 'vidminno'). Applicants must submit academic transcripts, a certified translation of diplomas, a curriculum vitae, and a comprehensive research proposal in Ukrainian or English.
