Suffolk University

Suffolk University · Boston, Massachusetts, United States

Suffolk University is a comprehensive private university located in the heart of downtown Boston on Beacon Hill, directly adjacent to the Massachusetts State House, the Supreme Judicial Court, and Boston's financial district. Renowned for its nationally top-ranked Suffolk University Law School (a pioneer in Legal Technology and Intellectual Property), the AACSB-accredited Sawyer Business School, and the College of Arts & Sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Suffolk's urban campus integrates students directly into Boston's political, legal, and financial institutions. Highlights include the modern 20 Somerset academic building, the Moakley Law Library, the Legal Innovation & Technology (LIT) Lab, and healthcare simulation suites in downtown Boston.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (average 3.4), personal statements, and extracurricular involvement on a test-optional basis. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ minimum GPA, official transcripts, resume, statement of purpose, and two academic/professional recommendations.
