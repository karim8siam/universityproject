Florida A&M University

Florida Agricultural and Mechanical University (FAMU) in Tallahassee is the #1 ranked public Historically Black College and University (HBCU) in the United States, renowned for its College of Pharmacy, joint FAMU-FSU College of Engineering, and agricultural research.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1887, FAMU is an 1890 land-grant university recognized nationally for engineering, pharmacy, architecture, agriculture, and business. Operating the unique FAMU-FSU College of Engineering—the only shared engineering college in the nation—FAMU delivers premier STEM education, leading cutting-edge research in medicinal chemistry, aerospace composites, and sustainable agriculture.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants require an accredited secondary school diploma, competitive high school GPA (average 3.6–3.9 weighted), standardized test scores (SAT/ACT), and official transcripts. Graduate applicants to engineering and pharmaceutical sciences require a recognized bachelor's degree in a STEM field, GRE scores (where applicable), personal statement, and letters of recommendation. Presidential and university graduate fellowships provide comprehensive funding.
