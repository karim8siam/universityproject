Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the German Science Council (Wissenschaftsrat) and FIBAA, SRH Berlin operates six specialized schools across Berlin, Dresden, and Hamburg, including the Berlin School of Technology, Berlin School of Popular Arts (SOPA), and Berlin School of Design and Communication (BSDC). The university teaches according to the CORE Principle (Competence-Oriented Research and Education) with five-week intensive modular learning and practical studio projects.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor's degree admission requires the German Allgemeine Hochschulreife, Fachhochschulreife, or international equivalent validated via uni-assist or SRH admissions. Creative majors require a portfolio review and interview. Master's degrees require an accredited bachelor's degree in a relevant field (minimum 180 or 210 ECTS). English-taught programs require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 87.
