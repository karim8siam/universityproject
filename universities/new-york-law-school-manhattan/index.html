New York Law School

New York Law School · New York, New York, United States

New York Law School is an independent, ABA-accredited law school located in Tribeca in lower Manhattan, New York City. Renowned for intellectual property law, cybersecurity jurisprudence, financial services regulation, and social justice advocacy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NYLS operates the Innovation Center for Law and Technology, the Center for Financial Services Law, and the Center for New York City Law. Situated steps from Wall Street, federal and state courts, and Silicon Alley tech enterprises.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Juris Doctor (JD) admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree, Law School Admission Test (LSAT) or GRE score, personal statement, and letters of recommendation evaluated through the Law School Admission Council (LSAC).
