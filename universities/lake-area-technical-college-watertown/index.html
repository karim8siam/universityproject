Lake Area Technical College

Lake Area Tech · Watertown, South Dakota, United States

Lake Area Technical College is a nationally recognized public technical college in Watertown, South Dakota, and a former recipient of the Aspen Prize for Community College Excellence, renowned for aviation maintenance, diesel technology, and robotics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lake Area Tech achieves a 99% graduate employment rate across 30+ cutting-edge technical disciplines. It features modern avionics hangars, heavy agricultural diesel repair bays, and specialized medical laboratory simulation centers.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admission requires a high school diploma or GED equivalent along with entrance assessment exams (ACCUPLACER or ACT scores). High-demand technical programs maintain priority application deadlines.
