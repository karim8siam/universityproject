HSF Meißen: Public Governance, Legal Informatics, Taxation & E-Government Guide

The Academy of Public Administration of Saxony (Hochschule Meißen - HSF Meißen & Fortbildungszentrum) is Saxony's premier state university of applied sciences for public administration, renowned for training civil servants in administrative law, digital e-government, municipal finance, and tax law.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Abitur, Fachhochschulreife, or recognized international equivalent secondary credential with solid grades in German, Mathematics, and Social Sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral degrees; graduates qualify for senior civil service career tracks (Höherer Dienst) and university master's articulation.
Language proficiency
German C1 / DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 required for official administrative jurisprudence and civil service legal studies.
Research statement / Proposal
Structured motivation dossier and passing score on the Saxon State civil service selection competition (Auswahlverfahren des Landespersonalausschusses).
Application fee
Zero application fee via the Saxon State civil service portal.
Required documentation
Certified high school diploma, CV, health fitness certificate for civil service appointment, and police clearance certificate (Führungszeugnis).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free (€0). As dual civil-service trainees (Beamtenanwärter), admitted German/EU students receive a monthly preparatory civil service salary (Anwärterbezüge) of approximately €1,400 to €1,600 per month during their studies.
