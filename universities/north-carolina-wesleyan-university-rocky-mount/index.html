North Carolina Wesleyan University

NC Wesleyan · Rocky Mount, North Carolina, United States

North Carolina Wesleyan University is a private Methodist liberal arts institution in Rocky Mount, North Carolina. Known for criminal justice, biomedical sciences, exercise science, business administration, and veteran education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NC Wesleyan features the School of Mathematics and Sciences, School of Business, and School of Social Sciences. Key facilities include the Thomas A. Betts Jr. Pavilion, Dunn Center for The Performing Arts, and the modern Health and Movement Science laboratories.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts (minimum 2.5 unweighted GPA) under holistic test-optional review. Graduate programs in criminal justice and strategic leadership require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.75 GPA.
