Polo Didattico Universitario Pegaso, Mercatorum e San Raffaele - Ceglie Messapica

The Polo Didattico Universitario in Ceglie Messapica (Brindisi) is an authorized higher education center serving the Valle d'Itria, integrating degree programs from Università Telematica Pegaso, Università Mercatorum, and Università San Raffaele Roma across health sciences, culinary tourism economics, pedagogy, and digital engineering.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating from the historic stone cultural center of Ceglie Messapica in the heart of the Valle d'Itria, this multi-university academic hub coordinates degree paths, proctored state examinations, and clinical internship placements across the provinces of Brindisi, Taranto, and Lecce. The center features specialized teaching labs for nutrition and dietetics, educational psychology seminar suites, digital classrooms, and gastronomic economics research units connected to local agricultural consortia.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to degree courses (Laurea Triennale, Laurea Magistrale, Master di I e II Livello) follows the national criteria of the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR). Candidates must hold an accredited Diploma di Maturità or international 12-year equivalent. Credit recognition (Riconoscimento CFU) is provided for prior university studies, military service, and verified professional healthcare activities. International candidates require a CIMEA comparability statement.

Twelve-month clinical nutrition fellowship examining antioxidant polyphenols in Apulian extra-virgin olive oil and their cellular epigenetic effects on cardiovascular longevity.

Funding: €15,000 annual research stipend in collaboration with regional health institutes Tuition: €0 Deadline: June 30, 2027

Merit scholarship supporting prospective nursing and health science students committing to healthcare service in community clinics and public hospitals across the province of Brindisi.
