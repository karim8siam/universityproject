Coventry University London: Global Business MBA, FinTech & Fashion Guide

Coventry University London is a dynamic postgraduate and professional campus located at University House on Middlesex Street, right in the heart of London's financial district, the Square Mile. Surrounded by major global banks, investment funds, and tech innovators near Liverpool Street Station, the campus specializes in career-focused Master's degrees, MBA programs, and specialized undergraduate degrees in international business, financial technology, international fashion marketing, and hospitality management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum lower second-class 2:2 Honours or international equivalent); relevant professional experience considered for executive MBA pathways.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Postgraduate research degrees (MPhil/PhD) offered in collaboration with Coventry University parent faculties and doctoral training centres; requires accredited Master's with merit/distinction.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 or 6.0 in all skill bands) or recognized equivalent international English qualification.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for MBA applicants: statement of purpose outlining management ambitions, international outlook, and leadership background.
Application fee
£0 direct online application fee.
Required documentation
Degree certificates, official transcripts, CV/resume, two references (one academic, one employer), copy of passport, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic postgraduate students ranges from £9,500 to £11,500 per year. International postgraduate tuition ranges from £17,200 to £21,500 per academic year. Coventry University London offers substantial International Pathways Scholarships providing tuition fee reductions of up to £3,000 for high-achieving applicants.
