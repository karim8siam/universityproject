University of Alabama System

UA System · Tuscaloosa, Alabama, United States

The University of Alabama System is Alabama's premier higher education enterprise, comprised of three doctoral research universities: The University of Alabama (Tuscaloosa), the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB), and the University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH). Conducting over $600 million in annual research, the UA System anchors the state in deep space rocketry at NASA Marshall, academic medicine at UAB Hospital, and cybersecurity.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Coordinated through constituent campuses (UA, UAB, UAH). High school diploma with college-preparatory curriculum; holistic evaluation of GPA, standardized exams, and leadership. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates across UA System universities. Fall priority admission January 15, 2027; Regular rolling admission closes May 1, 2027.
