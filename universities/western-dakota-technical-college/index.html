Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Rapid City · Black Hills Leader in Applied Engineering, Health Sciences & Trades. Western Dakota Technical College is an accredited public technical college located in Rapid City, South Dakota, nestled beneath the Black Hills. Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), WDTC provides industry-aligned Associate of Applied Science (AAS) degrees and diplomas in registered nursing, precision machining, civil engineering technology, and computer information systems.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or recognized state secondary credential. Program-specific testing (ACCUPLACER or ACT/SAT) and prerequisite verification for allied health and technical fields. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Rolling admissions; priority fall deadline July 1, 2027; spring priority deadline December 1, 2026.
