University of Bonn (Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität)

Founded in 1818, the University of Bonn (Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn) is one of Germany's most distinguished universities and one of only 11 certified German Universities of Excellence. Bonn boasts 6 Excellence Clusters—including the world-famous Hausdorff Center for Mathematics and ImmunoSensation. Home to numerous Nobel laureates and Fields medalists (such as Gerd Faltings and Peter Scholze), Bonn offers virtually tuition-free education, world-leading doctoral programs, and exceptional research infrastructure. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Bonn Hausdorff Center for Mathematics (HCM) Doctoral & Postdoctoral Fellowships

Funded by Germany's Excellence Strategy, the Hausdorff Center awards prestigious doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships working alongside Fields medalists and world leaders in pure and applied mathematics.

Uni Bonn PhD in Computer Science & Autonomous Robotics

Three to four-year research doctorate exploring mobile manipulation, humanoid robots, cognitive systems, 3D perception, and SLAM in Bonn's renowned Autonomous Intelligent Systems laboratories.

Uni Bonn Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (English-taught)

Two-year international Master of Science degree taught 100% in English, offering research specializations in algorithms, visual computing, data science, and intelligent systems.

Uni Bonn ImmunoSensation2 International Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral training within the ImmunoSensation2 Cluster of Excellence investigating innate immunity, neuro-immunology, and inflammatory disease mechanisms.

Uni Bonn Master of Science (MSc) in Physics (English-taught)

Two-year research-focused Master's program in collaboration with the University of Cologne, covering particle physics, photonics, condensed matter, and astrophysics.

Bonn Graduate School of Economics (BGSE) PhD Fellowships

Germany's top-ranked economics doctoral program, embedded within the ECONtribute Cluster of Excellence, specializing in microeconomic theory, behavioral economics, and public policy.

Uni Bonn Center for Development Research (ZEF) Summer Research Fellowship

Three-month summer research opportunity at ZEF engaging with international interdisciplinary development projects in sub-Saharan Africa, Central Asia, and Latin America.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent 180 ECTS credit qualification) in a relevant field with an above-average German grade equivalent (minimum 2.5 or better on the German 1-5 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed Master's degree (120 ECTS) with a research thesis graded at least 'good' (gut / 2.0 or better) and formal written acceptance by a Bonn doctoral supervisor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 to 7.0 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90+ for English-taught graduate programs; TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2 for German-taught programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Formal 3 to 5-page research proposal detailing research objectives, methodology, preliminary literature review, and academic work schedule.
Application fee
€0 application fee (Uni-Bonn does not charge application fees; nominal Uni-Assist fee may apply for international credential evaluation).
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificates, diploma supplements, statement of purpose, curriculum vitae, and two letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Public universities in North Rhine-Westphalia charge zero tuition fees for both EU and non-EU international students. Students pay only a nominal semester administration contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €330 per semester, which includes a free public transport ticket across the entire federal state of North Rhine-Westphalia and student union services. Doctoral positions are salaried civil service contracts (TV-L E13, 65%-100%).
