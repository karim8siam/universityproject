Southern Arkansas University

Southern Arkansas · Magnolia, Arkansas, United States

Southern Arkansas University (SAU) is a comprehensive public regional university nestled on a picturesque 1,400-acre campus in Magnolia, Arkansas. Known as the fastest-growing university in southern Arkansas, SAU is acclaimed for its ABET-accredited engineering program, pioneering game design and animation studio, high-tech agriculture center, and the Rankin College of Business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SAU comprises the College of Science and Engineering, the David F. Rankin College of Business, the College of Education and Human Performance, and the College of Liberal and Performing Arts. It features modern engineering laboratories, a 700-acre working agricultural farm, veterinary science facilities, and advanced cybersecurity computing clusters.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school grade point average (2.25 minimum) and ACT/SAT scores or test-optional holistic portfolio criteria. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.5+ minimum GPA (3.0 for computer science and engineering), academic transcripts, and program-specific letters of intent.
