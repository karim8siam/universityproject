Harvard University (Cambridge, Massachusetts)

Established in 1636, Harvard University is the oldest institution of higher learning in the United States and universally recognized as one of the world's preeminent research universities. Harvard's faculty and alumni have earned 161 Nobel laureates, 18 Fields Medalists, and 14 Turing Awards. Across the Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS) and the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences (GSAS), Harvard pioneers breakthrough investigations in quantum computing, artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and cosmology. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Harvard GSAS Doctoral Fellowship & Full Tuition Guarantee

Harvard's comprehensive five-year financial support guarantee for all admitted PhD students, providing full tuition coverage, health insurance, and an annual living stipend of USD $45,696.

Harvard PhD in Computer Science (SEAS)

Five-year doctoral research program inside the John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS) in Allston, conducting frontier investigations in artificial intelligence, cryptography, systems, and quantum computer science.

Harvard Master of Science in Data Science (SEAS)

Rigorous 3-semester Master of Science degree jointly offered by Computer Science and Statistics, focusing on statistical machine learning, deep learning, massive data management, and ethical data science.

Harvard Master of Science in Computational Science and Engineering (CSE)

Advanced postgraduate master's degree training researchers in mathematical modeling, high-performance parallel computing, stochastic simulation, and physical data analysis.

Harvard Center for Research on Computation and Society (CRCS) Placements

Interdisciplinary research center at Harvard SEAS bringing together computer scientists, public health experts, and social scientists to explore AI for social good, epidemic forecasting, and algorithmic governance.

Harvard PRISE (Program for Research in Science and Engineering)

Competitive 10-week residential summer research program offering undergraduate scholars full housing, meals, stipends, and direct laboratory apprenticeships alongside Harvard faculty.

Harvard Teaching Fellowships (TF / Teaching Assistantships)

Departmental academic teaching appointments providing instructional pedagogy training and competitive salary for conducting weekly section classes, grading problem sets, and managing laboratory courses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year U.S. Bachelor's degree or international equivalent with exceptional academic record (typically 3.7+ / 4.0 GPA) in computer science, mathematics, or physical sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Outstanding undergraduate or Master's degree with demonstrable scientific research ability, significant publications, and compelling letters of recommendation.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall (minimum 7.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 26 in each subscore).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page Statement of Purpose detailing research accomplishments, intellectual interests, and faculty alignment at Harvard SEAS/GSAS.
Application fee
USD $105 application fee submitted via the GSAS online application portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 3 academic letters of recommendation, resume / CV, and academic writing sample or code portfolio.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for Master's programs at Harvard SEAS is charged per academic year (typically USD $62,000 to $65,000 per year). For doctoral researchers, Harvard GSAS guarantees full funding for five years: 100% full tuition and health fees are completely paid, plus an annual tax-free living stipend of USD $45,696.
