GLOBIS University Sendai: Technovate MBA, Venture Creation & Tohoku Recovery Guide

Located in the business center of Sendai, GLOBIS University Graduate School of Management (Sendai Campus) is Japan's premier venture-focused business school, celebrated for its accredited Master of Business Administration (MBA), 'Technovate' programming integrating AI with management strategy, and leadership in Tohoku regional innovation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree from an accredited university (or evaluated equivalent) plus a minimum of 2 years of professional work experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Executive professional graduate school; offers accredited Master of Business Administration (MBA) degrees.
Language proficiency
Japanese fluency for Japanese MBA track; TOEFL iBT 85 or IELTS 6.5 for English MBA modules.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of Kokorozashi (personal life mission and strategic leadership ambition) of 1,000 to 1,500 words.
Application fee
¥36,000 admissions screening and interview assessment fee.
Required documentation
University graduation diploma, complete transcripts, career resume/CV, essays detailing leadership achievement, and two professional recommendations.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the Part-time & Online MBA program is approximately ¥3,200,000 for the full two-to-three-year curriculum. Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare educational training grants (up to ¥1,120,000 reimbursement), GLOBIS scholarship awards, and venture incubator seed funds are available.
