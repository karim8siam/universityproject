Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

The Netherlands Institute of Governance (Nederlands Instituut voor Governance - NIG) is the accredited Dutch-Flemish inter-university research school in public administration and political science. Recognized by the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences (KNAW) and coordinated from the University of Twente in partnership with Utrecht, Leiden, Erasmus University Rotterdam, and Belgian universities (KU Leuven, Antwerp, Ghent), NIG trains doctoral researchers and advances comparative public policy.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Comparative public administration, bureaucratic accountability, and institutional design
  • Crisis management, digital governance, and algorithmic public service delivery
  • Environmental governance, climate policy transitions, and European multi-level decision-making
  • Rigorous doctoral methodology: qualitative comparative analysis (QCA), process tracing, and advanced econometrics

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • NIG Inter-University Doctoral Training Program (Ph.D.) (Doctoral Curriculum) — Four-year curriculum providing specialized theoretical and methodological seminars for Ph.D. candidates in public administration and political science.
  • Advanced Research Master in Public Administration and Organizational Science (Research Master) — Two-year NVAO-accredited master's jointly offered with Utrecht University, Erasmus University Rotterdam, and Tilburg University.
  • NIG Winter School in Advanced Qualitative and Quantitative Methods (Postgraduate Course) — Intensive doctoral methodology seminars in process tracing, QCA, survey experiments, and natural language processing.
