St. Mary's College of Maryland

St. Mary's College of Maryland · St. Mary's City, Maryland, United States

St. Mary's College of Maryland (SMCM) is the designated public honors college of Maryland, located on the historic riverfront where Maryland was founded in 1634 in St. Mary's City. Acclaimed nationwide for offering an elite private liberal arts college experience at a public university cost, SMCM is celebrated for its waterfront marine science, colonial historical archaeology, atomic physics, and nationally ranked sailing team.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SMCM provides an intensive liberal arts curriculum centered on the signature 'St. Mary's Project' (SMP)—an independent eight-credit capstone research thesis. Its scenic 361-acre waterfront campus features the Marine Science Research Center, modern physics optics laboratories, and contiguous archaeological excavation sites exploring 17th-century colonial history.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic rigor, writing essays, intellectual depth, and leadership on a test-optional basis (average incoming GPA 3.6). The Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) requires an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, subject matter prerequisites, and academic recommendations.
