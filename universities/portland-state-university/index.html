Portland State University

Portland State · Portland, Oregon, United States

Portland State University is Oregon's premier urban public research university, located across 50 tree-lined acres in downtown Portland's University District. Nationally acclaimed for urban and regional planning, sustainability, computer science, social work, and environmental science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

PSU hosts the Maseeh College of Engineering and Computer Science, the Toulan School of Urban Studies and Planning (consistently ranked in the top 10 nationwide), the School of Business with the Karl Miller Center, and the Northwest Center for Alternatives to Pesticides. Its motto 'Let Knowledge Serve the City' anchors deep civic partnerships.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with a minimum 2.5 GPA (test-optional). Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA and discipline-specific materials (transcripts, letters, statement of purpose).
