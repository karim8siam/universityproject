International Christian University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Founded in 1949 on an expansive wooded campus in Mitaka, Tokyo, International Christian University (Kokusai Kirisutokyō Daigaku - ICU) is Japan's foremost bilingual liberal arts institution. Renowned for small-group seminars, dual English-Japanese instruction, global diplomacy, environmental science, and rotary peace research, ICU maintains deep international academic standing.

Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at ICU

Prestigious national scholarship awarded by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology to outstanding international graduate students.

Bilingual English-medium master's curriculum preparing leaders for international organizations, diplomacy, non-governmental advocacy, and civil governance.

Three-year doctoral research fellowship advancing research on multilateral conflict resolution, nuclear disarmament, and human security in the Asia-Pacific.

Field research project evaluating urban permaculture, agro-biodiversity preservation, and community-supported agriculture on ICU's natural campus woodland.

Semester-long policy internship placed within United Nations agencies in Tokyo, Japanese development NGOs, or international humanitarian missions.

ICU Peace Research Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year resident research fellowship investigating historical reconciliation, post-conflict transitional justice, and civic education in East Asia.

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of International Relations and East Asian Diplomacy

Full-time faculty appointment teaching undergraduate liberal arts seminars and supervising graduate research in Indo-Pacific foreign policy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 16 years of formal education) with solid academic standing (equivalent to 3.0/4.0 GPA or higher).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree from a recognized institution with a defended master's thesis and approved doctoral dissertation outline.
Language proficiency
For English track: TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS Academic 6.5. Japanese proficiency is not required for English-track graduate programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of academic purpose (1,000–1,500 words) and detailed research plan outlining theoretical foundations and methodology.
Application fee
¥30,000 application fee for graduate school admission.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, graduation certificate, standardized English scores, two letters of recommendation, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at ICU is approximately ¥1,070,000 per year (including tuition and facilities fees) plus a one-time matriculation fee of ¥200,000. International students are eligible for the ICU Non-Japanese Student Scholarship (covering 1/3, 1/2, or 100% of tuition) as well as direct Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarships.
