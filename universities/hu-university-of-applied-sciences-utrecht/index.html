Hogeschool Utrecht: Applied AI, Sustainable Building & NL Scholarship Guide

HU University of Applied Sciences Utrecht (Hogeschool Utrecht - HU) is one of the largest public applied research universities in the Netherlands, located at the vibrant Utrecht Science Park. Known for high-impact multidisciplinary research in artificial intelligence, digital design, smart sustainable cities, and healthcare innovation, HU educates over 38,000 students across modern research labs.

Two-year postdoctoral position evaluating algorithmic bias, clinical decision support validation, and data privacy frameworks in Dutch hospital networks.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree in business, computing, engineering, or related field from an accredited university; professional work experience may be considered for applied professional master's tracks.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Professional Doctorate (PD) candidates and joint PhDs awarded collaboratively in partnership with research universities like Utrecht University.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each subtest), TOEFL iBT 90, or Cambridge C1 Advanced.
Research statement / Proposal
Applied master's thesis or PD proposal: 2,000-word industrial innovation project plan addressing a real-world enterprise or societal challenge.
Application fee
€0 direct application fee (Studielink registration).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, curriculum vitae, motivation letter, and employer endorsement (for dual/professional master's tracks).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is legally fixed by the Dutch government at €2,530 per year for EU/EEA students. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition ranges from €9,800 to €14,500 per academic year. The Dutch Ministry NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) awards €5,000 to talented incoming international scholars.
