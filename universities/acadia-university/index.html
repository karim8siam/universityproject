Acadia University

Acadia University is based in Wolfville, Nova Scotia. This independent guide connects its graduate study, scholarship pathways, research areas, and public faculty profiles without presenting an unverified program as an open funded vacancy.

Country: CanadaWolfville, Nova ScotiaOfficial institution ↗Academic information last reviewed: 13 Oct 2026

Before you apply

No standalone Physics MSc or PhD confirmed at Acadia.

The Department of Physics advertises paid undergraduate summer research and publicly lists faculty. A separate Physics MSc/PhD is not on the current Acadia graduate degree list; a research contact is not equivalent to admission.

Degree
Generally a recognized four-year bachelor's or honours degree. Programs may require closely related prior study and a willing supervisor.
GPA
University-wide graduate baseline around 2.67/4.0, or 70% in major courses in the final two years. Some programs require more. Bangladesh-specific guidance differs: about 2.7/4.0 admission, 3.3/4.0 for internal scholarship consideration and 3.7/4.0 for typical external support.
IELTS Academic
Graduate tests after January 21, 2026: overall 7.0, with no band below 6.5. Older tests have separate requirements; some departments require more.
TOEFL
The graduate page uses its post-January-2026 updated scale. Refer directly to the official page for the accepted test date and scale.
GRE
Not stated as a blanket university-wide graduate condition; check each department. Absence of a universal rule is not a waiver.
Published research paper
Not listed as universally required. Research aptitude, CV, writing samples or research proposals may matter.
Recommendation letters
Required number depends on program. The sociology master's example requires two references; do not generalize this to every degree.
Graduate application fee
CAD $100 since September 21, 2026; Counselling is CAD $150.
Graduate tuition, 2026–27
Acadia lists CAD $19,906.82 per year for its standard two-year international graduate fee category. Different course/program formats and future years may cost more.
Scholarship/TA funding
Competitive AGS/AGTA consideration where eligible; no guaranteed amount. Some projects, course-streams and international categories are ineligible.
Graduate funding timing
February 1 is a common first-round deadline; some programs have different dates. Confirm the intended intake.
Undergraduate entrance scholarship timing
March 1, 2027 is a published scholarship application deadline; international and specialized eligibility varies.

Scholarship and research funding

University entrance scholarships, AGS/AGTA consideration and paid departmental research are different opportunities with different eligible applicants. Funding consideration never guarantees award.

Scholarship · Published deadline

2027 undergraduate entrance scholarships

International and Canadian students applying to eligible Acadia bachelor's programs may be considered for entrance awards. Initial offers are not a promise of additional competitive scholarships. Separate scholarship forms are due March 1, 2027; scholarships are not guaranteed.

Funding
Initial awards CAD $500–$2,500; selected awards up to CAD $80,000 over four years (competitive)
Application cost
CAD $40 undergraduate processing fee listed for international applicants (reconfirm on portal).

Graduate scholarship / teaching assistantship consideration

Applicants to qualifying research-based programs are considered for Acadia Graduate Scholarship and/or Graduate Teaching Assistantship if department requirements are met. The published February 1 first-round deadline is recurring, and does not establish every program's current opening date. Funding is not automatic.

Funding
Competitive AGS and/or AGTA, amount not guaranteed; departments allocate awards
Application cost
CAD $100 graduate application fee since Sep 21, 2026
Deadline
