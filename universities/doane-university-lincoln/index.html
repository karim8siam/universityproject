Doane University - Lincoln Campus

Doane University (Lincoln Campus) is a premier private university campus in Nebraska offering accelerated adult undergraduate and graduate degree programs in clinical mental health counseling, educational leadership, business analytics, and non-profit administration.

Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission and CACREP, Doane Lincoln specializes in preparing working professionals and graduate scholars. Its Master of Arts in Counseling operates an on-site community counseling clinic and maintains extensive clinical partnerships with regional mental health centers, schools, and social welfare institutions.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school completion or an accredited bachelor’s degree (minimum 2.80 GPA for graduate programs). Counseling applicants undergo group interview evaluations, background screening, and submission of personal reflection essays.
