Berea College: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1855 by abolitionist John Gregg Fee, Berea College is one of only nine federally recognized Work Colleges in the United States. Providing a world-class liberal arts education, Berea charges zero tuition to every single enrolled student—awarding each admitted undergraduate a four-year Tuition Promise Scholarship valued at over $175,000. Students participate in the college's Labor Program, working 10 to 15 hours per week across campus operations, research laboratories, and traditional Appalachian craft workshops.
