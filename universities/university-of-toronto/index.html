University of Toronto (U of T)

Founded in 1827, the University of Toronto (U of T) is Canada's leading institution of learning, discovery and knowledge creation. Consistently ranked among the top 25 universities globally, U of T secures over CAD $1.4 billion in annual research funding. From the discovery of insulin to pioneering deep learning under Geoffrey Hinton, U of T leads the world in artificial intelligence, medicine, aerospace, and theoretical physics. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

University of Toronto Doctoral Base Funding & Arts & Science Fellowships

U of T's guaranteed doctoral funding package ensuring all eligible doctoral stream students receive at least CAD $20,000 per year plus complete tuition coverage across Arts & Science, Applied Science, and Medicine.

University of Toronto PhD in Computer Science

Five-year doctoral research program in U of T's globally celebrated Department of Computer Science (DCS), offering frontier research in machine learning, neural networks, computer systems, graphics, and quantum computing.

University of Toronto Master of Science in Applied Computing (MScAC)

World-class 16-month master's degree combining 8 months of advanced graduate coursework in AI, data science, quantum, or cybersecurity with an 8-month paid applied research internship at top tech enterprises.

University of Toronto MASc in Aerospace Engineering (UTIAS)

Two-year research-intensive Master of Applied Science degree at the University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies (UTIAS), investigating autonomous flight, microsatellites, green aviation, and space robotics.

Vector Institute for Artificial Intelligence Research Placements

Frontier research placements inside the Vector Institute in downtown Toronto, collaborating with world-leading machine learning researchers on foundational neural architectures, AI safety, and health informatics.

University of Toronto Excellence Awards (UTEA) in Science

Competitive 14-week summer research award enabling undergraduate students to conduct full-time experimental or theoretical research in physical, engineering, and life sciences alongside leading U of T professors.

U of T Graduate Teaching Assistantships (CUPE 3902 Unit 1)

Departmental academic teaching appointments providing instruction leadership training and hourly remuneration for running weekly laboratory sections, grading exams, and tutoring undergraduate students.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Appropriate 4-year Bachelor's degree with standing equivalent to at least a mid-B (typically 75%+) in the final year of undergraduate study from an accredited institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree with standing equivalent to at least B+ (typically 78%+), demonstrated thesis research capability, and strong letters of academic recommendation.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5, or TOEFL iBT 93 overall (writing and speaking minimum 22).
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum-aligned 2-page research statement describing research questions, background literature, and faculty supervisor alignment.
Application fee
CAD $125 to CAD $150 university application fee depending on graduate department and credential evaluation.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 3 academic letters of reference, curriculum vitae, and statement of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for research-stream Master's and PhD programs is heavily subsidized. Under the Arts & Science Doctoral Base Funding package, full-time doctoral students receive guaranteed minimum financial support of CAD $20,000 per year plus full coverage of tuition and incidental fees (effective zero net tuition). International tuition for professional Master's programs ranges from CAD $35,000 to CAD $60,000.
