Academic overview & research priorities

Rooted in the radical creative legacy of the historic Bauhaus, campus.garten connects the Faculty of Architecture and Urbanism, Faculty of Art and Design, and Faculty of Civil Engineering. Scholars explore bio-based construction materials, urban microclimate cooling, soil remediation, and collective community ecologies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Prospective students apply through Bauhaus-Universität Weimar’s admissions portal. Architecture and design curricula require a comprehensive creative portfolio, statement of design philosophy, and language proficiency (English IELTS 6.5+ or German DSH-2 depending on the program).
