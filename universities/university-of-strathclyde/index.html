University of Strathclyde: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Founded in 1796 in the center of Glasgow as 'the place of useful learning', the University of Strathclyde is a leading UK technological university recognized twice as the UK University of the Year by Times Higher Education. Renowned for engineering, photonics, business management, and pharmaceuticals, Strathclyde hosts the landmark £89m Technology and Innovation Centre (TIC).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Strathclyde Engineering International Research Excellence PhD Studentship

Full-coverage doctoral scholarship covering international tuition, living stipends, and research supplies for three and a half years of PhD study.

Master of Science in Advanced Mechanical Engineering with Aerospace

Accredited master's curriculum exploring aerodynamics, spaceflight systems, finite element structural analysis, and advanced turbomachinery.

National Manufacturing Institute Scotland (NMIS) Fully Funded Doctoral Fellowship

Four-year industrial doctoral fellowship conducted at NMIS adjacent to Glasgow Airport developing net-zero manufacturing, robotics, and forging simulations.

Strathclyde Institute of Photonics Laser Diagnostics & Quantum Optics Project

Advanced photonics research placement investigating semiconductor disk lasers, gallium nitride micro-LED arrays, and quantum single-photon sources.

Offshore Renewable Energy (ORE) Catapult Wind Energy Engineering Practicum

Six-month engineering internship placed with the UK's leading offshore renewable energy technology center analyzing offshore wind turbine powertrains and blade testing.

Continuous Manufacturing and Advanced Crystallisation (CMAC) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Technology and Innovation Centre accelerating automated pharmaceutical manufacturing and continuous drug crystallization.

Lecturer in Digital Economy and FinTech Strategy

Permanent academic lectureship in Strathclyde Business School conducting research on blockchain financial systems, open banking, and regulatory technology.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK First or Upper Second Class (2:1) Honours degree or international equivalent (typically 60–65%+ from recognized university).
Doctoral admission eligibility
UK Master's degree with Merit or Distinction (or First Class Honours Bachelor degree) with strong research proposal and supervisor alignment.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no individual score below 5.5 or 6.0 depending on program.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research proposal (approx 1,500 words) defining aims, research context, proposed methodology, and supervisor nomination.
Application fee
£0 (Free direct online application via the Pegasus postgraduate portal).
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates and transcripts, English proficiency qualifications, CV, personal statement, and two academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate taught tuition typically ranges between £21,800 and £26,900 per year. Fully funded PhD research studentships (supported by EPSRC, UKRI, and Strathclyde Research Excellence) cover 100% of international tuition fees plus a tax-free annual living stipend of £19,237.
