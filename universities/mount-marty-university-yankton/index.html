Mount Marty University

Mount Marty · Yankton, South Dakota, United States

Mount Marty University is a private Catholic Benedictine university in Yankton, South Dakota, situated along the Missouri River. Renowned across the Northern Plains for nurse anesthesia, nursing, athletic training, and education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Mount Marty features the Graduate Program in Nurse Anesthesia, the Division of Nursing and Health Sciences, and the Division of Business. Its clinical anesthesia program is one of the oldest and most respected in the upper Midwest.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5) or transfer coursework under test-optional review. Nurse anesthesia and graduate health programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in nursing, RN licensure, and clinical ICU experience.
