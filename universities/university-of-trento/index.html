University of Trento (Università di Trento)

Founded in 1962, the University of Trento (Università di Trento - UniTrento) is consistently ranked as Italy's #1 public university for research quality and student satisfaction by ANVUR and national rankings. Nestled in the Italian Alps, UniTrento is an international research jewel boasting the renowned Department of Information Engineering and Computer Science (DISI), the Centre for Mind/Brain Sciences (CIMeC), and the Centre for Integrative Biology (CIBIO). With over 70% of doctoral students funded on competitive scholarships, UniTrento offers an exceptional environment for STEM and social science investigations. Interdisciplinary doctoral program at the Centre for Mind/Brain Sciences (CIMeC) using fMRI, MEG, EEG, and computational models to explore human cognition and neural mechanisms.

Two-year postdoctoral position at the Department of Physics and Nanoscience Laboratory investigating integrated photonic circuits and quantum light-matter interactions.

Joint international Master's degree between the University of Trento and the University of Innsbruck (Austria) specializing in atmospheric boundary layer physics and Alpine meteorology.

Six-week policy research traineeship at the School of International Studies evaluating EU external relations, migration policy, and transnational security architectures.

Three-year research position at the Centre for Integrative Biology (CIBIO) developing precision engineered CRISPR nucleases and therapeutic mRNA delivery platforms.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (Laurea or foreign equivalent, minimum 180 ECTS) in a relevant subject area with an above-average academic transcript (typically minimum 70%+ or 3.0/4.0 GPA).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or equivalent 120 ECTS post-bachelor degree) with excellent academic marks, strong thesis research background, and detailed research proposal.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 85+ for English-taught graduate programs; CEFR B2 certification.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 4-page research proposal detailing state of the art, research objectives, methodology, and prospective UniTrento department alignment.
Application fee
€15 to €30 application fee for Master's and PhD programs via the UniTrento online portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts with certified translations, curriculum vitae, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and research proposal.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral students receiving UniTrento PhD scholarships pay ZERO tuition fees (€0 tuition) and receive a guaranteed tax-free living stipend of €16,290 per year (increased by 50% during research visits abroad) plus an annual research budget of €1,629 to €2,443. For Master's students, tuition is income-based (up to €3,000/year for non-EU students), and UniTrento Uni-Honours and Opera Universitaria grants provide full tuition waivers and free student housing.
