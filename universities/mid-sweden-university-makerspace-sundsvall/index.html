Mid Sweden University Makerspace (Mittuniversitetet): Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Situated on the modern riverfront Campus Sundsvall of Mid Sweden University (Mittuniversitetet), the Makerspace integrates research from the Sensible Things that Communicate (STC) and Transformative Technologies research centres. Formerly known as Tvätteriet, this state-of-the-art facility features high-precision multi-material 3D printers, laser machining centers, wireless sensor testbeds, and the world-leading Swedish Winter Sports Research Centre (NVC) prototyping labs.
