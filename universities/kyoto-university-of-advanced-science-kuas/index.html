Kyoto University of Advanced Science

Kyoto University of Advanced Science (KUAS), revolutionized by Nidec founder Shigenobu Nagamori, is Japan's premier English-medium engineering powerhouse, internationally recognized for its Faculty of Engineering specializing in electric vehicle powertrains, robotics, mechatronics, and artificial intelligence.

Academic overview & excellence

KUAS teaches its entire engineering curriculum (bachelor's, master's, and doctorate) 100% in English. The flagship Uzumasa Campus features cutting-edge CNC precision machinery, rapid prototyping workshops, cleanrooms, and electric motor dyno test cells developed in direct partnership with leading Japanese global manufacturers.

Research admissions & criteria

Engineering admissions is conducted entirely in English with no Japanese requirement. Undergraduate applicants submit high school transcripts with strong math and physics scores. Graduate applicants submit bachelor's transcripts, statement of purpose, and English test scores (TOEFL iBT 80, IELTS 6.0, or Duolingo 105). KUAS offers generous Super On-Campus Scholarships covering up to 100% tuition and living stipends.

Two-year English-medium master's curriculum combining brushless DC motor design, power electronics, embedded firmware, autonomous robotics, and thermal management.

Three-year doctoral research program focusing on next-generation silicon carbide (SiC) inverters, humanoid robotic control, and drone aerodynamics.

Funding: 100% tuition waiver plus salaried university teaching/research assistantship (¥150,000/month) Tuition: ¥0 for qualified doctoral researchers Deadline: February 15, 2027

Industry-sponsored fellowship designing high-efficiency axial-flux electric traction motors and acoustic vibration damping for electric vehicles.
