University Hospital Zurich: Oncology, Precision Medicine & Neuroscience Guide

Tracing its foundational roots to 1204, the University Hospital Zurich (Universitätsspital Zürich - USZ) is one of Europe's premier academic medical centers, affiliated directly with the University of Zurich (UZH) and ETH Zurich. Renowned worldwide for pioneering organ transplantation, CAR-T immunotherapy, neurosurgery, and digital health, USZ conducts groundbreaking clinical and translational biomedical research.

Highly competitive national fellowship for physicians pursuing translational doctoral research in precision oncology, cardiovascular science, or immunology at USZ.

Joint UZH/ETH Zurich graduate curriculum delivering elite training in ultra-high-field MRI, clinical ultrasound, image-guided robotic surgery, and sensor electronics.

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment at the Department of Medical Oncology and Hematology investigating resistance mechanisms to CAR-T immunotherapy.

Cutting-edge translational project mapping tumor heterogeneity, immune checkpoint evasion, and clonal evolutionary trajectories in patient melanoma biopsies.

Six-month clinical neurophysiology fellowship training in high-density EEG, intraoperative neuromonitoring, and deep brain stimulation programming for Parkinson's disease.

Two-to-three year postdoctoral fellowship at the USZ Brain Tumor Center investigating glioblastoma stem cell invasion and oncolytic viral therapies.

University of Zurich & USZ Excellence Postdoctoral Grant in AI Radiology

Research grant developing self-supervised vision models for automated detection of micro-metastases in ultra-low-dose PET-CT and multi-parametric MRI scans.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science in human medicine, biology, biochemistry, or biomedical engineering from an internationally recognized institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (MSc) in natural sciences or medical degree (MD/Staatsexamen); competitive admission through the joint UZH/ETH Life Science Zurich Graduate School (LSZGS).
Language proficiency
German proficiency at C1 level required for clinical medical rotations; full English proficiency (C1 / IELTS 7.0+) for laboratory PhD and postdoctoral positions.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral research synopsis (2,000 words) defining clinical hypothesis, experimental methodology, and prospective USZ clinic/lab host group.
Application fee
CHF 100 registration fee for enrollment at University of Zurich / ETH Zurich.
Required documentation
Medical diploma / MSc certificate, complete official transcripts, comprehensive CV with publication record, and three letters of academic recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral researchers and MD-PhD candidates are fully salaried under Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) or Canton of Zurich salary scales. Annual PhD stipends range from CHF 48,000 to CHF 55,000; postdoctoral researcher salaries range from CHF 85,000 to CHF 105,000. Matriculation fees at the affiliated University of Zurich are approximately CHF 720 per semester.
