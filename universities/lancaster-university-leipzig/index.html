Lancaster University Leipzig

Lancaster University Leipzig is the German branch campus of the UK’s top-15 ranked Lancaster University, providing British degrees taught in English in the historic cultural city of Leipzig.

Academic overview & research priorities

Approved by the Saxon State Ministry for Higher Education, Research and the Arts, Lancaster Leipzig offers British bachelor and master degrees in computer science, software engineering, business management, and accounting, conferring official UK degree qualifications within the EU.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require recognized secondary credentials (German Abitur, UK A-Levels, International Baccalaureate, or equivalent). English language proficiency required: IELTS 6.5+ (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 87+. Direct foundation entry available.
