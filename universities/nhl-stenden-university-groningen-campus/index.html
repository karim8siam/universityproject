Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

NHL Stenden University of Applied Sciences is a dynamic multi-campus institution in the northern Netherlands. Its Groningen Campus is internationally celebrated for Design-Based Education (DBE), cutting-edge teacher training academies, social work innovations, and sustainable regional development in the vibrant university city of Groningen.

Institution type
Public University of Applied Sciences (Hogeschool)
Campus location
Groningen Campus, Rengerslaan / Eemsgolaan, Groningen
Accreditation
NVAO (Accreditation Organisation of the Netherlands and Flanders)
Admissions channel
May 1 for non-EU students; August 15 for EU/EEA students
Instruction language
English & Dutch (numerous English-medium international bachelors & masters)
Financial aid & support
NL Scholarship, NHL Stenden Talent Grants, DUO Dutch Student Financing

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Pioneering Design-Based Education (DBE) learning concept co-creating solutions with industry
  • Celebrated International Teacher Education for Primary Schools (ITEPS)—Europe's first 4-year degree for international schools
  • Vibrant student city environment in Groningen (ranked Europe's top student city)
  • Strong research professorships in Educational Innovation and Sustainable Circular Economy

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Education in International Teacher Education for Primary Schools (ITEPS) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year English-medium degree preparing educators for international and bilingual primary schools worldwide, with teaching practices on 3 continents.
  • Master of Science in Content and Language Integrated Learning (CLIL) (Graduate Degree) — Specialized postgraduate master's exploring bilingual pedagogy, cognitive language scaffolding, and intercultural classroom communication.
  • Bachelor of Social Work (Social Innovation Track) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year professional degree focusing on community resilience, youth mentoring, mental health outreach, and urban inclusivity.
