Leuven NanoCentre

The Leuven NanoCentre and historic Hollands College (founded 1617) constitute a prestigious inter-university research consortium linking premier Low Countries research universities in advanced semiconductor nanotechnology, quantum electronics, and biomolecular physics.

Academic overview & excellence

Hollands College serves as an intellectual nexus for high-level international doctoral colloquia and advanced symposia in quantum nanoscience, operating in tandem with the world-leading cleanroom facilities of the Leuven NanoCentre and the nearby Eindhoven-Brainport semiconductor ecosystem.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission is competitive and restricted to master's graduates and doctoral researchers in electrical engineering, applied physics, materials science, or physical chemistry. Applicants submit peer-reviewed research papers, an advanced doctoral proposal, and academic references.

Hollands College Quantum Nanotechnology Doctoral Fellowship

Four-year fully funded doctoral fellowship developing sub-2nm gate-all-around field effect transistors and 2D semiconductor spin qubits.

Funding: Full funding: €2,650 net monthly doctoral fellowship + cleanroom microfabrication budget Tuition: €0 (fully waived under consortium fellowship) Deadline: January 15, 2027

Master of Science in Nanoscience, Nanotechnology and Nanoengineering

Two-year European Master of Science providing comprehensive cleanroom training, quantum electron transport modeling, and nanodevice fabrication.

Funding: Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Scholarships (€1,400 monthly allowance + full tuition) Tuition: €3,000 EU / €10,500 non-EU per year Deadline: February 15, 2027
Fellowship

Cleanroom Semiconductor Lithography Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral residency investigating extreme ultraviolet (EUV) photolithography defects, atomic layer deposition, and 3D chiplet interconnects.

Funding: €52,000 annual research salary plus European semiconductor travel grant Tuition: None (postdoctoral fellow appointment) Deadline: March 1, 2027
Internship

Low Countries Brainport Semiconductor Industry Practicum

Six-month research internship developing advanced optical metrology and wafer wafer defect inspection algorithms in the Eindhoven-Leuven corridor.

Funding: €1,200 monthly industrial research stipend at leading semiconductor equipment manufacturers Tuition: Included in master's enrollment Deadline: April 1, 2027
Fellowship

Hollands College Interdisciplinary Humanities & Science Colloquium Residency

One-month residential scholarship examining ethical frameworks in quantum computing, artificial intelligence autonomy, and European science policy.

Funding: €3,000 research award plus residential accommodation at historic Hollands College Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 30, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Biophysics & Nanoscale Bio-Sensing

Two-year postgraduate degree analyzing single-molecule optical tweezers, solid-state nanopore DNA sequencing, and atomic force microscopy of protein assemblies.

Funding: Consortium science merit awards up to €5,000 Tuition: €3,000 EU / €10,500 non-EU Deadline: May 15, 2027

Certificate in Cleanroom Protocols, Vacuum Systems & Atomic Layer Deposition

Four-week intensive cleanroom immersion certifying researchers for Class 10/100 microfabrication environments, plasma etching, and sputtering systems.

Funding: European Semiconductor Skills Alliance (ESSA) industry vouchers Tuition: €2,500 certificate fee Deadline: June 10, 2027
