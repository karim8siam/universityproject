University of the West of England: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Traced to the Merchant Venturers' Navigation School established in 1595, UWE Bristol received university status in 1992 and now educates over 36,000 students across Frenchay Campus, Glenside Campus, and City Campus in Bristol. Renowned as a leader in enterprise and graduate employability, UWE Bristol co-hosts the famous Bristol Robotics Laboratory (BRL)—Europe’s premier academic robotics research hub—jointly with the University of Bristol. UWE excels in aerospace engineering with Rolls-Royce and Airbus, wildlife filmmaking with BBC Bristol, nursing, and sustainable architectural design.
