Bow Valley College: Health Care, Cloud Computing, Business & Creative Tech Guide

Bow Valley College is Alberta's premier comprehensive community college situated in downtown Calgary, renowned for industry-aligned career education, its Chiu School of Business, Centre for Entertainment Arts, and state-of-the-art simulation health centers.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of an Alberta Grade 12 or equivalent secondary education, or a recognized Bachelor's degree for post-baccalaureate diplomas.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on accredited career diplomas and post-graduate certificates.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 to 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each skill) or TOEFL iBT 83 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose outlining career background and technology or business focus.
Application fee
CAD $140 international student application processing fee.
Required documentation
Certified transcripts, proof of English proficiency, resume, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic learners is approximately CAD $5,200 to $7,000 annually. For international students, full-time tuition averages CAD $15,800 to $19,500 per year. Bow Valley College Entrance Scholarships and Alberta international bursaries provide tuition assistance.
