Merz Akademie (Hochschule für Gestaltung, Kunst und Medien Stuttgart)

Merz Akademie is a distinguished state-recognized university of design, art, and media situated in Stuttgart, Baden-Württemberg. Founded in 1918 by Albrecht Leo Merz as a progressive pedagogical reform academy, Merz Akademie emphasizes critical theory, social responsibility, and author-driven creative production across film, video, crossmedia publishing, and new media.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Stuttgart, Baden-Württemberg, Germany

Institutional Overview

The curriculum at Merz Akademie bridges critical cultural theory, artistic inquiry, and emerging digital technology. Offering accredited Bachelor of Arts and Master of Arts in Design degrees, students specialize in Crossmedia Publishing, Film and Video, New Media, or Visual Communication within open-access studios, sound stages, and digital fabrication labs.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at Merz Akademie are determined through artistic portfolio reviews (Mappe) and individual colloquiums evaluating conceptual rigor and intellectual curiosity. Applicants must hold the German Abitur/Fachhochschulreife or international secondary equivalent. Institutional scholarships, state BAföG, and project production grants support enrolled creators.
