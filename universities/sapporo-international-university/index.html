Sapporo International University

Sapporo International University (SIU) is a private university in Sapporo, Hokkaido, specializing in international tourism management, winter resort economics, outdoor adventure tourism, modern communication, and Japanese cultural studies.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in Hokkaido’s capital, SIU is celebrated for its Faculty of Tourism—pioneering academic curricula in international ski resort operations, eco-tourism, airline hospitality, and sports event management. The university features close collaborative linkages with Hokkaido’s premier powder snow ski destinations, including Niseko and Rusutsu.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions accept Japanese and international scholars through high school records and interviews. International students require JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 200, academic transcripts, and a statement of interest in tourism or intercultural studies.
