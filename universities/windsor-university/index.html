Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Albany · Distance and Hybrid Education in Business Management & Health Informatics. Windsor University is an independent private institution delivering flexible undergraduate and graduate degree pathways in business administration, healthcare management, and computer information systems. Serving adult learners, international practitioners, and career changers, Windsor University combines asynchronous digital learning with industry-aligned capstone research projects.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma or certified equivalent for undergraduate admissions; recognized bachelor's degree with minimum 2.5 GPA for master's programs. TOEFL iBT 70+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants from non-English instructional mediums. Rolling admissions across eight-week modular terms (August, October, January, March, May, July).
