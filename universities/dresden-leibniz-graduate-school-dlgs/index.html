Explore fully funded doctoral fellowships, PhD positions in urban spatial resilience, and ecological transformation research at DLGS Dresden.

Institution Overview & Profile

The Dresden Leibniz Graduate School (DLGS) is an elite joint academic doctoral institution co-founded by the Leibniz Institute of Ecological Urban and Regional Development (IOER), the Technische Universität Dresden (TUD - TU Dresden), and the Academy for Spatial Development in the Leibniz Association (ARL), offering world-class structured PhD training in sustainable spatial development, climate resilience, and urban transformation.

Institution TypeJoint Doctoral Research Graduate School (IOER & TU Dresden)

Key Academic Strengths

  • Collaboration with TU Dresden—a designated German University of Excellence—for doctoral degree conferral
  • Comprehensive funding package including monthly living stipend, research budget, and international conference travel
  • Interdisciplinary supervision combining spatial planning, geoinformatics, environmental economics, and social ecology
  • Access to high-performance spatial modeling clusters and real-world urban transformation testbeds across Europe

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Doctorate (Dr. rer. nat. / Dr.-Ing. / Dr. rer. pol.)

Structured Doctoral Program in Sustainable Spatial Transformation

Three-to-four-year structured doctoral fellowship focusing on climate-neutral cities, spatial justice, and biodiversity conservation in urban landscapes.

Doctorate (PhD / Dr.-Ing.)

Doctoral Research Track in Spatial Geoinformatics & Urban Modeling

Advanced computational modeling of urban land-use transitions, remote sensing analytics, and ecological network simulation.

Postdoctoral Fellowship

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Resilient Regions & Resource Efficiency

Two-year postdoctoral residency investigating regional circular economy frameworks and industrial site revitalization.
