University of the Arts London: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established as a federal university in 1986 and receiving university status in 2003, University of the Arts London (UAL) educates over 21,000 students across six historic constituent colleges: Camberwell College of Arts, Central Saint Martins (CSM), Chelsea College of Arts, London College of Communication (LCC), London College of Fashion (LCF), and Wimbledon College of Arts. Consistently ranked second in the world for Art and Design in the QS World University Rankings, UAL is a global creative powerhouse producing generation-defining fashion designers, artists, animators, and creative tech directors. UAL is also home to the Creative Computing Institute (CCI) and the Fashion, Textiles and Technology Institute (FTTI).
