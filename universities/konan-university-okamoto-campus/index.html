Institution Overview & Profile

Konan University (Okamoto Campus), founded in 1919 by prominent industrialist Hachisaburo Hirao in the prestigious Okamoto district of Higashinada Ward, Kobe, is one of western Japan's most distinguished private research universities—part of the revered 'Kan-Kan-Do-Ritsu / San-Kin-Ko-Ryu' higher education consortia—offering acclaimed faculties in economics, letters, law, science and engineering, intelligence and informatics, and the pioneering FIRST Institute for Nanotechnology.

Key Academic Strengths

  • FIBER (Frontier Institute for Biomolecular Engineering Research) leading groundbreaking nucleic acid and nanobio research
  • Faculty of Intelligence and Informatics pioneering artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and data science
  • Hirao School of Management (CUBE) delivering innovative practical business leadership and global management training
  • Prestigious alumni network in Japanese industry, banking, international trade, and scientific research

Notable Faculty & Researchers (3)

Prof. Dr. Naoki Sugimoto

Director of FIBER & Professor of Chemical Biology · Frontier Institute for Biomolecular Engineering Research (FIBER)

Prof. Dr. Naoki Sugimoto is an internationally celebrated biophysicist directing FIBER, renowned for pioneering research on non-canonical DNA/RNA structures (G-quadruplexes, i-motifs) under cellular molecular crowding conditions.

Non-Canonical Nucleic Acid Structures Molecular Crowding Biophysics DNA Nanotechnology

Prof. Dr. Daisuke Miyoshi

Professor of Biophysical Chemistry · Department of Applied Chemistry / FIBER

Prof. Dr. Daisuke Miyoshi researches the physical chemistry of nucleic acid folding, designing synthetic oligonucleotides and fluorescent biosensors for early diagnostic detection of oncological markers.

Thermodynamic Stabilization of Biomolecules Biosensors Targeted Gene Therapeutics

Prof. Dr. Kenji Matsui

Dean, Faculty of Intelligence and Informatics · Faculty of Intelligence and Informatics
