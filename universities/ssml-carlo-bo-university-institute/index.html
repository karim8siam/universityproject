SSML Carlo Bo University Institute (Scuola Superiore Mediatori Linguistici Carlo Bo)

SSML Carlo Bo (Scuola Superiore per Mediatori Linguistici Carlo Bo) is Italy’s historic, state-recognized university institute dedicated to linguistic mediation, conference interpreting, multilingual diplomatic negotiation, and cross-border digital communication.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1951 by the renowned literary critic and senator-for-life Carlo Bo, SSML Carlo Bo has served for more than seven decades as the gold standard of Italian higher education in specialized translation and simultaneous interpretation. Recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) under Ministerial Decree 38/2002 as equivalent to university bachelor’s degrees (Classe L-12), the institute operates five campuses across Rome, Milan, Bari, Bologna, and Florence. Graduates serve throughout European Union institutions, international diplomatic missions, multinational corporations, and global media networks.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Corso di Laurea Triennale in Mediazione Linguistica (Classe L-12) (Bachelor's) — Three-year university degree conferring official legal qualification in two foreign languages with specializations in International Business, Diplomacy, or Digital Media.
  • Master di Primo Livello in Interpretariato di Conferenza (Master's) — Intensive postgraduate professional master's training simultaneous interpreters in soundproof conference booths for international institutions and summits.
  • Master in Traduzione Specialistica Giuridico-Economica (Master's) — Postgraduate professional master's focusing on multinational contract law, financial disclosures, European court filings, and patent translation.
  • Corso di Perfezionamento in Localizzazione e Scrittura Multilingue per il Web (Certificate) — Postgraduate specialization in software string localization, gaming transcreation, and global digital content strategy.
