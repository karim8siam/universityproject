ESCF (TU/e Eindhoven): Operations Management, AI Logistics & PhD Guide

The European Supply Chain Forum (ESCF) is the premier collaborative research institute for supply chain innovation and operations management, embedded within the Department of Industrial Engineering and Innovation Sciences at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e). Situated in the heart of the Brainport Eindhoven high-tech region—surrounded by global leaders such as ASML, Philips, DAF Trucks, and NXP—ESCF bridges academic science and industrial application in circular supply chain analytics, spare-parts logistics, AI-driven demand forecasting, and autonomous freight operations.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering, Operations Research, Applied Mathematics, or Computer Science with a minimum cumulative GPA of 75% or Dutch equivalent (7.0+); quantitative aptitude in stochastic modeling and linear programming required.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science in Industrial Engineering, Management Science, or Quantitative Economics with high honors; selection through competitive academic vacancies.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 21 in all sections) or IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all subscores).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral applications: motivation letter, Master's thesis, and academic writing sample analyzing an operations research problem.
Application fee
€100 application evaluation fee for international degree holders.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diploma, complete academic transcripts, syllabus descriptions of quantitative mathematics and statistics courses, CV, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is the statutory Dutch rate of approximately €2,530 per academic year. Non-EU/EEA international tuition is approximately €17,500 to €19,800 per year. Fully funded doctoral candidates are employed as salaried university researchers earning €2,770 to €3,539 per month gross, with zero tuition and an 8.3% holiday allowance.
