Keio University

Founded in 1858 by modern intellectual pioneer Yukichi Fukuzawa, Keio University is Japan's oldest private institute of higher education and a premier member of Japan's Top Global University Project. Headquartered in Mita, Tokyo, Keio is celebrated for top-ranked research at the Faculty of Science and Technology (Yagami Campus), Keio University School of Medicine (Shinanomachi Campus), the Keio Quantum Computing Center (partnering with IBM Quantum), and pioneer internet technologies through the WIDE Project.

Keio University Design the Future Award & MEXT University Recommendation Scholarships

Prestigious Japanese government doctoral award administered through Keio University, offering complete financial support, tuition waivers, and airfare for premier international research scholars.

Keio Graduate School of Science and Technology International Graduate Program (IGP) PhD

All-English doctoral degree conducted at the Yagami Campus in Yokohama, featuring advanced research in mechanical engineering, electronics, applied chemistry, and systems design.

Keio Master of Science in Computer Science & Quantum Information Systems

Two-year English-taught Master's program in computer systems, quantum algorithms, network architecture, and distributed cloud computing with access to the Keio Quantum Computing Center.

Keio PhD in Molecular Medicine & Regenerative Biology

Four-year medical doctorate located at the Shinanomachi Campus in central Tokyo, conducting pioneering research in induced pluripotent stem (iPS) cells, neural regeneration, and precision oncology.

Keio Quantum Computing Center Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Research appointment at Japan's premier quantum computing center, collaborating with industry partners (including IBM) on quantum error mitigation, quantum chemistry simulations, and quantum machine learning.

Keio Keio-NUS CUTE Center Summer Research Traineeship in Mixed Reality & Media Computing

Ten-week research internship at the Keio Graduate School of Media Design (KMD), designing immersive augmented reality experiences, haptic interfaces, and creative digital media systems.

Keio Project Assistant Professor in Sustainable Polymer Chemistry & Biomaterials

Full-time faculty role conducting independent and collaborative research in bio-based degradable polymers, redox-active macromolecular materials, and energy storage electrolytes.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of a 16-year school education curriculum culminating in a recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA or equivalent grade B+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree or professional degree; submission of Master's thesis and passing marks in oral defense and doctoral screening.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 88 for English-based programs (International Graduate Program - IGP); Japanese JLPT N1 for Japanese-medium graduate divisions.
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research proposal detailing proposed doctoral research topic, theoretical framework, research methodology, and anticipated academic contributions.
Application fee
¥35,000 non-refundable screening fee submitted via the Keio online admissions portal.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, complete official academic transcripts, research proposal, 2 letters of recommendation, and English proficiency test report.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for graduate programs at Keio are approximately ¥1,200,000 to ¥1,450,000 per year. Admitted international scholars under the Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship receive 100% full tuition waivers, round-trip international airfare, and a monthly tax-free living stipend of ¥145,000 to ¥148,000 per month (approx. ¥1,750,000/year).
