Institutional Overview

Hamm-Lippstadt University of Applied Sciences (Hochschule Hamm-Lippstadt / HSHL) is an agile, modern state university founded in 2009 with twin state-of-the-art campuses in Hamm and Lippstadt. HSHL is celebrated for its interdisciplinary engineering degrees, mechatronics, biomedical technology, environmental economics, and close integration with automotive and industrial supply chains in North Rhine-Westphalia.
