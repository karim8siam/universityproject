East Texas A&M University-Dallas

East Texas A&M University-Dallas (formerly Texas A&M University-Commerce at Dallas) is a metropolitan instructional center located in downtown Dallas, providing degree programs in business analytics, cybersecurity, finance, visual communication, and urban educational leadership.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating in modern high-rise facilities on North Central Expressway in Dallas, ETAMU Dallas provides urban undergraduate and graduate students direct corporate linkages with the Dallas-Fort Worth Fortune 500 economic corridor. Programs emphasize artificial intelligence modeling, fintech, corporate cyber defense, and visual design communication.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school class rank and GPA (automatic admission for top 25% of Texas high school classes); test-optional. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree with a 2.75+ cumulative GPA and programmatic prerequisites.
