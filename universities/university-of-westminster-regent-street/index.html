University of Westminster - Regent Street

The historic Regent Street Campus (309 Regent Street) is the birthplace and headquarters of the University of Westminster, located in central London near Oxford Circus. Established as Britain's first polytechnic institution in 1838, it houses Westminster Law School and the Centre for the Study of Democracy.

Academic overview & excellence

Regent Street features the world-famous Regent Street Cinema (birthplace of British cinema in 1896), state-of-the-art mock courtrooms, the Westminster International Law Clinic, and advanced research centres in international dispute resolution, constitutional reform, and global human rights law.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate law admissions typically require A-Level grades ABB or IB Diploma 32 points. Postgraduate LLM and PhD programs require an upper second-class honours degree (2:1) in law or related social sciences and an academic research statement. English proficiency: IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in writing).
