Institution Overview & Profile

International Business School The Hague (IBSH), situated in the international City of Peace and Justice in The Hague, is an accredited boutique business school offering English-taught bachelor, master, and doctoral programs in international business administration, international relations and diplomacy, banking and finance, and digital marketing.

Institution TypePrivate International Business School
Main CampusThe Hague Campus (Visseringlaan), South Holland, Netherlands
AccreditationAccredited by ASIC (Premier Institution Status) / ATHE Partner / EduQua Recognized
Admissions RouteRolling trimester intakes (September, January, May)
Language of InstructionEnglish (100% English instruction; IELTS 6.0/6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80+)
Financial Aid & GrantsIBSH Merit Scholarships, Diplomatic Corps Reductions & Institutional Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Unique location in The Hague offering direct proximity to over 150 international courts, embassies, and multilateral agencies
  • Specialized Master of Arts in International Relations and Diplomacy with guest lectures by serving ambassadors
  • Small seminar classes guaranteeing close individual mentorship and personalized professional networking
  • Integrated corporate and diplomatic internship placements across international NGOs, courts, and multinational corporations

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) Enrolment

Internship

Structured 16-week professional internship placed within international non-governmental organizations, diplomatic missions, or international legal bodies in The Hague.

Funding: €600 to €1,100 monthly internship stipend paid by host organization Deadline: April 30, 2027

M.A. in International Relations and Diplomacy Enrolment

Two-year 120-ECTS postgraduate degree examining peace studies, bilateral negotiation, public international law, and diplomatic protocol.
