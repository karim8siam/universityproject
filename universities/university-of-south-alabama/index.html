University of South Alabama

South Alabama · Mobile, Alabama, United States

The University of South Alabama (South Alabama / USA) is a premier public doctoral research university and academic medical center located in Mobile on the historic Alabama Gulf Coast. Renowned for marine biology through the Dauphin Island Sea Lab, coastal civil engineering, chemical engineering, and advanced medicine at the USA Health system, South Alabama anchors the regional healthcare and aerospace manufacturing economy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

South Alabama encompasses ten colleges and schools: the Frederick P. Whiddon College of Medicine, College of Engineering, College of Nursing, College of Arts and Sciences, Mitchell College of Business, School of Computing, College of Allied Health Professions, and College of Education. South Alabama operates the Mitchell Cancer Institute and the USA Technology & Research Park.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5) and coursework. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
