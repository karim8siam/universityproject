Slippery Rock University

Slippery Rock · Slippery Rock, Pennsylvania, United States

Slippery Rock University (SRU) is a premier public university in western Pennsylvania and a four-star member of Pennsylvania's State System of Higher Education (PASSHE). Situated on an expansive 660-acre campus in Butler County, SRU is acclaimed nationwide for its top-tier Doctor of Physical Therapy program, safety management, exercise science, and environmental education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SRU comprises the College of Health Professions, the College of Engineering and Science, the College of Business, and the College of Liberal Arts. It features cutting-edge biomechanics laboratories, a 70-acre Macoskey Center for Sustainable Systems Education and Research, and an NSA-designated National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), college prep coursework, and optional standardized test scores. Graduate programs, including the competitive Doctor of Physical Therapy and MS in Occupational Safety, require GRE scores, clinical prerequisites, and PTCAS submissions.
