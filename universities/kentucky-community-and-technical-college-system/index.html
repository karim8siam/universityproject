Kentucky Community and Technical College System

KCTCS · Versailles, Kentucky, United States

The Kentucky Community and Technical College System (KCTCS) comprises 16 accredited colleges across more than 70 campuses throughout the Commonwealth of Kentucky. Headquartered in Versailles, KY, KCTCS educates over 100,000 students annually, offering university-parallel transfer associate degrees, high-demand healthcare pathways, automated manufacturing credentials, and advanced cybersecurity diplomas.

The Kentucky Community and Technical College System (KCTCS) comprises 16 accredited colleges across more than 70 campuses throughout the Commonwealth of Kentucky. Headquartered in Versailles, KY, KCTCS educates over 100,000 students annually, offering university-parallel transfer associate degrees, high-demand healthcare pathways, automated manufacturing credentials, and advanced cybersecurity diplomas.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Open admission policy with submission of official high school transcripts or GED credential. Specific health sciences programs maintain selective admission criteria. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international candidates without prior English-language secondary schooling.
