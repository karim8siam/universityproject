Columbia International University

Columbia International University (CIU) is a private university in Columbia, South Carolina, offering accredited undergraduate, graduate, and seminary programs in clinical mental health counseling, intercultural studies, business administration, biomedical science, and disaster relief.

Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by SACSCOC, CACREP, and ATS, CIU integrates academic rigor with servant leadership. Its Clinical Mental Health Counseling program and Zwemer Center for Intercultural Studies attract students from over 40 nations for research in trauma counseling, global literacy, and humanitarian aid.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation credentials with minimum 2.0 GPA and a Christian ministry reference. Graduate programs require an earned bachelor’s degree with 2.70+ GPA, personal statement, and department interview.
