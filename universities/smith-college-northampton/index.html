Smith College

Smith College · Northampton, Massachusetts, United States

Smith College is an internationally renowned private women's liberal arts college in Northampton, Massachusetts, and a distinguished member of the prestigious Seven Sisters and Five College Consortium. Smith is famous for establishing the Picker Program in Engineering Science—the first accredited engineering program at a women's college in the United States.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Smith College offers a flexible, open curriculum without distribution requirements, encouraging interdisciplinary intellectual exploration. Highlights include the Picker Engineering Program, the innovative Maya Lin-designed Neilson Library, the Botanic Garden, and world-renowned programs in political science, economics, computer science, and biological sciences.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Smith College practices holistic, test-optional undergraduate admissions for women, evaluating academic rigor, intellectual curiosity, essays, and leadership. The Smith College School for Social Work admits graduate students of all genders for its acclaimed MSW and Ph.D. clinical social work programs.
