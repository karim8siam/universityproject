ADG Business School: Dual Finance Degrees, Cooperative Banking & Faculty Guide

Operating from historic Montabaur Castle in Rhineland-Palatinate, ADG Business School is a specialized business school of the prestigious state-recognized Steinbeis University (Steinbeis-Hochschule). Established by the Academy of German Cooperatives (ADG), the school is Germany's premier academic partner for the cooperative financial network (Volksbanken Raiffeisenbanken) and corporate finance enterprises, offering dual, practice-integrated Bachelor of Arts and Master of Science degrees.

Genossenschaftliche FinanzGruppe Corporate Tuition Sponsorship Grant

Employer-funded tuition sponsorship and trainee salary supporting ambitious high school and university graduates through dual finance degrees at Schloss Montabaur.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Banking, Finance & Risk Management

Two-year dual master's program integrating advanced credit risk modeling, regulatory bank compliance (Basel IV/MaRisk), algorithmic wealth management, and corporate valuation.

Doctoral Research Fellowship in Financial Technology and Cooperative Economics

Doctoral research fellowship examining digital banking transformation, ESG compliance, and the structural resilience of cooperative financial institutions during economic shocks.

European Sustainable Finance and ESG Credit Risk Modeling Investigation

Empirical banking research project evaluating ESG risk integration, EU Taxonomy compliance, and carbon-adjusted credit ratings for German small-to-medium enterprise (Mittelstand) borrowers.

Cooperative Banking Corporate Client Treasury and Credit Analysis Practicum

Integrated dual banking practicum alternating theoretical block seminars at Montabaur Castle with hands-on corporate credit evaluation, asset management, and risk auditing at regional bank headquarters.

Schloss Montabaur Digital Banking and FinTech Leadership Fellowship

Academic development fellowship honoring high-potential banking executives pioneering digital product innovation, mobile customer journeys, and open banking protocols.

Professor of Financial Management and Corporate Valuation

Permanent professorial position lecturing undergraduate and graduate finance courses, supervising applied master's theses, and conducting research in cooperative banking.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in business administration, economics, or financial informatics with minimum grade 2.5 on the German grading scale, plus ongoing employment in a bank or financial enterprise.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Academic master's graduates articulate directly into doctoral research programs (Dr. rer. pol.) at Steinbeis University or partner German university research chairs.
Language proficiency
German language proficiency at CEFR C1 level (TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2); working knowledge of English (B2 level) for international financial management modules.
Research statement / Proposal
Application dossier submitted via the ADG Business School portal including study-work contract signed by corporate employer sponsor.
Application fee
Zero application fee; program admission and counseling are free of charge.
Required documentation
Certified university diploma certificates, official transcripts, CV, corporate training/employment verification, and valid ID copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for dual bachelor's programs is approximately €395 to €495 per month (€14,220 total across 36 months); tuition for master's programs is approximately €595 per month (€14,280 total across 24 months). In over 85% of cases, tuition fees are fully or substantially co-funded by the student's sponsoring corporate employer (such as a regional Volksbank, cooperative bank, or audit firm).
