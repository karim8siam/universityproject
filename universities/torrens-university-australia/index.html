Torrens University Australia: Business Analytics, AI Design, Health & Nursing Guide

Established in 2012 in Adelaide, South Australia, Torrens University Australia is an innovative, fast-growing national university operating campuses in Adelaide, Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane. Dedicated to experiential learning, Torrens integrates commercial partnerships with modern curriculum design across artificial intelligence, digital design, business analytics, global public health, and hospitality management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Australian bachelor's degree (or international overseas equivalent) in any discipline with a minimum pass grade (equivalent to 65% or 2.7/4.0 GPA), plus relevant professional resume.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree by research with high distinction (H1/H2A) or honours first class; demonstration of substantial peer-reviewed publications and competitive research proposal.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with no band score below 6.0, TOEFL iBT 79, or Pearson PTE Academic 58.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral and MPhil applicants: 2,500-word formal research proposal outlining research methodology, ethical considerations, and prospective supervisor alignment.
Application fee
$0 AUD online application fee for direct international and domestic applications.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree graduation certificates, comprehensive CV/resume, statement of intent, and two academic/professional reference letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at Torrens University Australia range between $24,000 and $32,000 AUD per year depending on the faculty and course level. Torrens provides international merit scholarships providing between 15% and 30% tuition fee remissions, alongside doctoral research bursaries covering full tuition.
