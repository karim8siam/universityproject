Academic overview & research priorities

Affiliated with the Churches of Christ, Harding combines liberal arts rigor with top professional schools including the College of Pharmacy, Carr College of Nursing, and College of Allied Health. Known for high national licensure pass rates and extensive global study centers.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with 3.00+ GPA (or qualifying ACT 19+ / SAT 1010+). Doctoral and graduate health programs require a bachelor’s degree, competitive prerequisite GPA, standardized health exam scores (PCAT/GRE/MCAT), and interview.
