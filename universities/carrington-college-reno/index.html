Institutional Overview

Carrington College, founded in 1967 and maintaining an accredited state-of-the-art healthcare campus in Reno, Nevada, is a dedicated institution of allied health and nursing education. Institutionally accredited by the Accrediting Commission for Community and Junior Colleges (ACCJC/WASC), Carrington prepares licensed practical nurses, medical assistants, dental hygienists, and respiratory therapists through clinical simulation labs and regional hospital externships.
