Tokyo Denki University (TDU)

Tokyo Denki University (TDU) is a prestigious private technology university in Tokyo, founded in 1907 with the historic motto "In the Technology Lies Its Value," internationally celebrated for robotics, electrical engineering, cybersecurity, and architectural engineering.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1907 by two young electrical engineers, Seiichi Hirota and Sadanosuke Ogawa, Tokyo Denki University has educated generations of Japan's premier engineers who built the nation's electrical, electronic, and industrial infrastructure. Today, TDU educates over 10,000 students across two ultra-modern campuses: the flagship Tokyo Senju Campus in Adachi-ku, Tokyo, and the green Saitama Hatoyama Campus. TDU operates world-class research institutes in robotics engineering, advanced materials, and cybersecurity, maintaining close joint-venture laboratories with Hitachi, Sony, Panasonic, and Mitsubishi Electric.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Advanced Science and Technology (Informatics and Robotics) (Doctorate) — Cutting-edge doctoral research exploring wearable exoskeleton control, autonomous bipedal navigation, and human-robot cooperative manipulation.
  • Master of Engineering in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (Master's) — Graduate program covering wide-bandgap semiconductors (SiC/GaN), high-voltage transmission, and wireless power transfer.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Robotics and Mechatronics (Bachelor's) — JABEE-accredited four-year degree featuring intensive studio design of mobile robotics, micro-actuators, and embedded real-time software.
  • Master of Engineering in Information and Communication Engineering (Master's) — Specialized master's curriculum focused on next-generation 6G mobile networks, cryptographic protocols, and quantum communication.
