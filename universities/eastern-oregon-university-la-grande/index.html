Academic overview & research priorities

Located in the scenic Grande Ronde Valley, EOU serves as an essential regional center for higher education and applied environmental research in the Pacific Northwest. Academic hallmarks include agricultural science partnerships with Oregon State University, outdoor science education, anthropology, and digital business administration.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation or GED with cumulative GPA >= 2.75. Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT) are test-optional. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+.
