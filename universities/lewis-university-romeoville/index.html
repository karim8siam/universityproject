Lewis University

Lewis Univ · Romeoville, Illinois, United States

Lewis University is a private Lasallian Catholic university in Romeoville, Illinois. Renowned nationally for aviation flight technology, air traffic control, cybersecurity, and nursing, Lewis operates an on-campus airport (Lewis University Airport - KLOT).

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lewis features the College of Aviation, Science and Technology, and the College of Nursing and Health Sciences. It is designated as an FAA Part 141 collegiate flight institution and a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), coursework, and essays under test-optional review. Flight training majors must secure an FAA Medical Certificate prior to flight course registration.
