South Dakota State University College of Nursing - Rapid City

SDSU Nursing Rapid City · Rapid City, South Dakota, United States

The South Dakota State University (SDSU) College of Nursing West River Center in Rapid City is a premier regional healthcare education and clinical simulation hub. Serving western South Dakota, the Black Hills, and neighboring Native American tribal nations, it prepares registered nurses, nurse practitioners, and nursing leaders dedicated to rural and indigenous healthcare excellence.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Operating in collaboration with Monument Health Rapid City Hospital and regional Indian Health Service (IHS) facilities, the Rapid City site features modern high-fidelity simulation labs, standardized patient suites, and telemedicine training modules. It offers accelerated BSN tracks, Master of Science in Nursing programs, and clinical Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) degrees.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate BSN applicants must complete prerequisite natural and behavioral science coursework with a minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA and acceptable TEAS examination scores. Graduate DNP and MSN programs require an active, unencumbered RN license, clinical nursing practice experience, professional references, and a clinical practice goal statement.
