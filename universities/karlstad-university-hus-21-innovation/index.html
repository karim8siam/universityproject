Karlstad University - Hus 21 Innovation Center: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located on the central campus of Karlstad University in Värmland, Hus 21 houses the world-famous Service Research Center (CTF - Centrum för tjänsteforskning), the Computer Science Privacy and Security Lab, and leading bio-economy innovation nodes. Hus 21 serves as a collaborative incubator where academic researchers, global enterprises (including Ericsson, Telia, and Volvo), and Swedish regional agencies co-create groundbreaking user-centric service ecosystems and sustainable bio-based materials.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
National Swedish University Admissions (Antagning.se); bachelor's degree for master's/doctoral study
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Swedish & English (Extensive array of master's and PhD programs taught 100% in English)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
SEK 900 (University Admissions Sweden application fee)
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees and student contributions vary across degree levels and residency status. Verified opportunity records list typical costs between SEK 140,000/year (approx. €12,500/year for non-EU/EEA students before scholarship waiver) and departmental funding packages.
