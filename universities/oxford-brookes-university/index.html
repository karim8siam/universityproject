Oxford Brookes University: Motorsport Engineering, Architecture & Hospitality Guide

Oxford Brookes University, located in the world-famous city of Oxford, is a top UK modern university globally renowned for educating Formula 1 race engineers (Motorsport Engineering), the prestigious School of Architecture, and international hospitality management.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Oxford Brookes Vice-Chancellor's PhD Studentship in Motorsport Aerodynamics

Doctoral studentship at the Centre for Automotive and Motorsport Engineering exploring unsteady CFD ground-effect aerodynamics, vortex control, and wind tunnel testing for high-performance race cars.

MSc in Motorsport Engineering (Formula 1 Engineering Pathway)

World-renowned master's program located in the UK's 'Motorsport Valley', providing advanced training in vehicle dynamics, race car telemetry, telemetry analytics, and composite structures.

Ph.D. in Sustainable Architecture & Net-Zero Retrofit Technologies

Doctoral research position at the Oxford Institute for Sustainable Development (OISD) modeling post-occupancy building performance, embodied carbon, and natural ventilation in historic UK housing stock.

Primate Conservation & Tropical Forest Habitat Fragmentation Project

World-renowned primate conservation research group conducting field camera-trap monitoring, acoustic sensors, and canopy corridor mapping to protect endangered primates across Southeast Asia and Africa.

Formula 1 Team Trackside Engineering & Vehicle Dynamics Practicum

Twelve-month full-time industrial engineering placement embedded directly inside an active Formula 1 team (e.g., Williams, Red Bull Racing, Alpine, Mercedes-AMG) based in the Oxfordshire region.

Oxford Institute for Sustainable Development Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating community energy resilience, climate adaptation in urban planning, and circular construction timber supply chains in the UK.

Senior Lecturer in Automotive Powertrain & Battery Energy Storage

Academic faculty post in the School of Engineering, Computing and Mathematics delivering lectures on electric vehicle powertrains, thermal management of lithium-ion battery packs, and race car electrification.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:1 or 2:2 Honours degree or international equivalent in a relevant discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First class or upper second class (2:1) UK Honours degree or Master's degree with Merit/Distinction in related fields.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with a minimum of 6.0 in reading and writing, and 5.5 in listening and speaking.
Research statement / Proposal
1,500-word doctoral research project proposal detailing methodology, literature review, and alignment with research groups.
Application fee
Zero application fee for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Degree certificates and transcripts, personal statement, two references (at least one academic), and passport scan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time domestic postgraduate tuition is £9,250 to £11,500 per year. International student tuition is £16,900 to £19,500 per year. Fully funded doctoral studentships cover 100% of fees plus an annual tax-free living stipend (£19,237).
