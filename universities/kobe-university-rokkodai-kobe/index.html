Kobe University - Rokkodai Main Campus

Kobe University is one of Japan’s prestigious National Seven Universities (formerly Kobe Higher Commercial School), overlooking Osaka Bay from Mount Rokko, celebrated as Japan’s birthplace of business administration and economics, alongside world-leading research in membranes, law, and biosystems.

Academic overview & research priorities

Renowned for establishing Japan’s first Faculty of Business Administration, Kobe University excels in international corporate management, computational science (partnered with the Fugaku supercomputer at RIKEN Kobe), membrane science technology, and international law. The Rokkodai Campus houses historic stone buildings designated as Tangible Cultural Properties.

Research admissions & international criteria

Graduate admissions require a recognized Bachelor or Master degree with competitive academic standing, research proposal, and letters of recommendation. English-taught graduate programs (KIMAP, GMAP, and Engineering) require TOEFL iBT 85+ or IELTS 6.5+.
