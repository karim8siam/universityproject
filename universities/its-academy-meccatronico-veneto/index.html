ITS Academy Meccatronico Veneto

ITS Academy Meccatronico Veneto is Italy’s leading government-accredited tertiary technical academy (Istituto Tecnico Superiore), training highly specialized engineers in mechatronic systems, smart robotics, additive manufacturing, and automated industrial IoT.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established under the Ministry of Education and Merit framework in the industrial heart of the Veneto region (Vicenza, Padua, Treviso, Verona, Venice, and Belluno), ITS Academy Meccatronico Veneto is recognized as Italy’s top-ranked higher technical institute for mechanical engineering and automation. With over 800 students across ten regional training hubs and an employment rate exceeding 98% within twelve months of graduation, the academy works directly with over 400 manufacturing leaders. Instruction is delivered by senior corporate engineers and researchers using advanced dual-training models, cyber-physical automation benches, and digital twins.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Tecnico Superiore per l'Automazione ed i Sistemi Meccatronici (EQF 5) (Higher Technical Diploma) — Two-year intensive diploma comprising 2,000 hours of training (with 800 hours embedded in advanced manufacturing firms) in PLC programming, robotic cells, and pneumatic circuits.
  • Tecnico Superiore per la Progettazione Meccanica ed i Materiali Innovativi (Higher Technical Diploma) — Specialized track focusing on parametric CAD modeling, FEM structural simulation, composite materials, and metal additive manufacturing.
  • Tecnico Superiore per la Digitalizzazione dei Processi Industriali (Smart Factory) (Higher Technical Diploma) — Curriculum developing expertise in industrial cloud integration, edge computing, SCADA systems, and predictive maintenance algorithms.
  • Corso di Perfezionamento in Robotica Collaborativa e Sicurezza Macchine (Certificate) — Post-diploma certificate in CE machine safety compliance, risk analysis, and collaborative robotics programming.
