Niagara University in Ontario: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Licensed by the Ontario Ministry of Colleges and Universities and accredited by the Ontario College of Teachers (OCT) and AACSB International, Niagara University in Ontario provides Canadian students and international professionals with cutting-edge graduate and professional degree pathways. Its modern urban campus in Vaughan is integrated into the Greater Toronto Area transit hub, enabling strong industry partnerships in educational leadership, finance, and cybersecurity.
