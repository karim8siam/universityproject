University of Nebraska at Kearney

UNK · Kearney, Nebraska, United States

The University of Nebraska at Kearney (UNK) is a premier public university established in 1905, situated on a 515-acre residential campus in Kearney, Nebraska. Celebrated for high-impact undergraduate research, healthcare professional training in partnership with UNMC at the Health Science Education Complex, biology, and aviation, UNK combines personalized faculty mentorship with the vast resources of the University of Nebraska system.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNK comprises three academic colleges: the College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business and Technology, and College of Education. UNK is home to the Health Science Education Complex (HSEC), delivering rural nursing, physician assistant, and physical therapy credentials, as well as the Nebraska Safety Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core competencies, GPA (minimum 2.5), or class rank. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
