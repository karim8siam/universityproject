University of Dallas

UDallas · Irving, Texas, United States

The University of Dallas (UDallas), established in 1956 in Irving, Texas, is a premier private Catholic liberal arts university. Celebrated for its rigorous Core Curriculum rooted in the Western literary and philosophical canon, its signature Rome Program on the Eugene Constantin Campus, and the Braniff Graduate School of Liberal Arts, UDallas fosters scholarly contemplation and moral leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UDallas comprises Constantin College of Liberal Arts, the Braniff Graduate School of Liberal Arts, the Satish & Yasmin Gupta College of Business, and the Neuhoff Institute for Ministry & Evangelization. Its faculty maintain deep scholarly traditions in political philosophy, classical literature, and cyber governance.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission operates through the Common Application (holistic review; test-optional; average GPA 3.8 unweighted). Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, submit analytical writing samples, statements of purpose, and academic recommendation letters.
