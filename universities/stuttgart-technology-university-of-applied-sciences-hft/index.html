Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Hochschule für Technik Stuttgart (HFT Stuttgart) is a premier university of applied sciences renowned for its pioneering expertise in sustainable building physics, climate-neutral architecture, smart city geomatics, and civil engineering nestled in Germany's high-tech industrial heartland.

Institution type
Public University of Applied Sciences (Fachhochschule)
Campus location
Stuttgart City Centre Campus (adjacent to University of Stuttgart)
Accreditation
Ministry of Science, Research and the Arts Baden-Württemberg / ASIIN / ZEvA
Admissions channel
Winter semester (July 15) and Summer semester (January 15)
Instruction language
German & English (several international Master's in English)
Financial aid & support
Deutschlandstipendium, Baden-Württemberg-STIPENDIUM, DAAD Grants

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Center for Sustainable Building Technologies (ZAF) and net-zero energy design
  • Pioneering International Master of Photogrammetry and Geoinformatics
  • Close partnerships with Daimler, Bosch, Porsche, and DLR aerospace center
  • State-of-the-art building physics acoustics and climate test labs

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Photogrammetry and Geoinformatics (Graduate Degree) — Internationally renowned English-taught master's covering 3D laser scanning, GIS algorithms, satellite geodesy, and digital twins.
  • Master of Science in Smart City Solutions (Graduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary program integrating urban renewable energy systems, sustainable mobility, and climate resilience planning.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Bauingenieurwesen (Civil Engineering) (Undergraduate Degree) — Rigorous German engineering degree focusing on structural mechanics, timber construction, and geotechnical engineering.
