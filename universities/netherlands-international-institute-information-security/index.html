Institutional Overview

Netherlands International Institute of Information Security (NIIIS) is a premier specialized graduate institute located at Nico Jessekade in Amsterdam. Operating at the cutting edge of European cyber resilience, NIIIS delivers certified executive master's, postgraduate diplomas, and applied security labs in offensive cybersecurity, industrial control system (ICS) defense, cryptographic engineering, and NIS2 regulatory compliance.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Dedicated European center focused exclusively on critical infrastructure (OT/ICS) protection and threat intelligence
  • Hands-on red team / blue team cyber range simulating real-time advanced persistent threat (APT) state cyberattacks
  • Strategic location in Amsterdam, one of the primary internet exchange (AMS-IX) capitals of the world
  • Close partnerships with Dutch National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) and European cyber agencies
