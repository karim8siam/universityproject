UnitelmaSapienza University (Polo di Bologna)

Attain Sapienza-backed university degrees in digital law, healthcare management, and economic crime analysis at UnitelmaSapienza Bologna.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

UnitelmaSapienza University is the premier digital university directly linked to Sapienza University of Rome. Its regional learning hub in Bologna delivers state-accredited undergraduate and master's degrees in legal sciences, public administration management, healthcare governance, and digital crime prevention.

Institution type
State-Accredited Online University (Sapienza University of Rome Linked)
Campus location
Polo Didattico di Bologna, Via Alfredo Calzoni, Bologna
Accreditation
Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) / ANVUR
Admissions channel
Continuous open enrolment throughout the academic year
Instruction language
Italian (selected Master's modules in English)
Financial aid & support
Discounts for Public Employees (PA 110 e lode), Merit Scholarships

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Direct academic affiliation and shared professors with Sapienza Università di Roma
  • Flexible asynchronous e-learning platform coupled with in-person Bologna exam polo
  • National benchmark for Healthcare Governance (Management delle Aziende Sanitarie)
  • Specialized Master's in Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics, and Anti-Money Laundering

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Laurea Magistrale a Ciclo Unico in Giurisprudenza (LMG-01) (Five-Year Master of Laws) — Complete five-year legal degree qualifying graduates for the judiciary, bar examinations, and notary professions.
  • Master di I Livello in Management e Funzioni di Coordinamento delle Professioni Sanitarie (Master's Degree) — State-mandated qualification for head nurses and healthcare coordinators across Italian regional health services (SSN).
  • Corso di Laurea in Scienze dell'Economia Aziendale (L-18) (Undergraduate Degree) — Three-year business economics degree with tracks in corporate sustainability, digital accounting, and financial management.
