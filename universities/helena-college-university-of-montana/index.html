Helena College University of Montana

Helena College · Helena, Montana, United States

Helena College University of Montana is a comprehensive public two-year institution in Montana’s state capital, offering transfer associate degrees, allied health programs, aviation maintenance, and computer networking technologies.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Affiliated with the Montana University System, Helena College provides direct transfer pathways to four-year state universities like University of Montana and Montana State University, alongside specialized hands-on technical trades and registered nursing licensure tracks.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Operates under open-door admissions for high school graduates and GED recipients. Selective competitive admission applies to the Associate of Science in Registered Nursing and Aviation Maintenance Technology.
