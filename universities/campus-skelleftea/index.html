Campus Skellefteå: Battery Tech, Wood Engineering & Computer Games Guide

Established in 1986 in the epicentre of Europe's green industrial transition, Campus Skellefteå (Västerbotten) is a dynamic university hub hosting pioneering faculties from Luleå University of Technology (LTU) and Umeå University. Situated beside major green-transition enterprises and game development studios, Campus Skellefteå leads the world in battery cell electrification research, advanced wood engineering, and computer game programming.

Northern Sweden Green Transition & Battery Technology Scholarship

Prestigious industrial scholarship supporting exceptional engineering candidates pursuing master's degrees in electrification and advanced materials at Campus Skellefteå.

Master of Science in Wood Technology and Computational Timber Construction

Two-year specialized master's curriculum delivered through LTU at Skellefteå focusing on cross-laminated timber (CLT), tall wooden building mechanics, and circular wood bioproducts.

Sub-Arctic Lithium-Ion Battery Recycling and Hydrometallurgy Doctoral Fellowship

Four-year university doctoral position conducted jointly with LTU and battery gigafactories in Skellefteå optimizing green leaching agents for battery cathode materials.

Campus Skellefteå AI in Industrial Timber Scanning and Sorting Project

Applied computer vision research project deploying 3D convolutional neural networks to predict timber board mechanical strength from high-speed sawmill CT logs.

Skellefteå Industrial Battery and Green Manufacturing Internship

Six-month master's thesis internship placed within battery manufacturing facilities and clean-tech equipment suppliers in Skellefteå analyzing electrode coating uniformity.

Campus Skellefteå Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Bio-Composites and Timber Mechanics

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment investigating adhesive-free timber joint mechanics, bio-based resin binders, and long-term acoustic damping in tall wooden buildings.

Skellefteå Municipality Arctic Innovation and Community Grant

Municipal scholarship celebrating students who contribute innovative engineering solutions and active civic engagement toward the sustainable transformation of northern Sweden.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in mechanical, materials, chemical, or electrical engineering, computer science, or architecture (minimum 180 ECTS) with solid mathematics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct doctoral research administered through Luleå University of Technology (LTU) or Umeå University; requiring an approved master's degree and scientific proposal.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper secondary school English 6 (IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 overall).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for advanced technical research bursaries: 1,000-word statement outlining methodology in battery recycling, engineered timber, or real-time graphics rendering.
Application fee
0 SEK for Swedish and EU/EEA citizens; 900 SEK centralized application fee via UniversityAdmissions.se for non-EU applicants.
Required documentation
Certified university transcripts and degree certificates, copy of valid passport, proof of English language competence, resume, and letter of motivation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 0 SEK for Swedish, Nordic, and EU/EEA/Swiss citizens, who are eligible for full Swedish state CSN study grants and loans (approx. 13,100 SEK per month). For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is approximately 140,000 to 160,000 SEK per year, supported by industrial green transition scholarships.
