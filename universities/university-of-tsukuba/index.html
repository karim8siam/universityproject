University of Tsukuba (Tsukuba Science City)

Located in the heart of Tsukuba Science City—Japan's premier scientific research metropolis containing over 300 national research institutes—the University of Tsukuba is a Designated National University Corporation and one of Japan's top research powerhouses. Associated with three Nobel laureates (Leo Esaki, Hideki Shirakawa, and Sin-Itiro Tomonaga), Tsukuba is internationally famous for pioneering the field of cybernics (fusing human, machine, and information systems, creating the HAL robotic exoskeleton), high-performance computing, structural genomics, and Olympic sports sciences. Two-year international master's degree preparing leaders for diplomatic careers, United Nations agencies, and think tanks with special emphasis on Asia-Pacific diplomacy and human security.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
16 years of formal education culminating in a recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent academic certification) with superior academic marks.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (or professional degree) in a relevant discipline with demonstrable thesis research and academic publications.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 80+ for international English-taught graduate programs; JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-medium courses.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2 to 3-page research plan in English or Japanese outlining research purpose, proposed methodologies, and prospective supervisor endorsement.
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee for general graduate application; completely waived for Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship recipients.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, graduation certificates, thesis abstract, research proposal, 2 recommendation letters, and prospective supervisor acceptance letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for national universities in Japan is standardized by the Ministry of Education (MEXT). Standard tuition is ¥535,800 per year (approx. USD $3,600/year) plus a one-time admission fee of ¥282,000. MEXT Japanese Government Scholarship recipients receive 100% full tuition and admission fee exemptions, round-trip international airfare, and a tax-free monthly living stipend of ¥144,000 (Master's) to ¥147,000 (PhD) per month.
