Takachiho University

Takachiho University is a historic private commercial university in Suginami-ku, Tokyo, renowned as Japan's first private higher commercial school, offering specialized degrees in business administration, commercial law, and human science.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded by Baron Shibusawa Eiichi’s contemporary, Dr. Ryukichi Endo, Takachiho excels in practical corporate entrepreneurship, accounting, business informatics, and consumer psychology. The university maintains small-group seminars and corporate internship pipelines across central Tokyo, preparing students for executive roles in commerce, financial consulting, and digital marketing.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation and entrance evaluation. International applicants require EJU Japanese score >= 200 or JLPT N2, official secondary school transcripts, and an academic interview.
