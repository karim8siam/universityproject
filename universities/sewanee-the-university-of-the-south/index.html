Sewanee | The University of the South

Sewanee · Sewanee, Tennessee, United States

The University of the South, commonly known as Sewanee, is a prestigious private Episcopal liberal arts university situated on a breathtaking 13,000-acre mountaintop campus called 'The Domain' on the Cumberland Plateau in Sewanee, Tennessee. Known for its historic School of Theology, the Sewanee Review, and exceptional forestry and natural resources research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Sewanee encompasses the College of Arts and Sciences and the School of Theology. The sprawling 13,000-acre Domain serves as an expansive living laboratory for environmental biology, forestry, hydrology, and cave biodiversity. Its historic collegiate gothic architecture, academic gown tradition, and world-class faculty provide an immersive liberal arts experience.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are holistic and test-optional, evaluating high school academic rigor, writing essays, leadership, and community service. Graduate theological admissions require official academic transcripts, theological essays, personal recommendations from diocese leadership or clergy, and interviews.
