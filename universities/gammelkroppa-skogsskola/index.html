Gammelkroppa skogsskola: Sustainable Forestry, Drone Remote Sensing & Bioeconomy Guide

Founded in 1860 near Filipstad in the forested heart of Värmland, Gammelkroppa skogsskola is Sweden's oldest and most prestigious specialist forestry engineering academy. Managing its own 1,200-hectare research and demonstration forest, Gammelkroppa provides intensive, field-grounded higher vocational education training Forest Engineers (Skogstekniker) in precision silviculture, LiDAR drone remote sensing, timber logistics, and boreal forest carbon modeling.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Gammelkroppa Foundation Sustainable Silviculture Excellence Bursary

Endowed scholarship awarding motivated students pursuing the two-year Forest Engineer (Skogstekniker) program at Gammelkroppa's historic Värmland campus.

Skogstekniker Higher Vocational Program (Forest Engineer Diploma)

Two-year accredited higher education qualification training operational forestry leaders in harvest logistics, mechanized thinning, GIS spatial analysis, and forest economics.

Scandinavian Boreal Forest Carbon Sequestration Doctoral Fellowship

Four-year university doctoral position conducted jointly with SLU utilizing Gammelkroppa's demonstration forests to quantify mycorrhizal fungal carbon storage.

Värmland Drone LiDAR and Multispectral Forest Inventory Project

Applied geomatics project testing aerial LiDAR and thermal imaging for early detection of European spruce bark beetle (Ips typographus) infestations across Värmland.

Swedish Forestry Industrial Management and Logging Practicum (LIA)

Twenty-week immersive internship placed within major Swedish forestry corporations (Sveaskog, Stora Enso, Mellanskog) managing timber purchasing and logging crews.

Gammelkroppa Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Continuous Cover Silviculture

Two-year postdoctoral position evaluating multi-layered forest stand structures, natural regeneration dynamics, and economic yields under continuous-cover forestry.

Wermländska Sällskapet Forest Conservation and Innovation Award

Commemorative regional scholarship established to recognize graduating forestry students who exhibit outstanding leadership, craftsmanship, and ecological stewardship.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited two-year higher vocational diploma (Yrkeshögskoleexamen - Skogstekniker) or bachelor's degree in forestry, biology, agriculture, or environmental science.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative doctoral research coordinated with the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU) and Karlstad University in boreal forest genetics and carbon cycling.
Language proficiency
Swedish eligibility corresponding to Svenska 1/2/3 for Swedish-taught vocational programs; English eligibility corresponding to Engelska 6 (IELTS 6.5) for international research projects.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for advanced forestry research bursaries: 750-word statement outlining practical experience in GIS mapping, silvicultural planning, or timber harvesting operations.
Application fee
0 SEK application fee for Swedish and EU/EEA citizens.
Required documentation
Certified high school / post-secondary transcripts, driving license (minimum B license for field training), resume with forestry or outdoor work experience, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 0 SEK for Swedish, Nordic, and EU/EEA/Swiss citizens, who are also entitled to Swedish state CSN study grants and loans (approx. 13,100 SEK per month). For non-EU/EEA international candidates, tuition is approximately 80,000 to 95,000 SEK per academic year, supported by forestry foundation bursaries.
