University of Windsor: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Windsor (UWindsor) is a comprehensive public research university situated at the busiest international border crossing in North America. Renowned as Canada's automotive research hub, UWindsor is celebrated for the Ed Lumley Centre for Engineering Innovation (CEI), advanced manufacturing, electrified transportation drives, and cross-border environmental science.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Mechanical Engineering (Automotive & Electric Drives)

Doctoral research conducted within the Ed Lumley Centre for Engineering Innovation, investigating electrified powertrain control, high-efficiency battery packs, and autonomous vehicle sensors.

Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence Track)

Two-year thesis-based Master of Science program focusing on neural networks, natural language processing, semantic web technologies, and privacy-preserving data mining.

Ontario Graduate Scholarship (OGS) & Queen Elizabeth II (QEII-GSST) at Windsor

Prestigious provincial and institutional merit awards co-funded by the Province of Ontario and the University of Windsor for high-achieving domestic and international graduate students.

PhD in Great Lakes Environmental Science (GLIER Aquatic Ecology)

Doctoral research situated at the Great Lakes Institute for Environmental Research (GLIER), analyzing microplastics, toxic cyanobacteria blooms, and invasive species in the Laurentian Great Lakes.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Computer Science & Cyber Security

Advanced doctoral training in automotive cyber security (CAN bus defense), distributed consensus mechanisms, and deep reinforcement learning architectures.

Clean Combustion & Alternative Fuels Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research post advancing hydrogen combustion engines, optical engine diagnostic lasers, and low-carbon e-fuel combustion chemistry.

CHARGELAB Electric Vehicle Battery & Drive Systems Research Internship

Six-month research internship at the CHARGELAB, developing high-efficiency permanent magnet synchronous motor controls and wireless charging pads for light electric transport.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A four-year bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline with a minimum cumulative average of 70% (B equivalent) in undergraduate coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with a minimum 77% average; approved research statement and confirmation of supervision from a graduate faculty member.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 83 (minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-5 page research proposal outlining research questions, literature review, proposed methodology, and laboratory facility requirements.
Application fee
CAD $125 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree awards, CV, three confidential letters of recommendation, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition at UWindsor averages CAD $16,000 to $22,000 per year. Admitted research-stream doctoral students receive guaranteed minimum funding packages of CAD $23,000 to $28,000 per year through University of Windsor Doctoral Fellowships, Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTAs), and supervisor research grants.
