Wichita State University

WSU · Wichita, Kansas, United States

Wichita State University is a major comprehensive urban public research university established in 1895, located in Wichita, Kansas—the 'Air Capital of the World'. Acclaimed for aerospace engineering and aeronautical research at the National Institute for Aviation Research (NIAR), mechanical engineering, computer science, and healthcare innovation at the Innovation Campus, Wichita State ranks #1 in the nation in industry-funded aerospace research expenditures with over $200 million annually.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Wichita State comprises nine academic colleges and institutes: the College of Engineering, Fairmount College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, W. Frank Barton School of Business, College of Health Professions, College of Applied Studies, Dorothy and Bill Cohen Honors College, and the College of Fine Arts. The university operates the National Institute for Aviation Research (NIAR).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and coursework with test-optional reviews. Graduate doctoral programs offer Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA) through NIAR ($24,000–$32,000/year plus tuition remission). Master's and doctoral applicants submit transcripts, statement of purpose, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
