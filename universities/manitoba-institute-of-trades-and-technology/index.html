MITT Winnipeg: Software Development, Cybersecurity & Applied Trades Guide

The Manitoba Institute of Trades and Technology (MITT) is a premier public polytechnic post-secondary institution based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. MITT provides hands-on, industry-aligned career education spanning information and communications technology, health sciences, industrial manufacturing trades, and applied business. Renowned for its industry-standard cyber ranges, software engineering studios, and work-integrated learning co-op terms, MITT boasts among the highest graduate employment rates in central Canada.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Post-graduate certificates require completion of an accredited undergraduate degree or three-year post-secondary diploma in a related technical discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; public technical institute focused on certificates, post-secondary diplomas, and post-graduate applied diplomas.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (with no sub-score below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 78 (minimum 17 in each section) for post-secondary technical diplomas; post-graduate programs require IELTS 6.5 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Not required; technical aptitude assessment and resume required for advanced computing and industrial design programs.
Application fee
$190 CAD non-refundable application fee for international applicants ($110 CAD for domestic applicants).
Required documentation
Certified high school and post-secondary transcripts, graduation certificates, government photo ID/passport, English proficiency score report, and curriculum vitae.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic students averages $4,500 to $7,800 CAD per year. International student tuition ranges from $15,800 to $19,500 CAD annually, which includes program equipment fees, industrial software licensing, and student support services. MITT awards over $200,000 CAD in student bursaries and technical achievement prizes annually.
