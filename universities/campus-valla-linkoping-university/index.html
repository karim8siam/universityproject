Campus Valla (LiU): Autonomous Systems, Applied Physics & Clean Energy Guide

Campus Valla is the flagship research campus of Linköping University (LiU), housing the Institute of Technology (LiTH), celebrated for world-class research in semiconductor materials, autonomous robotic systems, organic electronics, applied mathematics, and circular clean energy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS credits) in a relevant engineering, science, or computer science discipline; mathematics prerequisites apply.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Fully salaried doctoral employment; requires a Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS credits) and outstanding performance in relevant scientific coursework.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B from Swedish upper secondary school, or IELTS Academic 6.5 (minimum 5.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applications require a statement of purpose addressing specific advertised PhD project topics.
Application fee
SEK 900 international application fee via UniversityAdmissions.se.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete academic transcripts, English certificate, resume/CV, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is completely free for citizens of Sweden, EU/EEA, and Switzerland. International student tuition is approximately SEK 120,000 to SEK 160,000 per academic year. LiU International Scholarship waivers (25% to 75% tuition discount) and Swedish Institute (SI) Global Professionals scholarships are accessible.
