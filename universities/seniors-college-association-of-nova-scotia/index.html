Seniors' College Association of Nova Scotia

The Seniors' College Association of Nova Scotia (SCANS) provides peer-directed postsecondary academic courses, non-credit research seminars, and community fellowship across Halifax, Dartmouth, and Nova Scotia.

Academic overview & excellence

Founded in Halifax to advance lifelong academic inquiry, SCANS delivers rigorous university-level lecture series, humanities workshops, and scientific symposia led by retired university professors, independent researchers, and subject experts. Partnering with regional universities across Atlantic Canada, SCANS maintains academic excellence across literature, astrophysics, regional history, ethics, and political science.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission is open to individuals aged 50 and older residing in Nova Scotia and Atlantic Canada. An annual or term membership grants open access to all academic seminars and research lectures without examinations or formal grading. International and visiting fellows may audit seminars online through the SCANS digital portal.

Chair of Atlantic Maritime History Studies · Department of Historical Studies

Dr. Malcolm Fraser directs the historical inquiry series at SCANS, focusing on 18th and 19th-century naval trade networks, colonial privateering in Halifax Harbour, and regional shipbuilding.

Dr. Eleanor MacDonald

Senior Fellow in Gerontological Policy and Aging · Social Policy & Lifelong Learning Division

Dr. Eleanor MacDonald leads research seminars examining public policy frameworks for active aging, cognitive engagement through non-credit academic study, and community resilience.
