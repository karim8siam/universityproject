Academic overview & excellence

Founded in 1928 as the Osaka Railway School, Osaka Sangyo University is an engineering and industrial powerhouse located at the foot of Mount Ikoma in Daito, Osaka. OSU encompasses six faculties: Faculty of Engineering (Mechanical, Civil, Environmental, Electrical, Information), Faculty of Design & Art, Faculty of Economics, Faculty of Business Administration, Faculty of International Studies, and the Faculty of Sports & Health Sciences. The university's solar car research team has won multiple World Solar Challenge and Suzuka solar car championships.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a recognized high school diploma. International students apply through the Special Selection for Foreign Students with EJU mathematics and science examinations or Japanese JLPT N2. Graduate programs in Engineering require a relevant bachelor of engineering degree, portfolio/research plan, and interview.
