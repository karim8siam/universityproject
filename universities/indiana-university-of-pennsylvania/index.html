Indiana University of Pennsylvania

IUP · Indiana, Pennsylvania, United States

Indiana University of Pennsylvania (IUP) is a comprehensive public doctoral research university founded in 1875 in Indiana, Pennsylvania, 55 miles northeast of Pittsburgh. The largest doctoral-granting institution in the Pennsylvania State System of Higher Education (PASSHE), IUP is celebrated for safety sciences and industrial hygiene, clinical psychology, criminology and criminal justice, nursing, and teacher education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

IUP comprises five academic colleges: the Kopchick College of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Eberly College of Business, College of Health and Human Services, College of Arts, Humanities, Media, and Public Affairs, and the College of Education and Communications. The university operates the $90-million John J. and Char Kopchick Hall science complex.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and transcripts with test-optional application pathways. Graduate doctoral and master's admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.8–3.0 GPA, personal statement, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+). Graduate assistantships provide stipends and partial to full tuition waivers.
