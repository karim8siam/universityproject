University of Arkansas at Little Rock

UA Little Rock · Little Rock, Arkansas, United States

The University of Arkansas at Little Rock (UA Little Rock), founded in 1927, is a metropolitan public research institution located in Arkansas's capital city. UA Little Rock bridges academic scholarship with state government, medical centers, and corporate headquarters. It is celebrated for its William H. Bowen School of Law, the Center for Integrative Nanotechnology Sciences, and pioneering research in information quality and cyber security.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UA Little Rock comprises the Donaghey College of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM), the College of Humanities, Arts, Social Sciences, and Education (CHASSE), and the William H. Bowen School of Law. It is an R2 doctoral research university conducting over $35 million in sponsored research annually.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate applicants apply through UA Little Rock's online portal or Common App (minimum GPA 2.5; test-optional). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited bachelor's or master's degree, minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and a statement of academic purpose.
