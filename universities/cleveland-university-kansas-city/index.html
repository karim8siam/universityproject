Cleveland University-Kansas City

Cleveland University-Kansas City (CUKC) is an acclaimed private university in Overland Park, Kansas, recognized for over a century of leadership in chiropractic education, clinical rehabilitation, occupational therapy, and biomedical sciences.

Academic overview & excellence

CUKC's flagship Doctor of Chiropractic (D.C.) program is nationally accredited by the Council on Chiropractic Education (CCE). The university operates an on-campus public health centre providing extensive clinical patient internships, diagnostic imaging, and interprofessional sports rehabilitation.

Research admissions & criteria

Doctor of Chiropractic admission requires at least 90 semester undergraduate credits, including 24 credits in life/physical sciences with minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA. Master of Science and occupational therapy assistant degrees require relevant undergraduate prerequisites and admissions interviews.
