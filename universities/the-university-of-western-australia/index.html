The University of Western Australia (Perth)

Established in 1911 as Western Australia's first university, The University of Western Australia (UWA) is a member of the prestigious Group of Eight (Go8) and ranked among the world's top 100 research institutions. Located on the picturesque Swan River in Perth, UWA is internationally renowned for its leadership in ocean engineering, mineral exploration, agricultural science, radio astronomy (home to the International Centre for Radio Astronomy Research - ICRAR), and biomedical science. Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent qualification) from an accredited university with a minimum weighted average mark (WAM) of 60-65% depending on faculty.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1) or a Master's degree with a significant research dissertation component (at least 25% of total course volume) graded at 75%+.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no individual band below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 82 (reading 18, listening 20, speaking 20, writing 22); higher for law and clinical programs (IELTS 7.0/7.5).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 1,000 to 2,000-word research proposal detailing background, methodology, theoretical framework, and alignment with UWA academic supervisors.
Application fee
AUD $100 international application fee via UWA apply online portal; fee waivers available for nominated international scholarship candidates.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts and degree certificates, formal curriculum vitae, research proposal, and contact details of two academic referees.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate research candidates are supported by extensive Australian Government and UWA scholarship schemes. Recipients of the UWA International Research Training Program (RTP) or UWA Postgraduate Awards receive a full international tuition fee waiver, an annual tax-free living allowance of AUD $35,000 to $40,000 per year for up to 3.5 years, and overseas student health cover (OSHC).
