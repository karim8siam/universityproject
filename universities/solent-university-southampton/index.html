Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Solent University Southampton is a vibrant, modern university globally renowned for its world-leading Warsash Maritime Academy—one of the world's premier maritime education and training institutions—as well as naval architecture, superyacht design, audio engineering, and sports science.

Institution type
Public Modern Maritime & Creative University
Campus location
East Park Terrace City Campus, Southampton & Warsash Maritime School
Accreditation
Privy Council / Maritime and Coastguard Agency (MCA) / RINA / JAMES
Admissions channel

Instruction language
English (IELTS 6.0 with 5.5 minimum each band)
Financial aid & support
Warsash Cadetships (100% employer-sponsored), International Merit Scholarships

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Warsash Maritime School—world benchmark in commercial maritime officer training
  • BEng Yacht and Powercraft Design—internationally celebrated naval architecture program
  • Full-mission ship bridge and engine room simulators and 10-hectare manned model lake
  • Acoustics, music production, and broadcast audio facilities accredited by JAMES and APRS

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • BEng (Hons) Yacht and Powercraft Design (Undergraduate Degree) — RINA-accredited naval architecture degree focusing on hydrodynamics, hull structural engineering, carbon composite manufacturing, and CAD modeling.
  • BSc (Hons) Nautical Science (Merchant Navy Cadetship) (Undergraduate Degree) — Three-year fully sponsored deck officer cadetship preparing Master Mariners and Chief Mates with MCA Officer of the Watch (OOW) certification.
  • MSc Shipping Operations (Postgraduate Degree) — Advanced executive degree addressing international maritime law, chartering economics, port operations, and green maritime decarbonisation.
