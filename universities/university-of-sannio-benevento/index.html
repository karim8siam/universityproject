University of Sannio: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1998 in the historic Roman and Lombard city of Benevento, Unisannio educates over 6,000 students across historical palaces and state-of-the-art laboratory complexes. The university is globally famous in computer science for the Research Centre on Software Technology (RCOST), a pioneer in software maintenance, reverse engineering, and empirical software evaluation. Unisannio also leads international seismological testing through its large-scale structural dynamics shaking table laboratory and pioneers regional research in genomics, viticulture chemistry, and Mediterranean economics.
