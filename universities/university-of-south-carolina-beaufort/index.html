University of South Carolina Beaufort

USCB · Bluffton, South Carolina, United States

The University of South Carolina Beaufort (USCB) is a senior comprehensive public university in the Lowcountry region of South Carolina, with campuses in Bluffton, Beaufort, and Hilton Head Island. USCB is nationally recognized for marine ecology in the Port Royal Sound estuary, nursing, computational science, and hospitality management.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma with completed South Carolina college preparatory curriculum. Test-optional admission for students with 3.0+ cumulative GPA. TOEFL iBT 77+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants without prior English-medium instruction.
