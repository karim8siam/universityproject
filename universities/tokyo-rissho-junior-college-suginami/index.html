Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1950 by the Rissho Kosei-kai educational foundation in Horinouchi, Suginami Ward, Tokyo, Tokyo Rissho Junior College delivers intensive two-year associate degree education. The college specializes in modern communication, tourism and hospitality services, business information design, and early childhood care certification, offering guaranteed credit transfer to four-year partner universities across Tokyo.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires graduation from an accredited high school or equivalent secondary education credential, submission of academic transcripts, Japanese language proficiency (JLPT N2 or higher for standard tracks), and an oral interview. Tuition fee waivers of 30% to 50% are available for international scholars.
