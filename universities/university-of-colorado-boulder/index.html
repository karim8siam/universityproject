CU Boulder: Aerospace, Quantum Physics, Environmental Science & Computing Guide

The University of Colorado Boulder (CU Boulder) is a premier Tier-1 public research institution (R1) celebrated for its five Nobel laureates in physics and chemistry, top-ranked aerospace engineering sciences, JILA quantum research institute, and world-leading climate science at CIRES.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with minimum 3.0 cumulative undergraduate GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or Master's degree in aerospace, physics, computing, or related STEM field with minimum 3.2+ GPA and strong research credentials.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 minimum (100+ strongly recommended for TA appointments) or IELTS Academic 6.5 minimum (7.0+ recommended).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose (1,000 words) defining specific research objectives, technical accomplishments, and prospective faculty research matches.
Application fee
$60 for domestic applicants / $80 for international applicants via the CU Boulder Graduate Admissions application.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, statement of purpose, curriculum vitae, and three letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral students in engineering and physical sciences receive guaranteed full funding packages covering 100% of resident/non-resident tuition and student health insurance, plus annual living stipends of $35,000 to $43,000 through Graduate Research Assistant (GRA) and Graduate Part-Time Instructor (GPTI) positions.
