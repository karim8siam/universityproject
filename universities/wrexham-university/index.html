Institutional Overview

Wrexham University (Prifysgol Wrecsam), formerly known as Glyndŵr University, is an innovative public university situated in North East Wales with campuses in Wrexham and the OpTIC Centre in St Asaph. Renowned for its world-leading Centre for Ultra-Realistic Imaging (CURI), precision optical engineering, aeronautical engineering with Airbus, and game art design, Wrexham University is deeply integrated into Welsh industrial innovation.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • World-class OpTIC Technology Centre in St Asaph polishing ultra-precision mirrors for the European Extremely Large Telescope (E-ELT)
  • Strong industrial partnership with Airbus Broughton delivering industry-leading aeronautical engineering training
  • Ranked #1 in the UK for social inclusion and student upward mobility (Sunday Times Good University Guide)
  • Celebrated School of Creative Arts with award-winning alumni in game development, VFX, and concept art
