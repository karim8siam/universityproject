Takarazuka University Tokyo Shinjuku Campus

Takarazuka University (Tokyo Shinjuku Campus) is a premier media arts university in central Shinjuku, Tokyo, internationally acclaimed for its School of Media Arts, specializing in manga, anime production, game design, and illustration.

Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in the global heart of pop-culture and digital creativity in Shinjuku, Takarazuka trains professional creators in commercial manga storytelling, anime animation pipelines, concept art, character design, and 3D game engines. Faculty members are active mangaka, anime directors, and game industry veterans who provide direct studio mentoring.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation and a creative artistic evaluation: portfolio submission (original illustration, manga storyboards, or digital game projects) and a faculty interview. International applicants require JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 200.
