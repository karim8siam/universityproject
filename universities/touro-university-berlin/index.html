Touro University Berlin

Touro University Berlin is a dual-accredited American and German private university located on the historic Am Rupenhorn estate in Berlin, offering American undergraduate and graduate degrees in business, psychology, and Holocaust studies.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 2003 as part of the international Touro University System, Touro Berlin is recognized both by the US Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE) and the German Berlin Senate / Wissenschaftsrat. Situated in a historic Bauhaus villa overlooking the Havel river, Touro Berlin provides small seminars taught entirely in English, dual American and German degree options, and leading research centers in Holocaust studies and clinical psychology.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor's degree admission requires a US high school diploma, German Abitur, or recognized international secondary school diploma. Master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in business, psychology, or humanities (minimum GPA 2.7 US / 2.5 German equivalent). English proficiency must be demonstrated via TOEFL iBT 80+, IELTS 6.5+, or Duolingo 110+. Financial aid and merit scholarships are available for international scholars.
