European College of Parma: EU Law, European Food Safety & Diplomacy Guide

Established in 1988 in the historic ducal city of Parma (seat of the European Food Safety Authority - EFSA), the European College of Parma Foundation (Collegio Europeo di Parma) is a premier postgraduate institute for European studies. Backed by the University of Parma, regional European institutions, and the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the College delivers the prestigious Diploma in Advanced European Studies (DAES) training international diplomats, EU legal experts, and regulatory economists.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

European College of Parma Foundation Full Merit Scholarship

Prestigious foundation scholarship awarding full tuition and living expenses to exceptional international graduates aspiring to careers in European civil service and diplomacy.

Diploma in Advanced European Studies (DAES) / Master in European Studies

Ten-month intensive bilingual postgraduate diploma covering European institutional law, EU single market economics, European Green Deal policies, and diplomatic negotiation.

Parma-EFSA European Food Safety Law Collaborative Doctoral Bursary

Three-year doctoral research fellowship investigating European Food Safety Authority regulatory risk assessment, pesticide authorization law, and agri-biotech standards.

European Single Market and Competition Policy Applied Research Project

Applied legal and economic research project analyzing European Court of Justice case law regarding state aid control, digital antitrust, and foreign direct investment screening.

European Institutions and EFSA Regulatory Affairs Practicum

Four to six-month internship placing graduate scholars within the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), European Commission directorates in Brussels, or multinational public affairs consultancies.

European College of Parma Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in EU External Action

Two-year postdoctoral appointment examining European Union external trade agreements, strategic geopolitical partnerships, and international sanctions enforcement.

City of Parma European Integration and Mediterranean Dialogue Grant

Municipal scholarship supporting graduate students from Mediterranean partner countries enrolling in advanced European integration and diplomatic studies in Parma.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's or Master's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in law, economics, political science, international relations, or agro-food sciences with an above-average academic record.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative PhD pathways in European Union Law and Agri-Food Policy hosted in partnership with the University of Parma Doctoral School.
Language proficiency
Bilingual English and French or Italian competence: minimum English level B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5+, TOEFL 90) and working knowledge of French or Italian.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for postgraduate master's candidates: 1,000-word essay on a contemporary European integration challenge (e.g., European Green Deal, EU digital antitrust, Common Foreign Policy).
Application fee
€50 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified university diploma and academic transcripts, curriculum vitae in Europass format, letter of motivation, two reference letters, and language certificates.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the one-year Diploma in Advanced European Studies (DAES) is €8,500. The European College of Parma Foundation and the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs award extensive merit and needs-based scholarships providing partial or full tuition coverage and accommodation grants.
