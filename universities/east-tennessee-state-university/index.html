East Tennessee State University

East Tennessee State University (ETSU) is a major public doctoral research institution nestled in the Appalachian Mountains, acclaimed for the James H. Quillen College of Medicine, the world-famous Gray Fossil Site and Center of Excellence in Paleontology, and the College of Public Health.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1911 in Johnson City, ETSU offers more than 140 academic programs across eleven colleges. It is nationally distinguished for rural healthcare excellence (ranking top-tier nationally in family medicine at the Quillen College of Medicine), Appalachian studies, digital computing, and vertebrate paleontology centered at the 5-million-year-old Gray Fossil Site Museum.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts and GPA (minimum 2.3 for regular admission; 3.0+ for merit scholarships). ETSU is test-optional for ACT/SAT. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0, program prerequisites, statement of intent, and reference letters. International students must provide TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
