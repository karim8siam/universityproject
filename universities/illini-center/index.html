Illini Center

Illini Center · Chicago, Illinois, United States

The Illini Center, situated in the heart of downtown Chicago in the Loop, serves as the premier metropolitan facility for the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). Bridging world-class engineering, business, and public policy research with Chicago's global corporate and civic ecosystems, the Illini Center hosts top-ranked executive master's degree programs, financial engineering immersions, tech incubator showcases, and alumni innovation forums.
