University of Borås: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Borås (Högskolan i Borås / HB) is a prominent Swedish higher education and research institution located in the textile heartland of western Sweden. Celebrated internationally for the Swedish School of Textiles (Svenska Textilhögskolan) and its Swedish Centre for Resource Recovery, HB is a world authority on smart technical textiles, circular biotechnology, and information science.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Salaried PhD Position in Resource Recovery & Industrial Bioprocess Technology

Four-year salaried doctoral research post at the Swedish Centre for Resource Recovery, converting agricultural waste and textile waste into second-generation bioethanol, bioplastics, and fungal proteins.

PhD in Textile Materials Technology & Smart Functional Wearables

Doctoral research at the prestigious Swedish School of Textiles, engineering sensor-integrated conductive textiles, thermoelectric fabrics, and waterless digital textile dyeing.

Master of Science in Resource Recovery - Circular Biorefineries (English-taught)

Two-year Master of Science addressing global circular economy challenges, training biochemical engineers in aerobic and anaerobic digestions, syngas fermentation, and carbon recovery.

Master of Science in Textile Engineering (Advanced Technical Materials)

Specialized two-year engineering master's program taught at the Swedish School of Textiles, covering high-performance fiber composites, biomedical implants, and 3D knitting technology.

Master in Information Architecture & Digital Knowledge Management

Two-year distance/hybrid master's program from the famous Swedish School of Library and Information Science (SSLIS), instructing professionals in semantic metadata, enterprise search, and digital knowledge organization.

Swedish Centre for Resource Recovery Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research post advancing pilot-scale anaerobic membrane bioreactors and fungal fermentation systems for textile fiber recycling.

Smart Textiles Prototype Lab Graduate Research Internship

Six-month research internship at the Smart Textiles Prototype Lab in Borås, creating bio-based composite materials and recycling protocols for European fashion brands.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A completed bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in chemical engineering, biotechnology, textile technology, computer science, or library science.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (240 Swedish credits, including 60 credits at advanced level); application submitted in response to published doctoral employment vacancies.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B proficiency (IELTS 6.5 overall with no section under 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose, master's thesis or publication samples, and documentation demonstrating alignment with the advertised research specialization.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee through universityadmissions.se for non-EU students (SEK 0 for doctoral applications).
Required documentation
Bachelor's and Master's diplomas, certified transcripts, CV, copy of passport, and English language test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 100% free for all Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition for Master's programs is SEK 125,000 to SEK 145,000 per year. The University of Borås offers tuition scholarships covering 75% of tuition fees to top non-EU applicants. All doctoral students in Sweden are employed on full collective-agreement salaried contracts, earning SEK 32,000 to SEK 36,500 per month with zero tuition.
