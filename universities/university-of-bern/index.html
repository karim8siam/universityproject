University of Bern (Universität Bern)

Founded in 1834 in Switzerland's federal capital, the University of Bern (Universität Bern) is a comprehensive research university of international distinction and a founding member of the Guild of European Research-Intensive Universities. Associated with Albert Einstein (who served as privatdozent in Bern while publishing his Annus Mirabilis papers on special relativity) and renowned worldwide for space research—having supplied instruments for over 30 space missions, including the solar wind experiment deployed by Neil Armstrong on Apollo 11—Bern leads the world in ice-core climate science at the Oeschger Centre for Climate Change Research. Bern doctoral candidates are salaried academic employees with low tuition fees. Explore verified academic opportunities and admissions guidance.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Bern Oeschger Centre for Climate Change Research (OCCR) Doctoral Fellowships

Fully salaried four-year doctoral research fellowship at the world-renowned Oeschger Centre, analyzing Antarctic and Greenland ice cores to reconstruct greenhouse gas histories over 1.5 million years.

Uni Bern PhD in Space Exploration and Planetary Science

Three to four-year research doctorate at the Space Research & Planetary Sciences division participating directly in ESA and NASA missions (JUICE, CHEOPS, Comet Interceptor).

Uni Bern Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (Joint Master with Neuchâtel & Fribourg)

Two-year English-taught Master of Science jointly offered by the Universities of Bern, Neuchâtel, and Fribourg, specializing in Machine Learning, Distributed Systems, and Information Security.

Uni Bern Graduate School for Cellular and Biomedical Sciences (GCB) PhD Fellowships

Interdisciplinary doctoral program run jointly by the Faculty of Medicine, Faculty of Science, and Vetsuisse Faculty, investigating tumor microenvironments, infectious diseases, and stem cell therapy.

Uni Bern Postdoctoral Research Associate in Exoplanet Atmospheres & CHEOPS Science

Two-year postdoctoral position in the Center for Space and Habitability (CSH) analyzing photometric transit data from the space telescope CHEOPS and James Webb Space Telescope (JWST).

Uni Bern Master of International Law and Economics (MILE) at World Trade Institute

World's leading one-year postgraduate program at the World Trade Institute (WTI) combining international trade law, WTO dispute settlement, digital commerce, and climate border adjustments.

Uni Bern Jungfraujoch High Altitude Research Station Summer Fellowship

Four-week research fellowship located at the Sphinx Observatory on the Jungfraujoch (3,454 m above sea level), monitoring European free-tropospheric greenhouse gas background concentrations.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent 180 ECTS credit qualification) from an accredited university in an aligned discipline with above-average grades.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (120 ECTS) with excellent academic marks, strong thesis research background, and written confirmation of acceptance from an authorized Bern faculty supervisor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100 for English-taught graduate programs; C1 level German (TestDaF 4x4 or Goethe C1) for German-taught degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 3 to 5-page research proposal detailing research objectives, methodology, preliminary literature review, and academic work schedule.
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee for foreign credential evaluation via the Uni Bern online portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, certified copies of degree diplomas, curriculum vitae, personal statement, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at the University of Bern are exceptionally modest by global standards. Tuition for foreign students is only CHF 750 per semester (approx. USD $1,650/year). Furthermore, doctoral students are salaried academic employees of the canton of Bern, receiving guaranteed annual salaries of CHF 48,000 to CHF 55,000 per year according to Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) salary scales.
