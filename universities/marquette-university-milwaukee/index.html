Marquette University

Marquette Univ · Milwaukee, Wisconsin, United States

Marquette University is a prestigious private Jesuit Catholic research university in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Founded in 1881, Marquette is world-renowned for the Opus College of Engineering, Marquette University Law School, School of Dentistry, and biomedical rehabilitation robotics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Marquette comprises 11 nationally recognized colleges, featuring the joint Biomedical Engineering program with the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW), the athletic and human performance research center (AHPRC), and the Lubar Center for Public Policy at Eckstein Hall.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are selective and test-optional, reviewing secondary school course rigor, GPA (average 3.65), and essays. Graduate engineering and health science programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, strong quantitative foundations, and letters of recommendation.
