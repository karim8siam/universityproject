Apulia Libera Università: Mediterranean Ecology & Olive Biosecurity Guide

Based in Bari, the capital of the Apulia region, Apulia Libera Università (ALU) is an agile Italian higher education and advanced research institute. Renowned across southern Italy for agricultural biosecurity, Mediterranean agronomy, circular bioeconomy models, and Euro-Mediterranean maritime diplomacy, ALU partners with regional research consortiums to protect secular olive groves and advance regenerative agriculture.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ADISU Puglia Regional University Study Grant at ALU

Need- and merit-based regional government scholarship providing complete tuition waivers, campus accommodation, meal vouchers, and annual cash stipends to domestic and international students.

Master of Science in Mediterranean Agricultural Biosecurity & Crop Protection

Two-year graduate degree specializing in plant quarantine protocols, insect vector dynamics, microbial biocontrol agents, and genomic breeding for pathogen-resistant olive cultivars.

Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Xylella fastidiosa Microbial Genomics

Three-year doctoral research appointment in collaboration with the Italian National Research Council (CNR) investigating molecular mechanisms of tolerance in Leccino and FS17 olive varieties.

Secular Apulian Olive Grove Conservation & Soil Carbon Dynamics Project

Field-based scientific investigation tracking mycorrhizal fungi inoculation, soil organic matter accretion, and water retention in drought-stressed secular olive groves in the Salento peninsula.

CIHEAM Bari Mediterranean Agro-Food Chain Practicum & Internship

Sixteen-week practical internship at the Mediterranean Agronomic Institute of Bari analyzing protected designation of origin (PDO) olive oil traceability and organic certification systems.

ALU Postdoctoral Fellowship in Precision Viticulture & Climate Adaptation

Two-year postdoctoral position deploying IoT soil moisture probe arrays, multispectral UAV imagery, and regulated deficit irrigation to safeguard indigenous Apulian Primitivo vineyards.

Apulia Libera Università Merit Fee Exemption Grant

Institutional merit grant providing complete tuition relief to top-performing domestic and international students enrolling in graduate agricultural and environmental programs at ALU.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited three-year undergraduate degree (Laurea triennale or international bachelor's equivalent) in agricultural sciences, biology, economics, or environmental sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral research organized in conjunction with Italian public research bodies (CNR) and Mediterranean agronomic institutes (CIHEAM Bari).
Language proficiency
B2 certification in Italian (CELI, CILS) or English (IELTS 6.0+ / TOEFL iBT 80) according to the language of instruction.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate research: 2,000-word applied research proposal addressing Mediterranean crop resilience, pest vector control, or rural economic revitalisation.
Application fee
EUR 30 administrative application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma with CIMEA Statement of Comparability, official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, statement of research intent, and identity document.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Apulia Libera Università ranges from EUR 1,800 to EUR 3,200 per academic year. Regional ADISU Puglia study scholarships, European Social Fund (FSE) research bursaries, and tuition fee reductions based on family income documentation (ISEE) provide substantial economic assistance.
