Rutgers University - New Brunswick

Rutgers New Brunswick · New Brunswick, New Jersey, United States

Rutgers University - New Brunswick is the flagship campus of Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey, and a world-renowned Big Ten public research powerhouse (R1/AAU member), chartered in 1766 as Queen's College. Globally celebrated for mathematics, philosophy, computer science, biomedical engineering, pharmacy, and plant biology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Rutgers-New Brunswick spans five historic campuses (College Avenue, Busch, Livingston, Cook, and Douglass) connected along the Raritan River. It hosts the Center for Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science (DIMACS), the Rutgers Cancer Institute of New Jersey, the Waksman Institute of Microbiology, and the Ernest Mario School of Pharmacy.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are competitive and holistic. Undergraduate applicants submit transcripts, coursework profile, and optional test scores (test-optional). Graduate and doctoral applicants apply directly to individual graduate schools and departments, requiring strong discipline-specific academic credentials, research portfolios, and faculty mentor endorsements.
