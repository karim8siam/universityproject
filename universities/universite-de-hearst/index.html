Institutional Overview

Université de Hearst is an independent francophone public university in Northern Ontario with campuses in Hearst, Kapuskasing, and Timmins. Operating on a distinctive block-scheduling model, UHearst offers intensive immersion programs in business administration, psychology, interdisciplinary human studies, and management.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Modular block scheduling enabling in-depth study of one course at a time
  • Francophone Northern community development, indigenous reconciliation, and rural vitality
  • Applied management, human resources, and business analytics for community enterprises
  • Small-cohort interactive seminars and personalized faculty mentorship
