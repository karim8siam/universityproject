Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 2004 in Nishi Ward, Yokohama, Yashimagakuen University was established as Japan's first university approved to grant bachelor's degrees and national professional certifications through flexible synchronous and asynchronous e-learning. The university is acclaimed nationwide as Japan's top training institution for National Certified Librarians (Shisho) and Museum Curators (Gakugeiin).

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to the Faculty of Lifelong Learning requires secondary school graduation credentials (or qualifying adult life experience equivalency). There is no paper written entrance examination; admissions are based on holistic evaluation of application essays, learning motivation statements, and online orientation interviews. Tuition is calculated affordably per credit enrolled.
