Kobe City College of Nursing: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1996 in the wake of the Great Hanshin-Awaji Earthquake, Kobe City College of Nursing is a municipal public university dedicated to cultivating compassionate, highly skilled nursing professionals and disaster relief leaders. Located in Kobe Academic Town (Gakuen-toshi), the university features doctoral graduate programs, specialized disaster simulation centers, and direct integration into Kobe City Medical Center General Hospital.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
National University Entrance Examination (Kyotsu Test) and secondary school recommendation
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Japanese (primary instruction) & English (international disaster nursing collaborations)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
¥30,000
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
