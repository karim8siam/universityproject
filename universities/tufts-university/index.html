Tufts University

Tufts University · Medford, Massachusetts, United States

Tufts University is an elite private Carnegie R1 research university located across campuses in Medford/Somerville, Boston, and Grafton, Massachusetts. Renowned for The Fletcher School of Law and Diplomacy (the nation's oldest graduate school of international affairs), the Tufts School of Medicine, the School of Engineering, and the Cummings School of Veterinary Medicine, Tufts generates over $250M in annual research expenditures.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Tufts comprises ten constituent schools. Research powerhouses include the Center for Cellular Agriculture (pioneering cultivated meat), the Tufts Lyme Disease Initiative, the Allen Discovery Center (bioelectric regeneration), and the Jean Mayer USDA Human Nutrition Research Center on Aging (HNRCA).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective. Undergraduate applicants are reviewed holistically via the Common Application (test-optional). Graduate and professional programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.3+ GPA, program-specific standardized tests (GRE, MCAT, LSAT where required), statement of research objectives, and three letters of recommendation.
