Institutional Overview

Saxion University of Applied Sciences (Saxion Hogeschool), with over 27,000 students, maintains a campus located right by the historic railway station in Deventer, Overijssel. Famous for its educational theme 'Living Technology', Saxion Deventer specializes in international business, applied psychology, software engineering, urban design, and sustainable environmental technology, delivering programs in English and Dutch.
