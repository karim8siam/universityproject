Western New Mexico University

WNMU · Silver City, New Mexico, United States

Western New Mexico University (WNMU) is an open-access public Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) established in 1893, situated atop a hillside in historic Silver City, New Mexico, bordering the 3.3-million-acre Gila National Forest. Celebrated for social work, forestry and wildlife ecology, Southwestern archaeological anthropology, and the WNMU Museum housing the world's largest collection of prehistoric Mimbres pottery, WNMU provides accessible applied learning across the American Southwest.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WNMU offers undergraduate and graduate degrees through the College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business, College of Education, and School of Social Work. The university operates the Gila Center for Environmental Studies and the historic Fleming Hall museum.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation or GED credentials with open, test-optional admissions policies. Graduate master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, personal statement, two letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.0+). Graduate assistantships provide tuition reductions and stipends.
