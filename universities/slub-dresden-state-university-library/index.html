SLUB Dresden - Saxon State and University Library

SLUB Dresden · Dresden, Saxony, Germany

SLUB Dresden is one of Germany’s largest and most technologically advanced academic research libraries, operating as the university library of TU Dresden and a central national repository for digital humanities.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SLUB hosts the German Photothek, an internationally recognized digital collection center, extensive 3D artifact digitization laboratories, and the Open Science Lab. It provides world-class research infrastructure for bibliometrics, preservation informatics, and rare manuscript analysis.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Visiting research fellowships and doctoral residency grants require enrollment in a recognized PhD program or post-doctoral status in Digital Humanities, Information Science, History, or Data Science.
