University of Freiburg (Albert-Ludwigs-Universität)

Founded in 1457, the University of Freiburg (Albert-Ludwigs-Universität Freiburg) is one of Germany's oldest and most prestigious universities and a designated German University of Excellence. With 23 Nobel laureates associated with its faculties, Freiburg is internationally celebrated for pioneering microsystems engineering through IMTEK (one of the largest academic microsystems engineering faculties worldwide), the CIBSS Centre for Integrative Biological Signalling Studies, and the livMatS Cluster of Excellence for bio-inspired materials. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Freiburg livMatS Cluster of Excellence Doctoral & Postdoctoral Fellowships

Prestigious fellowship funded by Germany's Excellence Strategy at the livMatS Cluster, investigating self-adaptive, autonomous energy-harvesting living materials systems.

Uni Freiburg Master of Science (MSc) in Embedded Systems Engineering (English-taught)

Two-year English-taught Master's degree jointly offered by the Department of Microsystems Engineering (IMTEK) and the Department of Computer Science, focusing on IoT, cyber-physical systems, and smart sensors.

Uni Freiburg CIBSS Doctoral Fellowship in Integrative Biological Signalling

Doctoral training within the CIBSS Cluster of Excellence investigating molecular and cellular communication from single molecules to complex organismal behavior.

Uni Freiburg PhD in Autonomous Intelligent Systems & Robotics

Three to four-year doctoral research program in the Department of Computer Science developing navigation, 3D mapping, and deep learning for mobile autonomous systems.

Uni Freiburg Master of Science (MSc) in Applied Physics (English-taught)

Two-year English-taught program with experimental tracks in quantum technologies, photonics, atomic physics, and condensed matter physics.

Freiburg Black Forest Summer Research Internship in Forest Ecosystem Ecology

Two-month field research placement in the Black Forest analyzing canopy microclimates, tree physiology, and carbon flux under climatic stress.

Uni Freiburg Research Associate in Macromolecular Chemistry & Functional Polymers

Two-year full-time research position at the Institute for Macromolecular Chemistry synthesizing adaptive polymer networks for biomedical implants and soft actuators.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in relevant subject with above-average German grade equivalent (minimum 2.5 or better on the German 1-5 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed Master's degree (120 ECTS) with a research thesis graded at least 'good' (2.0 or better) and formal written endorsement by a Freiburg faculty supervisor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 to 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 90+ for English-taught graduate programs; TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2 for German-taught degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Formal 3 to 5-page research proposal detailing research questions, methodology, equipment requirements, and academic schedule.
Application fee
€0 application fee (no application processing fee charged by the University of Freiburg).
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificates, diploma supplements, CV, motivation letter, and two academic recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

The state of Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU international students a modest tuition fee of €1,500 per semester (€3,000 per year) for Bachelor's and Master's degrees, plus a €161 semester administration contribution. However, doctoral students are completely EXEMPT from this tuition fee (€0 tuition) and are employed on TV-L E13 civil service research contracts (€34,000 to €52,000/year gross) with full social security and healthcare.
