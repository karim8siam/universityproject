Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1972 as part of the Université du Québec network, Université TÉLUQ is North America's only French-language university dedicated exclusively to distance education, renowned for cognitive informatics, educational technologies, and environmental management.

Institution type
Public Distance Education University (Université du Québec network)
Campus location
Headquarters in Quebec City, Quebec (Global Online Delivery)
Accreditation

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Cognitive computing and semantic web architectures (LICEF Research Centre)
  • Distance education pedagogy, e-learning analytics, and adaptive learning environments
  • Environmental management, ecological footprint assessment, and climate policy
  • Human resources, labor relations, and digital workplace transformations

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Doctorat en informatique cognitive (Ph.D.) (Doctoral Degree) — Inter-university doctoral program investigating knowledge graphs, natural language processing, and artificial intelligence in education.
  • Maîtrise en gestion de l'environnement (M.Env.) (Graduate Degree) — Professional and research master's degree focusing on environmental impact assessment, corporate sustainability, and water resource governance.
  • Baccalauréat en informatique cognitive (Undergraduate Degree) — Unique undergraduate program combining computer science, cognitive psychology, and semantic data technologies.
