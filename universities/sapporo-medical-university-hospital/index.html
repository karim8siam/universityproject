Academic overview & research priorities

Designated by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare as a regional cancer treatment center and advanced perinatal maternal center, SapMed Hospital features state-of-the-art robotic surgery suites (da Vinci Xi), an autologous cell processing center (CPC), and comprehensive ECMO critical care facilities. It provides world-class post-graduate medical residency and specialized clinical fellowship training.

Research admissions & international criteria

Clinical residency, post-graduate fellowship programs, and clinical trial research appointments require a medical degree (MD/MBBS) and Japanese Medical Licensure (or approval under the Advanced Clinical Training system for foreign physicians). Nursing and allied health fellowships require active professional licensure.
