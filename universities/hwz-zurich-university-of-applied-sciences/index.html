HWZ Zurich: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

HWZ Zurich University of Applied Sciences in Business Administration (Hochschule für Wirtschaft Zürich, HWZ) is Switzerland's premier university of applied sciences exclusively dedicated to business administration, digital transformation, FinTech, and executive management. Located next to Zurich main station in the heart of Europe's leading financial center, HWZ is celebrated for its cutting-edge Center for Digital Business.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized bachelor's degree in business administration, economics, business informatics, or engineering with strong academic credentials.
Doctoral admission eligibility
HWZ offers executive doctoral degrees (Doctor of Business Administration - DBA) jointly with international university partners (e.g., University of Southern Queensland).
Language proficiency
German C1 (for German-taught executive modules) or English B2/C1 (for English-taught international tracks, IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88 minimum).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-3 page Statement of Purpose specifying current professional trajectory, research interest in digital transformation or FinTech, and managerial objectives.
Application fee
CHF 200 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree certificate and transcripts, comprehensive professional resume (CV), employer reference letters, and valid passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for master's programs at HWZ Zurich ranges from CHF 9,500 to CHF 14,000 per academic year. Swiss residents and eligible cross-border students can receive Cantonal higher education subsidies reducing fees by up to CHF 5,000. Corporate partnerships with Zurich banks, consultancies, and tech leaders offer sponsored fellowships covering up to 70% of tuition costs alongside paid corporate projects.
