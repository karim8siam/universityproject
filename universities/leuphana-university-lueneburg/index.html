Academic overview & excellence

Located in the historic Hanseatic city of Lüneburg south of Hamburg, Leuphana University is organized into four flagship faculties: Sustainability, Management & Technology, Cultural Studies, and Education. Established with a distinctive tripartite model comprising the College (undergraduate bachelor's), Graduate School (master's and doctoral), and Professional School (lifelong executive learning), Leuphana has been climate-neutral since 2014 and is consistently ranked among the world's top universities for ecological transformation.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate entry features an innovative admissions process combining high school grades with an on-campus aptitude test and personal interview. International master's and doctoral applicants apply directly through Leuphana's digital portal with recognized degrees in related fields. English-taught tracks require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 85.
