University of Verona: Medical Biotechnology, Economics & ESU Scholarships

The University of Verona (Università degli Studi di Verona) is a top-tier Italian state university renowned for excellence in medicine, biomedical sciences, agricultural biotechnology (viticulture and oenology), computer engineering, and law in the historic UNESCO World Heritage city of Verona.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Veneto Regional ESU Full Study Grant & Housing Award

Needs-and-merit based regional higher education award open to domestic and international students enrolled at the University of Verona.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Applied Life & Health Sciences (Full Ministerial Grant)

Doctoral research program based at the Borgo Roma University Hospital examining tumor immunology, molecular diagnostics, and cellular regenerative therapies.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Medical Bioinformatics & Genomic Data

Two-year international master's taught in English bridging high-throughput genomics, structural bioinformatics, personalized medicine algorithms, and machine learning.

Department of Computer Science Computer Vision & Robotics Laboratory Assistantship

Graduate research appointment investigating geometric computer vision, simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), and surgical robotic guidance.

Verona University Hospital (Azienda Ospedaliera) Clinical Research Practicum

Hospital-based internship rotating through genetic diagnostic labs, biobanks, and clinical trial coordination units in Verona.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Agrigenomics & Viticulture Biotechnology

Advanced research fellowship based at the Department of Biotechnology examining climate resilience in Vitis vinifera, drought adaptation genes, and sustainable vine protection.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Data Science & Business Analytics

English-taught degree combining statistical modeling, business intelligence, machine learning for financial markets, and data protection law.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Three-year undergraduate bachelor's degree (Laurea) or equivalent foreign qualification pre-evaluated via the Universitaly portal.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with competitive evaluation of research proposal, CV, and an oral presentation before the academic board.
Language proficiency
B2 English certificate (IELTS 6.0+, TOEFL 78+, or Cambridge B2 First); Italian B2 required only for Italian-taught courses.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral admission: detailed 5-page research proposal written in English outlining background, research questions, and experimental protocols.
Application fee
€30 to €50 application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree diploma, transcript of records with course syllabus, CIMEA Statement of Comparability, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is income-adjusted (based on ISEE parificato), ranging between €156 and €2,750 per year. Regional ESU scholarships (Ente Regionale per il Diritto allo Studio Universitario) provide qualifying students with full tuition fee waivers, free accommodation, and up to €7,200 annual cash grants.
