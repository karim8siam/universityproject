Lincoln University

Lincoln Univ MO · Jefferson City, Missouri, United States

Lincoln University is a public historically Black land-grant university (HBCU) in Jefferson City, Missouri. Founded in 1866 by Civil War soldiers of the 62nd and 65th Colored Infantries, Lincoln is celebrated for agricultural research, nursing, journalism, and public service.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lincoln operates the College of Agriculture, Environmental and Human Sciences, School of Nursing, and Carver and Busby research farms. Research strengths include specialty crop cultivation, small ruminant reproduction, organic soil science, and Missouri River water quality.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.0 unweighted) under test-optional review. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.75 GPA, departmental statement of goals, and discipline prerequisites.
