Queensland University of Technology: Degrees, Scholarships & Research Guide

Located in the heart of Brisbane across Gardens Point and Kelvin Grove campuses, Queensland University of Technology (QUT) is one of Australia's premier public research universities. Widely celebrated for real-world impact in robotics, biomedical engineering, creative industries, and data science, QUT provides cutting-edge graduate facilities.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Australian Bachelor degree or international equivalent with a minimum Grade Point Average of 4.5 on a 7-point scale (approx 65%+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Honours Class I or Master by Research degree with demonstrated research capacity, thesis publication, and departmental supervisor endorsement.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each subtest (or TOEFL iBT 79 with writing 21).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 4–6 page research proposal with methodology, timetable, and faculty supervisor nomination for research degrees.
Application fee
AUD $100 international application fee via the QUT Online Application portal.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree awards, English proficiency documentation, CV, and two referee reports.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate coursework tuition ranges between AUD $35,200 and $44,800 per year. For Higher Degree by Research (HDR) candidates, the Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) and QUT Postgraduate Research Awards cover 100% of tuition plus an indexed living stipend of AUD $34,500 per annum.
