Institution Overview & Profile

Duale Hochschule Baden-Württemberg Mosbach (DHBW Mosbach), situated in the Neckar-Odenwald region with campuses in Mosbach and Bad Mergentheim, delivers the world-renowned German dual study model. Enrolled students alternate every three months between university theory and fully salaried employment at premier industrial and IT corporate partners.

Institution TypePublic Cooperative State University (Duale Hochschule)
Main CampusLohrtalweg Campus, Mosbach & Schloss Campus, Bad Mergentheim
AccreditationState of Baden-Württemberg / ZEvA Accredited / Duale Hochschule System
Admissions RouteSigned training contract with an approved dual corporate partner + university matriculation
Language of InstructionGerman (TestDaF 4 / DSH-2 / Goethe C1); English B2 required for international modules
Financial Aid & GrantsMonthly corporate salary (€1,100–€1,600/month) paid throughout all 3 years + state BAföG eligibility

Key Academic Strengths

  • 100% employment rate with students receiving continuous corporate remuneration during both theory and practical phases
  • Over 1,100 corporate partner companies ranging from global giants (Audi, SAP, Würth) to innovative hidden champions
  • State-of-the-art laboratories for robotics, mechatronics, virtual reality, and smart factory automation
  • Seamless progression from Bachelor of Engineering to Master's at the DHBW Center for Advanced Studies (CAS)
