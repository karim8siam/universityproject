University of Erfurt Sports and Health Center

The University Sports and Health Center (Universitätssporthalle / USV Erfurt) at the University of Erfurt provides specialized academic training, biomechanical sports research, physical education teacher training, and university health management.

Academic overview & research priorities

Integrating university physical education and sports sciences, USV Erfurt partners with the Faculty of Education to offer degrees in sports education, adapted physical activities, kinesiology, and preventive public health.

Research admissions & international criteria

Students must pass the university physical sports aptitude test (Sporteignungsprüfung) alongside general higher education entrance requirements (Abitur). German language proficiency at DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 is required.
