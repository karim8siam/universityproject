University of Tennessee Space Institute

UTSI · Tullahoma, Tennessee, United States

The University of Tennessee Space Institute (UTSI) is an internationally recognized graduate research institute situated on 365 lakeside acres adjacent to the U.S. Air Force Arnold Engineering Development Complex (AEDC) in Tullahoma, TN. Founded in 1964, UTSI has produced nine NASA astronauts and leads cutting-edge research in hypersonic aerodynamics, optical diagnostics, scramjet combustion, and space systems.
