Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1855 by abolitionist Ovid Butler in Indianapolis, Butler University is a nationally distinguished private university renowned for its College of Pharmacy and Health Sciences, Lacy School of Business, Jordan College of the Arts, and strong undergraduate research initiatives.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) and pharmaceutical sciences research
  • Lacy School of Business experiential consulting and risk management
  • Butler Undergraduate Research Conference (URC) and faculty-mentored research
  • Dance, music performance, and theater at Jordan College of the Arts

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) (Doctoral Degree) — Top-ranked six-year or four-year professional doctorate accredited by ACPE, featuring advanced clinical pharmacotherapy rotations.
  • Master of Science in Data Analytics (Graduate Degree) — Applied data science degree combining machine learning, business intelligence, and cloud data infrastructure.
  • Bachelor of Science in Finance & Risk Management (Undergraduate Degree) — Experiential business degree featuring student-managed investment funds and captive insurance operations.
