Academic overview & research priorities

Ranked among the premier technological research universities globally, Georgia Tech conducts over $1.3 billion in annual sponsored research across its College of Engineering, College of Computing, College of Sciences, and Ivan Allen College of Liberal Arts. Home to leading hubs in robotics, artificial intelligence, clean energy, and biomedical nanomanufacturing.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective via the Common Application. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree with strong quantitative background (typically GPA 3.50+), GRE scores (department-dependent), research proposal, and TOEFL iBT 100+ / IELTS 7.5+.
