University of Siegen: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1972 and home to over 17,000 students, the University of Siegen spans historic urban and modern hilltop campuses in North Rhine-Westphalia. Renowned as the historic academic base of pioneering media theorist Friedrich Kittler, Uni Siegen hosts the DFG Collaborative Research Centre “Media of Cooperation” and the prestigious Center for Particle Physics Siegen (CPPS), which collaborates intimately with CERN. With world-class cleanrooms at the Center for Sensor Systems (ZESS), the university leads breakthrough discoveries in single-atom quantum gates, nanoscale 3D LiDAR sensors, and sustainable lightweight materials.
