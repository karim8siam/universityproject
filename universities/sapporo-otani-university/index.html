Sapporo Otani University

Sapporo Otani University is a historic private Buddhist university in Sapporo, Hokkaido, celebrated across Northern Japan for its conservatory-level Faculty of Arts (Music and Fine Arts) and Department of Social Welfare.

Academic overview & research priorities

Rooted in the Shin Buddhist tradition of mutual respect and compassion, Sapporo Otani provides rigorous artistic instruction, studio fine arts, and community social welfare programs. The university features world-class acoustic concert halls, fine art exhibition galleries, and specialized music therapy training programs.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation and specialized evaluations: performance auditions for music candidates, portfolio examinations for fine arts, and academic interviews for social welfare. Foreign applicants require JLPT N2 or equivalent.
