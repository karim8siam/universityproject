Institution Overview & Profile

UnitelmaSapienza University - Polo di Trani is the premier regional academic centre in Apulia directly linked to Sapienza University of Rome. Delivering fully accredited online undergraduate, master's, and postgraduate specialization diplomas, the Trani campus bridges southern Italy with Italy's premier public research university through blended tutoring, on-site exams, and administrative support.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Telematic University (Sapienza University of Rome System)
Main CampusPolo Didattico Trani, Palazzo Prometeo, Trani, Apulia

Admissions RouteDirect online matriculation with continuous enrollment throughout the calendar year

Key Academic Strengths

  • Direct academic affiliation and shared professorial standards with Sapienza University of Rome
  • Official examinations and face-to-face tutoring held locally in Trani for southern Italian students
  • Specialized Master's programs tailored for civil servants, healthcare directors, and legal professionals
  • Highly accessible continuous enrollment allowing students to commence studies in any month
