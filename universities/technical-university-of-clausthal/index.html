Technical University of Clausthal: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1775 in the Harz Mountains of Lower Saxony, TU Clausthal has evolved from a historic mining academy into an internationally recognized technological university educating over 4,000 students. With nearly 30% international enrollment, TU Clausthal focuses on the “Circular Economy” guiding research across three interlocking thematic clusters: Sustainable Energy Systems, Raw Materials and Resource Efficiency, and Advanced Materials and Processes. The university maintains cutting-edge pilot plants in battery recycling, geothermal drilling, polymer recycling, and hydrogen power.
