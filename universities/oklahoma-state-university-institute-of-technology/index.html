Oklahoma State University Institute of Technology

OSUIT · Okmulgee, Oklahoma, United States

Oklahoma State University Institute of Technology is the premier public university of applied technology in the Oklahoma State University System, situated in Okmulgee, Oklahoma. World-renowned for hands-on technical workforce training in heavy equipment, instrumentation, pipeline integrity, high-voltage electrical power, and automotive technology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OSUIT features industry-sponsored training complexes developed in partnership with Caterpillar, Komatsu, Ford, Toyota, Phillips 66, and American Electric Power. Students alternate semesters between intensive campus laboratory training and paid corporate internships across the nation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

OSUIT operates open-access admissions for high school graduates and GED recipients. Technical engineering programs (such as Heavy Equipment or High Voltage Power) require mechanical aptitude assessments, valid driver's licenses, and corporate sponsor interviews.
