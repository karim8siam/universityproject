Academic overview & research priorities

Located in the Piedmont region of North Carolina on a scenic 656-acre botanical campus, Elon is a national benchmark for engaged learning, study abroad (ranked #1 in US), undergraduate research, and internships. The university comprises Elon College of Arts and Sciences, the Martha and Spencer Love School of Business, the School of Communications, the School of Law in downtown Greensboro, and the School of Health Sciences.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission evaluates high school transcripts, academic rigor, extracurricular leadership, and writing essays. Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT) are optional. Graduate programs in Business, Interactive Media, Law, and Health Sciences require an accredited bachelor's degree, program prerequisites, letters of recommendation, and personal interview. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 80-100 or IELTS 6.5-7.5.
