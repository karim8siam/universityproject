Delaware Valley University

Delaware Valley University (DelVal) is an esteemed private university in Doylestown, Pennsylvania, acclaimed for its agricultural sciences, animal biotechnology, equine studies, landscape architecture, and signature Experience360 experiential learning program.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded as the National Farm School, DelVal manages over 1,000 acres of working agricultural land, livestock breeding centers, hydroponic greenhouse complexes, and biotechnology laboratories. Under the signature Experience360 curriculum, 100% of undergraduate students complete hands-on scientific research, clinical veterinary shadowing, or corporate agritech practicums.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require a high school diploma with minimum 2.50 cumulative GPA; DelVal is test-optional. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor’s degree with 2.80+ GPA, personal statement, and science prerequisite courses.
