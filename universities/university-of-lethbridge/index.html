University of Lethbridge: Neuroscience, RNA Biology & Quantum Research Portal

The University of Lethbridge (uLethbridge) in Alberta is a top-ranking Canadian comprehensive research university, internationally celebrated for the Canadian Centre for Behavioural Neuroscience (CCBN), the Alberta RNA Research and Training Institute (ARRTI), and state-of-the-art Science Commons.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

uLethbridge Board of Governors Graduate Doctoral Scholarship

High-value merit scholarship awarded to top-ranking incoming doctoral students conducting research in Biomolecular Sciences, Neuroscience, or Earth and Space Science.

Master of Science in Neuroscience & Behavioral Brain Research

Research-intensive Master of Science at the world-renowned Canadian Centre for Behavioural Neuroscience (CCBN) exploring brain plasticity, neurodegenerative recovery, and synaptic memory.

Ph.D. in Biomolecular Science & RNA Biochemistry Fellowship

Doctoral research program based at the Alberta RNA Research and Training Institute (ARRTI), utilizing cryo-EM, NMR, and X-ray crystallography to dissect non-coding RNA mechanisms.

Water Resources & Agricultural Irrigation Climate Adaptation Study

Field-based hydrological research investigation monitoring Oldman River basin snowpack melt, agricultural irrigation efficiency, and groundwater drought resilience.

Lethbridge Agricultural Research Centre Crop Biotechnology Practicum

Applied agricultural science placement at Canada's largest federal crop research center, performing genetic screening for drought resistance and biocontrol of canola pathogens.

Canadian Centre for Behavioural Neuroscience Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting two-photon in vivo imaging, optogenetics, and behavioral kinematics in rodent models of stroke recovery and cognitive aging.

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in Quantum Information & Condensed Matter

Full-time faculty appointment in the Department of Physics and Astronomy, conducting theoretical or experimental research in quantum algorithms, spin systems, or topological matter.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in relevant discipline with minimum 3.0 GPA (on 4.0 scale) in the last 20 university-level semester courses.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with minimum 3.5 GPA on a 4.0 scale and documented research productivity in scientific or humanities disciplines.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-3 page research statement detailing background, proposed thesis questions, methodology, and identified faculty supervisor.
Application fee
CAD $140 non-refundable graduate application fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, letter of intent, and three academic letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic graduate tuition averages CAD $6,800 to $8,500 annually. International student tuition is approximately CAD $18,900 to $21,800 annually. All admitted thesis-based graduate students receive guaranteed minimum funding packages (CAD $17,000 for Master's; CAD $21,000 for PhD).
