Southern New Hampshire University

SNHU · Manchester, New Hampshire, United States

Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU) is an accredited private, nonprofit university founded in 1932, operating an expansive 300-acre residential campus in Manchester, New Hampshire, alongside one of the largest and most innovative online higher education ecosystems in the world. Acclaimed by Fast Company as one of the world's most innovative organizations, SNHU is renowned for its low tuition model, competency-based learning, College of Engineering, Technology, and Aeronautics (CETA), and industry-aligned STEM curricula.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SNHU comprises the School of Business, the College of Engineering, Technology, and Aeronautics (CETA), the College of Liberal Arts, and the School of Arts, Sciences, and Education. Its Manchester campus features the 67,000-square-foot CETA building with wind tunnels, robotics labs, automated flight simulators, and cyber defense war rooms.

Research admissions & international qualifications

SNHU maintains an accessible, rolling admissions policy for online and campus students. Campus undergraduate applicants submit high school transcripts (test-optional). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75 GPA (provisional admission available for 2.0-2.74), official transcripts, and no GMAT/GRE requirements.
