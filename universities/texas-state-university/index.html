Texas State University

Texas State · San Marcos, Texas, United States

Texas State University is a major public Carnegie R2 doctoral research institution situated on the banks of the spring-fed San Marcos River in San Marcos, Texas. Acclaimed for the Meadows Center for Water and the Environment, the world-renowned Forensic Anthropology Center at Texas State (FACTS), and the McCoy College of Business, Texas State drives over $140M in annual research expenditures.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Texas State delivers nearly 200 undergraduate and graduate degrees across nine academic colleges. Flagship research centers include the 26-acre Freeman Ranch Forensic Anthropology Research Facility (the largest human taphonomy facility in the world), the STAR Park technology incubator, and the Edwards Aquifer Research and Data Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate class rank, GPA, and test scores (assured admission for top 25% of Texas high schools). Graduate programs require a recognized bachelor's degree with a 2.75+ GPA (3.0+ for doctoral studies), program-specific entrance exams (GRE/GMAT), statement of purpose, and academic letters.
