Baltic College (FHM): Health Tourism, SME Management & Sustainable Economics

Baltic College, integrated into the state-accredited Fachhochschule des Mittelstands (FHM Schwerin/Rostock), is a premier private university of applied sciences in northern Germany, renowned for international health tourism management, SME economics, and renewable energy logistics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in economics, tourism, healthcare, or social sciences from a recognized university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Applied university of applied sciences; cooperative doctoral degrees offered in partnership with University of Rostock and international partner universities.
Language proficiency
German proficiency (TestDaF 4 or DSH-2) for German tracks; IELTS 6.0/6.5 for English-taught international master's programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Motivation letter detailing commitment to SME business management or health tourism.
Application fee
€150 registration and internal aptitude screening fee.
Required documentation
University entrance qualification (Abitur or international secondary certificate), bachelor's diploma, CV, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for bachelor's and master's degree programs typically ranges from €450 to €650 per month (€5,400 to €7,800 annually). FHM Deutschlandstipendium grants, regional chamber of commerce bursaries, and corporate tuition partnerships are accessible.
