Hochschule für angewandtes Management

Hochschule für angewandtes Management (HAM), based in Ismaning near Munich with study centres across Germany, is Bavaria's largest private university of applied sciences, pioneering the semi-virtual hybrid study model in sports management, business administration, and organizational psychology.

Academic overview & excellence

HAM combines digital distance learning modules with intensive monthly classroom simulation blocks. Students participate in real-world corporate consulting projects, sports club management labs, and digital business development alongside academic coursework.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires the German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or recognized international secondary school certificate. Flexible enrollment intakes take place in March and September. English and German language tracks are available depending on program selection.
