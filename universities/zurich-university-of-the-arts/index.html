ZURICH, SWITZERLAND · EUROPE'S PREMIER ARTS & DESIGN UNIVERSITY

Zurich University of the Arts: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Zurich University of the Arts (Zürcher Hochschule der Künste / ZHdK), based at the iconic Toni-Areal in Zurich West, is one of Europe's largest, most prestigious, and technologically avant-garde arts and design universities. Renowned for its Department of Design, Immersive Arts Space, Centre for Computer Music and Sound Technology (ICST), and cutting-edge research in human-machine interaction, virtual reality, and sonic spatialization.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

PhD in the Arts & Design (Joint Doctorate with Partner Research Universities)

Fully funded practice-based doctoral research position at the Toni-Areal campus, investigating enactive interaction design, algorithmic aesthetics, immersive spatial media, and sound art.

Master of Arts in Design - Interaction Design (English-taught)

Two-year Master of Arts taught in English, educating design leaders in tangibility, speculative design futures, AI-driven conversational interfaces, and human-machine collaboration.

Immersive Arts Space Practice-Based Research Fellowship in Spatial Media & XR

Two-year research appointment at the Immersive Arts Space, utilizing 3D motion-tracking systems, 360-degree projection domes, and high-density ambisonic sound systems for live performance.

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships for Foreign Artists & Designers at ZHdK

Prestigious federal scholarships awarded by the Swiss Confederation to foreign artists and designers to pursue postgraduate master's studies or research at Swiss arts universities.

Master of Arts in Composition and Theory - Sound Technology & Computer Music (ICST)

Two-year master's degree at the Institute for Computer Music and Sound Technology (ICST), training electronic composers in 3D ambisonics, wave field synthesis, and acoustic spatialization.

Institute for the Performing Arts and Film (IPF) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the Institute for the Performing Arts and Film, examining artistic documentary filmmaking, digital archiving, and bodily performance in virtual spaces.

ZHdK GameLab Summer Interactive Media Research Traineeship

Six-month research internship at the ZHdK GameLab, building experimental game mechanics, narrative puzzles, and embodied physical controllers.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized bachelor's degree comprising at least 180 ECTS credits in design, interaction design, media arts, music, visual arts, or computer science.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 90-120 ECTS) in arts or design; admission to the specialized PhD in the Arts program in partnership with partner research universities.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency at C1 level (IELTS 7.0+, TOEFL iBT 95+) for English-taught master's degrees; German C1 for German-taught tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, detailed research/creative project proposal, and an extensive digital portfolio showcasing interactive projects or media works.
Application fee
CHF 100 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diploma, academic transcripts, portfolio, letter of motivation, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Zurich University of the Arts is CHF 720 per semester for Swiss and EU/EFTA citizens, and CHF 1,220 per semester (approx. USD $1,400) for international students. Doctoral candidates (enrolled in joint PhD programs in Arts and Design with partner universities like University of Zurich, Kunstuniversität Linz, or TU Vienna) receive salaried Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) or ZHdK research contracts earning CHF 47,000 to CHF 52,000 per year with zero tuition.
