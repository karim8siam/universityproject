Georgia Southern University

Georgia Southern University is a dynamic public Carnegie R2 doctoral research institution spanning campuses in Statesboro, Savannah, and Hinesville, leading in coastal science and engineering.

Academic overview & research priorities

Georgia Southern offers over 140 degree programs with distinguished strengths in civil and manufacturing engineering, logistics and supply chain management, public health epidemiology, and coastal ecology through the Institute for Coastal Plain Science.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with 2.50+ core GPA; SAT/ACT optional depending on GPA tier. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree with 3.00+ GPA, departmental statement of purpose, and TOEFL iBT 80+ / IELTS 6.5+.
