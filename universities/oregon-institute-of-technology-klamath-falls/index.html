Oregon Institute of Technology

Oregon Tech · Klamath Falls, Oregon, United States

Oregon Institute of Technology (Oregon Tech) is the premier public polytechnic university of the Pacific Northwest, with its main campus in Klamath Falls and an urban campus in Portland-Metro (Wilsonville). Renowned for renewable energy engineering, medical imaging technology, software engineering, and geothermal power.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Oregon Tech's Klamath Falls campus is 100% powered by on-campus geothermal wells and a 1.75-megawatt solar array. It boasts the Martha Anne Dow Center for Health Professions, the Geo-Heat Center (world authority on geothermal direct use), and specialized robotics and wind tunnel laboratories.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission evaluates high school GPA (minimum 3.0 recommended) and rigorous college-preparatory mathematics (algebra II, trigonometry, or precalculus) and laboratory sciences. Health technologies have competitive secondary admissions.
