Keyano College: Power Engineering, Trades & Environmental Science Guide

Keyano College is a comprehensive public community institution situated in Fort McMurray, the urban hub of the Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo in northern Alberta. Keyano operates world-class training facilities for power engineering, heavy industrial trades, environmental monitoring, nursing, and business administration. Through state-of-the-art power plant simulation facilities and partnerships with regional resource enterprises, Keyano equips graduates with accredited qualifications recognized across Canada.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Collaborative degree completion pathways and post-diploma certificates require recognized accredited bachelor's degree or two-year post-secondary diploma.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; Keyano is a comprehensive polytechnic and community college focused on diplomas, applied degrees, and university transfer programs.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (with no subtest score below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 79 for standard post-secondary diploma programs; nursing programs require IELTS 6.5 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Not required for undergraduate diplomas; statement of intent and safety certification required for industrial engineering and heavy trades programs.
Application fee
$107 CAD application fee for international applicants ($53.50 CAD for domestic Alberta residents).
Required documentation
High school transcripts, official post-secondary transcripts, certified English translations, copy of passport, and proof of required prerequisite courses (e.g., Math 30-1/30-2 and Physics 30 for engineering).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic students averages $4,500 to $6,500 CAD per year depending on the technical program. International student tuition ranges from $12,500 to $16,800 CAD annually. Keyano College awards over $350,000 CAD annually in entrance scholarships, athletic awards, and industry-sponsored bursaries.
