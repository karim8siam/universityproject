Kobe Design University: Product Design, Manga, Anime & Architecture Guide

Kobe Design University (Kobe DU) is a specialized higher education institution dedicated entirely to comprehensive design education and research, situated in Kobe—a designated UNESCO City of Design in Hyogo Prefecture. Founded in 1989, Kobe DU operates across seven specialized design departments: Product and Interior Design, Visual Design, Manga and Comic Art, Media Arts and Animation, Fashion and Textile Design, Architecture and Environmental Design, and Visual Communication, fostering creative practitioners who blend Japanese artisanal sensibility with advanced digital technologies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Arts or Bachelor of Science degree in design, architecture, fine arts, or related discipline; submission of a comprehensive creative design portfolio and thesis proposal.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Arts and Design; requires recognized Master's degree in design or related field, documented exhibition or publication history, and defense of the doctoral research plan.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 minimum required for studio coursework and critique seminars; JLPT N1 strongly recommended for theoretical design research.
Research statement / Proposal
Creative portfolio: 10-20 original works demonstrating design thinking, conceptual execution, sketching skill, and technical software fluency; written study plan in Japanese (1,200 characters).
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY application screening fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcripts, creative portfolio (booklet or USB), statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, JLPT certificate, and passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for undergraduate and graduate design programs is approximately ¥1,350,000 to ¥1,550,000 JPY per academic year (with an initial admission fee of ¥250,000 JPY). Kobe DU provides an International Student Tuition Exemption granting a 30% discount on tuition for all privately funded overseas students holding a college student visa.
