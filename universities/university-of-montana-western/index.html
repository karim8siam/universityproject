University of Montana Western

Montana Western · Dillon, Montana, United States

The University of Montana Western (UMW), located in the historic Beaverhead Valley in Dillon, Montana, is the only public university in the United States offering the revolutionary Experience One (X1) block scheduling program. Under X1, students take a single course at a time for 18 days, enabling immersive, full-time experiential field research across geology, horsemanship, biology, and environmental sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMW delivers undergraduate and professional degrees across departments of Biology, Environmental Sciences, Equine Studies, Fine Arts, Education, Business and Technology, and Mathematics. The campus is bordered by millions of acres of public lands, national forests, and trout-filled rivers that serve as an immediate outdoor laboratory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, GPA, and transcripts. Test scores are optional. International students must demonstrate English language proficiency (TOEFL 68+ or IELTS 6.0+).
