University of Arkansas Grantham

UA Grantham · Little Rock, Arkansas, United States

The University of Arkansas Grantham (UA Grantham), founded in 1951 and acquired by the University of Arkansas System, is an accredited public online university dedicated to serving working adults, military servicemembers, and veterans. UA Grantham offers career-focused associate, bachelor's, and master's degree programs across computer science, engineering technology, healthcare, and business administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UA Grantham comprises the College of Science, Engineering and Technology, the College of Humanities and Social Sciences, the Mark Skousen School of Business, and the School of Nursing. Its flexible asynchronous coursework is built on monthly start terms and digital laboratory simulation environments designed for adult learners.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are rolling with monthly starts. Undergraduate applicants must submit proof of high school graduation or GED; no standardized test scores required. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.5 cumulative GPA and official transcripts.
