West Coast University of Applied Sciences

West Coast University of Applied Sciences (Fachhochschule Westküste) in Heide is a state university pioneering renewable energy technology, green hydrogen systems, electrical engineering, and regional tourism economics in coastal Northern Germany.

Academic overview & research priorities

FH Westküste provides hands-on degree programs centered on environmental engineering, renewable wind energy grid integration, business administration, and digital informatics. Its specialized Research Institute for Hydrogen and Energy Technologies collaborates directly with the North Sea green energy corridor.

Research admissions & international criteria

Prospective students must hold a recognized German Hochschulzugangsberechtigung or international secondary qualification with certified German (DSH-2/TestDaF 4) or English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 80+ / IELTS 6.5) depending on the program track.
