RPTU University Kaiserslautern-Landau

RPTU University of Kaiserslautern-Landau (Rheinland-Pfälzische Technische Universität) is the only technical and research university in Rhineland-Palatinate, globally celebrated for computer science, artificial intelligence (DFKI), quantum optics, and environmental ecotoxicology.

Academic overview & research priorities

Formed through the historic merger of TU Kaiserslautern and the University of Koblenz-Landau, RPTU operates dual hubs: the Kaiserslautern campus excels in engineering, computer science, mathematics, and natural sciences closely integrated with Fraunhofer (ITWM, IESE) and DFKI; while the Landau campus specializes in environmental sciences, ecotoxicology, psychology, and educational sciences.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor's admissions require an Abitur or recognized international school-leaving certificate via uni-assist. Master's admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in a congruent STEM or environmental discipline with grade 2.5 or better. English-taught graduate programs require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90; German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4. PhD candidates apply directly to faculty research groups.
