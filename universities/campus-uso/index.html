Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree of at least 180 ECTS in biomedicine, nursing, public health, medicine, or related life science.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or equivalent (minimum 240 ECTS) in medical sciences, molecular biology, or biochemistry, and an approved doctoral research project.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B (IELTS Academic 6.5 with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research synopsis outlining proposed clinical hypothesis, laboratory methods, and translational patient relevance.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA applicants via Universityadmissions.se.
Required documentation
Certified transcripts, degree certificates, CV, research synopsis, and two letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free (SEK 0) for Swedish and EU/EEA citizens. Non-EU/EEA international students pay approximately SEK 140,000 to SEK 165,000 per academic year for health sciences. Fully salaried doctoral positions (Doktorandanställning) provide SEK 32,000/month with zero tuition for all nationalities.
