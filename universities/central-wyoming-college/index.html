Institutional Overview

Central Wyoming College (CWC) is a comprehensive public community and baccalaureate college serving the Wind River Basin and surrounding mountain communities in Wyoming. Operating its main campus in Riverton alongside centers in Jackson, Lander, and Dubois, CWC is celebrated for its Alpine Science Institute, nursing division, equine studies, broadcast media arts, and tribal community partnerships on the Wind River Reservation.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • World-renowned Alpine Science Institute (ASI) located in Sinks Canyon State Park specializing in glacial research
  • Pioneering Bachelor of Applied Science (BAS) degrees in Outdoor Education, Leadership, and Agricultural Business
  • Over 95% pass rate on NCLEX-RN among nursing graduates serving rural Wyoming hospital clinics
  • Historic educational collaboration and cultural integration with Eastern Shoshone and Northern Arapaho nations
