Università degli Studi di Bari Aldo Moro - Dipartimento Interateneo di Fisica

The Dipartimento Interateneo di Fisica 'Michelangelo Merlin' is a premier joint physics research division operated by Università degli Studi di Bari Aldo Moro and Politecnico di Bari, housing leading laboratories affiliated with INFN, CNR, and CERN in experimental particle physics, quantum optics, and astrophysics.

Academic overview & excellence

The department offers rigorous bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs in fundamental and applied physics. It plays major roles in the CMS and ALICE experiments at CERN's Large Hadron Collider and develops cutting-edge single-photon quantum key distribution technologies.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a secondary school diploma (Maturità) for bachelor's degrees or an accredited BSc in Physics/Mathematics with a minimum Italian graduation mark of 85/110 for master's programs. Doctoral candidates compete in national concorso examinations, evaluated on academic transcripts, research proposals, and oral defense.
