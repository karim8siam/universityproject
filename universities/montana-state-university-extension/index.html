Montana State University Extension

MSU Extension · Bozeman, Montana, United States

Montana State University Extension is the statewide outreach and applied research arm of Montana's land-grant university, serving all 56 Montana counties and seven American Indian reservations. Conducting vital research in rangeland ecology, drought-resistant cereal crops, livestock genomics, wildfire risk mitigation, and rural economic development, MSU Extension pairs academic science directly with Montana communities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Graduate study and fellowships administered through the Montana State University Graduate School. Bachelor's degree in agriculture, animal science, forestry, or related natural resource field with 3.0+ GPA. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international graduate scholars. Fall semester priority February 1, 2027; Spring priority October 15, 2026.
