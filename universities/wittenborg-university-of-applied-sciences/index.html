Wittenborg University of Applied Sciences: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Established in 1987 in Apeldoorn, with an executive campus in Amsterdam, Wittenborg University of Applied Sciences is one of the most international higher education institutions in the Netherlands, hosting students from over 100 nationalities. Accredited by NVAO and FIBAA, Wittenborg specializes in international business administration, digital marketing, tourism, and entrepreneurship.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree in business, economics, or related discipline (minimum 180 ECTS credits) or Bachelor's in other fields with relevant work experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Executive DBA and collaborative university doctoral programs require an accredited Master's degree and documented professional management experience.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in writing (or TOEFL iBT 90 with 21 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Letter of motivation (approx 500 words) describing career aspirations, global outlook, and academic goals.
Application fee
€0 (Direct online application via the Wittenborg admissions portal).
Required documentation
Certified copies of undergraduate degree and transcripts, English proficiency certificate, CV, copy of passport, and financial proof.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international Master of Science (MSc) and Master of Business Administration (MBA) programs is €9,800 to €14,300 per academic year. The university awards multiple internal scholarships, including the Wittenborg Gold Scholarship (€7,500 tuition fee reduction) and the Dutch Government NL Scholarship (€5,000).
