South Dakota School of Mines & Technology

South Dakota Mines · Rapid City, South Dakota, United States

South Dakota School of Mines & Technology (South Dakota Mines) is an elite public STEM research university located at the foothills of the Black Hills in Rapid City, South Dakota. Founded in 1885 during the Dakota gold rush to provide metallurgical and mining expertise, it is today a premier national engineering institution boasting exceptional starting career salaries and leading-edge partnerships with the Sanford Underground Research Facility (SURF).

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

South Dakota Mines offers comprehensive bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs in 20 STEM majors, including materials science, mining engineering, geological engineering, mechanical engineering, computer science, and biomedical engineering. The university operates state-of-the-art facilities including the Arbegast Materials Processing and Joining Laboratory and the Museum of Geology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with strong math and science coursework (average incoming GPA 3.6). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, physics, or physical science with a minimum 3.0 GPA, GRE scores (departmental policy), statement of purpose, and academic letters of reference.
