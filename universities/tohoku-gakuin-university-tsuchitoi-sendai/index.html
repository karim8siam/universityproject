Tohoku Gakuin University - Tsuchitoi Main Campus

Tohoku Gakuin University (TGU) is the oldest and largest private Christian comprehensive university in northern Japan, operating its historic Tsuchitoi Main Campus in central Sendai, renowned for law, economics, English literature, and regional data science.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1886 by missionary Masayoshi Oshikawa and William E. Hoy, TGU features the Letters, Economics, Business Administration, Law, Engineering, and Regional Studies faculties. The Tsuchitoi Campus houses registered Tangible Cultural Properties, the Institute of Christian Culture, and advanced regional data analytics laboratories.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, the Common Test for University Admissions or TGU institutional exams. International students require JLPT N2 or EJU scores and an in-person or online interview.
