Academic overview & research priorities

At Komaba, all University of Tokyo undergraduates spend their first two years in Junior Division liberal arts before advancing to specialized Senior Division faculties. Komaba also hosts celebrated full-degree English-medium programs including the PEAK undergraduate tracks (Programs in English at Komaba in Environmental Sciences and Japan in East Asia) and GPES (Graduate Program on Environmental Sciences). Its parkland campus in Meguro blends high-performance computational facilities, mathematical modeling laboratories, and humanities research institutes.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to PEAK programs requires secondary school completion, standardized test scores (SAT/ACT/IB/A-Levels), official academic transcripts, essays, and English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 100+ or IELTS 7.5+). Graduate programs (GPES and Mathematical Sciences) require a recognized bachelor's or master's degree, comprehensive research proposal, academic transcripts, and letters of recommendation. MEXT University Recommendation and Todai Komaba Merit Fellowships provide full funding.
