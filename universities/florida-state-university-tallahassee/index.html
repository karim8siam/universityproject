Florida State University

Florida State University (FSU) is a Carnegie R1 Very High Research Activity flagship institution in Tallahassee, home to the National High Magnetic Field Laboratory (MagLab), leading programs in high-energy physics, clinical psychology, film arts, and oceanography.

Academic overview & research priorities

FSU generates over $400 million in annual research expenditures. The National High Magnetic Field Laboratory—funded by the National Science Foundation—operates the world’s most powerful non-destructive magnet systems. FSU also features top-tier schools of Law, Medicine, Criminology, and Motion Picture Arts.

Research admissions & international criteria

Graduate admissions require a recognized Bachelor degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA in upper-division coursework, competitive GRE scores (program dependent), letters of recommendation, and a research statement. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+.
