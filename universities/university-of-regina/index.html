Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline with minimum 70% cumulative average (Canadian equivalent) from an approved institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in a congruent subject with minimum 75% cumulative GPA and proven thesis research experience.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 86 overall (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-page statement of research intent describing thematic focus, prospective academic supervisor, and methodology.
Application fee
CAD $125 non-refundable application fee submitted through the Faculty of Graduate Studies and Research (FGSR) portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, statement of intent, curriculum vitae, and two confidential letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic graduate tuition averages CAD $5,800 to $7,600 per year. International student graduate tuition is approximately CAD $12,900 to $17,500 annually. Research awards, FGSR graduate scholarships, and teaching assistantships provide robust financial backing.
