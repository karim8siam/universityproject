Truckee Meadows Community College

TMCC · Reno, Nevada, United States

Truckee Meadows Community College (TMCC) is a comprehensive public institution of the Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE), located on Dandini Boulevard overlooking Reno, Nevada. Renowned for applied workforce training and university transfer pathways, TMCC collaborates with the Tahoe Reno Industrial Center (home to the Tesla Gigafactory and Panasonic) to deliver cutting-edge advanced manufacturing, logistics, and allied health programs.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TMCC delivers associate degrees, technical certificates, and applied bachelor's degrees across five divisions: Technical Sciences, Liberal Arts, Sciences, Business and Social Sciences, and Health Sciences. The William N. Pennington Applied Technology Center anchors advanced robotics, automated manufacturing, and diesel technology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

TMCC maintains an open-door admission policy for high school graduates and GED holders. Specialized applied baccalaureate and health science programs (such as Nursing and Dental Hygiene) have selective criteria including prerequisite coursework, GPA thresholds (2.75+), and entrance testing.
