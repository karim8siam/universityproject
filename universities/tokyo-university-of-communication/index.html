Tokyo University of Communication

Tokyo University of Communication (Tokyo Tsushin Daigaku) is a state-recognized online and hybrid digital university based in Shinjuku, Tokyo, delivering degree programs in computer science, software engineering, and human welfare.

Academic overview & research priorities

Approved by MEXT (Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology), Tokyo University of Communication pioneers flexible, cloud-native university education through the Faculty of Information Management and Faculty of Human Welfare, equipping students with certified skills in full-stack cloud computing, AI data science, certified social worker licensure, and psychiatric healthcare.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation or equivalent qualification. Evaluated via documentary portfolio, statement of purpose, and online interview. International students require Japanese language proficiency (JLPT N2+ or equivalent).
