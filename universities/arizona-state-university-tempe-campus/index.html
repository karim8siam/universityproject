Institution Overview & Profile

Arizona State University - Tempe Campus is the historic flagship and primary research powerhouse of ASU, consistently ranked #1 in Innovation by U.S. News & World Report for ten consecutive years. An R1 Carnegie-classified institution with over $800M in annual research expenditures, ASU Tempe hosts the Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering, the Biodesign Institute, and the Julie Ann Wrigley Global Futures Laboratory.
