University College Twente (ATLAS Honours College)

University College Twente (UCT) is the prestigious residential honours college of the University of Twente, offering the world-unique Technology and Liberal Arts & Sciences (ATLAS) Bachelor of Science degree combining rigorous engineering with social sciences.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 2013 on the lush American-style campus of the University of Twente in Enschede, University College Twente is the first and only honours college in the Netherlands that seamlessly bridges engineering and technology with social sciences and the humanities. UCT’s groundbreaking ATLAS program (Academy of Technology and Liberal Arts & Sciences) does not rely on conventional exams; instead, students develop personalized semester projects evaluated by continuous feedback and student self-assessment portfolios. Graduates earn a prestigious Bachelor of Science (BSc) degree and gain direct entry into world-class master’s degrees in mechanical engineering, robotics, public policy, or artificial intelligence.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Technology and Liberal Arts & Sciences (ATLAS) (Bachelor's) — World-unique three-year residential honors degree integrating thermodynamics, calculus, programming, and electronics with public policy, sociology, and ethics.
  • Pre-Master Honors Track in Robotics and Mechatronic Systems (Bachelor's) — Customized STEM specialization within the ATLAS curriculum providing seamless admission into University of Twente's MSc Robotics program.
  • Pre-Master Honors Track in Sustainable Energy Technology and Policy (Bachelor's) — Integrated engineering-policy track focusing on hydrogen energy storage, battery life-cycle assessment, and EU green transition governance.
  • ATLAS Semester 6 Capstone Graduation Research Project (Capstone Project) — Individual six-month intensive scientific capstone project executed at international research institutes or corporate innovation labs.
