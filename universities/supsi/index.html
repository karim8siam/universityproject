SUPSI (Scuola universitaria professionale della Svizzera italiana)

Located in the Italian-speaking canton of Ticino, the Scuola universitaria professionale della Svizzera italiana (SUPSI) is one of Switzerland's nine recognized public universities of applied sciences. Renowned globally as the co-founder (with USI) of the Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (IDSIA - directed by deep learning pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber), the Institute for Applied Sustainability to the Built Environment (ISAAC), and the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI), SUPSI leads Swiss applied engineering, biomedical robotics, and clean energy innovation.

SUPSI IDSIA Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning Doctoral Fellowships

Doctoral research conducted within the world-renowned Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (IDSIA), investigating recurrent neural architectures, bio-inspired robotics, and autonomous drone navigation.

Nationally accredited Master of Science in Engineering profile delivered at the modern East Campus in Lugano-Viganello, specializing in embedded machine learning, cloud-edge architectures, and industrial cyber-physical systems.

Advanced Master of Science profile at the Institute for Applied Sustainability to the Built Environment (ISAAC), training engineers in building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV), battery storage systems, and zero-emission building design.

SUPSI Institute of Mechanical Engineering and Materials Technology (MEMTi) Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research conducted within the MEMTi institute in Manno, developing patient-specific orthopedic implants, wear-resistant coatings, and computational finite element models of human joints.

SUPSI IDSIA Robotics Lab Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Postdoctoral research role at the IDSIA Robotics Lab, developing autonomous micro-aerial vehicles capable of vision-based navigation in GPS-denied alpine disaster zones and industrial search-and-rescue.

SUPSI Building-Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer research traineeship at the ISAAC solar testing center in Mendrisio, performing outdoor solar module characterization, electrical degradation analysis, and architectural facade integration testing.

SUPSI Senior Researcher in Machine Learning for Industrial Automation

Senior research engineering post at the Department of Innovative Technologies, directing Innosuisse research consortia developing automated defect detection, predictive maintenance models, and robotic manipulation for Swiss precision manufacturing.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in engineering, computer science, architecture, or health sciences with above-average academic standing (minimum grade B or 5.0/6.0 on the Swiss grading scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 90 to 120 ECTS credits) with high honors; doctoral candidates are employed under Swiss research contracts and registered in cooperation with Swiss research universities (such as USI, ETH Zurich, or EPFL).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) and IDSIA research tracks; Italian B2/C1 for Italian-medium degree tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A motivation letter (1,000 words) and portfolio demonstrating technical engineering projects, scientific computing, or experimental research.
Application fee
CHF 100 non-refundable application fee for Swiss degree holders; CHF 150 for applicants with foreign university qualifications.
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree diplomas, official grade transcripts, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at SUPSI are among the most accessible in Switzerland: standard semester tuition is CHF 800 to CHF 1,000 per semester for Swiss residents and CHF 1,600 per semester for international students. Doctoral research assistants (assistenti di ricerca) are employed on formal Swiss cantonal research contracts, receiving an annual salary of CHF 52,000 to CHF 64,000 per year gross under Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) and Innosuisse funding scales.
