Academic overview & excellence

Founded in 1988 upon the educational philosophy of 'Global Vision, Local Action,' Osaka International University is located in Moriguchi City adjacent to Osaka. OIU operates two primary faculties: the Faculty of Global Business (International Economics, Management, Tourism) and the Faculty of Human Sciences (Psychology, Sports Science, Early Childhood Education). The campus features an active International Exchange Center hosting students from over 20 countries.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires completed high school education. International applicants apply through special entrance examinations with EJU or JLPT N2 certification. Master's programs in International Studies and Human Sciences require an undergraduate degree and submission of a research plan. English programs require TOEFL iBT 70 or IELTS 5.5.
