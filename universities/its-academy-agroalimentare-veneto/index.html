ITS Agroalimentare Veneto: Agribusiness Degrees, Viticulture & Faculty Guide

Headquartered in the UNESCO World Heritage prosecco hills of Conegliano, Veneto, with secondary campuses across Verona, Padua, and Buttapietra, the Istituto Tecnico Superiore (ITS) Academy Agroalimentare Veneto is Italy's leading tertiary academy for the agrifood sector. Established under Ministry of Education (MIM) statutes, the academy delivers accredited two-year Higher Technical Diplomas (Diploma di Tecnico Superiore - EQF Level 5) specializing in precision agriculture, sustainable enology, food marketing, and digital agritech.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
High school graduation diploma (Diploma di Maturità) or equivalent five-year secondary school qualification, alongside passing scores on the institutional admission entrance test.
Doctoral admission eligibility
EQF Level 5 higher technical diplomas articulate into university bachelor's degrees (Laurea Triennale) in Agricultural Sciences, Food Technology, or Enology with credit recognition.
Language proficiency
Italian language proficiency at B2/C1 level required for technical lectures and farm rotations; working knowledge of technical English (B1/B2) for export marketing modules.
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via the ITS portal including academic CV, secondary transcripts, written motivational statement, and individual admission interview.
Application fee
€30 non-refundable application examination fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school diploma certificate, certified transcripts, medical fitness clearance for agricultural machinery laboratories, and valid identity card.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In accordance with Italian Ministry of Education and Veneto Regional funding, tuition fees are heavily subsidized at approximately €600 to €800 per academic year. Admitted students are eligible for full regional scholarship grants (Borse di Studio Regionali) through Veneto ESU, providing up to €5,000 annually in living allowances, meal vouchers, and complete tuition reimbursement.
