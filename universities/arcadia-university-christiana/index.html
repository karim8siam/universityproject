Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from an accredited institution with minimum overall and science GPA of 3.0; completion of anatomy, physiology, chemistry, and genetics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Christiana campus focuses on clinical graduate degrees (MMS, DPT clinical affiliates); doctoral applicants consult Arcadia main campus.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 89 or IELTS 6.5 minimum for non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
CASPA personal narrative describing motivation for healthcare service and clinical teamwork.
Application fee
Submitted via CASPA ($179 first designation) plus Arcadia supplemental fee.
Required documentation
Official college transcripts, minimum 200 hours of direct patient care experience (PCE), and three letters of recommendation including one PA/physician.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the 24-month Master of Medical Science (MMS) in Physician Assistant program is approximately $48,000 per academic year. Health professions scholarships, National Health Service Corps (NHSC) loan repayments, and institutional awards are available.
