Texas Woman's University

Texas Woman's · Denton, Texas, United States

Texas Woman's University (TWU) is the nation's largest public university system primarily focused on women, operating campuses in Denton, Dallas, and Houston. Classified as a Carnegie R2 high-research institution, TWU is a national leader in nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, nutrition, and women's leadership studies.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TWU delivers accredited programs through the College of Nursing, the College of Health Sciences, the College of Arts and Sciences, the College of Professional Education, and the Merrilee Alexander Kick College of Business and Entrepreneurship. Its clinical centers in the Texas Medical Center in Houston and Dallas Medical District drive cutting-edge healthcare delivery and clinical trials.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcript, class rank, and GPA (assured admission for top 25% of Texas high school classes; test-optional). Graduate and professional healthcare admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, prerequisite science completions, and centralized application packets (e.g., NursingCAS, OTCAS, PTCAS).
