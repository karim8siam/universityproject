University of Iowa

UIowa · Iowa City, Iowa, United States

The University of Iowa, founded in 1847 in Iowa City, is the state's flagship public Carnegie R1 research university and a founding member of the Big Ten Conference. Internationally celebrated as the world capital of creative writing—home to the legendary Iowa Writers' Workshop, the first creative writing degree program in the US—UIowa is equally renowned for the Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine, pioneering space physics, and hydraulic engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UIowa encompasses 12 colleges, notably the Carver College of Medicine, College of Engineering, College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, Tippie College of Business, and College of Public Health. Its world-class research institutes include IIHR—Hydroscience & Engineering, the Iowa Neuroscience Institute, and the Holden Comprehensive Cancer Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are evaluated through the Common Application (holistic review; RAI score or GPA 3.7+ median; test-optional). Graduate and professional programs are highly competitive, requiring accredited baccalaureate degrees, competitive GPAs (minimum 3.3–3.5), writing samples or research proposals, and letters of recommendation.
