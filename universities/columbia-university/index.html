Columbia University in the City of New York

Established in 1754 as King's College, Columbia University is the oldest institution of higher learning in New York State and a founding member of the Ivy League. Ranked consistently among the top 15 universities in the world, Columbia is the birthplace of FM radio, the laser, MRI technology, and the Manhattan Project's early foundations. Operating state-of-the-art facilities across Morningside Heights, Manhattanville, and the Irving Medical Center, Columbia provides guaranteed multi-year funding packages for doctoral candidates, world-leading professional master's degrees, and distinguished faculty. Columbia PhD in Computer Science (Fully Funded)

Five-year doctoral program covering foundational AI, natural language processing, computer vision, cryptography, distributed systems, quantum computing, and computational biology.

Columbia SEAS Dean's Fellowship & Doctoral Research Assistantships

Prestigious fellowship awarded by the Fu Foundation School of Engineering to top incoming doctoral researchers across mechanical, biomedical, electrical, and chemical engineering.

Columbia Master of Science (MS) in Data Science

Interdisciplinary 30-credit Master's degree jointly offered by Computer Science, Statistics, and Industrial Engineering through the Data Science Institute in NYC.

Columbia Postdoctoral Research Scientist in Nanotechnology & 2D Materials

Two-year postdoctoral position at the Columbia Nano Initiative and Center for Precision-Assembled Quantum Materials (PAQM) synthesizing and measuring novel low-dimensional quantum devices.

Columbia Master of Public Administration in Environmental Science and Policy (MPA-ESP)

Intensive 12-month program at the Columbia Climate School and SIPA training leaders in climate mitigation, environmental law, renewable finance, and planetary stewardship.

Columbia Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF) in Biological Sciences

Ten-week immersive summer research experience in Morningside Heights conducting mentored hands-on experiments in genetics, neurobiology, or structural biology.

Columbia Postdoctoral Fellow in the Society of Fellows in the Humanities

Prestigious two-year postdoctoral appointment offering independent intellectual freedom to conduct interdisciplinary research and teach one undergraduate course per semester at Columbia.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Conferred Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a strong academic transcript (typically 3.5+ GPA for competitive STEM and data science programs).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate or Master's degree in an aligned discipline with exceptional academic achievement, demonstrable research capability, and strong faculty alignment.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 100+ (sub-scores 24+ recommended); required for applicants whose primary language of instruction was not English.
Research statement / Proposal
Concise 2 to 3-page Statement of Academic Purpose detailing specific research inquiries, methodology, relevant laboratory experience, and faculty members of interest.
Application fee
USD $110 to $120 application fee submitted through Columbia GSAS or SEAS application portals; fee waivers available for eligible applicants.
Required documentation
Complete official electronic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, and writing sample where required.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

All admitted PhD students in the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences (GSAS) and Fu Foundation School of Engineering and Applied Science (SEAS) receive full financial support for up to five years. This guaranteed funding package includes a 100% tuition waiver, full payment of mandatory health fees and comprehensive health insurance premiums, and an annual living stipend of USD $45,000 to $48,000 per year.
