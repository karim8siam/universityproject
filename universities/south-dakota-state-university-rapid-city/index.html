Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma with 2.6+ GPA or ACT score 18+; transfer nursing students require 2.7+ cumulative prerequisite collegiate GPA (chemistry, anatomy, physiology, microbiology). TOEFL iBT 71+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Accelerated BSN priority deadline March 1, 2027; Traditional BSN priority deadline December 1, 2026.
