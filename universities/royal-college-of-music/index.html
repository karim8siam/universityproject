Royal College of Music: Electroacoustic Music, Performance & Doctoral Studies

Founded in 1771 by King Gustav III, the Royal College of Music (Kungliga Musikhögskolan / KMH) in Stockholm is Sweden's oldest and foremost institution for higher education in music. Renowned for its avant-garde electroacoustic studios, world-class jazz, classical performance, and innovative artistic research in partnership with KTH.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Fine Arts in Music (180 ECTS) or equivalent degree; admission is determined primarily through rigorous musical auditions, portfolios, and interviews.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Artistic doctoral degree (240 ECTS) in music performance, composition, or music education; conferred in partnership with Malmö Academy of Music or KTH Royal Institute of Technology.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B (IELTS Academic 6.5 with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
For artistic doctoral calls: 5-page artistic research plan outlining musical problematics, methodology, performance outcomes, and contextualization.
Application fee
900 SEK application fee via Universityadmissions.se (EU/EEA students exempt).
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree certificate, academic transcripts, audio/video audition portfolio, CV, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition is approximately 190,000 to 220,000 SEK per year. PhD candidates are employed as full-time university researchers with monthly salaries starting at ~31,500 SEK/month, paying zero tuition fees.
