University of West Georgia

UWG · Carrollton, Georgia, United States

The University of West Georgia (UWG) is a comprehensive public doctoral research university situated on a scenic 645-acre campus in Carrollton, Georgia, 45 miles west of Atlanta. Internationally renowned as the birthplace of humanistic psychology in the United States, UWG is also celebrated for teacher education, nursing simulation excellence at the Tanner Health System School of Nursing, and music.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UWG comprises six colleges and schools: the College of Arts, Culture and Scientific Inquiry (CACSI), Tanner Health System School of Nursing, Richards College of Business, College of Education, School of Communication, Film, and Media, and University College. Key centers include the Center for Public History and the Carrollton Observatory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5) and coursework. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
