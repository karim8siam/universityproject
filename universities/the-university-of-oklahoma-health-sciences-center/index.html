The University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center

OU Health Sciences · Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, United States

The University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center (OUHSC) is the flagship academic healthcare and biomedical research center of the state of Oklahoma, located in Oklahoma City. Encompassing seven health professional colleges and the NCI-designated Stephenson Cancer Center, OUHSC is a national powerhouse in oncology clinical trials, diabetes metabolic research, neuroscience, and vision science at the Dean McGee Eye Institute.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OUHSC features seven distinct colleges: the College of Medicine, the College of Pharmacy, the College of Dentistry, the Fran and Earl Ziegler College of Nursing, the Hudson College of Public Health, the College of Allied Health, and the Graduate College. Its campus integrates major clinical hospitals including OU Health University of Oklahoma Medical Center and Oklahoma Children's Hospital.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions to professional healthcare programs require centralized applications (AMCAS, PharmCAS, AADSAS, NursingCAS, SOPHAS). Prerequisites include comprehensive college coursework in biology, organic chemistry, biochemistry, and physics, along with standardized examinations (MCAT, DAT, GRE) and situational judgment assessments.
