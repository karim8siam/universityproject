ISSR Giovanni Paolo I - Veneto Orientale

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Collegially sponsored by the Dioceses of Treviso, Belluno-Feltre, and Vittorio Veneto and academically linked to the Theological Faculty of Northern Italy (FTTR), the Istituto Superiore di Scienze Religiose 'Giovanni Paolo I' is a state-recognized higher education institute specializing in philosophical ethics, religious heritage, and intercultural dialogue.

Institution type
Pontifical & State-Recognized Higher Institute (affiliated with FTTR / MIUR)
Campus location
Treviso (Central Campus), with academic centres in Vittorio Veneto and Belluno
Accreditation
Congregation for Catholic Education (Holy See) / Italian Ministry of Education (MIUR)
Admissions channel
ISSR Registrar Office (Segreteria Generale Treviso)
Instruction language
Italian
Financial aid & support
Diocesan tuition bursaries, CEI scholarships, and regional Veneto student grants

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Intercultural dialogue and interreligious hermeneutics
  • Bioethics, medical humanities, and social ethics
  • Pedagogy of religious education with Italian state teacher qualification
  • Preservation of Venetian ecclesiastical cultural heritage and sacred art history

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Laurea Magistrale in Scienze Religiose (Pedagogical Track) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year master's degree fulfilling Italian MIUR requirements for secondary school teachers in philosophical and religious education.
  • Laurea Triennale in Scienze Religiose (Undergraduate Degree) — Three-year foundational baccalaureate covering philosophical anthropology, biblical languages, ethics, and social doctrine.
  • Master Universitario in Bioetica e Cura della Persona (Postgraduate Master) — Clinical and ethical specialization for healthcare professionals, hospital chaplains, and ethics committee consultants.
