Kyoto Pharmaceutical University (Kyoyaku)

Explore pharmaceutical doctoral fellowships, cancer drug discovery research, and clinical pharmacy degrees at Kyoto Pharmaceutical University in Yamashina, Kyoto.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1884 as the Kyoto German School of Medicine and Pharmacy, Kyoto Pharmaceutical University (Kyoto Yakka Daigaku, known as Kyoyaku) is one of Japan's oldest and most prestigious private pharmaceutical institutions, celebrated for groundbreaking drug discovery chemistry, clinical pharmacotherapy, and cancer molecular biology.

Institution type
Private Specialized Pharmaceutical University
Campus location
Misasagi & Nakauchi Campuses, Yamashina-ku, Kyoto
Accreditation
Japan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / MEXT / JPEC
Admissions channel
Kyoto Pharmaceutical University Center for Admissions
Instruction language
Japanese and English (graduate research tracks in English)
Financial aid & support
Kyoyaku Doctoral Research Fellowships, MEXT scholarships, and pharmaceutical industry endowed awards

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Synthetic organic chemistry and targeted drug discovery
  • Cancer molecular biology, monoclonal antibodies, and precision oncology
  • Clinical pharmacology, therapeutic drug monitoring, and hospital pharmacy
  • Pharmaceutical nanotechnology and liposomal drug delivery systems

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Doctor of Philosophy in Pharmaceutical Sciences (4-Year Ph.D.) (Doctoral Degree) — Advanced doctoral degree for pharmacists and molecular scientists researching novel bioactive compounds and clinical therapies.
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (6-Year Clinical Pharmacist Track) (Undergraduate Degree) — Six-year professional degree leading to eligibility for the Japanese National Pharmacist Examination, with extensive hospital residencies.
  • Master of Pharmaceutical Sciences (2-Year Research Track) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year master's curriculum concentrating on medicinal chemistry, pharmacology, and biopharmaceutical formulation.
