Academic overview & research priorities

Located in Palatine, Illinois, Harper College serves northwestern Cook County with cutting-edge academic facilities, including the Canning Student Center, state-of-the-art simulation hospital, manufacturing lab, and partnerships with UIUC, DePaul, and Northern Illinois University.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open-admission community college policy for general degree and certificate seekers (high school diploma or GED). Selective admission applies for nursing, dental hygiene, radiologic technology, and surgical technology. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+.
