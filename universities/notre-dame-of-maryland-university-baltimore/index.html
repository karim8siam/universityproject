Notre Dame of Maryland University

Notre Dame of Maryland · Baltimore, Maryland, United States

Notre Dame of Maryland University is a distinguished private Catholic university situated on an idyllic 58-acre wooded campus in northern Baltimore, Maryland. Celebrated for leadership in pharmacy education, nursing, biomedical sciences, and inclusive teacher preparation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NDMU features the School of Pharmacy with state-of-the-art pharmaceutics and sterile compounding labs, the School of Nursing, and the Sister Alma Science Center. Its historic Gibbons Hall and Loyola/Notre Dame Library serve as core academic anchors in Baltimore's university corridor.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission is holistic, requiring high school transcripts with a recommended 3.0+ GPA and optional standardized test scores. Professional and graduate programs require program-specific prerequisite science degrees and competitive interviews.
