Lakehead University: Comprehensive Graduate Programs, Research & Scholarships

Lakehead University is a comprehensive Canadian research institution recognized for its leadership in boreal ecology, environmental biotechnology, northern health, engineering, and Indigenous-focused scholarship across dynamic campuses in Thunder Bay and Orillia, Ontario.

Lakehead Graduate Assistantship (GA) & Research Award

Merit-based graduate teaching and research assistantship funding awarded to incoming full-time thesis-based master's and doctoral students at Lakehead University.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Biotechnology & Environmental Systems

Interdisciplinary doctoral program investigating bioremediation, forest biomass valorization, molecular genetics, and northern ecosystem resilience.

Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence

Advanced graduate curriculum featuring machine learning, high-performance computing, cybersecurity, and applied deep learning systems.

Biorefining Research Institute (BRI) Graduate Research Assistantship

Funded laboratory research focusing on value-added biochemicals, lignin valorization, green pulping technologies, and sustainable bioplastics.

Centre for Advanced Studies in Engineering & Northern Infrastructure Practicum

Industry-embedded internship with northern infrastructure consultancies and mining consortia solving cold-climate geotechnics and structural resilience.

Northern Policy Institute Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral appointment analyzing regional economic diversification, remote healthcare access, and indigenous community resource co-management.

Master of Environmental Studies (MES) in Northern Environments & Resource Policy

Interdisciplinary master's curriculum combining environmental impact assessment, geographic information systems, and climate adaptation strategies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year bachelor's degree from an accredited university with minimum 70% to 75% (B standing) over the final two years of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's degree with high academic standing (minimum 75% or B+ average) and demonstrated research potential.
Language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 80 (minimum 19 in each subscore); Duolingo 110 accepted for selected programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research thesis streams: 2–3 page research intent outlining prospective supervisor alignment and methodology.
Application fee
$125 CAD non-refundable institutional application fee via Lakehead Graduate Studies Portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, 2–3 confidential academic reference letters, curriculum vitae, and statement of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Lakehead University averages approximately $23,500 to $27,800 CAD per year for international master's and PhD candidates. Substantial graduate assistantships (GA) paying $10,800–$14,500 CAD plus research fellowships (GRA) offset costs for thesis-stream students.
