Nagoya University of Economics (Inuyama Campus)

Nagoya University of Economics (Nagoya Keizai Daigaku - Meikeidai) is a reputable private co-educational university situated in the historic castle city of Inuyama, Aichi Prefecture, near Nagoya. Founded in 1979 by Ichimura Gakuen, the university specializes in practical economics, corporate jurisprudence, business administration, and human lifestyle sciences.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Inuyama, Aichi Prefecture, Japan

Institutional Overview

The university comprises the Faculty of Economics, Faculty of Business Administration, Faculty of Law, and the Faculty of Human Life and Science. Programs feature small seminar cohorts, strong English-language and Asian business tracks, and dedicated career placement modules aligned with the Chubu manufacturing and automotive heartland.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions include Japanese university entrance examinations (Kyotsu Test), recommended school nominations, and comprehensive selection exams. International applicants apply via the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students (EJU) with JLPT N2/N1 proficiency. Tuition reductions of 30% to 50% are awarded to international scholars.
