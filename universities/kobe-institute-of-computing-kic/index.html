Kobe Institute of Computing (KIC)

Kobe Institute of Computing (Graduate School of Information Technology - KIC) is a pioneering professional graduate university in Kobe, internationally celebrated for training digital leaders from over 100 countries in ICT for Social Development (ICT4D), IoT engineering, and AI.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1958 as Kobe Denshi and elevated in 2005 to an independent professional graduate school, Kobe Institute of Computing (KIC) focuses on "Tankyu" (social inquiry) through practical information technology. Partnered with the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) through the ABE Initiative (African Business Education Initiative) and Japanese government scholarship frameworks, KIC educates students from Asia, Africa, Europe, and the Americas. The graduate school is renowned for turning digital prototypes into real-world solutions for agriculture, healthcare logistics, disaster risk management, and cybersecurity.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Information Systems (Global Innovator Course - English Track) (Master's) — Two-year professional master's taught entirely in English focusing on deploying IoT networks, cloud computing, and machine learning to solve socioeconomic challenges.
  • Master of Science in Information Systems (ICT Innovation Course - Japanese Track) (Master's) — Advanced professional computing degree training software architects in enterprise database systems, mobile software development, and digital security.
  • Graduate Certificate in IoT Sensor Integration and Edge Computing (Certificate) — Six-month intensive certificate exploring micro-controllers, wireless mesh networking (LoRaWAN), and embedded computer vision.
  • Executive Program in Digital Transformation for Public Sector Leaders (Certificate) — Specialized executive course for government and NGO officials in digital identity, open data governance, and public e-services.
