Academic overview & excellence

Affiliated with the Lutheran Church–Missouri Synod and accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), Concordia Wisconsin features six academic schools: Pharmacy, Health Professions, Nursing, Business, Arts and Sciences, and Education. CUW's School of Pharmacy is one of only two pharmacy schools in Wisconsin, featuring state-of-the-art sterile compounding cleanrooms and pharmacology laboratories.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate applicants submit high school transcripts with a recommended minimum GPA of 2.75. Pharmacy and graduate healthcare programs require standardized application portals (PharmCAS, PTCAS) with competitive undergraduate prerequisite coursework. International applicants submit TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
