Griffith University: Marine Biology, Nursing Excellence & RTP Fellowships

Griffith University is a major Australian public research university based in Southeast Queensland across five vibrant campuses. Internationally renowned for world-ranked nursing, marine science (Coastal and Marine Research Centre), clean water technology, environmental science, and drug discovery at the Griffith Institute for Drug Discovery (GRIDD).

Australian Government RTP & Griffith University Postgraduate Research Scholarship (GUPRS)

Highly competitive flagship doctoral scholarship funding domestic and international scholars conducting impactful research across all disciplines.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Marine Ecology & Coral Reef Resilience

Doctoral research conducted with the Coastal and Marine Research Centre evaluating Great Barrier Reef thermal tolerance, larval connectivity, and seagrass restoration.

Master of Global Public Health & Tropical Disease Epidemiology

Postgraduate master's degree preparing leaders in global health policy, biosecurity, vector-borne disease control, and pandemic preparedness.

Griffith Institute for Drug Discovery (GRIDD) Chemical Biology Assistantship

Applied chemical biology vacancy investigating NatureBank biodiscovery library compounds against drug-resistant tuberculosis and parasitic infections.

Gold Coast University Hospital Clinical Health Placement

Clinical immersion training across the co-located Gold Coast Health and Knowledge Precinct, rotating through acute tertiary care, trauma, and clinical research trials.

Australian Rivers Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship in Catchment Hydrology

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the world-leading Australian Rivers Institute analyzing catchment runoff dynamics, riparian wetlands, and climate adaptation.

Master of Cyber Security & Threat Intelligence

Industry-aligned master's curriculum exploring network security, digital forensics, incident response, blockchain security, and security operations center (SOC) analytics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed bachelor's degree from a recognized institution with a minimum GPA of 4.5 on a 7-point scale (or credit average / 65%+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-class honours degree (H1) or research master's degree with a demonstrated track record of scholarly publications.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no individual band below 6.0) or PTE Academic 58+; Nursing and Clinical disciplines require 7.0 overall (minimum 7.0 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for Higher Degree Research (PhD/MPhil): 2,000-word proposal aligned with a confirmed Griffith academic supervisor.
Application fee
Free directly through the Griffith International Online Application Portal.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates, academic transcripts, two confidential referee reports, CV, and verified English proficiency report.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition typically ranges from $33,500 to $42,000 AUD per year depending on the faculty. Australian Government RTP and Griffith University Postgraduate Research Scholarships (GUPRS) provide full tuition offsets and tax-free living stipends of $33,500 AUD/year for up to 3.5 years.
