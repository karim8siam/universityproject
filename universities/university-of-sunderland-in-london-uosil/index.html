Institutional Overview

The University of Sunderland in London (UoSiL), established in 2012 in the heart of London's Canary Wharf financial district (Harbour Exchange), offers career-focused degrees in business, finance, tourism, digital technologies, and healthcare. Designed for international and domestic professionals, UoSiL provides flexible four-intake academic schedules (October, January, April, June).
