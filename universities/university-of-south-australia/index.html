University of South Australia: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of South Australia (UniSA) is Australia's university of enterprise and a founding member of the Australian Technology Network (ATN). Renowned for industry-connected postgraduate research, UniSA leads nationwide efforts in future industries, defense technology, precision cancer medicine, and advanced materials engineering.

Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) & UniSA President's Scholarships

Prestigious Australian Government and institutional scholarships for domestic and international students pursuing higher degrees by research (PhD and Master by Research) at UniSA.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Data Science & Causal Machine Learning

Doctoral research position within the Centre for Industrial AI and Data Science exploring causal inference, trustworthy machine learning, and high-dimensional health data mining.

Future Industries Institute Doctoral Fellowship in Advanced Microfluidics & Sensors

Doctoral research position situated in UniSA's flagship Future Industries Institute (FII), developing microfluidic lab-on-a-chip diagnostics and point-of-care environmental sensors.

Master of Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (Coursework & Research)

Two-year postgraduate degree delivering advanced training in cloud computing, big data analytics, neural networks, and a semester-long capstone industry research project.

PhD in Precision Cancer Medicine & Pharmaceutical Sciences

Cancer research fellowship housed within the Centre for Cancer Biology (CCB), an alliance between UniSA and SA Pathology, developing targeted kinase inhibitors and RNA therapeutics.

Defence & Aerospace Engineering Postdoctoral Fellowship

Postdoctoral research role collaborating with Defence Science and Technology Group (DSTG) on autonomous uncrewed surface vessels, sensor fusion, and robust control systems.

UniSA Sustainable Water Resources Graduate Research Practicum

Hands-on research internship at the Mawson Lakes campus in conjunction with SA Water, investigating stormwater harvesting, aquifer recharge, and filtration membrane longevity.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized three or four-year Australian bachelor degree or international equivalent with credit average (GPA 5.0/7.0 or 65%+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year honours degree with First Class (H1) or Second Class Upper (H2A), or research Master's with demonstrated research output; confirmed academic supervisor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (reading and writing 6.0+) or TOEFL iBT 79 (reading 18, writing 21).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 4-6 page research proposal setting out research methodology, expected contributions, and laboratory facility requirements.
Application fee
AUD $0 for international research degree applications submitted online.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree awards, research proposal, CV, 2 academic referee reports, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate research degree tuition at UniSA ranges from AUD $36,000 to $43,000 per year, but candidates accepted into Master by Research or PhD programs are prioritized for Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarships or UniSA President's Scholarships that cover 100% of tuition and deliver an indexed living allowance of AUD $34,210 per year.
