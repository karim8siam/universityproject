Waseda University (Shinjuku, Tokyo)

Founded in 1882 by former Prime Minister Okuma Shigenobu, Waseda University in central Tokyo is widely regarded as Japan's most prestigious private research university. Waseda has educated seven Japanese Prime Ministers, world-renowned corporate leaders (including the founders of Sony, Uniqlo, and Honda), and celebrated authors such as Haruki Murakami. Internationally celebrated for pioneering humanoid robotics (having created WABOT-1 in 1973, the world's first full-scale anthropomorphic humanoid robot), advanced battery technologies, and global political economy, Waseda offers comprehensive English-medium graduate programs and generous scholarship packages. Three-year research doctoral program taught entirely in English exploring humanoid robotics (Humanoid Robotics Institute), surgical robotics, smart actuators, and bio-mechatronics.

Two-year English-taught Master's program providing advanced training in distributed network architectures, deep learning algorithms, computer vision, and IoT protocols in central Tokyo.

Waseda PhD in Applied Chemistry & Advanced Energy Materials

Three-year doctoral program investigating organic radical batteries, solid electrolytes, porous coordination polymers, and sustainable energy storage systems.

Two-year English-taught Master's program preparing students for doctoral research or international finance careers, with specializations in Microeconomic Theory, Macroeconomics, and Applied Econometrics.

Six-week mentored summer laboratory experience on the Nishi-Waseda Campus, giving international students direct access to humanoid robot teleoperation and bipedal locomotion labs.

Waseda Graduate School of Asia-Pacific Studies (GSAPS) Master of Arts in International Relations

World-renowned two-year bilingual/English Master's program analyzing East Asian geopolitical diplomacy, transnational business networks, and international development cooperation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
16 years of formal education culminating in a recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent academic certification) with superior academic marks.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (or professional degree) in a relevant discipline with demonstrable thesis research and academic publications.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 to 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 85+ for international English-taught graduate programs; JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-medium courses.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2 to 3-page research plan in English outlining research purpose, proposed methodologies, and prospective supervisor endorsement.
Application fee
¥30,000 screening fee for general international graduate application; completely waived for Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship recipients.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, graduation certificates, thesis abstract, research proposal, 2 recommendation letters, and prospective supervisor acceptance letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for graduate degrees in STEM at Waseda is approximately ¥1,200,000 to ¥1,400,000 per year (approx. USD $8,500/year). Waseda University Partial Tuition-Waiver Scholarships provide 50% tuition reductions to high-achieving international students, while MEXT Japanese Government Scholarship recipients receive 100% full tuition waivers, round-trip international airfare, and a tax-free monthly living stipend of ¥144,000 (Master's) to ¥147,000 (PhD) per month.
