NorQuest College

NorQuest College is the largest publicly funded comprehensive community college in the Edmonton region, serving more than 21,000 learners in practical healthcare, business technology, community studies, and applied health research.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1965 in the heart of downtown Edmonton, NorQuest College plays a pivotal role in Alberta's workforce development and post-secondary educational landscape. NorQuest operates the state-of-the-art Singhmar Centre for Learning, housing high-fidelity healthcare simulation suites, the Colbourne Institute for Inclusive Leadership, and the NorQuest Health Research and Applied Innovation Center. The college emphasizes experiential learning, intercultural competencies, and immediate employment pathways across interdisciplinary health, digital media, business management, and foundational sciences.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Diploma in Practical Nursing (Diploma) — Two-year clinical diploma accredited by the College of Licensed Practical Nurses of Alberta (CLPNA) with over 900 hours of acute and community practicum.
  • Diploma in Interdisciplinary Therapy Assistant (Diploma) — Comprehensive two-year credential preparing graduates in physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and speech-language pathology assistance.
  • Diploma in Machine Learning Analyst & Applied AI (Diploma) — Applied computing diploma focused on automated data pipelines, predictive models, and cloud machine learning deployment.
  • Certificate in Health Care Aide (HCA) (Certificate) — Government-standardized clinical program providing essential care skills in long-term care and home-care settings.
