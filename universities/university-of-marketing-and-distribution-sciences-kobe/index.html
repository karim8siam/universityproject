University of Marketing and Distribution Sciences

University of Marketing and Distribution Sciences (Ryūtsū Kagaku Daigaku) is a premier private university in Kobe, Japan, founded by Daiei retail founder Isao Nakauchi as Japan’s first institution dedicated to distribution and marketing sciences.

Academic overview & research priorities

Located in Kobe Research Park in Nishi-ku, UMDS offers specialized undergraduate and graduate programs across the Faculty of Commerce, Faculty of Economics, and Faculty of Human Societies, recognized nationally for retailing logistics, service marketing, and Asian trade.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation and entrance examinations. International applicant track requires EJU (Japanese as a Foreign Language score >= 220) or JLPT N2+, written essay, and interview. Graduate admissions evaluate research proposals and oral thesis defense.
