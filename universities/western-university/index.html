Western University (University of Western Ontario)

Founded in 1878 in London, Ontario, Western University (University of Western Ontario) is a founding member of Canada's U15 group of research-intensive universities. Consistently ranked among the world's top 120 universities, Western is internationally renowned for neuroscience and cognitive imaging at the Western Brain and Mind Institute, world-leading wind engineering at the Boundary Layer Wind Tunnel Laboratory, the renowned Ivey Business School, and musculoskeletal health research. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Western Graduate Research Scholarship (WGRS) & Doctoral Fellowships

Western's premier merit-based doctoral funding guarantee, providing guaranteed living stipends and full tuition fee offsets to domestic and international doctoral scholars across all disciplines.

Western PhD in Computer Science (Thesis-Stream)

Four-year research doctorate exploring machine learning, medical image analysis, bioinformatics, distributed cloud systems, and theoretical foundations of computation.

Western PhD in Neuroscience at the Brain and Mind Institute

Interdisciplinary doctoral program based at the Western Brain and Mind Institute using state-of-the-art 3T and 7T MRI scanners to study cognitive development, memory, and neurodegeneration.

Western Master of Engineering Science (MESc) in Civil & Wind Engineering

Two-year research-based Master's degree working directly with the Boundary Layer Wind Tunnel Laboratory and the WindEEE Dome, studying severe storm effects on high-rise structures and bridges.

Western Robarts Research Institute Summer Student Research Fellowship

Four-month competitive summer research placement at Robarts Research Institute conducting translational research in cardiovascular health, medical imaging, and neurobiology.

Western Master of Science (MSc) in Management (Business Analytics Track)

Direct-entry 16-month Master of Science at the world-renowned Ivey Business School combining case-method business decision-making with big data predictive analytics.

Western Research Associate in Advanced Functional Materials & Clean Energy

Two-year full-time research position in the Department of Chemistry developing advanced operando synchrotron characterization methods for next-generation solid-state battery interfaces.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree (Honours) from an accredited university with a minimum 70% average (equivalent to B standing or 3.0/4.0 GPA) across the last two years of study; competitive STEM programs typically require 78%+ (B+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in a relevant discipline with minimum 78% average (B+ or 3.3/4.0 GPA) and demonstrable thesis research capability.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 20 in each section); higher for Computer Science (IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 92).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of academic purpose detailing research questions, methodological approach, and alignment with Western faculty mentors.
Application fee
CAD $150 non-refundable application fee submitted via the Western School of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies portal.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 2 to 3 academic reference letters, and statement of research intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for research-based graduate students is offset by departmental funding packages and scholarships. For full-time doctoral students in good standing, Western guarantees minimum annual funding of CAD $24,000 to $28,000 per year for four years (comprising Western Graduate Research Scholarships, graduate teaching assistantships, and supervisor research stipends) plus international tuition differential awards.
