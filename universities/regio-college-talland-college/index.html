Regio College (Talland College)

Explore applied precision technology degrees, Zaanstreek industrial apprenticeships, nursing clinical diplomas, and Dutch DUO funding at Talland College.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Zaandam & Purmerend, North Holland, Netherlands

Institutional Overview

Regio College, now unified as Talland College following its merger with Horizon College, is the flagship regional higher vocational institution (mbo/hbo-doorstroom) in the Zaanstroom and Waterland region of North Holland. With cutting-edge technical campuses on Kruseman van Eltenweg in Zaandam and Purmerend, Talland College excels in precision engineering, smart logistics, sustainable culinary arts, nursing, and maritime technologies.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Historic industrial heritage in the Zaanstreek food production, maritime shipping, and machinery corridor
  • Specialist MBO Level 4 higher technical diplomas with guaranteed accelerated progression to HBO universities of applied sciences
  • State-of-the-art technical simulation centers for mechatronics, smart electrical installation, and green logistics
  • Robust dual learning (BBL - Beroepsbegeleidende Leerweg) models with 100% paid company employment
