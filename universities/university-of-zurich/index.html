University of Zurich (Universität Zürich)

Founded in 1833 as the first university in Europe to be established by a democratic state rather than a monarch or church, the University of Zurich (Universität Zürich - UZH) is Switzerland's largest university, enrolling over 28,000 students. With 12 Nobel laureates in its history—including Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen, Albert Einstein, and Erwin Schrödinger—UZH is a premier global research hub and a founding member of the League of European Research Universities (LERU). UZH doctoral candidates receive guaranteed salaried employment contracts, while Master's students benefit from world-class research institutes in the vibrant financial and technological capital of Zurich. Prestigious joint degree between the University of Zurich and ETH Zurich, ranked among the world's top quantitative finance master's programs with direct ties to Zurich's financial sector.

UZH Life Science Zurich Graduate School (LSZGS) PhD Fellowships

Joint graduate school between UZH and ETH Zurich offering 17 specialized PhD programs ranging from Cancer Biology and Neurosciences to Systems Biology and Biomolecular Structure.

Two-year postdoctoral position in the Department of Physics participating in the European Space Agency's LISA mission and direct dark matter search experiments (XENONnT).

Five-year doctoral program ranked among the top 3 in continental Europe, training researchers in microeconomic theory, macroeconomics, behavioral economics, and neuro-economics.

Eight-week mentored research internship investigating Alpine ecosystem responses to climatic variations and biodiversity conservation across Swiss field stations.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent 180 ECTS credit qualification) from an accredited university in an aligned discipline with above-average grades.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (120 ECTS) with excellent academic marks, strong thesis research background, and written confirmation of acceptance from an authorized UZH faculty supervisor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100 for English-taught graduate programs; C1 level German (Goethe-Zertifikat C1 or TestDaF 4x4) for German-taught programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 3 to 5-page research proposal detailing research objectives, methodology, preliminary literature review, and academic work schedule.
Application fee
CHF 100 application fee for applicants with Swiss credentials; CHF 500 for applicants with foreign university qualifications (credited against semester fees upon matriculation).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, certified copies of degree diplomas, curriculum vitae, personal statement, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at the University of Zurich are exceptionally low by international standards. Regular tuition is only CHF 720 per semester (plus an international student surcharge of CHF 500 per semester for foreign students, totaling approx. CHF 1,220/semester or CHF 2,440/year). Doctoral students are salaried employees of the canton of Zurich, receiving a guaranteed annual salary of CHF 48,000 to CHF 56,000 per year according to official Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) salary scales.
