Northwest College

Northwest College · Powell, Wyoming, United States

Northwest College is a vibrant public two-year institution situated in Powell, Wyoming, nestled near Yellowstone National Park. Noted for distinguished visual arts, photographic communications, welding technology, and outdoor recreation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Northwest College operates the Cabre Building, the Yellowstone Conference Center, and specialized darkroom, printmaking, and CAD modeling labs. Its close proximity to the Absaroka Mountain Range and Yellowstone River basin creates unique outdoor ecological learning labs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Northwest College offers open-enrollment admissions for secondary school graduates and GED recipients. International candidates must submit proof of English proficiency (TOEFL 61+ or IELTS 5.5) and financial certification.
