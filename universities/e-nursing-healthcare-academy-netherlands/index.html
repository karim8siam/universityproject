Academic overview & excellence

Headquartered in the medical hub of Utrecht, E-nursing Netherlands is the leading provider of continuing higher education for registered nurses (BIG-register), nurse practitioners, and clinical nurse specialists. Accredited by the Quality Register V&VN (Verpleegkundigen & Verzorgenden Nederland) and the European Federation of Nurse Educators, E-nursing delivers modular evidence-based curricula in advanced pharmacotherapy, oncology infusion protocols, perioperative care, and palliative pain management.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to accredited clinical modules requires a recognized postsecondary nursing qualification (MBO-Verpleegkunde Level 4, HBO-V Bachelor of Nursing, or international equivalent) and registration in the Dutch BIG-register or European professional nursing register. Advanced practitioner diplomas require an active clinical employment contract at an accredited teaching hospital or healthcare institution.

Twelve-month specialized post-bachelor program covering arterial blood gas interpretation, invasive hemodynamics, ventilator waveforms, and acute sepsis resuscitation.

Masterclass in Clinical Pharmacotherapy and Medication Safety

Six-month advanced curriculum for prescribing nurse practitioners investigating pharmacokinetics, polypharmacy interactions in geriatric care, and therapeutic drug monitoring.

Funding: V&VN Kwaliteitsregister accredited (60 continuous professional education points) Tuition: €1,850 total tuition Deadline: September 15, 2027
Scholarship

Dutch National Nurse Leadership & Clinical Innovation Scholarship

Annual merit award recognizing nurses who implement innovative digital care pathways, remote patient telemetry, or bed-side delirium prevention protocols.

Funding: Full €2,500 course fee waiver and €500 travel allowance for international nursing conferences Tuition: 100% waived Deadline: June 30, 2027
Fellowship

Pediatric Oncology and Chemotherapy Administration Fellowship

Nine-month specialized fellowship training oncology nurses in extravasation management, central venous access line maintenance, and pediatric palliative comfort care.

Funding: €10,000 clinical fellowship stipend in collaboration with Princess Máxima Center Tuition: Waived Deadline: May 15, 2027
Internship

Twenty-week hospital internship rotating through emergency triage, stroke units, and postoperative surgical wards at UMC Utrecht.

Funding: Hospital collective labor agreement (CAO UMC) monthly compensation of €1,400 Tuition: €0 placement fee Deadline: April 30, 2027
PhD

Doctoral Research Fellowship in Digital Nursing Informatics and Telehealth

Four-year joint doctoral research program in partnership with Utrecht University evaluating clinical decision support algorithms and remote biometric sensor telemetry in home care.

Funding: NWO Healthcare Innovation Doctoral Grant providing €38,000 annually for four years Tuition: €0 tuition fee Deadline: March 31, 2027

Six-week professional certification in TIME wound assessment, diabetic foot ulcer debridement, compression bandage kinetics, and vacuum-assisted wound closure (VAC).
