University of Central Arkansas

UCA · Conway, Arkansas, United States

The University of Central Arkansas (UCA), established in 1907 in Conway, Arkansas—known as the 'City of Colleges'—is a comprehensive public university celebrated for its leadership in healthcare professions, honors education, cybersecurity, and teacher preparation. UCA is home to Arkansas's premier Doctor of Physical Therapy program and the state-of-the-art Cyber Range.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UCA offers undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degrees through six colleges: the College of Health and Behavioral Sciences, the College of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, the College of Business, the College of Education, the College of Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences, and the Schedler Honors College. Its campus features the integrated Health Science Center and the Conway Regional Health System clinical training facility.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission operates on rolling deadlines through UCA's application portal (test-optional; recommended GPA 2.75+). Graduate and professional programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and department-specific exams or interviews.
