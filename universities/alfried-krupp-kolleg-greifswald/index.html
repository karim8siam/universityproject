Stiftung Alfried Krupp Kolleg Greifswald

The Alfried Krupp Kolleg Greifswald is an internationally renowned institute for advanced study (Wissenschaftskolleg) on the Baltic Sea, fostering elite postdoctoral research in plasma physics, Baltic regional studies, environmental humanities, and bioethics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Established by the Alfried Krupp von Bohlen und Halbach Foundation in cooperation with the University of Greifswald and the State of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, the Kolleg provides residential fellowships to world-class scholars. Fellows collaborate closely with the University of Greifswald (founded 1456) and the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics (Wendelstein 7-X stellarator), conducting unfettered fundamental research across disciplines.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission is highly competitive through international fellowship competition. Krupp Senior Fellowships require a distinguished professorial record and international publication reputation. Krupp Junior Fellowships require a completed doctoral degree within the previous six years with exceptional honors. International scholars submit a detailed research project prospectus (5-10 pages), CV, and publication portfolio.
