National University

National Univ · San Diego, California, United States

National University is a major private non-profit research institution headquartered in San Diego, California. As the flagship of the National University System, it is a national leader in educator credentialing, cybersecurity, nursing, and military adult learning.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

National University houses the Sanford College of Education, School of Technology and Engineering, and the School of Health Professions. It operates innovative one-course-per-month formats, virtual simulation labs, and cybersecurity training ranges.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation or transfer coursework under open-access and supportive admissions criteria. Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.5 to 3.0 GPA and professional goals statement.
