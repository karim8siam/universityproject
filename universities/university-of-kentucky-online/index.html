Research admissions & international qualifications

Freshmen/Transfers: High school diploma or 24+ transferable college credits with 2.5+ GPA. Graduate applicants: Conferred bachelor's degree with 3.0+ GPA. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates without prior English-medium degrees. Fall priority admission July 1, 2027; Spring priority November 15, 2026; Summer priority April 15, 2027.
