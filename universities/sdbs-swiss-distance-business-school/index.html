SDBS Swiss Distance Business School: Digital Economy, Sustainable Finance & AI Guide

Headquartered in Zurich with operations in Visp (Valais), the SDBS Swiss Distance Business School is a modern Swiss private business institution dedicated to flexible, technology-empowered graduate education. Integrating Swiss educational precision with digital pedagogical delivery, SDBS specializes in executive master's credentials, quantitative sustainable finance, circular economy management, and artificial intelligence for business strategy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (or equivalent 180 ECTS) in economics, business administration, engineering, or related field with a minimum GPA of 2.7 on a 4.0 scale (or European grade C+), plus at least one year of professional experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Business Administration (DBA); requiring a recognized Master's degree, minimum 3.0 GPA, significant management experience, and defensible doctoral research prospectus.
Language proficiency
English proficiency at CEFR B2/C1 level (IELTS Academic 6.5, TOEFL iBT 85, or Duolingo English Test 110) or certified degree taught entirely in English.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for Executive MBA and DBA candidates: 1,500-word research or capstone project proposal addressing strategic challenges in the applicant's industry.
Application fee
150 CHF non-refundable application and dossier evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified copies of post-secondary degrees and official transcripts, curriculum vitae, copy of passport, two reference letters, and statement of executive purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at SDBS Swiss Distance Business School ranges between 14,000 CHF and 22,000 CHF for complete master's programs. SDBS provides Swiss Excellence Distance Education Bursaries offering up to 30% tuition fee reductions for international students and flexible monthly payment plans.
