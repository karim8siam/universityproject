HEP Vaud: Educational Sciences, Collaborative Doctorates & Research in Lausanne

Haute école pédagogique du canton de Vaud (HEP Vaud) is a prominent Swiss public university institution based in Lausanne on Lake Geneva. It is dedicated to teacher education, pedagogy, digital learning technologies, and advanced research in educational sciences in partnership with the University of Lausanne (UNIL) and EPFL.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Joint HEP Vaud & UNIL Doctoral Fellowship in Educational Sciences

Four-year salaried doctoral research fellowship delivered in partnership with the University of Lausanne (UNIL) investigating learning science, pedagogy, and educational sociology.

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship (ESKAS) at HEP Vaud

Prestigious federal scholarship awarded by the Swiss Confederation to foreign researchers pursuing doctoral or postdoctoral research in Lausanne.

Master of Science in Special Education & Inclusive Pedagogy (MScE)

Postgraduate master's degree training pedagogical specialists in neurodiversity, learning difficulties, classroom inclusion, and educational intervention design.

Digital Learning & Educational Technology Research Laboratory Assistantship

Applied research post investigating the integration of artificial intelligence tools, digital literacy, and computational thinking in Swiss primary and secondary curricula.

Vaud Cantonal Schools Clinical Pedagogical Internship

Field-based teaching practicum in Vaud public schools mastering classroom management, differentiated instruction, and educational assessment.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Didactics of Sciences & Environmental Education

Two-year postdoctoral appointment examining climate change education, critical thinking development, and experimental science inquiry in schooling.

Master of Advanced Studies (MAS) in School Management & Educational Leadership

Executive master's program preparing school administrators and educational leaders in institutional governance, change management, and educational law.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized bachelor's degree in education, social sciences, or discipline corresponding to secondary teaching subjects; French proficiency at C1 level required for teaching tracks.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 120 ECTS or 300 ECTS total) in educational sciences, psychology, or related field; admission conducted jointly with UNIL Faculty of Social and Political Sciences.
Language proficiency
French C1 level (DALF C1) for French-taught pedagogy programs; English B2/C1 for international educational technology and research teams.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research project exposé (5–8 pages) outlining pedagogical questions, theoretical framework, and proposed empirical methodology.
Application fee
150 CHF administrative application fee.
Required documentation
University degrees, certified transcripts, CV, criminal record excerpt, language certificates, and statement of research motivation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for international students at HEP Vaud are modest, set by cantonal regulations at approximately 500 to 720 CHF per semester. Fully funded doctoral assistant positions (Assistant-e doctorant-e) provide salaried contracts of 60,000 to 75,000 CHF per year.
