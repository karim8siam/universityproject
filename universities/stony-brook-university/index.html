Stony Brook University

Stony Brook · Stony Brook, New York, United States

Stony Brook University (State University of New York at Stony Brook) is a world-renowned public flagship research university on Long Island's North Shore. Classified as an R1 Doctoral University and a member of the Association of American Universities (AAU), Stony Brook co-manages the prestigious Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) for the U.S. Department of Energy. It is globally famous for inventing Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), the Simons Center for Geometry and Physics, and premier programs in physics, computer science, and medicine.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Stony Brook encompasses the College of Arts and Sciences, the College of Engineering and Applied Sciences, the Renaissance School of Medicine, and the School of Marine and Atmospheric Sciences (SoMAS). Highlights include the Simons Center for Geometry and Physics, the Center of Excellence in Wireless and Information Technology (CEWIT), and the Advanced Energy Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly competitive (average GPA 3.9, middle 50% SAT 1340-1510, test-optional). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ minimum GPA (3.5+ for competitive STEM doctorates), GRE scores (department-specific), rigorous research statements of purpose, and three academic references.
