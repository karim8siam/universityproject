Academic overview & research priorities

Based in Eindhoven—the high-tech epicenter of the Netherlands—Eindhoven DJ University bridges musical creativity with cutting-edge audio technology. The academy features state-of-the-art studio suites equipped with analog synthesizers, Dolby Atmos immersive sound monitors, and digital audio workstations (Ableton, Logic Pro, Pro Tools). In collaboration with Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE) and Dutch electronic music festivals, the university trains producers, mastering engineers, and club acoustic designers.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a completed secondary school education (MBO/HAVO or international equivalent) and a musical portfolio demonstrating original track compositions or live mixing capabilities. International applicants require working English proficiency (IELTS 5.5 or equivalent).
