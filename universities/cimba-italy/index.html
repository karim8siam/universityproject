CIMBA Italy: Executive Leadership, Neuroscience & International Business Guide

Established in 1985 in the historic Veneto foothills of Mount Grappa, CIMBA Italy (Consortium of Universities for International Business Studies) is a premier European business education centre. Governed in partnership with a consortium of 36 top US research universities led by the University of Iowa's Tippie College of Business, CIMBA delivers AACSB-accredited Master of Business Administration degrees, neuro-leadership coaching, and behavioral management research.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

CIMBA International Executive Leadership & Merit Fellowship

Prestigious merit fellowship awarded to high-potential international professionals entering the full-time University of Iowa MBA program at CIMBA Italy.

Full-Time Master of Business Administration (AACSB Accredited)

Eleven-month intensive, AACSB-accredited MBA delivered entirely in English combining rigorous finance and analytics coursework with pioneering neuro-leadership coaching.

Consortium Doctoral Research Fellowship in Neuro-Leadership & Behavioral Economics

Doctoral research fellowship conducted at CIMBA's Behavioral Laboratory utilizing heart-rate variability (HRV) and electroencephalography (EEG) to study executive stress and strategic decision-making.

Veneto Export Manufacturing & Supply Chain Resilience Research Project

Field-based empirical study analyzing supply chain diversification, supplier risk management, and circular manufacturing among export-oriented small and medium enterprises in the Veneto region.

Benetton & Geox European Corporate Consulting Practicum

Twelve-week high-impact corporate consulting internship solving real-world digital supply chain and market expansion challenges for leading Veneto multinational enterprises.

CIMBA Postdoctoral Fellowship in Organizational Neuroscience & Cognitive Leadership

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment administering CIMBA's LIFE (Leadership Initiative For Excellence) program and researching neurobiological predictors of adaptive leadership.

CIMBA Italy Global Diversity and Inclusion Grant

Tuition grant awarded to students from emerging markets and underrepresented backgrounds who demonstrate academic ambition and a commitment to inclusive corporate leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree from a recognized institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0; completion of college-level algebra or calculus and statistics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral research in organizational behavior and behavioral economics conducted in partnership with consortium member universities.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80, IELTS Academic 6.5 (no band below 6.0), or Duolingo English Test 110+ for non-native English applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for executive and research admissions: 1,000-word statement of leadership philosophy and proposed capstone organizational transformation project.
Application fee
EUR 50 / $50 USD graduate application fee.
Required documentation
Official post-secondary academic transcripts, GMAT/GRE score report (or approved waiver), two letters of recommendation, and comprehensive CV.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the full-time AACSB-accredited CIMBA MBA is approximately EUR 29,500 (inclusive of tuition, Italian study residence, and European executive consulting trips). Comprehensive merit scholarships covering up to EUR 10,000, graduate assistantships providing EUR 4,000/semester, and European corporate sponsorships offer robust financial assistance.
