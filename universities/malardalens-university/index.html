Mälardalen University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Mälardalen University (MDU), elevated to Sweden's newest full research university in 2022, is an international technological center in the Stockholm-Mälar region. Globally recognized for its embedded systems, software engineering, and industrial robotics research, MDU collaborates intimately with multinational industry leaders like ABB, Volvo, and Bombardier.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed bachelor's degree (equivalent to Swedish Kandidatexamen 180 credits) in an engineering or technical discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (equivalent to 240 Swedish higher education credits, including 60 credits at advanced level); application submitted against competitive advertised vacancies.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B proficiency (IELTS 6.5 overall with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, master's thesis or preprints, and statement of intent demonstrating alignment with the advertised research position.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee through universityadmissions.se for non-EU students (SEK 0 for doctoral job applications).
Required documentation
Bachelor's and Master's diplomas, full official transcripts, passport copy, English test results, and two references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 100% free for all Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition for Master's programs is SEK 135,000–165,000 per year. Non-EU students can apply for the MDU Scholarship covering 100% of tuition fees. All PhD candidates in Sweden are employed as staff members on collective-agreement salaried contracts, earning SEK 32,000 to SEK 37,000 per month with zero tuition.
