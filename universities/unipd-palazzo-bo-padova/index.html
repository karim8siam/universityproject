Università degli Studi di Padova - Complesso Monumentale di Palazzo Bo

Palazzo Bo is the historic heart and ceremonial seat of the University of Padua (founded 1222), home to the world's oldest permanent anatomical theatre (1595), Galileo Galilei's lectern, and Europe's premier faculties of jurisprudence and legal history.

Academic overview & excellence

UniPd Palazzo Bo houses the central rectorate, the historic Law School, and advanced research archives in European legal traditions, human rights, and forensic medicine. Padua is celebrated globally as the birthplace of modern scientific medicine and humanistic legal thought.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to law and human rights programs requires a high school diploma with TOLC-SU humanities evaluation for bachelor's or single-cycle master's (Laurea Magistrale a Ciclo Unico in Giurisprudenza). Postgraduate and doctoral admissions require an accredited master's degree, research dossier, and competitive interview.

Galileo Galilei Doctoral Scholarship in Legal History & Human Rights

Three-year fully funded PhD investigating European constitutionalism, Roman private law transmission, and international human rights jurisprudence.

Funding: €16,243 net annual scholarship plus 50% allowance for overseas research semesters Tuition: €0 tuition for doctoral candidates Deadline: February 15, 2027
Master's

Master of Arts in Human Rights and Multi-Level Governance (English-Taught)

Two-year international master's degree conducted in English at the Human Rights Centre, analyzing international humanitarian law, refugee protection, and NGO advocacy.

Funding: Padua International Excellence Scholarships (€8,000/year plus full fee waiver) Tuition: €2,600/year (subsidized according to ISEE down to €0) Deadline: March 31, 2027
Fellowship

Palazzo Bo Historical Archive & Ancient Book Fellowship

One-year archival research fellowship cataloging 16th-century legal incunabula and anatomical drawings preserved in the Palazzo Bo historical collections.

Funding: €22,000 post-master's archival research grant (Assegno di Ricerca) Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 15, 2027
Internship

Venice Commission & Council of Europe Rule of Law Practicum

Six-month legal research practicum assisting with constitutional analysis, election monitoring briefs, and judicial independence reports.

Funding: €1,000 monthly living stipend plus travel to Strasbourg and Venice sessions Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 1, 2027
Master's

Laurea Magistrale a Ciclo Unico in Giurisprudenza (Single-Cycle Master in Law)

Five-year qualifying law degree qualifying graduates for the Italian Bar examination (Avvocato), judiciary concorso (Magistrato), and notary public practice.

Funding: ESU Padova student welfare housing and meal subsidies Tuition: €2,600 per year (reduced based on ISEE) Deadline: July 15, 2027
Scholarship

Elena Lucrezia Cornaro Piscopia Women in Science Entrance Scholarship

Merit award dedicated to outstanding female scholars enrolling in graduate and doctoral STEM and jurisprudence programs at Padua.

Funding: €5,000 annual scholarship honoring the world's first woman to earn a PhD (Padua, 1678) Tuition: Full tuition waiver Deadline: June 1, 2027
Course

Corso di Alta Formazione in Diritto della Proprietà Intellettuale & AI

Eight-week executive masterclass covering the EU AI Act, generative model copyright liabilities, algorithm patenting, and software trade secrets.

Funding: Continuing legal education bar credits awarded Tuition: €1,200 course tuition Deadline: September 15, 2027
