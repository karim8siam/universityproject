University of Calgary (Calgary, Alberta)

Founded in 1966 in Calgary, Alberta, the University of Calgary (UCalgary) is one of Canada's top comprehensive research institutions and a member of the prestigious U15. With over $500 million in annual sponsored research funding, UCalgary leads in energy transitions, biomedical engineering, quantum technologies, and data science. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

University of Calgary Graduate Award Competition (GAC) Doctoral Fellowships

UCalgary's central merit-based competition distributing over $15 million annually to incoming and continuing doctoral researchers across all faculties.

UCalgary PhD in Computer Science (Thesis)

Four-year doctoral program focusing on machine learning, human-computer interaction, visual analytics, cybersecurity, high-performance computing, and quantum software.

UCalgary Master of Science (MSc) in Electrical and Computer Engineering

Two-year research-based Master's degree at the Schulich School of Engineering offering specialized tracks in autonomous systems, wireless communications, and microelectronics.

UCalgary Quantum City Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Quantum Information

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the Institute for Quantum Science and Technology (IQST) developing quantum communications networks and superconducting sensors.

UCalgary Biomedical Graduate Summer Research Fellowship (BGS-SRF)

Four-month competitive summer research placement at the Cumming School of Medicine providing direct mentored benchwork in immunology, cancer biology, or neurosciences.

UCalgary Research Associate / Lab Manager in Sustainable Catalysis & Clean Energy

Full-time research management position supervising advanced analytical instrumentation (NMR, XRD, GC-MS) and coordinating multi-partner green hydrogen research grants.

UCalgary Master of Arts (MA) in Economics

Rigorous 12-to-24 month graduate program with specialized streams in environmental economics, financial econometrics, and regulatory policy analysis.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year baccalaureate degree from a recognized institution with a minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA (equivalent to a B or 75%) over the last two undergraduate years (approx. 60 units).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in a relevant discipline with a minimum 3.5/4.0 GPA (equivalent to an A- or 80%) and demonstrable thesis research experience.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 20 in each section); higher for Computer Science and Engineering (IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 97).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of research interest articulating proposed doctoral investigations, theoretical foundations, and prospective supervisor alignment.
Application fee
CAD $125 for Canadian/permanent resident applicants; CAD $145 for international applicants via the online graduate portal.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 2 to 3 academic reference letters, and research proposal/writing sample.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for thesis-based graduate degrees is heavily subsidized and offset by departmental guaranteed funding packages. For full-time doctoral students in good standing, UCalgary faculties provide minimum guaranteed base funding of CAD $24,000 to $28,000 per year for four years (comprising graduate assistant research stipends, teaching assistantships, and university fellowships).
