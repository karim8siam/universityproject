Rowan University

Rowan University · Glassboro, New Jersey, United States

Rowan University is a rapidly rising public top-tier research university (R2) located in Glassboro, New Jersey, with medical campuses in Camden and Stratford. Globally famous for the Henry M. Rowan College of Engineering, two medical schools (Cooper Medical School of Rowan University and Rowan-Virtua School of Osteopathic Medicine), and the Jean & Ric Edelman Fossil Park.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Rowan operates the South Jersey Technology Park, Cooper Medical School in Camden, and the groundbreaking Edelman Fossil Park & Museum. Its engineering college is renowned worldwide for undergraduate engineering clinics where students work on real-world industrial research from their freshman through senior years.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.7) with test-optional application options. Admissions to the Henry M. Rowan College of Engineering requires strong mathematics (calculus ready) and chemistry/physics. Graduate and medical programs require discipline-specific centralized applications (AMCAS, AACOMAS).
