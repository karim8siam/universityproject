QA Higher Education (Birmingham Campus)

QA Higher Education (Birmingham Campus) is a leading UK higher education provider situated in the vibrant commercial heart of Birmingham. Working in close partnership with top UK universities including Ulster University, Northumbria University, and Solent University, QA Higher Education delivers market-aligned undergraduate degrees, master's programs, and professional computing apprenticeships.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions consider international secondary qualifications or vocational diplomas, with foundation year pathways for students bridging into degree-level study. Postgraduate programs require a UK second-class honours degree or equivalent international bachelor's. English proficiency requires IELTS 6.0 (undergraduate) or IELTS 6.5 (postgraduate). Partner university regional scholarships and UK student finance are available.
