University of Music and Performing Arts Munich

The University of Music and Performing Arts Munich (Hochschule für Musik und Theater München - HMTM) is one of Europe's most renowned conservatories, cultivating world-class soloists, orchestral conductors, opera singers, and composers.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1846 and headquartered in the historic Arcisstraße in Munich's Kunstareal, HMTM offers premier conservatory education across orchestral performance, conducting, keyboard instruments, jazz, historical performance, opera, and theatre directing. HMTM maintains intimate artistic collaborations with the Bavarian State Opera, Bavarian Radio Symphony Orchestra, and Munich Philharmonic.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission is highly competitive and determined by rigorous live audition examinations evaluating virtuosity, artistic maturity, and music theory. Graduate and Meisterklasse masterclasses require a recognized bachelor's or diploma degree in music performance, a comprehensive repertoire list, and an artistic portfolio. International students receive subsidized tuition under Bavarian state regulations.
