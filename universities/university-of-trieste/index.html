University of Trieste (Università degli Studi di Trieste)

Founded in 1924, the University of Trieste (Università degli Studi di Trieste - UniTS) is a prestigious public research university in northeastern Italy, located in the European Capital of Science (Trieste). Renowned as a global hub for international theoretical physics and synchrotron radiation, UniTS partners intimately with the Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP), the International School for Advanced Studies (SISSA), and the Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste light source, offering world-class doctoral education and state-of-the-art laboratory research.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UniTS International PhD Scholarships in Physics & Synchrotron Radiation (Elettra)

Doctoral research program joint with Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste and the FERMI Free Electron Laser, focusing on time-resolved X-ray spectroscopy, quantum materials, and topological insulators.

UniTS Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) in Data Science and Scientific Computing

Prestigious joint two-year Master of Science in English organized by UniTS and SISSA, providing advanced mathematical foundations and computing skills for machine learning, high-performance computing, and computational modeling.

UniTS PhD in Molecular Biomedicine & Structural Virology (ICGEB)

Three-year research doctorate conducted in partnership with the International Centre for Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology (ICGEB) in Trieste, deciphering molecular structures of emerging pathogens and cancer signaling cascades.

UniTS Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) in Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering

Two-year Master of Science in naval architecture partnering with global shipbuilding giant Fincantieri (headquartered in Trieste), designing cruise vessels, naval surface units, and green hydrogen-powered marine propulsion.

Trieste Quantum Technology & Matter Postdoctoral Fellowship

Postdoctoral research fellowship in theoretical and experimental quantum technologies, developing quantum communication protocols and simulating strongly correlated quantum systems at the Trieste Science Park.

Trieste Miramare Marine Protected Area Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer research placement at the Miramare Marine Protected Area adjacent to Miramare Castle, monitoring northern Adriatic marine biodiversity, posidonia seagrass restoration, and coastal water chemistry.

UniTS Assistant Professor (RTD-A) in Advanced Materials & Nanocatalysis

Three-year fixed-term Assistant Professorship (Ricercatore a Tempo Determinato Tipologia A) in the Department of Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences, synthesizing single-atom catalysts and 2D nanomaterials for electrocatalytic water splitting.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years, equivalent to Italian Laurea) with strong academic standing (minimum 70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA); subject-specific prerequisites in physical sciences, engineering, or life sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized second-cycle Master's degree (Italian Laurea Magistrale or equivalent) with high honors; passing evaluation in the annual public competitive entrance exam (concorso pubblico).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 85; B2 English proficiency certificate accepted for several English-taught Laurea Magistrale programs.
Research statement / Proposal
A formal doctoral research project (progetto di ricerca) of 3,000 to 5,000 words detailing background literature, objectives, methodology, work packages, and bibliography.
Application fee
€30 public competition application fee submitted via the UniTS online Esse3 student portal.
Required documentation
Graduation diploma with official translation, academic transcript of records, detailed curriculum vitae, research project proposal, and 2 letters of reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at the University of Trieste are progressively calculated based on family economic indicators (ISEE Parificato). For international students from non-OECD nations, tuition is typically capped at €156 to €1,100 per year. Admitted doctoral candidates receive fully funded state scholarships of €1,400 net per month (€16,243/year gross) plus a 50% stipend increase during approved research stays abroad and €3,000 research mobility allowances.
