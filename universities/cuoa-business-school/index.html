CUOA Business School: Executive MBA, Lean Management & International Finance Guide

Founded in 1957 at Villa Valmarana Morosini in Altavilla Vicentina (Veneto), CUOA Business School is one of Italy's oldest and most prestigious management academies. Backed by Italian regional universities (Padua, Venice Ca' Foscari, Verona, Trieste) and major industrial conglomerates, CUOA is celebrated across Europe for executive business education, lean enterprise transformation, international banking, and luxury supply chain management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited three-year Laurea Triennale or Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in economics, engineering, humanities, or sciences with satisfactory academic record.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct executive research doctorates and DBA programs coordinated in consortium with accredited Italian university faculties (Padua and Verona).
Language proficiency
Italian proficiency level B2/C1 for Italian-taught masters; English proficiency B2/C1 (TOEFL iBT 85+, IELTS 6.5) for International MBA programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for MBA and master's candidates: 1,000-word essay detailing professional career objectives and proposed organizational innovation project.
Application fee
€100 non-refundable application and candidate selection interview fee.
Required documentation
Certified copy of degree certificate, transcript of records, detailed curriculum vitae, motivation letter, copy of passport, and two letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at CUOA Business School ranges from €9,500 to €18,000 for specialized master's programs and €24,000 to €32,000 for executive MBA courses. CUOA provides substantial merit scholarships covering up to 50% of tuition, alongside Italian regional vouchers and zero-interest bank loans.
