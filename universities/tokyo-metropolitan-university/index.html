Tokyo Metropolitan University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Tokyo Metropolitan University (TMU / 東京都立大学) is the only public university established by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government. Renowned for its sprawling Minami-Osawa research campus in western Tokyo, TMU ranks among Japan's top institutions in advanced materials science, solid-state battery chemistry, robotics, urban environmental engineering, and biomedical sciences.

Tokyo Government Asian Human Resources Development Scholarship at TMU

Prestigious fellowship sponsored by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government for talented international postgraduates from Asian nations pursuing Master's or PhD degrees at TMU.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Advanced Battery Chemistry & Solid-State Electrolytes

Doctoral research program within the Department of Applied Chemistry, synthesizing ceramic and polymer electrolytes for high-energy all-solid-state lithium and sodium batteries.

Master of Science in Mechanical Systems Engineering & Humanoid Robotics

Two-year Master of Science program taught in English, instructing students in bipedal locomotion, pneumatic artificial muscle actuators, and assistive robotics for aging societies.

PhD in Urban Environmental Science & Climate Disaster Resilient Infrastructure

Doctoral research at the Graduate School of Urban Environmental Sciences, developing computational models of urban microclimates, flood inundation in mega-cities, and resilient infrastructure.

Master of Science in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence (English Program)

Two-year master's program providing advanced coursework in deep neural network optimization, natural language processing, automated software verification, and distributed cloud systems.

Two-year postdoctoral position within the Department of Physics, synthesizing topological Dirac semimetals, non-centrosymmetric superconductors, and 2D van der Waals heterostructures.

TMU Minami-Osawa Robotics & Automation Summer Research Traineeship

Eight-week intensive summer research internship at the Minami-Osawa Campus, developing obstacle avoidance algorithms for autonomous wheelchair robots and collaborative arm manipulators.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education culminating in a recognized bachelor's degree; submission of undergraduate transcripts and approval from a prospective TMU research advisor.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completion of an accredited Master's degree or professional graduate degree; pre-consent from a prospective doctoral supervisor prior to application submission.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 80+, IELTS 6.0+, or TOEIC 750+) for English-medium graduate programs; JLPT N2 or N1 for Japanese-taught tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research proposal (around 2,000 words) defining background, research questions, experimental design, and targeted laboratories.
Application fee
¥30,000 examination fee (exempt for Tokyo Metropolitan Government scholarship recipients).
Required documentation
Certificate of graduation, academic transcripts, research plan, CV, two letters of recommendation, language certificate, and supervisor acceptance letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Tokyo Metropolitan University is set at the standardized public rate of ¥520,800 per year (approx. USD $3,500). Admitted international students benefit from TMU's extensive tuition exemption system, which waives 50% or 100% of tuition fees for eligible students. International scholars on the Tokyo Government Asian Human Resources Fund receive a 100% tuition waiver plus an exceptional monthly living stipend of ¥180,000 to ¥200,000 per month.
