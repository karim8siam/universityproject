Uppsala University (Uppsala universitet)

Founded in 1477, Uppsala University is the oldest university in Sweden and all of Scandinavia. Consistently ranked among the top 100 universities worldwide, Uppsala has educated 16 Nobel laureates including Svante Arrhenius, Dag Hammarskjöld, and 2022 Nobelist Svante Pääbo. Across the Ångström Laboratory and the Biomedical Centre (BMC), Uppsala conducts frontier research in battery technology (Battery 2030+), evolutionary genomics, computer systems, and quantum materials. Uppsala University President's Club Scholarships & King Carl Gustaf Scholarships

Uppsala's premier merit-based scholarship covering full tuition fees for outstanding non-EU/EEA international students admitted to two-year Master's degree programs at Uppsala University.

Uppsala University Salaried Doctoral Employment (Doktorandanställning)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointments across Uppsala research divisions, combining cutting-edge scientific investigation with teaching duties under standard Swedish employment contracts and comprehensive health coverage.

Uppsala Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year English-taught Master's program (120 ECTS) based at the Information Technology department, covering algorithms, machine learning, embedded systems, software engineering, and an individual 30-ECTS thesis.

Uppsala Master of Science in Applied Biotechnology

Interdisciplinary two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) inside the Biomedical Centre (BMC), partnering with Uppsala's vibrant life science cluster in biopharmaceuticals, synthetic biology, and molecular diagnostics.

Uppsala Ångström Laboratory Research Placements

Europe's foremost advanced materials and energy physics research center at Ångström Laboratory, housing state-of-the-art cleanrooms, synchrotron beamlines, and battery storage labs under the Battery 2030+ initiative.

Uppsala International Summer Research Traineeships & ERASMUS+ Placements

Two to six-month structured research traineeships inside Uppsala research laboratories in electrochemistry, solar cell materials, and structural biology.

Uppsala Graduate Teaching & Laboratory Demonstrator Assistantships

Campus employment positions (övningsassistenter / labbassistenter) enabling graduate students to tutor weekly recitation sessions, mentor programming workshops, and assist in grading laboratory exams.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Engineering, Computer Science or related quantitative field (minimum 180 ECTS credits), with a completed undergraduate thesis project.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master's degree (minimum 120 ECTS) in relevant discipline satisfying Swedish third-cycle admission criteria, with demonstrable scientific publication or thesis excellence.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper secondary school English 6: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (writing minimum 20).
Research statement / Proposal
For Master's: statement of purpose and academic transcripts via University Admissions Sweden. For PhD: comprehensive research statement matching specific advertised doctoral vacancies.
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying through University Admissions Sweden (waived for EU/EEA citizens).
Required documentation
Degree certificate, complete official transcripts with grading rubric, English proficiency test score, and copy of valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Swedish higher education regulations, EU/EEA and Swiss citizens study completely tuition-free. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition for master's programs is SEK 72,500 to SEK 82,500 per semester (approx. SEK 290,000 to SEK 330,000 for a two-year 120 ECTS program). The Uppsala University Scholarship Programme covers 100% of tuition fees for outstanding admitted Master's candidates. Doctoral candidates hold salaried employment contracts and study tuition-free.
