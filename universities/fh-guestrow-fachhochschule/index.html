FH Güstrow

Fachhochschule für öffentliche Verwaltung, Polizei und Rechtspflege des Landes Mecklenburg-Vorpommern (FH Güstrow) is the premier state university of applied sciences educating future civil service administrators, police commissioners, and judicial officers.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating under the Ministry of the Interior of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, FH Güstrow offers dual bachelor's degree programs integrating legal jurisprudence, public administrative management, criminalistics, and constitutional law with paid state civil service employment.

Research admissions & criteria

Candidates undergo a rigorous state selection process including cognitive aptitude tests, physical assessments (for police tracks), and an interview. Successful applicants are appointed civil servants on revocable terms (Beamte auf Widerruf) and receive monthly salaries throughout study.

Verified opportunities & funding programs (7)

Scholarship

Mecklenburg-Vorpommern State Public Administration Civil Service Bursary

Guaranteed monthly civil service stipend (Anwärterbezüge) provided by the State of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern for public administration students.

Funding: €1,350 monthly civil service salary throughout entire 3-year bachelor's degree Tuition: €0 (state-funded; zero tuition) Deadline: November 30, 2026
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Arts in Public Administration (Allgemeine Verwaltung)

Three-year dual bachelor's curriculum combining constitutional, municipal, and civil law with administrative economics and public human resources.

Funding: Fully salaried study program with direct civil service appointment upon graduation Tuition: €0 tuition fees Deadline: January 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Arts in Police Services (Polizeivollzugsdienst)

Rigorous three-year policing degree encompassing criminal law, criminology, tactical intervention, forensic sciences, and situational psychology.

Funding: Monthly civil service candidate remuneration (€1,400+ gross) Tuition: €0 (state-financed) Deadline: February 1, 2027
Course

Diplom-Rechtspfleger (FH) Judicial Administration Program

Specialized three-year university education training independent judicial decision-makers for civil registry, insolvency courts, and land registries.

Funding: Full civil service pay across 3-year judicial training track Tuition: €0 tuition fees Deadline: January 31, 2027
Internship

Judicial & Administrative Practicum Residency (Praxismodule)

Twelve months of practical administrative internships in regional courts, district offices, and police inspectorates in Northern Germany.

Funding: Paid practical training phases within state ministries and municipal authorities Tuition: Included in civil service dual enrollment Deadline: March 31, 2027
Course

Advanced Seminar in Digital Public Administration & E-Government

Specialist certificate exploring modern digital governance, cybersecurity compliance (BSI IT-Grundschutz), and AI adoption in municipal registries.

Funding: State continuing training grant for public servants Tuition: €0 for civil servants Deadline: April 30, 2027
Fellowship

State Merit Award for Outstanding Legal Thesis

Annual scholarly thesis prize honoring exemplary research on administrative modernizations and police law in federal Germany.

Funding: €1,500 prize plus publication in state administrative legal journals Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 30, 2027
