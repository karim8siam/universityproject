Medical University of South Carolina

MUSC · Charleston, South Carolina, United States

The Medical University of South Carolina is an elite public academic health sciences center in Charleston, South Carolina. Founded in 1824, MUSC is the oldest medical school in the American South, recognized globally for clinical excellence, biomedical discoveries, and the NCI-designated Hollings Cancer Center.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MUSC comprises six colleges: Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Dental Medicine, Health Professions, and Graduate Studies. It operates the MUSC Health hospital system and conducts world-class research in neuroscience, cancer immunology, stroke treatment, and organ transplantation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions to MD, DMD, PharmD, and PhD biomedical science programs are highly competitive, requiring exceptional academic GPAs, standardized exam scores (MCAT/DAT/GRE), extensive clinical and laboratory experience, and multi-round interviews.
