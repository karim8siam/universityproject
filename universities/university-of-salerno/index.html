University of Salerno: Pharmacy, Engineering & ADISURC Regional Scholarships

The University of Salerno (Università degli Studi di Salerno) inherits the legacy of the historic Schola Medica Salernitana, the oldest medical school in medieval Europe. Located on modern Anglo-Saxon-style campuses in Fisciano and Baronissi, it is renowned for pharmacy, chemical engineering, computer science, and legal studies.

English-taught master's degree exploring industrial food preservation, olive oil and wine valorization, biodegradable biopolymers, and smart food supply chains.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Three-year undergraduate degree (Laurea) or international bachelor's degree evaluated via the national Universitaly pre-enrolment platform.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with competitive examination of CV, research proposal, and an academic interview (available via video conference).
Language proficiency
B2 English certificate (IELTS 6.0+, TOEFL 78+, or Cambridge B2 First); Italian B2 required only for courses taught in Italian.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral admission: 3,000–5,000 word research proposal written in English outlining objectives, state of the art, and experimental methodology.
Application fee
€30 application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Universitaly summary, bachelor's degree certificate with Declaration of Value (DoV) or CIMEA comparability statement, and transcript of records.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is means-tested based on equivalent economic indicator (ISEE parificato), ranging from €156 to approximately €2,200 per year. Regional ADISURC scholarships (Azienda per il Diritto allo Studio Universitario della Regione Campania) provide eligible students with 100% tuition waivers, free campus housing, and up to €7,000 cash grants.
