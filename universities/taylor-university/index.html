Taylor University

Taylor University · Upland, Indiana, United States

Taylor University is a premier interdenominational Christian liberal arts university located in Upland, Indiana. Ranked consistently among the top regional Midwest colleges, Taylor is widely recognized for its high-placement Computer Science and Cybersecurity program, undergraduate satellite missions at the Center for Missions Computing, and distinctive intentional residential community.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Taylor offers over 100 undergraduate majors spanning the School of Natural and Applied Sciences, the School of Humanities, Arts and Biblical Studies, and the School of Professional Studies. Its Euler Science Complex features state-of-the-art physics laboratories, wind tunnels, collaborative software studios, and high-performance computing clusters.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applicants are evaluated holistically based on high school academic performance, spiritual pilgrimage essay, standardized tests (test-optional available), and guidance counselor or pastor recommendations. Academic merit scholarships provide substantial annual support for high-achieving domestic and international scholars.
