Jönköping Academy: Health Improvement, Welfare Systems & Faculty Guide

Established as a collaborative center of excellence between Jönköping University (School of Health and Welfare), Region Jönköping County, and the municipalities of Jönköping County, Jönköping Academy for Improvement of Health and Welfare (Jönköping Academy) is a globally celebrated research and education institution. Renowned worldwide for pioneering the Dartmouth-Jönköping clinical microsystem model and co-production of healthcare, the Academy offers accredited master's and doctoral programs in Quality Improvement and Leadership in Health and Welfare.

Jönköping University International Academic Scholarship

Merit-based tuition scholarship awarded by Jönköping University to exceptional international students admitted to master's programs in health and welfare systems.

Master of Science in Quality Improvement and Leadership in Health and Welfare

Advanced interdisciplinary master's degree focusing on improvement science, clinical microsystems, patient co-production, and strategic leadership in health and social care.

Fully Funded Doctoral Employment (Doktorandanställning) in Health Improvement Science

Four-year salaried doctoral research employment investigating systemic healthcare co-production, patient-reported outcome measures, and organizational resilience.

Clinical Microsystems and Healthcare Co-Production Empirical Project

Applied research project examining the practical implementation of clinical microsystem tools and patient co-design methodologies across primary and acute care hospitals in Sweden.

Region Jönköping Healthcare Quality Improvement and Clinical Audit Practicum

Practical 200-hour work-integrated placement embedding students in Ryhov County Hospital to conduct clinical pathway audits, flow mapping, and patient safety reviews.

Jönköping Academy International Visiting Scholar Research Fellowship

International visiting fellowship supporting collaborative research, comparative health policy investigations, and joint publication alongside Swedish health leaders in Jönköping.

Senior Lecturer (Universitetslektor) in Quality Improvement and Health Leadership

Permanent faculty position teaching master's and doctoral coursework, supervising Ph.D. students, and leading empirical health services improvement research in Jönköping.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree of at least 180 ECTS credits (or international equivalent) within health, nursing, medicine, social work, engineering, or public administration, plus at least two years of relevant work experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master's degree in health sciences, social welfare, or industrial engineering with a minimum of 240 ECTS credits, including at least 60 credits at advanced level.
Language proficiency
English language proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper secondary school English 6 (IELTS Academic 6.5 with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90).
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted through University Admissions Sweden (antagning.se) including CV, proof of professional clinical experience, and statement of intent.
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying via University Admissions Sweden.
Required documentation
Certified degree diplomas, complete official transcripts of records with grading scale, proof of relevant professional experience, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In accordance with Swedish higher education law, citizens of the EU/EEA and Switzerland study tuition-free. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is approximately SEK 60,000 per semester (SEK 120,000 per academic year for the 60-ECTS Master's). Admitted PhD candidates are hired as university employees under Swedish doctoral salary collective agreements (SEK 32,000 to SEK 36,000 per month) with zero tuition fees.
