University of Hyogo

The University of Hyogo is a major public research university in the Kansai region of Japan, renowned for advanced materials science using the SPring-8 synchrotron radiation facility, supercomputing informatics, economics, and environmental disaster management.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 2004 by integrating three historic prefectural higher education institutions (Kobe University of Commerce founded in 1929, Himeji Institute of Technology founded in 1944, and the College of Nursing Art and Science), the University of Hyogo enrolls over 6,500 students across six faculties and fourteen graduate schools. The university operates world-leading scientific complexes, notably the NewSUBARU synchrotron radiation facility, and is directly linked to the world’s most powerful SPring-8 and SACLA X-ray free-electron laser complexes in Harima Science Garden City.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Synchrotron Radiation Materials Science (SPring-8 & NewSUBARU)
  • High-Performance Supercomputing & Computational Life Sciences (Fugaku Partner)
  • Global Business Economics & Econometrics (Historic Kobe Commerce Lineage)
  • Disaster Nursing, Public Health & Earthquake Resilience

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Materials Science and Synchrotron Radiation Technology (Doctorate) — World-class doctoral program conducted at the Harima Science Campus and NewSUBARU synchrotron ring analyzing atomic surface structures and extreme-UV lithography.
  • Bachelor of Global Business Course (GBC - English-Taught) (Bachelor's) — Four-year undergraduate degree taught entirely in English at the Kobe Campus, welcoming international students to master economics, business strategy, and Asian trade.
  • Master of Engineering in Advanced Mechanical and Electrical Engineering (Master's) — Graduate engineering curriculum in Himeji focusing on precision robotics, micro-fluidics, semiconductor sensors, and thermal power systems.
  • PhD in Disaster Nursing and Global Health (Doctorate) — Internationally recognized program created following the Great Hanshin-Awaji Earthquake, developing emergency disaster triage protocols and post-catastrophe care.
