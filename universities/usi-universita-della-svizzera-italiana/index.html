USI Università della Svizzera italiana (Lugano, Switzerland)

Established in 1996 in Lugano and Mendrisio, Ticino, Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) is one of Switzerland's 12 public universities. Despite its young age, USI has earned global recognition: its Faculty of Informatics is internationally ranked among Europe's top computing centres, affiliated with the Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (IDSIA, co-directed by deep learning pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber), and collaborating with the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS). Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in computer science, mathematics, or related quantitative discipline from a recognized university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science degree in relevant discipline; appointment occurs through competitive selection to open salaried doctoral assistant positions.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (all informatics and data science master's are taught entirely in English).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive curriculum vitae, statement of research interests, and description of technical software/analytical projects.
Application fee
CHF 100 application administrative fee; standard semester tuition of only CHF 2,000 to CHF 4,000/semester (waived for doctoral assistants).
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, degree certificate, CV, 2 academic reference letters, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is completely free of charge for all doctoral candidates. Doctoral researchers are employed as salaried university assistants (Assistenti dottorandi), earning competitive gross annual salaries of CHF 47,040 to CHF 52,000 per year with comprehensive Swiss social healthcare and retirement coverage.
