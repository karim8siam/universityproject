North Carolina Wesleyan University

NC Wesleyan · Rocky Mount, North Carolina, United States

North Carolina Wesleyan University (NCWU) is a dynamic private, multi-faith university established in 1956, situated on a scenic 200-acre campus in Rocky Mount, North Carolina. Acclaimed for undergraduate biology and biomedical research, criminal justice, organizational management, and teacher education, NCWU offers small class sizes, individualized faculty research mentorship, and vibrant international student community engagement.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NCWU offers bachelor's degrees and graduate programs through the School of Mathematics and Sciences, School of Business, School of Social Sciences & Humanities, and the School of Education. The university operates the Dunn Center for the Performing Arts.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, secondary school preparation, and personal statements with test-optional reviews. International merit scholarships covering up to $15,000/year are automatically awarded to eligible undergraduate candidates based on academic records.
