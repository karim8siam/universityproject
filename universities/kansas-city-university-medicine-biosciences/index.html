Kansas City University

Kansas City Univ · Kansas City, Missouri, United States

Kansas City University (KCU) is a private healthcare-focused university with campuses in Kansas City and Joplin, Missouri. Founded in 1916, KCU operates one of the oldest osteopathic medical colleges in the United States and the College of Dental Medicine.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

KCU comprises the College of Osteopathic Medicine, College of Dental Medicine, College of Biosciences, and Center for Clinical Research. Research initiatives emphasize neurosciences, musculoskeletal disorders, anatomical variations, and community oral health disparities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (DO) applicants submit AACOMAS applications with MCAT scores, science coursework, and healthcare experience. Biomedical Sciences master's programs require a bachelor's degree in biological or physical sciences with minimum 3.0 GPA.
