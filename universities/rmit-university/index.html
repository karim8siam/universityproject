RMIT University (Melbourne City Campus)

Founded in 1887 as the Working Men's College of Melbourne, RMIT University (Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology) is one of Australia's premier institutions of technology and design. Ranked #1 in Australia and #18 globally for Art and Design (QS) and renowned for aerospace, computer science, and blockchain innovation, RMIT operates vibrant global campuses anchored in the heart of Melbourne's central business district. RMIT Vice-Chancellor's PhD Excellence Scholarships

RMIT's most prestigious doctoral research award, providing top living stipends, professional development allowances, and tuition waivers for high-achieving domestic and international researchers.

RMIT Master of Artificial Intelligence

Two-year industry-aligned Master's degree teaching deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, autonomous agents, and ethical AI deployment.

RMIT PhD in Aerospace and Aviation Engineering

Three to four-year research doctorate exploring autonomous unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), composite aerostructures, computational fluid dynamics, and space systems.

RMIT ARC Centre of Excellence in Exciton Science Postdoctoral Fellow

Two-year postdoctoral position in the ARC Centre of Excellence in Exciton Science developing next-generation photovoltaic materials and light-emitting semiconductor devices.

RMIT Master of Animation, Games and Interactivity (MAGI)

Ranked among the world's top 10 animation schools, this two-year studio-based Master's degree trains directors, VR creators, and technical artists in cutting-edge interactive narrative design.

RMIT Centre for Urban Research (CUR) Summer Sustainability Fellowship

Eight-week summer research placement investigating climate resilience in urban microclimates, urban biodiversity conservation, and zero-carbon building transitions in Melbourne.

RMIT Blockchain Innovation Hub Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Two-year full-time research position at the world-renowned RMIT Blockchain Innovation Hub studying decentralized governance, smart contract institutions, and digital asset market structure.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a minimum grade point average (GPA) of 2.5/4.0 or 60%+ average mark.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1, 80%+ WAM) or Master's degree with a significant research dissertation component graded at H1/80%+.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-test) or TOEFL iBT 79 (reading 13, listening 12, speaking 18, writing 21); higher for Law and Communication (IELTS 7.0).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 1,000 to 2,000-word research proposal describing background, methodology, objectives, and confirmed RMIT academic supervisor alignment.
Application fee
AUD $100 international application fee via the RMIT online portal; fee waivers available for nominated scholarship candidates.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV, research proposal, and contact details for two academic referees.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate research candidates are supported by competitive scholarships. Recipients of the RMIT Research Stipend Scholarship (RRSS) or RTP receive a 100% full international tuition fee waiver, a tax-free living allowance of AUD $34,841 per year for up to 3.5 years, single overseas student health cover (OSHC), and a relocation grant.
