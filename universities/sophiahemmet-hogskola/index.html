Sophiahemmet Högskola: Healthcare Science, Nursing Leadership & Doctoral Studies

Founded in 1884 under the patronage of Queen Sophia of Sweden, Sophiahemmet Högskola (Sophiahemmet University) in Stockholm is Sweden's most prestigious non-profit higher education institution in healthcare and nursing sciences, renowned for clinical nursing, palliative care, palliative oncology, and healthcare governance in collaboration with Karolinska Institutet.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Science in Nursing (180 ECTS) or equivalent professional healthcare degree from an accredited institution via Universityadmissions.se.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS with 60 ECTS at advanced level) in nursing science or healthcare sciences with a completed empirical thesis.
Language proficiency
Swedish C1 (TISUS) for clinical specialization programs; English 6 (IELTS 6.5, no band below 5.5) for international health research tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral employment calls: 5-page research plan outlining clinical problem, ethical board considerations, methodology, and patient outcomes.
Application fee
900 SEK application fee via Universityadmissions.se (EU/EEA students exempt).
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diplomas, professional nursing license, transcript of records, CV, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition is approximately 140,000 to 160,000 SEK per year. PhD candidates are employed as full-time salaried researchers with starting salaries of ~31,000 SEK/month, paying zero tuition fees.
