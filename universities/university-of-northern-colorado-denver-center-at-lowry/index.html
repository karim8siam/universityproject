University of Northern Colorado Denver Center at Lowry

UNC Denver Center at Lowry · Denver, Colorado, United States

The University of Northern Colorado Denver Center at Lowry is UNC's metropolitan extended campus serving the Denver Aurora corridor. Providing flexible evening, weekend, and hybrid master's and doctoral programs, the Lowry Center specializes in urban teacher leadership, speech-language pathology, clinical mental health counseling, and higher education administrative leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Denver · Metropolitan Graduate Center for Education, Speech Pathology & Leadership. The University of Northern Colorado Denver Center at Lowry is UNC's metropolitan extended campus serving the Denver Aurora corridor. Providing flexible evening, weekend, and hybrid master's and doctoral programs, the Lowry Center specializes in urban teacher leadership, speech-language pathology, clinical mental health counseling, and higher education administrative leadership.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0 for regular master's admission. Specialized prerequisites for clinical programs. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates whose medium of instruction was not English. Fall priority deadline March 1, 2027; Spring priority deadline October 15, 2026; Summer priority March 15, 2027.
