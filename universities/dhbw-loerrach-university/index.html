DHBW Lörrach: Mechatronics, Computer Science, Health & International Business Guide

Duale Hochschule Baden-Württemberg Lörrach (DHBW Lörrach) is a renowned state university situated in the trinational border region of Germany, Switzerland, and France, pioneering the German cooperative education model where students rotate quarterly between university theory and paid corporate work at top engineering multinationals.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
General higher education entrance qualification (Abitur), Fachhochschulreife, or recognized foreign equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on accredited Bachelor of Science and Bachelor of Engineering degrees.
Language proficiency
German C1/DSH-2 for German-taught degrees; English B2/C1 for international corporate partner placements.
Research statement / Proposal
A formal training contract (Ausbildungsvertrag) concluded with an approved DHBW partner company (Dualer Partner).
Application fee
Zero application fee through DHBW or corporate partner.
Required documentation
Certified school diploma, CV, corporate training contract, and health insurance confirmation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free (€0). As dual students employed with corporate partner companies (Dualer Partner), students receive guaranteed monthly employment salaries averaging €1,100 to €1,600 per month throughout their entire three-year degree.
