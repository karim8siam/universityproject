Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (Sveriges lantbruksuniversitet)

Established in 1977 through the merger of Sweden's historic veterinary, forestry, and agricultural colleges, the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (Sveriges lantbruksuniversitet - SLU) is a world-class life sciences research powerhouse. Consistently ranked by QS as #3 in the world in Agriculture and Forestry, SLU develops sustainable solutions for biological natural resources, planetary boundaries, veterinary medicine, and renewable bio-based economies. With campuses in Uppsala, Alnarp, and Umeå, SLU doctoral candidates are employed as salaried academic staff members under Swedish collective agreements with zero tuition fees. Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in biology, environmental science, agronomy, forestry, or related natural science discipline from an internationally recognized university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
University degree at advanced (Master's) level comprising at least 240 ECTS credits (including at least 60 credits at advanced level) with demonstrable scientific methodology training.
Language proficiency
English 6 equivalent: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in written test); required for all non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral positions, an individual study plan (ISP) formulated with the designated principal investigator based on an established research project description.
Application fee
SEK 900 international application fee paid to University Admissions Sweden (antagning.se); SEK 0 application fee for doctoral salaried employment positions.
Required documentation
Certified bachelor's transcripts, degree diploma, curriculum vitae, documentation of relevant field/laboratory experience, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In Sweden, doctoral education is 100% free of tuition fees for all students regardless of citizenship. Furthermore, SLU doctoral candidates are hired on fully funded salaried employment contracts (doktorandanställning) under Swedish collective agreements, receiving a starting salary of approximately SEK 32,000 to SEK 36,000 per month with full healthcare, parental leave, and pension benefits. For Master's students, SLU and Swedish Institute scholarships cover 100% of tuition fees.
