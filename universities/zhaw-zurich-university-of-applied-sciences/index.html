ZHAW Zurich University of Applied Sciences: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

ZHAW Zurich University of Applied Sciences (Zürcher Hochschule für Angewandte Wissenschaften) is one of Switzerland's largest and most technologically advanced public universities of applied sciences, with main campuses in Winterthur, Zurich, and Wädenswil. Renowned for its School of Engineering, Institute of Applied Information Technology (InIT), and Life Sciences and Facility Management, ZHAW conducts extensive applied research directly with Swiss industry giants and Innosuisse.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ZHAW & University of Zurich Joint Doctoral Fellowship in Big Data & Large Language Models

Funded four-year doctoral research post conducted jointly between ZHAW Institute of Applied Information Technology (InIT) and the University of Zurich, building scalable neural question-answering engines over enterprise data lakes.

Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) - Data Science Specialization

Prestigious Swiss Master of Science in Engineering taught in English, combining high-level applied theory with hands-on industrial research in deep learning, cloud architectures, and computer vision.

Joint PhD in Renewable Energy Systems & Power Electronics (ZHAW-ETH Zurich Track)

Doctoral research at the Institute of Energy Systems and Fluid Engineering (IEFE), developing high-efficiency wide-bandgap (SiC/GaN) inverters for solar microgrids in collaboration with ETH Zurich.

Master of Science in Engineering - Information and Cyber Security

Two-year English-taught Master of Science training cyber defense specialists in cloud infrastructure security, penetration testing, malware analysis, and industrial automation protocols.

PhD in Bioprocess Technology and Food Biotechnology (Wädenswil Campus)

Doctoral studentship at the Institute of Chemistry and Biotechnology (ICBT) overlooking Lake Zurich in Wädenswil, developing continuous bioprocessing systems for therapeutic recombinant proteins.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence and Decision Analytics

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the Institute of Data Analysis and Process Design (IDP), advancing algorithmic fairness, explainable machine learning, and ethical scoring models.

ZHAW ICT-Accessibility Lab Graduate Research Internship

Six-month research internship at the ICT-Accessibility Lab in Winterthur, creating multimodal speech and tactile digital interfaces for visually and motor-impaired individuals.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor of Science degree in engineering, computer science, data science, or natural sciences with above-average grades (ECTS grade A or B).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science degree with excellent academic marks; application submitted in response to funded joint PhD positions with partner cantonal or federal research universities.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency at C1 level (IELTS 7.0+, TOEFL iBT 95+, or Cambridge C1 Advanced) for English-taught master's degrees; German C1 for German-taught modules.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose detailing previous laboratory engineering projects, programming experience, and career trajectory.
Application fee
CHF 100 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, detailed diploma supplement/transcript, CV, motivation letter, language certificates, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at ZHAW is CHF 720 per semester for Swiss and EU/EFTA citizens, and CHF 1,220 per semester (approx. USD $1,400) for international students. Full-time graduate research assistants and practice-based doctoral researchers (enrolled in joint PhD programs with ETH Zurich or the University of Zurich) receive salaried Swiss employment contracts earning CHF 50,000 to CHF 65,000 per year.
