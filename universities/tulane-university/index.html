Tulane University

Tulane University · New Orleans, Louisiana, United States

Tulane University is a prestigious private Carnegie R1 research university and a member of the Association of American Universities (AAU), situated along historic St. Charles Avenue in New Orleans, Louisiana. Renowned for the School of Public Health and Tropical Medicine (the oldest in the US), the Tulane School of Medicine, Tulane Law School, and the ByWater Institute, Tulane generates over $220M in annual research expenditures.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Tulane operates ten constituent schools and colleges across uptown and downtown New Orleans. Flagship research centers include the Tulane National Primate Research Center (one of seven federally funded primate centers), the ByWater Institute (river-coastal delta dynamics), the Tulane Cancer Center, and the A.B. Freeman School of Business.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective (test-optional holistic review via Common Application). Graduate and professional programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.3+ GPA, standardized test scores (GRE, MCAT, LSAT, GMAT where required), statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
