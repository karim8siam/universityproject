Minnesota State University, Mankato

Minnesota State Mankato · Mankato, Minnesota, United States

Minnesota State University, Mankato is the second-largest public university in Minnesota and the flagship campus of the Minnesota State system. Celebrated for automotive engineering technology, aviation flight training, civil engineering, and speech-language pathology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Minnesota State Mankato features the innovative Iron Range Engineering model, the North Star Aviation flight school at Mankato Regional Airport, and the Automotive Engineering Technology testing cells. It is a premier regional hub for STEM innovation and healthcare.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 3.0 or top 50% class rank) with test-optional review. Graduate engineering and health science programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA and discipline-specific prerequisites.
