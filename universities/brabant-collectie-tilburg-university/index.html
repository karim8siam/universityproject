Brabant-Collectie Tilburg: Cartography, Religious History & Cultural Heritage Guide

Housed within Tilburg University Library, the Brabant-Collectie is an internationally significant academic heritage research repository, curating over 250,000 historical books, ancient maps, religious manuscripts, and prints documenting the cultural, political, and socio-economic history of the Duchy of Brabant and the Low Countries.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's or Master's degree in history, art history, religious studies, historical geography, cultural heritage, or digital humanities.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral researchers matriculate through Tilburg School of Humanities and Digital Sciences (TSHD); require Master's degree and approved dissertation proposal.
Language proficiency
Dutch and English (reading comprehension of 16th-19th century Dutch and Latin advantageous for primary archive investigations).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research proposal (1,500 words) specifying exact archival collections, maps, or manuscripts to be consulted.
Application fee
Free research visit application for academic scholars.
Required documentation
Curriculum vitae, research proposal, publication list, and one academic supervisor letter of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

The Brabant-Collectie is an academic heritage research library within Tilburg University; visiting research fellowships provide monthly stipends of €1,200 to €2,500. University master's tuition at Tilburg University is €2,530 for EU/EEA and approximately €16,000 for international students. Brabant heritage grants and Prins Bernhard Cultuurfonds bursaries are available.
