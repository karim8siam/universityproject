Colorado School of Mines

Colorado School of Mines is a world-renowned public research university in Golden, Colorado, focused on engineering, applied science, energy resources, and Earth sciences, consistently ranked as the global #1 university for mineral and mining engineering.

Academic overview & excellence

Mines conducts cutting-edge research in sustainable mineral extraction, space mining resources, advanced metallurgical engineering, quantum materials, and renewable geothermal energy, holding extensive research partnerships with the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) and USGS.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions requires top math and science high school credentials (average GPA 3.8+ unweighted). Graduate master's and doctoral admissions require an accredited engineering or STEM bachelor's degree (minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA), statement of purpose, and GRE scores for specific engineering disciplines.
