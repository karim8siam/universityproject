Regis University

Regis University · Denver, Colorado, United States

Regis University is a prominent private Jesuit Catholic university located on a scenic campus in Denver, Colorado. Renowned for its Loretto Heights School of Nursing, the Rueckert-Hartman College for Health Professions, ABET-accredited computer science and cybersecurity, and values-centered business leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Regis features the Rueckert-Hartman College for Health Professions, the Anderson College of Business and Computing, and historic Main Hall with panoramic views of the Rocky Mountains. It is a National Center of Academic Excellence in Information Assurance (CAE-IA) designated by the NSA and DHS.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), coursework rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Rueckert-Hartman health professions and graduate programs in computer science, business, or nursing require relevant undergraduate credentials, minimum 3.0 GPAs, and clinical clearances.
