Adelaide University

Explore comprehensive degree pathways, competitive postgraduate funding, undergraduate entrance reductions, paid summer research scholarships, and world-renowned research institutes including the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) and School of Physical Sciences.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review verified undergraduate and postgraduate opportunities across multiple faculties:

Global Citizens Scholarship (15%–30%)

Automatic tuition fee reduction for commencing international students with high GPA (15% for GPA 5.5/7; 30% for GPA 6.5/7) across all coursework degrees.

Master of Science (Medical Radiation Physics)

Two-year specialized physics degree providing rigorous laboratory and clinical training in radiotherapy, nuclear imaging, and instrumentation.

Summer Research Scholarships (AUD $1,500)

Six-week funded research internship for continuing undergraduate and postgraduate students working inside physical sciences and engineering research teams.

Master of Computer Science (AI & Machine Learning)

Advanced two-year postgraduate degree covering deep learning, computer vision, and autonomous systems developed alongside AIML.

AIML Research Internship & Honours Pathway

Postgraduate research and project placement in Australia's premier artificial intelligence institute at Lot Fourteen.

Master of Engineering (Mechanical & Aerospace)

Engineers Australia-accredited professional master's degree in propulsion, aerodynamic design, and autonomous aerospace systems.

Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA)

Salaried academic tutoring and laboratory demonstrating positions for enrolled postgraduate and doctoral researchers ($54–$158/hr).

PhD and MPhil Research Degrees

Doctor of Philosophy (4 years) and Master of Philosophy (2 years) across STEM and humanities with AURS/RTPS funding.

Admissions and language requirements

Coursework Master's GPA
Typically minimum 4.5 or 5.0 on a 7.0 scale depending on discipline; science and engineering programs require relevant background.
Research degrees (PhD/MPhil)
Research master's or first-class Honours with verified research training and written principal supervisor support.
IELTS Academic
Usually 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 each band); some health and education degrees require 7.0 overall.
GRE / Standardized tests
Not required for Australian university admissions.
Application fee
AUD $150 for international student applications.
