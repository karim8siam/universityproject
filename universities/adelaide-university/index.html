PhD and Master of Philosophy

The PhD is normally four years full-time; the MPhil is normally two years. Applicants need suitable research preparation and available supervision. PhD routes include an appropriate research Master's or qualifying Honours/coursework Master's with substantial research training. For several entry pathways the university specifies weighted average mark 65 (or GPA 5.0/7) overall and 75 (or GPA 6.0/7) in the research component. Do not directly convert a foreign CGPA using these thresholds without the university's assessment.

Research admission can be considered outside a funding round, but scholarships have separate deadlines. International applicants must obtain written support from an eligible principal supervisor before applying. Official PhD admission ↗ · Official MPhil admission ↗

2027 tuition and scholarship rules

The official 2027 PhD international annual tuition bands are AUD 42,600 for many humanities/business areas, AUD 51,600 for physics, computing, engineering and most STEM fields, and AUD 54,800 for medicine, dentistry and veterinary fields. The reviewed MPhil page displays 2026 fees, so confirm its 2027 tuition separately.

Major AURS/RTPS research scholarships can provide a stipend and full tuition waiver to successful eligible applicants. The published 2026 AURS full-time stipend was AUD 36,500 per year; the 2027 figure was not confirmed. The Graduate Research Talent Scheme Round 1 closes 2 November 2026 and accepts domestic applicants plus international applicants who completed or are completing a relevant Australian or New Zealand qualification. Overseas-only qualification holders are not eligible for that particular round.

The China Scholarship Council partnership has a separate university application window from 1 October 2026 to 1 February 2027, restricted to eligible Chinese applicants. Research internship scholarships exist as a category, but no specific current internship vacancy was verified.
