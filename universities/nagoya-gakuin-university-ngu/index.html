Institutional Overview

Nagoya Gakuin University (NGU), tracing its roots back to 1887 with the founding of the Anglo-Japanese School by Methodist missionary Frederick C. Klein, is a prominent private Christian university in central Japan. Operating its flagship Shirotori and Hibino campuses in downtown Nagoya alongside its Seto campus, NGU is noted for its faculties in Commerce, Global Liberal Studies, Rehabilitation Sciences, and Sports Health.
