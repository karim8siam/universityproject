The University of Sydney (USYD)

Founded in 1850 in Camperdown, Sydney, The University of Sydney is Australia's first university and a leading member of the prestigious Group of Eight (Go8). Consistently ranked among the top 20 universities globally in the QS World University Rankings, Sydney attracts over AUD $500 million in annual research funding. The university excels internationally in computer vision, quantum computing, biomedical nanotechnology, and public health. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

University of Sydney International Research Scholarships (USydIS)

The University of Sydney's premier graduate research award offering complete tuition coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $40,109 to exceptional domestic and international PhD candidates each year.

University of Sydney PhD in Computer Science & Engineering

Four-year advanced doctoral research degree conducting world-leading investigations across artificial intelligence, autonomous robotics, cybersecurity, biomedical nanotechnology, and quantum control systems.

University of Sydney Master of Computer Science

Two-year research-oriented Master's degree comprising advanced coursework and a major year-long research thesis project under individual faculty mentorship, serving as a direct pathway to doctoral research.

University of Sydney Master of Data Science

Professional postgraduate master's degree training data scientists in statistical machine learning, visual data analytics, natural language processing, and scalable cloud computing infrastructure.

Sydney Quantum Academy (SQA) Collaborative Research Placements

Interdisciplinary research collaboration hub linking Sydney, UNSW, UTS, and Macquarie, developing quantum hardware, error correction algorithms, and quantum software architectures.

Sydney Engineering Summer Research Internships

Structured 8-to-12-week summer research internship placement embedding students inside world-leading laboratories in robotics, biomedical implants, and telecommunications.

University of Sydney Postgraduate Teaching Demonstratorships

Sessional academic employment enabling graduate students to tutor weekly exercise classes, supervise engineering computer labs, conduct workshops, and grade assessments across undergraduate degree programs.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Australian Bachelor's degree (Honours) or equivalent international qualification with minimum weighted average mark (WAM) of 65%+ (higher 75%+ for quantitative CS/Data Science).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year undergraduate degree with First Class Honours (80%+ / H1) or a Master's degree with a substantial research thesis component (minimum 25% of total credits).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each section) or TOEFL iBT 85 (writing 19, reading/listening/speaking 17); higher thresholds (IELTS 7.0) for professional engineering disciplines.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2 to 3-page research proposal specifying research questions, background literature, methodology, and potential faculty supervisors.
Application fee
AUD $150 application processing fee for international applicants (waived for specific scholarship applicants and partner institutions).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, grading scale guide, curriculum vitae, two academic referee reports, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under the Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) and University of Sydney International Research Scholarships (USydIS), domestic and selected international PhD candidates study completely tuition-free with full fee offsets (saving up to AUD $140,000) plus an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $40,109 for up to 3.5 years.
