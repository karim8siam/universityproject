Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Designated California's third polytechnic university in 2022, Cal Poly Humboldt is located along California's Redwood Coast in Arcata. It is internationally renowned for forestry, wildlife conservation, marine biology (operating the oceanographic research vessel Coral Sea), renewable energy engineering, and Indigenous natural resource management.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Forestry and wildland fire management in the ancient coast redwoods
  • Marine biology and coastal oceanography (Telonicher Marine Lab & R/V Coral Sea)
  • Environmental resources engineering and microgrid renewable power systems
  • Wildlife conservation, fisheries biology, and Indigenous ecological knowledge

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Natural Resources (Forestry, Watershed, & Wildland Sciences) (Graduate Degree) — Premier field research degree examining forest ecosystem dynamics, carbon sequestration, and wildfire resilience.
  • Bachelor of Science in Environmental Resources Engineering (ERE) (Undergraduate Degree) — Nationally acclaimed ABET-accredited engineering degree dedicated entirely to clean water, renewable energy, and air quality systems.
  • Bachelor of Science in Marine Biology (Undergraduate Degree) — Rigorous marine science program utilizing direct oceanographic sampling along the Pacific coast and Humboldt Bay.
