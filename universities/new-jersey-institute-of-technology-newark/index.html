New Jersey Institute of Technology

NJIT · Newark, New Jersey, United States

New Jersey Institute of Technology is a top-tier public Carnegie R1 doctoral research university in Newark, New Jersey. Celebrated worldwide for computing, civil infrastructure, biomedical engineering, data science, and solar physics research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NJIT houses the Newark College of Engineering, Ying Wu College of Computing, and the Big Bear Solar Observatory in California. It conducts over $165M in annual research in smart materials, additive manufacturing, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.6), calculus readiness, and STEM rigor under test-optional review. Graduate degree admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, CS, or physical science with minimum 3.0 GPA and letters of recommendation.
