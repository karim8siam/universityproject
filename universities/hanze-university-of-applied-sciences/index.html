Hanze University of Applied Sciences (Hanzehogeschool Groningen)

Founded in 1798, Hanze University of Applied Sciences (Hanzehogeschool Groningen) is the oldest multi-sector university of applied sciences in the Netherlands. Situated in the vibrant knowledge city of Groningen, Hanze is a northern European leader in applied energy transition research (at the internationally acclaimed EnTranCe Centre of Expertise for Energy Transition), healthy ageing at the Zernike Campus, international business, and digital society solutions. With students from over 100 countries, Hanze delivers rigorous professional master's programs and collaborative applied research fellowships.

Hanze Scholarship for Master's Students & NL Scholarship Programme (€10,000 Award)

Prestigious Dutch government and university-funded scholarship providing financial assistance to talented international students from outside the European Economic Area admitted to Hanze master's programs.

Hanze Master of Science (MSc) in Renewable Energy - European Master in Renewable Energy (EUREC)

Prestigious European EUREC Master's program delivered across partner European institutions, providing hands-on training in solar photovoltaic systems, wind turbine engineering, and grid integration at EnTranCe.

Hanze Master of Science (MSc) in Data Science for Life Sciences (English-Taught)

Two-year applied Master's program bridging big data analytics, machine learning, and biological genomics, preparing graduates for biomedical data science roles in pharmaceutical and biotechnology sectors.

EnTranCe Centre of Expertise for Energy Transition Doctoral / Applied Research Fellowship

Collaborative applied doctoral research at the EnTranCe testing facility on the Zernike Campus, developing regional green hydrogen distribution, biomethane production, and smart hybrid electricity grids.

Hanze Research Centre for Biobased Economy Applied Research Associate

Applied research post investigating the extraction of high-value green chemicals and bio-based composite polymers from regional agricultural by-products and plant proteins.

Hanze Health Hub & Healthy Ageing Summer Practice Traineeship

Ten-week hands-on clinical and technology internship developing wearable sensor technologies and physical exercise interventions to support independent living among geriatric populations.

Hanze Senior Researcher / Lecturer in Hydrogen Systems & Decentralized Smart Grids

Senior academic role teaching in international renewable energy Master's programs and leading applied industry research projects at EnTranCe on hydrogen electrolysis, fuel cell systems, and local energy communities.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits, equivalent to Dutch HBO or WO Bachelor) with good academic standing (minimum 65-70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA) in an appropriate field of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (HBO Master or WO Master) in a relevant technical or scientific discipline; doctoral candidacies are conducted in collaboration with research universities (such as the University of Groningen) through university-of-applied-sciences professorships (lectoren).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no individual sub-score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 21 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
A motivation letter (1,000 words) and portfolio demonstrating professional experience, engineering design projects, or research problem analysis.
Application fee
€0 application fee for EU students; €100 non-refundable handling fee for non-EU/EEA applicants (deducted from tuition upon enrollment).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete transcript of records, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, English test report, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students in Master of Science programs at Hanze is approximately €8,500 to €15,000 per year (statutory tuition of €2,601/year applies to EU/EEA citizens). Outstanding international applicants are eligible for the prestigious NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) providing €5,000 to €10,000 in living support, as well as Hanze Scholarship tuition waivers.
