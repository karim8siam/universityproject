Kyoto Koka Women's University

Master clinical nutrition, registered nursing, and Buddhist humanist healthcare sciences at Kyoto Koka Women's University in Kyoto.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Rooted in the Buddhist ethos of Shinran Shonin ('Compassion and Sincerity'), Kyoto Koka Women's University is a prestigious women's university in western Kyoto celebrated for its top-tier Faculty of Healthcare Sciences, nationally leading registered dietitian training, clinical nursing, and child development psychology.

Institution type
Private Buddhist Women's University (Koka Gakuen Educational Corporation)
Campus location
Kadonocho Campus, Nishikyogoku, Ukyo-ku, Kyoto
Accreditation
Japan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare
Admissions channel
General entrance exams in January/February; international student call in November/December
Instruction language
Japanese (JLPT N2/N1 required for clinical programs)
Financial aid & support
Koka Gakuen Foundation Scholarships, JASSO International Student Honors

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Nation-leading pass rates on the National Exam for Registered Dietitians (Kanri Eiyoshi)
  • Cutting-edge Clinical Simulation Centre for nursing and pediatric healthcare training
  • Grounded in Mahayana Buddhist philosophy emphasizing holistic, compassionate patient care
  • Vibrant student exchange agreements and clinical observation partnerships across Asia and Oceania

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Health and Nutrition (Registered Dietitian Course) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year specialized degree training clinical dietitians in medical nutrition therapy, sports nutrition, and institutional food systems.
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (Department of Nursing) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year comprehensive nursing degree preparing registered nurses and public health nurses for hospital and community clinics.
  • Master of Psychological Sciences (Clinical Psychology Course) (Graduate Degree) — Certified training program preparing licensed clinical psychologists (Rinsho Shinrishi) and certified public psychologists (Konin Shinrishi).
