Alabama A&M University: STEM Degrees, Land-Grant Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Founded in 1875 by former slave Dr. William Hooper Councill, Alabama Agricultural and Mechanical University (AAMU) is an esteemed public historically Black land-grant university located in Normal, adjacent to the aerospace hub of Huntsville, Alabama. Renowned for its ABET-accredited College of Engineering, Technology and Physical Sciences, as well as nationally recognized programs in Agricultural and Environmental Sciences, AAMU prepares scholars for high-impact careers in space exploration, defense systems, cyber operations, and biological innovation.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

AAMU Presidential Academic STEM Merit Scholarship

Prestigious institutional scholarship providing full tuition, room, and board for outstanding incoming freshmen entering STEM disciplines at Alabama A&M University.

Master of Science in Computer Science and Cybersecurity Systems

Two-year graduate program offering rigorous curriculum in algorithms, distributed systems, network defense, artificial intelligence, and cloud software engineering in the Huntsville tech corridor.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Applied Physics and Optics Research Assistantship

Funded doctoral fellowship conducting cutting-edge experimental research in optical sensors, non-linear optics, crystal growth, and space radiation physics partnered with NASA Marshall Space Flight Center.

USDA-NIFA Agricultural Watershed and Soil Carbon Sequestration Investigation

Land-grant experimental research project assessing carbon sequestration mechanisms, cover-crop impacts on soil fertility, and agricultural runoff remediation across Tennessee Valley watersheds.

NASA Marshall Space Flight Center Aerospace Engineering Cooperative Practicum

Prestigious engineering co-op placement embedding students at NASA Marshall Space Flight Center in Huntsville, contributing to propulsion testing, payload systems, and structural mechanics.

Councill Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Plant Genetics and Biotechnology

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting advanced functional genomics, CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing, and stress physiology research in crop species at the Winfred Thomas Agricultural Research Station.

Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering and Embedded Systems

Tenure-track faculty appointment teaching undergraduate and graduate electrical engineering courses, developing an externally funded research laboratory, and advising graduate students.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Earned Bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited institution with minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale in relevant technical or scientific disciplines.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science degree in engineering, physics, plant science, or related quantitative field with minimum graduate GPA of 3.2/4.0 and documented research potential.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS Academic 6.5 minimum score for applicants from non-English speaking backgrounds.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose outlining research objectives, specific faculty labs of interest, and long-term academic or industrial goals.
Application fee
USD $45 non-refundable graduate application fee (USD $20 for undergraduate).
Required documentation
Official university transcripts from all attended institutions, three letters of recommendation, resume/CV, and financial guarantee affidavit for international applicants.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate in-state tuition is approximately USD $5,012 per semester (USD $10,024 per academic year); out-of-state and international undergraduate tuition is approximately USD $9,318 per semester (USD $18,636 annually). Graduate tuition is assessed at approximately USD $422 per credit hour for in-state and USD $844 per credit hour for out-of-state. Full-time graduate research assistants normally receive 100% tuition waivers and monthly stipends.
