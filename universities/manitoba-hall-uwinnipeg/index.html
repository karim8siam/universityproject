Academic overview & excellence

Manitoba Hall serves as the central administrative and pedagogical hub of the University of Winnipeg campus, integrating foundational liberal arts education with cutting-edge digital scholarship. It accommodates diverse departments including English, History, Classics, and Mathematics, providing high-technology seminar spaces, undergraduate research labs, and academic advising suites. Scholars at Manitoba Hall engage with community-partnered research, indigenous knowledge initiatives, and prairie environmental studies, benefiting from Winnipeg's vibrant cultural and scientific network.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to undergraduate and postgraduate programs based at Manitoba Hall is administered by the University of Winnipeg Admissions Office. International applicants typically present a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0/4.0 (or 70% equivalent), an academic English test score (IELTS 6.5 overall with no band under 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 86), official secondary or post-secondary transcripts, and a statement of academic intent. Graduate inquiries require research proposals matching department faculty supervision areas.
