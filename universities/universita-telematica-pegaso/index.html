Università Telematica Pegaso: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established by Ministerial Decree in 2006, Università Telematica Pegaso is headquartered in the historic Palazzo Zapata in Naples, maintaining decentralized examination and learning centres across every Italian region. Serving over 100,000 enrolled students, UniPegaso is officially recognized by the Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR) and ANVUR. The university combines state-of-the-art interactive e-learning platforms with comprehensive research departments in educational technology, sports science, digital civil engineering, and corporate law.
