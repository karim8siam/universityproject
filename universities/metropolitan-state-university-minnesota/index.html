Metropolitan State University

Metro State · St. Paul, Minnesota, United States

Metropolitan State University is a comprehensive public urban university in St. Paul and Minneapolis, Minnesota, and a core member of the Minnesota State system. Renowned for cybersecurity, nursing, urban teacher education, and community-engaged learning.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Metro State is designated as a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD) and operates the Minnesota Cyber Range. Signature schools include the College of Sciences, College of Nursing and Health Sciences, and College of Management.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school performance or transferable college credit under test-optional, student-centered criteria. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 GPA, personal essays, and professional resumes.
