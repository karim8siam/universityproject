Montana Tech · Butte, Montana, United States

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Montana Tech houses the School of Mines and Engineering and the College of Letters, Sciences, and Professional Studies. World-class facilities include the Underground Mine Education Center, Center for Advanced Materials Processing (CAMP), and petroleum recovery test labs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.5) with strong high school calculus and laboratory science coursework. Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering or science with minimum 3.0 GPA, personal statement, and letters of recommendation.
