University of Campania Luigi Vanvitelli: Medicine, Architecture & Green Tech Guide

Established in 1991 as the Second University of Naples, the University of Campania Luigi Vanvitelli (Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli) is a prestigious Italian public research institution distributed across Caserta, Naples, Aversa, and Capua. Celebrated for medicine and surgery, baroque architectural heritage, sustainable fashion design, and aerospace structures, Vanvitelli pairs ancient heritage with European technological innovation.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ADISURC Campania Regional Scholarship for University Studies

Regional government grant providing comprehensive economic support, campus lodging, meal allowances, and complete tuition waivers for domestic and international students.

Single Cycle Master's Degree (MD) in Medicine and Surgery (English Taught)

Six-year European Union accredited medical degree taught entirely in English at the historic Naples polyclinic covering preclinical biomedical sciences, clinical rotations, and surgical training.

MUR Italian Ministerial Doctoral Fellowship in Aerospace Science & Technology

Three-year PhD scholarship funded by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) at the Capua aerospace research hub developing composite structures for micro-satellites.

Reggia di Caserta Baroque Architecture & Landscape Conservation Research

Collaborative research project with the Royal Palace of Caserta (Reggia di Caserta) analyzing historical hydraulic engineering of the Carolino Aqueduct and baroque masonry resilience.

Campania Aerospace Technological District (DAC) Engineering Internship

Six-month industrial research internship in Capua and Pomigliano d'Arco developing avionics telemetry and computational aerodynamics simulations with Leonardo and DAC partner firms.

Vanvitelli Postdoctoral Research Fellowship (Assegno di Ricerca) in Oncological Pathology

One-year renewable research fellowship (Assegno di Ricerca) at the Department of Precision Medicine investigating microRNA signatures and biomarker profiles in colorectal carcinomas.

UniCampania International Student Merit Fee Waiver and Scholarship

Merit-based international scholarship providing complete tuition exemptions to high-ranking foreign students admitted to English- or Italian-taught master's degrees at Vanvitelli.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in a related discipline; evaluation of university transcripts and curricular prerequisites.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with verified dissertation; admission awarded via competitive Italian national public competition (concorso pubblico).
Language proficiency
B2 certification in Italian for Italian-taught tracks; B2/C1 English (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 85) for English-taught degrees (e.g. Medicine and Surgery, Data Science).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral concorso: detailed 3,000-word research proposal outlining research methodology, state of the art, and alignment with Vanvitelli department laboratories.
Application fee
EUR 30 to EUR 50 administrative concorso competition fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma with CIMEA Statement of Comparability or Declaration of Value, official transcripts, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is determined based on the family financial indicator (ISEE parificato for international students). For eligible students with ISEE under EUR 22,000, tuition is EUR 0. Maximum annual tuition is approximately EUR 2,400. Regional ADISURC scholarships offer full tuition waivers, free student housing, cafeteria meals, and annual living grants up to EUR 7,000.
