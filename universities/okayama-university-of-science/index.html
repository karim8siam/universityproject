Okayama University of Science

Okayama University of Science (岡山理科大学 - OUS), founded by Kake Educational Institution on Mount Ridai in Okayama, is Western Japan's premier private science and technology university, featuring the renowned Faculty of Veterinary Medicine and Dinosaur Research Institute.

Academic overview & excellence

OUS operates prominent research institutes in paleontology, closed-circulation land-based aquaculture, aerospace robotics, and veterinary biosciences. Its Imabari Campus hosts one of Japan's newest and most technologically advanced veterinary teaching hospitals.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires 12 years of formal education or secondary school graduation. International applicants take the EJU examination or submit JLPT scores (N2/N1). Graduate science and engineering candidates submit academic transcripts, research plans, and attend an oral interview.
