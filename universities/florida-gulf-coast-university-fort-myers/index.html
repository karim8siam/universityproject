Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1991 with an environmental ethos embedded into its founding charter, FGCU features a 400-acre nature preserve on campus and leads cutting-edge research through the Water School—a premier institute investigating red tide algal blooms, Everglades restoration, and coastal resilience. Its U.A. Whitaker College of Engineering and Lutgert College of Business drive innovation across Southwest Florida.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants require an accredited secondary school diploma with competitive GPA (average 3.7–4.3 weighted), standardized test scores (SAT or ACT), and official transcripts. Master's and doctoral programs in environmental science, bioengineering, and health sciences require a recognized bachelor's degree in a related field, research proposal or letter of intent, and letters of recommendation.
