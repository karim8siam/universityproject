Saint Louis University

SLU · St. Louis, Missouri, United States

Saint Louis University is a world-class Jesuit research university (R1) founded in 1818, making it the oldest university west of the Mississippi River, located in midtown St. Louis, Missouri. Internationally acclaimed for the School of Medicine, Saint Louis University School of Law, Parks College of Engineering, Aviation and Technology (the first federally certified school of aviation in the US), and health law.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SLU features the SLUCare Physician Group medical center, the Doisy Research Center, the Center for Vaccine Development, Parks College, and the Chaifetz School of Business. It is consistently ranked among the top 5 programs nationwide for health law by U.S. News.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.9), rigorous coursework, and optional test scores (test-optional). School of Medicine (MD) applicants submit AMCAS applications, MCAT scores, and prerequisite science labs. Graduate programs require accredited bachelor's degrees with minimum 3.0 GPAs.
