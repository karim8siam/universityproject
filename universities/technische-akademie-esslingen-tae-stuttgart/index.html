TAE Esslingen · Ostfildern, Baden-Württemberg, Germany

Technische Akademie Esslingen (TAE) in Ostfildern/Stuttgart is one of Germany’s premier institutions for advanced technical higher education and engineering degree partnerships.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Partnering with state universities and industrial leaders across Baden-Württemberg, TAE provides accredited bachelor’s and master’s engineering degrees in mechatronics, automotive electronics, medical technology, and industrial tribology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires an accredited vocational qualification or university entrance certificate (Abitur/Fachhochschulreife), relevant industrial experience, and completed enrollment agreements.
