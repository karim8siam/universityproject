Macromedia University of Applied Sciences

Hochschule Macromedia (Macromedia University of Applied Sciences) is a leading German private university specializing in media, design, digital management, brand communication, and the creative arts. With major campus hubs in Munich, Berlin, Cologne, Hamburg, and Stuttgart, Macromedia delivers globally accredited English and German undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Institutional Overview

Accredited by the German Council of Science and Humanities (Wissenschaftsrat) and FIBAA, Macromedia partners directly with Fortune 500 multinationals, gaming studios, and global creative agencies. Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Macromedia admits students for winter (October) and summer (March) semesters. Applicants submit high school certificates or bachelor's degrees, a digital portfolio for design programs, and English proficiency (IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 72) for English-taught tracks. Merit-based scholarships covering up to 30% of tuition are awarded to outstanding creative applicants.
