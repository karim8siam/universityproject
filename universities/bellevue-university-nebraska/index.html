Bellevue University: NSA Center of Cyber Excellence, Healthcare & MBA Guide

Bellevue University in Bellevue, Nebraska, is an accredited private university recognized by the NSA and DHS as a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense, renowned for career-aligned online and on-campus bachelor's, master's, and doctoral (PhD in Human Capital Management) degrees.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.5 cumulative GPA; professional work experience evaluated for executive master's tracks.
Doctoral admission eligibility
PhD in Human Capital Management requires a Master's degree, strong managerial track record, and a structured dissertation research statement.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 71 or IELTS 6.0 for international applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Personal statement detailing career objectives and how the degree program facilitates professional advancement.
Application fee
$50 application processing fee (frequently waived online).
Required documentation
Official college transcripts, resume/CV, personal statement, and two professional or academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition averages $350 to $440 per credit hour. Graduate tuition ranges from $615 to $660 per credit hour. Bellevue University Kirkpatrick Signature scholarships, military service grants, and corporate partner tuition discounts are widely available.
