Campus Nynäshamn: Marine Engineering, Sustainable Tourism & Vocational Excellence

Campus Nynäshamn is an innovative Swedish municipal higher education and vocational college situated in the Stockholm Archipelago, partnering with KTH Royal Institute of Technology and Stockholm University to offer accredited qualifications in coastal marine technology, eco-tourism, and sustainable port logistics.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Swedish State CSN Higher Education Grant & Study Loan (Studiemedel)

Comprehensive Swedish state financial aid package providing monthly non-repayable grants and low-interest student loans for students enrolled in accredited studies at Campus Nynäshamn.

Higher Vocational Diploma (YH-Examen) in Coastal Marine Engineering & Boat Systems

Two-year 400 YH-point engineering qualification focusing on electric boat propulsion, marine battery systems, composite hull repair, and port shore power installations.

Industrial Ph.D. in Baltic Sea Coastal Electrification & Green Shipping Corridors

Four-year cooperative industrial doctoral project operated alongside KTH Royal Institute of Technology, evaluating mega-watt charging hubs for electric ferries in the Port of Nynäshamn.

Stockholm Archipelago Marine Biodiversity & Seagrass Meadow Restoration Study

Applied marine ecology study tracking eelgrass (Zostera marina) restoration, water clarity improvement, and coastal fish nursery protection in shallow bays around Nynäshamn.

Port of Nynäshamn & Baltic Ferry Logistics Curricular Work Placement (LIA)

Twenty-week practical internship embedded at ferry terminals and maritime logistics companies in Nynäshamn coordinating roll-on/roll-off cargo, customs clearance, and cold-ironing shore power.

Archipelago Sustainable Tourism & Eco-Enterprise Fellowship

Twelve-month applied fellowship examining circular tourism business models, off-grid island energy systems, and sustainable visitor management across southern Stockholm archipelago.

Senior Lecturer in Marine Propulsion & High-Voltage Electrical Systems

Permanent senior faculty position leading laboratory instruction in electric boat powertrains, battery management systems, and safety compliance for marine vessels.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in Engineering, Marine Science, or Environmental Planning through partner university affiliations.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Industrial doctoral research placements operated in partnership with KTH Royal Institute of Technology or Stockholm University.
Language proficiency
Swedish B / Svenska 3 for Swedish vocational courses; English 6 (IELTS 6.5) for university partner degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, verified grade transcripts, and motivation letter detailing interest in marine sustainability.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the Swedish Higher Vocational Education portal (YH) or SEK 900 via Antagning.se for university degrees.
Required documentation
Certified high school or university transcripts, proof of prerequisite coursework, and identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. Higher vocational programs (Yrkeshögskolan) are state-financed and tuition-free for all admitted residents. Non-EU international university partner courses range from SEK 115,000 to 150,000 annually.
