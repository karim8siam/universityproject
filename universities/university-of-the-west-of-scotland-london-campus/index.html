University of the West of Scotland (London Campus)

Study career-accelerating MBA, project management, and global health master's degrees at UWS London in the Docklands financial district.

Institution Overview & Profile

University of the West of Scotland - London Campus, situated in the historic Export Building at Republic in the London Docklands, is a state-of-the-art urban campus delivering career-focused postgraduate and undergraduate degrees in business, international healthcare management, project management, and information technology in the UK's financial capital.

Institution TypePublic University Branch Campus (University of the West of Scotland)
Main CampusThe Export Building, Republic, 1 Clove Crescent, London Docklands
AccreditationPrivy Council / Quality Assurance Agency for Higher Education (QAA) / CMI
Admissions RouteUWS London Direct Portal & UCAS (intakes in Jan, May, and Sept)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS Academic 6.0 with no sub-score below 5.5 for UG; 6.5 for PG)
Financial Aid & GrantsUWS International Excellence Scholarships (£2,000–£3,500) & UK Postgraduate Loans

Key Academic Strengths

  • Modern innovative campus located inside the tech and creative hub of Republic in London Docklands
  • Flexible three intakes per year (September, January, and May) facilitating rapid career progression
  • Degrees accredited by professional bodies including the Chartered Management Institute (CMI)
  • Direct connections with City of London corporate headquarters, financial institutions, and the NHS
