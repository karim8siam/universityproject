Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1789, the University of King's College is the oldest chartered university in Canada. Renowned for its world-class Foundation Year Programme (FYP) and top-tier School of Journalism, King's offers a rich collegial life in association with Dalhousie University.

Institution type
Public Collegial Liberal Arts & Journalism University
Campus location
Coburg Road Quadrangle, Central Halifax (adjacent to Dalhousie)
Accreditation

English (IELTS 7.0 for Journalism / 6.5 for Arts)
Financial aid & support
Major Entrance Scholarships, King's Journalism Bursaries, Nova Scotia Aid

Institutional Research Strengths

  • The Foundation Year Programme (FYP)—celebrated great books curriculum
  • Master of Journalism (MJ) in investigative and data-driven reporting
  • Full academic and athletic integration with Dalhousie University
  • Intimate residential collegiate environment with vibrant choral and literary traditions

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Foundation Year Programme (FYP) (First-Year Interdisciplinary Degree) — Immersion in great texts from antiquity to the contemporary world, blending philosophy, literature, and political thought.
  • Master of Journalism (MJ) (Graduate Degree) — Professional graduate degree focusing on investigative reporting, open-source intelligence, data visualization, and broadcast media.
  • Master of Fine Arts in Creative Nonfiction (MFA) (Graduate Degree) — Low-residency professional writing program mentoring authors in publishing narrative nonfiction and memoir.
