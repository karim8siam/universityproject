Boston College: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1863 and set upon an iconic collegiate Gothic campus six miles west of downtown Boston, Boston College is classified among the R1: Doctoral Universities – Very High Research Activity. Boston College comprises eight premier schools—including the Carroll School of Management, the Lynch School of Education and Human Development, Boston College Law School, and the Morrissey College of Arts and Sciences—fostering rigorous scientific inquiry grounded in ethical reflection and humanistic values.
