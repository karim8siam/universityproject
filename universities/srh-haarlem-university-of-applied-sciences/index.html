SRH Haarlem: Digital Transformation, Creative Media, Business & Applied AI Guide

SRH Haarlem University of Applied Sciences (Haarlem Campus) is a vibrant international university situated inside the iconic, renovated 'Koepel' monument in Haarlem (minutes from Amsterdam), delivering state-of-the-art bachelor's and master's programs in Digital Transformation, Business Psychology, Creative Media, and Applied Artificial Intelligence.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in business, computer science, psychology, or communications from an accredited higher education institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent PhD degrees; focus on hands-on professional Master of Science degrees.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-skill) or TOEFL iBT 88 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
A 500-800 word personal motivation letter explaining choice of degree and future aspirations in digital transformation or creative enterprise.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the SRH Haarlem direct online admissions portal.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, official transcripts, CV, motivation letter, and valid passport identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international graduate students averages €10,500 to €13,000 per academic year. Early-bird tuition fee reductions of up to €1,500, innovation merit scholarships, and flexible installment plans are available for qualified applicants.
