Westphalian Technical College

Westphalian Technical College (Westfälische Hochschule Gelsenkirchen, Bocholt, Recklinghausen) is a premier university of applied sciences renowned for mechanical engineering, internet security, medical technology, and applied life sciences.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating campuses across Gelsenkirchen, Bocholt, and Recklinghausen, Westfälische Hochschule hosts cutting-edge research centers including the Institute for Internet Security (if(is)), the Laboratory for Renewable Energies, and the Center for Robotics and Mechatronic Systems.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require a German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or recognized foreign equivalent via uni-assist. German-taught curricula require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4; English tracks require TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5.
