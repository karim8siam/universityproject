Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Nestled in the green heart of Germany in the Thuringian Forest, Hochschule Schmalkalden is an internationally focused public university of applied sciences renowned for mechanical engineering, computer science, electrical engineering, business administration, and business law.

Institution type
Public University of Applied Sciences (Fachhochschule)
Campus location
Main Campus, Schmalkalden, Thuringia
Accreditation
Thuringian Ministry of Economy, Science and Digital Society / ACQUIN
Admissions channel
Department of Student Affairs (Dezernat für studentische Angelegenheiten)
Instruction language
German and English (multiple English-taught graduate programs)
Financial aid & support
Zero tuition fees, Deutschlandstipendium, and Erasmus+ mobility grants

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Precision tool manufacturing, forming technologies, and plastics engineering
  • Applied computer science, machine learning, and cybersecurity
  • Business law, corporate restructuring, and international commercial law
  • Electrical engineering, embedded sensors, and renewable power electronics

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Applied Computer Science (English Track) (Graduate Degree) — Four-semester international master's degree focusing on software development, mobile computing, and intelligent systems.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering & Toolmaking (Undergraduate Degree) — Practical engineering degree embedded in Thuringia's world-famous precision tool and manufacturing cluster.
  • Master of Laws in Corporate Restructuring & Insolvency Law (LL.M.) (Graduate Degree) — Specialized legal program examining corporate reorganization, bankruptcy proceedings, and turnaround finance.
