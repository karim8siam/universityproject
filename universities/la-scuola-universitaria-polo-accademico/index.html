Academic overview & excellence

Situated in the renowned academic city of Padua, La Scuola Universitaria bridges traditional classroom pedagogy with innovative digital learning infrastructures. The institution offers comprehensive support for undergraduate and graduate scholars, housing state-of-the-art computer labs, psychology observation rooms, legal clinic workshops, and engineering modeling suites. Research initiatives center on human-computer interaction, organizational psychology in Veneto manufacturing clusters, and European corporate sustainability.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to degree tracks administered by La Scuola Universitaria is conducted according to Italian ministerial guidelines. Candidates for Laurea Triennale courses must hold a high school diploma (Maturità) or equivalent international secondary certification. Candidates for Laurea Magistrale must hold an accredited Bachelor's degree with compatible curricular credits (CFU). Non-Italian applicants must submit a Declaration of Value and demonstrate Italian B2 proficiency.
