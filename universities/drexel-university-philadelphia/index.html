Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1891 by financier Anthony J. Drexel, the university is anchored in University City, Philadelphia. Drexel is an innovation powerhouse pioneered by its world-class cooperative education program (placing students with over 1,600 multinational employers) and high-impact R1 research institutes, including the A.J. Drexel Nanomaterials Institute (MXenes discovery), School of Biomedical Engineering, and College of Computing & Informatics.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applications submit through the Common Application or Coalition Application with transcripts, counselor recommendation, and essays. Standardized tests (SAT/ACT) are test-optional for most programs. Graduate master's and PhD programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in a congruent discipline (minimum GPA 3.0-3.3), GRE scores (program-dependent), letters of recommendation, and research statement. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 90+ or IELTS 6.5-7.0.
