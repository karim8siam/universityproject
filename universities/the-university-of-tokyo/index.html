The University of Tokyo (東京大学 - Todai)

Established in 1877 as Japan's first imperial university, The University of Tokyo (Todai) is consistently ranked as Japan's #1 university and among the top institutions across Asia. Todai has produced 17 Prime Ministers of Japan, 16 Nobel laureates, and 3 Fields Medalists. Across its Hongo, Komaba, and Kashiwa campuses, Todai conducts frontier research in physics, computer science, robotics, and biomedical chemistry. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

University of Tokyo Fellowship (Todai Fellowship)

The University of Tokyo's premier international doctoral fellowship, providing full tuition and admission fee exemptions plus ¥200,000 per month to outstanding privately financed international PhD students.

Todai PhD in Information Science and Technology

Three-year doctoral curriculum inside the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology at Hongo campus, offering world-class research in computer science, mathematical informatics, and intelligent systems.

Todai Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year English-medium research Master's degree inside the Department of Computer Science, focusing on theoretical foundations, programming languages, computer systems, and machine learning.

Todai Global Science Graduate Course (GSGC) in Physics

Integrated five-year Master's and doctoral program in the Graduate School of Science, conducted entirely in English and offering monthly living stipends of ¥180,000 and complete tuition fee exemptions.

Todai Institute for Cosmic Ray Research (ICRR) Collaborative Placements

World-leading experimental research placements inside Todai ICRR in Kashiwa, participating in neutrino oscillation experiments at Super-Kamiokande, gravitational wave detection at KAGRA, and high-energy gamma-ray astronomy.

University of Tokyo Amgen Scholars Summer Research Program

Prestigious eight-week summer research program (June–August) offering undergraduate scholars from around the world direct laboratory research apprenticeships under Todai faculty in molecular biology, chemistry, and bioengineering.

Todai Research Assistantships (RA) & Teaching Assistantships (TA)

University employment opportunities for enrolled Master's and PhD students providing financial assistance for assisting in seminar instruction, computer lab supervision, and experimental research projects.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education culminating in an accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent 4-year undergraduate diploma) in a relevant technical field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree or professional degree with demonstrated research accomplishments, publications, or Master's thesis of superior academic merit.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 90+ for English-language graduate degree tracks (e.g., GSGC, IME, IPADS).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 3-page research proposal (研究計画書) outlining the intended academic research theme, methodology, and identified faculty supervisor.
Application fee
¥10,000 to ¥30,000 application examination fee depending on graduate school and international applicant category.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, official university academic transcripts with grading rubric, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and passport scan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Japanese national university regulations, annual tuition is standard across all faculties at ¥535,800 per year (approx. USD $3,600), plus a one-time matriculation fee of ¥282,000. Recipient scholars of the University of Tokyo Fellowship (Todai Fellowship) or Japanese Government MEXT scholarship receive 100% full tuition exemption plus monthly living allowances of ¥200,000.
