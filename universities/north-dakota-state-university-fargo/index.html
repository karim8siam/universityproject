North Dakota State University

NDSU · Fargo, North Dakota, United States

North Dakota State University is a premier public land-grant Carnegie R1 doctoral research university in Fargo, North Dakota. World-renowned for agricultural science, coatings and polymeric materials, pharmacy, precision agriculture, and civil engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NDSU comprises the College of Agriculture, Food Systems, and Natural Resources, College of Engineering, and the Department of Coatings and Polymeric Materials. It operates the Northern Crops Institute and over 18,000 acres of agricultural research land.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.75 automatic admission) with strong math and science coursework. Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
