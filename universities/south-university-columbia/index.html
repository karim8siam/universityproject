South University, Columbia

South University Columbia · Columbia, South Carolina, United States

South University, Columbia is a private institution located in South Carolina's capital city, dedicated to career-focused education in healthcare, business, and behavioral sciences. It is widely recognized across the Midlands for its flagship Master of Science in Physician Assistant Studies program, accredited nursing curriculum, and modern physical therapy assistant training facilities.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

South University, Columbia features modern clinical examination labs, surgical simulation suites, high-fidelity anatomical models, and smart lecture theaters. It collaborates closely with Prisma Health, Lexington Medical Center, and regional healthcare providers to deliver rigorous clinical rotations and didactic healthcare instruction.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate programs require high school graduation or GED equivalent with entrance placement assessments. The Master of Science in Physician Assistant (PA) program is highly selective, requiring an accredited bachelor's degree, prerequisite natural sciences with lab coursework, competitive GRE scores, clinical patient care hours, and CASPA submission.
