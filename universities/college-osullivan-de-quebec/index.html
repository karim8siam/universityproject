Institution Overview & Profile

Collège O'Sullivan de Québec, located in historic Quebec City, is a recognized private college (établissement collégial privé subventionné) providing pre-university and technical DCS (DEC) and ACS (AEC) diplomas in computer programming, 3D animation, web development, cybersecurity, and paralegal technology.

Institution TypePrivate Subsidized Post-Secondary College (Collège d'enseignement professionnel)
Main CampusRue Saint-Jean Campus, Downtown Quebec City
AccreditationMinistère de l'Enseignement supérieur du Québec (MES)
Admissions RouteSRAM application portal and direct college admissions (Fall / Winter)
Language of InstructionFrench (B2/C1 TFI, DELF, or TEF) with select bilingual tracks
Financial Aid & GrantsQuebec Loans and Bursaries Program (AFE) & O'Sullivan Foundation Scholarships

Key Academic Strengths

  • DEC programs fully recognized by Quebec universities for advanced credit transfer towards bachelor degrees
  • Specialized computer laboratories equipped with high-performance rendering and cybersecurity simulation pods
  • Direct cooperative internships with Quebec City's thriving video game development and IT cluster
  • Small seminar classes guaranteeing close pedagogical mentorship by active industry practitioners

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Diplôme d'études collégiales (DEC)

DEC in Computer Science - Web & Mobile Development

Three-year technical collegiate program covering full-stack software development, relational databases, mobile architectures, and cloud services.

Diplôme d'études collégiales (DEC)

DEC in 3D Animation & Digital Imagery

Production pipeline training in 3D character design, texturing, digital lighting, compositing, and real-time game engine integration.

Attestation d'études collégiales (AEC)

AEC in Network Administration & Cybersecurity

Intensive training for professionals in firewalls, intrusion detection systems, enterprise server architecture, and security policies.
