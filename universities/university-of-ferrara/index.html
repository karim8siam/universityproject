University of Ferrara: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Ferrara (Università degli Studi di Ferrara, UniFe), founded in 1391, is one of Italy's oldest and most prestigious universities—famously where Nicolaus Copernicus received his doctorate in canon law in 1503. Located in the UNESCO World Heritage Renaissance city of Ferrara, UniFe excels in pharmaceutical discovery, astrophysics, digital architectural restoration, and green mechanical engineering.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

PhD in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences (Drug Discovery & Nanocarrier Targeted Delivery)

Three-year doctoral curriculum researching synthetic peptide conjugates, lipid-based nanoparticle formulations for central nervous system crossing, and green catalytic synthesis.

Laurea Magistrale (M.Sc.) in Physics (Astrophysics, Cosmology & Nuclear Physics)

Two-year master's program closely connected to INFN (National Institute for Nuclear Physics), exploring cosmic microwave background polarization, dark matter detection, and high-energy particle astrophysics.

UniFe International Student Merit Fee Waiver and ER.GO Regional Accommodation Grant

Competitive scholarship package funded by the Emilia-Romagna regional authority providing complete financial backing, dormitory placement, and tuition relief for foreign students.

Center for Neurosciences and Cognitive Rehabilitation Research Assistantship

One-to-two year research contract at UniFe's Department of Neuroscience investigating neural correlates of motor control, robotic exoskeletons for stroke rehabilitation, and transcranial stimulation.

Ferrara Renaissance Architectural Conservation & Historic Heritage Restoration Studio Practicum

Practical five-month architectural conservation internship utilizing 3D laser scanning, photogrammetry, and seismic vulnerability assessments on Renaissance palazzi and UNESCO masonry structures.

Copernicus Senior Postdoctoral Fellowship in High-Performance Scientific Computing & Fluid Dynamics

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship developing parallel finite element solvers and lattice Boltzmann models for turbulent flows, run on the Leonardo Tier-0 supercomputer at nearby CINECA.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Mechanical and Advanced Materials Engineering for Green Mobility

Specialized engineering master's degree connected to the Italian Motor Valley (Ferrari, Lamborghini, Ducati), focusing on lightweight composite materials, EV battery pack cooling, and electric vehicle acoustics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A 3-year bachelor's degree (Laurea) or recognized foreign equivalent with at least 180 ECTS credits in a coherent academic discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) or foreign second-cycle degree; competitive evaluation of research proposal, CV, and oral academic interview.
Language proficiency
Italian-taught courses: B2 Italian certification. English-taught degrees: B2 English certification (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 78 minimum).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2,500-word research proposal outlining scientific rationale, methodology, state of the art, and anticipated doctoral impact.
Application fee
€50 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Undergraduate diploma, full transcript of records with CIMEA Statement of Comparability, curriculum vitae, passport copy, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Ferrara is strictly income-based, ranging from €0 to €2,800 per year based on family ISEE parificato (equivalent economic situation index). Students with an ISEE below €24,000 pay zero tuition fees and are eligible for ER.GO regional board scholarships granting up to €6,800 annually plus free housing and dining vouchers. Admitted PhD candidates receive guaranteed ministerial scholarships of €16,243 per year plus 50% funding supplements during approved research stays abroad.
