Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

The Labrador Institute (now the Labrador Campus of Memorial University) is a premier northern-led research and education centre dedicated to Indigenous Arctic sovereignty, circumpolar ecosystems, climate adaptation, and northern community health.

Institution type
Public Northern Research Campus / Memorial University
Campus location
Happy Valley-Goose Bay & North West River, Labrador
Accreditation
Universities Canada / Government of Newfoundland & Labrador
Admissions channel
Memorial University School of Graduate Studies
Instruction language
English (with Inuttitut and Innu-aimun linguistic integrations)
Financial aid & support
Northern Scientific Training Program (NSTP) & MUN Graduate Fellowships

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Circumpolar Arctic ecology and permafrost dynamics
  • Inuit and Innu community health governance and traditional knowledge systems
  • Sustainable northern food systems and boreal agriculture
  • Climate change vulnerability and Subarctic coastal monitoring

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Arctic and Subarctic Studies (MASS) (Graduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary northern-led curriculum focused on Indigenous place-based leadership, resource governance, and circumpolar policy.
  • Doctor of Philosophy in Interdisciplinary Northern Studies (Doctoral Degree) — Research doctorate examining climate adaptation, northern environmental change, and community-engaged knowledge co-production.
  • Bachelor of Science in Northern Environmental Science (Undergraduate Degree) — Field-intensive program investigating subarctic hydrology, boreal forest resilience, and wildlife management.
