Oklahoma Wesleyan University

OKWU · Bartlesville, Oklahoma, United States

Oklahoma Wesleyan University is an evangelical Christian liberal arts university located in Bartlesville, Oklahoma. Known for excellence in registered nursing, business administration, criminal justice, exercise science, and digital marketing.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OKWU's 35-acre campus features the Lyon Hall of Science, the Drake Library, and the Chesapeake Energy School of Business. It is committed to integrating biblical worldview with professional mastery and practical community service.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with a minimum 2.0 GPA. Test-optional admission is available for students with a 2.5+ GPA. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0 GPA.
