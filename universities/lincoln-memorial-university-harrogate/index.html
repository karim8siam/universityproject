Lincoln Memorial University

Lincoln Memorial Univ · Harrogate, Tennessee, United States

Lincoln Memorial University is a private university in Harrogate, Tennessee, situated at the historic Cumberland Gap. LMU is an Appalachian healthcare powerhouse, operating the DeBusk College of Osteopathic Medicine, College of Veterinary Medicine, and Duncan School of Law.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

LMU comprises the DeBusk College of Osteopathic Medicine (DCOM), College of Veterinary Medicine (CVM), Caylor School of Nursing, and Abraham Lincoln Library and Museum. It is a regional pioneer in rural medicine, veterinary surgical training, and primary healthcare delivery.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.75) under test-optional policies. DO and DVM programs require competitive applications through AACOMAS / VMCAS, prerequisite science coursework, clinical shadowing, and formal admissions interviews.
