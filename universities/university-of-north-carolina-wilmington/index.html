University of North Carolina Wilmington

UNC Wilmington · Wilmington, North Carolina, United States

The University of North Carolina Wilmington (UNCW), established in 1947, is North Carolina's premier coastal university, situated between the Cape Fear River and the Atlantic Ocean. Nationally celebrated for its world-class marine biology, oceanography, and coastal ecology research centered at the waterfront Center for Marine Science (CMS), UNCW is also recognized for nursing, computer science, and environmental studies.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNCW comprises the College of Science and Engineering, College of Health and Human Services, Cameron School of Business, College of Humanities, Social Sciences, and the Arts, and Watson College of Education. Its 5-acre waterfront Center for Marine Science operates research vessels, shellfish hatcheries, and oceanographic instrumentation labs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate applicants apply via the Common Application (average GPA 4.0 weighted; test-optional). Graduate and doctoral applicants require a baccalaureate degree from an accredited institution with a minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and a statement of academic objectives.
