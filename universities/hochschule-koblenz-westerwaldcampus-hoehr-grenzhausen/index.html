Hochschule Koblenz - WesterwaldCampus Höhr-Grenzhausen

The WesterwaldCampus of Hochschule Koblenz in Höhr-Grenzhausen is Europe's world-renowned academic center for advanced ceramics engineering, refractory materials science, and artistic ceramic design.

Academic overview & excellence

Located in the historic Westerwald ceramic hub near Koblenz, this specialized campus houses the Department of Materials Engineering, Glass and Ceramics alongside the Institute for Ceramic Art and Design (IKKG). In close collaboration with the European Ceramic Technology Center (FGK), the campus pioneers research into high-temperature thermal barrier coatings, biocompatible dental ceramics, solid-oxide fuel cell electrolytes, and monumental sculptural porcelain.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate programs require the German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or international equivalent recognized via uni-assist. Master of Engineering candidates must possess a bachelor's degree in materials science, chemistry, physics, or mechanical engineering with a minimum grade of 2.5. Design programs require submission of an artistic portfolio and successful completion of an aptitude examination. Instruction is primarily in German (C1) with selected English modules.

Seven-semester accredited engineering degree exploring powder metallurgy, sintering kinetics, silicate glass technology, and high-performance technical ceramics.

Master of Engineering in Ceramic Science and Advanced Materials

Three-semester advanced degree covering fracture mechanics of brittle materials, 3D additive manufacturing of technical ceramics, and thermoelectric energy converters.

Funding: Research assistantships (HiWi) funded by the FGK Ceramic Research Institute Tuition: €0 tuition fees Deadline: January 15, 2027
Master's

Master of Fine Arts in Ceramic Art and Experimental Glass (IKKG)

Four-semester studio MFA program at the Institute for Ceramic Art and Design, investigating experimental kiln firing, sculptural clay installations, and architectural glass casting.

Funding: Rhineland-Palatinate Graduate Art Fellowship providing €8,000 project funding Tuition: €0 tuition fees Deadline: May 15, 2027
PhD

Doctoral Research Scholarship in Solid-State Electrolytes for Ceramic Batteries

Three-year doctoral thesis in joint supervision with TU Darmstadt investigating lithium-conducting garnet-type oxide ceramics and solid-state electrolyte interfaces.

Funding: Full TV-L E13 research salary (€48,000 gross annually) funded through Federal BMBF projects Tuition: €0 tuition fee Deadline: April 30, 2027
Internship

European Ceramic Technology Center (FGK) Industrial R&D Internship

Five-month industrial internship utilizing scanning electron microscopy (SEM), X-ray diffraction (XRD), and high-temperature dilatometry to characterize refractory composites.

Funding: Paid 20-week research work term at €1,200 monthly plus laboratory supplies allowance Tuition: €0 placement fee Deadline: June 1, 2027
Scholarship

Deutschlandstipendium in Refractory Engineering and Sustainable Kiln Technologies

Merit-based national scholarship supported by the German Refractories Association (VDFFI) recognizing top engineering scholars developing hydrogen-fired ceramic kilns.

Funding: €3,600 annual merit award (€300 monthly for 12 months) co-funded by ceramic industry sponsors Tuition: Tuition is free Deadline: August 15, 2027
Course

Four-week hands-on professional certificate in lithography-based ceramic manufacturing (LCM), binder jetting, and thermal debinding protocols.

Funding: Federal qualification funding (Qualifizierungschancengesetz) eligible Tuition: €1,600 total course fee Deadline: September 30, 2027
