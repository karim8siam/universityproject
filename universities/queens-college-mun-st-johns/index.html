Queen's College (MUN)

Queen's College is an historic ecclesiastical and academic institution affiliated with Memorial University of Newfoundland (MUN) in St. John's. Founded in 1841 by Bishop Aubrey Spencer, Queen's College delivers graduate theology degrees, pastoral care certifications, and interdisciplinary philosophical research rooted in Atlantic community traditions.

Official institutional website ↗Location: St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada

Institutional Overview

Operating on the Prince Philip Drive campus of Memorial University, Queen's College offers Bachelor of Theology, Master of Theological Studies, Master of Divinity, and Doctor of Ministry degrees. The college combines rigorous textual exegesis, historical theology, and bioethics with applied supervised pastoral education in provincial hospitals and parish networks.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions to Queen's College are open to candidates of all backgrounds seeking academic theology, pastoral chaplaincy, or ethics qualifications. Graduate applicants require a recognized undergraduate degree (min 2.75 GPA on a 4.0 scale), academic transcripts, references, and a statement of academic intent. Bursaries and tuition endowments are available to theological scholars.
