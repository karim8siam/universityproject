Avans University of Applied Sciences: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Avans University of Applied Sciences (Avans Hogeschool), consistently ranked as the #1 large university of applied sciences in the Netherlands by the Keuzegids HBO for over a decade, is situated across modern campuses in Breda, 's-Hertogenbosch, and Tilburg. Renowned for its Centre of Expertise Biobased Economy (CoE BBE), Sustainable Energy Transition, and Data Science programs.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Master of Science in Biobased Sciences & Circular Economy

One-year professional Master of Science taught in English, training circular economy engineers in bio-based plastics, plant extraction chemistry, and enzymatic upcycling of industrial organic waste.

Avans NL Scholarship for Non-EEA International Masters Students

Competitive scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science and Avans for outstanding international students applying for English-taught degrees.

Master of Science in Sustainable Energy System Transition

Applied Master's program preparing energy engineers to model regional smart grids, district heat networks, battery energy storage installations, and industrial green hydrogen integration.

Centre of Expertise Biobased Economy (CoE BBE) Applied Research Fellowship

Two-year applied research post at the Centre of Expertise Biobased Economy in Breda, formulating drop-in bioplastic coatings and natural fiber composites for European construction and packaging.

Master of Science in Applied Data Science & Industrial AI

One-year applied master's degree training data engineers in predictive maintenance algorithms, computer vision quality control, and edge AI implementations for manufacturing lines.

Sustainable Business Transitions Research Fellowship

One-year fellowship investigating circular economy business models, closed-loop supply chains, and European Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive compliance.

Avans Smart Grid & Battery Systems Graduate Engineering Internship

Six-month research internship at the Energy Transition lab in Breda, testing battery peak-shaving algorithms and renewable microgrid balance across commercial logistics parks.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A relevant bachelor's degree in engineering, environmental technology, applied chemistry, data science, or business administration.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree; practice-based doctoral candidacies conducted in collaboration with research university partner consortia.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, letter of motivation (approx. 500 words), and documented engineering or project work portfolio.
Application fee
€0 application fee via Studielink.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcripts, CV, motivation letter, copy of passport, and proof of English language proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is set at the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition ranges from €9,800 to €12,500 per year for bachelor's and master's degrees. Non-EU international students can apply for the prestigious NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) providing a €5,000 tuition grant in the first year of study.
