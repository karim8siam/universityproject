Technische Hochschule Georg Agricola (THGA Bochum)

Study post-mining engineering, geotechnology, and sustainable industrial recycling at Germany's historic mining academy, THGA Bochum.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1816, Technische Hochschule Georg Agricola (THGA Bochum) is Germany's premier institution for mining engineering, post-mining georesource stewardship, industrial heritage preservation, and applied materials science, operating at the heart of the Ruhr metropolitan area.

Institution type
State-recognized Private Technical University (DMT-LB Sponsored)
Campus location
Herner Straße Campus, Bochum, North Rhine-Westphalia
Accreditation
Ministry of Culture and Science of NRW / ASIIN / FIBAA
Admissions channel
Winter semester (September start) and Summer semester (March start)
Instruction language
German (Bachelor's) & German/English (Master's programs)
Financial aid & support
RAG-Stiftung Scholarships, Deutschlandstipendium, NRW-Stipendium

Institutional Research Strengths

  • World-unique Research Institute of Post-Mining (FZN)
  • Deep underground mine water management and geo-monitoring technologies
  • Endowed support from RAG-Stiftung ensuring exceptional laboratory infrastructure
  • Direct industrial co-ops across European heavy machinery and energy infrastructure

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Geoengineering and Post-Mining (Graduate Degree) — Internationally unique engineering master's dealing with ground subsidence, mine flooding management, and subsurface energy storage.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Maschinenbau (Mechanical Engineering) (Undergraduate Degree) — Applied mechanical engineering education emphasizing manufacturing automation, simulation, and thermal turbomachinery.
  • Master of Science in Materials Engineering and Industrial Recycling (Graduate Degree) — Specialized engineering master's addressing circular metallurgy, battery recycling, and composite material characterisation.
