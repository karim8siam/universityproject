Meijo University

Meijo University is one of Central Japan's most celebrated private universities, world-renowned for its Nobel Laureates in Physics and Chemistry—including Sumio Iijima (discoverer of carbon nanotubes), Isamu Akasaki (blue LED pioneer), and Akira Yoshino (lithium-ion battery inventor).

Academic overview & excellence

Meijo's main Tempaku Campus in Nagoya hosts world-leading research hubs in carbon nanomaterials, semiconductor crystals, battery chemistry, and aerospace engineering, deeply integrated with the Greater Nagoya Chubu manufacturing super-cluster.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate programs require the Common University Admissions Test or EJU for international applicants with JLPT N2/N1. Graduate engineering and science programs evaluate undergraduate academic transcripts (minimum GPA 3.0/4.0), laboratory interviews, and research proposals in advanced materials and electronics.

PhD

Meijo Nobel Laureates Legacy Doctoral Research Fellowship

Three-year elite doctoral fellowship working in the laboratories of Meijo's Nobel laureates, investigating high-capacity solid-state lithium batteries and single-walled carbon nanotube semiconductors.

Funding: ¥180,000 monthly research salary plus full tuition waiver and nanolab research budget Tuition: ¥0 for fellowship recipients (regular tuition ¥1,100,000/year) Deadline: January 15, 2027

Master of Science in Materials Chemistry & Nanotechnology

Two-year graduate degree in carbon nanomaterials synthesis, transmission electron microscopy (TEM), catalytic fuel cell membranes, and polymer electrolytes.

Funding: Meijo Graduate Academic Honors Scholarship (30–100% tuition waiver) Tuition: ¥1,100,000 per academic year Deadline: February 15, 2027
Fellowship

Gallium Nitride (GaN) Blue LED & Power Semiconductor Fellowship

Postdoctoral research fellowship at the Akasaki Institute developing ultra-wide bandgap GaN power electronics for electric trains and renewable microgrids.

Funding: ¥3,600,000 annual postdoctoral research salary at the Akasaki Research Center Tuition: ¥0 Deadline: March 31, 2027
Internship

Toyota Group & Meijo Automotive Powertrain Co-op Practicum

Six-month practical industrial research term testing battery degradation thermal management, regenerative braking systems, and lightweight carbon-fiber chassis components.

Funding: ¥160,000 monthly corporate internship stipend placed at Aichi automotive R&D centers Tuition: Included in enrollment Deadline: April 30, 2027
Bachelor's

Bachelor of Science in Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering

Four-year accredited engineering degree covering supersonic aerodynamics, rocket propulsion, composite structures, and flight control systems in Nagoya's aerospace cluster.

Funding: Chubu Aerospace Industry Consortium scholarship eligibility Tuition: ¥1,480,000 per academic year Deadline: January 25, 2027
Scholarship

Research grant supporting graduate student projects exploring chirality-controlled growth of single-walled carbon nanotubes under Prof. Sumio Iijima's institute.

Funding: ¥1,000,000 project grant for student-led nano-materials characterization Tuition: ¥0 Deadline: May 15, 2027
Course

Ten-week technical course on lithium-ion thermal runaway prevention, state-of-charge (SoC) estimation algorithms, and battery recycling pyrometallurgy.

Funding: Aichi Prefecture industrial worker upskilling subsidy eligible Tuition: ¥160,000 total course fee Deadline: August 31, 2027
