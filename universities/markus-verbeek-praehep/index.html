Institution Overview & Profile

Markus Verbeek Praehep (MVP), founded in 1896 and based in Hilversum with training hubs in Utrecht, Amsterdam, and Rotterdam, is the Netherlands' foremost specialized higher education institution for chartered accountancy (Registeraccountant - RA), accounting consultant certification (Accountant-Administratieconsulent - AA), tax advisory, financial auditing, and corporate compliance.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Specialized Higher Institute (NVAO & CEA Accredited)
Main CampusHilversum Head Office (Marathon 1) & Regional Lecture Centres in Utrecht and Amsterdam
AccreditationDutch-Flemish Accreditation Organisation (NVAO) / Commissie Eindtermen Accountantsopleiding (CEA)
Admissions RouteModular higher professional enrollment with recognized prior accounting or business qualifications
Language of InstructionDutch (C1 for Dutch fiscal law and accounting standards); English accounting literature
Financial Aid & GrantsEmployer Study Cost Reimbursement (Studiekostenregeling) & Levenlanglerenkrediet (DUO)

Key Academic Strengths

  • Over 125 years of accredited leadership in Dutch accountancy and financial education
  • Full CEA and NBA (Koninklijke Nederlandse Beroepsorganisatie van Accountants) accreditation for RA and AA titles
  • Dual study model tailored for working professionals in Big Four accounting firms and regional financial practices
  • High first-time pass rates in national integrated final practical examinations
