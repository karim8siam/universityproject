Institutional Overview

Yorkville University is an accredited Canadian university offering practitioner-oriented degree programs with major campuses in Toronto (Ontario) and Vancouver (British Columbia), alongside its historic founding in New Brunswick. Known for its prestigious Toronto Film School affiliation, interior design school, and national Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology, Yorkville emphasizes flexible, year-round degree acceleration.
