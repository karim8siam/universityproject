University of the Sacred Heart, Tokyo

University of the Sacred Heart (Seishin Joshi Daigaku) is a historic, prestigious Catholic women’s university located in Hiroo, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo, celebrated for humanities, international studies, and global social justice.

Academic overview & research priorities

Established in 1916 by the Society of the Sacred Heart (founded in France in 1800) and chartered in 1948 as one of Japan’s first women’s universities under the modern system, Sacred Heart Tokyo educated Empress Emerita Michiko and is renowned for its Faculty of Modern Liberal Arts, Sacred Heart Institute for Sustainable Futures (SHISF), and multilingual education.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions for women evaluate secondary school graduation via general examinations, comprehensive selection (Sogo-gata), or international student admissions (JLPT N1/N2 or EJU). Graduate schools are coeducational for master’s and doctoral degrees, requiring research proposals and foreign language exams.
