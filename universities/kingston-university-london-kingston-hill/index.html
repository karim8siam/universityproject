Kingston University London (Kingston Hill Campus): Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located just south of Richmond Park in Royal Kingston upon Thames, Kingston Hill Campus accommodates the Kingston Business School, the School of Law, and cutting-edge healthcare simulation wards. Home to the multi-million-pound Business and Law building, modern mock law courtrooms, and Bloomberg trading terminals, Kingston Hill is internationally celebrated for enterprise innovation, social entrepreneurship, and career-led academic programs.
