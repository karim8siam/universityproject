Eastern Washington University

Eastern Washington University (EWU) is a regional comprehensive public university in Cheney and Spokane, WA, renowned for its Catalyst high-tech building in Spokane, computer science and cybersecurity, freshwater fisheries ecology, and urban planning.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1882, EWU operates its historical residential campus in Cheney and the high-tech Catalyst Building in Spokane's University District (the region's first zero-energy, zero-carbon commercial office building). The university excels in STEM through the College of Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics, featuring close research alignments with Amazon, Avista, F5 Networks, and regional water protection agencies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5; 3.0+ for guaranteed admission). EWU is completely test-blind/test-optional. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0, prerequisites in the discipline, personal statement, and recommendation letters. International applicants must provide TOEFL iBT 71 or IELTS 6.0.
