Cégep Garneau: Computer Science Technology, Physiotherapy & Nursing Guide

Established in 1969 in Quebec City, Cégep Garneau is one of Canada's most distinguished collegiate institutions, educating over 8,000 students across pre-university science tracks, healthcare technologies, and advanced computer science. Home to cutting-edge simulation laboratories and applied research centers, Garneau prepares students for university transfers and high-skill technology careers.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Quebec Secondary School Diploma (DES) or international equivalent secondary credential (baccalauréat) with strong prerequisites in mathematics, chemistry, and physics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral pathways hosted in partnership with Université Laval and the Centre hospitalier universitaire de Québec (CHU de Québec).
Language proficiency
French language proficiency at level B2/C1 (TCF, TEF, or DELF) for regular French-taught curricula; English B2 for bilingual technology streams.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for applied science research bursaries: 750-word statement outlining interest in medical technologies, software engineering, or physiotherapy innovation.
Application fee
$30 CAD centralized application fee via Service régional d'admission au collégial de Québec (SRACQ).
Required documentation
Certified transcripts and secondary graduation diplomas with official French translations, birth certificate, proof of French proficiency, and financial guarantee.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 0 CAD for Quebec resident students. For international candidates, collegiate tuition is approximately $13,800 to $17,500 CAD per year, with Quebec government tuition exemption awards granting local Quebec fee status to high-achieving international scholars.
