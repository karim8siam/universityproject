ISBA München | Internationale Studien- und Berufsakademie

ISBA München (Internationale Studien- und Berufsakademie) is a state-accredited private university of cooperative education in Munich, offering dual Bachelor degree programs in sports management, digital marketing, real estate economics, and event management.

Academic overview & research priorities

ISBA delivers an integrated dual study model: students alternate quarterly between academic seminars in Munich and hands-on corporate rotations at partner corporations, sports clubs, luxury hospitality groups, and financial brokerages. Corporate partners cover 100% of student tuition while providing a monthly trainee salary.

Research admissions & international criteria

Requirements include German Abitur, Fachhochschulreife, or an equivalent foreign higher education entrance qualification, along with a signed dual employment contract with an approved partner company. An admissions assessment and interview are conducted.
