Concordia University, Nebraska

Concordia University, Nebraska is a private Lutheran university in Seward, Nebraska, offering accredited degrees in education, nursing, biology, computer science, and agricultural business, acclaimed for preparing church and public school educators across North America.

Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), Concordia Nebraska delivers exceptional education programs, clinical healthcare simulation, and agricultural science training. The university operates modern natural science research laboratories and comprehensive online graduate educator academies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with a minimum 2.50 GPA; test-optional admissions policies apply. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor’s degree with a 2.80+ GPA and state educator certification for specialized master’s tracks.
