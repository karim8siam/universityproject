Academic overview & excellence

Situated in the central Dutch transportation hub of Amersfoort, Connect :-: Ways delivers postgraduate executive masterclasses, INCOSE-aligned systems engineering certifications, and organizational cybernetics coaching. The center partners with Dutch high-tech leaders (ASML, Philips, Thales) and public infrastructure bodies (Rijkswaterstaat) to design methodologies for managing ultra-complex socio-technical systems.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires an accredited bachelor's or master's degree in engineering, computer science, mathematics, or business administration, alongside at least two years of professional experience in technical project management or systems architecture.
