Lehigh University

Lehigh Univ · Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, United States

Lehigh University is an elite private Carnegie R1 research university in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. Founded in 1865, Lehigh is world-renowned for its P.C. Rossin College of Engineering, structural engineering labs, materials research, Lehigh Business, and interdisciplinary innovation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lehigh comprises the Rossin College of Engineering, College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business, and the College of Health. Flagship research centers include the Center for Advanced Materials and Nanotechnology (CAMN) and the ATLSS Engineering Research Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective with holistic evaluation of high school curriculum rigor, GPA, standardized test scores (test-optional), and personal essays. Graduate engineering and science programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, strong quantitative background, and departmental faculty sponsorship.
