AHU Lund: Higher Education Pedagogy, Digital Learning & Academic Leadership

The Centre for Educational Development (Avdelningen för högskolepedagogisk utveckling - AHU) at Lund University is Sweden's foremost academic center for scholarship of teaching and learning (SoTL), higher education pedagogical research, doctoral supervisor certification, and digital learning environments.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Master's degree or ongoing appointment as a university lecturer, researcher, or doctoral candidate in higher education.
Doctoral admission eligibility
PhD candidates in educational sciences require an accredited Master's degree in education, sociology, or cognitive sciences with high academic distinction.
Language proficiency
Swedish and English (courses offered in both languages; English proficiency equivalent to English 6/B).
Research statement / Proposal
Teaching philosophy statement or proposed pedagogical development project plan (1,000-1,500 words).
Application fee
Free application for Swedish and EU university employees.
Required documentation
Curriculum vitae, verified degree certificates, teaching dossier or list of instructional responsibilities, and recommendation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for Lund University faculty and researchers. For external participants, course modules typically range from SEK 12,000 to SEK 28,000. Swedish Research Council (Vetenskapsrådet) educational grants and Erasmus+ academic staff mobility funding are widely utilized.
