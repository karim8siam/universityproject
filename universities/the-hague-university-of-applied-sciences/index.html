The Hague University of Applied Sciences (De Haagse Hogeschool)

Located in the International City of Peace and Justice, The Hague University of Applied Sciences (De Haagse Hogeschool - THUAS) is an internationally oriented professional university educating over 26,000 students from more than 120 nations. Adjacent to international courts, embassies, and the Dutch Parliament, THUAS leads applied research in global governance, cybersecurity, digital forensics at the Centre of Expertise Cyber Security, sustainable urban transitions, and international communication management.

THUAS World Citizen Talent Scholarship & NL Scholarship (€5,000 Award)

Prestigious Dutch government and university-funded scholarship providing financial assistance to talented international students from outside the European Economic Area admitted to THUAS master's programs.

THUAS Master of Science (MSc) in Cyber Security Academy (English-Taught)

Two-year executive Master's program delivered in partnership with the Cyber Security Academy The Hague, covering critical infrastructure protection, digital forensics, threat modeling, and cyber governance.

THUAS Master of Science (MSc) in International Communication Management (English-Taught)

One-year intensive Master's program training strategic communication directors in crisis communication, cross-cultural stakeholder management, and reputation governance in international NGOs and corporations.

THUAS Centre of Expertise Cyber Security Doctoral / Applied Research Fellowship

Collaborative applied doctoral research at the Centre of Expertise Cyber Security in The Hague, developing operational cyber resilience models, automated threat detection tools, and digital crime investigation methodologies.

THUAS Mission Zero Applied Research Associate in Circular Urban Transitions

Applied research post within the Mission Zero Centre of Expertise, coordinating regional projects on renewable district heating, circular building materials, and local energy cooperatives in South Holland.

THUAS Ethical Hacking & Digital Forensics Summer Traineeship

Ten-week hands-on digital forensics internship conducting simulated penetration testing, malware reverse engineering, and threat analysis in THUAS's state-of-the-art cyber testing laboratory.

THUAS Senior Researcher / Lecturer in Cyber Security Governance & International Law

Senior academic role teaching in international cybersecurity and public management master's programs and directing research projects at the Centre of Expertise Cyber Security on national cybersecurity strategies and diplomatic cyber norms.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits, equivalent to Dutch HBO or WO Bachelor) with good academic standing (minimum 65-70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA) in an appropriate field of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (HBO Master or WO Master) in a relevant discipline; doctoral candidacies are conducted in collaboration with research universities (such as Leiden University or TU Delft) through professorships (lectoraten).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no individual sub-score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
A motivation letter (1,000 words) and portfolio demonstrating professional experience, case analysis, or policy research.
Application fee
€0 application fee for EU students; €100 non-refundable handling fee for non-EU/EEA applicants (deducted from tuition upon enrollment).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete transcript of records, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, English test report, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students in Master of Science programs at THUAS is approximately €9,000 to €13,500 per year (statutory tuition of €2,601/year applies to EU/EEA citizens). Outstanding international applicants are eligible for the prestigious NL Scholarship (€5,000 merit award) as well as THUAS World Citizen Talent Scholarships.
