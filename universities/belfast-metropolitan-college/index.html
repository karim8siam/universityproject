Belfast Metropolitan College: Cyber Security, Software Engineering & Cloud Technologies Guide

With origins dating to 1906, Belfast Metropolitan College (Belfast Met) is the largest further and higher education college in Northern Ireland, operating world-class facilities including its iconic Titanic Quarter campus. Renowned as a UK Cyber Security Centre of Excellence in technical education, Belfast Met partners with Queen's University Belfast and global tech employers (PwC, Deloitte, Microsoft) to deliver higher national diplomas, foundation degrees, and applied research apprenticeships.

Three-year research fellowship leading Innovate UK Knowledge Transfer Partnerships (KTP) linking college prototyping labs with Northern Ireland manufacturing firms.

Targeted educational grant promoting female participation and leadership in software engineering, renewable energy technology, and aerospace mechanics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Honours degree or equivalent level 6 qualification in computing, engineering, or physical sciences; or level 5 Higher National Diploma with merit/distinction profile for top-up bachelor's degrees.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative PhD pathways facilitated via university partnerships with Queen's University Belfast and Ulster University.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.0 overall (with at least 5.5 in each individual skill component), Cambridge English C1 Advanced, or Pearson PTE 54.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for advanced technology research fellowships: 1,000-word proposal detailing technical approach to cyber threat hunting, cloud automation, or composite engineering.
Application fee
£0 application fee for direct online college higher education applications.
Required documentation
Certified GCSE and Level 3/A-Level/University certificates and transcripts, valid passport, English language qualification, and academic reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Higher education tuition at Belfast Metropolitan College is approximately £2,800 to £3,200 per academic year for Northern Ireland/UK resident students, and £7,500 to £9,200 per year for international candidates—representing outstanding value compared to traditional UK universities. Department for the Economy (DfE) Assured Skills academies also offer fully funded training.
