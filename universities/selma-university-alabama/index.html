Selma University

Selma University · Selma, Alabama, United States

Selma University is a historic private Baptist Historically Black University (HBCU) founded in 1878 in the historic Civil Rights landmark city of Selma, Alabama. Renowned for theological education, pastoral leadership, African American religious history, and community health.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Selma University features the historic Dinkins Hall, the Pollard-McCall Library, and the Selma Bible College. It has played a pivotal historic role in educating African American ministers, educators, and civil rights leaders in the Alabama Black Belt for nearly a century and a half.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Selma University operates an accessible, open-admissions policy welcoming high school graduates and transfer students. Applicants submit high school transcripts or GED documentation, a personal statement of educational purpose, and pastoral or community recommendations.
