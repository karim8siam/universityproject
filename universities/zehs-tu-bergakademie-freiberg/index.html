ZeHS | TU Bergakademie Freiberg

The Centre for Efficient High-Temperature Processes and Materials Conversion (ZeHS) at TU Bergakademie Freiberg is Germany’s premier national research institute for zero-emission metallurgy, high-temperature synthesis, refractory ceramics, and mineral recycling.

Academic overview & research priorities

Constructed as an interdisciplinary mega-laboratory, ZeHS unites mineralogy, chemical thermodynamics, mechanical engineering, and metallurgical chemistry. It hosts unique pilot-scale melting furnaces, flash calcination units, and advanced synchrotron X-ray diffraction facilities to eliminate carbon emissions from heavy industry.

Research admissions & international criteria

Doctoral candidates and graduate students enroll through TU Bergakademie Freiberg’s Faculty of Materials Science and Technology. Admissions require a Master of Science in Materials Science, Metallurgy, Chemistry, or Mechanical Engineering.
