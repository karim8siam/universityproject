Montclair State University

Montclair State · Montclair, New Jersey, United States

Montclair State University is a leading public Carnegie R2 doctoral research university located in Montclair, New Jersey. Known for computer science, environmental coastal research, educational pedagogy, business, and performing arts.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Montclair State comprises the College of Science and Mathematics, Feliciano School of Business, and Cali School of Music. It operates cutting-edge facilities including the Clean Energy and Sustainability Analytics Center and PSEG Sustainability Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school coursework and grades under a holistic test-optional admissions policy. Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, professional resume, and letters of recommendation.
