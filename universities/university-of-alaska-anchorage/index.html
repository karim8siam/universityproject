University of Alaska Anchorage

University of Alaska Anchorage · Anchorage, Alaska, United States

The University of Alaska Anchorage (UAA) is the largest public university in Alaska, nestled in the state's economic and cultural center between the Chugach Mountains and Cook Inlet. UAA is internationally known for its specialized research in Arctic and subarctic environments, cold climate engineering, circumpolar indigenous health disparities, aviation technology, and natural resource governance.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UAA comprises the College of Arts and Sciences, College of Engineering, College of Health, College of Business and Public Policy, and Community and Technical College. It operates unique research enterprises including the Institute of Social and Economic Research (ISER), the Alaska Center for Conservation Science, and the ConocoPhillips Integrated Science Building.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with a minimum 2.5 GPA (test-optional). Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, submit transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a statement of professional intent.
