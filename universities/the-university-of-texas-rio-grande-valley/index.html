The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

UTRGV · Edinburg, Texas, United States

The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley (UTRGV) is a premier public research institution and one of the largest Hispanic-Serving Institutions (HSIs) in the United States, operating vibrant campuses across Edinburg, Brownsville, and South Padre Island. Acclaimed for its innovative School of Medicine, the STARGATE space communications facility (partnering with SpaceX Starbase), and coastal marine science, UTRGV drives transformative health and aerospace innovation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UTRGV comprises 11 colleges and schools, including the School of Medicine, the College of Engineering and Computer Science, and the School of Earth, Environmental, and Marine Sciences (SEEMS). Research flagships include the South Texas Diabetes and Obesity Institute (STDOI), STARGATE space radiofrequency center, and the Coastal Studies Laboratory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school class rank and GPA (assured admission for top 25% of Texas classes). Graduate and professional admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, program-specific entrance examinations, letters of recommendation, and personal statements.
