Oxford International Career Colleges - Halifax Campus

Oxford International Career Colleges (OICC) Halifax is a premier Canadian vocational and technical institution situated in downtown Halifax, Nova Scotia. Offering accredited career diplomas in digital media, continuing care, business administration, and network engineering, OICC provides accelerated, job-focused education aligned with Atlantic Immigration and regional labour market demands.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Institutional Overview

The curriculum at OICC Halifax prioritizes hands-on practical skills, employer-driven industry practicums, and small interactive seminar cohorts. Programs are recognized by the Nova Scotia Department of Advanced Education and integrated with leading industry standard certifications across healthcare assistance, enterprise software development, hospitality management, and digital technologies.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at OICC Halifax operate on flexible monthly and quarterly cohort intakes designed for Canadian and international students. Applicants must hold a secondary school diploma or equivalent mature student status, with demonstrated English proficiency (IELTS Academic 6.0 or Duolingo 105). Practical co-op placements ensure graduates transition directly into Nova Scotia's expanding services and tech economies.
