iba | Duales Studium Erfurt

iba (Internationale Berufsakademie) Erfurt is Germany’s premier private university of cooperative education, delivering accredited dual Bachelor degree programs with guaranteed corporate monthly salaries and complete tuition coverage across business, physiotherapy, and social work.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating on a distinctive model where students alternate weekly between university academic lectures and practical employment at corporate partner firms, iba Erfurt achieves a 95%+ graduate employment rate upon graduation. Programs are accredited by the Central Evaluation and Accreditation Agency (ZEvA).

Research admissions & international criteria

Applicants must hold a German Abitur, Fachhochschulreife, or international equivalent. The university matches qualified applicants with verified corporate training partners who pay the student’s tuition fees in full along with a monthly wage.
