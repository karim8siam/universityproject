Rocky Vista University Montana

RVU Montana · Billings, Montana, United States

Rocky Vista University Montana College of Osteopathic Medicine (RVU-MCOM) is Montana's first comprehensive four-year medical school, located on an expansive modern medical campus in Billings, Montana. Celebrated for osteopathic manipulative medicine, rural primary care physician training, military medicine, and medical simulation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

RVU-MCOM features a 138,000-square-foot medical education building equipped with 16 osteopathic clinical skill suites, an advanced anatomical dissection lab, high-fidelity patient simulation theaters, and extensive research facilities. Students complete third- and fourth-year clinical clerkships across rural hospitals and regional healthcare systems in Montana, Wyoming, and Idaho.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Medical school admission is highly selective through AACOMAS. Applicants must complete an accredited bachelor's degree with prerequisite biology, chemistry, and physics laboratory courses, submit MCAT examination scores, and demonstrate clinical healthcare shadowing and rural medicine commitment.
