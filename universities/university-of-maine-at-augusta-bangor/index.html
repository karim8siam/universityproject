University of Maine at Augusta - Bangor

UMA Bangor · Bangor, Maine, United States

The University of Maine at Augusta Bangor Campus, situated on an expansive park-like campus in Bangor, ME, is the central northern hub of UMA within the University of Maine System. UMA Bangor houses Maine's premier bachelor's degree in Aeronautical Sciences (professional aviation flight training), the state's leading Dental Hygiene clinic, and the Maine Cyber Range.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, HiSET, or GED credential with open access admissions. Flight aviation and dental hygiene programs require selective admissions and interview processes. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Rolling admissions throughout the year; Fall semester priority August 1, 2027; Spring semester priority December 15, 2026.
