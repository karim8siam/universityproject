The Open University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established by Royal Charter in 1969 and headquartered on a sprawling 110-acre campus in Walton Hall, Milton Keynes, The Open University has transformed higher education for over 2.2 million learners worldwide. With an active enrollment of over 170,000 students, the OU combines world-leading open-access digital learning pedagogy with premier scientific research. The OU’s Space Science faculty built instruments for the European Space Agency’s Rosetta mission, the Beagle 2 Mars lander, and the ExoMars rover. Through the Knowledge Media Institute (KMi) and Institute of Educational Technology, the OU leads international research in AI-driven personalized learning and planetary habitability.
