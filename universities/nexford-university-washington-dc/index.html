Nexford University

Nexford · Washington, District of Columbia, United States

Nexford University is an innovative, DEAC-accredited online university headquartered in Washington, D.C. Providing 100% online, competency-based global degree programs in business analytics, artificial intelligence, and digital transformation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Nexford University features the School of Business and the School of Technology. Programs are designed around real-time employer skills data and competency-based modules, serving international scholars and working professionals across over 100 countries.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation or GED equivalent. Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.5 GPA and professional goals statement. Fully online admissions with rolling monthly intakes.
