Technische Oberschule Stuttgart

TO Stuttgart · Stuttgart, Baden-Württemberg, Germany

Technische Oberschule Stuttgart (part of GSIH Stuttgart) is Baden-Württemberg’s renowned state institution enabling qualified vocational technicians to attain the Allgemeine Hochschulreife (general university matriculation) in STEM disciplines.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Specializing in technical physics, advanced applied mathematics, electrical engineering fundamentals, and computer science, TO Stuttgart serves as an elite bridge between vocational craftsmanship and university-level engineering faculties.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires completion of a recognized technical vocational apprenticeship (Berufsausbildung) and the Mittlere Reife (intermediate school certificate) with qualifying grades in German, English, and Mathematics.
