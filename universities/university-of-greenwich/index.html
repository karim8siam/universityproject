University of Greenwich: Maritime Studies, Natural Resources, AI & Engineering Guide

The University of Greenwich is an iconic London public university based at the historic Old Royal Naval College on the Thames, home to the internationally recognized Natural Resources Institute (NRI), Greenwich Maritime Centre, and advanced computing faculties.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:2 or 2:1 honours degree or recognized international equivalent in engineering, computing, agriculture, or business.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree with merit or distinction, accompanied by a 4-page original research proposal and faculty supervisory agreement.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-skills) or equivalent secure English language test (SELT).
Research statement / Proposal
A detailed 1,500-word research proposal outlining research methodology, timeline, and alignment with Greenwich research centers.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the university direct application portal.
Required documentation
Degree certificates, official transcripts, two academic references, curriculum vitae, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international graduate students ranges from £15,500 to £19,000 per academic year. The International Scholarships Award offers up to £3,000 in tuition fee discounts. Vice-Chancellor's PhD Scholarships provide fully funded tuition and UKRI-standard living stipends.
