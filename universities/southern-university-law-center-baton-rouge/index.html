Southern University Law Center

SULC · Baton Rouge, Louisiana, United States

Southern University Law Center (SULC) is a prestigious public historically Black law school located in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Created in 1947 following a civil rights lawsuit by Charles J. Hatfield III to ensure legal education access for African Americans in Louisiana, SULC has evolved into one of the nation's most racially and ethnically diverse law schools, renowned for championing civil rights litigation, trial advocacy, social justice, and clinical legal education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SULC offers Juris Doctor (J.D.) degree programs through full-time day and part-time evening divisions, along with an innovative Master of Laws (LL.M.) in Indigenous Law. The Law Center operates eight experiential clinical legal education clinics, including the Civil Rights Clinic, the Criminal Defense Clinic, the Disaster Relief Clinic, and the Low-Income Taxpayer Clinic.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admission to the Juris Doctor program requires an accredited bachelor's degree from a recognized institution, a competitive score on the Law School Admission Test (LSAT), official transcripts registered through CAS (Credential Assembly Service), a personal statement detailing legal motivations and commitment to justice, and two letters of recommendation.
