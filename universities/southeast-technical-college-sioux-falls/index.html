Southeast Technical College

Southeast Tech · Sioux Falls, South Dakota, United States

Southeast Technical College (Southeast Tech) is an accredited public technical college in Sioux Falls, South Dakota's largest commercial and medical center. Southeast Tech provides workforce-aligned education across 65+ associate degrees, diplomas, and technical certificates, serving as a vital training engine for the regional healthcare, advanced manufacturing, and IT industries.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Southeast Tech features specialized training facilities including the Sullivan Health Science Center, the Scarbrough Center for Construction Technology, modern diesel and automotive bays, and industrial mechatronics automation cells. The college maintains a remarkable 97%+ graduate career placement rate.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Southeast Tech operates open admissions for students with a high school diploma or GED equivalent. Technical and healthcare programs (such as Diagnostic Medical Sonography, Registered Nursing, and Surgical Technology) require specialized prerequisite criteria, background checks, and placement testing.
