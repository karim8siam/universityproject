University of Waterloo (Waterloo, Ontario)

Founded in 1957 in Waterloo, Ontario, the University of Waterloo is widely recognized as Canada's most innovative university and the global pioneer of cooperative education (co-op). Waterloo is an international powerhouse in computer science, software engineering, mathematics, and quantum computing, housing the world-renowned Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC) and collaborating closely with the nearby Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Waterloo President's Graduate Scholarship & International Doctoral Student Award (IDSA)

Waterloo's flagship doctoral awards package providing guaranteed financial assistance and international fee remissions for outstanding students pursuing doctoral research across all faculties.

Waterloo PhD in Computer Science & Quantum Information

One of the world's leading computer science doctoral programs, offering cutting-edge research in quantum computing, artificial intelligence, systems, algorithms, and cryptography.

Waterloo Master of Mathematics (MMath) in Computer Science (Thesis)

Rigorous two-year thesis-based Master of Mathematics degree providing deep theoretical foundational training and faculty-supervised original computer science research.

Waterloo Master of Applied Science (MASc) in Electrical & Computer Engineering

Research-intensive Master of Applied Science program focusing on wireless communications, robotics, nanophotonics, embedded systems, and power engineering.

Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC) Graduate Research Placements

Interdisciplinary research placements at one of the world's premier quantum centres, conducting theoretical and experimental investigations into superconducting qubits, optics, and algorithms.

Waterloo Co-operative Education & Undergraduate Research Assistantships (URA)

Canada's largest academic co-op and research assistantship ecosystem, connecting students with cutting-edge university laboratory projects and leading high-tech industry partners.

Waterloo Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) & Research Assistantships (GRA)

Salaried academic teaching appointments under the Canadian Union of Public Employees (CUPE), providing graduate scholars with instructional experience and supplemental income.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
4-year Honours Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in computer science, mathematics, or engineering with standing equivalent to at least 78% (B+ to A- average).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in relevant discipline with high academic standing (minimum 80% or A- average) and demonstrable independent research capability.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.5 overall (minimum 7.0 in writing and speaking) or TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 26 in writing and speaking).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of research interest outlining background, proposed methodology, and alignment with target Waterloo faculty supervisors.
Application fee
CAD $125 non-refundable application fee submitted through the Waterloo online portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, curriculum vitae, and personal statement of research interests.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for research-stream graduate degrees is supported by departmental funding packages and scholarships. For doctoral students in good standing, Waterloo provides guaranteed minimum base funding of CAD $28,500 per year (often exceeding CAD $35,000 in CS and engineering) plus the International Doctoral Student Award (IDSA) which substantially offsets international tuition fees.
