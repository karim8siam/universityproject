Academic overview & excellence

Situated in the central business district of Nagoya (the engine of Japan's automotive and manufacturing exports), NUCB Business School is widely recognized as a premier global business institution in Asia. Ranked #1 in Japan by QS and the Financial Times, NUCB delivers 100% case-method instruction in English and Japanese. The 14-story Nagoya Marunouchi Campus features Harvard-style amphitheaters, trading rooms, and incubator suites for family business successors and multinational corporate executives.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate degree admission requires a high school diploma, with international applicants submitting SAT/ACT scores or national exams, alongside proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0). The MBA and MSc programs require a recognized bachelor's degree, a minimum of 2 to 3 years of post-graduate professional work experience (for MBA), resume, official transcripts, essays, and an evaluative interview. GMAT or GRE scores are recommended.
