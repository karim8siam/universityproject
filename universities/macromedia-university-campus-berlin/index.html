Academic overview & research priorities

State-recognized by the Berlin Senate and accredited by FIBAA, Macromedia Berlin offers practical, project-based degree programs in design, media management, film directing, animation, and digital technologies in close partnership with Berlin's booming media and startup ecosystems.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require secondary school leaving credentials (Abitur, Fachabitur, or equivalent foreign diploma) and successful completion of an admissions portfolio interview. English and German degree tracks are offered (English B2/C1 required for English-taught programs).
