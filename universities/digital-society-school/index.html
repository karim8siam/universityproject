Digital Society School: Digital Transformation, Design & AI Ethics Guide

Operating within the Faculty of Digital Media and Creative Industries at Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (HvA), the Digital Society School (DSS) is an international postgraduate research and design institute located on the Amstel Campus in Amsterdam. Grounded in the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), DSS brings together multidisciplinary teams of designers, software engineers, data scientists, and social researchers to create tangible digital solutions addressing societal challenges.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

DSS Social Tech for Good International Merit Grant

Merit-based tuition scholarship awarded to talented international designers and software technologists dedicated to solving UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Postgraduate Professional Traineeship in Digital Transformation and Design

Full-time 20-week intensive studio program embedding scholars in multidisciplinary sprint teams to prototype ethical AI, digital inclusion, and climate resilience platforms.

Doctoral Research Fellowship in Responsible AI and Human-Centered Digital Systems

Four-year salaried doctoral research fellowship evaluating ethical AI architectures, algorithmic accountability, and participatory civic tech platforms in metropolitan Amsterdam.

Amsterdam Climate Resilience and Urban Digital Twin Design Project

Applied design research project creating interactive 3D digital twin visualizations and citizen sensor networks to simulate urban heat mitigation and water drainage.

Studio Design and Inclusive UX Prototyping Work Placement

Studio work-placement designing accessible digital interfaces, voice UI systems, and assistive technologies for individuals with sensory or cognitive disabilities.

DSS Digital Society Leadership and UN SDG Innovation Fellowship

Prestigious fellowship supporting visionary design practitioners developing open-source digital tools that empower local communities and promote social inclusion.

Senior Studio Track Lead and Technology Design Coach

Full-time faculty leadership role mentoring international student sprint teams, liaising with municipal and corporate project partners, and advancing social tech research.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited Bachelor's or Master's degree in Interaction Design, Computer Science, Data Science, Social Sciences, or Media Studies with strong digital design capabilities.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Postgraduate professional traineeships and research tracks articulate directly into European applied doctoral research and university PhD appointments in human-computer interaction.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated professional English proficiency (minimum IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90 or native equivalent).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive digital design portfolio, project case studies, CV, and a motivation statement detailing alignment with specific UN Sustainable Development Goals.
Application fee
Zero application fee for standard digital traineeship and track admissions.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diploma copy, certified academic transcripts, digital portfolio link, resume, and two letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the intensive 20-week professional Digital Traineeship program is approximately €2,850 for EU/EEA students and €4,250 for non-EU international participants. DSS offers substantial fellowship sponsorships, social innovation grants, and industry project bursaries that frequently cover up to 100% of enrollment fees for exceptional candidates.
