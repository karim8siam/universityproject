FernUniversität in Hagen: Computer Science, Mathematics & PhD Research Guide

FernUniversität in Hagen is Germany's only state-funded distance-education research university, founded in 1974. With over 70,000 students, full doctoral and habilitation granting rights, and regional study centers across Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and Europe, FernUni Hagen is a powerhouse in theoretical computer science, mathematics, economics, psychology, and law.

Salaried Doctoral Research Associate (PhD) in Theoretical Computer Science

Three-to-four year salaried doctoral position in the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, researching algorithmic complexity, parameterized algorithms, and computational logic.

DAAD Doctoral Completion Scholarship at FernUniversität in Hagen

DAAD-funded completion grant supporting international doctoral candidates during the final writing and defense phase of their doctoral dissertation.

Master of Science in Practical Computer Science and Software Engineering

State-accredited master's program providing advanced specialization in distributed systems, software verification, compiler construction, and database theory.

Research Institute for Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Representation Assistantship

Scientific research assistantship contributing to knowledge representation, semantic web ontologies, and belief revision algorithms within the Knowledge-Based Systems research group.

German Digital Education & E-Learning Technology Industry Practicum

Six-month practicum at the Center for Learning and Innovation (ZLI), designing adaptive AI tutoring tools and scalable digital testing systems for over 70,000 students.

Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Cryptography & IT Security

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment investigating lattice-based post-quantum cryptographic primitives, homomorphic encryption, and secure multiparty computation.

Master of Science in Mathematical Economics and Operations Research

Interdisciplinary master's degree preparing analysts in stochastic modeling, financial mathematics, linear and nonlinear optimization, and algorithmic game theory.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) from a recognized university with an academic grade of 2.5 or better on the German grading scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Master of Science, Master of Arts, or German Diplom) with above-average grades in relevant discipline and oral faculty examination.
Language proficiency
German C1 (DSH-2, TestDaF 4x4, or Goethe-Zertifikat C1) for German-taught degrees; English B2/C1 for international doctoral and computer science research groups.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral admission: 3,000-word research proposal agreed upon with an appointed FernUniversität faculty chair holder.
Application fee
€0 (No application fee for direct admission via FernUni portal).
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's certificates with certified German or English translations, transcripts of records, curriculum vitae, and statement of research intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is largely free of tuition fees in accordance with German public university law. Students pay only a modest course material fee (Materialbezugsgebühr) of approximately €11 per ECTS credit plus a semester administrative fee of €60. Salaried doctoral positions (Wissenschaftliche Mitarbeiter) receive standard TV-L E13 salaries (€50,000–€55,000/year gross).
