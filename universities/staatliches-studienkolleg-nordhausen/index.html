Staatliches Studienkolleg Nordhausen

Studienkolleg Nordhausen · Nordhausen, Thuringia, Germany

Staatliches Studienkolleg Nordhausen is Thuringia’s official state preparatory college, qualifying international university applicants from non-EU education systems for German university matriculation (Feststellungsprüfung - FSP).

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Affiliated with Nordhausen University of Applied Sciences, the Studienkolleg offers intensive two-semester foundation courses across technical disciplines (T-Kurs), business economics (W-Kurs), biological sciences (M-Kurs), and humanities (S-Kurs/G-Kurs).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applicants must demonstrate a recognized foreign secondary school leaving qualification and German language certification at B1 or B2 level, followed by successful completion of the state entrance examination (Aufnahmeprüfung).
