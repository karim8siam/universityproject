Willamette University

Willamette · Salem, Oregon, United States

Willamette University is a distinguished private liberal arts and professional university founded in 1842 in Salem, Oregon, making it the oldest university in the Western United States. Situated directly across the street from the Oregon State Capitol and State Supreme Court, Willamette is renowned for political science and law at the College of Law, business and public administration at the Atkinson Graduate School of Management, data science, and environmental science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Willamette encompasses five colleges and schools: the College of Arts and Sciences, College of Law, Atkinson Graduate School of Management, School of Computing and Information Sciences, and the Pacific Northwest College of Art (PNCA in Portland). The university operates the Zena Sustainability Park—a 305-acre living research forest.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate secondary school transcripts, essays, and extracurriculars with test-optional policies. Graduate admissions for law, business, and computing require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, resume, statement of purpose, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+). Merit scholarships are awarded upon admission.
