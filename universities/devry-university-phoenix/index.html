Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC) and ACBSP, DeVry emphasizes hands-on laboratory simulation, industry certifications (CompTIA, Cisco, AWS), and flexible hybrid education. Its College of Engineering and Information Sciences collaborates with Fortune 500 tech companies to align curricula with current digital workforce requirements.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation or GED, with diagnostic assessments in mathematics and English. Graduate admission requires an accredited bachelor’s degree with a 2.50+ GPA.
