York University

Established in 1959 in Toronto, York University is Canada's third-largest university and a world-renowned multidisciplinary research center. Famous globally for planetary science and space instruments at the Centre for Research in Earth and Space Science (CRESS - contributing to NASA's OSIRIS-REx and Phoenix Mars missions), the Lassonde School of Engineering, Osgoode Hall Law School, and the Schulich School of Business, York offers guaranteed doctoral funding packages, state-of-the-art research laboratories, and expansive global partnerships.

York Graduate Fellowship & Lassonde Doctoral Entrance Awards

York's cornerstone merit-based doctoral funding guarantee, providing guaranteed living stipends, teaching assistantships, and tuition offsets for admitted thesis-stream graduate researchers.

York PhD in Earth and Space Science (CRESS Planetary Exploration)

World-renowned doctoral research at the Centre for Research in Earth and Space Science (CRESS), designing laser altimeters, atmospheric lidar systems, and planetary rovers for space exploration missions.

Lassonde PhD in Computer Science & Autonomous Robotics

Doctoral research conducted within the Centre for Vision Research and the Lassonde School, investigating robot navigation, SLAM algorithms, deep reinforcement learning, and human-robot interaction.

York Master of Environmental Studies (MES) in Climate & Urban Sustainability

Canada's pioneering interdisciplinary Master of Environmental Studies at the Faculty of Environmental and Urban Change, allowing students to design customized research trajectories in urban ecology, renewable policy, and climate resilience.

Robarts Centre for Canadian Studies Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Robarts Centre, investigating Canadian social institutions, multicultural public policy, and constitutional governance in comparative international perspective.

York Centre for Vision Research (CVR) Summer Student Fellowship

Twelve-week mentored summer research fellowship at the internationally acclaimed Centre for Vision Research, investigating biological visual processing, virtual reality perception, and computational vision models.

York Postdoctoral Fellow in Space Instrumentation & Lidar Remote Sensing

Full-time research role at CRESS developing planetary lidar systems, optical spectrometers, and autonomous calibration algorithms for upcoming lunar and planetary surface exploration missions.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree (Honours) from an accredited university with a minimum B+ average (equivalent to 77-79% or 3.3/4.0 GPA) in the last two years of study; competitive STEM programs typically require 80%+.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in a relevant discipline with a minimum B+ average (77%+ or 3.3/4.0 GPA) and demonstrable thesis research capability.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 88 (minimum 20 in each section); Computer Science and Lassonde Engineering require IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 90.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2 to 3 page Statement of Academic Intent detailing research questions, theoretical framework, methodology, and confirmed alignment with York faculty mentors.
Application fee
CAD $130 non-refundable graduate application fee submitted via York's online portal.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 3 academic letters of recommendation, and statement of research intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time doctoral students in research degree programs benefit from guaranteed funding packages. York guarantees minimum annual funding of CAD $22,500 to $26,000 per year for four years (combining York Graduate Fellowships, teaching assistantships, and graduate research assistantships) plus international doctoral tuition offset grants.
