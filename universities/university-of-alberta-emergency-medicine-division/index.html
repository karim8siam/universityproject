University of Alberta Hospital - Division of Emergency Medicine

The University of Alberta Hospital Division of Emergency Medicine at the Walter C. Mackenzie Health Sciences Centre is the primary Level 1 adult trauma and acute resuscitation academic teaching centre serving northern Alberta, the Yukon, and Northwest Territories.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating as an internationally acclaimed academic medical center, the UAH Division of Emergency Medicine integrates state-of-the-art trauma bays, pediatric acute wings at the Stollery Children's Hospital, hyperbaric medicine facilities, and aeromedical dispatch. In collaboration with the Faculty of Medicine & Dentistry at the University of Alberta, the division spearheads groundbreaking investigation in resuscitation physiology, neurotrauma, point-of-care ultrasonography (POCUS), and health equity in emergency care.

Research admissions & criteria

Postgraduate residency positions are matched through the Canadian Resident Matching Service (CaRMS). Applicants must hold an MD or equivalent from an accredited medical school. Clinical subspecialty fellowships (e.g., Trauma Resuscitation, Pediatric Emergency, Hyperbaric Medicine) require board certification in Emergency Medicine (FRCPC or CCFP-EM). International medical graduates must meet Alberta College of Physicians and Surgeons (CPSA) licensure requirements.
