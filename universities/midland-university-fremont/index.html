Midland University

Midland Univ · Fremont, Nebraska, United States

Midland University is a private liberal arts and professional university in Fremont and Omaha, Nebraska, affiliated with the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America. Celebrated for its unique '4-Year Graduation Guarantee', nursing, business analytics, and athletic training.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Midland features the Dunklau School of Business in Omaha, the Luther College of Arts and Sciences, and the School of Nursing. Academic programs emphasize hands-on internships, leadership development, and intercollegiate athletics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional, reviewing high school transcripts (minimum 2.5 recommended GPA) and personal essays. Nursing applicants must meet clinical prerequisite grades and TEAS testing thresholds.
