Academic overview & excellence

Operating academic bootcamps and residential research intensives across Amsterdam and the historic estate of Doorn in Utrecht, Indigo University Experience prepares international secondary scholars for transition into premier European research universities (such as UVA, VU Amsterdam, Utrecht, Leiden, and TU Delft). Students engage in university-level scientific inquiry, academic English writing, Dutch language immersion, and collaborative group research symposia.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission is open to ambitious secondary school students aged 15–19 preparing for Dutch or European bachelor's degree studies. Applicants must submit official secondary school transcripts with a minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0 (or equivalent European grading), proof of English proficiency (IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 75, or high school English instruction verification), and a personal academic statement.
