Nevada State University

Nevada State · Henderson, Nevada, United States

Nevada State University is a four-year comprehensive public institution in Henderson, Nevada, and part of the Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE). Recognized as a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) celebrated for nursing, education, and speech-language pathology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Nevada State University features the School of Nursing, School of Education, and School of Liberal Arts, Sciences, and Business. Key facilities include the Dawson Building, the Glenn and Ande Christenson School of Education building, and advanced simulation healthcare suites.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.5 automatic admission) with test-optional policies. Nursing and Speech-Language Pathology programs have competitive secondary application requirements.
