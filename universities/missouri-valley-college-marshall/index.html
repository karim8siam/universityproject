Missouri Valley College

Missouri Valley · Marshall, Missouri, United States

Missouri Valley College is a private four-year liberal arts college in Marshall, Missouri, affiliated with the Presbyterian Church (USA). Known for agribusiness, athletic training, nursing, and community counseling.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Missouri Valley offers degree programs across the Divisions of Business, Science and Mathematics, Nursing, and Social Sciences. Features the Malcolm Center for Student Life, Burns Athletic Complex, and agricultural research plots.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.0 unweighted) or transfer college performance under holistic review. Graduate counseling programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA and character references.
