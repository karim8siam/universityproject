Texas A&M International University

TAMIU · Laredo, Texas, United States

Texas A&M International University (TAMIU) is an international, Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) of the Texas A&M University System, located along the US-Mexico border in Laredo, Texas. TAMIU is acclaimed for its AACSB-accredited A.R. Sanchez, Jr. School of Business, leading international logistics and trade research, and accessible bilingual educational pathways.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TAMIU delivers over 70 undergraduate and graduate programs through the A.R. Sanchez, Jr. School of Business, the College of Arts and Sciences, and the College of Nursing and Health Sciences. Key research centers include the Texas Center for Border Economic and Enterprise Development and the Western Hemispheric Trade Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate freshman admissions evaluate high school class rank and GPA (top 25% automatic admission). Graduate applicants require an earned bachelor's degree with a 2.50+ cumulative GPA (3.0+ on last 60 hours), statement of purpose, official transcripts, and program-specific letters of recommendation.
