Chamberlain University - Las Vegas

Chamberlain University Las Vegas is an established private healthcare and nursing institution located on Covington Cross Drive in Summerlin, Las Vegas, Nevada. Tracing its heritage over 135 years to the Deaconess School of Nursing, Chamberlain delivers CCNE-accredited Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) degrees, advanced practice nurse practitioner degrees, and doctoral healthcare credentials.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at Chamberlain Las Vegas review academic transcripts, HESI A2 Admission Assessment scores (minimum 73% composite score), and background requirements. Students can complete a full BSN in as few as nine semesters of year-round study. Military educational grants, institutional scholarships, and federal Title IV financial aid are accessible to qualified students.
