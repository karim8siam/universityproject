CDI College: Cybersecurity, Healthcare Assistance, Legal Studies & Network Admin Guide

CDI College is one of Canada's most established career training colleges with campuses across Manitoba, Alberta, BC, Ontario, and Quebec, providing fast-track vocational training in Network and Database Administration, Cybersecurity, Paralegal Studies, and Health Care Aide services.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
High school graduation diploma or mature student status (19+ years old) with passing marks on the college entrance assessment.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on fast-track career-ready diploma qualifications.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 5.5 to 6.0 overall or institutional English proficiency examination.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of career goals and commitment to fast-track vocational course completion.
Application fee
CAD $150 non-refundable registration processing fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school graduation transcripts, valid photo ID, entrance exam scores, and proof of legal residency or study visa.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition ranges from CAD $11,500 to $16,800 depending on diploma duration. Flexible modular payment arrangements, student line of credit support, and career advancement bursaries are accessible.
