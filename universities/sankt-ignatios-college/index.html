Sankt Ignatios College: Eastern Christian Studies & Byzantine Heritage Guide

Based in Södertälje in Stockholm County and operating in academic partnership with University College Stockholm (Enskilda Högskolan Stockholm - EHS), Sankt Ignatios College is an internationally recognized theological academy. Dedicated to Eastern Christian studies, Syriac studies, Byzantine patristics, iconology, and diaspora sociology, Sankt Ignatios pairs ancient manuscript preservation with modern Swedish academic rigor.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Sankt Ignatios Foundation International Theological Fellowship

Endowment scholarship supporting international scholars pursuing advanced study in Byzantine theology, Syriac language traditions, and diaspora cultural identity.

Master of Arts in Eastern Christian Studies

Two-year accredited master's program delivered in partnership with EHS covering patristic theology, ancient Syriac and Greek philology, and contemporary Eastern Christian communities.

Joint Doctoral Research Fellowship in Syriac Studies and Patristics

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment examining late antique Syriac Christian literature, manuscript transmission, and theological translations from Greek into Syriac.

Södertälje Diaspora Identity & Eastern Christian Sociology Project

Empirical sociological field research investigating integration, liturgical retention, and transnational networks among Middle Eastern Christian diaspora communities in Sweden.

Byzantine Iconography & Manuscript Conservation Practicum

Twelve-week intensive studio practicum in traditional Byzantine egg-tempera iconography, gilding techniques, and manuscript conservation at the Sankt Ignatios Art Studio.

Sankt Ignatios Senior Research Fellowship in Orthodox Liturgical Theology

Two-year research appointment leading the Sankt Ignatios Liturgical Studies research group and organizing international symposia on early Christian euchological texts.

Swedish Institute (SI) Master's Fellowship for Global Leaders

Swedish government scholarship fully financing master's studies in Sweden for talented scholars from eligible countries advancing human rights and interfaith peace.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in theology, religious studies, history, or humanities from a recognized university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral research conducted through joint EHS and partner Swedish research university faculties; requires completed research master's degree (120 ECTS).
Language proficiency
English 6 proficiency equivalent: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate research: 1,500-word research proposal outlining proposed focus in Syriac literature, Byzantine liturgical theology, or diaspora sociology.
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee for non-EU/EEA applicants via University Admissions Sweden.
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree diploma, official transcript of records, statement of motivation, CV, and verified English proficiency certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Swedish law, education is tuition-free for citizens of Sweden, EU/EEA countries, and Switzerland. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is approximately SEK 95,000 to SEK 110,000 per academic year. Admitted students can apply for Sankt Ignatios foundation bursaries, EHS study grants, and Swedish Institute (SI) scholarships.
