University of Northern British Columbia: Forestry, Boreal Ecology & Indigenous Research Guide

The University of Northern British Columbia (UNBC) is consistently ranked among Canada's top primarily undergraduate research universities, renowned as Canada's Green University for groundbreaking research in natural resource management, climate change, and rural community health.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with minimum 3.00 GPA (B average) in the last 60 credits of undergraduate study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree with minimum 3.33 GPA (B+ average) and evidence of substantial research or publication competence.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2-3 page research statement identifying prospective faculty supervisor and thesis methodology.
Application fee
CAD $76.50 for domestic applicants / CAD $153 for international applicants via UNBC Graduate Admissions.
Required documentation
Official post-secondary transcripts, curriculum vitae, statement of academic intent, and three confidential referee reports.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Domestic graduate tuition averages CAD $5,600 to $7,200 annually. International student graduate tuition is approximately CAD $14,800 to $18,500 per year. Substantial funding is provided through Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA) and northern research endowments.
