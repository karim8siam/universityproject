University of Maryland, Baltimore County

UMBC · Baltimore, Maryland, United States

The University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC) is an elite Carnegie R1 research university internationally celebrated for undergraduate teaching innovation and minority leadership in STEM. Founded in 1966, UMBC is the home of the world-renowned Meyerhoff Scholars Program, producing more African American MD-Ph.D. recipients than any other college in the United States.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMBC offers premier programs through the College of Engineering and Information Technology (COEIT), College of Natural and Mathematical Sciences (CNMS), and College of Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences (CAHSS). UMBC is closely affiliated with the NASA Goddard Earth Sciences Technology and Research (GESTAR II) consortium and the Cybersecurity Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are competitive and holistic. Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, submit a statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and meet English language proficiency thresholds (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
