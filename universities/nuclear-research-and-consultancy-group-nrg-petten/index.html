Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located along the North Sea dunes in Petten, the Nuclear Research and Consultancy Group (NRG / PALLAS) is Europe's premier nuclear research centre and the operator of the High Flux Reactor (HFR). NRG produces over 60% of Europe's medical radioisotopes (treating over 30,000 patients worldwide daily) and conducts cutting-edge research in molten salt reactors, nuclear waste transmutation, radiation materials science, and nuclear safety.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Medical radioisotopes production and targeted alpha therapy research (Lutetium-177, Actinium-225)
  • Next-generation nuclear energy (Molten Salt Reactors - MSR and Small Modular Reactors - SMR)
  • High-flux neutron irradiation testing of materials and reactor vessel steels
  • Nuclear safety assessment, decommissioning engineering, and radioactive waste management

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • NRG Doctoral Research Fellowship in Molten Salt Nuclear Chemistry (Doctoral Degree) — Four-year cooperative Ph.D. with TU Delft or TU Eindhoven investigating molten fluoride salt corrosion and fission product behavior.
  • Postdoctoral Fellowship in Medical Isotope Target Chemistry (Postdoctoral Fellowship) — Two-year research appointment developing high-purity Actinium-225 separation chemistry for targeted radionuclide cancer therapy.
  • Nuclear Safety Engineering and Radiation Protection Professional Practicum (Graduate Practicum) — Six-month specialized laboratory training in radiological shielding, neutron activation analysis, and thermal-hydraulic reactor modeling.
