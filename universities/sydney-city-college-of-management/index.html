Sydney City College of Management: IT, Accounting, Business & Hospitality Guide

Sydney City College of Management (SCCM) is an established Australian Registered Training Organisation (RTO) delivering CRICOS-accredited qualifications in Telecommunications, Cyber Security, Professional Accounting, and Business Administration in the heart of Sydney and Parramatta.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Satisfactory completion of an Australian Year 12 senior secondary certificate, AQF Diploma, or overseas equivalent.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent PhD degrees; diploma credits articulate toward university degree programs across Australia.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 5.5 to 6.0 overall (minimum 5.0 in each skill) or recognized equivalent PTE score.
Research statement / Proposal
Genuine Student (GS) statement outlining career pathway alignment and educational background.
Application fee
AUD $200 international enrollment processing fee.
Required documentation
Certified educational transcripts, passport bio-data page, English test scores, and completed enrollment documentation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Course tuition ranges from AUD $9,000 to $14,000 annually. Flexible term-by-term installment arrangements, scholarship fee remissions, and early application incentives are provided for eligible international learners.
