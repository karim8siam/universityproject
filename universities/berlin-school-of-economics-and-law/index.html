Berlin School of Economics and Law: Digital Business, International Finance & Public Law Guide

Established in 1971, the Berlin School of Economics and Law (Hochschule für Wirtschaft und Recht Berlin - HWR Berlin) is one of Germany's largest and premier public universities of applied sciences, educating over 12,000 students across two Berlin campuses (Schöneberg and Lichtenberg). HWR Berlin is nationally renowned for practice-oriented education in international business, public management, law, security studies, and industrial engineering.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited bachelor's degree or equivalent (180 or 210 ECTS) in economics, business administration, law, or social sciences with a minimum German grade of 2.5 (gut) and substantial quantitative credits.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral qualifications administered through joint doctoral centers (Promotionszentren) and university partnerships with Freie Universität Berlin and Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin.
Language proficiency
German proficiency at level C1 (DSH-2, TestDaF 4x4) for German-taught tracks; English proficiency C1 (TOEFL iBT 95+, IELTS 6.5 with 6.0 in all bands) for English-taught master's degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research master's and doctoral tracks: 1,500-word research proposal outlining empirical methodology in international corporate governance or digital public policy.
Application fee
€75 centralized uni-assist evaluation fee for international applicants with non-German undergraduate degrees.
Required documentation
Certified university diploma and transcript of records, certified translations, CV in tabular format, letter of motivation, and proof of language competence.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

As a premier German public university, HWR Berlin charges 0 EUR tuition for all domestic, EU, and international students. Students pay only a standard semester fee (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately 315 EUR per semester, which includes the Berlin public transportation semester ticket and student welfare services.
