HDK-Valand: Craft, Design, Film, Fine Art & Photography Guide

HDK-Valand - Academy of Art and Design at the University of Gothenburg is one of Northern Europe's most influential art and design faculties, uniting historic traditions in craft, furniture design, contemporary fine art, photography, and film production.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree of at least 180 ECTS credits in fine art, design, craft, photography, or equivalent artistic training.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in fine arts, design, or craft (minimum 240 ECTS) and an approved doctoral research proposal in artistic practice.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B (IELTS Academic 6.5 with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
A digital portfolio of 10-15 artistic works, accompanied by a written project statement (approx. 1,000 words) describing planned artistic inquiries.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee for non-EU/EEA applicants via Universityadmissions.se.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificate, CV, digital artistic portfolio, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is completely free (SEK 0) for Swedish and EU/EEA/Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is approximately SEK 240,000 to SEK 280,000 per academic year. The University of Gothenburg Axel Adler Scholarship provides full tuition waivers for international applicants.
