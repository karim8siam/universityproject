Institution Overview & Profile

The Duale Hochschule Schleswig-Holstein (DHSH) is a state-recognized, privately funded university of applied sciences with campuses in Kiel, Lübeck, and Flensburg, established by the business association Wirtschaftsakademie Schleswig-Holstein to deliver dual bachelor and master programs combining academic study with intensive on-the-job management training.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Private Dual University of Applied Sciences
Main CampusCampuses in Kiel (Headquarters), Lübeck, and Flensburg
AccreditationFIBAA / Schleswig-Holstein Ministry of Education, Science and Cultural Affairs
Admissions RouteDual study contract with an affiliated northern German enterprise
Language of InstructionGerman (CEFR C1 / TestDaF 4x4 / DSH-2)
Financial Aid & Grants100% employer tuition coverage plus monthly apprenticeship salary

Key Academic Strengths

  • Rigorous dual model alternating 10-week academic theory blocks with 12-week enterprise operational phases
  • Over 90% immediate post-graduation corporate hiring rate across partner corporations
  • Close partnerships with leading maritime, logistics, banking, and IT firms across northern Germany
  • Modern campuses equipped with enterprise simulation labs and digital collaboration spaces
