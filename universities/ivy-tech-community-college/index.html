Ivy Tech Community College

Ivy Tech · Indianapolis, Indiana, United States

Ivy Tech Community College is the nation’s largest singly accredited statewide community college system, delivering affordable career credentials, registered nursing degrees, advanced manufacturing, and direct university transfer pathways across Indiana.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Operating over 40 campus locations throughout Indiana, Ivy Tech serves as the primary engine for the state’s workforce development. Programs include AS transfer degrees with guaranteed admission to Indiana University and Purdue University, alongside the Ivy+ free textbook initiative.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Operates under open-door admissions for all high school graduates, GED recipients, and adult learners. Selective competitive admission applies to nursing, paramedic science, and dental hygiene cohorts.
