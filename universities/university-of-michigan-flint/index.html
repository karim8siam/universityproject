University of Michigan-Flint

The University of Michigan-Flint (UM-Flint) is a premier regional public research institution within the renowned University of Michigan system. Situated on a scenic 76-acre downtown campus along the Flint River, UM-Flint delivers over 120 undergraduate and 40 graduate degrees in nursing, engineering, physical therapy, management, and arts and sciences, granting prestigious University of Michigan degrees.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Flint, Michigan, USA

Institutional Overview

UM-Flint is classified as a Master's University with higher research activity. It is acclaimed for its College of Health Sciences, home to top-ranked Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) and Doctor of Anesthesia Practice (DrAP) programs, and the College of Innovation & Technology (CIT), which advances research in artificial intelligence, battery storage, and cybersecurity.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Undergraduate admission considers cumulative high school GPA (3.0+ average), college preparatory curriculum, and optional standardized tests (ACT/SAT). International applicants require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 84. UM-Flint offers substantial merit scholarships, including the True Blue Tuition Fellowship for Michigan residents and generous international freshman awards.
