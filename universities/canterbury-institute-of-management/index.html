Canterbury Institute of Management: Business Analytics, Accounting & IT Guide

With campuses in Darwin (Northern Territory), Sydney, and Melbourne, the Canterbury Institute of Management (CIM) is an accredited Australian higher education provider recognized by TEQSA. CIM delivers industry-focused undergraduate and graduate degrees in professional accounting, business analytics, information systems management, and hospitality leadership, preparing graduates for CPA Australia and ACS professional accreditation.

Collaborative Doctor of Philosophy research candidature exploring Northern Territory supply chain vulnerabilities, port logistics automation, and Indo-Pacific trade flows.

Twelve-week professional work-integrated learning placement placed with mid-tier accounting firms and corporate offices in Darwin and Sydney managing tax returns and bookkeeping.

Two-year academic research appointment investigating Australian Privacy Principles (APP) compliance in cloud data repositories and automated cyber threat intelligence.

Darwin Regional Student Study Support and Living Bursary

Regional financial support grant assisting international and domestic students who choose to relocate to and study in the Northern Territory.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Australian Bachelor's degree (or overseas equivalent) in any discipline with a minimum pass grade (equivalent to 60% or 2.5/4.0 GPA), plus relevant resume.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative doctoral pathways hosted in partnership with Australian university research institutes in business intelligence and regional migration economics.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (with no individual band below 6.0), TOEFL iBT 79, or Pearson PTE Academic 58.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for postgraduate research bursaries: 1,000-word statement describing analytical approach to enterprise resource planning or cloud supply chain security.
Application fee
$0 AUD online application fee for domestic and international students.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree graduation certificates, proof of English language proficiency, CV/resume, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Canterbury Institute of Management averages $18,000 to $22,000 AUD per academic year. CIM offers International Academic Excellence Scholarships providing up to $4,000 AUD per year in tuition fee reductions, alongside flexible trimester payment schedules.
