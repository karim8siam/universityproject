Rasmussen University - Kansas City/Overland Park

Rasmussen Overland Park · Overland Park, Kansas, United States

Rasmussen University is an accredited private higher education institution operating modern campuses across the United States, including its regional healthcare campus in Overland Park, Kansas, serving the greater Kansas City metropolitan area. Highly recognized for nursing, medical assisting, surgical technology, and health information management.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Rasmussen's Overland Park campus features modern healthcare simulation laboratories equipped with high-fidelity electronic manikins, mock surgical operating theaters, and sterile compounding suites. Students alternate interactive digital curriculum with clinical rotations at top Kansas City healthcare systems (HCA Midwest, Saint Luke's, AdventHealth).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school diploma or GED equivalency. Pre-nursing and allied health programs require the TEAS entrance exam or Rasmussen entrance assessment, background clearances, and immunization records for clinical hospital practicums.
