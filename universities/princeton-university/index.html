Princeton University (Princeton, New Jersey)

Founded in 1746 as the College of New Jersey, Princeton University is one of the world's most renowned research universities, ranked #1 among US National Universities for 14 consecutive years by U.S. News. With 75 Nobel laureates affiliated with its faculty and alumni, Princeton is globally famous for foundational discoveries in theoretical physics, mathematics, computer science (Alan Turing and John von Neumann), and public affairs. With one of the world's largest per-student endowments, Princeton provides 100% guaranteed full funding to all regular doctoral students for their entire multi-year degree. Princeton PhD in Computer Science (100% Fully Funded)

Five-year doctoral program covering foundational theory, algorithms, machine learning, systems, programming languages, security, computational biology, and computer architecture.

Princeton Centennial Fellowships & Doctoral Support in Mathematics

Princeton's legendary doctoral mathematics program, closely affiliated with the Institute for Advanced Study, producing Fields Medalists and leaders across all branches of modern mathematics.

Princeton Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) in Operations Research & Financial Engineering

Two-year research-based Master of Science in Engineering program combining stochastic modeling, convex optimization, machine learning, and quantitative finance.

Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL) Postdoctoral Research Associate

Two-year postdoctoral research position at the U.S. Department of Energy's Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL) advancing fusion energy science and spherical tokamak dynamics.

Princeton Molecular Biology Summer Undergraduate Research Program (MOL-SURP)

Nine-week immersive summer research program conducting mentored laboratory benchwork in Princeton's Lewis Thomas and Icahn Laboratories in genetics, structural biology, and cancer biology.

Princeton PhD in Economics (100% Fully Funded)

World-leading five-year doctoral program covering microeconomic theory, macroeconomics, international trade, finance, behavioral economics, and econometrics.

Princeton Society of Fellows in the Liberal Arts Postdoctoral Fellowship

Prestigious three-year postdoctoral appointment in Princeton's Society of Fellows, providing fellows with dedicated intellectual freedom to pursue scholarship and teach one seminar per semester.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Conferred Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with outstanding academic credentials (typically 3.7+ GPA for STEM and quantitative master's programs).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Conferred Bachelor's or Master's degree in an aligned discipline with exceptional scholastic achievement, proven research potential, and strong faculty alignment.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 8.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 105+ (speaking 28 recommended) for applicants whose native language is not English.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 3-page Statement of Academic Purpose detailing research questions, methodology, theoretical framework, and alignment with Princeton faculty.
Application fee
USD $75 application fee submitted through the Princeton Graduate School application portal; fee waivers automatically available based on financial need or partner programs.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, statement of academic purpose, and research writing sample.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Princeton provides 100% full financial support for all enrolled PhD students for all years of regular program enrollment (typically 5 years). This guaranteed package includes a 100% tuition and student healthcare fee waiver (worth over $62,000/year) plus an annual living stipend of USD $50,400 to $53,000 per year (among the highest doctoral stipends in the world).
