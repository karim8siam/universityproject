Emporia State University (ESU) is a historic comprehensive public university in the Flint Hills of Kansas, celebrated for the Teachers College, the National Teachers Hall of Fame, the School of Library and Information Management (oldest in the western US), and forensic science.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1863 as the Kansas State Normal School, ESU is the third-oldest public university in Kansas. Situated in Emporia on a picturesque 234-acre campus adjacent to the tallgrass prairie ecosystem of the Flint Hills, the university is organized into four colleges: The Teachers College, School of Library and Information Management (SLIM), School of Business and Technology, and College of Liberal Arts and Sciences.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.25 for guaranteed admission; test-optional for ACT/SAT). Graduate school admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 2.75-3.0, academic transcripts, statement of objectives, and letters of recommendation. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 72+ or IELTS 6.0+.
