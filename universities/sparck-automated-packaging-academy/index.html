Sparck B.V. Academic Logistics & Packaging Center

Academic overview & research priorities

With industrial engineering roots stretching back to 1924 in Drachten (Friesland), Sparck Technologies operates the Academic Logistics & Packaging Center. The center develops the world's most advanced 3D automated packaging machines (such as CVP Impack and CVP Everest) that measure each order, fold a custom cardboard box in seconds, and eliminate plastic bubble wrap. The institute partners with top European universities (TU Delft, University of Groningen) and retail giants to conduct applied research in warehouse robotics, machine vision, and carbon footprint reduction.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to higher technical certificates and industrial research fellowships requires a Bachelor of Engineering or Science in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Computer Science, or Logistics Management. Working engineers and university researchers apply directly through the Sparck Pro academic portal.
