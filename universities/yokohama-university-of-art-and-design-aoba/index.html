Yokohama University of Art and Design

Yokohama University of Art and Design (Yokohama Bijutsu Daigaku) is a creative arts and design university in Aoba-ku, Yokohama, renowned for fine art, visual communication design, character modeling, and industrial craft.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating an open-studio campus in northern Yokohama, the university fosters individual creative identity across its Department of Art and Design. Specialized courses span contemporary painting, sculpture, ceramic glass crafts, visual design, illustration, and dynamic media animation.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation credentials, practical artistic drawing/design examinations (Jitsugi Shiken) or an extensive portfolio review, accompanied by an interview. International students require JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese.
