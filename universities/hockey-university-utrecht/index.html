Academic overview & research priorities

Operating from the sports heart of Utrecht, Hockey University is the recognized academic training arm partnering with the Royal Dutch Hockey Federation (KNHB) and top Hoofdklasse hockey clubs. The university provides accredited coaching diplomas (KNHB levels 3, 4, and master coach), computer vision video analysis programs, and sports injury biomechanics research. Dutch field hockey is globally dominant, and the university formalizes its tactical methodologies for international coaches.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a completed secondary school education or higher vocational qualification (HBO) in sports science, physiotherapy, or physical education. Elite athletic experience or certified coaching credentials are required for advanced masterclasses. English-taught international modules require CEFR B2.
