Texas A&M University

Texas A&M · College Station, Texas, United States

Texas A&M University is the flagship land-grant, sea-grant, and space-grant research institution of Texas, located in College Station. Ranked among the premier Carnegie R1 public research powerhouses in the world with over $1.2B in annual research expenditures, Texas A&M is renowned for its world-class Dwight Look College of Engineering, College of Agriculture and Life Sciences, Mays Business School, and Cyclotron Institute.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Texas A&M comprises 17 colleges and schools, operating global research centers including the Texas A&M Engineering Experiment Station (TEES), the Texas A&M Transportation Institute (TTI), and the George H.W. Bush Presidential Library and Museum. It leads national and global discovery in aerospace engineering, nuclear engineering, agricultural genomics, veterinary medicine, and quantum physics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly competitive (top 10% Texas auto-admit, holistic review for national and international applicants). Graduate and doctoral applicants require a regionally accredited baccalaureate degree (3.0+ GPA recommended), competitive GRE scores (program-dependent), letters of recommendation, CV, and a rigorous statement of purpose.
