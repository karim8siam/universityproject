Venice International University: Globalization, Sustainability & Cultural Heritage Guide

Venice International University (VIU) is a prestigious global consortium of 20 world-leading universities located on the island of San Servolo in Venice, offering high-level interdisciplinary programs in environmental sustainability, global governance, and digital heritage.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Enrollment in or completion of a recognized graduate or master's program at a partner university, or accredited foreign Bachelor's/Master's with strong academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Active PhD candidacy in sustainability, social sciences, or humanities, with an approved research abstract.
Language proficiency
Fluent English (IELTS 6.5+, TOEFL iBT 88+, or proof of English-medium university instruction).
Research statement / Proposal
A 1-2 page statement explaining motivation, connection to Venice's ecological challenges, and interdisciplinary focus.
Application fee
Zero application fee for students from member universities; €50 for open summer institutes.
Required documentation
Official academic transcript, curriculum vitae, motivation statement, and one faculty letter of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is fully waived for students and doctoral candidates enrolled at any of VIU's 20 member institutions (e.g., Boston College, Duke, Tsinghua, Ca' Foscari). External international participants pay course fees ranging from €1,200 to €3,500 with accommodation bursaries available.
