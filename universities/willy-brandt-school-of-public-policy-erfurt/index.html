Willy Brandt School of Public Policy

The Willy Brandt School of Public Policy at the University of Erfurt is Germany’s oldest public policy school, offering an interdisciplinary, globally recognized Master of Public Policy (MPP) taught entirely in English.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating as a flagship institute within the University of Erfurt, the Brandt School specializes in international conflict management, development policy, public sector innovation, and sustainable ecological transitions, drawing students from over 50 countries.

Research admissions & international criteria

Applicants to the flagship MPP must hold a recognized Bachelor degree in social sciences, economics, law, or related disciplines, demonstrate at least one year of professional or civil society experience, and provide proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 90+ / IELTS 6.5+).
