University College London (UCL)

Founded in 1826 in Bloomsbury, London, University College London (UCL) is consistently ranked among the world's top 10 universities. As England's first university to admit students regardless of religion or gender, UCL generates over £1.7 billion in annual income and is associated with 30 Nobel laureates. Co-founding the national Alan Turing Institute and maintaining intimate partnerships with Google DeepMind in Kings Cross, UCL leads the world in Machine Learning, Neuroscience, Computational Statistics, and Architectural Design. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UCL Research Excellence Scholarships (RES)

UCL's premier doctoral fellowship awarded to 40 outstanding international and domestic PhD scholars annually, providing comprehensive tuition fee coverage and £22,278/year maintenance.

UCL PhD in Computer Science

Three to four-year doctoral research program inside UCL's Department of Computer Science in Bloomsbury, investigating Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, Cryptography, and Financial Computing.

UCL MSc in Machine Learning

World-renowned one-year taught master's degree (180 UK credits / 90 ECTS) at the Centre for Computational Statistics and Machine Learning (CSML), providing rigorous mathematical foundations in deep learning, graphical models, and reinforcement learning.

UCL MSc in Robotics and Computation

Cutting-edge one-year postgraduate master's program based at UCL Robotics in Bloomsbury, focusing on autonomous robotic manipulation, surgical robotics, vision-based navigation, and computational kinematics.

UCL Centre for Artificial Intelligence Research Placements

Frontier artificial intelligence research center in Bloomsbury, conducting foundational research in deep learning, multi-agent systems, and neural rendering in collaboration with the Alan Turing Institute.

UCL Engineering Summer Research Internships

Competitive summer research placement program, enabling undergraduate students to work directly alongside leading researchers on live scientific investigations in photonics, biomedical implants, and nanotech.

UCL Postgraduate Teaching Assistantships (PGTA Pathway)

Departmental academic tutoring positions for enrolled doctoral researchers, providing pedagogical training and hourly remuneration for supervising undergraduate lab classes, leading tutorials, and grading coursework.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK Upper Second-Class (2:1) Honours Bachelor's degree or international equivalent (typically 80%+ or 3.3/4.0 GPA) in computer science, mathematics, engineering, or physical sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with Merit or UK First-Class Honours Bachelor's degree with demonstrable scientific research ability, publications, or thesis excellence.
Language proficiency
UCL Standard/Good/Advanced English levels: Level 2 requirement: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all subscores) or TOEFL iBT 96 overall (minimum 24 in reading/writing, 22 in speaking/listening).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 3-page research proposal detailing research hypothesis, novelty, methodology, and identified UCL academic research group.
Application fee
£90 application fee for postgraduate taught courses; free application for postgraduate doctoral research (PhD).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 2 academic reference letters, curriculum vitae, personal statement of purpose, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate taught master's programs ranges from £35,000 to £42,500 per year for overseas international students (£16,500 to £20,500 for UK home students). For doctoral researchers, the UCL Research Excellence Scholarships (RES) completely cover all tuition fees (home or overseas) and provide an annual tax-free stipend of £22,278 for 3 years.
