Academic overview & research priorities

Headquartered in Kaiserslautern with major instructional centers in Landstuhl and Ramstein, UMGC Europe is regionally accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE). Offering flexible hybrid, evening, and accelerated classroom courses, UMGC specializes in applied technology, national security, emergency management, and management degrees tailored for international professionals and military personnel.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants require an accredited secondary school diploma or equivalent (GED), official academic transcripts, and English language proficiency. Graduate degrees in Cybersecurity and Cloud Computing require a recognized bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0. Federal tuition assistance, GI Bill benefits, and institutional military-community scholarships are widely accessible.
