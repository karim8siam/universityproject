Academic overview & excellence

Founded in 1898 by the Vincentian Order, DePaul University operates two vibrant urban campuses in Chicago: the historic Lincoln Park Campus and the Loop Campus in Chicago's financial and legal center. DePaul comprises ten colleges and schools, notably the Jarvis College of Computing and Digital Media (one of the largest computing colleges in the nation), the Driehaus College of Business (home of the nation's first entrepreneurship center), and the nationally top-ranked Theatre School and School of Cinematic Arts.

Research admissions & criteria

Freshman admission considers high school GPA (average admitted 3.7 unweighted), academic rigor, and optional standardized test scores. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA. International applicants submit TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5.
