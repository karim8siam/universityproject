Midway University

Midway Univ · Midway, Kentucky, United States

Midway University is a private university located on a 200-acre working horse farm in Midway, Kentucky, in the heart of Bluegrass horse country. Renowned globally for equine studies, nursing, pre-veterinary science, and business management.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Midway operates an on-campus equine center with an indoor riding arena, pastures, jumping courses, and breeding barns. Key divisions include the School of Health Sciences and the School of Business, Equine and Sport Studies.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5 recommended) under test-optional review. Equine studies and nursing applicants must submit specialized program applications and clinical immunization records.
