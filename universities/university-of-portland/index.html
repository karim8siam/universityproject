University of Portland

UP · Portland, Oregon, United States

The University of Portland (UP) is an elite private Catholic university established in 1901, perched on a bluff overlooking the Willamette River in Portland, Oregon. Celebrated for small-class undergraduate and graduate mentorship, top-ranked programs in civil and mechanical engineering through the Shiley School of Engineering, distinguished nursing education, and ethical business leadership, UP is a Pacific Northwest academic cornerstone.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UP comprises the College of Arts and Sciences, the Donald P. Shiley School of Engineering, the Pamplin School of Business, the School of Education, and the School of Nursing and Health Innovations. The university operates the Shiley Engineering labs and the Beauchamp Recreation and Wellness Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate applicants. Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
