University of Maine

UMaine · Orono, Maine, United States

The University of Maine (UMaine), established in 1865 on Marsh Island in Orono, Maine, is the state's flagship public land-grant and sea-grant Carnegie R1 research university. UMaine is globally renowned for the Advanced Structures and Composites Center (ASCC)—home to the world's largest prototype 3D printer and offshore floating wind turbine technology—the Climate Change Institute, forest bioproducts, and marine ecology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMaine consists of five colleges, including the College of Earth, Life, and Health Sciences, the Maine College of Engineering and Computing, the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, and the Graduate School. It operates the Darling Marine Center on the mid-coast and over 14,000 acres of research forests across Maine.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are evaluated through the Common Application (rolling admissions; test-optional; average GPA 3.4+). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited bachelor's or master's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and clear research interests matching faculty labs.
