Oregon State University-Cascades

OSU-Cascades · Bend, Oregon, United States

Oregon State University-Cascades is a modern, net-zero energy branch campus of Oregon State University located in the high-desert outdoor recreation hub of Bend, Oregon. Acclaimed for outdoor products engineering, energy systems engineering, tourism and outdoor leadership, and computer science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OSU-Cascades operates Tykeson Hall, Edward J. Ray Hall (with high-tech engineering maker labs), and the Outdoor Products Innovation Lab. Surrounded by the Cascade Mountains, the campus serves as a living laboratory for renewable microgrids, mass-timber sustainability, and outdoor apparel prototyping.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions follow the holistic standards of Oregon State University, reviewing high school academic transcripts, coursework strength, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Transfer students from Central Oregon Community College benefit from seamless dual-enrollment pathways.
