Walsh College

Walsh · Troy, Michigan, United States

Walsh College is an independent, non-profit business and technology institution founded in 1922, situated in Troy, Michigan, within the vibrant Metro Detroit commercial corridor. Renowned for practical, practitioner-taught business education, Walsh is celebrated for its premier Master of Accountancy with outstanding CPA pass rates, cybersecurity at the Cyber Lab, and automotive corporate finance.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Walsh specializes exclusively in upper-division undergraduate and graduate education across two main divisions: the School of Business and the School of Technology. Walsh operates the state-of-the-art Center for Cybersecurity Leadership and Defense and the Finance Lab featuring Bloomberg terminals.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate transfer admissions evaluate prior college coursework (minimum 60 semester credits or associate degree with GPA 2.0+). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, professional resume, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
