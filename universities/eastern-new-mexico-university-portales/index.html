Eastern New Mexico University

Eastern New Mexico University (ENMU) is New Mexico’s largest regional comprehensive university and a designated Hispanic-Serving Institution in Portales, noted for speech-language pathology, forensic anthropology, wildlife management, and digital media arts.

Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission, ENMU provides high-quality, affordable education across the High Plains. The university operates the Miles Mineral Museum, the Blackwater Draw National Historic Landmark archaeological research center, and a national-caliber speech-language pathology clinic serving rural southwestern communities.

Research admissions & international criteria

Accessible undergraduate admissions requiring high school graduation with 2.50+ GPA; ENMU is test-optional. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor’s degree with 2.80+ GPA and relevant departmental prerequisites.
