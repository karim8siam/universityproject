Deep Springs College

Deep Springs College is an iconic, highly selective two-year liberal arts institution situated on an isolated cattle ranch and alfalfa farm in California’s high desert, built on the three foundational pillars of academics, student self-governance, and manual labor.

Academic overview & research priorities

Deep Springs admits only 12 to 15 students annually, awarding every matriculant a full two-year scholarship covering 100% of tuition, room, and board. Students engage in rigorous seminars in political philosophy, literature, and geology while working at least 20 hours per week operating the ranch and governing all college affairs.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions are exceptionally competitive through a two-stage holistic application: Stage 1 involves extensive philosophical essays and academic transcripts; Stage 2 invites finalists to spend several days living and working at the valley campus for faculty and student body interviews.
