Tokyo Polytechnic University (Tokyo Kogei), based at its creative campus in Nakano and engineering campus in Atsugi, is Japan's founding institution for photographic arts, manga, animation, game design, and optical imaging engineering.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1923 as the Konishi Professional School of Photography (by the founder of Konica), Tokyo Polytechnic University is a pioneering fusion of technological engineering and media arts. Operating the Faculty of Media Arts at its flagship urban campus in Nakano—Tokyo's celebrated pop-culture hub—and the Faculty of Engineering in Atsugi, Tokyo Kogei has educated generations of Japan's top anime directors, manga artists, game designers, and optical engineers.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission to the Faculty of Media Arts (Photography, Manga, Animation, Game Design, Interactive Media) requires high school completion, submission of a creative portfolio (drawings, manga chapters, short animations, or digital media), practical drawing assessment, and JLPT N2 Japanese proficiency. Master's degrees require an accredited bachelor's degree, comprehensive thesis or artistic project proposal, and interview.
