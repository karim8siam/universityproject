Kungl. Vitterhetsakademien: Humanities Research, Archaeology & Antiquities Guide

Founded in 1753 by Queen Louisa Ulrika, Kungl. Vitterhetsakademien (The Royal Swedish Academy of Letters, History and Antiquities) is one of Sweden's most prestigious royal academies, dedicated to advancing scholarly excellence, archaeological excavations, historical heritage preservation, and doctoral research fellowships in the humanities.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Fellowships and project grants require a Master's degree or ongoing doctoral affiliation at an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Postdoctoral fellowships and research prizes require a completed PhD in archaeology, history, classical studies, art history, or philology.
Language proficiency
Swedish and English (publications and grant proposals accepted in Swedish, English, French, and German).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive scholarly research project proposal (5-10 pages) with itemized research budget and primary archive citations.
Application fee
Free grant application via the Academy's research portal.
Required documentation
Doctoral dissertation summary, complete academic curriculum vitae, list of peer-reviewed publications, and letters of reference from two senior scholars.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

The Academy does not charge tuition; it is a learned royal research society that funds and awards extensive research grants, postdoctoral fellowships (SEK 300,000 to SEK 500,000 annually), and international conference travel stipends in the humanities and social sciences.
