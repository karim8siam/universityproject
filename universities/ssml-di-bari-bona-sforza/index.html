Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Named after Bona Sforza, Duchess of Bari and Queen of Poland, the Scuola Superiore per Mediatori Linguistici (SSML) Bona Sforza in Bari is a state-accredited university institute of linguistic mediation authorized by the Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR) to award recognized Laurea degrees in diplomatic translation, conference interpreting, and international trade communication.

Institution type
State-Recognized University Institute of Linguistic Mediation (MUR accredited)

Puglia Region ADiSU scholarships, merit-based tuition waivers, and Erasmus+ mobilities

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Diplomatic translation and international institutional relations
  • Simultaneous and consecutive conference interpreting
  • Linguistic mediation for international commerce and maritime logistics
  • Cross-cultural digital communication and multilingual content localization

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Corso di Laurea Triennale in Mediazione Linguistica (Classe L-12) (Undergraduate Degree) — Three-year official Italian university degree equivalent to university diplomas in modern languages, focusing on two foreign languages plus professional translation.
  • Master di Primo Livello in Traduzione Giuridica ed Economica (Postgraduate Master) — Specialized master's program training sworn translators and legal-economic interpreters for international courts and multinational corporations.
  • Corso di Perfezionamento in Interpretariato di Conferenza (Professional Certificate) — Advanced simultaneous booth interpreting training with international conference simulations.
