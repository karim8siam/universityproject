Montreat College

Montreat College · Montreat, North Carolina, United States

Montreat College is a private Christian liberal arts college in Montreat, North Carolina, nestled in the Blue Ridge Mountains. Known nationally for its pioneering cybersecurity program, outdoor education, business, and environmental science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Montreat College operates the Center for Cybersecurity, the School of Arts and Sciences, and the Adult and Graduate Studies division. Its cyber lab is recognized as a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense by the NSA.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require high school graduation or GED with minimum 2.5 GPA, personal statement outlining faith and educational objectives, and one academic recommendation.
