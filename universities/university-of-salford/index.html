University of Salford: Acoustics, Energy House Labs, Cyber Security & MediaCityUK Guide

The University of Salford is a pioneering British civic university in Greater Manchester, famous for Europe's leading Acoustics and Audio Engineering research laboratories, its revolutionary Energy House 2.0 climate test facility, and its media campus at MediaCityUK alongside the BBC and ITV.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:2 or 2:1 honours degree or international equivalent in engineering, audio technology, computer science, or architecture.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with good standing in a congruent discipline and an approved 3-4 page research proposal.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 to 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band) or equivalent SELT certificate.
Research statement / Proposal
A 1,500-word research proposal describing experimental methodology, acoustic/energy laboratory needs, and literature review.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the university direct application portal.
Required documentation
Degree certificates, transcripts, two academic references, personal statement, and passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition averages £15,900 to £18,500 per academic year. The Salford International Excellence Scholarship awards discounts of up to £3,500. Fully funded doctoral studentships through EPSRC and industry partners cover full tuition and UKRI stipends.
