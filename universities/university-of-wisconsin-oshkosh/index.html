University of Wisconsin–Oshkosh

UW Oshkosh · Oshkosh, Wisconsin, United States

The University of Wisconsin–Oshkosh (UW Oshkosh / UWO) is a premier public doctoral-granting university established in 1871 along the shores of Lake Winnebago and the Fox River in Oshkosh, Wisconsin. Celebrated as a national leader in campus sustainability—operating the nation's first commercial dry anaerobic biodigester—UW Oshkosh is equally renowned for nursing, aviation management in the home of EAA AirVenture, and business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UW Oshkosh spans four colleges: the College of Letters and Science, College of Nursing, College of Business, and College of Education and Human Services. Key research hubs include the Environmental Research and Innovation Center (ERIC), the Environmental Sciences Center, and the University of Wisconsin Oshkosh Aviation Program.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students, evaluating high school GPA (minimum 2.5). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
