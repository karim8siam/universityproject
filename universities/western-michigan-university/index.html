Western Michigan University

WMU · Kalamazoo, Michigan, United States

Western Michigan University is a comprehensive public Carnegie R2 doctoral research university founded in 1903 in Kalamazoo, Michigan. Internationally acclaimed for the College of Aviation—one of the largest and most prestigious collegiate flight training and aeronautical science programs in North America—as well as the College of Engineering and Applied Sciences, paper and chemical engineering, and blind rehabilitation, WMU is a vital driver of Michigan's advanced manufacturing and aerospace sectors.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WMU spans seven degree-granting colleges: the College of Aviation (located in Battle Creek), College of Engineering and Applied Sciences, Haworth College of Business, College of Health and Human Services, College of Arts and Sciences, College of Education and Human Development, and the College of Fine Arts. The university operates the Business Technology and Research (BTR) Park.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic records with test-optional reviews. Graduate doctoral programs offer Graduate Teaching and Research Assistantships ($18,000–$25,000/year plus full tuition scholarships). Master's and doctoral applicants submit transcripts, statement of purpose, three recommendation letters, and English proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
