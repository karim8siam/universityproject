Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Stiftung Universität Hildesheim is an innovative public university organized as a foundation under public law in Lower Saxony. Renowned for its UNESCO Chair in Cultural Policy, pioneering creative writing programs, translation science, and machine learning information systems.

Institution type
Public University organized as a Foundation (Stiftungshochschule)
Campus location
Main Campus (Marienburger Platz), Kulturcampus Domäne Marienburg, and Bühler-Campus
Accreditation
Ministry of Science and Culture of Lower Saxony / ZEvA
Admissions channel
Winter semester (July 15) and Summer semester (January 15)
Instruction language
German & English (international Master's programs taught in English)
Financial aid & support
Deutschlandstipendium, Hilde Domin Programme, Lower Saxony Doctoral Grants

Institutional Research Strengths

  • UNESCO Chair in Cultural Policy for the Arts in Development
  • Kulturcampus Domäne Marienburg—a medieval castle transformed into an arts center
  • Pioneering Institute for Creative Writing and Cultural Journalism
  • Information Systems and Machine Learning Lab (ISMLL) with high citation impact

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Data Analytics (Graduate Degree) — Top-ranked English-taught master's focusing on deep learning, predictive modeling, big data architectures, and computer vision.
  • Master of Arts in Kulturvermittlung (Cultural Mediation & Arts Management) (Graduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary master's combining museum curation, theatre dramaturgy, cultural diplomacy, and arts administration.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Internationale Kommunikation und Übersetzen (Translation & International Communication) (Undergraduate Degree) — Professional degree preparing translators, technical writers, and terminologists in German, English, and Spanish/French.
