Canterbury Institute of Management: Business Information Systems, Accounting & Project Management Guide

Canterbury Institute of Management (CIM) is an accredited Australian higher education provider delivering TEQSA-registered Bachelor degrees in Business, Information Systems, and Accounting with modern campuses in Darwin (Northern Territory) and Sydney.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of an Australian Year 12 senior secondary certificate, AQF Associate Degree, or recognized overseas equivalent with satisfactory grades.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral programs; focus on accredited undergraduate higher education degrees.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each skill) or recognized equivalent PTE Academic score.
Research statement / Proposal
Genuine Student (GS) statement detailing career vision and choice of regional Darwin study.
Application fee
AUD $200 international enrollment application fee.
Required documentation
Certified high school or post-secondary transcripts, passport bio-data page, English test certificate, and financial capacity evidence.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition averages AUD $14,500 to $18,000 per academic year for international students. Regional study incentives for the Northern Territory, merit scholarships of up to AUD $4,000, and flexible per-semester installment scheduling are available.
