Ulm University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1967 as a specialized university of medicine and natural sciences, Ulm University sits atop the Oberer Eselsberg within the bustling “Science City” (Wissenschaftsstadt Ulm). Educating over 10,000 students, Uni Ulm regularly ranks among the world’s top young universities. It anchors landmark collaborative research institutes including the Helmholtz Institute Ulm (HIU) for Electrochemical Energy Storage, the POLiS Cluster of Excellence for post-lithium batteries (joint with KIT), and the Centre for Quantum BioSciences (ZQB). Ulm’s clinical medical faculty and actuarial finance faculties rank among the strongest in Europe.
