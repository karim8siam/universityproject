Wageningen Bioveterinary Research: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located in Lelystad, Flevoland, Wageningen Bioveterinary Research (WBVR) is an integral component of Wageningen University & Research (ranked #1 in the world for Agriculture and Forestry). WBVR serves as the official national reference institute for statutory animal infectious diseases (such as Avian Influenza, African Swine Fever, and Foot-and-Mouth Disease) and emerging zoonotic threats. Operating high-containment biosafety level 3 and 4 (BSL-3/BSL-4) laboratories, WBVR leads global veterinary science in animal vaccine development, antimicrobial resistance surveillance, diagnostics, and vector-borne virology in close partnership with the European Commission and WHO.
