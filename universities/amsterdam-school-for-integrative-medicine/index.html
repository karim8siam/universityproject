Amsterdam School for Integrative Medicine: Lifestyle Healthcare & Clinical Research

The Amsterdam School for Integrative Medicine & Health is an accredited Dutch professional institute pioneering evidence-based integrative healthcare, mind-body clinical interventions, lifestyle medicine, clinical phytotherapy, and patient-centered chronic disease prevention.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's or Master's degree in medicine, nursing, physiotherapy, clinical psychology, pharmacy, or biological sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctorates; conducts clinical research trials in collaboration with Dutch university medical centers (Amsterdam UMC).
Language proficiency
Proficiency in Dutch or English depending on module track (minimum IELTS 6.5 or equivalent for international masterclasses).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of clinical intent describing integration of evidence-based complementary therapeutics into professional practice.
Application fee
€100 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Medical or healthcare practitioner diploma, professional registration (BIG-register if applicable), resume/CV, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for professional postgraduate diploma programs averages €3,500 to €6,800 per academic year. Dutch healthcare continuing professional development (CPD) allowances, accredited foundation bursaries, and clinical research grants are accessible.
