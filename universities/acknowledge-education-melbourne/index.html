Acknowledge Education: Nursing, Social Work, Civil Engineering & Community Services Guide

Acknowledge Education (including Stott's College and Melbourne Language Centre) is an established Australian tertiary education provider with over 135 years of history, delivering accredited Bachelor and Master degrees in Nursing, Social Work, and Civil Engineering across Melbourne, Sydney, Perth, and Brisbane.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of an Australian Bachelor's degree or recognized overseas equivalent in a congruent field of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on accredited professional Bachelor and Master qualifications.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 to 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in writing and other bands; 7.0 each band for Nursing).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose (500-800 words) highlighting clinical or engineering aspirations in Australia.
Application fee
AUD $250 international student application processing fee.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificate, passport bio-page, English proficiency evidence, and GTE documentation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate and postgraduate tuition averages AUD $14,000 to $22,000 per academic year depending on degree stream. Academic merit fee remissions, early-bird bonuses, and flexible term installment plans are available for international students.
