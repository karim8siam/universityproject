Institutional Overview

University of West London (UWL), celebrated as 'The Career University', is a premier public university located across thriving campuses in St Mary's Road (Ealing) and Paragon (Brentford). Known for its London College of Music (LCM), Claude Littner Business School, and Heathrow College of Hospitality and Tourism, UWL achieves industry-leading student satisfaction and top graduate employability rankings across the UK capital.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Ranked #1 university in London for student satisfaction (National Student Survey)
  • Home to the historic London College of Music (LCM) founded in 1887 with world-class recording studios
  • Prime aviation and tourism hub adjacent to London Heathrow Airport offering full-flight simulator training
  • Cutting-edge School of Computing and Engineering with dedicated CISCO networks and cyber security testing labs
