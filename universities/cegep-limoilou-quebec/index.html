Cégep Limoilou: Engineering Tech, Geomatics, Health Sciences & Trades Guide

Cégep Limoilou is a historic public college in Québec City, renowned for advanced technological education, applied research centers (CERFO for forestry and geomatics, and CMQ for metallurgy), mechanical engineering technology, and comprehensive pre-university science tracks.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of Canadian Grade 12 or secondary school diploma (DES) with specific prerequisites in advanced mathematics, chemistry, and physics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Public cégep offering pre-university DEC (2 years) and technical vocational DEC (3 years); university transfer agreements in place with Université Laval.
Language proficiency
French proficiency (DELF/DALF B2 or TCF Tout Public with minimum 400 points) as primary instruction is in French.
Research statement / Proposal
Letter of intent describing educational project and technical career alignment in Québec.
Application fee
CAD $30 application fee via SRAM (Service régional d'admission du Montréal) or SRACQ.
Required documentation
Secondary school transcripts, graduation diploma, French language test certificate, birth certificate, and valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for Québec residents. International student tuition is approximately CAD $14,500 to $19,500 per academic year depending on technical laboratory streams. Québec international mobility scholarships and Cégep Limoilou foundation bursaries are awarded annually.
