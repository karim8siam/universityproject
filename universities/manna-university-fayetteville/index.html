Manna University

Manna Univ · Fayetteville, North Carolina, United States

Manna University is a private Christian biblical and leadership university in Fayetteville, North Carolina. Manna offers accredited degree programs in Christian counseling, divinity, intercultural studies, and organizational leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Manna University features the College of Divinity, School of Christian Counseling, and the Center for Leadership Development. Programs are offered on-campus and through flexible online formats tailored for community pastors, non-profit directors, and military chaplains.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school diploma or GED, personal statement of faith and vocational objectives, and character references. Graduate theology and leadership programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with minimum 2.5 GPA.
