Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

The École nationale d'administration publique (ENAP) is a specialized university of the Université du Québec network dedicated exclusively to the study and modern practice of public administration, public policy analysis, and state governance across Francophone and international public sectors.

Institution type
Public Specialized Graduate University (Université du Québec)
Campus location
Québec City, Montréal, Gatineau, Saguenay, and Trois-Rivières
Accreditation
Ministère de l'Enseignement supérieur du Québec / NASPAA Affiliate
Admissions channel
Trimester intakes (Fall, Winter, Summer terms)
Instruction language
French (principal language of instruction and examination)
Financial aid & support
Fondation de l'ENAP Fellowships, Mitacs Globalink, Québec Government Aid

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Sole university in Canada exclusively focused on graduate public administration
  • Master of Public Administration (MPA) for executives and public policy analysts
  • Centre for Research on Public Governance and State Modernization (CERGO)
  • Extensive international capacity-building programs across Francophone Africa and the Americas

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Maîtrise en administration publique (MPA - Public Administration) (Graduate Degree) — Flagship master's degree preparing executives and analysts in public budgeting, administrative law, and human capital strategy.
  • Doctorat en administration publique (Ph.D.) (Doctoral Degree) — Advanced research doctorate investigating contemporary governance transformations, digital government, and policy outcomes.
  • Diplôme d'études supérieures spécialisées (DESS) en gestion publique (Postgraduate Diploma) — One-year applied professional program focused on operational management in municipal, provincial, and healthcare agencies.
