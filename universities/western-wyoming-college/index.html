Western Wyoming College

Western Wyoming · Rock Springs, Wyoming, United States

Western Wyoming Community College is an innovative public institution situated in Rock Springs, Wyoming, surrounded by the Red Desert and the Green River Basin. Famous for its indoor enclosed campus architecture and life-size dinosaur fossil displays, Western delivers outstanding applied associate degrees, bachelor of applied science programs, and university transfer pathways in energy engineering, nursing, and paleontology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Rock Springs · Energy, Natural Resources & Premier Transfer College. Western Wyoming Community College is an innovative public institution situated in Rock Springs, Wyoming, surrounded by the Red Desert and the Green River Basin. Famous for its indoor enclosed campus architecture and life-size dinosaur fossil displays, Western delivers outstanding applied associate degrees, bachelor of applied science programs, and university transfer pathways in energy engineering, nursing, and paleontology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Open admission policy with high school diploma, home school completion, or GED credential. Specific health sciences and technical programs require selective admission standards. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 5.5+ for international candidates without prior English-medium secondary schooling.
