Teikyō Junior College

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1962, Teikyo Junior College provides intensive two-year career-oriented and academic degree programs across the Department of Early Childhood Care and Education, the Department of Life Sciences (Food & Nutrition), and the Department of Contemporary Business and Information Studies. The college integrates rigorous practical training in affiliated kindergartens, clinical nutritional centers, and medical administration software labs, preparing graduates for professional certifications and direct transfer into four-year faculties of Teikyo University.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation or recognized secondary credential. Candidates are evaluated via general entrance examinations, recommendation interviews, and career objective assessments. International applicants must demonstrate Japanese language competence (JLPT N2 or equivalent).
