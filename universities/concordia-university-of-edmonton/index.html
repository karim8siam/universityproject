Concordia University of Edmonton: Cybersecurity, Information Security & Biology Guide

Founded in 1921 in Edmonton, Alberta, Concordia University of Edmonton (CUE) is an accredited public boutique university recognized globally for expertise in information technology security, artificial intelligence, cellular biology, and environmental science. CUE's Centre for Applied Artificial Intelligence and cybersecurity laboratories partner with Canadian technology leaders to provide rigorous graduate education and practical apprenticeships.

CUE Presidential Graduate Excellence Scholarship in Information Security

Merit-based award supporting exceptional graduate scholars entering master's degree programs in Information Systems Security and Applied Artificial Intelligence at CUE.

Master of Science in Information Systems Security Management (MISSM)

Industry-leading two-year master's program providing comprehensive mastery in digital forensics, cloud infrastructure protection, ethical hacking, and cybersecurity governance.

Joint Doctoral Research Fellowship in Applied Cryptography & Distributed Systems

Doctoral research fellowship conducted through CUE's Centre for Applied Artificial Intelligence investigating post-quantum cryptographic primitives and zero-knowledge proofs.

North Saskatchewan River Aquatic Ecosystem and Riparian Health Project

Field-based scientific investigation monitoring benthic macroinvertebrate communities, microplastic deposition, and riverine ecosystem recovery across central Alberta.

Alberta Artificial Intelligence Industry Practicum & Cyber Lab Residency

Four-month immersive practicum placing students in municipal cybersecurity security operations centres (SOC), energy sector IT departments, and technology start-ups.

CUE Research Fellowship in Biodiversity Genomics & Wetland Conservation

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment deploying environmental DNA (eDNA) metabarcoding to quantify amphibian and avian species richness in Western Canadian boreal wetlands.

Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES) at Concordia

Provincial merit award recognizing outstanding domestic and international students pursuing master's and doctoral degrees across all disciplines at CUE.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year baccalaureate degree (or international equivalent) with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0 in the final 60 credits of undergraduate study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral research pathways; direct research transfers via joint Alberta doctoral consortium frameworks.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (with no individual band below 6.0), TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 21 in all sub-scores), or Duolingo English Test 115+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate research programs: 1,500-word statement of intent specifying proposed research questions in cybersecurity, machine learning, or environmental science.
Application fee
$100 CAD non-refundable application fee for Canadian applicants; $150 CAD for international applicants.
Required documentation
Official post-secondary academic transcripts, two confidential letters of recommendation from academic professors, curriculum vitae, and statement of research intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Concordia University of Edmonton is approximately $820 CAD per 3-credit course for domestic students and $1,450 CAD per 3-credit course for international candidates. Comprehensive entrance scholarships, Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarships (AGES) up to $12,000 CAD, and research assistantships provide generous financial backing.
