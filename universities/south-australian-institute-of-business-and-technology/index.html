South Australian Institute of Business & Technology: Degrees & Guide

Located on the University of South Australia's City East campus in Adelaide, the South Australian Institute of Business & Technology (SAIBT) is an accredited higher education provider offering direct bachelor pathway programs into UniSA. Specializing in engineering, information technology, health science, and business, SAIBT provides small-group academic transition.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

SAIBT Early Bird & Academic Merit Pathway Bursary

Tuition assistance bursary awarded to incoming international students enrolling in diploma pathway programs leading into the University of South Australia.

Diploma of Engineering (Articulated into UniSA Bachelor of Engineering Honours)

Eight-course higher education diploma granting direct second-year entry into UniSA honours bachelor programs across civil, electrical, mechanical, and mechatronic engineering.

UniSA-SAIBT Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Cyber-Physical Defense Systems

Four-year joint doctoral research fellowship conducting advanced cybersecurity and autonomous defense systems research in South Australia's defense precinct.

Lot Fourteen Innovation Precinct Applied AI Software Project

Research internship located in Adelaide's Lot Fourteen innovation precinct working alongside the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) on computer vision.

Adelaide International Business Enterprise & Trade Practicum

Twelve-week professional practicum embedded within South Australian commercial enterprises analyzing market export logistics, supply chains, and customer relationship analytics.

Adelaide Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Space Systems & Small-Sat Design

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment contributing to the Australian Space Agency ecosystem, designing payload integration and telemetry for low-Earth orbit CubeSats.

Senior Lecturer in Academic Literacy and Tertiary Transition

Permanent academic leadership role designing bridge curricula, diagnostic language support, and mentoring international students transitioning into degree studies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of an Australian equivalent Bachelor's degree or higher education diploma with demonstrated academic merit.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Articulated postgraduate research programs require admission via the University of South Australia with accredited honours or master's degrees.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 5.5 to 6.0 overall depending on diploma stream (or TOEFL iBT 60–78).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of purpose detailing chosen degree pathway and academic progression timeline.
Application fee
AUD $0 (Free direct online application via the SAIBT portal).
Required documentation
Certified high school or post-secondary transcripts, English language proficiency documentation, copy of passport, and visa history.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for standard 8-course academic diplomas range between AUD $28,400 and AUD $34,800 total. SAIBT and the University of South Australia offer competitive international scholarships, including the International Student Early Bird Bursary (AUD $2,000) and UniSA International Merit Scholarships covering 15% to 50% of articulated bachelor tuition.
