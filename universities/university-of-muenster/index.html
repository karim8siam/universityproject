University of Münster: Excellence Clusters, Doctoral Fellowships & Master's

The University of Münster (Universität Münster) is one of Germany's largest and most prestigious research universities, renowned for its Clusters of Excellence in Mathematics and Multiscale Imaging, world-class battery research at MEET, nanosciences, and humanities.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

MEET Battery Research Center Doctoral Fellowship (TV-L E13)

Fully funded 3-year doctoral appointment at MEET investigating next-generation solid-state lithium metal batteries, electrolyte stability, and sustainable recycling.

Mathematics Münster Graduate School (MMGS) PhD Fellowship

Doctoral funding from the Cluster of Excellence 'Mathematics Münster: Dynamics - Geometry - Structure' supporting brilliant young mathematicians.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Geoinformatics & Spatial Data Science

English-taught graduate curriculum exploring machine learning for earth observation, spatial sensor networks, open geodata standards, and autonomous navigation.

Cells in Motion Interfaculty Centre (CiM) Molecular Imaging Assistantship

Graduate research project examining cellular dynamics, vascular morphogenesis, and leukocyte extravasation using high-end intravital microscopy.

Fraunhofer Research Institution for Battery Cell Production (FFB) Industrial Practicum

Hands-on engineering practicum at the Fraunhofer FFB pilot plant in Münster mastering automated electrode slurry coating, cell assembly, and quality verification.

Cluster of Excellence Religion & Politics Postdoctoral Fellowship

Prestigious fellowship at Germany's leading interdisciplinary cluster examining religious pluralism, conflict mediation, and political secularization across eras.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Physics with Specialization in Nanophysics & Photonics

Comprehensive master's program taught in English with direct laboratory access to the Center for Soft Nanoscience (SoN) and the Center for Nanotechnology (CeNTech).

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) with above-average grades (German grade 2.5 or better, or international equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or equivalent (Staatsexamen, Diplom) with at least 'good' (gut) academic distinction and formal supervisor acceptance.
Language proficiency
English-taught programs require CEFR B2/C1 (TOEFL iBT 90+ or IELTS 6.5+); German-taught programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral admissions: detailed 5–10 page research exposé with timeline and formal supervisory agreement (Promotionsvereinbarung).
Application fee
€0 (no application fee for direct university portal submission).
Required documentation
Certified degree diplomas, diploma supplement with transcript of records, CV in Europass format, and letter of motivation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 100% free for all domestic and international students at the University of Münster. Students only pay a semester social contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €330 per semester, which includes a regional public transport ticket throughout North Rhine-Westphalia.
