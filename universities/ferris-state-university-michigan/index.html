Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1884 by Woodbridge N. Ferris in Big Rapids (with a major health campus on Grand Rapids' 'Medical Mile'), Ferris State is unique in Michigan for combining vocational technical mastery with elite professional doctorates. The university houses the Michigan College of Optometry (the only optometry school in Michigan), one of only two plastics engineering technology bachelor's programs in the US, and national Centers of Excellence in cyber intelligence.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5; test-optional for ACT/SAT). The Michigan College of Optometry requires a bachelor's degree, prerequisite science coursework, and Optometry Admission Test (OAT) scores. The College of Pharmacy requires pre-pharmacy prerequisites and PharmCAS application. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
