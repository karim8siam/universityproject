TU Dortmund University: Chemical Engineering, Computer Science & Statistics Portal

TU Dortmund University (Technische Universität Dortmund), founded in 1968 in Germany's Ruhr industrial heartland, is a leading technical research university celebrated for having Europe's premier Faculty of Statistics, world-class Biochemical and Chemical Engineering (BCI), and foundational computer science research.

TU Dortmund Research School Excellence Doctoral Fellowship

Prestigious doctoral fellowship supporting talented international researchers in machine learning, biochemical engineering, or mathematical statistics at the TU Dortmund Graduate Center.

Master of Science in Data Science & Statistical Machine Learning

Unique interdisciplinary English-taught Master of Science offered jointly by the Faculties of Statistics, Computer Science, and Mathematics, exploring high-dimensional statistics, deep learning, and big data systems.

Salaried Doctoral Researcher (TV-L E13) in Bioprocess Engineering & Continuous Crystallization

Three-year funded doctoral position at the Department of Biochemical and Chemical Engineering (BCI) developing continuous microfluidic crystallization and downstream purification for biological therapeutics.

Collaborative Research Centre (SFB 876) Resource-Constrained Data Analysis Project

Flagship DFG collaborative research center developing real-time machine learning algorithms optimized for low-power edge devices, embedded FPGA sensors, and ultra-fast cyber-physical networks.

Bayer / Evonik Ruhr Chemical Park Curricular Industrial Engineering Practicum

Six-month full-time engineering internship situated at major chemical manufacturing parks in the Ruhr region (Evonik Marl, Bayer Bergkamen), optimizing reaction kinetics and distillation columns.

Lamarr Institute for Machine Learning and AI Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the prestigious Lamarr Institute in Dortmund exploring foundational trustworthy AI, neuro-symbolic reasoning, and algorithmic fairness.

W3 Professorship in Industrial Robotics & Cyber-Physical Production Systems

Tenured full professorship in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, directing research in human-robot collaboration, autonomous production cells, and digital manufacturing.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science in Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics, or Natural Sciences with minimum 180 ECTS credits and strong mathematical grounding.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (M.Sc., Diplom) with above-average grade (mindestens 'gut') in engineering, computer science, mathematics, or physics.
Language proficiency
English programs: TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 6.5. German programs: DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral candidates submit a research plan aligned with a university chair; Masters applicants submit detailed transcript module guide.
Application fee
€75 evaluation fee via uni-assist for international applicants with non-German degrees.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcript of records, module descriptions, curriculum vitae, and language proficiency certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees for domestic and international students. All enrolled students pay only the social semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €340 per semester, which covers student union services and provides a regional public transit pass (NRW-Ticket).
