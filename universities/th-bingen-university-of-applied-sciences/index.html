University of Applied Sciences Bingen

Technische Hochschule Bingen (TH Bingen - University of Applied Sciences Bingen) is an engineering and life sciences university overlooking the Rhine, acclaimed for viticulture and enology, environmental process technology, mechanical engineering, and smart precision farming.

Academic overview & research priorities

Tracing its roots to the 1897 Rheinisches Technikum Bingen, TH Bingen operates two dynamic faculties: Technology, Informatics and Economy (Faculty 1) and Life Sciences and Engineering (Faculty 2). Situated where the Nahe river meets the Rhine, TH Bingen is internationally famous for its experimental vineyards, renewable biomass reactors, agricultural drone robotics, and automotive propulsion laboratories.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or recognized foreign university entrance qualification verified via uni-assist. Master's admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, life sciences, viticulture, or informatics with minimum German grade 2.5. German proficiency DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4 is required for German-taught programs; English B2/C1 for international study components.
