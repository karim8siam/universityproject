University of Kiel: Marine Sciences, GEOMAR, Precision Medicine & Doctorates

Kiel University (Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel / CAU), founded in 1665, is Schleswig-Holstein's premier research university. Located on the Baltic coast, CAU is internationally renowned for marine and climate sciences in partnership with GEOMAR, precision medicine, nanosciences, and ancient human-environmental interactions (ROOTS).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

GEOMAR Helmholtz Centre & CAU Ocean Sciences Doctoral Fellowship (TV-L E13)

Doctoral research appointment at GEOMAR Helmholtz Centre investigating Atlantic meridional overturning circulation (AMOC), ocean deoxygenation, and marine methane hydrates.

Cluster of Excellence Precision Medicine in Chronic Inflammation (PMI) Fellowship

Prestigious doctoral fellowship from Germany's Excellence Cluster PMI investigating chronic inflammatory diseases of the gut, lung, and skin using single-cell genomics.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Marine Geosciences (English-Taught Flagship)

Internationally renowned master's taught in English exploring seafloor exploration, paleoceanography, marine sedimentology, and seismic reflection imaging aboard research vessels.

Kiel Nano, Surface and Interface Science (KiNSIS) Laboratory Assistantship

Applied research post within KiNSIS developing bio-compatible magnetic sensors, memristive devices for neuromorphic computing, and functional nanostructured surfaces.

Baltic Sea Coastal Ecosystem Research Vessel Practicum

Practical scientific deployment aboard research vessels (e.g., RV Alkor or RV Elisabeth Mann Borgese) collecting CTD water columns, benthos cores, and plankton acoustics.

Cluster of Excellence ROOTS Postdoctoral Fellowship in Socio-Environmental Connectivity

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at Cluster of Excellence ROOTS investigating how ancient societies adapted to climate crises, resource bottlenecks, and epidemics.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Climate Physics: Meteorology & Physical Oceanography

Joint master's taught in English with GEOMAR providing rigorous quantitative foundations in global climate modeling, atmospheric turbulence, and ocean-atmosphere coupling.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in a corresponding discipline with above-average grades (German grade 2.5 or better, or international equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or Diplom with academic distinction and formal written acceptance from a CAU doctoral supervisor.
Language proficiency
English-taught programs require CEFR B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 90+); German-taught programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral candidates: detailed 5–10 page research exposé outlining theoretical framework, methodology, research vessel/lab requirements, and timeline.
Application fee
€0 (applications via CAU online portal).
Required documentation
Certified degree certificate, transcript of records with ECTS grading scale, CV, statement of motivation, and language certificates.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 100% free for all domestic and international students at Kiel University. Students only pay a semester social contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €280 per semester, which includes a statewide public transportation ticket throughout Schleswig-Holstein and Hamburg.
