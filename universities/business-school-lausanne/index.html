Business School Lausanne: Sustainable Business, DBA & International Scholarships

Business School Lausanne (BSL) is an internationally accredited Swiss business school located on the shores of Lake Geneva near EPFL and UNIL in Lausanne. A founding member of the World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD) academic partners, BSL is celebrated for pioneering sustainable business, circular economics, and executive doctoral research.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

BSL Sustainability Leadership & Social Impact Scholarship

Merit-based scholarship supporting international master's and MBA candidates demonstrating exceptional commitment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) in Sustainable Business & Governance

Executive doctoral program for senior managers and consultants producing action-oriented research on regenerative business models, stakeholder governance, and corporate sustainability.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Sustainable Finance & Impact Investing

Specialized postgraduate program taught in English in Switzerland's financial corridor covering green bond structuring, ESG portfolio analytics, impact measurement, and carbon accounting.

Institute for Business Sustainability (IBS) Applied Research Assistantship

Applied research post investigating corporate circular economy adoption, true-cost accounting, and supply chain decarbonization alongside Swiss multinationals.

World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD) Practicum

Six-month practicum based in Geneva or Lausanne collaborating with international sustainability initiatives on corporate net-zero pledges and circular packaging roadmaps.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Regenerative Economics & Stakeholder Capitalism

Two-year postdoctoral appointment examining business models that restore natural capital, doughnut economics application in cities, and multi-capital accounting.

Master of International Business in Digital Transformation & Smart Enterprises

Dynamic master's degree training future executives in platform business models, enterprise AI deployment, agile organizational design, and digital ethics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree in business, economics, social sciences, or related discipline from a recognized institution with sound academic performance.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) requires an MBA or relevant master's degree plus at least 5 years of significant managerial or professional experience.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90+; Cambridge C1 Advanced.
Research statement / Proposal
For DBA applicants: 3–5 page research concept note outlining real-world managerial problem, sustainability implications, and proposed empirical methodology.
Application fee
150 CHF administrative application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's diplomas, certified transcripts, CV, two professional/academic letters of reference, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for master's programs at BSL is approximately 36,000 to 42,000 CHF for the full program. Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) tuition is approximately 45,000 CHF over 3 years. BSL Sustainability Excellence Scholarships provide fee reductions of up to 30% for high-achieving international candidates.
