University of Milan: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Milan (Università degli Studi di Milano, known as 'La Statale'), founded in 1924, is the largest university in Lombardy and a founding member of the League of European Research Universities (LERU). Centered around the historic Ca' Granda and the futuristic MIND (Milano Innovation District) science park, La Statale is internationally acclaimed for molecular medicine, pharmacology, bioinformatics, and international law.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctoral Program in Molecular and Cellular Biology (MIND Milano Innovation District)

Three-year doctoral candidacy conducted at the MIND Innovation District alongside the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) and Human Technopole, studying RNA biology and epigenetics.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Bioinformatics for Computational Genomics (Joint with PoliMi)

Cutting-edge interdisciplinary program offered jointly with Politecnico di Milano, covering next-generation genomic sequencing algorithms, structural proteomics, and AI in personalized medicine.

UniMi International Excellence Scholarships and Lombardy DSU Welfare Grant

Premier institutional scholarship program awarding 155 merit awards annually to the highest-achieving international students enrolling in English-medium master's programs at La Statale.

Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences Research Fellowship in Radioligands and Nanomedicine

Two-year research appointment designing targeted peptide radioligands for cancer theranostics, synthesizing chelating conjugates, and evaluating pharmacokinetic clearance profiles.

Milan Global Governance & International Human Rights Legal Clinic Practicum

Four-month practical clinical legal residency drafting amicus curiae briefs, advising non-governmental organizations, and participating in mock hearings on human rights and international trade.

INFN Milano Joint Postdoctoral Fellowship in High-Energy Particle Physics (LHC Experiments)

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship with INFN Milano analyzing proton-proton collision datasets from the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), testing Higgs boson coupling anomalies, and searching for dark photons.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Environmental Change and Global Sustainability (ECGS)

Multidisciplinary English-taught master's degree exploring climate mitigation strategies, alpine glaciology, environmental governance, and circular bio-economy value chains.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A 3-year bachelor's degree (Laurea) or recognized foreign equivalent with at least 180 ECTS credits in a coherent academic field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with top marks; competitive evaluation of CV, research project proposal, and formal oral interview.
Language proficiency
For English-taught courses: minimum B2 English certification (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 78). For Italian courses: B2 Italian certificate.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2,500-word research proposal outlining scientific background, project objectives, experimental methodology, and potential socio-economic impact.
Application fee
€30 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, CIMEA Statement of Comparability, curriculum vitae, passport copy, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Milan is calculated progressively based on family income (ISEE parificato), ranging from €0 to €4,000 per year. International students with an ISEE under €23,000 are completely exempt from tuition fees and qualify for DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) regional scholarships providing up to €8,000 per year in cash stipends plus subsidized accommodation and meals. PhD scholarships offer guaranteed ministerial stipends of €16,243 per year plus research travel supplements.
