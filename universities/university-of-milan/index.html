University of Milan (Milan, Italy)

Founded in 1924 in the heart of Milan, the University of Milan (Università degli Studi di Milano, known as La Statale) is one of Italy's premier public research universities and the sole Italian member of the prestigious League of European Research Universities (LERU). La Statale is internationally celebrated for biomedical research, cancer genomics (partnered with IFOM and IEO), pharmaceutical sciences, and computational bioinformatics. University of Milan Excellence Scholarships & Regional DSU Grants

Merit and need-based international scholarship scheme providing comprehensive living allowances, free accommodation, and zero tuition fees for top-tier graduate students.

Statale Doctoral Fellowships (Borse di Dottorato) in Molecular Medicine & Life Sciences

Premier 3-year doctoral research program in molecular oncology, translational medicine, and genomics, collaborating with the European Institute of Oncology (IEO).

University of Milan Master of Science in Bioinformatics for Computational Genomics

Cutting-edge joint master's program taught in English with Politecnico di Milano, combining machine learning, next-generation sequencing analytics, and computational genomics.

University of Milan Master of Science in Data Science and Economics (DSE)

Interdisciplinary master's degree taught in English, focusing on algorithmic data science, machine learning models, predictive economic analytics, and business intelligence.

FIRC Institute of Molecular Oncology (IFOM) & IEO Research Placements

World-class research placements at Italy's premier molecular oncology campus, conducting frontier investigations into DNA damage mechanisms, tumor metastasis, and immunotherapy.

Statale Erasmus+ Traineeship & International Laboratory Research Placements

Structured funded research traineeships enabling graduate scholars to conduct experimental thesis investigations in leading academic and industrial laboratories across Europe.

Statale 150-Hour Student Academic Collaboration Fellowships (Collaborazioni a Tempo Parziale)

Paid student work contracts inside university computer centres, libraries, and administrative research divisions, providing flexible financial support for degree candidates.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years / 180 ECTS) in relevant subject with minimum grade equivalent to 70%+ in Italian university grading.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Specialistica/Magistrale or international equivalent), competitive admission examination/interview, and submission of a scientific research proposal.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency for English-taught degrees: Academic IELTS 6.0/6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 80+ (or CEFR B2/C1 certificate).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 3 to 5-page research project proposal submitted with the doctoral application.
Application fee
€30 to €50 application fee; tuition fees are calculated progressively based on family ISEE (typically €156 to €2,800/yr, fully waived for scholarship holders).
Required documentation
Complete official transcripts, degree certificates with Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) or CIMEA comparability certificate, CV, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is calculated progressively according to family financial standing (ISEE), with regional DSU grants providing complete tuition waivers and free student housing. For doctoral researchers, ministerial fellowships (Borse di Dottorato) provide a tax-exempt stipend of €16,243 net/year, plus a 50% monthly stipend supplement for research conducted abroad and an annual 10% research budget.
