Pepperdine University - DC Campus

Pepperdine Washington DC · Washington, DC, United States

Pepperdine University is an elite private research university affiliated with the Churches of Christ, maintaining its Washington, DC permanent academic campus in the downtown cultural corridor on Pennsylvania Avenue. Renowned for public policy, international diplomacy, constitutional law, and corporate finance.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Pepperdine's Washington, DC campus operates as an integral global academic hub hosting the School of Public Policy, the Seaver College DC Academic Internship Program, and the Caruso School of Law Washington Semester. Students complete congressional, think tank, and federal agency fellowships alongside advanced policy seminars.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission to Pepperdine is competitive and holistic (average high school GPA 3.7). Admission to DC graduate programs through the School of Public Policy or Caruso Law requires an accredited bachelor's, professional recommendations, and analytical writing samples.
