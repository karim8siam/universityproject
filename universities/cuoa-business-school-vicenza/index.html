Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited university; minimum 2 years of work experience for specialized executive masters.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Executive DBA candidates require a Master's degree and at least 5 years of significant managerial responsibility.
Language proficiency
B2/C1 English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 85+ or IELTS 6.5) for English-taught master programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae with motivation letter outlining managerial impact and strategic leadership objectives.
Application fee
€100 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Undergraduate degree transcripts, official graduation diploma, two professional letters of recommendation, and identity documentation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition ranges from €12,000 for specialized master's programs to €28,000 for the Executive MBA. Merit scholarships, regional Fondazione CUOA grants, and corporate sponsorships cover up to 50% of tuition fees.
