The University of Texas at Arlington

UT Arlington · Arlington, Texas, United States

The University of Texas at Arlington (UTA) is a world-class public Carnegie R1 research university located in the heart of the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. Generating over $130M in annual research expenditures, UTA is home to the largest nursing program in Texas, the renowned College of Engineering, the Shimadzu Center for Advanced Analytical Chemistry, and the Aerodynamics Research Center.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UTA comprises nine colleges and schools offering over 180 degree programs. Key research institutes include the Aerodynamics Research Center (operating hypersonic arc-jet wind tunnels), the North Texas Genome Center, the Center for Transportation Equity, Decisions and Dollars (CTEDD), and the Automation & Robotics Research Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school curriculum, GPA, and test scores (assured admission for top 25% of Texas classes). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, program-specific GRE/GMAT scores (waivers available), a statement of research intent, and three letters of recommendation.
