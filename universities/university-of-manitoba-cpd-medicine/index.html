Academic overview & excellence

CPD Medicine serves as the premier accreditation and lifelong learning vehicle for licensed physicians, surgeons, medical residents, and healthcare allied teams throughout Manitoba, Nunavut, and Northwestern Ontario. Based at the historic Bannatyne Campus adjacent to the Health Sciences Centre in Winnipeg, CPD Medicine integrates cutting-edge simulation laboratories, point-of-care ultrasound training suites, rural telehealth education, and evidence-based clinical update symposiums accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada and the College of Family Physicians of Canada.

Research admissions & criteria

Enrollment in CPD Medicine advanced fellowships, clinical educator certificates, and postgraduate specialization tracks requires possession of an accredited medical degree (MD, MBBS, or equivalent), active or eligible provincial medical licensure, good standing with relevant national medical colleges, and submission of clinical practice portfolios. International medical graduates applying for clinical educator tracks must demonstrate academic English proficiency (IELTS Academic 7.0 or equivalent).
