Kwansei Gakuin University - Kobe Sanda Campus

The Kobe Sanda Campus (KSC) of Kwansei Gakuin University is a premier STEM and policy hub in the Kansai science city region, hosting four faculties of Science, Engineering, Biological Sciences, and Architecture.

Academic overview & excellence

Set against the scenic Rokko mountain range in Sanda, Hyogo Prefecture, Kobe Sanda Campus represents Kwansei Gakuin University's major scientific research center. The campus houses world-leading research groups in silicon carbide (SiC) semiconductor crystal growth, artificial photosynthesis, organic photovoltaics, biomechatronics, and architectural seismology. KSC collaborates directly with the Supercomputer Fugaku at RIKEN Center for Computational Science in Kobe, the SPring-8 synchrotron radiation facility in Harima, and major Kansai electronics and chemical industries.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to undergraduate engineering, science, and policy degrees at Kobe Sanda Campus is open through General Entrance Examinations, Admissions Office (AO) Global Talent screenings, and EJU international examinations. Graduate school admissions (M.Sc., M.Eng., Ph.D.) require an accredited bachelor's degree in a physical science or engineering discipline (minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA), verified English proficiency (TOEIC 750+, TOEFL iBT 80+, or IELTS 6.0+), a detailed research plan, and prior consent from a prospective laboratory professor.

Two-year master's program utilizing photocatalytic metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) and molecular catalysts to achieve high-efficiency solar water splitting and carbon dioxide reduction into solar fuels.

Funding: Kwansei Gakuin University Graduate School Tuition Reduction Scholarships of 50% Tuition: ¥1,050,000 per year Deadline: February 1, 2027

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting molecular dynamics simulations of membrane protein complexes and drug-target binding affinities using the world-leading Fugaku supercomputer in Kobe.

Funding: ¥3,600,000 annual postdoctoral research salary and direct supercomputer CPU node allocation Tuition: ¥0 Deadline: April 30, 2027

Distinguished faculty & researchers (3)

Prof. Dr. Tadaaki Kaneko

Professor of Semiconductor Physics and Director of the SiC Research Center · Department of Nanotechnology for Sustainable Energy

Prof. Dr. Tadaaki Kaneko is a world-renowned solid-state physicist who pioneered high-quality silicon carbide (SiC) epitaxial wafer growth technologies, driving commercial adoption of high-efficiency electric vehicle inverters.

Prof. Dr. Shinya Mineva

Professor of Applied Chemistry and Molecular Photochemistry · Department of Applied Chemistry for Environment

Prof. Dr. Shinya Mineva investigates visible-light-driven catalytic water oxidation, molecular dye-sensitized photocatalysts, and solar chemical reactor designs at the Kobe Sanda Campus.

Artificial Photosynthesis Photocatalysis Solar Hydrogen

Prof. Dr. Tomoya Nihei

Professor of Structural Engineering and Earthquake Resistance · Department of Architecture

Prof. Dr. Tomoya Nihei conducts dynamic shake-table testing of base-isolated residential and commercial buildings, developing smart damping systems for high-magnitude subduction zone earthquakes.

Seismic Isolation Base-Isolated Structures Earthquake Mitigation
