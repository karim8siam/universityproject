Zurich Elite Business School: Applied Economics & AI Business Leadership Guide

Established in 2012 in the international financial capital of Zurich, Zurich Elite Business School (ZEBS) is a dynamic Swiss higher education institution. Partnered with Fortune 500 corporations and European academic research centers, ZEBS delivers accredited Bachelor, Master, and Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) degrees specializing in applied artificial intelligence, organizational cybernetics, digital transformation, and sustainable finance.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits or international 3-4 year equivalent) in business, engineering, economics, or computer science with GPA 3.0/4.0.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) entry requires an accredited Master of Science or MBA degree with minimum three years of professional leadership experience.
Language proficiency
English proficiency at CEFR C1 level (IELTS Academic 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 90+); bilingual programs require working proficiency in English and German.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for executive Master and DBA programs: 2,000-word applied research proposal addressing real-world enterprise transformation or AI deployment.
Application fee
CHF 120 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma certificates, official transcripts of records, comprehensive CV, two professional letters of recommendation, and personal statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the full-time Master in Applied AI and Management is approximately CHF 18,000 per year; executive DBA tuition is approximately CHF 12,000 per year. Corporate fellowship sponsorships from Swiss multinational partners, merit-based tuition grants covering up to CHF 6,000, and flexible installment payment models provide strong financial support.
