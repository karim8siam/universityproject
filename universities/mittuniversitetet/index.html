Mid Sweden University (Mittuniversitetet): Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Mid Sweden University (Mittuniversitetet / MIUN) is a dynamic comprehensive research university situated in the geographical center of Sweden across campuses in Sundsvall and Östersund. Renowned globally for its Sensible Things that Communicate (STC) research center in IoT and sensor systems, the Fibre Science and Communication Network (FSCN), and the Swedish Winter Sports Research Centre (NVC).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Sensible Things that Communicate (STC) Salaried PhD in Industrial IoT & Smart Vision

Four-year salaried doctoral research post within the STC research center, developing ultra-low-power intelligent camera nodes and wireless mesh sensor networks for industrial forest and mining operations.

PhD in Cellulose Nanomaterials and Sustainable Energy Storage (FSCN Group)

Doctoral research at the Fibre Science and Communication Network (FSCN), synthesizing cellulose nanofibrils and conductive biopolymer membranes for large-scale supercapacitors and batteries.

Master of Science in Embedded Sensor Systems (English-taught)

Two-year Master of Science preparing hardware-software engineers in real-time operating systems, low-power embedded AI, wireless IoT protocols, and optical measurement sensors.

Master of Science in Ecotechnology and Sustainable Development

Two-year master's program taught at the Östersund campus, analyzing environmental impact assessments, carbon footprinting, industrial ecology, and renewable resource management in boreal zones.

PhD in Sports Science & Physiological Biomechanics (Swedish Winter Sports Centre)

Doctoral research based at the Swedish Winter Sports Research Centre (NVC) in Östersund, monitoring elite athlete endurance physiology, high-altitude performance, and wearable biomechanical sensors.

STC Postdoctoral Fellowship in Edge AI for Autonomous Industrial Monitoring

Two-year postdoctoral position in Sundsvall developing tinyML model compression and neuromorphic hardware accelerators for predictive maintenance in paper pulp mills.

FSCN Biopolymer Materials Summer Graduate Research Internship

Eight-week summer research placement at the FSCN laboratory in Sundsvall, testing bio-based barrier coatings and lignin-reinforced bioplastics for sustainable packaging.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A completed bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in computer engineering, electrical engineering, chemical engineering, or environmental sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (240 Swedish credits, including 60 credits at advanced level); application submitted in response to advertised doctoral vacancies.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B proficiency (IELTS 6.5 overall with no section under 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of research intent, master's thesis or publication samples, and documentation demonstrating alignment with the advertised research specialization.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee through universityadmissions.se for non-EU students (SEK 0 for doctoral applications).
Required documentation
Bachelor's and Master's diplomas, certified transcripts, CV, copy of passport, and English language test scores.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 100% free for all Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition for Master's programs is SEK 120,000 to SEK 140,000 per year. Top international applicants can receive the Mid Sweden University Tuition Fee Scholarship covering 75% of tuition fees. All doctoral candidates in Sweden are employed as university staff on full collective-agreement salaried contracts, earning SEK 32,500 to SEK 36,500 per month with zero tuition.
