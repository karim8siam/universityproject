EIOM Padua: Osteopathic Medicine Degrees, Clinical Research & Faculty Guide

Established in Padua, the European Institute for Osteopathic Medicine (EIOM - Istituto Europeo per la Medicina Osteopatica) is one of Italy's premier higher educational and clinical research institutions for osteopathic healthcare. Operating in compliance with the European Standard CEN EN 16686 and Italian healthcare professionalization statutes, EIOM delivers full-time and part-time professional degree pathways, an advanced clinical training clinic, and empirical biomechanical research.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

EIOM Clinical Excellence and Academic Merit Scholarship

Merit-based financial bursary supporting high-achieving healthcare students matriculating into full-time osteopathic medicine training at EIOM Padua.

Professional Master's Degree Pathway in Osteopathy (T2 Healthcare Stream)

Advanced post-graduate osteopathy program designed for licensed medical professionals and physiotherapists, integrating structural, visceral, and craniosacral manual therapy.

Post-Graduate Research Fellowship in Clinical Biomechanics and Fascial Neurobiology

Clinical research fellowship conducting empirical investigations into fascial innervation, autonomic nervous system regulation, and musculoskeletal biomechanics in chronic pain patients.

Osteopathic Manual Treatment in Chronic Musculoskeletal Disorders Investigation

Prospective clinical trial evaluating the therapeutic efficacy of standardized osteopathic manipulative treatment on chronic cervicalgia and lumbar mobility limitations.

EIOM Teaching Clinic Supervised Patient Clinical Internship (1,000 Hours)

Mandatory 1,000-hour hands-on clinical internship in the on-campus EIOM clinic in Padua, examining real patients, formulating treatment plans, and performing supervised manual treatments.

EIOM Pediatric Osteopathy and Developmental Postural Care Fellowship

Postgraduate fellowship training healthcare practitioners in delicate craniosacral techniques and postural biomechanics for neonates, infants, and pediatric patients.

Senior Clinical Tutor and Lecturer in Biomechanics and Palpatory Anatomy

Full-time clinical faculty appointment supervising student clinical consultations, teaching functional anatomy and biomechanics, and contributing to institutional research in Padua.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Full-time 5-year degree (T1 stream) requires secondary school diploma (Maturità); part-time 4-to-5-year master's level stream (T2) requires recognized degree in Medicine, Physiotherapy, or Health Sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Advanced clinical osteopathy credentials articulate with international university Master of Science and doctoral health research programs in biomechanics and musculoskeletal sciences.
Language proficiency
Italian language fluency (minimum B2 level for lectures and clinical patient interaction); working knowledge of scientific English for medical literature reviews.
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via the EIOM portal including academic transcripts, CV, medical fitness certificate, and individual motivational interview.
Application fee
€100 non-refundable clinical evaluation and entrance interview fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school diploma or university healthcare degree certificate, certified academic transcripts, valid identification, and medical certificate of suitability for manual healthcare practice.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the full-time five-year professional osteopathy program is approximately €5,500 to €6,800 per academic year, inclusive of comprehensive clinical clinic rotations, laboratory materials, and liability insurance. EIOM offers installment payment schedules and academic excellence merit bursaries providing up to €1,500 annual tuition reductions.
