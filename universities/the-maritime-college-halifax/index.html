The Maritime College

The Maritime College in Halifax is an accredited maritime vocational and nautical training institution offering Transport Canada-certified marine engineering, navigation, sea safety, and commercial offshore vessel crew credentials.

Academic overview & excellence

Instruction integrates full-mission bridge simulators, operational marine engine propulsion labs, and hands-on sea survival fire training. Programs strictly adhere to International Maritime Organization (IMO) STCW standards and prepare deck and engine officers for Canadian and global fleet deployments.

Research admissions & criteria

Candidates must be at least 18 years of age, possess a valid Marine Medical Certificate approved by Transport Canada, and have completed Grade 12 mathematics and physics. Industry-sponsored cadetships frequently provide full or partial tuition coverage.
