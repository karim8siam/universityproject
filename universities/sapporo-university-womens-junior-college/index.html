Sapporo University Women's Junior College

Sapporo University Women's Junior College is an established higher education college in Sapporo, Hokkaido, offering career-oriented two-year degree curricula in career design, business informatics, child welfare, and international cultural studies.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating in close academic integration with Sapporo University, the Women's Junior College equips women with practical skills in corporate administration, financial spreadsheets, preschool child education, and bilingual communication. Graduates transition directly into commercial enterprises throughout Hokkaido or articulate into upper-division university bachelor programs.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation or 12 years of formal schooling. International applicants require JLPT N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 200, statement of academic intent, high school transcripts, and an admissions interview.
