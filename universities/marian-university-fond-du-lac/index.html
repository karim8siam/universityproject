Marian University

Marian Univ · Fond du Lac, Wisconsin, United States

Marian University is a private Roman Catholic university in Fond du Lac, Wisconsin, established in 1936 by the Congregation of Sisters of St. Agnes. Marian is celebrated for nursing, criminal justice, forensic science, and educational leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Marian offers 40+ degree programs across the School of Nursing and Health Professions, School of Criminal Justice, and School of Business. It features high-fidelity healthcare simulation hospital suites and modern forensic microscopy and DNA analysis laboratories.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.2) and transcripts under test-optional policies. Nursing applicants must meet clinical prerequisite requirements in anatomy, physiology, and chemistry.
