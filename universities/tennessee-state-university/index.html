Tennessee State University

Tennessee State · Nashville, Tennessee, United States

Tennessee State University (TSU) is a comprehensive, historically Black land-grant institution (HBCU) located in Nashville, Tennessee. Classified as a Carnegie R2 high-research university, TSU is recognized for its leading programs in agricultural biotechnology, engineering and computer science, health sciences, and educational leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TSU houses the College of Agriculture, the College of Engineering, Technology and Computer Science, and the College of Health Sciences. Its renowned Otis L. Floyd Nursery Research Center and Center of Excellence for Information Systems drive major research funded by NASA, USDA, NSF, and the Department of Defense.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5) and standardized test scores or test-optional materials. Graduate programs require a recognized bachelor's degree with a 2.75+ cumulative GPA (3.0+ for doctoral studies), GRE/GMAT if mandated by department, official transcripts, and departmental recommendations.
