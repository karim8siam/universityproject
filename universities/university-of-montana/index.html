University of Montana

UM · Missoula, Montana, United States

The University of Montana (UM) is a premier Carnegie R1 public flagship research university nestled at the base of Mount Sentinel in Missoula, Montana. Renowned worldwide for forestry, wildlife biology, freshwater ecology, toxicology, and environmental humanities, UM leverages its breathtaking Northern Rockies setting as a living laboratory for conservation discovery.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UM encompasses 11 colleges and schools including the W.A. Franke College of Forestry and Conservation, College of Humanities and Sciences, Skaggs School of Pharmacy, Alexander Blewett III School of Law, and the College of the Arts and Media. UM operates the Flathead Lake Biological Station (FLBS)—one of the oldest freshwater field stations in the world.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and college prep curriculum. Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, provide three letters of recommendation, a statement of purpose, and meet English proficiency criteria (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
