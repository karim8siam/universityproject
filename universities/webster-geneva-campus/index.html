Webster Geneva Campus: International Relations, Cybersecurity & Fellowships Guide

Webster Geneva Campus (Webster University Geneva), established in 1978 in Bellevue on the shores of Lake Geneva, is the Swiss campus of Webster University. Operating in direct proximity to the United Nations, WHO, CERN, and hundreds of international diplomatic missions, Webster Geneva provides globally recognized American-accredited bachelor's and master's degrees in international affairs, psychology, management, and cybersecurity.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited higher-education institution with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.75 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not currently offering independent doctoral degrees; collaborative research transfer and master's credentials with international partner institutions.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80+, IELTS Academic 6.5+, Cambridge C1 Advanced, or Duolingo English Test 120+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate admissions: 500-word statement of academic intent and professional goals detailing alignment with Geneva's international sector.
Application fee
CHF 100 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Official undergraduate transcripts, certified diploma copy, two letters of academic or professional recommendation, updated CV, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is approximately CHF 1,070 per credit hour (total program tuition for a 36-credit master's degree is approximately CHF 38,520). Webster Geneva awards substantial merit scholarships of up to 40% tuition fee reduction for high-achieving international applicants, alongside graduate assistantships.
