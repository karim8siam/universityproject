Avenue Campus

Avenue Campus is the historic humanities and arts campus of the University of Southampton, a world top-100 Russell Group research university, housing world-class centres in archaeology, modern languages, digital humanities, and English literature.

Academic overview & excellence

Bordering Southampton Common, Avenue Campus boasts the Archaeological Computing Research Group, dedicated digital preservation labs, and extensive rare book archives. Southampton is consistently ranked in the UK top 10 for Archaeology, Linguistics, and History.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate programs require standard UK A-Level grades (typically AAB-ABB) or International Baccalaureate (34-36 points). Postgraduate research degrees (PhD/MPhil) require an accredited bachelor's degree with upper second-class honors (2:1) and an approved doctoral research proposal. English proficiency: IELTS 6.5 to 7.0.
