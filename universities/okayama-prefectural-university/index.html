Okayama Prefectural University: Health Sciences, Computer Science & Systems Engineering Guide

Okayama Prefectural University (OPU) is a premier public university in western Japan, recognized for excellence across its Faculty of Computer Science and Systems Engineering, Health and Welfare Science, and Design, providing advanced technological and clinical research in a scenic Seto Inland Sea region.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited four-year university with a Bachelor's degree in engineering, computing, health science, or design.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or professional graduate degree in computer science, systems engineering, or health sciences.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 or English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 75+ or IELTS 6.0+) for graduate laboratory research tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2,000-character Japanese or 1,000-word English research proposal detailing research objectives, methodology, and laboratory choice.
Application fee
¥30,000 application examination fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, research proposal, statement of purpose, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is standardized at ¥535,800 annually for graduate degree programs, with an entrance fee of ¥282,000 (reduced to ¥141,000 for residents of Okayama Prefecture). Full and 50% tuition fee reduction exemptions and JASSO scholarships are offered for international applicants.
