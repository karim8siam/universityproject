Monash University (Clayton, Melbourne)

Established in 1958 in Melbourne, Victoria, Monash University is Australia's largest university and a prominent member of the Group of Eight (Go8). Ranked among the top 45 universities globally, Monash is world-renowned as a research giant, ranking #2 globally in Pharmacy and Pharmacology (QS) and operating the state-of-the-art Monash Clayton Innovation Precinct—housing the Australian Synchrotron, the Melbourne Centre for Nanofabrication, and CSIRO headquarters. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Monash Graduate Scholarship (MGS) & Monash International Tuition Scholarship (MITS)

Monash's flagship university-wide research award scheme providing full living support and tuition waivers for top domestic and international doctoral scholars.

Monash Data Futures Institute PhD in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Interdisciplinary doctoral program at the Monash Data Futures Institute addressing trustworthy AI, automated reasoning, computer vision, natural language processing, and AI ethics.

Monash Master of Pharmaceutical Science

Two-year professional master's at Monash Faculty of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences (ranked #2 globally), preparing researchers for drug design, clinical trials, and biopharmaceuticals.

Monash Australian Synchrotron Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Three-year postdoctoral research appointment collaborating directly with the Australian Synchrotron beamline facilities at the Clayton campus on advanced condensed matter imaging.

Monash Sustainable Development Institute (MSDI) Summer Research Traineeship

Eight-week summer research placement at MSDI investigating circular water networks, urban climate resilience, and renewable energy adoption models.

Monash Master of Applied Econometrics

Two-year advanced program at Monash Business School covering high-dimensional financial econometrics, causal inference, time-series forecasting, and machine learning in macroeconomic policy.

Monash Research Fellow in Green Hydrogen Catalysis & Electrochemical Engineering

Two-year full-time research position at the ARC Centre of Excellence for Green Electrochemical Transformation developing zero-emission green hydrogen catalysts.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with a minimum weighted average mark (WAM) of 60-65% depending on faculty.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1, 80%+ WAM) or Master's degree with a significant research thesis component (at least 25% of total degree) graded at H1/80%+.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-test) or TOEFL iBT 79 (reading 13, listening 12, speaking 18, writing 21); higher for Law, Medicine, and Pharmacy (IELTS 7.0/7.5).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 1,000 to 2,000-word research proposal describing background, methodology, objectives, and confirmed Monash academic supervisor.
Application fee
AUD $100 international application fee via the Monash online portal; fee waivers available for nominated international scholarship candidates.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree completion certificates, CV, research proposal, and contact details for two academic referees.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate research students are supported by competitive scholarships. Recipients of the Monash International Tuition Scholarship (MITS) or RTP receive a 100% full international tuition fee waiver, a tax-free living allowance of AUD $35,013 per year for up to 3.5 years, single overseas student health cover (OSHC), and a relocation grant.
