Institutional Overview

Università Telematica Pegaso, Italy's premier accredited online university recognized by Ministerial Decree, maintains a modern examination and regional learning centre in Foggia, Puglia. Delivering state-accredited degree qualifications in economics, education, law, civil engineering, and sports science, the Foggia center provides local student support, physical exams, and blended workshops.
