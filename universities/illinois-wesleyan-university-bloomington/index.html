Illinois Wesleyan University

IWU · Bloomington, Illinois, United States

Illinois Wesleyan University (IWU) is a top-ranked private national liberal arts university in Bloomington, Illinois, celebrated for pre-med sciences, registered nursing, fine arts, and actuarial mathematics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

IWU exclusively educates undergraduate students, fostering intimate faculty-student collaborative research, guaranteed clinical rotations in top medical centers, and exceptional graduate school and medical school acceptance rates exceeding 80%.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are holistic and test-optional, reviewing high school coursework rigor, cumulative GPA (average 3.8), personal statements, and extracurricular distinction.
