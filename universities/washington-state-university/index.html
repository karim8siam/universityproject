Washington State University

WSU · Pullman, Washington, United States

Washington State University (WSU) is Washington's statewide flagship public land-grant Carnegie R1 research university, founded in 1890 in the rolling Palouse hills of Pullman, Washington. Renowned worldwide for agricultural science and tree fruit genomics, veterinary medicine at the College of Veterinary Medicine, smart power grid electric transmission at the Energy Systems Innovation Center, and clean hydrogen technology, WSU drives transformative research across the Pacific Northwest.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WSU encompasses 11 academic colleges including the Voiland College of Engineering and Architecture; College of Agricultural, Human, and Natural Resource Sciences (CAHNRS); College of Veterinary Medicine; Elson S. Floyd College of Medicine; and the Carson College of Business. Key research hubs include the Energy Systems Innovation Center (ESIC) and the Hydrogen Properties for Energy Research (HYPER) Laboratory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic performance, GPA, and curriculum. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 7.0+).
