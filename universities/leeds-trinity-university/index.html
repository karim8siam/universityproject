Leeds Trinity University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Research Guide

Established in 1966 and granted full university title in 2012, Leeds Trinity University is a renowned public university based on a picturesque campus in Horsforth and a state-of-the-art new city-centre campus at 1 Trevelyan Square in downtown Leeds. Celebrated nationally for pioneering mandatory professional work placements embedded in every undergraduate degree, Leeds Trinity delivers top-tier programs in Journalism, Media, Psychology, Business, Education, and Computer Science.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with a minimum UK 2:2 Honours (or international equivalent GPA 2.7/4.0) in a relevant subject area from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Merit or Distinction) in a relevant discipline, alongside a comprehensive doctoral research proposal and two academic references.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.0 overall with no band score below 5.5 (or IELTS 6.5 for Journalism, Creative Writing, and Psychology; TOEFL iBT 79 or 88 equivalent).
Research statement / Proposal
Undergraduate applications via UCAS; postgraduate applications submitted directly online via the Leeds Trinity Application Portal with personal statement.
Application fee
Zero application fee for direct postgraduate applications; standard UCAS fee applies for undergraduate entry.
Required documentation
Degree certificates, complete academic transcripts, English language proficiency certificate, passport copy, and two academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition for UK domestic students is standardly £9,250 per year. For international students, undergraduate and postgraduate taught master's tuition generally ranges from £12,500 to £15,000 per academic year. Leeds Trinity offers generous International Academic Scholarships providing up to £2,000 to £4,000 fee waivers in the first year of study.
