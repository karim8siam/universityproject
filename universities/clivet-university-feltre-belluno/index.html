Clivet University: Heat Pumps, Thermodynamic HVAC & Decarbonization Guide

Clivet University in Feltre (Belluno, Veneto) is the specialized corporate training and engineering research academy of Clivet S.p.A. (Midea Group), celebrated for state-of-the-art applied engineering in inverter heat pumps, commercial hydronic chillers, air renewal systems, and building decarbonization.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Engineering degree (mechanical, electrical, energy, or civil engineering) or certified technical background in thermodynamic systems.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent academic doctorates; conducts applied thermodynamic research in partnership with University of Padua and University of Trento.
Language proficiency
Italian and English (modules delivered in both languages for international heating/cooling engineers).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of technical intent detailing building energy modeling or heat pump retrofit projects.
Application fee
Free online technical registration for industry professionals and engineering students.
Required documentation
Curriculum vitae, engineering degree certificate or HVAC technician license, and company/academic sponsorship letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for professional engineering modules ranges from €800 to €2,500. European HVAC engineering training bursaries, corporate installer sponsorships, and Veneto regional green tech innovation grants are accessible.
