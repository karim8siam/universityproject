University of Colorado Denver

CU Denver · Denver, Colorado, United States

The University of Colorado Denver (CU Denver) is Colorado's premier public urban research university, situated in the heart of downtown Denver on the Auraria Campus. Deeply integrated with the state capital, commercial financial district, and regional arts scene, CU Denver is internationally celebrated for architecture and urban planning, public affairs, recording arts, and business analytics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

CU Denver offers undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral degrees across seven schools and colleges: the College of Architecture and Planning, the School of Public Affairs, the College of Engineering, Design and Computing, the Business School, the College of Arts & Media, the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, and the School of Education & Human Development.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate applicants apply via CU Denver's application or the Common App (rolling admissions; test-optional). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, transcripts, personal statements, and letters of recommendation.
