St. Michaël College - Pre-University Science & Humanities Institute

St. Michaël College in Zaandam is a renowned pre-university gymnasium and atheneum (VWO) institution in the Amsterdam metropolitan area, celebrated for its intensive STEM honors curriculum, university research partnerships with Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, and European bilingual education.

Academic overview & excellence

Located in the Zaanstreek innovation corridor just north of Amsterdam, St. Michaël College provides rigorous preparation for students advancing toward doctoral and master's tracks across Dutch research universities. The college houses modern physics and molecular chemistry laboratories, a digital makerspace, and specialized research seminar suites for senior academic profiling papers (Profielwerkstuk). In formal collaboration with VU Amsterdam and the University of Amsterdam, scholars conduct hands-on research in water management ecology, renewable energy engineering, and European civic policy.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to the senior VWO (Gymnasium and Atheneum) academic tracks and university bridge programs at St. Michaël College requires a Dutch primary school recommendation for VWO level or international equivalent secondary school credentials showing high marks in Mathematics, Natural Sciences, and Languages. Non-native Dutch speakers entering the bilingual English stream must demonstrate English fluency (CEFR B2/C1) and basic Dutch.
