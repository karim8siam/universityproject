University of South Carolina

South Carolina · Columbia, South Carolina, United States

The University of South Carolina is the historic public flagship research university of South Carolina, founded in 1801 in Columbia. A designated Carnegie R1 research powerhouse, South Carolina is internationally celebrated for hosting America's #1 international business program at the Darla Moore School of Business, nuclear and aerospace engineering, public health, sports science, and hydrogen energy technology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

South Carolina encompasses 16 colleges and schools including the Darla Moore School of Business, Molinaroli College of Engineering and Computing, Arnold School of Public Health, School of Medicine Columbia, and the School of Law. The university is home to the McNair Center for Aerospace Innovation and the Center for Hydrogen Research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly competitive through the Common Application. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
