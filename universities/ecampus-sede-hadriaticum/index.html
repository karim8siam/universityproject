Università eCampus - Polo di Studio Hadriaticum

The Polo di Studio Hadriaticum is an authorized academic center of Università eCampus located on the Adriatic coast in Abruzzo, delivering accredited degree courses in engineering, psychology, law, and literature across central-southern Italy.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating from modern facilities in Pescara and San Salvo, Polo Hadriaticum offers comprehensive academic tutoring, state exam proctoring, and graduate research support for the Central Adriatic region. The center coordinates five university faculties: Law, Economics, Engineering, Letters, and Psychology. Facilities include multimedia lecture rooms, digital civil and structural engineering testing labs, psychological diagnostic workshops, and dedicated career counseling offices connected to regional Adriatic commercial networks.

Research admissions & criteria

Enrollment at Polo Hadriaticum follows standard Italian university regulations established by MUR. For Laurea Triennale courses, applicants require a valid high school diploma (Maturità) or equivalent international qualification. For Laurea Magistrale programs, an accredited bachelor's degree in a related discipline is required. The center provides comprehensive credit evaluation (prevalutazione CFU) and assistance for international students seeking Declaration of Value.
