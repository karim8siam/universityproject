Loyola University New Orleans

Loyola New Orleans · New Orleans, Louisiana, United States

Loyola University New Orleans is a private Jesuit Catholic university in New Orleans, Louisiana, situated on historic St. Charles Avenue. Renowned for its College of Music and Media, Loyola College of Law, and environmental communications.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Loyola is celebrated for the first College of Music and Media in the Jesuit network, training Grammy-winning musicians and digital storytellers. Key units include the College of Nursing and Health, the Center for Environmental Communications, and the College of Law.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), writing samples, and creative portfolios/auditions for music and design majors under test-optional review. Law applicants submit LSAC applications with LSAT/GRE scores.
