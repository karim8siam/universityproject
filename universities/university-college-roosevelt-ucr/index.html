University College Roosevelt (UCR Middelburg)

University College Roosevelt (UCR) is the prestigious international honors liberal arts and sciences undergraduate college of Utrecht University, located in the historic provincial capital of Middelburg, Zeeland.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 2004 as a collaboration between Utrecht University and the Province of Zeeland and named in honor of the Roosevelt family (whose ancestral roots lie in Zeeland), UCR is an intensive residential liberal arts college hosting around 600 students from more than 60 nations. Housed in the magnificent Gothic City Hall of Middelburg and surrounding heritage buildings, UCR confers official Bachelor of Arts (BA) and Bachelor of Science (BSc) degrees from Utrecht University. Students construct personalized interdisciplinary curricula across Arts & Humanities, Social Sciences, Science, and Biomedical Sciences, characterized by small seminar classes (maximum 25 students) and compulsory undergraduate research.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Interdisciplinary Science and Engineering (Bachelor's) — Pre-engineering and scientific curriculum combining mathematics, physics, earth and environmental science, and data computing.
  • Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Biomedical Sciences and Pre-Medicine (Bachelor's) — Rigorous life sciences major preparing graduates for top medical schools (SUMMA at Utrecht) and biomedical master's programs.
  • Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Social Sciences and International Relations (Bachelor's) — Interdisciplinary study of international law, economics, political theory, anthropology, and human rights philosophy.
  • Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Arts and Humanities (Bachelor's) — Comprehensive liberal arts degree covering history, philosophy, rhetoric, musicology, and literature with intensive seminar discussions.
