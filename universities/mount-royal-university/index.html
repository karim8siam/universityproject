Mount Royal University: Degrees, Scholarships & Research Guide

Founded in 1910 in Calgary, Alberta, Mount Royal University (MRU) is a premier public undergraduate research institution. Celebrated for high-impact undergraduate research, community mental health, environmental science, and business entrepreneurship, MRU blends personalized class sizes with advanced laboratory infrastructure.

Mount Royal President's International Student Entrance Scholarship

Competitive entrance scholarship granted to top international students entering full-time undergraduate degree programs at Mount Royal University.

Bachelor of Health and Physical Education (Advanced Honours Research Pathway)

Leading Canadian health curriculum delivering rigorous laboratory research training in neuromuscular biomechanics, exercise physiology, and community chronic disease prevention.

MRU Rocky Mountain Ecosystems Collaborative Doctoral Research Fellowship

Four-year joint doctoral fellowship investigating hydrological shifts, glacier retreat, and watershed ecological resilience along the eastern slopes of the Canadian Rockies.

MRU Centre for Community Disaster Research (CCDR) Project

Interdisciplinary research placement studying social vulnerability, wildfire evacuation trauma, and community resilience in rural and Indigenous Albertan communities.

Calgary Social Innovation Hub & Community Enterprise Practicum

Hands-on practicum with the Institute for Community Prosperity evaluating social finance, systemic philanthropy, and non-profit business models across western Canada.

Mount Royal Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Cellular Stress and Toxicology

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating aquatic toxicant biomarkers, heavy metal bioaccumulation, and microplastic cellular translocation in freshwater species.

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of Child Studies and Social Innovation

Tenure-track academic position teaching undergraduate child studies courses, supervising honours theses, and conducting research on early childhood mental health.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree from a recognized post-secondary institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent 73% average).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral pathways with Alberta research universities require recognized master's credentials and departmental research alignment.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 86 with minimum 20 in each component, or IELTS Academic 6.5 with no band below 6.0.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of academic purpose (500–750 words) outlining career trajectory, community engagement, and disciplinary focus.
Application fee
CAD $100 non-refundable application fee for international applicants via ApplyAlberta.
Required documentation
Official secondary and post-secondary transcripts, certified English translations, proof of English language proficiency, and two references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International undergraduate tuition typically ranges from CAD $22,400 to CAD $26,800 per academic year (30 credits) depending on the faculty. Mount Royal awards substantial merit scholarships, including the President's International Entrance Scholarship (CAD $7,500) and donor-supported research bursaries.
