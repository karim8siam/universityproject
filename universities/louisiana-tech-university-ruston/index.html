Louisiana Tech University

Louisiana Tech · Ruston, Louisiana, United States

Louisiana Tech University is a public doctoral research university in Ruston, Louisiana, renowned for its College of Engineering and Science, the Institute for Micromanufacturing (IfM), trenchless technology, and cyber engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Louisiana Tech is a pioneer in micro- and nanotechnology, establishing one of the nation's earliest nanomanufacturing centers. Academic strengths include the Trenchless Technology Center (TTC), Center for Biomedical Engineering and Rehabilitation Science, and cyber intelligence research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.5) and standardized test scores (ACT/SAT). Graduate STEM admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering or computing with minimum 3.0 GPA.
