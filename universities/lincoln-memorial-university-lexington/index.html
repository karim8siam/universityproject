Research admissions & international qualifications

Bachelor's degree with rigorous pre-veterinary/pre-medical science prerequisites (organic chemistry, biochemistry, genetics, physics). Veterinary Medical College Application Service (VMCAS) submission. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates without prior English-language higher education degrees. VMCAS national application deadline September 15, 2027; Secondary application deadline October 1, 2027.
