Northwestern Michigan College

Northwestern Michigan · Traverse City, Michigan, United States

Northwestern Michigan College is a renowned public community college located along Grand Traverse Bay in Traverse City, Michigan. It is globally acclaimed for the Great Lakes Maritime Academy, Great Lakes Water Studies Institute, and aviation technology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NMC hosts the Great Lakes Campus, featuring the 224-foot training ship State of Michigan, the Great Lakes Maritime Academy (one of six state maritime academies in the US), the Great Lakes Culinary Institute, and a state-of-the-art flight training center at Cherry Capital Airport.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Open-access policy for standard associate degree pathways. Admission to the Great Lakes Maritime Academy and Aviation Flight Training requires federal physical fitness standards, US Coast Guard / FAA medical certifications, and competitive review.
