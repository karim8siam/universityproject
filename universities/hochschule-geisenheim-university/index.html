Hochschule Geisenheim University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1872 and enjoying unique statutory status as a 'new-type' research university with independent doctoral degree-awarding rights, Hochschule Geisenheim University is a global leader in specialized agricultural and environmental sciences. Its historic campus on the banks of the Rhine incorporates extensive experimental vineyards, wine cellars, greenhouse complexes, and climate research stations, spearheading international research into climate-adapted agriculture and sustainable food systems.
