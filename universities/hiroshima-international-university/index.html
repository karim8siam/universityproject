Hiroshima International University: Clinical Pharmacology & Medical Tech Guide

Established in 1998 in Kure and Higashi-Hiroshima, Hiroshima International University (Hiroshima Kokusai Daigaku - HIU) is a prominent Japanese private medical and health sciences institution. Renowned across western Japan for clinical pharmacy, medical radiological technology, rehabilitation sciences, and clinical psychology, HIU integrates advanced laboratory diagnostics with patient-centered community care.

HIU International Pharmaceutical Science Graduate Scholarship

Tuition waiver scholarship awarded to outstanding international researchers pursuing graduate degrees in clinical pharmacology, drug delivery systems, and cancer therapeutics at HIU.

Master of Science in Medical Radiological Technology and Nuclear Medicine

Two-year postgraduate curriculum delivering advanced training in radiation therapy planning, ultra-high-resolution CT imaging, and radiopharmaceutical biodistribution.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Pharmaceutical Formulation and Nanomedicine

Three-year doctoral position investigating polymeric nanoparticle carriers for targeted brain delivery through the blood-brain barrier and oral peptide delivery systems.

Seto Inland Sea Marine Natural Products Drug Discovery Project

Collaborative research project isolating bioactive cyclic peptides and polyketides from Seto Inland Sea marine microorganisms to discover novel antibiotic and antitumour leads.

Hiroshima University Hospital Advanced Clinical Pharmacy Residency

Six-month clinical residency training in therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM), anti-microbial stewardship, and intensive care clinical pharmacy in tertiary Hiroshima medical centres.

HIU Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Neuro-Rehabilitation Robotics

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment developing wearable robotic gait orthoses and surface EMG bio-signals for motor recovery after cerebral infarction.

MEXT Japanese Government (Monbukagakusho) Scholarship at HIU

Premier Japanese government scholarship granting full tuition exemption, monthly living stipends, and international flights for doctoral researchers in health sciences.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year university degree (or 16-year education equivalent) in pharmacy, medical technology, nursing, or biological sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completion of Master's degree (Shushi) in medical sciences or six-year professional pharmacy degree; submission of dissertation and passing oral defense exam.
Language proficiency
Japanese proficiency at JLPT N2 or N1 for clinical and hospital training; English proficiency (CEFR B2 / IELTS 6.0+) for doctoral biomedical laboratory tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2,000-character Japanese (or 1,500-word English) graduate research plan detailing experimental hypothesis and proposed host laboratory professor.
Application fee
JPY 35,000 graduate entrance examination fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma, complete transcript of records, JLPT or English proficiency test report, letter of recommendation, and research proposal.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Hiroshima International University is approximately JPY 750,000 to JPY 900,000 per year, plus an initial matriculation fee of JPY 200,000. Privately financed international scholars are eligible for HIU tuition reduction grants (covering 30% to 50% of tuition fees) and national JASSO study allowances of JPY 48,000 per month.
