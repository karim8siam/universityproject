Dickinson State University

Dickinson State University (DSU) is a regional public university in Dickinson, North Dakota, renowned for its agricultural studies, Theodore Roosevelt Center, pre-nursing clinical sciences, and teacher education.

Academic overview & research priorities

Home to the Theodore Roosevelt Center and digital presidential archive, DSU is an anchor institution in Western North Dakota. The university excels in range livestock management, beef cattle nutrition, digital historical research, and STEM educator preparation, serving rural communities across the High Plains.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with a minimum 2.00 GPA; DSU is test-optional. Nursing and teacher education require formal secondary admission following completion of foundational coursework with a 2.75+ GPA.
