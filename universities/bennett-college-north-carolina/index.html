Bennett College

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1873, Bennett College is one of only two historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs) in the United States dedicated exclusively to the education of women ('Bennett Belles'). Renowned for its civil rights legacy, leadership development, social justice advocacy, and STEM education.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Historic HBCU exclusively educating women since 1926
  • Pivotal headquarters for 1960 Greensboro sit-in civil rights organizing
  • Vibrant Global Studies and Social Justice Leadership Institute
  • Strong medical school and graduate health sciences feeder tracks

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Interdisciplinary Health Studies (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year curriculum integrating community health epidemiology, bioethics, public health policy, and maternal health advocacy.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Global Studies and Social Justice (Undergraduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary major examining African diaspora diplomacy, human rights law, peace studies, and international advocacy.
  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science & Information Technology (Undergraduate Degree) — Rigorous computing degree focusing on algorithmic problem-solving, cyber security, data analytics, and ethical AI.
