Dartmouth College: Ivy League Doctoral Fellowships, Master's & Research Grants

Dartmouth College is a world-renowned Ivy League institution in Hanover, New Hampshire, combining premier undergraduate education with world-leading research in computer science, biotechnology, polar environmental science, and biomedical engineering through the Guarini School, Thayer School of Engineering, and Geisel School of Medicine.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Guarini School of Graduate Studies Fully Funded PhD Fellowship

Comprehensive 5-year Ivy League doctoral funding package supporting doctoral scholars across artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, algorithms, and computational biology.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Engineering Sciences at Thayer School of Engineering

Unified engineering doctoral degree without departmental boundaries, emphasizing biotechnology, energy technologies, and advanced semiconductors.

Master of Science (M.S.) in Quantitative Biomedical Sciences (QBS)

Interdisciplinary master's bridging bioinformatics, health data science, and epidemiology at the Geisel School of Medicine and Dartmouth Cancer Center.

Institute of Arctic Studies Polar Environmental Research Assistantship

Field-based research analyzing polar ice core paleoclimatology, permafrost thaw dynamics, and Arctic environmental geopolitics.

Dartmouth Energy Collaborative & Irving Institute Climate Practicum

Interdisciplinary summer fellowship working with regional grid operators, federal laboratories, and international clean-energy non-profits.

Neukom Institute for Computational Science Postdoctoral Fellowship

Independent 3-year fellowship for scholars whose research bridges computation with the arts, natural sciences, or social inquiry.

Master of Science (M.S.) in Computer Science with Concentration in Cyber Security

Intensive graduate degree providing rigorous foundations in cryptographic protocols, system vulnerability analysis, and network security.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with exceptional academic achievement (typical undergraduate GPA 3.6+ on a 4.0 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate or master's degree in a relevant field; all accepted PhD candidates receive full funding packages covering tuition and generous living stipends.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100+, IELTS Academic 7.0+, or Duolingo English Test 130+.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive statement of purpose detailing intellectual trajectory, specific research questions, and alignment with 2–3 Dartmouth faculty mentors.
Application fee
$75 to $100 USD (fee waivers readily available based on financial need).
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, CV/resume, and research writing sample.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at Dartmouth is approximately $65,500 USD per year. However, all full-time PhD students admitted to the Guarini School of Graduate and Advanced Studies receive a 100% tuition waiver and a guaranteed annual living stipend of at least $40,000 USD for 5 years.
