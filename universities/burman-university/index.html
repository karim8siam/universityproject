Burman University: Biology, Behavioral Science & Scholarships Guide

Burman University is an accredited independent degree-granting university located on a scenic hilltop campus in Lacombe, Alberta. Founded in 1907, Burman offers rigorous programs in the natural sciences, behavioral sciences, business, and education, emphasizing undergraduate laboratory research, small class sizes, and ethical community service.

Burman University President's Academic Excellence Scholarship

Prestigious entrance scholarship recognizing high-achieving scholars entering biology, psychology, or natural sciences degree programs at Burman University.

Bachelor of Science in Biology: Parkland Ecology & Cellular Genetics Route

Four-year comprehensive science degree featuring hands-on field biology in the Alberta Aspen Parkland ecosystem, molecular genetics laboratories, and senior thesis research.

Central Alberta Aquatic and Wetland Ecosystems Research Fellowship

Ten-week field and laboratory research fellowship sampling central Alberta lakes, tracking freshwater invertebrate populations, and assessing agricultural nutrient runoff.

Lacombe Community Behavioral Science and Mental Health Practicum

Applied 300-hour professional internship embedded in youth centers, crisis intervention facilities, and family counseling agencies in the Red Deer and Lacombe regions.

Burman Faculty Research Grant in Neurobiology and Stress Physiology

One-year renewable research fellowship conducting physiological monitoring, cortisol assays, and cognitive stress resilience experiments in Burman's behavioral physiology lab.

Bachelor of Education in Elementary and Secondary STEM Teaching

Teacher certification program accredited by the Alberta Government, preparing future educators with hands-on school practicums, digital pedagogy, and STEM curriculum design.

Burman University Leadership and Campus Community Grant

Community leadership scholarship recognizing matriculating students who demonstrate exceptional service, ethical initiative, and commitment to civil society.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from an accredited institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent) in relevant coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not currently offering independent doctoral degrees; collaborative research transfer and master's credentials with partner Canadian universities.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 83 (minimum 20 in each component), IELTS Academic 6.5 (no individual score below 6.0), or Canadian Academic English Language (CAEL) 70.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for senior honours and research fellowships: 1,500-word research statement aligned with an active Burman faculty laboratory mentor.
Application fee
$100 CAD non-refundable application fee for North American students ($150 CAD for international applicants).
Required documentation
Official secondary and post-secondary transcripts, two confidential reference letters, personal statement of purpose, and proof of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is approximately $525 CAD per credit hour ($15,750 CAD per full-time academic year for 30 credits). International students pay standard tuition plus an international differential fee. Burman awards over $1.2 million annually in entrance scholarships, leadership awards up to $6,000 CAD, and undergraduate research fellowships.
