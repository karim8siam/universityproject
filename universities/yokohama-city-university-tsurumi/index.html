Yokohama City University - Tsurumi Campus

Yokohama City University’s Tsurumi Campus is an international powerhouse for structural biology, supramolecular biophysics, and drug design, co-located directly with the RIKEN Center for Biosystems Dynamics Research.

Academic overview & research priorities

Home to the Graduate School of Medical Life Science, YCU Tsurumi houses state-of-the-art ultra-high-field nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectrometers (up to 950 MHz) and cryo-electron microscopy suites. Faculty and graduate scholars pioneer molecular targeted drug discovery and cryo-EM protein structures.

Research admissions & international criteria

International applicants to the Graduate School of Medical Life Science must hold a Bachelor of Science in biology, chemistry, pharmacy, or physics. English-taught graduate programs require certified English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 80+ / IELTS 6.5+) and a faculty lab interview.
