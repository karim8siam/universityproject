North Campus at North Carolina State University

NC State North Campus · Raleigh, North Carolina, United States

North Campus represents the foundational historic academic heart of North Carolina State University (NC State) in Raleigh, home to the internationally preeminent Wilson College of Textiles, the College of Design, and the College of Humanities and Social Sciences. Blending world-leading polymer synthesis, medical textile engineering, architecture, and advanced physics research, North Campus drives technological innovation across the Research Triangle.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through the NC State University Graduate School or Undergraduate Admissions via Common App. TOEFL iBT 80+ (minimum 18 in each section) or IELTS Academic 6.5+ (minimum 6.5 in each section) for international applicants. Fall semester priority funding deadline January 10, 2027; Regular deadline March 1, 2027; Spring admission deadline October 15, 2026.
