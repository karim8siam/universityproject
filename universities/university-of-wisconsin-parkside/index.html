University of Wisconsin-Parkside

UW-Parkside · Kenosha, Wisconsin, United States

The University of Wisconsin-Parkside (UW-Parkside) is a comprehensive public university founded in 1968 on a 700-acre natural prairie and woodland preserve in Kenosha, Wisconsin, midway between Milwaukee and Chicago. Renowned for its pre-health professional placement rates in medical and dental schools, molecular biology, business, and criminal justice, UW-Parkside anchors the Southeast Wisconsin manufacturing and logistics hub.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UW-Parkside comprises four colleges: the College of Natural and Health Sciences, College of Business, Economics, and Computing, College of Social Sciences and Professional Studies, and the College of Arts and Humanities. The campus houses extensive prairie restoration sites and the Rita Tallent Picken Regional Center for Arts and Humanities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students, evaluating high school core GPA (minimum 2.5). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
