Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Charlotte & Global Online · Flexible Open Higher Education. North Carolina Open University (NCOU) provides open-access higher education combining asynchronous digital learning systems with applied competency-based assessment. Situated with administrative offices in Charlotte, NC, NCOU delivers international degree programs across software engineering, artificial intelligence, healthcare administration, and international commerce.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or recognized international secondary school leaving certificate. Conferred bachelor's degree for postgraduate master's and doctoral programs. TOEFL iBT 70+ or IELTS 6.0+; English medium instruction certificates accepted. Continuous monthly intake with terms beginning on the first Monday of every calendar month.
