Toyota Technological Institute: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1981 by Toyota Motor Corporation with a massive multi-billion-yen endowment, Toyota Technological Institute (TTI) is situated on the Hisakata Campus in Tempaku Ward, Nagoya. Maintaining an extraordinary student-to-faculty ratio of roughly 10:1, TTI focuses exclusively on advanced engineering, smart mobility, semiconductors, and artificial intelligence. TTI also founded the prestigious Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC), an internationally acclaimed computer science research institute affiliated with the University of Chicago. With world-class cleanrooms, molecular beam epitaxy (MBE) systems, and close ties to Toyota Central R&D Labs, TTI provides extraordinary research funding per student.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Direct institutional application through TTI Admissions Office
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Japanese & English (English-taught graduate engineering programs)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
Free (matriculation fee waived for scholarship recipients)
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
