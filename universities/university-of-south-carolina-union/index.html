Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Union · Upstate South Carolina Regional Palmetto College Campus. The University of South Carolina Union is a regional Palmetto College campus located in Union, South Carolina, serving Union, Laurens, and surrounding counties. Delivering accessible associate degrees, seamless bachelor's degree-completion tracks, and cutting-edge simulation nursing education, USC Union combines small class sizes with the resources and research prestige of the University of South Carolina System.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or approved secondary homeschool portfolio. Open admissions policy with placement assessment in mathematics and English composition. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants from non-English instructional backgrounds. Fall semester priority July 1, 2027; Spring semester priority December 1, 2026.
