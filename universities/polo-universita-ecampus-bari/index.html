Official institutional website ↗Location: Bari, Apulia, Italy

Institutional Overview

Polo Università eCampus Bari is the primary regional academic center for Università eCampus in Apulia, situated at the prestigious Impact Hub within Fiera del Levante. Offering five major faculties (Engineering, Economics, Law, Humanities, and Psychology), the Bari campus serves as a thriving physical and digital node for undergraduate, master's, and postgraduate specialization programs.
