The University of Tulsa

University of Tulsa · Tulsa, Oklahoma, United States

The University of Tulsa (TU) is a premier private Carnegie R2 research university located in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Nationally celebrated for its world-leading McDougall School of Petroleum Engineering, the Cyber Corps cybersecurity initiative (designated a National Center of Academic Excellence in Information Assurance), and the College of Law, TU is a powerhouse in energy, cyber defense, and applied science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TU operates five colleges: the College of Engineering and Computer Science, the Kendall College of Arts and Sciences, the Collins College of Business, the Oxley College of Health Sciences, and the College of Law. Key research centers include the Tulsa University Separation Technology Projects (TUSTP), the Cyber Security Information Research Center, and the Gilcrease Museum research collections.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are selective, evaluating high school academic rigor, GPA, standardized test scores (test-optional available), and leadership. Graduate and doctoral programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, program-specific GRE/GMAT scores (waivers available), statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
