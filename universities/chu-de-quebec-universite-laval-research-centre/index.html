CHU de Québec-Université Laval Research Centre: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Affiliated directly with the Faculty of Medicine at Université Laval, the CHU de Québec-Université Laval Research Centre brings together over 500 clinical and basic science investigators and more than 1,200 graduate students and postdoctoral fellows. Operating across specialized hospital axes—including the Centre Hospitalier de l'Université Laval (CHUL), L'Hôtel-Dieu de Québec, and Hôpital Saint-François d'Assise—the institute bridges bench-to-bedside discovery in precision medicine, genomics, reproductive health, and regenerative therapies.
