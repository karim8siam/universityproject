The University of Edinburgh (Edinburgh, Scotland)

Founded in 1583 in Edinburgh, Scotland, The University of Edinburgh is one of the ancient universities of the United Kingdom and ranked consistently among the world's top 25 to 30 institutions. The engine of the Scottish Enlightenment (alumni include David Hume, Adam Smith, and Charles Darwin), Edinburgh is globally celebrated for artificial intelligence (the School of Informatics is Europe's largest academic computing research centre), medicine, and theoretical physics. Edinburgh Doctoral College Scholarships (Principal's Career Development Scholarships)

The University of Edinburgh's premier doctoral funding scheme, supporting exceptional international researchers across all disciplines with full fees and living stipends.

Edinburgh PhD in Informatics (Artificial Intelligence & Data Science)

Doctoral research inside Europe's largest academic computing centre, offering world-class research in probabilistic machine learning, robotics, natural language processing, and quantum computing.

Edinburgh MSc in Computer Science

Intensive 1-year Master of Science degree covering advanced algorithms, computer systems architecture, formal verification, and distributed computation.

Edinburgh MSc in Artificial Intelligence

One of the world's longest-running and most celebrated master's degrees in AI, providing rigorous training in deep learning, natural language processing, automated planning, and computer vision.

Bayes Centre Data Science & Artificial Intelligence Placements

The University of Edinburgh's flagship innovation hub for data science and AI, collaborating across robotics, space satellites, medical informatics, and financial technology.

Edinburgh School of Informatics Summer Research Internships

Intensive 8 to 10-week summer research placements inside Informatics research labs, working directly under faculty guidance on cutting-edge machine learning and robotics projects.

Edinburgh Postgraduate Teaching Assistantships & Demonstratorships (T&D)

Contractual academic employment for registered doctoral researchers, assisting in tutorial leadership, coding laboratory exercises, and student assessment.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:1 honours degree or international equivalent (typically GPA 3.3/4.0 or 75-80%+) in computer science, mathematics, or relevant quantitative discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
UK 2:1 honours degree plus Master's with merit or distinction (or international equivalent), with demonstrable independent research competence.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.0 or 6.5 in all components) or TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 20 in each component).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-3 page research project proposal detailing research questions, proposed methodology, theoretical context, and faculty advisor alignment.
Application fee
£60 application fee for select competitive taught master's degrees; PhD applications are free of charge.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 2 confidential academic reference letters, personal statement of research interests, and curriculum vitae.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for international postgraduate research students are fully covered under the Edinburgh Doctoral College Scholarships (Principal's Career Development Scholarships). Recipients receive 100% full tuition fee coverage at international rates plus annual tax-free living stipends indexed to the UKRI rate (£19,237 to £20,500 per year for 3.5 to 4 years).
