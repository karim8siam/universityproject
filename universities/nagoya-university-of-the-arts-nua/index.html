Nagoya University of the Arts (NUA)

Nagoya University of the Arts (NUA - Nagoya Geijutsu Daigaku) is a premier private Japanese institution of visual arts, design, music, and performing arts situated in Kitanagoya, Aichi Prefecture. Established in 1970, NUA operates two expansive campuses: the East Campus (dedicated to Music and Performing Arts) and the West Campus (devoted to Fine Arts, Design, and Art Education).

Official institutional website ↗Location: Kitanagoya, Aichi Prefecture, Japan

Institutional Overview

NUA delivers Bachelor of Arts, Master of Fine Arts (MFA), and Master of Music (MMus) degrees across specialized disciplines including Instrumental Performance, Composition, Musical Theatre, Industrial Design, Ceramic Arts, Manga & Illustration, and Digital Media. Studios feature concert halls, professional recording suites, foundry casting facilities, and cutting-edge digital fabrication centers.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions evaluate creative aptitude through specialized practical examinations (auditions for music, drawing and portfolio presentations for fine arts and design). International students apply via special entrance exams with JLPT N2 or equivalent Japanese competence. Tuition reductions, alumni memorial awards, and instrument loan subsidies support enrolled artists.
