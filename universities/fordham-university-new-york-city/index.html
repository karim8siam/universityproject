Fordham University

Fordham University is the Jesuit University of New York, operating historic campuses at Rose Hill in the Bronx and Lincoln Center in Manhattan, celebrated for ethical legal scholarship, Wall Street-connected business finance, and data science.

Academic overview & research priorities

Home to the Gabelli School of Business, Fordham School of Law, and the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences, Fordham combines rigorous Jesuit humanism with New York City’s financial, technological, and cultural ecosystems. Its Center for Cybersecurity is designated a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions use the Common Application with holistic review. Graduate programs require an accredited Bachelor degree (typically 3.0+ GPA), GRE or GMAT (where applicable), statement of intent, and letters of recommendation.
