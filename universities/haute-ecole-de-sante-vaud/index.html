HESAV: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Haute École de Santé Vaud (HESAV), located in Lausanne, Canton of Vaud, is the premier French-speaking Swiss university of applied sciences for health professions, integrated within the prestigious HES-SO network (University of Applied Sciences and Arts Western Switzerland). Situated in the health and biosciences innovation hub adjacent to the Lausanne University Hospital (CHUV), HESAV is renowned for nursing sciences, physiotherapy, radiography, and midwifery.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Master of Science (MSc) in Health Sciences (Joint HES-SO / UNIL Program)

Two-year specialized interprofessional master's degree delivered jointly with the University of Lausanne, advancing clinical expertise, healthcare quality management, and translational health services research.

Laboratory of Clinical Movement Analysis and Neuro-Rehabilitation Research Fellowship

Six-month research residency within HESAV's biomechanics lab evaluating gait symmetry, robotic exoskeleton assistance, and neuromuscular recovery in stroke and Parkinson's patients.

Canton of Vaud Higher Education Merit Grant and Swiss Health Leadership Award

Need-and-merit based funding program supported by the Vaud Department of Health and Social Action (DSAS) to support talented students pursuing advanced degrees in applied healthcare.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Chronic Disease Management and Digital Health Interventions

Two-year postdoctoral position designing remote patient monitoring apps, evaluating digital interventions for diabetic self-care, and assessing nurse-led telemedicine protocols across Canton Vaud.

CHUV Lausanne University Hospital Advanced Clinical Practice Practicum

Five-month advanced clinical immersion inside the intensive care units, emergency departments, and rehabilitation wards of the Centre Hospitalier Universitaire Vaudois (CHUV) in Lausanne.

Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship in Nursing and Health Sciences (Joint HESAV / UNIL)

Four-year joint doctoral candidacy conducted between HESAV and the Faculty of Biology and Medicine at UNIL, exploring palliative care interventions, patient safety culture, and healthcare ethics.

Certificate of Advanced Studies (CAS) in Clinical Ethics and Healthcare Law

Postgraduate certificate curriculum for senior healthcare professionals examining end-of-life decision-making, patient autonomy, informed consent, and resource allocation in Swiss medical institutions.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized Bachelor of Science degree in Nursing, Physiotherapy, Radiography, Midwifery, or Health Sciences from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
HESAV conducts collaborative doctoral programs (PhD in Nursing Sciences or Health Sciences) jointly with the University of Lausanne (UNIL) Faculty of Biology and Medicine.
Language proficiency
Proficiency in French (minimum CEFR B2/C1 level) as clinical coursework and clinical hospital rotations are conducted in French; English B2 required for research literature.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-3 page Letter of Motivation and professional clinical research plan detailing healthcare improvement objectives and methodological orientation.
Application fee
CHF 150 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, certified transcripts of records, comprehensive professional resume (CV), clinical license copies, and valid passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for master's programs at HESAV is approximately CHF 1,000 to CHF 1,500 per academic year for Swiss residents and European students (approx. CHF 1,500/year for non-EU/EEA students under HES-SO statutory rates). Swiss cantonal training bursaries and paid clinical health internships at CHUV and regional medical clinics provide student compensation averaging CHF 1,500 to CHF 2,500 per month.
