Kiel University of Applied Sciences

Kiel University of Applied Sciences (Hochschule Kiel) is the largest university of applied sciences in Schleswig-Holstein, internationally recognized for naval architecture, renewable energy engineering, computer science, and media economics.

Academic overview & excellence

Located on the eastern shore of the Kiel Fjord, FH Kiel is an engineering and innovation powerhouse comprising six faculties: Computer Science & Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Media, Agriculture, Social Work & Health, and Business Management. The university operates specialized marine research institutes, the Center for Renewable Energy, and close industry partnerships with German maritime and green energy conglomerates.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate degree admission requires a German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or international equivalent validated through uni-assist. Master's programs require a relevant bachelor's degree with a minimum grade equivalent of 2.5 (German grading system). English-taught engineering programs require TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0; German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4.
