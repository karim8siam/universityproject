Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1917 as part of the progressive Seijo Gakuen educational movement, Seijo University emphasizes self-directed learning, individuality, and small-group seminar discussions. The leafy Setagaya campus unites the Faculty of Economics, Faculty of Arts and Literature (celebrated for Japanese folklore and film studies founded by Kunio Yanagita), Faculty of Law, and the acclaimed Faculty of Social Innovation, which addresses emerging technological and social challenges.

Research admissions & international criteria

International applicants submit the EJU score (Japanese and general subjects), personal statement, and take a campus-based essay exam and interview. Graduate school admissions require a recognized bachelor's degree, a detailed research plan in Japanese (or English for select tracks), transcripts, and an academic interview. JLPT N1 is normally expected for humanities and law tracks.
