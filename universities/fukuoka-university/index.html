Fukuoka University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Fukuoka University (Fukuoka Daigaku), founded in 1934 in the dynamic Asian gateway city of Fukuoka on the island of Kyushu, is the largest private comprehensive university in western Japan. Renowned worldwide for pioneering the 'Fukuoka Method' (semi-aerobic landfill technology adopted by the UN in over 20 countries), Fukuoka University excels in environmental waste engineering, cardiovascular medicine, regenerative pharmacology, and sports science.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctoral Program in Environmental Waste Engineering ('Fukuoka Method' Semi-Aerobic Landfill)

Three-year doctoral candidacy within the internationally recognized Environmental Waste Research Laboratory, optimizing semi-aerobic landfill stabilization, leachate bio-filtration, and greenhouse gas mitigation.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Pharmaceutical Sciences & Molecular Drug Discovery

Four-year doctoral curriculum researching synthetic organocatalysis, targeted small-molecule kinase inhibitors for oncology, and lipid nanoparticle drug delivery systems.

Master of Engineering in Mechanical Systems and Disaster Prevention Robotics

Two-year graduate engineering program developing search-and-rescue tracked rovers, aerial drone telemetry for landslide monitoring, and earthquake seismic isolation dampers.

Fukuoka University International Student Special Academic Merit Award

Merit-based tuition discount granted directly by Fukuoka University to top-performing international graduate students pursuing degrees in humanities, social sciences, or natural sciences.

Fukuoka University Hospital Cardiovascular Regenerative Medicine Research Fellowship

Research appointment at Fukuoka University Hospital investigating induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) cardiac muscle patches for post-infarction myocardial regeneration and heart failure models.

Kyushu Regional Environmental Circular Economy & Waste Sorting Practicum

Four-month practical training internship examining industrial symbiosis in Kitakyushu and Fukuoka Eco-Towns, automated sensor-based polymer sorting, and zero-waste municipal systems.

JSPS / Fukuoka University Postdoctoral Fellowship in East Asian Maritime Trade & Economic History

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship exploring Fukuoka's historical role as Japan's ancient trade gateway (Hakata), early modern maritime commerce with the Ryukyu Kingdom and Qing Dynasty China.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education leading to a recognized bachelor's degree from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
A recognized master's degree or professional degree; submission of prior academic thesis, research plan, and pre-approval from a prospective Fukuoka supervisor.
Language proficiency
Proficiency in Japanese (JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-medium divisions) or English (TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.0 for international science and engineering programs).
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-5 page research outline including specific problem formulation, academic rationale, experimental apparatus required, and target completion schedule.
Application fee
¥35,000 examination screening fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificates, academic transcripts, master's thesis abstract, CV, 2 confidential letters of recommendation, and supervisor pre-approval letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international graduate programs at Fukuoka University averages ¥800,000 to ¥1,100,000 per year (approximately USD $5,500 to $7,500). Non-Japanese graduate students are eligible for Fukuoka University Special Scholarships (providing 30% to 50% tuition reductions), JASSO Honors Scholarships (¥48,000 per month), and Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarships providing 100% tuition waivers and monthly stipends of ¥145,000.
