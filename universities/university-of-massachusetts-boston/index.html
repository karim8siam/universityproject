University of Massachusetts Boston

UMass Boston · Boston, Massachusetts, United States

The University of Massachusetts Boston (UMass Boston), founded in 1964, is Boston's only public research university, occupying a spectacular waterfront campus on Columbia Point overlooking Boston Harbor and neighboring the John F. Kennedy Presidential Library. Recognized as an urban research institution and third-most diverse university in the US, UMass Boston is celebrated for coastal and marine sciences, cybersecurity, gerontology, cancer biology, and public policy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMass Boston comprises the School for the Environment, College of Science and Mathematics, College of Management, McCormack Graduate School of Policy and Global Studies, and Manning College of Nursing and Health Sciences. The university is home to the Center for Personalized Cancer Therapy, the Coastal Adaptation Center, and the Gerontology Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are conducted via the Common Application (minimum recommended GPA 3.0; test-optional). Graduate and doctoral applicants require an accredited baccalaureate degree, minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and a statement of academic objectives.
