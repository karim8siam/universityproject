The Texas A&M University System RELLIS Campus

TAMUS RELLIS · Bryan, Texas, United States

The Texas A&M University System RELLIS Campus is a premier 2,400-acre collaborative research and higher education center in Bryan, Texas. Uniting ten Texas A&M System regional universities with leading defense agencies, private industry, and Blinn College, RELLIS conducts cutting-edge research in hypersonic aerodynamics, autonomous connected mobility at the Bush Combat Development Complex (BCDC), and cyber-physical infrastructure.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Transfer admission requires 45+ transferable semester credits with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 to 2.5 depending on the partnering Texas A&M System university degree program. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international candidates. Fall priority admission July 1, 2027; Spring priority admission December 1, 2026.
