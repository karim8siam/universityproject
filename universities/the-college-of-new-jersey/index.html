The College of New Jersey

TCNJ · Ewing, New Jersey, United States

The College of New Jersey (TCNJ) is a highly selective public undergraduate-focused institution situated in Ewing Township, New Jersey. Consistently ranked the #1 Public Regional University in the North by U.S. News & World Report, TCNJ is renowned for its undergraduate research culture, exceptional four-year graduation rate, and acclaimed programs in engineering, education, and biology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TCNJ comprises seven schools: Science, Engineering, the Arts and Communication, Business, Education, Humanities and Social Sciences, and Nursing and Health Sciences. Featuring modern laboratories in the STEM Complex and Armstrong Hall, TCNJ pairs students directly with faculty on competitive NSF-, NIH-, and state-funded research initiatives.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective (average admitted GPA 3.65+; test-optional). Evaluation is holistic, assessing high school honors/AP coursework, extracurricular leadership, personal statement, and letters of recommendation. Specialized programs (e.g. 7-year accelerated medical BS/MD with Rutgers NJMS) require competitive standardized tests and separate interview rounds.
