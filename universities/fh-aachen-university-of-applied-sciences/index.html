FH Aachen University of Applied Sciences

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

With over 15,000 students across Aachen and Jülich, FH Aachen is consistently ranked among Germany's top universities of applied sciences, internationally celebrated for aerospace engineering, renewable energy technology, biomedical engineering, and automotive mechanics.

Institution type
Public University of Applied Sciences (Fachhochschule)
Campus location
Aachen (Main Technology Campus) & Jülich (Solar/Biomedical Campus)
Accreditation
Ministry of Culture and Science of North Rhine-Westphalia / ASIIN
Admissions channel
FH Aachen Student Service Center (Studierendensekretariat)
Instruction language
German and English (multiple fully English-taught engineering programs)
Financial aid & support
Zero tuition fees for domestic/EU students, Deutschlandstipendium, and NRW doctoral stipends

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Aerospace engineering, flight mechanics, and space satellite systems
  • Solar thermal energy, hydrogen technology, and Jülich Solar Campus research
  • Biomedical engineering, medical physics, and biotechnology
  • Mechanical engineering and automotive mechatronics

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Aerospace Engineering (Graduate Degree) — Top-ranked European engineering degree focusing on aircraft propulsion, composite structures, and space payload design.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Renewable Energy Systems (Undergraduate Degree) — Hands-on engineering program located at the Jülich Solar Campus investigating photovoltaics, wind energy, and thermal storage.
  • Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering (Graduate Degree) — English-taught graduate program combining medical device development, biosensors, and clinical biomechanics.
