University of Michigan: Top-Ranked Engineering, AI, Medicine & Rackham Fellowships

The University of Michigan (UMich) in Ann Arbor is one of the world's greatest public research universities, conducting over $1.8 billion in annual research expenditures. Ranked among the top 10 globally, UMich is legendary for the College of Engineering, Michigan Medicine, Mcity autonomous vehicle testing, and the Rackham Graduate School.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Rackham Merit & Departmental Doctoral Fellowship (Full 5-Year Funding)

Comprehensive 5-year doctoral fellowship funding exceptional PhD candidates conducting high-impact research in artificial intelligence, autonomous robotics, and computer architecture.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Robotics at Michigan Robotics Institute

Top-ranked interdisciplinary robotics doctoral program based in the $75M Ford Robotics Building, advancing legged locomotion, robotic prosthetics, and space autonomy.

Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering & Neural Prosthetics

Two-year master's curriculum exploring brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), regenerative tissue engineering, targeted drug delivery, and microfluidic diagnostic chips.

Mcity Autonomous Vehicle Proving Ground Research Assistantship

Field-based research post at Mcity, the world's premier purpose-built testing environment for connected and automated vehicles, evaluating edge-case safety algorithms.

Michigan Medicine & Biointerfaces Institute Clinical Engineering Practicum

Hospital-collaborative engineering practicum working alongside Michigan Medicine clinicians developing microfluidic cancer cell isolators and point-of-care diagnostics.

Michigan Society of Fellows Postdoctoral Fellowship

One of the most prestigious postdoctoral fellowships in the United States, granting 3-year independent research appointments and faculty affiliation across UMich departments.

Master of Science in Information (MSI) in UX Research & Big Data Analytics

World-leading professional master's degree exploring human-computer interaction, qualitative user experience testing, data curation, and algorithm design for social platforms.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with high academic distinction (typical undergraduate GPA 3.6+ on a 4.0 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field; all full-time PhD students admitted to Rackham Graduate School receive guaranteed multi-year funding packages covering full tuition, living stipends, and health insurance.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100+, IELTS Academic 7.5+, or Duolingo English Test 130+.
Research statement / Proposal
Academic Statement of Purpose detailing specific research achievements, scholarly goals, and alignment with 2–3 faculty research laboratories.
Application fee
$90 USD (fee waivers available for applicants with financial hardship or affiliated with diversity pipeline programs).
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, 3 confidential letters of recommendation, CV/resume, and Personal Statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at UMich averages approximately $55,000 USD per year for out-of-state/international students. However, all full-time PhD students in engineering and natural sciences receive 100% tuition coverage and guaranteed annual research/teaching stipends of at least $38,000 to $43,000 USD for 5 years.
