Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1946 along the scenic Hillsborough River in Temple Terrace near Tampa, Florida College provides an intimate collegiate environment grounded in Christian character and service. Accredited by SACSCOC, the college offers undergraduate degrees in elementary education, biblical studies, business administration, communication, and sports kinesiology.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires graduation from an accredited high school or GED certificate with a minimum unweighted GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale. Applicants submit official academic transcripts, standardized test scores (SAT, ACT, or CLT), and a personal reference from a high school counselor, minister, or educator. Institutional merit scholarships provide significant tuition discounts.
