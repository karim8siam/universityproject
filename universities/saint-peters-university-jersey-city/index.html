Saint Peter's University

Saint Peter's · Jersey City, New Jersey, United States

Saint Peter's University is a Jesuit Catholic university located in Jersey City, New Jersey, just minutes from Lower Manhattan. Acclaimed for data science, cybersecurity, nursing, accountancy, and NCAA Division I basketball glory (2022 Elite Eight 'Peacock' Cinderella run).

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Saint Peter's features the Data Science Institute, the Cyber Security Center of Excellence, the Mac Mahon Student Center, and the School of Nursing. Its prime location in the New York metropolitan area provides students with direct access to Wall Street, Jersey City's financial district, and top medical centers.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.3) with test-optional application options. Master of Science in Data Science and Cybersecurity programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA in quantitative, computing, or business disciplines.
