Explore certified osteopathy diplomas, naturopathy curricula, humanistic counseling practicums, and lifelong learning grants at Università Popolare Santa Sofia.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Padova & Milan, Italy

Institutional Overview

Università Popolare Santa Sofia is an accredited Italian folk university institution (Università Popolare) affiliated with CNUPI (Confederazione Nazionale delle Università Popolari Italiane). Dedicated to lifelong learning and cultural democratization, Santa Sofia delivers certified diplomas in osteopathy, naturopathy, humanistic counseling, phytotherapy, and holistic health disciplines across northern Italy.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Rigorous certified training in bio-natural disciplines complying with Law 4/2013 for professional practitioners
  • Pioneering multi-year diploma programs in osteopathy and traditional European naturopathy
  • Emphasis on humanistic psychology, active empathetic listening, and holistic patient counseling
  • Experiential classroom weekends allowing working adults to balance professional careers with tertiary study
