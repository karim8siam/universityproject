Penn State Beaver

Penn State Beaver · Monaca, Pennsylvania, United States

Penn State Beaver is a student-centered commonwealth campus of the Pennsylvania State University located on 100 wooded acres in Monaca, Pennsylvania, northwest of Pittsburgh. Renowned for engineering, project management, cybersecurity analytics, and seamless Penn State 2+2 transitions.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Penn State Beaver offers complete four-year baccalaureate degrees in high-demand fields as well as the world-renowned Penn State 2+2 plan (starting at Beaver and completing at University Park or another campus in over 275 majors). It features the Michael Baker Jr. Engineering Building, the Beaver campus police training facility, and advanced laboratory science suites.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Penn State uses a centralized undergraduate admissions application (via MyPennState or Common App). Admissions evaluates high school GPA (average 3.2-3.6), coursework rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Transfer students require a minimum 2.0 GPA for university admission.
