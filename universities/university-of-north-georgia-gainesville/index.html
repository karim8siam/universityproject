University of North Georgia - Gainesville

UNG Gainesville · Oakwood, Georgia, United States

The University of North Georgia's Gainesville Campus, located in Oakwood, GA, is the largest campus within UNG, designated by the Georgia General Assembly as The Military College of Georgia and a state leadership institution. UNG Gainesville delivers comprehensive bachelor's and graduate degrees in engineering physics, cybersecurity, education, and biological conservation.
