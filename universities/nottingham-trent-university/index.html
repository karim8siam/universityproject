Nottingham Trent University: Smart Materials, Forensic Science & Sustainability Guide

Nottingham Trent University (NTU) is an acclaimed British public research university, awarded Queen's Anniversary Prizes for world-leading research in medical devices, smart textiles, food security, and optical imaging, celebrated for outstanding graduate employability.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

NTU Vice-Chancellor's PhD Studentship in Smart Textiles & Wearable Electronics

Doctoral studentship based at the Advanced Textiles Research Group (ATRG) developing electronic yarn (E-yarn), embedded physiological sensors, and solar-harvesting fabrics.

MSc in Biomedical Science & Pharmacology

IBMS-accredited Master of Science exploring molecular toxicology, antimicrobial resistance, cancer immunotherapy, and advanced biomedical diagnostics.

Ph.D. in Forensic Science & Non-Destructive Explosives Detection

Doctoral research position developing handheld Raman spectroscopy and chemical sensors for trace detection of illicit narcotics and peroxide-based improvised explosives.

Urban Vertical Farming & Controlled Environment Agriculture Project

Agritech research investigation evaluating LED photosynthetic spectral recipes, aeroponic nutrient recycling, and crop yield optimization in urban vertical farm installations.

Boots UK & NTU Pharmaceutical Product Development Practicum

Twelve-month paid industrial placement situated at Boots UK headquarters in Nottingham, formulating skincare products, conducting stability trials, and testing active ingredients.

Medical Technologies Innovation Facility (MTIF) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the Medical Technologies Innovation Facility developing biocompatible 3D-printed orthopedic implants and microfluidic diagnostic chips.

Senior Lecturer in Cyber Security & Network Forensics

Academic faculty post in the School of Science and Technology delivering undergraduate and postgraduate modules in cloud security, malware reverse engineering, and threat intelligence.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:1 or 2:2 Honours degree or international equivalent in a relevant discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First class or upper second class (2:1) UK Honours degree or Master's degree with Merit/Distinction in related fields.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with a minimum of 5.5 (or 6.0 for science and law) in each sub-test.
Research statement / Proposal
1,500-word doctoral research project proposal outlining methodology, literature gap, and alignment with NTU research centers.
Application fee
Zero application fee for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Degree certificates and transcripts, personal statement, two references (academic preferred), and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time domestic postgraduate tuition ranges from £9,000 to £11,500 per year. International student tuition is £16,500 to £18,900 per year. Fully funded PhD studentships provide 100% tuition coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend (£19,237).
