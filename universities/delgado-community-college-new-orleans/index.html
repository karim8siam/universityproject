Delgado Community College

Delgado Community College is Louisiana’s oldest and largest community college, located across several campuses in the Greater New Orleans area, celebrated for its Charity School of Nursing, maritime technology, culinary arts, and industrial trades.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating the historic Charity School of Nursing (founded 1894), the Maritime and Industrial Training Center in New Orleans East, and culinary arts apprenticeships, Delgado trains healthcare practitioners, offshore marine pilots, and industrial technicians central to the Gulf Coast economy. The college offers comprehensive associate degrees, university transfer agreements, and workforce certifications.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open-enrollment admissions for secondary school graduates or GED holders. The Charity School of Nursing and allied health programs require competitive clinical prerequisites, ATI TEAS examination scores, and background screening.
