RWTH Aachen University (Aachen, Germany)

Established in 1870 in Aachen, North Rhine-Westphalia, RWTH Aachen University is Germany's largest technical university and a prominent founding member of the TU9 alliance. RWTH is internationally celebrated as an engineering powerhouse, driving breakthroughs in autonomous robotics, advanced metallurgy, automotive technology, and high-performance computing. Explore tuition-free degree programs, salaried doctoral employment contracts, and world-class faculty profiles.

RWTH Aachen Excellence & Deutschlandstipendium Scholarships

Prestigious national merit scholarship co-funded by the German Federal Government and industrial partners, supporting high-achieving engineering and science scholars.

RWTH Salaried Doctoral Positions (Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in TV-L E13)

Full-time salaried doctoral research contracts across RWTH's world-leading engineering and informatics institutes, developing cutting-edge technologies in industry-partnered projects.

RWTH Master of Science in Robotic Systems Engineering

Cutting-edge international master's degree focusing on autonomous robotic manipulators, mobile robotics, artificial intelligence for cyber-physical systems, and human-robot interaction.

RWTH Master of Science in Computer Science (Informatik)

Highly flexible, research-intensive master's program taught in English, offering specializations in software verification, machine learning, computer graphics, and distributed systems.

Jülich Aachen Research Alliance (JARA) Advanced Research Placements

Joint research initiative between RWTH Aachen and Forschungszentrum Jülich, enabling graduate scholars to utilize Europe's fastest supercomputers for quantum and brain simulation.

RWTH Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP International)

Ten-week structured summer research internship program in RWTH Aachen laboratories, providing international undergraduate scholars with direct research project mentorship.

RWTH Student & Graduate Assistantships (HiWi Positions)

Part-time student assistantships (*Hilfswissenschaftler/HiWi*) across university institutes, providing instructional experience, code evaluation, and practical research experience.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in engineering or computer science closely aligned with RWTH curricula, demonstrating rigorous foundations in mathematics and mechanics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Relevant Master's degree (grade 'gut' or better, typically 2.0 or higher in German grading scale); formal confirmation of doctoral supervision by an RWTH university chair professor.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency for English-taught degrees: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90 (German programs require TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 5 to 10-page scientific exposé describing doctoral research framework, methodology, and work plan.
Application fee
No tuition fee; nominal semester social contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approx. €320/semester including public regional transit ticket.
Required documentation
Certified university transcripts, detailed module handbook/course descriptions, curriculum vitae, and academic letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free of charge for all domestic and international students. Doctoral candidates are predominantly employed on salaried public-sector research employment contracts (Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in under TV-L E13), earning gross salaries of €52,000 to €58,000 per year with comprehensive statutory health insurance and pension benefits.
