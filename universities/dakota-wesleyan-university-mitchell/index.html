Dakota Wesleyan University

Dakota Wesleyan University (DWU) is a private university in Mitchell, South Dakota, affiliated with the United Methodist Church, celebrated for its Donna Willrodt Christiansen College of Nursing, agricultural leadership initiatives, and George and Eleanor McGovern Center for Leadership and Public Service.

Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission and CCNE, DWU provides exceptional nursing education, athletic training, wildlife conservation, and digital business. The university’s McGovern Center fosters civic engagement, rural community leadership, and social justice research across the Northern Plains.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with a minimum 2.50 GPA; DWU is test-optional. Nursing and athletic training programs require prerequisite college biology/chemistry coursework with a 2.75+ cumulative GPA.
