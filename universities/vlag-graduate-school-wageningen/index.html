Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Accredited by the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences (KNAW), the VLAG Graduate School (Biobased, Chemical, Food and Health Sciences) is an internationally celebrated research and doctoral training school based at Wageningen University & Research (WUR). Bringing together over 100 research groups, 500 Ph.D. candidates, and 120 postdoctoral fellows, VLAG is the global leader in food technology, nutritional biology, biomolecular chemistry, and circular biobased production systems.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Food technology, structured foods, plant-based meat analogs, and soft matter physics
  • Nutritional genomics, human gut microbiome, and metabolic health
  • Biomolecular chemistry, synthetic biology, and industrial biotechnology
  • Biobased circular materials, green biorefinery, and biopolymer processing

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • VLAG Ph.D. Training Program in Food Science & Nutrition (Doctoral Degree) — Four-year paid doctoral research position with personalized VLAG training plan, international secondments, and advanced doctoral masterclasses.
  • Master of Science in Food Technology (Specialization in Sustainable Food Process Engineering) (Graduate Degree) — Top-ranked two-year master's program at Wageningen University investigating high-moisture extrusion, food microstructures, and product sensory design.
  • Master of Science in Molecular Life Sciences (Biological Chemistry Track) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year graduate degree exploring protein crystallography, enzymology, and bio-nanotechnology.
