Stanford University (Stanford, California)

Established in 1885 in the heart of Silicon Valley, Stanford University is widely recognized as one of the world's most innovative research institutions. Stanford's faculty and alumni have founded companies that generate more than USD $2.7 trillion in annual revenue (including Google, Hewlett-Packard, Cisco, and NVIDIA) and have received 85 Nobel laureates, 29 Turing Awards, and 20 Fields Medalists. Stanford leads the world in artificial intelligence, electrical engineering, nanophotonics, and bioengineering. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Knight-Hennessy Scholars Program & Stanford Graduate Fellowships (SGF)

Stanford's premier multidisciplinary graduate fellowship, awarding up to 100 fully funded graduate scholarships annually to students across all seven Stanford schools to address global challenges.

Stanford PhD in Computer Science

Five-year doctoral research program inside the Gates Computer Science Building in Silicon Valley, conducting frontier investigations in artificial intelligence, computer vision, robotics, systems architecture, and theory.

Stanford Master of Science in Computer Science

Globally celebrated terminal Master of Science degree (45 units) offering specialized tracks in Artificial Intelligence, Information Management & Analytics, Systems, Human-Computer Interaction, and Theory.

Stanford Master of Science in Electrical Engineering

World-leading 45-unit graduate degree program in Packard Building, offering cutting-edge specializations in Integrated Circuits, Quantum and Photonic Systems, Robotics & Autonomous Systems, and Signal Processing.

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) Placements

Interdisciplinary artificial intelligence institute at Stanford bringing together computer scientists, neuroscientists, ethicists, and medical researchers to advance human-centered foundation models and AI safety.

Stanford CURIS (Undergraduate Research in Computer Science)

Signature 10-week summer research internship program pairing students directly with Stanford Computer Science faculty mentors to conduct novel investigations in AI, graphics, systems, and security.

Stanford Course Assistantships (CA / Teaching Assistantships)

Departmental academic teaching appointments providing instructional training and substantial financial compensation for leading weekly problem sections, holding office hours, and grading assignments.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year U.S. Bachelor's degree or international equivalent with exceptional academic marks (typically 3.7+ / 4.0 GPA) in computer science, electrical engineering, or quantitative sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Outstanding undergraduate or Master's degree with demonstrable scientific research ability, significant publications, and compelling letters of recommendation.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 25 in each subscore); Stanford does not accept IELTS scores.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page Statement of Purpose detailing research accomplishments, intellectual interests, and faculty alignment at Stanford.
Application fee
USD $125 graduate application fee submitted via the Stanford Graduate Admissions portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 3 academic letters of recommendation, resume / CV, and academic writing sample or code portfolio.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for Master's programs at Stanford is charged based on unit enrollment (typically USD $62,000 to $68,000 per academic year for full-time graduate study). For doctoral researchers, Stanford provides guaranteed full funding for the regular duration of the PhD: 100% full tuition coverage plus annual living stipends of USD $52,000 to $56,000.
