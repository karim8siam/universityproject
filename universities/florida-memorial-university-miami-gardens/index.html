Florida Memorial University

Academic overview & research priorities

As South Florida's only Historically Black University and the birthplace of the hymn 'Lift Every Voice and Sing' (composed by FMU alumni J. Rosamond and James Weldon Johnson), Florida Memorial University occupies a historic place in American higher education. FMU is particularly renowned for operating one of the few collegiate commercial aviation flight training programs at an HBCU, featuring modern multi-engine aircraft and flight simulators.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate entrance requires graduation from an accredited high school with a minimum GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale, official academic transcripts, and personal statement. Aviation flight students must pass an FAA Class 1 or 2 medical examination. Graduate MBA applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree, resume, and two letters of recommendation.
