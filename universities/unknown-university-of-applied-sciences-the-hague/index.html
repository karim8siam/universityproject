Unknown University of Applied Sciences: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located in the impact innovation cluster of The Hague, Unknown University of Applied Sciences (formerly known as Unknown Group / Global School for Entrepreneurship) is an accredited Dutch university of applied sciences recognized by NVAO. Rejecting traditional lecture-hall rote learning, Unknown UAS operates a venture-accredited model where students found, validate, launch, and scale real commercial enterprises as their core degree requirement. Mentored by experienced serial founders, angel investors, and venture capitalists, students master customer acquisition, unit economics, fundraising, and sustainable impact entrepreneurship.
