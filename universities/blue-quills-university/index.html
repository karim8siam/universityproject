Institutional Overview

University nuhelot’įne thaiyots’į nistameyimâkanak Blue Quills (UnBQ) is Canada's first Indigenous-owned and operated university institution. Formed in 1970 following a historic sit-in reclaiming the former residential school facility, Blue Quills is governed jointly by seven First Nations in Treaty 6 territory, offering transformative Indigenous-centered degrees in education, leadership, Cree and Dene languages, and social work.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Historic landmark institution representing self-determination and reclamation of Indigenous education
  • World-class revitalization of nehiyawewin (Cree) and Dënesųłı̨né (Dene) languages and oral archives
  • WINHEC-accredited Master's and PhD programs grounded in Indigenous ceremonies, protocols, and cosmologies
  • Holistic community learning model integrating restorative healing, land-based education, and ecological stewardship
