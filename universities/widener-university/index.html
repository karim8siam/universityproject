Widener University

Widener · Chester, Pennsylvania, United States

Widener University is a private doctoral civic-engagement university founded in 1821, situated on a 110-acre campus in Chester, Pennsylvania, within the greater Philadelphia metropolitan area. Renowned for professional education in engineering, nursing, clinical psychology, social work, and law through the Widener University Delaware Law School and Commonwealth Law School, Widener integrates rigorous academic theory with experiential community clinic leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Widener comprises seven schools and colleges: the School of Engineering, School of Nursing, College of Arts & Sciences, School of Business Administration, Center for Human Sexuality Studies, School of Law (Delaware Law School in Wilmington, DE, and Commonwealth Law School in Harrisburg, PA), and the College of Health and Human Services.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, essays, and extracurricular involvement with test-optional reviews. Graduate doctoral and master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, personal statement, professional resume, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+). Graduate scholarships and assistantships are available.
