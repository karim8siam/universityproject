Pima Medical Institute - Las Vegas

Pima Medical Las Vegas · Las Vegas, Nevada, United States

Pima Medical Institute is a premier private allied healthcare educational institution founded in 1972, operating an expansive regional health science campus in Las Vegas, Nevada. Renowned for specialized training in radiography, respiratory therapy, veterinary technology, physical therapist assistant, and diagnostic medical sonography.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Pima Medical Las Vegas features modern medical laboratories equipped with digital radiographic imaging suites, mechanical ventilator training workstations, ultrasound machines, and surgical veterinary suites. Students alternate classroom instruction with intensive clinical externships across major hospital systems in the Las Vegas Valley (UMC, Sunrise Health, St. Rose Dominican).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admission requires a high school diploma or recognized equivalency. Applicants must take the Wonderlic Scholastic Level Exam (SLE) meeting program-specific score benchmarks, pass an admissions interview, and clear criminal background and drug screening checks for clinical placements.
