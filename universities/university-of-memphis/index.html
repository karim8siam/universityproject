University of Memphis

UofM · Memphis, Tennessee, United States

The University of Memphis is an elite Carnegie R1 public research university located in Memphis, Tennessee. Celebrated for world-class research in biomedical engineering, audiology and speech-language pathology, supply chain logistics, cognitive psychology, and earthquake hazard science, UofM serves as a cultural and technological pillar of the Mid-South.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UofM encompasses 13 colleges and schools including the Herff College of Engineering, College of Arts and Sciences, Loewenberg College of Nursing, Fogelman College of Business and Economics, and the School of Public Health. Key research institutes include the FedEx Institute of Technology, the Center for Earthquake Research and Information (CERI), and the Institute for Intelligent Systems (IIS).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, course curriculum, and optional standardized tests. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
