Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Tecnopolis Parco Scientifico e Tecnologico is the premier science and technology hub of the University of Bari Aldo Moro (UniBa) and the Puglia region. Established in 1984, it spearheads tech transfer, telematics, artificial intelligence for smart agriculture, digital health, and industrial spin-off incubation.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Historic technology park pioneer in Southern Italy, founded in 1984
  • Puglia Innovation Hub for AI, IoT sensor networks, and satellite earth observation
  • Direct integration with University of Bari PhD schools and Politecnico di Bari labs
  • Incubator hosting over 40 high-tech startups in biotech, cleantech, and digital health

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Programma di Ricerca Dottorale in Intelligenza Artificiale e Telemedicina (Doctoral Degree) — Four-year joint doctoral fellowship with University of Bari exploring federated machine learning in clinical telemetry and diagnostics.
  • Master di II Livello in Open Innovation & Technology Transfer (Postgraduate Master) — Specialized post-master's curriculum training innovation managers, patent analysts, and university-industry liaison officers.
  • Incubazione e Accelerazione Imprese Deep Tech (TecnoStart) (Incubator Residency) — Nine-month seed incubation program providing lab prototyping facilities, seed investment access, and IP commercialisation mentoring.
