Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA (on a 4.0 scale) in undergraduate coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or Master's degree with strong research background, minimum 3.2+ GPA, and compelling faculty research alignment.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 minimum (100+ recommended for TA appointments) or IELTS Academic 7.0 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page Statement of Purpose and Personal History and Diversity Statement detailing specific research goals and laboratory matches.
Application fee
$135 for domestic applicants / $155 for international applicants via the UC Davis Graduate Studies portal.
Required documentation
Official university transcripts, statement of purpose, personal history statement, curriculum vitae, and three letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral students in STEM typically receive full funding guarantees covering 100% of California resident or non-resident tuition and health insurance, plus annual living stipends of $34,000 to $42,000 through Teaching Assistantships (TA), Graduate Student Researcher (GSR) appointments, and fellowships.
