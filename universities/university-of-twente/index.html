University of Twente (Enschede)

Founded in 1961 in Enschede, the University of Twente (Universiteit Twente - UT) is the Netherlands' premier entrepreneurial technical university, embodying its motto 'High Tech, Human Touch'. Situated on the only true American-style campus in the Netherlands, Twente is home to the world-renowned MESA+ Institute for Nanotechnology (one of the largest academic nanotechnology institutes globally), the TechMed Centre for medical robotics, and the Faculty of Geo-Information Science and Earth Observation (ITC). UT doctoral candidates are employed as salaried staff members under Dutch university agreements with zero tuition fees. Twente MESA+ Institute for Nanotechnology Salaried Doctoral Positions

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment at the world-class MESA+ NanoLab (featuring 1,250 m² of cleanroom facilities) exploring quantum electronics, lab-on-a-chip sensors, and 2D materials.

UTwente Master of Science (MSc) in Robotics (English-taught)

Two-year multidisciplinary Master's degree preparing engineers in medical robotics, physical human-robot interaction, autonomous mobile systems, and robotic surgery.

UTwente PhD in Cybersecurity and Formal Verification

Four-year doctoral position at the Formal Methods and Tools (FMT) group investigating rigorous software verification, quantum-safe cryptography, and safety-critical distributed systems.

UTwente Master of Science (MSc) in Biomedical Engineering at TechMed Centre

Two-year curriculum at the Technical Medical Centre (TechMed) specializing in Bioengineering Technologies, Medical Device Design, and Imaging & In Vitro Diagnostics.

UTwente ITC Faculty PhD in Geo-Information Science and Remote Sensing

Four-year doctoral research program at the renowned Faculty ITC investigating satellite radar interferometry, natural disaster early warning systems, and climate change land-use dynamics.

UTwente MESA+ Summer Cleanroom Research Traineeship in Nanotechnology

Six-week research placement in the MESA+ NanoLab training students in cleanroom lithography, thin-film deposition, and atomic force microscopy.

UTwente Postdoctoral Researcher in Membrane Science & Clean Water Technology

Two-year postdoctoral position in the Membrane Science and Technology cluster developing fouling-resistant membranes for industrial wastewater circularity and lithium recovery.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited university in an aligned discipline with high academic standing (Dutch grading equivalent 7.0/10 or international GPA 3.2+/4.0).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (or equivalent 120 ECTS postgraduate degree) in an aligned discipline with demonstrable thesis research capability.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral candidates, an extensive 3 to 5-page research plan detailing theoretical context, research questions, experimental design, and prospective UT faculty mentors.
Application fee
€100 handling fee for non-EU applicants with non-Dutch diplomas via Studielink (reimbursed upon registration).
Required documentation
Certified university transcripts, degree certificates, detailed curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and two academic recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In the Netherlands, PhD candidates are not students but university employees ('promovendus') hired on salaried contracts governed by the Collective Labour Agreement for Dutch Universities (CAO-NU). Doctoral salaries start at €2,770/month gross in year 1 and rise to €3,539/month gross in year 4, plus an 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% end-of-year bonus (SEK/EUR zero tuition charged). For Master's programs, the University of Twente Scholarship (UTS) provides €3,000 to €22,000 per year towards tuition fees.
