Howard University

Howard · Washington, District of Columbia, United States

Howard University is a world-renowned private, federally chartered Historically Black University (HBCU) in Washington, D.C., recognized as a top research institution in engineering, medicine, law, and social sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Howard holds Carnegie R1 research classification, producing more African American PhDs in science and engineering than any other institution. Hallmarks include its College of Medicine, Moorland-Spingarn Research Center, and cutting-edge artificial intelligence and health equity centers.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are competitive, reviewing academic coursework, essays, leadership records, and letters of recommendation. Graduate and professional schools require relevant bachelor's degrees, competitive test scores (GRE/MCAT/LSAT if applicable), and research dossiers.
