Nagoya University of Foreign Studies (NUFS)

Nagoya University of Foreign Studies (NUFS - Nagoya Gaikokugo Daigaku) is a premier private university of foreign languages and international cultural studies located in Nisshin, adjacent to Nagoya. Renowned for its world-class 'Small-Group Discussion System' (1 native-speaker professor to 4 students), NUFS is a top Japanese institution for international mobility, diplomatic preparation, and global linguistics.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Nisshin, Aichi Prefecture, Japan

Institutional Overview

NUFS comprises the School of Foreign Studies, School of Contemporary International Studies, School of Global Business, and the Graduate School of International Studies. It boasts over 340 global partner universities in 42 countries, sending more than 70% of its students abroad through fully funded study-abroad programs with full credit recognition.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Undergraduate admission evaluates high school academic performance, English language qualifications (EIKEN, TOEIC, TOEFL, IELTS), and personal interviews. International applicants apply via the Global Japan Program or EJU. The university offers substantial Global Leader Scholarships, tuition waivers for high-scoring applicants, and housing bursaries.
