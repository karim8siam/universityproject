University of Insubria: Biotechnology, Physics & DSU Study Grants Guide

The University of Insubria (Università degli Studi dell'Insubria) is an esteemed public research university located in Como and Varese in the scenic Italian lakes region of Lombardy. Renowned for world-leading research in quantum optics, structural biology, environmental sciences, and theoretical computer science, Insubria maintains strong ties with CERN and European research facilities.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Lombardy Regional DSU Full Scholarship & Campus Housing Grant

Comprehensive regional welfare scholarship open to domestic and international students ensuring full access to higher education at Insubria regardless of financial background.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Chemical and Environmental Sciences

Three-year doctoral program researching atmospheric aerosol pollution, heterogeneous green catalysis, wastewater remediation, and advanced nanomaterials in Como.

Master of Science in Molecular and Industrial Biotechnology

Two-year English-taught master's degree focusing on recombinant protein production, biocatalysis, structural biology, and molecular pharmacology.

Quantum Optics & Laser Spectroscopy Laboratory Research Assistantship

Laboratory research assistantship analyzing quantum photon correlation, single-photon detectors, and non-classical states of light in collaboration with CERN and CNR.

Lombardy Biomedical & Diagnostic Industry Internship

Six-month clinical diagnostics and pharmaceutical placement within biomedical enterprises in the Varese and Milan life sciences corridor.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Structural Biology & Protein Crystallography

Two-year postdoctoral position determining atomic resolution structures of enzyme complexes involved in neurodegenerative pathways using synchrotron radiation.

Master of Science in Computer Science: Intelligent Systems & Data Analytics

English-taught master's degree based in Como, exploring computer vision, natural language processing, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Three-year undergraduate degree (Laurea) or recognized international bachelor's degree evaluated via the national Universitaly pre-enrolment platform.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) with competitive examination involving academic CV evaluation, research proposal assessment, and an oral interview (available online).
Language proficiency
B2 English certificate (IELTS Academic 6.0+, TOEFL iBT 78+, or Cambridge B2); Italian language proficiency not required for English-taught master's or PhDs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral applicants: 3,000-word research proposal formatted according to Insubria doctoral school guidelines.
Application fee
€30 application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, CIMEA Statement of Comparability or Declaration of Value (DoV), curriculum vitae, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is based on family income (ISEE parificato), ranging from €156 regional fee to a maximum of approximately €2,400 per year. Regional DSU Lombardia scholarships provide eligible domestic and international students with full tuition waivers, free university housing in Como or Varese, and annual cash stipends of up to €7,500.
