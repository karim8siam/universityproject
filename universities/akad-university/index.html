AKAD University: Digital Engineering, Data Science & Industry 4.0 Guide

Founded in 1959 in Stuttgart, AKAD University (AKAD Bildungsgesellschaft mbH) is a state-accredited pioneer of digital distance education and engineering sciences in Germany. Offering rigorous Bachelor of Engineering, Master of Science, and MBA programs, AKAD specializes in cloud computing, data science, industrial automation, and green digital technologies designed for working professionals and international scholars.

AKAD Postdoctoral Fellowship in Circular Economy Systems & Digital Product Passports

Two-year postdoctoral position developing open standards and blockchain-backed digital product passports for tracking battery raw materials and electronic component recycling across Europe.

Deutschlandstipendium National Merit Grant at AKAD University

Prestigious national scholarship co-funded by the German Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) and industry partners to support high-achieving university scholars.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science or Bachelor of Engineering degree (minimum 180 or 210 ECTS) in computing, engineering, or related discipline with German grade 2.5 or better.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Cooperative doctoral degrees (Kooperative Promotion) organized in conjunction with partner state research universities in Germany and the UK.
Language proficiency
German proficiency at TestDaF 4 or DSH-2 for German-taught degrees; IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88 for English-taught graduate curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
For cooperative doctoral research: detailed 2,500-word applied research concept outlining industrial data science or digital engineering applications.
Application fee
EUR 0 for direct online university enrollment.
Required documentation
Bachelor's degree diploma, complete transcript of records, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and verified employment or professional credentials.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at AKAD University is structured as monthly study fees, averaging approximately EUR 390 to EUR 490 per month (total degree tuition approximately EUR 13,000 to EUR 16,500). Multiple corporate sponsorship models, Deutschlandstipendium awards of EUR 300/month, and tax-deductible educational allowances in Germany provide substantial financial relief.
