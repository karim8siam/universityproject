Touro University Nevada

Touro University Nevada · Henderson, Nevada, United States

Touro University Nevada (TUN) is a private, non-profit health sciences and medical university located in Henderson, Nevada. Home to the largest medical school in Nevada—the College of Osteopathic Medicine—and the College of Health and Human Services, TUN is acclaimed for physician assistant training, occupational therapy, physical therapy, and the pioneering Center for Autism and Developmental Disabilities.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TUN focuses exclusively on graduate and professional healthcare education. Its state-of-the-art campus features high-fidelity clinical simulation laboratories, a human gross anatomy dissection suite, and the on-campus Touro Health Center, providing primary care and autism therapy to Southern Nevada communities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly competitive and processed through centralized application services (AACOMAS for Osteopathic Medicine, CASPA for PA, OTCAS, PTCAS, NursingCAS). Applicants require a completed bachelor's degree with rigorous science prerequisite coursework (minimum 3.0 cumulative and science GPA), clinical shadowing hours, and letters of recommendation.
