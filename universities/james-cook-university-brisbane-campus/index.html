JCU Brisbane: Master of Information Technology, Data Science & MBA Guide

James Cook University Brisbane is the modern metropolitan campus of James Cook University, situated on Queen Street in the central business district of Brisbane, Queensland. Operated in educational partnership with Russo Higher Education, JCU Brisbane delivers TEQSA-accredited undergraduate and postgraduate degrees in computer science, business analytics, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and international tourism management. It features dedicated Job Ready programs and direct corporate internship links across southeast Queensland.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (AQF Level 7) or equivalent in any discipline for conversion master's pathways; relevant IT or quantitative bachelor's for advanced computing degrees.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Postgraduate coursework and applied research master's pathways; doctoral research candidates collaborate directly with JCU parent faculty and supervisory panels.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands), PTE Academic 58, or TOEFL iBT 86 (with minimum 21 in all sections).
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research project units: 1,000-word proposed technical project overview or corporate capstone statement of intent.
Application fee
$0 AUD direct online international application fee.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree award certificates, English proficiency certificate, updated curriculum vitae, and passport identification page.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate coursework degrees at JCU Brisbane averages $32,800 to $36,400 AUD per academic year. Generous International Student Scholarships provide tuition fee reductions of up to 25% for scholars maintaining high academic performance throughout their degree.
