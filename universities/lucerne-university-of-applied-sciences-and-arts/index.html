Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts: Engineering, Fintech & Design Guide

Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts (Hochschule Luzern - HSLU) is the premier public higher education institution of central Switzerland, renowned across Europe for cutting-edge programs in computer science, blockchain/FinTech, energy systems, building technology, and digital design.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree from a recognized university or university of applied sciences in a related field with grade ECTS B or Swiss grade 5.0.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (M.Sc., M.A.) with distinguished standing; doctoral degrees are conferred cooperatively with Swiss cantonal or federal universities (ETH, EPFL, UniLu).
Language proficiency
German C1 for German-medium programs; English C1 (IELTS 7.0 or Cambridge English Advanced C1) for English Master's degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed statement of purpose, portfolio of scientific or architectural work, and verified CV in Swiss format.
Application fee
CHF 150 administrative application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of grades with ECTS credits, CV, passport copy, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for Swiss and EU/EEA students is CHF 800 per semester (CHF 1,600 / ~$1,850 USD per year). Tuition for non-EU international students is CHF 1,050 to 1,500 per semester. Appointed doctoral and scientific researchers hold Swiss federal employment contracts with annual salaries starting at CHF 60,000.
