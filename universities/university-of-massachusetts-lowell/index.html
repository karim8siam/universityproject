University of Massachusetts Lowell

UMass Lowell · Lowell, Massachusetts, United States

The University of Massachusetts Lowell (UMass Lowell) is a top-tier Carnegie R1 public research university located along the historic Merrimack River in Lowell, Massachusetts. Renowned for plastics engineering, robotics, cybersecurity, clean energy, and nanomanufacturing, UMass Lowell is a driving engine of technological commercialization and STEM innovation in New England.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMass Lowell features six colleges: the Francis College of Engineering, Kennedy College of Sciences, Zuckerberg College of Health Sciences, Manning School of Business, College of Fine Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, and the School of Education. The university operates the Massachusetts Biomanufacturing Center and the New England Robotics Validation and Experimentation (NERVE) Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate academic transcripts, rigor of coursework, and extracurricular leadership. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
