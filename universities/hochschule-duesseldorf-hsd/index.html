Hochschule Düsseldorf (HSD - University of Applied Sciences)

Hochschule Düsseldorf (HSD) is one of the largest universities of applied sciences in North Rhine-Westphalia, hosting over 11,000 students across its modern Derendorf campus in architecture, design, electrical engineering, media, and social sciences.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Formed in 1971 from prestigious predecessor academies—including the Werkkunstschule Düsseldorf and several engineering schools—Hochschule Düsseldorf combines historic design excellence with high-tech engineering and social sciences. Located on a state-of-the-art campus in Düsseldorf-Derendorf, HSD features seven faculties: Architecture (PBSA), Design, Electrical Engineering and Information Technology, Mechanical and Process Engineering, Media, Social Sciences and Cultural Studies, and Business Studies. The Peter Behrens School of Arts within HSD is celebrated internationally for avant-garde exhibition design, sustainable urban architecture, and typography.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Arts in Exhibition Design and Interior Architecture (PBSA) (Master's) — World-renowned design master's focusing on immersive narrative spaces, museum scenography, and interactive spatial media.
  • Master of Science in Medieninformatik (Media Informatics) (Master's) — Cutting-edge program exploring computer graphics, spatial audio, human-computer interaction, and generative artificial intelligence.
  • Bachelor of Engineering in Elektrotechnik und Informationstechnik (Bachelor's) — Comprehensive engineering degree covering smart grid technologies, microelectronics, and automated industrial drives.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Kommunikationsdesign (Communication Design) (Bachelor's) — Celebrated design curriculum training art directors in typography, digital branding, editorial media, and motion graphics.
