Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Aomori University Tokyo specializes in undergraduate Bachelor of Software Engineering and Management Information; graduate applicants affiliate with parent university graduate schools.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offered at Tokyo Campus; undergraduate degree programs.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 minimum for degree admission; EJU Japanese score of 200+ points.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of personal reason for choosing Tokyo Campus (Shibo Riyusho) and technical career aspirations.
Application fee
¥30,000 entrance examination fee.
Required documentation
High school graduation certificate, 3-year academic transcript, JLPT/EJU score certificate, personal statement, and valid residence card/passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual undergraduate tuition is approximately ¥1,150,000 to ¥1,350,000. Institutional tuition reductions up to 30% are awarded to qualifying international students, supplemented by JASSO scholarships and Kohseigakuen educational grants.
