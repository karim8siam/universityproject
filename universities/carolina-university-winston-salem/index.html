Institution Overview & Profile

Carolina University, situated in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, is a private non-profit university offering accredited undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degree programs across business administration, computer science, public health, psychology, and interdisciplinary theological leadership.

Institution TypePrivate Non-Profit University
Main CampusWinston-Salem Campus (Franklin Street), North Carolina
AccreditationTransnational Association of Christian Colleges and Schools (TRACS) / CHEA Recognized
Admissions RouteRolling admissions with flexible monthly module starts
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 69+ / Duolingo 95+)
Financial Aid & GrantsCarolina Opportunity Scholarships, Presidential Academic Awards & Federal Aid

Key Academic Strengths

  • Accelerated 7-week modular academic calendar enabling flexible, continuous degree progression
  • Robust STEM computer science and data analytics curricula with state-of-the-art cyber laboratories
  • Interdisciplinary PhD in Leadership offering concentrations in educational leadership, business, and healthcare
  • Dynamic campus community fostering ethical servant leadership, global service, and community research
