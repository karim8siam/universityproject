Rivier University

Rivier University · Nashua, New Hampshire, United States

Rivier University is a leading private Catholic university founded by the Sisters of the Presentation of Mary, located on a scenic 68-acre campus in Nashua, New Hampshire. Renowned for its premier nursing and health professions school, cybersecurity, clinical psychology, and business administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Rivier features the modern Linda Robinson Fallon Family Center for Nursing and Health Sciences, the Cybersecurity Labs, and the modern Science and Innovation Center. Rivier is the largest provider of nurses in New Hampshire and anchors critical clinical pipelines throughout northern New England.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5 recommended) with test-optional application options. Graduate nursing and professional programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0 GPA, active unencumbered nursing licenses (for MSN/DNP), and clinical background clearances.
