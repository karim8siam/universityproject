The Citadel, the Military College of South Carolina

The Citadel · Charleston, South Carolina, United States

The Citadel, the Military College of South Carolina, is a historic landmark institution located in Charleston, South Carolina. Renowned for its South Carolina Corps of Cadets and the civilian Citadel Graduate College, The Citadel has been ranked the #1 Public College in the South by U.S. News & World Report for over 13 consecutive years, acclaimed for engineering, cybersecurity, and principled leadership.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The Citadel comprises five schools: the School of Engineering, the Tommy & Victoria Baker School of Business, the Swain Family School of Science and Mathematics, the School of Humanities and Social Sciences, and the Zucker Family School of Education. Its Department of Cyber and Computer Sciences is designated a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Corps of Cadets undergraduate admissions require high school graduation, physical readiness standards, minimum GPA of 2.50, and leadership review. Citadel Graduate College (civilian evening/online) requires an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, resume, letters of recommendation, and department-specific materials.
