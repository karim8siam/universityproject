University of Lucerne: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Lucerne (Universität Luzern) is Switzerland's youngest public research university, located directly on Lake Lucerne beside the central station. Renowned for its focus on the human sciences, law, economics, and its pioneering Faculty of Health Sciences and Medicine, UniLu conducts world-class research in rehabilitation, health systems, and international comparative law.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

SNSF Salaried PhD Position in Health Sciences & Rehabilitation Research

Three to four-year salaried doctoral research appointment within the Department of Health Sciences and Medicine, exploring chronic disability interventions, rehabilitation epidemiology, and patient-centered outcomes.

Master of Science in Health Sciences (English-taught)

Two-year interdisciplinary Master of Science taught in English, preparing students for leadership roles in international health organizations, clinical trial registries, and healthcare consultancy.

PhD in Health Economics & Health Policy (Center for Health, Policy and Economics)

Doctoral research position investigating health insurance incentives, hospital reimbursement structures, and the economic evaluation of novel pharmaceuticals in Switzerland.

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships for Foreign Scholars at Lucerne

Prestigious federal scholarships awarded by the Swiss Confederation to international postgraduates to pursue doctoral or postdoctoral research at Swiss public universities.

Master of Science in Economics and Data Science (Faculty of Economics & Management)

Quantitative Master's degree combining rigorous economic theory with advanced data science techniques, causal inference, and machine learning algorithms for business decision-making.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Health Systems & Patient-Reported Outcomes

Two-year postdoctoral position examining patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) in national spinal cord injury cohorts and health system learning cycles.

Swiss Paraplegic Research & UniLu Clinical Epidemiology Graduate Internship

Four to six-month clinical research internship based at Swiss Paraplegic Research in Nottwil, analyzing long-term health outcomes and secondary complications in neurological patients.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized bachelor's degree comprising at least 180 ECTS credits in a relevant subject from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 90-120 ECTS) with above-average grades; written confirmation of supervision from a full professor of the University of Lucerne.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency at C1 level (IELTS 7.0+, TOEFL iBT 95+) for English-taught master's and doctoral degrees; German C1 for German-taught tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
A comprehensive 5-8 page research proposal including state of the art, scientific questions, research design, methodology, and timetable.
Application fee
CHF 100 non-refundable application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's and Master's diplomas and transcripts, CV, statement of motivation, doctoral supervisor confirmation letter, and language certificates.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at the University of Lucerne are modest by global standards: CHF 810 per semester for Swiss residents and CHF 1,110 per semester (approx. USD $1,280) for international students. Doctoral candidates are fully exempt from general tuition fees and are typically employed on salaried Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) doctoral contracts earning CHF 47,040 to CHF 50,040 per year.
