The University of Texas at Austin

UT Austin · Austin, Texas, United States

The University of Texas at Austin is the world-renowned flagship Carnegie R1 public research university of Texas and a member of the Association of American Universities (AAU). Driving over $800M in annual sponsored research, UT Austin is celebrated for the Cockrell School of Engineering, the Dell Medical School, the McCombs School of Business, the College of Natural Sciences, and the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC).

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UT Austin comprises 18 colleges and schools. Key research installations include the Texas Advanced Computing Center (housing Frontera and Vista supercomputers), the J.J. Pickle Research Campus, the McDonald Observatory in West Texas, the Marine Science Institute in Port Aransas, and the Energy Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly competitive (top 6% Texas auto-admit; holistic review for international and out-of-state). Graduate and doctoral applicants require a recognized bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, program-specific standardized test scores (GRE/GMAT where required), statement of purpose, CV, and three letters of recommendation.
