Montana State University - Northern

MSU-Northern · Havre, Montana, United States

Montana State University - Northern is a regional public university in Havre, Montana, and part of the Montana State University System. Celebrated for industry-leading programs in diesel technology, applied engineering, nursing, and agriculture.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MSU-Northern houses the College of Technical Sciences, College of Health Sciences, and College of Arts, Sciences, and Education. Its Diesel Technology Center is recognized globally, partnering with heavy equipment leaders like Caterpillar and Komatsu.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation or GED under an open-access admissions policy for Montana residents and standard GPA criteria for non-residents. Technical programs require safety orientation and program prerequisites.
