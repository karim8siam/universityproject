International University of Resources

The International University of Resources (IUR) is a global consortium of specialized resource universities coordinated from Freiberg, driving international doctoral education, critical minerals processing, eco-responsible mining geology, and circular metallurgy.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded by TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Montanuniversität Leoben, and partner universities across five continents, IUR coordinates joint postgraduate degrees, doctoral summer schools, and cross-border research initiatives in lithium, cobalt, rare earth extraction, and mine tailing remediation. The network equips engineers and geoscientists to manage global raw materials sustainably.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open to graduate scholars and doctoral candidates enrolled at IUR partner institutions with backgrounds in geosciences, mineralogy, metallurgy, or resource economics. Applications require academic transcripts, English proficiency (TOEFL 80 / IELTS 6.5), and a research statement.
