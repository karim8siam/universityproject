Institution Overview & Profile

Kobe Women's University (Port Island Campus), established in the heart of Kobe's world-leading Biomedical Innovation Cluster (KBIC) on Port Island, houses the Faculty of Nursing and Faculty of Health and Welfare, providing premier bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs in acute clinical nursing, nutritional science, and public healthcare.

Institution TypePrivate Women's University (Joshi Daigaku)
Main CampusPort Island Campus (Minatojima Nakamachi), Chuo Ward, Kobe, Japan
AccreditationJapan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / MEXT
Admissions RouteGeneral entrance exams, recommendation tracks, and graduate research screenings
Language of InstructionJapanese (JLPT N1/N2) with specialized English medical terminology
Financial Aid & GrantsKobe Women's University Special Honor Scholarships & Hyogo Medical Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • Direct integration into the Kobe Biomedical Innovation Cluster (KBIC)—Japan's largest biomedical cluster
  • Consistently outstanding pass rates on national examinations for Nurses, Public Health Nurses, and Midwives
  • Cutting-edge simulation hospital wards featuring advanced adult, pediatric, and obstetric simulation robots
  • Collaborative research with RIKEN Center for Biosystems Dynamics Research located adjacent on Port Island

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Bachelor of Science (Gakushi)

Bachelor of Science in Nursing (Kangogakubu)

Four-year comprehensive nursing curriculum preparing graduates for national licensing as Registered Nurses (Kangoshi) and Public Health Nurses (Hokenjo).

Master of Science & Doctorate

Graduate School of Nursing Science

Advanced practice nursing tracks in critical acute care, chronic illness management, oncological nursing, and community gerontology.

Bachelor of Science (Gakushi)

Bachelor of Health Sciences in Nutrition & Dietetics

Four-year degree training Registered Dietitians (Kanri Eiyoshi) in clinical medical nutrition, metabolic therapy, and food technology.

Verified Academic Opportunities (7)

Scholarship

Kobe Women's Medical & Nursing Excellence Scholarship

Full or half tuition scholarship awarded to students entering the Faculty of Nursing demonstrating top performance on academic entrance examinations.

Funding: ¥500,000 to ¥1,000,000 annual tuition waiver renewable upon academic merit Deadline: January 25, 2027
Bachelor's

B.Sc. in Nursing Science (National Licensing Track) Enrolment

Four-year professional nursing degree integrating fundamental pathology, acute surgical care, pediatric nursing, and 1,000+ clinical hospital hours.

Funding: Hospital nursing student loans (providing full tuition reimbursement in exchange for post-graduation service) available Deadline: February 10, 2027
Internship

Kobe Biomedical Innovation Cluster (KBIC) Hospital Clinical Practicum

Clinical rotations placed across Kobe University Hospital, Kobe City Medical Center General Hospital, and Port Island biomedical research clinics.

Funding: Academic clinical credit toward national nurse licensing; hospital meal subsidies Deadline: April 30, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Advanced Nursing Practice Enrolment

Two-year postgraduate degree preparing Certified Nurse Specialists (CNS) in cancer nursing, geriatric nursing care, and hospital infection prevention.

Funding: MEXT nursing scholarships and hospital continuing education grants Deadline: January 15, 2027
Grant

RIKEN Port Island Biomolecular Nutrition Research Grant

Grant supporting student researchers analyzing metabolic biomarkers, gut microbiome interactions, and functional food compounds on Port Island.

Funding: ¥300,000 project research grant and access to mass spectrometry equipment Deadline: June 30, 2027
Course

Midwifery Specialization Advanced Clinical Diploma

One-year intensive post-BSN credential delivering theoretical and practical obstetrical delivery training to qualify for national Midwife licensing (Josan-shi).

Funding: Prefectural maternal health subsidies available Deadline: February 15, 2027
Fellowship

Port Island Geriatric Healthcare Technology Fellowship

Postdoctoral fellowship investigating robotic nursing aids, wearable health telemetries, and smart healthcare integration for Japan's super-aged society.

Funding: ¥2,400,000 annual research fellowship stipend plus collaborative lab access at KBIC Deadline: September 30, 2027
