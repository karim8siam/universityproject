Edge Hill University: Artificial Intelligence, Biosciences & Health Sciences Guide

Founded in 1885 and based on a picturesque 160-acre campus in Ormskirk, Lancashire (near Liverpool and Manchester), Edge Hill University is an award-winning British public university holding a triple-Gold rating in the UK Teaching Excellence Framework (TEF). Renowned for world-leading research in biomedical sciences, artificial intelligence, clinical medicine, and social policy, Edge Hill combines state-of-the-art laboratory facilities with high student satisfaction.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited UK 2:2 honours degree (or international equivalent with minimum 55-60% or 2.8/4.0 GPA) in computing, biology, psychology, or related field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with merit or distinction (or first-class honours bachelor's degree) in a relevant subject area with an approved research proposal.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (with no individual sub-test below 6.0), Pearson PTE 61, or Cambridge English C1 Advanced.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for postgraduate research (MRes/PhD): 1,500-word research proposal articulating research questions, theoretical context, and methodology.
Application fee
£0 application fee for direct online postgraduate applications.
Required documentation
Certified degree certificates and transcripts, copy of valid passport, two academic reference letters, CV, and personal statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Edge Hill University is £9,250 per year for UK domestic students and £16,500 to £17,500 per year for international postgraduate students. Edge Hill provides International Postgraduate Excellence Scholarships offering up to £3,000 in tuition reductions, alongside fully funded UKRI doctoral studentships.
