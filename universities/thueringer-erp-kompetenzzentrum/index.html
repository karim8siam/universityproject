Thüringer ERP-Kompetenzzentrum

ERP Thüringen · Jena, Thuringia, Germany

Thüringer ERP-Kompetenzzentrum (based at Ernst-Abbe-Hochschule Jena) is a premier academic center for Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), supply chain digitalization, SAP technologies, and business informatics in Central Germany.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The center conducts empirical research on cloud ERP migration, AI-driven process mining, business process modeling, and digital manufacturing workflows, collaborating closely with SAP, regional SMEs, and international research universities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires admission into undergraduate or graduate programs in Business Informatics (Wirtschaftsinformatik), Computer Science, or Industrial Engineering at EAH Jena or affiliated Thuringian universities.
