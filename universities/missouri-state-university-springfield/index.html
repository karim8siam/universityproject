Missouri State University

Missouri State · Springfield, Missouri, United States

Missouri State University is the state's second-largest public university, located in Springfield, Missouri, with a distinctive statewide mission in public affairs. Celebrated for computer science, agriculture, business, and biomedical sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Missouri State encompasses the College of Natural and Applied Sciences, William H. Darr College of Agriculture, and College of Business. Key facilities include the Roy Blunt Jordan Valley Innovation Center and Mountain Grove fruit experiment station.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 3.25 automatic or comprehensive holistic review) with test-optional consideration. Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, personal goals statement, and letters of recommendation.
