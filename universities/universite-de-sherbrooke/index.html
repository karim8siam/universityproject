Université de Sherbrooke: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1954 in the Eastern Townships of Quebec, Université de Sherbrooke hosts over 31,000 students across three major campuses: the Main Campus, the Health Campus, and the Longueuil Campus in Greater Montreal. Renowned for pioneering North American cooperative work-study programs, UdeS spearheads global breakthroughs through the Institut Quantique (IQ), the Interdisciplinary Institute for Technological Innovation (3IT), and the Centre de Recherche du CHUS. Research programs bridge theoretical discovery and industrial integration in quantum computing, sustainable materials, genomics, and digital health.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Direct institutional application through Bureau de la registraire
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
French (primary instruction) & English (advanced laboratory research)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
CAD $105
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees and student contributions vary across degree levels and residency status. Verified opportunity records list typical costs between CAD $9,200/year (Quebec residents) or CAD $22,400/year (International; covered by fellowship) and departmental funding packages.
