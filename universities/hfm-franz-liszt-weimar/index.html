Hochschule für Musik Franz Liszt Weimar

The Hochschule für Musik Franz Liszt Weimar is an internationally renowned German institution for musical excellence, founded by Franz Liszt himself in 1872, bridging European classical traditions with contemporary musicological scholarship and artistic innovation.

Academic overview & excellence

HfM Weimar provides comprehensive education in instrumental and vocal performance, conducting, composition, opera, musicology, and arts management. Its historical venues, including the Fürstenhaus and the Belvedere Palace music center, provide world-class acoustic spaces.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires passing demanding entrance auditions and theoretical musical tests. High school graduation (Abitur or international equivalent) and German language competency (TestDaF level 3 or B2) are required for undergraduate studies. Advanced post-master's degrees emphasize international soloist careers.

Two-year master's degree in symphonic and operatic conducting, providing regular live rehearsals and performances with regional German professional orchestras.

Funding: Staatskapelle Weimar podium conducting practicum stipend: €600/month Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: March 15, 2027
PhD

Promotion (Dr. phil.) in Musicology & Cultural History

Doctoral research program operated jointly with Friedrich Schiller University Jena, investigating Liszt archives, 19th-century musical aesthetics, and Weimar classicism.

Funding: Joint Weimar-Jena DFG doctoral fellowship: €1,500/month for 3 years Tuition: €0 tuition Deadline: April 1, 2027
Fellowship

Postgraduate research fellowship analyzing European classical music festival management, audience diversification, and digital concert hall economics.

Funding: €6,500 project grant co-funded by Klassik Stiftung Weimar Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 15, 2027
Internship

Six-month stage practicum for enrolled opera master's students, participating in rehearsals and stage productions at the German National Theatre Weimar.

Funding: €950 monthly practical training stipend at DNT Weimar Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 1, 2027
Bachelor's

Four-year undergraduate degree offering world-class instruction in classical guitar, renaissance lute, chamber music, and historical fingerstyle technique.

Funding: Deutschlandstipendium and Thuringia student support available Tuition: €0 tuition per semester Deadline: March 1, 2027
