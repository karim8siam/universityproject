Campus Lidköping: Smart Manufacturing, Automation & Higher Vocational Education

Campus Lidköping (Campus Västra Skaraborg) is a premier Swedish higher education campus and vocational college in Västra Götaland, collaborating with University of Skövde, Chalmers University of Technology, and regional manufacturing giants to offer accredited degrees in industrial automation, renewable energy, and digital health.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Swedish State CSN Student Aid & Living Grant (Studiemedel)

Comprehensive Swedish state financial aid package providing monthly non-repayable grants and low-interest student loans for students enrolled in accredited studies at Campus Lidköping.

Higher Vocational Diploma (YH-Examen) in Industrial Automation & PLC Robotics

Two-year 400 YH-point engineering qualification specializing in Siemens/ABB PLC automation, SCADA systems, industrial robot programming, and Industry 4.0 networking.

Industrial Ph.D. in Cyber-Physical Manufacturing & Digital Twins

Four-year industrial doctoral project operated in collaboration with University of Skövde, deploying real-time digital twins onto automated manufacturing cells in Skaraborg.

Västra Götaland Sustainable Industrial Electrification & Heat Recovery Study

Regional energy efficiency initiative modeling industrial waste heat capture, district heating interconnects, and heavy vehicle electric charging depots.

LIA (Lärande i Arbete) Industrial Robotics Work Placement (20 Weeks)

Twenty-week embedded industrial engineering internship hosted at leading automation equipment manufacturers, robot integrators, and automotive suppliers in Skaraborg.

Skaraborg Regional Innovation & Circular Economy Fellowship

Twelve-month applied fellowship examining circular biomaterials, agricultural byproduct valorization, and carbon accounting across Lake Vänern regional enterprises.

Lead Lecturer & Program Manager in Automation Engineering

Permanent senior faculty position leading curriculum development, laboratory instruction in industrial robotics, and liaising with regional manufacturing executives.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in Engineering, IT, or Business Administration through accredited partner university affiliations.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Industrial doctoral studentships hosted in partnership with University of Skövde or Chalmers University of Technology.
Language proficiency
Swedish B / Svenska 3 for Swedish-taught vocational programs; English 6 (IELTS 6.5) for university partner programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, high school / university transcripts, and personal statement of technical career goals.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the national vocational portal (Yrkeshögskolan) or SEK 900 via Antagning.se for university degrees.
Required documentation
Certified grade transcripts, proof of prerequisite mathematics and physics/computer science coursework, and identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. Higher vocational programs (Yrkeshögskolan) are state-funded and tuition-free for all admitted residents. Non-EU international university partner courses range from SEK 110,000 to 145,000 annually.
