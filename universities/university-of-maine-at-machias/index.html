University of Maine at Machias

UMaine Machias · Machias, Maine, United States

The University of Maine at Machias (UMM), established in 1909, is Maine's coastal regional campus of the University of Maine, situated in the rugged Downeast coastal region along the Machias River. Renowned for its direct access to rocky intertidal shores, mudflats, and salt marshes, UMM offers renowned experiential programs in marine biology, aquaculture, environmental studies, and creative arts.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMM provides associate and bachelor's degrees through the Division of Environmental and Biological Sciences and the Division of Professional Studies. It works in close partnership with the Downeast Institute (DEI)—a premier marine research laboratory on Great Wass Island—providing undergraduate research vessels and shellfish hatcheries.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission operates on rolling deadlines through the UMaine System online application or Common App (test-optional; recommended GPA 2.5+). Applicants submit high school transcripts and personal statements.
