Hiroshima Institute of Technology: Robotics, Architecture & Engineering Guide

Hiroshima Institute of Technology (HIT - Hiroshima Kogyo Daigaku) is a prominent private engineering and technology university located in Saeki-ku, Hiroshima. Founded in 1961, HIT features state-of-the-art research centers in earthquake engineering, robotic mechatronics, satellite remote sensing, environmental design, and smart infrastructure.

Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarship at HIT

Prestigious Monbukagakusho scholarship funding international graduate students pursuing master's or doctoral engineering degrees at Hiroshima Institute of Technology.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Mechatronics and Intelligent Robotics

Doctoral research program advancing humanoid robotics, soft robotic actuators, autonomous mobile robots, and computer vision for industrial manufacturing.

Master of Engineering in Architectural Design & Earthquake Resilience

Two-year master's degree focusing on seismic structural engineering, base-isolation systems, timber architecture, and urban disaster mitigation.

Satellite Remote Sensing & GIS Environmental Research Assistantship

Applied satellite research analyzing Earth observation radar telemetry to detect coastal erosion, landslide displacement, and forest biomass change in Western Japan.

Chugoku Industrial Automotive & Machinery Practicum

Three-month cooperative engineering internship partnering with Mazda suppliers and machinery manufacturing corporations in the greater Hiroshima industrial belt.

JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowship at HIT in Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems (MEMS)

Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) fellowship supporting an international postdoctoral scholar conducting cleanroom silicon micromachining and piezoelectric sensor research.

Master of Science in Information Systems & Sensor Network Engineering

Comprehensive master's degree focusing on IoT sensor telemetry, wireless ad-hoc networking, edge computing security, and real-time distributed data systems.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited four-year university with a Bachelor of Engineering or Bachelor of Science degree (or international 16-year education equivalent).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completion of a recognized master's degree (Master of Engineering or Science) and successful oral defense of academic thesis.
Language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-taught tracks; CEFR B2 (TOEFL iBT 75+ or IELTS 6.0+) for English research laboratory programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate admissions: 2,000-character (or 3-page English) research proposal aligned with a confirmed HIT laboratory professor.
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY application screening fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, research proposal, certificate of English or Japanese proficiency, and letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is approximately ¥980,000 JPY to ¥1,180,000 JPY per year. International students benefit from institutional tuition reductions (30% to 50% waiver), and eligible scholars can secure Japanese Government (MEXT) University Recommendation Scholarships covering 100% of tuition plus monthly stipends.
