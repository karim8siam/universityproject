BSBI Berlin: Global Degrees, Innovation Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Located in the heart of Germany's vibrant startup capital with main campus facilities in Alte Post in Berlin-Neukölln and regional study hubs in Hamburg, Paris, and Athens, the Berlin School of Business and Innovation (BSBI) is an international business school. In academic partnership with prestigious European institutions including Università Telematica Internazionale UNINETTUNO and University for the Creative Arts (UCA), BSBI delivers undergraduate and graduate degrees centered on enterprise, leadership, and digital innovation.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

BSBI International Leadership and Academic Merit Scholarship

Tuition reduction scholarship awarded to high-achieving international scholars enrolling in full-time business and management degrees at the BSBI Berlin campus.

Global Master of Business Administration (Global MBA)

Accredited 18-month executive program delivering core competencies in strategic management, international marketing, corporate finance, and enterprise innovation in Berlin.

Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) in Strategic Enterprise Innovation

Three-year practitioner-scholar doctoral program designed for business executives to produce original applied research in digital disruption, organizational agility, and corporate sustainability.

Berlin Startup Ecosystem and Digital Venture Incubator Project

Empirical management research initiative evaluating angel investment rounds, tech incubator acceleration, and cross-border founder talent mobility in the Berlin startup ecosystem.

German Corporate and Tech Startup Work Placement (18-Month Job-Seeker Transition)

Professional career service placement connecting international graduates with corporate internships and supporting post-study transition into Germany's 18-month job-seeker visa.

BSBI Female Leaders in Tech and Business Fellowship

Empowerment fellowship dedicated to advancing women in corporate leadership, venture finance, and digital tech startups across Germany and Europe.

Lecturer in Strategic Financial Management and Corporate Economics

Full-time academic faculty appointment lecturing corporate finance, financial analysis, and strategic economics to international graduate students at the Berlin campus.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from a recognized undergraduate university (Bachelor's degree of at least 180 ECTS) in business, humanities, or technical fields with minimum pass grade.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree from a recognized institution with a strong academic average, a detailed doctoral research proposal, and a demonstrated commitment to international management research.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 80 or equivalent approved language examination.
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via the BSBI online portal including certified transcripts, motivation letter, CV, and passport copy.
Application fee
Zero application fee for international student admissions evaluations.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's graduation certificates, certified academic transcripts, English language certificate, comprehensive CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for undergraduate programs is approximately €7,000 to €9,000 per academic year. Postgraduate master's tuition generally ranges from €9,000 to €13,500 total for the full program (€15,000 for the Global MBA). BSBI offers high-value International Scholarship Schemes providing up to 30% to 50% tuition fee reductions based on academic merit and socioeconomic diversity.
