CQUniversity: Engineering, Health Sciences & RTP Research Fellowships

CQUniversity (Central Queensland University) is one of Australia's most dynamic regional research universities, operating campuses across Queensland and nationwide. Renowned for world-leading research in precision agriculture, railway engineering, clean energy systems, coastal marine management, and rural health, CQUniversity consistently achieves top national employment outcomes.

Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Stipend at CQU

Prestigious Australian Commonwealth scholarship providing full funding and living allowances for international doctoral candidates conducting advanced research at CQUniversity.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Rail and Heavy Haul Transport Engineering

Doctoral research conducted within Australia's premier railway research center, investigating locomotive train simulation, track structural health, and predictive maintenance.

Master of Sustainable Energy Engineering & Grid Modernization

Engineers Australia-accredited professional master's degree focusing on microgrid integration, renewable solar and wind systems, and hydrogen energy distribution.

Precision Agriculture & Livestock Remote Sensing Research Assistantship

Applied agricultural research analyzing IoT ear-tag telemetry, automated cattle weighing, and satellite biomass indexing across Queensland cattle properties.

Gladstone Hydrogen Ecosystem & Clean Energy Industrial Practicum

12-week industrial placement embedded in Gladstone's emerging green hydrogen and ammonia production hub, studying electrolyzer performance and process safety.

CQU Postdoctoral Fellowship in Coastal Marine Ecosystem Restoration

Full-time research appointment at the Coastal Marine Ecosystems Research Centre (CMERC) in Gladstone, restoring Great Barrier Reef coastal nurseries and blue carbon habitats.

Master of Public Health in Rural and Indigenous Health Services

Postgraduate degree focusing on epidemiological monitoring, health equity, and healthcare delivery models tailored for regional, rural, and First Nations communities.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed bachelor's degree from an Australian university or recognized international equivalent with a minimum GPA of 4.5 on a 7-point scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-class or second-class division A honours degree (H1 or H2A) or research master's with demonstrated peer-reviewed publication outputs.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands), PTE Academic 58+, or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing minimum 21). Health degrees require 7.0 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for PhD and Master by Research: 2,500-word research proposal including methodology, state of research, and formal CQUniversity supervisor support.
Application fee
$0 (No application fee when applying directly via the CQUniversity online portal).
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree awards, English test certificate, research proposal, curriculum vitae, and two referee reports.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for international students range from AUD $28,000 to $36,000 per year depending on the discipline. The Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) provides full tuition fee offsets and annual living stipends of AUD $33,500 for domestic and international PhD candidates.
