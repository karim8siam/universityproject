Institutional Overview

Musashi University (Musashi Daigaku), established in 1922 by industrialist Nezu Kaichirō on its lush Ekoda campus in Nerima, Tokyo, is celebrated as the pioneer of seminar-based university education (Zemi) in Japan. With intimate cohorts across its Faculties of Economics, Humanities, and Sociology, Musashi boasts a unique dual-degree parallel program with the University of London, fostering global multilingual leadership.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Pioneer of small-group seminar (Zemi) education in Japan, maintaining an average of 13 students per seminar across all four years
  • Exclusive University of London (UoL) Parallel Degree Programme allowing students to earn two bachelor's degrees simultaneously in Tokyo
  • Prime Tokyo location just 6 minutes from Ikebukuro on the Seibu Ikebukuro Line
  • Consistently ranked in the top 10 universities in Japan for employment support and graduate job placement
