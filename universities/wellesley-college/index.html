Wellesley College

Wellesley · Wellesley, Massachusetts, United States

Wellesley College is an elite private liberal arts women's college founded in 1870 in picturesque Wellesley, Massachusetts, 12 miles west of Boston. Celebrated as one of the Seven Sisters and among the highest-ranked undergraduate institutions in the world, Wellesley is acclaimed for developing pioneering female leaders in science, diplomacy, economics, and technology, maintaining academic cross-registration partnerships with MIT, Babson, and Olin College.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Wellesley offers over 50 departmental and interdepartmental undergraduate majors across the natural sciences, social sciences, arts, and humanities. The college operates the Science Center, Wellesley College Botanic Gardens, the Whitin Observatory, and the Davis Museum.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are highly selective with test-optional application options via Common Application or Coalition Application. Wellesley is committed to meeting 100% of demonstrated financial need for all admitted students, including international undergraduates, through generous grant aid without loan obligations.
