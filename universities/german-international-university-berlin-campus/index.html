Institution Overview & Profile

German International University (GIU) Berlin Campus is a state-recognized German university of applied sciences located in the historic Borsigturm industrial heritage complex in Berlin-Tegel. Established with the support of German partner universities, GIU Berlin offers globally focused English-taught degree programs in computer science, engineering, business administration, and architectural design.

Key Academic Strengths

  • All bachelor and master curricula fully taught in English in the heart of Berlin's technology hub
  • Close partnerships with prominent German industrial conglomerates for thesis internships
  • International dual degree opportunities with affiliated German and transnational university campuses
  • Cutting-edge laboratories for robotics, industrial automation, and software engineering

Verified Academic Opportunities (7)

Scholarship

GIU Berlin Global Academic Excellence Scholarship

Merit-based partial to full tuition scholarship awarded to outstanding international students admitted to undergraduate and graduate programs.

Funding: 25% to 50% tuition reduction (valued up to €4,500 per academic year) Deadline: June 15, 2027
Bachelor's

B.Sc. Computer Science & Software Systems Enrolment

7-semester English-taught bachelor program covering software design patterns, database architecture, machine learning basics, and mandatory industry internship.

Funding: Eligible for German student visa work authorization (140 full days/year) and GIU payment plans Deadline: July 31, 2027
Internship

Berlin Industrial Robotics & Automation Practicum

20-week mandatory industrial internship conducted within Berlin-Brandenburg manufacturing plants, automotive labs, or robotics startups.

Funding: €900 to €1,400 monthly industry compensation Deadline: May 1, 2027
Course

B.Eng. Mechatronics Engineering Enrolment

Seven-semester curriculum teaching programmable logic controllers, industrial drive mechanics, CAD modeling, and micro-electromechanical sensors.

Funding: GIU merit waivers available for top engineering entrance scores Deadline: July 15, 2027
Grant

GIU Women in Technology & Engineering Grant

Targeted grant aimed at closing the gender gap in technical fields by funding female students entering mechatronics and computer science programs.

Funding: €3,000 direct tuition reduction across first academic year Deadline: June 30, 2027
Master's

M.Sc. International Business & Technology Management

Three-semester master's program instructing future corporate managers in global supply chain optimization, tech transfer, and financial modeling.

Funding: Graduate assistantships available with tuition fee reductions Deadline: August 15, 2027
Fellowship

Berlin Smart City & Urban Mobility Fellowship

Graduate research fellowship investigating electric vehicle fleet telemetry, charging grid optimization, and smart city infrastructure in Berlin.

Funding: €6,000 project research grant and workstation access at GIU Berlin labs Deadline: September 30, 2027
