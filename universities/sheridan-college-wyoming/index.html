Sheridan College

Sheridan College · Sheridan, Wyoming, United States

Sheridan College is a comprehensive public community college nestled at the base of the Bighorn Mountains in Sheridan, Wyoming. As part of the Northern Wyoming Community College District (NWCCD), it offers career-ready technical degrees, healthcare credentials, precision agriculture, and seamless university transfer pathways.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Sheridan College features modern facilities including the Whitney Academic Center, the Mars Agriculture Center, the Thorne-Rider Campus, and advanced machining workshops. It serves as an economic and technical catalyst for northern Wyoming, offering specialized training in nursing, diesel mechanics, welding, cyber defense, and equine science.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Sheridan College maintains an open-door admissions policy for secondary school graduates and GED recipients. Selective programs in nursing, dental hygiene, and industrial trades have competitive prerequisites, clinical background evaluations, and program-specific application deadlines.
