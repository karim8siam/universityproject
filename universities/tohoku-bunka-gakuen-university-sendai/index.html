Academic overview & research priorities

Located on a leafy hillside campus in Kunimi, Aoba Ward, Sendai, TBGU was founded with the mission of harmonizing professional healthcare sciences with technical innovation. Its Faculty of Medical Sciences operates premier clinical training facilities in physical therapy, occupational therapy, and radiological technology, while the Faculty of Science and Technology leads regional research in sustainable architecture and information processing systems.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation credentials, Japanese proficiency (JLPT N2 or N1 for Japanese-track programs), academic transcripts, and personal interviews. Transfer and graduate applicants to Health Sciences must hold recognized diplomas in allied healthcare disciplines and present an approved clinical research plan.
