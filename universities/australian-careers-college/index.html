Australian Careers College: Vocational & Higher Education Pathways Guide

Established in 1999 and headquartered in sunny Brisbane, Queensland, Australian Careers College (ACC) is a recognized Australian tertiary education institution delivering accredited vocational diplomas, advanced diplomas, and articulated higher education pathways in business administration, early childhood education, community services, and health care.

ACC Queensland International Student Academic Merit Bursary

Financial support bursary awarded to incoming international students enrolling in full-time diploma qualifications at Australian Careers College Brisbane campus.

Diploma of Community Services and Youth Welfare Pathway

Nationally recognized diploma training caseworkers, youth advocates, and community outreach coordinators, including 100 hours of mandatory supervised work placement.

Advanced Diploma of Business and Strategic Management Pathway

Higher-level business qualification focusing on strategic enterprise planning, risk management, and digital marketing, designed as a direct university articulation pathway.

Brisbane Community Social Welfare and Migrant Settlement Field Study

Field-based investigation evaluating vocational training barriers, language support systems, and social inclusion outcomes among newly arrived migrants in south-east Queensland.

Queensland Community Services and Disability Support Work Practicum

Supervised 100-hour professional work placement embedding students in accredited disability support services, youth care centers, and senior day-care facilities across Brisbane.

ACC Vocational Excellence and Leadership Teaching Fellowship

Developmental fellowship supporting emerging educators in designing student-centered, industry-aligned training materials for multicultural adult learners.

Senior Trainer and Assessor in Community Services and Health Care

Full-time educator role delivering classroom lectures, assessing competency portfolios, and supervising clinical student work placements at the Brisbane campus.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Diploma and Advanced Diploma programs require completion of Australian Year 12 (or recognized international secondary equivalent) with strong foundational English competence.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Advanced diploma qualifications articulate with full block credit transfer into accredited Australian bachelor's degree programs across partner universities.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5 (or PTE Academic 50 or TOEFL iBT 64).
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via the ACC online portal including certified academic transcripts and genuine temporary entrant (GTE/GS) statements.
Application fee
AUD $200 non-refundable international admissions enrollment fee.
Required documentation
Certified high school completion certificate, academic transcripts, valid passport, English test report, and statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for accredited vocational and higher education diploma streams averages approximately AUD $6,000 to $8,500 per academic year (AUD $3,000 to $4,250 per semester). ACC offers international student support bursaries and flexible quarterly payment plans to support international scholars living in Queensland.
