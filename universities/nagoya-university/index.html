Nagoya University (名古屋大学 - Meidai)

Established in 1871 in central Japan's industrial heartland, Nagoya University (Meidai) is one of Japan's prestigious Designated National Universities. Renowned globally for producing 6 Nobel laureates in the 21st century (including Hiroshi Amano and Isamu Akasaki for inventing the Blue LED, and Ryoji Noyori in Chemistry), Nagoya University leads the world in gallium nitride (GaN) power semiconductors, high-energy particle physics at KMI, and autonomous vehicle informatics with Toyota. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Nagoya University Doctoral Mentoring Fellowship & MEXT Grants

Nagoya University's premier international doctoral fellowship, providing full tuition and admission fee exemptions plus living allowances for outstanding privately financed and government-sponsored international PhD students.

Nagoya University PhD in Informatics

Three-year doctoral curriculum inside the Graduate School of Informatics at Higashiyama campus, offering world-class research in autonomous driving systems, intelligent signal processing, and computer vision.

Nagoya University Master of Science in Physics

Two-year English-medium research Master's degree inside the Department of Physics (home to Sakata model and Nobel-winning CP violation discoveries), investigating particle physics, cosmology, and condensed matter.

Nagoya University Master of Engineering in Materials Science

Two-year Master of Engineering program inside Nagoya University's Graduate School of Engineering, offering world-renowned training in GaN wide-bandgap semiconductors, power electronics, and advanced alloys.

Kobayashi-Maskawa Institute (KMI) Particle Physics Placements

Frontier particle physics research placements inside KMI at Nagoya University (named after Nobel laureates Makoto Kobayashi and Toshihide Maskawa), participating in Belle II flavor physics, dark matter searches, and quantum field theory.

Nagoya University Summer Intensive Program (NUSIP) in Automotive Engineering

Prestigious six-week summer research and study program (June–July) offering international engineering scholars direct laboratory research in autonomous vehicles, electric powertrains, and robotics in partnership with Japan's automotive industry.

Nagoya University Teaching Assistantships (TA) & Research Assistantships (RA)

University employment opportunities for enrolled Master's and PhD students providing financial assistance for assisting in seminar instruction, computer lab supervision, and experimental semiconductor fabrication.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education culminating in an accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent 4-year undergraduate diploma) in relevant discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree or professional degree with demonstrated research accomplishments, publications, or Master's thesis of superior academic merit.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 80+ for English-language graduate degree tracks (e.g., G30 International Programs).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2 to 3-page research proposal detailing the intended research questions, background literature, methodology, and faculty supervisor alignment.
Application fee
¥10,000 to ¥30,000 examination fee depending on graduate school and international applicant category.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, official transcripts with grading rubric, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and passport scan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Japanese national university regulations, annual tuition is standard across all faculties at ¥535,800 per year (approx. USD $3,600), plus a one-time admission fee of ¥282,000. Recipient scholars of the Japanese Government MEXT scholarship or Nagoya University Doctoral Mentoring Fellowship receive 100% full tuition exemption plus monthly living allowances of ¥180,000 to ¥200,000.
