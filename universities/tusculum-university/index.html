Tusculum University

Tusculum University · Greeneville, Tennessee, United States

Tusculum University, founded in 1794, is Tennessee's first higher education institution and the 28th-oldest university in the United States. Located in Greeneville in the foothills of the Great Smoky Mountains, Tusculum is a private Presbyterian-affiliated institution celebrated for its civic arts curriculum, small cohort learning, and career-focused degree programs across healthcare, education, natural sciences, and business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Tusculum offers undergraduate and graduate programs through the College of Science, Technology & Mathematics, College of Health Sciences, College of Business, and College of Education. Its picturesque 140-acre historic campus features the Doak House Museum, the Paul E. Hayden Technology Center, and state-of-the-art nursing simulation laboratories supporting regional Appalachian healthcare needs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate applicants apply via Tusculum's institutional application or the Common Application with high school transcripts (test-optional; minimum 2.5 recommended GPA). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree, minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA depending on program, letters of recommendation, and a statement of purpose.
