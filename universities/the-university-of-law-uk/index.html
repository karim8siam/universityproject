The University of Law

The University of Law (ULaw) is the United Kingdom’s largest and longest-established specialist provider of legal education, having trained more practicing lawyers in England and Wales than any other academic institution.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Tracing its pedigree back to 1863 as Gibson & Weldon and later the College of Law, The University of Law was granted full university status and taught degree awarding powers in 2012. ULaw operates more than fifteen vibrant campuses across London (Bloomsbury and Moorgate in the financial City), Birmingham, Bristol, Leeds, Manchester, Nottingham, Guildford, and international hubs in Hong Kong and Berlin. ULaw is renowned for pioneer programs in the Solicitors Qualifying Examination (SQE), the Bar Practice Course (BPC), and specialized Master of Laws (LLM) degrees partnered with Magic Circle law firms.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • LLM Legal Practice (SQE1 and SQE2) (Master's) — Comprehensive master's degree preparing law graduates for both stages of the centralized Solicitors Qualifying Examination alongside practical client casework.
  • Bar Practice Course (BPC) (Postgraduate Diploma) — BSB-accredited advocacy and procedural litigation program qualifying graduates to enter pupillage as barristers in England and Wales.
  • LLB (Hons) Law (Senior Status / 3-Year Qualifying) (Bachelor's) — Qualifying law degree offering rigorous modules in public law, torts, equity, commercial transactions, and moot court competitions.
  • MSc Legal Technology (Artificial Intelligence in Legal Practice) (Master's) — Pioneering interdisciplinary degree examining automated contract analysis, legal prompt engineering, blockchain smart contracts, and e-discovery.
