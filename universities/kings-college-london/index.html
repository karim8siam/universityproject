King's College London (Strand, London)

Founded in 1829 by King George IV and the Duke of Wellington, King's College London (KCL) is England's fourth-oldest university and a core member of the prestigious Russell Group. Ranked among the world's top 40 universities, King's has made profound contributions to science and society—including Rosalind Franklin and Maurice Wilkins' pivotal X-ray crystallography of DNA, and Peter Higgs' conceptualization of the Higgs boson. Situated in central London across Strand, Waterloo, Guy's, and St Thomas' campuses, King's offers world-class doctoral studentships, advanced Master's programs, and distinguished research leaders. King's College London Doctoral Studentships in Informatics & AI

King's flagship funded doctoral studentship scheme supporting groundbreaking research in machine learning, cybersecurity, autonomous systems, and medical imaging.

King's Master of Science (MSc) in Artificial Intelligence

Comprehensive one-year Master of Science course covering deep learning, reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, computer vision, and AI philosophy at the historic Strand campus.

King's Wellcome Trust 4-Year PhD in Cellular and Molecular Mechanisms of Health & Disease

Four-year cohort-based doctoral training program comprising three laboratory rotation projects in year one followed by a three-year primary PhD project at Guy's campus.

King's Postdoctoral Research Associate in Quantum Photonics & Nanomaterials

Two-year postdoctoral position in the Department of Physics investigating light-matter interactions at the nanoscale and nanostructured materials for quantum information processing.

King's Master of Arts (MA) in War Studies

World-leading one-year postgraduate program within King's renowned Department of War Studies exploring international security, diplomacy, strategic doctrine, and global conflict resolution.

King's Chemistry & Bio-imaging Undergraduate Summer Traineeship

Eight-week hands-on summer research studentship in central London labs investigating molecular imaging probes, radiopharmaceuticals, and chemical biology.

King's Research Fellow in Surgical Robotics & Medical Mechatronics

Three-year research fellowship at the School of Biomedical Engineering & Imaging Sciences based at St Thomas' Hospital developing AI-assisted robotic surgical tools.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or international equivalent, typically minimum 3.3/4.0 GPA or 65%+ UK mark equivalent) in an aligned discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with Merit or Distinction (or UK 1st Class Honours undergraduate degree) with demonstrable research expertise and supervisor agreement.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 to 7.0 overall (minimum 6.0 to 6.5 in all sub-tests depending on faculty band) or TOEFL iBT 92 to 100.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 1,500 to 2,000-word research proposal describing original research questions, literature context, methodological design, and prospective KCL supervisor alignment.
Application fee
£75 to £120 non-refundable application fee for taught postgraduate courses; £0 to £75 for research degrees via King's Apply portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, certified English translations, degree certificates, CV, 2 academic reference letters, and research proposal.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for international doctoral students are covered by competitive research councils and King's studentships. Full-award recipients under the King's Doctoral Studentship scheme receive a 100% international tuition fee waiver, a tax-free living stipend aligned with London UKRI rates (currently £21,237 per year for 3.5 to 4 years), and an annual research training support grant (RTSG) of £1,000 to £2,500.
