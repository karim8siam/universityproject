Park University at Malmstrom AFB

Park University Malmstrom · Great Falls, Montana, United States

Park University is a pioneering non-profit private university founded in 1875, with campus centers nationwide including its established military installation center at Malmstrom Air Force Base in Great Falls, Montana. Noted for military logistics, cybersecurity, criminal justice, and public administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Operating through the College of Management and the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences, Park's Malmstrom AFB campus provides accelerated degree pathways for military servicemembers, veterans, and civilian aerospace contractors. It features modern hybrid computer classrooms and defense aerospace partnerships.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Park University offers rolling, open-friendly admissions for undergraduate candidates. Military applicants receive military transcript evaluation (JST/CCAF) and tuition assistance alignment. Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's with a 2.75 GPA.
