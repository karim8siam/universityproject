University of Florida: AI Research, Medicine & Engineering Flagship Guide

The University of Florida (UF) in Gainesville is a top-five public research university (AAU member, R1 Carnegie Classification), boasting over $1.25 billion in annual research expenditures, the world-class HiPerGator AI supercomputer (in partnership with NVIDIA), and preeminent colleges of engineering, medicine, and agriculture.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UF Graduate School Preeminence Doctoral Fellowship

UF's most prestigious merit-based university-wide award granted to top incoming doctoral scholars in engineering, biomedical sciences, AI, and natural sciences.

Master of Science in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence

Herbert Wertheim College of Engineering flagship Master's program utilizing the HiPerGator AI supercomputer to study foundation models, computer vision, cybersecurity, and distributed systems.

Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences & Gene Therapy Doctoral Fellowship

Doctoral research position at the UF Powell Gene Therapy Center and McKnight Brain Institute developing adeno-associated virus (AAV) vectors for treating genetic and neurodegenerative diseases.

HiPerGator Supercomputing & Climate AI Modeling Project

High-performance computing project deploying GPU-accelerated deep neural networks on NVIDIA SuperPOD architectures to predict extreme hurricane storm surges across the Gulf Coast.

Scripps Biomedical Research at UF Innovate Industry Practicum

Four-month intensive pharmaceutical research internship embedded within biomedical biotech startups at UF Innovate | Sid Martin Biotech incubator in Alachua, Florida.

UF Center for Coastal Solutions Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship developing coupled hydrodynamic-ecological models to mitigate harmful red tide algal blooms and restore coastal estuaries in Florida.

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Tenure-track academic faculty opening in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, conducting funded research in field robotics, collaborative manipulation, or UAV control.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Earned Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with minimum 3.0 upper-division GPA on a 4.0 scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Earned Master's or exceptional Bachelor's degree with minimum 3.5 GPA and established research track record.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 (with minimum speaking score of 23 for teaching assistantships) or IELTS Academic 6.0 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral statement of purpose (1,000 words) specifying research domain and prospective faculty laboratory supervisors.
Application fee
$30 non-refundable graduate application fee.
Required documentation
Official university transcripts, statement of purpose, curriculum vitae, and three letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In-state graduate tuition is approximately $535 per credit hour ($12,740/year). Out-of-state and international graduate tuition is approximately $1,255 per credit hour ($30,130/year). Appointed graduate assistants (RAs/TAs) and doctoral fellows receive 100% tuition waivers, health insurance, and annual living stipends ($26,000–$36,000).
