Medical Career & Technical College

MCTC Kentucky · Richmond, Kentucky, United States

Medical Career & Technical College is an accredited healthcare technical college in Richmond, Kentucky. MCTC specializes in career-focused diplomas and associate degrees in diagnostic medical sonography, practical nursing, and medical assisting.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MCTC provides accelerated, hands-on healthcare training featuring high-fidelity diagnostic ultrasound laboratories, medical simulation mannequins, and clinical externships with hospital systems across Central Kentucky.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admission requires a high school diploma or GED equivalent, entrance exam assessment, clinical background check, drug screening, and immunization compliance.
