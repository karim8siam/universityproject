University of Hartford

UHart · West Hartford, Connecticut, United States

The University of Hartford (UHart), founded in 1957 on a scenic 350-acre suburban campus in West Hartford, Connecticut, is a comprehensive private university celebrated for world-class performing arts, engineering, and healthcare professions. It is home to The Hartt School—one of the nation's premier performing arts conservatories—and the College of Engineering, Technology, and Architecture (CETA).

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UHart offers programs through seven distinct schools: The Hartt School, the College of Engineering, Technology, and Architecture, the College of Education, Nursing and Health Professions, the Barney School of Business, the Hartford Art School, the College of Arts and Sciences, and Hillyer College.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission operates on rolling deadlines through the Common Application (test-optional; recommended GPA 3.0+). The Hartt School and Hartford Art School require auditions or artistic portfolios. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, transcripts, letters of recommendation, and personal statements.
