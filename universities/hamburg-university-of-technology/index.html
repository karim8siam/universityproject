Hamburg University of Technology: Engineering, Aviation & Cyber-Physical Systems Guide

Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH - Technische Universität Hamburg) is a premier German public research university founded in 1978, renowned for aerospace engineering with Airbus, sustainable maritime technology, green energy systems, and microelectronics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science in Engineering or Computer Science with minimum 180 ECTS credits and strong mathematical/theoretical fundamentals.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (M.Sc., Diplom) with above-average grade (mindestens 'gut') in engineering, computer science, or natural sciences.
Language proficiency
English taught programs: TOEFL iBT 90 or IELTS 6.5. German taught programs: TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral candidates submit a research statement aligned with an institute research chair; Masters candidates submit comprehensive motivation letter.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the TUHH self-service applicant portal.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records with credit points, module handbook / syllabus, CV in Europass format, and language certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees for domestic and international students. All students pay only the standard semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €340 per semester, covering administrative costs and the regional public transport semester ticket.
