International Academy in Switzerland: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Based in Zurich, Switzerland, the International Academy in Switzerland (IAS, affiliated with the Swiss International University SIU) provides internationally oriented higher education programs specializing in international management, global finance, organizational leadership, and executive doctoral research. Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS credits or equivalent) with documented professional work experience and academic merit.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Accredited Master's degree (minimum 120 ECTS credits) in business administration, economics, or related social sciences, plus a defensible doctoral research prospectus.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency (IELTS Academic 6.5, TOEFL iBT 85, or certified prior education conducted entirely in English).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 5–8 page research proposal detailing research problem, theoretical framework, methodology, and practical managerial contributions.
Application fee
CHF 150 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree certificates and transcripts, CV/resume, personal statement, two reference letters, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for executive master's degree programs is approximately CHF 14,000 to CHF 18,500 per academic year. For doctoral programs (Doctor of Business Administration / PhD), tuition is CHF 8,500 to CHF 11,000 per year over a 3-year research cycle. Merit-based academic tuition fee reductions up to 30% are awarded to qualifying international scholars.
