The City of Liverpool College

The City of Liverpool College is the premier college of further and higher education in the Liverpool City Region. Headquartered at The Learning Exchange on Roscoe Street in downtown Liverpool, the college provides comprehensive university-level qualifications, foundation degrees, Higher Technical Qualifications (HTQs), and industry apprenticeships in creative arts, engineering, computing, and culinary arts.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Liverpool, England, United Kingdom

Institutional Overview

The college partners with leading regional universities including Liverpool John Moores University and the University of Central Lancashire to award accredited foundation degrees and full honours degrees. Its flagship creative hub, the Arts Centre on Myrtle Street, and the STEM Centre for Engineering provide cutting-edge industrial fabrication suites, recording studios, and digital suites.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions for higher education programs require Level 3 qualifications (A-Levels, BTEC Extended Diploma, or Access to Higher Education Diploma). International applicants must demonstrate English language competence (IELTS 6.0). Tuition fees are competitively priced at £7,500 - £8,500 per year for higher education degrees, with UK Student Loans Company funding available.
