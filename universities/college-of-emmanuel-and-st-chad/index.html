University of Emmanuel & St Chad

The College of Emmanuel & St. Chad, federated with the Saskatoon Theological Union and affiliated with the University of Saskatchewan, is Canada's premier Anglican theological seminary serving the ecclesiastical provinces of Rupert's Land.

Academic overview & excellence

Offering accredited Master of Divinity (M.Div.), Master of Theological Studies (M.T.S.), and Doctor of Ministry (D.Min.) degrees, the college integrates classical biblical exegesis, Indigenous ministries, eco-theology, and pastoral leadership development.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to graduate theological degrees requires an accredited Bachelor of Arts or equivalent degree with minimum 70% average. International scholars submit English proficiency test scores (TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5) and ecclesial endorsement letters.
