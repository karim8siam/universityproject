Northern Arizona University

Northern Arizona · Flagstaff, Arizona, United States

Northern Arizona University is a high-research public doctoral university situated 7,000 feet above sea level in Flagstaff, Arizona. Celebrated for forestry, climate science, planetary astronomy at Lowell Observatory, microbiology, and indigenous health disparities.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NAU features the College of the Environment, Forestry, and Natural Sciences, the Pathogen and Microbiome Institute (PMI), and the School of Informatics, Computing, and Cyber Systems. Research centers manage the 4,000-acre Centennial Forest and partner with the USGS Astrogeology Science Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core competency coursework (minimum 3.0 unweighted core GPA for guaranteed admission). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
