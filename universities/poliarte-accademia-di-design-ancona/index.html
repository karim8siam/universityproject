Poliarte Accademia di Design Ancona: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1972 and legally recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) under the Higher Artistic, Musical and Dance Education (AFAM) sector, Poliarte Accademia di Design delivers accredited Diploma Accademico di I and II Livello degrees. Nestled in Ancona within the heart of the Marche industrial design district, Poliarte maintains direct design laboratory partnerships with prestigious nautical shipyards (such as Ferretti Group and CRN), luxury fashion houses (Tod's), and ergonomic furniture manufacturers.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Secondary school diploma plus portfolio presentation and motivational interview
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Italian & English (International design master's courses offer bilingual tracks)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
€140 (Regional tax)
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.
