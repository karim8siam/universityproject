Academic overview & research priorities

Operating prominent campuses across Tokyo (Itabashi, Hachioji, Kasuga) and Tochigi (Utsunomiya), Teikyo University is a medical and STEM research powerhouse. Its Itabashi medical campus houses the School of Medicine, School of Pharma-Sciences, and the Graduate School of Public Health—which operates a famous academic partnership with the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. The Utsunomiya campus excels in aerospace engineering and automotive robotics.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate international admissions require EJU scores or Teikyo entrance examination, high school transcripts, and interview. Graduate School of Public Health and Medical Sciences require a bachelor's degree in medicine, pharmacy, biology, or allied health, research proposal, and English or Japanese proficiency. International MPH applicants require TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5.
