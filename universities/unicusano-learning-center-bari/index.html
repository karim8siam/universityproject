Institution Overview & Profile

Università degli Studi Niccolò Cusano (Polo di Bari) is a major regional learning centre of the MUR-accredited Italian telematics university Unicusano, providing undergraduate, single-cycle master, and postgraduate degree programs in engineering, law, economics, psychology, and educational sciences with multimedia e-learning and on-site exam facilities in Bari.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Telematic University Campus (Università Telematica Statale Riconosciuta MUR)
Main CampusVia Domenico Nicolai Campus, Downtown Bari, Apulia
AccreditationItalian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) / ANVUR
Admissions RouteOpen rolling admissions with mandatory non-selective verification test
Language of InstructionItalian (B2/C1) with select English curriculum modules
Financial Aid & GrantsUnicusano 'Borse di Studio Reti Formative' & Regional ADISU Puglia Partnerships

Key Academic Strengths

  • Full MUR legal equivalence conferring identical qualifications to traditional Italian public universities
  • Flexible blended instructional methodology combining 24/7 digital lectures with local tutor-assisted seminars in Bari
  • Comprehensive faculties encompassing Civil, Mechanical, Industrial, and Biomedical Engineering
  • Integrated regional career service facilitating internship placements with Apulian industrial and service companies
