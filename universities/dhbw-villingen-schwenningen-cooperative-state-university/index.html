Institution Overview & Profile

Duale Hochschule Baden-Württemberg Villingen-Schwenningen (DHBW VS), situated in the Black Forest industrial and medical technology cluster, is a premier state university delivering dual Bachelor's degrees in Business Administration, Business Information Systems, Social Work, and Healthcare Management, combining salaried company apprenticeships with rigorous academic trimesters.

Institution TypePublic Cooperative State University (Duale Hochschule)
Main CampusFriedrich-Ebert-Straße Campus, Villingen-Schwenningen, Black Forest
AccreditationState of Baden-Württemberg / ZEvA Accredited / Duale Hochschule System
Admissions RouteSigned training contract with an approved partner company + university matriculation
Language of InstructionGerman (DSH-2 / TestDaF 4 / Goethe C1); selected English business modules
Financial Aid & GrantsMonthly corporate salary (€1,050–€1,550/month) throughout all 3 years + BAföG eligibility

Key Academic Strengths

  • Continuous monthly remuneration from corporate partner firms throughout all three years of study
  • Over 900 corporate partners across the Black Forest, Lake Constance, and Swiss border regions
  • Specialized faculties for Healthcare Management, Social Work, Business Informatics, and Banking
  • Nearly 90% of graduates receive immediate permanent employment offers from their partner firms
