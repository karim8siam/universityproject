Fort Valley State University

Fort Valley State University (FVSU) is a premier 1890 Land-Grant HBCU located in central Georgia, internationally renowned for its Agricultural Research Station, the world-class Georgia Small Ruminant Research Center, and veterinary technology.

Academic overview & research priorities

As Georgia’s only 1890 land-grant university, FVSU leads the state in undergraduate degrees awarded to African American students in agriculture, mathematics, and computer science. Its Agricultural Research Station is a global hub for goat and sheep genetics, parasitology, and bioenergy crop breeding.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admission requires high school diploma with minimum 2.5 GPA. Graduate admissions require an accredited Bachelor degree with minimum 2.7 GPA, GRE scores for select programs, transcripts, and letters of recommendation.
