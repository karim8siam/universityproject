The Open University Business School Switzerland: Degrees & Research Guide

With dedicated Swiss representation and tutorial centres in Geneva and Zurich, The Open University Business School (OUBS) is an elite global business school holding triple accreditation from AACSB, AMBA, and EQUIS—a distinction achieved by less than 1% of business schools worldwide. Known across Switzerland for flexible executive MBA curricula, digital learning leadership, and doctoral research in management.

OUBS Switzerland Executive Women in Leadership Bursary

Merit bursary designed to support senior female executives and entrepreneurs residing in Switzerland advancing toward corporate board leadership.

Triple-Accredited Executive Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Globally recognized AACSB, AMBA, and EQUIS accredited modular MBA curriculum combining strategic financial management, digital transformation, and global marketing.

OUBS Doctoral Research Fellowship in Public Management and Civic Governance

Three-to-four year doctoral fellowship investigating administrative reform, digital public services, and international non-governmental organization governance in Geneva.

Centre for Voluntary Sector Leadership (CVSL) Swiss Non-Profit Governance Project

Applied research project analyzing leadership resilience, board accountability, and cross-sector partnerships within Geneva-based international humanitarian organizations.

Geneva International Organization & UN Policy Practicum

Executive internship placed with Geneva-based international secretariats, trade associations, and non-governmental policy advocacy networks.

OUBS Senior Postdoctoral Fellowship in Behavioral Ethics and Corporate Culture

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship investigating psychological safety, whistleblowing mechanisms, and ethical compliance culture within Swiss and European financial institutions.

Professor of Digital Work and Organizational Transformation

Senior professorial chair leading research and executive teaching on algorithmic management, hybrid remote workplace dynamics, and organizational agility.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (or equivalent professional qualification) plus a minimum of three years relevant managerial or professional work experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in business, economics, or social sciences with distinction, and an approved doctoral research prospectus.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with 6.0 in each subtest (or documented prior higher education in English).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2,000-word doctoral research proposal identifying theoretical contribution, methodology, and empirical context.
Application fee
CHF 0 (Free direct online registration for Swiss executive programs).
Required documentation
Certified copies of degree certificates, complete academic transcripts, CV/resume, personal statement, and two professional references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the modular executive MBA in Switzerland is approximately CHF 8,200 to CHF 9,800 per year (total approx CHF 24,500 over the 3-year executive cycle). Corporate sponsorship programs and executive bursaries up to 20% are available for qualifying professionals working in Switzerland.
