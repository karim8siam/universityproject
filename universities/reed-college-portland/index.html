Reed College

Reed College · Portland, Oregon, United States

Reed College is an internationally distinguished private undergraduate liberal arts and sciences college located on a 116-acre campus in southeastern Portland, Oregon. Globally famous for the mandatory freshman Humanities 110 course, the student-run Reed Research Reactor (the only TRIGA nuclear reactor operated primarily by undergraduates in the world), and ranking among the highest per-capita producers of future Ph.D. recipients in the United States.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Reed features the historic Eliot Hall, the Vollum College Center, the Reed Research Reactor, and the 28-acre Reed Canyon nature preserve. All graduating seniors must complete and orally defend a yearlong scholarly senior thesis.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective with a holistic review emphasizing intellectual curiosity, academic rigor, analytical writing, and self-directed scholarly motivation. Reed is test-blind/test-optional. International students must submit TOEFL (100+), IELTS (7.0+), or Duolingo (120+). Reed meets 100% of demonstrated financial need for all admitted students.
