Nanzan University (Nagoya Campus)

Nanzan University (Nanzan Daigaku) is a world-renowned private Catholic co-educational university situated in the leafy Yagoto hills of Showa-ku, Nagoya. Founded in 1946 by the Society of the Divine Word (SVD), Nanzan is celebrated globally for the Center for Japanese Studies (CJS), the Nanzan Institute for Religion and Culture, and top-ranked faculties in global liberal arts, economics, law, policy studies, and science and engineering.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Nagoya, Aichi Prefecture, Japan

Institutional Overview

Guided by the Latin motto 'Hominis Dignitati' (For Human Dignity), Nanzan comprises 8 faculties and 6 graduate schools. The campus was masterfully designed by modernist architect Antonin Raymond, featuring state-of-the-art facilities such as the Global Plaza for multilingual exchange, extensive anthropological museums, and high-performance computing centers.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions include general entrance examinations, the Kyotsu Test, and international entrance pathways. International applicants to the Center for Japanese Studies or undergraduate degrees submit transcripts, English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 80 / IELTS 6.5), or JLPT scores. Nanzan offers substantial merit scholarships, SVD Founder's Bursaries, and full tuition reduction programs.
