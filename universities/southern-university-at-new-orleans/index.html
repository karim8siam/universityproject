Southern University at New Orleans

SUNO · New Orleans, Louisiana, United States

Southern University at New Orleans (SUNO) is an accredited public historically Black university located in the historic Pontchartrain Park neighborhood of New Orleans, Louisiana. As a vital member of the Southern University System, SUNO is acclaimed for its pioneering Millie M. Charles School of Social Work, its FEPAC-accredited undergraduate Forensic Science program, its innovative Master of Arts in Museum Studies, and its deep commitment to urban community empowerment.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SUNO operates across two campuses: the Park Campus and the Lake Campus on Lake Pontchartrain. It comprises the College of Arts and Sciences, the College of Business and Public Administration, the College of Education and Human Development, and the Millie M. Charles School of Social Work. The university houses state-of-the-art forensic DNA laboratories, cybersecurity learning suites, and the Center for African and African American Studies.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 2.0) and completion of standard college preparatory coursework (test-optional). The Master of Social Work (MSW) program requires an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.5+ minimum GPA (3.0 for Advanced Standing), personal essays on social justice, and professional references.
