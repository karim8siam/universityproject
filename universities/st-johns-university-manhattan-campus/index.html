St. John's University Manhattan Campus

St. John's Manhattan · New York, New York, United States

St. John's University Manhattan Campus is a premier academic center situated at Astor Place in the heart of Greenwich Village and the East Village in Manhattan, New York. Home to the internationally acclaimed Maurice R. Greenberg School of Risk Management, Insurance and Actuarial Science (part of the Peter J. Tobin College of Business), it is recognized worldwide as a preeminent hub for actuarial science, financial catastrophe modeling, and global risk management.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Located within walking distance of Wall Street, Silicon Alley, and multinational financial corporations, the Manhattan Campus features cutting-edge trading rooms, Bloomberg terminals, actuarial analytics suites, and the world-renowned Kathryn and Shelby Cullom Davis Library (housing the largest collection of risk management and insurance literature in the world).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.6), mathematical proficiency, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Graduate programs in Actuarial Science, Enterprise Risk Management, and Finance require an accredited bachelor's degree with strong quantitative prerequisites (multivariable calculus, linear algebra, statistics), GMAT/GRE scores (waivers available for GPA > 3.25), and professional references.
