Auburn University: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Established in 1856, Auburn University is a top-ranked public land-grant flagship research institution classified among Carnegie R1 (Very High Research Activity) universities. World-renowned for aerospace engineering, cybersecurity, agriculture, and veterinary medicine, Auburn supports graduate scholars with comprehensive funding packages.

Doctoral research assistantship conducting funded aerospace research in hypersonics, additive manufacturing of rocket combustion chambers, and spacecraft flight dynamics.

Hands-on cooperative internship at the National Center for Additive Manufacturing Excellence working alongside NASA and aerospace contractors on fatigue testing.

Two-year postdoctoral appointment synthesizing novel solid-state lithium metal electrolytes, cathode coatings, and conducting synchrotron characterization.

Tenure-track faculty position responsible for establishing an externally funded research program in foundation models, computer vision, or trustworthy autonomous agents.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited 4-year US Bachelor's degree or international equivalent with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's or Master's degree in a quantitative or disciplinary field with demonstrated research capability and faculty alignment.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79 (with minimum 16 in each band), IELTS Academic 6.5, or Duolingo English Test 110.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive Statement of Purpose outlining research interests, professional objectives, and targeted faculty laboratories.
Application fee
USD $70 non-refundable application fee for international graduate applicants.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts and degree certificates, English language proficiency test scores, three letters of recommendation, and CV/resume.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International out-of-state graduate tuition is approximately USD $32,400 per academic year (based on 18 credit hours). However, over 80% of admitted research-based graduate students receive Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA) or Teaching Assistantships (GTA) that provide a 100% tuition waiver plus an annual living stipend of USD $22,000 to $30,000.
