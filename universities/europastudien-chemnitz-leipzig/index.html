Europastudien - Chemnitz & Leipzig European Studies Network

Europastudien is a premier inter-university academic consortium uniting Chemnitz University of Technology and Leipzig University, delivering interdisciplinary, bilingual degree programs in European integration, comparative politics, regional economic cohesion, and Central-Eastern European cross-border governance.

Academic overview & research priorities

Europastudien integrates political science, European law, cross-cultural communication, and economic history. Students benefit from mandatory foreign exchange terms, specialized training in European Union institutions, and direct research access to cross-border Euroregions along the Polish and Czech borders.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate entry requires the German Abitur or foreign university entrance qualification with certified English (B2) and German (C1) proficiency. Graduate applicants require a recognized Bachelor degree in social sciences, European studies, or humanities.
