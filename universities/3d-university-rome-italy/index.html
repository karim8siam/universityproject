Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Secondary school diploma or recognized university degree in design, architecture, fine arts, computer science, or relevant digital portfolio.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctorates; operates applied digital production laboratories in collaboration with European film academies.
Language proficiency
Italian and English (modules offered with bilingual English/Italian instruction).
Research statement / Proposal
Creative portfolio showing 3D models, 2D concept art, graphic design, or video reel (minimum 5 pieces).
Application fee
€100 registration and portfolio review fee.
Required documentation
Curriculum vitae, academic qualification certificates, digital portfolio link, and letter of motivation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for professional master courses typically ranges from €4,500 to €8,900 for full-year comprehensive certifications. Italian cinema production bursaries, early enrollment grants, and hardware workstation sponsorships are available.
