University of New Hampshire

UNH · Durham, New Hampshire, United States

The University of New Hampshire (UNH) is New Hampshire's flagship public land-, sea-, and space-grant research university, established in 1866 in picturesque Durham, New Hampshire. A designated Carnegie R1 institution, UNH is internationally celebrated for space physics instrumentation on NASA missions, ocean mapping at the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM), sustainability, and interdisciplinary marine science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNH encompasses six colleges and schools including the College of Engineering and Physical Sciences (CEPS), College of Life Sciences and Agriculture (COLSA), College of Liberal Arts, Peter T. Paul College of Business and Economics, and the UNH Franklin Pierce School of Law. Key research centers include the Institute for the Study of Earth, Oceans, and Space (EOS) and the InterOperability Laboratory (IOL).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic for undergraduates. Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, provide three letters of recommendation, a statement of purpose, and meet English language proficiency requirements (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
