Tennessee Tech University

Tennessee Tech · Cookeville, Tennessee, United States

Tennessee Tech University (TTU) is the state's flagship public technological university, situated in Cookeville amidst the Upper Cumberland region. Renowned for its Carnegie R2 research standing, Tennessee Tech excels in engineering, cybersecurity, smart manufacturing, and environmental water resources.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

TTU features the College of Engineering, the College of Arts and Sciences, and the College of Agriculture and Human Ecology. Three state-funded Centers of Excellence—the Cybersecurity Education, Research and Outreach Center (CEROC), the Center for Energy Systems Research (CESR), and the Center for Manufacturing Research (CMR)—anchor its high-impact research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school diploma with minimum 2.50 GPA and 17 ACT (or test-optional holistic review). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75+ GPA for master's programs and 3.0+ for doctoral degrees, alongside transcripts, GRE scores (departmental waivers available), and recommendation letters.
