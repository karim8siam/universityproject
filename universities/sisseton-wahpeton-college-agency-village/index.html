Sisseton Wahpeton College

Sisseton Wahpeton · Agency Village, South Dakota, United States

Sisseton Wahpeton College (SWC) is an accredited tribal college located in Agency Village on the Lake Traverse Reservation of the Sisseton Wahpeton Oyate in northeastern South Dakota. Dedicated to empowering the Dakota community, SWC delivers associate degree programs and vocational certifications rooted in the Dakota language, culture, and communal self-determination.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SWC provides specialized academic programs in Dakota Studies, Sustainable Food Systems, Behavioral Health, Computer Information Systems, and General Arts and Sciences. The college serves as a regional anchor for cultural preservation, indigenous horticulture, and professional skill enhancement.

Research admissions & international qualifications

SWC practices open enrollment for high school graduates and GED completers. Applications are submitted online or at the Agency Village admissions office, requiring high school transcripts or GED scores, immunization records, and tribal enrollment verification (if applicable).
