Technical University Berlin (Technische Universität Berlin)

Founded in 1879, Technical University Berlin (Technische Universität Berlin - TU Berlin) is a member of Germany's TU9 alliance of leading institutes of technology and a cornerstone of the Berlin University Alliance (an elite Consortium of Excellence). World-renowned for engineering, computer science, urban planning, and catalysis at the UniSysCat (Unifying Systems in Catalysis) Cluster of Excellence, TU Berlin educates over 35,000 students tuition-free in the heart of Germany's capital, powering European technological innovation.

TU Berlin UniSysCat Cluster of Excellence Doctoral Fellowships in Catalysis

Doctoral research at Germany's premier Cluster of Excellence Unifying Systems in Catalysis (UniSysCat), investigating chemical and biological catalytic cascades to produce green fuels and sustainable chemical feedstocks.

TU Berlin PhD in Computer Science & Distributed Big Data Systems (BIFOLD)

Doctoral research conducted within the Berlin Institute for the Foundations of Learning and Data (BIFOLD), developing next-generation database architectures, distributed query optimizers, and scalable ML infrastructures.

TU Berlin Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science (English-Taught)

Tuition-free two-year Master of Science taught entirely in English in Berlin, covering artificial intelligence, cognitive algorithms, distributed systems, and computer security.

TU Berlin Master of Science (MSc) in Sustainable Energy Systems

Two-year interdisciplinary Master's degree examining smart electricity grids, renewable power integration, thermal energy storage, and industrial energy efficiency in European energy markets.

Einstein Center Digital Future (ECDF) Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Interdisciplinary research appointment at the Einstein Center Digital Future in Berlin, investigating smart city data infrastructures, trusted AI systems, and digital health technologies.

TU Berlin Institute of Optics & Atomic Physics Summer Traineeship

Two-month summer research traineeship investigating semiconductor quantum dots, single-photon sources, and terahertz spectroscopy in advanced optical physics laboratories.

TU Berlin Postdoctoral Research Associate in Green Hydrogen Production & Electrolysis

Full-time academic research appointment in the Department of Process Engineering, engineering scalable membrane electrode assemblies (MEA) and pressurized water electrolysis stacks for industrial green hydrogen generation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in a relevant engineering, natural science, or computing discipline with an above-average final grade (German grade 2.5 or better, equivalent to 75%+ or 3.0/4.0 GPA).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 300 ECTS total) with a thesis graded 'good' (gut) or 'very good' (sehr gut); written supervision commitment (Betreuungszusage) from a TU Berlin professor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 88 for English-taught master's degrees; German C1 (TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2) for German-medium programs.
Research statement / Proposal
A 5-page doctoral research proposal outlining state of the art, hypothesis, experimental design, and preliminary timeline.
Application fee
€0 application fee; no tuition fees charged at public German research universities.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma certificates, full academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, research proposal, and signed professor supervision agreement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

TU Berlin charges zero tuition fees for all students regardless of nationality. Students pay only a mandatory semester fee (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €315 per semester, which includes the Deutschlandticket semester public transit pass. Doctoral candidates are employed as academic staff on TV-L E13 salaried positions (€48,000 to €54,000/year gross) with complete health and pension coverage.
