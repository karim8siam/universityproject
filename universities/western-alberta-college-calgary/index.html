Academic overview & excellence

Located in the central business district of Calgary, Western Alberta College provides specialized postsecondary diplomas licensed under the Private Vocational Training Act of Alberta. The college maintains modern computing labs, cloud architecture simulators, and medical informatics training suites designed to bridge academic theory with practical employment in Alberta's diversified economy.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires an Alberta High School Diploma or recognized Canadian/international equivalent with minimum 65% aggregate. Mature students (19+) may qualify through standardized entrance assessments. International students require IELTS 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band) or equivalent TOEFL/Duolingo scores.
