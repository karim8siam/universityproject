Amherst College: Physics, Neuroscience & Need-Blind Full Aid Guide

Amherst College, established in 1821 in Western Massachusetts, is widely recognized as one of the very premier undergraduate institutions in the world. Operating an open curriculum with zero distribution requirements, Amherst combines unmatched laboratory access with one of only a handful of genuinely need-blind admission policies globally for both domestic and international students.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Amherst is an exclusively undergraduate liberal arts college; graduate research conducted through Five College Consortium partnerships (UMass Amherst, Smith, Mount Holyoke, Hampshire).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate degree programs prepare students for fully funded doctoral admission at top-tier worldwide universities (70%+ alumni complete graduate degrees).
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 100+, IELTS Academic 7.5+, or Duolingo English Test 130+; waivers available for students whose primary secondary school instruction was in English.
Research statement / Proposal
Senior honors thesis proposal required in major department during junior year (comprehensive 20–40 page research project).
Application fee
$75 USD application fee (automatically waived for all students with financial need).
Required documentation
Common Application or Coalition Application, secondary school transcript, School Report, two academic teacher recommendations, and counselor recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is $66,650 USD per year, with total cost of attendance approximately $85,000 USD. Amherst is 100% need-blind for all students (domestic and international) and meets 100% of calculated financial need with loan-free aid packages (average scholarship award exceeds $64,000 USD/year).
