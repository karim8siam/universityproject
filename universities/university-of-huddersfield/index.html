Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:2 or 2:1 honours degree or international equivalent in engineering, pharmacy, business, or computing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in a relevant subject with merit or distinction, and an approved 3-page research proposal.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 to 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in all bands) or equivalent SELT certificate.
Research statement / Proposal
A 1,000-1,500 word research outline detailing methodology, novelty, and laboratory resource requirements.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the university direct online portal.
Required documentation
Degree certificates, transcripts, two academic references, personal statement, and valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition averages £16,500 to £18,500 per academic year. The Huddersfield International Scholarship provides up to £4,000 annually. Fully funded doctoral studentships through EPSRC and IRR cover full fees and standard UKRI living stipends.
