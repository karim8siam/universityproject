University of South Carolina Aiken

USC Aiken · Aiken, South Carolina, United States

The University of South Carolina Aiken (USC Aiken) is a top-ranked public regional comprehensive university established in 1961 in Aiken, South Carolina. Located near the Savannah River National Laboratory (SRNL) nuclear corridor and celebrated as the equestrian heartland of the South, USC Aiken delivers exceptional programs in industrial process engineering, cybersecurity, nursing, biology, and equestrian business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

USC Aiken spans four colleges and schools: the College of Sciences and Engineering, School of Nursing, School of Business Administration, and the College of Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences. The campus hosts the Ruth Patrick Science Education Center and the Advanced Manufacturing Collaborative.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students, evaluating high school core GPA (minimum 2.5). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.0+).
