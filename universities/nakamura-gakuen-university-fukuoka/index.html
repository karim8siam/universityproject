Nakamura Gakuen University

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded by Haru Nakamura with the pioneering philosophy of "Education through Food," Nakamura Gakuen is a national leader in registered dietitian national examination success, clinical nutritional biochemistry, and functional food development. The university operates advanced nutritional sciences laboratories and maintains extensive clinical partnerships with major hospital networks across Kyushu.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation and competitive entrance exams in chemistry and biology. International applicants require JLPT N1/N2 or EJU Japanese score >= 220, secondary school transcripts, and an academic faculty interview.
