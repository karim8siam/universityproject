The University of Chicago

Founded in 1890 by John D. Rockefeller and William Rainey Harper, The University of Chicago (UChicago) is an intellectual powerhouse situated in Hyde Park on Chicago's South Side. Renowned for rigorous debate, free inquiry, and transformative research, UChicago is affiliated with 99 Nobel laureates, the birth of the 'Chicago School' of economics, the creation of the first controlled nuclear reaction (Chicago Pile-1), the world-famous Booth School of Business, and the revolutionary Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering. All admitted PhD students receive full multi-year funding guarantees.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UChicago Kenneth C. Griffin Department of Economics Fully Funded Doctoral Fellowships

World's most celebrated doctoral program in economics, birthplace of 34 Nobel Prizes in Economic Sciences, providing five years of guaranteed living stipends, comprehensive health insurance, and full tuition waivers.

UChicago Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering (PME) PhD in Quantum Engineering

Cutting-edge doctoral research at the Chicago Quantum Exchange (CQE) connecting UChicago with Argonne National Laboratory and Fermilab, fabricating solid-state quantum bits, quantum sensors, and quantum repeater networks.

UChicago PhD in Computer Science & AI Systems

Doctoral research conducted within the Department of Computer Science and the Data Science Institute (DSI), investigating machine learning theory, privacy-preserving systems, human-computer interaction, and quantum computer architectures.

UChicago Harris School of Public Policy Master of Public Policy (MPP)

Rigorous two-year Master of Public Policy rooted in microeconomics, game theory, and empirical statistics, training global leaders to apply quantitative evidence to solve pressing social and economic policy challenges.

Enrico Fermi Institute (EFI) Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Prestigious postdoctoral appointment at the Enrico Fermi Institute, conducting foundational theoretical and experimental research in particle physics, cosmology, dark matter searches, and quantum gravity.

Chicago Quantum Exchange (CQE) Summer Research Traineeship

Ten-week National Science Foundation REU summer research traineeship collaborating with UChicago and Argonne National Laboratory scientists on quantum communication testbeds, diamond NV centers, and quantum optics.

UChicago Postdoctoral Scholar in Cellular Metalloenzymes & Chemical Biology

Postdoctoral research role in the Department of Chemistry and Institute for Biophysical Dynamics, investigating catalytic mechanisms of metalloenzymes involved in epitranscriptomic RNA methylation and cellular stress responses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree with high academic distinction (minimum 3.5/4.0 GPA or equivalent first-class standing); quantitative programs require rigorous multivariable calculus and linear algebra preparation.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's or Master's degree with superior academic achievement; demonstrated capacity for independent creative research through honors theses or published papers.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 7.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 100 (minimum 25 in each sub-score); Booth and Economics require IELTS 7.5 / TOEFL 105.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2 to 3 page Candidate Statement of Research Purpose articulating research questions, theoretical interests, faculty with whom the applicant wishes to work, and intellectual motivations.
Application fee
$90 non-refundable application fee submitted via UChicago's graduate application portal (fee waivers available for eligible applicants).
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 3 letters of academic recommendation, statement of purpose, and GRE scores (where required by individual departments).

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

The University of Chicago guarantees 100% full funding packages for all admitted PhD students in the Divisions of the Biological Sciences, Physical Sciences, Social Sciences, Humanities, and the Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering. The standard doctoral package covers 100% of full tuition ($62,000/year graduate rate), comprehensive UChicago Student Health Insurance (U-SHIP, valued at over $4,800/year), full student life fees, and an annual living stipend of $45,000 to $48,000.
