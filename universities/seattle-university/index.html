Seattle University

Seattle University · Seattle, Washington, United States

Seattle University is a prestigious Jesuit Catholic university situated on a vibrant 50-acre urban campus in Seattle's Capitol Hill neighborhood. Renowned for computer science and software engineering, the Seattle University School of Law, Albers School of Business and Economics, and the Sinegal Center for Science and Innovation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Seattle U is anchored in the Pacific Northwest tech corridor, featuring the Jim and Janet Sinegal Center for Science and Innovation, Sullivan Hall (School of Law), and the Center for Business Ethics. Its proximity to Amazon, Microsoft, Boeing, and Fred Hutch Cancer Center fuels deep corporate sponsorships and research clinics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.65), curriculum rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Graduate computing, law, and business programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, professional references, and analytical essays.
