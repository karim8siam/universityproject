Academic overview & excellence

Situated in the UNESCO World Heritage Hanseatic city of Wismar, Hochschule Wismar unites three core faculties: Engineering, Business, and Design. The university operates a specialized Department of Maritime Studies at the historic Baltic seaport of Rostock-Warnemünde, housing full-mission ship handling simulators and marine engine testbeds. It also delivers the world-renowned international Master in Architectural Lighting Design and accredited online degrees via its subsidiary WINGS.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate programs require the German Fachhochschulreife or an equivalent international secondary credential evaluated via uni-assist. Master's candidates must hold a relevant bachelor's degree with a minimum grade of 2.5 (German grading system). Design programs require an artistic portfolio and entrance test. Language requirements require German C1 (DSH-2 / TestDaF 4) for German-taught tracks, or IELTS 6.5 for English-taught master's degrees.
