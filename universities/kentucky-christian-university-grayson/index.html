Kentucky Christian University

Kentucky Christian · Grayson, Kentucky, United States

Kentucky Christian University is a private Christian university in Grayson, Kentucky, affiliated with the Christian churches and churches of Christ, offering accredited degrees in nursing, biblical studies, education, and business administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

KCU houses the Yancey School of Nursing, Keeran School of Bible and Ministry, and Sack School of Business. Academic programs emphasize character development, clinical healthcare outreach in the Appalachian foothills, and ethical business stewardship.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions require high school transcripts (minimum 2.5 cumulative GPA), personal statement of faith and character, and standardized test scores or test-optional evaluation. Nursing candidates must meet clinical prerequisites and TEAS exam thresholds.
