Design-Geschichte Bayern

Design-Geschichte Bayern (Bavarian Design History Forum) is the preeminent research network and archival foundation dedicated to documenting, theorizing, and exhibiting 20th- and 21st-century industrial, automotive, and graphic design in Bavaria.

Academic overview & research priorities

Collaborating closely with Die Neue Sammlung – The Design Museum at the Pinakothek der Moderne, the Academy of Fine Arts Munich, and Munich University of Applied Sciences, the forum investigates Bavarian design modernism, the Ulm School legacy, automotive aerodynamics, and digital user interfaces.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open to graduate students, doctoral researchers, and museum curators in design history, art history, industrial design, and material culture upon competitive submission of research proposals.

Director of Die Neue Sammlung and Honorary Professor of Design History · Curatorial Board / Design-Geschichte Bayern

Prof. Dr. Angelika Nollert directs Die Neue Sammlung – The Design Museum in Munich, one of the world’s leading design institutions, curating major international retrospectives on industrial form and sustainable future materials.

Dr. Florian Hufnagl

Founding Fellow and Senior Design Historiographer · Bavarian Design Archive Research Unit

Dr. Florian Hufnagl has pioneered the academic documentation of Bavarian corporate design languages, analyzing how industrial design helped shape West Germany’s post-war economic miracle (Wirtschaftswunder).
