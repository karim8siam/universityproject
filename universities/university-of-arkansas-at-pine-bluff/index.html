University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff

UAPB · Pine Bluff, Arkansas, United States

The University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff (UAPB), founded in 1873 as Branch Normal College, is the second-oldest public university in Arkansas and a premier Historically Black 1890 Land-Grant Institution. Located in the Arkansas Delta, UAPB is internationally recognized for its Aquaculture and Fisheries Center of Excellence—the only comprehensive aquaculture program in Arkansas—along with strong programs in agriculture, STEM, and education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UAPB consists of the School of Agriculture, Fisheries and Human Sciences (SAFHS), the School of Arts and Sciences, the School of Business and Management, and the School of Education. Its expansive facilities include a 43-acre aquaculture research station with over 100 research ponds, fish pathology laboratories, and commercial hatcheries.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with a minimum 2.0 GPA (test-optional). Graduate applicants to MS and PhD programs must possess an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA in relevant scientific fields, submit letters of recommendation, and secure faculty research sponsorship.
