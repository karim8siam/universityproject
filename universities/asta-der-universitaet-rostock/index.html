AStA der Universität Rostock

The General Students’ Committee (AStA) of the University of Rostock is the democratically elected executive body representing over 13,000 students at Northern Europe’s oldest university, administering student welfare funds, civic democracy fellowships, and emergency social stipends.

Academic overview & research priorities

AStA Rostock coordinates student self-governance, social welfare counseling, student mobility programs (Semesterticket), and campus sustainability initiatives. In close partnership with the University of Rostock Senate, AStA sponsors research into student social demographics, university mental health, and affordable academic housing.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open to all matriculated undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students at the University of Rostock. Fellowship applicants submit student enrollment proof, academic transcripts, and a civic engagement portfolio.
