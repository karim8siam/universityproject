New College, University of Oxford

New College is one of the oldest, largest, and academically most renowned constituent colleges of the University of Oxford. Founded in 1379 by William of Wykeham, Bishop of Winchester, New College features magnificent medieval architecture, including the 14th-century Great Quadrangle, historic cloister, and the original City Wall of Oxford, while fostering cutting-edge scholarship across humanities, physical sciences, medicine, and mathematics.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Oxford, England, United Kingdom

Institutional Overview

Students at New College study across the full spectrum of Oxford undergraduate and postgraduate faculties, taught through intensive, world-famous Oxford weekly tutorials by leading tutorial fellows. The college maintains an extraordinary musical tradition with its world-famous New College Choir and boasts a vibrant graduate community housed in modern Weston Buildings.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Undergraduate admission is fiercely competitive, coordinated through UCAS by October 15. Candidates are selected based on academic transcripts (AAA/A*AA at A-Level or 39-40 IB points), admissions tests (PAT, MAT, LNAT, UCAT), and intensive Oxford academic interviews. Over £1.5 million in college bursaries and full Clarendon/College graduate scholarships are awarded annually.
