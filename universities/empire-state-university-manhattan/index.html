Academic overview & research priorities

Part of the SUNY system, SUNY Empire Manhattan specializes in innovative degree customization, prior learning assessment, and hybrid educational delivery across business management, labor studies, cybersecurity, and public policy.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school diploma or equivalent. Undergraduate admissions are rolling, welcoming transfer credits, military training, and professional learning portfolios. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+.
