Royal Naval College: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1829 by King William I and situated on the naval base in Den Helder, North Holland, the Royal Naval College (KIM) operates as the maritime component of the Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA). KIM delivers fully accredited Bachelor of Science degrees accredited by NVAO (the Accreditation Organisation of the Netherlands and Flanders) in Military Operational Systems (including Nautical Navigation), Military Systems and Technology (Marine Engineering), and Civil & Military Engineering. Midshipmen combine rigorous academic study with naval seamanship, military leadership, ocean voyages aboard HNLMS Van Kinsbergen, and underwater survival.
