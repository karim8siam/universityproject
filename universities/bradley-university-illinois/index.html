Bradley University

Bradley University is a top-ranked private comprehensive university in Peoria, Illinois, internationally celebrated for its Caterpillar College of Engineering and Technology, top-five ranked game design programs, and transformative undergraduate research.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1897 by philanthropist Lydia Moss Bradley, Bradley University educates nearly 5,400 undergraduate and graduate students across five distinguished colleges. Bradley is celebrated for its deep industrial ties to Fortune 100 giant Caterpillar Inc., whose global headquarters was historically founded in Peoria. The Caterpillar College of Engineering and Technology and the Department of Interactive Media within the Slane College of Communications and Fine Arts consistently rank among North America’s best programs for video game development, mechanical design, nursing, and computer science.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Game Design and Interactive Media (Bachelor's) — Princeton Review top-ranked curriculum offering intensive studios in 3D game engines, procedural level design, multiplayer networking, and character animation.
  • Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering (MSME) (Master's) — Advanced engineering degree focusing on thermo-fluids, robotics dynamics, finite element analysis, and heavy equipment automation.
  • Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) (Doctorate) — CAPTE-accredited professional clinical doctorate providing over 30 weeks of hands-on clinical internships and human anatomy cadaver dissection.
  • Master of Science in Computer Science: Artificial Intelligence Specialization (Master's) — Graduate computing program emphasizing deep neural networks, computer vision, distributed cloud databases, and software reliability.
