UNSW Sydney (The University of New South Wales)

Established in 1949 in Kensington, Sydney, The University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney) is a founding member of Australia's prestigious Group of Eight (Go8) and ranked in the world's top 20 universities (QS 2024/2025). UNSW is globally celebrated for pioneering silicon quantum computing (the Centre for Quantum Computation and Communication Technology - CQC2T led by Australian of the Year Prof. Michelle Simmons), world-record photovoltaic solar cell efficiencies, and artificial intelligence. UNSW University International Postgraduate Award (UIPA) & RTP Scholarships

UNSW's premier competitive international doctoral scholarship scheme, awarding full tuition remissions and generous tax-free living stipends to outstanding global research scholars.

UNSW PhD in Computer Science and Engineering

Doctoral research program inside Australia's top-ranked engineering faculty, offering world-class research in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, database systems, and operating systems.

UNSW Master of Information Technology (Artificial Intelligence & Data Science)

Comprehensive two-year professional computing degree offering specialized streams in artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, cybersecurity, and data science.

UNSW Master of Engineering in Photovoltaics and Solar Energy

World-leading solar energy master's program from the institution that pioneered modern silicon solar cells, focusing on photovoltaic manufacturing, grid integration, and clean energy storage.

Centre for Quantum Computation and Communication Technology (CQC2T) Placements

World-renowned research placements at the forefront of atomic-scale silicon quantum computing, conducting single-atom spin manipulation and quantum architecture development.

UNSW Engineering Taste of Research & Summer Placements

Prestigious 60-day summer research internship program allowing high-performing engineering students to collaborate directly with leading UNSW research groups on frontier projects.

UNSW Casual Academic Teaching Fellowships & Lab Demonstratorships

Paid academic teaching contracts for postgraduate researchers, leading tutorial discussions, laboratory demonstration sessions, and coursework assessment in computing and engineering.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree with a minimum equivalent to an Australian credit average (65%+) or high credit (70%+) depending on faculty and discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree with Class I Honours (or Class II Division I) or Master's degree with significant research dissertation demonstrating high academic distinction.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each subband) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 23 in writing, 22 in reading/listening/speaking).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-3 page research project proposal detailing aims, significance, methodology, and endorsed UNSW academic supervisor alignment.
Application fee
AUD $125 non-refundable international application fee submitted via the UNSW online Apply portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, degree completion certificates, grading scale keys, 2 academic referee reports, detailed CV, and project outline.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international research candidates is 100% fully covered under the University International Postgraduate Award (UIPA) and Research Training Program (RTP). Recipients receive full international tuition fee waivers, Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), and tax-free living allowances of AUD $37,684 per year (indexed annually) for 3.5 to 4 years.
