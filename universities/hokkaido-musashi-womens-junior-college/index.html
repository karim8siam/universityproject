Hokkaido Musashi College: Liberal Arts, Economics, English & Career Guide

Hokkaido Musashi Women's Junior College in Sapporo is one of northern Japan's most respected women's higher education institutions, celebrated for its Department of Liberal Arts and Economics, Department of English Communication, exceptional graduate employment rates, and study abroad pathways.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Two-year junior college offering Associate Degrees (Tanki Daigaku Shogo); graduates qualify for junior-year transfer into 4-year universities across Japan and abroad.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate junior college; does not confer master's or doctoral degrees.
Language proficiency
JLPT N2 minimum for international student degree enrollment; EJU Japanese score >= 200.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of personal study motivation (Shibo Riyusho) and career progression plan.
Application fee
¥30,000 international entrance examination fee.
Required documentation
High school graduation diploma, 3-year academic transcript, JLPT certificate, personal statement, and recommendation from secondary school principal.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual tuition is approximately ¥1,050,000 to ¥1,180,000. Institutional tuition exemption grants (up to 50% waiver), Musashi Gakuen alumni scholarships, and JASSO student support loans are available.
