Middle Tennessee State University

MTSU · Murfreesboro, Tennessee, United States

Middle Tennessee State University is a prominent public Carnegie R2 doctoral university in Murfreesboro, Tennessee. Renowned for its world-class Recording Industry program, aerospace flight training, concrete industry management, and equine science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MTSU features the College of Media and Entertainment (alumni include dozens of Grammy and CMA winners), the Department of Aerospace (fleet of Diamond DA40 training aircraft), and the unique Concrete Industry Management (CIM) program.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school GPA >= 3.0 or minimum 22 ACT / 1100 SAT. Graduate aerospace and recording industry programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, portfolios, and letters of recommendation.
