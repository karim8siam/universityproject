NSCAD University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located along the historic harbourfront of Halifax, Nova Scotia, NSCAD University was founded by Anna Leonowens and gained international acclaim in the 1970s as a global powerhouse of conceptual art. Today, NSCAD hosts vibrant undergraduate and graduate studios across its Fountain Campus, Academy Campus, and Port Campus. The university offers immersive training in ceramics, jewellery design, textiles, fine arts, film, and graphic design, emphasizing sustainable craft practices, indigenous material traditions, and experimental digital arts.
