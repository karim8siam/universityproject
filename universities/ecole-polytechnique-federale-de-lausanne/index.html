École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)

Founded in 1853 on Lake Geneva in Lausanne, EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) is one of the world's most dynamic science and engineering institutions, consistently ranked in the global top 15. Partnered with ETH Zurich in the Swiss Federal ETH Domain, EPFL excels in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, Microengineering, and Renewable Energy. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

EPFL Master's Excellence Fellowships

EPFL's premier merit fellowship awarded to exceptional incoming international and Swiss Master's candidates, providing CHF 20,000 per year living allowance and guaranteed student housing in Lausanne.

EPFL Doctoral Assistantships (Doctorat Salarié)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointment inside EPFL's 22 doctoral programs, conducting frontier scientific investigations with state-of-the-art supercomputing and laboratory infrastructure.

EPFL Master of Science in Computer Science

World-class two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) in the School of Computer and Communication Sciences (IC), offering specializations in Machine Intelligence, Information Security, Software Systems, and Theory.

EPFL Master of Science in Data Science

Comprehensive two-year Master of Science program (120 ECTS) jointly offered by Computer Science and Mathematics, covering mathematical statistics, machine learning, distributed optimization, and big data systems.

EPFL Center for Intelligent Systems (CIS) Research Projects

Multidisciplinary research center at EPFL bringing together robotics, computer science, and microengineering to investigate autonomous mobile robots, intelligent sensors, and edge AI systems.

Summer@EPFL International Research Fellowship in Computer Science

Highly selective fully funded summer research program (July to August/September) inside EPFL's School of Computer and Communication Sciences (IC), offering international students direct research collaboration with faculty mentors.

EPFL Student Teaching Assistantships (Sous-assistants)

Departmental academic employment (sous-assistant) for enrolled undergraduate and graduate students at EPFL, providing competitive hourly compensation for conducting weekly tutorial exercise sessions and supervising lab sections.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Science degree from an internationally accredited university in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or Physics with top-tier academic marks.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science degree (minimum 120 ECTS) in relevant quantitative field with documented research achievements and admission into one of EPFL's 22 doctoral schools (EDIC, EDPO, etc.).
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to CEFR C1: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100 for English-taught graduate curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page statement of purpose detailing academic milestones, research interests, and preferred EPFL laboratories.
Application fee
CHF 150 application fee for holders of foreign degrees (CHF 50 for holders of Swiss degrees).
Required documentation
Complete official transcripts, certified copy of degree diploma, curriculum vitae in English, motivation essay, and three academic reference letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Swiss federal law, tuition at EPFL is heavily subsidized and among the lowest in the world for an elite institution. Tuition is CHF 730 per semester (CHF 1,460 per year) for Swiss residents, and CHF 2,190 per semester for international students. Recipients of the EPFL Master's Excellence Fellowship receive CHF 10,000 per semester plus guaranteed housing. Doctoral assistants are paid salaried employees receiving CHF 54,000 to CHF 57,000 per year.
