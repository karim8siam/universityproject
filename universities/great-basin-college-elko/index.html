Great Basin College

Great Basin College is a pioneering comprehensive college in Elko, Nevada, serving rural northeastern Nevada with premier programs in mining technology, digital instrumentation, land management, and nursing.

Academic overview & research priorities

Part of the Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE), GBC is a national leader in modern mining technology, electrical instrumentation, diesel mechanics, and land surveying, partnering closely with the global gold mining industry in the Carlin Trend.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open-door admissions policy for associate degrees and certificate programs (high school diploma or GED). Bachelor degree programs require associate degree completion or equivalent credits with 2.00+ GPA. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+.
