Wilmington University

WilmU · New Castle, Delaware, United States

Wilmington University (WilmU) is a private, nonprofit doctoral research institution founded in 1968 in New Castle, Delaware. Nationally recognized for accessible career-oriented education, cybersecurity, healthcare and doctoral nursing practice, business administration, and educational technology, WilmU serves as a primary provider of flexible degree programs for working professionals and international scholars throughout the Mid-Atlantic.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WilmU comprises seven colleges: the College of Technology, College of Health Professions & Natural Sciences, College of Business, College of Education & Liberal Arts, College of Social & Behavioral Sciences, School of Law, and the Division of Doctoral Studies. The university is a designated National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school diplomas or equivalencies with open, test-optional policies. Graduate doctoral and master's admissions require an accredited bachelor's or master's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, professional resume, personal statement, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+). Merit scholarships and graduate assistantships are available.
