University of Wisconsin-Whitewater at Rock County

UW-Whitewater at Rock County · Janesville, Wisconsin, United States

The University of Wisconsin-Whitewater at Rock County is a thriving branch campus in Janesville, Wisconsin. Known for its state-of-the-art engineering laboratories, accessible associate degrees, and direct bachelor's degree-completion pipelines with UW-Whitewater's top-ranked business and education colleges, Rock County delivers premier public university education to southern Wisconsin.
