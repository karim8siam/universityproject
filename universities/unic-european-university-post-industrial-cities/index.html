Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

The European University of Post-Industrial Cities (UNIC) is a premier European University Alliance funded by the European Commission, anchored in the Netherlands by Erasmus University Rotterdam (Campus Woudestein). Uniting 10 leading European universities, UNIC drives urban resilience, post-industrial economic transition, superdiversity, and cross-border joint European degrees.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Alliance of 10 universities across Rotterdam, Bochum, Cork, Bilbao, Liège, Oulu, Zagreb, Istanbul, Lodz, and Malmö
  • Pioneering UNIC CityLabs connecting academic researchers with municipal policymakers and citizen groups
  • Cross-border joint European Master's and micro-credentials with seamless credit portability
  • Deep focus on just transition, post-industrial regeneration, and superdiversity in urban centers

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Joint Master in Post-Industrial Urban Transformation (Joint European Master's) — Two-year trans-European master's studying industrial heritage repurposing, circular urban metabolism, and socially just green transitions.
  • Joint Master in Superdiversity in Education and Society (Joint European Master's) — Interdisciplinary European degree addressing linguistic diversity, inclusive urban schooling, and migration governance.
  • UNIC European Micro-Credentials in Urban Resilience (Postgraduate Certificate) — Modular, stackable European credits covering climate adaptation, participatory budgeting, and smart city digital citizenship.
