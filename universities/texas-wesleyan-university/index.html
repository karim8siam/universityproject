Texas Wesleyan University

Texas Wesleyan · Fort Worth, Texas, United States

Texas Wesleyan University is a distinguished private institution affiliated with the United Methodist Church, situated in the historic Polytechnic Heights neighborhood of Fort Worth, Texas. Texas Wesleyan is nationally celebrated for its Graduate Program of Nurse Anesthesia (GPNA)—one of the largest and most prestigious nurse anesthesia programs in the United States—alongside strong programs in business, education, and forensic science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Texas Wesleyan operates four academic schools: the School of Health Professions, the School of Business Administration, the School of Education, and the School of Arts and Sciences. Its specialized clinical simulation center and advanced health sciences laboratories prepare healthcare leaders, certified registered nurse anesthetists (CRNAs), and innovative professionals.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school grade point average, test scores (test-optional available), and leadership potential. The GPNA and graduate health science programs maintain rigorous admissions requiring an unencumbered RN license, minimum one year of critical care ICU experience, a competitive collegiate science GPA (3.0+ minimum), and faculty interview panels.
