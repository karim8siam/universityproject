Liverpool Guild of Students

The Liverpool Guild of Students is one of the UK's largest and most historic student unions and civic leadership institutes, established in 1892 at the Russell Group University of Liverpool, empowering student enterprise, democratic governance, sustainability advocacy, and community volunteer projects across Merseyside.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating from its landmark Mount Pleasant campus headquarters, the Guild provides accredited leadership development programs, green sustainability fellowships, charity management apprenticeships, and social innovation accelerators alongside the University of Liverpool.

Research admissions & criteria

Guild leadership academies and postgraduate bursaries are open to students enrolled at the University of Liverpool across all undergraduate and postgraduate faculties. Fellowship positions require demonstrated extracurricular leadership, social entrepreneurship, or union officer representation.
