Institution Overview & Profile

Nova Scotia Community College (NSCC) Ivany Campus, situated on the Dartmouth waterfront overlooking Halifax Harbour, is Atlantic Canada's premier polytechnic hub for advanced manufacturing, environmental sustainability, applied oceans technology, and digital media.

Institution TypePublic Polytechnic / Community College
Main CampusIvany Campus, Dartmouth Waterfront, Halifax Regional Municipality
AccreditationNova Scotia Department of Advanced Education / Polytechnics Canada
Admissions RouteNSCC Central Admissions Portal (rolling evaluation)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5, or Duolingo 105+)
Financial Aid & GrantsNSCC Foundation Bursaries & Atlantic Student Aid

Key Academic Strengths

  • Ocean technology, hydrographic survey, and marine instrumentation
  • Energy sustainability engineering technology and LEED-certified campus operations
  • Applied computer science, interactive media design, and cyber security
  • Integrated cooperative work terms with Atlantic Canadian maritime industry partners

Verified Academic Opportunities (7)

Scholarship

NSCC Foundation International Student Entrance Bursary

Merit- and need-based tuition fee reduction for newly admitted international polytechnic students pursuing technical diplomas at NSCC Ivany.

Funding: CAD $3,500 direct tuition reduction applied in the first academic year Deadline: April 30, 2027
Master's

Diploma in Oceans Technology Admission & Co-op Placement

Direct-entry technical diploma with mandatory paid marine industry co-op work term across the Halifax marine research corridor.

Funding: Paid industry cooperative education term (CAD $18–$24/hour) Deadline: March 31, 2027
Fellowship

Clean Energy Technology Research & Innovation Fellowship

Applied research fellowship with the NSCC Applied Research group investigating building energy performance and microgrid reliability.

Funding: CAD $16,000 research stipend and materials allowance for technical capstone projects Deadline: May 15, 2027
Master's

Diploma in Information Technology Cybersecurity Enrolment

Two-year specialized diploma preparing students for critical infrastructure security operations, network forensics, and ethical hacking.

Funding: Eligible for NSCC Rapid Response Technology Bursaries (CAD $1,500–$2,500) Deadline: March 31, 2027
Grant

Atlantic Immigrant Career Loan and Transition Award

Financial assistance program for new permanent residents and international diploma holders transitioning to credentialed maritime professions.

Funding: Up to CAD $5,000 for licensing exam fees, equipment, and professional bridging coursework Deadline: June 30, 2027 (Rolling evaluation)
Internship

Centre for Ocean Ventures & Entrepreneurship (COVE) Student Internship

Four-month paid technical internship hosted at COVE Dartmouth working alongside ocean tech startups on autonomous marine sensing.

Funding: CAD $4,800/month industry stipend plus prototype lab access Deadline: February 28, 2027
Scholarship

Women in Trades, Technology & Energy Leadership Scholarship

Dedicated scholarship program encouraging female-identifying students to excel in male-underrepresented STEM and trades diplomas.

Funding: CAD $3,000 annual scholarship and dedicated mentorship from Nova Scotia industry leaders Deadline: May 30, 2027
