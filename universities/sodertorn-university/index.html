Södertörn University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Södertörn University (Södertörns högskola), situated on the multidisciplinary Flemingsberg campus in southern Stockholm, is a dynamic Swedish public higher education institution. Internationally distinguished for the Centre for Baltic and East European Studies (CBEES), environmental governance, critical philosophy, digital media, and life sciences in partnership with Karolinska Institutet, Södertörn is a bastion of progressive research.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

PhD in Baltic and East European Studies (CBEES Doctoral Research School)

Four-year salaried doctoral appointment within CBEES, examining geopolitical transformations, democratic resilience, environmental cooperation, and memory politics across the Baltic Sea and Eastern Europe.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Environmental Governance and Sustainable Public Policy

Two-year English-taught master's degree focusing on European environmental law, climate change negotiations, biodiversity offsets, and urban sustainability governance.

Södertörn University International Student Tuition Fee Waiver

Merit-based tuition waiver awarded directly by Södertörn University to highly talented non-EU/EEA students admitted to full-time master's programs on the Stockholm Flemingsberg campus.

Environmental Science and Molecular Toxicology Research Assistantship

Research role investigating endocrine disrupting chemicals, microplastic vector toxicity, and biochemical stress markers in Baltic Sea coastal fish populations.

Media Technology & Digital Culture Stockholm Tech Hub Practicum

Five-month immersion in the Stockholm tech ecosystem analyzing algorithmic curation on social media platforms, digital labor dynamics, and public digital service accessibility.

Rein Vihalemm Postdoctoral Fellowship in Science and Technology Studies (STS)

Two-year postdoctoral position in the Department of Philosophy and History of Ideas examining the epistemic authority of climate models, algorithmic bias in governance, and scientific consensus formation.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Media Technology (Interactive Media and User Experience)

Two-year graduate program delivering advanced capabilities in interactive product design, augmented reality interfaces, usability testing, and ethical digital transformation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A bachelor's degree (equivalent to a Swedish Kandidatexamen) in a relevant humanities, social science, or environmental discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Second-cycle degree (Master's level) with at least 240 ECTS credits, an approved research thesis, and strong alignment with doctoral research schools.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B from Swedish upper secondary school, or IELTS Academic 6.5 (no section below 5.5), or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
A 4-5 page research proposal outlining research questions, theoretical framework, state of the art, empirical sources, and project timetable.
Application fee
SEK 900 application fee administered via University Admissions Sweden (antagning.se).
Required documentation
Certified bachelor's diploma, transcript of records, master's thesis, CV, letter of intent, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for non-EU/EEA international students at Södertörn University ranges from SEK 90,000 to SEK 130,000 per academic year (EU/EEA students pay zero tuition fees). International applicants are eligible for Swedish Institute (SI) Global Professionals Scholarships (covering 100% tuition plus SEK 12,000/month living stipend) and Södertörn University Tuition Fee Waivers. Doctoral candidates are fully employed on Swedish academic salaries (SEK 32,500 to 36,000/month) with comprehensive healthcare and pension.
