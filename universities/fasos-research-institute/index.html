FASoS Research Institute: European Studies, PhD Contracts & Faculty Guide

Operating within the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (FASoS) at Maastricht University—birthplace of the European Union—the FASoS Research Institute is a world-renowned European center for interdisciplinary research in European studies, science and technology studies (STS), globalization, and modern European history. Renowned for its distinctive Problem-Based Learning (PBL) pedagogy and close proximity to Brussels, FASoS hosts numerous European Research Council (ERC) and Dutch NWO research projects.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Maastricht University Holland-High Potential Global Scholarship

Prestigious full scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education and Maastricht University, covering full tuition and living expenses for outstanding non-EU students.

Master of Science in European Studies: Governance and Policy in the EU

One-year, 60-ECTS English-taught master's program analyzing EU institutional bargaining, common foreign policy, regulatory harmonization, and democratic legitimacy in Europe.

Salaried Doctoral Researcher (Promovendus) in Science, Technology & Society (STS)

Four-year salaried doctoral research employment investigating how emerging digital technologies, artificial intelligence, and biomedical innovations shape European society and public policy.

Maastricht European Borderlands and Cross-Border Governance Project

Empirical social science research initiative assessing cross-border labor mobility, emergency services integration, and environmental regulations between the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany.

Brussels European Institutions and Policy Consultancy Curricular Internship

Four-to-six-month professional internship placing graduate students within European Commission directorates-general, European Parliament offices, and international NGOs in Brussels.

FASoS Modern European History and Heritage Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment exploring contested historical narratives, European memory politics, and digital cultural heritage archiving in the European Union.

Assistant Professor (Universitair Docent) in European Public Policy and Governance

Tenure-track faculty position teaching Problem-Based Learning (PBL) seminars in European public policy, conducting high-impact empirical research, and supervising master's and doctoral theses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Arts or Bachelor of Science degree in European Studies, Political Science, History, Sociology, or International Relations with strong academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed Master of Arts or Master of Science degree with high honors (minimum grade 7.5/10 on the Dutch scale) in a relevant discipline, alongside a comprehensive doctoral research proposal.
Language proficiency
Proof of English proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in writing) or TOEFL iBT 90 (writing minimum 22); Dutch not required for English-track research programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Application submitted via Studielink and the Maastricht University portal including certified transcripts, motivation letter, CV, and scholarly writing sample.
Application fee
€100 non-refundable application handling fee for applicants with non-Dutch prior qualifications.
Required documentation
Bachelor's/Master's graduation diplomas, complete official transcripts of all study years, certified English translations, language certificates, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA, Swiss, and Surinamese citizens is the statutory Dutch tuition rate of approximately €2,530 per academic year. For non-EU/EEA international students, master's tuition ranges from €14,000 to €16,500 per academic year. Salaried doctoral researchers (Promovendi) are hired as university staff members under the Dutch CAO NU collective agreement (€2,770 to €3,539 per month) with zero tuition fees.
