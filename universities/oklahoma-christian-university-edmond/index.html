Oklahoma Christian University

Oklahoma Christian · Edmond, Oklahoma, United States

Oklahoma Christian University is a dynamic private Christian university situated on a scenic 200-acre campus in Edmond, Oklahoma. Widely celebrated for ABET-accredited engineering, computer science with gaming and animation, nursing, and business analytics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OC is home to the Herold Science Hall, the Prince Engineering Center with advanced robotics and wind tunnel testing facilities, and the top-tier Gaming and Animation Lab. OC students have built an international reputation in software engineering competitions and collegiate esports.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions consider secondary school GPA (minimum 2.75 recommended) with test-optional paths. International applicants submit TOEFL (79+), IELTS (6.5+), or Duolingo (105+). Graduate programs require a recognized bachelor's degree with minimum 3.0 GPA.
