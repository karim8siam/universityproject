The University of Tennessee at Chattanooga

UTC · Chattanooga, Tennessee, United States

The University of Tennessee at Chattanooga (UTC) is a vibrant public Carnegie R2 metropolitan doctoral institution situated in downtown Chattanooga, Tennessee. Acclaimed for its National Center for Computational Engineering (SimCenter), the College of Engineering and Computer Science, and pioneering research on EPB's commercial quantum networking testbed, UTC drives regional technological and industrial innovation.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UTC is organized into five academic colleges: Arts and Sciences, Engineering and Computer Science, the Gary W. Rollins College of Business, Health, Education and Professional Studies, and the Honors College. Flagship research centers include the SimCenter (supercomputing modeling and CFD), the Center for Urban Informatics and Progress (CUIP), and the Applied Infrastructure Academy.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.85 with 20 ACT, or 3.0+ test-optional). Graduate admissions require an earned bachelor's degree with a 2.70+ cumulative GPA (3.0+ for doctoral tracks), official transcripts, letters of recommendation, and program-specific standardized test scores.
