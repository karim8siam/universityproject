Academic overview & research priorities

Located in the vibrant Presikhaaf district of Arnhem in Gelderland, Presikhaaf University functions as an innovative open community college and applied educational research center. Partnering with the Municipality of Arnhem, HAN University of Applied Sciences, and regional social housing cooperatives, the institution delivers accredited diplomas in community youth work, urban agriculture, social entrepreneurship, and digital literacy for multicultural neighborhoods.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission is open to individuals with secondary school completion (VMBO/HAVO/MBO) or mature adult learners qualifying through experiential learning portfolios. International fellows participate in community regeneration field courses with English proficiency at CEFR B2.
