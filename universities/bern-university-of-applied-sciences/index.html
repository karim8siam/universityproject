Bern University of Applied Sciences: Wood Engineering, Medical Informatics & Automotive Guide

Bern University of Applied Sciences (BFH - Berner Fachhochschule) is a premier Swiss public university located in the bilingual canton of Bern, internationally celebrated for Europe's leading Institute for Wood Engineering in Biel, digital health and medical informatics, cybersecurity, and automotive engineering.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Swiss Confederation Excellence Scholarship & Research Fellowship at BFH

Federal scholarship granted by the Swiss Confederation for international scientists conducting cooperative doctoral or postdoctoral investigations in engineering or forestry at BFH.

Master of Science in Wood Technology & Timber Engineering (Biel Campus)

Europe's foremost master's program in timber engineering and bio-based composites, based at the Biel campus, exploring multi-story timber buildings, adhesive chemistry, and robotic CNC joinery.

SNSF Cooperative Doctoral Position in Medical Informatics & Clinical AI Diagnostics

Four-year cooperative doctoral fellowship conducted jointly with University of Bern Medical School, developing federated machine learning algorithms for multi-hospital EHR data privacy.

Swiss Innosuisse Autonomous Electric Mountain Transit & Battery Pack Project

Applied automotive engineering study at the Biel Vehicle Technology Laboratory, developing thermal runaway mitigation and regenerative braking for heavy electric buses operating on steep Alpine roads.

Swiss MedTech & Precision Watchmaking Micro-Manufacturing Practicum

Six-month professional engineering placement embedded within world-famous precision watchmakers (Swatch Group, Rolex) or surgical instrument manufacturers in the Jura arc region.

BFH Institute for Human Centered Engineering Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Institute for Human Centered Engineering (HuCE) developing non-invasive optical sensor implants, retinal prosthetics, and robotic assistive exoskeletons.

Professor of High-Performance Timber Construction & Structural Mechanics

Permanent professorship at the Institute for Timber Construction, Structures and Architecture in Biel, leading applied research in tall timber buildings, structural glulam adhesives, and seismic resistance.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree from a recognized university or university of applied sciences in engineering, computer science, or forestry/wood science with above-average grades.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (M.Sc., M.A.) with high standing; doctoral degrees are conferred cooperatively with Swiss universities (Uni Bern, ETH Zurich, EPFL).
Language proficiency
German C1 or French C1 for bilingual programs; English C1 (IELTS 7.0 or Cambridge English Advanced) for international Master of Science tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, detailed motivation statement, and portfolio of technical engineering projects.
Application fee
CHF 100 administrative application registration fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of grades with ECTS credits, CV, passport copy, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for Swiss and EU/EEA students is CHF 750 per semester (CHF 1,500 / ~$1,720 USD per year). Tuition for non-EU international students is CHF 950 per semester. Appointed doctoral candidates and scientific collaborators hold salaried Swiss federal employment contracts starting at CHF 60,000.
