Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Northwest Missouri State is home to the Agricultural Learning Center at the R.T. Wright Farm, the Dean L. Hubbard Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, and the Mozingo Outdoor Education Recreation Point. All full-time students receive laptop and textbook rental included with tuition.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation with a minimum 2.0 unweighted GPA and test-optional holistic review. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75 minimum GPA and program-specific prerequisites.
