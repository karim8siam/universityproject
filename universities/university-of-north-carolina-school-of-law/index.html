University of North Carolina School of Law

UNC Law · Chapel Hill, North Carolina, United States

The University of North Carolina School of Law at Chapel Hill is the state's oldest professional school and one of the premier public law schools in the nation. Renowned for excellence in constitutional jurisprudence, legal writing, banking law, and environmental advocacy, Carolina Law trains ethical attorneys and federal jurists through eleven rigorous clinical law programs and influential legal journals.
