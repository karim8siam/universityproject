Carleton University

Established in 1942 in Canada's national capital city, Carleton University is an internationally recognized comprehensive research institution renowned for public policy, international affairs (the prestigious Norman Paterson School of International Affairs), aerospace engineering, journalism, cybersecurity, and cognitive science. Situated adjacent to the National Research Council (NRC), government scientific laboratories, and Canada's largest technology park in Kanata North, Carleton offers exceptional graduate funding opportunities, interdisciplinary research centers, and global scholarly impact.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Carleton Departmental Graduate Scholarship & Teaching Assistantship Package

Carleton's cornerstone merit-based graduate funding package providing guaranteed multi-year living stipends, teaching assistantships, and tuition offsets for admitted thesis-stream graduate researchers.

Carleton PhD in Computer Science & Cybersecurity (OCICS)

Offered jointly through the Ottawa-Carleton Institute for Computer Science (OCICS), offering world-class research in cybersecurity, machine learning, distributed systems, algorithms, and human-computer interaction.

Norman Paterson School of International Affairs (NPSIA) Master of Arts in International Affairs

Canada's foremost graduate program in international affairs, preparing future diplomats, intelligence analysts, and global policy scholars in security, global political economy, and international conflict resolution.

Carleton Master of Applied Science (MASc) in Aerospace Engineering

Rigorous thesis-based MASc in aerospace engineering leveraging Carleton's wind tunnel facilities, flight simulators, and gas turbine research laboratories.

Carleton Centre for Advanced Studies in Visualization and Data Analytics (C-ASVDA) Research Fellowship

Pre-doctoral and doctoral research fellowship focused on immersive visual analytics, explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), and big data modeling in partnership with national science institutions.

Ottawa-Carleton Institute for Biology Summer Research Fellowship

Intensive 16-week mentored summer research traineeship investigating stress physiology, neurobiology, and ecosystem conservation in state-of-the-art laboratory facilities.

Carleton Postdoctoral Fellow in Wireless Networking & 6G Systems

Full-time academic research appointment investigating high-altitude platform stations (HAPS), cell-free massive MIMO, and intelligent wireless surfaces for beyond-5G/6G communication systems.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Honours Bachelor's degree from a recognized university with minimum B+ standing (77-79% or 3.3/4.0 GPA) in the final two years of study; competitive STEM applicants generally present an A- (80%+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree in a relevant field with a minimum A- average (80% or 3.7/4.0 GPA) and demonstrable thesis research capacity.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 20 in reading and listening, 22 in speaking and writing); competitive computer science and international affairs require IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 100.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-page detailed research statement detailing prospective research aims, methodological framework, and confirmed alignment with Carleton faculty supervisors.
Application fee
CAD $110 non-refundable application fee submitted through the Carleton Central graduate admissions system.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, 2 to 3 academic reference letters, and statement of research intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate research tuition at Carleton is significantly offset by guaranteed departmental funding packages. Full-time doctoral candidates in good standing receive guaranteed funding packages averaging CAD $22,000 to $28,000 per year for four years (combining teaching assistantships, research assistantships, and Carleton departmental scholarships) plus international doctoral tuition differential bursaries.
