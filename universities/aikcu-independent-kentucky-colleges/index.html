Association of Independent Kentucky Colleges and Universities

The Association of Independent Kentucky Colleges and Universities (AIKCU) represents 18 non-profit higher education institutions across the Commonwealth of Kentucky, administering statewide undergraduate scholarship endowments, private college access initiatives, and collaborative STEM research grants.

Academic overview & research priorities

AIKCU champions academic excellence, affordability, and cooperative collegiate programs across member campuses including Berea College, Centre College, Bellarmine University, and Transylvania University. The consortium coordinates specialized workforce fellowships, nursing access initiatives, and Appalachian regional development studies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Centralized scholarship applications and student transfer programs are open to matriculated students at AIKCU member institutions. Prospective undergraduate candidates apply directly through individual member colleges with competitive high school GPA benchmarks and FAFSA completion.
