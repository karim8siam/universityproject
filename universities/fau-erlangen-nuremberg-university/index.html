University of Erlangen-Nuremberg

Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU) is one of Germany's largest and most innovative research universities, internationally renowned for materials science, artificial intelligence, medical engineering, and the Erlangen Center for Astroparticle Physics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1743 in northern Bavaria across Erlangen and Nuremberg, FAU comprises five major faculties: Faculty of Engineering, Faculty of Sciences, Faculty of Medicine, Faculty of Humanities & Theology, and the School of Business, Economics & Society. FAU is anchored in the 'Medical Valley' high-tech innovation cluster, operating deep research alignments with Siemens Healthineers, Fraunhofer (IIS, birthplace of MP3), and Max Planck.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor's admission requires the German Allgemeine Hochschulreife, Fachhochschulreife, or international equivalent validated via uni-assist. Master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in a congruent STEM, medical, or humanities field with minimum German grade 2.5. English-taught programs require IELTS 6.5-7.0 or TOEFL iBT 90+; German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4x4. PhD candidates apply directly to faculty research chairs.
