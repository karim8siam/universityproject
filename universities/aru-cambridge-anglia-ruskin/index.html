Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:1 or recognized international equivalent undergraduate degree in relevant discipline; 2:2 considered with relevant work experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree with merit or distinction, detailed research proposal (1,500-2,000 words), and supervisor alignment.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 or 6.0 in each sub-test depending on program).
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applicants require a structured research methodology and literature review proposal.
Application fee
Direct online application via ARU portal is free of charge; undergraduate applications via UCAS (£28.50).
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, complete academic transcripts, passport copy, personal statement, and two academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

UK undergraduate tuition is £9,250 per year. International undergraduate tuition ranges from £15,900 to £19,200 annually. Postgraduate international tuition is £16,500 to £20,500. International Merit Scholarships, Vice Chancellor's awards, and UKRI research council studentships are available.
