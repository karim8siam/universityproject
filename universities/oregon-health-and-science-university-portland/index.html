Oregon Health & Science University

OHSU · Portland, Oregon, United States

Oregon Health & Science University is Oregon's only public academic health center, located atop Marquam Hill in Portland, Oregon. World-renowned for cancer research at the Knight Cancer Institute, neurosciences at the Vollum Institute, biomedical informatics, and retinal gene therapy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OHSU encompasses the School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Dentistry, OHSU-PSU School of Public Health, the Knight Cancer Institute, and the Oregon National Primate Research Center. The campus is connected to Portland's South Waterfront biotechnology hub by the Portland Aerial Tram.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly competitive. MD applicants must complete AMCAS applications, MCAT examination, and prerequisite laboratory sciences. Graduate programs (Ph.D., M.S.) in biomedical sciences require competitive undergraduate research backgrounds, GRE (where applicable), and faculty interviews.
