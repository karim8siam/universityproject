University of North Florida

UNF · Jacksonville, Florida, United States

The University of North Florida (UNF) is a nationally ranked public doctoral university nestled on a pristine 1,381-acre wildlife sanctuary campus in coastal Jacksonville, Florida. Renowned for coastal and marine biology at the Coastal and Marine Biology Flagship Program, transportation and logistics, mechanical engineering, and nursing, UNF blends rigorous academics with vibrant First Coast coastal ecosystems.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNF comprises six academic colleges: the College of Arts and Sciences, Coggin College of Business, Brooks College of Health, College of Computing, Engineering and Construction (CCEC), College of Education and Human Services, and the Hicks Honors College. The university operates the Taylor Engineering Research Institute and the Sawmill Slough Preserve.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and standardized test scores (SAT/ACT required by Florida law). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
