University Medical Center Groningen (UMCG)

University Medical Center Groningen (UMCG), affiliated with the University of Groningen, is one of the largest academic hospitals in Europe, world-renowned for its "Healthy Ageing" focus, the Lifelines cohort biobank, solid organ transplantation, and proton beam therapy.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Tracing its medical roots back to the founding of the University of Groningen in 1614, UMCG is an internationally celebrated medical research citadel employing over 13,000 staff, educating nearly 4,000 medical and biomedical students, and treating more than 800,000 patients annually. UMCG is designated as the Dutch national referral center for multiorgan transplantation and home to the Lifelines Cohort Study—a premier three-generation prospective biobank monitoring 167,000 participants over 30 years. UMCG operates Europe’s first pencil-beam scanning proton therapy center and spearheads global breakthroughs in mitochondrial medicine, immunogenetics, and oncological radiobiology.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Medical Sciences (Graduate School of Medical Sciences - GSMS) (Doctorate) — Elite four-year doctoral research curriculum in immunology, oncology, neuroscience, or cardiology within high-impact UMCG research institutes.
  • Bachelor of Science in Medicine (Global Health Profile - English-Taught) (Bachelor's) — One of the few English-taught medical bachelor degrees in the European Union, featuring problem-based learning, patient simulation, and clinical clerkships.
  • Master of Science in Clinical and Psychosocial Epidemiology (Research Master) (Master's) — NVAO-accredited top-rated research master using big data from the Lifelines biobank to study chronic disease etiology.
  • Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences (Biology of Ageing) (Master's) — Two-year research master exploring cellular senescence, telomere dynamics, metabolic reprogramming, and regenerative organ repair.
