Universitätsklinikum Brandenburg an der Havel

UKB MHB · Brandenburg an der Havel, Brandenburg, Germany

Universitätsklinikum Brandenburg an der Havel (UKB) is a premier university hospital and the primary clinical teaching campus for the Medizinische Hochschule Brandenburg Theodor Fontane (MHB).

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UKB operates state-of-the-art clinical centers in oncology, neurosurgery, cardiovascular medicine, and rural community health. Its academic research focuses on patient-centered clinical trials, telemedicine in underserved rural regions, and molecular pathology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires enrollment in the MHB Brandenburg human medicine or psychology curriculum, completed state medical examinations (Staatsexamen), or a recognized Master's/MD in clinical sciences.
