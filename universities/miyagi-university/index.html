Institutional Overview

Miyagi University (Miyagi Daigaku / MYU), established in 1997 with campuses in Taiwa (Kurokawa District) and Nagamachi (Sendai), is a forward-thinking public prefectural university in the Tohoku region. Renowned for its School of Nursing, School of Project Design (integrating business management, IT, and regional architectural planning), and School of Food, Agricultural and Environmental Sciences, MYU is a recognized leader in disaster recovery, sustainable agri-food systems, and regional revitalization.
