Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1875 as a Soto Zen Buddhist academy, Tohoku Fukushi University is celebrated nationwide for establishing Japan's modern welfare and caregiving paradigms. Across its Kunimi and Sendai Station campuses, TFU operates specialized research centers including the Research Institute of Social Welfare, the Kansei Fukushi Research Institute, and affiliated healthcare clinics, cultivating professionals dedicated to human well-being and social equity.

Research admissions & international criteria

Applicants to undergraduate programs must hold a secondary school diploma and demonstrate Japanese proficiency (JLPT N2 or N1). Graduate degree programs in Comprehensive Welfare and Clinical Psychology require a bachelor's degree, submission of an academic research proposal, entrance examinations in specialized welfare theory, and an oral interview. Scholarships include TFU international tuition exemptions and Soto Buddhist scholarship awards.
