Louisiana State University

LSU · Baton Rouge, Louisiana, United States

Louisiana State University is the flagship public Carnegie R1 research university of Louisiana and the LSU System, located in Baton Rouge. Renowned for petroleum engineering, coastal wetlands ecology, agricultural sciences, and physics at the LIGO Livingston observatory.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

LSU houses the Craft & Hawkins Department of Petroleum Engineering, College of Agriculture, and the Center for River Studies. Key research strengths include hurricane storm surge modeling, carbon capture and underground sequestration, and deepwater drilling safety.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core GPA (minimum 3.0 recommended), academic rigor, and test-optional review. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree, 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and department-specific GRE or portfolio requirements.
