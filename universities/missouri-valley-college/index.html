Missouri Valley College

Missouri Valley · Marshall, Missouri, United States

Missouri Valley College (MVC) is an independent four-year liberal arts college established in 1889, situated on a 150-acre campus in Marshall, Missouri, in the heart of the Missouri River agricultural basin. Celebrated for agricultural business, nursing and health sciences, biology, criminal justice, and intercollegiate athletics, MVC serves a vibrant international student body drawn from over 40 countries worldwide.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

MVC offers undergraduate and graduate programs through five divisions: the Division of Agribusiness, Division of Business, Division of Nursing and Health Sciences, Division of Math and Sciences, and the Division of Education. The university operates the Malcolm Center for Student Life and the Burns Athletic Complex.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, secondary school preparation, and personal statements with test-optional reviews. International undergraduate merit scholarships covering $8,000 to $16,000/year are automatically awarded based on academic transcripts and athletic/artistic talent.
