Academic overview & excellence

Nestled between Mount Elbert and Mount Massive in the high Colorado Rockies, CMC Leadville delivers world-class bachelor's and associate degrees uniquely integrated with the alpine environment. Home to the historic Timberline Campus, the college houses the National Avalanche Center partnerships, the natural resource management field station, and the nationally acclaimed Ski Area Operations (SAO) academy. Students study real-time climate dynamics, sub-alpine watershed ecology, and mountain rescue logistics.

Research admissions & criteria

CMC Leadville offers rolling admissions with open access for high school graduates and transfer students. Bachelor of Science in Ecosystem Science & Stewardship candidates require high school or college biology and algebra with a 2.5 minimum GPA. International students must present certified high school or college transcripts, evidence of English proficiency (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 61), and demonstrate financial self-sufficiency for F-1 visa issuance.
