NewU University

NewU · Washington, District of Columbia, United States

NewU University is an innovative private, non-profit university in downtown Washington, D.C. Pioneers an accelerated 3-year bachelor's degree model focused on high-demand disciplines in computer science, data science, entrepreneurship, and psychology.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NewU operates schools of Computer Science, Business & Entrepreneurship, and Psychology. Its curriculum eliminates traditional general education bloat, focusing strictly on experiential project-based learning and corporate tech apprenticeships in the DC metro area.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions evaluate high school academic records (minimum 2.5 unweighted GPA) or college transfer credits. Standardized tests (SAT/ACT) are entirely test-blind, with applications judged on intellectual passion and interview.
