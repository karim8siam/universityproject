Samford University

Samford University · Birmingham, Alabama, United States

Samford University is Alabama's top-ranked private Christian university, situated on a majestic 247-acre Georgian-colonial campus in suburban Birmingham, Alabama. Internationally celebrated for the McWhorter School of Pharmacy, Cumberland School of Law, Ida Moffett School of Nursing, and Brock School of Business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Samford features the College of Health Sciences (uniting nursing, pharmacy, health professions, and public health on a state-of-the-art 223,000 sq ft campus), the historic Cumberland School of Law (one of the oldest law schools in the nation), and the Brock School of Business. It is consistently ranked #1 in Alabama for undergraduate teaching.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.85), coursework rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Professional programs (PharmD, JD, DNP, PA) require specialized centralized applications (PharmCAS, LSAT/CAS, CASPA) and competitive interview processes.
