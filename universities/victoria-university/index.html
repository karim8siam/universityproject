Victoria University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Victoria University (VU) is a dual-sector university based in Melbourne, Australia, internationally celebrated for its award-winning VU Block Model of focused single-subject learning, world-top-10 sports science research at the Institute for Health and Sport (IHES), and innovative sustainable water technologies at the Institute for Sustainable Industries & Liveable Cities (ISILC).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Exercise and Sport Science (Biomechanics & High-Performance Athletic Training)

Doctoral research candidacy hosted within the Institute for Health and Sport (IHES), investigating neuromuscular fatigue, wearable kinematics sensor analytics, or elite athletic recovery physiology.

Master of Engineering (Civil & Environmental Water Resource Systems)

Engineers Australia-accredited master's program taught via the VU Block Model, covering advanced hydrology, municipal stormwater harvesting, membrane water treatment, and resilient urban infrastructure design.

Victoria University International Postgraduate Research Scholarship (VU-IPRS)

Competitive university-funded doctoral scholarship open to exceptional international students pursuing doctoral degrees in applied artificial intelligence, clean energy, cyber security, or public health.

Institute for Sustainable Industries & Liveable Cities (ISILC) Clean Water Separation Technologies Grant

Applied research project at ISILC developing low-energy polymeric and ceramic membrane distillation prototypes to purify industrial wastewater and recover valuable minerals from saline brine.

VU Block Model Applied Machine Learning & Cyber Security Industry Internship

Six-month immersive workplace placement placing VU postgraduate students in Melbourne financial, governmental, and cybersecurity organizations deploying real-time threat intelligence and AI pipelines.

Sir Zelman Cowen Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Inclusive Education and Social Cohesion

prestigious two-year postdoctoral fellowship conducting participatory research on educational access, migrant integration, and policy interventions across Melbourne's diverse western suburbs.

Master of Applied Information Technology and Enterprise Analytics

Comprehensive two-year postgraduate degree delivering specialized competencies in statistical learning, cloud databases, predictive enterprise analytics, and machine learning operational deployment.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
An Australian bachelor's degree or recognized equivalent with a minimum weighted average mark (WAM) of 60% or relevant work experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year bachelor's degree with First Class Honours, or master's degree with a significant research thesis (at least 25% research component) and high distinction.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no individual band score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing 21, speaking 18, reading 13, listening 12).
Research statement / Proposal
A comprehensive 2,000-word research proposal including background context, research questions, methodologies, project timeline, and ethics considerations.
Application fee
AUD $0 online application fee for international direct applicants.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree awards, research proposal, CV, two academic referee reports, and supervisor pre-endorsement form.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition at Victoria University ranges from AUD $28,000 to $36,000 per year. Fully funded doctoral candidates receive Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) or VU International Postgraduate Research Scholarships (VU-IPRS) providing an annual tax-free living allowance of AUD $34,400 plus 100% tuition fee offsets and health cover.
