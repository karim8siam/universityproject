Kyoto Prefectural University of Medicine

Kyoto Prefectural University of Medicine (KPUM), situated on the banks of the Kamo River, is one of Japan's oldest and most prestigious medical colleges (founded 1872), pioneering cutting-edge discoveries in genomic oncology, cardiovascular surgery, and regenerative ophthalmology.

Academic overview & excellence

KPUM operates the University Hospital and the Children's Medical Center, delivering world-class medical training and biomedical research. It maintains joint laboratories with Kyoto University's iPS cell center (CiRA) and the RIKEN Center for Integrative Medical Sciences.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to the School of Medicine is among the most competitive in Japan, requiring the National Common Test and secondary medical entrance examinations including clinical interviews. Graduate School of Medical Science (Doctor of Medical Science / Ph.D.) requires an accredited M.D. or 6-year bachelor's degree in life sciences.
