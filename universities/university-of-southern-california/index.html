University of Southern California

USC · Los Angeles, California, United States

The University of Southern California (USC) is one of the world's leading private research universities and a distinguished member of the Association of American Universities (AAU). Located in the heart of Los Angeles, USC is world-famous for cinematic arts at the USC School of Cinematic Arts, engineering and AI at the Viterbi School of Engineering, biomedical breakthroughs at the Keck School of Medicine, and international business at the Marshall School.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

USC spans 23 schools and units including the Viterbi School of Engineering, School of Cinematic Arts, Keck School of Medicine, Marshall School of Business, Gould School of Law, and the Annenberg School for Communication and Journalism. Leading research institutes include the Information Sciences Institute (ISI)—where the Internet's domain name system (DNS) was invented—and the Institute for Creative Technologies (ICT).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are among the most selective globally. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a high GPA (typically 3.5+), statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 100+ or IELTS 7.5+).
