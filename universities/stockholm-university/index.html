Stockholm University (Stockholms universitet)

Established in 1878, Stockholm University is one of Europe's leading centers for higher education and research, set within the world's first national city park in Frescati. Associated with 7 Nobel laureates (including Svante Arrhenius and Paul Crutzen), Stockholm University leads globally in Environmental Science at the Stockholm Resilience Centre (originator of the Planetary Boundaries framework), Computer and Systems Sciences in Kista (DSV), and Theoretical Physics at the Oskar Klein Centre. Stockholm University Scholarship Scheme for International Master's Students

Stockholm University's premier merit-based scholarship covering full tuition fees for outstanding non-EU/EEA international students admitted to two-year Master's degree programs at Stockholm University.

Stockholm University Salaried Doctoral Employment (Doktorandanställning)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointments across Stockholm University research departments, combining cutting-edge scientific investigation with teaching duties under standard Swedish employment contracts and comprehensive health coverage.

Stockholm University Master of Science in Computer and Systems Sciences

Two-year English-taught Master's program (120 ECTS) based at the Department of Computer and Systems Sciences (DSV) in Kista, covering Artificial Intelligence, Information Security, Data Science, and Software Quality.

Stockholm University Master of Science in Theoretical Physics

Interdisciplinary two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) inside the Oskar Klein Centre at AlbaNova, offering world-class research tracks in General Relativity, String Theory, Quantum Information, and Particle Cosmology.

Stockholm Resilience Centre (SRC) Research Placements

World-famous sustainability research institute based at Stockholm University, advancing cross-disciplinary investigations into planetary boundaries, biosphere stewardship, and human resilience under climate disruption.

Stockholm Summer Research Placements inside Oskar Klein Centre

Two to six-month structured research traineeships inside the Oskar Klein Centre for Cosmoparticle Physics, collaborating on dark matter searches, IceCube neutrino data, and gravitational wave theory.

Stockholm Teaching Assistantships & Laboratory Exercise Mentoring

Campus employment positions (övningsassistenter / labbassistenter) enabling graduate students to tutor weekly recitation sessions, mentor programming workshops, and assist in grading laboratory exams.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science, Systems Science, Physics, or related quantitative field (minimum 180 ECTS credits), with a completed undergraduate thesis project.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed second-cycle Master's degree (minimum 120 ECTS) in relevant discipline satisfying Swedish third-cycle admission criteria, with demonstrable scientific publication or thesis excellence.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper secondary school English 6: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (writing minimum 20).
Research statement / Proposal
For Master's: statement of purpose and academic transcripts via University Admissions Sweden. For PhD: comprehensive research statement matching specific advertised doctoral vacancies.
Application fee
SEK 900 centralized application fee for non-EU/EEA students applying through University Admissions Sweden (waived for EU/EEA citizens).
Required documentation
Degree certificate, complete official transcripts with grading rubric, English proficiency test score, and copy of valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Swedish higher education regulations, EU/EEA and Swiss citizens study completely tuition-free. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition for master's programs is SEK 67,500 to SEK 75,000 per semester (approx. SEK 270,000 to SEK 300,000 for a two-year 120 ECTS program). The Stockholm University Scholarship Scheme covers 100% of tuition fees for outstanding admitted Master's candidates. Doctoral candidates hold salaried employment contracts and study tuition-free.
