Academic overview & excellence

Located in the historic educational hub of Utrecht, Studium Utrecht Academy delivers flexible associate diplomas, higher vocational certificates (HBO level), and teacher training qualifications. The academy specializes in innovative pedagogy, personalized remedial learning technologies, adult cognitive transitions, and applied behavioral educational support.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires a completed Dutch HAVO or VWO secondary diploma, MBO level 4 diploma, or international equivalent. Adult learners aged 21 and older without standard qualifications may gain entry via the 21+ Colloquium Doctum entrance examination. Fluency in Dutch (NT2 level B2) or English is required depending on the course medium.

Verified opportunities & funding programs (7)

Bachelor's

Higher Vocational Diploma in Remedial Pedagogy and Special Educational Needs

Four-year accredited HBO diploma training educational specialists to diagnose learning disabilities (dyslexia, dyscalculia), design individualized learning plans, and support neurodivergent youth.

Funding: Dutch DUO student finance eligible and Studium academic excellence bursaries up to €1,500 Tuition: €2,530 annual statutory tuition (EU) / €7,800 institutional fee Deadline: April 15, 2027
Master's

Master of Education in Adult Learning Dynamics and Lifelong Curriculum

Two-year part-time master's program exploring andragogy, cognitive flexibility in mature learners, digital curriculum design, and organizational learning cultures.

Funding: €2,000 Dutch Teacher Grant (Lerarenbeurs) from the Ministry of Education Tuition: €3,400 per academic year Deadline: May 15, 2027
Certificate

Certificate in Digital Learning Analytics and Adaptive Educational Tech

Post-graduate certificate examining student engagement telemetry, adaptive LMS algorithmic pipelines, and privacy ethics under European AI and GDPR regulations.

Funding: €1,000 STAP-equivalent lifelong learning voucher support Tuition: €1,750 modular tuition Deadline: March 31, 2027
Fellowship

Applied Research Fellowship in Neurodiversity-Affirming Classroom Practices

Investigates sensory classroom design, executive function coaching, and reduced-anxiety testing environments for autistic and ADHD secondary school students.

Funding: €5,000 research fellowship funded by Dutch Educational Research Consortium Tuition: Tuition fully covered Deadline: June 1, 2027
Certificate

Certificate in Academic Coaching and Student Mental Well-Being

Four-month practical training for high school and university student advisors on motivational interviewing, burn-out prevention, and study habit scaffolding.

Funding: Regional school board professional development subsidies Tuition: €1,450 course fee Deadline: July 15, 2027
Scholarship

Bursary for Second-Chance Higher Education Seekers (21+ Pathway)

Supports mature adult learners without standard diplomas who pass the Colloquium Doctum to enter higher vocational diploma tracks.

Funding: €1,200 direct tuition reduction and free preparatory tutoring for entrance exams Tuition: Subsidized through bursary Deadline: May 30, 2027
Grant

Educational Innovation and Game-Based Learning Research Grant

Funds the creation of pedagogical game mechanics that reinforce high school history, mathematics, and scientific inquiry through cooperative play.

Funding: €8,000 project grant for prototyping serious educational board games and digital simulations Tuition: Not applicable Deadline: August 31, 2027
