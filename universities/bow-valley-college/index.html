Bow Valley College: Software Development, Digital Health & Data Analytics Guide

Established in 1965 in the heart of downtown Calgary, Bow Valley College is Alberta's premier comprehensive community college, educating over 17,000 students annually across creative technologies, health sciences, business administration, and applied research. Bow Valley's Centre for Entertainment Arts and Iniikokmaan Indigenous Centre pioneer industry-aligned post-diploma credentials and software development apprenticeships.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited bachelor's degree or two-year post-secondary diploma with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent) for advanced post-diploma certificates.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; comprehensive college institution prioritizing post-graduate certificates, applied research, and technology apprenticeships.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band), TOEFL iBT 83, or Pearson PTE 59.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for applied research bursaries: 500-word statement outlining experience in software engineering, cloud architecture, or health data informatics.
Application fee
$140 CAD non-refundable application fee for international students ($43 CAD for domestic Alberta applicants).
Required documentation
Certified official academic transcripts with certified English translations, copy of passport, proof of English language proficiency, and resume.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Bow Valley College averages $6,200 to $8,400 CAD annually for Canadian domestic students and approximately $17,500 to $21,000 CAD per year for international candidates. Comprehensive entrance scholarships, Alberta Student Aid, and applied research bursaries up to $5,000 CAD provide generous assistance.
