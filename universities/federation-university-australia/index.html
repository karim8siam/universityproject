Federation University Australia: Mining, AI & Australian RTP Scholarships

Federation University Australia (FedUni) is one of Australia's premier regional dual-sector universities, with historic roots in the Ballarat School of Mines established in 1870. Famous for hosting the Ballarat Technology Park with IBM and global enterprise partners, FedUni delivers world-class research in artificial intelligence, carbon transition, mining geotechnical engineering, and nursing.

Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Stipend at Federation

Prestigious Commonwealth scholarship funding exceptional domestic and international doctoral scholars conducting research across Federation University institutes.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Applied Cyber Security & Threat Intelligence

Doctoral research conducted within the Centre for Informatics and Applied Optimization (CIAO), investigating intrusion detection, malware telemetry, and cloud security architectures.

Master of Engineering Technology (Renewable Energy & Electrical Power)

Engineers Australia-accredited master's program specializing in grid integration of wind and solar farms, power storage systems, and regional microgrid control.

Future Regions Research Centre (FRRC) Carbon Transition Assistantship

Applied environmental research analyzing brown coal mine rehabilitation, landform stability, and renewable energy economic transitions in Victoria's Latrobe Valley.

Ballarat Technology Park IBM Co-operative Computing Practicum

Six-month professional cooperative education placement embedded at IBM within Ballarat Technology Park, engineering enterprise cloud services and cybersecurity tools.

Federation Postdoctoral Fellowship in Geotechnical Engineering & Mine Safety

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship developing numerical simulation models and seismic sensor networks for open-pit and underground mine slope stability.

Master of Health (Health Informatics & Digital Health Leadership)

Postgraduate degree focusing on electronic medical record implementation, telehealth architectures, clinical data governance, and predictive healthcare analytics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed recognized bachelor's degree from an Australian university or international equivalent with a minimum credit average (65%+ or GPA 4.5/7.0).
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-Class Honours (H1) or research master's degree with a demonstrated track record of scholarly peer-reviewed publications.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands), PTE Academic 58+, or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing minimum 21); Health disciplines require 7.0 overall.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for Higher Degree by Research (PhD/MPhil): 2,000-word research proposal aligned with an active Federation University research center.
Application fee
AUD $0 when applying directly online via the Federation University portal.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree awards, research proposal, CV, English language test certificate, and two referee reports.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for international graduate students ranges from AUD $27,000 to $33,500 per year. The Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) provides full tuition fee offsets and annual living stipends of AUD $35,000 for domestic and international PhD candidates.
