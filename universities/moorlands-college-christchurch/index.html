Institution Overview & Profile

Moorlands College, founded in 1948 in Christchurch, Dorset (with regional centres in Devon and the Midlands), is an accredited UK higher education institution celebrated for its applied theology, Christian leadership, community youth work, and intercultural mission degrees, validated by the University of Gloucestershire and recognized by the UK Quality Assurance Agency (QAA).

Institution TypeAccredited Theological Higher Education College
Main CampusChristchurch Campus (Dorset) & Regional Centres in Devon and the Midlands
AccreditationValidated by University of Gloucestershire / Reviewed by QAA / Registered with OfS
Admissions RouteDirect online application & interview process (UCAS code: M85)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS Academic 6.0 for BA, 6.5 for MA with minimum 6.0 in writing)
Financial Aid & GrantsMoorlands College Bursary Fund & UK Student Finance (Tuition & Maintenance Loans)

Key Academic Strengths

  • All undergraduate and postgraduate degrees fully validated by the University of Gloucestershire
  • Over 75 years of specialized training in applied theology, pastoral counseling, and youth mission
  • Unique placement-based study tracks combining classroom theology with weekly church or NGO field practice
  • High student satisfaction ratings in the UK National Student Survey (NSS)
