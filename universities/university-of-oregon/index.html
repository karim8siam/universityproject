University of Oregon

The University of Oregon is the flagship public research university of the state of Oregon, renowned for the Phil and Penny Knight Campus for Accelerating Scientific Impact, sustainable architecture, marine biology, and quantum physics.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1876 in Eugene along the scenic Willamette River, the University of Oregon is a prestigious Carnegie R1 (Very High Research Activity) institution and an elected member of the Association of American Universities (AAU). UO enrolls over 23,000 students across nine colleges and schools. The university is home to world-class scientific facilities including the USD $1 billion Knight Campus for Accelerating Scientific Impact, the Oregon Institute of Marine Biology (OIMB) in Charleston, and the Institute of Molecular Biology (IMB) where molecular genetics and zebrafish genomics were famously pioneered.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Bioengineering (Knight Campus) (Doctorate) — Cutting-edge doctoral research at the Knight Campus focusing on synthetic biomaterials, tissue engineering, neuro-engineering, and therapeutic delivery.
  • PhD in Physics (Quantum Optics and Condensed Matter) (Doctorate) — World-renowned physics program actively contributing to LIGO gravitational wave astronomy, quantum measurement, and superconducting materials.
  • Master of Architecture (MArch in Sustainable Design) (Master's) — NAAB-accredited professional degree recognized globally for mass timber technology, passive solar heating, and carbon-neutral building design.
  • Bachelor of Science in Marine Biology (OIMB Immersion) (Bachelor's) — Immersive coastal undergraduate degree based at the Oregon Institute of Marine Biology featuring daily ocean research on research vessel Pluteus.
