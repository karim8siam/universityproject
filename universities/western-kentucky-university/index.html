Western Kentucky University

WKU · Bowling Green, Kentucky, United States

Western Kentucky University (WKU) is a public comprehensive university founded in 1906, situated on a hill commanding the Barren River valley in Bowling Green, Kentucky. Acclaimed for journalism and photojournalism at the School of Media & Communication, karst hydrogeology and cave science through the Crawford Hydrology Laboratory, mechanical engineering, and applied physics, WKU serves as an essential center of innovation and regional economic development in south-central Kentucky.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WKU comprises five undergraduate and graduate colleges: the Potter College of Arts & Letters, Gordon Ford College of Business, College of Education and Behavioral Sciences, Ogden College of Science and Engineering, and the College of Health and Human Services. WKU operates the Crawford Hydrology Laboratory and partners with Mammoth Cave National Park.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and coursework with test-optional application pathways. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, personal statement, two to three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+). Graduate assistantships provide stipends and full or partial tuition waivers.
