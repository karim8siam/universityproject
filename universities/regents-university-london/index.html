Regent's University London

Regent's University London is a premier private non-profit cosmopolitan university situated within 11 acres of royal private gardens in Regent’s Park, renowned for international business, luxury brand management, psychotherapy, and acting.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1984 on the historic site of Bedford College (the first higher education college for women in the UK, founded in 1849), Regent’s University London is one of the most culturally diverse independent universities in the world. With students representing over 140 nationalities, Regent's provides an intimate, boutique learning experience characterized by small class sizes, high executive contact, and a global alumni network spanning royalty, business tycoons, and creative directors. Programs are centered in the European Business School London and the School of Psychotherapy and Psychology.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • MA Luxury Brand Management (Master's) — World-renowned master's program analyzing luxury value creation, heritage brand strategy, omnichannel clienteling, and sustainable craftsmanship in Mayfair fashion houses.
  • BA (Hons) International Business (Bachelor's) — Four-year degree incorporating integrated study abroad terms at partner business schools across Europe, Asia, and the Americas, with mandatory foreign language mastery.
  • Doctorate in Counseling Psychology (DPsych) (Doctorate) — HCPC-approved and BPS-accredited professional doctorate qualifying counseling psychologists through 450 hours of supervised clinical client therapy.
  • BA (Hons) Acting for Stage and Screen (Bachelor's) — Intensive conservatory-style acting program training actors in voice, stage combat, Stanislavski/Meisner techniques, and camera performance.
