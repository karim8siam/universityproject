Western Carolina University

WCU · Cullowhee, North Carolina, United States

Western Carolina University is a comprehensive public doctoral university founded in 1889, located in the picturesque Cullowhee Valley between the Blue Ridge and Great Smoky Mountains in Western North Carolina. As a constituent campus of the University of North Carolina system, WCU is nationally recognized for emergency medical care and forensic science, Appalachian cultural ecology, engineering technology, and the NC Promise tuition plan.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WCU spans six colleges: the College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business, College of Education and Allied Professions, College of Engineering and Technology, College of Fine and Performing Arts, and the College of Health and Human Sciences. WCU operates the Western Carolina Forensic Osteology Research Station (FOREST), one of only a few human decomposition research facilities in the world.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and transcripts. Eligible in-state and out-of-state undergraduates benefit from the NC Promise tuition plan ($2,500/semester tuition for out-of-state). Graduate applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
