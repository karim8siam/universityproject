Academic overview & excellence

Sundance College emphasizes career readiness through intensive modular schedules, certified instructor-led labs, and integrated guaranteed industry practicum work terms that prepare graduates directly for licensing and immediate workplace entry.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requirements include completion of a Canadian high school diploma or mature student examination (Wonderlic SLE standard), with English language proficiency (IELTS 5.5-6.0 or Duolingo 95+). Dedicated financial aid advisors assist students with Manitoba Student Aid and institutional bursaries.
