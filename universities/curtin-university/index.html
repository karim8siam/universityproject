Curtin University (Perth, Australia)

Established in 1966 in Perth, Curtin University is Western Australia's largest and most culturally diverse university, ranked in the top 1% of universities worldwide. Curtin is globally renowned for mineral and mining engineering (ranked #2 in the world), computer science, and radio astronomy through the International Centre for Radio Astronomy Research (ICRAR) and Square Kilometre Array (SKA). Verified degree programs and opportunities

Curtin University Australian Government RTP & Strategic Doctoral Scholarships

Premier Australian doctoral research scholarship providing complete tuition coverage and an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $35,000 for high-achieving domestic and international PhD candidates at Curtin.

Curtin University PhD in Computing and Information Systems

Four-year advanced doctoral research program conducting world-leading research in high-performance computing, artificial intelligence, blockchain, cybersecurity, and data-intensive radio astronomy.

Curtin Master of Predictive Analytics

Two-year postgraduate master's degree preparing graduates to extract meaningful insights from big data, combining machine learning algorithms, statistical forecasting, and cloud computing for industry decision-making.

Curtin Master of Professional Engineering (Chemical Engineering)

Engineers Australia (EA) accredited professional engineering master's degree focusing on clean hydrogen energy, mineral carbonation, sustainable battery chemical processing, and industrial plant optimization.

Curtin International Centre for Radio Astronomy Research (ICRAR) Placements

Frontier astrophysical research placements inside ICRAR Curtin, developing cutting-edge software and signal processing hardware for the Murchison Widefield Array (MWA) and the global Square Kilometre Array (SKA).

Curtin Western Australian Energy & Mining Industry Research Internships

Structured 12-week vocational research internship embedding graduate engineers with Western Australian resource, energy, and engineering pioneers (BHP, Rio Tinto, Woodside, Chevron).

Curtin Sessional Academic Teaching & Lab Demonstratorships

Sessional campus academic employment enabling graduate students to instruct weekly tutorial classes, supervise computing and engineering laboratories, and grade assignments across undergraduate faculties.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with minimum course weighted average (CWA) of 65%+ or equivalent 3.0/4.0 GPA.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1) or Master by Research degree with a substantial research thesis component (at least 25% of total degree credits).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all subscores) or TOEFL iBT 79 (reading 13, listening 13, speaking 18, writing 21).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 2 to 3-page research proposal outlining the intended research problem, methodology, timeline, and identified Curtin faculty supervisor.
Application fee
Zero application fee when applying directly online via the Curtin application portal.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, grading scale guide, curriculum vitae, 2 academic referee reports, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under the Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP), domestic and selected international doctoral researchers receive complete 100% tuition fee offsets (saving up to AUD $120,000) plus an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $35,000 for up to 3.5 years.
