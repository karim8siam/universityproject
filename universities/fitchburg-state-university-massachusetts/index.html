Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1894 as the State Normal School in Fitchburg, the university combines rigorous liberal arts inquiry with distinguished professional professional training. The university operates state-of-the-art sound stages, game development studios, nursing high-fidelity simulation centers, and the landmark 4+1 Police Program (the first of its kind in the US to combine a bachelor's and master's degree with full municipal police academy certification).

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5; 3.0+ for test-free guaranteed admission). Standardized tests (SAT/ACT) are optional. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 2.8, academic transcripts, resume, and letters of recommendation. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.5.
