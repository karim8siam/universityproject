IMMS Institut für Mikroelektronik - und Mechatronik-Systeme

IMMS Institut für Mikroelektronik- und Mechatronik-Systeme is a renowned applied research institute in Thuringia affiliated with TU Ilmenau, bridging university research and industrial production in mixed-signal ASIC microelectronics, MEMS sensors, wireless sensor networks, and industrial IoT automation.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating state-of-the-art microelectronics design suites, cleanroom testing laboratories, and environmental simulation chambers, IMMS conducts advanced R&D in automotive sensor ICs, biomedical implants, ultra-low-power radio transceivers, and precision mechatronics. In close partnership with the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology at TU Ilmenau, IMMS offers doctoral research candidacies, master theses, and applied industrial internships.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open to postgraduate and doctoral researchers in electrical engineering, computer science, mechatronics, and semiconductor physics. Fellowship applicants submit an accredited master’s degree transcript, scientific CV, and a portfolio of circuit design or embedded systems projects.
