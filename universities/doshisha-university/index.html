Doshisha University (同志社大学)

Founded in 1875 in the ancient imperial capital of Kyoto by Protestant pioneer Joseph Hardy Neesima (Niijima Jo), Doshisha University is one of western Japan's oldest and most prestigious private universities, anchor of the renowned Kan-Kan-Do-Ritsu league. Situated across historic campuses adjacent to the Kyoto Imperial Palace (Imadegawa Campus) and the Kansai Science City (Kyotanabe Campus), Doshisha is internationally celebrated for global studies, intelligent mechanical systems, bio-engineering, theology, and international business.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of a 16-year school education curriculum culminating in a recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3.0/4.0 GPA or equivalent grade B+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree or professional degree; submission of Master's thesis and passing marks in oral defense and doctoral screening.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 85 for English-taught graduate programs; Japanese JLPT N1 for Japanese-medium graduate divisions.
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-page research proposal detailing proposed doctoral research topic, theoretical framework, research methodology, and anticipated academic contributions.
Application fee
¥35,000 non-refundable screening fee submitted via the Doshisha online admissions portal.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, complete official academic transcripts, research proposal, 2 letters of recommendation, and English proficiency test report.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees for graduate programs at Doshisha are approximately ¥950,000 to ¥1,250,000 per year. Admitted international students benefit from Doshisha University Graduate School Tuition Reduction Scholarships covering 20% to 100% of tuition, and MEXT University Recommendation Scholarship recipients receive 100% full tuition waivers, round-trip international airfare, and a monthly tax-free living stipend of ¥145,000 per month.
