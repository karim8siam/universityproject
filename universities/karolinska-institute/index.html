Karolinska Institute (Karolinska Institutet, Stockholm)

Founded in 1810 by King Karl XIII, Karolinska Institute (Karolinska Institutet - KI) in Stockholm is Sweden's single largest center of medical academic research and accounts for over 40% of all medical research conducted in Sweden. World-renowned as the home of the Nobel Assembly (which annually selects the laureates for the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine), KI consistently ranks among the top 5 medical universities globally. KI doctoral candidates are employed as salaried staff members with full Swedish employment benefits, while Master's students benefit from cutting-edge clinical infrastructure adjacent to Karolinska University Hospital. Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completed Bachelor's degree (or equivalent Swedish Kandidatexamen) in biomedicine, biotechnology, medicine, public health, or related biological discipline from an internationally recognized university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
University degree at advanced (Master's) level comprising at least 240 ECTS credits (including at least 60 credits at advanced level) with demonstrable scientific methodology training.
Language proficiency
English 6 equivalent: Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT 90 (minimum 20 in written test); required for all non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral positions, an individual study plan (ISP) formulated with the designated principal investigator based on an established research project description.
Application fee
SEK 900 international application fee paid to University Admissions Sweden (antagning.se); SEK 0 application fee for doctoral salaried employment positions.
Required documentation
Certified bachelor's transcripts, degree diploma, curriculum vitae, documentation of relevant clinical/laboratory experience, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In Sweden, doctoral education is 100% free of tuition fees for all students regardless of citizenship. Furthermore, KI doctoral candidates are hired on fully funded salaried employment contracts (doktorandanställning) under Swedish collective agreements, receiving a starting salary of approximately SEK 32,000 to SEK 36,000 per month with full healthcare, parental leave, and pension benefits. For Master's students, Karolinska Institutet Global Master's Scholarships cover full tuition fees.
