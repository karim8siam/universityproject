Kobe Shinwa University

Excel in accredited teacher education, clinical child psychology, and competitive sports science at Kobe Shinwa University in Kobe.

Official institution website ↗Independent Academic Atlas

Institution Overview & Profile

Kobe Shinwa University (formerly Kobe Shinwa Women's University, coeducational since 2023), established in 1966 in the green hills of Kita-ku, Kobe, is a prestigious Japanese university recognized nationwide for its exceptional teacher preparation (consistently ranking top in Hyogo for elementary teacher employment), sports pedagogy, clinical psychology, and early childhood education.

Institution TypePrivate Coeducational University (Shinwa Gakuen Educational Foundation)
Main CampusSuzurandai Campus, Kita-ku, Kobe, Hyogo Prefecture
AccreditationJapan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / MEXT Accredited
Admissions RouteComprehensive AO Admissions, EJU & International Student Portal
Language of InstructionJapanese (JLPT N2 required for undergraduate curricula; N1 for teacher certification)
Financial Aid & GrantsShinwa International Student Tuition Waivers (up to 50%) & Gakuen Founder Bursaries

Key Academic Strengths

  • #1 in Hyogo Prefecture for public elementary school teacher employment success
  • Nationally elite women's volleyball and track and field collegiate athletic programs
  • Department of Child Development with dedicated on-campus Shinwa Child Study Centre
  • Fully coeducational since April 2023 with modern sports training and psychological observation facilities

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Undergraduate Degree

Undergraduate Degree

Master's Degree

Graduate School of Psychology (Clinical Psychology Master's)
