HDK-Valand: Craft, Fine Art, Design & Doctoral Artistic Research Guide

HDK-Valand - Academy of Art and Design is the internationally acclaimed artistic faculty of the University of Gothenburg (Göteborgs universitet), situated in central Gothenburg, Sweden. Formed from the union of the historic Valand Academy of Fine Art (est. 1865) and the School of Design and Crafts (HDK, est. 1848), HDK-Valand is one of Europe's largest and most comprehensive environments for contemporary arts, design, craft, film, photography, and literary composition, leading the Nordic region in doctoral artistic research.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Fine Arts, Bachelor of Design, or equivalent international artistic degree (180 ECTS); submission of creative portfolio, written artist project description, and admissions interview.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Artistic Doctorate (PhD in Artistic Research in Fine Arts, Design, or Crafts); requires recognized master's degree in arts or design, documentation of artistic excellence, and a pioneering doctoral project proposal.
Language proficiency
English language proficiency equivalent to English 6 from Swedish upper secondary school (IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no subtest below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90).
Research statement / Proposal
Creative portfolio and artist project statement: 10-15 documentation samples of artistic practice and a 1,000-word statement outlining proposed artistic investigations.
Application fee
900 SEK national Swedish university application fee for non-EU/EEA international applicants.
Required documentation
Higher education artistic degree certificates, official transcripts, digital portfolio (submitted via portal), project description, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is 0 SEK for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. International student tuition ranges from 280,000 SEK to 345,000 SEK per academic year depending on specialized studio equipment usage. Admitted international students can apply for University of Gothenburg Axel Adler Scholarships covering 100% of tuition fees.
