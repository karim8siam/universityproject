BSBI Berlin: Global MBA, Data Analytics, Digital Marketing & Creative Industries Guide

Berlin School of Business and Innovation (BSBI) is an international business school located in Berlin's historic Alte Post building in Neukölln, delivering accredited bachelor's, master's, and doctorate degrees in partnership with Università Telematica Internazionale UNINETTUNO and the University for the Creative Arts (UCA).

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS) in business, humanities, engineering, or computing from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or MBA with good academic standing, accompanied by a 3,000-word doctoral research proposal and interview.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 to 6.5 overall (minimum 5.5 to 6.0 in each band) or equivalent medium of instruction (MOI) certification.
Research statement / Proposal
A 500-word personal statement detailing academic motivation, career plans, and interest in Berlin's startup ecosystem.
Application fee
Zero application fee through the BSBI direct online portal.
Required documentation
Bachelor's certificate, official transcripts, CV, personal statement, copy of passport, and two letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate programs averages €11,000 to €14,500 for the full degree. High-achiever scholarships of up to 50% tuition reduction, early-bird fee discounts, and flexible monthly payment plans are available for international candidates.
