University of Indianapolis

UIndy · Indianapolis, Indiana, United States

The University of Indianapolis (UIndy), established in 1902 and affiliated with the United Methodist Church, is a comprehensive private university located just south of downtown Indianapolis. UIndy is internationally celebrated for health sciences leadership—home to the acclaimed Krannert School of Physical Therapy, the School of Occupational Therapy, and the Archeology & Forensics Laboratory—along with programs in engineering, education, and business.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UIndy offers undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degrees through the College of Health Sciences, the Shaheen College of Arts & Sciences, the R.B. Annis School of Engineering, the School of Nursing, and the School of Business. Its modern facilities include the Health Pavilion, the Annis Hall Design Studios, and the Human Identification Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions operate on rolling deadlines via the Common Application (test-optional; recommended GPA 3.0+). Graduate and clinical doctoral programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, competitive prerequisite GPAs, standardized test scores where applicable, and letters of recommendation.
