Brigham Young University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Founded in 1875 by Brigham Young in the shadow of the Wasatch Range, BYU is the flagship university of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, educating over 34,000 students. BYU consistently ranks among the top universities in the United States for undergraduate education and return on investment, backed by exceptionally low tuition rates subsidized by Church tithes. The university hosts top-tier research programs across the Marriott School of Business, the Ira A. Fulton College of Engineering, and the College of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, excelling in micro-electro-mechanical systems (MEMS), turbomachinery, organic synthesis, and computational linguistics.
