Tokyo University of Foreign Studies: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1873 as the Tokyo School of Foreign Languages (preceded by the Tokugawa Shogunate’s Institute for the Study of Barbarian Books in 1857), TUFS educates 4,500 students on its modern suburban campus in Fuchu, Tokyo. TUFS is designated as one of Japan’s Top Global Universities, offering education and research in over 80 world languages and 14 global geographic regions. TUFS alumni staff Japan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, diplomatic embassies, UN agencies, and global trading companies. The university is home to the Research Institute for Languages and Cultures of Asia and Africa (ILCAA).
