Institutional Overview

The University of Cumbria in London, situated on East India Dock Road in the East End of London, is the capital campus of the University of Cumbria. Specializing in primary teacher education (PGCE and BA), adult nursing, business management, and occupational health, the London campus provides direct routes into inner-city London schools, NHS trusts, and public sector leadership.

  • Over 98% employment rate for London primary teacher education graduates
