University of Nebraska Medical Center

UNMC · Omaha, Nebraska, United States

The University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC) is a world-class academic health sciences center located in Omaha, Nebraska. Internationally revered for biocontainment isolation care, infectious disease preparedness, solid organ transplantation, and cancer research at the Fred & Pamela Buffett Cancer Center, UNMC trains premier clinical practitioners and biomedical investigators.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNMC encompasses six colleges: Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Dentistry, Public Health, and Allied Health Professions, alongside graduate biomedical training programs. UNMC operates the National Quarantine Center, the Global Center for Health Security, and the Munroe-Meyer Institute for developmental disabilities.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are competitive and administered through the Graduate Studies office or centralized application services (AMCAS, PharmCAS, SOPHAS). Biomedical doctoral applicants require strong undergraduate STEM preparation, minimum 3.0 GPA, letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
