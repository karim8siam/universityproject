University of Nebraska Omaha

UNO · Omaha, Nebraska, United States

The University of Nebraska Omaha (UNO) is Nebraska's premier metropolitan research university, situated in the heart of Omaha. Internationally renowned for biomechanics research, aviation, national security studies at NCITE, public administration, and computer science, UNO connects cutting-edge academic scholarship with Fortune 500 corporate and defense partnerships.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNO houses six colleges: the College of Information Science & Technology (IS&T), College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business Administration, College of Education, Health, and Human Sciences, College of Public Affairs and Community Service (CPACS), and College of Communication, Fine Arts and Media. Key institutes include the Biomechanics Research Building and NCITE.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduates. Graduate applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, provide a statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and meet English proficiency standards (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
