University of Idaho Integrated Design Lab

UI IDL · Boise, Idaho, United States

The University of Idaho Integrated Design Lab (IDL) in Boise is a nationally recognized research and graduate teaching facility within the College of Art and Architecture. Dedicated to transforming the built environment, the IDL conducts groundbreaking research in whole-building energy modeling, passive daylighting simulation, thermal envelope optimization, and decarbonization strategies for utility companies and architectural firms across the Pacific Northwest.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Boise · Premier Architectural Energy Research, Daylighting & Net-Zero Building Science. The University of Idaho Integrated Design Lab (IDL) in Boise is a nationally recognized research and graduate teaching facility within the College of Art and Architecture. Dedicated to transforming the built environment, the IDL conducts groundbreaking research in whole-building energy modeling, passive daylighting simulation, thermal envelope optimization, and decarbonization strategies for utility companies and architectural firms across the Pacific Northwest.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Enrollment through the University of Idaho College of Graduate Studies (M.Arch, M.S. in Architecture, or Ph.D. in Design). Accredited bachelor's degree in architecture, mechanical engineering, or building science with 3.0+ GPA. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international graduate applicants. Fall graduate admission priority January 15, 2027; Regular deadline March 15, 2027.
