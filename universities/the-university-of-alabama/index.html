The University of Alabama

University of Alabama · Tuscaloosa, Alabama, United States

The University of Alabama (UA) is the flagship public Carnegie R1 research university of the state of Alabama, located in Tuscaloosa along the Black Warrior River. Generating over $130M in annual research expenditures, UA is home to the National Water Center, the Alabama Water Institute (AWI), the Culverhouse College of Business, and the prestigious College of Engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UA delivers 13 academic divisions offering comprehensive bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs. Its research power is anchored by the Alabama Life Research Institute (ALRI), the Alabama Transportation Institute, the Center for Advanced Public Safety (CAPS), and the NOAA National Water Center located directly on campus.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA and academic rigor (test-optional). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA (or last 60 hours), program-specific GRE/GMAT scores (waivers available), statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
