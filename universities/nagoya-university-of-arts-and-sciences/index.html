Academic overview & excellence

Located in the eastern educational corridor of the Greater Nagoya metropolis, Nagoya University of Arts and Sciences integrates four forward-looking faculties: Media Studies (filmmaking, animation, game design, and digital music), Design (visual communication, space and interior design, and fashion), Nutritional Sciences (management nutrition and health food research), and Nursing. NUAS provides students with industry-grade 4K television studios, motion-capture stages, and clinical simulation suites.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate domestic and international candidates through general entrance tests, the Common Test, and comprehensive evaluation (AO) admissions. International students must present EJU scores (Japanese as a Foreign Language), JLPT N2 or higher, an artistic portfolio (for Media and Design faculties), and an interview. Graduate programs require a bachelor's degree in a related artistic or scientific field and submission of a thesis or master's creative portfolio.
