Swansea University: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Established in 1920 on the scenic south Wales coast, Swansea University (Prifysgol Abertawe) is a top UK research-intensive university. Known as the historical birthplace of the Finite Element Method in engineering, Swansea boasts the beachfront £450m Bay Campus, leading global research in materials engineering, computational mechanics, medicine, and marine biology.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Swansea University Research Excellence PhD Studentship (EPSRC/UKRI)

Full-coverage doctoral scholarship covering international tuition, living stipends, and research supplies for three and a half years of PhD study.

Master of Science in Computational Engineering and Artificial Intelligence

Cutting-edge master's degree exploring finite element methods, computational fluid dynamics, surrogate machine learning models, and high-performance parallel computing.

SPECIFIC Innovation Centre Doctoral Fellowship in Solar Photovoltaic Coatings

Four-year EPSRC Industrial Doctoral Studentship conducting scalable roll-to-roll printing of next-generation solar coatings on building facades.

Centre for Sustainable Aquatic Research (CSAR) Marine Ecology Project

Applied marine research project examining microalgae cultivation, recirculating aquaculture systems, and coastal benthic habitat restoration in Swansea Bay.

Tata Steel UK & Swansea Materials Engineering Industrial Practicum

Six-month practical engineering placement at nearby Port Talbot industrial facilities evaluating low-carbon green steelmaking, scrap recycling, and hydrogen reduction.

Swansea Quantum and High-Energy Physics Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at Swansea's Particle Physics and Cosmology Theory group contributing to CERN ALPHA antihydrogen laser spectroscopy experiments.

Lecturer in Criminology and Digital Crime Analytics

Permanent academic lectureship in the Hillary Rodham Clinton School of Law conducting research on online illicit markets, cyber terrorism, and AI surveillance ethics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:1 Honours degree (or international equivalent with minimum 60–65% depending on undergraduate university tier).
Doctoral admission eligibility
UK Master's degree with Merit/Distinction (or First Class Honours Bachelor degree) with strong research proposal and supervisor agreement.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each component (or equivalent Cambridge C1 Advanced).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research proposal (approx 1,500 words) defining objectives, methodology, and relevance to Swansea research centers.
Application fee
£0 (Direct free online application for postgraduate courses).
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, English language qualification, personal statement, CV, and two academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate taught tuition typically ranges from £19,600 to £23,800 per year. Fully funded doctoral research studentships (supported by EPSRC, UKRI, and Swansea Research Excellence) provide 100% tuition coverage for domestic and international students, along with a tax-free annual stipend of £19,237 (indexed annually).
