Institution Overview & Profile

Bethel College, chartered in 1887 in North Newton, Kansas, is the oldest Mennonite college in North America. Known for its rigorous undergraduate liberal arts, nursing, peace and conflict studies, and sciences, Bethel consistently ranks among the top national liberal arts colleges in Washington Monthly for social mobility, student community service, and research output.
