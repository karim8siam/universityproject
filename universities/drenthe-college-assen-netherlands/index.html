Drenthe College: Smart Healthcare, Logistics & Sustainable Engineering Guide

Drenthe College in Assen is a leading regional vocational college (MBO) in the northern Netherlands, celebrated for advanced technical training in healthcare technology (Zorg & Welzijn), green construction engineering, smart warehouse logistics, and automotive sensor mechanics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
MBO Level 4 Specialist and Middle Management diplomas require a completed VMBO Theoretical/Combined diploma or international secondary equivalent (minimum 10-11 years of schooling).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Vocational higher education college; graduates qualify for direct advancement to Universities of Applied Sciences (HBO bachelor's programs).
Language proficiency
Dutch proficiency (minimum B1/B2 for technical and healthcare courses) as instruction is conducted in Dutch.
Research statement / Proposal
Intake interview and motivation statement assessing professional career affinity in trades, care, or IT.
Application fee
Free online application through the Dutch national education portal (CAMBO / studiekeuze123).
Required documentation
Secondary school graduation certificate, prior grade transcripts, proof of identity, and residence permit if applicable.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Statutory annual tuition set by the Dutch Ministry of Education (Lesgeld) is approximately €1,419 for full-time MBO Level 3 and 4 courses. Dutch student finance (DUO) study grants, regional healthcare traineeship stipends, and employer apprenticeships (BBL tracks) are available.
