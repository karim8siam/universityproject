Northern Kentucky University

Northern Kentucky · Highland Heights, Kentucky, United States

Northern Kentucky University is a public metropolitan university in Highland Heights, Kentucky, just seven miles southeast of downtown Cincinnati. Renowned for cybersecurity, informatics, health innovation, business, and law.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NKU houses the College of Informatics, the Salmon P. Chase College of Law, and the College of Health and Human Services. The College of Informatics is housed in the $50M Griffin Hall, featuring the Digitorium and a full cyber threat operations center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.75 for standard admission; test-optional). Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.75 to 3.0 GPA, personal statement, and letters of recommendation.
