Institution Overview & Profile

Gisma University of Applied Sciences is a state-recognized private university with a flagship campus in Potsdam (Innovation Hub) and a campus in Berlin, offering AMBA-accredited and FIBAA-accredited English-taught degree programs in computer science, artificial intelligence, international business management, and digital engineering.

Institution TypeState-Recognized Private University of Applied Sciences

Financial Aid & GrantsGisma Merit Scholarships, Early Bird Discounts & Germany Post-Study Work Visa

Key Academic Strengths

  • Brand-new flagship campus in Potsdam SAP Innovation Center ecosystem with high-tech AI laboratories
  • AMBA-accredited Global MBA and globally aligned STEM business analytics programs
  • Strong corporate partner network providing student internships with Amazon, SAP, Tesla Gigafactory, and Zalando
  • 18-month German post-study work visa eligibility for international graduates
