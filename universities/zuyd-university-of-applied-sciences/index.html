Zuyd University of Applied Sciences: Circular Materials, Healthcare & Arts Guide

Located across Heerlen, Sittard, and the European cultural capital of Maastricht in southern Netherlands, Zuyd University of Applied Sciences (Zuyd Hogeschool) is a dynamic Dutch public university. Anchored inside the world-class Brightlands Chemelot chemical and materials innovation campus and Brightlands Maastricht Health Campus, Zuyd leads pioneering research in circular plastics, sustainable built environments, and applied health technology.

NL Scholarship (Holland Scholarship) at Zuyd Hogeschool

Prestigious national scholarship assisting talented non-EU/EEA students enrolling in full-time bachelor's and master's degrees at Zuyd University of Applied Sciences.

Master of Science in Facility and Real Estate Management

One-year internationally accredited dual-degree master's (awarded jointly with University of Greenwich) focusing on smart buildings, sustainable real estate, and circular asset life-cycles.

Dutch National Professional Doctorate (PD) in Energy and Sustainable Materials

Four-year salaried Professional Doctorate (PD) based at Brightlands Chemelot Campus investigating chemical recycling of mixed polymer waste and thermal energy integration.

Brightlands Circular Plastics & Bio-Based Polymeric Materials Investigation

Collaborative research project at the Chemelot Circular Hub evaluating mechanical recycling limits, bio-based monomer synthesis, and life-cycle carbon footprints of novel bioplastics.

Brightlands Maastricht Health Campus Assistive Health Technology Internship

Twenty-week practical internship co-designing rehabilitative wearable sensor devices and smart home ambient monitoring systems for elderly patients recovering from stroke.

Zuyd Postdoctoral Lector Fellowship in Sustainable Built Environments

Two-year research appointment leading the Sustainable Built Environment lectorate investigating deep thermal retrofits, timber modular construction, and urban microclimate cooling.

Zuyd Excellence Study Award in Applied Engineering and ICT

Merit award sponsored by the Zuyd Foundation providing direct tuition deductions to high-achieving international applicants entering computer science and engineering degrees.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 ECTS credits) in engineering, materials science, business, or healthcare from a recognized institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Professional Doctorate (PD) in Energy & Sustainability; entry requires relevant Master of Science degree and joint research placement at Brightlands Chemelot Campus.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 90 for English-taught master's degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Postgraduate applied research concept (1,500 words) defining industrial sustainability challenges or healthcare innovation in collaboration with regional industry.
Application fee
EUR 0 for Dutch centralized Studielink registration.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete transcript of records with ECTS breakdown, curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, and English proficiency certificate.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

For EU/EEA and Swiss citizens, statutory annual tuition is set by the Dutch government at approximately EUR 2,530. For non-EU/EEA international students, institutional tuition ranges from EUR 8,900 to EUR 11,200 per year. The Dutch Ministry of Education NL Scholarship (Holland Scholarship) provides EUR 5,000 in direct study grants to eligible international matriculants.
