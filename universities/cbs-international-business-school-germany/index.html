Institution Overview & Profile

CBS International Business School, founded in 1993 with flagship campuses in Cologne, Mainz, Potsdam, and Aachen, is a leading private business university in Germany accredited by the German Science Council (Wissenschaftsrat), FIBAA, and the International Accreditation Council for Business Education (IACBE).

Institution TypeState-Recognized Private University of Applied Sciences (Hochschule)
Main CampusHardefuststraße Campus, Cologne (plus Mainz, Potsdam, and Berlin-Schönefeld)
AccreditationGerman Science Council (Wissenschaftsrat) / FIBAA Premium Seal / IACBE
Admissions RouteCBS Online Assessment & Personal Interview (rolling)
Language of InstructionEnglish (TOEFL iBT 83, IELTS 6.5, or Duolingo 115) or German C1 for German tracks
Financial Aid & GrantsCBS Best-Performer Scholarships & EU/International Tuition Discounts

Key Academic Strengths

  • 100% English-taught Bachelor's, Master's, and full-time MBA degrees
  • Compulsory integrated internships with DAX companies and international multinationals
  • Top rankings in CHE (Centrum für Hochschulentwicklung) for international orientation and practical relevance
  • Global network of over 160 partner universities worldwide

Scholarship

CBS Best-Performer Scholarship for International Students

Merit-based tuition reduction covering up to 50% of tuition fees for outstanding international candidates admitted to English-taught degree programs.

Funding: Up to 50% tuition reduction (saving approximately €6,000–€7,500 per year) Deadline: May 15, 2027 (Winter intake)
Master's

Fast-Track International MBA Admission & Corporate Fellowship

Intensive 12-month post-experience Master of Business Administration in English, featuring corporate consulting field trips in Germany.

Funding: Early-bird tuition discount of €1,500 plus corporate sponsorship eligibility Deadline: June 30, 2027
Master's

Master of Science in Business Analytics & Artificial Intelligence

Two-year postgraduate degree blending machine learning algorithms, cloud business intelligence, and predictive consumer analytics.

Funding: Eligible for German Brain Capital educational fund (income-share agreement) Deadline: May 15, 2027
Grant

CBS Digital Transformation Innovation Grant

Venture seed grant funded by the CBS Entrepreneurship Hub supporting student founders creating digital AI and fintech startups.

Funding: €5,000 prototype stipend, dedicated co-working space in Cologne, and mentor matching Deadline: April 30, 2027
Internship

German DAX Corporate Consulting Internship Practicum

Compulsory four-to-six-month corporate internship in Frankfurt, Cologne, or Munich with firms like Lufthansa, Bayer, or DHL.
