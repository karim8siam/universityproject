University of Bergamo: Engineering, Economics & Diritto allo Studio Scholarships

The University of Bergamo (Università degli Studi di Bergamo) is a progressive Italian state university located in Lombardy's industrial and cultural heartland, distinguished for engineering manufacturing excellence, international economics, digital humanities, and legal studies.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Lombardy Regional DSU Full Scholarship & Accommodation Grant

Needs-and-merit based regional scholarship open to all enrolled international students ensuring equal access to higher education in Lombardy.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Engineering & Applied Sciences (Full Ministerial Grant)

Doctoral research program closely linked with Lombardy's advanced manufacturing corridor, covering mechatronics, logistics optimization, and sustainable materials.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Management Engineering & Smart Industry

Two-year international master's taught in English exploring supply chain digitalization, predictive maintenance, energy management, and Industry 4.0 paradigms.

Kilometro Rosso Innovation District Research Internship

Curricular research internship hosted inside the Kilometro Rosso science park collaborating with Brembo, ABB, and industrial AI labs on cyber-physical automation.

Department of Economics Applied Econometrics & Big Data Assistantship

Twelve-month research position focusing on empirical microeconometrics, labor market dynamics in northern Italy, and regional economic resilience.

Postdoctoral Fellowship in Human-Computer Interaction & Assistive Robotics

Advanced postdoctoral study developing intuitive robotic control interfaces, wearable haptics, and assistive cognitive devices for eldercare and industry.

Master of Arts (M.A.) in Clinical Psychology for Individuals, Families & Organizations

English-taught master's exploring clinical assessment, systemic family counseling, organizational well-being, and neuropsychological rehabilitation.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Three-year undergraduate degree (Laurea) or international bachelor's degree evaluated through the Universitaly pre-enrolment portal.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with competitive evaluation of curriculum vitae, publications, and oral research presentation.
Language proficiency
B2 English level (IELTS 6.0+, TOEFL 72+, Cambridge FCE, or medium-of-instruction certificate); Italian required only for Italian-taught curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral applicants: 3,000–5,000 word research proposal written in English outlining research objectives and bibliography.
Application fee
€30 to €52 administrative pre-evaluation fee depending on call.
Required documentation
Universitaly application summary, degree certificate with Declaration of Value (DoV) or CIMEA Statement of Comparability, and transcript.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is based on family financial evaluation (ISEE parificato), ranging from €156 to approximately €2,800 per year. Regional DSU scholarships (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) provide qualifying international students with full tuition waivers, free cafeteria meals, and annual cash stipends up to €7,000.
