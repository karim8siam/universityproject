University System of Georgia

USG · Atlanta, Georgia, United States

The University System of Georgia (USG) governs 26 public higher education institutions across Georgia, including four premier research universities: Georgia Institute of Technology, the University of Georgia, Georgia State University, and Augusta University. Enrolling over 344,000 students and conducting more than $1.3 billion in annual sponsored research, USG is an economic engine powering cybersecurity, robotics, infectious disease vaccine discovery, and public health.
