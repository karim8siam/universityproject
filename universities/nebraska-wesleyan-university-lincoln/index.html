Nebraska Wesleyan University

Nebraska Wesleyan · Lincoln, Nebraska, United States

Nebraska Wesleyan University is an independent Methodist liberal arts university in Lincoln, Nebraska. Renowned for pre-health sciences, theatre arts, business, social work, and undergraduate scientific research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Nebraska Wesleyan offers 106 majors, minors, and pre-professional programs across the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences. Features the Duane W. Acklie Hall of Science, the Olin Hall of Science, and the McDonald Theatre.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions evaluate high school grade point average, academic trajectory, and extracurricular involvement under a test-optional framework. Graduate programs in nursing and athletic training require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA.
