Southern California State University

SCSU California · El Monte, California, United States

Southern California State University (SCSU) is an accredited private university situated in El Monte, California, in the heart of the San Gabriel Valley within greater Los Angeles. Specializing in high-demand degree programs in Business Administration, Computer Information Systems, and Acupuncture & Traditional Chinese Medicine, SCSU prepares multicultural professionals for leadership in cross-border commerce and integrative healthcare.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

SCSU comprises the School of Business, the School of Information Technology, and the School of Oriental Medicine. Its campus features modern multimedia seminar rooms, computer laboratories, and an on-campus community acupuncture teaching clinic serving the San Gabriel Valley metropolitan community.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation or equivalent with English proficiency for international applicants. Graduate programs in business and computer systems require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.5+ GPA. The Master and Doctor of Acupuncture & Oriental Medicine require 60+ semester units of prior collegiate coursework including general biology and chemistry.
