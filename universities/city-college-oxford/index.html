Institution Overview & Profile

City College Oxford, situated in the historic city of Oxford, is an accredited independent higher and further education college offering Pearson BTEC Higher National Certificates (HNC) and Higher National Diplomas (HND) in business, computing, health and social care, and hospitality management, providing structured pathway progression into UK university degree completion programs.

Institution TypeIndependent Higher & Further Education College
Main CampusHurst Street Campus, Oxford, Oxfordshire, UK
AccreditationPearson Edexcel Approved Centre / BAC Accredited
Admissions RouteRolling tri-mester intake (September, January, May)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS 5.5/6.0 overall with no band below 5.5)
Financial Aid & GrantsCity College Oxford Academic Merit Bursaries & Student Finance England Eligibility (via designated partners)

Key Academic Strengths

  • Rigorous Pearson BTEC Level 4 HNC and Level 5 HND qualifications recognized globally by universities and employers
  • Direct pathway for graduates to enter the final third year ('top-up') of honours degree programs at UK universities
  • Small tutorial classes offering personalized academic guidance in the academic heart of Oxford
  • Industry-focused technical modules in cloud computing, cyber security, systems analysis, and enterprise finance
