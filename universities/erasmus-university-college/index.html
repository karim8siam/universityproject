Erasmus University College: Liberal Arts & Sciences, Problem-Based Learning Guide

Erasmus University College (EUC) is the international honours undergraduate college of Erasmus University Rotterdam, housed in a monument building in central Rotterdam, celebrated for its multidisciplinary Bachelor of Liberal Arts and Sciences (BSc), intensive Problem-Based Learning (PBL), and global societal impact.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
EUC offers an intensive 3-year undergraduate honours Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Liberal Arts and Sciences; graduates automatically qualify for prestigious master's degrees globally.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate honours college; doctoral research candidates matriculate through Erasmus University Rotterdam graduate schools.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 100.
Research statement / Proposal
Motivation letter detailing why EUC's multidisciplinary Problem-Based Learning approach aligns with your educational philosophy.
Application fee
€100 non-refundable EUR institutional application fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school diploma (IB diploma, European Baccalaureate, or national equivalent with high mathematics grade), official transcripts, CV, and academic recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

EU/EEA statutory tuition is approximately €4,700 per year (including statutory tuition plus institutional fee). Non-EU/EEA international tuition is approximately €14,900 to €16,500 annually. EUC Excellence Scholarships, Erasmus Trustfonds grants, and Dutch student finance (DUO) are available.
