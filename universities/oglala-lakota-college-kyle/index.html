Oglala Lakota College

Oglala Lakota College · Kyle, South Dakota, United States

Oglala Lakota College is a chartered tribal college serving the Oglala Sioux Tribe on the Pine Ridge Indian Reservation in southwestern South Dakota. It is globally recognized for Lakota cultural preservation, indigenous education, tribal law, and environmental science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OLC operates nine instructional centers distributed across the Pine Ridge Reservation, alongside the Woksape Tipi Tribal Archives and Library, and the Math and Science Department in Kyle. It is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission and provides associate, baccalaureate, and master's degrees tailored to tribal sovereignty and community revitalization.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Oglala Lakota College operates an open-admission policy welcoming tribal members, descendants, and non-tribal students. Degree-seeking students must submit high school transcripts or GED scores and complete Lakota language placement orientations.
