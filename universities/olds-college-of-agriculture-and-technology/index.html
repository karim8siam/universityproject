Olds College of Agriculture & Technology: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1913 in central Alberta between Calgary and Red Deer, Olds College of Agriculture & Technology is a nationally recognized leader in smart agriculture, veterinary medicine, horticulture, and land management. The institution’s living laboratory—the Olds College Smart Farm—serves as North America’s preeminent testing ground for agricultural IoT sensors, drone multispectral imagery, robotic tractors, and artificial intelligence in crop nutrition. Through its Werklund Centre and Centre for Innovation, Olds College partners directly with agribusinesses to accelerate sustainable food production.
