Academic overview & excellence

Representing more than 1,200 full-time academic staff, research professors, and librarians at the University of Manitoba's Fort Garry and Bannatyne campuses, UMFA champions collegiate governance, public research funding, and scholarly integrity. Founded in 1951, UMFA fosters academic development through specialized research awards, labor equity initiatives, and higher education policy symposia.

Research admissions & criteria

UMFA awards and research grants are available to full-time faculty members, postdoctoral scholars, and academic professionals appointed at the University of Manitoba. Collaborative awards involve peer-reviewed adjudication with provincial, national (CAUT), and international academic bodies.
