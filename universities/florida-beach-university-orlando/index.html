Academic overview & research priorities

Operating instructional facilities in Orlando and along the Space Coast, Florida Beach University provides practical, industry-aligned curricula designed for the hospitality, tourism, and coastal leisure sectors. The university integrates hands-on resort practicums, digital marketing bootcamps, and shoreline ecosystem management to equip students with direct commercial competencies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a recognized secondary school diploma or GED certificate, submission of high school transcripts, a statement of career motivation, and proof of English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 70+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants). Transfer credits from community colleges and professional industry certifications are recognized through holistic portfolio assessment.
