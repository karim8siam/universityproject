Institution Overview & Profile

Kobe Kaisei College (Kobe Kaisei Joshi Gakuin Daigaku), founded in 1955 by the Roman Catholic Congregation of the Sisters of the Holy Family in Nada Ward, Kobe, is an esteemed private women's college providing specialized undergraduate education in English studies, French language, child development, intercultural communication, and Japanese teacher licensure.

Institution TypePrivate Catholic Women's College (Joshi Daigaku)
Main CampusNada Ward Campus (Aotanicho), Kobe, Hyogo, Japan
AccreditationJapan University Accreditation Association (JUAA) / MEXT Accredited
Admissions RouteGeneral entrance examination, Recommendation admissions (Suisen), and International applicant screening
Language of InstructionJapanese (JLPT N2/N1 for standard degree study) with intensive English/French tracks
Financial Aid & GrantsKaisei Sisterhood Merit Scholarships, JASSO Honors Scholarships & Hyogo Prefectural Grants

Key Academic Strengths

  • Distinguished tradition in European linguistic instruction with small-group tutorial classes in English and French
  • Department of Child Education offering dual national certifications in Nursery Teacher (Hoikushi) and Kindergarten Teacher
  • Scenic hillside campus overlooking Kobe Harbour and Osaka Bay with serene chapel architecture
  • Robust study abroad exchange agreements with partner Catholic universities in France, Canada, and the United States

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

Bachelor of Arts (Gakushi)

Bachelor of Arts in English Studies & Global Communication

Four-year curriculum developing high-level bilingual fluency, British and American literature, translation methodologies, and cross-cultural business communication.

Bachelor of Arts (Gakushi)

Bachelor of Arts in Child Development & Early Education

Accredited four-year educator program delivering certified licensing in kindergarten pedagogy, infant development, and childhood psychology.

Undergraduate Certificate

Specialized Certificate in Japanese Language Pedagogy

Two-year teacher training curriculum preparing graduates to instruct Japanese as a foreign language in schools and international institutions.
