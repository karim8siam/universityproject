Nebraska State College System

NSCS · Lincoln, Nebraska, United States

The Nebraska State College System (NSCS) governs Nebraska's three public state colleges: Chadron State College in the Pine Ridge, Peru State College on the Missouri River, and Wayne State College in northeast Nebraska. Dedicated to accessible, student-centered education, NSCS excels in teacher preparation, rural health science pathways, business administration, and criminal justice, driving economic vitality across rural Nebraska.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Lincoln · Serving Nebraska Through Chadron State, Peru State & Wayne State Colleges. The Nebraska State College System (NSCS) governs Nebraska's three public state colleges: Chadron State College in the Pine Ridge, Peru State College on the Missouri River, and Wayne State College in northeast Nebraska. Dedicated to accessible, student-centered education, NSCS excels in teacher preparation, rural health science pathways, business administration, and criminal justice, driving economic vitality across rural Nebraska.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma or GED equivalent. Open and accessible admissions policy reviewing high school coursework, with automatic admission for students meeting core graduation standards. TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international students across NSCS colleges. Rolling admissions; priority fall deadline June 1, 2027; spring priority deadline December 1, 2026.
