Iowa State University

Iowa State · Ames, Iowa, United States

Iowa State University of Science and Technology is a public land-grant Carnegie R1 research university in Ames, Iowa, renowned for agricultural engineering, veterinary medicine, aerospace, and the Ames National Laboratory.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Iowa State is a founding member of the Association of American Universities (AAU) and the birthplace of the first electronic digital computer (Atanasoff-Berry Computer). Flagship research facilities include the Virtual Reality Applications Center, the Bioeconomy Institute, and the Center for Nondestructive Evaluation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, core course requirements (RAI index), and test scores under test-optional guidelines. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree, minimum 3.0 GPA, statements of purpose, and department-specific criteria.
