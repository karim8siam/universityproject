Ravensbourne University London

Ravensbourne University London is an innovative specialist university in design, digital media, fashion, architecture, and creative computing, based in an iconic architectural building next to The O2 on the Greenwich Peninsula.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1962 and granted full university status in 2018, Ravensbourne University London is an internationally celebrated specialist creative institution renowned for exceptionally high graduate employment in the UK creative industries. Housed in a landmark RIBA Award-winning open-plan building designed by Foreign Office Architects, Ravensbourne operates cutting-edge television studios, 3D prototyping labs, motion-capture suites, and virtual production stages. The university maintains deep partnerships with industry giants including the BBC, Cisco, Apple, Epic Games, and Warner Bros.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • BA (Hons) Digital Film Production (Bachelor's) — ScreenSkills Select-accredited degree providing hands-on training in cinema cameras (ARRI/RED), multi-camera live television broadcast, and color grading.
  • MA User Experience Design (UX Design) (Master's) — Industry-facing master's developing expertise in cognitive interaction architecture, user research methodologies, rapid wireframing, and design systems.
  • BA (Hons) Fashion: Digital Fashion and Design (Bachelor's) — Cutting-edge fashion curriculum combining traditional pattern cutting and garment construction with 3D digital apparel software (CLO 3D).
  • MSc Computer Science (Applied AI and Creative Technologies) (Master's) — Transformative graduate computing program exploring machine learning for media generation, shaders, real-time graphics in Unreal Engine, and web development.
