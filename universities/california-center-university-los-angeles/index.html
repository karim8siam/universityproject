Academic overview & research priorities

CCU delivers hybrid and on-campus degree curricula designed around modern enterprise demands. Programs emphasize digital management, practical informatics, data analytics, and cross-border commercial strategy in close proximity to Southern California technology hubs.

Research admissions & international criteria

Graduate admission requires an earned bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution with a minimum 2.50 GPA, official transcripts, resume, and statement of intent. International students must demonstrate English language proficiency (TOEFL 70 or IELTS 6.0).
