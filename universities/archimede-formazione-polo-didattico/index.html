Archimede Formazione - Polo Didattico Universitario

Archimede Formazione is an officially recognized university learning hub and examination centre situated in the Salento region of Puglia. In partnership with major accredited Italian telematics universities, Archimede Formazione offers undergraduate and master's degree pathways, professional certifications, and lifelong learning credentials across education, psychology, health management, and economic sciences.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Lecce & Brindisi, Puglia, Italy

Institutional Overview

The educational approach at Archimede Formazione blends continuous online digital lectures with personalized in-person tutoring at regional learning sites in Lecce and Brindisi. Programs are structured according to Italian ministerial guidelines, providing structured exam preparation, university credit (CFU) validation, and professional teacher accreditation courses.

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Matriculation is open throughout the academic year. Prospective students submit an upper secondary school diploma (Maturità) or bachelor's degree for postgraduate programs. Affordable regional tuition schedules, student installment plans, and incentives for working professionals and public service employees ensure accessible higher education.
