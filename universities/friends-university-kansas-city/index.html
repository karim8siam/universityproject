Friends University - Kansas City Center

Friends University Kansas City Center is an innovative regional campus of Friends University in Overland Park, delivering adult professional education, executive management degrees, marriage and family therapy, and cyber operations.

Academic overview & research priorities

Affiliated with the Quaker tradition of integrity and social responsibility, Friends University’s Kansas City Education Center specializes in evening and hybrid degree programs. It hosts the nationally accredited Master of Science in Family Therapy and professional graduate degrees in business and healthcare leadership.

Research admissions & international criteria

Graduate admission requires an earned Bachelor degree from an accredited institution with a minimum cumulative 2.75 GPA, two recommendations, an essay on professional ethics, and relevant prerequisite coursework.
