Stockton University

Stockton University · Galloway, New Jersey, United States

Stockton University is a top-ranked public undergraduate and graduate university set on a stunning 1,600-acre campus within the Pinelands National Reserve in Galloway, New Jersey, with an oceanfront Atlantic City campus on the Boardwalk. Acclaimed for coastal marine science (operating its own Marine Field Station), healthcare disciplines (Doctor of Physical Therapy and Nursing), environmental studies, and hospitality management.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Stockton comprises the School of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, the School of Health Sciences, the School of Business, the School of Social and Behavioral Sciences, and the School of Arts and Humanities. It features the Marine Field Station at Nacote Creek with research vessels, acoustic seafloor mappers, and the state-of-the-art Unified Science Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (average 3.5), coursework rigor, and optional SAT/ACT scores. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ minimum GPA, GRE scores (program-dependent), statement of purpose, and academic letters of reference.
