Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located on the Falls Road in West Belfast, St Mary's University College is a distinguished university college academically affiliated with Queen's University Belfast. Established in 1900, it is celebrated for excellence in primary and secondary teacher education, Irish-medium teacher training, liberal arts, and human development.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Initial Teacher Education (Bachelor of Education - BEd Primary and Post-Primary)
  • Irish-medium education (oideachas lán-Ghaeilge) and Celtic cultural studies
  • Liberal Arts (BA) in human development, business, drama, and religious studies
  • Community cohesion, peace studies, and inclusive educational pedagogy

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Education (BEd Hons) Primary Education (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year professional degree awarded by Queen's University Belfast leading to registered teacher status with the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (GTCNI).
  • Bachelor of Arts (BA Hons) in Liberal Arts (Undergraduate Degree) — Three-year interdisciplinary degree combining human development, European history, international business, and Irish studies.
  • Postgraduate Certificate in Education (PGCE) Irish-Medium Education (Postgraduate Degree) — One-year intensive qualification training secondary educators for bilingual and Irish-medium immersion schools.
