Mount Holyoke College

Mount Holyoke · South Hadley, Massachusetts, United States

Mount Holyoke College is a prestigious private liberal arts college for women and gender-diverse students in South Hadley, Massachusetts. The oldest of the historic Seven Sisters institutions and a member of the Five College Consortium.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Mount Holyoke offers 48 departmental and interdepartmental majors with cross-registration at Amherst, Smith, Hampshire, and UMass Amherst. World-class facilities include the Miller Worley Center for the Environment and Kendade Hall for Science.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly competitive, evaluating high school academic rigor, writing samples, intellectual curiosity, and letters of recommendation under a long-standing test-optional policy.
