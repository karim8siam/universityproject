Northeastern Illinois University

Northeastern Illinois · Chicago, Illinois, United States

Northeastern Illinois University is a comprehensive public institution located on Chicago's northwest side. Designated as a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI), NEIU is celebrated for computer science, teacher education, urban community leadership, and social work.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NEIU comprises the College of Arts and Sciences, Daniel L. Goodwin College of Education, and the College of Business and Technology. Operates satellite centers including El Centro in Avondale and the Jacob H. Carruthers Center for Inner City Studies in Bronzeville.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.5 for automatic admission) under a test-optional policy. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.75 to 3.0 GPA, statement of goals, and recommendations.
