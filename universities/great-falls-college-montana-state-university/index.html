Great Falls College Montana State University

Great Falls College Montana State University is a two-year public comprehensive college in central Montana, delivering market-aligned programs in allied health, dental hygiene, welding, and cybersecurity.

Academic overview & research priorities

An affiliate of Montana State University, GFC MSU provides vital healthcare, technical, and general education pathways across northern and central Montana, featuring advanced simulation hospitals, industrial cybersecurity labs, and direct transfer articulation to four-year MSU campuses.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open-access admissions policy for general admission (high school diploma or GED equivalent). Selective admissions apply for competitive allied health programs (Dental Hygiene, Physical Therapist Assistant, Radiologic Technology). International applicants require TOEFL iBT 61+ or IELTS 5.5+.
