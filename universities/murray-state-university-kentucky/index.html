Murray State University

Murray State · Murray, Kentucky, United States

Murray State University is a nationally recognized public regional university in Murray, Kentucky. Celebrated for agriculture, occupational safety and health, engineering physics, telecommunications systems management, and veterinary science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Murray State comprises the Hutson School of Agriculture, Jesse D. Jones College of Science, Engineering and Technology, and the Arthur J. Bauernfeind College of Business. Home to the Hancock Biological Station and Watershed Studies Institute.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.0 automatic or test-flexible review). Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with 2.75 to 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, and recommendations.
