Institutional Overview

Université du Québec en Outaouais (UQO), part of the Université du Québec network, maintains main campuses in Gatineau (facing Ottawa) and Saint-Jérôme in the Laurentians. UQO is recognized for high-impact research in cybersecurity, forestry ecology, social work, nursing, psychoeducation, and project management.
