Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1973 and named after Nobel Peace Prize laureate Carl von Ossietzky, the University of Oldenburg is the home of the Hearing4all Cluster of Excellence—one of the world's most distinguished centers for auditory acoustics and cochlear implant innovation. Its research is intimately linked with the Helmholtz Institute for Functional Marine Biodiversity (HIFMB) and the German Aerospace Center (DLR) Institute of Networked Energy Systems.

Research admissions & international criteria

Master's applicants must hold an accredited bachelor's degree in a relevant field with a minimum GPA of 2.5 on the German grading scale. Programs such as Engineering Physics and Neuroscience are conducted in English and require TOEFL iBT 90+ or IELTS 6.5+. Doctoral admissions require an approved research proposal and written confirmation from an Oldenburg university professor.
