Wentworth Institute of Technology

Wentworth · Boston, Massachusetts, United States

Wentworth Institute of Technology is an independent, polytechnic university established in 1904, situated on a 31-acre urban campus along the Fenway and Longwood Medical Area in Boston, Massachusetts. Renowned for its nationally recognized cooperative education (co-op) model, engineering, architecture, computer science, and construction management, Wentworth connects hands-on laboratory education with Boston's high-tech and biotech industry leaders.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Wentworth comprises five schools: the School of Architecture & Design, School of Computing & Data Science, School of Engineering, School of Management, and the School of Sciences & Humanities. The university requires two semesters of full-time professional co-op employment for graduation.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school math and science coursework, GPA, and personal essays with test-optional reviews. Graduate master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in a STEM discipline, a minimum 3.0 GPA, professional resume, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+). Merit scholarships are awarded automatically upon admission.
