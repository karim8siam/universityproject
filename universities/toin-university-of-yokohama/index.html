Academic overview & research priorities

Situated in the green hills of Aoba Ward, Yokohama, Toin University of Yokohama comprises the Faculty of Biomedical Engineering, Faculty of Law, Faculty of Modern Society, and the Graduate School of Engineering. Under the leadership of Distinguished Professor Tsutomu Miyasaka—frequently cited as a top contender for the Nobel Prize in Chemistry—Toin is an international epicenter for printable perovskite photovoltaics, medical ultrasound systems, and regenerative tissue engineering.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate programs require high school graduation, submission of academic transcripts, and Japanese proficiency (JLPT N2). Graduate applicants to the Graduate School of Engineering (Materials Science, Biomedical Engineering) must hold an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, physics, or chemistry, and provide a formal research plan aligned with an identified laboratory principal investigator.
