University of Nebraska High School and Extended Studies

UNHS & Extended Studies · Lincoln, Nebraska, United States

The University of Nebraska High School (UNHS) and Extended Studies, accredited by Cognia and the Nebraska Department of Education, is a world-renowned college-preparatory distance education enterprise operated by the University of Nebraska. Serving learners across all 50 states and over 100 foreign countries for nearly a century, UNHS provides rigorous Advanced Placement (AP), dual-credit collegiate coursework, and STEM foundational bridges designed to prepare gifted students for elite university matriculation.
