The University of Texas at Dallas

UT Dallas · Richardson, Texas, United States

The University of Texas at Dallas (UT Dallas) is a premier Carnegie R1 public research university located in Richardson, Texas, in the epicenter of the Telecom Corridor. Founded by the founders of Texas Instruments (Eugene McDermott, Cecil Green, and J. Erik Jonsson), UT Dallas is globally renowned for computer science, electrical engineering, cybersecurity, bioengineering, and management science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Comprising eight schools, UT Dallas is anchored by the Erik Jonsson School of Engineering and Computer Science, the Naveen Jindal School of Management, and the School of Behavioral and Brain Sciences. Its campus features the Natural Science and Engineering Research Laboratory (NSERL) cleanroom complex, the Texas Analog Center of Excellence (TxACE), and the Center for Applied AI.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate class rank, GPA, and curriculum rigor with strong mathematics preparation. Graduate and doctoral applicants require a baccalaureate degree from an accredited institution with a minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA, competitive GRE/GMAT scores (program-specific waivers available), a statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
