University of Providence

Providence · Great Falls, Montana, United States

The University of Providence is a private Catholic university founded in 1932 by the Sisters of Providence, situated on a 44-acre campus in Great Falls, Montana. Partnering with Providence St. Joseph Health—one of the largest non-profit healthcare systems in the United States—the university is renowned for nursing, healthcare administration, applied forensic science, and exercise science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

The university comprises two primary schools: the School of Health Professions and the School of Liberal Arts and Sciences. Serving as the primary educational arm of Providence St. Joseph Health, UP provides direct clinical training pipelines, healthcare simulation facilities, and criminal justice internships across the Northern Rockies.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are test-optional and holistic, evaluating high school transcripts and GPA (minimum 2.5). Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
