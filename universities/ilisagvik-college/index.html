Iḷisaġvik College

Iḷisaġvik · Utqiaġvik, Alaska, United States

Iḷisaġvik College is Alaska's only federally recognized tribal college, situated at the top of the world in Utqiaġvik (formerly Barrow), Alaska. Governed by the North Slope Borough and accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities (NWCCU), Iḷisaġvik weaves indigenous Iñupiaq values with accredited degree programs in Arctic climate sciences, indigenous health, applied business, and industrial trades.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Utqiaġvik · America's Northernmost Tribal College on the Arctic Ocean. Iḷisaġvik College is Alaska's only federally recognized tribal college, situated at the top of the world in Utqiaġvik (formerly Barrow), Alaska. Governed by the North Slope Borough and accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities (NWCCU), Iḷisaġvik weaves indigenous Iñupiaq values with accredited degree programs in Arctic climate sciences, indigenous health, applied business, and industrial trades.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Open door admissions policy welcoming indigenous and non-indigenous students. High school diploma, GED, or completed placement assessment required for degree enrollment. Standard English proficiency assessment; Iñupiaq language immersion pathways offered. Fall semester priority admission August 1, 2027; Spring semester priority December 15, 2026.
