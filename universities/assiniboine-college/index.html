Assiniboine College: Sustainable Agribusiness, Environmental Technologies & Healthcare Guide

Established in 1961 in Brandon, Manitoba, Assiniboine College is a premier Canadian post-secondary institution renowned for practical, career-connected education across agriculture, environment, technology, and health sciences. Home to the Russ Edwards School of Agriculture & Environment, Assiniboine conducts field-leading applied research in sustainable crop nutrition, soil biodiversity, precision farming, and digital automation across the Canadian Prairies.

Russ Edwards School of Agriculture Prairie Leadership Scholarship

Prestigious entrance scholarship established to support Canadian and international students demonstrating academic merit and leadership in sustainable agribusiness.

Post-Graduate Certificate in Sustainable Food Systems and Agronomy

One-year advanced post-graduate credential providing intensive training in precision soil management, smart greenhouse operations, and agri-food supply chains.

Prairies Canada Applied Soil Health & Field Crop Research Fellowship

Graduate-level applied research fellowship in collaboration with provincial agricultural scientists analyzing biochar amendments and root rhizosphere dynamics.

Assiniboine Centre for Sustainable Crop Research Graduate Project

Applied laboratory research investigating moisture retention properties of cover crops and fungal endophyte interactions in prairie soil under climate variability.

Civil Engineering Technology Cooperative Work-Term Placement

Four-month cooperative education placement with Manitoba provincial infrastructure authorities and civil consultancies designing municipal roadworks and drainage systems.

Prairies Digital Automation & Smart Agribusiness Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral position spearheading unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) multispectral imagery and autonomous combine fleet telemetry at Assiniboine's test farm.

Assiniboine Foundation Global Diversity and Academic Achievement Award

Tuition award celebrating international scholars enrolling in high-demand diploma and post-graduate programs at Assiniboine's Victoria Avenue East campus in Brandon.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree or two-year post-secondary diploma from an accredited institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.8 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent) for advanced post-graduate certificates.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; applied college institution prioritizing advanced diplomas, post-graduate credentials, and applied research apprenticeships.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic overall score of 6.5 (no sub-band lower than 6.0), TOEFL iBT score of 79+, or Pearson PTE Academic score of 58+.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for applied research bursaries: 750-word statement outlining practical experience in agricultural technology, soil science, or civil engineering.
Application fee
$110 CAD non-refundable application fee for domestic applicants; $200 CAD for international students.
Required documentation
Certified official academic transcripts with English translations, valid passport photo page, proof of English language proficiency, and letter of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Assiniboine College averages $4,800 to $6,500 CAD annually for Manitoba domestic students and approximately $16,800 to $19,500 CAD per academic year for international students. Extensive foundation bursaries, Manitoba Agriculture entrance scholarships, and applied research stipends provide substantial support.
