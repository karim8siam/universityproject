Bunkyo University Tokyo Adachi: International Studies, Business & Media Guide

Bunkyo University's flagship Tokyo Adachi Campus, opened in 2021 near Hanahata, houses the Faculty of International Studies and Faculty of Business Administration, celebrated for bilingual education, media production, cross-cultural diplomacy, and educational pedagogical excellence.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from a recognized 4-year undergraduate university, submission of bachelor's graduation thesis or research equivalent, and interview.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree in international relations, linguistics, communication, or business administration, plus submitted research proposal.
Language proficiency
JLPT N1 or N2 for Japanese-based courses; TOEFL iBT 75 or IELTS 6.0 for international tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Research plan (Kenkyu Keikakusho) of 2,000 characters outlining methodology and primary sources.
Application fee
¥35,000 application screening fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, official language score, statement of academic goals, and recommendation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual undergraduate tuition is approximately ¥1,200,000 to ¥1,380,000. Graduate tuition averages ¥780,000 per year. Bunkyo University provides a 30% international student tuition waiver, Bunkyo Educational Foundation awards, and external JASSO honors scholarships.
