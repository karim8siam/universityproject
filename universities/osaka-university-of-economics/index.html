Academic overview & excellence

Tracing its lineage to the Showa Higher Commercial School established in 1932, Osaka University of Economics is situated in the Higashiyodogawa ward of Osaka City. OUE spans four undergraduate faculties: Economics, Business Administration, Information & Decision Sciences, and Human Sciences, alongside four master's and doctoral graduate divisions. The university houses the Institute of Small Business Research and the historic Japanese Corporate History Archives, holding one of the nation's most exhaustive collections of pre-war business records.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate entry requires completion of 12 years of formal education. Foreign applicants apply through the Special International Student Entrance Examination submitting EJU scores in Japanese and Mathematics. Graduate admissions require an undergraduate degree in economics, business, or related social sciences, with evaluation based on a formal research plan and oral examination.
