North Carolina Central University

NCCU · Durham, North Carolina, United States

North Carolina Central University is a premier public Historically Black University (HBCU) in Durham, North Carolina, and part of the Research Triangle. Renowned for biomedical discovery, law, pharmaceutical sciences, and jazz studies.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NCCU features the Julius L. Chambers Biomedical/Biotechnology Research Institute (BBRI), the Biomanufacturing Research Institute and Technology Enterprise (BRITE), and the School of Law. It is a national leader in addressing health disparities and biomanufacturing workforce training.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.5) under holistic test-optional review. Graduate programs in pharmaceutical sciences, biology, and law require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, and recommendation letters.
