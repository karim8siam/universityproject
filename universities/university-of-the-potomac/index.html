Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Potomac combines urban experiential learning with international multicultural cohorts across computer science, information technology, business administration, and digital forensics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma or recognized equivalent for undergraduate programs; conferred bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with 2.5+ GPA for graduate master's and doctoral degrees. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Rolling admissions with six intake terms per year: Fall I August 15, 2027; Fall II October 15, 2026; Spring I January 10, 2027.
