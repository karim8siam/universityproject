Colby College

Colby College is one of America's most selective and prestigious private liberal arts colleges, situated on Mayflower Hill in Waterville, Maine. It is distinguished globally for the Davis Institute for Artificial Intelligence, climate science, and generous need-blind financial aid.

Academic overview & excellence

Colby was the first liberal arts college in the United States to establish an institute dedicated entirely to artificial intelligence (Davis Institute for AI). Colby emphasizes undergraduate research, the unique January Term (Jan Plan), and fully funded global internships through the DavisConnects guarantee.

Research admissions & criteria

Admissions is highly competitive (acceptance rate under 7%). Colby meets 100% of demonstrated financial need without loans for all admitted students, including international scholars. Standardized tests (SAT/ACT) are optional.

Pioneering carbon-neutral undergraduate environmental program utilizing Colby's 128-acre Perkins Arboretum and Maine coastal marine research stations.

Funding: Colby financial aid guarantee covering all demonstrated financial need Tuition: $66,800 per year (need-based grant reduces cost to family ability to pay) Deadline: January 5, 2027
Internship

Colby's universal funding guarantee providing every student with financial resources to undertake research, internships, or global experiences.

Funding: Up to $5,000 guaranteed grant funding for unpaid global internships and field research Tuition: $0 Deadline: March 1, 2027
