Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Based on dynamic waterfront campuses in Docklands and Stratford near London's tech and financial hubs, the University of East London (UEL) enrolls over 40,000 students across 160 countries. UEL is celebrated for its 'Vision 2028' career-first curriculum, pioneering research in sustainable engineering, artificial intelligence, psychology, and public health.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Sustainable civil engineering and structural carbon reduction
  • Artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and cybersecurity (BCS accredited)
  • Psychology and behavioral sciences (BPS accredited, top modern UK department)
  • Sports therapy, public health, and urban community nursing

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence Track) (Graduate Degree) — One-year intensive or two-year industrial placement degree exploring deep neural networks, computer vision, and cloud computing.
  • Bachelor of Engineering (BEng Hons) in Civil Engineering (Undergraduate Degree) — Accredited engineering degree concentrating on urban infrastructure resilience, low-carbon materials, and BIM software modeling.
  • Doctor of Clinical Psychology (DClinPsy) (Professional Doctorate) — Rigorous HCPC and BPS-accredited doctorate with funded NHS clinical trainee contracts serving East London communities.
