University of Passau: Computer Science, Legal Informatics, AI & European Studies Guide

The University of Passau (Universität Passau) is a modern, research-intensive Bavarian university situated on a scenic riverfront campus, celebrated internationally for its Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics, legal informatics, and interdisciplinary European governance studies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or congruent field with a minimum German grade of 2.7 (or equivalent 70%+ score).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree (minimum 300 ECTS) with above-average grades (minimum 2.0 gut) and a formal supervision agreement with a Passau professor.
Language proficiency
English B2/C1 (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 87+) for English-taught Master's; German DSH-2 / TestDaF 4 for German-taught degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
A 2-3 page statement of purpose indicating prospective academic supervisor and specialization area.
Application fee
Zero application fee via the university's online application system.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificate, curriculum vitae, motivation letter, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is completely free (€0) for all students regardless of nationality. Students pay only a modest semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €110 per semester including local bus transit. Generous scholarships through DAAD and Bavarian research grants are available.
