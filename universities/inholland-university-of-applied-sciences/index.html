Inholland University of Applied Sciences: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

With state-of-the-art campuses across The Hague, Amsterdam, Delft, and Haarlem, Inholland University of Applied Sciences (Hogeschool Inholland) is one of the Netherlands' leading public universities of applied sciences. Famous for aeronautical engineering, sustainable agriculture, digital business, and media design, Inholland pairs theoretical education with deep industry immersion.

NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) at Inholland

Competitive scholarship funded by the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science for international students from outside the EEA.

Master in Aeronautical Engineering and Lightweight Structures

Advanced applied master's program based at the Delft Composite Laboratory focusing on lightweight thermoset composites, aircraft structural integrity, and sustainable aviation.

Inholland Collaborative Professional Doctorate in Sustainable Aviation & Bio-Composites

Four-year professional doctorate conducted in partnership with Dutch technical universities and aerospace composite manufacturers.

Inholland Agri-Food and Sustainable Greenhouse Horticulture Research Project

Applied research fellowship situated in the Westland horticultural hub developing circular nutrient solutions, LED photosynthetic optimization, and autonomous indoor crop monitoring.

Amsterdam Creative Digital Tech & Immersive Media Practicum

Twenty-week practical internship embedded within innovative Amsterdam creative agencies developing virtual reality experiences, serious gaming, and generative user interfaces.

Inholland Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Thermoplastic Composite Recycling

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Delft Composite lab advancing induction welding of carbon-fiber reinforced thermoplastic composites for zero-waste airframes.

Senior Lecturer and Researcher in Sustainable Business Innovation

Permanent faculty appointment leading classroom teaching, curriculum design, and client-sponsored research projects on circular business models.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree in engineering, business, or related applied sciences (minimum 180 ECTS credits).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Professional doctorate and collaborative university research programs require accredited Master's credentials and industrial partnership approval.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each section, or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, letter of motivation (500 words), and portfolio of technical or business case studies.
Application fee
€0 (Studielink centralized Dutch national application registration).
Required documentation
Certified copy of higher education diplomas, complete grade transcripts, proof of English language proficiency, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

For Dutch and EU/EEA students, tuition is the statutory Dutch rate of €2,601 per academic year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition ranges from €8,950 to €10,400 per year depending on the engineering or business discipline. The Dutch Ministry of Education's NL Scholarship offers €5,000 to qualifying incoming non-EEA students.
