Academic overview & excellence

PoliBa LabZERO unites mechanical, civil, and electrical engineers with architectural scholars to design smart, net-zero buildings and urban microgrids. The facility operates advanced thermal imaging, building-integrated photovoltaics testing, and carbon capture demonstration rigs.

Research admissions & criteria

Bachelor's applicants must pass the national CISIA TOLC-I engineering admission test and hold an accredited secondary school diploma. Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) applicants require a recognized BSc in Engineering with a minimum GPA of 75% and B2 English proficiency for international programs.

PoliBa Zero-Carbon Urban Engineering Doctoral Fellowship

Three-year PhD scholarship investigating building-integrated renewable energy systems, geothermal heat pump optimization, and lifecycle emissions modeling for Mediterranean climates.

Funding: €16,243 annual doctoral scholarship plus €1,500 annual conference research budget Tuition: €0 tuition for doctoral candidates Deadline: February 28, 2027
Master's

Laurea Magistrale in Building Engineering & Architecture (Zero-Energy Track)

Two-year European-accredited graduate degree in sustainable architectural engineering, energy performance simulation (EnergyPlus), and smart facade engineering.

Funding: ADISU Puglia regional housing and meal vouchers eligible Tuition: €1,750 per year (waived for low-income brackets down to €0) Deadline: April 15, 2027
Fellowship

Puglia Industrial Decarbonization Post-Doctoral Research Award

Postdoctoral research fellowship in collaboration with Taranto industrial cluster evaluating direct hydrogen iron-ore reduction and industrial waste-heat recovery.

Funding: €28,500 gross annual research fellowship (Assegno di Ricerca) for 2 years Tuition: €0 Deadline: March 31, 2027
Internship

Mediterranean Solar Thermal & Heat Storage Practicum

Twenty-week practical engineering internship testing phase-change thermal storage materials and concentrating solar collectors.

Funding: €750 monthly student training stipend at LabZERO test facilities Tuition: €0 Deadline: May 15, 2027
Bachelor's

Laurea in Ingegneria dei Sistemi Edilizi (Bachelor in Building Systems Engineering)

Three-year first-cycle degree covering thermodynamics, structural mechanics, BIM modeling, HVAC engineering, and environmental acoustics.

Funding: Merit tuition waiver for top 10% entrance scores Tuition: €1,500/year (statutory max) Deadline: July 31, 2027
Scholarship

Campus LabZERO Green Campus Transition Grant

Competitive student innovation grant supporting practical campus energy efficiency retrofits, electric micro-mobility, and urban biodiversity gardens.

Funding: €3,000 project implementation grant for student-led circular campus initiatives Tuition: €0 Deadline: April 30, 2027
Course

Twelve-week professional specialization course in Autodesk Revit, Dynamo visual scripting, openBIM IFC protocols, and IoT digital twin facility management.

Funding: Order of Engineers CPD credits certified Tuition: €1,400 course fee Deadline: September 30, 2027
