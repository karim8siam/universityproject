Epitech Berlin

Epitech Berlin is the German campus of the European Institute of Information Technology, renowned across Europe for its project-based, peer-learning pedagogy in software engineering, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.

Academic overview & excellence

Operating in the heart of Berlin's startup ecosystem, Epitech delivers practical European Master's and Bachelor's level software architecture programs. Students master C, C++, Python, algorithms, and full-stack software development through continuous coding projects and mandatory industry internships.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission is based on analytical aptitude and motivational interviews rather than prior computer science diplomas. Applicants undertake the intensive introductory 'Piscine' coding bootcamp. English language competency (B2/C1) is required for international degree cohorts.
