Academic overview & excellence

Created by the National Assembly of Quebec in 1968 to democratize access to higher education, the Université du Québec network encompasses institutions such as UQAM, UQTR, UQAR, UQAC, UQO, UAT, INRS, and ÉTS. The headquarters in Quebec City administers system-wide academic governance, inter-institutional graduate diplomas, major scientific research funding initiatives, international mobility agreements, and provincial educational technology infrastructures. Scholars benefit from province-wide research consortia in genomics, arctic development, forestry ecology, artificial intelligence, and digital arts.

Research admissions & criteria

Admissions to inter-institutional graduate programs and system-wide research fellowships administered by the Université du Québec require application through the central admissions bureau or constituent university registrars. Candidates must demonstrate an undergraduate degree (Baccalauréat) or Master's with a minimum cumulative average of 3.2/4.3 (equivalent to 75%), fluency in academic French (DELF/DALF B2/C1 or TFI 750), official academic transcripts, letters of intent, and confirmed research supervisor support.
