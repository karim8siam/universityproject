Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Northumbria University London Campus is the dynamic capital outpost of Northumbria University Newcastle—named Times Higher Education University of the Year. Located in the financial heart of the City of London (near Liverpool Street), the campus delivers world-class double-accredited (AACSB) master's degrees in global business, fintech, digital marketing, and cybersecurity.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • MSc Business with International Management (with Advanced Practice) (Postgraduate Degree) — Two-year master's combining multinational business strategy with a guaranteed 15-week company internship or business consulting clinic.
  • MSc Cyber Security Technology (with Advanced Practice) (Postgraduate Degree) — Two-year technical master's covering ethical hacking, cloud security architecture, digital forensics, and network defense.
  • MSc Digital Marketing (with Advanced Practice) (Postgraduate Degree) — CIM-accredited program analyzing programmatic advertising, social media analytics, customer journey mapping, and conversion optimization.
