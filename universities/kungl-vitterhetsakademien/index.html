Kungl. Vitterhetsakademien: Humanities, Archaeology & Heritage Research

The Royal Swedish Academy of Letters, History and Antiquities (Kungliga Vitterhets Historie och Antikvitets Akademien), founded in 1753 by Queen Lovisa Ulrika, is Sweden's national royal academy promoting excellence, postdoctoral fellowships, and research grants across the humanities, archaeology, philology, and cultural heritage conservation.

Vitterhetsakademien National Humanities Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Prestigious national research fellowship awarded to exceptional early-career scholars in history, archaeology, classical languages, or cultural heritage studies.

Master's Research Travel Bursary & Archival Residency in Swedish Cultural History

Competitive grant enabling graduate thesis students enrolled at Swedish or Nordic universities to conduct primary source archival research at the Royal Academy library and heritage collections.

Ph.D. in Nordic Archaeology & Cultural Heritage Conservation Fellowship

Four-year cooperative doctoral fellowship operated alongside Stockholm University, utilizing isotope bioarchaeology and dendrochronology to analyze early medieval Scandinavian settlement patterns.

Swedish Historical Monuments & Landscape Preservation Research Project

Interdisciplinary heritage study monitoring stone weathering, bio-deterioration, and climatic threats across historic cultural properties owned by the Academy, including Borgs by on Öland and Stjernsund.

National Heritage Board & Academy Archival Conservation Practicum

Five-month professional curatorial internship based at the Vitterhetsakademiens Library in Stockholm, restoring rare 17th-century manuscripts, numismatic collections, and digital catalogs.

Lovisa Ulrika Royal Academy Senior Research Fellowship

Two-year senior fellowship honoring the Academy's royal founder, awarded to an established humanities scholar to complete a landmark monograph for publication in the Academy's series.

Research Officer & Academic Editor in Historical Publications

Permanent editorial and research administration post managing peer-review processes, academic journal publishing, and symposia for the Royal Swedish Academy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Nomination and eligibility require completion of a recognized Master of Arts or equivalent degree in Humanities, Archaeology, or Cultural Heritage.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral candidates must be matriculated at partner Swedish universities (Stockholm, Uppsala, Lund, Gothenburg).
Language proficiency
Swedish and/or English at advanced scholarly level (CEFR C1/C2).
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 5-page research proposal detailing scholarly methodology, archival sources, and contributions to humanistic or archaeological knowledge.
Application fee
Zero application fee for research fellowship competitions.
Required documentation
Doctoral dissertation summary, publication list, curriculum vitae, detailed project plan, and two letters of academic nomination.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Zero tuition fees. Kungl. Vitterhetsakademien operates as a premier grant-giving and fellowship-conferring national research institution. Fellowships and researcher grants provide tax-exempt stipends (SEK 350,000–550,000/year) and institutional project funding.
