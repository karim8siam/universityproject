Utah Tech University

Utah Tech · St. George, Utah, United States

Utah Tech University is an open-access comprehensive public polytechnic university established in 1911, located in sunny St. George in the red rock country of Southwestern Utah. Renowned for active, hands-on 'open, inclusive, comprehensive, polytechnic' learning in healthcare, software engineering, mechanical engineering, and digital design, Utah Tech connects academic degree programs with the rapidly growing Silicon Slopes South tech economy.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Utah Tech comprises six colleges: the College of Science, Engineering & Technology, College of Health Sciences, College of Humanities & Social Sciences, College of the Arts, College of Education, and the College of Business. The university operates the Atwood Innovation Plaza and the modern Science, Engineering & Technology Building.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation credentials, core coursework, and transcripts with open and test-optional pathways. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.0+).
