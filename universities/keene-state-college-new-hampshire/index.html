Keene State College

Keene State · Keene, New Hampshire, United States

Keene State College is a public liberal arts college in Keene, New Hampshire, and a prominent campus of the University System of New Hampshire. Keene State is nationally recognized for its premier Safety and Occupational Health Applied Sciences program and sustainable architecture.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Keene State offers nationally recognized undergraduate and graduate programs in occupational safety, environmental studies, education, and film production. It hosts the Cohen Center for Holocaust and Genocide Studies and an OSHA Training Institute Education Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic records, writing samples, and extracurricular engagement under test-optional review. Safety Sciences master's degree applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree and science/engineering foundations.
