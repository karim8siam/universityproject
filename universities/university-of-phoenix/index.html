Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Phoenix · Pioneer in Professional Online Education, Healthcare & Information Technology. The University of Phoenix is an accredited higher education institution based in Phoenix, Arizona, specializing in accessible career-aligned undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral degree programs for adult learners and working professionals. Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), UOPX provides rigorous career-focused education across healthcare administration, cybersecurity, data science, and business management.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or accredited secondary school credential for undergraduate admission; accredited bachelor's degree with 2.5+ GPA for master's programs; master's degree with 3.0+ GPA for doctoral programs. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ for international students whose medium of instruction was not English. Flexible cohort start dates year-round with rolling admissions every 5 to 6 weeks.
