Indiana Tech

Indiana Tech · Fort Wayne, Indiana, United States

Indiana Tech is a private university in Fort Wayne, Indiana, specializing in engineering, computer science, cybersecurity, digital forensics, and business administration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Indiana Tech emphasizes career readiness through ABET-accredited engineering programs, extensive cyber defense competitions where its collegiate cyber team consistently ranks nationally, and an accelerated adult education division.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts, academic course preparation, and personal statements under test-optional guidelines. International students submit TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo scores.
