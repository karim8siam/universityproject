Hochschule Esslingen: Automotive Systems, Automation & Industry 4.0 Guide

Hochschule Esslingen (Esslingen University of Applied Sciences) is one of Germany's most highly ranked public universities of applied sciences, situated in the heart of Europe's premier automotive and industrial engineering region (Stuttgart area). Partnering directly with Daimler, Porsche, Bosch, and Festo, Esslingen delivers world-class practical engineering and applied research.

DAAD STIBET International Academic Graduation Grant at Esslingen

German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) funded scholarship supporting international master's students during their final experimental or industrial master's thesis.

Master of Engineering (MEng) in Automotive Systems & Autonomous Driving

Flagship 3-semester international graduate program taught in English, covering ADAS perception sensors, electric powertrain architecture, and vehicle dynamics.

Cooperative Doctoral Candidacy (PhD) in Industrial Robotics & Smart Automation

Doctoral research position conducted through the Baden-Württemberg Doctoral Center, developing collaborative cobots and computer vision for high-precision manufacturing.

Hydrogen Fuel Cell & Battery Systems Laboratory Research Assistantship

Applied research investigating degradation mechanisms in proton-exchange membrane (PEM) fuel cells and lithium-ion battery packs under cold climate operating cycles.

Stuttgart Automotive Cluster Mandatory Industrial Internship

Mandatory six-month engineering placement embedded within Tier-1 automotive and industrial corporations such as Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Bosch, or Mahle.

Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Connected Mobility & Cyber-Physical Systems

Full-time postdoctoral appointment developing secure 5G vehicular communication protocols and real-time edge computing frameworks for cooperative automated vehicles.

Master of Engineering in Design and Development in Automotive and Mechanical Engineering

Accredited master's program emphasizing lightweight structural design, finite element analysis (FEA), composite materials, and computer-aided engineering (CAE).

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor of Engineering or Bachelor of Science degree in mechanical, automotive, electrical, or mechatronic engineering with minimum German grade of 2.5 (or international equivalent / GPA 3.0+).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctoral degrees awarded collaboratively through the Baden-Württemberg Doctoral Association (Promotionsverband BW) and partner research universities.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS Academic 6.5; German A1/A2 required during first year of study for daily integration and corporate internships.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for cooperative doctoral candidates: 3,000-word proposal aligned with an accredited Esslingen professor and university co-supervisor.
Application fee
€0 (Central university portal) or €75 via uni-assist for international credential evaluation.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, academic transcript of records, APS certificate (for China/India/Vietnam), motivation letter, and two academic recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is legally set by the state of Baden-Württemberg at €1,500 per semester for non-EU/EEA international students, plus an administrative semester fee of approximately €180. EU/EEA citizens pay zero tuition. Generous DAAD STIBET graduation grants and industrial company thesis stipends provide substantial support.
