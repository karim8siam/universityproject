Ottawa University Arizona

OUAZ · Surprise, Arizona, United States

Ottawa University Arizona (OUAZ) is the residential campus of historic Ottawa University, located in Surprise, Arizona in the northwest Phoenix metropolitan area. Known for innovative Adawe advising, accelerated business degrees, exercise science, and collegiate athletics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

OUAZ's modern campus includes Founders Hall, the O'Dell Center for Athletics, and state-of-the-art biological science and human performance laboratories. Students benefit from an integrated block schedule allowing focused courses and accelerated degree completion.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admission requires high school graduation with a minimum 2.0 GPA (test-optional). International candidates submit proof of English proficiency (TOEFL 70+ / IELTS 6.0+). Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's with a 2.75 GPA.
