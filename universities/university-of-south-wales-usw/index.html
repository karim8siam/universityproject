Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

The University of South Wales (Prifysgol De Cymru) is one of the largest universities in the UK, operating major campuses in Pontypridd, Cardiff, and Newport. Internationally recognized for its National Cyber Security Academy (GCHQ/NCSC certified), Aerospace Centre with operational flight simulators, and award-winning Cardiff film and animation studios.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • National Cyber Security Academy—certified by GCHQ and the NCSC
  • USW Aerospace Centre with full-size Jetstream commercial aircraft and CAE flight simulator
  • Cardiff Campus Creative Graduate School—top UK incubator for animation and visual effects
  • Extensive partnerships with Airbus, GE Aviation, BBC Cymru Wales, and Welsh Police Forces

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • BSc (Hons) Applied Cyber Security (Undergraduate Degree) — NCSC-certified degree delivered in the National Cyber Security Academy covering penetration testing, network defense, and malware reverse engineering.
  • BEng (Hons) Aeronautical Engineering (Undergraduate Degree) — RAeS-accredited aerospace program focusing on propulsion, composite aircraft structures, CFD aerodynamics, and avionics.
  • MA Animation (Computer Animation / Stop-Motion) (Postgraduate Degree) — ScreenSkills Select accredited master's training visual storytellers in 3D modeling, character rigging, and industry pipeline production.
