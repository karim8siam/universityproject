Griffith University (Gold Coast): Biomedical Sciences, Marine Ecology & Law Guide

Griffith University's Gold Coast campus is the university's largest campus, located in Southport, Queensland, at the heart of the Gold Coast Health and Knowledge Precinct. Ranked in the top 2% of universities globally, the Gold Coast campus is home to over 21,000 students and leading institutes including the Institute for Glycomics, the Cities Research Institute, and the Griffith Institute for Tourism. It delivers internationally acclaimed research and degrees in medicine, allied health, marine engineering, coastal ecology, and architecture.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Australian Bachelor's degree (AQF Level 7) or international equivalent with a minimum cumulative GPA of 4.5 on a 7.0 scale; specialized health and engineering programs may require specific undergraduate prerequisites.
Doctoral admission eligibility
First-class or upper second-class Honours degree (AQF Level 8) or Research Master's degree with a substantial thesis component; confirmed support from an eligible Griffith academic supervisor.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) for most postgraduate programs; medical, nursing, and law programs require IELTS 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5 or 7.0.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for doctoral and MPhil applicants: 2-4 page research proposal outlining research questions, literature review, proposed methodology, and alignment with Griffith research institutes.
Application fee
$50 AUD application fee for international direct applicants (often waived for registered partner agents).
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree conferral certificates, CV/resume, two academic referee reports, research proposal (HDR only), and passport identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic postgraduate students ranges from $9,000 to $14,000 AUD per year under Commonwealth Supported Places (CSP). International tuition averages $33,500 to $43,000 AUD per year for business, arts, and science degrees, and up to $48,000 AUD for engineering and pharmacy. Griffith awards over $10 million AUD in international scholarships annually, including Vice Chancellor's International Scholarships covering up to 50% of tuition.
