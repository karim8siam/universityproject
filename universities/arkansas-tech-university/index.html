Arkansas Tech University: Mechanical Engineering, Cybersecurity & Emergency Management Guide

Established in 1909 in Russellville in the scenic Arkansas River Valley, Arkansas Tech University (ATU) is a comprehensive public doctoral university celebrated for applied STEM degrees, nationally recognized emergency management and disaster science programs, ABET-accredited mechanical and electrical engineering, and fisheries and wildlife biology research.

Collaborative doctoral research fellowship evaluating freshwater fish population dynamics, agricultural runoff ecotoxicology, and biodiversity restoration in the Arkansas River basin.

Funded research project assessing additive manufacturing microstructure integrity, fatigue testing in aerospace aluminum alloys, and heat transfer enhancement.

Six-month cooperative education placement with Entergy's Arkansas Nuclear One generating station and regional power utilities executing electrical distribution and systems engineering.

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship leading spatial modeling projects on community flood evacuation dynamics, AI disaster damage assessment, and social vulnerability mapping.

Foundation scholarship assisting incoming undergraduate and graduate scholars who demonstrate academic dedication, moral leadership, and community service.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from a regionally accredited institution with a minimum cumulative undergraduate GPA of 2.70 out of 4.0 (or 3.00 in the last 60 undergraduate hours).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Doctor of Education (Ed.D.) in School Leadership; requiring an accredited master's degree, minimum 3.25 graduate GPA, and education leadership portfolio.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no individual band below 6.0), or Duolingo English Test 105+ for international non-native English speakers.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for master's thesis applicants: 1,000-word statement of research intent describing proposed investigation in mechanical engineering, emergency informatics, or biology.
Application fee
$50 USD non-refundable application fee for international applicants ($0 for domestic undergraduate applications).
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts from all post-secondary institutions, financial certification of support, copy of passport, two letters of recommendation, and statement of purpose.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Arkansas Tech University is approximately $8,800 USD per year for Arkansas domestic undergraduate students and approximately $15,800 USD per year for out-of-state/international students. ATU provides International Academic Excellence Scholarships covering up to $4,500 USD/year, alongside graduate teaching assistantships.
