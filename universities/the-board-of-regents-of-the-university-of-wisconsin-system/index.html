The Board of Regents of the University of Wisconsin System

UW Board of Regents · Madison, Wisconsin, United States

The Board of Regents of the University of Wisconsin System is the constitutional governing body responsible for establishing policies, academic program approvals, and financial governance across the 13 universities and 26 campuses of the UW System. Guided by the legendary Wisconsin Idea—that education should influence people's lives beyond the classroom—the Regents oversee a $6.8 billion budget and world-leading research in nuclear fusion, dairy genomics, stem cells, and biotechnology.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions standards established by the Board of Regents across all UW campuses. High school diploma with college-preparatory credits (4 English, 3 math, 3 science, 3 social science). TOEFL iBT 71+ (92+ UW-Madison) or IELTS 6.0+ (7.0+ UW-Madison). UW-Madison Early Action November 1, 2026; Regular decision January 15, 2027; comprehensive campuses rolling through June 1, 2027.
