Kinder-Uni Rostock

Kinder-Uni Rostock is a premier university science communication and youth outreach academy founded by the University of Rostock and the Hanseatic City of Rostock.

Academic overview & research priorities

Affiliated with the historic University of Rostock (founded 1419), Kinder-Uni Rostock provides university lectures, interactive laboratory sessions, and STEM experimentation workshops taught by leading university professors, inspiring the next generation of researchers.

Research admissions & international criteria

Open enrollment for school students aged 8 to 12 across Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. University education student internships and researcher didactic fellowships require enrollment at University of Rostock or an affiliated German university.
