University of Mannheim: Economics, Business School & Doctoral Excellence

The University of Mannheim (Universität Mannheim) is widely regarded as 'the Harvard of Germany', famous for Germany's top-ranked Department of Economics and Mannheim Business School, housed inside the baroque Mannheim Palace. It excels in empirical economics, quantitative social sciences, and data science.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Center for Doctoral Studies in Economics (CDSE) Full PhD Fellowship

Germany's top-ranked doctoral program in economics modeled after elite US PhD programs, covering microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, and behavioral economics.

Center for Doctoral Studies in Business (CDSB) Doctoral Fellowship

Five-year doctoral training program at Mannheim Business School specializing in finance, accounting, taxation, marketing, management, and information systems.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Economics (Mannheim Master in Economics - MME)

Germany's highest-ranked economics master's program taught in English, offering rigorous foundations in empirical econometrics, game theory, and macroeconomic policy.

Mannheim Center for Data Science (MCDS) Research Assistantship

Interdisciplinary data science post analyzing natural language processing for political texts, algorithmic bias, and large-scale social network dynamics.

ZEW - Leibniz Centre for European Economic Research Applied Practicum

Applied policy internship at ZEW Mannheim working on European competition policy, green innovation economics, and digital market regulation.

Collaborative Research Center SFB 884 Postdoctoral Fellowship in Political Economy

Postdoctoral fellowship at DFG Collaborative Research Center 'Political Economy of Reforms' studying democratic backsliding, populist voting behavior, and fiscal reforms.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Data Science (Mannheim Master in Data Science - MMDS)

Interdisciplinary master's combining computational data analytics, machine learning, text analytics, and social data science taught entirely in English.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Relevant bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in economics, business administration, social sciences, or informatics with above-average final grade (equivalent to German 2.5 or better).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences (GESS) requires an outstanding bachelor's or master's degree, strong quantitative background, and high GRE/GMAT scores.
Language proficiency
C1 English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 95+ or IELTS Academic 7.0+) for English-taught master's and all GESS doctoral programs.
Research statement / Proposal
For GESS PhD applicants: letter of motivation (2–3 pages) explaining research interests, academic trajectory, and mathematical background; GRE General Test required.
Application fee
€0 (applications via University of Mannheim portal).
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, transcript of records with ECTS grading scale, GRE/GMAT test report, CV, and two academic referee letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for domestic, EU/EEA students, and all doctoral researchers is €0 (only a semester social contribution of ~€205 applies). For non-EU/EEA master's students, Baden-Württemberg state law sets tuition at €1,500 per semester (€3,000/year). GESS doctoral students receive full scholarships of €1,450/month.
