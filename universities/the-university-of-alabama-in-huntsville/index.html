The University of Alabama in Huntsville

UAH · Huntsville, Alabama, United States

The University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH) is a top-tier Carnegie R1 public research university located in Cummings Research Park—the second-largest research park in the United States. Situated adjacent to NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center and Redstone Arsenal in 'Rocket City,' UAH is nationally acclaimed for aerospace engineering, space plasma physics, atmospheric science, and cybersecurity.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UAH operates six colleges: Engineering, Science, Business, Arts, Humanities & Social Sciences, Nursing, and Honors. Its prominent research centers include the Center for Space Plasma and Aeronomic Research (CSPAR), the Rotorcraft Systems Engineering and Simulation Center, and the Center for Cybersecurity Research and Education (CCRE).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.9) and standardized scores (ACT/SAT optional). Graduate admissions require an accredited baccalaureate degree with a 3.0+ cumulative GPA, official transcripts, department-specific GRE scores (where required), and recommendations.
