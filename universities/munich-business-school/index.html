Institutional Overview

Munich Business School (MBS), founded in 1991 in the Bavarian capital of Munich, is a premier state-recognized private business university. Holding FIBAA system accreditation, MBS specializes in international business, sports business, digital transformation management, and innovation entrepreneurship with mandatory foreign exchange semesters and corporate internships.
