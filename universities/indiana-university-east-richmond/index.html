Indiana University East

IU East · Richmond, Indiana, United States

Indiana University East (IU East) is a regional public university in Richmond, Indiana, offering prestigious Indiana University bachelor’s and master’s degrees in nursing, mathematics, business, biochemistry, and digital media.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

IU East serves Eastern Indiana and Western Ohio with small class sizes, world-class faculty mentoring, and a nationally recognized online degree completion division. Flagship programs include undergraduate nursing, pure mathematics, and natural science research.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school graduation with cumulative GPA >= 2.5 under test-optional guidelines. Reciprocity tuition rates apply to qualifying Ohio border counties.
