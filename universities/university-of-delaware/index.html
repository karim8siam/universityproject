Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university with minimum 3.0 undergraduate GPA (on a 4.0 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or Master's degree in engineering, chemistry, biology, or computing with minimum 3.2+ GPA and strong research background.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 79 minimum (100+ recommended for TA) or IELTS Academic 6.5 minimum (7.0+ recommended).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of objectives (1-2 pages) describing research interests, relevant laboratory experience, and intended faculty advisors.
Application fee
$75 non-refundable application fee via the UD Graduate College portal.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, statement of purpose, resume/curriculum vitae, and three letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral students in engineering and physical sciences receive full tuition scholarships covering 100% of tuition costs, comprehensive student medical insurance, and guaranteed annual living stipends of $33,000 to $39,000 via Graduate Research Assistant (GRA) and Teaching Assistant (TA) appointments.
