University of Massachusetts Dartmouth

UMass Dartmouth · Dartmouth, Massachusetts, United States

The University of Massachusetts Dartmouth (UMass Dartmouth) is a vibrant public doctoral research university situated on a scenic 710-acre campus on the South Coast of Massachusetts. Nationally distinguished for marine science and ocean engineering through the School for Marine Science & Technology (SMAST), UMass Dartmouth drives regional blue economy innovation, advanced materials engineering, and public law.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UMass Dartmouth spans seven colleges and schools: the College of Engineering, School for Marine Science & Technology (SMAST), Charlton College of Business, College of Arts & Sciences, College of Nursing & Health Sciences, College of Visual & Performing Arts, and the UMass Law School. The university is a designated Carnegie R2 research institution.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic through the Common App for undergraduates. Graduate applicants must present a bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, official transcripts, statement of purpose, two to three letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
