Academic overview & research priorities

Formerly known as Musashi Institute of Technology (Musashi Tech) and part of the Tokyu Group, Tokyo City University operates prominent campuses at Setagaya (Tokyo), Yokohama (Kanagawa), and Todoroki. TCU is celebrated for producing Japan's first hydrogen-fueled internal combustion engine truck, its Atomic Energy Research Laboratory, and cutting-edge degrees in urban civil infrastructure, electric vehicles, and computer science.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate entrance requires high school graduation credentials, math/physics competency, JLPT N2 or N1 (or IELTS 6.0+ for designated English coursework), and entrance examination. Graduate degree programs in Integrative Science and Engineering require a recognized bachelor's or master's degree, comprehensive research proposal, and laboratory supervisor endorsement prior to application.
