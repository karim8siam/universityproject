Foundation Academy of Amsterdam: Human Rights, Global Studies & Ethics Guide

The Foundation Academy of Amsterdam is an innovative, community-based international higher education institution situated in Amsterdam. Dedicated to the democratization of global higher education, social justice, and refugee empowerment, the Academy offers accredited and validated undergraduate, graduate, and certificate programs in Human Rights, Global Leadership, Peace Studies, and International Sustainable Development, welcoming students from over 40 nationalities through hybrid and on-campus pedagogy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree in humanities, social sciences, law, or international development, or equivalent international credential; evidence of civic activism or community engagement.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not applicable; alternative higher education academy focusing on certificates, diplomas, and validated master's degrees.
Language proficiency
Proficiency in English at CEFR B2 level (validated through personal interview, previous English-medium education, or standard test like IELTS 6.0 / Duolingo 105).
Research statement / Proposal
Personal statement of purpose (500-800 words) describing commitment to global social justice, community human rights advocacy, and ethical leadership.
Application fee
€50 administrative application fee (waivers available for displaced scholars and asylum seekers).
Required documentation
Undergraduate degree certificate, academic transcripts, curriculum vitae, statement of purpose, one letter of recommendation, and copy of passport or residence card.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is deliberately structured to maintain global accessibility, averaging €2,800 to €4,500 per academic year. Generous Global Justice Scholarships and Refugee Higher Education Grants provide up to 100% tuition waivers for displaced students and grassroots human rights defenders.
