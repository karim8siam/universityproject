Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1965 by eminent astrophysicist Dr. Toshio Araki with the counsel of Nobel laureate Hideki Yukawa, Kyoto Sangyo University is a comprehensive private university located on the scenic Kamigamo campus in northern Kyoto, internationally renowned for astrophysics (housing the Koyama Astronomical Observatory), life sciences, information science, and economics.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Observational astrophysics and infrared spectroscopy (Koyama Astronomical Observatory 1.3m telescope)
  • Life sciences, protein structural biology, and mitochondrial molecular research
  • Computer science, networked embedded systems, and natural language processing
  • International economics, business administration, and modern foreign languages

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Astrophysics (Koyama Observatory Track) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year research master's degree utilizing the 1.3-meter Araki Telescope for infrared spectroscopy of comets, novae, and stellar atmospheres.
  • Doctor of Philosophy in Life Sciences (Ph.D.) (Doctoral Degree) — Four-year advanced research degree investigating mitochondrial biogenesis, protein folding mechanisms, and plant stress physiology.
  • Bachelor of Science in Information Science and Technology (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year degree providing rigorous training in software architecture, cybersecurity, machine learning, and data engineering.
