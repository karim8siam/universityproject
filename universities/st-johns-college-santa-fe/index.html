St. John's College

St. John's College Santa Fe · Santa Fe, New Mexico, United States

St. John's College in Santa Fe, New Mexico, is an extraordinary private liberal arts college nestled in the foothills of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains. Operating a sister campus in Annapolis, Maryland, St. John's is internationally renowned for its distinctive 'Great Books' curriculum. Without conventional majors, lecture halls, or textbooks, all students engage in rigorous, discussion-based Socratic seminars exploring foundational texts of philosophy, literature, mathematics, and science spanning 3,000 years.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

At St. John's, professors are called 'tutors' and guide discussion rather than lecturing. The college offers a single undergraduate Bachelor of Arts in Liberal Arts, alongside the Graduate Institute's Master of Arts in Liberal Arts and Master of Arts in Eastern Classics (studying Sanskrit, classical Chinese, Daoism, Confucianism, and Buddhism). Its mountain campus features the Peterson Student Center, the Meem Library, and an astronomical observatory.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are test-optional and holistic, evaluating an applicant's intellectual curiosity, reading habits, and capacity for thoughtful seminar discourse through analytical essays. Graduate Institute applications require an accredited bachelor's degree, intellectual essays reflecting on foundational texts, and academic references.
