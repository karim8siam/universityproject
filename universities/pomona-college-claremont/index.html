Pomona College

Pomona College · Claremont, California, United States

Pomona College is one of the world's most prestigious undergraduate liberal arts colleges and the founding member of the Claremont Colleges consortium in Claremont, California. Renowned for extraordinary academic rigor, need-blind admissions with 100% demonstrated need met with zero loans, high medical/law school placement, and pioneering student-faculty research.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Pomona spans a 140-acre campus with access to the combined resources of the Claremont Colleges (7 institutions sharing libraries, labs, and cross-registration). It features the Seeley G. Mudd Science Center, the Benton Museum of Art, and the Sontag Center for Collaborative Creativity ('The Hive').

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are highly selective with an acceptance rate below 7%. Pomona evaluates high school curriculum rigor, standardized test scores (test-optional), personal essays, extracurricular impact, and recommendations. Pomona meets 100% of demonstrated financial need with grants rather than loans for all domestic and international admitted students.
