Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma with 2.5+ GPA for freshmen; transfer applicants with 12+ collegiate credits require 2.0+ GPA. Seamless transfer pathways for technical college associate degree holders. TOEFL iBT 71+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants whose primary language is not English. Rolling admissions throughout the year; Fall semester priority August 1, 2027; Spring semester priority January 5, 2027.
