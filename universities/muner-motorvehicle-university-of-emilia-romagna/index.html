Institutional Overview

MUNER (Motorvehicle University of Emilia-Romagna) is a unique, world-renowned academic consortium established through the collaboration of four historic universities (Bologna, Ferrara, Modena & Reggio Emilia, and Parma) and the world's most legendary automotive and motorsport companies: Ferrari, Lamborghini, Ducati, Dallara, Maserati, Pagani, Haas F1, AlphaTauri, Magneti Marelli, and HPE COXA. Taught entirely in English, MUNER delivers cutting-edge master's degrees in high-performance automotive and electric vehicle engineering.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Direct laboratory and wind tunnel training with Ferrari, Ducati, Lamborghini, and Dallara chief engineers
  • Specialized tracks in High Performance Car Design, Racing Car Design, Advanced Powertrain, and Autonomous Driving
  • Hands-on telemetry analysis, vehicle dynamics track testing at Varano and Fiorano circuits
  • Over 95% immediate employment rate in premier motorsport teams and luxury hypercar manufacturers

Featured Academic Degrees & Curricula

Master's Degree

Master of Science in Advanced Automotive Engineering (AAE)

Two-year 120-ECTS English-taught degree with tracks in Racing Car Design, High Performance Car Design, Powertrain, and Advanced Motorcycle Engineering.

Master's Degree

Master of Science in Electric Vehicle Engineering (EVE)

Cutting-edge degree covering battery chemistry, high-voltage electric motors, battery management systems (BMS), and power electronics.

Master's Degree

Master of Science in Electronic Technologies for Electric Vehicles (ETEV)

Specialized postgraduate program focusing on automotive microelectronics, vehicular telematics, sensors, and functional safety (ISO 26262).
