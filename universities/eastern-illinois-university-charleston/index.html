Eastern Illinois University

Eastern Illinois University (EIU) is a public comprehensive university in Charleston, Illinois, celebrated for its College of Education, pre-health sciences, renewable energy center, and graduate programs in clinical psychology and technology.

Academic overview & research priorities

Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission, EIU is home to the Center for Clean Energy Research and Education (CENCERE), the Tarble Arts Center, and renowned educator preparation academies. EIU consistently achieves high graduation and job placement rates among Midwest regional universities, combining accessible tuition with personalized faculty mentorship.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions require high school graduation credentials with minimum 2.50 GPA; EIU is test-optional. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor’s degree with a 2.75+ cumulative GPA and department review.
