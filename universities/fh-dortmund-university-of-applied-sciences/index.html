Fachhochschule Dortmund

Fachhochschule Dortmund (FH Dortmund - University of Applied Sciences and Arts) is one of the largest universities of applied sciences in the industrial Ruhr region, renowned for computer science, embedded automotive systems, award-winning photography and design, and biomedical technology.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating eight vibrant faculties across three campuses in Dortmund (Sonnenstraße, Emil-Figge-Straße, and Max-Ophüls-Platz), FH Dortmund bridges engineering rigor and creative arts. The university is an international hub for applied software engineering, digital business, robotic drive systems, and European documentary photography.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor's admission requires the German Fachhochschulreife, Abitur, or foreign secondary equivalent validated via uni-assist. Design degrees require a portfolio review and artistic entrance test. Master's degrees require an accredited bachelor's degree in a congruent field with minimum German grade 2.5. English-taught programs require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 88; German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4.
