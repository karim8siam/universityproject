Bunkyo Gakuin University: Health Sciences, Global Business & Faculty Guide

Established in 1924 by educator Ishiko Shimada and celebrating its centennial milestone, Bunkyo Gakuin University is a premier private Japanese university with campuses in the heart of Tokyo (Hongo Campus, Bunkyo-ku) and the suburban city of Fujimino, Saitama. With faculties spanning Health Medical Technology, Human Studies, Foreign Studies, and Business Administration, Bunkyo Gakuin provides compassionate, highly accredited education preparing healthcare practitioners, physical therapists, and bilingual global executives.

Bunkyo Gakuin University International Student Special Tuition Reduction Award

Merit-based tuition waiver awarded to privately financed international students admitted to undergraduate and graduate faculties at Bunkyo Gakuin University.

Master of Science in Clinical Laboratory Technology and Healthcare Sciences

Two-year graduate program providing advanced laboratory research training in clinical biochemistry, diagnostic microbiology, medical genetics, and clinical immunology.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Clinical Psychology and Cognitive Behavioral Science

Three-year doctoral program providing clinical training and empirical research methodologies in clinical psychological assessment, counseling therapies, and neurodevelopmental support.

Molecular Biomarker Discovery in Clinical Diagnostics Research Project

Applied biomedical research initiative validating high-sensitivity fluorescent immunoassay techniques for early detection of metabolic disorders and oncological biomarkers.

Tokyo University Hospital and Regional Healthcare Center Clinical Practicum

Intensive 16-week clinical internship rotating students through clinical chemistry, microbiology, and transfusion medicine laboratories at university hospitals in central Tokyo.

Shimada Memorial Global Education and Cultural Leadership Fellowship

Research fellowship honoring founder Ishiko Shimada, supporting collaborative scholarship on women's higher education, global citizenship, and multicultural coexistence.

Associate Professor of Clinical Biochemistry and Molecular Diagnostics

Tenured faculty position teaching undergraduate clinical chemistry lectures, leading practical laboratory courses, and directing diagnostic research at the Hongo campus in Tokyo.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited undergraduate university (minimum 16 years of schooling) with a Bachelor's degree in health sciences, business, humanities, or social welfare.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree satisfying MEXT third-cycle criteria in medical technology, clinical psychology, or business administration.
Language proficiency
Japanese language proficiency at JLPT N2 or N1 level required for lecture comprehension and clinical hospital laboratory rotations; English-taught global courses require TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.0.
Research statement / Proposal
Formal research proposal in Japanese (1,500–2,000 characters) detailing clinical or academic research objectives.
Application fee
¥35,000 non-refundable graduate application examination fee.
Required documentation
Certified degree diplomas, academic transcripts of all undergraduate years, JLPT certificate, personal financial support statement, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition in humanities and business is approximately ¥820,000 to ¥980,000 per year; health medical technology tuition is approximately ¥1,350,000 annually reflecting clinical laboratory expenses (plus a first-year matriculation fee of ¥250,000). Privately financed international students with student visa status receive Bunkyo Gakuin's special 30% tuition fee reduction scholarship.
