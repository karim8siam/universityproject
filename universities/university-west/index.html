University West: Work-Integrated Learning, Thermal Spraying & Robot Automation

University West (Högskolan Väst) is Sweden's government-designated premier university for Work-Integrated Learning (AIL). Located in Trollhättan near Gothenburg, it hosts the world-class Production Technology Centre (PTC) specializing in additive manufacturing, thermal spraying, and robotic welding alongside GKN Aerospace and Volvo.

Collaborative research project alongside GKN Aerospace evaluating microstructure evolution and residual stress in wire-feed additive manufacturing of superalloys.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) with at least 90 ECTS in a relevant engineering, IT, or business subject area via Universityadmissions.se.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS with 60 ECTS at advanced level) with a completed scientific degree project.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B (IELTS 6.5 with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT 90 with minimum 20 in writing).
Research statement / Proposal
For doctoral positions (fully salaried PhD employment): CV, publication sample, and research motivation addressing the advertised project.
Application fee
900 SEK application fee paid via Universityadmissions.se (EU/EEA students exempt).
Required documentation
Certified bachelor's degree diploma, complete transcripts of records, proof of English, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens. For non-EU/EEA students, master's tuition is approximately 130,000 to 145,000 SEK per year. All PhD candidates are employed as university staff on full monthly salaries (starting at ~31,000 SEK/month), paying zero tuition fees.
