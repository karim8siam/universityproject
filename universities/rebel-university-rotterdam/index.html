Academic overview & excellence

Located in the innovative maritime harbor of Rotterdam at the historic Wijnhaven, Rebel University operates as the knowledge development and professional academy of Rebel. It delivers masterclasses, research fellowships, and executive master's modules in complex infrastructure finance, offshore wind economic modeling, urban climate adaptation, and blended finance for the Global South. It partners regularly with Erasmus University Rotterdam and TU Delft.

Research admissions & criteria

Programs at Rebel University target ambitious professionals, infrastructure directors, municipal planners, and graduate economists. Formal admission to advanced executive seminars requires a recognized bachelor's or master's degree in economics, finance, engineering, or public administration, alongside at least two years of relevant sector experience. Instruction is predominantly conducted in English.
