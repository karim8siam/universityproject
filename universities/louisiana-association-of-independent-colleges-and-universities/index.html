Louisiana Association of Independent Colleges and Universities

LAICU · Baton Rouge, Louisiana, United States

The Louisiana Association of Independent Colleges and Universities (LAICU) represents Louisiana's ten non-profit independent higher education institutions—including Tulane University, Loyola University New Orleans, Xavier University of Louisiana, Dillard University, and Centenary College. Conducting groundbreaking research in tropical infectious diseases, environmental coastal law, pharmacy, and jazz composition, LAICU institutions confer over 20% of all bachelor's degrees and 35% of all professional medical/law degrees in Louisiana.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Baton Rouge · Statewide Consortium of Ten Independent Higher Education Institutions. The Louisiana Association of Independent Colleges and Universities (LAICU) represents Louisiana's ten non-profit independent higher education institutions—including Tulane University, Loyola University New Orleans, Xavier University of Louisiana, Dillard University, and Centenary College. Conducting groundbreaking research in tropical infectious diseases, environmental coastal law, pharmacy, and jazz composition, LAICU institutions confer over 20% of all bachelor's degrees and 35% of all professional medical/law degrees in Louisiana.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Applications processed through member independent universities (Tulane, Loyola, Xavier, Dillard, Centenary, Franciscan Missionaries, etc.). Holistic review of high school GPA and secondary achievements. TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.5+ (100+ Tulane) for international candidates. Early action priority deadline November 15, 2026; Regular decision deadline January 15, 2027.
