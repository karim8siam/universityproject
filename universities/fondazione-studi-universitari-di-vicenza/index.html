Fondazione Studi Universitari di Vicenza (Università di Vicenza Hub)

Fondazione Studi Universitari di Vicenza (UniVicenza) is the premier public-private university polo of the Veneto region, federating degree programs and cutting-edge research laboratories of the University of Padua and the University of Verona.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1990 by municipal, provincial, and industrial institutions to provide higher education for one of Europe’s most export-intensive industrial districts, the Fondazione Studi Universitari di Vicenza hosts over 4,500 students in the historic San Nicola campus and the Complesso Universitario di Viale Margherita. The polo hosts the Department of Management and Engineering (DTG) of the University of Padua, as well as renowned degree courses in International Business, Multimedia Economics, and Mechatronics Engineering from both Padua and Verona. Its laboratories lead Italian industry in laser sintering, metal mechanics, supply chains, and digital business.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Laurea Magistrale in Ingegneria Gestionale (Management Engineering, UniPD) (Master's) — Flagship two-year master's exploring global operations, supply chain resilience, lean product development, and industrial data analytics.
  • Laurea Magistrale in Ingegneria Meccatronica (Mechatronics Engineering, UniPD) (Master's) — Advanced degree combining electromechanical design, automated robotics control, micro-sensors, and predictive machine maintenance.
  • Corso di Laurea Triennale in Economia e Commercio (International Business, UniVR) (Bachelor's) — Three-year economics degree focused on international trade law, accounting standards, and cross-border commercial strategy.
  • Dottorato di Ricerca in Ingegneria Meccatronica e dell'Innovazione Meccanica (UniPD) (Doctorate) — Three-year doctoral research program based at the Vicenza DTG campus focusing on industrial robotics, precision tooling, and smart manufacturing.
