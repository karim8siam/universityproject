Academic overview & excellence

Operating seamlessly in partnership with the University of Alberta Faculty of Medicine & Dentistry, the University of Alberta Hospital campus delivers cutting-edge tertiary patient care coupled with world-class translational biomedical research. It houses specialized units including the Mazankowski Alberta Heart Institute, the Stroke Ambulance Research Program, and the Alberta Transplant Institute. Trainees and clinical investigators benefit from state-of-the-art surgical suites, human genomics sequencing facilities, and dedicated phase-I/II clinical trial infrastructure.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission to residency programs, clinical research fellowships, and graduate degrees based at the University of Alberta Hospital requires formal application through the University of Alberta Faculty of Graduate & Postdoctoral Studies or the Post-Graduate Medical Education (PGME) office. Candidates must hold an accredited MD, MBBS, or relevant MSc/PhD degree, submit verification of clinical license eligibility, academic transcripts (minimum 3.3/4.0 GPA), verified English proficiency (IELTS 7.0+), and letters of reference from department directors.

Distinguished faculty & researchers (3)

Dr. A.M. James Shapiro

Professor of Surgery and Director of Clinical Islet Transplant Program · Department of Surgery & Alberta Transplant Institute

Dr. A.M. James Shapiro is the world-renowned surgeon who pioneered the Edmonton Protocol for clinical islet cell transplantation, transforming the clinical management and curative prospects of brittle Type 1 diabetes.

Islet Transplantation Edmonton Protocol Type 1 Diabetes

Dr. Darren Freed

Professor of Surgery and Director of Research, Mazankowski Heart Institute · Division of Cardiac Surgery

Dr. Darren Freed is an international leader in thoracic organ preservation and ex-vivo machine perfusion, developing innovative clinical technologies to recondition and evaluate donor hearts prior to transplantation.

Ex-Vivo Organ Perfusion Heart Transplantation Ventricular Assist Devices

Dr. Ashfaq Shuaib

Professor of Medicine and Director of the Stroke Program · Division of Neurology

Dr. Ashfaq Shuaib has led numerous pivotal international clinical trials in acute stroke management, neurovascular protection, and advanced collateral circulation imaging at the University of Alberta Hospital.

Stroke Neuroprotection Cerebrovascular Imaging Collateral Circulation
