Flinders University

Founded in 1966 in Adelaide, Flinders University is a member of Australia's Innovative Research Universities (IRU) network, internationally renowned for medical research (integrated at Flinders Medical Centre), maritime archaeology, defense technologies and marine robotics at the Tonsley Innovation District, clean technology, and paleontology. Consistently ranked in the world's top 2% of universities, Flinders offers comprehensive graduate research scholarships and state-of-the-art research hubs.

Premier Commonwealth and university-funded doctoral scholarship providing complete financial independence, tuition waivers, and health coverage for outstanding international research scholars.

Internationally renowned doctoral training in maritime archaeology, underwater heritage management, and digital 3D shipwreck documentation across Australia and the Indo-Pacific.

Advanced two-year coursework and research Master's degree delivered at the Tonsley Innovation District, covering machine learning, autonomous systems, and advanced network cybersecurity.

Doctoral research conducted within Flinders Medical Centre and the Institute for Nanoscale Science and Technology, designing targeted drug delivery systems and point-of-care clinical diagnostics.

Applied robotics research fellowship situated in South Australia's Tonsley high-tech precinct, developing robotic manipulation, marine inspection drones, and defense manufacturing automation.

Eight-week intensive summer vacation research placement working directly with leading researchers in clean vortex fluidic technology, functional graphene materials, and surface chemistry.

Full-time research role investigating temperate reef resilience, coastal seagrass restoration, and environmental DNA (eDNA) monitoring along South Australia's Southern Ocean coastline.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (Honours Class IIA or higher) with a minimum Grade Point Average of 5.0 on a 7.0 scale (equivalent to 70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA) in an appropriate field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Four-year Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours, or a research Master's degree with a significant thesis component demonstrating rigorous research capability.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no individual band score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing 21, speaking 18); medical and legal research degrees require IELTS 7.0 (minimum 6.5).
Research statement / Proposal
A 1,000 to 1,500 word formal research proposal outlining research questions, literature review, proposed methodology, and identified Flinders supervisory team.
Application fee
AUD $100 international student application processing fee submitted via the Flinders University online application system.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree parchment copies, detailed curriculum vitae, evidence of academic writing, and two academic referee reports.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate research candidates selected for the Australian Government Research Training Program (AGRTP) or Flinders University Research Scholarship (FURS) receive 100% full tuition fee offset sponsorships for up to 3.5 years, along with an annual tax-free living stipend of AUD $33,500 to $36,000 per year and overseas student health cover (OSHC).
