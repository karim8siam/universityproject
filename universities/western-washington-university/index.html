Western Washington University

WWU · Bellingham, Washington, United States

Western Washington University is a top-ranking public comprehensive master's university established in 1893, perched along Bellingham Bay overlooking the Salish Sea and North Cascades in Bellingham, Washington. Celebrated for environmental science and marine biology at the Huxley College of the Environment (now College of the Environment), manufacturing engineering technology at the Vehicle Research Institute (VRI), psychology, and marine science at the Shannon Point Marine Center, WWU is an environmental and clean-tech innovation beacon in the Pacific Northwest.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

WWU encompasses seven colleges: the College of the Environment, College of Science and Engineering, College of Humanities and Social Sciences, College of Business and Economics, Fairhaven College of Interdisciplinary Studies, Woodring College of Education, and the College of Fine and Performing Arts. The university operates the Shannon Point Marine Center in Anacortes, Washington.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA, course rigor, and personal statements with test-optional reviews. Graduate master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three recommendation letters, and English proficiency (TOEFL 86+ or IELTS 7.0+). Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) provide stipends of $16,000–$18,000/year plus tuition waivers.
