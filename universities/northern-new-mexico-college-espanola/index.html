Northern New Mexico College

Northern New Mexico · Española, New Mexico, United States

Northern New Mexico College is a public Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) and Native American-Serving Nontribal Institution (NASNTI) in Española, New Mexico. Known for electromechanical engineering technology, environmental science, nursing, and Spanish colonial arts.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Northern New Mexico College features the Department of Engineering and Technology and the Department of Nursing and Health Sciences. Maintains strategic research and workforce alliances with Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) and the surrounding Eight Northern Indian Pueblos.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Northern New Mexico College maintains an open-door admissions policy for all high school graduates or GED recipients. Bachelor of Engineering and Registered Nursing programs require prerequisite mathematics and science competency benchmarks.
