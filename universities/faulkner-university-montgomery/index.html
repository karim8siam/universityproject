Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1942, Faulkner University operates its central campus in the capital city of Montgomery, alongside extended learning centers in Birmingham, Mobile, and Huntsville. The university comprises five colleges: Thomas Goode Jones School of Law (ABA-accredited Juris Doctor), College of Health Sciences, College of Arts and Sciences, Harris College of Business, and V.P. Black College of Biblical Studies.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.0; test-optional for ACT/SAT). The Thomas Goode Jones School of Law requires a completed bachelor's degree, LSAT score, and Law School Admission Council (LSAC) registration. Graduate healthcare programs (DPT, SLP, PA) require a bachelor's degree, prerequisite science coursework, and CAS-portal submissions.
