University of Urbino: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Research Guide

Founded in 1506 in the hilltop UNESCO World Heritage city of Urbino, the University of Urbino Carlo Bo (Università degli Studi di Urbino Carlo Bo) is one of the oldest universities in Europe. Renowned for pharmaceutical chemistry, biomolecular sciences, sports science, environmental law, and Renaissance humanities, UniUrb integrates historic scholarship with modern laboratories.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ERDIS Marche Regional Government Full Study Grant & Housing Bursary

Comprehensive public regional need-and-merit scholarship guaranteeing tuition-free study, meal vouchers at university refectories, and housing in Urbino.

Single-Cycle Master in Pharmacy and Industrial Pharmaceutical Chemistry

Five-year accredited single-cycle master's program conferring professional pharmacist licensing and training in synthetic drug design and pharmaceutical toxicology.

Doctoral Fellowship in Biomolecular and Health Sciences at UniUrb

Three-year fully funded doctoral research program exploring targeted cancer therapeutics, mitochondrial bioenergetics, and epigenetic biomarkers of neurodegeneration.

Mount Cimone Atmospheric Research Station & Apennine Climate Project

Research placement participating in continuous atmospheric greenhouse gas monitoring, aerosol mass spectrometry, and ozone tracking in the northern Apennines.

Galleria Nazionale delle Marche Renaissance Conservation Practicum

Practical curatorial internship conducted inside the Ducal Palace of Urbino (Palazzo Ducale) analyzing panel paintings by Piero della Francesca and Raphael.

UniUrb Postdoctoral Assegno di Ricerca in Medicinal Chemistry and Drug Discovery

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship designing and synthesizing small-molecule enzyme inhibitors targeting neurodegenerative diseases and antibiotic resistance.

Ricercatore a Tempo Determinato (RTD-A) in Environmental Law and European Policy

Three-year assistant professorship delivering undergraduate law lectures and conducting research on European Union climate law and circular economy compliance.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (Laurea Triennale or equivalent minimum 180 ECTS credits) in a related disciplinary field.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Second-cycle Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale) with honors standing, selected through national public competitive examination (concorso pubblico).
Language proficiency
CEFR B2 level English certification (IELTS Academic 6.0, Cambridge B2, or TOEFL iBT 78) for international English-medium programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Curriculum vitae, academic transcripts, motivation essay, and detailed research plan (for doctoral applicants).
Application fee
€20 pre-enrollment evaluation fee via the Esse3 online student portal.
Required documentation
Certified university diploma, official transcript of records, CIMEA Statement of Comparability or Declaration of Value, and valid passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at UniUrb is calculated based on the student's family economic status (ISEE parificato). Students with an ISEE below €24,000 qualify for the Italian 'No Tax Area' with zero tuition fees (paying only the mandatory €140 regional tax). International students can apply for the ERDIS Marche regional scholarship, which covers 100% of tuition, free meals, and cash stipends up to €6,800 per year.
