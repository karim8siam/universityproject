Mount Saint Vincent University

Mount Saint Vincent University (MSVU), anchored by its landmark Seton Academic Centre in Halifax, is a nationally distinguished Canadian university celebrated for its pioneering commitments to women's higher education, applied human nutrition, early childhood education, and social justice.

Academic overview & excellence

MSVU offers personalized undergraduate and master's degree programs with class sizes among the lowest in Canada. The Seton Academic Centre houses modern food development laboratories, digital media broadcast centers, and the Atlantic Tourism and Hospitality Management institute.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission requires graduation from an accredited high school or recognized university degree with a minimum grade average of 70% (B- or 2.7 GPA). International students submit proof of English proficiency (IELTS 6.5 with no band below 6.0, or TOEFL iBT 86). Master's applicants require a four-year bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA.
