Institutional Overview

Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli - Facoltà di Medicina e Chirurgia represents one of Italy's most prestigious and historic centers for medical education and clinical research. Operating major teaching hospital complexes in Naples (Sant'Andrea delle Dame) and Caserta, Vanvitelli offers world-renowned single-cycle degrees in Medicine and Surgery (both in Italian and English), medical biotechnology, and specialized medical residency schools.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Historic 500-year medical teaching tradition fused with contemporary biomedical genomics and precision medicine
  • Home to the English-taught 6-year Single-Cycle Master's in Medicine and Surgery attracting students from over 60 nations
  • Immense clinical case exposure through the two University Polyclinic complexes in Naples and Caserta
  • Pioneering research center in cardiovascular genetics, ophthalmology, rare diseases, and immuno-oncology
