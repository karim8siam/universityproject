Illinois State · Normal, Illinois, United States

Illinois State University (ISU) is Illinois’s oldest public university, celebrated as a national powerhouse in teacher education, actuarial science, cybersecurity, registered nursing, and applied computer science.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Located in Bloomington-Normal, ISU is recognized as a Center of Actuarial Excellence (CAE) by the Society of Actuaries and designated an NSA Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense. The Mennonite College of Nursing provides elite simulation-driven clinical education.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions are test-optional, reviewing high school coursework rigor, cumulative GPA (average 3.4), and essays. Graduate programs require a bachelor's degree with minimum 2.8–3.0 GPA, official transcripts, and program-specific writing samples.
