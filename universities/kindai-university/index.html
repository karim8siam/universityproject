Kindai University: Aquaculture Innovation, Medicine & Robotics Engineering Guide

Kindai University (近畿大学) in Higashiosaka, Osaka, is one of Japan's largest and most dynamic private universities, internationally renowned for pioneering the world's first complete aquaculture farming of Pacific bluefin tuna ('Kindai Tuna'), advanced regenerative medicine, and autonomous agricultural robotics.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

MEXT Japanese Government University Recommendation Scholarship

Prestigious Monbukagakusho (MEXT) scholarship allocated directly through Kindai University for top international applicants enrolling in Fisheries, Agriculture, or Applied Science.

Master of Science in Aquaculture & Marine Biotechnology

World-famous Master's degree hosted at the Fisheries Laboratory of Kindai University, providing hands-on training in bluefin tuna breeding, fish genomics, and marine ecology.

Ph.D. in Cyber-Physical Mechatronics & Agricultural Robotics

Doctoral research program developing autonomous fruit harvesting robots, AI orchard vision inspection, and precision greenhouse climate control systems.

Kindai Tuna Genome Editing & Selective Breeding Investigation

Flagship biological research project at Shirahama Fisheries Station utilizing CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing to enhance disease resistance and feed efficiency in cultured tuna.

Kansai Medical Devices & Bio-Robotics Industry Internship

Three to six-month technical internship working with medical diagnostic device manufacturers and surgical robotics developers in the Osaka-Kobe biomedical cluster.

JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowship for Overseas Researchers at Kindai

Prestigious two-year international postdoctoral fellowship conducted in partnership with Kindai faculty in organic chemistry, functional materials, or pharmacology.

Specially Appointed Assistant Professor in Artificial Intelligence & Big Data

Full-time faculty appointment in the Faculty of Informatics, teaching deep learning, cloud architectures, and supervising undergraduate capstone software engineering projects.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of 16 years of formal education with a Bachelor's degree from an accredited university.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree or professional equivalent in science, agriculture, medicine, or engineering.
Language proficiency
Japanese JLPT N2/N1 for Japanese-medium divisions; English CEFR B2 (TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.0) for international English research laboratories.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed research plan (Kenkyu-Keikaku-sho) in English or Japanese (1,500-2,000 words) approved by prospective laboratory professor.
Application fee
¥35,000 application examination fee.
Required documentation
Graduation certificate, academic transcript, research plan, letters of recommendation, and certificate of Japanese/English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual graduate tuition ranges from ¥800,000 to ¥1,150,000 (~$5,300 to $7,600 USD). International students are eligible for 30% to 50% tuition reduction scholarships, and premier doctoral researchers receive Japanese Government MEXT or JSPS research fellowships.
