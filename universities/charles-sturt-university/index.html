Charles Sturt University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Charles Sturt University (CSU) is a premier multi-campus regional Australian university situated in Bathurst, Wagga Wagga, Orange, and Albury-Wodonga. Globally recognized for agriculture, veterinary medicine, water security, and rural health, CSU houses the world-renowned Gulbali Institute for Agriculture, Water and Environment, driving transdisciplinary research across the Murray-Darling Basin.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Agriculture, Water and Environment (Gulbali Institute Fellowship)

Three-and-a-half year doctoral scholarship within the Gulbali Institute investigating Murray-Darling hydrological flow modeling, drought-tolerant crop genetics, or soil carbon sequestration.

Master of Cyber Security and Applied Threat Intelligence

Comprehensive two-year postgraduate program covering digital forensics, cloud security posture management, cryptographic systems, and offensive penetration testing in state-of-the-art cyber ranges.

Charles Sturt Rural and Regional Australian Development International Award

Tuition discount scholarship designed to attract talented international scholars to CSU's regional campuses, fostering regional industry partnerships, sustainability, and community healthcare.

National Wine and Grape Industry Centre (NWGIC) Viticulture Research Fellowship

Applied research residency at the NWGIC investigating climate change adaptations in grapevine phenology, smoke taint mitigation techniques, and precision canopy management.

AgriPark Commercial Smart Farm Drone and IoT Sensor Internship

Five-month immersion at CSU's 2,000-hectare Global Digital Farm, piloting autonomous tractors, deploying soil moisture IoT telemetry arrays, and validating multispectral crop health algorithms.

Rural Health Research Institute (RHRI) Postdoctoral Fellowship in Regional Epidemiology

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the RHRI examining healthcare access disparities, chronic disease management models, and telehealth efficacy in regional and remote Indigenous communities.

Master of Environmental Management and Sustainable Water Resources

Two-year graduate degree delivering specialized training in river basin ecology, catchment modeling, environmental impact assessment, and climate change adaptation policies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
An Australian bachelor's degree or recognized equivalent with a minimum weighted average mark (WAM) of 65% or substantial professional experience.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (H1) or Master by Research with high distinction; confirmed research supervisor and approved project outline.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no individual band score below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 79 (writing 21, speaking 18).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2,000-word research proposal articulating objectives, significance to regional/national priorities, proposed methodology, and resource requirements.
Application fee
AUD $0 online direct application fee for international applicants.
Required documentation
Certified degree transcripts, award certificates, curriculum vitae, 2 academic referee letters, and supervisor pre-endorsement form.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International postgraduate tuition at Charles Sturt University ranges from AUD $28,000 to $34,000 per year. Fully funded doctoral candidates receive Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) or CSU Postgraduate Research Scholarships providing a tax-free annual stipend of AUD $33,500, complete tuition waivers, and relocation allowances.
