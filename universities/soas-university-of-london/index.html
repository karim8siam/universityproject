SOAS University of London: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1916 and situated in the historic academic heart of Bloomsbury, SOAS University of London educates 6,000 students from more than 130 nations. As a member institution of the University of London, SOAS holds a unique global position, offering unrivaled regional expertise across humanities, social sciences, law, and languages. The SOAS Library is one of the United Kingdom’s five designated National Research Libraries, housing over 1.5 million volumes, manuscripts, and archives. SOAS researchers actively shape international diplomatic negotiations, United Nations policies, and cultural heritage preservation worldwide.
