University of the Sunshine Coast: Aquaculture, Coastal Ecology & Forestry Guide

Established in 1996 in Sippy Downs, the University of the Sunshine Coast (UniSC) is one of Australia's fastest-growing and highest-rated modern public universities. Ranked globally for sustainability and ocean science, UniSC leads international research in sustainable aquaculture, tropical forestry conservation, coastal dune geomorphology, and sports biomechanics.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

UniSC Research Training Program (RTP) International Doctoral Scholarship

Prestigious doctoral scholarship funding international PhD candidates conducting cutting-edge research in sustainable aquaculture, tropical forestry, or coastal adaptation at UniSC.

Master of Science in Tropical Aquaculture and Marine Conservation

Two-year postgraduate research degree featuring intensive fieldwork at the UniSC Bribie Island Research Centre focusing on crustacean genomics, finfish nutrition, and coral propagation.

UniSC Doctoral Research Fellowship in Subtropical Forest Restoration Ecology

Three-and-a-half year PhD fellowship at the Forest Research Institute investigating indigenous tree species propagation, carbon sequestration, and post-fire ecosystem recovery.

K'gari (Fraser Island) Coastal Dune Geomorphology & Hydrology Research

World Heritage site field investigation deploying ground-penetrating radar and geochemical isotopic tracers to study groundwater dynamics in K'gari's iconic perched dune lake systems.

Queensland Department of Agriculture and Fisheries Aquaculture Internship

Twelve-week industry internship at government research hatcheries working on commercial sea cucumber (holothurian) breeding, microalgae culture, and disease diagnostics.

UniSC Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in High Performance Sports Biomechanics

Two-year postdoctoral research appointment at the Sunshine Coast Olympic and Paralympic Training Centre developing automated inertial sensor tracking for elite Paralympic athletes.

UniSC International Student Academic Excellence Scholarship

Automatic-assessment merit scholarship granting 15% tuition relief across undergraduate and master's degree programs for high-achieving international candidates at UniSC.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (AQF Level 7) in science, engineering, or related discipline with a minimum cumulative GPA of 4.5 on a 7.0 scale (or equivalent 60%+ average).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours (Class I) or Master's degree with at least a 25% research component and distinction-level thesis grade.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands), TOEFL iBT 85 (minimum 21 in writing), or Pearson PTE Academic 58+.
Research statement / Proposal
Detailed 1,500 to 2,000-word doctoral research project outline aligned with a nominated UniSC principal research supervisor.
Application fee
$0 AUD for online postgraduate research and coursework applications.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts and degree certificates, research curriculum vitae, two academic supervisor referee reports, and evidence of English proficiency.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Australian and eligible New Zealand research candidates receive full tuition offset via the Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP). International student tuition for laboratory science degrees is approximately $32,000 AUD to $36,800 AUD per year. Competitive UniSC RTP and International Postgraduate Research Scholarships provide full tuition waivers and annual stipends of $33,500 AUD.
