TU Delft (Delft University of Technology)

Established in 1842, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft) is the oldest and largest Dutch public technical university, ranked among the top 15 engineering and technology institutions in the world. TU Delft is globally celebrated for Aerospace Engineering (ranked #1 worldwide), Civil Engineering, Architecture, and Quantum Computing through QuTech. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

TU Delft Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships

TU Delft's most prestigious merit scholarship awarded to outstanding non-EU/EEA international students admitted to MSc programmes, providing full tuition coverage and annual living expense allowances.

TU Delft Salaried PhD Positions (Promovendi under CAO-NU)

Four-year salaried doctoral research appointments across TU Delft departments, conducting frontier industrial and fundamental investigations under standard Dutch collective employment contracts.

TU Delft Master of Science in Aerospace Engineering

World-ranked #1 two-year Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) offering world-class research tracks in Aerodynamics & Wind Energy, Flight Performance & Propulsion, Space Exploration, and Aerospace Structures.

TU Delft Master of Science in Computer Science

Two-year Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) in the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science (EEMCS), offering tracks in Data Science & Technology, Software Technology, and Artificial Intelligence.

QuTech Quantum Computing Research Placements

Frontier quantum technology institute founded by TU Delft and TNO, developing scalable quantum computing architectures, topological qubits, and quantum internet protocols.

TU Delft High-Tech Aerospace & Maritime Industrial Internships

Industrial and academic graduation research internships embedding graduate engineers within European aerospace centers, wind tunnel research facilities, and naval architecture firms.

TU Delft Student Assistantships (Student-Assistenten)

Part-time campus employment for enrolled students coaching exercise sessions, running computer laboratory sessions, and assisting faculty in grading assignments.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Science degree in Engineering, Computer Science or related quantitative field (minimum 180 ECTS credits) matching specific foundational course requirements with minimum CGPA of 75%+ or 3.2/4.0.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master of Science degree (120 ECTS) with excellent academic marks, strong thesis research evidence, and successful competitive interview for an advertised doctoral vacancy.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all subscores) or TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 22 in each component).
Research statement / Proposal
For Master's: motivation statement, detailed course descriptions, and CV via Studielink and Osiaan. For PhD: comprehensive research statement aligned with advertised vacancy calls.
Application fee
€100 application fee for international applicants holding non-Dutch degrees (refunded upon successful matriculation).
Required documentation
Complete official transcripts, degree certificate, detailed descriptions of core undergraduate mathematics/programming modules, curriculum vitae, and passport scan.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Dutch national higher education regulations, EU/EEA students pay the statutory tuition fee of €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA international students, tuition is approximately €21,515 per year for Master's programs. Outstanding admitted international students can receive the Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship, covering 100% of tuition fees plus a €30,000 living allowance grant over 2 years. Doctoral candidates (Promovendi) are paid university employees under the Dutch CAO-NU collective agreement.
