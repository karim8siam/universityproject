Hellenic American University

HAU · Nashua, New Hampshire, United States

Chartered by the State of New Hampshire and accredited by the New England Commission of Higher Education (NECHE), Hellenic American University delivers transatlantic bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs connecting campuses in Nashua, NH and Athens, Greece. The university emphasizes intercultural applied linguistics, business informatics, cybersecurity, and clinical psychology.
