INN University (Rena): Game Tech, Forestry Economics & Crisis Management Guide

Inland Norway University of Applied Sciences (INN University) operates specialized cross-border research and educational partnerships with Swedish universities (Karlstad and Dalarna), celebrated for its Rena campus game development studios (Hammar Game Collective), forestry economics, and international crisis management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in computing, forestry, economics, crisis management, or related discipline with a minimum C-grade average.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 120 ECTS) with a grade of B or better; doctoral positions are fully salaried state research appointments.
Language proficiency
English proficiency equivalent to Norwegian English 1 / Swedish English 6 (IELTS Academic 6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 90).
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applications require a detailed 5-to-10-page research project plan in game technology, applied ecology, or crisis governance.
Application fee
Free direct online application through the university admissions portal.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete academic transcripts, English proficiency certificate, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for Nordic and EU/EEA citizens. Non-EU/EEA international tuition ranges from NOK 130,000 to NOK 170,000 per academic year. Erasmus+ cross-border mobility grants, Norwegian state educational fund (Lånekassen) loans, and forestry industry research awards are available.
