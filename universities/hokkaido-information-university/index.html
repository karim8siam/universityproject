Hokkaido Information University: Medical Informatics, AI Systems & Game Design Guide

Established in 1989 in Ebetsu (adjacent to Sapporo, Hokkaido), Hokkaido Information University (Do-Johodai) is a pioneer in Japanese practical computing and health information systems. With extensive facilities in medical informatics, artificial intelligence, video game programming, and satellite business administration, HIU is recognized nationwide for linking digital technology with regional healthcare and food science analytics.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited 16-year bachelor's degree in information technology, computer science, management, or natural sciences with satisfactory academic standing.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative doctoral research coordinated with national Japanese universities and medical faculties in health data telematics.
Language proficiency
Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) N2 or N1 for Japanese-taught tracks; TOEFL iBT 78 or IELTS 6.0 for international IT research students.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for graduate applicants: 2,000-character Japanese or 1,200-word English research proposal (Kenkyu Keikakusho) in software engineering or medical informatics.
Application fee
¥30,000 JPY graduate entrance examination screening fee.
Required documentation
Undergraduate graduation certificate and academic transcripts, JLPT/TOEFL scorecard, research proposal, two letters of recommendation, and financial guarantee.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Hokkaido Information University is approximately ¥880,000 to ¥1,120,000 JPY per year (plus a one-time admission fee of ¥200,000 JPY). International students are eligible for a 30% to 50% tuition reduction scholarship based on academic review, in addition to JASSO monthly honors scholarships.
