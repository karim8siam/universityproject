Zentrum für Sonnenenergie- und Wasserstoff-Forschung Baden-Württemberg (ZSW)

The Centre for Solar Energy and Hydrogen Research Baden-Württemberg (ZSW) is one of Europe’s leading applied research institutes for photovoltaics, battery technology, green hydrogen electrolysis, and renewable fuel synthesis.

Academic overview & research priorities

With state-of-the-art facilities in Stuttgart, Ulm, and Widderstall, ZSW conducts world-class research bridging laboratory discovery to industrial pilot production. Key pillars include high-efficiency thin-film CIGS and perovskite solar cells, gigawatt-scale battery cell manufacturing processes, and PEM water electrolysis.

Research admissions & international criteria

Graduate thesis students, PhD researchers, and postdocs enroll via partner universities including the University of Stuttgart and Ulm University. Candidates must hold a recognized Master of Science in Physics, Chemistry, Electrical Engineering, or Materials Science.
