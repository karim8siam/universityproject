Acknowledge Education: Degree Programs, Scholarships & Academic Faculty Guide

With educational roots dating back over 140 years to 1883, Acknowledge Education is a prestigious Australian tertiary education provider headquartered on Exhibition Street in Melbourne, with campuses across Sydney, Brisbane, and Perth. Regulated by TEQSA and ASQA, Acknowledge Education delivers accredited undergraduate degrees in Community Services, Social Work, Business, and Health Sciences.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of an Australian Bachelor's degree (or AQF Level 7 equivalent) in social sciences, health, or related human services disciplines with a minimum pass grade.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate programs articulate into postgraduate honours and master's degree research pathways across partner Australian universities.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall with no individual band below 6.0 (or equivalent PTE Academic score of 58 or TOEFL iBT 79).
Research statement / Proposal
Applicants submit verified secondary or post-secondary transcripts, certified English translations, and a genuine student statement of intent.
Application fee
AUD $250 non-refundable international admissions assessment fee.
Required documentation
Certified copies of educational qualifications, valid passport, English language certificate, statement of purpose, and resume for mature-age entry.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for accredited bachelor's degree programs is approximately AUD $8,500 to $10,500 per semester (AUD $17,000 to $21,000 per academic year across 8 standard units of study). Eligible Australian citizens may access FEE-HELP loan schemes, while international students can apply for merit-based academic scholarships providing up to 20% tuition fee remissions.
