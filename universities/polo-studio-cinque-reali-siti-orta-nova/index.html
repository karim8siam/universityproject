Polo di Studio Universitario Cinque Reali Siti - Orta Nova

The Polo di Studio Universitario Cinque Reali Siti at Palazzo Studi Padre Pio in Orta Nova is a premier digital higher education and telematics academic center in the Tavoliere delle Puglie, providing accredited undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs in jurisprudence, educational pedagogy, economics, and agricultural management.

Academic overview & excellence

Serving the historic Tavoliere agrarian region of northern Apulia, the Polo di Studio Cinque Reali Siti delivers recognized Italian state degrees in partnership with accredited telematic universities. The academic complex at Palazzo Studi Padre Pio provides digital multimedia classrooms, proctored university examination halls, academic mentoring suites, and specialized laboratories in agro-environmental law and rural economic development. Scholars engage with sustainable Mediterranean farming governance, educational psychology, and local public administration.

Research admissions & criteria

Enrollment in degree programs and professional master's diplomas at Polo di Studio Cinque Reali Siti follows the regulations of the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR). Candidates must hold a secondary school diploma (Diploma di Maturità) for Laurea Triennale courses or an accredited Laurea for Laurea Magistrale and Master programs. International qualifications require a Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) or CIMEA comparability certificate, alongside Italian language proficiency (B2).

Two-year advanced degree qualifying graduates for roles as pedagogical coordinators, educational consultants, and specialized inclusion instructors in school and social welfare institutions.

Merit-based scholarship dedicated to young scholars from the province of Foggia pursuing university degrees in digital humanities, rural education, or legal administration.

One-year executive professional master (60 ECTS) instructing healthcare professionals in hospital budgeting, clinical risk management, health economics, and administrative digital record keeping.
