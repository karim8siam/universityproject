North Idaho College

North Idaho · Coeur d'Alene, Idaho, United States

North Idaho College is a comprehensive public community college situated on the scenic shores of Lake Coeur d'Alene in Coeur d'Alene, Idaho. Renowned for nursing, forestry, mechanical trades, dental hygiene, and university transfer education.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

North Idaho College operates the Meyer Health and Sciences Building, Parker Technical Education Center in Rathdrum, and the Workforce Training Center. Provides seamless articulation agreements with the University of Idaho, Boise State, and Lewis-Clark State.

Research admissions & international qualifications

North Idaho College maintains an open-door admissions policy for high school graduates or GED recipients. Competitive health professions programs (Registered Nursing, Dental Hygiene) require secondary application and prerequisite sciences.
