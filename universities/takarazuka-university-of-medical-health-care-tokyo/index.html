Academic overview & research priorities

Operating its modern urban facility in Nishi-Shinjuku, Takarazuka University of Medical Health Care Tokyo Campus combines classical Japanese therapeutic manual medicine with modern sports science. TUMH educates national certified Judo Therapists (Judo Seifukushi), Acupuncturists, and Moxibustionists who serve top professional athletic teams, national Olympic training centers, and regional orthopedic clinics.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate entrance requires graduation from an accredited high school, submission of academic transcripts, a statement of vocational purpose, and Japanese language ability (JLPT N2 or higher). International candidates may also enroll in the accredited Japanese Language and Healthcare Preparatory Bekka course prior to entering full degree tracks.
