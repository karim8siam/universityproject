Free University of Bozen-Bolzano: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The Free University of Bozen-Bolzano (Libera Università di Bolzano / Freie Universität Bozen / unibz) is Italy's leading multilingual public-private research university, situated in the heart of the Dolomites. Renowned for its multilingual curriculum (English, German, Italian), unibz consistently ranks top among Italian universities for research quality, student-to-professor ratio, and international orientation in computer science, sustainable agriculture, and engineering.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

PhD in Advanced-systems Engineering & Computer Science

Fully funded doctoral program at unibz focusing on ontology-based data integration, business process technologies, software engineering, robotics, and cyber-physical systems.

European Master in Computational Logic / Master in Computer Science

Two-year Master of Science taught entirely in English, training students in automated deduction, description logics, cloud data infrastructure, and AI system engineering.

PhD in Mountain Environment and Agriculture (Sustainable Ecosystems)

Unique doctoral training program addressing alpine agriculture, hydrology, climate change impacts on mountain ecosystems, and circular bioeconomy in the Alps.

Autonomous Province of South Tyrol Merit & Need Graduate Study Grants

Regional financial support scheme funded by the Autonomous Province of Bozen/Bolzano to ensure accessible higher education for deserving international students enrolled at unibz.

Master in Energy Engineering & Building Sustainability

Joint Master of Science offered in collaboration with the Vienna University of Technology (TU Wien), specializing in renewable energy systems, decarbonized building design, and thermal grid optimization.

NOI Techpark Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Human-Robot Interaction

Applied research post at the NOI Techpark innovation hub in Bolzano, developing safe collaborative robot manipulators and field robotics for alpine forestry and industry 4.0 automation.

Master in Eco-Social Design (Transformative Design for Sustainable Practices)

Pioneering interdisciplinary Master's program equipping designers to build resilient social-ecological transformations, community commons, and regenerative regional production models.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree in matching field with minimum 180 ECTS and certified English proficiency at B2 or C1 level.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or international Master of Science); competitive ranking based on curriculum vitae, research project proposal, and interview.
Language proficiency
Proof of language proficiency according to the program regulations: English (IELTS 6.5+, TOEFL iBT 88+ or Cambridge Advanced); many master's are taught entirely in English.
Research statement / Proposal
A 4-6 page doctoral research project outlining scientific objectives, state of the art, methodology, and expected timeline.
Application fee
€50 application fee per study program.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma, diploma supplement, curriculum vitae in Europass format, motivation letter, language certificates, and two academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at unibz is modest, capped at approximately €1,364 per year for bachelor's and master's degrees. International students can apply for Autonomous Province of South Tyrol study grants of up to €6,000 per year plus free meals. For doctoral programs, every admitted student receives a fully funded institutional scholarship of €17,500 to €22,000 net per year with no tuition fees.
