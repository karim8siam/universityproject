University of Utah

The University of Utah (the U) is the flagship public research institution of Utah and an AAU member, internationally recognized for pioneering computer graphics, the Utah Artificial Heart, genetics at the Huntsman Cancer Institute, and clean energy innovation.

Official university portal ↗Independent researcher review

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1850 in the foothills of the Wasatch Mountain Range in Salt Lake City, the University of Utah is a premier Carnegie R1 research university serving over 35,000 students. The university holds historic status in computer science as the birthplace of modern 3D computer graphics (founded by Ivan Sutherland and David Evans, training Pixar and Adobe founders). The U also operates the world-renowned Huntsman Cancer Institute, the John and Marcia Price College of Engineering, and University of Utah Health, generating over USD $680 million in annual sponsored research awards.

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • PhD in Computer Science (School of Computing) (Doctorate) — World-renowned doctoral curriculum in computer graphics, scientific visualization, distributed systems, and quantum architecture.
  • Master of Science in Entertainment Arts and Engineering (EAE) (Master's) — Consistently ranked the #1 video game development graduate program in the world by the Princeton Review.
  • PhD in Biomedical Engineering (Price College of Engineering) (Doctorate) — Elite research doctorate developing neural prostheses (inventor of the Utah Electrode Array), bionic limbs, and cardiac assist devices.
  • Bachelor of Science in Mining Engineering (Bachelor's) — One of America's oldest mining engineering programs specializing in critical minerals, copper extraction, and sustainable mine tailings management.
