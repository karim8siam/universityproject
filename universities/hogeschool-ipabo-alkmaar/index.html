Institutional Overview

Hogeschool IPABO (Instituut voor Pedagogiek en Onderwijskunde), operating an established campus on Gabriël Metsustraat in Alkmaar (North Holland) alongside its Amsterdam campus, is an accredited, specialized Dutch university of applied sciences entirely dedicated to primary teacher education (pabo). Renowned for pedagogical innovation, early childhood bilingualism, and inclusive classroom leadership, IPABO maintains deep partnerships with primary school boards across the North Holland region.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Specialized monocentric focus ensuring 100% institutional resources are dedicated to pedagogical excellence
  • Immediate full-year school immersion placements throughout primary schools in North Holland
  • Top-rated National Student Survey (NSE) scores for teacher accessibility and curriculum quality
  • Pioneering research center in Culturally Responsive Pedagogy and Early Literacy

Featured Academic Degrees & Curricula

Bachelor's Degree

Bachelor of Education in Primary School Teaching (Pabo Voltijd)

Four-year professional bachelor's degree qualifying graduates as fully certified Dutch primary school teachers (Groepsleerkracht basisonderwijs).

Bachelor's Degree

Academische Pabo (Joint Bachelor with University)

Four-year double-degree combining practical teacher training with university-level educational sciences and pedagogical research.

Post-Graduate Diploma

Zij-instroom in het Beroep (Lateral Entry Primary Teaching)

Two-year intensive customized program enabling career changers holding existing bachelor's degrees to teach in primary schools while completing certification.

Scholarship

Tegemoetkoming Leraren (Dutch National Teacher Tuition Subsidy)

Government grant from the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science (OCW) reimbursing tuition fees for students training to become certified teachers.

Funding: Up to €2,530 annual direct tuition reimbursement Deadline: June 30, 2027
Course

Bachelor Leraar Basisonderwijs (Pabo Voltijd) Enrolment

Four-year full-time professional education degree blending child psychology, language acquisition, mathematics pedagogy, and classroom internships in Alkmaar.

Funding: DUO student grant, public transport pass (studentenreisproduct), and performance grant Deadline: May 1, 2027
Internship

Noord-Holland Primary School Teaching Internship (LIO-Stage)

Final-year intensive Teacher-in-Training (Leraar in Opleiding - LIO) placement with primary schools in Alkmaar, Hoorn, or Den Helder with independent classroom teaching.

Funding: €1,200 - €1,800 monthly trainee teacher salary (LIO-vergoeding) Deadline: April 15, 2027
Course

Academische Pabo (Dual Degree Education & Pedagogy) Enrolment

Four-year elite dual track combining pabo teacher training at IPABO with educational sciences research at university level.

Funding: Eligible for statutory tuition and DUO master's loan transition Deadline: May 1, 2027
Grant

IPABO Pedagogical Research Center Grant

Research grant supporting action research projects on multilingual classroom learning, digital pedagogical tools, and child wellbeing in urban schools.

Funding: €2,500 research stipend and project publishing budget Deadline: April 1, 2027
Course

Zij-instroom in het Beroep (Lateral Entry Certification) Enrolment

Two-year paid pathway for university graduates transitioning into primary school teaching while earning full professional licensure.

Funding: Subsidized through Dutch government Zij-instroom grant covering 100% of study costs Deadline: June 15, 2027
Scholarship

Erasmus+ European Teacher Exchange Grant at IPABO

EU scholarship enabling IPABO education students to undertake a semester practicum in Scandinavian or European bilingual primary schools.

Funding: €380 - €450 monthly mobility grant plus travel allowance Deadline: March 15, 2027
