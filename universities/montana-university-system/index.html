Montana University System

MUS · Helena, Montana, United States

The Montana University System (MUS) is the unified public higher education system of Montana, governed by the Montana Board of Regents from the state capital in Helena. Encompassing 16 public universities, colleges of technology, and tribal college partnerships—including flagship campuses Montana State University and the University of Montana—MUS provides cutting-edge education in wilderness medicine, mining engineering, astrophysics, and environmental law.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Helena · Constitutional Higher Education Governance for Big Sky Country. The Montana University System (MUS) is the unified public higher education system of Montana, governed by the Montana Board of Regents from the state capital in Helena. Encompassing 16 public universities, colleges of technology, and tribal college partnerships—including flagship campuses Montana State University and the University of Montana—MUS provides cutting-edge education in wilderness medicine, mining engineering, astrophysics, and environmental law.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions coordinated through individual MUS campuses. College-prep high school curriculum (4 years English, 3 years math, 3 years social studies, 2 years lab science) with minimum 2.5 GPA or ACT 22 / SAT 1120 for four-year flagship entry. TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS 6.5+ across MUS institutions. Rolling admissions; priority fall deadline February 1, 2027; spring priority deadline November 1, 2026.
