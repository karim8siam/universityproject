Stanford University Centre

The Stanford University Centre in Oxford, situated at Stanford House on High Street, is the premier British academic centre of Stanford University operated through the Bing Overseas Studies Program (BOSP) in partnership with the University of Oxford.

Academic overview & excellence

Scholars participate in the renowned Oxford tutorial system, paired one-on-one with distinguished Oxford University dons while undertaking advanced coursework in British politics, biomedical ethics, modern European history, Shakespearean literature, and engineering economics.

Research admissions & criteria

Admission is open to Stanford undergraduates and visiting scholars maintaining high academic achievement (cumulative GPA 3.3+). Visiting fellows undergo a rigorous academic selection process reviewing research plans, tutorial topic viability, and faculty recommendations.
