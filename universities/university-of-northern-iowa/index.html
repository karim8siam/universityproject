University of Northern Iowa

UNI · Cedar Falls, Iowa, United States

The University of Northern Iowa (UNI) is a high-ranking public comprehensive university located in Cedar Falls, Iowa. Founded in 1876 as the state normal school, UNI is celebrated for producing Iowa's top educators, housing the nationally acclaimed Wilson College of Business with its pristine CPA pass rates, operating the Metal Casting Center for 3D sand-mold additive manufacturing, and leading Great Plains tallgrass prairie restoration.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNI spans four colleges: the College of Education, David W. Wilson College of Business, College of Humanities, Arts and Sciences, and College of Social and Behavioral Sciences. The university is home to the Tallgrass Prairie Center and the UNI Metal Casting Center, a national leader in foundry additive manufacturing.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school Regent Admission Index (RAI) score based on GPA, class rank, and core coursework. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
