Theological University Apeldoorn (TUA): Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Dating back to 1894 and situated in a leafy historic quarter of Apeldoorn, Theological University Apeldoorn (TUA) is a state-accredited academic research university specializing in Reformed theology, classical languages (Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek, Latin), and moral philosophy. Home to the world-renowned Classic Reformed Theology research center and collaborating closely with Oxford and Zurich, TUA offers accredited bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees that attract international theological scholars from across the globe.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Dutch Vwo diploma with Latin/Greek or relevant university bachelor's degree; language entry tests
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Dutch & English (Master of Theology and PhD degrees available 100% in English)
