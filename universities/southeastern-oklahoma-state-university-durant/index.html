Southeastern Oklahoma State University

Southeastern Oklahoma State · Durant, Oklahoma, United States

Southeastern Oklahoma State University is a public regional university located in Durant, Oklahoma—the headquarters of the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma. Known as 'The Campus of the Rising Sun', Southeastern is internationally renowned for its School of Aviation Sciences at Eaker Field, its vibrant Choctaw and Native American cultural partnerships, accredited business school, and premier teacher education programs.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Southeastern features the Aviation Sciences Institute (operating its own fleet of modern training aircraft and advanced flight simulators at Eaker Field), the John Massey School of Business, the School of Education and Behavioral Sciences, and the School of Arts and Sciences. It maintains strong academic ties to regional aerospace hubs in Oklahoma and North Texas.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (minimum 2.7) or ACT composite score (minimum 20). Aviation flight candidates must obtain an FAA First or Second Class Medical Certificate and TSA clearance. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 2.75 minimum GPA, transcripts, and program-specific letters of intent.
