Marche Polytechnic University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Marche Polytechnic University (Università Politecnica delle Marche / UNIVPM) is a public technological university based in Ancona on Italy's central Adriatic coast. Renowned for marine biology (deep-sea coral and benthic ecosystems), biomedical robotics, sustainable agricultural economics, and industrial engineering, UNIVPM is a leading Italian research institution with strong ties to European maritime industry.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Marine Biology and Deep-Sea Ecology

Doctoral research conducted within the Department of Life and Environmental Sciences (DiSVA), studying deep-sea Mediterranean biodiversity, cold-water coral reefs, and climate impacts on marine food webs.

PhD in Industrial Engineering & Collaborative Robotics (Industry 4.0)

Doctoral research at the Department of Industrial Engineering and Mathematical Sciences (DIISM), developing collaborative human-robot manufacturing lines, vibration diagnostics, and smart materials.

ERDIS Marche Regional Agency for the Right to Higher Education Study Grant

Regional financial support scheme funded by the Marche Regional Authority (ERDIS) for domestic and international students enrolled in degree courses at UNIVPM.

Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering (Medical Robotics & Diagnostics)

Two-year Master of Science taught in English, preparing specialized engineers in wearable biometric sensors, robotic prosthetic devices, and deep-learning ultrasound image analysis.

Master of Science in Environmental Biology and Marine Science

English-taught Master of Science including hands-on research on the Adriatic sea, instructing students in marine protected area governance, coral restoration, and aquaculture sustainability.

Department of Information Engineering Postdoctoral Fellowship in Healthcare AI

Two-year postdoctoral position in the VRAI (Vision, Robotics & Artificial Intelligence) Lab, developing computer vision algorithms for surgical guidance and tele-rehabilitation monitoring.

UNIVPM Marine Technology & Coastal Hydrodynamics Graduate Traineeship

Six-month research internship at the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering (DICEA), modeling breakwater wave dissipation, harbor sediment transport, and coastal storm resilience.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A recognized bachelor's degree comprising at least 180 ECTS credits in engineering, computer science, marine science, or economics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or international Master of Science); admission evaluated via curriculum vitae, research project proposal, and an oral examination/interview.
Language proficiency
B2 level certificate in English (IELTS 6.0+, TOEFL iBT 78+) for English-taught degrees; Italian B2 for Italian-taught degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
A 3-5 page research project proposal demonstrating critical knowledge of the state of the art, proposed methodologies, and alignment with UNIVPM research groups.
Application fee
€30 to €50 administrative application fee.
Required documentation
Bachelor/Master diploma, academic transcripts, Europass CV, research project, declaration of value or CIMEA statement of comparability, and two recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Marche Polytechnic University is progressive and based on the ISEE Parificato economic indicator. Students with an ISEE below €25,000 pay €0 tuition. Maximum annual tuition is capped at approximately €2,200 per year. Admitted international students are eligible for ERDIS Marche regional study grants providing up to €7,500 per year, free student hall accommodation, and free cafeteria dining. All PhD candidates receive funded scholarships of €16,243 to €20,000 net per year.
