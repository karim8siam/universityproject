KTH Södertälje: Sustainable Production Logistics & Circular Industrial Systems Guide

KTH Södertälje is the specialized industrial research and education campus of KTH Royal Institute of Technology, co-located in Södertälje Science Park alongside global industrial giants Scania and AstraZeneca, focused on circular manufacturing, smart production logistics, and digital twin engineering.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor of Science in mechanical engineering, industrial engineering, or chemical engineering (180 ECTS) with at least 30 ECTS in mathematics and mechanics.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (minimum 240 ECTS) in production engineering or systems logistics; doctoral students receive full Swedish university salaries.
Language proficiency
English 6 / English B from Swedish upper secondary school, or IELTS 6.5 (minimum 5.5 across all components) or TOEFL iBT 90.
Research statement / Proposal
Doctoral applications require motivation letter addressing advertised industrial research projects.
Application fee
SEK 900 via national portal UniversityAdmissions.se for non-EU applicants.
Required documentation
Bachelor's diploma, complete university transcripts, English test results, curriculum vitae, and passport copy.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free for citizens of EU/EEA and Switzerland. International student tuition is approximately SEK 155,000 per academic year. KTH Scholarship waivers, Scania industrial fellowship awards, and Swedish Institute (SI) scholarships are accessible.
