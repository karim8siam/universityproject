Institution Overview & Profile

Twente Pathway College (TPC), located on the picturesque American-style campus of the University of Twente in Enschede, provides rigorous International Foundation Year programmes in Engineering & Technology and Behavioural & Social Sciences, in partnership with Navitas, delivering guaranteed direct progression into world-leading Bachelor of Science degrees at the University of Twente.

Institution TypeInternational Research University Pathway College (University of Twente & Navitas)
Main CampusUniversity of Twente Campus, Drienerl, Enschede, Overijssel
AccreditationDutch Ministry of Education Recognized University Partner / Navitas
Admissions RouteDirect online application via Navitas Twente Portal (September start)
Language of InstructionEnglish (IELTS Academic for UKVI/International 5.0 to 5.5 overall with minimum 5.0 in writing)
Financial Aid & GrantsTPC Academic Merit Scholarships & University of Twente Scholarship (UTS) on progression

Key Academic Strengths

  • Located directly on the University of Twente campus, the Netherlands' only true American-style residential campus
  • Guaranteed progression into premier UT Bachelor of Science degrees: Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering
  • World-class high-tech ecosystem hosting the MESA+ Institute for Nanotechnology and DesignLab
  • Full access to University of Twente student associations, sports complexes, and innovation incubators

Featured Academic & Degree Programs

University Foundation

International Foundation Year in Engineering and Technology

Three-term rigorous preparation in advanced mathematics, physics, and programming guaranteeing entry into Year 1 of BSc Mechanical, Electrical, or Computer Science at UT.

University Foundation

International Foundation Year in Behavioural and Social Sciences

Specialized academic pathway leading directly into Bachelor of Science degrees in Psychology, Communication Science, or Management, Society & Technology.

University Foundation

Foundation in International Business Administration (IBA)

Interdisciplinary business preparation integrating technology entrepreneurship, digital innovation, and corporate finance at UT.
