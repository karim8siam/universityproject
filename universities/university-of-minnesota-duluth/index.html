University of Minnesota Duluth

The University of Minnesota Duluth (UMD) is a prominent public research university within the University of Minnesota system, situated overlooking the western tip of Lake Superior in Duluth, Minnesota. UMD integrates the personalized instruction of a medium-sized institution with the extensive scientific and doctoral research resources of a world-class land-grant and sea-grant university system.

Official institutional website ↗Location: Duluth, Minnesota, USA

Institutional Overview

UMD comprises the Swenson College of Science and Engineering, Labovitz School of Business and Economics, College of Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences, and the University of Minnesota Medical School Duluth Campus. UMD is internationally recognized for freshwater limnology through the Large Lakes Observatory (LLO) and the Natural Resources Research Institute (NRRI).

Research Profile, Admissions & Scholarships

Admissions at UMD evaluate high school course rigor, unweighted GPA (3.4+ middle 50%), and optional ACT/SAT scores. Graduate and medical school applications require discipline-specific GRE/MCAT scores, letters of recommendation, and research proposals. UMD awards numerous merit scholarships including the UMD Chancellor's Scholarship and full graduate research assistantships.
