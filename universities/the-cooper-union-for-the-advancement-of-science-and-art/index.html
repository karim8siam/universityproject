The Cooper Union for the Advancement of Science and Art

The Cooper Union · New York, New York, United States

The Cooper Union for the Advancement of Science and Art is an internationally venerated private college located in Manhattan's East Village, New York City. Founded in 1859 by industrialist and philanthropist Peter Cooper under the vision that education should be accessible to all, Cooper Union is acclaimed for the Irwin S. Chanin School of Architecture, the School of Art, and the Albert Nerken School of Engineering.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Cooper Union comprises three world-renowned professional schools: the Irwin S. Chanin School of Architecture, the School of Art, and the Albert Nerken School of Engineering. Every admitted undergraduate student receives an automatic half-tuition scholarship (currently $22,275 annually), with the institution steadily executing its historic plan to restore 100% full-tuition scholarships for all undergraduate scholars.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are among the most competitive in the United States. Engineering applicants require outstanding academic records in advanced calculus and physics. Architecture and Art applicants must submit extensive original artistic portfolios and complete the legendary, rigorous Cooper Union Home Studio Test.
