Hochschule Mittweida - University of Applied Sciences

Hochschule Mittweida (University of Applied Sciences Mittweida) is a historic Saxon engineering institution celebrated for laser application technologies, blockchain informatics, forensic science, and media engineering.

Academic overview & research priorities

Renowned for its Laserinstitut Hochschule Mittweida (LHM) and Blockchain Competence Center, HS Mittweida provides cutting-edge laboratory education combining micro-materials processing, forensic cyber investigation, automation, and applied biotechnology.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require a German Hochschulzugangsberechtigung or international secondary diploma recognized via uni-assist. German proficiency at DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 level is required for German curricula; English programs require TOEFL iBT 80+ or IELTS 6.0+.
