Brandon University: Prairie Ecology, School of Music & Graduate Research

Brandon University is a distinguished public university in southwestern Manitoba, celebrated across Canada for its internationally renowned School of Music, groundbreaking prairie environmental science, Indigenous studies, rural development research, and personalized graduate education.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Brandon University Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA) & Teaching Fellowship

Merit-based graduate research and teaching assistantships awarded to incoming full-time thesis-based master's students in environmental science, rural development, and education.

Master of Science (MSc) in Environmental & Life Sciences (Prairie Agro-Ecology)

Rigorous thesis-based master's degree investigating prairie wetland conservation, carbon sequestration in agricultural soils, and aquatic toxicogenomics in the Assiniboine River basin.

Collaborative Doctoral Candidacy in Prairie Agricultural Soil Microbiology with UManitoba

Four-year joint doctoral research fellowship investigating soil fungal mycorrhizae, biological nitrogen fixation, and microbial diversity in regenerative prairie cropping.

Rural Development Institute (RDI) Northern Communities Research Assistantship

Applied research post at RDI evaluating rural economic diversification, food security in remote northern communities, and Indigenous resource governance models.

Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada (AAFC) Brandon Research Centre Practicum

Hands-on research practicum at the federal AAFC Brandon Centre mastering crop rotational modeling, greenhouse gas flux measurements, and precision fertilizer management.

School of Music Postdoctoral Fellowship in Contemporary Performance & Acoustic Ecology

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Brandon School of Music investigating prairie soundscape preservation, community music therapy, and experimental acoustic performance.

Master of Music (MMus) in Collaborative Piano & Chamber Performance

Internationally celebrated performance master's training collaborative pianists in art song repertoire, instrumental sonata collaboration, and chamber music leadership.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Four-year undergraduate degree from an accredited university with a minimum grade point average of 3.0 (B average) in the last 60 credit hours of study.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral opportunities available in environmental biology and rural studies in partnership with the University of Manitoba.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 86 (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
For research master's (MSc Environmental Science / Rural Development): 2–3 page statement of research intent describing alignment with faculty supervisors.
Application fee
$140 CAD non-refundable international application fee submitted online.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, 2–3 confidential academic reference letters, CV, and statement of research intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International graduate tuition at Brandon University is among the most affordable in Canada, averaging approximately $15,800 to $18,500 CAD per year. Graduate teaching assistantships (GTA) and research fellowships provide $8,000 to $14,000 CAD per year in financial support.
