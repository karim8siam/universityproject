Institution Overview & Profile

Università Primo Levi, established in 1989 in the heart of historic Bologna, is one of Italy's most prestigious institutions of continuous university-level education (educazione permanente). Dedicated to democratic access to culture, the arts, philosophy, scientific literacy, and civic engagement, Primo Levi engages thousands of adult and continuing scholars each year through over 250 advanced academic courses.

Institution TypeAccredited Continuous Higher Education University (Istituzione Culturale Superiore)

Key Academic Strengths

  • Over 35 years of recognized leadership in adult higher learning and intergenerational cultural dialogue
  • Academic faculty drawn from the University of Bologna (Alma Mater) and leading cultural institutions
  • Rich curriculum covering art history, philosophy, contemporary geopolitics, literature, and astronomy
  • Partnership with Cineteca di Bologna, MAMbo (Modern Art Museum), and regional archaeological institutes
