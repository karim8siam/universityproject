Lehrpark der Fachhochschule Erfurt

The Lehrpark der Fachhochschule Erfurt is an extensive 6-hectare botanical and landscape research teaching park, driving sustainable urban forestry, nursery production, and garden design in Thuringia.

Academic overview & research priorities

Housing the Faculty of Landscape Architecture, Horticulture and Forestry at FH Erfurt, the Lehrpark serves as a living laboratory with over 3,000 woody plant taxa, experimental green roofs, perennial trial gardens, and greenhouse climate test chambers focusing on climate-resilient urban green spaces.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions through Fachhochschule Erfurt for bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Landscape Architecture and Horticultural Science. Requirements: Fachhochschulreife or Abitur, proof of a 6 to 12-week pre-study practical internship in green industries, and German language proficiency (DSH-2/TestDaF 4).
