Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1958 as Brevard Engineering College to educate scientists and engineers working on the American space program at Cape Canaveral, Florida Tech is an R2 high research university. Its faculty and alumni have participated in every major NASA manned and unmanned mission, operating premier facilities including the Buzz Aldrin Space Institute, FAA Center of Excellence for Commercial Space Transportation, and high-performance wind tunnels.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate applicants require strong secondary preparation in calculus, physics, and chemistry (average GPA 3.7+), standardized test scores, and personal essays. Master's and doctoral degree applicants require an accredited bachelor's or master's degree in engineering or physical sciences (minimum GPA 3.0/4.0), GRE scores, statement of research intent, and letters of recommendation. Graduate research assistantships provide full tuition remission and competitive monthly stipends.
