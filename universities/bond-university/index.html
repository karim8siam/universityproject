Bond University

Established in 1989 on the Gold Coast, Bond University is Australia's first private, non-profit university, recognized nationally for scoring the highest student satisfaction ratings in Australia for nearly two decades. Renowned for its world-class Faculty of Health Sciences & Medicine, the Institute for Evidence-Based Healthcare (IEBH), the Clem Jones Centre for Regenerative Medicine, and cutting-edge business analytics and sports science, Bond delivers accelerated three-semester-per-year postgraduate degrees and generous higher-degree research scholarships.

Bond University Postgraduate Research Scholarships (BUPRS) & HDR Stipends

Bond's flagship doctoral research award providing complete financial support, tuition offsets, and living allowances for outstanding domestic and international PhD scholars across all faculties.

Bond PhD in Evidence-Based Healthcare & Clinical Epidemiology

Doctoral research conducted within the world-renowned Institute for Evidence-Based Healthcare (IEBH), investigating antimicrobial resistance stewardship, non-pharmacological interventions, and clinical research transparency.

Bond Master of Science (MSc) in Business Analytics & Artificial Intelligence

Accelerated two-year Master's degree delivered in 16 months across Bond's tri-semester calendar, covering predictive analytics, big data engineering, machine learning pipelines, and managerial decision modeling.

Bond PhD in Regenerative Medicine & Stem Cell Therapeutics

Doctoral research based at the Clem Jones Centre for Regenerative Medicine, investigating induced pluripotent stem cells, neurodegenerative disease models, and tissue engineering for chronic ocular conditions.

Centre for Professional Legal Education Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Bond Faculty of Law, examining international dispute resolution, online mediation frameworks, and artificial intelligence integration in legal pedagogy.

Bond Institute for Health & Sport Summer Research Traineeship

Eight-week summer vacation research placement within the Tactical Research Unit (TRU), evaluating physical load monitoring, injury prevention, and ergonomic performance in military and tactical populations.

Bond Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Health Systems & Meta-Research

Postdoctoral research fellow post at the Institute for Evidence-Based Healthcare, analyzing biases in clinical trial reporting, evaluating health policy evidence translation, and implementing meta-research methodologies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years) from an accredited university with a minimum credit average (65% or 5.0/7.0 GPA scale); competitive health programs require relevant clinical prerequisites.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree with First Class Honours, or recognized Master's degree with a substantial thesis component (minimum 25% research) and demonstrated scholarly output.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (no individual band below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 79; Medicine and Health Sciences require IELTS 7.0 (minimum 6.5 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
A formal research proposal of 1,000 to 2,000 words defining research questions, methodology, significance, and proposed Bond academic supervisors.
Application fee
AUD $0 application fee for direct online postgraduate research applications.
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree award certificates, comprehensive CV, research proposal, and 2 academic referee appraisals.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Higher Degree by Research (HDR) candidates awarded Bond University Postgraduate Research Scholarships (BUPRS) or Australian Government RTP fee offsets receive 100% full international tuition waivers for up to 3.5 years, along with a living stipend of AUD $32,000 to $34,500 per year (indexed annually) and standard overseas student health cover (OSHC).
