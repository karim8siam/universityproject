University of Arizona Cooperative Extension

UA Cooperative Extension · Tucson, Arizona, United States

The University of Arizona Cooperative Extension, operated through the College of Agriculture, Life and Environmental Sciences (CALES) at the University of Arizona, brings cutting-edge scientific research directly to Arizona's rural and urban communities. Spanning all 15 Arizona counties and five tribal nations, UA Extension spearheads global advancements in desert crop genetics, Colorado River water conservation, controlled environment agriculture, and heat resilience.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Graduate degrees and funded extension fellowships administered via UA Graduate College and CALES departments (Biosystems Engineering, Hydrology & Atmospheric Sciences, Environmental Science). TOEFL iBT 79+ or IELTS Academic 7.0+ for international graduate applicants. Fall semester priority funding deadline January 15, 2027; Spring deadline September 1, 2026; Extension field internships accepted on rolling schedule.
