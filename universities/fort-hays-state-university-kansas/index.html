Fort Hays State University

Fort Hays State University (FHSU) is a forward-thinking public university on the high plains of Kansas, nationally renowned for affordable higher education, paleontological research at the Sternberg Museum of Natural History, and cybersecurity.

Academic overview & research priorities

FHSU is celebrated for its award-winning FHSU Online division and on-campus institutes including the Sternberg Museum of Natural History, housing world-class Cretaceous marine fossil collections, and the Robbins College of Business and Entrepreneurship.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions follow Kansas Board of Regents standards (min 2.25 GPA or 21 ACT / 1060 SAT). Graduate programs require a Bachelor degree with a minimum 2.5 GPA (3.0 for last 60 credit hours), letters of recommendation, and personal statement.
