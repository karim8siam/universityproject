Università Ca' Foscari (Ca' Foscari University of Venice)

Founded in 1868 as Italy's first business school, Università Ca' Foscari Venezia is a world-class public university housed in gothic palazzi overlooking the Grand Canal of Venice. Globally celebrated for economics, management, Asian and Middle Eastern languages and cultures, environmental humanities, and climate science, Ca' Foscari hosts Italy's foremost doctoral program in the Science and Management of Climate Change (in collaboration with the Euro-Mediterranean Center on Climate Change - CMCC) and expansive European research projects.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Ca' Foscari PhD Scholarships in Science and Management of Climate Change

Joint doctoral research program with the Euro-Mediterranean Center on Climate Change (CMCC), training future leaders in climate dynamics, vulnerability assessment, adaptation economics, and international climate treaties.

Ca' Foscari Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) in Economics and Finance (English-Taught)

Two-year international Master of Science in English offering specialized tracks in Quantitative Finance, Risk Management, and Sustainable Economics at Ca' Foscari's Department of Economics in San Giobbe.

Ca' Foscari PhD in Environmental Sciences & Coastal Lagoon Dynamics

Doctoral research conducted at the Scientific Campus in Via Torino, leveraging the Venice Lagoon natural laboratory to investigate marine sediment contamination, sea level rise impacts, and coastal wetland restoration.

Ca' Foscari Master of Arts in Cultural Heritage and Digital Humanities

Two-year English-taught Master's program combining Venetian art history, architectural heritage preservation, photogrammetry, and digital archiving for global museums and cultural institutions.

The New Institute Centre for Environmental Humanities (NICHE) Postdoctoral Fellowship

Postdoctoral research fellowship at NICHE in Venice, exploring coastal human-water relationships, environmental literature, and public humanities responses to ecological crises.

Ca' Foscari Polar Science & Ice Core Chemistry Summer Traineeship

Eight-week summer research placement at the Institute of Polar Sciences (CNR-ISP) on the Ca' Foscari scientific campus, analyzing trace organic markers and paleoclimate dust in Alpine and Antarctic ice cores.

Ca' Foscari Assistant Professor (RTD-A) in Sustainable Finance & ESG Metrics

Three-year fixed-term Assistant Professorship (Ricercatore a Tempo Determinato Tipologia A) at the Department of Management, conducting empirical research on green bonds, ESG investment metrics, and climate financial risk.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years, equivalent to Italian Laurea) with strong academic standing (minimum 70% or 3.0/4.0 GPA); subject-specific curricular prerequisites in economics, humanities, or sciences.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized second-cycle Master's degree (Italian Laurea Magistrale or equivalent) with high honors; passing evaluation in the annual public competitive entrance exam (dottorato di ricerca).
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 85; B2 certificate accepted for several English-taught Laurea Magistrale curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
A formal doctoral research proposal of 3,000 to 5,000 words outlining research questions, literature review, methodology, and expected impact.
Application fee
€30 public competition application fee submitted via the Ca' Foscari online application portal.
Required documentation
Graduation diploma with official translation, academic transcript of records, detailed curriculum vitae, research project proposal, and 2 letters of reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition fees at Ca' Foscari are calculated on a progressive scale according to the student's family economic situation (ISEE Parificato). For international students from non-OECD countries, tuition is capped at €156 to €1,200 per year. Admitted doctoral candidates receive fully funded state scholarships of approximately €1,400 net per month (€16,243/year gross) plus a 50% stipend increase during research periods abroad and €3,000 research mobility allowances.
