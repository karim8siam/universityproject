Institution Overview & Profile

Università Telematica Pegaso - Sede di Ancona serves as the central Marche regional university centre for Italy's largest state-accredited telematic university. Offering Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR) accredited degrees in education, computer engineering, law, and physical education, the Ancona centre coordinates in-person exam sessions, academic advising, and regional corporate agreements.

Key Academic Strengths

  • Official ANVUR-accredited degrees carrying identical legal value to traditional state universities
  • Localized in-person and digital exam sittings right in Ancona for Marche region residents
  • Leading national provider of teaching qualification credits (24/30/60 CFU percorsi abilitanti docenti)
  • State-of-the-art 24/7 digital learning environment with video lectures, interactive quizzes, and e-tutors
