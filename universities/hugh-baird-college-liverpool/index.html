Hugh Baird College: Healthcare, Maritime Logistics & Hugh Baird University Centre

Hugh Baird College in the Liverpool City Region (Bootle) is a leading further and higher education institution featuring the Hugh Baird University Centre (validated by the University of Central Lancashire - UCLan), the Port Academy Liverpool for maritime port logistics, and specialized nursing and health sciences.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Higher Education foundation degrees require 64-80 UCAS tariff points from A-Levels, BTEC Level 3, or international secondary qualifications.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate higher education centre; degrees validated and conferred by the University of Central Lancashire (UCLan).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each component) for international applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Personal statement detailing career focus in maritime operations, healthcare, or digital computing.
Application fee
Free direct application for college courses; UCAS fee applies for full-time university-level foundation degrees.
Required documentation
Complete academic transcripts, secondary school certificates, passport copy, personal statement, and one referee contact.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for UK higher education degrees at Hugh Baird University Centre is approximately £7,500 to £9,000 per year (covered by Student Finance England). International student tuition averages £9,200 to £12,000 annually. Port Academy scholarships, NHS healthcare traineeships, and adult learner loans are available.
