Colorado College

Colorado College is a nationally ranked top-tier liberal arts institution at the foot of Pikes Peak, renowned for its innovative 'Block Plan'—an intensive academic calendar where students and professors immerse themselves in one single class at a time for three-and-a-half weeks.

Academic overview & excellence

The Block Plan provides unprecedented flexibility for field-based geological surveys, environmental field research across the Rocky Mountains, and international immersion courses. Colorado College is the eighth college in the US to achieve campus carbon neutrality.

Research admissions & criteria

Admissions is highly selective (admit rate ~14%). Colorado College accepts the Common Application with test-optional policy. International applicants submit secondary transcripts, teacher evaluations, and proof of English proficiency (TOEFL 100, IELTS 7.5, or Duolingo 125). Need-based financial aid is awarded to eligible students.
