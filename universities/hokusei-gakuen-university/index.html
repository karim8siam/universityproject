Hokusei Gakuen University: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Established in 1887 in Sapporo, Hokkaido, Hokusei Gakuen University (Hokusei Gakuen Daigaku) is a prominent private liberal arts university. Celebrated for pioneering social welfare science, international economics, bilingual intercultural communication, and environmental community policy in northern Japan, Hokusei Gakuen maintains strong international partnerships.

Hokusei Gakuen International Student Tuition Exemption Scheme

Tuition support scheme granted to privately funded international students admitted to undergraduate and graduate programs at Hokusei Gakuen.

Master of Social Welfare and Gerontological Policy

Recognized master's program examining social safety nets, elder care management, and community-based social work in rapidly aging regional Japanese populations.

Doctoral Fellowship in Social Welfare Systems & Community Health Governance

Three-year doctoral research fellowship investigating health disparities in remote northern communities, dementia care infrastructure, and welfare policy reform.

Hokkaido Regional Environment and Tourism Economics Research Project

Field research project evaluating snow tourism sustainability, winter sports carbon footprints, and agricultural branding across Hokkaido.

Sapporo Municipal Social Welfare Agency Clinical Practicum

Comprehensive supervised clinical social work practicum placed within Sapporo municipal community centers, rehabilitation facilities, and geriatric care centers.

Hokusei Gakuen Postdoctoral Fellowship in Intercultural Communication Studies

Two-year resident research fellowship investigating bilingual pedagogy, second language pragmatics, and intercultural adaptation among foreign workers in Japan.

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of International Economics and Asian Trade

Full-time faculty appointment teaching undergraduate international economics seminars and conducting research on trade agreements and East Asian supply chains.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 16 years of education) with demonstrated academic foundation in social work, economics, or humanities.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree from a recognized institution with a defended master's thesis and approved doctoral dissertation outline.
Language proficiency
Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) N2 or N1 for Japanese-taught tracks, or IELTS 6.0/TOEFL iBT 75 for designated international seminars.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of academic purpose (1,000 words in Japanese or English) and detailed research plan outlining thesis methodology.
Application fee
¥30,000 application examination fee.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, graduation certificate, standardized language scores, two letters of recommendation, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual tuition for international graduate students is approximately ¥780,000 to ¥920,000 plus an initial matriculation fee of ¥200,000. Qualifying international students receive a 30% tuition fee reduction under Hokusei Gakuen's International Student Tuition Exemption Scheme, alongside private foundation and JASSO study stipends.
