American InterContinental University: Online Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Established in 1970, American InterContinental University (AIU) is an accredited American university with system headquarters in Schaumburg, Illinois, and major campuses in Atlanta, Georgia, and Houston, Texas, alongside an extensive global online division. Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC) and the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP), AIU specializes in flexible, career-focused degree programs across Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Business Administration, Healthcare Management, and Criminal Justice.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Earned Bachelor's degree from a regionally or nationally recognized accredited institution with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 on a 4.0 scale (program-specific thresholds may apply).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate and graduate degree programs provide academic foundations articulating into doctoral research and executive D.B.A. programs.
Language proficiency
Proof of English language proficiency (TOEFL iBT 71, IELTS Academic 6.0, or equivalent approved proficiency assessment) for international applicants.
Research statement / Proposal
Online application submission via AIU portal including official transcripts, employment history, and attestation of high school graduation or college degrees.
Application fee
USD $50 application processing fee (frequently waived for military personnel and corporate educational partner affiliates).
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, certified English translations for overseas credentials, government-issued photo ID, and financial support documentation for F-1 visa campus study.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Undergraduate tuition is charged at approximately USD $336 to $412 per credit hour (approximately USD $13,440 to $15,800 per academic year based on full-time enrollment of 36-40 credits). Graduate tuition averages USD $514 to $640 per credit hour (approximately USD $15,420 to $19,200 for a 30-credit Master's degree). AIU provides institutional merit grants, Lifelong Learning scholarships, and up to 100% military tuition assistance allowances.
