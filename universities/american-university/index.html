American University (Washington, D.C.)

Chartered by an Act of Congress in 1893, American University (AU) is a prestigious private research university in Washington, D.C. AU leverages its strategic capital location to connect research with global policymaking, diplomacy, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence ethics. Explore verified graduate degrees, fully funded doctoral fellowships, Capitol Hill internships, and distinguished faculty profiles.

American University Dean's Fellowships & Graduate Merit Awards

AU's top merit fellowship awarded to high-achieving incoming graduate students, providing full tuition coverage and competitive annual living stipends to support full-time research in Washington, D.C.

American University PhD in Computer Science

Cutting-edge doctoral research program within the College of Arts and Sciences in Washington, D.C., focusing on artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, machine learning fairness, quantum information, and computer vision.

American University Master of Science in Data Science

Interdisciplinary 30-credit master's program bridging statistical modeling, machine learning algorithms, big data architectures, and ethical data governance with direct access to Washington tech and federal employers.

American University Master of Arts in International Relations (SIS)

Flagship graduate degree inside AU's world-ranked School of International Service (SIS), preparing students for diplomacy, international security, conflict resolution, and global environmental governance.

American University Center for Data Science & AI Research Placements

Multidisciplinary research appointments investigating ethical artificial intelligence, algorithmic transparency, disinformation detection, and public health data analytics in collaboration with D.C. think tanks.

American University Washington Semester & Capitol Hill Fellowships

Signature experiential research and policy internship program embedding students inside U.S. Congressional offices, federal departments, international embassies, NGOs, and national media outlets.

American University Graduate Assistantships (GA / TA / RA)

Departmental teaching and research assistantships providing tuition credits and competitive hourly stipends for conducting recitation sessions, grading problem sets, and managing scientific lab instruments.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year U.S. Bachelor's degree or international equivalent with minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or Master's degree in related field with outstanding academic record (minimum 3.5 GPA) and demonstrated research aptitude.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (minimum 6.5 in all bands) or TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 22 in each subscore).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page Statement of Purpose detailing research goals, academic preparation, and faculty alignment in Washington, D.C.
Application fee
USD $55 to $100 graduate application fee depending on school/program.
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, two to three letters of recommendation, resume / CV, and writing sample.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Graduate tuition at American University is charged per credit hour (typically USD $1,972 per credit hour, or approximately USD $35,500 to $45,000 per academic year for master's programs). Competitive doctoral candidates receive Dean's Fellowships providing 100% full tuition coverage plus annual living stipends of $20,000 to $28,000.
