Bart College: Digital Arts, 3D Animation, Cybersecurity & Commercial Design Guide

Collège Bart is a venerable private college founded in 1918 in downtown Québec City, renowned for college diplomas (DEC/AEC) in 3D animation, digital video game design, computer graphic arts, web development, cybersecurity, and administrative management.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of Canadian secondary school diploma (DES) or international equivalent for college diploma (DEC) technical entry.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering doctoral degrees; provides technical college degrees (DEC Technique) and intensive adult attestations (AEC).
Language proficiency
French proficiency (B2/C1) or English depending on instructional cohort; French language support courses available.
Research statement / Proposal
Creative digital portfolio (10-15 drawings, 3D assets, graphic designs, or coding samples) for digital arts tracks.
Application fee
CAD $50 application processing fee.
Required documentation
Secondary school transcripts, graduation certificate, identity proof, digital portfolio, and letter of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Annual tuition for international students ranges from CAD $14,000 to $18,500. Québec Government international tuition exemption awards, Bart Foundation creative achievement scholarships, and paid video game studio internships are available.
