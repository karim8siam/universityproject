Swiss International Institute Lausanne: Digital Management, UX Design & IT Guide

Headquartered in the Lake Geneva innovation corridor in Lausanne (Vaud), the Swiss International Institute Lausanne (SIIL) is a forward-thinking Swiss private higher educational institute delivering multilingual distance and blended graduate degrees. Specializing in international management, digital interface design, web development, and brand strategy, SIIL equips global professionals with practical competencies aligned with the Swiss knowledge economy.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

SIIL Swiss Digital Excellence and Innovation Scholarship

Merit-based international award granting substantial tuition fee reductions to talented global candidates pursuing master's degrees in management and digital technologies.

Master of Science in International Management and Digital Business (M.Sc.)

Two-year flexible master's program providing advanced instruction in cross-border e-commerce strategy, digital marketing analytics, agile supply chains, and enterprise IT management.

SIIL Collaborative DBA Fellowship in Digital Platform Economics

Three-year practitioner doctorate developing applied empirical frameworks for digital ecosystem competition, multi-sided marketplace network effects, and algorithmic pricing.

Lake Geneva Digital Economy and Human-Computer Interaction Project

Applied human-computer interaction research project evaluating cognitive load metrics, micro-interaction feedback, and accessibility compliance across mobile software platforms.

Swiss Tech Hub & E-Commerce Remote Consulting Practicum

Four-month remote consulting practicum working alongside Swiss digital retail start-ups and international branding consultancies analyzing customer conversion funnels.

SIIL Senior Postdoctoral Fellow in Digital Brand Strategy and AI Media

Two-year research fellowship conducting empirical investigations on brand authenticity, consumer trust in generative AI creative campaigns, and omnichannel digital retail.

SIIL Women in Digital Technology and Design Bursary

Targeted educational grant promoting female participation, leadership, and digital creativity in UX design, web development, and digital marketing.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (or international equivalent 180 ECTS) in business, computer science, communications, or design with a minimum GPA of 2.7 on a 4.0 scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Direct collaborative DBA research tracks coordinated with international university faculties in digital business models and platform economics.
Language proficiency
Proficiency in the language of instruction: English (IELTS 6.0, TOEFL 80) or French (B2 CEFR) for multilingual study tracks.
Research statement / Proposal
Required for master's capstone: 750-word project concept outlining digital transformation or UX strategy for an international enterprise.
Application fee
100 CHF non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified copies of undergraduate degree and transcripts with English/French translations, passport copy, resume/CV, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at Swiss International Institute Lausanne is approximately 4,500 to 7,200 CHF per academic year for master's programs—representing exceptional value for Swiss higher education. SIIL provides Swiss Digital Innovation Bursaries granting up to 25% tuition reductions and modular monthly installment plans.
