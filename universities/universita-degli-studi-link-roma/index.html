Università degli Studi Link: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Established in 1999 and officially recognized by the Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR) in 2011, Università degli Studi Link is headquartered in the majestic historic Casale San Pio V complex near the Vatican in Rome, with a vibrant southern campus in Matera (Basilicata). Link Campus is celebrated across Europe for its specialized interdisciplinary research in geopolitical risk analysis, cybersecurity, strategic intelligence, international commercial arbitration, and interactive creative digital entertainment. The university blends classical humanities with applied diplomacy simulations.
