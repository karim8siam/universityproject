Academic overview & research priorities

Tracing its origins back to the Daito Accounting Institute founded in 1943, Tokyo Fuji University operates its Faculty of Business Administration in the vibrant student and cultural district of Takadanobaba in central Tokyo. The university is widely acclaimed for pioneering event producer and entertainment industry management curricula in Japan, as well as maintaining exceptional pass rates for national tax accountant (Zeirishi) examinations.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate entrance requires graduation from an accredited high school, Japanese proficiency (JLPT N2 or higher for standard tracks), academic transcripts, and personal interviews. Transfer and graduate applicants to Business Management and Tax Accounting must hold a recognized bachelor's degree in economics, business, or law, and submit a detailed tax law or corporate management study proposal.
