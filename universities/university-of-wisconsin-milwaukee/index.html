University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee

UWM · Milwaukee, Wisconsin, United States

The University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee (UWM) is an elite Carnegie R1 public research university located blocks from Lake Michigan in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Renowned for hosting the only dedicated School of Freshwater Sciences in the United States, UWM is also globally celebrated for advanced architecture at SARUP, biomedical physics, advanced manufacturing, and film production.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UWM spans 15 colleges and schools including the School of Freshwater Sciences, School of Architecture and Urban Planning (SARUP), College of Engineering & Applied Science, Peck School of the Arts, Zilber College of Public Health, and the Lubar College of Business. Key research facilities include the Great Lakes Research Facility and the Connected Systems Institute (CSI).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic performance and coursework. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 2.75–3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
