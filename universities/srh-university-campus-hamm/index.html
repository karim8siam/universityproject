Academic overview & research priorities

Based in the historic canal and railway logistics hub of Hamm in North Rhine-Westphalia, SRH Hamm delivers applied bachelor's and master's degrees tailored to European freight corridors, green energy transition, and psychosocial healthcare. The campus operates advanced logistics simulation labs, renewable energy testbeds, and close partnerships with regional transport corporations.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admission requires a German university entrance qualification (Abitur / Fachhochschulreife) or recognized international equivalent. Master's programs require an accredited bachelor's degree in engineering, logistics, economics, or social sciences (minimum German grade 2.5). English-taught tracks require IELTS 6.5; German programs require DSH-2 or TestDaF 4.
