Academic overview & excellence

Founded in 1881, Dakota State underwent a dramatic transformation in 1984 when the South Dakota Legislature mandated its specialized mission in computer management and information technology. Today, through The Beacom College of Computer and Cyber Sciences and the MadLabs (Madison Cyber Labs), DSU conducts multi-million dollar classified and unclassified defense research for the Department of Defense, NSA, Homeland Security, and the financial sector.

Research admissions & criteria

Undergraduate freshman admission requires a high school GPA of 2.6 or ACT composite score of 18. Graduate programs in Cyber Operations and Computer Science require an undergraduate degree in computer science or related discipline with a minimum 3.0 GPA. Federal security clearance pathways may apply for select defense research labs.
