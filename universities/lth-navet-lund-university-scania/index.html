Faculty of Engineering LTH - Navet (Lund University): Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Located on the landmark Ole Römers väg engineering campus in Lund, Navet serves as the vibrant student innovation hub and collaborative interface for Lund University's Faculty of Engineering (LTH - Lunds Tekniska Högskola). Educating over 10,000 engineering students and hosting world-leading research centers (including NanoLund and the MAX IV Laboratory adjacent synchrotron facilities), LTH Navet links cutting-edge engineering student teams with Medicon Village, IDEON Science Park, and multinational engineering innovators.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
National University Admissions Sweden; rigorous high-level calculus, physics, and chemistry prerequisites
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized master's or honours degree in relevant discipline; alignment with supervising research faculty.
Language proficiency
Swedish & English (Broad portfolio of master's and doctoral degrees taught 100% in English)
Research statement / Proposal
Required for research programs: 3-5 page research statement outlining methodology, objectives, and faculty alignment.
Application fee
SEK 900
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV/resume, letters of recommendation, and statement of intent.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems
