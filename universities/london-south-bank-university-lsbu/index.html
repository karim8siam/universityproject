London South Bank University: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Tracing its lineage to the Borough Polytechnic Institute founded in 1892, London South Bank University is situated on a vibrant campus in the central London borough of Southwark, just minutes from Elephant and Castle and London Bridge. LSBU is internationally acclaimed for professional accreditation (including ICE, CIBSE, IMechE, and NMC), high graduate employability, and applied research in acoustic engineering, renewable heating, and urban healthcare delivery.
