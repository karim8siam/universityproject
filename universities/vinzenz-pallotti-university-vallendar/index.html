Vinzenz Pallotti University Vallendar

Vinzenz Pallotti University Vallendar (VPU) is a state-recognized, church-sponsored private university specializing in human sciences, clinical psychotherapy, existential psychology, and theological philosophy.

Academic overview & research priorities

Perched above the Rhine valley in Vallendar near Koblenz, VPU delivers accredited academic programs bridging scientific psychotherapy, systemic psychology, medical humanities, and Catholic theology. The university houses research institutes for palliative care ethics, clinical neuropsychology, and interreligious dialogue, fostering dialogue between empirical research and philosophical anthropology.

Research admissions & international criteria

Bachelor's admission requires the German Allgemeine Hochschulreife, Fachhochschulreife, or international equivalent. Master's in Clinical Psychology requires a B.Sc. in Psychology compliant with the German Psychotherapist Act (PsychThG). Doctoral candidates must present a recognized university master's degree (minimum grade 2.0 or 'gut') and an approved research prospectus.
