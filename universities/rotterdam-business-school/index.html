Rotterdam Business School: International Business, Supply Chain, Finance & Logistics Guide

Rotterdam Business School (RBS) is the international business faculty of Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences (Hogeschool Rotterdam), situated in Europe's largest seaport, internationally known for hands-on master's programs in Global Supply Chain Management, International Finance, and Sustainable Business Consultancy.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree (minimum 180 or 240 ECTS) in business administration, economics, logistics, or engineering from a recognized institution.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral degrees; focus on professional practice-oriented Master's degrees.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each sub-score) or TOEFL iBT 90 overall (minimum 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
A 1,000-word motivation essay outlining professional career goals, reasons for selecting Rotterdam, and relevant business experience.
Application fee
Zero application fee via Studielink and the university application portal.
Required documentation
Certified bachelor's diploma, official transcripts, CV, motivation letter, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for EU/EEA students is set at the statutory Dutch fee of approximately €2,530 per year. For non-EU/EEA international students, master's tuition is approximately €11,500 to €13,200 per year. The NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) awards €5,000 to eligible non-EEA students.
