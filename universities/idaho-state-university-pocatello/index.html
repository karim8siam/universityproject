Idaho State University

ISU Pocatello · Pocatello, Idaho, United States

Idaho State University (ISU) is a public doctoral research university in Pocatello, Idaho, recognized as the state’s lead institution for health professions, pharmacy, nuclear engineering, and geoscience.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

ISU collaborates closely with Idaho National Laboratory (INL) on nuclear reactor modeling, radioisotope production, and advanced cybersecurity. Its Kasiska Division of Health Sciences operates campuses in Pocatello and Meridian, offering premier doctoral clinical programs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school GPA (min. 2.5) under test-optional policies. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, competitive GPA, department prerequisites, and letters of recommendation.
