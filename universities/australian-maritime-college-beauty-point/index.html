Australian Maritime College Beauty Point Campus: Academic Guide, Degrees & Scholarships

Operated as an elite specialized institute of the University of Tasmania, the Australian Maritime College (AMC) Beauty Point Campus is situated on the picturesque Tamar River estuary in northern Tasmania. The Beauty Point facility is home to AMC Search and the Centre for Maritime Safety and Survival, featuring a deep-water jetty, survival training pool, full-mission bridge simulators, and an advanced fleet of training vessels including the 35-metre TV Bluefin. The campus delivers world-standard training accredited by AMSA and IMO in marine engineering, nautical science, marine surveying, and aquaculture logistics.
