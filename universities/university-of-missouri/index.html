University of Missouri

Mizzou · Columbia, Missouri, United States

The University of Missouri (Mizzou) is the flagship public land-grant research university of Missouri and a distinguished member of the Association of American Universities (AAU). Established in 1839 as the first public university west of the Mississippi River, Mizzou is internationally renowned for operating the nation's most powerful university research nuclear reactor (MURR), founding the world's first school of journalism, and pioneering biomedical and agricultural discovery.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Mizzou spans 13 major academic divisions including the College of Agriculture, Food and Natural Resources (CAFNR), College of Engineering, School of Medicine, School of Journalism, and the College of Veterinary Medicine. Key research hubs include the University of Missouri Research Reactor (MURR)—the sole domestic producer of vital medical radioisotopes—and the Bond Life Sciences Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school core coursework and GPA. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 80+ or IELTS 6.5+).
