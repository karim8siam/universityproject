University of Leeds

Founded in 1904 by Royal Charter, the University of Leeds is a major civic research powerhouse and founding member of the prestigious Russell Group and the N8 Research Partnership. Consistently ranked in the world's top 85 universities, Leeds is celebrated globally for groundbreaking research at the Bragg Centre for Materials Research (named after Nobel laureates William Henry and William Lawrence Bragg), the Priestley Centre for Climate Futures, the Astbury Centre for Structural Molecular Biology, and the Leeds Institute for Data Analytics (LIDA).

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Leeds NERC Panorama Doctoral Training Partnership (DTP) Earth & Climate Studentships

Doctoral training conducted at the Priestley Centre for Climate Futures and the School of Earth and Environment, developing global climate models, ice sheet melt simulations, and aerosol-cloud interactions.

Leeds PhD in Structural Molecular Biology at the Astbury Centre

Doctoral research conducted within the world-renowned Astbury Centre for Structural Molecular Biology, using state-of-the-art cryo-electron microscopy and high-field NMR to resolve atomic structures of amyloid fibrils and viral assemblies.

Leeds Master of Science (MSc) in Advanced Computer Science (Cloud & AI Systems)

British Computer Society (BCS) accredited one-year Master of Science offering specialized research modules in parallel computing, deep learning, graph algorithms, and cloud infrastructure.

Leeds Bragg Centre PhD in Terahertz Photonics & Quantum Electronics

Doctoral research conducted within the Bragg Centre for Materials Research, developing terahertz quantum cascade lasers, high-speed wireless transceivers, and spectroscopic sensors for medical imaging.

Leeds Institute for Transport Studies (ITS) Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Institute for Transport Studies, one of the world's leading transport research institutions, analyzing travel behavior, electric vehicle adoption, and transport policy appraisal.

Leeds School of Chemistry Summer Research Fellowship

Eight-week mentored summer research project investigating continuous flow reactors, atmospheric aerosol chemistry, and sustainable catalysis methodologies.

Leeds Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Medical Robotics & Surgical Technologies

Tenured-track Assistant Professorship (Lectureship) delivering teaching in mechatronics and leading independent laboratory research in surgical robotics, capsule endoscopy, and flexible robotic catheters at the Leeds Institute of Medical Technologies.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK Upper Second Class Honours degree (2:1) or international equivalent (minimum 65-70% or 3.2/4.0 GPA) in a relevant discipline; computing and engineering require solid quantitative coursework.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized Master's degree with Merit or Distinction (or UK 2:1 Honours degree with significant research experience) and a detailed research proposal aligned with Leeds research themes.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 88; Medicine, Business, and Law require IELTS 7.0 (minimum 6.5 in all bands).
Research statement / Proposal
A formal research proposal of 1,500 to 2,500 words outlining research questions, literature context, methodology, proposed timeline, and identified Leeds academic supervisor.
Application fee
£0 application fee for postgraduate research (PhD) applications; £25 for selected taught business master's degrees.
Required documentation
Complete official academic transcripts, degree award certificates, 2 academic reference letters, detailed research proposal, and personal statement.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Full-time doctoral researchers awarded UKRI (EPSRC, NERC, BBSRC, MRC, ESRC) studentships or Leeds Doctoral Scholarships receive 100% full tuition fee coverage (at home/international rates) and an annual tax-free living stipend of at least £19,237 per year (aligned with UKRI national minimum rate) plus an annual research training support grant (RTSG) for laboratory consumables and conference travel.
