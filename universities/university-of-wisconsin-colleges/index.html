University of Wisconsin Colleges

UW Colleges · Madison, Wisconsin, United States

The University of Wisconsin Colleges branch campus network provides foundational collegiate education and guaranteed bachelor's degree transfer pathways across thirteen communities in Wisconsin. Coordinated through the University of Wisconsin System administration in Madison, UW Colleges delivers accredited Associate of Arts and Sciences (AAS) degrees, undergraduate research mentoring, and high-tech STEM transfer pipelines.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Madison · Statewide Collegiate Transfer Pathways, Liberal Arts & STEM Access. The University of Wisconsin Colleges branch campus network provides foundational collegiate education and guaranteed bachelor's degree transfer pathways across thirteen communities in Wisconsin. Coordinated through the University of Wisconsin System administration in Madison, UW Colleges delivers accredited Associate of Arts and Sciences (AAS) degrees, undergraduate research mentoring, and high-tech STEM transfer pipelines.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma, GED, or approved secondary completion certificate. Open access admissions with guaranteed transfer to any UW System four-year university upon completion of the AAS degree. TOEFL iBT 71+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Rolling admissions; priority fall deadline July 1, 2027; spring priority deadline December 1, 2026.
