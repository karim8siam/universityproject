University of Tennessee Southern

UT Southern · Pulaski, Tennessee, United States

The University of Tennessee Southern (UT Southern), founded in 1870 as Martin Methodist College and joining the University of Tennessee system in 2021, is the primary public baccalaureate institution serving Southern Middle Tennessee in Pulaski. Celebrated for nursing and healthcare professions, business management, criminal justice, and interdisciplinary environmental biology, UT Southern provides personal mentorship paired with statewide UT system resources.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UT Southern delivers undergraduate and graduate education through four divisions: the Jeanette M. Travis School of Nursing and Health Sciences, Division of Business, Division of Mathematics and Sciences, and the Division of Humanities and Social Sciences. The campus is expanding healthcare simulation and agricultural education.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate students, evaluating high school GPA (minimum 2.5). Graduate nursing applicants require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, unencumbered RN license, statement of purpose, two letters of recommendation, and English proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
