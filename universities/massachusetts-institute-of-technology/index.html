Massachusetts Institute of Technology (Cambridge, Massachusetts)

Founded in 1861 in Cambridge, Massachusetts, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) is globally recognized as the preeminent technological research institution. With 101 Nobel laureates, 26 Turing Award winners, and 8 Fields Medalists, MIT faculty and alumni have invented fundamental pillars of modern science including radar, digital computing, RSA encryption, the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), and mRNA vaccine delivery. MIT Presidential Fellowships & Departmental Fellowships

MIT's most prestigious competitive graduate award, recruited across all engineering and science departments to support the world's most promising first-year doctoral researchers.

MIT PhD in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS)

The world's highest-ranked EECS doctoral program, offering unmatched research opportunities in artificial intelligence, robotics, quantum computing, computer systems, and microelectronics.

MIT Master of Science in Technology and Policy (TPP)

Rigorous two-year interdisciplinary Master of Science program bridging cutting-edge engineering innovation with international energy policy, cybersecurity governance, and economic regulation.

MIT Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering

Pioneering thesis-based graduate degree providing foundational mastery and laboratory research in biomechanics, robotics, micro-nano manufacturing, and clean thermal fluid systems.

MIT Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL) Placements

The largest research laboratory at MIT and the historical birthplace of the internet, personal computing, and robotics, pursuing breakthroughs in foundation models, computer vision, and theory.

MIT Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP)

Pioneering undergraduate research program founded in 1969, engaging over 90% of MIT undergraduates in faculty-led scientific investigations and patent-producing laboratory work.

MIT Graduate Research & Teaching Assistantships (GRA / GTA)

Academic research and teaching appointments providing graduate scholars with full tuition remissions, medical insurance, and competitive salaries while instructing MIT undergraduate courses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited four-year U.S. Bachelor's degree or international equivalent with exceptional academic marks (typically 3.8+ / 4.0 GPA) in STEM disciplines.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Outstanding undergraduate or Master's degree in relevant discipline with demonstrable scientific research ability, significant publications, and compelling letters of recommendation.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency: Academic IELTS 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 100 overall (minimum 25 in each subscore); requirements vary by department.
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 2-page Statement of Objectives detailing scientific accomplishments, research interests, and alignment with target MIT laboratory groups.
Application fee
USD $75 to $105 graduate application fee submitted directly via departmental admissions portals (waivers widely available).
Required documentation
Complete official university transcripts, 3 academic letters of recommendation, resume / CV, and academic writing sample or research papers.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for doctoral candidates is 100% fully covered for the regular duration of the PhD. All admitted PhD students in good standing receive guaranteed financial support packages comprising full tuition waivers, individual health insurance, and monthly living stipends (USD $45,000 to $52,000 per year) via research assistantships (RA), teaching assistantships (TA), or fellowships.
