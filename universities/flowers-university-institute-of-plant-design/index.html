Flowers University - Institute of Floral Art and Plant Design

Flowers University is an internationally recognized botanical design and master floristry academy in Munich, pioneering eco-floristry, architectural botanical installations, sustainable floricultural supply chains, and living plant aesthetics.

Academic overview & research priorities

Collaborating with the Weihenstephan horticultural research institutes and European floral design guilds, Flowers University delivers master-level vocational diplomas and professional certifications. Students master sculptural plant mechanics, zero-plastic foam design techniques, commercial flower farming economics, and wedding event botanical styling.

Research admissions & international criteria

Applicants must hold a certified vocational background in floristry, horticulture, landscape design, or fine arts, accompanied by a visual portfolio of floral or botanical compositions and an interview.

Master Florist Gregor Lersch

Visiting Master of Floral Architecture and International Design Mentor · Master Floristry and Botanical Sculpture Division

Gregor Lersch is globally revered as one of the founding fathers of modern architectural floristry, authoring over 30 design books and pioneering the structural integration of wild vegetal materials without synthetic adhesives.

Prof. Dr. Bernhard Bauer

Professor of Horticulture and Botanical Supply Chain Economics · Department of Agricultural Floriculture

Prof. Dr. Bernhard Bauer researches post-harvest vascular occlusion in cut flowers, designing natural polysaccharide preservative solutions to replace silver thiosulfate and extend commercial vase longevity.
