Institutional Overview

University of the West of Scotland (UWS) is a dynamic, multi-campus public university tracing its heritage back to 1897. Operating ultra-modern campuses across Paisley, Lanarkshire, Ayr, and Dumfries (plus a campus in London), UWS is recognized for its top-tier nursing and midwifery academy, artificial intelligence and cyber research, thin-film engineering, and sports science laboratories.
