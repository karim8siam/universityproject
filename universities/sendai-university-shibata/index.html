Sendai University

Sendai University is Northern Japan’s sole specialized higher education university of physical education and sports sciences, acclaimed for training Olympic athletes, athletic trainers, physical education teachers, and sports business managers.

Academic overview & research priorities

Operating extensive sports science research laboratories, Olympic-standard gymnastics arenas, artificial turf fields, and high-speed motion capture biomechanics suites, Sendai University leads Tohoku in sports medicine, coach education, and sports data analytics. Its athletes compete successfully at national intercollegiate championships and Olympic Games.

Research admissions & international criteria

Admissions require high school graduation and physical fitness testing or competitive athletic achievements. Foreign applicants require JLPT N2, secondary school records, an athletic sports resume, and an admissions interview.
