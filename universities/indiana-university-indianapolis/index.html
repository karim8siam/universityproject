Indiana University Indianapolis

IU Indianapolis · Indianapolis, Indiana, United States

Indiana University Indianapolis (formerly IUPUI) is Indiana’s premier urban research institution, home to the state’s largest health sciences campus, including the IU School of Medicine and Luddy School of Informatics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Located in downtown Indianapolis, IU Indianapolis anchors the 16 Tech Innovation District. The campus drives over $500 million in annual biomedical research across the IU School of Medicine, School of Dentistry, School of Nursing, and cutting-edge artificial intelligence labs.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school academic rigor, cumulative GPA (average 3.5), and essays under test-optional guidelines. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree, competitive GPA, statement of purpose, and program-specific credentials.
