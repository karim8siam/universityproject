Indiana University Fort Wayne (IUFW)

IU Fort Wayne · Fort Wayne, Indiana, United States

Indiana University Fort Wayne (IUFW) is Indiana University’s dedicated health sciences campus in Fort Wayne, offering accredited clinical degrees in nursing, dental hygiene, radiography, and medical imaging.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

Co-located on the Fort Wayne university campus, IUFW focuses exclusively on preparing healthcare practitioners through direct partnerships with Parkview Health and Lutheran Health Network. Students train in state-of-the-art simulation hospitals and dental clinics.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Requires standard undergraduate admission to Indiana University followed by a selective secondary application for clinical healthcare cohorts based on prerequisite GPAs and interview ratings.
