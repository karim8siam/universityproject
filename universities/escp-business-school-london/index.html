ESCP Business School London: Master's in Management, Fellowships & Faculty Guide

Established in 1819, ESCP Business School is the world's oldest business school and holds triple accreditation (AACSB, EQUIS, AMBA). Operating across six European urban campuses in London, Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Turin, and Warsaw, ESCP's London campus in Hampstead delivers internationally celebrated degree programs including the Master in Management (ranked top 5 globally by the Financial Times), specialized MSc degrees, and executive doctorates.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

ESCP European Excellence and Diversity Merit Scholarship

Merit-based financial award supporting high-potential international candidates matriculating into the flagship Master in Management program at ESCP London.

Master in Management (MiM) European Multi-Campus Degree

Two-year European master's program combining studies across London, Paris, Berlin, Madrid, or Turin, featuring dual degrees, 40+ specialisations, and mandatory international internships.

Executive Ph.D. in Business Administration and Global Management

Practitioner-scholar doctoral program designed for senior leaders to generate original research in digital corporate strategy, sustainable finance, and international governance.

ESCP Sustainable Business and Energy Transition Research Initiative

Empirical management research project analyzing corporate decarbonization strategies, renewable investment financing, and European Green Deal regulatory compliance.

London Financial District Corporate Finance and Private Equity Practicum

Mandatory corporate work placement embedding students within investment banking, strategy consulting, or private equity firms across the City of London and Canary Wharf.

ESCP FinTech and Digital Banking Postdoctoral Fellowship

Two-year research fellowship investigating decentralized finance, algorithmic risk management, and the regulatory oversight of artificial intelligence in European banking.

Associate Professor of Finance and Quantitative Methods

Permanent faculty position teaching graduate finance coursework, conducting high-impact research, and mentoring doctoral candidates at the ESCP London campus in Hampstead.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree or equivalent (minimum 180 ECTS / UK 2:1 Honours) from an accredited university in any academic discipline with demonstrated analytical proficiency.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completion of an accredited Master of Science or MBA degree with outstanding academic distinction (UK Merit or Distinction equivalent) and a comprehensive research proposal.
Language proficiency
English language proficiency at CEFR C1 level (IELTS Academic 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5, or TOEFL iBT 100).
Research statement / Proposal
Online application via ESCP portal including competitive entrance tests (GMAT, GRE, or ESCP SHL test), essays, and rigorous individual admission interview.
Application fee
£180 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Certified university degree certificates, complete academic transcripts, CV, two professional/academic reference letters, and valid passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for the flagship Master in Management program is approximately £23,500 per academic year for European and international students. Executive MBA tuition is approximately £78,000 total. ESCP provides extensive financial support including European Excellence Scholarships, Diversity Grants, and corporate foundation fellowships covering up to 50% of tuition.
