Ottawa University Arizona

OUAZ · Surprise, Arizona, United States

Ottawa University Arizona (OUAZ), founded in 2017 as the comprehensive residential campus of historic Ottawa University (est. 1865), is situated in Surprise, Arizona. OUAZ pairs residential student life and collegiate athletics with rigorous liberal arts, STEM, and professional business curricula anchored by the revolutionary 'Adawe' personal coaching and mentorship model.

Research admissions & international qualifications

High school diploma with 2.5+ cumulative GPA for freshman admission; transfer applicants require 2.0+ GPA with 12+ college semester hours. Holistic personal evaluation. TOEFL iBT 70+ or IELTS 6.0+ for international applicants. Rolling admissions throughout the year; Fall priority deadline July 1, 2027; Spring priority December 1, 2026.
