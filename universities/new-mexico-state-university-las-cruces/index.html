New Mexico State University

NMSU · Las Cruces, New Mexico, United States

New Mexico State University is the comprehensive land-grant and space-grant research university of New Mexico, located in Las Cruces. A premier Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) renowned for aerospace engineering, agriculture, astronomy, and border health.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

NMSU features the College of Agricultural, Consumer and Environmental Sciences, College of Engineering, and the Physical Science Laboratory (PSL). It operates the Apache Point Observatory and the historic Fabian Garcia Science Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school unweighted GPA (minimum 2.75 for guaranteed admission or holistic review). Graduate degree programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, and three letters of recommendation.
