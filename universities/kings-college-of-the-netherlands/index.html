Institutional Overview

King's College of the Netherlands (KCN) is an international Christian higher education college situated on Erasmusweg in The Hague, the international city of peace and justice. Delivering English-taught bachelor's, master's, and executive certificates in theology, international leadership, peace studies, and digital ethics, KCN bridges classical Christian scholarship with contemporary global humanitarian diplomacy.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Strategic location in The Hague, providing direct proximity to over 200 international courts, embassies, and NGOs
  • Specialized degrees in Christian diplomacy, international humanitarian assistance, and conflict mediation
  • Small multicultural student body representing over 40 nationalities fostering personal mentorship
  • Experiential field practicums embedded within international human rights and refugee advocacy organizations
