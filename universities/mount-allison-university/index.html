Mount Allison University: Degrees, Scholarships & Research Guide

Established in 1839 in Sackville, New Brunswick, Mount Allison University is consistently ranked as Canada's top primarily undergraduate university by Maclean's magazine. Renowned for intense faculty mentorship, experiential learning, coastal marshland ecology, biochemistry, and Canadian arts, Mount Allison offers exceptional scholarship funding.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Mount Allison Bell Achievement International Entrance Award

Mount Allison's most prestigious entrance scholarship, rewarding international academic excellence, integrity, and proactive community leadership.

Master of Science in Coastal and Wetland Environmental Science

Research-intensive master's degree conducted on the Bay of Fundy salt marshes examining tidal sediment dynamics, carbon budgets, and coastal habitat restoration.

Bay of Fundy Marine Microbial Ecology Collaborative Doctoral Fellowship

Four-year joint doctoral research fellowship investigating benthic microbial communities and extreme tidal nutrient turnover in Atlantic Canada.

Mount Allison Centre for Clean Energy Chemistry Research Placement

Funded laboratory research fellowship designing novel organometallic catalysts for artificial photosynthesis and green hydrogen generation.

Owens Art Gallery Curatorial and Collections Practicum

Immersive curatorial internship at Canada's oldest university art gallery, working on archival preservation, provenance research, and exhibition design.

Mount Allison Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Salt Marsh Wetland Restoration

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship leading field research on dykeland realignment, managed coastal retreat, and salt marsh vegetation recovery in the Chignecto Isthmus.

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Data Systems

Tenure-track faculty appointment delivering undergraduate courses in algorithms, distributed systems, and machine learning while maintaining active research.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Baccalaureate degree from a recognized institution with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 out of 4.0 (equivalent to a B or 73% average).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Collaborative doctoral pathways with regional Atlantic graduate schools require an accredited master's degree and faculty sponsorship.
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with no individual band score below 6.0 (or TOEFL iBT 90 with 20 in each section).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of interest (500–800 words) describing academic accomplishments, extracurricular leadership, and research aspirations.
Application fee
CAD $50 standard application fee via the Mount Allison online portal.
Required documentation
Official secondary and post-secondary transcripts, certified English translations, English proficiency test results, and two academic references.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

International undergraduate tuition is approximately CAD $21,190 per academic year. Mount Allison awards over CAD $2 million annually in entrance scholarships, including the premier Bell Achievement Award ($44,000 over 4 years) and International Entrance Scholarships ($5,000 to $10,000 per year).
