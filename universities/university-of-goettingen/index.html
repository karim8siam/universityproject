University of Göttingen (Georg-August-Universität)

Founded in 1737 by King George II of Great Britain and Elector of Hanover, the University of Göttingen (Georg-August-Universität Göttingen) is one of Germany's most historic and renowned institutions of higher education. Associated with over 45 Nobel laureates—including Max Born, Werner Heisenberg, Max Planck, and James Franck—and legendary mathematicians like Carl Friedrich Gauss and David Hilbert, Göttingen offers virtually tuition-free education, world-renowned graduate schools, and state-of-the-art facilities in partnership with four Max Planck Institutes. Göttingen Graduate Center for Neurosciences, Biophysics, and Molecular Biosciences (GGNB) Fellowships

Joint excellence doctoral program run in collaboration with four Max Planck Institutes and the German Primate Center, providing world-class training in molecular biology and biophysics.

Uni Göttingen Master of Science (MSc) in Applied Data Science (English-taught)

Two-year English-taught Master's degree combining scalable machine learning, statistical inference, data privacy, and applied domain specializations in bioinformatics or digital humanities.

Uni Göttingen PhD in Physics & Max Planck Particle Astrophysics

Doctoral research program at the II. Institute of Physics collaborating directly with CERN's ATLAS experiment and the Max Planck Institute for Solar System Research.

Uni Göttingen PhD in Molecular Chemistry & Catalysis

Three to four-year doctoral training program in the Faculty of Chemistry developing earth-abundant metal catalysts, C-H bond activation, and green synthesis methodologies.

Uni Göttingen Master of Science (MSc) in Development Economics (English-taught)

Germany's leading two-year Master's degree in development economics, offering rigorous training in econometric impact evaluation, microfinance, rural poverty alleviation, and climate shocks.

Göttingen Forest Ecology and Agroforestry Summer Research Internship

Two-month field research placement in the Faculty of Forest Sciences investigating temperate canopy microclimates and root-soil microbial interactions.

Uni Göttingen Postdoctoral Research Associate in Solid-State NMR Spectroscopy

Two-year postdoctoral position utilizing Göttingen's world-class 1.2 GHz NMR spectrometer facilities to resolve complex membrane protein assemblies and polymer electrolyte interfaces.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS) in relevant subject with above-average German grade equivalent (minimum 2.5 or better on the German 1-5 scale).
Doctoral admission eligibility
Completed Master's degree (120 ECTS) with a research thesis graded at least 'good' (2.0 or better) and formal written endorsement by a Göttingen faculty supervisor.
Language proficiency
Academic IELTS 6.5 to 7.0 overall or TOEFL iBT 90+ for English-taught graduate programs; TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2 for German-taught degrees.
Research statement / Proposal
Formal 3 to 5-page research proposal detailing research objectives, methodology, preliminary literature review, and academic work schedule.
Application fee
€0 application fee (no application fee charged by the University of Göttingen).
Required documentation
Certified academic transcripts, degree certificates, diploma supplements, CV, motivation letter, and two academic recommendation letters.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Public universities in Lower Saxony charge ZERO tuition fees (€0 tuition) for both domestic and international students in regular Bachelor's, Master's, and doctoral programs. Students pay only a nominal semester administration contribution (Semesterbeitrag) of approximately €390 per semester, which includes a free regional train ticket across Lower Saxony and parts of Germany. Doctoral candidates are employed on salaried research contracts (TV-L E13, 65%-100%, approx. €34,000 to €52,000/year gross).
