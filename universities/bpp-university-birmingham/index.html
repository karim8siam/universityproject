Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
UK 2:2 or international equivalent undergraduate degree in any discipline for law conversion (PGDL/SQE) or relevant business degree for accounting.
Doctoral admission eligibility
BPP focuses strictly on career-focused professional undergraduate and postgraduate qualifications; does not offer academic PhDs.
Language proficiency
IELTS 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 in all components) for law and business masters.
Research statement / Proposal
Personal statement detailing professional ambitions in the UK legal, accounting, or tech sectors.
Application fee
Free direct online application through the BPP portal.
Required documentation
Degree transcripts and certificates, personal statement, CV/resume, copy of passport, and one professional or academic reference.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition for postgraduate law programs (LLM/SQE) ranges from £14,500 to £17,500. MSc in Accounting & Finance is approximately £15,000 for international students. Dean of Law scholarships, Future Leader bursaries, and law firm training contracts are accessible.
