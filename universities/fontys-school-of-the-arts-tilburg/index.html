Fontys School of the Arts: Music, Contemporary Circus, Dance & Theatre Guide

Fontys School of the Arts (Fontys Academy of the Arts) in Tilburg is one of Europe's most innovative creative arts conservatories, celebrated for the Academy of Circus and Performance Art (ACaPA), Dance Arts in Context, the Rockacademie, Master of Music, and performing arts technology.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor of Music, Dance, Theatre, or Fine Arts (or verified professional artistic portfolio equivalent) for Master's programs.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Artistic practice-based doctoral research offered in partnership with Dutch research universities (such as Leiden University Academy of Creative and Performing Arts).
Language proficiency
IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band) or TOEFL iBT 80 for international English-taught bachelor and master programs.
Research statement / Proposal
Artistic portfolio, live audition or video recordings, and creative project development proposal (500-1,000 words).
Application fee
Audition fee typically €50 to €75 depending on artistic discipline.
Required documentation
Secondary school diploma or bachelor's degree certificate, artistic portfolio/media links, motivation letter, CV, and copy of passport.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

EU/EEA statutory tuition is approximately €2,530 per academic year. Non-EU/EEA international tuition is approximately €8,900 to €11,500 annually. Holland Scholarship (NL Scholarship) awards of €5,000, Fontys Creative Talent bursaries, and Brabant cultural stipends are accessible.
