Academic overview & research priorities

Founded in 1871 and situated on a lush 48-acre campus arboretum 16 miles west of downtown Chicago, Elmhurst University offers more than 70 undergraduate majors and 15 master's degrees. The university operates the acclaimed Elmhurst Speech-Language-Hearing Clinic, state-of-the-art nursing simulation laboratories, the Elmhurst University Jazz Band, and extensive internships across Chicago's Fortune 500 corporate corridor.

Research admissions & international criteria

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school transcripts, personal essay, and counselor recommendation. Elmhurst is test-optional for ACT/SAT. Graduate programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0, academic transcripts, resume, and letters of recommendation. International applicants require TOEFL iBT 79+, IELTS 6.5+, or Duolingo 105+.
