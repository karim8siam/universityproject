The George Washington University

George Washington · Washington, District of Columbia, United States

The George Washington University (GW) is an elite private Carnegie R1 research university located in the heart of Washington, D.C., blocks from the White House, the World Bank, and the State Department. Acclaimed for the Elliott School of International Affairs, GW Law, the Milken Institute School of Public Health, and the School of Engineering and Applied Science, GW generates over $320M in annual research expenditures.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

GW spans three vibrant campuses: Foggy Bottom, Mount Vernon, and the GW Virginia Science and Technology Campus (VSTC). Its Science and Engineering Hall features a three-story high-bay structural testing laboratory, nanofabrication cleanrooms, and high-performance computing clusters that support pioneering research with NASA, NIH, NSF, and national laboratories.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate high school curriculum rigor, GPA, personal essays, and recommendations on a test-optional basis. Graduate and doctoral programs require an accredited bachelor's degree with a 3.0+ GPA, program-specific GRE/GMAT/MCAT scores (where mandated), professional writing samples, and academic letters of recommendation.
