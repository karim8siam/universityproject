Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Established in 1850 on the historic Strawberry Hill estate in Twickenham, Greater London, St Mary's University is a leading UK institution renowned for world-class sports science, Olympic athlete training, strength and conditioning, Catholic theology, and teacher education.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Sports science, biomechanics, and strength and conditioning (UKSCA accredited)
  • High-performance endurance running (St Mary's Endurance Performance and Coaching Centre - EPACC)
  • Primary and secondary teacher education (Ofsted Outstanding rating)
  • Sports rehabilitation, physiotherapy, and human bioethics

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Science in Strength and Conditioning (Graduate Degree) — Internationally renowned graduate program training elite performance coaches for Premier League, Olympic, and NFL athletic organizations.
  • Bachelor of Science (Hons) in Sports Rehabilitation (Undergraduate Degree) — BASRaT-accredited clinical degree specializing in musculoskeletal injury diagnosis, manual therapy, and return-to-play rehabilitation.
  • Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Sport, Health and Applied Science (Doctoral Degree) — Research doctorate examining exercise physiology, gait biomechanics, athlete monitoring, and sports psychology.
