University of Naples Federico II: Degrees, Scholarships & Faculty Guide

The University of Naples Federico II (Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II), established by Holy Roman Emperor Frederick II in 1224, is the world's oldest state-funded public university. Located in Naples, Federico II is renowned globally for aerospace engineering, volcanology, pharmaceutical discovery, agricultural science in Portici, and hosting Europe's flagship Apple Developer Academy at the San Giovanni a Teduccio campus.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Doctoral Program in Aerospace and Industrial Engineering (Hypersonic Aerodynamics & Satellite Systems)

Three-year doctoral curriculum in one of Europe's oldest aerospace departments, performing plasma wind tunnel tests, orbital debris tracking, and CubeSat satellite integration.

Apple Developer Academy at Federico II Software & iOS App Architecture Practicum

Nine-month immersive training program at the San Giovanni a Teduccio high-tech campus, mastering Swift programming, SwiftUI interface design, machine learning with CoreML, and startup venture pitching.

ADISURC Campania Regional Higher Education Scholarship and Accommodation Award

Comprehensive regional welfare and merit grant awarded by the Campania regional board, guaranteeing full tuition exemption, campus dormitory accommodation, and dining hall credits.

Department of Agricultural Sciences (Portici) Mediterranean Agroecology Research Assistantship

Two-year research appointment situated at the historic Royal Palace of Portici campus, evaluating regenerative viticulture, bio-stimulant foliar applications, and Mediterranean soil organic matter conservation.

Vesuvius Observatory & INGV Postdoctoral Fellowship in Volcanology and Geohazard Geophysics

Two-year postdoctoral fellowship with the historic Vesuvius Observatory analyzing bradyseismic ground uplift in Campi Flegrei, hydrothermal degassing fluxes, and magma chamber seismic tomography.

Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Data Science and Advanced Machine Learning

Two-year English-medium master's curriculum covering deep neural architectures, distributed big data frameworks (Spark/Hadoop), natural language processing, and ethical artificial intelligence governance.

Laurea Magistrale (M.Sc.) in Medical Biotechnology and Molecular Medicine

Two-year research-oriented graduate degree in cellular immunology, gene therapy vectors, monoclonal antibody engineering, and personalized oncological therapeutic development.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
A 3-year bachelor's degree (Laurea) or recognized foreign equivalent with at least 180 ECTS credits in a coherent academic discipline.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master of Science (Laurea Magistrale) or foreign second-cycle degree; competitive evaluation of research proposal, CV, and oral academic interview.
Language proficiency
Italian-taught courses: B2 Italian certification. English-taught degrees: B2 English certification (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 78 minimum).
Research statement / Proposal
A 2,500-word research proposal outlining scientific rationale, methodology, state of the art, and anticipated doctoral impact.
Application fee
€30 non-refundable application evaluation fee.
Required documentation
Undergraduate diploma, full transcript of records with CIMEA Statement of Comparability, curriculum vitae, passport copy, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition at the University of Naples Federico II is strictly income-based, ranging between €0 and €2,600 per year based on family ISEE parificato. Students with an ISEE below €24,000 pay zero tuition fees and qualify for ADISURC regional scholarships offering up to €7,200 per year in cash stipends plus free student accommodation and meal vouchers. Doctoral candidates receive guaranteed ministerial stipends of €16,243 per year plus 50% funding increases during research stays abroad.
