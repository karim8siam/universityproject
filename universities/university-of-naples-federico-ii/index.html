University of Naples Federico II (Naples, Italy)

Established in 1224 by Frederick II, Holy Roman Emperor, the University of Naples Federico II (Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II - UniNa) is celebrating over 800 years of academic history as the world's oldest publicly funded university. With over 80,000 students, UniNa is a global research powerhouse in aerospace engineering, particle physics (collaborating with CERN and INFN), robotics, and digital technologies (hosting Europe's first Apple Developer Academy). Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years / 180 ECTS) in relevant subject with minimum grade equivalent to 70%+ in Italian university grading.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale or international equivalent), competitive admission examination/interview, and submission of a scientific research proposal.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency for English-taught degrees: Academic IELTS 6.0/6.5 overall or TOEFL iBT 80+ (or CEFR B2 certificate).
Research statement / Proposal
Comprehensive 3 to 5-page research project proposal submitted with the doctoral application.
Application fee
€30 to €50 application fee; tuition fees are calculated progressively based on family ISEE (typically €156 to €2,500/yr, fully waived for scholarship holders).
Required documentation
Complete official transcripts, degree certificates with Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) or CIMEA comparability certificate, CV, and motivation letter.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is calculated progressively according to family financial standing (ISEE), with regional ADISURC grants providing complete tuition waivers, free student canteen meals, and student housing. For doctoral researchers, ministerial fellowships (Borse di Dottorato) provide a tax-exempt stipend of €16,243 net/year, plus a 50% monthly stipend supplement for research conducted abroad and an annual 10% research budget.
