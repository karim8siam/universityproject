Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Located at the foot of the scenic Black Hills, Black Hills State University (BHSU) is a premier regional comprehensive university renowned for its partnership with the Sanford Underground Research Facility (SURF)—conducting deep underground neutrino and dark matter physics—as well as outdoor education and environmental sustainability.

Institution type
Public Comprehensive University (South Dakota Board of Regents)
Campus location
Main Campus Spearfish & BHSU-Rapid City Campus, South Dakota
Accreditation
Higher Learning Commission (HLC) / AACSB / CAEP
Admissions channel

Instruction language
English (IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 61)
Financial aid & support
South Dakota Opportunity Scholarship, BHSU Foundation Awards

Institutional Research Strengths

  • BHSU Underground Campus at Sanford Underground Research Facility (SURF 4,850 feet deep)
  • Pioneering Bachelor of Science in Outdoor Education and experiential wilderness management
  • AACSB-accredited School of Business with entrepreneurship incubators
  • Center for American Indian Studies fostering Lakota cultural and linguistic preservation

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Physics (Underground & Particle Physics Emphasis) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year physics degree with direct laboratory research opportunities at SURF in neutrino astrophysics and dark matter detection.
  • Master of Science in Integrative Genomics (Graduate Degree) — Two-year research-based master's studying ecological genomics, evolutionary genetics, and bioinformatics.
  • Bachelor of Science in Outdoor Education (Undergraduate Degree) — Interdisciplinary major covering wilderness emergency medicine, outdoor adventure programming, and national park stewardship.
