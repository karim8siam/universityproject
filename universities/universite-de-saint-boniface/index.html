Institutional Overview

Université de Saint-Boniface, founded in 1818 in Winnipeg, Manitoba, is the premier francophone university in Western Canada. Affiliated with the University of Manitoba, USB offers accredited university degrees in arts, science, education, business administration, and social work alongside specialized health sciences diplomas.

Academic Strengths & Distinctive Focus

  • Western Canada's historic cradle of French-language higher education and research
  • Biomedical laboratory sciences and bilingual nursing education (École des sciences infirmières)
  • Interdisciplinary Canadian studies, Métis history, and linguistic pluralism
  • Cooperative education placements across Manitoba's public, scientific, and corporate sectors
