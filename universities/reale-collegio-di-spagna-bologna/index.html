Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1364 by Cardinal Gil Álvarez de Albornoz, the Reale Collegio di Spagna (Real Colegio Mayor de San Clemente de los Españoles) in Bologna is the oldest surviving foreign university college in continental Europe. Operating as an autonomous non-profit foundation under the high patronage of the Crown of Spain, it grants prestigious fully funded doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships affiliated with the University of Bologna.

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Comparative and European private law, civil law, and international jurisprudence
  • Historical and political sciences, Roman law, and constitutional theory
  • Humanities, classical philosophy, and Spanish-Italian cultural diplomacy
  • Longitudinal legal scholarship and high-level judicial training

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Beca de Doctorado del Real Colegio de España en Bolonia (Doctoral Residency) — Elite three-year fully funded doctoral fellowship providing private residence, full board, University of Bologna tuition, and living stipend.
  • Beca Posdoctoral de Investigación Jurídica e Histórica (Postdoctoral Fellowship) — One-year residential fellowship for advanced scholars investigating comparative European law, political thought, and historical archives.
  • Programa de Estancias Breves de Investigación para Catedráticos (Visiting Professorship) — Research residency for visiting professors and senior legal scholars utilizing the Collegio's historic library.
