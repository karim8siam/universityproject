Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

ArtEZ University of the Arts in Arnhem is one of the Netherlands' foremost institutions for higher arts education. Its Master of Theatre Practices (now Home of Performance Practices) is an internationally celebrated graduate laboratory for experimental choreography, body-based sonic arts, post-dramatic theatre, and ecological dramaturgy.

Institution type
Public Specialized Arts University (Hogeschool voor de Kunsten)
Campus location
Onderlangs 9 Campus, on the Rhine River banks, Arnhem
Accreditation
NVAO (Accreditation Organisation of the Netherlands and Flanders)
Admissions channel
Annual auditions and portfolio assessments in February and April
Instruction language
English (international master's cohort)
Financial aid & support
NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship), ArtEZ Talent Grants, DUO Study Finance

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Internationally renowned Master of Performance Practices accredited with highest European honors
  • Prime riverside campus in Arnhem equipped with black box theatres and sound labs
  • Close partnerships with Holland Festival, Spring Festival Utrecht, and Tanzquartier Wien
  • Interdisciplinary framework merging contemporary dance, somatic activism, and performance philosophy

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Master of Performance Practices (MA) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year research-based master's for choreographers, performance artists, and theatre makers developing transformative artistic methodologies.
  • Bachelor of Dance (Dance Artist / Choreography) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year comprehensive dance conservatory training performers in contemporary floorwork, partnering, improvisation, and somatic techniques.
  • Bachelor of Theatre in Education (Docent Theater) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year pedagogical and artistic degree qualifying theatre makers to teach, direct community plays, and foster social arts projects.
