Campus Lidköping: Higher Vocational Care, Digital Health & Folkbildning Guide

Campus Lidköping, operating in close collaboration with ABF and regional health authorities in Västra Götaland, is a major higher vocational education (Yrkeshögskola - YH) center specializing in welfare technology, specialized healthcare nursing, and lifelong community education.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Completion of Swedish upper secondary school (Gymnasieexamen) or equivalent international secondary school credentials with eligibility in Swedish and Healthcare.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Not offering independent doctoral studies; focus on accredited professional higher vocational qualifications (Yrkeshögskoleexamen).
Language proficiency
Swedish 2 or Swedish as a Second Language 2 (Svenska som andraspråk 2); English 5 / English A equivalent.
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of vocational intent describing commitment to municipal elderly care or welfare technology implementation.
Application fee
Zero application fee through the Swedish YH application portal.
Required documentation
Certified upper secondary grades, work experience certificates in health or social care, and valid identification.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Tuition is free (SEK 0) for Swedish and EU/EEA citizens under Swedish state funding through Myndigheten för yrkeshögskolan (MYH). Swedish CSN student financial aid (studielån och studiebidrag) provides approximately SEK 12,000 per month for eligible learners.
