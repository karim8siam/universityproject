Kyoto University of Foreign Studies (KUFS)

Master global diplomacy, conference translation, and cultural diplomacy across 19 languages at Kyoto University of Foreign Studies.

Institutional Overview & Campus Profile

Founded in 1947, Kyoto University of Foreign Studies (Kyoto Gaikokugo Daigaku) is a premier private language and international affairs university in western Kyoto. Operating under the motto PAX MUNDI PER LINGUAS ('World Peace through Languages'), KUFS offers intensive education in 19 foreign languages, international relations, translation science, and Japanese language pedagogy.

Institution type
Private Foreign Studies and International Affairs University

Institutional Research Strengths

  • Instruction in 19 world languages coupled with area studies and international diplomacy
  • Department of Global Studies taught 100% in English (no Japanese prerequisite for admission)
  • State-of-the-art multilingual self-access learning hub (NINJA - Navigating Information for Japanese and Global Autonomy)
  • Strong placement record in Japanese foreign diplomacy, UN agencies, and global trading companies (Sogo Shosha)

Featured Academic Degrees & Professional Programs

  • Bachelor of Arts in Global Studies (Department of Global Studies) (Undergraduate Degree) — Four-year English-taught major examining international peace and conflict, sustainable development, and cross-cultural communication.
  • Bachelor of Arts in Global Tourism (Undergraduate Degree) — Specialized degree exploring cultural heritage management, sustainable eco-tourism, and airline/hospitality governance.
  • Master of Arts in Language and Culture (Graduate School of Foreign Studies) (Graduate Degree) — Two-year master's program with specializations in translation and interpreting, applied linguistics, and comparative cultural studies.
