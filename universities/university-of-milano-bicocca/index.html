University of Milano-Bicocca (Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca)

Established in 1998 in Milan's vibrant northern industrial innovation hub, the University of Milano-Bicocca (UNIMIB) is one of Italy's most dynamic and research-intensive public universities. Designed as a modern campus university, Bicocca enrolls over 38,000 students across state-of-the-art facilities in Greco-Pirelli. Bicocca leads Italian and European research in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Materials Science, Nanomedicine, and Marine Ecology. Verified degree programs and opportunities

Review checked academic pathways and funding options below:

Bicocca Doctoral Fellowships (Borse di Dottorato di Ricerca)

Three-year ministerial and university doctoral scholarships covering living stipends, international research mobility supplements, and full tuition waivers for top-ranking candidates in the annual public doctoral competition.

Bicocca PhD in Computer Science

Three-year doctoral curriculum in the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication (DISCo), conducting research in machine learning, big data analytics, natural computing, and cyber security.

Bicocca Master of Science in Data Science

Two-year English-taught Master of Science (120 ECTS) in the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication (DISCo), focusing on big data infrastructure, statistical learning, business intelligence, and deep neural networks.

Bicocca Master of Science in Materials Science

World-class two-year Master's degree (120 ECTS) offered in English inside the Department of Materials Science, featuring specializations in Optoelectronics, Photovoltaics, Nanomaterials, and Energy Storage.

Bicocca Nanomedicine and Optoelectronics Laboratory Placements

Cutting-edge laboratory research positions inside the Department of Materials Science at Bicocca, developing silicon photonics, luminescent solar concentrators, targeted nanomedicines, and next-generation battery anodes.

Bicocca International Traineeships & ERASMUS+ Research Internships

Two to six-month structured laboratory traineeships enabling international students to participate in experimental biological, chemical, and physical sciences research at Bicocca's university laboratories.

Bicocca 150-Hour Student Collaborations (Collaborazioni a tempo parziale)

Official university part-time employment contracts (150 hours total) assisting in university libraries, computer laboratories, science museums, and international student reception desks across Bicocca campuses.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Accredited Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years, 180 ECTS) in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or Engineering satisfying curricular prerequisite requirements.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Recognized second-cycle Master's degree (Laurea Magistrale, 120 ECTS) with high graduation mark (typically 100/110+) and successful public evaluation of research project proposal and oral examination.
Language proficiency
Demonstrated English proficiency equivalent to CEFR B2/C1: Academic IELTS 6.0/6.5 minimum or TOEFL iBT 80+ for English-medium graduate curricula.
Research statement / Proposal
For PhD: detailed research project proposal of 3 to 5 pages outlining research aims, state of the art, methodology, and expected scientific contributions.
Application fee
€10 to €30 SegreterieOnline concorso registration fee.
Required documentation
University degree certificate, official transcripts with grading table, CV, motivation letter, and identity document.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Under Italian public university regulations, annual tuition fees are income-based (calcolata in base all'ISEE parificato). For international students from non-EU countries, tuition is capped between €1,000 and €3,200 per year. Admitted PhD students holding ministerial scholarships study 100% tuition-free and receive an annual living allowance of €16,243.
