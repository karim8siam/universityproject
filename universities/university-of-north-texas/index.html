University of North Texas

UNT · Denton, Texas, United States

The University of North Texas (UNT) is an elite Carnegie R1 public research university located in Denton, Texas, within the dynamic Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. Internationally renowned for having one of the world's most prestigious music institutions in the College of Music—featuring the legendary One O'Clock Lab Band—UNT is equally celebrated for materials science, additive manufacturing at Discovery Park, environmental philosophy, and logistics.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UNT spans 14 colleges and schools including the College of Music, College of Engineering, College of Science, College of Visual Arts and Design (CVAD), G. Brint Ryan College of Business, and the College of Information. Key research hubs include the 300-acre Discovery Park engineering facility and the Center for Agile and Adaptive Additive Manufacturing (CAAAM).

Research admissions & international qualifications

Undergraduate admissions evaluate class rank, high school GPA, and test scores. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
