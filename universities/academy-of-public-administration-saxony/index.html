HSF Meißen: Public Governance Degrees, State Scholarships & Faculty Guide

Hochschule Meißen (FH) und Fortbildungszentrum (HSF Meißen), situated overlooking the historic porcelain city of Meissen, is the Free State of Saxony's premier public university for public administration, tax law, social security governance, and state judicial systems. Operating as a state-funded dual higher education institution, students are appointed as civil service candidates (Beamtenanwärter) receiving full monthly salaries throughout their studies.

Verified degree programs and opportunities

Saxon State Civil Service Training Stipend (Anwärterbezüge)

State-guaranteed monthly stipend program providing complete financial independence and civil service health insurance benefits for dual administration students.

Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) in General Public Administration and E-Government

Three-year dual bachelor's degree program integrating constitutional law, public financial management, administrative informatics, and municipal governance.

Master of Science in Digital Governance and Administrative Informatics (Feeder)

Advanced postgraduate program training digital transformation officers to spearhead OZG implementation, public cloud infrastructure, and AI workflow integration in Saxony.

Saxon Municipal Digitalization and Public Data Sovereignty Project

Empirical administrative research project evaluating digital public services, citizen portal usability, and cross-agency data interoperability across central and eastern Saxony.

Saxon State Ministry of Finance and Tax Administration Dual Practicum

Structured dual internship rotating students through corporate taxation, real property assessment, and fiscal audit divisions across Saxon municipal tax offices.

Meißen Public Leadership and European Governance Fellowship

Specialized academic fellowship promoting cross-border public policy research and administrative collaboration in Central European euro-regions.

Professor (W2) in Public Administrative Law and Municipal Governance

Tenured W2 professorship responsible for lecturing core public law curricula, directing administrative research, and examining civil service candidates.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Graduation from an accredited Bachelor's degree in Public Administration, Administrative Law, or Business Economics with minimum grade 2.5 on the German grading scale.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Undergraduate and master's dual programs prepare civil servants for higher civil service (Höherer Dienst) leadership and doctoral research partnerships with Saxon universities.
Language proficiency
German language proficiency at CEFR C1/C2 level (TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2) required for public law curriculum and civil service legal practice.
Research statement / Proposal
Applicants undergo centralized selection through the Saxon State Personnel Office (Landespersonalamt Sachsen) including cognitive and legal aptitude assessments.
Application fee
Zero application fee; centralized civil service application is entirely free of charge.
Required documentation
Abitur or certified secondary school certificate, comprehensive CV, German language certificate, and declaration of loyalty to the German Constitution.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

In accordance with Saxon higher education legislation and civil service training regulations, students pay zero tuition fees. Admitted candidates are appointed as preparatory civil servants (Beamtenanwärter) receiving a guaranteed monthly gross training salary of approximately €1,450 to €1,600 throughout their three-year degree program.
