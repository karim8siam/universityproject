University of Texas at El Paso

UTEP · El Paso, Texas, United States

The University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP) is a premier Carnegie R1 public research university and America's leading Hispanic-serving doctoral institution, established in 1914. Situated on the U.S.-Mexico border against the Franklin Mountains, UTEP features distinctive Bhutanese architecture and is world-renowned for 3D additive manufacturing at the W.M. Keck Center, border health, aerospace propulsion, and biomedical sciences.

Academic overview & strategic disciplines

UTEP comprises nine colleges and schools: the College of Engineering, College of Science, College of Health Sciences, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, Woody L. Hunt College of Business, College of Liberal Arts, and College of Education. Flagship research centers include the W.M. Keck Center for 3D Innovation and the Aerospace Center.

Research admissions & international qualifications

Admissions are holistic and test-optional for undergraduate applicants. Graduate admissions require an accredited bachelor's degree with a minimum 3.0 GPA, statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and English language proficiency (TOEFL 79+ or IELTS 6.5+).
