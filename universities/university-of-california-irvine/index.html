UC Irvine: Computer Science, Earth Systems Science, Medicine & Physics Guide

The University of California, Irvine (UC Irvine / UCI) is a prestigious AAU top-tier public research university in Southern California, celebrated for founding the Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences, three Nobel Prizes in chemistry and physics, and pioneering Earth System Science research.

Admissions, language requirements & application process

Master's admission eligibility
Bachelor's degree from a recognized institution with minimum cumulative 3.0 GPA in undergraduate studies.
Doctoral admission eligibility
Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant discipline with strong research background and minimum 3.2+ GPA.
Language proficiency
TOEFL iBT 80 minimum (26+ speaking recommended for TA eligibility) or IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (minimum 6.0 each band).
Research statement / Proposal
Statement of Purpose detailing specific research experiences, proposed research directions, and intended faculty advisors.
Application fee
$135 for domestic applicants / $155 for international applicants via UCI Graduate Division.
Required documentation
Official academic transcripts, statement of purpose, personal history statement, resume/CV, and three letters of recommendation.

Tuition fees and verified scholarship systems

Doctoral students in STEM and informatics receive fully funded packages providing 100% coverage of tuition and student fees, comprehensive UC SHIP health insurance, and guaranteed minimum annual stipends of $36,000 to $44,000 through GSR and TA appointments.
